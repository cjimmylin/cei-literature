## Abstract

Suite of Marathi NLP resources including MahaCorpus, MahaSent (sentiment), MahaNER, MahaHate datasets and MahaBERT/MahaGPT models. Comprehensive Marathi NLP ecosystem including hate speech and sentiment datasets.

## Benchmark

## Data Description

Suite of Marathi NLP datasets: MahaCorpus (24.8M sentences), MahaSent (sentiment, 15.8K), MahaNER (25K sentences), MahaHate (25K tweets)
