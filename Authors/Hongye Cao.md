# Hongye Cao

## Research Summary

Hongye Cao is affiliated with the School of Intelligence Science and Technology at Nanjing University and China Mobile Research Institute in Beijing. Their research focuses on AI safety, particularly evaluating and improving the security of large language models against adversarial attacks. As lead author of SafeDialBench (2025), Cao developed a fine-grained benchmark for assessing LLM safety in multi-turn dialogues across 7 jailbreak attack strategies, featuring over 4000 multi-turn dialogues in both Chinese and English. This work moves beyond single-turn safety evaluation to assess models' ability to maintain safety consistency over prolonged interactions where context and adversarial pressure accumulate. The research addresses critical gaps in evaluating LLMs' capabilities in detecting and handling unsafe information.
