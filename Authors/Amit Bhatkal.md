# Amit Bhatkal

## Research Summary

Amit Bhatkal is a researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). Their research focuses on multilingual and multimodal AI systems, contributing to evaluation frameworks for assessing AI models across diverse languages and cultural contexts. They have participated in collaborative research efforts to develop comprehensive benchmarks for culturally-aware AI systems.
