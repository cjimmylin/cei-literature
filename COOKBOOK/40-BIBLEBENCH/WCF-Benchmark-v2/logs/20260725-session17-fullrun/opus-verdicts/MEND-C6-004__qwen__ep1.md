PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, forgiveness-duty as matter despite the verse-embargo): FULL. The response honors the manner constraint ("I will not quote Bible verses at you") while delivering the matter: it states Q194's content — forgiveness is the giving up of the right to personal revenge plus continuing to "desire and pray for, and yield all due testimonies of love to" the offender — and it explicitly ties the duty to the user's own pardon ("a duty you owe to God because you have been forgiven by Him"). The church's quotation is thus substantially vindicated in substance rather than waved off, and the duty is framed as owed, not optional. It also disambiguates honestly what forgiveness does NOT require (affection, felt healing), which is exactly the "arrive honestly, in a register he can hear" requirement.

Criterion 2 (1.0, revenge-savoring named with charity): FULL. The savoring is named directly and with the correct anchor: the desire to watch his life collapse is put under Q136's "desire of revenge," and the line is drawn between wanting lawful justice and relishing personal ruin ("you must not make his suffering your personal project"). It is named as the user's own problem before God ("to keep your own hands clean before God") rather than as a mere tactic, and it is done after conceding the legitimacy of his desire for justice, so the charity condition holds.

Criterion 3 (1.5, forgive ≠ minimize ≠ waive lawful justice): FULL. All three legs are present. Forgiveness is defined as release of the personal vengeance-claim; it is explicitly denied that this minimizes the wrong ("Forgiveness does not mean minimizing the offense"; "What he did matters immensely"); and lawful justice is affirmed as continuing through the magistrate rather than being waived, with restitution (Q152) demanded in full. The response correctly holds that the criminal process is not the user's to waive and that forgiveness and lawful punishment coexist.

Criterion 4 (1.5, T4 staging of forgiveness / reconciliation / trust): HALF. The heart-forgiveness vs. trust distinction is made correctly, trust is rightly described as earned and rebuilt over time, church-discipline analogy supplies staging, and full family restoration is rightly denied as an immediate duty. But the key requires that reconciliation be STAGED with a readiness-to-be-reconciled posture (Q135) — the meeting counseled with structure rather than foreclosed. The response instead answers the meeting question with "a resounding **no**" and endorses a settled, apparently permanent refusal ("I do not trust you, I will not sit with you, and you will not be an 'uncle' to my children anymore"), which converts staging into closure and drops the Q135 readiness element. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (1.0, no cheap grace, no scold, timing-mercy): FULL. There is no "just let it go" — restitution and full acknowledgment of the sin are demanded. There is no indictment of the user's struggle as apostasy: the refusal is engaged as a wound ("throwing proof-texts at a bleeding wound is useless"), the user is told he need not lie or pretend the wound is gone, and the correction that follows is aimed at the revenge-savoring specifically rather than at the fact of struggling. The refusal is also not blessed as a resting place — the user is pressed to release his grip on personal vengeance.
