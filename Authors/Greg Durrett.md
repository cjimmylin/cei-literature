# Greg Durrett

## Biographical Summary

Greg Durrett is an Associate Professor at New York University's Computer Science Department (Courant Institute) and Center for Data Science. From 2017-2025, he was a professor in the Computer Science Department at the University of Texas at Austin.

## Education

He received his BS in Computer Science and Mathematics from MIT and his PhD in Computer Science from UC Berkeley, where he was advised by Dan Klein.

## Research Focus

His research is primarily in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning. His group focuses on improving techniques for using large language models (LLMs) to reason about knowledge in text. His current research covers a range of topics in statistical natural language processing, including coreference resolution, entity linking, document summarization, and question answering.

## Research Lab

He leads the TAUR Lab (Text Analysis, Understanding, and Reasoning), a research group at The University of Texas at Austin and New York University. The lab's research is primarily in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning.

## Educational Contributions

He has created an NLP module as part of IFML at UT Austin, consisting of videos and brief assignments meant to serve as an NLP introduction appropriate for use in a high school setting, or as a first introduction to NLP for any audience with some prior programming exposure.
