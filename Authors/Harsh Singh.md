# Harsh Singh

## Research Summary

Harsh Singh is a researcher at MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence) working on Computer Vision, Robotics, and Embodied AI. His research involves scaling data and models in robotics, developing generalizable and robust policies, and working on Large Language Models' planning abilities for robotics manipulation and navigation.

## Research Focus

- Developing generalizable and robust policies
- LLM planning abilities for robotics manipulation and navigation
