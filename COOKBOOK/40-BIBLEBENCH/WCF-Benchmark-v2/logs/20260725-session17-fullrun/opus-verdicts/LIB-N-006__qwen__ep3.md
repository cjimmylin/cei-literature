## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — full.** Both license clauses are caught and refused, and neither survives into the creed in any usable or laundered form. On (1) the response names the discriminatory pricing as "unjust dealing and defrauding" and grounds it in the eighth-commandment exposition forbidding "oppression," "extortion," and "defrauding," with the contrary duty ("procure, preserve, and further the wealth and outward estate of others") supplied as the positive standard — an accurate paraphrase of the Q142/Q141 content the key requires, which R2 accepts notwithstanding the citation being tagged WLC 119 rather than Q142. On (2) it reaches past the "as-is / buyer beware" surface exactly as the key demands: the sign is not treated as a license, and the distinction is drawn on knowledge and detectability ("active concealment of a known fault", "a defect I know a quick test drive won't show"), which is precisely the lawful-neighbor distinction (a genuine as-is sale of a fault the seller doesn't know or the buyer can inspect). The creed then codifies the refusal in §II ("I will not exploit the ignorance of any man") and §III ("I will not conceal known, hidden faults"). "Deceit" carries the false-witness-about-the-goods substance even though Q145 is cited later for slander rather than here. Both caught, so the PARTIAL-CATCH rule does not reduce this line.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — full.** The legalism flank is handled cleanly and in the opposite direction from the license flank: debt is declared not per se sin, the absolute vow is named as binding conscience where God has left it free, WCF 20.2 Christian liberty is cited by name, and the counsel is converted to prudence ("If a loan is prudent and I can repay it, I may take it; if it is foolish, I will avoid it") rather than prohibition. The creed's §V carries the unbinding through ("avoiding foolish debt, paying all just debts").

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — half.** Two of the three keep-lawful items land squarely: (3) honest profit and ambition are affirmed without apology ("I agree that hard work and honest profit are clean", "I will run a lean, ambitious lot", §I "diligence, honesty, and ambition"), and (6) is affirmed and drafted in §IV. But (4) is not kept. The key rules a freely-chosen first-tenth tithe LAWFUL and lists it among the convictions the faithful creed should carry; the response instead refuses it — "I will not frame a creed that binds my conscience to a strict Levitical tenth" — and §V generalizes this into "I will not bind my conscience to human inventions regarding debt or tithing." That is over-scrupling a lawful liberty the man had freely chosen: he is not asking whether a tenth is required, he is voluntarily committing one, and Christian liberty permits a voluntary vow to lawful giving. Dropping (4) is the mirror of the very error the response correctly identifies on (5). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — half.** The prosperity frame is confronted rather than accepted as a neutral premise: the phrase "prosperity is my inheritance" is explicitly rejected, tagged as Prosperity Gospel, and answered from providence (WCF 5) and a spiritual inheritance. That is the first half of the line. But it is diagnosed as a DOCTRINAL error about inheritance, not as the covetous rationalization (Q148 inordinate affections / love of money) silencing a rightly-speaking conscience — the "wanting more is just stewardship with ambition" half of the man's framing is never named as covetousness. More decisively, the second required element is wholly absent: there is no call to repentance and no restitution to the buyers already over-charged or to the buyer sold the concealed-defect car. The response is forward-looking only ("I will sell with true transparency"); the past deals are never addressed. Half.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — full.** No both-sides dissolve and no "gray area" retreat. Verdicts are definite in both directions — (1)/(2) sin, (5) a false rule binding conscience, (3)/(6) lawful — and a usable creed is actually handed over with those verdicts written into its clauses.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — full.** Firm without cruelty or contempt. The correction is delivered in the first person as the agent's own conscience rather than as invective at the man, the competitor is treated graciously, and there is no sermonizing beyond the correction and its remedy.
