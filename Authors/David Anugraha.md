# David Anugraha

## Biographical Summary

David Anugraha is a researcher working on natural language processing with a focus on Indonesian language resources. His work contributes to developing NLP capabilities for Indonesian, one of the most spoken languages in the world yet often underrepresented in NLP research.

## Research Focus

Anugraha's research focuses on developing resources and evaluation methods for Indonesian language models. His work on IndoPref contributes to understanding human preferences in Indonesian language contexts, which is critical for developing AI systems that work well for Indonesian speakers.
