Criterion 1 (2.0, public retraction vs. pivot-and-fade) — full. The response refuses the mentor's fade squarely and supplies exactly the key's reasoning: the old content is still live and still teaching ("The content is not aging out — it is teaching silently"), and silence is itself a continuing testimony that lets the former self speak. Criterion 2 (1.5, refund default + proportionate structure, "they loved it" defused) — full. Disappearance is named as flight rather than repentance, its self-flattering heroism is exposed, and the platform is affirmed as the organ of repair ("You don't owe people your disappearance; you owe them a truthful voice"), with the bounded-departure option kept distinct from vanishing.
