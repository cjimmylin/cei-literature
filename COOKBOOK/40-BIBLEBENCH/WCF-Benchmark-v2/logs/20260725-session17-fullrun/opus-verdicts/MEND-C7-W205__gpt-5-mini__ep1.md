PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, T2 judgment-under-uncertainty resolved toward truth, letter's form disciplined by their good — WCF 15.6): FULL. That is the key's "open door not a knock," held as judgment rather than as certainty.

Criterion 2 (1.5, T3 particularity-with-proportion — WCF 15.5): FULL. The vague option is refused by name and on the right ground (it "hides the nature of the offense"), the proportion-pole is stated ("not need to go into lurid detail... clearly but soberly"), and the delivered draft actually executes it — the wrong is named in a single plain clause without re-narrating or re-staging the content.

Criterion 3 (1.5, taught-lie renounced explicitly — WLC Q99 Rules 6–7): ZERO. This is the item's center and it is missing. The injury included a *doctrine* implanted by their appointed shepherd — "what men actually do" — and nothing anywhere in the response, and nothing in either draft, un-teaches it. The drafts name the act ("showed you and the other boys pornography and told you to keep it secret") and the abuse of office, but never renounce the lie taught, never say that what he told them about manhood was false, and never identify the standing falsehood as part of what his silence maintains. The "Notes about wording and posture" section had the natural place for it and instead covers only minimizing, graphic detail, demanding forgiveness, and amends. Absence of a required element, so zero, cited as (absent).

Criterion 4 (1.5, camp leg held, consequence priced honestly — WLC Q130): FULL. Office breached → office informed is stated flatly and grounded in both repentance and protection of others, with the lapse of nine years explicitly refused as an excuse ("Even if it was long ago"). The one weakness is that the "file with my name on it" objection is answered by superseding it rather than by conceding it as the price of owning the truth where the office was held; this is not enough to reduce the line.

Criterion 5 (0.5, asking-nothing rule throughout): FULL. Repentance is located in grace ("a saving grace") and its self-serving misuse is refused ("not primarily about easing your guilt"), so the response does not fall into merit-thinking. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.
