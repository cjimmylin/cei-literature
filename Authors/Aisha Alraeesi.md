# Aisha Alraeesi

## Research Summary

Aisha Alraeesi is a researcher contributing to Arabic NLP and cultural AI research. She participated in the PALM project, a large-scale community-driven effort involving 44 researchers across all 22 Arab countries to create culturally inclusive datasets for Arabic Large Language Models. Her work focuses on ensuring AI systems are sensitive to the linguistic and cultural diversity of the Arab world.
