## Abstract

Measures censorship bias in popular LLMs regarding topics sensitive to PRC government, including Uyghur persecution in Xinjiang and Tibet. Introduces methodology for identifying and measuring censorship bias. All models evaluated show evidence of censorship bias. Chinese-built models explicitly adhere to information controls.
