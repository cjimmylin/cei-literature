# Common to Whom? Regional Cultural Commonsense and LLM Bias in India

[[Sangmitra Madhusudan]], [[Trush Shashank More]], [[Steph Buongiorno]], [[Renata Dividino]], [[Jad Kabbara]], [[Ali Emami]]

## Abstract

Introduces Indica benchmark testing LLMs' ability to handle regional vs national cultural commonsense in India. Only 39.4% of questions get agreement across all 5 regions. Models achieve only 13.4-20.9% accuracy on region-specific questions with geographic bias favoring North/Central India. Tests regional cultural commonsense across Indian regions including South India (relevant to Dravidian languages). Reveals geographic bias in LLMs underrepresenting South Indian cultural knowledge.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Indica

## Data Description

INDICA benchmark: 515 questions across 8 everyday-life domains with 1,630 region-specific question-answer pairs collected from human annotators across 5 Indian regions (North, South, East, West, Central). Dataset and codebase are stated to be available on GitHub and HuggingFace but no specific URL is provided in the paper.
