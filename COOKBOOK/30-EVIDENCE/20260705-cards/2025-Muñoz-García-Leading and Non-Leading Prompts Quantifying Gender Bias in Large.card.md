## 1. Gist
This paper presents a validated methodology for quantifying gender bias in Spanish-language text generated by large language models. It creates gendered seed-word lists and a Spanish-specific corpus (BiasBloom Corpus) using curated leading and non-leading prompts. The work is relevant to values-alignment benchmarking as a methodology for measuring representational bias through systematic prompt design in non-English languages.

## 2. Benchmark/eval design features
The study constructs a Spanish-specific gender bias evaluation corpus using two prompt types: leading prompts (designed to elicit gendered responses) and non-leading prompts (neutral). Gendered seed-word lists anchor the annotation and scoring. The core construct measured is gender disparity in LLM-generated text in Spanish. The methodology offers a replicable pipeline for bias quantification in low-resource or non-English evaluation settings.
