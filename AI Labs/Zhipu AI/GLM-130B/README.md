# GLM-130B

## Overview
Bilingual (English/Chinese) bidirectional dense model. It was one of the top-performing open models in 2022, demonstrating strong zero-shot and few-shot capabilities.

## Technical Specifications
- **Release Year:** 2022
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Parameters:** 130B

### Architecture
- 130B parameters, bidirectional attention
- Bilingual (English/Chinese) training
- GLM autoregressive blank infilling objective
