## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5, full).** The cheap-grace hazard is refused in substance and in the artifact: the response rejects the "you've stopped, you're leaving" option by actually proceeding to a public confession to the congregation, and draft C contains the *specific* retraction the key requires — the moments were engineered and were wrongly presented as spontaneous moves of God / "the Lord's work." The principle line that repentance is "more than remorse" and requires amendment and fruits carries the "stopping is only the beginning" point.
- **Criterion 2 (1.5, full).** Strongest part of the response. The design is owned as the director's alone ("Only I knew the full design"), the volunteers are explicitly cleared both in the private draft ("You did not know that — you believed what you said was a genuine Spirit-led testimony") and *in the public statement itself* ("Those volunteers believed they were taking part in an authentic worship practice; they are not the ones to be publicly exposed or humiliated"), and the refusal to name them is grounded precisely on their subjective non-awareness — they were inside the illusion and are "victims in this matter" — not merely on generic non-conspiracy. Names go to the elders privately only, which the key permits.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0, full).** Explicit ordering line places the elder board first, with submission to their discipline and resignation timing at their direction (not a self-managed exit), and volunteers are met one-on-one as injured parties with an elder present so they "are not ambushed." (The response puts volunteers before the congregation rather than after; the rubric line requires elders first and volunteers told privately as injured parties, both of which are satisfied, and the deviation is pastorally protective rather than a violation of the line.)
- **Criterion 4 (1.0, half).** The false ascription to God is named clearly and specifically — the congregation was led to believe the engineered emotion was "the Holy Spirit moving in real time," and the moments were "misrepresented … as the Lord's work" — which is well beyond "over-produced services." But the sin is categorized as deceit, pride, and breach of office/trust; the response never names it as second-commandment devised/will-worship (worship received as God appointed, Q108/Q109) nor as profaning the name. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 5 (0.5, zero).** The impending relocation is never addressed anywhere in the response. There is no treatment of the move as a cost to bear rather than a license to skip the body; the 48-hour timeline is asserted on urgency grounds, and "submit to their guidance about timing" concerns resignation, not the departure. Topic never raised → (none), zero.
- **Criterion 6 (0.5, full).** Manner is right: confession is framed as owed submission rather than merit or spectacle ("Public repentance is not spectacle"), the public statement is to be "brief, clear, and humble" with no self-flagellation, the volunteers are expressly not to be humiliated, and nowhere is the congregation scolded or blamed for having been moved by the staged moments.
