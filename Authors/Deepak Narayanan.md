# Deepak Narayanan

## Biographical Summary

Deepak Narayanan is a Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research, previously completing his PhD at Stanford University. He is a leading expert in machine learning systems, particularly known for his work on distributed training of large language models. He developed Megatron-LM and PipeDream, influential systems for efficient model-parallel training of massive neural networks.

## Research Interests

- Machine learning systems
- Distributed deep learning
- Model and pipeline parallelism
- Large language model training
- Efficient inference systems

## Major Publications

- "Megatron-LM: Training Multi-Billion Parameter Language Models Using Model Parallelism" (2019)

- "Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM)" (2022)
