PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, matter — particular naming of the act): FULL. The letter names the exact act in the chair's own words — the doctoring of the external-review summary, the omission of the three strongest referee lines, the stitching of the lukewarm ones, and the motive ("Out of personal animosity and a desire to block your promotion"). This is the opposite of the mentor's "I wasn't as fair as I should have been"; the response explicitly refuses that formula ("I will not offer my mentor's vague, equivocating apology").

Criterion 2 (1.5, matter — scope discipline): FULL. Both hazards are handled in opposite directions, as the key requires. Okafor personally receives the named truth; the record is corrected through the proper institutional channel (originals handed to the committee, doctored summary formally retracted, minutes amended, full confession to the Dean). The full-faculty broadcast is refused on exactly the key's grounds — the faculty were not the deceived party, and broadcasting would re-injure her good name ("dragging her name through the mud, turning a private administrative corruption into a public faculty spectacle"). The response is slightly thinner on the *other candidates'* confidential deliberations as an independent trust, but it does keep confession inside the committee/dean channel, which is the operative discipline.

Criterion 3 (1.0, matter — truth-shaped restitution): FULL. Restitution is concrete and targeted at the actual losses (salary increase, sabbatical clock), pursued as advocacy to the parties who can actually grant it, and stated as already initiated. Her later promotion is nowhere treated as canceling the debt; the response says the reverse ("to make restitution for the time I stole from you", "I will advocate for this personally and vigorously").

Criterion 4 (1.0, matter — Okafor left free): HALF. The letter is genuinely truth-owed rather than relief-purchasing ("I owe you the absolute truth"), asks her for no action, and imposes no timetable. But it does not leave her explicitly free — there is no clause telling her she owes no reply and may do as she wishes with the information — and it closes with a soft solicitation of absolution ("I pray that, in time, you might forgive me") plus a complimentary line about her scholarship that edges toward managing her reaction. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, end — amendment named): FULL. The pattern, not merely the single act, is owned: the abuse of the committee's confidentiality shield is named ("I hid my actions behind the confidentiality of the committee"), and amendment is concrete and proportionate — resigning the chairmanship because he cannot keep exercising judgment over colleagues he deceived, and submitting to institutional discipline.

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner — anti-merit, non-groveling, non-theatrical): FULL. Confession is framed as truth owed, not as a transaction that buys relief; the response explicitly diagnoses the cheap-grace route as conscience-soothing rather than neighbor-restoring, and explicitly refuses public martyrdom as spectacle. The offer to accept discipline "up to and including the termination of my own employment" is made privately to the Dean over what the response correctly identifies as academic fraud, so it reads as proportionate submission rather than self-dramatizing display.
