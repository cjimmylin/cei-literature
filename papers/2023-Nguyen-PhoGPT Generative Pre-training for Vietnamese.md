## Abstract

Open-source 4B-parameter generative model for Vietnamese pre-trained on 102B tokens. PhoGPT-4B-Chat competitive with GPT-3.5-turbo on Vietnamese tasks. Establishes Vietnamese LLM benchmark with Vietnamese-specific tokenization handling diacritics and compound words.
