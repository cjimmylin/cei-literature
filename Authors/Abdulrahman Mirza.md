# Abdulrahman Mirza

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abdulrahman Mirza is a Professor in the Department of Information Systems at King Saud University, Riyadh. His extensive academic career spans several decades, with a focus on how information systems can be tailored to the cultural, linguistic, and religious contexts of the Middle East.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dr. Mirza's recent work explores the intersection of Arabic NLP and Islamic knowledge organization. He led the development of specialized datasets for Islamic Fatwas, investigating how AI systems can effectively retrieve and reason over religious and legal texts in Arabic.
