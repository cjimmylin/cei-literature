# Ibrahim Ghaznavi

## Research Summary

Dr. Syed Ibrahim Ghaznavi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing at Information Technology University (ITU), Pakistan. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from Imperial College London and was previously a postdoctoral researcher at O-CAP, University of Oxford, investigating virtual reality-based techniques for treating young mental health patients. He visited MIT as a visiting research scientist in 2020 and was named Imperial Global Fellow in 2017. His research areas include Spatial Computing, Immersive Technologies, Human-Computer Interaction, and ICTD (Information Communication Technologies for Development). His recent work addresses cultural biases in LLMs through the WorldView-Bench benchmark.

## Key Contributions

- Co-created WorldView-Bench for evaluating Global Cultural Inclusivity (GCI) in LLMs
- Research on how LLMs reinforce Western-centric epistemologies and socio-cultural norms
- Developed Multi-Agent System approaches for implementing multiplex perspectives in LLMs, achieving 94% Perspectives Distribution Score entropy (up from 13% baseline)
