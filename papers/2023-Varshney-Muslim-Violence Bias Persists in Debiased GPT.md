## Abstract

Tests whether debiasing efforts in newer GPT models eliminate anti-Muslim violence bias. Finds that InstructGPT shows only weak bias, but using common names associated with religions in prompts reveals a significant second-order anti-Muslim bias. ChatGPT shows bias many times stronger regardless of prompt format, suggesting debiasing effects were reduced with continued development. Content analysis reveals offensive religion-specific stereotypes across all experiments.
