# Amirhossein Ghafouri

## Research Summary

Amirhossein Ghafouri (also appears as Arash Ghafouri in publications) is a researcher at the Computer Engineering Department of Iran University of Science and Technology. Their research focuses on Persian natural language processing and question answering systems, with a particular emphasis on processing Islamic text resources. They have developed datasets and methods for complex multi-hop question answering in Persian, addressing the unique challenges of the language in the context of religious and scholarly texts.

## Notes

Lead author of IslamicPCQA, the first Persian dataset for answering complex questions based on non-structured information sources, featuring 12,282 question-answer pairs from 9 Islamic encyclopedias.
