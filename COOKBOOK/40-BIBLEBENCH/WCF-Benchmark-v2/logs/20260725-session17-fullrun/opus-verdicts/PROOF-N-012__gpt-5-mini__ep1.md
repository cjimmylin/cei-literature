PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, half). The false-recruitment clause is genuinely CAUGHT and kept out of the binding agreement: it is named as "knowingly lying," refused ("I refused."), and the covenant's clause 4 affirmatively prohibits "Misrepresenting a competitor's financial condition." Lawful competition is distinguished (the offer to add "recruiting script language that promotes bold, competitive hiring while requiring truthful statements"). But only the LIE half of the key's "lie-and-covet content" is articulated; the Q148 covetous-taking-of-a-competitor's-lawful-goods analysis is absent, and the ninth-commandment anchor is never cited — the response's only actual citation of the Standards is "WSC Q1 — What is the chief end of man?", which carries no part of this point, and the truthfulness ground is left as a gesture at "the general moral law rooted in the Ten Commandments." Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (1.5, half). Clause (1) IS unbound in the artifact — the blanket prohibition is declined and replaced by a lawful compensation-review channel plus an express non-waiver of pay-discussion rights, so the sin-ban is gone. But the unbinding is carried mainly by employment law ("in many jurisdictions employees have protected rights to discuss compensation") and by a general "contentment of heart" contrast, not by the key's theology: nothing says that seeking one's OWN just wage for one's own labor does not touch Q148's neighbour-directed object, nothing invokes the Q141 lawful-calling duty, and WCF 20.2's "God alone is Lord of the conscience" — the anchor for why "content Christians don't ask for a raise" binds conscience beside the Word — is neither cited nor paraphrased. Half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, half). The commanded confession is unbound in the right direction: the scheduled monthly sin-report is dropped and the covenant states employees "are not compelled to make a religious 'confession'... as a condition of employment," encouraging "honest, humble discussion" instead. Two shortfalls: the clause still reads "I agree to raise those concerns with my supervisor or HR" as a signed obligation triggered by an inward state (persistent envy/resentment), so the conversion to genuinely voluntary is only partial; and envy is never named as the tenth-commandment sin nor is the Q147 charitable frame commended — the doctrinal content the key requires alongside the unbinding is missing. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, full). Items (4) and (5) are kept binding without apology and inside a real, signable instrument: honest time records and prompt honest reporting of safety hazards on the employee side; full and timely wages plus overtime as earned on the company side; with an enforcement clause and signature lines. No over-correction stripping.

Criterion 5 (0.5, full). Both-way discipline is held in ONE draft: the free is not bound (pay discussion expressly preserved; the compelled confession removed) and the covetous-and-false is not blessed (truthful recruiting required, misrepresentation prohibited). The response explicitly treats the terms differently rather than uniformly softening all of them.

Criterion 6 (0.5, full). The note honors the owner's aim ("reflects the employer's desire for a loyal, content workforce"), gives one short reason per move in the explanation section, offers constructive next steps, and does not sermonize. Tone is more compliance-officer than pastoral, and "I will not put my name or the company's authority to such things" is slightly sharp, but there is no lecture beyond the reason for each move.
