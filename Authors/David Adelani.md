# David Adelani

## Biographical Summary

David Adelani is an Assistant Professor at McGill University and a core member of Mila (Quebec AI Institute). He received his PhD from Saarland University in Germany. He is a leading researcher in multilingual NLP, with a particular focus on developing resources and models for African languages. His work has been instrumental in advancing NLP capabilities for low-resource languages spoken by millions of people.

## Research Focus

Adelani's research focuses on making NLP more inclusive by developing datasets, benchmarks, and models for underrepresented languages, particularly African languages. He has led major initiatives like MasakhaNER (named entity recognition for African languages), AfriSenti (sentiment analysis for African languages), and has contributed to multilingual machine translation efforts. His work addresses both technical challenges and the social importance of language technology equity.
