# Andrea Seveso

## Biographical Summary

Andrea Seveso is a Research Fellow at the University of Milano-Bicocca, based in the Department of Statistics and Quantitative Methods. His work focuses on natural language processing with a particular emphasis on cultural awareness in AI systems and explainable AI methods.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research investigates how AI systems can better understand and represent diverse cultural contexts, particularly in non-English languages. He has developed large-scale benchmarks for evaluating natural language understanding in Italian, spanning multiple knowledge domains.

### Significant Achievements

Seveso is a co-author of ITALIC, a comprehensive benchmark dataset of 10,000 multiple-choice questions designed to evaluate natural language understanding of Italian language and culture across 12 domains. This work was presented at NAACL 2025. He has also conducted significant research on explainable AI, evaluating post-hoc methods for text classification and exploring the use of LLMs to augment XAI applications.

## Major Publications

- [ITALIC: An Italian Culture-Aware Natural Language Benchmark](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.68/) (2025) - Co-authored with Daniele Potertì, Edoardo Federici, Mario Mezzanzanica, and Fabio Mercorio.
- [Explainable AI for Text Classification: Lessons from a Comprehensive Evaluation of Post Hoc Methods](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/XAI2024) (2024) - Research on XAI evaluation.
