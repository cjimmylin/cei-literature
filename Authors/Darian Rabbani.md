# Darian Rabbani

## Biographical Summary

Darian Rabbani is a researcher working on AI security and safety, with a focus on understanding and evaluating vulnerabilities in large language models. His research addresses critical security concerns in deployed AI systems.

## Research Focus

Rabbani's work focuses on evaluating prompt injection attacks against language models, a significant security concern as LLMs are increasingly deployed in real-world applications. His research helps identify vulnerabilities and develop defenses against adversarial inputs designed to manipulate AI system behavior.
