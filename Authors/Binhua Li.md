# Binhua Li

## Biographical Summary

Binhua Li is an AI Researcher at Alibaba Group, where he has contributed significantly to the development of the Qwen large language model series. He earned his MS from Peking University. He is a specialist in natural language processing and dialog systems, with a particular focus on bridging the gap between natural language and structured code or data.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Binhua’s research focuses on advanced NLP tasks, including large-scale text-to-SQL, code generation, and in-context learning. He is a key contributor to the Qwen model family, specifically working on multimodal capabilities and code-specific large language models like Qwen-Coder. His recent work involves synthesizing data for software engineering agents and expanding the multilingual reach of AI.

### Significant Achievements
He co-authored the Qwen3 technical report and has been instrumental in building industry-leading benchmarks for text-to-SQL (e.g., BigBench). His work on ExploraCoder demonstrates advanced planning and exploration capabilities in AI coding assistants. He is a core member of Alibaba's foundation model research team.
