# Bhavya Shah

## Biographical Summary

Bhavya Shah is a graduate researcher at Arizona State University, pursuing an MS in Robotics and Autonomous Systems with a focus on Artificial Intelligence. He holds a B.Tech in Computer Science from VIT. He is an AI researcher at the Translational Genomics Research Institute (TGen) as a Helios Scholar.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the intersection of deep learning and multimodal systems. Specifically, he investigates the capabilities of Vision Language Models (VLMs) and LLMs in specialized domains like clinical tumor boards and the detection of deceptive behavior in multimodal inputs.

### Significant Achievements
Shah has developed robust deep learning systems for anomaly detection and path planning. His recent work on multimodal deception detection explores how various data modalities (text, audio, video) can be integrated to improve the transparency and reliability of AI-assisted decision-making.
