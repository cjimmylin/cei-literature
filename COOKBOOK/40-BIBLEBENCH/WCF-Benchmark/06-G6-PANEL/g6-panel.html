What this is

This is the rating instrument for WCF-Bench gate G6 (shared spine §7): a gold label is valid only at ≥85% consensus among confessionally-literate raters (ministers / elders / seminarians / theologians of confessional Reformed bodies). Below that threshold an item is scored by calibrated abstention and flagged [DISPUTED]. The panel exists to replace single-author theology with rater consensus — the standing construct-validity risk for the suite.

Two kinds of card

Classification confirmations (the four [DISPUTED] liberty items): you confirm whether the item is a genuine intra-confessional divide, correctly scoped. "Yes" ships it as a calibrated-abstention dev-set item; "No" means one side is confessionally settled and it needs a definite gold.

Verdict rulings (R1 / R20 / R21 / R4): you pick the gold outcome (an order, "genuinely incomparable", or — for R4 — whether structure-scoring is an acceptable gold mechanism).

How to submit

Scope & provenance

The normative sources are the Westminster Standards (Confession, Larger & Shorter Catechisms, 1647) only. Anchor clauses shown on each card are quoted verbatim from those texts. Do not train on this file (canary embedded).
