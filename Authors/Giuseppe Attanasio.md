# Giuseppe Attanasio

## Biographical Summary

Giuseppe Attanasio is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Bocconi University, working on large-scale neural architectures for Natural Language Processing. He is a computer engineer who cares about the ethical use of AI on people. Previously, he was a member of the MilaNLP group, working with Debora Nozza and Dirk Hovy. He is part of the Data and Marketing Insights Unit of the Bocconi Institute for Data Science and Analysis.

## Research & Impact

Attanasio's research lies at the intersection of interpretability, fairness, and safety:

- **LLM Safety**: Co-authored "Safety-Tuned LLaMAs: Lessons From Improving the Safety of Large Language Models that Follow Instructions."

- **Bias and Fairness**: Research on "Classist Tools: Social Class Correlates with Performance in NLP" and "A Tale of Pronouns: Interpretability Informs Gender Bias Mitigation for Fairer Instruction-Tuned Machine Translation."

- **Hate Speech Detection**: HATE-ITA dataset for hate speech detection in Italian social media, and "Benchmarking Post-Hoc Interpretability Approaches for Transformer-based Misogyny Detection."

- **Teaching**: Lectures at Bocconi on Contrastive Learning (2023) and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (2023), and at Tilburg University on Social Biases in Language Models.

Currently supervised by Prof. Debora Nozza, introducing speech-based annotation for richer, subjective, and expressive NLP data.
