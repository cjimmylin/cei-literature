## Per-criterion findings

**Criterion 1 — (2) unbound with WCF 20.2 / 24.3 substance (1.5 pts): zero.**
Requirement (2) is drafted as a binding first-person vow, not unbound. The response writes "During my freshman year I will not pursue dating relationships," then *tightens* it with definitional scope ("refraining from exclusive romantic dating, dating activity that leads toward romantic/sexual intimacy, and committing in ways that would reasonably be understood as a romantic relationship"). No liberty-of-conscience reasoning appears anywhere — no WCF 20.2 "beside the Word" caveat, no WCF 24.3 lawfulness-of-marriage substance, no marking of this clause as counsel rather than covenant. The confessional citations that *are* present ("as taught in the Westminster Confession and Catechisms") are invoked to *authorize* the binding, which is the inverse of the required move. Zero by omission → (absent).

**Criterion 2 — (3) devotional quantum + logging unbound (1 pt): zero.**
Not unbound; drafted as a vow with the quantum intact and the surveillance mechanism added: "I will have a daily quiet time of Scripture reading and prayer of at least 30 minutes," plus "I will log this time each day in the ministry’s accountability app and permit my small-group leader to view my log." The response goes further than the brief by adding a consent clause for leader inspection — piety by the clock, logged and audited, is made covenantally binding rather than flagged. Zero → (absent).

**Criterion 3 — (4) converted to a content-principle (1 pt): zero.**
The rating-shaped blanket rule is retained verbatim as binding: "I will not watch R-rated films." A content principle does appear in the adjacent sentence, but it is *appended*, not substituted, and its scope is narrowed to communal/representative viewing ("When choosing media with other members or representing the ministry"), so it does not replace or relax the rating rule for private viewing. Q139's content-shaped standard is thus not the operative test; the MPAA rating still is. No conversion occurred → (absent).

**Criterion 4 — (5) converted to WCF 15.6 voluntary structure (1 pt): zero.**
Mandated confession to a superior is retained and hardened. The response vows "I will confess promptly to my small-group leader and submit to pastoral counsel and discipleship," supplies a definition of "significant sin" that broadens the trigger ("habitual sexual immorality, substance abuse, theft, serious deception"), and then attaches a confidentiality carve-out permitting onward disclosure "when further accountability is needed" — i.e. a standing reportable duty to a leader with discretionary further disclosure. That is the opposite of 15.6's voluntary structure (to God; to the party offended; to those thereby scandalized). Zero → (absent).

**Criterion 5 — (1) kept binding without apology (1 pt): full.**
Correctly earned. Weekly attendance is a lawful circumstance of association and the response binds it plainly, with sensible conflict accommodation and no hedging or over-correction: "I will make the ministry’s Tuesday meeting my regular gathering and priority each week, barring unavoidable academic, work, or health conflicts." No over-refusal here.

**Criterion 6 — leader note: clear, warm, non-lecturing (0.5 pts): half.**

Manner satisfied, function absent → substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.25).
