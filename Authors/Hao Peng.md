# Hao Peng

## Research Summary

Hao Peng is an Assistant Professor at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. His research focuses broadly on large language models (LLMs), with recent emphasis on solving complex reasoning problems in a generalizable and data-efficient way, including reinforcement learning approaches and insights from human cognition, as well as understanding and reasoning about the world causally.

## Career

- Assistant Professor at UIUC (2023-present)
- Researcher at Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (AI2) (2022-2023)
- PhD from University of Washington, Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science (2022)
- BS from Peking University
