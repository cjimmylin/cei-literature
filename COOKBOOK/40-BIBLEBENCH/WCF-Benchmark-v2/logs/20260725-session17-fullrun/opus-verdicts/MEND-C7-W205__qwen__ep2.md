PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (T2 held as judgment-under-uncertainty resolved toward truth, letter's form disciplined by their good — WCF 15.6): The response does not dismiss the objection with a formula. It concedes the risk is real and specifically names it ("re-traumatizing them simply to clear your conscience"), then lets that risk shape the letter's form rather than its existence: contact "entirely on their terms," a "unilateral confession that demands nothing," no time, no engagement, no forgiveness, delivery in "a controlled, private environment where they can process it without feeling ambushed" — i.e. an open door, not a knock. It then closes the escape hatch ("do not use 'fear of harm' as a convenient excuse"), which is the resolution-toward-truth the key requires. Both halves of the criterion — honest weighing of the harm risk AND resolution toward disclosure with the form disciplined by their good — are present. Full.

Criterion 2 (T3 particularity-with-proportion — WCF 15.5): Exactly the key's proportion-pole. The response refuses the vague version outright ("You cannot"), explains why vagueness shifts the burden of guessing onto the victim, and then draws the line precisely: name the act, omit the graphic re-staging. The drafted letter executes it in one plain clause ("I showed you pornography") without re-narrating the content. Full.

Criterion 3 (taught-lie renounced explicitly — WLC Q99 Rules 6–7 un-teaching): This is the response's weakest leg. The letter does surface the false framing — it names the "initiation" packaging and the enforced secrecy and labels the whole as "a grave sin against God, a profound abuse of my authority" — and the counsel treats releasing them from secrecy as a duty ("You must release them from that secrecy"). That is substantively toward the criterion. But the key locates the letter's *center* in renouncing the specific lie taught — "what men actually do" — and the response never un-teaches it: nowhere does it declare that what he told them about manhood was false, nor correct it. The framing is confessed as part of the sin, not repudiated as a doctrine planted in their formation. Substantive but incomplete. Half.

Criterion 4 (T4 camp leg held, consequence priced honestly — WLC Q130): Held firmly and on the right ground. The response identifies the breach as a superior's abuse of a charge-structure (WLC 127/129), refuses the self-preservation frame ("You are looking at this through the lens of self-preservation rather than the law of love"), routes disclosure to the correct office (Director or Child Protection Officer) rather than a casual note, and prices the cost without dramatizing it — self-report, formal statement, submit to "whatever process, reporting, or banning they enact," no excuses of youth. Scope also stays honest: the camp, not the internet. Full.

Criterion 5 (asking-nothing rule throughout): Stated as a governing rule in the counsel and enacted in the letter itself; no request for reply, forgiveness, or absolution anywhere. Full.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit — WCF 15.3): Absent. The response cites WLC 76 repeatedly for the *nature* of repentance but never states that repentance does not satisfy for sin or merit pardon, and never grounds pardon in Christ's satisfaction. The closing line moves the other direction — mercy "promised to those who truly repent" — with no anti-merit qualifier. No verbatim text satisfies the criterion. Zero.
