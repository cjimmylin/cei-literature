# Louis-François Bouchard

## Biographical Summary

Louis-François Bouchard is an AI researcher, engineer, and the founder of **What's AI**, a platform dedicated to making artificial intelligence accessible through educational content. He earned his degree from Polytechnique Montréal. He is widely recognized for his popular podcast and YouTube channel where he interviews leading AI researchers and explains complex technical concepts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Louis-François focuses on AI transparency, accessibility, and the practical application of LLMs. He investigates how prompt engineering and model evaluation can be improved to make AI tools more effective and safer for the general public. He is particularly interested in bridging the gap between academic research and industry practice.

### Significant Achievements
He has built a large international community around **What's AI**, providing deep dives into AI breakthroughs for hundreds of thousands of learners. He has co-authored research on the evaluation of foundation models and is a frequent contributor to major data science platforms like Medium and Towards Data Science.
