# Mohit Bansal

## Biographical Summary

Mohit Bansal is the John R. Louise S. Parker Professor and Director of the MURGe-Lab (Multimodal Understanding, Reasoning, and Generation) at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. He received his PhD from UC Berkeley and was a Research Scientist at Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago (TTIC) before joining UNC. He is one of the most prolific and influential researchers in modern NLP.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Mohit's research spans natural language processing with emphasis on multimodal learning, language generation, and reasoning. His lab develops methods for grounding language in visual and physical environments, building more trustworthy and robust NLP systems, and advancing vision-language understanding.

### Significant Achievements
He has published hundreds of papers at top venues (ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, NeurIPS, ICLR) and has been recognized with numerous awards including NSF CAREER, DARPA Young Faculty Award, and Google Faculty Research Awards. His work on multimodal models, text generation, and NLP robustness has been highly influential in the field.
