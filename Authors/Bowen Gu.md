# Bowen Gu

## Research Summary

Bowen Gu is a researcher working on medical AI applications, particularly focusing on probabilistic predictions from large language models in healthcare settings. His work examines how LLMs can be applied to medical decision-making with appropriate uncertainty quantification. He collaborated with researchers at Harvard Medical School on studies of LLM capabilities in clinical contexts.
