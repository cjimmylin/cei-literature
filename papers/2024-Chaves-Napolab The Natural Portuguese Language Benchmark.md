## Abstract

Collection of Portuguese datasets for LLM evaluation with guidelines ensuring natural Portuguese text and expert annotations. All datasets have translations in Catalan, English, Galician, and Spanish.

## Data Description

TeenyTinyLlama: two compact open-source language models (160M and 460M parameters) pre-trained from scratch in Brazilian Portuguese, along with Pt-Corpus-Instruct (~6.2B tokens) training dataset. No benchmark is introduced; the paper evaluates the models on existing translated benchmarks.
