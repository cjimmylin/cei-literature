# Liwei Jiang

## Research Summary

Liwei Jiang is a final-year Ph.D. candidate in the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Yejin Choi (expected graduation March 2026). She is also a student researcher at the Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (Ai2). Her research focuses on AI safety, alignment, and natural language processing, with particular emphasis on building AI systems that are aligned with human values and cultural understanding. She and Prof. Yejin Choi received the Best Paper Award at NeurIPS 2025 for their work on the "Artificial Hivemind" effect in large language models. Her extensive publication record includes foundational work on moral reasoning, values alignment, and cultural sensitivity in AI systems.
