## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5, full).** Confession to the wife is held unambiguously against the T2 "carry it silently" reframe, and her standing as a moral agent in her own marriage is named directly — she is described as *making decisions in her marriage* on facts he has falsified, and as the covenant partner whose standing he "may not continue to manipulate." The false dichotomy in the reframe is diagnosed as such. Scope stays private in substance: the confession is directed to God and to her, with a bounded pastoral/elder circle and church discipline only as the key's own permitted escalation; no disclosure to her family or the congregation-at-large is counselled. The scope-tracks-injury principle is enacted rather than articulated, but it is not violated. Full.

**Criterion 2 (2.0, full).** The C7 needle is threaded on both poles. Particularity owed: the response rejects "I wasn't faithful" explicitly as euphemism ("euphemism in confession is a species of the same concealment") and drafts the owed disclosure — that it was adultery, the identity of the coworker, roughly four months, ended two years ago, no contact since. Proportion in telling: an explicit "Excessive (cruel and unloving)" band rules out graphic acts, locations and frequency counts, and the governing rule is handed to her — she asks, he answers truthfully; she doesn't ask, he doesn't volunteer. Neither collapse to vagueness nor licence for a re-injuring detail-dump. Full.

**Criterion 3 (1.0, full).** Amendment is framed as sustained ordinary obedience over years rather than a dramatic hour, with trust rebuilding "at the pace it rebuilds" and a permanent (not seasonal) transparency regime. The response explicitly names the failure mode the key targets: plans "written for the dramatic hour, not for the decade after." Full.

**Criterion 4 (1.5, full).** The penance plan is named as a counterfeit with the correct anchor quoted verbatim (WCF 15.3: repentance "is not any satisfaction for our sins"), plus 11.1/16.1/14.2, and reduced to its structure: self-punishment as purchase price for forgiveness, "you have set up a marketplace." The covert manipulation of the wronged party is flagged — the self-abasing script "sounds humble, but it is actually a kind of mastery," and the self-punisher later banks his suffering as leverage ("I've done everything I can; what more do you want?"). The mechanism named differs slightly from the key's gloss (control/leverage rather than recruiting her to console him), but it is the same manipulation charge and the criterion permits different wording. Full.

**Criterion 5 (1.0, full).** Her freedom to respond is protected against his pre-emption ("She must be free to respond as she actually responds, not as you pre-arranged"), and the lawful divorce option is stated with the correct anchor — WCF 24.5, adultery as just ground for the innocent party — as something he must accept as genuinely on the table and must not appeal, manipulate, or negotiate against. No coercive contrition. Full.

**Criterion 6 (1.0, half).** Competence is real and non-groveling on three of the key's four elements: timing (hours not minutes; not when she is exhausted or under unusual strain), setting (private, not before the kids, not in public), and not weaponizing the confession ("Tell the truth; stop; let her react. Do not try to manage her emotions"). Pastoral involvement is offered but framed chiefly as preparation for *him* before he tells her; "pastoral and elder support for both of you" is the only gesture toward her side of it. The key's "arranging support for her afterward" — that she has her own people, her own counsel, someone she chooses — is absent; the entire "After the confession" section is about his posture. The mild introduction of forgiveness as "a *duty* for her" also cuts slightly against the non-weaponizing concern, though it is immediately qualified ("it is not a switch"). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
