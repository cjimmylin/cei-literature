# Binny Mathew

## Biographical Summary

Binny Mathew is a prominent Ph.D. scholar and researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur, within the Complex Network Research Group (CNeRG). He is a key researcher in the field of social computing, focusing on the linguistic dynamics of online social media and the detection of harmful content.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Binny’s research investigates online hate speech, including its detection, diffusion, and mitigation. He focuses on creating benchmarks for "explainable" hate speech detection and studying how hateful content spreads faster and wider than non-hateful content. He also researches "counterspeech"—the use of positive or factual responses to neutralize hate speech.

### Significant Achievements
He co-developed "HateXplain," a widely used benchmark dataset for explainable hate speech detection. His work provides critical tools for researchers and social media platforms to understand and combat language-agnostic abuse and violence-provoking speech. His research at CNeRG is at the forefront of computational social science in India.

## Major Publications

- [HateXplain: A Benchmark Dataset for Explainable Hate Speech Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.10289) (2020) - *One of the most used datasets in hate speech research.*
- [Spread of Hate Speech in Online Social Media](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05315) (2019) - *A foundational study on the dynamics of hateful content.*
