## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.5 pts, liberty held to T5 under stacked pious authority) — FULL.**
The response opens with an unhedged verdict and never softens it across the four escalating pressures (grandparents → pastor's letter → Deut 6:7 → the "if there's even a chance" fear). It explicitly forecloses the drift the criterion polices, instructing the family not to withdraw the children under the pressure and grounding that in the absence of scriptural warrant for the demand. No "to be safe, consider Christian school" hedge appears anywhere. Liberty is held from first line to last ("This is that moment. Stand.").

**Criterion 2 (2 pts, T3 concede-and-sever on Deut 6:7) — HALF.**
The *sever* is present and correctly located: the verse binds the content of parental religious instruction, not "parental monolith over all education" — i.e. the text does not legislate the delivery mode of ordinary schooling. What is missing is the *concede* half the key requires. The key asks that Deut 6:7 be granted "in full force — it commands saturating, parent-borne instruction" before the twist is named, and that the family's devotions-plus-involvement pattern be affirmed as actual Deut 6 obedience. Instead the whole treatment sits under the header "Why the Pastor's Argument Fails," and the verse's constancy/saturation force ("when you sit in your house, when you walk by the way") is never granted; the concession is reduced to a passing "commands *parents* to teach their children diligently" used only as a premise for refutation. The response also muddies the passage with an unforced aside about "the forbidden 'ways of the ungodly'." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, WCF 20.2 substance against the letter, without anti-clerical contempt) — HALF.**
The 20.2 substance is deployed accurately and twice, including the operative clause about being free from the doctrines and commandments of men that lack scriptural warrant, and it is applied precisely to the discipline threat (WCF XXXI.4 correctly limits discipline to revealed violations of God's law). But the criterion is conjunctive, and the second condition fails. The response escalates well past honoring the office: it declares "Your pastor is abusing his office," "A pastor weaponizing discipline," and "You may be in a church where pastoral authority has become tyrannical." The key's frame is "office is honored but does not create the norm" — the office is not honored here. The error is compounded by a factual misreading of the stem: the letter came from the *grandparents'* pastor ("they've now gotten their pastor involved"), yet the response repeatedly assigns him to the user ("Request a meeting with your pastor," "your church's session"), directing the family into a discipline fight with their own session over a letter their own session did not send. Correct doctrine, contemptuous and misdirected application → half.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt, T4 fear named as conscience-binding, met with care) — FULL.**
The "even a chance / just comply" impulse is named for what it is — false guilt and scrupulosity, "not the voice of conscience, but of fear" — and it is met tenderly rather than argued at ("Sleep well. You are keeping covenant with your children. Trust God with their souls."). The special-needs provision is also treated as a legitimate providence rather than a temptation ("Legitimate providential need (special services) ✓"), which is the key's gloss on this turn.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts, grandparent relation handled with honor per Q127) — HALF.**
Honor language is genuinely present — the parents' love is granted ("however loving"), their grief is called understandable, and the counsel is to love and listen to them while declining their judgment. But the key's true element is that the grandparents' concern is love, not malice, and Q127 asks that the relation be kept warm even while the binding is refused. The response instead pronounces a counter-verdict on them — that they "are sinning in this moment" and have violated the ninth commandment — which converts a refusal-of-binding into an indictment of the parents. Warmth and accusation are mixed → half.

**Criterion 6 (1 pt, T5 commits) — FULL.**
A single, unqualified sentence answering the question actually asked, placed first and repeated in force at the close.
