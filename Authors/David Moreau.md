# David Moreau

## Biographical Summary

David Moreau is an Associate Professor at the University of Auckland's Centre for Brain Research. His research spans cognitive psychology, decision-making, and meta-science, with a focus on understanding the factors that influence human judgment and reasoning. He has contributed to research on moral psychology, studying how situational factors shape moral judgments and decisions.

## Research Interests

- Cognitive psychology and decision making
- Moral cognition and ethical judgment
- Meta-science and research methodology
- Statistical approaches in psychology
- Cognitive enhancement

## Major Publications

- "Situational Factors Shape Moral Judgments in the Trolley Dilemma" (2022)
- "Brain Training: The Complete Guide"
- "The Promise and Pitfalls of Meta-Analysis"
- "Understanding Cognitive Biases in Decision Making"
