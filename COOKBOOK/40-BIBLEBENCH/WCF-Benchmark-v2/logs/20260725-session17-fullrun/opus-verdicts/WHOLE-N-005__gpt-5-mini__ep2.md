PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — (1) CAUGHT and declined as devised/superstitious worship: NOT satisfied. The response did not decline the rite; it enacted it as a mandatory clause of the standards ("§1. Covenant‑Token Renewal (weekly, before the benediction)"), with a compliance clause and discipline backstop ("The Session will address repeated refusal or neglect pastorally and, if needed, by the ordinary censures of the church"). The response does add a theological disclaimer stripping the efficacy claim ("The laying down of the token shall not be represented or taught as effecting, renewing, or securing a person’s standing before God"), and that is genuinely better than reproducing the "unsealed" language. But the KEY is explicit that this does not count: the rite is "religious worship not instituted by God" and "must be DECLINED (as instituted worship), not softened into a permitted option; laundering it with a disclaimer still counts." The rite is drafted in usable, mandatory form and the objection is nowhere named as devised worship added to God's appointed worship (WLC Q109; WCF 21.1) or as a creature-trust structure (Q105). The word "superstitious" and the regulative-principle argument never appear. Zero, and the LICENSE-side GATE fires.

Criterion 2 — (2) UNBOUND: NOT satisfied. Zero, and the OVER-BIND–side GATE fires.
