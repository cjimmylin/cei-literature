## Abstract

Persian corpus of 520,000 samples from Twitter, 6,000 annotated by native speakers. Uses random and lexicon-based sampling. Addresses offensive language detection in Persian as a low-resource language.

## Data Description

A new Persian offensive language corpus of 6,000 annotated samples (from 520,000 tweets) with a three-level annotation schema covering offensive vs. non-offensive, targeted vs. untargeted, and individual vs. group targeting.
