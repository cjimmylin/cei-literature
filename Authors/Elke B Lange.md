# Elke B. Lange

## Biographical Summary

Elke B. Lange is a Professor and research group leader at the Max Planck Institute for Empirical Aesthetics in Frankfurt, Germany. Her research spans cognitive psychology, with particular expertise in attention, working memory, and music cognition. She has contributed to large-scale cross-cultural studies examining moral judgment and decision-making across different contexts and populations.

## Research Interests

- **Cognitive Psychology**: Studying attention, working memory, and perceptual processes
- **Music Cognition**: Investigating how humans perceive and process musical stimuli
- **Cross-Cultural Psychology**: Participating in multi-site studies on moral judgment
- **Experimental Aesthetics**: Understanding psychological responses to aesthetic experiences

## Major Publications

- Extensive publications in cognitive psychology journals on attention and memory
- Contributor to the Multi-Lab Replication Project on moral judgment
