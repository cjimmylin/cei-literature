# Shalaka Satheesh

## Research Summary

Researcher at Bonn-Rhein-Sieg University of Applied Sciences, Department of Computer Science in Sankt Augustin, Germany. Research expertise includes Machine Learning and Computer Vision with recent focus on German NLP and gender bias. Recent work (2025) includes GG-BBQ (German Gender Bias Benchmark for Question Answering) presented at 6th Workshop on Gender Bias in NLP. Also worked on emotion recognition in oral history interviews and fairness evaluation in NLP systems.
