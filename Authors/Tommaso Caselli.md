# Tommaso Caselli

## Research Summary

Tommaso Caselli is a Senior Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Arts, University of Groningen (Rijksuniversiteit Groningen), which he joined in September 2017. He works in Computational Semantics at the Center for Language and Cognition (CLCG). His research interests include timeline and storyline extraction, perspectives identification (including fake news detection), narrative understanding, content types detection, and abusive language.
