# Scott Emmons

## Biographical Summary

Scott Emmons is a PhD student at UC Berkeley working on AI safety and reinforcement learning. His research focuses on understanding the risks and ethical implications of reward-based AI systems, particularly how misspecified rewards can lead to harmful outcomes. He has contributed important work on measuring trade-offs between reward optimization and ethical behavior, as well as methods for extracting wellbeing-related signals from language models.

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety**: Identifying and mitigating risks in AI systems
- **Reward Modeling**: Understanding how reward specifications affect agent behavior
- **Ethics in RL**: Studying when rewards justify potentially harmful means
- **Wellbeing Measurement**: Using language models to understand human wellbeing

## Major Publications

- "Do the Rewards Justify the Means? Measuring Trade-Offs Between Rewards and Ethical Behavior" (2023) - Examines ethical trade-offs in reward optimization
- "Uncovering Latent Human Wellbeing in Language Models" (2024) - Methods for measuring wellbeing using LLMs
