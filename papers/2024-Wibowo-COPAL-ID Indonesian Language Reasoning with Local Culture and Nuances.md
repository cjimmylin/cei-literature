## Abstract

Indonesian common sense reasoning dataset incorporating local and cultural nuances. 559 instances in standard Indonesian and Jakartan dialect. Three locality categories: Culture, Local Terminology, Language nuances.

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
