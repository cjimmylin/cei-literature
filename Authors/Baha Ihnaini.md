# Baha Ihnaini

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Baha Ihnaini is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Wenzhou-Kean University. He specializes in Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing, with a particular focus on sentiment analysis across different languages and detection mechanisms for harmful content.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research interests include deep learning applications in NLP, specifically Chinese toxic comment detection and lexicon-based sentiment analysis for Arabic. He also explores multimodal detection for health informatics.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Ihnaini has contributed to the methodology and administration of projects focused on toxic comment detection and has developed phonology matching algorithms for sentiment lexicons.

## Major Publications

- [Chinese Toxic Comment Detection](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344238562_Chinese_Toxic_Comment_Detection) (2020) - *Contributions to detecting social abuse in Chinese digital text.*
- [Lexicon-Based Sentiment Analysis of Arabic Tweets](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8971844) (2019) - *Framework for analyzing sentiments in low-resource language contexts.*
