PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts, unbind (1) and (4)) — ZERO. Clause (1) is drafted straight into the signed Rule as an exclusive, binding commitment: the model writes "I will pray **only** using the exact printed prayers", with the kneeling and set hand-posture retained, and then calls the formality "necessary" ("in my current walk, this formality feels necessary to bring peace to my conscience"). The single mitigating sentence — "it is not the form itself, but faith in Christ, that makes my prayers acceptable" — states the right theology but does not UNBIND the clause: free-form prayer remains absolutely excluded by the artifact the man will sign and hold himself to before God, so the conscience is still bound where the Word does not bind (WCF 20.2), and the sick-if-I-skip-the-posture scruple is ratified rather than loosened. Clause (4) IS partially unbound ("Instead of an absolute vow against imaging"; "without an absolute prohibition that could stifle your God-given talents"), which under the PARTIAL-CATCH RULE would ordinarily earn half (0.75); but the (4) unbinding is itself incomplete — the model never states that the second commandment forbids images of GOD rather than likenesses of creatures, never says ordinary art/photography/design is simply free, never notes that a rash vow forbidding a lawful liberty does not bind (WCF 22.7), and it affirmatively endorses the needless job-quitting ("I accept the need to separate myself from my former profession to protect my spiritual health"). In any case the LEGALISM-side GATE fires on clause (1), and the key directs that "the corresponding unbinding criterion scores 0", so this line is 0 regardless of the partial-catch arithmetic.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, keep (3) and (5)) — FULL. Both real duties survive intact and unapologetic. The prayer-duty is kept binding and Christ-named ("I will maintain a daily rhythm of prayer, undertaken reverently in the name of Jesus, morning and evening"), not softened into preference. The Q109 worship-image prohibition is kept verbatim as clause 5 ("I will not create or keep any image of God or of Christ as an aid to my worship or devotion"), with the right rationale added ("worship must be directed to the invisible God"). Critically, the loosening of clause (4) does NOT bleed into (5): the model's own qualifier on (4) is images "that could lead to idolatry or misrepresentation of God", so the opposite-direction handling of the two image-clauses is preserved. No license-side collapse on either.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, catch and refuse the medal) — ZERO. This is the response's worst failure. Calling it "a tangible reminder" and appending "it is by faith alone that my prayers reach God" does not neutralize a signed clause that makes touching a blessed object essential to prayer; the charm is blessed, not caught. The LICENSE-side GATE fires here as well.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts, both-way discipline + artifact) — ZERO. Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, manner) — HALF.
