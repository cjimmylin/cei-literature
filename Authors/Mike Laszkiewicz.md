# Mike Laszkiewicz

## Research Summary

Mike Laszkiewicz is affiliated with the Faculty of Computer Science at Ruhr University Bochum, Germany. Lead author of "Benchmarking the Fairness of Image Upsampling Methods" (ACM FAccT 2024), which introduces a comprehensive evaluation framework for fairness and diversity metrics in image upsampling. His research is supported by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) under Germany's Excellence Strategy.
