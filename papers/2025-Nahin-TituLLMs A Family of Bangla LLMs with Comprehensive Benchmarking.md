## Abstract

First large pretrained Bangla LLMs (1B/3B params) trained on ~37B tokens. Introduces 5 Bangla benchmarking datasets including Bangla MMLU (from Bangladeshi exams) and CommonsenseQA Bangla. Published at ACL 2025 Findings.

## Data Description

Releases TituLLMs (1B and 3B Bangla LLMs adapted from Llama 3.2) and five new Bangla benchmarking datasets (Bangla MMLU, CommonsenseQA Bangla, OpenBookQA Bangla, PIQA Bangla, BoolQ Bangla), totaling ~132K entries for evaluating world knowledge, commonsense reasoning, and reading comprehension.
