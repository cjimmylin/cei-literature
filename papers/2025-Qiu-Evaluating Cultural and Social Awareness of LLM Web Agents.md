## Abstract

Introduces CASA, a benchmark for assessing LLM agents' sensitivity to cultural and social norms in online shopping and social discussion forums. Agents achieve less than 10% awareness coverage and over 40% violation rates.

## Data Description

CASA benchmark contains 1,225 user queries and 622 observations across two web-based tasks (online shopping and social discussion forums) spanning 17 countries, designed to evaluate LLM agents' cultural and social norm awareness.
