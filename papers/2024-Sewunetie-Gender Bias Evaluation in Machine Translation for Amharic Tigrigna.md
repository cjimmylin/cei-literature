## Abstract

Benchmark datasets evaluating gender bias in MT for three low-resource languages. 2,400 gender-balanced sentences. Found 72% of Amharic and 93% of Afaan Oromoo sentences exhibited gender bias.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A5
**Relevance:** Directly evaluates gender bias in Amharic NLP systems. Quantifies bias rates in translation. Relevant to fairness assessment for African language AI systems.
