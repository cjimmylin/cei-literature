## Abstract

Shared task on transforming toxic text to non-toxic while preserving content across 9 languages including Spanish. Evaluation on style transfer accuracy, content preservation, and fluency. Extended to 15 languages in PAN 2025.

## Data Description

Multilingual parallel text detoxification dataset (TextDetox) for 9 languages (English, Spanish, German, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Ukrainian, Russian, Amharic), with toxic-to-non-toxic parallel sentence pairs used in the PAN 2024 shared task.
