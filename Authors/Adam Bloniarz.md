# Adam Bloniarz

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Adam Bloniarz is a researcher and software engineer at Google, part of the Factual Accuracy and Trust (FACT) team. He earned his PhD from UC Berkeley. His work addresses the fundamental challenge of ensuring that generative AI outputs are grounded in verifiable reality.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adam focuses on the development of factuality benchmarks and grounding leaderboards. He co-leads the FACTS Grounding Leaderboard project, which provides a comparative analysis of how well frontier models can cite and reason over external documents without introducing factual errors or hallucinations.
