## Abstract

First monolingual pre-trained models for Burmese based on BERT and ELECTRA architectures. Foundation models for Myanmar NLP research. Foundation Burmese language models. Infrastructure for future Myanmar values/ethics evaluation tasks.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Myanmar BERT/ELECTRA

## Data Description

Spanish BERT (BETO) pre-trained model plus GLUES benchmark compiling Spanish NLP tasks: XNLI, PAWS-X, NER, POS tagging, MLDoc, dependency parsing, and QA.
