## Abstract

Introduces Typhoon 2 family of Thai LLMs with a safety classifier (Typhoon2-Safety) adapted for Thai language. Develops a Thai culturally aware safety dataset through a six-step pipeline identifying sensitive topics in Thai culture. Includes Thai Sensitive Topic Test Set with 9,587 prompt-response pairs. Includes Thai-specific safety classifier and culturally aware safety dataset. Typhoon2-Safety detects Thai-sensitive content. The pipeline identifies and generates training data for Thai cultural sensitivities.
