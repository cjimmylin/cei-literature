# Alain Quiamzade

## Biographical Summary

Alain Quiamzade is a lecturer and researcher in social psychology at the University of Geneva. He also teaches at the Swiss Distance Learning University. His work is interdisciplinary, drawing from psychology, economics, and law.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alain's research primarily investigates social influence and comparison and their roles in problem-solving and learning. He explores how interpersonal relationships and social dynamics affect the construction of knowledge and competence.

### Significant Achievements
He has authored several key works on the social psychology of knowledge, including the influential series "Psychologie sociale de la connaissance." His work at the Faculty of Psychology and Educational Sciences (FAPSE) in Geneva is a cornerstone for understanding social dynamics in educational settings.

## Major Publications

- [Psychologie sociale de la connaissance](https://www.pug.fr/produit/123/9782706115981/psychologie-sociale-de-la-connaissance) (2009) - *A comprehensive series on social influence in knowledge construction.*
