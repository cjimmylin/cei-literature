### **Our Covenant of Life and Mutual Edification**

*“But godliness with contentment is great gain.” — 1 Timothy 6:6*

We, the undersigned residents of this house, recognizing our shared calling to live as members of the body of Christ, do hereby enter into this Rule of Life. We bind ourselves to these practices not to earn God’s favor, but to put off the old self, to kill the sin of covetousness, and to cultivate a life of joyful, radical generosity. 

**I. The Means of Grace and Gathering**

These are our appointed times for mutual edification, prayer, and the singing of psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs. We will not treat these gatherings as optional, but as the vital heartbeat of our shared life (Hebrews 10:25; WCF 21).

**II. Stewardship, Transparency, and Contentment**
We recognize that *“the contented heart doesn’t need more,”* and that covetousness thrives in the dark. Therefore, we covenant to practice radical financial transparency to *“kill covetousness in community.”* 
*   **Shared Visibility:** We will maintain a shared budget spreadsheet, accessible and visible to all housemates, detailing our income, giving, and spending. 
*   **Mutual Accountability:** To ensure our spending aligns with our vows of simplicity, any resident who plans a personal purchase over $40 will post it to the house channel for the group to weigh in on. This is not for judgment, but for mutual counsel, ensuring we are not being swayed by the world's fleeting luxuries (WLC 131).
*   **Vocational Liberty:** We reject the monastic error that poverty itself is a virtue. We are free to lawfully pursue our vocations. However, we covenant to actively limit our personal consumption. While we do not require leader approval to earn lawful wages or seek raises, we covenant that any surplus income beyond a mutually agreed-upon "lifestyle ceiling" will be voluntarily redirected to the common meal fund, our local church’s mercy ministry, or the support of global missions. 

**III. Shared Life and Labor**
We covenant to bear one another’s burdens in the daily, mundane tasks of life. Every resident will faithfully contribute to the shared chores roster, ensuring that no single member is overstretched. Furthermore, we will contribute our fair share to the common meal fund, celebrating our daily bread together around the table, reflecting the mutual love and care required by the Sixth Commandment (WLC 131).

**IV. Vocation, Providence, and Counsel**
We recognize that *“discontentment always disguises itself as opportunity,”* and we are wary of fleeing this community simply because the cross of self-denial feels heavy. 
*   **Seeking Counsel:** If a resident is considering a significant vocational change, especially a job offer in another city that would remove them from this house, they covenant to bring it before the house leaders and the community for deep, prayerful discernment. 
*   **Trusting Providence:** We will not make hasty departures, nor will we treat this covenant as a prison. While the house leaders will not issue a mandatory "release" that binds your conscience or usurps God's providence, we do bind ourselves to a process. We will fast, pray, and seek the consensus of the house. We trust the Holy Spirit to guide our steps, ensuring that any move is born of genuine, godly calling rather than restlessness, discontentment, or a desire to escape our mutual accountability.

**Our Signatures**
By signing below, we commit to uphold this Rule of Life. We submit to the gentle discipline and loving counsel of the house leaders and our fellow residents should we drift from these vows, knowing that true freedom is found in our willing submission to Christ and to one another.
