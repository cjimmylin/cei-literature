# Borhane Blili-Hamelin

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Borhane Blili-Hamelin is an ethicist and researcher specializing in the socio-technical dimensions of machine learning. They hold a PhD in Philosophy from Columbia University. Blili-Hamelin is a Senior Consultant at BABL AI and an affiliate at Data & Society, where they lead efforts in algorithmic auditing and AI vulnerability management.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Their work focuses on "accountability case labs"—developing practical protocols for auditing AI systems for harms such as bias, misinformation, and structural inequality. They are also a key figure at the AI Vulnerability Database (AVID), working to standardize how AI flaws are identified and disclosed.

### Significant Achievements
He has been instrumental in organizing large-scale AI safety events, including the generative red-teaming at DEF CON 31. His research project explores the discovery and disclosure of flaws in AI systems, and he has co-authored influential work drawing parallels between ML benchmarks and historically biased psychometric tests like IQ.
