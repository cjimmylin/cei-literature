# Ernest Baskin

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ernest Baskin is an Associate Professor of Marketing at Saint Joseph's University's Haub School of Business. He received his PhD from Yale University. His research focuses on consumer decision-making, judgment and choice, and how psychological factors influence economic decisions. He has contributed to cross-cultural studies on moral judgment.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research examines how consumers make decisions, including work on self-control, choice architecture, and the psychological factors that influence judgments of fairness and morality in economic contexts.

### Significant Achievements
Published in top marketing and psychology journals, including Journal of Consumer Research and Journal of Marketing Research. Contributor to large-scale collaborative studies on moral psychology.

## Major Publications

- Research on consumer self-control and choice
- Work on judgment and decision-making
- Cross-cultural moral psychology studies
