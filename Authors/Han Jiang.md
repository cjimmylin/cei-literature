# Han Jiang

## Biographical Summary

Han Jiang is a researcher focusing on AI safety and value alignment in large language models. Their work addresses critical challenges in evaluating LLM capabilities and values through innovative testing methodologies that adapt to model capabilities.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Their research focuses on developing dynamic evaluation methods for assessing the values of large language models. They investigate how to overcome limitations of static benchmarks that suffer from "evaluation chronoeffect," where existing benchmarks may leak into training data or become saturated, leading to overestimation of model capabilities.

### Significant Achievements
Jiang co-authored "Raising the Bar: Investigating the Values of Large Language Models via Generative Evolving Testing," proposing GETA (Generative Evolving Testing Approach), a novel generative evolving testing approach based on adaptive testing methods. Unlike traditional methods that rely on a static test item pool, GETA dynamically generates test items tailored to model capability, providing more accurate and robust evaluation of LLM values. The work was published on arXiv and OpenReview, receiving multiple revisions with the most recent version from June 2025.
