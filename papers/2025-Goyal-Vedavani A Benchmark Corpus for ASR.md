## Abstract

> First comprehensive ASR benchmark for Vedic Sanskrit poetry. 54 hours of audio from the Rig Veda and Atharva Veda with precise prosodic and rhythmic features. IndicWhisper performs best. Directly tests AI on core Hindu Vedic texts.

## Dataset Information

30,779 labelled audio samples, 54 hours (20,782 Rig Veda + 9,997 Atharva Veda verses)


## Data Description

Vedavani is a 54-hour Sanskrit ASR dataset of 30,779 labelled audio samples from the Rig Veda and Atharva Veda with an 80/10/10 train/val/test split. Multiple ASR models (Whisper variants, IndicWhisper, MMS, HuBERT, Wav2Vec2 variants) are benchmarked on the corpus using WER and CER metrics.
