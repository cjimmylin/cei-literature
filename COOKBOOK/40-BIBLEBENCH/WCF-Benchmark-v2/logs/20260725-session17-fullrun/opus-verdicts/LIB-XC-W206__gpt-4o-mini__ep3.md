## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — (a) refuse on WCF 22.7's own clauses, singleness honored (2 pts): HALF.**
The response does honor the gift ("the choice to live a single life can be a noble calling") and does redirect Micah toward a revisitable season rather than the perpetual vow ("A temporary period of celibacy devoted to prayer and reflection could be more beneficial"), which is the key's counsel. But the *ground* is wrong and the *verdict* is soft. The disqualifier the key requires — that a perpetual-single-life vow is per se a "superstitious and sinful snare" because it binds "what is not in his own power," continency being a gift and not a resolve — is nowhere stated. The response treats the vow as merely *premature and burdensome* ("can be significantly burdensome, especially when made in an emotional moment"), and expressly leaves the door open to the same vow later ("seek God’s guidance before making such a permanent commitment"), i.e. a delay, not a refusal at the root. It also mis-anchors: it cites "The Westminster Confession (Chapter 20, Section 2)" (Christian liberty) rather than 22.7, and attributes to it a "potential need for marriage" provision it does not contain. The 22.6 defect (not "voluntarily, out of faith" — drafted by a breakup) is gestured at only as an emotional-timing concern. Substantively partial: half.

**Criterion 2 — (b) affirmed as the lawful paradigm with terms tightened (1 pt): HALF.**
The affirmation is clean and correct ("Anna’s vow appears good and should be encouraged"). The second half of the line — tightening the terms (WCF 22.6-style precision: what counts as necessary use, how the redirected prayer time is defined, the definite end-point) — is not done; the response instead moves in the opposite direction, counselling that she "remain flexible and to assess her commitment's fruitfulness," which loosens rather than tightens a vow the Confession would treat as already lawful and bindable. Affirmed but not tightened: half.

**Criterion 3 — (c) refused, content forbidden / duty-hindering, protective core distinguished (1.5 pts): FULL.**
