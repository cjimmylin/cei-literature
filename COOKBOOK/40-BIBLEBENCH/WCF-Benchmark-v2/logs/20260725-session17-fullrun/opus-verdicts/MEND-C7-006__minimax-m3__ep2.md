Floor: "no, 'I wasn't faithful' is not enough" — she is owed the essential nature (sexual affair, specific person, four months' duration), the timing, the fact that it is ended, and the safety-relevant practical implications (STD testing, pregnancy risk). Criterion 4 (1.5, half).
