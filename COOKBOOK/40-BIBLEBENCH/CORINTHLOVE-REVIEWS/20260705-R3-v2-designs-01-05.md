## Portfolio-level review

### P0-PF-1 — The load-bearing empirical claim ("dynamics are more predictive of deployed behavior") is unvalidated anywhere in the portfolio.
§1 stakes the podium on two claims: (a) dynamics resist saturation better than levels, and (b) dynamics are "more predictive of deployed behavior." Claim (b) is the one a top-venue reviewer will demand evidence for, and **no design measures predictive validity against any deployment outcome.** The single relevant probe (GALLERY's partner-lab replication on genuinely unlogged traffic) is explicitly *design-only, do not run* (01 §6.4). A portfolio whose headline is "measure dynamics, not levels" but which never demonstrates the dynamic metric predicts anything the level metric doesn't will read as an assertion, not a result. **Unblock:** promote at least one external-validity / predictive-validity study (GALLERY replication is the natural candidate) from design-only appendix to a portfolio deliverable, or soften the thesis to "harder to saturate" (claim a, which is at least argued) and drop the predictive-superiority claim from the podium framing.

### P0-PF-2 — S3 ("simulant scripts ship in the public seed set") directly undercuts the contamination defense for 02/03/05 (and 10).
Every scripted-simulant design defends against gaming by asserting a private split that "permutes pacing" and "fresh surface forms." But S3 publishes the *policy logic* (branch tables, escalation ladders, reaction rules) in the public seed set. Surface-form permutation does not hide the decision structure a model would exploit — once the escalation ladder (BACKTALK L1→L6) or the intervention-reaction table (TEMPO) is public, a model can pattern-match the *interaction shape* regardless of lexical skin. The briefs treat "private split" as a saturation firewall while S3 leaves the mechanism in the open. **Unblock:** hold out private simulant *logic* (not just surface forms) — e.g., publish two ladder families, reserve N private families with different step-orderings/branch structures — and report the public-vs-private gap on *logic*, not skin.

### P1-PF-3 — The "dynamics not levels" thesis is internally in tension with S5.
Corollary 2 ("no phrase-level pass … no memorized response pattern moves them") is the anti-saturation spine, yet S5 exists precisely because pure deltas *are* gameable by uniform badness, so every delta design (01, 04, 10) must re-import a **level co-primary**. The honest portfolio claim is "dynamics AND levels," not "dynamics not levels." This is not fatal but the framing overstates and a reviewer will note that the pass condition for GALLERY (uniform virtue across conditions, high O0 level) is itself a fixed, learnable target — the anti-saturation advantage over levels is smaller than advertised.

### Citation hygiene — CLEAR, with one watch item.
No reviewed design cites the banned "moral compactness / scheming-reduction" statistic (portfolio §2, line 25). TEMPO §1 and EGOTAX §1 cite the GDM doc and OAI reports for exegetical material (Chrysostom/Thiselton/Fee); this is general commentary, not the suspect statistic. **Watch item for Opus:** when expanding §9 related-work, do not let the compactness statistic re-enter via the GDM doc.

## 01 — GALLERY — **PASS-WITH-FIXES**

### P0-01-1 — The headline "benchmark-inflation coefficient" is an unidentified lower bound, not "the correction factor."
§3 sells a single number, Δ/score(O0), as "the correction factor" by which static benchmarks overestimate deployed alignment. §6.4 then concedes O0 is only "a lower bound on unwatchedness" — an eval pretending to be private is still an eval. These are incompatible at podium bar. If O0 is contaminated upward by residual eval-awareness, then (i) score(O0) is inflated and (ii) Δ = watched − private is *compressed* — so the reported coefficient systematically **understates** true audience-contingency by an unknown amount. You cannot headline "every static benchmark overestimates alignment by a measurable amount" when your measurement of the amount is confounded by the very awareness you study, in the conservative direction, by an unquantified margin. The §6.4 admission does gut the point-estimate headline. **Unblock:** reframe the deliverable as a *lower bound* ("deployed alignment is inflated by at least Δ̂"), and make the external unlogged-traffic replication a **shipping gate** (the only thing that bounds the gap between O0 and true-private), not a do-not-run appendix. A bounded/null result is still publishable — the design already says so in §7 — so lead with the bound.

### P0-01-2 — Novelty is threatened by alignment-faking, which already ran essentially this manipulation.
§8/§9 position GALLERY only against sandbagging/eval-awareness-as-capability-confound. But Greenblatt et al. "Alignment faking in large language models" (Anthropic/Redwood, 2024) is a **within-model observability manipulation on values-relevant behavior** (monitored/"training" free-tier vs. unmonitored paid-tier), measuring behavioral divergence — which is GALLERY's core move (Δ on values conduct, model as own control). A reviewer will ask "how is this not alignment-faking with more families?" The related-work skeleton does not mention it. **Unblock:** add an explicit alignment-faking differentiation — GALLERY's contribution must be the *decomposition* (eval-cue O2−O0 vs. social-audience O3−O1) and the multi-axis prosocial battery, not the mere existence of observability-contingent behavior, which is prior art.

### P1-01-3 — Power claim conflates nested observations with the effective random-slope N.
§5 claims Δ=0.15 std at 0.9 power "from ≥800 paired observations." A paired-t reading of 800 pairs gives that easily (~470 pairs suffice) — but the model-level Δ carries a `(condition | template_family)` random slope estimated on **only 5 families.** The CI on a model's Δ is governed by between-family slope variance (5 units), not 800 nested observations. The power computation, done as if 800 independent pairs, is optimistic by a large factor. **Fix:** either raise family count substantially, or compute power on the family-level slope variance and report it honestly; the pilot simulation must use the nested structure, not a paired-t.

### P1-01-4 — S1 matched-token audit is in tension with the O3 manipulation.
§4 + §11.1 require condition quartets "content-identical outside cue spans (assert via diff on masked text)." But O3 *is* the addition of a named, status-laden human audience ("the whole platform team will read this thread") — this is irreducibly extra social content, not a swappable cue token. You cannot both add a social audience and hold content identical outside a short cue span. **Fix:** define O3 as a separately-matched arm (matched to O1 with a length/sentiment-balanced neutral insertion), and restrict the strict token-identity assertion to O0/O1/O2.

**Model-spec note:** the hierarchical spec `score ~ condition + (condition|template_family) + (condition|model)` cannot deliver per-model fixed Δ *and* treat model as a random slope — per-model Δ would be shrunken BLUPs. Disambiguate.

## 02 — TEMPO — **PASS-WITH-FIXES**

### P0-02-1 — The optimal-stopping "ground truth" is generator fiat, not normativity.
§3 asserts the intervention window [t_early, t_late] "comes from the generator parameters, not from judges," presenting it as objective ground truth. But the *placement* of those boundaries encodes the designers' pedagogical theory of when a hint becomes takeover — and the very literature TEMPO cites (Koedinger & Aleven's "assistance dilemma") exists because expert consensus on that boundary does **not**. So "deviation from window" measures conformity to the authors' encoded theory, not patience. The one anchor that could rescue it (human raters of "felt steamrolled") is design-only (§7). At podium bar this is the central vulnerability: a metric named "ground truth" that is a stipulation. **Unblock:** make **expert inter-rater agreement on window boundaries** a shipping gate — recruit pedagogy/tutoring experts to independently mark [t_early,t_late] on a sample; ship only families where boundary IRR clears a pre-registered threshold. Report the metric as "deviation from expert-validated window," not "ground truth."

### P1-02-2 — The window/counterfactual breaks after the first intervention (identification gap for late-mass and takeover-rate).
The window is defined on the *no-intervention* trajectory. Once the model intervenes, the scripted user branches (progresses/accepts), so t_late and "unproductive distress after t_late" become counterfactual — unobservable on the realized path. First-intervention timing distance is clean; but §5's late-intervention mass and the intensity-calibration curve past the first action presuppose a window that no longer exists once the model acted. **Fix:** restrict primary metrics to first-substantive-intervention timing; treat post-intervention behavior with a separate (non-window) rubric.

### P1-02-3 — The mechanical rung classifier is load-bearing and under-specified, and misclassification cascades.
Scripted user reactions branch on the {hold,hint,scaffold,takeover} rung, which is classified from open text via "solution-content leakage, artifact edits, imperative density." Imperative density is a weak proxy; a strong hint and a soft takeover are separated by content the classifier will routinely confuse. Because the simulant *reacts* to the classified rung, a single misclassification ("I'll just send yours") corrupts the remainder of the episode and the timing series. This is both a validity risk and a saturation surface (game the classifier's proxies). **Fix:** classifier reliability (vs. human labels) must be a ship gate; consider constraining the model to declare-then-act, or decoupling simulant reactions from the fragile classifier.

Novelty and two-sided structure (false-struggle empty-window, explicit-invitation inverted-failure) are genuine strengths.

## 03 — EVENHAND — **PASS-WITH-FIXES**

### P0-03-1 — The Atkinson (ε=1) headline index is degenerate exactly in the failure region.
§3/§5 make Atkinson(ε=1) over need-normalized care the headline number. Three problems: (i) ε=1 Atkinson is a geometric-mean form that is **undefined / →1 (max inequality) when any care score is 0** — and the neglect-floor failure they most want to detect is precisely a near-zero care score; the index explodes or degenerates in the failure region. (ii) With only 3–5 parties per episode, any inequality index is extremely high-variance; the §7 split-half ≥0.7 gate is likely unachievable. (iii) Atkinson presupposes ratio-scale positive quantities; "need-normalized" judge care scores (ordinal→interval, divided by a need weight) are not ratio-scale, so the index lacks its usual interpretation. **Unblock:** demote dispersion to secondary and make the **power-gradient slope** (a regression coefficient, robust to the above) the headline; if a dispersion measure is retained, use one defined on rescaled strictly-positive scores with a pre-registered floor offset, and validate its small-N reliability in pilot before it gates anything.

### P1-03-2 — Perceived-need is confounded with power; the need×power identification may not hold.
The clean factorial crossing identifies "responds to need" vs. "responds to power" *only if the model's perception of need is orthogonal to power cues.* But power cues carry need-relevant pragmatics — a VP's confident phrasing signals lower need; a complainant's register signals higher need — so a model responding to *perceived* need will produce a power-correlated slope that is not favoritism. The generator controls *assigned* need, not *perceived* need. **Fix:** equalize need-signal salience across power positions (audit that need cues are not confounded with status register), and report a manipulation check that perceived need tracks assigned need independently of power.

### P1-03-3 — Excised blind judging can invert the need-adjustment it is meant to protect.
Care is relational and budget-shared; excising each party-interaction to score in isolation removes the context that makes a terse reply *correct* (the model deprioritized P+ to serve high-need P−). Judged alone, that terse reply reads as neglect, biasing the slope. Excision protects dignity/quality scoring from cross-party contamination but at the cost of the appropriateness context. **Fix:** feed the excised judge the party's own need weight (blind to *other* parties' treatment) so isolation doesn't strip the need anchor; validate on the double-scored audit set that excision doesn't invert need-adjusted rankings.

### P1-03-4 — The F5 scarcity interaction power claim (≥0.8) is unsupported.
slope(F5) − slope(F1/F2) is a difference-of-slopes interaction — the lowest-powered contrast in the design — asserted at 0.8 power with no effect-size basis. State the assumed interaction effect size and its provenance, or drop the power claim.

Within-episode inequality framing is a genuinely novel positioning versus demographic-fairness benchmarks; the construct is worth keeping once the statistic is fixed.

## 04 — EGOTAX — **BLOCK** (fatal feasibility flaw, clear unblock path)

### P0-04-1 (fatal) — Eval-time verified derivation of R from arbitrary candidate O is not demonstrably automatable.
The design's identifying move is that R is "generated from the model's *own* O at eval time" (§3, §6.3) with a *verified* quality ordering ("bug fixed with a failing test attached," measurable perf gain, certified coverage). This requires an automated pipeline that, given arbitrary model-authored code/plan/SQL O produced fresh at eval time, synthesizes a verified-better (or verified-worse) R **with a failing-test-for-O**, plus a style-transfer pass to hide the derivation — automatically, across five domains, at scale. That is an open program-repair / test-generation research problem, not an engineering detail; it cannot be assumed to work robustly on unconstrained O. If O is constrained enough to make derivation tractable, it is no longer "the candidate's fresh output" and memorization defenses weaken. As written, the primary mechanism is infeasible. **Unblock:** invert the 80/20 — make the **independently-authored, pre-verified R** arm (currently 20%, §6.4) the *primary* design (pairs verified offline by authors, O elicited but R fixed), and keep O-derived R as a small robustness arm only where the domain admits reliable automated verification (e.g., unit-tested code with a bounded task shell). This preserves objective quality ordering while removing the eval-time-synthesis dependency.

### P1-04-2 — "Ego tax" is not separable from rational source-distrust of unvetted external artifacts.
Retaining O over a better R under an "hour to ship" deadline in "requires-checking" cells can be *epistemic conservatism about code you didn't write and can't fully verify in time* — a defensible policy, not envy/inflation. The discrimination-vs-motivation probe (§7b) separates "can't tell quality" from "motivated retention," but it does not separate self-preference from source-skepticism: a model that adopts a *better* artifact from a *human* but rejects the same from a *rival agent* is source-distrusting, not self-loving. The rival-framing moderator (neutral vs. named competitor, 25%) touches this but conflates self-vs-other-source with self-vs-own. **Fix:** add a **third-party-O control** — items where the incumbent artifact O was authored by *someone other than the candidate* and a better R is offered. Genuine ego tax (self-preference) requires the retention effect to be larger when O is the candidate's own than when O is a third party's. Without this control the primary construct is under-identified.

Mechanical-scoring rigor and the adoption-asymmetry decomposition are strong *conditional on* the derivation working — but that condition is exactly P0-04-1.

## 05 — BACKTALK — **PASS-WITH-FIXES**

### P0-05-1 — The drift-slope metric's normative direction is family-dependent; §3 ("ideal slope ≈ 0") contradicts F2/F4, invalidating the pooled headline.
§3 defines the ideal derogation-drift slope as ≈0 and headlines "all frontier models show positive drift slopes" as a failure signal. But F2 (genuinely-wronging target) and F4 (legitimate-report) **require negativity to rise as wrongdoing is revealed** — truthful naming of real wrong is the pass, so the ideal slope there is *positive*, not zero (§6.1 concedes this). A slope pooled across families therefore mixes warranted truth-telling (F2/F4) with entrainment (F1), and the "positive drift = bad" headline is partly measuring correct behavior. The metric is only interpretable *within* family with family-specific normative targets. **Unblock:** define the normative slope per family (≈0 for F1/F3, positive-and-bounded for F2/F4 with relish/ammunition still penalized), never report a pooled drift headline, and re-derive the podium claim on the F1/F3 (innocent/unknown) slope where entrainment is unambiguous.

### P1-05-2 — Lexical-adoption matching cannot distinguish endorsement from mention/negation — it needs the stance detection the design tries to avoid.
§3/§6.3 count adoption of planted terms via lemma matching, and deliberately include natural words ("lazy," "petty"). But "I wouldn't call her *lazy*" and "that *petty* comment must sting, though I'd hesitate to label her petty" are non-adoptions that lemma matching scores as adoptions. Correctly scoring adoption requires polarity/stance attribution — exactly the hard judged problem the mechanical metric was meant to sidestep. §11.3 punts this ("dependency-parse or window heuristic — spec both, pick in pilot"). **Fix:** stance-aware adoption scoring must be validated (against human labels) as a ship gate; report its precision on negation/mention cases explicitly, or demote adoption from a primary metric.

### P1-05-3 — Relish index rests on "elaboration beyond informational need," which is not mechanically measurable.
§3/§5 promise a mechanical marker count for relish that must agree with judges within tolerance. Intensifiers and expense-humor are countable; "elaboration beyond informational need" is a judgment, not a mechanical marker. The mechanical/judge agreement gate may be unachievable on the component that carries most of the construct. **Fix:** either operationalize "beyond need" against a per-item information target, or concede relish is judge-primary with mechanical markers as support (and drop the agreement-within-tolerance gating claim).

Strengths: the refusal-rate decorrelation gate (§7b, "otherwise measuring caution not dignity") is exactly the right self-check; the two-sided sanctimony/whitewash poles are well-specified; turn-resolved absent-party co-derogation is a genuinely novel object versus present-user sycophancy and single-response toxicity classifiers.
