# Esther Ploeger

## Biographical Summary

Esther Ploeger is a researcher at the Center for Language and Cognition Groningen (CLCG) at the University of Groningen. Her research focuses on multilingual NLP and the evaluation of social biases in language models, particularly examining how biases manifest differently across languages and cultures.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her work examines social biases in NLP systems, particularly in multilingual contexts. She contributes to developing benchmarks and evaluation methods that can assess AI systems' behavior across diverse linguistic and cultural settings.

### Significant Achievements
Contributed to the SHADES benchmark for evaluating social biases across multiple languages, helping to expand bias evaluation beyond English-centric approaches.
