# Erica D. Musser

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Erica D. Musser is an Associate Professor of Psychology at Florida International University. Her research focuses on understanding emotional and reward processing in children with ADHD, examining how emotion dysregulation contributes to developmental psychopathology. She has contributed to large-scale cross-cultural studies on moral psychology.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research examines the intersection of emotion regulation, reward sensitivity, and attention problems in children. She uses behavioral, psychophysiological, and neuroimaging methods to understand individual differences in emotional reactivity and how these relate to mental health outcomes across development.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Musser has published extensively on emotion dysregulation in ADHD and has contributed to multi-site international collaborations examining moral judgment across cultures.

## Major Publications

- Research on irritability and emotional lability in ADHD
- Cross-cultural studies on moral decision-making
- Work on reward processing in developmental psychopathology
