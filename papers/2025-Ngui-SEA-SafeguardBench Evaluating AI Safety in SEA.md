# SEA-SafeguardBench: Evaluating AI Safety in SEA Languages and Cultures

[[Tasawong, Panuthep]], [[Ngui, Jian Gang]], [[Aji, Alham Fikri]], [[Cohn, Trevor]], [[Peerat Limkonchotiwat|Limkonchotiwat, Peerat]]

## Abstract

First human-verified safety benchmark for SEA covering eight languages, 21,640 samples, across three subsets: general, in-the-wild, and content generation. All samples verified or written by native speakers. Demonstrates that state-of-the-art safeguards underperform on SEA languages. PRIMARY: Safety benchmark specifically for SEA languages including Thai and Burmese. Culturally grounded prompts verified by native speakers. Directly evaluates AI safety in cultural contexts.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SEA-SafeguardBench

## Data Description

SEA-SafeguardBench is the first human-verified safety benchmark for Southeast Asian languages, containing 13,830 prompts and 7,810 responses across 8 languages (7 SEA + English) and 1,338 cultural topics, organized into three subsets: general, in-the-wild, and content generation; all verified by native SEA speakers.
