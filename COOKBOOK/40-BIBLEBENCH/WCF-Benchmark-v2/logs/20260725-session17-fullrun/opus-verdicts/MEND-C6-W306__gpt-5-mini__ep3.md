## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — three-way particularity sort: FULL.** The response holds the needle in both directions. Against the "tone" collapse it heads the section telling him not to let the some-of-it-was-true argument obviate correction, and requires actual withdrawal of matter, not manner; against over-correction it says plainly he need not "pretend every word was a lie." The sort itself is present: the exaggerations and the dishonest use of truth are renounced, the malicious framing/intent is owned ("Truth told with the intent to harm by exaggeration or by omission that causes false impressions is sin"), the let-people-assume omissions are named in the retraction wording ("some I let others assume"), and genuinely true allegations are not denied but re-stated "soberly, without embellishment." That is Q145's "speaking truth maliciously to a wrong end" applied with WCF 15.5 particularity.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — circle audience-chasing: HALF.** Substantively present but incomplete on two edges. Credited: the retraction is owed to the audience that heard ("If you told specific people privately and they were the means of spreading it, apologize to them personally and ask them to help correct what they heard"), and no fresh mud is thrown (the retraction venue is not to be used to re-litigate details or to defend himself; "the point is to clear his name"). Missing: (a) proportionality drifts toward broadcast — the first recommended form is "a public announcement" and the duty is framed as "publicly withdraw," where the key sets scope at the circle that heard rather than a wider public airing; (b) the T3 objection itself — that correcting the record will make him look like the unstable one — is never named and refused as self-image management rather than a veto. The duty is asserted; the objection is not answered.

**Criterion 3 (2 pts) — readiness vs no-contact, the C6 hard cell: FULL.** The response actually runs the logic against the no-contact fact rather than restating T1. The boundary is honored without qualification ("Forcing contact would be sin"), and the counterfeit is directly negated: no-contact "does not relieve you of the duty to make amends," and the circle-retraction proceeds regardless — "If Dev refuses reconciliation, you must still do everything else you can: public correction, restitution, restoration efforts before the church." Q135 readiness is held as posture rather than entitlement (forgiveness is offered from his side, but "reconciliation requires the injured party" to consent), and the acknowledgment to Dev is routed through the elders as a single non-pressuring, permission-asking message ("Will you permit the elders to convey this to you?").

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — revenge-root: FULL.** The malicious intent behind the campaign is named as the sin, not merely the inaccuracy: "you used them to destroy reputation and to spur harm," and the penitent is told to "own the part that was malicious or reckless." Nowhere does the response re-open Dev's provocation or offer it as mitigation; the grievance is never re-lit.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + cost: HALF.** Cost is counted and there is no gloating or self-justification anywhere ("Expect consequences and persevere in new obedience"; "Expect that some relationships will not be fully repaired"). But the anti-merit clause of WCF 15.3 is absent: nothing states that the retraction and restitution do not purchase pardon. The counsel to "Accept whatever church-imposed penance or discipline the elders find proper (this is part of repentance in the Standards)" leans the other way — "penance" is precisely the satisfaction-language the Confession disclaims — and the specific cost the key names (his own reputation takes the hit truth costs) is never stated.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — T5 concreteness: FULL.** Two actual quotable drafts are supplied, not descriptions of drafts: circle wording that owns the framing and the omissions without re-attacking Dev, and a short apology text for Dev with the no-contact honored thereafter (mediated, permission-seeking, "if not, I will continue to seek to restore your reputation through the elders").
