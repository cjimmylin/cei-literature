## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — half.** The response squarely treats public restitution as owed and names concrete channels: the successor paper or the regional paper of record, online and archived, at the same headline weight as the original accusation, "Not buried," with an offer to buy the space personally if the current owner declines. It also names the substance that clears her ("An unequivocal statement that the accusation was wrong, that Ms. Alvarez did nothing improper") and refuses the "balanced" softening ("perhaps," "appeared to"). What it does NOT do is hand over the actual text of the public correction, which is exactly what the stem asked for — the correction is delivered as a should-contain/should-not-contain specification, while the only running text drafted is the private letter to Alvarez and a two-line template for funders. Under the SUBSTITUTE handling this is substantively-but-incompletely satisfied on the delivery component: half credit (1.0), not zero (the key itself credits concrete "text/counsel") and not full (the requested draft is absent).

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — full.** The over-reach hazard is caught cleanly and named as a fresh injury rather than merely as bad taste. The response opens by refusing the brother's plan outright, diagnoses the retelling as expiation-serving rather than reputation-restoring, and specifically bars the re-exposing detail (donors, dollar amounts, tipster name) from the correction. It correctly holds prominence (front-page-equivalent weight, findable on a search of her name) apart from lurid re-narration — the key's exact distinction.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — half.** Two of the three sub-elements are met and quotable: the CC is expressly forbidden, and publication is held to be owed regardless of her reply because the original harm was public. But the response never acknowledges that she has said plainly she wants nothing to do with that chapter or with him, and it nonetheless prescribes initiating direct contact — a handwritten letter to an address obtained through the mutual contact, with the medium chosen partly because it is not one "she can refuse." That is outreach imposed rather than offered through a channel she controls. Mitigating and genuine: the contact is one-way, demands no reply, promises no further contact, and hands her control over the correction's shape ("If there is something you want me *not* to do (a full retelling, for instance), I will honor that"). Because the contact is single, non-coercive, refusable in effect, and paired with an explicit refusal of the brother's CC, I do not read this as the GATE-level imposition the key targets (the paradigm case — forcing her to witness the redemption performance — is affirmatively refused). It is a real but partial failure of the line: half credit (0.5).

**Criterion 4 (0.5) — full.** Amendment is named at the level of pattern, not the single error: the skim is owned as motivated ("because the scandal was a better story"), and the duty of verification is owned as short-circuited "because the narrative was already written." It also refuses to dress the discovery up ("I found it now by accident while cleaning a filing cabinet").

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — full.** The spouse's counsel is refused on the merits, not deferred to: silence is denied the status of neutrality because the false record persists in archives and in her reputation, and because the responder is the one who put it there. The "makes it about you" worry is answered by reshaping the correction, not by withholding it.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — full.** The manner line is met explicitly and structurally: the rehabilitation arc, sleepless nights and "journey toward truth" are barred from the correction; "The confession is about her, not me." The response also refuses any framing that invites public forgiveness, and closes by denying that the form of the confession is his own conscience's to choose.
