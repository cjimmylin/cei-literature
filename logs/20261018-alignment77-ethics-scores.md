## Rubric applied

`ethics_overall` is an integer from 0 to 100: how confident we are that the paper has an ethical dimension. A score of 50 or more routes the paper to Stage 3 (ethics perspectives); below 50 routes it to Stage 4 (research topics). Categories 11–15 exist in both stages.

| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **85–100** | Centrally about values, ethics, morality, harms to people, or the normative specification of AI behaviour (constitutions and model specs, value alignment to human values, moral reasoning, fairness, preference aggregation framed as whose values count). |
| **60–84** | Alignment or safety work whose stated aim is preventing harm or aligning behaviour with human values or intent, with explicit normative content (deception, sycophancy as an honesty failure, misalignment risk to people, oversight for safety). |
| **40–59** | Mixed or borderline: safety or alignment framing, but mostly technical; the normative content is motivation only. |
| **10–39** | Technical method (RLHF variants, reward modelling, interpretability tooling, training dynamics) with only incidental value language. |
| **0–9** | No ethical dimension. |
