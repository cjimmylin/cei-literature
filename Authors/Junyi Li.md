# Junyi Li

## Research Summary

Postdoctoral research fellow at City University of Hong Kong, Department of Data Science, working with Prof. ZHAO Xiangyu. Previously at University of Maryland and National University of Singapore. Author of HaluEval, a large-scale hallucination evaluation benchmark for LLMs (2023). Published research on LLM steerability toward data-driven personas, demonstrating 57-77% improvements over baselines through persona-based steering approaches.
