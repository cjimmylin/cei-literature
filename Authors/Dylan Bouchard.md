# Dylan Bouchard

## Biographical Summary

Dylan Bouchard is a Principal AI Researcher at CVS Health, where they lead initiatives on the responsible development of generative AI in the healthcare sector. Their work focuses on the technical implementation of ethics and fairness assessments for internal LLM applications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bouchard's research focuses on the "LangFair" framework—a Python library for assessing use-case-specific bias and fairness in Large Language Models. They aim to provide actionable metrics that allow developers to identify and mitigate toxicity, stereotypes, and disparate impacts in clinical and administrative AI tools.

### Significant Achievements
As a key figure in the CVS Health Tech community, Bouchard has pioneered "Bring Your Own Prompts" (BYOP) evaluation methods. Their work on detecting LLM hallucinations at generation time helps ensure the safety of AI-assisted health decision-making.
