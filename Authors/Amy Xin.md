# Amy Xin

## Research Summary

Amy Xin is a researcher at Tsinghua University working on large language model evaluation and instruction following. Her research focuses on understanding how LLMs handle complex constraints in agentic scenarios. She contributed to AGENTIF, a benchmark for systematically evaluating LLM instruction following ability in real-world agentic applications, featuring realistic instructions averaging over 1,700 words with complex constraint structures.
