## Abstract

Creates approximately 1 million human-translated words of new benchmark data in 8 low-resource African languages covering 160+ million speakers. Includes cultural appropriateness adjustments that yield 3.0% performance boost, demonstrating that culturally grounded evaluation matters. Provides publicly available benchmarks, translations, and code.
