## Abstract

Proposes methodology for detecting emerging coded antisemitic terminology using LLMs, based on the hypothesis that coded terminology begets more coded terminology. Uses semantic similarity to known antisemitic terminology via fine-tuned LLMs to filter emergent expressions. Addresses the evolving nature of hate speech where communities invent new coded terms to evade detection.
