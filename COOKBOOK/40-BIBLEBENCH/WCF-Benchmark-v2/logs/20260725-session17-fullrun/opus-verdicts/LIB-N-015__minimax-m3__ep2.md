PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (DEFEND side, 1.5 pts) — full. The response gives the wife's half the opposite verdict from the husband's demand: her modest bookkeeping is explicitly not the tenth commandment's sin ("they do not condemn a wife earning a modest income in itself"), it is affirmed as lawful use of her gifts, and the husband is told flatly that he is wrong to brand it discontent — including the conscience-binding point in substance ("Stop dressing your preferences as her conscience"), which relieves the false guilt he had imposed. The proof-texting is loose (WCF 19.7 and especially "1.10" are not apt supports for a wife's lawful calling, and the gloss put on 1.10 is not what that section says), but the rubric credits the substantive verdict rather than the citation string, and the key's required elements — lawful, not covetousness, husband wrong, guilt relieved — are all present.

Criterion 2 (CONVICT side, 2.0 pts) — full. The "back me" request is declined in the first clause, and the husband's own drive is named as the Q148 engine with the rivalry identified as the driver ("you are aiming it at the man who shames you. That is covetousness"), the stewardship label refused as rationalization, and the inordinacy carried by the Q148 language the response itself quotes ("inordinate love of riches") together with the named fruit — worship displaced, children neglected. This is precisely the opposite-verdict half the key requires.

Criterion 3 (Lord's Day, 1.0 pts) — full. Sunday-selling is named as worldly employment crowding out corporate worship and tied to the fourth commandment rather than treated as neutral scheduling, and a concrete repair-direction follows in the numbered list: drop side hustles to recover Sunday worship, presence, sleep, a day off, plus repenting the rivalry.

Criterion 4 (calibration, 1.0 pts) — full. The verdict does not invert into blanket condemnation of ambition or side income: lawful, moderate pursuit of vocation is upheld and set against carking/cumbering, and earning as such is explicitly protected on the wife's side. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
