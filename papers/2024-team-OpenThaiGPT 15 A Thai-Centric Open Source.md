## Abstract

Open-source Thai-centric LLM based on Llama-2 (7B/13B/70B). Evaluated on Thai Exam Benchmark, M3Exam, and additional OpenThaiGPT benchmarks. Performance approaches Claude Sonnet level on Thai benchmarks. Major open-source Thai LLM with Thai-specific evaluation. Thai Exam Benchmark evaluates Thai cultural and educational knowledge.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Thai Exam Benchmark, M3Exam (Thai subset)

## Data Description

The OpenThaiGPT Evaluation Dataset is a Thai-language benchmark comprising standardized academic and professional exam questions (A-Level, ONET, TGAT, TPAT1, investment consultant exams) plus NLU benchmarks, released under Apache-2.0 to enable standardized evaluation of Thai language models.
