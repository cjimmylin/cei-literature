# Catherine Olsson

## Biographical Summary

Catherine Olsson is a Member of Technical Staff at Anthropic, where she works on the interpretability team building tools to understand how neural networks function internally. She holds a Master of Engineering in Computer Science and Cognitive Science from MIT. Previously, she worked at Google Brain on Ian Goodfellow's adversarial ML team and at OpenAI, bringing deep experience in AI safety research.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Mechanistic Interpretability**: Working on Anthropic's efforts to reverse-engineer neural networks to understand their internal mechanisms.
- **AI Safety Research**: Contributing to building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.
- **Transformer Understanding**: Research on understanding how transformer architectures process and learn information.

### Significant Achievements
- **Cross-Institutional Impact**: Contributed to AI safety research across three leading organizations (Google Brain, OpenAI, Anthropic).
- **Adversarial ML Expertise**: Worked on both adversarial attacks/defenses and generative adversarial networks at Google Brain.
- **80,000 Hours Podcast**: Featured discussing career paths into AI alignment for ML engineers.

## Major Publications
- **A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment** (2021) - Research on using dialogue systems to study AI alignment
- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** (2022) - Foundational work on training harmless AI
- **In-context Learning and Induction Heads** - Research on transformer mechanisms
