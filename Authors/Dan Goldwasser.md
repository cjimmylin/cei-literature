# Dan Goldwasser

## Affiliation
Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, Purdue University

## Research Summary

Dan Goldwasser is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Purdue University, leading research in natural language processing and machine learning. His work focuses on understanding and generating natural language with particular attention to cultural context, commonsense reasoning, and social media analysis. He has made significant contributions to grounding NLP systems in cultural and social context, which is essential for building AI systems that work across diverse populations.
