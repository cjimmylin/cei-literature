# Aiyana K. Willard

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aiyana K. Willard is a Reader in the Department of Psychology at Brunel University London. She holds a PhD from the University of British Columbia and has held postdoctoral positions at the University of Oxford and UT Austin. Her research explores the evolutionary and cultural origins of belief systems and morality.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aiyana's research program investigates how cognitive and cultural mechanisms influence supernatural beliefs and religious prosociality. She is a key contributor to the "Evolution of Religion and Morality" initiative, studying how belief systems enforce cooperative norms and how new spiritual beliefs evolve in contemporary Western societies.

### Significant Achievements
She has published extensively on the psychology of atheism, witchcraft beliefs, and the transmission of myths and folktales. Her interdisciplinary approach combines anthropology and psychology to understand the deep-seated predispositions humans have toward religious and moral reasoning.
