# Chengyi Ju

## Biographical Summary

Chengyi Ju is an algorithm researcher at Alibaba Future Laboratory. She collaborates closely with researchers from the Alibaba DAMO Academy. Her work spans large-scale multi-modal searching, question-answering systems, and increasingly, the alignment of AIGC technologies with human values.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Value Alignment**: Leading research on "NaVAB" (Native Value Alignment Benchmark) to evaluate how LLMs align with diverse national values (China, US, UK, France, Germany).
- **Multi-modal Systems**: Developing super-large-scale pre-training systems for image-text data in e-commerce contexts.
- **AIGC Technologies**: Investigating generative models (GPT, VLM) and their application in search and discovery.

### Significant Achievements
- **NaVAB Benchmark**: First author of the NaVAB benchmark, which introduces a pipeline for extracting and benchmarking national values to ensure culturally aware AI alignment.

## Major Publications
- **NaVAB: Benchmarking Multi-National Value Alignment for Large Language Models**: A key contribution to cross-cultural AI safety.
