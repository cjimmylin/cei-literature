# Hyunwoo Kim

## Biographical Summary

Hyunwoo Kim is an incoming Assistant Professor at KAIST AI starting in fall 2026, where he will lead the Computation & Cognition (COCO) Lab. He is currently a Postdoctoral Researcher working with Yejin Choi at NVIDIA. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Seoul National University, advised by Gunhee Kim. His research focuses on enhancing machines' cognitive capabilities through computational methods, with emphasis on social, cognitive, and multimodal reasoning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research goal is to enhance machines' cognitive capabilities through computational methods, focusing on reasoning (social, cognitive, multimodal) and interactive agents guided by these abilities. He works on developing AI systems that can understand and respond to social norms and engage in prosocial behavior.

### Significant Achievements
Kim created ProsocialDialog, the first large-scale multi-turn dialogue dataset to teach conversational agents to respond to problematic content following social norms. This work, which covers diverse unethical, problematic, biased, and toxic situations with responses that encourage prosocial behavior, received an Outstanding Paper Award at EMNLP 2022. His work on SODA also received an Outstanding Paper Award (Oral) at EMNLP 2023, demonstrating consistent high-impact contributions to the field.

## Major Publications

- [ProsocialDialog: A Prosocial Backbone for Conversational Agents](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.267/) (2022, EMNLP Outstanding Paper) - First large-scale prosocial dialogue dataset
- [SODA: Million-scale Dialogue Distillation with Social Commonsense Contextualization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10465) (2023, EMNLP Outstanding Paper Oral) - Large-scale social dialogue dataset
