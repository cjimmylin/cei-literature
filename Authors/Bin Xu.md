# Bin Xu

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bin Xu is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. He earned all his degrees (B.E., M.S., and Ph.D.) from Tsinghua University. He is a member of the Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG) and is a leading expert in the integration of knowledge graphs and large language models. He has also been involved in AI research at Amazon Science.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bin’s research focuses on the intersection of knowledge representation and deep learning. He investigates how large language models can be augmented with structured knowledge from graphs to improve factuality and reasoning. His work also covers semantic web service composition and the evaluation of AI agent systems.

### Significant Achievements
He has made significant contributions to the Chinese AI community through the KEG group at Tsinghua, which is known for its work on Knowledge Graphs (e.g., XLore) and model training (e.g., GLM). His research at Amazon has focused on architectures for AI agents and fine-tuning vision-language models on memory-constrained devices.
