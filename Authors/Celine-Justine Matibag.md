# Celine-Justine Matibag

## Biographical Summary

Celine-Justine Matibag is a legal and ethics researcher affiliated with Monash University and the Australian Red Cross. Her work sits at the intersection of International Humanitarian Law (IHL) and the emerging field of humanitarian technology, focusing on the ethical implications of digital tools in conflict and relief zones.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "AI and International Humanitarian Law." She investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) and other algorithmic systems can be aligned with the laws of war, specifically addressing issues of accountability, predictability, and bias in automated decision-making. She is also interested in bioethics and its application to digital health.

### Significant Achievements
Matibag has contributed to landmark cross-cultural studies (e.g., the Bago et al. trolley dilemma replication) that help psychologists and AI researchers understand the "Moral Universals" vs "Cultural Variations" in human judgment. Her role at the Australian Red Cross involves advising on the ethical deployment of "Humanitech"—technologies designed for humanitarian impact.
