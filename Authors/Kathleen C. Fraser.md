# Kathleen C. Fraser

## Research Summary

Kathleen Fraser is a Research Officer in the Text Analytics group at the National Research Council Canada (NRC) in Ottawa, where she investigates language technologies for healthcare and social good. Her research focuses on algorithms to automatically detect speech and language markers of cognitive or mental state, examining stereotyping and bias in social media text and machine learning models, and evaluating frontier AI models for safety risks with associated mitigations. She represented Canada at the inaugural meeting of the International AI Safety Institutes in 2024 and participated in an international joint testing exercise at the AI Action Summit in Paris in early 2025. She holds a PhD in computer science from the University of Toronto.
