# Zhaowei Zhang

## Research Summary

Zhaowei Zhang is a third-year PhD Candidate at the Institute for AI, School of Intelligence Science and Technology, Peking University, in the PAIR-Lab led by Prof. Yaodong Yang. He is also affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of General Artificial Intelligence, BIGAI. His research focuses on AI alignment, AI for governance, and multi-agent systems. He developed ValueDCG for measuring comprehensive human value understanding ability of LLMs, advancing methods to evaluate how well AI systems can comprehend and align with diverse human values.
