## Abstract

Applies MORALDIRECTION framework to multilingual models across five languages using Moral Foundations Questionnaire. Finds multilingual models encode differing moral biases that don't necessarily correspond to cultural differences in human opinions.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2, B5
**Relevance:** Establishes that multilingual LMs have language-dependent moral biases. Methodology applicable to Vietnamese moral bias investigation.
