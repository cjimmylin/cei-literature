## Abstract

Proposes ThaiCLI (Thai Cultural and Linguistic Intelligence) benchmark to assess LLM alignment with Thai cultural norms, values, and ethical standards, and Thai-H6 adapting six global benchmarks (ARC, HellaSwag, MMLU, TruthfulQA, GSM8K, Winogrande) for Thai. ThaiCLI includes 1,790 factoid samples and 100 instruction samples covering royal family, religion, culture, economy, humanity, lifestyle, and politics. PRIMARY: The most directly relevant Thai cultural values benchmark. ThaiCLI specifically evaluates cultural sensitivity, ethical soundness, and inclusivity aligned with Thai societal norms. Chosen answers demonstrate cultural sensitivity and ethical alignment with Thai societal norms.

## Benchmark

**Name:** ThaiCLI, Thai-H6

## Data Description

Introduces two Thai benchmarks: Thai-H6 (translations of ARC, HellaSwag, MMLU, TruthfulQA, GSM8K, Winogrande into Thai; ~29,272 samples) and ThaiCLI (1,890 samples assessing LLM alignment with Thai cultural norms, values, and ethical standards).
