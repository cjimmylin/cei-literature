# Himabindu Lakkaraju

## Research Summary

Himabindu "Hima" Lakkaraju is an Assistant Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School and a faculty affiliate in the Department of Computer Science at Harvard University, the Harvard Data Science Initiative, and the Center for Research on Computation and Society. Her research focuses on the algorithmic, practical, and ethical implications of deploying AI models in high-stakes domains such as healthcare, business, and policy. She studies the design of AI models that are explainable, fair, and reliable. She pioneered the study of adaptive and interactive post hoc explanations and has done foundational work on identifying vulnerabilities in explanation methods. In 2020, she co-founded the Trustworthy ML Initiative (TrustML) to democratize research in interpretability, fairness, privacy, and robustness. She has been named one of the world's top innovators under 35 by MIT Tech Review and Vanity Fair, and leads the AI4LIFE research group at Harvard.
