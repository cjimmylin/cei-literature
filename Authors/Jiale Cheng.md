# Jiale Cheng

## Research Summary

Jiale Cheng is a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, affiliated with the Conversational AI (CoAI) Group and Zhipu AI. His research focuses on large language model evaluation, alignment, prompt optimization, and critique generation. He works closely with Jie Tang, Minlie Huang, and Hongning Wang.

## Key Contributions

- AlignBench: Benchmarking Chinese Alignment of Large Language Models (ACL 2024)
- Black-Box Prompt Optimization for LLM alignment without model training (ACL 2024)
- VPO: Video Prompt Optimization for text-to-video generation safety
- LongSafety: Research on safety vulnerabilities in long-context LLMs
- VisionReward: Fine-grained multi-dimensional reward model for images and videos
