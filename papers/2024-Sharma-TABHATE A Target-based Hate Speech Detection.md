## Abstract

Target-based hate speech detection dataset in Hindi using deep learning and transformer models for hate speech detection. Target-based hate speech detection for Hindi with cultural targeting annotations.

## Benchmark

**Name:** TABHATE

## Data Description

TABHATE: a manually annotated corpus of 2,020 Hindi tweets labelled for target-based hate speech (individual targeted, community targeted, none), the first dataset of its kind for Hindi with target-based classification.
