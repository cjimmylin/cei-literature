## Abstract

First study on adapting modern LLMs for the Sámi language (Northern Sámi). Compiles the SAmi LLM Token (SALT) dataset with ~22M tokens. Experiments with ~7B parameter models on multilingual training. Finds decoder-only models under sequential multilingual training perform best for ultra-low-resource languages.

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_23
**Relevance:** First LLM training study for Sámi language. Evaluates model capabilities for an indigenous Nordic language. Relevant to Sami cultural inclusion in AI, though primarily focused on language modeling rather than cultural values benchmarking.
