# Abulhair Saparov

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abulhair Saparov is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Purdue University. Previously, he was a post-doctoral associate at the NYU Center for Data Science. His research focuses on the intersection of machine learning, natural language understanding, and complex reasoning processes in AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abulhair's research program investigates how large language models perform deductive and causal reasoning. He develops neuro-symbolic methods for knowledge representation and evaluates the truthfulness and mechanistic interpretability of AI systems. His work aims to build models that can reason reliably in open-world settings.

### Significant Achievements
His work at NYU with He He pioneered synthetic datasets for analyzing reasoning failures in transformers. He has also made contributions to Bayesian nonparametrics and scalable inference, applying his methods to linguistics and biology.

## Major Publications

- [Language Models are Greedy Reasoners: A Systematic Analysis of Discontinuity in Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01240) (2022) - *Critically analyzes the reasoning capabilities of LLMs.*
- [Testing the Reasoning Abilities of Language Models using synthetic datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *Developed benchmarks for logical reasoning in NLP.*
