## Abstract

Evaluates GPT-3.5, GPT-4.1, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Claude 3 Opus on 4,500 manually labeled YouTube/TikTok comments for toxic language detection in Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian. Best F1=0.82.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B4
**Relevance:** Directly evaluates LLM toxicity detection capabilities in Serbian and Croatian, demonstrating prompt design impact on low-resource Balkan languages.
