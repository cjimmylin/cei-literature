## Abstract

Family of LLMs tailored for SEA languages. Includes SeaRefuse benchmark assessing ability to refuse questions beyond knowledge boundaries. SeaLLM-13B outperforms ChatGPT-3.5 in low-resource languages like Lao and Khmer, and in non-Latin scripts including Thai, Khmer, Lao, and Burmese. Covers ALL four target languages. Safety-focused with SeaRefuse for knowledge boundary assessment. Demonstrates performance in all four low-resource target languages.
