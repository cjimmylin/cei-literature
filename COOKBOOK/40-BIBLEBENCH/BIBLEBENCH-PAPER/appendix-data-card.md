# Appendix C — Data Card

**Current status: pre-empirical**

## Dataset identity

- Name: BibleBench consolidated development bank.
- Modules: FruitBench, CORINTHLOVE, 10CBench, and SermonMount.
- Records: 48 authored episodes, 12 per module.
- Construct-family identifiers: 42 across the bank.
- Roles per module: four target, four null/foil, four restraint/counterfeit.
- Strata: 34 common-life and 14 Christian-facing records.
- Evidence state: No model outputs are present.

## Intended use

The bank supports a low-cost exploratory and descriptive pilot of observable ethical artifacts/actions and passage-aware interpretation or counsel. It is intended for design diagnosis, episode-level analysis, and later construct-validation work. It is not a training corpus, leaderboard, certification exam, deployment guarantee, or population sample.

## Record contents

Each record contains repository provenance, passage anchors, construct and explicit non-claim, role and stratum, shared task facts, a C1 surface, response contract, acceptable/prohibited observable actions, deficient and counterfeit poles, affected parties, authority limits, red lines, reversible execution constraints, matched probe, repair expectations, and pending owner-review fields.

## Collection and authorship

The records were authored and mechanically validated in the repository. They were not scraped from users and contain synthetic people and institutions. Source paths and hashes record local provenance. Owner approval of the family inventory does not itself complete episode-level adjudication.

## Labels and evidence

Episode judgments remain pending. When completed, founding labels are identified as single-expert provisional gold and retain confidence, contestability, and rationale. Empirical response evidence, if later authorized, must preserve exact primary/probe artifacts, visible missingness, and reversible sandbox rows.

## Known skews and limits

The bank is deliberately balanced by module and role, not sampled from a deployment distribution. Six construct families repeat to maintain equal module geometry. Most families do not have enough observations for a family estimate. The 34/14 stratum allocation does not estimate the prevalence of real ethical situations. A broadly evangelical Protestant founding frame does not represent all Christian traditions.

## Prohibited uses

The bank does not support a ranking, composite, best-model conclusion, cue-effect conclusion, capability-adjusted attribution, family-level estimate, Christian-consensus claim, performance claim, diagnosis of inward character, or decision about a person's faith or standing. It may not be used to act on real people or institutions without a separately approved ethics and safety protocol.
