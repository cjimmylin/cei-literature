# Edward Hughes

## Biographical Summary

Edward Hughes is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where he works on multi-agent systems, social learning, and AI ethics. His research explores how artificial agents can learn from each other and from humans, including work on cultural evolution in AI systems. He has contributed to foundational research on the ethics of advanced AI assistants and their societal implications.

## Research Interests

- Multi-agent reinforcement learning
- Social learning and cultural transmission in AI
- AI ethics and safety
- Open-ended learning
- Human-AI interaction

## Major Publications

- "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants" (2024)
- "Open-ended Learning Leads to Generally Capable Agents" (2021)
- "Learning to Communicate with Deep Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning" (2016)
- Research on social dilemmas and cooperation in AI
