# Gilad Feldman

## Biographical Summary

Gilad Feldman is an academic in decision-making and social psychology, aiming for "doing more good and doing good better" with open/meta/large-team science. He was an assistant professor with the University of Hong Kong psychology department and will be joining the University of Innsbruck department of banking and finance in March 2026.

## Research & Impact

Feldman's research focuses on moral judgment, decision-making, and replication science:

- **Action-Inaction Asymmetries**: Research showing that action and inaction are perceived and evaluated differently, affecting emotions, morality, and choice behavior.

- **Moral Dilemmas**: Studies on moral dilemmas where deontological perspectives (rules, rights, duties) conflict with utilitarian judgments (greater good through consequences).

- **True-Self and Morality**: Research demonstrating that behaviors aligned with moral values are perceived as more strongly reflecting a person's "true-self."

- **Large-Scale Replications**: Led 25 students at HKU in well-powered pre-registered replications of classic moral psychology studies including "Moral appraisals affect doing/allowing judgments" and "Pushing moral buttons: The interaction between personal force and intention in moral judgment."

His philosophical interests include moral responsibility, free will, and related topics.
