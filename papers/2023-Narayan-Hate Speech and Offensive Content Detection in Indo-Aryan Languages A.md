## Abstract

Comparative analysis of LSTM vs transformer models for hate speech classification across five Indo-Aryan languages. BERT Base Multilingual Cased achieves F1 0.70525 for Assamese and 0.83009 for Bodo. Evaluates mBERT, XLM-R, IndicBERT, MuRIL-BERT.
