# Yupeng Chang

## Research Summary

Yupeng Chang is a researcher at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Jilin University. He is co-first author of the comprehensive survey "A Survey on Evaluation of Large Language Models" published in ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology. This influential work, completed in collaboration with 16 authors from institutions including Microsoft Research, Westlake University, and Carnegie Mellon University, presents a systematic review of LLM evaluation methods across three key dimensions: what to evaluate, where to evaluate, and how to evaluate.
