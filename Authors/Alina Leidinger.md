# Alina Leidinger

## Biographical Summary

Alina Leidinger is a PhD Candidate at the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC) at the University of Amsterdam. Her research is at the forefront of ensuring that language technologies are both technically robust and ethically sound.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alina's research investigates the vulnerabilities of LLM guardrails and the systematic biases present in safety-tuned models. She advocates for "Data Feminism for AI," applying intersectional frameworks to the evaluation and development of NLP systems. Her work on adversarial robustness helps identify where models fail to protect marginalized voices.

## Major Publications
- [Data Feminism for AI: A Framework for Intersectional Fairness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *Proposing social-justice frameworks for AI evaluation.*
