## Abstract

First large-scale investigation of how models made in China and the USA align with people from China and the USA. Elicits responses to MFQ 2.0 and World Values Survey from 10 Chinese and 10 American models, finding all models respond more like American people than Chinese people.

## Data Description

The paper conducts the first large-scale investigation comparing how 10 Chinese-made and 10 American-made LLMs align with Chinese and American human values. Models were presented with the Moral Foundations Questionnaire 2.0 (36 items, 6 moral dimensions) and the World Values Survey (19 ethical values items) in both English and Chinese, with and without national identity personas. Responses were compared against data from thousands of Chinese and American human participants, finding that all LLMs respond more like American people.
