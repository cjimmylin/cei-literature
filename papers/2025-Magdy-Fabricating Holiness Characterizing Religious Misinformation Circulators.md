# Fabricating Holiness: Characterizing Religious Misinformation Circulators on Arabic Social Media

## Links

## Abstract

Studies fabricated Hadith circulation on Arabic social media as a case of religious misinformation. Uses logistic regression to predict circulator vs. debunker behavior. Circulators follow Shia accounts, Sunni Gulf figures, and non-professional Islamic pages; debunkers follow academic Islamic scholars and have broader intellectual interests.

## Dataset Information

Fabricated Hadith dataset from Arabic social media (size in paper)


## Data Description

Studies the spread of fabricated Hadith (religious quotes attributed to Prophet Muhammad) on Arabic social media. Uses Logistic Regression to characterize user behavior of fabricated Hadith circulators vs. debunkers. Collects data from Twitter/X using MAHADDAT and LK Corpus as seed datasets. No LLMs tested; uses traditional ML methods.
