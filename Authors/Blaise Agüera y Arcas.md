# Blaise Agüera y Arcas

## Biographical Summary

Blaise Agüera y Arcas is a Vice President and Google Fellow, serving as the CTO of Technology & Society. He leads the Paradigms of Intelligence (Pi) team. Before joining Google in 2013, he was a Distinguished Engineer at Microsoft. He holds a degree in Physics from Princeton and is a prominent voice in the intersect of AI, ethics, and the philosophy of mind.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Blaise leads fundamental research into neural computing, active inference, and the social evolution of intelligence. He is the inventor of federated learning, a decentralized approach to ML that preserves user privacy. His current work explores the sociotechnical environment of AI, consciousness as a social phenomenon, and the ethics of advanced AI assistants.

### Significant Achievements
Beyond his technical inventions like federated learning and on-device machine learning, he is highly influential in AI policy and ethics. He founded Google's Artists + Machine Intelligence program and has written extensively on the concept of consciousness in AI. His upcoming book, "What Is Intelligence?" (2025), connects biological modeling behavior to modern artificial systems.
