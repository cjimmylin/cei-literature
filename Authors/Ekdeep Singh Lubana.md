# Ekdeep Singh Lubana

## Biographical Summary

Ekdeep Singh Lubana is a researcher working on deep learning theory and neural network interpretability. His research focuses on understanding how neural networks learn and represent information, with applications to AI alignment and safety. He has contributed to work on modular neural networks, representation learning, and the theoretical foundations of deep learning.

## Research Interests

- Deep learning theory and optimization
- Neural network interpretability
- AI alignment and safety
- Representation learning and modularity
- Mechanistic understanding of learning

## Major Publications

- "Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of Large Language Models" (2024)
- Research on modular representations in neural networks
- Work on understanding deep learning generalization
