## Abstract

AI, in its current form, can be argued to be utilitarian (a form of consequentialism), by design. In supervised learning, an objective function is optimized to maximize accuracy or minimize error in predictions, while in reinforcement learning, a reward function is maximized. This paper examines the relationship between Machine Ethics and AI Alignment fields and their approaches to ethical AI.

## Data Description

This position paper explores the relationship between machine ethics and AI alignment as research disciplines. It discusses ongoing research in both fields, clarifying their goals: machine ethics aims to embed ethical theories within AI, while AI alignment ensures AI behaves as intended by humans. No dataset or benchmark is created; the paper is a conceptual comparison and discussion piece.
