# Benchmarking Multimodal Models for Ukrainian Language Understanding Across Academic and Cultural Domains

## Authors

[[Semenova]], [[Lesia]]

## Metadata

**Year:** 2024

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv

**Benchmark:** UACUISINE

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14647

## Abstract

Multimodal benchmark based on 20 popular Ukrainian dishes for culture testing. Identifies cultural misattribution issue where Ukrainian Borsch (UNESCO heritage) was mislabeled as 'Russian Red Borscht'.

## Data Description

Introduces ZNO-Vision (4,306 multimodal questions from Ukrainian university entrance exams across 12 disciplines) and UACUISINE (140 QA pairs on 20 Ukrainian dishes) as new Ukrainian-language multimodal benchmarks.
