# Diego Alves

## Research Summary

Diego Alves is a researcher working on natural language processing and explainable AI. His research focuses on developing interpretable methods for analyzing moral and ethical content in text, including the application of Moral Foundations Theory to NLP systems. He has contributed to work on explainable text classification for moral content analysis.
