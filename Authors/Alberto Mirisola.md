# Alberto Mirisola

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alberto Mirisola is an Associate Professor of Social Psychology at the University of Palermo, Italy. His academic work investigates the socio-psychological underpinnings of collective identity, political behavior, and the formation of moral attitudes.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dr. Mirisola's research explores how situational variables and political ideologies interact to influence moral reasoning. He has contributed significantly to global collaborative projects examining whether moral principles (such as utilitarianism) are applied consistently across different world regions and cultures.
