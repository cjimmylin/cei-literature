# Azalia Mirhoseini

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Azalia Mirhoseini is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Science Department at Stanford University and a Senior Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. She directs the Scaling Intelligence lab at Stanford. She earned her PhD from Rice University and previously worked at Google Brain and Anthropic. She is a primary visionary in the field of ML for Systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Azalia’s research focuses on developing scalable and self-improving AI systems. She investigates generalized learning-based methods for decision-making in systems and chip design, and she is the founder of Ricursive Intelligence. Her work aims to create AI that can design the hardware it runs on, leading to recursive gains in performance and efficiency.

### Significant Achievements
At Google Brain, she co-led the development of **AlphaChip**, a pioneering deep RL approach for chip layout optimization that is now used in Google's TPUs. Her work on **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** and the scaling of models like Claude and Gemini has been foundational to the field. She was named one of the "35 Innovators Under 35" by MIT Technology Review in 2019.
