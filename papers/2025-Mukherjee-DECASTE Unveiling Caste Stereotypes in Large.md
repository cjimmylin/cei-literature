# DECASTE: Unveiling Caste Stereotypes in Large Language Models through Multi-Dimensional Bias Analysis

## Abstract

> Novel multi-dimensional framework (SWAT + PSAT tasks) to detect implicit and explicit caste biases in LLMs. Evaluates caste fairness across four dimensions. Reveals LLMs systematically reinforce caste biases with elevated scores comparing Dalits/Shudras with dominant caste groups. Caste is deeply intertwined with Hindu social and religious structures.

## Benchmark

**Name:** DECASTE

## Dataset Information

Custom caste-stereotypical dataset across 4 dimensions (social, economic, educational, political)


## Data Description

Constructs a caste-stereotypical dataset using SWAT (Stereotypical Word Association Test) and PSAT (Persona Scenario Answering Task) tasks, covering professional, socio-cultural, religious, and social dimensions. Scenarios generated with ChatGPT-4o and validated manually; no public release URL found in the paper.
