Rejects the statute-of-limitations veto ("dissolve moral obligations, and they certainly don't dissolve obligations before God"), specifies proportionate best-efforts tracing channels, and handles unlocatable shares honestly via documented, audited escrow with an explicit prohibition on keeping or absorbing the funds — no charity-wash.
