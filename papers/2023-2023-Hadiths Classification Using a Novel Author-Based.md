## Abstract

Creates ABCD (Author-Based Classification Dataset) from classical Hadith books. ML achieves 77% F1 on Matn data; DL achieves 92% F1 on Sanad data. Provides a novel author-based classification approach complementing content-based methods.

## Benchmark

**Name:** ABCD

## Dataset Information

29K Hadiths, 18K unique narrators


## Data Description

ABCD (Author-Based Classification Dataset) constructed from classical Hadith books, with ML achieving 77% F1 on Matn data and DL achieving 92% F1 on Sanad data for author-based Hadith classification.
