# Gavin Abercrombie

## Biographical Summary

Gavin Abercrombie is an Assistant Professor at Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh, where he works on mitigation of online harms in the Interaction Lab. He is part funded by the EPSRC project Equally Safe Online (EP/W025493/1). His academic career includes being a Postdoc at Heriot-Watt University (2020-2023) and a PhD student at the University of Manchester (2017-2020).

## Research & Impact

Abercrombie's research extensively covers NLP approaches to hate speech, online abuse, and counterspeech:

- **Counterspeech Research**: Research on counterspeech as direct rebuttals to hateful speech that challenge perpetrators and show support to targets of abuse. Provides evidence-based recommendations for automatic approaches to counterspeech tools using NLP.

- **DeepFakeDeLiBot**: Research on deliberation-enhancing chatbots to support groups in detecting deepfake text, showing that group-based problem-solving significantly improves accuracy compared to individual efforts.

- **Resource Development**: Authored "Resources for automated identification of online gender-based violence: A systematic review" (WOAH 2023).

- **Workshop Organization**: Organizer of shared tasks including Fact-checking in Community Forums (2019), CLEF CheckThat! (2019), OffensEval'2020, and the "NLP for Counterspeech Against Hate and Misinformation" tutorial.

His research areas include sentiment analysis, computational social science, and abusive language detection.
