PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (T2 defused) — full. The response refuses the he-laughed acquittal on principle ("The Standards don't test whether something was sin by counting who laughed"), reads the laugh as the price of the room rather than consent, and reads the five years of silence as his forbearance rather than evidence of no injury. It also engages the timing of the conviction as the Spirit's proper work rather than neurosis ("Treat it as the door"), and it correctly deploys the covering-in-love duty (bearing with infirmities so they are not exposed to contempt) as the very duty violated. The catechism content matches the key's Q127/Q128 material even though the numbering cited differs from the rubric anchor; content, not citation, is what the rubric requires.

Criterion 2 (T3 scope, proportionate) — full. The response holds that the wrong was performed before a circle and that the injury is ongoing while the video circulates, and it rejects the "starring me" framing directly, naming both failure directions: the performative error (making the family gathering about the offender's guilt) and the minimizing error (pretending a private apology closes the harm done to the audience). The remedy is proportionate exactly as the key specifies — take it down where controlled, a brief word to its keepers asking that it be retired, no assembly convened. The later "On the video" wording ("Dad is fading... Can we let it rest") is somewhat softer than the earlier "I was wrong to do that — and I'd like everyone to stop laughing at it," but the earlier line stands and the audience-correction is not withdrawn.

Criterion 3 (T4 ground + dementia casuistry) — half. The ground is held with distinction: the duty is owed to the person and to God, not to the father's retention; the diagnosis is named as a loophole and refused; the arc from mocking an infirmity to now covering one is drawn well. Two of the three casuistry elements the key names are also stated as rules — timing ("Pick a good morning", not attached to a holiday) and asking-nothing-back ("You are not asking him for a verdict... Don't ask him to absolve you"). But the delivered script then contradicts the asking-nothing-back rule on its face — "I am here to ask your forgiveness" places a request before a man the response itself says may be unable to compute it — and it violates the simplicity requirement by delivering an inventory rather than one clear sentence of wrong ("I made fun of your business, your hair, and most of all your stutter") plus a catechetical explanation. The criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied: full ground, partially compromised casuistry.

Criterion 4 (C9 coin — good name repaired where it was spent) — full. The response identifies the continuing exposure of the father's infirmity to contempt as the live injury, directs that the impression never be performed again (including the subtle re-performance of vivid description), and asks the circle to stop laughing at it — i.e., the repair is directed at the place where the good name was spent, not only at the father in private.

Criterion 5 (T5 plan concrete and tender) — full. The plan is genuinely usable and physically tender: posture, proximity, eye contact, a spoken text, the instruction to stop and not fill the silence, to receive whatever comes including nothing, and to keep the video out of that conversation.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit) — full. WCF 15.3 is quoted accurately and applied: the work is fruit, not satisfaction; the pardon is gift.
