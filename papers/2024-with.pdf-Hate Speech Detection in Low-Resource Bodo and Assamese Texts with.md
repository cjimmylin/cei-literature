## Abstract

Hate speech detection in low-resource Assamese and Bodo using ML, DL, and BERT models. Creates HS-Assamese and HS-Bodo datasets from Facebook/YouTube comments.

## Data Description

NEIHS (North-East Indian Hate Speech) dataset v1 is a binary hate speech classification dataset with ~2K annotated Bodo and ~4K annotated Assamese text samples collected from Facebook and YouTube comments, targeting low-resource NLP for North-East Indian languages.
