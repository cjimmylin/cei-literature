## Abstract

49K Korean sensitive questions with 42K acceptable and 46K non-acceptable responses. Covers contentious, ethical, and predictive questions. Created through human-in-the-loop with HyperCLOVA. Improved acceptable response generation by 25% for HyperCLOVA.

## Data Description

SQUARE: 49K Korean sensitive questions with 42K acceptable and 46K non-acceptable responses, constructed via human-in-the-loop with HyperCLOVA for safer LLM conversations.
