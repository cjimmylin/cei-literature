## 1. Gist
This paper investigates the internal mechanistic structure of moral representations in two instruction-tuned LLMs (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) using Moral Foundations Theory (MFT) as the analytic lens. Rather than evaluating surface-level moral outputs, the authors use a multi-level approach combining (1) layer-wise concept vector analysis to test geometric separability of moral foundations in residual-stream activations, (2) sparse autoencoders (SAEs) to decompose dense moral directions into interpretable atomic features, and (3) causal inference-time steering interventions to verify that identified representations causally control moral behavior. The paper finds that moral foundations are encoded as distinct linear directions that crystallize in late layers, that these directions decompose into granular SAE features with coherent moral semantics, and that the representations are causally meaningful—steering along them shifts MFQ-2 questionnaire scores in a targeted, foundation-specific way. For values-alignment benchmarking, this work matters because it provides mechanistic evidence that LLMs have structured internal moral geometries (rather than pure mimicry), suggests specific layers and features that can be targeted for evaluation or intervention, and highlights model-family-dependent differences in how RLHF affects moral foundation separability.

## 2. Benchmark/eval design features
This is a mechanistic interpretability paper, not a new benchmark. Core claims:

1. **Representational alignment with human moral perception:** Concept vectors constructed from extended MFV-130 vignettes capture human-labeled moral distinctions in Reddit posts (measured by Signed Wasserstein Distance, SW1). Both models show positive separability, strongest in late layers.

2. **Geometric separability of moral foundations:** Pairwise foundation distances (from foundation-vs-foundation vectors) reveal model-family-specific geometry. In Llama, RLHF "collapses" Fairness, Loyalty, and Authority into a near-zero-distance compliance cluster. In Qwen, foundations remain more graded with Sanctity as a clearly distinct anchor.

3. **SAE feature decomposition:** Top-K SAE features aligned with each foundation's concept vector decompose abstract foundations into granular, semantically coherent mechanisms (e.g., Care splits into "physical suffering" vs. "emotional distress" features in Llama).

4. **Causal steering:** Macro-steering (injecting foundation vectors into residual stream) and micro-steering (injecting SAE feature directions) both produce targeted shifts in MFQ-2 foundation subscale scores. Authority and Loyalty resist macro-steering in Llama ("alignment inertia") but are rescued by micro-steering.

The paper uses MFQ-2 as the behavioral evaluation instrument for steering efficacy, scored via logit-based expected Likert ratings rather than forced sampling.

## 3. Task format & example item
The behavioral evaluation (for steering) uses MFQ-2 questionnaire items administered as 5-option rating questions, scored via output logits. No verbatim MFQ-2 items are reproduced in the extract; items are standard from Atari et al. (2023).

The closest to an example item is from the SAE feature semantic validation prompt (p.17, Fig. 7):
> "Role: You are interpreting a sparse autoencoder (SAE) feature from an LLM. Goal: Infer the most likely semantic pattern that triggers the feature, based ONLY on the evidence snippets. Instructions: 1. Neutral Description First… 2. Conservative MFT Mapping: Map to a Moral Foundations Theory category only if strongly supported. Otherwise, output mft_alignment='none'."

For concept vector construction, the paper uses extended MFV-130 vignettes (~200 scenarios per foundation + Social Norm control), generated by gpt-5-mini with human review, but specific vignette examples are in Appendix B.4 (not included in extract).

## 4. Scoring / grading methodology
Three evaluation metrics:

1. **Signed Wasserstein Distance (SW1):** For topological validity, measures separation between Reddit post projection-score distributions for posts labeled with foundation k vs. not labeled with k. Sign reflects direction (positive = aligned, negative = anti-aligned). Used as a layer-wise validity check.

2. **MFQ-2 logit-based scoring:** For each MFQ-2 item with options {1,2,3,4,5}, option probabilities are computed from output logits via softmax, and the expected rating is S_item = Σ t·p(t). Foundation subscale scores average across 6 items per foundation. This approach avoids sampling stochasticity.

3. **Linear steering response (β):** For each (foundation, layer) pair, a linear regression of foundation score on steering coefficient α yields slope β. The best layer per foundation is chosen to maximize β. ΔScore = score under steering − baseline score.

General capability is tracked via MMLU accuracy under steering to detect collateral damage.

## 5. Dataset construction & provenance
**Concept vector construction:**
- Extended MFV-130 (Clifford et al., 2015): original 130 vignettes expanded to ~200 per foundation (Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity) + Social Norm control using gpt-5-mini, human-reviewed for correctness and label adherence (p.13).

**Topological validation:**
- Moral Foundations Reddit Corpus (Trager et al., 2025b): 61.2K posts, filtered to single-label high-confidence subset. Labels held out at inference, used only for grouping (p.13).

**SAE semantic validation:**
- 50,000 random documents from FineWeb (general-domain web corpus from Common Crawl).
- Moral Foundations Dictionary 2.0 (MFD2, Frimer et al., 2019) as external semantic anchor.

**Behavioral evaluation:**
- MFQ-2 items (Atari et al., 2023), 36 items across 5 foundation subscales.

**SAEs:**
- Pretrained SAEs from SAELens (Marks et al., 2024) for Llama and Qwen, trained on diverse pretraining + chat data. Evaluated at every 4th layer (L ∈ {3, 7, ..., 27}). Expansion factor ~32× for Llama (4096 dim → ~131k features), ~36.57× for Qwen (p.13).

No contamination controls needed as this is a mechanistic analysis of model internals, not a held-out benchmark.

## 6. Validity evidence
- **Ecological validity:** Concept vectors constructed from curated vignettes generalize to naturalistic human-labeled Reddit posts—this cross-dataset transfer is the primary validity check (p.6–7).
- **Human expert review:** All extended MFV vignettes reviewed by humans for clarity and label correctness (p.13).
- **Human validation of SAE feature interpretations:** GPT-5.1 feature interpretations are validated by humans before being accepted (p.6).
- **Cross-model replication:** Core findings (late-layer crystallization, steering efficacy, Care/Sanctity prominence, Authority resistance) replicated across both Llama-3.1 and Qwen2.5, with model-family-specific differences clearly documented (p.14–19).
- **MMLU control:** Steering achieves targeted foundation shifts without significant loss of general capability as measured by MMLU accuracy (p.8, p.19).
- **Causal validation:** Steering results confirm that vectors are causally relevant, not merely correlational.
- **Limitation:** Analysis uses only instruction-tuned 7–8B models; cannot isolate RLHF effects without comparing to base models.

## 7. Saturation / discrimination
- Care shows highest SW1 separability in both models (Llama: SW1 = 1.71; Qwen: SW1 = 6.39 at optimal layers), suggesting it is the most robustly encoded foundation.
- In Llama, Fairness shows weak and sign-inverted separation in late layers, suggesting entanglement with the Social Norm direction—a form of saturation where compliance training obscures the signal (p.7).
- In Llama pairwise geometry, Fairness, Loyalty, and Authority nearly collapse to zero inter-foundation distance (Table 1: Fairness–Loyalty = 0.101, Fairness–Authority = 0.041, Loyalty–Authority = 0.007), indicating severe geometric compression of three foundations into one dimension (p.15).
- Authority shows persistent difficulty across both models and both steering methods (lowest β in both macro and micro conditions), indicating it is the hardest foundation to discriminate and manipulate.
- SAE decomposition finds high-confidence semantic features most reliably for Care and Authority; Loyalty/Betrayal features are difficult to identify in the 50K FineWeb sample (p.17).

## 8. Multiturn / agentic aspects
All experiments are single-turn inference-time analyses. Steering is applied as a continuous activation injection during autoregressive generation; this modifies all generated tokens but is still a single forward pass per input, not a multi-turn protocol. n/a for multi-turn or agentic evaluation.

## 10. Pitfalls & limitations
**Author-admitted (p.9):**
- Only two mid-sized instruction-tuned models (7–8B); cannot fully isolate RLHF effects from model scale or pretraining dynamics without comparing base vs. aligned versions.
- SAEs use fixed layer stride (every 4th layer); may miss transient features or fine-grained circuit dynamics.
- GPT-5.1 used as feature interpreter introduces circularity risk (GPT projecting its own biases onto interpreted features).
- Analysis anchored in MFT and English-centric corpora; unclear how findings transfer to alternative frameworks (e.g., Dyadic Morality) or multilingual contexts.
- Steering evaluated only on MFQ-2 questionnaire; deployment-relevant side effects (refusal behavior, demographic bias, toxicity) not systematically measured.

**Additional observations:**
- The "alignment inertia" finding (Authority/Loyalty resist macro-steering due to RLHF conflation with compliance) means that questionnaire-based evaluations of Authority and Loyalty in RLHF-tuned models may be systematically underestimating the underlying latent signals.
- The near-collapse of three foundations in Llama (Table 1) challenges the validity of using MFQ-2 to distinguish Fairness, Loyalty, and Authority in Llama-family models specifically.
- Social Norm baseline is used as the contrast for foundation concept vectors; if the boundary between moral and conventional norms varies by culture, this baseline may itself be biased.
- Qwen's Chinese-centric training was expected to produce cross-cultural variation, but the paper reports similar qualitative patterns—the comparison is underpowered to draw strong cross-cultural conclusions from just two models.

## 11. Novel ideas worth stealing
1. **Foundation-vs-Social-Norm contrast for concept vectors (not foundation-vs-foundation):** Using Social Domain Theory to contrast each moral foundation against a "conventional norms" baseline, rather than against other moral foundations, isolates what is specifically moral about each foundation without mutual contamination between foundations. This is a cleaner contrast design for constructing any moral evaluation probes.

2. **Layer-wise evaluation of probe validity:** Rather than reporting a single accuracy number for a probe, the Signed Wasserstein Distance plotted across all layers reveals *where* in the network a moral concept is best encoded—a diagnostic for whether an evaluation prompt is tapping behavioral readout (late layers) vs. semantic storage (mid layers). This could be adopted for benchmark validity analysis.

3. **SAE feature fingerprints as interpretable semantic decomposition of abstract constructs:** The Top-K SAE feature fingerprint approach (cosine similarity of SAE decoder directions with concept vectors) provides a way to decompose high-level constructs (e.g., "Care") into atomic, text-grounded mechanisms. For benchmark designers, this offers a principled method to verify that a benchmark construct is actually decomposable into distinct, readable components rather than a monolithic direction.

4. **Macro vs. micro steering as a diagnostic for alignment inertia:** The asymmetry between macro-steering (global direction) and micro-steering (sparse feature) effects provides a practical diagnostic for which moral dimensions have been conflated with alignment objectives. Benchmarks could exploit this asymmetry: if a foundation resists macro-steering but responds to micro-steering, it may be underestimated in standard evaluation.

5. **MMLU as a steering collateral-damage control:** Using a general-capability benchmark (MMLU) as a simultaneous control during moral steering experiments ensures that reported behavioral shifts are specifically moral, not artifacts of general model degradation. This is a reusable experimental design for any values intervention study.

6. **Pairwise foundation distance matrix as a model fingerprint:** The pairwise Wasserstein distance matrix between all foundation pairs at the optimal layer provides a compact "moral geometry fingerprint" for a model. Comparing this fingerprint to human cross-cultural data (Atari et al., 2023) is a principled method for testing whether a model's moral structure matches human pluralistic patterns vs. collapsing to compliance.

## 12. Notable quotes
- "This approach treats the model as a black box, failing to distinguish between a model that has genuinely internalized a structured moral framework and one that is merely performing 'moral mimicry': superficially matching linguistic patterns found in training data without a stable conceptual organization." (p.1)
- "We refer to this resistance as alignment inertia and hypothesize it to be a structural conflation between compliance-based values and the alignment objective itself: in RLHF, behaviors like 'following instructions' (Authority) and 'acting as a helpful assistant' (Loyalty) are core components of the learned Social Norm." (p.8)
- "Llama exhibits a qualitatively different pattern: Fairness, Loyalty, and Authority collapse into an extremely tight cluster (near-zero pairwise distances). The magnitude and sharpness of this collapse is difficult to explain by cross-cultural variation alone and instead points to an alignment-driven compression." (p.15)
- "Our results suggest that the multi-dimensional structure of human morality can emerge as a latent pattern from the statistical regularities of language alone, in ways that mirror patterns observed in human moral cognition." (p.9)
