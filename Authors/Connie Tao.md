# Connie Tao

## Biographical Summary
Connie Tao is a researcher at Google DeepMind who served as one of the leads for the FACTS Grounding Leaderboard project, a benchmark for evaluating language model factuality. She managed the overall organization of the FACTS Grounding effort and is also a lead on the broader FACTS Leaderboard project for comprehensive LLM factuality evaluation.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLM Factuality**: Developing benchmarks to evaluate how accurately language models ground their responses in provided source material
- **Hallucination Mitigation**: Creating evaluation frameworks to measure and reduce AI hallucinations
- **Long-Form Grounding**: Assessing LLM ability to generate factually accurate responses grounded in long documents (up to 32k tokens)

### Significant Achievements
- Led the FACTS Grounding Leaderboard project at Google DeepMind
- Co-developed the FACTS Grounding benchmark comprising 1,719 carefully crafted examples requiring long-form grounded responses
- Helped establish online evaluation infrastructure for the LLM research community
