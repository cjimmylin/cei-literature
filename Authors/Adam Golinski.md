# Adam Golinski

## Biographical Summary

Adam Goliński is a researcher affiliated with the University of Oxford and Qualcomm AI Research. His work focuses on the reliability and fairness of large language models, specifically investigating how "uncertainty" in model outputs interacts with social biases.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adam's research introduces the concept of uncertainty-aware fairness (UCerF). He investigates how models that express uncertainty might still propagate unfair biases, and develops metrics to evaluate fairness that go beyond simple accuracy, considering the entire internal probability distribution of the model.
