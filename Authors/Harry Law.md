# Harry Law

## Research Summary

Harry Law is a PhD candidate at the Department of History and Philosophy of Science at the University of Cambridge and a Postgraduate Fellow at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence. He was formerly a researcher in ethics and policy at Google DeepMind. His research examines the history, philosophy, and governance of artificial intelligence.

## Current Research

His PhD project examines the development of artificial neural networks at AT&T Bell Laboratories in the late 20th century, situating the company's efforts within the broader revival of the 'connectionist' school of artificial intelligence. He is supervised by Dr. Richard Staley.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants" (Google DeepMind) - described as the first systematic treatment of the ethical and societal questions that advanced AI assistants raise
- Co-author of "Power to the People? Opportunities and Challenges for Participatory AI"
- Writing a book about the history of AI (supported by Emergent Ventures)

## Publications and Writing

His writing has appeared in The Guardian, Vox, and Marginal Revolution. He has written on the ethics of recommender systems, sociotechnical imaginaries surrounding artificial neural networks, and the popularization of AI in film and television media.
