## Abstract

Multilingual collection of hate speech and abusive language datasets in 15 African languages, annotated by native speakers with three classes (hate, abusive/offensive, neutral) and six target attributes (ethnicity, politics, gender, disability, religion, other). Covers 7 of 9 target languages (Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Amharic, Twi, Somali, Zulu). Directly relevant to toxicity/hate speech evaluation with cultural context. Native speaker annotation ensures cultural relevance.
