# Akanksha Subudhi

## Research Summary

Akanksha Subudhi is a researcher working on multimodal machine learning applications, particularly in the area of deception detection. Her research combines behavioral and physiological data to develop more accurate detection systems. She collaborates with research teams led by Gargi Joshi and Ketan Kotecha on projects published in high-impact journals such as Scientific Reports.
