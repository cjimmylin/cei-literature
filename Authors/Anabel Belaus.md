# Anabel Belaus

## Biographical Summary

Anabel Belaus is a researcher in psychology affiliated with Universidad Nacional de Cordoba in Argentina. Her research focuses on moral psychology, particularly the cross-cultural factors that influence moral decision-making. She has contributed to large-scale international replication studies examining the universality of moral judgments.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Cross-Cultural Moral Psychology**: Investigating how cultural factors influence moral decision-making across different populations.
- **Moral Dilemmas**: Studying responses to classic moral dilemmas like the trolley problem across diverse samples.
- **Replication Studies**: Contributing to international efforts to verify the robustness of psychological findings.

### Significant Achievements
- Contributor to a major international replication study on moral judgments in the trolley dilemma, spanning 45 countries and thousands of participants.

## Major Publications
- **Situational factors shape moral judgments in the trolley dilemma in Eastern, Southern, and Western countries in a culturally diverse sample** (Nature Human Behaviour, 2022) - Cross-cultural replication of moral judgment research
