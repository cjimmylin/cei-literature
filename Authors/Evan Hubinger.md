# Evan Hubinger

## Biographical Summary

Evan Hubinger is a Research Scientist at Anthropic working on AI alignment and safety. He is known for his foundational work on mesa-optimization and deceptive alignment, which have become central concepts in AI safety research. Prior to Anthropic, he worked at MIRI (Machine Intelligence Research Institute) and contributed to the development of the Coconut programming language.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research at Anthropic focuses on understanding and preventing deceptive alignment, developing interpretability methods, and ensuring AI systems remain aligned with human values. He works on both theoretical frameworks for understanding alignment failures and practical methods for detecting and preventing them.

### Significant Achievements
Co-author of the highly influential "Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems" paper, which introduced the concept of mesa-optimization and deceptive alignment. These concepts have become foundational in AI safety discourse and have influenced research agendas across the field.

## Major Publications

- **Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems** (2019) - Foundational paper on mesa-optimization
- **Sleeper Agents: Training Deceptive LLMs That Persist Through Safety Training** (2024) - Empirical work on deceptive alignment
- Research on AI deception and truthfulness in LLMs
