# Cristina Menghini

## Biographical Summary

Cristina Menghini is a Senior Researcher at Meta Superintelligence Labs, where she leads loss of control risk assessments for frontier AI models. Previously, she was at Scale AI leading evaluation development (SEAL leaderboards, RLI, MASK, EnigmaEval), and before that a postdoc at Brown University's BATS lab with Prof. Stephen Bach. She completed her PhD at Sapienza University studying structural biases in information networks.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Safety Risk Assessment**: Leading assessments of catastrophic risks from frontier AI deployment at Meta.
- **Loss of Control Evaluation**: Developing methods to assess risks of AI systems operating outside intended bounds.
- **LLM Evaluation Benchmarks**: Creating comprehensive evaluation frameworks for language model safety and capabilities.

### Significant Achievements
- **NeurIPS 2023 SoLaR Best Paper Award**: "Low-Resource Languages Jailbreak GPT-4" (with Yong Zheng-Xin and Stephen Bach).
- **WSDM 2021 Best Paper Honorable Mention**: "RePBubLik: Reducing polarized bubble radius with link insertions."
- **SEAL Leaderboards**: Led development of comprehensive LLM evaluation leaderboards at Scale AI.
- **MASK Benchmark**: Contributed to major LLM evaluation benchmark.

## Major Publications
- **Low-Resource Languages Jailbreak GPT-4** (NeurIPS 2023 SoLaR Best Paper) - Demonstrating safety vulnerabilities in low-resource languages
