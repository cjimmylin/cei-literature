# Chris Lu

## Biographical Summary

Chris Lu is a Research Scientist at OpenAI. Previously, he was a key researcher at Sakana AI and a student at the University of Oxford. His work explores the frontiers of autonomous agents, specifically their ability to perform complex, open-ended tasks like scientific research.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Automated Science**: Developing agents capable of conducting end-to-end scientific research, from idea generation to paper writing and peer review ("The AI Scientist").
- **Multi-Agent Systems**: Investigating the emergent behaviors of large populations of AI agents.
- **Open-Endedness**: Creating environments and algorithms that allow AI systems to continuously learn and adapt without fixed objectives.

### Significant Achievements
- **The AI Scientist**: Primary author of this provocative paper, which demonstrated the potential (and risks) of fully automated scientific discovery pipelines.

## Major Publications
- **The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery**: A pioneering work in the field of automated research.
