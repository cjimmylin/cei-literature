## Abstract

Examines whether LLMs hold implicit assumptions about religious identities by generating 175 sermons specifying different combinations of race and religion. Finds Evangelical Protestant pastors had easier-to-read sermons while Jewish rabbis and Muslim imams had more difficult synthetic texts. Novel readability-based bias measurement approach. Tests five major LLMs for intersectional race-religion bias.

## Dataset Information

175 AI-generated sermons


## Data Description

The authors generated 175 sermons using five major LLMs by specifying different combinations of race and religion, then analyzed readability metrics to detect intersectional bias.
