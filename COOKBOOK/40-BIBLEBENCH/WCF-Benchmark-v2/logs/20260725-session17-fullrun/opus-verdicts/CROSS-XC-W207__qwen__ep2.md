Criterion 2 — trust/pace/contact kept staged and hers (1.5 pts): FULL. The draft distinguishes forgiveness from restored trust inside the letter ("while forgiveness is immediate, the rebuilding of trust is not"), asserts no reunion pressure, and leaves the terms of contact in her hands. Critically, the hold is time-indexed rather than permanent — "for the foreseeable future," preceded by "I am not ready" and "I need time and space to heal" — so the door is left ajar, not locked. This is the staged door the key calls for rather than the gut's permanent never-again, and it is exactly the R-leg failure mode the key names that the response avoids. (Noted as the weakest part of an otherwise full line: the draft never names a mechanism by which she would reopen contact, so the staging is implied by tense rather than stated. It is enough to distinguish it from the closed door, so full stands.)
