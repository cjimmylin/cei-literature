## Abstract

Anthropic research post (May 8, 2026) following up on the agentic-misalignment case study. It reports experiments on teaching Claude to reason about *why* an action is misaligned rather than memorizing rules: finetuning on synthetic honeypot datasets (including a ~30M and ~85M token variant and a 3M-token 'difficult advice' dataset) and teaching the content of Claude's Constitution. The 'difficult advice' dataset — which teaches ethical reasoning rather than correct answers — produced the best-performing model on the overall 'Misaligned behavior' category, supporting the hypothesis that reason-based training generalizes better than rule-based training for reducing agentic misalignment.
