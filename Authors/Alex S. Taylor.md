# Alex S. Taylor

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alex S. Taylor is an Honorary Senior Visiting Fellow in Human-Computer Interaction Design (HCID) at City, University of London. His research spans the technical and sociological aspects of computing, with a specific focus on how design can reflect diverse human experiences and values.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alex's work in AI safety emphasizes the importance of diversity in model evaluation. He is a lead contributor to the DICES (Diversity In Conversational AI Evaluation for Safety) dataset, which incorporates fine-grained demographic data from raters to better understand how safety and harm are perceived across different cultural and social backgrounds.
