## Abstract

This paper presents an ethical decision-making framework that refines a pre-trained reinforcement learning model using a task-agnostic ethical layer, where the LLM embodies consequentialist, deontological, virtue, social justice, and care ethics as moral principles to assign belief values to recommended actions.
