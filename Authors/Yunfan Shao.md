# Yunfan Shao

## Research Summary

Yunfan Shao is a researcher at Fudan University's School of Computer Science and Shanghai Key Laboratory of Intelligent Information Processing, also affiliated with Shanghai AI Laboratory. His work focuses on developing LLM agents for role-playing applications. He created Character-LLM, a trainable agent that teaches LLMs to act as specific historical and fictional characters by editing profiles as experiences and training models to be personal simulacra, enabling more authentic character-based AI interactions.
