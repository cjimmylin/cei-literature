# Richard Ngo

## Research Summary

Independent AI safety researcher and philosopher working on AI governance and alignment. Previously AI governance researcher at OpenAI (2021-November 2024) and AI safety researcher at Google DeepMind. Left OpenAI in November 2024 to pursue mostly-independent research on AI governance and alignment. Known for work on first principles of AGI safety and contributions to AI safety discourse through writings on the AI Alignment Forum and LessWrong.
