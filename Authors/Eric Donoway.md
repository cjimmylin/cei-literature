# Eric Donoway

## Biographical Summary

Eric Donoway is a researcher at Apollo Research, an organization focused on AI safety research. His work addresses the challenge of goal misgeneralization in AI systems, where AI pursues objectives that differ from what was intended by designers. He contributes to developing frameworks and methods for analyzing and measuring goal-directed behavior in AI systems.

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety**: Ensuring AI systems behave safely and as intended
- **Goal Misgeneralization**: Understanding when AI pursues unintended objectives
- **AI Alignment**: Aligning AI systems with human intentions
- **Safety Evaluation**: Developing methods to assess AI system safety
