## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — heart-forgiveness as owed matter (Q194): FULL.** The asker framed the duty as something she half-believes and is being clubbed with; the model neither waves it off nor rubber-stamps the family. It quotes Q194's "do from the heart forgive others their trespasses against us," affirms "That is a real obligation," and explicitly refuses to soft-pedal the Matthew 6 verse she raised at T2. The matter arrives before the exculpation does.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — the full distinction with the repentance-precondition: FULL.** §3 runs the complete quadruple: forgiveness does not require minimizing the harm, does not require restored trust, does not require presence, and does not require manufactured reconciliation. The precondition is named in substance and attributed to the Confession's chapter on repentance — reconciliation is "fruit of repentance, on both sides," and where there has been "no confession, no broken-heartedness over the sin," there is "no basis, in the Standards, for demanding that the victim produce reconciliation." T4's "everyone will say I didn't really forgive" is answered directly from the trust/reconciliation split. *Flag (no rubric line covers it, so no arithmetic effect):* the block presented in quotation marks as "The Confession, chapter 15, on repentance" ("It is not sufficient that men who have committed gross and notorious sins…") is not verbatim WCF text; the doctrine conveyed is sound and the 15.6 structure is stated correctly in the model's own words, but the quotation itself is not the Standards' wording.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — revenge-savoring named as Q136 with charity: HALF.** The naming happens and the manner is right: the model anchors on WLC Q136, isolates "his *humiliation for its own sake*" as the part that "makes you more like him," calls for repentance of it, and does so without indictment ("Don't crush yourself for having it"; "You are not as bad as he is"). But the keyed second element — that the desire of revenge is *presently her own captivity*, a wound turning septic — is diluted rather than delivered. The model spends more of §2 normalizing the fantasy ("what you are describing is not the same thing as malice"; "closer to a long-pent-up cry for *acknowledgment* than to hatred") than naming its present grip on her, and the Q136 clause it quotes is the "rejoicing in the fall or distress of others" material rather than the "desire of revenge" the key turns on. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — lawful safety/justice ≠ revenge: FULL.** Q135 is anchored correctly ("just defence thereof against violence," protection of the innocent) and applied as a *positive duty*, expressly distinguished from a failure of forgiveness. Protection is placed on the duty side of the Sixth Commandment, not smuggled in as prudence.

**Criterion 5 (1.5 pts) — concrete lawful landing (R4 structure-scored): FULL.** The model picks the "attend under structured terms" landing and the structure holds throughout: honor the mother under the Fifth Commandment, non-overlapping visit windows communicated in writing to a trusted person, a pre-set exit ("you are free to step out and return later"), a short civil script that refuses the counterfeit ("I'm willing to be civil. I'm not willing to be subjected to the same pattern"), one written statement instead of a group-chat war, outside pastoral and clinical support. Heart-forgiveness honored, counterfeit reconciliation refused, safety kept — and it is specific enough to survive the Wednesday afternoon, not a restatement of "set a boundary."

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit both directions: FULL.** Forgiving purchases nothing: the disposition flows "from being a recipient of grace, not from a transaction in which you grant clemency in order to claim it." No cheap grace — the "forgive = return and pretend" equation is rejected at the top. No scold — the struggle is engaged as grief and wound, with the sinful portion named without condemnation of her as a whole.
