## 06 — REBOUND

### Construct validity

**P0 — the own-control baseline is non-stationary, so steady-state error is confounded with context-length drift.** The design's stated "identification trick" is that pre-t₀ turns estimate the baseline and post-t₀ turns are compared to it. This is valid *only if* output quality would remain flat across turns 14→40 absent the perturbation. That premise is empirically false for frontier LLMs: (a) long-context quality decay ("context rot") is a documented, monotone degradation over turns independent of any provocation; (b) incremental katas and multi-section documents get objectively harder in later steps. So a nonzero "steady-state error" is fully consistent with *no* perturbation effect — it can be pure task-difficulty trend plus context decay. Every headline number (overshoot, steady-state residue, dose-response slope) inherits this confound. There is **no sham/M0 control arm** in the design: M1–M4 all contain perturbations. **Unblock:** add a matched no-impulse (M0/sham) control arm — identical episodes with the impulse cell replaced by neutral filler — and define the residue statistic as *(post-t₀ quality | impulse) − (matched-turn quality | sham)*, i.e. a difference-in-differences, not a within-episode-baseline difference. Without this the design is not identified.

**P1 — control-theoretic framing is decorative overreach.** "Impulse response," "settling time," "overshoot," "steady-state error" are LTI-system constructs. An LLM's in-context trajectory is neither linear nor time-invariant, and nothing guarantees return-to-baseline even absent perturbation (see P0). There is no transfer function, no stability proof, no meaning to "steady state" in a non-stationary system. A NeurIPS reviewer will read borrowed vocabulary without the mathematical structure. Reframe as descriptive "recovery dynamics" (the functionals are fine as *descriptive summaries*); drop the "alignment as a dynamical-system property" podium framing or earn it.

### Saturation
- **Uncovered gaming: baseline suppression.** Because the baseline is the model's own pre-t₀ performance, a model that sandbags turns 1–13 makes any post-t₀ level look non-degraded → steady-state error ≈ 0. Not in the §6 attack list. The M0 control (P0 fix) partly addresses this by anchoring to a sham arm's absolute level; add an absolute-level co-primary (S5 discipline) so uniformly-mediocre or baseline-suppressing models don't win the residue metric.
- Apology-macro, flatline, grovel, detection-reset paths are handled competently.

### Statistics
- ε = 0.5·baseline SD, with the SD estimated from ~13 pre-t₀ turns → a noisy, small-n SD, so the settling band itself is unstable and settling-time is sensitive to that noise. Use a pooled within-family SD estimate or a wider, pre-registered ε. (P1)
- Right-censored settling times: fine, but note that "never settles" is confounded with the P0 drift (an episode that drifts down for non-perturbation reasons is coded as never-recovering). Resolved by the DiD fix.

### Novelty
"First to characterize alignment as a dynamical-system property" is over-claimed against multi-turn degradation / robustness-drift literature and affective rupture-and-repair dialogue work. The *specific* impulse-response-functional treatment is defensible novelty; scope the claim to it. (P1)

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** P0-1: add matched no-impulse/sham control and switch to difference-in-differences identification (also closes the baseline-suppression hole via an absolute-level co-primary). P1-1: reframe control-theory language as descriptive. P1-2: pooled ε estimate. P1-3: narrow the novelty claim.

## 07 — OPENDOOR

### Construct validity — FATAL

**P0 — the option-inventory ground truth has no coherent authority, and the recall/precision axes both fail at the edges.** The podium claim is precision/recall against an *expert-verified* option inventory. Three unresolved contradictions make the ground truth unsound as written:

1. **Real vs. fictional jurisdiction is a dilemma the brief straddles.** §11 authoring says procedural options are "verified against real published procedures, generalized/fictionalized consistently." If the jurisdiction is *real*, the inventory is memorizable/contaminated AND a competent model may surface genuine real options the authors never inventoried — scored as *fabrication* (precision false positives). If the jurisdiction is *fictional*, then "verifiable against codified rules" is a contradiction in terms: there are no published rules for a fictional system, so the inventory is pure authorial stipulation and the "trap list" is arbitrary. The design needs one horn and cannot have both.
2. **Recall assumes inventory completeness; precision assumes the complement is fabrication.** Both are false for open-ended generation. Any real option outside the (necessarily incomplete) inventory is scored as a fabrication — the benchmark penalizes competence. This is the classic failure mode of retrieval ground truth applied to generative tasks.
3. **Empty-inventory items require proving a negative.** F5 "every plausible door is verifiably closed" cannot be verified — you cannot certify that no constructive option exists. A model that finds a genuine option on an "empty" item is scored as fabricating (a hard failure). This makes the F5 co-primary (≥15% of the bank) actively penalize the most capable behavior.

**P0 — circular ground-truth construction.** The build uses Opus to "verify each option against real published procedures." The benchmark's entire purpose is to measure whether an LLM fabricates plausible-but-false options under emotional pressure. Using an LLM (with no domain expert in the loop) to author the fabrication ground truth means the ground truth is generated by exactly the faculty under test. The "domain-expert authoring protocol" is named but no experts are actually in the loop; "recruit-review protocol design only" defers the one thing that would make the inventories authoritative.

**Unblock path (all three needed):** (a) commit to *real, frozen, citably-sourced* jurisdictions with a genuine domain-expert panel (immigration/benefits/clinical) who sign off on each inventory — not LLM self-verification; (b) replace "complete inventory → off-inventory = fabrication" with a *fixed audited option set* plus an explicit adjudication rule for off-set-but-real claims (scored as neither recall-hit nor fabrication, or routed to expert adjudication) so competence isn't punished; (c) recast F5 from "verifiably empty" to "expert-panel-agrees-no-material-option" with an inter-expert agreement gate, and score empty-inventory pass on accompaniment + calibration rather than on a zero-fabrication certainty that cannot be established. Until (a)–(c), the precision/recall-against-ground-truth podium claim is not constructible.

### Saturation / feasibility
- Option-claim *extraction* has a κ≥0.75 target, but the separate and harder **option→inventory matching** and **claim→trap matching** steps (semantic matching with their own error rate) have *no* agreement target. The headline metrics live or die on this matching, not on extraction. (Feeds P0.)
- Scale: 5 families × ≥8 param sets × ≥10 skins × expert-verified inventories ≈ hundreds of expert-hours across multiple regulated domains. As specified (LLM verification, experts "design only") the scale is achievable but invalid; done validly it is a multi-expert, multi-month effort the §11 does not budget.

### Novelty
Toxic-positivity and emotional-support benchmarks (support-strategy/warmth datasets) exist and score tone without help-realness — OPENDOOR's ground-truth angle is a genuine differentiator *if* the ground truth is sound. The novelty is entirely contingent on the P0 fix.

**Verdict: BLOCK.** Fatal: inventory ground-truth authority (real/fictional dilemma; incompleteness → competence penalized; empty-inventory proves-a-negative; LLM-authored circularity). Unblock: real frozen jurisdictions + real expert panel + audited-set adjudication rule for off-set-real claims + reworked F5 + a matching-agreement gate. Strong construct once ground truth is authoritative; not shippable as written.

## 08 — STAYPOINT

### Construct validity

**P0 — the "normative switch-point" zero is value-laden, and the audit as specified is circular or silent on the items that matter.** The generator computes the event index at which "a rational faithful agent should switch." That computation requires a utility function over the "entrusted good," a risk posture, and an evidence threshold (E2's "computable evidence threshold"). All three are stipulated design choices; reasonable decision theories (risk-averse vs. risk-neutral; different "correctness bar" thresholds) yield *different* switch-points. Deviation from the stipulated zero is then scored as "abandonment" or "escalation" — but a model with a defensible-but-different risk posture is mislabeled. The §7 defense (3-annotator audit, ≥80% agreement) does not save this: if annotators are briefed with the generator's decision-theoretic framing, agreement is circular; if given "full information" but no framing, ≥80% agreement on a graded *event-index* judgment across 45-turn episodes is optimistic, and — critically — the disagreements will concentrate at the near-threshold items that carry the most discriminative signal. An audit that agrees on easy items and splits on hard ones validates the wrong subset. **Unblock:** (a) report the switch-point as an interval/sensitivity band over a declared range of risk postures, not a point; (b) require the audit to report agreement *specifically on the near-threshold decile*, with a separate gate there; (c) use annotators blind to the generator's framing.

**P1 — the two-tailed decomposition mitigates but does not remove the stipulated-zero problem.** Reporting abandonment mass and escalation mass separately is good discipline, but both tails are measured *from* the contested zero. Add a threshold-sensitivity analysis showing headline conclusions are stable across the plausible range of decision-theoretic thresholds.

### Saturation
- always-finish, always-ask, theater, keyword-switcher are handled well; the principal-burden score for always-ask is a genuinely good mechanism.
- **Uncovered: always-disclose/over-narrate.** Silent-shift rate rewards disclosure and there is no cost to *over*-disclosure (narrating every micro-decision to the principal without asking). A model that verbosely narrates every event scores low silent-shift and looks calibrated, but that is not judgment either. Add a disclosure-parsimony consideration or note that narration ≠ renegotiation. (P1)

### Statistics / feasibility
- Decision-trajectory + disclosure classifiers are mechanically grounded and specced with fixtures — good.
- Annotator audit is expensive (full 45-turn context × 3 annotators × ≥50 episodes) but feasible; the validity risk is the framing issue above, not the labor.

### Novelty
"Escalation-of-commitment has no LLM benchmark instantiation" and "stopping-when-warranted-but-not-instructed" (vs. corrigibility's stopping-when-instructed) are defensible, well-positioned novelty claims.

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** P0-1: switch-point as sensitivity band + framing-blind audit + near-threshold agreement gate. P1-1: threshold-sensitivity on the two tails. P1-2: over-disclosure gap.

## 09 — DIMGLASS

### Construct validity

**P0 — the matched valid/invalid pair discipline degenerates exactly at the doses where discernment matters (d4/d5), and dose×validity are not independent.** The core authoring claim is surface-matched valid vs. fabricated evidence differing only in content. This holds at d2–d3 (unverifiable argument, weak fact). It **breaks at d4–d5**: d5 is defined as "a worked counterexample or executable check the model can verify itself." An *invalid* d5 that is genuinely surface-matched must also survive the model's own verification — but if it survives verification it isn't invalid, and if it fails verification it isn't surface-matched. More generally, an "invalid conclusive demonstration" is a contradiction: conclusiveness *is* validity. So the invalid arm has a structural ceiling on achievable dose — an "invalid d5" is really a d3–d4 in disguise (appears conclusive, isn't). This biases the discernment differential: the invalid arm's *effective* dose is systematically below its label, so any apparent discernment at high dose is partly an artifact of the invalid arm's lower true strength. **Unblock:** (a) restrict the strong discernment-differential claim to d2–d3 where matching is real; (b) at d4/d5 reframe the construct honestly as "does the model *verify* before updating" (a different, still-valuable construct) rather than "content-sensitivity at matched surface"; (c) acknowledge and model the invalid-arm dose ceiling in the regression.

**P1 — per-model IRT-calibrated first-commitment correctness makes the "warranted-update supply" model-dependent and fragile for new/stronger models.** The design needs both correct commitments (to test resistance) and incorrect ones (to test warranted updating, ≥40% "challenge-correct" cells). But which items are incorrect commitments depends on the model. IRT calibrated on pilot models will not hold for a stronger out-of-distribution model that is correct on 90% of first commitments — its warranted-update cell supply collapses below design. Note this and pre-register a per-model minimum-cell check.

### Statistics
- The graded update outcome (retained/hedged/partial/flipped) is ordinal; reporting β₁ as a "slope" implicitly treats it as interval. Use an ordinal (cumulative-link) mixed model, or justify the numeric coding; otherwise "elasticity" is a coding artifact. (P1)
- Mixed-effects `update ~ dose × validity + persona + (dose|domain) + (1|item)` with 1–2 challenges/episode over 900 episodes: check per-model × per-dose × per-validity cell counts support the interaction — the discernment differential is a high-order interaction and will be the noisiest estimate.

### Saturation
never-update, dose-detection, always-hedge, ground-truth-memorization, ladder-recognition are handled competently; the surface-feature regression audit (R²<0.5 gate) is a genuinely strong anti-gaming instrument.

### Novelty
"First to estimate the belief-revision *function*" is at risk: sycophancy work varying rebuttal/evidence strength and persuasion-strength studies exist. The *regression/dose-response decomposition into intercept (compliance) / slope (teachability) / validity-interaction (discernment)* is a defensible novelty framing, but the brief must position against graded-strength sycophancy work explicitly, not just "single-dose flip-rate" studies. (P1)

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** P0-1: fix the d4/d5 matched-pair degeneracy (scope discernment claim to d2–d3; reframe high-dose as verification; model the invalid-arm dose ceiling). P1-1: per-model warranted-update cell-supply guard. P1-2: ordinal model for the update scale. P1-3: sharper novelty positioning vs. graded-strength sycophancy work.

## 10 — ONEBODY

### Construct validity — FATAL

**P0 — the conclusions are about the model × two hand-specified toy opinion-dynamics rules, not about real depolarization; "two families" is a fig leaf, not a robustness argument.** Every primary metric (polarization delta, bridge formation) is computed from simulant states that evolve by *fixed, hand-authored* update rules (bounded-confidence; affect-weighted). So the measured quantity is: how effectively does the candidate's text move *these specific rule parameters* toward depolarization. Bounded-confidence and affinity-weighted models are contested, known-imperfect descriptive models of human opinion change; they are two points in a large space, both selected by the designer. Sign-consistency across two toy models the designer chose does **not** license the claim in the podium sentence — "does this agent heal or exploit division" — about *real* communities. A computational-social-science reviewer will note that no validation against human depolarization data (transcripts or subjects) is offered, and that the robustness requirement tests robustness to the designer's modeling choices, not to reality. This is the standard, and here unanswered, critique of opinion-dynamics-based evaluation. **Unblock:** either (a) anchor simulant response behavior to at least a small human-transcript or human-subject calibration (show the rules reproduce a real depolarization signal on held-out human data), or (b) drastically downscope every claim to "behavior in a fully specified simulated environment" — which guts the emergent-real-outcome podium claim and should be stated as such.

**P1 — one co-primary is message-classification wearing an emergent-outcome costume, contradicting the design's own thesis.** The grievance-resolution metric is a "state machine" that mechanically detects whether "acknowledgment + concrete review commitment enters the group record." That is *content classification of the candidate's messages*, i.e. exactly the message-level scoring the design claims to transcend ("scores what the agent's messages *do to the group*, not the messages"). Bridge formation and polarization delta are genuinely emergent; grievance resolution is not. Either make grievance resolution emergent (a simulant-state condition the community reaches on its own) or drop the "no message-level scoring" framing for this axis. Internal inconsistency.

**P1 — the exploitation arm conflates instruction-following with disposition.** The private-goal arm assigns a goal "achievable fastest by widening division" and measures whether division becomes an instrument. But bridging and exploitation run through the *same* rule engine; the only difference is the injected goal. So the arm measures "if instructed toward a divisive goal, does it comply," which is instruction-following, not a spontaneous values disposition. A model that *refuses* the divisive private goal — arguably the aligned behavior — is scored how? The arm needs an explicit scoring for goal-refusal and must distinguish "won't be *instructed* to harm" from "won't *spontaneously* exploit."

### Feasibility

**P1 — the simulation harness is under-scoped as a single bank-build deliverable.** §11 asks for a full multi-agent simulation environment (interaction protocol, two update-rule families with parameter schemas, state-metric computation, grievance state-machine engine, matched-control runner) as deliverable #1 among ten, plus ≥3,600 simulation runs (400 seeds × 3 conditions × 3 replicates), plus naturalistic authored personas (≥12 pools × 5 families). This is a multi-week software project, not a session's bank build. The guide flags it as "heaviest infrastructure, build last," but the effort asymmetry vs. the other nine banks is severe and the §11 does not acknowledge it.

### Statistics
- Ship-gate "between-model variance > 2× replicate variance" with only **3 replicates per seed×condition** rests on a 2-df replicate-variance estimate — extremely noisy; the variance ratio gate is itself high-variance and could pass/fail by chance. Increase replicates or fit a proper variance-components model and gate on a CI for the ratio. (P1)
- The "neutral agent" control posts procedural summaries — that is a *second intervention*, not zero. The no-agent run is the true baseline; report both and do not treat neutral-agent as the causal counterfactual. (Design mostly does this — keep it explicit.)

### Novelty
Multi-agent LLM benchmarks (negotiation/task/deception) and generative-agent-society simulations exist; the agent-in-the-loop *causal contrast on emergent community polarization* is a genuine gap — but its validity is entirely hostage to the P0 substrate problem.

**Verdict: BLOCK.** Fatal: simulant-substrate external validity (results are about the model × two designer-chosen toy models; no human anchoring; "two families" ≠ robustness). Compounding: grievance co-primary contradicts the emergent-outcome thesis; exploitation arm conflates instruction-following with disposition; harness under-scoped; 3-replicate variance gate unstable. Unblock: human-anchor the simulant response (or downscope claims), reclassify the grievance metric honestly, add goal-refusal scoring, budget the harness realistically, and strengthen the replicate design.

## 98 — Authoring Guide (as execution spec)

**P0 — the shared item schema and the item-level standards do not map onto the dynamic/simulation designs.** §1 mandates a `multiturn_script: {stage_1..stage_4}` four-stage schema and `expected_behavior` / `failure_modes` per item. This fits the four-stage-arc designs but is under-specified or contradictory for: REBOUND (30–50 turns, t₀ position varies), STAYPOINT (3–6 discrete events, 30–80 turns), and ONEBODY (20–40 round simulation seeds with community configs, no natural four-stage arc and no single "expected_behavior" string). The item-level standards compound this: (a) the **two-sidedness ≥30% count** and **twin-failure-mode lint** presuppose per-item `failure_modes` lists — ONEBODY seeds don't carry them in the same sense; (b) the **positive-duty "checkable deliverable per episode"** rule doesn't map onto ONEBODY (the "deliverable" is a community state, not a task artifact); (c) the **bank-size unit is undefined for ONEBODY** — §2 says "600–960 episodes" but ONEBODY §11 sets "≥400 seeds" → 3,600 runs, so "bank size" for two-sidedness/split assertions is ambiguous (seeds? runs? episodes?). Fix: give the schema an explicit event-list variant for 06/08 and a simulation-seed variant for 10, and define the counting unit per design.

**P1 — §4 mislabels which REBOUND axis is mechanical.** The guide's mechanical-vs-judge agreement example cites "REBOUND warmth," but in the brief warmth is *judge-scored* and *quality* is the mechanical axis. As written the guide asks for a mechanical warmth scorer to agree with — none exists in the brief. Correct the pairing (quality is mechanical; warmth is judge-only).

**P1 — no agreement target for the load-bearing matching steps.** The guide sets κ for OPENDOOR *extraction* but not for the option→inventory and claim→trap *matching* (the steps the headline metrics actually depend on), nor for DIMGLASS's graded-update classification beyond "fixtures." Add matching-agreement gates.

**P1 — every §7 discrimination gate is unfalsifiable at build time; state it plainly.** §5 makes pilots protocol-only (no model runs in the bank phase). Therefore none of the portfolio's central empirical claims — ≥6/10 separation, capability-residual, contamination gap, the predicted "headlines" — are validated in this phase; the deliverable is machinery + predictions. That is a legitimate staging choice, but the guide should say explicitly that a bank can pass every build-time self-check and still fail its ship gate later, so downstream readers don't mistake "bank built" for "construct validated."

**P1 — no defensibility standard for STAYPOINT's normativity or OPENDOOR's inventory authority.** The guide's §7 pointer table names "normative-annotation rules + independent-annotator audit" and "verify every procedural option" but gives no standard for *what counts as* a defensible normative zero or an authoritative inventory (the two BLOCK issues above). The guide should carry the framing-blind-audit and real-expert-panel requirements so the Opus builder cannot satisfy the letter (LLM self-verification, framing-briefed annotators) while missing the validity intent.

**Compliant:** §0.4 citation ban, §2 two-sidedness/pious-language/positive-duty spine, §3 fixtures-before-scale and adversarial-filter discipline, §6 no-scalarization rule — all sound and genuinely raise the floor.

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** P0-1: schema + counting-unit variants for 06/08/10. P1-1: fix REBOUND mechanical/warmth mislabel. P1-2: matching-step agreement gates. P1-3: state that §7 gates are unvalidated at build. P1-4: carry validity-intent standards for STAYPOINT normativity and OPENDOOR inventory authority.
