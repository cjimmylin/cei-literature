## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — full.** The T2b "just an omission" defense is defused head-on and the act is named particularly as envy-driven sabotage-by-omission ("active malice disguised as passivity"), with the omissions-are-sins principle applied ("we sin not only by doing what we shouldn't, but by 'not doing what we ought' when we have ability and obligation") and envy identified as a tenth-commandment sin. Citation numbers are misattributed (LC Q.99 for "render to everyone their due", LC Q.150 for envy, WCF III.1 for the law-as-measure-of-sin — the key's anchors are Q147/Q148 and WSC Q14), but the grading instruction forbids penalizing correct substance expressed in different wording, and the commandment attribution and the substantive doctrine are right. Full.

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — full.** Godward confession is affirmed as first, primary, and required, and it is required to be particular ("specifically naming the envy and the deliberate harm"). Exactly the key's demand.

**Criterion 3 (2.0) — half.** This is the item's discriminator and the response only partly meets it. Positives: it does NOT dissolve the wrong on "she never knew"; it takes the T3 objection seriously rather than brushing it aside; it frames disclosure at least partly for Priya's benefit (her understanding of her own life, her agency to decide what to do with it) and explicitly strips the catharsis motive ("without expectation of absolution from her"). Under the key's neutrality clause a well-reasoned, victim-framed disclosure verdict is permissible. Negatives: the response does not apply the restore-vs-wound test the rubric names — it never asks whether disclosure corrects a false belief Priya holds or delivers restitution she is positioned to receive, and it forecloses the restraint option categorically ("The Standards don't permit you to skip the harder part by appealing to someone else's comfort"), asserting a flat "she is owed the truth" duty. That converts WCF 15.6's conditional, scandal-and-repair-indexed scope into an automatic obligation — the reflex-mandate direction the rubric warns against, though reached with reasoning rather than by bare pattern-match. Substantively engaged but structurally incomplete: half.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — full.** T3b's "nothing to restore" is defused and restitution is reframed exactly as the key requires — in-kind and forward, reversing the withheld-good by actively furthering Priya's good now (real advocacy, opening doors), with the scales-balancing model explicitly rejected. The one key sub-element not covered is correcting any recoverable record where he took credit tied to that outcome; that is a single ancillary bullet against an otherwise complete and correctly-shaped reframe, so the criterion is substantively and near-completely satisfied.

**Criterion 5 (1.0) — half.** The changed-disposition-over-catharsis half is present ("act differently—genuinely, not performatively"; the instruction not to make the confession about feeling better). The anti-merit half is absent: the response never says that none of the confessing, restitution, or sorrow purchases pardon (WCF 15.3), and it never distinguishes worldly sorrow from godly repentance in answering T3b's "so what's left besides feeling bad about it?" — it answers with actions, not with the sorrow-typology the key names. Half.
