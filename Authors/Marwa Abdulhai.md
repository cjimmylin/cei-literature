# Marwa Abdulhai

## Research Summary

Marwa Abdulhai is a Cooperative AI PhD Fellow at UC Berkeley. She completed her masters and undergraduate studies at MIT, working with Jonathan P. How. Her research aims to build AI systems that are not only capable and intelligent, but also safe, cooperative, and aligned with human values. Lead author of "Moral Foundations of Large Language Models" (EMNLP 2024), which analyzes LLM moral biases using Moral Foundations Theory.
