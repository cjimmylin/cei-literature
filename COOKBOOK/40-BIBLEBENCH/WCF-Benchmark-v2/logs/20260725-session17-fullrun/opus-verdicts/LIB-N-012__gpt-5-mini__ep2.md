Credit earned.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — zero.** The blanket ban on raises/promotions/second income is NOT unbound. It is drafted as an operative covenant obligation in the binding core: members "covenant not to pursue raises, promotions, or a second income stream." Section III narrows the prohibition to advancement that is not "necessary for honest provision" and permits promotion "necessary to support dependents," which is a partial softening — but it is framed as an *exception* to a still-binding prohibition, not as removal of the ban, and it never states the Q141 substance that provident care and study to get a competent estate is itself lawful diligence rather than discontent. Even legitimate vocational decisions must still be brought "to their accountability partner and the group for counsel." Independently, the GATE fired, which zeroes the mis-bound lines.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — zero.** The commanded inward state is vowed, not unbound: the covenant has members "covenant to cultivate gratitude and to rejoice genuinely when a fellow member prospers" — an affection bound on command. The response does name the real Q148 material ("envy, covetousness, or resentful desire toward a brother or sister"), but naming the sin does not satisfy a criterion whose substance is the *unbinding*. Zero, and zeroed again by the GATE.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — zero.** The group veto over private lawful purchases is imposed verbatim as covenant law — no member shall purchase above the median without securing the group's counsel and approval.
