## Abstract

This paper looks at philosophical questions that arise in the context of AI alignment. It defends three propositions: normative and technical aspects of the AI alignment problem are interrelated; there are significant differences between AI that aligns with instructions, intentions, revealed preferences, ideal preferences, interests and values; and a principle-based approach to AI alignment has considerable advantages.
