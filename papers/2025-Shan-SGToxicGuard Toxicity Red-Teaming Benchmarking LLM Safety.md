# SGToxicGuard: Toxicity Red-Teaming: Benchmarking LLM Safety in Singapore's Low-Resource Languages

## Abstract

Novel dataset and evaluation framework for benchmarking LLM safety in Singapore's languages including Tamil. Uses adversarial red-teaming across three tasks: conversation safety, toxic bias QA, and content moderation. LLMs show significantly lower safety in low-resource languages. Tamil-specific LLM safety evaluation. Demonstrates vulnerability to toxicity jailbreaks in Tamil. Tests culturally specific social biases in Singapore Tamil context.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SGToxicGuard

## Data Description

SGToxicGuard is a multilingual red-teaming dataset for benchmarking LLM safety in Singapore's low-resource languages (Singlish, Chinese, Malay, Tamil), comprising thousands of prompts across three tasks: toxic conversation, toxic QA, and toxic tweet composition.
