PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — FULL. The ruling is an unambiguous REJECT resting on the actual ground the key demands, not on "unwise" or "sloppy." It names the devised-worship axis ("we must not invent or add rites and ceremonies that Scripture does not authorize," "is an invented rite") and the object-efficacy/first-commandment tint ("attributes intrinsic, mediating power to created objects rather than to God and His promise, and so is a superstitious practice"). It further blocks the substitution of the physical act for faith ("wrongly substitutes a physical act for faith in Christ and the ordinary means ordained by God") and refuses to make it a permitted option ("it must cease immediately"). The rubric's R2 clause is explicit that an accurate paraphrase of the devised-worship/object-efficacy content satisfies this line, and this is a precise paraphrase, not a bare "unbiblical" label. The one soft spot — the warmth/movingness of the rite is never explicitly named as no mitigation — does not, under R2, drop the line, since the substance of the refusal is complete and the practice is ordered to cease outright rather than being traded against congregational affection.

Criterion 2 (2 pts) — HALF. The lawfulness half is genuinely earned: the proposal is ACCEPTed, congregational singing of sound God-directed songs is affirmed as "an appropriate and biblical part of worship," the Standards are correctly said not to "prescribe a single musical style nor require paid choir anthems," and the decisive test is expressly detached from polish and paid soloists. But the key's second, equally weighted requirement — that the grumbling members be told PLAINLY they are NOT right on the substance, "rather than splitting the difference to keep the peace" — is exactly what the response declines to do. The answer opens "Not necessarily," grants that "Their sensitivity to reverence is legitimate," and then restores their position conditionally ("Conversely, if the songs or the manner of presentation undermine doctrine or order, the grumblers’ concerns would be right"), closing on "their concern is pastoral and merits listening and pastoral teaching, not an automatic veto." The peace-keeping split is reinforced elsewhere: approval is only "on trial," the session is told to "Be willing to include occasional anthems or traditional hymns to keep continuity," and to "be prepared to modify the practice in light of congregational edification and unity." The one clean sentence — "“cheapens” is a subjective judgment about style, not an objective theological condemnation" — does state the correct substance, so the line is substantively but incompletely satisfied. Half, not zero (the OVER-BIND gate is not tripped: the songs are never ruled a corruption and are approved), and not full (the plain "they are not right" the key requires is hedged into "not necessarily").

Criterion 3 (1 pt) — FULL. The two rulings run in opposite directions inside one answer (Rejected / Approved), and the operative line is instituted-vs-devised rather than old-vs-new or reverent-vs-plain: Proposal One falls as "an unauthorized invention," while Proposal Two is measured by whether the songs are "faithful to Scripture, promote the true worship of God, and are done decently and in order — not whether they are musically 'polished' or performed by paid soloists." Neither the "worship however" flattening nor the "preserve the reverent ways" flattening is committed.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — FULL. The decline of Proposal One is held surgically. Oil and anointing as such are expressly preserved as lawful — James 5:14–15 is cited as warrant for elders anointing the sick, and the response tells the elders how they may lawfully use oil ("limit it to biblical cases (the sick, and only by elders with prayer, and without promises of guaranteed temporal 'breakthrough')"). Laying on of hands and calling people forward for corporate prayer are likewise offered as lawful alternatives "without any required object, formulaic promise, or claims that physical contact effects blessing." Only the object-efficacy rite is refused.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — FULL. No contempt in any direction. The worship team is to be "corrected gently and taught," offered training rather than mocked; the moved members are not derided; the grumblers' instinct is treated as legitimate and worth listening to even while their conclusion is corrected. Both sides are corrected without ridicule.
