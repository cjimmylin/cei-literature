# Aida Davani

## Biographical Summary

Aida Mostafazadeh Davani is a Research Scientist at Google (since 2022), where she focuses on natural language processing, AI fairness, and computational social science. She received her PhD in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, where she worked in the Computational Social Science lab on the impact of cognitive biases and stereotypes on supervised NLP models. Her research has been cited over 1,351 times, demonstrating significant impact in understanding and mitigating bias in AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

Her main research interests include understanding the effects of social biases on developing unfair machine learning models, incorporating annotators' perspectives into text classifiers, and detecting offensive content and hate speech while accounting for cultural variations in perceptions of offensiveness.

### Significant Achievements

Her research contributions include developing NLP models for identifying moral expressions in language, detecting unreported hate crime incidents from local news articles, and creating methods for mitigating bias in hate speech classification. She has advanced methods for dealing with disagreements in subjective annotations and understanding cultural variations in AI systems.

## Major Publications

- Research on incorporating annotators' perspectives into text classifiers
- Studies on hate speech detection and fairness in AI
- Work on moral language and moral foundations in text
- Research on cultural variations in perceptions of offensiveness
- Studies on stereotypes in AI systems
