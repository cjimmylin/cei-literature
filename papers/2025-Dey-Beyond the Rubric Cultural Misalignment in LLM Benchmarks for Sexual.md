## Abstract

Evaluates LLM-based health chatbot using HealthBench, finding Western bias in legal framing, diet assumptions, and cost models. Qualitative analysis by health experts reveals culturally appropriate responses are penalized by Western-normed rubrics.

## Data Description

This paper evaluates cultural misalignment in HealthBench, an LLM benchmark by OpenAI, by testing Myna Bolo, an LLM-based chatbot for sexual and reproductive health in underserved Indian communities. It extracted 637 SRH queries from HealthBench and evaluated 330 single-turn conversations. While HealthBench's automated grader rated responses low, qualitative analysis by health experts found many responses were culturally appropriate, revealing Western bias in the benchmark's rubrics.
