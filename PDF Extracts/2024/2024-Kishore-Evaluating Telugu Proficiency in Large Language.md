Evaluating Telugu Proﬁciency in Large Language Models:
A Comparative Analysis of ChatGPT and Gemini

Abstract
The growing prominence of large language mod-
els (LLMs) necessitates the exploration of their
capabilities beyond English. This research inves-
tigates the Telugu language proﬁciency of Chat-
GPT and Gemini, two leading LLMs. Through a
designed set of 20 questions encompassing greet-
ings, grammar, vocabulary, common phrases, task
completion, and situational reasoning, the study
delves into their strengths and weaknesses in han-
dling Telugu. The analysis aims to identify the
LLM that demonstrates a deeper understanding
of Telugu grammatical structures, possesses a
broader vocabulary, and exhibits superior perfor-
mance in tasks like writing and reasoning.
By
comparing their ability to comprehend and use
everyday Telugu expressions, the research sheds
light on their suitability for real-world language
interaction. Furthermore, the evaluation of adapt-
ability and reasoning capabilities provides insights
into how each LLM leverages Telugu to respond
to dynamic situations. This comparative analysis
contributes to the ongoing discussion on multilin-
gual capabilities in AI and paves the way for future
research in developing LLMs that can seamlessly
integrate with Telugu-speaking communities.
1
Introduction
Large language models (LLMs) are rapidly trans-
forming the landscape of human-computer inter-
action. These robust AI systems possess the re-
markable ability to process and generate human-
like text, fostering advancements in areas like ma-
chine translation, creative writing, and automated
chatbots. However, a critical aspect of LLM de-
velopment lies in ensuring their proﬁciency ex-
tends beyond dominant languages like English. As
the world embraces a multilingual digital envi-
ronment, LLMs capable of understanding and in-
teracting with diverse languages become increas-
ingly crucial.
This paper focuses on Telugu, a Dravidian lan-
guage spoken by over 80 million people world-
wide, primarily in the Indian states of Telangana
and Andhra Pradesh. Telugu boasts a rich liter-
ary heritage and cultural signiﬁcance, highlight-
ing the need for AI systems that can effectively
engage with Telugu-speaking communities. This
research investigates the Telugu language capabil-
ities of two prominent LLMs: ChatGPT and Gem-
ini. By conducting a comparative analysis, we aim
to assess their strengths and weaknesses in han-
dling Telugu across various aspects.
The present study utilizes a set of 20 meticu-
lously designed questions to evaluate the LLMs’
grasp of Telugu grammar, vocabulary, common
phrases, and their ability to perform tasks within
the language.
This comprehensive approach al-
lows us to gain valuable insights into how Chat-
GPT and Gemini navigate the intricacies of Tel-
ugu. The analysis will delve into their understand-
ing of basic greetings and introductions, their abil-
ity to identify and utilize grammatical structures,
and the breadth of their Telugu vocabulary. Fur-
thermore, the evaluation will explore their compe-
tence in handling everyday expressions and their
capacity to complete tasks like writing essays or
reasoning through situational contexts. Through
this in-depth analysis, we aim to contribute to the
ongoing dialogue around multilingual capabilities
in LLMs and identify areas where further develop-
ment is necessary.

3
Methodology
To evaluate the Natural Language Understanding
(NLU) and Natural Language Generation (NLG)
capabilities of the two prominent Large Language
Models (LLMs) in Telugu, a structured approach
was devised to systematically assess their compre-
hension and contextual understanding of the lan-
guage. The methodology consisted of formulat-
ing a set of questions covering different aspects of
language understanding and language generation,
ranging from basic comprehension to domain-
speciﬁc knowledge.
These questions were de-
signed to elicit responses that would reveal the
LLMs’ proﬁciency in understanding Telugu text
and processing it in a meaningful way.
The process began by identifying key areas of
interest within NLU and NLG, including entity
recognition, sentiment analysis, query understand-
ing, and domain speciﬁcity, among others. Based
on these areas, a comprehensive set of questions
was developed to probe the LLMs’ capability es
in each domain. Care was taken to ensure that the
questions were diverse and representative of real-
world language usage, encompassing both general
conversational scenarios and domain-speciﬁc con-
texts.
The questions were formulated in Telugu to ac-
curately reﬂect the language’s structure, syntax,
and vocabulary.
Special attention was given to
crafting questions that varied in complexity and
linguistic nuance, allowing for a thorough evalu-
ation of the LLMs’ language comprehension abil-
ities. Additionally, efforts were made to include
questions that required contextual understanding
and inference, challenging the models to go be-
yond surface-level language processing and delve
into deeper semantic understanding.
To maintain consistency and fairness in the eval-
uation process, the same set of questions was
posed to each LLM under consideration. This en-
sured a standardized approach to assessing their
performance and allowed for direct comparisons
between different models. Furthermore, the ques-
tions were designed to be scalable, allowing for
the evaluation of LLMs of varying sizes and ar-
chitectures.
Overall, the methodology aimed to
provide a rigorous and systematic assessment of
the NLU capabilities of LLMs in Telugu, offering
valuable insights into their strengths, limitations,
and areas for improvement.
This study utilizes a set of 20 questions de-
signed to evaluate the LLMs’ grasp of Telugu
across various aspects. The questions cover:
• Understanding Basics: These questions as-
sess the ability to greet, introduce oneself,
and engage in small talk.
• Grammar and Vocabulary:
These ques-
tions test knowledge of grammatical struc-
tures, vocabulary range, and ability to iden-
tify sentence components.

--- Page 3 ---\n\n• Common Phrases and Expressions: These
questions evaluate the understanding of ev-
eryday expressions and the ability to use
them in context.
• Tasks and Capabilities: These questions as-
sess the ability to perform tasks like writing,
translation, and reasoning in Telugu.
• Adaptability and Reasoning: These ques-
tions test the LLMs’ ability to respond to sit-
uations and use reasoning in Telugu.
The questions were posed to both ChatGPT and
Gemini, and their responses were analyzed for ac-
curacy, ﬂuency, and task completion.
4
Results
The analysis of responses from ChatGPT and
Gemini to the 20 designed questions revealed a
nuanced picture of their Telugu language capabil-
ities. Here’s a breakdown of the ﬁndings across
various categories:
• Understanding Basics: Both LLMs effec-
tively handled greetings and introductions in
Telugu, demonstrating their ability to engage
in fundamental social interactions.
How-
ever, questions requiring more nuanced un-
derstanding, such as responding to polite in-
quiries or using humour, revealed limitations.
ChatGPT’s responses tended to be more lit-
eral, while Gemini displayed a slightly better
grasp of conversational subtleties.
• Grammar and Vocabulary:
The analy-
sis exposed variations in grammatical proﬁ-
ciency between the two models. Gemini dis-
played a more comprehensive understanding
of Telugu grammar, accurately constructing
sentences and identifying components within
them. ChatGPT, while demonstrating a func-
tional grasp of basic grammar, occasionally
produced grammatically incorrect sentences.
In terms of vocabulary, both LLMs possessed
a decent range of common Telugu words.
However, Gemini exhibited a broader vocab-
ulary, including more speciﬁc and nuanced
terms, suggesting a richer exposure to Telugu
text data during its training.
• Common Phrases and Expressions: Evalu-
ating the understanding and use of everyday
Telugu expressions proved insightful. Both
models demonstrated an ability to compre-
hend and use basic phrases for greetings,
thanks, and requests.
However, when pre-
sented with more idiomatic expressions or
proverbs, Gemini displayed a more apparent
advantage. It not only understood the mean-
ing but also incorporated them seamlessly
into its responses, suggesting a deeper under-
standing of Telugu cultural nuances.
• Tasks and Capabilities: The evaluation of
task completion abilities revealed contrast-
ing strengths. ChatGPT excelled at tasks re-
quiring factual knowledge retrieval.
When
presented with questions about historical ﬁg-
ures or geographical locations in Telugu, it
provided accurate and informative responses.
However, Gemini demonstrated a superior
capability in creative tasks.
It successfully
composed a short essay on a Telugu festival,
showcasing its ability to organize thoughts
and generate coherent text within a speciﬁc
theme.
• Adaptability and Reasoning: The ﬁnal sec-
tion assessed the LLMs’ ability to respond to
situational contexts and demonstrate reason-
ing in Telugu. Both models displayed a lim-
ited capacity in this area. While they could
provide basic suggestions based on keywords
in the questions, their responses lacked the
depth and ﬂexibility required for complex
reasoning tasks. This suggests a need for fur-
ther development in areas of natural language
understanding and the ability to draw infer-
ences within the Telugu language.
Overall,
the
analysis
highlights
the
unique
strengths and weaknesses of ChatGPT and Gem-
ini in handling Telugu. While both models possess
a functional understanding of the language, Gem-
ini demonstrates a slight edge in terms of gram-
matical accuracy, vocabulary breadth, and cultural
awareness. However, ChatGPT exhibits an advan-
tage in tasks requiring factual knowledge retrieval.
These ﬁndings offer valuable insights into the cur-
rent state of Telugu language capabilities in LLMs
and pave the way for further research and devel-
opment efforts.

ChatGPT and Gemini’s Telugu language capabil-

ugu, their performance diverges based on the task
at hand.
Gemini’s ability to provide accurate,
comprehensive answers with detailed explanations
highlights a deeper grasp of Telugu grammar, vo-
cabulary, and cultural context. This is further cor-
roborated by its superior performance in natural
language generation tasks, where it successfully
generated poems, stories, and situational instruc-
tions that were both creative and coherent. Con-
versely, ChatGPT’s focus on factual knowledge re-
trieval, evident in its ability to list Telugu alpha-
bets and convert numerals, suggests a more rudi-
mentary approach to language understanding. Its
shortcomings in natural language generation tasks,
characterized by short answers and limited expla-
nations, reﬂect a need for further development in
areas like creative text production and reasoning
within the Telugu language.
These ﬁndings raise interesting questions about
the training data and the underlying architecture
of each LLM. Perhaps ChatGPT’s training data
prioritized factual accuracy over creative expres-
sion, leading to its proﬁciency in tasks requiring
the retrieval of readily available information. Con-
versely, Gemini’s training data might have incor-
porated a wider range of Telugu text formats, in-
cluding poems, stories, and cultural references,
fostering its superior performance in tasks requir-
ing creative language use and cultural understand-
ing.
The observed strengths and weaknesses also
suggest potential areas for future research and de-
velopment. For ChatGPT, incorporating diverse
Telugu text formats during training could enhance
its natural language generation capabilities and
equip it to handle tasks beyond factual retrieval.
Conversely, Gemini’s success in creative tasks
underscores the importance of such approaches
in LLM development.
Furthermore, both mod-
els could beneﬁt from advancements in natural
language understanding, allowing them to reason
more effectively within the Telugu language and
respond to complex situational contexts.
In conclusion, this study’s ﬁndings contribute
signiﬁcantly to the ongoing exploration of multi-
lingual capabilities in LLMs. By highlighting the
strengths and weaknesses of ChatGPT and Gemini
in handling Telugu, the research paves the way for
further development efforts that can create LLMs
capable of seamless integration with diverse lan-
guage communities. As LLMs continue to evolve,
ensuring their proﬁciency extends beyond domi-
nant languages like English remains crucial. This
research serves as a stepping stone towards achiev-
ing that goal, fostering the creation of LLMs that
can effectively communicate and engage with peo-
ple across the globe, regardless of their native lan-
guage.

Conclusion
The comparative analysis of ChatGPT and Gem-
ini’s Telugu language capabilities presented in this
research offers valuable insights into the current
state of multilingual LLMs. While both models
demonstrate a base level of understanding, their
performance diverges signiﬁcantly. Gemini’s su-
perior ability to provide comprehensive answers,
engage in natural language generation tasks, and
demonstrate cultural awareness positions it as the
more proﬁcient LLM in handling the intricacies
of Telugu. Conversely, ChatGPT’s strength lies in
factual knowledge retrieval, suggesting a need for
broader training data and development in areas re-
quiring creative expression and reasoning within
the Telugu language.
These ﬁndings highlight the critical role of
training data and architecture in shaping LLM ca-
pabilities. By incorporating diverse text formats
and cultural nuances during training, developers
can foster LLMs that excel in natural language
understanding and generation across different lan-
guages. The present study underscores the impor-
tance of such an approach for fostering true mul-
tilingual capabilities in LLMs. As the ﬁeld of AI
continues to evolve, this research serves as a step-
ping stone towards creating LLMs that can seam-
lessly integrate with diverse language communi-
ties, bridging communication gaps and fostering a
more inclusive digital landscape. Future research
efforts should focus on expanding training data,
reﬁning LLM architecture, and enhancing natural
language understanding to create LLMs that can
effectively communicate and engage with people
across the globe, celebrating the richness and di-
versity of human languages.

Limitations
The current study acknowledges several limita-
tions that warrant consideration when interpreting
the ﬁndings. Firstly, the chosen set of 20 ques-

only encompass part of the spectrum of Telugu
language capabilities. Expanding the question set
to include more nuanced tasks, complex reason-
ing scenarios, and a more comprehensive range of
natural language generation formats could provide
a more thorough understanding of each LLM’s
strengths and weaknesses. Additionally, the eval-
uation relied solely on the responses generated by
the LLMs. Incorporating human evaluation along-
side automated analysis could offer valuable in-
sights into the naturalness, ﬂuency, and cultural
appropriateness of the generated text. This would
provide a more comprehensive picture of how ef-
fectively each LLM communicates in Telugu.
Furthermore, the study focused solely on the
performance of ChatGPT and Gemini. Including
additional LLMs with a focus on Telugu language
capabilities would have allowed for a broader
comparison and a more nuanced understanding of
the current landscape in Telugu-enabled AI. This
would have highlighted the unique strengths and
weaknesses of various models, fostering a more
comprehensive analysis of the ﬁeld. Finally, the
study is limited by the ongoing development na-
ture of LLMs.
Both ChatGPT and Gemini are
constantly being improved and updated. As they
evolve, their performance in handling Telugu is
likely to change. Future research efforts should
consider re-evaluating these models periodically
to track their progress and identify areas where
they continue to excel or require further develop-
ment.
By acknowledging these limitations, the re-
search emphasizes the need for further exploration
in this domain. Expanding the scope of evalua-
tion methods, incorporating a more comprehen-
sive range of LLMs, and conducting periodic re-
evaluations will contribute signiﬁcantly to the on-
going development of robust and versatile multi-
lingual LLMs.
