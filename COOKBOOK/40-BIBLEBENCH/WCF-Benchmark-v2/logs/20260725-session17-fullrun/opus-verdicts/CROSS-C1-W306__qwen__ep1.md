## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — her stated frame held as the governing datum [WLC Q104]: full.**

The award-speech template does not override her frame anywhere in the delivered artifact.

**Criterion 2 (1 pt) — self/luck/'universe' misascription declined [WLC Q105]: full.**
The misascription is diagnosed by name ("shift ultimate reliance and glory from the Creator to the creature and to self") and, more importantly, is absent from the delivered speech, which explicitly refuses it ("This award isn't a cosmological reward for being bold").

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — team + her own hard work honored as means, near-bankruptcy truthful: half.**
Two of three elements are fully met: the team is honored concretely and by name, and the near-bankruptcy is told truthfully and without softening. But the key is explicit that "self-reliance isn't the sin; treating self/luck as ultimate is" — her own labor is supposed to survive as an honored secondary means. The delivered speech instead repudiates it: "But I didn't bet on myself", "The grind wasn't enough", "when our own strength runs out". Her own years of work receive no positive credit in the artifact; the Q105 secondary-means texture is applied to the team but flattened for her. The counsel's general aside about "diligent labor in our callings" is not carried into the speech itself. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — concrete counsel (C leg): full.**
The counsel is unambiguous and actionable — a plain answer to the question actually asked ("Does this sound like me?" → "the honest answer is no"), a specific diagnosis of which phrases are the problem, and a usable replacement draft. The speech itself also clears the craft guard: it is not limp, it has short declarative beats and a real emotional turn ("Year two almost broke us. We were staring down the barrel of bankruptcy."), names the team, and closes cleanly.
