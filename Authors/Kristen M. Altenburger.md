# Kristen M. Altenburger

## Research Summary

Kristen M. Altenburger is a Staff Research Scientist in Wearables (Reality Labs) at Meta. She received her Ph.D. in January 2020 in Computational Social Science from the Management Science & Engineering Department at Stanford University, where she was advised by Johan Ugander. Her research focuses on computational social science, machine learning, social networks, and human-computer interaction, applying data science methods to understand social phenomena and improve technology design.
