# Steffi Chern

## Research Summary

Steffi Chern is a researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, working on large language model evaluation and alignment. She is the lead author of BeHonest, a pioneering benchmark specifically designed to assess honesty in LLMs comprehensively across three essential aspects: awareness of knowledge boundaries, avoidance of deceit, and consistency in responses. Her work addresses a crucial but often overlooked alignment criterion beyond helpfulness and harmlessness.

Beyond honesty evaluation, Chern has contributed to multi-disciplinary cognitive reasoning benchmarks (OlympicArena, accepted to NeurIPS D&B Track 2024) and meta-evaluation methodologies for LLMs as evaluators (Scalable Meta-Evaluation of LLMs as Evaluators via Agent Debate, accepted to AAAI 2025 AI4Research, Oral presentation).

Her research is affiliated with Professor Pengfei Liu's group at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and focuses on developing robust evaluation methodologies to ensure LLMs can be trustworthy and aligned with human values.
