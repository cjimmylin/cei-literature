## Abstract

Contextually ensembled translation framework with human-in-the-loop validation for Urdu reasoning benchmarks. Translates MGSM, MATH-500, CommonSenseQA, and OpenBookQA into Urdu. First comprehensive Urdu reasoning benchmark with cultural and linguistic nuance preservation.
