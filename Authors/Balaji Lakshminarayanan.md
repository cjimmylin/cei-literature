# Balaji Lakshminarayanan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Balaji Lakshminarayanan is a Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind in Mountain View. He earned his PhD from the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit at University College London (UCL), where he was advised by Yee Whye Teh. He is a primary investigator in the reliability of machine learning systems and has been at Google since 2016.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Balaji’s research centers on making machine learning models more robust and reliable. He focuses on uncertainty quantification, out-of-distribution detection, and selective prediction for foundation models, particularly in the context of Large Language Models (LLMs). His work aims to ensure that AI systems are aware of their own limitations and can provide reliable outputs in diverse scenarios.

### Significant Achievements
He is widely recognized for his pioneering work on "Deep Ensembles," a state-of-the-art method for uncertainty quantification in deep learning. His research has had a significant impact on the safety and reliability of AI, with over 35,000 citations. He is a regular contributor to top-tier conferences such as NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.
