# Guoan Wang

## Biographical Summary

Guoan Wang is a Ph.D. student in the Department of System Engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology, under the supervision of Assistant Professor Feng Liu. Prior to that, he received his master's degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at East China Normal University, where he was supervised by Associate Professor Lei Chen. ## Research Focus

Wang's research focuses on medical AI, particularly on developing comprehensive benchmarks and models for medical image analysis using multimodal learning approaches.

## Notable Contributions

Wang is one of the co-first authors on the paper "GMAI-MMBench: A Comprehensive Multimodal Evaluation Benchmark Towards General Medical AI," accepted by NeurIPS 2024.

GMAI-MMBench is described as the most comprehensive general medical AI benchmark with well-categorized data structure and multi-perceptual granularity to date. It is constructed from 284 datasets across 38 medical image modalities, 18 clinical-related tasks, 18 departments, and 4 perceptual granularities in a Visual Question Answering (VQA) format. The researchers evaluated 50 LVLMs, and results show that even advanced GPT-4o only achieves an accuracy of 53.96%, indicating significant room for improvement.
