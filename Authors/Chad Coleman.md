# Chad Coleman

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Chad Coleman is a dual-track academic and industry leader. He earned his PhD in Cognitive Science and Machine Learning from Columbia University. He is currently an Adjunct Professor at both NYU and Columbia, a founding member of NYU's Computational Logic Lab, and has served as the Senior Director of AI at ZoomInfo.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Modeling Human Cognitive Behavior"—specifically applying machine learning to predict risks and analyze social dynamics in professional environments (People Analytics). In the context of AI, he investigates the "Ethical Logic" of Large Language Models, auditing how models like GPT-4 apply moral principles in decision-making tasks.

### Significant Achievements
Coleman's work bridges the gap between theoretical cognitive science and real-world AI governance. His recent audits of the "Ethical Logic" of six major LLMs provided critical evidence of "Moral Drift" and the inconsistencies in how various Frontier models interpret deontological vs. utilitarian frameworks.
