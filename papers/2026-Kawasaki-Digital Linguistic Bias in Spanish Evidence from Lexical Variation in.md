## Abstract

Examines how LLMs capture geographic lexical variation across 21 Spanish-speaking countries with 900+ lexical items. Spain, Equatorial Guinea, Mexico/Central America, La Plata River varieties recognized best. Chilean variety most difficult. Data volume does not explain performance gaps.
