## Abstract

Virtue theory is a natural approach toward designing AI systems, given that AI design essentially aims at creating agents with excellent dispositions. This has led to research on artificial virtues, though this program has focused narrowly on moral virtues in an Aristotelian mould, which unduly constrains possibilities.

Drawing on philosophical tradition, the paper proposes a three-dimensional classification system of possible artificial virtues based on:

1. **Domain**: The domain in which virtue is an excellence
2. **Norm**: The norm that makes a virtue an excellence
3. **Mode**: The mode of how the virtue delivers the excellence

## Key Contribution

- Aristotelian-only framework
- Limited virtue taxonomy
