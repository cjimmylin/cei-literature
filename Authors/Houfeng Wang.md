# Houfeng Wang

## Research Summary

Houfeng Wang is a Professor at the Institute of Computational Linguistics, Peking University, in the Department of Computer Science and Technology. He has an extensive publication record in NLP spanning multiple areas including question answering, spoken language understanding, grammatical error correction, text classification, sentiment analysis, and text summarization. Notable works include research on attentive interactive neural networks for answer selection in community QA (AAAI), joint models for intent determination and slot filling (IJCAI 2016), Bi-LSTM for Chinese grammatical error diagnosis (NLP-TEA@COLING 2016), text level graph neural networks (2019), and unpaired sentiment-to-sentiment translation. He frequently publishes in top venues including ACL, AAAI, EMNLP, IJCAI, COLING, and NLPCC.
