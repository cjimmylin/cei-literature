# Alex Ingerman

## Biographical Summary

Alex Ingerman is a Product Manager at Google Research, specializing in the deployment of privacy-preserving and ethical AI systems. He previously held leadership roles at Amazon Machine Learning. His work focuses on the intersection of technical architecture (specifically federated learning) and the societal impact of AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alex contributes to the ethical framework development for advanced AI assistants. He researches the risks and opportunities of agentic AI systems that plan and execute actions on behalf of users, focusing on transparency, user agency, and the prevention of deceptive model behaviors.

## Major Publications
- [The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05386) (2024) - *Defining the responsible boundaries for agentic AI.*
