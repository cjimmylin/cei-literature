# Guohai Xu

## Biographical Summary

Guohai Xu is a researcher currently affiliated with Alibaba Group, Hangzhou. His research focuses on AI alignment and value evaluation in large language models.

## Research Focus

Xu's research addresses the critical challenge of aligning large language models with human values, particularly in the context of Chinese language models. His work examines how to evaluate and ensure that AI systems behave in accordance with societal values and safety standards.

## Notable Contributions

Xu is a co-author of "CValues: Measuring the Values of Chinese Large Language Models from Safety to Responsibility," a preprint from July 2023. The research addresses an important topic in AI alignment:

"With the rapid evolution of large language models (LLMs), there is a growing concern that they may pose risks or have negative social impacts. Therefore, evaluation of human values alignment is becoming increasingly important."

The CValues work addresses the gap in values alignment evaluation for Chinese LLMs, as previous work mainly focused on assessing the performance of LLMs on certain knowledge and reasoning abilities.

## Other Research

Xu has also contributed to research on multimodal learning, including mPLUG-DocOwl, a modularized multimodal large language model for document understanding (July 2023).
