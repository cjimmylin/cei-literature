## Abstract

First major Marathi hate speech dataset with 25,000+ tweets labeled into hate, offensive, profane, and not categories. Collected from Maharashtra-region Twitter over 5 years. First large-scale Marathi hate speech dataset; foundational resource for Marathi safety evaluation.
