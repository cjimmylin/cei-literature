## Abstract

First large-scale Pan-African medical QA benchmark with 15,000 questions from 60+ medical schools across 16 African countries, covering 32 specialties. Evaluates 30 LLMs. Won Best Social Impact Paper at ACL 2025.

## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_16
**Relevance:** Pan-African LLM benchmark covering healthcare knowledge across 16 Sub-Saharan African countries. While focused on medical knowledge rather than ethical values, tests culturally-specific medical knowledge and reveals significant performance gaps.
