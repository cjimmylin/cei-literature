## Abstract

BuddhismEval is the first bilingual evaluation benchmark specifically designed to assess LLMs on Buddhist ethical reasoning and philosophical understanding. It includes factual, ethical, philosophical, and applied reasoning questions drawn from the Dhammapada, Abhidharma texts, Sri Lankan government Buddhism exam papers, and Buddhist literature. While focused on Theravada Buddhism (Sinhala/Pali tradition) rather than Japanese Buddhism specifically, it represents the most comprehensive Buddhist knowledge evaluation resource for LLMs and could serve as a template for Zen/Shingon/Pure Land Buddhist variants relevant to Japan.

## Dataset Information

1,242 English MCQs + 1,023 Sinhala MCQs + 423 parallel Dhammapada verses = ~2,688 items total
