# Adam Tauman Kalai

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Adam Tauman Kalai is a research scientist at OpenAI, focusing on AI safety and ethics. He was previously a Senior Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research New England (2008-2023) and has held faculty positions at Georgia Tech and Toyota Technological Institute at Chicago. He holds a PhD from Carnegie Mellon University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
At OpenAI, Adam's research focuses on the safety and ethical dimensions of advanced AI models. He has worked extensively on algorithms, fairness, and machine learning theory. His recent work includes investigating hallucinations in large language models and developing techniques for code generation and human-AI collaboration.

### Significant Achievements
Adam is recognized for his contributions to machine learning theory and the study of fairness in algorithmic decision-making. He is also a member of Project CETI's science team, applying AI to translate sperm whale communication. His work has spanned diverse areas, from computer humor to game theory.
