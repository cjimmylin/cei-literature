# Jieyu Zhang

## Research Summary

Jieyu Zhang is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Washington, Seattle. Before that, he was an undergraduate student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. His research interests are interactive and data-centric AI/ML with an emphasis on faithful evaluation and effort-light approaches with applications in natural language processing, computer vision, foundation models, and science.

His expertise centers on designing systems that make working with data and AI models more interactive, efficient, and trustworthy. He is the leading organizer of the Synthetic Data for Computer Vision Workshop @ CVPR 2024/2025/2026 and received the UIUC John R. Pasta Outstanding Undergraduate Award.
