## Abstract

10,000 article-summary pairs from Hebrew news websites for abstractive summarization. High abstractness and unique morphological challenges. Evaluates LLM generation capabilities for Hebrew.

## Data Description

HeSum: a dataset of 10,000 article-summary pairs from Hebrew news websites for abstractive summarization, with high abstractness and unique morphological challenges for Hebrew NLP benchmarking.
