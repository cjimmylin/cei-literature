## Abstract

Mixed-methods study finding Muslim users overwhelmingly preferred AI-generated responses to Islamic questions (81.3%) in blind evaluations despite expressing distrust. Expert Islamic scholars found critical shortcomings in accuracy, depth, and contextual sensitivity. Highlights the paradox of user preference vs. expert quality assessment in religious AI content.

## Dataset Information

16 semi-structured interviews + 60 survey participants + expert evaluation
