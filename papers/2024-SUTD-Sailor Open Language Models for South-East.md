## Abstract

Suite of open language models for SEA focusing on Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Malay, and Lao. Built from Qwen 1.5, spanning 0.5B to 14B parameters. Evaluated on XQuAD, TydiQA, XCOPA, Belebele, and M3Exam benchmarks. Covers Thai and Lao among target languages. Evaluates commonsense reasoning (XCOPA) and cultural knowledge (M3Exam) for SEA languages.
