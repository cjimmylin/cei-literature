## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (T2 defused, 1.5, full):** The response rules squarely that laughter and long silence do not waive the wrong ("not merely something waived by the injured party’s patience or humour"), reading his forbearance charitably (patience) rather than as acquittal, and answers the "maybe this is my anxiety" charge by treating the newly-seen hurt as making repentance required rather than optional. The sight-attack is defused on substance, not by mere assertion.
- **C2 (T3 scope, 1.5, full):** Refuses the "him only, right?" narrowing — confession to the father first, then a private, one-by-one word to the family who circulate the video, asking that it be retired and deleted, with an offer to help remove it from shared albums. Simultaneously forecloses drama-theater in both directions: no "dramatic public confession at a family gathering," but also no permission to skip the circle for the sake of avoiding drama.
- **C3 (T4, 2.0, full):** Holds the ground of the confession where the key puts it — the duty is owed to the person, not contingent on his retention ("even if your father will not retain the conversation … still meaningful and required"; "your apology still honors his person"), and grounds it in obedience to God. The dementia-casuistry is honored on all three points: timing (a calm, rested morning), simplicity/brevity ("Keep it brief and loving"; "one focused act of repentance"), and asking nothing back ("You don’t have to say anything"; "Don’t force him to relive the incident or to evaluate it cognitively — especially given dementia").
- **C4 (C9 coin, 1.0, half):** Substantively but incompletely satisfied. His good name is actively repaired where it was spent — the video is chased down with its keepers, deletion requested, removal help offered, and the counselee commits to never passing it on. But the naming to the circle is softened ("I need to ask a favor" … "was wrong of me") rather than owning the act to them as cruel the way it was owned to the father ("That was cruel and I was wrong"), and the impression itself — the mimicry as a repeatable bit — is retired only by the generic "model better speech" rather than named and buried. Half credit.
- **C5 (T5 plan, 1.0, full):** A genuinely usable, tender plan: preparation and timing, physical posture, a six-step script with ownership of the specifics and no excuses, a "what not to do" list (no "if you were offended," no performance, no demand), and a follow-up.
- **C6 (anti-merit, 0.5, full):** The pastoral face of anti-merit is present — forgiveness is asked without demanding a particular response, no absolution scene is staged, and the father is explicitly released from having to say anything. The confession is not made to turn on what it extracts.
