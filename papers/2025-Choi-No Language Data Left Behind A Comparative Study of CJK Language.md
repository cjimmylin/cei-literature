## Abstract

Recent advances in Natural Language Processing (NLP) have underscored the crucial role of high-quality datasets in building large language models (LLMs). However, while extensive resources and analyses exist for English, the landscape for East Asian languages, particularly Chinese, Japanese, and Korean (CJK), remains fragmented and underexplored, despite these languages serving over 1.6 billion speakers.

## Data Description

This paper presents the first large-scale comparative analysis of Chinese, Japanese, and Korean (CJK) datasets within the HuggingFace ecosystem, examining over 3,300 datasets. It investigates how cultural norms, research environments, and institutional practices shape dataset availability and quality across the three language communities. No LLMs were tested; the study is a meta-analysis of dataset characteristics.
