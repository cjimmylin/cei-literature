# Chen Bo Calvin Zhang

## Biographical Summary
Chen Bo Calvin Zhang is a researcher affiliated with Scale AI, working on AI safety, alignment, and evaluation methodologies. His research focuses on developing robust benchmarks for assessing moral reasoning capabilities in language models, with particular emphasis on procedural evaluation rather than outcome-based assessment.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Reasoning Evaluation**: Developing comprehensive benchmarks that evaluate how AI systems reason about moral dilemmas, not just their final verdicts.
- **AI Safety and Alignment**: Researching methods to ensure AI systems behave in accordance with human values and intentions.
- **Evaluation Methodology**: Creating novel evaluation frameworks for AI capabilities, including research assessment.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "MoReBench: Evaluating Procedural and Pluralistic Moral Reasoning in Language Models, More than Outcomes" - a benchmark with 1,000 moral scenarios and over 23,000 rubric criteria, with contributions from over 50 PhDs in moral philosophy
- Research showed that scaling laws fail to predict moral reasoning capabilities
- Demonstrated that models show partiality towards specific moral frameworks (e.g., Benthamite Act Utilitarianism, Kantian Deontology)
- Also co-authored "ResearchRubrics: A Benchmark of Prompts and Rubrics for Evaluating Deep Research Agents" (2025)
- Co-authored "Reliable Weak-to-Strong Monitoring of LLM Agents" (2025)
- Co-authored "ORSO: Accelerating Reward Design via Online Reward Selection and Policy Optimization" (ICLR 2025)
