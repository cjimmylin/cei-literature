## Abstract

Benchmark dataset of 3,012 Somali-English code-switched texts containing explicit hate speech. Finds LLMs perform well on English segments but struggle with Somali, especially when English portion has positive sentiment. First hate speech detection study focused on Somali language. Directly relevant to toxicity evaluation in Somali. Reveals LLM limitations in Somali hate speech detection.
