# Anaelia Ovalle

## Research Summary

Anaelia Ovalle is a PhD candidate at UCLA working on fairness and bias in AI systems, with a particular focus on multilingual and multicultural contexts. Her research examines how large language models perpetuate stereotypes across different languages and cultural contexts. She contributed to the SHADES project, a multilingual parallel dataset for examining culturally-specific stereotypes in LLMs across 16 languages and 20 regions worldwide.
