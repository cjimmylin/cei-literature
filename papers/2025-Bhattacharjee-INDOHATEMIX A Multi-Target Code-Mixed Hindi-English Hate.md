## Abstract

Diverse code-mixed Hindi-English hate speech dataset from Koo platform capturing religious and political targeting with transliteration. LLMs significantly outperform traditional transformers. Hindi-English code-mixed hate speech with religious and political targeting; culturally contextualized.
