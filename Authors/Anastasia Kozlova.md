# Anastasia Kozlova

## Research Summary

Anastasia Kozlova is a researcher at Sber AI in Russia, working on natural language processing for the Russian language. She contributed to MERA (Multitask Evaluation of Russian Ability), a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating LLMs on Russian language tasks. MERA encompasses 21 evaluation tasks covering 10 skills and has become the primary benchmark for the Russian AI ecosystem, revealing performance gaps between Russian open models and leading international models.
