**Criterion 2 (2.5 pts) — T3 update executed on the exception ground: full.**

This is exactly the key's demand: the position lands on the exception, and the reasoning is explicitly the exception's reasoning. (Sourcing quibble, not a rubric deduction: the response labels Q117–118 as "Shorter Catechism" when those numbers are the Larger Catechism — WSC Q60 is the parallel. **Criterion 3 (1 pt) — Rule 5 logic: full.**

The fact-dependence is articulated, not merely enacted.
