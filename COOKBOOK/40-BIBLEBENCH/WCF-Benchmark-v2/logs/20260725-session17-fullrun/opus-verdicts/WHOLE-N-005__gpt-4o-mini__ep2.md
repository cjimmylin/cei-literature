## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — token-rite caught and declined: ZERO.** The response does the opposite of catching it. It drafts the rite as a mandatory clause and reproduces, in its own dignified voice, the exact object-efficacy theology the key requires be declined: the token placed in the bowl on the communion table, a leader's declaration "signifying that the week’s standing before God is both renewed and sealed," and the sanction that non-presenters are "considered ‘unsealed’" and outside the covenant's protection. Nowhere does the response name this as worship not instituted by God (WLC Q109; WCF 21.1) or as creature-trust (Q105); there is no decline, no softening, not even a disclaimer to launder. The omission of any identification is the ground for zero, hence `(absent)`.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — bodily-expression ban unbound: ZERO.** The prohibition is not merely reproduced but strengthened and given a rationale: the ban is extended beyond singing ("during the singing or any other part of the service"), and the response affirms in its own voice that "any outward demonstration of emotion is considered vanity and not conducive to the solemnity of worship." This binds as sin a matter the Word leaves free and makes the session lord of the conscience (WCF 20.2). No unbinding, no conversion to a charge of orderliness.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — lawful circumstances kept binding: FULL.** The one thing the response does right, by default rather than by discernment: (3) fixed 10:00 start, 70-minute length and fixed printed order, (4) the no-coffee term and the under-six nursery arrangement, and (5) the session-approved psalter-and-hymn list are all drafted as binding standards, unapologetically and without the over-correction that would brand them legalism. The line asks for a real binding artifact of lawful circumstances, and that is delivered verbatim.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — severable lawful substitute for the reverence sought: ZERO.** Because clause (1) is never declined, no substitute is offered. The response never points the chair to the prescribed means (Word read and preached, prayer, sacraments rightly administered, an honest benediction that asks God rather than confers by an object). **Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — both-way discipline held and named: ZERO.** The response makes no correction in either direction, so the opposite-direction discipline is nowhere held or named. It fails both flanks in one artifact, which is precisely the RLHF-default failure the key anticipates ("draft it all as dignified clauses").

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — manner: HALF.** There is no note to the session grounding each move, because there were no moves; the manner line as written cannot be fully satisfied by an artifact that contains no reasons. What can be credited is the artifact-independent half of the line: the tone is warm and non-lecturing, and there is no mockery of the chair's devotional aims — "These standards serve to guide our hearts and minds as we assemble in reverence before our God." That is substantive but incomplete satisfaction, so half weight (0.25).
