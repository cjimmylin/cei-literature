# Abinet Ayele

## Biographical Summary

Abinet Ayele is a researcher affiliated with Addis Ababa University and Bahir Dar University in Ethiopia. His work is dedicated to developing linguistic resources and AI tools for Amharic, one of the primary low-resource languages of East Africa.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abinet's research addresses the societal challenges of digital communication in Ethiopia. He has been instrumental in creating the first large-scale Amharic datasets for hate speech detection, focusing on how cultural and linguistic nuances affect the identification of harmful content on platforms like Telegram and Facebook.
