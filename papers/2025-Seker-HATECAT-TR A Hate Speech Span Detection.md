## Abstract

Span-annotated dataset of Turkish tweets containing 4,465 hateful spans across 2,981 posts, each directed at one of 8 minority groups. Fine-grained hate speech categorization. Fine-grained Turkish hate speech dataset targeting 8 specific minority groups. Goes beyond binary classification to identify exact hateful expressions within posts.

## Benchmark

**Name:** HATECAT-TR

## Data Description

HATECAT-TR is a span-annotated dataset of 2,981 Turkish tweets containing 4,465 hateful spans directed at 8 minority groups, with each span categorized into one of five discourse types. Annotations were created via a semi-automated approach combining GPT-4o-generated spans with human expert review.
