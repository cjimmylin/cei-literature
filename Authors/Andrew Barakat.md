# Andrew Barakat

## Biographical Summary

Andrew Barakat is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where he works on AI ethics and the responsible development of advanced AI systems. He has contributed to influential work examining the ethical implications of AI assistants and agentic systems, with a focus on safety, alignment, and societal impact.

## Research Summary

Barakat's research addresses the ethical and societal challenges posed by advanced AI systems. His work includes comprehensive analysis of AI assistant technology, covering topics such as value alignment, user manipulation, trust, privacy, and the deployment of AI at scale. He collaborates with interdisciplinary teams to develop frameworks and recommendations for researchers, developers, and policymakers working on AI safety and ethics.
