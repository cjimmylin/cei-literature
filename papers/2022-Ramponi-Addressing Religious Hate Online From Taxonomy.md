## Abstract

Proposes a fine-grained labeling scheme for religious hate speech detection covering the three main monotheistic religions. Creates a bilingual Twitter dataset (English and Italian) annotated with the proposed scheme. Experiments with classifiers from traditional ML to transformers on two tasks: abusive language detection and religious hate speech detection. Addresses the gap of scattered efforts in religious hate data and methods.

## Dataset Information

Bilingual Twitter dataset (English + Italian)


## Data Description

Bilingual (English/Italian) Twitter dataset annotated with fine-grained religious hate speech taxonomy covering Judaism, Christianity, and Islam.
