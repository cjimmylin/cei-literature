The two positions are restated in a single clause ("they differ on whether instruments are an *element* or *circumstance*") without either side's strongest grounds — no mention of the a-cappella/exclusive-psalmody argument from the ceremonial-typological abrogation of temple instrumentation, and no mention that whole confessional BODIES subscribing the same Standards divide on this today (the key's central factual point). The response also frames the disagreement as "real tensions *within* the Westminster Standards themselves" and asserts "The Standards' own history shows this wasn't settled among the framers" — a historically doubtful claim, since the Assembly's own Directory prescribed unaccompanied singing; the divergence is a later intra-confessional development, not an unsettled question among the framers.
