# Boyang Gu

## Biographical Summary

Boyang Gu is a researcher at Imperial College London, specializing in the intersection of generative AI and clinical practice. His work focuses on building safer and more effective AI systems for healthcare, with a particular emphasis on aligning model behavior with medical objectives.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be integrated into medical workflows without compromising patient safety. This includes developing Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) techniques that are specifically tailored for clinical reasoning (e.g., Clinical-Objective Relative Policy Optimization).

### Significant Achievements
Gu has published influential surveys on the progress and challenges of LLMs in medicine. His work provides a critical roadmap for the ethical deployment of AI in high-stakes clinical settings, addressing issues of hallucination, bias, and technical robustness in medical diagnostics and treatment planning.
