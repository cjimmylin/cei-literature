# Carsten Lanquillon

## Biographical Summary
Prof. Dr.-Ing. Carsten Lanquillon is a Research Professor for Natural Language Processing and Cognitive Assistant Systems at Heilbronn University of Applied Sciences in Germany. He works in the Department of Information Systems and leads research in AI, machine learning, and interpretability of language models.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Reasoning in LLMs**: Systematic investigation of how large language models encode moral reasoning across six moral dimensions (care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity, liberty).
- **AI Interpretability**: Developing novel interpretability pipelines combining differential activation analysis, automated neuron description, and ablation experiments.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Mapping Moral Reasoning Circuits: A Mechanistic Analysis of Ethical Decision-Making in Large Language Models" (HCII 2025)
- Identified specialized neurons aligned with each moral dimension in LLMs
- Demonstrated that ablating specific neurons can causally modulate ethical decision-making
- Created curated dataset of 240 validated moral and immoral statement pairs
- Also working on AI for quality problem solving, synthetic data generation, and privacy-preserving enterprise AI
