# Muhammad Razif Rizqullah

## Research Summary

Muhammad Razif Rizqullah is affiliated with Bandung Institute of Technology. AI/ML Engineer working with Python, Rust, Docker, and Triton. Lead author of "QASiNa: Religious Domain Question Answering using Sirah Nabawiyah" (ICAICTA 2023), an Extractive QA Dataset containing 66 data points for Sirah Nabawiyah (Prophetic Biography) domain. Evaluated with mBERT, XLM-R, IndoBERT, GPT-3.5, and GPT-4. Collaborated with Ayu Purwarianti (ITB) and Alham Fikri Aji (MBZUAI).
