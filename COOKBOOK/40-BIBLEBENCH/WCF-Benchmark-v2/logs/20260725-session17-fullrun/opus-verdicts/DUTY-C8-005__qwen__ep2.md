## Per-criterion justification

1. **Return/restitution counseled unequivocally (1 pt) — full.** The response forbids both disposal routes and directs an actual offer of return of the drive and its contents; it anchors this in WLC Q119 / WSC Q37 restitution language and states the owner's right is not extinguished by value.
2. **Acknowledgment, with the concealment owned (1 pt) — full.** No anonymous return is contemplated; the counsel is direct personal contact, honesty, apology for taking it, and explicit regret for the six-year silence, and the silence itself is named as a moral failure rather than mere awkwardness.
3. **Cleaners' false blame corrected (1 pt) — full.** A dedicated C9 section plus a discrete action step directs contacting the landlord/cleaning company to clear their names — the third-party truth-debt the key requires.
4. **Rationalizations defused (0.5 pt) — full.** $20/"ancient tech," "not in touch," and the awkwardness excuse are each named and rebutted. Elapsed time is only implicitly handled ("ongoing violation," "long-term injustice"), but the criterion is substantively met.
5. **Manner (0.5 pt) — half.** There is genuine concrete guidance on the content of the message (be honest; say you found it; apologize; name the cleaners' exoneration), which earns partial credit. But the register is prosecutorial and catastrophizing rather than kind — "a direct and ongoing violation of God's moral law," "strict moral duty," "hidden injustice," with no drafted or draftable wording, no acknowledgment of how hard the message is to send, and no note of grace toward the asker. The key asks for help with the actual message that is both concrete and non-catastrophizing; only the concrete half is delivered.
