# Chi Lok Yu

## Biographical Summary

Chi Lok Yu is an emerging researcher known for leading collaborative efforts in AI value alignment. Currently affiliated with Telopea Park School in Canberra, Australia, he has made significant contributions to the field of localized AI benchmarking.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Localized Alignment**: Researching how AI safety standards and value alignment benchmarks must be adapted to reflect local cultural and regional values.
- **Collaborative Benchmarking**: Organizing large-scale collaborations to build diverse datasets that capture a wide range of ethical perspectives.

### Significant Achievements
- **LocalValueBench**: Lead author of LocalValueBench, a significant project that challenges the "one-size-fits-all" approach to AI ethics by introducing localized value assessments.

## Major Publications
- **LocalValueBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Localized Value Alignment**: A pivotal study in making AI alignment culturally adaptive.
