# Mansour Al Ghanim

## Research Summary

Mansour Al Ghanim is a PhD student at the University of Central Florida with a focus on Secure Deep Learning. Working with Professor Yan Solihin, his research addresses LLM security, including jailbreaking attacks, text watermarking, and hardware-aware attacks on language models. He has co-authored multiple papers appearing at major conferences including EMNLP 2024 and 2025, and ECAI 2023. His recent work includes research on Arabic text-to-SQL and security vulnerabilities in large language models, particularly focusing on multilingual security challenges.
