## Abstract

Family of LLMs for SEA languages achieving state-of-the-art performances on SEA-HELM. Community participation with native speakers at each stage of dataset construction to ensure cultural authenticity. Produces natural SEA sentences 57.71% of time (best among evaluated models). Covers ALL four target languages with native speaker involvement ensuring cultural authenticity. Benchmarked on SEA-HELM including cultural evaluation pillar.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SEA-LION

## Data Description

SEA-LION family of LLMs (Llama-SEA-LION-8B-IT and Gemma-SEA-LION-9B-IT) for 11 Southeast Asian languages are released, along with pre-training and instruction-tuning datasets with community native-speaker participation. Benchmarked on SEA-HELM including cultural evaluation pillars.
