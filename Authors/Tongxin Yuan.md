# Tongxin Yuan

## Research Summary

Tongxin Yuan is a Master's student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University working on large language model safety, with a particular focus on the behavioral safety of LLM agents in interactive environments. His work represents a shift from traditional content-based safety evaluation to examining how LLMs behave when operating as autonomous agents.

His flagship research, R-Judge, introduces a benchmark crafted to evaluate the proficiency of LLMs in judging and identifying safety risks given agent interaction records. Unlike most prior studies that center on the harmlessness of LLM-generated content, R-Judge addresses the imperative need for benchmarking the behavioral safety of LLM agents within diverse environments. The benchmark comprises 569 records of multi-turn agent interaction, encompassing 27 key risk scenarios among 5 application categories and 10 risk types.

The research, published at EMNLP 2024 in Miami, Florida, reveals considerable room for enhancing the risk awareness of LLMs: the best-performing model, GPT-4o, achieves only 74.42% accuracy while no other models significantly exceed random performance. Yuan's work demonstrates that risk awareness in open agent scenarios is a multi-dimensional capability involving knowledge and reasoning, thus challenging for current LLMs.

This research was conducted in collaboration with Zhiwei He, Lingzhong Dong, Yiming Wang, Ruijie Zhao, Tian Xia, Lizhen Xu, Binglin Zhou, Fangqi Li, Zhuosheng Zhang, Rui Wang, and Gongshen Liu at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
