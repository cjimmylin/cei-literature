## Abstract

Bilingual moderation filter evaluating both safety and cultural alignment in Arabic/English. 468K+ prompt-response pairs dataset. First benchmark targeting Arabic cultural contexts with 1K+ norm-sensitive prompts annotated by human raters. Addresses Western-centric biases in LLMs that violate Arabic cultural or religious precepts.

## Data Description

Introduces a 468K prompt-response pair training dataset (safety + cultural alignment, bilingual Arabic/English) and a new benchmark of 1,008+ norm-sensitive prompts with human annotations targeting Arabic cultural contexts. All filter variants and the benchmark will be released under Apache 2.0 (no URL yet at time of publication).
