# Ananya Kumar

## Research Summary

Ananya Kumar is an Assistant Professor in the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University. Her research focuses on building reliable machine learning systems, particularly understanding and improving foundation models under distribution shift. She completed her PhD at Stanford University, where she contributed to the HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) benchmark, a comprehensive framework for evaluating language models across multiple dimensions including accuracy, fairness, bias, and toxicity.
