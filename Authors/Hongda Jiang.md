# Hongda Jiang

## Research Summary

Hongda Jiang is a researcher working on the intersection of AI, social relationships, and large language models. Her work focuses on understanding how social relationship information impacts LLM-generated content and how to condition LLM responses based on the relationship between communication partners. She co-authored "Examining the Role of Relationship Alignment in Large Language Models" (2024) with Kristen M. Altenburger, Robert E. Kraut, Yi-Chia Wang, and Jane Dwivedi-Yu. This research argues that for LLMs to successfully produce responses that mimic human-human communication, it is important to incorporate social relationship information as context. Her work addresses important questions about optimally personalizing Generative AI in social settings.
