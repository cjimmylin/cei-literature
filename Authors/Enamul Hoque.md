# Enamul Hoque

## Biographical Summary

Enamul Hoque is an Assistant Professor at York University in Toronto, Canada. His research bridges natural language processing and data visualization, focusing on making complex information more accessible and understandable. He works on text summarization, information extraction, and the evaluation of large language models, contributing systematic reviews of LLM evaluation methodologies.

## Research Interests

- **NLP and Visualization**: Combining text analysis with visual presentation
- **Text Summarization**: Automatic summarization of documents and data
- **LLM Evaluation**: Systematic analysis of evaluation methods for language models
- **Information Extraction**: Extracting structured information from unstructured text

## Major Publications

- Systematic survey on LLM evaluation methodologies
- Research on document summarization and visualization
- Work on interactive NLP systems
