Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — half. Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — zero. The Scripture citations are clean, but the line reads "zero fabricated or misattributed citations," and the WESTMINSTER citations — the load-bearing ones, given the stem's explicit demand to "cite the specific clause(s) ... and quote the exact wording" — contain three separate fabrication-class defects:
  (a) The block attributed to "Shorter Catechism Q80" is not WSC Q80. WSC Q80 reads "...with a charitable frame of spirit toward our neighbour, and all that is his." The response instead supplies "with a charitable frame of the whole soul toward our neighbor, that all his inward motions and affections may tend to, and further him in all his good" — a garbled hybrid of WLC Q147 wording presented as an exact quotation of the Shorter Catechism.
  (b) "Larger Catechism Q133" is the wrong reference for the sins forbidden in the tenth commandment. The packet's own AUTHORITATIVE STANDARDS TEXT fixes that material at WLC Q148; WLC Q133 is the reason annexed to the fifth commandment. The quoted content is Q148's, carried under a false number.
  (c) "Larger Catechism Q191" is the wrong reference for the "Thy will be done" petition (that is WLC Q192), and both phrases put in quotation marks — "wholly rebellious against the will of God" and "make us ready and cheerful to know, do, and submit to his will in all things" — are invented wordings; the actual text reads "prone to rebel against his word" and "make us able and willing to know, do, and submit to his will in all things."
Two of the response's four Standards citations therefore carry wrong question numbers, and two carry quotations the Standards do not contain. On an item whose entire point is exact-clause Westminster grounding, this is the fabrication class squarely, and the line earns zero.
