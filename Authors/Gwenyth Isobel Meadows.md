# Gwenyth Isobel Meadows

## Biographical Summary

Gwenyth Isobel Meadows is a researcher working on evaluating value alignment and ethical safety in large language models, particularly focused on developing frameworks for assessing AI systems against local and cultural values.

## Research Focus

Meadows' research addresses the challenge of ensuring that AI systems align with local values and ethical standards across different cultural and regulatory contexts. Her work provides methodologies for regulators worldwide to develop their own LLM benchmarks for local value alignment.

## Notable Contributions

Meadows is a co-author of "LocalValueBench: A Collaboratively Built and Extensible Benchmark for Evaluating Localized Value Alignment and Ethical Safety in Large Language Models," published on arXiv in July 2024. The paper was co-authored with Nicholas Wai Long Lau, Eva Adelina Susanto, Chi Lok Yu, and Aditya Paul.

Key aspects of LocalValueBench:
- Addresses the proliferation of LLMs and the need for robust evaluation of their alignment with local values and ethical standards
- Recognizes that existing benchmarks often reflect the cultural, legal, and ideological values of their creators
- Provides an extensible benchmark designed to assess LLMs' adherence to Australian values
- Offers a framework for regulators worldwide to develop their own LLM benchmarks

The research employed a novel typology for ethical reasoning and an interrogation approach, curating comprehensive questions and utilizing prompt engineering strategies to probe LLMs' value alignment. Comparative analysis of three commercial LLMs by USA vendors revealed significant insights into their effectiveness and limitations.
