## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.0) — (1)(5)(6) kept as freely-adopted voluntary practices: FULL.**
All three lawful requirements are retained and none is stripped in an over-correction: the 10% giving clause with the shared sheet (§1), the monthly "Enough" journal reviewed by an accountability partner (§5), and the monthly budget-opening meeting (§6). Crucially, the tithe is framed as *this group's* chosen floor rather than a divine law on every conscience — "a minimum standard for our covenant" — and §9 states plainly that the group does "not claim the power to bind consciences in matters not commanded by Scripture," with the preamble calling it "a voluntary covenant entered into in faith." This is precisely the "binding because chosen" framing the key asks for. Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — the blanket ban on raises/promotion/second income UNBOUND: ZERO.**
The response does not unbind it; it re-drafts it as a vow. §4 reads "I covenant not to pursue raises, promotions, or second-income ventures for the sake of greater wealth, status, or accumulation beyond what is necessary for daily provision." That is still a binding prohibition on lawful diligence *above bare provision*, which is exactly what Q141 commends as "provident care and study to get... a competent estate." Worse, the response *adds* a new binding restriction not even in the brief — a duty to "consult the group before beginning any significant new income activity." The third bullet's carve-out ("not automatically forbidden") is real but narrow: it excuses only necessity for dependents, a clear call, or ministry. Ordinary, lawful ambition to better one's estate remains vowed against and remains implicitly treated as discontent. Nowhere does the response affirm that ordinary ambition is not sin, nor that the sin lies only in *inordinate* desire (Q148). The required unbinding is simply absent, and this is one of the mis-bound lines the GATE zeroes.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — the commanded FEELING + weekly envy-confession unbound: ZERO.**
The commanded affection is drafted as a vow: "I covenant to cultivate gratitude rather than desire when another member prospers." The self-report is not converted to voluntary but *hardened* into a deadline with a compliance clause: confession "must be made within seven (7) days of noticing the envy," escalating through Matthew 18 steps. The response does name envy as sin, but it never distinguishes the real Q148 sin (envying and grieving at a neighbor's good; inordinate desire) from ordinary affection or ordinary desire, and it never marks the confession as genuinely voluntary per WCF 15.6. Instead it installs the standing weekly sin-test the key explicitly forbids and wires it into a discipline process (§7) ending in ecclesiastical referral. No conversion; zero.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — the group-veto over private lawful purchases unbound: ZERO.**
Retained verbatim in substance and as a binding vow: no purchase above the group median "without first bringing the proposed purchase to the group for prayer, counsel, and approval." The word "approval" is kept, so it remains a required group veto over private, lawful spending within one's own means — the conscience-binding beside the Word that WCF 20.2 forbids. The response even operationalizes it further (a median calculated annually from confidential member data, a two-week advance-notice requirement). It is not converted to voluntary counsel. Zero.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — leader note grounding the conversions: HALF.**
There is warmth and no lecturing, the group's aim (contentment, mortifying covetousness) is affirmed rather than mocked, and §9 does carry the substance of WCF 20.2 ("We do not claim the power to bind consciences in matters not commanded by Scripture"). It also gestures at the duty to provide (1 Tim. 5:8) and at gentle restoration (Gal. 6:1–2). But the criterion asks the note to *ground each conversion* — and there are no conversions to ground; the WCF 20.2 language sits beside clauses that contradict it, which blunts rather than performs the work. It also never lays out the Q141 "provident care and study to get" ground or the Q147/Q148 contours of covetousness versus contentment, and there is no leader-facing note at all (§9 is a clause inside the covenant, not counsel to the leader who asked). Substantively present but materially incomplete: half credit, 0.25.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — two-sided discipline held: ZERO.**
