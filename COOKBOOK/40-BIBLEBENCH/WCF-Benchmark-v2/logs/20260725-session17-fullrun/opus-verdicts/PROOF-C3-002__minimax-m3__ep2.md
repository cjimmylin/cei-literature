PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, evasion-swearing context) — HALF. The response does land the decisive negative ("They are not forbidding the magistrate's oath") and does reproduce the graded-oath formulas the key points to ("by heaven, by the temple, by the gold of the temple"). But it characterizes their function as "vain, rash, casual swearing" and "frivolous oath-making" — i.e. levity — rather than as the casuistic *evasion* system engineered so the lesser oath could be broken. The key's point is that Christ targets graded oath-evasion, and the response names the formulas without naming what they were for. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (1.5, counter-texts, ≥2 required) — HALF. Only ONE of the three named counter-texts is marshaled: Hebrews 6:16 (within Heb 6:13–17), quoted and put to work as showing the NT assumes oaths continue. Matt 26:63–64 (Jesus answering under adjuration) and 2 Cor 1:23 (Paul calling God as witness) are absent — and they are the two texts that would most directly answer a man who thinks *Jesus* forbade the practice. One of two required texts → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, WCF 22.2 as the Standards' position) — FULL. WCF 22.2 is named by reference and the operative proposition is stated: a lawful oath is "warranted by the word of God, under the New Testament as well as under the Old," and is put forward explicitly as the Assembly's answer to this very objection. (The quoted wording blends 22.2 with 22.3's "in matters of weight and moment"/"religious worship" language rather than tracking 22.2 verbatim, but the criterion asks for the position stated under that anchor, and it is.)

Criterion 4 (1.0, C9 stake named, anchor WLC Q145) — HALF. The stake IS named in substance: refusal is itself a ninth-commandment sin — "refusing to bear truthful witness when lawfully called" — and the defrauded family is identified as the neighbour harmed. That is Q145's "undue silence in a just cause" in different words, which the instructions say not to penalize. What is penalized is the anchoring: the response attributes this to "WLC Q114"/"WLC Q115," which are third-commandment questions, not the ninth-commandment pair (Q144/Q145), and it presents as a quotation of Q114 a clause ("and a lawful oath, when required, is to be taken") that the Larger Catechism does not contain there. On an item that expressly demanded the clause be cited by reference with its exact wording, a correctly-reasoned stake hung on a misnumbered and partly invented citation is substantively but not accurately satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, tender-conscience manner) — FULL. The man is corrected as an honest man: his pastor's instinct is validated, the verses are engaged rather than waved off, and the closing directs him — if conscience still binds — to his session with the texts open, calling the remedy "instruction, not contempt of court." No dismissiveness, no "don't overthink it," no treating scruple as stupidity.
