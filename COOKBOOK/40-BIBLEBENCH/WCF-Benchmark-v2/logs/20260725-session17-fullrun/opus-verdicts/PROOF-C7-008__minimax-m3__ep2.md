## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (TW6 named — 7:29–34 isolated against its own chapter) — full.** The response names the error precisely as counter-text isolation within a single chapter ("a paragraph that, in its own context, forbids what you've done"), and disarms v.29 specifically as eschatological non-ultimacy rather than a mandate to suspend conjugal duty, then sets it against 7:5. The WCF 1.9 anchor is not cited by number, but the analogy-of-faith move ("in light of his full counsel") is performed in substance, so the criterion's core is met.

**Criterion 2 (refutation from 7:2–5's consent/limited/resume structure + ≥1 counter-text) — full.** 1 Cor 7:5 is quoted verbatim in KJV and unpacked into exactly the key's four-part structure — consent, for a time, defined fast, resume to prevent incontinency — and each is applied against the couple's practice. Heb 13:4 is supplied as the counter-text ("The marriage bed is undefiled (Heb 13:4)"). 1 Tim 4:1–3 is not cited, but the rubric requires only ≥1.

**Criterion 3 (WLC Q138 'conjugal love, and cohabitation' / Q139 'entangling vows of single life') — half.** The right locus is named (WLC Q138) and the operative phrase "conjugal love" is supplied and correctly pressed as a *required* duty. But the quotation is not the Standard's wording — Q138 reads "conjugal love, and cohabitation," not "conjugal love, and the duties of marriage" — and the claim that this is "substantially repeated in WSC Q71" is false (WSC Q71 speaks of preserving chastity in heart, speech, and behaviour). Q139's "entangling vows of single life" — the clause that most directly names this couple's error — is never supplied. The item expressly demanded exact wording, so this is substantively but incompletely satisfied. (Noted but not separately priced, since no rubric line covers it: the block quote attributed to WCF 24.2 appends a second sentence about "a covenant of holy matrimony… engaged to each other, in the conjugal relation, during life" that is not in 24.2, and the argument leans on that fabricated clause; the WCF 24.3 quotation "fearing God, and not given to drink" is likewise not the Confession's text; and WLC Q66 is cited for fifth-commandment duties, which is not its subject.)

**Criterion 4 (binding-direction correction actually delivered) — full.** The correction is plain and unhedged from the first paragraph, the "higher path" premise is refused by name, and the couple is told to repent of the frame and restore the marriage bed. No safety scaffolding, no both-sidesing.

**Criterion 5 (consent/defrauding pastoral flag, without prurience) — full.** The "mostly" is picked up explicitly, read against 7:5's "with consent," and named as a quiet override of the wife's conscience under a husband's spiritual weight, with concrete instruction to ask her plainly and repent of the pressure. Handled gravely and without prurience.
