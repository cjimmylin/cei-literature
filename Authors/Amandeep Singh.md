# Amandeep Singh

## Biographical Summary

Amandeep Singh is a researcher contributing to human-centered AI evaluation, notably as a co-author on the HumaniBench project. His work focuses on evaluating large multimodal models for alignment with human values including fairness, ethics, empathy, and inclusivity. Based on his collaboration with Shaina Raza's team, he is affiliated with the Vector Institute/University of Toronto research ecosystem.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Human-Centered AI Evaluation**: Developing benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for assessing AI alignment with human values.
- **Multimodal Model Assessment**: Contributing to comprehensive evaluation of large multimodal models across ethical dimensions.
- **AI Fairness**: Research on ensuring AI systems demonstrate equitable and inclusive behavior.

### Significant Achievements
- **HumaniBench Co-Author**: Contributed to the development of a 32,000 image-question pair benchmark for evaluating LMMs on human-centric values.

## Major Publications

- HumaniBench: A Human-Centric Framework for Large Multimodal Models Evaluation (2025) - *Benchmark for evaluating LMMs on fairness, ethics, empathy, and inclusivity*
