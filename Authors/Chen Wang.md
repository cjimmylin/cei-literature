# Chen Wang

## Biographical Summary

Chen Wang is a researcher at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), associated with the Data Dynamics Lab (D2 Lab) and Professor Lina Yao's research group. His work focuses on the intersection of Generative AI, cybersecurity, and AI safety.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Security**: Investigating stealthy vulnerabilities and memory corruption issues in LLM-powered agents (e.g., "DrunkAgent").
- **AI Safety in Generative Models**: Developing frameworks and evaluations for the safe and robust deployment of large language models.
- **Visual Navigation**: Researching efficient and generalizable environmental understanding for autonomous agents.

### Significant Achievements
- **Strategic AI Safety**: Contributed to comprehensive surveys and critical reviews on AI safety in Generative AI, particularly focusing on alignment and robustness.

## Major Publications
- **DrunkAgent: Stealthy Memory Corruption in LLM-Powered Recommender Agents**: Research connecting LLM logic with cybersecurity vulnerabilities.
- **AI Safety in Generative AI Large Language Models**: A survey on safety risks and mitigation strategies.
