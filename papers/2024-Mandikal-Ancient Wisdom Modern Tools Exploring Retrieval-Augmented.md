## Abstract

> Creates VedantaNY-10M dataset from 612 Advaita Vedanta lectures. Benchmarks RAG vs non-RAG LLMs for long-form QA on Hindu philosophy. RAG significantly outperforms standard model with fewer hallucinations. Outstanding Paper award at ACL 2024 workshop. Directly tests LLM knowledge of Hindu Vedantic philosophy.

## Benchmark

**Name:** VedantaNY-10M

## Dataset Information

10M+ tokens from 612 lectures on Advaita Vedanta philosophy


## Data Description

VedantaNY-10M: a 10M-token dataset of transcribed public discourses on Advaita Vedanta philosophy (>750 hours from Vedanta Society of New York YouTube), curated for RAG and long-form QA evaluation.
