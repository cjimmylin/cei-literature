# Aakanksha Chowdhery

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aakanksha Chowdhery is a Research Leader at Reflection AI and an Adjunct Professor at Stanford University. She was previously a Senior Staff Research Scientist and Technical Lead at Google, where she co-led the development of the 540B PaLM (Pathways Language Model) and was a lead researcher for Gemini. She earned her PhD from Stanford University, where she was awarded the Marconi Young Scholar Award. She is a pioneer in the pre-training, scaling, and fine-tuning of trillion-parameter models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aakanksha’s research currently focuses on agentic large language models and autonomous self-improving agents, specifically for software engineering applications. At Reflection AI, she leads efforts to build agents that can autonomously navigate complex codebases. She also co-teaches "Self-Improving AI Agents" at Stanford.

### Significant Achievements
She was the technical lead for PaLM, the first model to demonstrate the power of the Pathways system for efficient distributed training across thousands of TPU v4 chips. She has been a core contributor to PaLM-E, MedPaLM, and the foundation of the Gemini series. Her work has set new state-of-the-art results across hundreds of language understanding and generation benchmarks.
