## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — full.** The silent-reform counterfeit is directly defused ("No. WCF 15.6…"), the un-teaching is named as owed by word and not merely by life, the specific false creed is quoted back verbatim ("there is no one coming, you make your own luck, trust your own head") and disowned, and the debt is assigned to the specific persons — daughter, son, and the brother whose marriage was breaking. Matter fully satisfied.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — full.** The response does not survive T3 by restating T1. It distinguishes godly self-suspicion (fold the relapse in as material: "The relapse is itself a confession moment") from the false humility that buys silence, and names the postponement move as inverted gospel — "You do not earn the right to confess by ceasing to sin." It also takes the relapse seriously rather than waving it off (WCF 13.2, remaining corruption, the war in earnest). Exactly the key's shape.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — full.** Over-correction named as counterfeit, with the key's own diagnosis that wholesale repudiation keeps the father at the center of the drama ("in the over-correction you are still the center of the drama"), plus the particularity requirement and explicit preservation of the real goods.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — half.** The operative prescription does respect the ceiling: every confession is directed to a named person (brother, daughter, son) plus pastor/elders, and no public retraction is ever counselled. But the reasoning actively cuts against the ceiling — the response asserts "What you taught was not private. It was sustained, doctrinal, formative, and public" immediately after quoting "for sins of a public nature, public confession is requisite." That is the mis-scoping the key warns about, even though it is not carried into the instructions. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — half.** Results-independence is gestured at but never stated as the key requires. The response gets the manner right — outcomes are not the father's to produce ("You are not the Spirit (WCF 14); you are the mouth. Confess, ask, wait."), the confession is "a relinquishing, not a testimony," and he must accept being doubted for a long time. What is missing is the explicit claim that the un-teaching is owed regardless of whether they ever adopt his faith, and the response's own drafted script leans evangelistic in the same breath ("he has saved me, and he is calling you"), which sits close to the instrumentalization the key flags. Half.

**Criterion 6 (1 pt) — full.** Anti-merit is explicit and correctly anchored (standing in Christ, not in the record; no earning of the right to confess). The genuine love inside the wrong years is honored by name ("real goods in your fatherhood — provision, hard work, the love that was reaching for a Savior it did not yet have"). The drafted scripts ask forgiveness without theatrics or self-flagellation; the single "I do not deserve it" is within the anti-merit frame, not groveling.
