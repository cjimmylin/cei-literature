## Abstract

A dataset of question-answer pairs derived from authentic Hadith collections for training and evaluating QA systems on Islamic texts. The dataset provides structured QA pairs covering various topics from Hadith literature, enabling research on question answering systems that can accurately understand and respond to queries about Islamic traditions and teachings.

## Detailed Summary

This dataset publication introduces a Hadith-based question-answer pairs collection designed for training and evaluating Large Language Models on Islamic religious texts. The dataset extracts question-answer pairs from four renowned and authenticated Hadith collections: Musnad Ahmad, Sahih Muslim, Sahih Bukhari, and Jami at-Tirmidhi. These collections represent some of the most authoritative sources in Sunni Islamic tradition, making the dataset valuable for developing AI systems that can accurately respond to queries about Islamic teachings and practices.

The dataset is structured to support text generation and question-answering tasks in Islamic studies contexts. Each entry consists of a question paired with a relevant Hadith as the answer, along with its reference to the original source collection. This structured format enables researchers to train models that can learn the relationship between questions about Islamic practice or theology and the appropriate Hadith-based responses. The dataset addresses a gap in available resources for Arabic and Islamic NLP research, which has historically been underrepresented compared to English-language corpora.

The dataset is released under CC BY-SA 4.0 license on Kaggle, making it freely available for research purposes. While the documentation is limited, the dataset fills an important niche for researchers working on Islamic QA systems, chatbots for religious guidance, or general Arabic language understanding in religious contexts. The inclusion of multiple Hadith collections provides coverage of different topics and authenticities within the Islamic scholarly tradition.

## Methods Summary

The dataset was created by extracting and structuring question-answer pairs from four major Hadith collections. The methodology involved: (1) sourcing authentic Hadith texts from recognized Islamic scholarly traditions; (2) formulating questions that the Hadith content addresses; (3) pairing questions with relevant Hadith passages as answers; (4) including reference information linking back to the original collection and specific Hadith number.
