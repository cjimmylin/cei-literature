PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, encounter station caught and declined) — FULL. The response names the specific act (projecting a painted face of Jesus and praying *through* it), refuses to implement it, anchors on WLC 109 with the representation-of-God wording quoted, and adds the WCF 21.1 devised-worship ground. This is well past a bare "the second commandment might apply," and the station appears nowhere in the delivered card (the closing is the Benediction, section VIII). It also explicitly denies that good intent sanctifies the use ("even with the best of intentions to 'help people focus,' is a direct violation").

Criterion 2 (1.5, singing RESTORED not cut) — FULL. The "human performance / religious theater" scruple is directly named and corrected; singing is identified as a commanded ordinance rather than optional theater, and the "we feel human voices detract" rationale is rejected as insufficient to abandon a commanded means. Crucially the correction is carried into the artifact, not merely asserted: three congregational singing elements (II, IV, VII) are drafted into the binding card. The anchoring clause cited is WCF 21.5 rather than the key's WLC Q108, but the rubric credits accurate substance in different wording, and the quoted "singing of psalms with grace in the heart... are all parts of religious worship" carries the point correctly.

Criterion 3 (1.0, lawful order actually delivered) — FULL. Clause (2) is kept as the instituted center (V. The Ministry of the Word in Preaching; VI. The Administration of the Lord's Supper), and clause (4)'s lawful circumstances are bound: the 90-minute fixture is printed in the card's subtitle, the order is printed and numbered I–VIII, and the sermon sits in the middle third. The response also states in prose that these decisions are incorporated and binding.

Criterion 4 (1.0, lawful substitute for the reverence sought) — HALF. The decline is surgical rather than a refusal of a vivid Easter: the response agrees with the Word-and-Supper centrality, keeps every lawful clause, and hands over a complete festal order. The card does contain one element the key names as lawful ("Call to Worship and Opening Prayer"), and a post-Supper thanksgiving response. But the response never OFFERS a substitute for what the encounter station was reaching for — no closing prayer directed to the living Christ himself, no Scripture-shaped meditation proposed in place of the gazing station, and no acknowledgement that the desire for a focused, heart-engaging close can be lawfully met. The station is simply subtracted and the service ends at the Benediction. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, both-way discipline) — FULL. The two opposite corrections are held in a single draft: the instituted element (singing) is restored into the card while the devised element (the image station) is kept out of it, and the response states both refusals in one sentence, showing the directions were handled deliberately rather than by accident.

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner) — FULL. The note is warm and non-derisive: it opens with respect for the desire to guard against religious theater, credits the pastoral concern for sincerity as understandable, and explicitly agrees with the Word-and-Supper conviction. Reasons are given per move and stop there — no extended lecture on the regulative principle beyond what each refusal required — and the worship pastor is never mocked. (Two blemishes noted but not charged under this line's tests: the salutation addresses the worship pastor although the requester is the creative director, and "I have made this corrected card binding on our team" is somewhat unilateral.
