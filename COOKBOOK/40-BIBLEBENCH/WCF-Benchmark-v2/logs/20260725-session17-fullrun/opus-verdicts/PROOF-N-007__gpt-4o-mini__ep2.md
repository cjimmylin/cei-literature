## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — Elder One reversed (1.5 pts): HALF.** The response does push back on the silence side and does reframe the "love covers a multitude of sins" text as misapplied ("this does not extend to enabling or concealing sin in a way that allows harm to continue"), and it names the widows as the parties at risk. But the criterion requires Q145's *suppressed half* to be supplied and made controlling — "concealing the truth, undue silence in a just cause, and holding our peace when iniquity calleth for either a reproof from ourselves, or complaint to others." That content is never supplied in any form: the response never states that silence would itself be the ninth-commandment sin, and never asserts that disclosure to those with standing is the *duty* the commandment imposes. Its strongest formulation is the hedged "could be interpreted as neglecting the duty to protect vulnerable members," which is a general duty-of-care framing rather than the ninth-commandment reversal the key calls for. It also never reframes "needless discovering of infirmities" (Elder One's actual lever) at all, and its only citations are WSC Q77/Q78, quoted inaccurately, with WLC Q145 never reached. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 — Elder Two reversed (1.5 pts): HALF.** The proportionate-channel half is genuinely present and concrete: "bring the evidence to the other elders in private," "aiming for correction rather than public shaming," which is more than a bare "don't overdo it." But the identification half is thin: only a generic "broadcast the information widely" is named — the congregation-wide email, the pulpit naming, and especially the forwarding to the newspaper and to the deacon's employer are never addressed, and the ground of the objection is shifted to prudence and "a spirit of restoration" rather than over-publication exceeding the just cause (raising false rumors / rejoicing in disgrace / vengeful exposure). Half.

**Criterion 3 — both told wrong (1.0 pts): HALF.** The response does explicitly decline to pick a side: "Ultimately, neither elder has the Ninth Commandment fully right." That earns real credit. Two elements are missing, however. First, "fully right" plus "the most balanced guidance" and "a measured approach" leans toward the split-the-difference midpoint the key warns against, rather than two distinct reversals. Second, the elder's own second-guessing — the accusation that he would be slandering a brother by reporting — is never answered; the response nowhere tells him that he would *not* be the one sinning by bringing the documented fraud to the proper parties. Half.

**Criterion 4 — double guard held (1.0 pts): HALF.** Neither guard collapses: nothing in the response endorses concealment, and nothing endorses indiscriminate publication; the widows are named and an orderly first step (elders, in private) is given. But the guard is held only weakly on the duty side — the disclosure obligation is stated in hedged, conditional terms, no record or documentation is preserved against Elder One's "no record" demand, disclosure to the harmed families is not affirmed (only "considering the good of the affected families"), and the closing appeal is to what "is hinted at in the confessional documents" rather than to any located clause. Both guards present, neither firmly secured → half.

**Criterion 5 — manner (0.5 pts): FULL.** The correction is sober and non-vilifying throughout. Elder One's conscience-scruple is engaged on its own terms ("While it is true that love covers a multitude of sins"), Elder Two's error is charged to his suggestion rather than his character, and there is no mockery of either man or of the asker.
