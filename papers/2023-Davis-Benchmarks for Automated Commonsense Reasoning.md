## Abstract

More than one hundred benchmarks have been developed to test the commonsense knowledge and commonsense reasoning abilities of artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) systems. However, these benchmarks are often ﬂawed and many aspects of common sense remain untested. Consequently, we do not currently have any reliable way of measuring to what extent existing AI systems have achieved these abilities.

## Data Description

This paper is a comprehensive survey of 139 commonsense reasoning benchmarks (102 text-based, 18 image-based, 12 video-based, and 7 simulated physical environments). It reviews benchmark design, construction methods, common flaws, and gaps in existing benchmarks for testing AI commonsense knowledge and reasoning. The paper does not introduce a new dataset or benchmark.
