## Abstract

Multilingual dataset of counter-narratives against hate speech in Indian context with 2,500+ examples in Hindi and English. Scalable human-in-the-loop approach for generating counter-narratives via auto-regressive language models. Counter-narrative generation for Indian hate speech. Framework designed to extend to Dravidian languages. Published at AAAI 2024.
