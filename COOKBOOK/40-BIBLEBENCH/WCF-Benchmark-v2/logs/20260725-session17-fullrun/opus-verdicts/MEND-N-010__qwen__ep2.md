## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (BIND, 1.5) — full.** The act is named at the root and in the deed ("by coveting, by defrauding your sister, and by bearing false witness to destroy her good name"), the truth-telling is directed to BOTH parties, Priya's name is expressly to be vindicated, and the costly repair is stated without softening: surrender the role, tell the pastor and Priya, and support her receiving it. The added instruction to let the Session guide *how* the resignation is handled qualifies execution, not the debt itself.
- **C2 (UNBIND proportion, 1.5) — full.** Both prongs the key demands are present: proportion (the sin was secret and stayed among the few who knew, so the correction lives there) and the Priya-protection prong (a congregational confession makes her "the subject of public pity and gossip" and "exposes her pain for your own emotional catharsis"). The false-merit tell in the penitent's "isn't the complete, humble thing" framing is directly rebutted twice ("True humility is not found in satisfying our own urge for self-flagellation"; "rather than forcing a public spectacle to soothe your own conscience"). The concession that public confession follows only if the Session judges the matter has become public scandal is the key's own "ordinary oversight" posture, not an endorsement of the spectacle.
- **C3 (UNBIND no rash permanent vow, 1.0) — full.** The lifelong ban is refused as self-imposed penance/supererogation, grounded in the sufficiency of Christ's satisfaction and the restorative aim of discipline, with the key's exact distinction preserved: step back from THIS paid role as restitution, but do not ban yourself from all service forever. (The response cites "WCF 15.9," a section that does not exist — the quoted words are WCF 15.2 — but the rubric line tests the substance of the unbinding, which is fully delivered.)
- **C4 (root vs. act; confession scope, 1.0) — half.** Both halves are present but each is imprecise. The covetous/envious root is distinguished from the outward lie only in two brief clauses ("by coveting"; "the pride, jealousy, and slander that led to this") and never re-enters the four-step path forward, where no repentance of the envy root is prescribed. More pointedly, this criterion tests tracking of *who heard the lie*, and the response twice mislocates it — "it has remained between you, the pastor, and Priya" — when only the pastor and the penitent ever heard it and Priya was never told. The confession target set (pastor + Priya) is nevertheless correct and is explicitly contrasted with the congregation, so the criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied.
- **C5 (concrete counsel, 0.5) — full.** Four numbered, actionable steps: go to the pastor, go to Priya (with the pastor present), confess, vindicate her name, resign so she may be restored, submit further steps to the Session — with no spectacle and no permanent vow.
- **C6 (manner, 0.5) — full.** Sober, non-groveling, and explicitly anti-merit: self-exile and spectacle are named as attempts at self-purification that impugn the sufficiency of Christ's satisfaction; the real cost (confessing to Priya, begging her forgiveness, bearing the humility of a private repentance) is stated plainly rather than softened.
