# George Kosteletos

## Biographical Summary

George Kosteletos holds a PhD on the Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence, Mind and Neurosciences from the Department of Philosophy at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA). He is an adjunct lecturer at NKUA and at the Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences of the Open University of Cyprus. He conducts postdoctoral research at NKUA and has been an academic fellow and appointed researcher at the Medical School of Athens, working with the NKUA Applied Philosophy Research Lab and the Laboratory of Psycho-physiology (1st Department of Psychiatry, Eginition Hospital).

## Research & Impact

Kosteletos specializes in Philosophy of AI and Technoethics:

- **Virtue Ethics in AI**: Co-authored "Virtue in the Machine: Beyond a One-size-fits-all Approach and Aristotelian Ethics for Artificial Intelligence" (2025), exploring the application of Aristotelian virtue to AI systems as classified in the EU AI Act. Proposes a virtuous-based 'AI Seal of Excellence' certification process.

- **Moral Machines**: Co-authored "Towards Moral Machines: A Discussion with Michael Anderson and Susan Leigh Anderson" (2021), discussing requirements for autonomous AI systems to demonstrate ethical behavior guided by moral values.

- **Autonomous Weapons Ethics**: Co-authored "Licensed to Kill: Autonomous Weapons as Persons and Moral Agents," critically examining attribution of moral status to AI systems using Lethal Autonomous Systems as a case study.

He is a member of the Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence of the National Commission on Bioethics and Technoethics (Greece).
