## Background

Aníbal M. Astobiza graduated in Philosophy from the University of Deusto, earned a Master in Social Psychology and Doctor in Cognitive Sciences and Humanities from the University of the Basque Country/Euskal Herriko Unibertsitatea. He has conducted postdoctoral research at the IFS-CSIC, the Oxford Uehiro Centre for Practical Ethics at Oxford University, and the Center for Bioethics at Harvard University.

## Recent Research

His recent work focuses on AI ethics in healthcare contexts, including a systematic review on trust and AI in healthcare examining both philosophical and practical issues (Monash Bioethics Review, 2025), and research on applying large language models to analyze linguistic colexification and ambiguity within bioethical scenarios (Journal of Bioethics Inquiry, 2025).

He has also published on care robots and assistive robotics, examining to what extent technology can satisfy people's personal needs in the field of care (Springer's "Ethics of Artificial Intelligence," 2023). His work explores the intersection of AI technology, ethics, and human values across multiple domains including healthcare, caregiving, and neurotechnology.
