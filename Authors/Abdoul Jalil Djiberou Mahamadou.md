# Abdoul Jalil Djiberou Mahamadou

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abdoul Jalil Djiberou Mahamadou is a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Stanford Centre for Biomedical Ethics. He is part of the Stanford-GSK.ai fellowship, where his work focuses on the ethical, legal, and social implications (ELSI) of AI in healthcare and drug discovery. He previously held postdoctoral roles at Simon Fraser University and Clermont Auvergne University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdoul's research investigates the ethics of AI in healthcare settings, specifically focusing on algorithmic bias, fairness, and the inclusion of diverse stakeholder perspectives in risk assessments. He works towards developing explainable and just AI systems for global health applications.
