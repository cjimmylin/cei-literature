## Abstract

Benchmark focusing on scientific QA and truthfulness in 6 African languages via human translation. Uhura-TruthfulQA tests truthfulness on health, law, finance, and politics topics. LLMs are more prone to generating false claims in low-resource African languages. Truthfulness evaluation (TruthfulQA adaptation) directly relevant to AI safety and values alignment. Shows LLMs generate more false claims in African languages. Safety benchmark for low-resource languages.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Uhura (Uhura-ARC-Easy, Uhura-TruthfulQA)
