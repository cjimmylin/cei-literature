# Alham Fikri Aji

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alham Fikri Aji is an Assistant Professor of Natural Language Processing at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) and an adjunct faculty at Monash Indonesia. He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Edinburgh. He is a key leader in the development of NLP resources for Southeast Asian languages, co-founding the IndoNLP community.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alham’s research focuses on making NLP efficient and accessible. He works on model compression, distillation, and the creation of large language models for underrepresented languages (particularly Indonesian and Malay). He investigates how to maintain high performance while reducing the computational cost of deep learning.

### Significant Achievements
He has created benchmarks like **Global PIQA** and **SEA-SafeguardBench** to assess cultural understanding and safety in LLMs across diverse languages. His work on efficient machine translation has been widely cited, and he serves on the advisory board of SIGSEA.
