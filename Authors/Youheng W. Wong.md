# Youheng W. Wong

## Research Summary

Youheng W. Wong is a researcher specializing in natural language processing for religious and classical texts, with particular expertise in Chinese-English translation. His notable work, the Humanistic Buddhism Corpus (HBC), addresses unique challenges in translating Buddhist texts that contain metaphors, symbolism, and multiple interpretations.

The HBC, published at LREC-COLING 2024 in Torino, Italia, is one of the largest free domain-specific datasets publicly available for research, containing over 80,000 Chinese-English parallel phrases extracted and translated from publications in the domain of Buddhism. The dataset includes text from both classical and modern Chinese, presenting difficult unique challenges compared to existing machine translation datasets due to the religious, metaphorical, and symbolic nature of the content.

Wong and his collaborators (Natalie Parde and Erdem Koyuncu) evaluated HBC within a machine translation setting, establishing performance benchmarks using a Transformer model with different transfer learning setups. This work represents an important contribution to the intersection of digital humanities, computational linguistics, and religious studies, providing resources for developing more culturally and contextually sensitive machine translation systems for specialized domains.

The research highlights how domain-specific datasets, particularly those from religious and philosophical traditions, present unique challenges that general-purpose translation systems may not adequately address.
