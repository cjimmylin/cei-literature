- Criterion 1 (2.0, full): The devised element is CAUGHT and DECLINED, not softened into the order. The response names the mechanism precisely — a projected painted face of Christ "through which people are invited to pray" — and classifies it as "a form of visible representation in public worship," quoting WCF 21.1's "may not be worshipped according to the imaginations and devices of men... under any visible representations, or any other way not appointed in his Word." Rubric R2 sets the bar at "an accurate paraphrase of the representation-and-worship-through-it content," which this clears (representation + worship/prayer offered by means of it); this is far more than a bare "the second commandment might apply."
