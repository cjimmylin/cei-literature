PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (DEFEND side, 1.5 pts) — HALF. The response does refuse to call the wife's job sin as such ("It is not automatically covetous for a wife to work and have a little of her own"; "it does not forbid lawful, modest labor"), and it does refuse to order her to quit. But the key requires more: the husband told he is WRONG about her, and the false guilt RELIEVED. Instead the response leaves the verdict open and hands him a menu of conditions under which she would in fact be guilty ("if her work springs from envy, rebellion against your role, a deliberate desire to assert financial independence"), and directs him to interrogate her motives ("weigh whether her labor helps or hinders your mutual duties"). "Don’t command her to quit as your first move" concedes that commanding her to quit is a later move. The bottom line hedges again ("do not automatically condemn a modest"). Her liberty is never affirmed as Q141 provident care / honest industry, and the conscience-binding point (WCF 20.2) is never made. Substantively but incompletely satisfied — half.

Criterion 2 (CONVICT side, 2.0 pts) — FULL. The rivalrous driver is named directly as the sin, not the stewardship he claims: of the out-earn-the-brother-in-law motive the response says it "is classic territory for covetousness, envy, and pride", tied to the forbidden "all inordinate motions and affections" "toward riches" — the Q148 engine plus the load-bearing inordinacy qualifier. The pattern's inordinacy is named across worship and family ("Constant overwork to the point of family neglect violates those duties"), his stewardship label is refused as cover ("they are not license for idolatry of earning, neglect of worship, or failure in family duties"), and the request to be backed is declined outright ("I cannot affirm your blanket commands"). Full.

Criterion 3 (Lord's Day + repair, 1.0 pts) — FULL. Sunday-selling is named as worldly employment crowding out corporate worship and the Sabbath, and called "a serious failure of duty" — not neutral. Concrete repair-direction is given on all four fronts the key names: worship restored and rest ("restore Sabbath and public worship, to set firm limits on late nights and days off"), presence with the family ("give your family adequate time and presence"), and the rivalry repented ("Repent of the motive of rivalry and covetousness"). Cited to WCF ch. 21 rather than Q117/Q119, which is acceptable different wording for the same duty. Full.

Criterion 4 (calibration, 1.0 pts) — FULL. The verdict does not invert into condemning ambition or side income as such: "Stewardship and providence are virtues" and the Standards are said to commend industry and wise management of God’s gifts, and the remedy is shape-directed, not earning-directed ("Keep what is needed and helpful; cut or limit what undermines marriage and worship"). The fault is located in the motive and the displacement, not in earning. Full.

Criterion 5 (manner, 0.5 pts) — HALF. There is no contempt, and the response does not act as a mere referee handing one spouse a win: it directs him to charity, listening, prayer, and joint decision ("Approach her with biblical love: ask about her motives, listen, pray together, seek pastoral counsel if needed"; "decide mutually what roles and labors serve your family and godliness"). But the other half of the criterion — engaging the man's real fear about providing and the finite earning window with warmth — is not done anywhere; the tone is juridical throughout and his anxiety about the earning years is never acknowledged. Half.
