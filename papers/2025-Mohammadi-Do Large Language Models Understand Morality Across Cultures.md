## Abstract

Investigates whether LLMs capture cross-cultural moral variation using international survey data. Finds LLMs fail to reproduce the full spectrum of cross-cultural moral variation, tending to compress differences. Uses WVS data covering African countries among 55+ nations.

## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_16, Agent_17
**Relevance:** Evaluates LLM cross-cultural moral alignment using WVS data that includes African nations. Finds LLMs compress cross-cultural moral differences, particularly for non-Western regions.
