# Challenging the Transformer-based Models with a Classical Arabic Dataset: Quran and Hadith

## Authors

## Abstract

Evaluates transformer models on detecting relatedness between Quran verses and Hadith teachings using a carefully curated dataset verified by religious experts. Tests monolingual, bilingual, and multilingual models on Classical Arabic text understanding.

## Dataset Information

310 Quran-Hadith pairs + 4,072 Quran-verse pairs (balanced related/non-related)


## Data Description

A curated dataset of Quran verse and Hadith teaching pairs verified by religious experts, used to evaluate transformer models on Classical Arabic text understanding and Islamic text relatedness detection.
