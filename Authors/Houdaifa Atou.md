# Houdaifa Atou

## Research Summary

Houdaifa Atou is a researcher focused on Arabic Natural Language Processing and large language models. He co-authored "Addax at WojoodNER 2024: Attention-Based Dual-Channel Neural Network for Arabic Named Entity Recognition" (ArabicNLP 2024), which presented a BERT-based NER system using a two-channel parallel hybrid neural network with attention, achieving second place (90.13% micro-F1) in the competition. He is a co-author on the PALM project, a culturally inclusive and linguistically diverse dataset for Arabic LLMs built by 44 researchers across the Arab world. He also contributed to "GemMaroc: Unlocking Darija Proficiency in LLMs with Minimal Data," addressing how open-source LLMs marginalize Moroccan Arabic, and "NileChat: Towards linguistically diverse and culturally aware LLMs for local communities" (EMNLP 2025).
