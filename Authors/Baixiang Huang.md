# Baixiang Huang

## Biographical Summary

Baixiang Huang is a CS PhD student at Emory University, mentored by Dr. Kai Shu. Her research focuses on improving the factuality, safety, and robustness of foundation models, with a specific interest in model editing techniques.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her primary research area is model editing, which allows for precise modifications to LLMs. She investigates how this can be used to steer agent behavior toward beneficence or, conversely, how it might be used to inject harm.

### Significant Achievements
She has published research on the "double-edged sword" of model editing, highlighting the security and ethical implications of being able to modify LLM behaviors at runtime.

## Major Publications

- [Model Editing as a Double-Edged Sword: Steering Agent Ethical Behavior Toward Beneficence or Harm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12942) (2023) - *Key study on the ethical control and vulnerability of AI agents.*
- [Can Editing LLMs Inject Harm?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12942) (2023) - *Investigation into the safety risks of model manipulation.*
