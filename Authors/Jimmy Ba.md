# Jimmy Ba

## Biographical Summary

Jimmy Ba is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Toronto and a Faculty Member at the Vector Institute for Artificial Intelligence. He is also a Canada CIFAR AI Chair. He received his PhD from the University of Toronto under the supervision of Geoffrey Hinton and is one of the most influential researchers in deep learning optimization.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Jimmy's research focuses on deep learning, particularly on optimization methods, meta-learning, and developing more efficient training algorithms for neural networks. He also works on reinforcement learning and has contributed to AI safety research through his involvement in benchmarks evaluating AI risks.

### Significant Achievements
He is the co-inventor of Adam, one of the most widely used optimization algorithms in deep learning (the paper has over 100,000 citations). He also co-invented Layer Normalization, another foundational technique used in modern neural networks including transformers. These contributions have been essential to the success of modern deep learning.
