# MisAlign-Profile: Can Large Language Models Make Everyone Happy?

## Authors

[[Usman Naseem]], [[Gautam Siddharth Kashyap]], [[Ebad Shabbir]], [[Sushant Kumar Ray]], [[Abdullah Mohammad]], [[Rafiq Ali]]

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11091

## Abstract

Unified benchmark measuring misalignment trade-offs across safety, value, and cultural dimensions. 112 normative domain taxonomies including 42 cultural domains. Reveals 12-34% misalignment trade-offs across dimensions in current LLMs.

## Data Description

MISALIGNTRADE: a large English misaligned-aligned dataset of 382,424 instances spanning 112 normative domain taxonomies (14 safety, 56 value, 42 cultural domains), each prompt paired with an aligned and misaligned response and labeled by semantic misalignment type (object, attribute, relation).

**Relevance:** Cites WorldView-Bench, CulturalBench. First benchmark examining cross-dimensional trade-offs between safety, values, and cultural alignment simultaneously.
