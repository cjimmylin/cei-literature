## Abstract

European Portuguese decoder LLM pre-trained on 35B tokens. Introduces CALAME-PT, the first Portuguese zero-shot language-modeling benchmark inspired by LAMBADA.

## Data Description

CALAME-PT: the first zero-shot Portuguese language modeling benchmark inspired by LAMBADA, used to evaluate GlórIA and other Portuguese/multilingual decoder models (Gervásio-PTPT, mGPT) on next-word prediction.
