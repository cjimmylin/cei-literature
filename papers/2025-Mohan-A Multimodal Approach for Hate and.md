## Abstract

Multimodal dataset comprising videos with audio and textual transcripts annotated for 4 hate speech categories: offensive, sexist, racist, and casteist. First multimodal hate speech dataset for Tamil. Multimodal Tamil hate speech dataset with culturally specific categories including casteist content. Addresses values-relevant hate speech detection.
