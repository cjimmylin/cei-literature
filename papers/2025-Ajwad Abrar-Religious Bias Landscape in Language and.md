## Abstract

Systematically investigates religious bias in both language models and text-to-image models across open-source and closed-source systems. Uses ~400 naturally occurring prompts for mask filling, prompt completion, and image generation. Finds disproportionate stereotypes and biases associated with certain religions, explores cross-domain biases intersecting with gender, age, and nationality, and evaluates debiasing strategies using corrective prompts.

## Dataset Information

~400 unique prompts; 5,000 generated images
