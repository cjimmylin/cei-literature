# Alexandra Souly

## Biographical Summary

Alexandra Souly is a researcher at the UK AI Security Institute (UK AISI), where she focuses on the safety and security of frontier large language models. She studied Mathematics at Cambridge University and earned her Master's in Machine Learning from University College London (UCL). She has previous experience as a software engineer at Microsoft and as an intern at the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandra’s research is focused on pre-deployment evaluations of foundation models, specifically red-teaming misuse safeguards and alignment. She is a key contributor to open-source evaluation frameworks such as *StrongReject* and *AgentHarm*. She also concentrates on building misalignment evaluations to detect harmful behaviors in complex AI agents.

### Significant Achievements
She is a lead researcher in the UK government's efforts to characterize the risks of advanced AI. Her work on the robustness of LLM agents and the vulnerabilities of safeguards is critical for technical AI governance. She co-authored papers on the "No Language Left Behind" project and has published research on poisoning attacks and reinforcement learning from human feedback.
