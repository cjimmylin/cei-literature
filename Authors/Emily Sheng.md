# Emily Sheng

## Biographical Summary

Emily Sheng is a Research Scientist at Microsoft Research, specializing in fairness and bias in natural language processing systems. She completed her PhD at USC, where she developed foundational work on measuring and mitigating biases in language generation. Her research has been highly influential in establishing methods and benchmarks for evaluating social biases in LLMs, contributing to more equitable AI systems.

## Research Interests

- **Fairness in NLP**: Developing methods to measure and improve fairness in language systems
- **Bias in Language Models**: Identifying and mitigating social biases in text generation
- **AI Ethics**: Understanding the ethical implications of language technologies
- **Social Impact Assessment**: Evaluating how AI systems affect different communities

## Major Publications

- "The Woman Worked as a Babysitter: On Biases in Language Generation" (2019) - Foundational work on bias in text generation
- "Societal Biases in Language Generation: Progress and Challenges" (2021) - Survey of bias research
- "FairPrism: Evaluating Fairness-Related Harms in Text Generation" (2023) - Comprehensive fairness evaluation framework
