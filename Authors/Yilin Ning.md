# Yilin Ning

## Research Summary

Yilin Ning is a Senior Research Fellow at the Centre for Quantitative Medicine, Duke-NUS Medical School. Her research focuses on fair and ethical AI, biostatistics, epidemiology, and statistical programming. She has been involved in recent publications including a January 2026 publication in Nature Health on large language models in global health, demonstrating her work at the intersection of AI and healthcare.
