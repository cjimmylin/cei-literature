# Christopher Michael Rytting

## Biographical Summary
Christopher Michael Rytting is a researcher focused on understanding the extent to which large language models capture human values, behaviors, and attitudes. His dissertation work demonstrated that LLMs exhibit "algorithmic fidelity" - modeling ideas and behaviors of the populations whose data they were trained on. He currently works at the Laude Institute in San Francisco, studying how AI/language models help or hurt human skill-learning.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Algorithmic Fidelity**: Investigating whether LLMs can channel real humans, take surveys like them, and serve as proxies for human research subjects
- **AI for Democratic Discourse**: Developing chat interventions to improve online political conversations at scale
- **Pluralistic Alignment**: Exploring how to align AI systems with diverse human values

### Significant Achievements
- Published in PNAS (2023) on leveraging AI for democratic discourse
- Demonstrated empirically that LLMs can serve as effective proxies for humans across multiple social science domains
- Contributed to foundational work on pluralistic alignment methodologies
