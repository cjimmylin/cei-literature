# Elizabeth Clark

## Biographical Summary

Elizabeth Clark is a Research Scientist at Google Research, previously completing her PhD at the University of Washington. Her research focuses on natural language generation, particularly creative applications like storytelling and the evaluation of generated text. She has made important contributions to understanding how humans evaluate machine-generated text and the challenges of distinguishing human from AI writing.

## Research Interests

- Natural language generation evaluation
- Human evaluation methodology for NLP
- Creative AI and automated storytelling
- Human-AI collaborative writing
- Distinguishing human vs machine-generated text

## Major Publications

- "All That's 'Human' Is Not Gold: Evaluating Human Evaluation of Generated Text" (ACL 2021)
- "Creative Writing with a Machine in the Loop: Case Studies on Slogans and Stories" (2018)
- Research on story generation and evaluation
- Work on human-AI collaborative writing tools
