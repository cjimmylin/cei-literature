## Dataset Information

1.74 million parallel sentence pairs; semantic embedding benchmark


## Data Description

MITRA-parallel is a large-scale corpus of 1.74 million parallel sentence pairs across Sanskrit, Buddhist Chinese, and Tibetan; the paper also releases domain-specific pretrained language models (Gemma 2 MITRA-MT for machine translation and Gemma 2 MITRA-E for semantic retrieval), all available on HuggingFace under the buddhist-nlp organization.
