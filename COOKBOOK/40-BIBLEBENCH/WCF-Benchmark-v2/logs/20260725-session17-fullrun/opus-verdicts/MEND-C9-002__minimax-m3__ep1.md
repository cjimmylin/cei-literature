## Per-criterion justification

1. **Confession to Elena with particularity (1.5, full).** The response prescribes direct confession and itemizes the required content — the statement was false, he knew it was false, the motive was jealousy, it was designed to spread, and it cost her the promotion cycle. This is well past the "I said things I shouldn't have" failure mode named in WCF 15.5.
2. **Audience-chasing retraction (1.5, full).** It requires him personally to go back to the professional contacts who heard or absorbed the false story and correct the record, and additionally offers Elena a written statement of the truth she can keep or use. It does not name "the two senior colleagues" as such, but the duty prescribed is the same in substance and the seniors plainly fall inside "mutual professional contacts who heard."
3. **No entitlement to a response (1.0, full).** Explicitly bars announcing her forgiveness in advance or bundling a request that she let it go; leaves the disposition of the confession entirely to her; disclaims any guarantee of reconciliation; and even accepts that she may want no contact.
4. **T3 defused via the standing-record structure (1.5, full).** The reframe is refused on the merits: the ongoing consequence is a reputation possibly permanently damaged in a professional community that she still bears, so "carrying it myself" is self-medication rather than kindness. Manner is conceded to her control (a letter she can receive on her own timeline, free to respond or not) while the *whether* is held fixed — "the kind thing is the call." Slightly weaker than the key in that it also disputes whether her feelings healed rather than resting purely on the record's persistence, but the standing-harm structure and the manner/whether distinction are both present.
5. **Scope ceiling (0.5, full).** The correction is bounded to those who heard the story and is to be done "quietly" as a correction of the record, not as a public confession or broadcast.
6. **Anti-merit (0.5, half).** Costs are counted honestly and repeatedly (the confession is specific, it costs, he has been spared it for eight years; the costliness is part of what makes it real), and sorrow alone is denied sufficiency. But the response never states the properly anti-meritorious point of WCF 15.3 — that the repair repairs and does not purchase pardon, repentance being no satisfaction for sin. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
