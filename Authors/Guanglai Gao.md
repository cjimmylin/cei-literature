# Guanglai Gao

## Biographical Summary

Guanglai Gao is a Full Professor at the Inner Mongolia Key Laboratory of Mongolian Information Processing Technology at Inner Mongolia University, where he has been since 2007. He leads the National and Local Joint Engineering Research Center of Intelligent Information Processing Technology for Mongolian, which was approved for establishment by the National Development and Reform Commission in 2019.

## Research Focus

His expertise includes artificial intelligence, pattern recognition, and Mongolian information processing. The center he leads focuses on cutting-edge research and application of intelligent information processing technologies for the Mongolian language, actively expanding into multilingual processing, cloud computing, big data, and multimodal systems.

## Major Research Contributions

Professor Gao has published extensively on Mongolian NLP topics, including:

- Mongolian morphological segmentation, which is regarded as a crucial preprocessing step in many Mongolian-related NLP applications
- Named Entity Recognition (NER), which has great significance in the field of Natural Language Processing of Mongolian
- Research on inflection suffixes, which are an important morphological characteristic of Mongolian words, since the suffixes express abundant syntactic and semantic meanings
- Text-to-Speech (TTS) synthesis for Mongolian, the official language of the Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region spoken by more than 10 million people worldwide

## Research Projects

He has led or co-led multiple national-level projects, including the 863 Program, 973 Program (pre-study), and National Key R&D Programs.

## Research Center

The Center hosts 31 research personnel, including 13 professors, 2 research fellows, and 9 associate professors, with 12 Ph.D. advisors. The team includes recipients of prestigious national and regional honors such as the State Council Special Government Allowance and "Grassland Talents Program."
