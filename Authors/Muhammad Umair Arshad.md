# Muhammad Umair Arshad

## Biographical Summary

Muhammad Umair Arshad is a researcher whose work focuses on hate speech detection and the interpretability of AI models used for content moderation. His research emphasizes the importance of transparency and explainability in systems designed to identify harmful content online.

## Research Focus

Muhammad's research addresses a critical gap in hate speech detection: the need for models that are not only accurate but also interpretable. His work on the HateInsights dataset demonstrates that high-performing classifiers often fail on explainability metrics, arguing that effective content moderation requires transparent reasoning capabilities.

## Key Contributions

- Created the HateInsights dataset with dual annotations (class labels + rationales)
- Research demonstrating the gap between classification accuracy and explainability
- Analysis of model plausibility and faithfulness in hate speech detection
