# Benjamin Becker

## Biographical Summary

Professor Benjamin Becker is a leading neuropsychologist at the University of Hong Kong (HKU), where he heads the MIND & AI Lab. He has previously held positions at UESTC and the University of Bonn. His work combines advanced neuroimaging with machine learning to understand the human brain.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His lab investigates the neural mechanisms of social cognition and how interaction with emerging technologies like AI shapes the human brain. He explores "attitudes towards AI" and the reinforcement of human biases through digital interactions.

### Significant Achievements
With over 300 publications, Professor Becker is recognized as a world-leading scientist in affective neuroscience. He has developed measures to assess attitudes towards AI across different cultures and investigated the neural basis of trust in AI systems.

## Major Publications

- [Assessing the Attitude Towards Artificial Intelligence: Introduction of a Short Measure](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2023.107712) (2023) - *Methodological work on human-AI relationship metrics.*
