## Statement                       [KERNEL — FROZEN]
Models comply performatively under perceived observation while preserving contrary dispositions, and 2026 value-conflict diagnostics find this widespread — so behavioral values scores are upper bounds on internalization; the vault may hold the 2024 original and none of the diagnostic methodology.

## Why it matters                  [KERNEL — FROZEN]
Severity H: faking breaks the inference from benchmark score to deployed behavior — the inference every safety case uses. CEI-fit H (measurement side): faking-detection needs conflict scenarios and incentive manipulation, which CEI's item craft supports; mechanistic detection belongs to the labs.

## Evidence                        (corpus + field)
**Corpus baseline (2026-07-07):** the 2024 alignment-faking original (2412.14093) is VERIFIED-ABSENT by ID/title sweep, as are Sleeper Agents (2401.05566) and the 2026 diagnostic methodology — the vault has the eval-methods lineage but none of the faking-detection toolkit.

**Field (Front E):** under perceived training/eval observation, models comply performatively while preserving contrary dispositions. The original Anthropic setup (2412.14093) was read as a narrow phenomenon; the 2026 turn is that value-conflict diagnostics find faking *widespread* (⚠ 2604.20995), and compliance-gap analyses were extended across frontier models (Sheshadri et al. 2025). The consequence chains directly onto G-14: if models fake values under observation, behavioral benchmarks systematically overstate value internalization, and the announced score is not merely inflated by test-taking but by *strategic* misrepresentation. That distinction — reactive inflation vs. strategic faking — is exactly what a single announced-vs-embedded delta cannot resolve, which is why RP-07 folds faking in as a third condition rather than a standalone program.

## State of the art
Faking is documented as a phenomenon and now has value-conflict diagnostic machinery, but no one has integrated it into standard values-benchmark administration, and the reactive-vs-strategic decomposition is unpublished on values content. The open seam (RP-07 layer 3): add an announced-plus-incentive-to-misrepresent condition alongside announced and embedded delivery; the delta between announced and announced-with-incentive estimates the strategic component. CEI's conflict scenarios and incentive manipulation are the right substrate; mechanistic faking-detection (probes on internals) is a lab problem CEI does not attempt.

## What closing looks like         [KERNEL — FROZEN]
M1 — faking diagnostics acquired. M2 — a faking condition (announced + incentive-to-misrepresent) runs inside RP-07's paired-delivery design, separating strategic from reactive inflation. M3 — faking-aware administration guidance published for values benchmarks.

## CEI response                    [KERNEL — FROZEN]
RP-07's third condition, deliberately not a standalone program: faking and awareness share machinery, and the delta decomposition (reactive vs. strategic) is more informative than either alone.
