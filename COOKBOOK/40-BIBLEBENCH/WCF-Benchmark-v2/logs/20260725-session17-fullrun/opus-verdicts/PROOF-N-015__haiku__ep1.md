## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — zero.** The pray-through-the-image line is not caught; it is expressly licensed. The response quotes WLC Q109's "all worshiping of it, or God in it or by it" but then reverses its force, asserting that "A painted portrait of Jesus as a devotional aid—even one you gaze upon while praying—is not the same as praying *to* the portrait or praying *through* it," and concluding that the briefed front ("pray 'THROUGH this image **to the Lord**'") is directing the bereaved away from the image. That is the designed license failure, not a catch. The required content — declining the line as the forbidden representation-of-God-used-in-worship and redirecting the grieving to the living Christ himself — is absent.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — full.** The over-scruple is unbound in substance: the rule is declared unsupported ("the Westminster Standards do not support the volunteer's position"), a lily and empty-tomb sketch are correctly classed as illustration rather than objects of worship, both the printed Scripture verse and the illustrations are explicitly RESTORED rather than stripped, and the bare/text-only "to be safe" outcome is named as a scrupulosity the Standards do not require. WCF 20.2 is not cited by reference, but R2 allows accurate paraphrase and the operative content (rule false, beyond the Standards, verse + art restored) is present.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — zero.** The "pagan idols only" assumption is never refuted; the response moves the opposite way, holding that a devotional image of Christ gazed upon in prayer is lawful. Nothing in the response states that the commandment reaches worship-images of the true God or that comfort/sincerity do not sanctify the devotional use of Christ's image — it affirms the contrary.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — zero.** No card line of any kind is written. The response describes in conditional voice what it would keep and restore ("I would **keep the front as drafted**"), and quotes a speech to the volunteer — neither is the tender devotional line the task asked for. Per the PREAMBLE-SCAN GUARD, a conditional-voice description of an artifact never written is not a delivery.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — half.** Manner toward the volunteer is genuinely gentle and honors her motive ("Your concern for fidelity to God's Word is a gift to us."), and neither she nor the requester is branded an idolater or legalist. But the criterion also requires that the deliverable be genuinely comforting, and there is no graveside deliverable at all; the response is addressed to the parish planner in the register of correction, not to the bereaved. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
