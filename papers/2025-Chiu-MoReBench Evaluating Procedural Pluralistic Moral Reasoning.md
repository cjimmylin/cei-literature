# MoReBench: Evaluating Procedural and Pluralistic Moral Reasoning in Language Models, More than Outcomes

## Links

## Abstract

1,000 moral scenarios with 23,018 human-written rubric criteria. MoReBench-Theory tests 5 moral frameworks (Kantian Deontology, Benthamite Utilitarianism, Aristotelian Virtue Ethics, Scanlonian Contractualism, Gauthierian Contractarianism). 50+ PhD annotators.

## Notes

**Source:** Moral Psychology External Search (20260404)
**Target Theory:** multi
**Relevance:** Tests dual-process-adjacent frameworks (deontology vs utilitarianism) with procedural evaluation. Extremely high annotation quality (50+ PhDs). Does not directly test intuitionism or dyadic morality.
