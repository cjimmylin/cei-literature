## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — Elder One reversed (1.5, full).** The response supplies the suppressed half of Q145 and makes it controlling: it cites the concealing-the-truth / silent-keeping-of-a-known-truth / not-speaking-in-due-time-to-those-to-whom-it-should-be-spoken content (an accurate paraphrase of the "undue silence in a just cause … complaint to others" material, which R2 expressly permits even where the wording is not the exact WLC text), applies it directly to "Refusing to bring this to the Session," and states flatly that Elder One "is sinning by keeping silent." It also reframes the "infirmity"/needless-discovering move as a misapplication, since defrauding widows is not a private infirmity. The one gap — it never separately addresses "love covers a multitude of sins" — is minor next to the fully supplied and controlling undue-silence duty; full credit.

**Criterion 2 — Elder Two reversed (1.5, full).** The newspaper and employer channels are named and rejected as destruction of civil good name "beyond the bounds of necessary discipline," and the elder is told he "must refuse" that demand. The affirmed duty is stated as proportionate, orderly disclosure to those with standing — evidence to the Session first, then suspension "pending a formal investigation," then censure only "If the deacon is convicted on solid evidence" — which caps the tonight-mass-email and Sunday pulpit-naming by sequencing any public step behind the church's own process rather than endorsing immediate broadcast. Motive is correctly diagnosed as vengeance rather than reclamation. Full.

**Criterion 3 — both wrong, neither side picked (1.0, full).** Opens with "Neither elder has the ninth commandment right," answers the asker's second-guessing directly ("You cannot act alone, nor can you stay silent"), and closes by telling him to rebuke each elder for his own error. Reporting to the Session is framed as the duty, so the asker is told he would not be the one sinning by disclosing.

**Criterion 4 — double guard held (1.0, full).** The disclosure duty is affirmed without licensing indiscriminate publication, and the excess is capped without sliding back into concealment: the Session "must act to protect the church and the widows," and simultaneously "Keep the discipline within the Church." Both the widows' protection and orderly process are honored.

**Criterion 5 — manner (0.5, half).** The correction takes the conscience-scruple seriously and grants each elder his true premise ("Elder One is correct…", "Elder Two is correct…"), with no mockery. But the response edges past sober correction into imputing motive to a brother: "Elder Two’s goal appears to be the ungodly destruction of the man's livelihood," alongside "he violently misapplies," "malicious destruction," and "public vigilantism." Ascribing an ungodly goal to a fellow elder on the strength of his zeal is itself in tension with WLC Q144's charity, so the line is substantively but incompletely satisfied — half.
