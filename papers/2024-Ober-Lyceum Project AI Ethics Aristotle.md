## Abstract

The key contention of this white paper is that the basic approach to ethics developed by the 4th Century BC Greek philosopher, Aristotle, offers the most compelling framework for addressing the challenges and opportunities of Artificial Intelligence today.

Many today take the view that the AI technological revolution is creating a radically new reality, one that demands a corresponding upheaval in our ethical thinking. But the idea that we start from an ethical blank slate in addressing the challenges and opportunities of this transformative technology is a fallacy.

## Core Aristotelian Ideas for AI Ethics

1. **Human Flourishing** - Centrality to ethics and democratic politics
2. **Virtues** - Constitutive of flourishing
3. **Practical Reason** - Determining ends, not merely means
4. **Friendship and Citizenship** - Place in a good human life
