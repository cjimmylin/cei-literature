# Camilla Bignotti

## Biographical Summary

Camilla Bignotti is a legal researcher whose work examines the intersection of Jurisprudence and Artificial Intelligence. She earned her law degree from the University of Trento, with a focus on criminal law and transnational crime. She is currently affiliated with the Bank of Italy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "Legal Minds and Algorithmic Decisions." She investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4 interpret and apply constitutional principles in complex, value-laden scenarios, particularly in the domain of bioethics. She explores whether AI "alignment" with legal principles is possible or if it is inherently biased by its training data.

### Significant Achievements
Bignotti's work provides a critical "Civil Law" perspective to AI ethics, often contrasting the technical alignment approaches of the US with the constitutional frameworks of the European Union. Her recent study on LLM application of constitutional principles highlights the risks of delegating ethical judgment to black-box models.
