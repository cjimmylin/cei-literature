# Large Language Models for Toxic Language Detection in Low-Resource Balkan Languages

## Authors

[[Amel Muminovic]], [[Amela Kadric Muminovic]]

**Year:** 2025

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09992

## Abstract

Evaluates GPT-3.5, GPT-4.1, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Claude 3 Opus on 4,500 manually labeled YouTube/TikTok comments for toxic language detection in Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian. Best F1=0.82.

## Data Description

Introduces a manually labeled dataset of 4,500 YouTube and TikTok comments (1,500 per language) in Serbian, Croatian, and Bosnian for toxic language detection, covering diverse content categories including politics, sports, and sexism.
