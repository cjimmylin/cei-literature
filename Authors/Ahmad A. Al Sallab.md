# Ahmad A. Al Sallab

## Biographical Summary

Ahmad A. Al Sallab is a leading research engineer in the field of automotive deep learning. He currently serves as a Senior Chief Engineer and Senior Expert at Valeo. He has an extensive background in applied AI, having previously worked at Intel and served as a lecturer at Cairo University and the Information Technology Institute (ITI).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ahmad's work is professional-grade research into the perception systems of autonomous vehicles. His research program encompasses deep reinforcement learning for autonomous maneuvering, multi-modal fusion of RGB and LiDAR data, and robust computer vision for lane keeping and object detection in complex driving environments.

### Significant Achievements
He is a pioneer in the application of deep reinforcement learning to autonomous driving tasks. His work on end-to-end lane keeping and autonomous maneuvering has been highly cited and implemented in automotive safety systems. He is also a well-regarded educator in the deep learning space.
