# Carolin Holtermann

## Biographical Summary
Carolin Holtermann is a research assistant and doctoral candidate at the University of Hamburg Business School, working with Prof. Dr. Anne Lauscher. She completed her Master's degree in Data Science at the University of Mannheim in 2021, researching the intersection of Computational Argumentation, Ethics and Sustainability in AI, as well as Modular Learning.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Multilingual Bias in LLMs**: Researching AI distortions and countermeasures, examining how language models reflect cultural stereotypes across languages.
- **Fair NLP Systems**: Working on argumentative language modeling and fairness considerations in computational systems.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of "SHADES: Towards a Multilingual Assessment of Stereotypes in Large Language Models" (NAACL 2025) - a landmark dataset covering 20 geopolitical regions and languages
- Co-author of "GIMMICK: Globally Inclusive Multimodal Multitask Cultural Knowledge Benchmarking" (ACL 2025) - assessing cultural knowledge across 144 countries
- Author of "Fair and Argumentative Language Modeling for Computational Argumentation" (ACL 2022)
- Research showing that language models systematically associate German dialects with negative stereotypes
