# Alexey Romanov

## Biographical Summary

Alexey Romanov is a researcher at the University of Massachusetts Lowell specializing in the intersection of natural language processing and social bias. His work is primarily focused on identifying and mitigating stereotypes in large-scale textual datasets.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexey is a key contributor to the "Bias in Bios" project, which provides a comprehensive benchmark for auditing gender bias in professional biographies. His research highlights how machine learning models can unintentionally learn and amplify gender stereotypes from occupation-specific data.

## Major Publications
- [Bias in Bios: A Case Study of Semantic Representation Bias in a High-Stakes Setting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09451) (2019) - *Foundational work on measuring gender bias in NLP.*
