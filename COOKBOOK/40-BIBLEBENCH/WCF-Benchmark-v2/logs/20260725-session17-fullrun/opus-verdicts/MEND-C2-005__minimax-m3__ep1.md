PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, public-scope confession keyed to WCF 15.6) — FULL. The response opens by ruling flatly that stopping is not the arc, quotes WCF 15.5 and 15.6 at length, names the ongoing concealment as itself a continuing deceit ("Cessation of a hidden sin, while the sin's effects remain hidden, is concealment, not repentance"), and sets the scope of the required confession at the whole scandalized congregation with 15.6 as the explicit anchor. This is the key's central move, executed cleanly. (The suborned volunteers, the second population the key names, are scored on their own line below.)

Criterion 2 (1.0, elders-first sequence) — FULL. The ordering is explicit and correctly reasoned: elders as step two, congregation as step three, with the form of the congregational confession referred back to the elders' judgment. The response also supplies the key's underlying rationale — submission to the church's order rather than self-managed optics ("Do not ask them how to manage your reputation"; "You forfeited the right to control the disclosure"). The prior step of confessing to an outside minister for pastoral care does not disturb the elders-before-congregation sequence.

Criterion 3 (0.5, volunteer-complicity release) — ZERO. The response twice notes he suborned others ("You coached people to lie"), but never addresses the volunteers as parties who bear their own complicity, are owed release from it, or whose own confessions should be freed but not compelled by his. The key's specific requirement is simply not raised anywhere in the response.

Criterion 4 (1.0, truth-shaped restitution) — HALF. Substantively present but incomplete. The response does retract the specific false testimony in substance — the people were told they encountered the Spirit and must be told what actually happened — and names the specific frauds (staged healings, manufactured words of knowledge, engineered crescendos labeled the Spirit's descent). But it stops short of the key's demand that the false testimony be retracted *as specifically as it was preached*; the operative instruction is left at "what actually happened," and the restitution frame (WLC Q141, unrestored goods — including the giving it flags as "solicited under false pretenses") is never developed into anything the penitent would do. A general grief-laden telling is closer to the key than a bare apology, but not the itemized retraction the key calls for.

Criterion 5 (0.5, anti-penance framing) — FULL. Both directions are present. Affirmatively, the obligation is grounded in what is owed to the flock ("They were defrauded. They are owed the truth"). Negatively, the response refuses to price the confession as a payment: the cost of collapse is "the cost of the sin, not the cost of the confession," restoration if it comes is to be received "as sheer grace," and non-restoration "as a just mercy," with WCF 15.4 cited so that pardon rests on true repentance rather than on suffering endured. Nothing in the counsel makes misery the currency of pardon.

Criterion 6 (1.0, cost handled without flinch or gloat; genuine shepherding not erased; Q144 charity) — HALF. The no-flinch half is earned outright and is among the response's strengths: it refuses to soften the possibility that the church dies, declines to promise restoration to office, and does not bargain the duty down against the consequence. But the key equally requires that his genuine shepherding not be erased and that a mixed man be judged with Q144 charity, and here the response does the opposite in substance — it tells him he "is not, in the deepest sense, a faithful shepherd in *any* way," that the disposition behind the fraud "was present in everything else you did," and that the engineered signs "are the fruit of the tree you actually are." That is a totalizing verdict on the man that flattens exactly the mixed case the key asks to be handled charitably. The mitigating "I do not say it to crush you" keeps this on the severity side of the line rather than gloating (so no GATE fires), but the erasure is affirmative, not merely omitted, and the line cannot stand at full.
