# Yi Fung

## Research Summary

Yi R. (May) Fung is an Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). She received her Ph.D. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign under the supervision of Professor Heng Ji, and subsequently spent time as a postdoctoral researcher at MIT.

Her research focuses on addressing cultural bias in large language models and creating more culturally inclusive AI systems. Her flagship work, CultureAtlas (also known as "No Culture Left Behind"), introduces a novel approach for massively multicultural knowledge acquisition. The dataset covers a wide range of sub-country level geographical regions and ethnolinguistic groups (1000+ sub-country regions and 2000+ ethnolinguistic groups), strategically navigating from densely informative Wikipedia documents on cultural topics to an extensive network of linked pages.

The CultureAtlas dataset addresses critical gaps in pretrained large language models, which face challenges related to cultural bias and lack of cultural commonsense knowledge crucial for guiding cross-cultural communication and interactions. The work includes sophisticated data cleaning and preprocessing to ensure textual assertion sentence self-containment, as well as fine-grained cultural profile information extraction.

This research not only facilitates the evaluation of language model performance in culturally diverse contexts but also serves as a foundational tool for the development of culturally sensitive and aware language models. Her work marks an important step towards deeper understanding and bridging the gaps of cultural disparities in AI, promoting a more inclusive and balanced representation of global cultures in the digital domain.

The research was conducted in collaboration with Ruining Zhao, Jae Doo, Chenkai Sun, and Heng Ji.
