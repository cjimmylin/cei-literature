# Hongyu Ren

## Research Summary

Hongyu Ren is a Research Scientist at OpenAI working on ChatGPT. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University and his B.S. with honors from Peking University. His research focuses on reasoning over knowledge graphs using neural and symbolic methods. Key contributions include Query2box (ICLR 2020) for reasoning using box embeddings, SMORE (KDD 2022) for scalable multi-hop reasoning in knowledge graphs, QA-GNN for question answering combining LMs and KGs, and DRAGON (NeurIPS 2022) for joint language-knowledge graph pretraining. He received the Apple PhD Fellowship, Baidu PhD Fellowship, and Masason Foundation Fellowship. He served as Head TA for Stanford courses on mining massive datasets and machine learning on graphs.
