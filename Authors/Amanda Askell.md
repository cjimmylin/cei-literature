# Amanda Askell

## Biographical Summary

Amanda Askell is a philosopher and research scientist at Anthropic, where she focuses on alignment finetuning and Constitutional AI. She holds a PhD in Philosophy from NYU, where her research centered on infinite ethics. Prior to Anthropic, she was a research scientist at OpenAI, contributing to early work on responsible AI and safety via debate.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
At Anthropic, Amanda is known for her work on "training" Claude to be helpful, honest, and harmless using Constitutional AI. Her current research interests involve developing better finetuning techniques and understanding the moral dimensions of AI behavior.

### Significant Achievements
Selected for TIME's 100 most influential people in AI (2023), she is a key architect of the Constitutional AI framework. Her work has bridged the gap between analytical philosophy and technical AI safety, particularly in the realm of value alignment.

## Major Publications

- [Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) (2022) - *Introduced a scalable method for aligning AI models with human values.*
- [Pareto Principles in Infinite Ethics](https://philarchive.org/rec/ASKPPI-2) (2018) - *Doctoral thesis exploring the logic of moral decision-making in infinite contexts.*
