# David A. Brewster

## Biographical Summary

David A. Brewster is a researcher working at the intersection of AI ethics and animal ethics. His research examines how artificial intelligence systems handle moral questions concerning non-human animals, contributing to the emerging field of AI fairness as it extends beyond human-centric considerations.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Speciesism in AI**: Investigating whether and how large language models exhibit biases against non-human animals.
- **AI Fairness**: Extending fairness frameworks to include non-human moral patients.
- **Moral Philosophy and AI**: Examining how AI systems reflect and potentially entrench moral biases.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of the SpeciesismBench paper, which systematically evaluates how LLMs treat moral questions involving non-human animals.

## Major Publications
- **Speciesism in AI: Evaluating Discrimination Against Animals in Large Language Models** (2025) - First systematic study of speciesist bias in LLMs

David A. Brewster's work on speciesism in AI represents an important expansion of AI ethics research beyond human-centric concerns. The SpeciesismBench study reveals that while LLMs may condemn racism and sexism, they often rationalize harm to farmed animals, reflecting entrenched cultural norms around animal exploitation. This research argues for expanding AI alignment frameworks to explicitly include non-human moral patients.
