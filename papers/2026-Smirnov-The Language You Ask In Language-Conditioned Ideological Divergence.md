## Abstract

Experimental comparison of LLM-generated political analyses using Russian vs Ukrainian prompts on identical Ukrainian civil society document. Russian output echoes Russian state discourse; Ukrainian output adopts Western liberal-democratic framing.

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B3
