## Abstract

The first African policy-based safety benchmark built from adversarial queries authored by 155 domain experts across sensitive fields. It evaluates guardian models for culturally grounded risk signals across 10 African languages, revealing that English-centric benchmarks overestimate real-world multilingual safety and that dynamic models still struggle to fully localize African-language contexts.

## Dataset Information

Adversarial queries from 155 domain experts; 10 African languages
