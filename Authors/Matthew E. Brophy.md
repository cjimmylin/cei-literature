# Matthew E. Brophy

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Matthew E. Brophy is an Assistant Professor of Philosophy at High Point University. He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Miami. His work focuses on the ethical dimensions of emerging technologies, particularly how traditional philosophical frameworks can be adapted to the challenges posed by artificial intelligence and digital innovation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Matthew’s research lies at the intersection of ethics and technology. He investigates the moral status of AI agents, the ethical implications of automated systems, and the impact of technology on human flourishing and autonomy. He is also interested in professional and corporate ethics in the context of the digital economy.

### Significant Achievements
He is an active contributor to academic and public discussions on AI ethics, frequently publishing in philosophy journals and participating in public forums. He is recognized for his pedagogical contributions to applied ethics and his ability to translate complex ethical theories into practical guidance for the governance of new technologies.
