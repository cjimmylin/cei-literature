# Kma Solaiman

## Research Summary

KMA Solaiman (Khaled "KMA" Solaiman) is an Assistant Teaching Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), where he directs the H.A.R.M.O.N.I. Lab. His research lies at the intersection of Robust Machine Learning, Multimodal Reasoning, and Human-Centered AI, focusing on developing systems that can adapt to novelty, align across modalities, and support real-world decision-making with interpretable AI. As lead author of BiasLab (ICML 2025 MoFA Workshop), Solaiman released a dataset of 300 political news articles annotated for perceived ideological bias along dual-axis sentiment scales (Democrat vs. Republican), enriched with rationale indicators. This work enhances explainable bias detection by providing annotations that explain why text is biased, enabling models to move beyond simple classification to justifying their verdicts on ideological slant.
