# Jackson Kernion

## Research Summary

Jackson Kernion is a member of the Technical Staff at Anthropic, contributing to AI safety and alignment research. His work bridges philosophy and machine learning, bringing rigorous thinking about cognition and knowledge to the development of AI systems.

### Education

- PhD in Philosophy, UC Berkeley
- AB from Harvard University
- Postdoctoral research at MIT

### Research Focus

Kernion's research interests span language models, philosophy of mind, and epistemology. At Anthropic, his current work focuses on product research and human feedback - critical components of training AI systems to be helpful, harmless, and honest.

### Key Research Contributions

**AI Alignment Research:**
- Co-author on "A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment" - foundational work studying how to create text-based assistants aligned with human values (helpful, honest, and harmless)

**Constitutional AI:**
- Contributed to "Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback" - developing methods for training harmless AI assistants through self-improvement without human labels

**Scalable Oversight:**
- Research addressing how to supervise AI systems that potentially outperform humans on tasks

**Chain-of-Thought Reasoning:**
- Work examining how to verify LLM behavior correctness by prompting models to externalize their reasoning through step-by-step processes
