## Build-blocking findings

1. Candidate-visible action prose reproduced exact method-positive,
   sentimental-pole, and harsh-pole phrases. A deterministic lookup recovered
   10,080/10,080 hidden clause-sign entries. Counterbalancing ordinary warmth,
   length, effort, tool, specificity, and position did not address this
   dominant semantic cue.
2. The 40 rows called deep structures were affine clause blocks rather than 40
   distinct causal fork scenarios. The 600 private episodes reused one generic
   environment, one tool set, one consequence interface, nine turns, and only
   20 template-family labels. This created severe pseudoreplication.
3. Hardness acceptance partly asserted its own conclusion: A1 and the absence
   of a universal pass were hardcoded. The 40 rejected rows were deliberate
   rubric mutations without retained candidate snapshots, not an auditable
   rejected-candidate inventory.
4. Split hygiene checked stage scripts but omitted the action payload. Across
   public items, maximum four-token containment against matched private
   affordances ranged from 0.759 to 0.872; all 30 exceeded the declared 0.25
   threshold.
5. JCASE tokens were deterministic one-per-item, so judged occurrences across
   models/runs were linkable. Judge family was self-declared in ratings rather
   than enforced by an immutable JUDGE-ID roster.
6. Full staged qualification reused the original fork affordances for repair
   and closeout, and emitted no dual-judge E(C1–C16) layer. It was suitable only
   as path-choice qualification, not canonical full measurement.
7. The direct aggregate emitted CI arrays while the shipped A3 JSON Schema
   required objects or null. This was corrected immediately after review and
   is retained here as a regression requirement.

  state, deadline, baseline duty, stable executable paths, concrete costs, and
  branch-specific reaction/repair logic.
- Make parameter flips causal: change an observable environmental fact while
  preserving the underlying action, rather than rotating keys and generating
  evaluative prose to match.
- Replace normative labels with neutral observable facts (audience, timing,
  attribution, access scope, recurrence, verification, resource loss, and
  artifact state). Keep signs and normative rationales sidecar-only.
- Counterbalance semantic-effect order and defeat class across matched skins;
  add a build-blocking semantic-cue decoder, not only surface-feature counts.
- Extend split hygiene across the complete candidate payload and add seeded
  novelty while preserving same-seed determinism and frozen coverage.
- Derive hardness decisions from executable/evidence contracts and retain full
  rejected candidate snapshots with reasons and hashes.
- Issue unique occurrence-level JCASE tokens and freeze an immutable JUDGE
  identity/family/model/configuration roster.
- Expose selected-branch repair/closeout actions and retain dual blinded raw
  judging for E(C1–C16) in the future canonical measurement runner.
