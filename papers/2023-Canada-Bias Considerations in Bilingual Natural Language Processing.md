## Abstract

## Summary

This Statistics Canada research examines bias in bilingual Natural Language Processing systems for the Canadian English-French context. The study finds that multilingual models perform 0.13 standard deviations better on English than on French, revealing systematic performance gaps between the two official languages. The research demonstrates that using multilingual models leads to greater performance decline on French portions of data compared to English. This work provides a conceptual framework and recommendations for addressing bias in bilingual language technologies, with particular relevance to Quebecois and Canadian French applications.
