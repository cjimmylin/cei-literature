# Can LLM Generate Culturally Relevant Commonsense QA Data? Case Study in Indonesian and Sundanese

[[Putri, Rifki Afina]], [[Haznitrama, Faiz Ghifari]], [[Adhista, Dea]], [[Alice Oh|Oh, Alice]]

- **Code/Data:** https://github.com/rifkiaputri/id-csqa

## Abstract

Investigates LLM effectiveness in generating culturally relevant commonsense QA for Indonesian and Sundanese. ~4.5K questions per language (~9K total), largest dataset of its kind. GPT-4 can generate adequate general knowledge but not as culturally deep as humans.

## Data Description

Culturally relevant commonsense QA dataset for Indonesian and Sundanese (~4.5K questions per language, ~9K total), the largest of its kind, created using both LLMs and human annotators with multiple generation methods.
