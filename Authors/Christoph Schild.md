# Christoph Schild

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Christoph Schild is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Psychology at the University of Siegen. He completed his Ph.D. at the Personality and Social Behavior Lab at the University of Copenhagen (2017-2020). His research spans moral psychology, personality, and behavioral ethics.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Foundations and Personality**: Exploring relationships between Honesty-Humility and moral foundations (care/harm, justice/fairness, loyalty/betrayal, authority/subversion, sanctity/degradation)
- **Dishonesty Research**: Investigating predictors of monetarily incentivized dishonesty and creative justifications for unethical behavior
- **Cross-Cultural Moral Psychology**: Examining how situational factors shape moral judgments across cultures
- **First Impressions**: Researching initial social perceptions and their consequences

### Significant Achievements
- Published 54 research papers with 773 citations
- Contributed to large-scale international study on moral judgments in the trolley dilemma across cultures
- Research on Machiavellianism and unethical justifications
- Presented at Applied Ethics and Morality Research Group seminar at Prague University of Economics and Business
