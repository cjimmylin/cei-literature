# Abdellah El Mekki

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abdellah El Mekki is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of British Columbia (UBC). He completed his PhD in Computer Science at Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P) in Morocco. His research seeks to make AI models more inclusive and culturally aware, particularly for Arabic and other under-represented languages.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdellah's research focuses on the intersection of multilingual NLP and AI values. He investigates how large language models (LLMs) can be aligned with diverse cultural values and how to improve performance for low-resource languages through cross-lingual transfer learning.

## Major Publications
