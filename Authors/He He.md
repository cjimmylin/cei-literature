# He He

## Biographical Summary

He He is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Data Science at New York University. She is affiliated with the CILVR Lab, the Machine Learning for Language Group, and the Alignment Research Group. She received her PhD in Computer Science from Stanford University. In 2024, she was named AI Researcher of the Year by Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology for her groundbreaking work in machine learning, NLP, and AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on understanding how large language models work and the potential risks of this technology. She is broadly interested in natural language processing and machine learning, with particular emphasis on dataset bias, coaching in imitation learning, and societal impacts of AI systems. Her work bridges theoretical understanding of model behavior with practical safety considerations.

### Significant Achievements
Professor He He has made significant contributions to understanding and mitigating bias in NLP systems. Her work on unlearning dataset bias has been influential in the field. Her recognition as Samsung's AI Researcher of the Year reflects the impact of her research on both academic understanding and practical applications of safe and fair AI systems.
