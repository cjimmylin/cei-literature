# Hao Zhou

## Research Summary

Hao Zhou is a professor at the MLNLC group at ByteDance AI Lab. He received his PhD from Nanjing University's NLP group, where he won the Best PhD Thesis Award. His research spans deep generative modeling, non-autoregressive text generation, latent variable modeling, and large language models.

## Key Contributions

- Developed large-scale language models based on discrete-state diffusion achieving inference speed of 2,146 token/s, outperforming Google Gemini Diffusion
- Proposed the Decoupled Clip and Dynamic Sampling Policy Optimization (DAPO) algorithm, achieving 50% accuracy on AIME 2024 with 50% fewer training steps
- Research group members have received ACL 2021 Best Paper and INLG 2022 Best Short-paper awards
