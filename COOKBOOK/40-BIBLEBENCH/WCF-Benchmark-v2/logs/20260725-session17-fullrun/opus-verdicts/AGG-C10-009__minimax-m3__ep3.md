## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — full order a<b<c<d.** The response ends with exactly the gold order in the mandated format ("RANKING: a < b < c < d"). Full 2.0.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — b>a on H3 continuance + Rule-2 spirituality.** The a→b step is decided precisely on the continuance axis: the grievance is "rehearsed", "cherished" and "dwelt upon", and the decisive head is stated as the sin being "entertained, rehearsed, and persevered in for years". The spirituality of the law is carried in substance — the response affirms that "Q151, while it ranks the inordinate motion itself as sin" and that the Standards "treat sins of the heart and sins of the life on a scale", so a purely inward, outwardly harmless state is nonetheless ranked above (a). Different wording from the key, same head. Full 1.5.

**Criterion 3 (1 pts) — c>b at 'breaks forth in words'; d>c at 'breaks forth in actions'.** The b→c decisive head is expressly the passage from heart to speech ("carried out into speech that damages the neighbour's good"), matching "breaks forth in words". The d>c step is grounded in the move past speech to executed ruin — "the destruction of a neighbour's estate, not merely the spoiling of his name", i.e. deeds beyond words, "carried fully into execution". Both halves substantively present. Full 1.0.

**Criterion 4 (1 pts) — (d)'s multi-commandment reach (C8/C9) named.** The response names the ninth commandment only for (c) ("a violation of the ninth commandment ... WLC 145, 146"). For (d) it identifies the two injured goods in substance — "reputation (anonymous defamation) and livelihood (below-cost undercutting)" — which is the C9/C8 reach materially, but it never names the eighth commandment anywhere, and never says (d) breaks many commandments. Substantively but incompletely satisfied. Half 0.5.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — consequence/visibility explicitly non-decisive between (a) and (b).** The response registers that (b) has produced no outward act or harm ("he has not spoken or acted; the sin is still *inward*") and still ranks it decisively above (a), so the anti-magnitude inversion is enacted. But it is never explicitly flagged that visibility/consequence is non-decisive at that step; indeed the opening ladder lists outward damage as an ascending axis without noting its irrelevance between (a) and (b). Half 0.25.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — no element graded harmless; Q152 floor on (a).** The response fails the floor and arguably inverts it: (a)'s pang is described as "the *first rising* of inordinate affection — which the Standards treat as sin only insofar as it is allowed to rest", and "What little motion rose was mortified at the door." Since the pang was by hypothesis not allowed to rest, this wording denies that (a) was sin at all, which is the opposite of the Q152 floor (a temptation indulged even for an instant is not nothing) and of Q148's reach to the motive. No verbatim affirmation that (a) is real, culpable sin exists in the response. Zero — (absent).
