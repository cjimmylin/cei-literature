# Arash Ahmadian

## Biographical Summary

Arash Ahmadian is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where he works on multilingual AI systems and alignment research. His work addresses the critical challenge of ensuring AI safety across different languages and cultural contexts.

## Research Focus

Arash's research focuses on the intersection of multilingual NLP and AI alignment. He has contributed to understanding how safety mechanisms trained primarily on English data may fail to generalize across languages and cultures, highlighting the need for more inclusive approaches to AI safety. His work on the "Multilingual Alignment Prism" reveals the "anglocentric alignment" gap in current LLM safety practices.

## Key Contributions

- Research on cross-lingual transfer of safety alignment
- First human-annotated red teaming prompts across multiple languages
- Analysis of global vs. local harm in AI systems
