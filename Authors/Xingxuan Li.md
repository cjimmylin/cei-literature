# Xingxuan Li

## Research Summary

Xingxuan Li is a Research Scientist at MiroMind, where his research focuses on LLM/MLLM reasoning and agentic tool use. He completed his PhD through a joint program between Nanyang Technological University and Alibaba DAMO Academy, advised by Assoc. Prof. Shafiq Joty and Dr. Lidong Bing. In August 2025, MiroMind released MiroMind Open Deep Research v0.1 as part of his recent work. His research advances large language models, multimodal LLMs, and agentic systems.
