# Benjamin Grant Purzycki

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Benjamin Grant Purzycki is an Associate Professor in the Department of the Study of Religion at Aarhus University in Denmark. He is a sociocultural anthropologist who engages in the cognitive, evolutionary, and ethnographic sciences of sociality and religion. Previously, he held positions at the Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology in Leipzig (Senior Researcher, 2016-2019), University of British Columbia, and University of Connecticut.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research investigates how humans make sense of gods' minds and how explicit religious beliefs and rituals correspond to local pressures that require collective coordination. He examines the cognitive foundations of religious concepts and their role in promoting cooperation and prosocial behavior across diverse cultural contexts. He has conducted extensive fieldwork in the Tyva Republic in southern Siberia using ethnographic and experimental methods.

### Significant Achievements

Purzycki co-authored the influential book "Religion Evolving: Cultural, Cognitive, and Ecological Dynamics" with Richard Sosis, which argues for integrating isolated research findings on the various components that constitute religion. He was a key investigator in the Evolution of Religion and Morality project, which assessed the role of religion in cooperation and prosocial behaviour using ethnographic and experimental methods across eight different field sites worldwide.
