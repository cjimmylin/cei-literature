# Qalb: Largest State-of-the-Art Urdu Large Language Model for 230M Speakers with Systematic Continued Pre-training

## Authors

[[Hassan, Muhammad Taimoor]], [[Ahmed, Jawad]], [[Awais, Muhammad]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Hassan, Muhammad Taimoor; Ahmed, Jawad; Awais, Muhammad

**Year:** 2026

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08141

## Abstract

Largest Urdu LLM achieving 90.34 weighted average score. Notes that model may produce culturally biased or harmful content, highlighting importance of alignment assessment. State-of-the-art Urdu LLM with discussion of cultural bias and safety concerns.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Qalb

## Data Description

A large-scale Urdu pre-training corpus of 1.97 billion tokens (1.84B Urdu + 140M English Wikipedia) curated from news, literature, government, and social media sources, plus the resulting Qalb language model; authors state both will be released on HuggingFace but no URL is provided in the paper.
