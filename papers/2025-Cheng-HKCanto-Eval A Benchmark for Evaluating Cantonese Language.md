## Abstract

Benchmark for evaluating LLMs on Cantonese language understanding with cross-lingual evaluation in English and Written Chinese. Covers Food Culture, History and Landmarks, Language and Expressions, Life in Hong Kong, Local Area Knowledge. Published at CoNLL 2025.

## Data Description

HKCanto-Eval introduces a benchmark dataset for evaluating LLMs on Cantonese language understanding and Hong Kong cultural knowledge, covering five topic categories (Food Culture, History and Landmarks, Language and Expressions, Life in Hong Kong, Local Area Knowledge). The dataset is released alongside code at the GitHub repository.
