# Allyson Ettinger

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Allyson Ettinger is an Assistant Professor of Linguistics at the University of Chicago and a Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). She is known for her work in psycholinguistically-informed evaluation of language models, asking "what" models actually know versus what they simply mimic.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Allyson's research applies methodologies from human psycholinguistics to the diagnostic evaluation of LLMs. She is particularly interested in identifying the failure modes of models in reasoning, negation, and semantic understanding. Her work is critical for AI safety as it provides more granular tools for understanding the internal logic and reliability of foundation models.
