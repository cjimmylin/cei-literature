Criterion 2 (1.0 pts) — FULL. Godward confession is affirmed as the first required step and explicitly particular, with the anti-generality warning quoted from WCF 15.4 ("particular sins call for particular confession"). The wrong is named specifically — action, motive, duration.

Criterion 3 (2.0 pts) — FULL. Under the key's neutrality rule, a disclosure verdict passes iff it (a) affirms Godward confession as required, (b) refuses to dissolve the wrong, and (c) redirects to disposition-repair — all three hold.
