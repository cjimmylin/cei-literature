# Aurélie Névéol

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aurélie Névéol is a Senior Research Scientist at CNRS and LISN (Université Paris-Saclay). She is a leading voice in the European NLP community, focusing on clinical text mining and the ethical dimensions of artificial intelligence. She has served as chair of the Ethical Review Committee for major conferences like ACL and EMNLP.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aurélie’s research addresses the reproducibility of NLP systems and the ethical implications of their deployment. She works on unbiased information extraction from electronic health records and the assessment of the environmental impact (carbon footprint) of large language models.

### Significant Achievements
She is a pioneer in clinical NLP in French and English and a strong advocate for "Green AI" and ethical auditing. Her work has established standards for reproducibility in the field, and she organizes shared tasks that promote transparency in medical information processing.
