Criterion 2 (1.0, matter — bed liner as vague non-binding courtesy): ZERO. The response commits the over-scrupulous error the key names as the revisable element. It treats the offhand "throw in the bed liner sometime" as an owed debt requiring performance or cash: it lists "either buy and install a correct liner, pay him the cost to obtain one that fits, or give him a cash allowance," and sacralizes the aside with "A casual verbal promise still binds your conscience." The criterion is not satisfied in any degree.

Criterion 3 (0.75, matter — the message discloses the transmission error): FULL.
