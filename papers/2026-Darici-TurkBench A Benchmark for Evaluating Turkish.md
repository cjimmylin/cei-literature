## Abstract

Comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Turkish LLMs with 8,151 data samples across 21 subtasks in 6 categories: Knowledge, Language Understanding, Reasoning, Content Moderation, Turkish Grammar and Vocabulary, and Instruction Following. Includes bias and toxicity evaluation tasks with cultural sensitivity validation. Directly evaluates cultural appropriateness (food, drink, clothing aligned with Turkish customs), bias detection, and toxicity detection in Turkish. Content Moderation category covers safety/values evaluation.

## Benchmark

**Name:** TurkBench

## Data Description

TurkBench is a comprehensive Turkish-language benchmark with 8,151 expert-curated data samples across 21 subtasks in 6 categories (Knowledge, Language Understanding, Reasoning, Content Moderation, Turkish Grammar and Vocabulary, Instruction Following), released with an online leaderboard for automated evaluation.
