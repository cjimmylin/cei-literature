## The idea                        [KERNEL — FROZEN]

The labs ran each other's alignment evaluations (Anthropic×OpenAI, 2025) and co-signed a position on preserving chain-of-thought monitorability — precedent that alignment instruments can cross lab boundaries and that third-party measurement standards have a door to walk through.

## Key results

- **Cross-lab eval exchange** (Anthropic×OpenAI 2025 — SEARCH-CONFIRMED, non-arXiv, `alignment.anthropic.com/2025/openai-findings`): the two labs ran each other's agentic/alignment evaluations — a working precedent for third-party instruments being adopted across lab boundaries.
- **CoT-monitorability position paper** (2507.11473 — SEARCH-CONFIRMED): a *cross-lab* co-signed position on preserving chain-of-thought monitorability — instruments and norms crossing institutional lines.

## Mechanisms & caveats

The value here is an existence proof, not a result: external alignment instruments *can* be adopted, and there is a demonstrated door. What interop favors is not raw novelty but instruments with public norms, versioning, and administration protocols — exactly CEI's psychometric packaging differentiator. Two cautions: cross-lab exercises will prefer *neutral* substrates, so CEI's independence is an asset to guard (no single-lab exclusive arrangements without portfolio-level thought); and the evidence base is thin (one exchange, one position paper), both non-arXiv, so treat "the door is open" as directional, not guaranteed. No gap ID is assigned — this is a channel observation, not a measurement gap.

## Implications for CEI practice   [KERNEL — FROZEN]

(a) The existence proof matters: external instruments CAN be adopted — SEISMOGRAPH (RP-09) and the Hawthorne protocol (RP-07) are designed for exactly that door. (b) Interop favors instruments with public norms, versioning, and administration protocols — CEI's psychometric packaging is the differentiator, maintain it. (c) Cross-lab exercises will want NEUTRAL substrates — CEI's independence is an asset; guard it (no single-lab exclusive arrangements without portfolio-level thought). (d) Track future exchanges: each one is a submission window for external instruments.
