## Abstract

3,565 manually created sentences measuring 16 gender stereotypes across English and 9 Slavic languages. Evaluates masked/generative LMs and MT systems. Discovers significant stereotypical reasoning in all models.

## Data Description

Releases GEST, a manually created dataset of 3,565 gender-neutral sentences measuring 16 gender stereotypes across English and 9 Slavic languages, designed to evaluate gender-stereotypical reasoning in masked LMs, generative LMs, and machine translation systems.
