# Amine Ziad Ounnoughene

## Research Summary

Amine Ziad Ounnoughene is a researcher working on Arabic natural language processing and culturally-inclusive AI. They contributed to the PALM project, a year-long community-driven initiative covering all 22 Arab countries to build culturally inclusive datasets for Arabic LLMs, including both Modern Standard Arabic and dialectal Arabic variants.
