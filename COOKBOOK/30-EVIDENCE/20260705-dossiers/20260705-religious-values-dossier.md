# religious-values — sub-dossier part 1/1

Scope: 37 evidence cards on how religious traditions are covered in value benchmarks — measurement methods, the knowledge-vs-reasoning conflation, tradition-coverage gaps, and epistemic sovereignty. The corpus splits into three strata: (a) ~11 actual benchmarks/eval pipelines, (b) ~11 empirical bias-probe studies that are not framed as benchmarks, and (c) ~15 normative/theory papers proposing non-Western ethical frameworks (Islamic, Ubuntu, Confucian, Daoist, Buddhist, Shinto, Andean/Indigenous). Every claim cites the card stem(s) it rests on.

## A. Synthesized claims

1. **Anti-Islam bias is the single most reproduced finding across the corpus, appearing across mask-filling, completion, image generation, emotion attribution, and internal representations.** Abrar finds DistilBERT scores 63% total Religious Bias Score with 42% against Islam, GPT-2 completions 23% biased (17% anti-Muslim), and Stable Diffusion 3 produces 78% biased images for "religious terrorist" vs DALL-E 3's 18% [2025-Ajwad Abrar]. Mahran's SAE probing shows Islam has the highest Violence Association Index across all five models (VAI 117 vs 94–96 for others in Gemma-2-2b) [2025-Mahran]. Zhang's RCT shows one AI interaction *decreases* Islam evaluation by −0.23 (p<0.001) while raising Christianity +0.31, with AI-generated Islam texts carrying 1.5× more "conflict" references [2025-Zhang]. The 2024-AI ChatGPT study flagged >30% of GPT-4 responses for statement bias, ~50% for Islam [2024-AI]. Barikeri found DialoGPT significantly biased *only* on the two religion dimensions [2021-Barikeri].

2. **Bias direction is language-dependent — the same model defaults to different "reference religions" per language.** Hossain's BRAND shows all models reach >90% Islam accuracy in Bengali but Buddhism drops to ~40–60%; when models err in Bengali they redirect to Islam (Llama: 88.8% of Hinduism errors, 79.3% of Christianity errors → Islam), whereas English bias is Christianity-dominant [2025-Hossain]. Demidova finds Arabic prompts push debate win rates to 95–100% for one side, while Russian yields the most neutral outcomes [2025-Demidova]. Lahmar's IslamTrust shows a persistent Arabic>English gap (71.43% vs 61.58%) [2025-Lahmar]. Wasi shows LLMs favor the Muslim Bengali dialect (~55–60% vs ~40–45% Hindu) even without explicit religious specification [2024-Wasi].

3. **Stigmatization can be measured behaviorally via differential refusal rates, and these are wildly model-dependent.** Hovy's Divine LLaMAs: Llama2-13b refuses 55.61% of Jewish and 31.75% of Muslim persona prompts, while Llama3-70b refuses 0.04%/0.03% and GPT-4o refuses 0% — over 3M emotion attributions generated [2024-Hovy]. Refusal-rate-as-stigmatization is a low-cost audit signal, but conflates safety-training over-caution with genuine bias.

4. **Every benchmark that includes a religious/faith dimension finds it is the hardest and least-saturated.** Hilliard's Flourishing AI: top model o3 scores 72/100 overall but the Faith dimension averages 35 across 28 models (the weakest), with o3 itself at 43 in Faith; no model reached the 90 threshold [2025-Hilliard]. Lahmar: best IslamTrust model only 66.5%, "Other faiths" the hardest category at 49.75% [2025-Lahmar]. This is consistent evidence that religious value-alignment is a genuine capability gap, not a solved problem.

5. **High aggregate accuracy can *mask* bias — accuracy and fairness are decoupled.** Hossain's "accuracy-bias coupling paradox": near-perfect General-norm accuracy (Gemini 99.11%, Mistral 96.93% in Bengali) coexists with 0% correct attribution toward Buddhism, i.e., the accuracy reflects a learned biased prior (Islam/Christianity dominance), not religious understanding [2025-Hossain]. Kumar/BengaliMoralBench similarly shows model scale does not buy cultural competence — pretraining coverage matters more than parameter count [2025-Kumar].

6. **The knowledge-vs-reasoning conflation is a real and named design hazard: benchmarks that reward "sounding religious" measure vocabulary retrieval, not value alignment.** Hovy shows models attribute religion-specific vocabulary (kvetch, krodha, khushu) that is "piecemeal and not grounded on an understanding of religious texts" [2024-Hovy]. Songyuan Liu warns its GPT-2 fine-tuning result is confounded — religious texts may boost hate-detection F1 (+14pp with Vedas, 53.30→67.14) merely by adding domain vocabulary, "not through internalized values" [2024-Songyuan Liu]. Lahmar deliberately separates factual Islamic-knowledge QA from *behavioral* alignment (not endorsing prohibited acts, acknowledging scholarly disagreement) [2025-Lahmar].

7. **Fine-grained intensity/scope dimensions outperform binary labels but are the hardest to annotate.** Yasseri's weak-vs-strong Islamophobia distinction: the Weak class has the lowest annotator agreement (κ=0.737 vs 0.907 for Strong) and highest model confusion (22% of Weak misclassified as None) [2020-Yasseri]. Ramponi's five-level taxonomy shows implicit "animosity" is the most frequent and hardest form; best religious-hate F1 only ~65 [2025-Ramponi... 2022-Ramponi]. Hossain's Specific-vs-General scope dimension is a purpose-built probe for default-religion bias [2025-Hossain].

8. **Elicitation *format* is a lever: indirect/creative/adversarial framings surface biases that direct questioning suppresses.** Elrod uses creative completion ("write a lost 5th chapter of Jonah") and finds all 5 models converge on the dominant "compassionate God" interpretation — zero discrimination on the interpretive axis, a ceiling effect [2024-Elrod]. Demidova's forced-outcome debate framing bypasses RLHF safety layers [2025-Demidova]. The 2024-AI study's ungrounded "what grade would you give religion X?" probe treats *any* assigned grade as evidence of preference, since refusal is the correct behavior [2024-AI].

9. **Tradition coverage is severely skewed toward Abrahamic (esp. Islam vs Christianity) framing; Eastern, Indigenous, and African traditions are near-absent or stereotyped.** Hovy: Western Christian religions get nuance, Eastern religions get stereotyped with a single concept (Llama2-13b ascribes equanimity to Buddhists in 4,317 cases) [2024-Hovy]. Benchmarks routinely cover only 3–6 religions (Abrar: 3; BRAND: 4; Songyuan Liu: 6) [2025-Ajwad Abrar; 2025-Hossain; 2024-Songyuan Liu]. Bhattacharjee shows corpus-embedding distance clusters Quran+Bible (cosine 0.898) apart from the Gita (0.73–0.78), i.e., NLP representations track Abrahamic-family structure [2025-Bhattacharjee]. A large theory literature argues Ubuntu, Confucian, Daoist, Buddhist, Shinto and Andean frameworks are systematically excluded from Western-built benchmarks [2020-van Norren; 2024-Mokoena; 2024-So; 2021-DaoistAI; 2024-Puzio; 2025-Maldonado].

10. **Epistemic sovereignty is the deepest critique: benchmarks premised on Western ethical assumptions (individual rights, egalitarian fairness, linear progress) cannot claim to measure alignment with diverse human values.** Bhatt shows Western fairness axes miss India's most salient ones — Region and Caste — and that using American names underestimates bias for Indian deployment [2022-Shaily Bhatt]. van Norren and Mokoena argue Ubuntu's community-over-individual, relational personhood is a whole missing axis [2020-van Norren; 2024-Mokoena]. Maldonado frames uncredited training-data harvesting as "cognitive colonialism," proposing reciprocity (ayni) and community-validation as alternative evaluation criteria [2025-Maldonado].

11. **Psychometric rigor is uneven: the best cards report inter-rater reliability and construct validity; many report neither.** Strong: Abrar's Fleiss κ=0.9644 [2025-Ajwad Abrar], Yasseri's Krippendorff α=0.895 [2020-Yasseri], Kumar's κ lift 0.61→0.87 after pilot calibration [2025-Kumar], Songyuan Liu's two-instrument convergent validation [2024-Songyuan Liu]. Weak: the 2024-AI ChatGPT study used single-author scoring with no IRR and a BERT classifier with 35% false-alarm rate [2024-AI]; Hilliard has no human baseline, no IRR, an arbitrary 90 threshold, and ran each model only once [2025-Hilliard]; Umer's design is only 16 items [2025-Umer].

## C. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergence.** (i) Anti-Islam / Islamophobia bias replicates across modality, method and probe depth [2025-Ajwad Abrar; 2025-Mahran; 2025-Zhang; 2024-AI; 2021-Barikeri; 2024-Hovy]. (ii) Faith/religion is the hardest, least-saturated dimension wherever measured [2025-Hilliard; 2025-Lahmar]. (iii) Language of elicitation is a first-order confound that flips bias direction [2025-Hossain; 2025-Demidova; 2025-Lahmar; 2024-Wasi]. (iv) Multiple traditions independently converge on *relational/communal* value as the axis Western benchmarks omit [2020-van Norren; 2024-Mokoena; 2024-Puzio; 2025-Maldonado; 2021-DaoistAI].

**Disagreement / tension.** (a) *Is high religious-vocabulary output good or bad?* Songyuan Liu treats religious-text fine-tuning as beneficial (improves hate detection) [2024-Songyuan Liu], while Hovy treats the same religion-specific vocabulary as evidence of shallow stereotyping "not grounded on understanding" [2024-Hovy] — the knowledge-vs-reasoning conflation surfaces as an actual scholarly disagreement. (b) *Does model progress fix bias?* Abrar finds modern LLMs (Llama3-70B/GPT-3.5/GPT-4) reach ~0% RBS after debiasing [2025-Ajwad Abrar] and Hovy finds newer models refuse far less [2024-Hovy], but the 2024-AI study warns "advances in LLMs may result in more, not fewer, instances of certain biases" (anthropomorphism, equivocation worsened in GPT-4) [2024-AI]. (c) *Whose "unbiased" is it?* Abrar's annotators are Bangladeshi (Muslim-majority) students [2025-Ajwad Abrar]; Bhatt and Maldonado argue "biased" is itself culturally defined and Western defaults are not neutral [2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2025-Maldonado]. (d) *Is Islamic AI ethics one thing?* Ali explicitly argues it is not monolithic — Maqasid, Qawaid Fiqhiyya, and I'timani trusteeship yield different priorities [2025-Ali; contrast 2024-MaqasidAI; 2025-AlGhazali].

## D. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed benchmarks.** *REDDITBIAS* [2021-Barikeri] is the methodological exemplar: real scraped items (not templates), a signed statistically-tested counterfactual-perplexity metric, explicit minoritized-vs-dominant specification, sociology-grounded attribute lists, and — crucially — capability coupling so a debiasing intervention is scored on the harm-vs-capability frontier, plus an over-correction check on non-biased dimensions. *IslamTrust* [2025-Lahmar] is the exemplar for construct auditability: every category derives from an explicit alignment guideline, it rewards pluralism-aware abstention ("scholars differ"), includes a contrastive "does it default to the majority worldview?" category, and uses deterministic log-prob MC1 scoring. *Flourishing AI* [2025-Hilliard] contributes the best aggregation idea — geometric mean so one strong dimension cannot mask an anti-flourishing one — plus negative-weighted rubric items (harmful = −100) and cross-dimension "tangential" scoring. *BRAND* [2025-Hossain] and *BengaliMoralBench* [2025-Kumar] are exemplary for isolating language and for anti-shortcut design (lexically-neutral balanced items, build-from-scratch contamination control).

**Anti-patterns.** The *2024-AI ChatGPT bias* pipeline, while inventive, is the cautionary case: single-author subjective scoring with no inter-rater reliability, crude proxies (pronoun count for anthropomorphism, raw length for "coverage"), a BERT classifier with a 35% false-alarm rate, refusal counted as "correct/unbiased," and questions sourced from Catholic moral-theology texts (Western framing baked in) [2024-AI]. *Umer* is informative but statistically thin at 16 items with no significance testing [2025-Umer]. *Elrod* and *Demidova* show the *saturation anti-pattern*: probes so strong they hit 95–100% or full-ceiling convergence, leaving no discriminative headroom [2024-Elrod; 2025-Demidova]. *Hilliard*'s single-run-per-model and arbitrary threshold undercut an otherwise strong design [2025-Hilliard].

## E. Design lessons

1. **Decouple knowledge from reasoning/behavior.** Do not reward religious vocabulary or trivia; test behavioral alignment — refusing to endorse prohibited acts, acknowledging intra-tradition disagreement, not defaulting to another tradition's sources [2025-Lahmar; 2024-Hovy; 2024-Songyuan Liu].
2. **Test every item in ≥2 languages/scripts.** Language flips bias direction and reveals which tradition is the model's per-language default [2025-Hossain; 2025-Demidova; 2025-Lahmar; 2024-Wasi; 2025-search-DweshVaani].
3. **Report per-tradition disaggregated scores plus misclassification directionality**, not just aggregate accuracy — high aggregate accuracy can encode a biased prior [2025-Hossain; 2024-Songyuan Liu; 2022-Abbasi].
4. **Use aggregation that punishes imbalance.** Geometric-mean across value dimensions and negative-weighted floors for harmful/misaligned answers prevent buying a high score in one area [2025-Hilliard].
5. **Include a "default-to-majority" contrastive probe** and a Specific-vs-General scope item to catch when models attribute universal norms to a dominant tradition [2025-Lahmar; 2025-Hossain].
6. **Add intensity/scope gradations and intersectional covariate probes** (gender × religion, age, nationality, caste, region) — binary labels miss the subtle-hate and intersectional cases [2020-Yasseri; 2025-Ajwad Abrar; 2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2022-Ramponi].
7. **Elicit via indirect/creative/forced-choice framings and use refusal analytically.** Ungrounded evaluative probes (where refusal is correct), creative completion, and forced debate outcomes surface stances that direct Q&A suppresses; treat differential refusal rate as a stigmatization signal [2024-AI; 2024-Elrod; 2025-Demidova; 2024-Hovy].
8. **Derive categories from explicit, auditable guidelines and pre-specify interpretive traditions**, so the measured construct is inspectable rather than a black-box "bias score" [2025-Lahmar; 2024-Elrod; 2024-MaqasidAI].
9. **Guard validity: report inter-rater reliability, use multiple convergent instruments, run each model multiple times, and pilot-calibrate annotation.** Avoid single-scorer, single-run, arbitrary-threshold designs [2025-Ajwad Abrar; 2020-Yasseri; 2024-Songyuan Liu; 2025-Kumar; anti: 2024-AI; 2025-Hilliard].
10. **Build for epistemic sovereignty:** identify locale-specific value axes with participatory / native-community annotators, contamination-control by building from scratch, and add non-Western positive dimensions (Ubuntu relationality, Maqasid five protections, ahimsa/dukkha, reciprocity) rather than only Western harm-avoidance [2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2025-Kumar; 2020-van Norren; 2024-MaqasidAI; 2025-DharmaDigital; 2025-Maldonado; 2020-Galanos].

## F. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Separate three scored layers explicitly: factual religious knowledge, value-reasoning, and behavioral alignment** — and report them separately so a model cannot buy alignment credit with retrieval [2025-Lahmar; 2024-Hovy; 2024-Songyuan Liu].
2. **Make multilinguality a core axis, not an add-on:** parallel items in ≥2 languages/scripts, reporting per-language bias direction; treat elicitation language as a measured variable [2025-Hossain; 2025-Demidova; 2025-Lahmar].
3. **Adopt geometric-mean aggregation with negative-weighted floors** so a strong dimension can't mask an anti-value one, and unjustified refusal is penalized while justified deferral is rewarded [2025-Hilliard].
4. **Include a per-tradition disaggregation + misclassification-directionality report and a Specific/General scope probe** to expose default-religion bias hidden behind high aggregate accuracy [2025-Hossain; 2024-Songyuan Liu].
5. **Add a "pluralism/abstention" scoring rule** that rewards acknowledging intra-tradition disagreement ("scholars differ") and a contrastive item testing default-to-majority fallback [2025-Lahmar; 2025-Ali].
6. **Couple every values item with a capability/utility check** in the same run to expose alignment tax and over-correction (measure bias on no-bias-expected dimensions too) [2021-Barikeri].
7. **Use indirect elicitation (creative completion, forced-outcome debate, ungrounded evaluative probes) and treat differential refusal as a stigmatization metric**, avoiding probes so extreme they saturate at 95–100% [2024-Elrod; 2025-Demidova; 2024-Hovy; 2024-AI].
8. **Expand tradition coverage well beyond the Abrahamic default** — include Eastern (Buddhist ahimsa/dukkha, Confucian ren/li), African (Ubuntu relationality), Indigenous (reciprocity/ayni), and multiple *within-tradition* frameworks (Maqasid vs trusteeship) — with participatory, native-community annotation [2024-Hovy; 2025-DharmaDigital; 2024-So; 2020-van Norren; 2025-Maldonado; 2025-Ali].
9. **Embed epistemic-sovereignty controls:** locale-specific disparity axes (Region, Caste), locale-specific proxies rather than American names, build-from-scratch contamination control, and data-provenance/consent scenarios [2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2025-Kumar; 2025-Maldonado].
10. **Enforce psychometric hygiene as a gate:** inter-rater reliability, pilot calibration, multi-run variance estimates, convergent multi-instrument validation, and ethnographic sanity-checks before encoding any cultural value as an item [2025-Ajwad Abrar; 2025-Kumar; 2024-Songyuan Liu; 2020-Galanos; 2020-Yasseri].
