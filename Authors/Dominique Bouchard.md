# Dominique Bouchard

## Biographical Summary

Dominique Bouchard is a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Toronto and a researcher at the Vector Institute. She earned her B.A.Sc. and M.A.Sc. from the University of Toronto. Her research focuses on the intersection of machine learning and society, with a particular emphasis on making large-scale AI systems more fair and transparent.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dominique’s research focuses on identifying and mitigating bias in foundation models. She investigates the developer responsibility for model outcomes and the development of rigorous benchmarks for assessing fairness in natural language processing. She is particularly interested in how automated systems impact social equity in high-stakes domains like hiring and healthcare.

### Significant Achievements
She has co-authored several key papers on AI fairness and the social implications of machine learning. She is an active participant in workshops and conferences dedicated to AI ethics and has been a recipient of several research scholarships. Her work contributes to the foundational understanding of bias in generative AI.
