# Genta Indra Winata

## Biographical Summary

Genta Indra Winata is currently at Capital One AI Foundations. Previously, he was a Senior Research Scientist at Bloomberg LP. He completed his Ph.D. at the Electronic and Computer Engineering Department and Center for AI Research (CAiRE) at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Institut Teknologi Bandung.

## Research & Impact

Winata's research focuses on multilingual NLP and code-switching:

- **Code-Switching Research**: Authored "The Decades Progress on Code-Switching Research in NLP: A Systematic Survey on Trends and Challenges" (Findings of ACL 2023). Found that while LLMs show potential in multilingual settings, they exhibit limited proficiency in code-switching contexts, and smaller fine-tuned models substantially outperform larger LLMs with prompting methods.

- **Multilingual Continual Learning**: Co-authored research on overcoming catastrophic forgetting in massively multilingual continual learning (Findings of ACL 2023).

- **Indonesian NLP**: Significant contributions to Indonesian NLP including IndoNLU and IndoRobusta benchmarks, studying robustness against diverse code-mixed Indonesian local languages.

His work addresses the reality that in multilingual communities, people use two or more languages interchangeably during conversation.
