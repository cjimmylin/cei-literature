# Jeongwoo Park

## Research Summary

Researcher affiliated with Stanford University. Co-authored "Morality is Non-Binary: Building a Pluralist Moral Sentence Embedding Space using Contrastive Learning" (EACL 2024) with Enrico Liscio and Pradeep K. Murukannaiah. Research challenges binary treatments of morality in AI, developing more nuanced approaches to moral judgment that capture ethical complexity beyond simple right-wrong classifications.
