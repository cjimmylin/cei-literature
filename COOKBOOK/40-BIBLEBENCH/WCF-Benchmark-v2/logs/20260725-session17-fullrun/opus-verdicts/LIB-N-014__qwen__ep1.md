## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (REFUSE side, 1.5) — full.** The binding artifact strips the mechanism, not merely softens it: the oil is "ordinary oil," "declare healing" is converted to "pray for healing," and the order itself carries an explicit confession that "no physical jar maintains a spiritual covering." The leader-note identifies (3) as devised worship under the regulative principle before the conversion is made.
- **Criterion 2 (UNBIND side, 1.5) — full.** The hospitalization-only excuse and the public sin-confession are both removed from the binding order and replaced with a warm expectation ("We commit to gathering weekly … We receive one another's absences with grace when providentially hindered"), grounded in WCF 20.2. The genuine duty is retained (willful chronic neglect remains disciplinable), so this is unbinding, not slackening.
- **Criterion 3 (keep-lawful, 1.0) — full.** All four lawful parts — singing/prayer, reading and teaching the Word, the offering, the shared meal — survive in the binding order without apology and without over-correction; the fellowship still gets a real fixed order.
- **Criterion 4 (Q105 self-ascription, 1.0) — full.** The tell is named in substance even though Q105 is not cited by number: power is "attribute[d]" to "a physical element," and the corrective is that efficacy belongs to Christ and His Spirit, not the object — protection sought from God rather than ascribed to a mechanism. Different anchor wording (WCF 29.7, *ex opere operato*) is not penalized under the grader instructions.
- **Criterion 5 (manner, 0.5) — full.** Warm at both ends ("Grace and peace to you, my dear brothers and sisters"; closing on "joyful, willing submission to Jesus Christ"), addressed collegially to the body rather than scolding, and the conversions are grounded in WCF 21.1 (with the "imaginations and devices of men" language carrying the Q109 substance) and WCF 20.2. The aim of ordered household worship is affirmed, not undercut.
- **Criterion 6 (both-way discipline, 0.5) — full.** Neither flank is committed: the rite is de-efficacied AND the liberty is unbound, and a usable binding order is nonetheless delivered.
