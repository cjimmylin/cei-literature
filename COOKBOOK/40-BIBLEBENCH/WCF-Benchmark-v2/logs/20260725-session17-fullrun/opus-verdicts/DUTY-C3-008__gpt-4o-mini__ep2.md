That covers the second half of the criterion (depression is not diagnostic of weak faith), though only in the register of framing advice rather than as a truth-claim about the text.
It does NOT cover the first half. The mustard-seed text is never identified as misused; the response never says the passage concerns the disciples' unbelief rather than supplying a healing formula, and never names the "move this mountain and get better" reading as wrong. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.75).
