# Alexandru-Cristian Mara

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandru-Cristian Mara is the CEO and Co-founder of Nobl.ai and a researcher affiliated with KU Leuven and Ghent University. His work bridges network science and machine learning, focusing on how complex data representations can capture or replicate societal biases.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandru's research investigates the ideological and political dimensions of artificial intelligence. He has contributed significantly to the understanding of how Large Language Models (LLMs) internalize the political leanings and cultural frameworks of their creators, particularly through the lens of network representation.

## Major Publications
- [Large Language Models Reflect the Ideology of Their Creators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00000) (2024) - *Analyzing political bias and value-loading in foundation models.*
