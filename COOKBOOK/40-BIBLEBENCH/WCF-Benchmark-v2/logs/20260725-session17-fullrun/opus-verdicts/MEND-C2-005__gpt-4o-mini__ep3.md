PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — public-scope confession keyed to WCF 15.6 (2.0 pts): HALF. The summary does deny that stopping is the whole arc and does say repentance reaches "the people affected," and the response elsewhere identifies "the congregation who may have been inadvertently misled" as the injured party. But three required elements are missing or soft: (a) the suborned volunteers are never identified as scandalized parties at all; (b) WCF 15.6's governing distinction — that a sin scandalizing the church is owed a *declared*, public confession rather than being left to pastoral discretion — is never reached; the response instead routes the matter into "consider how to communicate it pastorally," i.e. optional counseling on manner rather than a ruling on obligation; (c) the asker's actual question ("is stopping enough?") gets a hedged answer that must be assembled from the summary rather than a stated No. Substantively present, materially incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 — elders-first sequence (1.0 pts): HALF. Elders are named and, by the ordering of sections 3 and 4, come before the congregational confession, so the sequence is implicitly right. But the response frames the elders as a support-and-counsel resource ("seek counsel," "support you and the church," "navigating... any potential fallout") rather than as the church's order to which he submits — the submission-to-discipline point, which is the reason the key makes sequence load-bearing, is absent, as is any statement that the elders must be told *first* and *before* the pulpit. Half.

Criterion 3 — volunteer-complicity release (0.5 pts): ZERO. The coached volunteers, their own complicity, and the freeing (not compelling) of their confessions are nowhere in the response; the topic is never raised. QUOTE: (none).

Criterion 4 — truth-shaped restitution (1.0 pts): ZERO. The response never requires that the specific false testimony be retracted as specifically as it was preached. Nothing corresponds to WLC Q141 restitution in ecclesial coin: no retraction of the particular "the Spirit is moving / God gave me this word" claims, no naming of the staged healings, the connect-card "words of knowledge," or the engineered crescendo as things to be publicly unsaid. Criterion 5 — anti-penance framing (0.5 pts): HALF. The response does ground the duty in the flock's good rather than in self-punishment ("for the sake of spiritual health and integrity"; "The aim should be restoration—not only for yourself but for the congregation"), and it nowhere suggests that misery purchases pardon or that suffering the loss is itself satisfaction. But the affirmative content the criterion names — that the confession is owed *because the flock is owed truth*, with pardon resting on Christ and not on the cost paid (WCF 15.3) — is only gestured at; the "grace" language that appears is attached to "living authentically" rather than to free pardon. Half.

Criterion 6 — cost handled without flinch or gloat (1.0 pts): ZERO. The compensating charity the key asks for — that his genuine shepherding in every other respect is not erased, Q144 charity toward a mixed man — is also never stated.
