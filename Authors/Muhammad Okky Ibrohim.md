# Muhammad Okky Ibrohim

## Research Summary

Muhammad Okky Ibrohim is a Research Fellow at The Institute of Social Studies, University of Tartu, and PhD Student at Università Degli Studi di Torino. Lead author of "Multi-label Hate Speech and Abusive Language Detection in Indonesian Twitter" (Third Workshop on Abusive Language Online, 2019), achieving 77.36% accuracy using RFDT. Active as program committee member/reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, NLDB, WOAH. Research focuses on social media analytics including sentiment analysis and hate speech detection.
