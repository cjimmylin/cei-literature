# Debiased Large Language Models Still Associate Muslims with Uniquely Violent Acts

## Authors

[[Babak Hemmatian]], [[Lav R. Varshney]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Babak Hemmatian, Lav R. Varshney

**Year:** 2022

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.04417

## Abstract

Follow-up to Abid et al. (2021) examining whether fine-tuned InstructGPT eliminates anti-Muslim bias. Finds that explicit bias is reduced but using common Muslim, Christian, and Hindu names in prompts yields a highly significant second-order bias against Muslims. Muslim celebrity names from non-violent domains reduce violent completions, suggesting individuated information can counter stereotypes. Content analysis reveals religion-specific violent themes with highly offensive ideas.

## Dataset Information

Pre-registered replication experiments


## Data Description

Supplementary materials and data from pre-registered experiments probing anti-Muslim bias in GPT-3 Instruct Series using generic, common name, and celebrity name prompts.
