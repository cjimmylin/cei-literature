# Chunhua Liu

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Chunhua Liu completed her PhD in 2024, focusing on leveraging human word associations as a source of commonsense knowledge for NLP models. Her research bridges computational linguistics and cognitive psychology, with particular emphasis on how cultural differences shape behaviors and norms, and how these can be learned and aligned by large language models.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Cultural Differences in AI**: Investigating how cultural variations shape daily behaviors and norms, and how NLP models can learn to understand these differences
- **Moral Values in LLMs**: Using word associations as low-level representations to obtain more robust pictures of LLMs' moral reasoning
- **Commonsense Reasoning**: Incorporating human word associations as commonsense knowledge to improve neural model reasoning

### Significant Achievements
- Published at ACL 2025 on comparing moral values between Western English-speaking societies and LLMs
- Created large datasets of LLM-generated word associations resembling human word association patterns
- Developed novel approaches using word associations to assess moral values, avoiding issues with direct prompting
