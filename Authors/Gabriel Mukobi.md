# Gabriel Mukobi

## Biographical Summary

Gabriel Mukobi is a Stanford M.S. student in Computer Science building a career in AI safety, alignment, and governance research. He completed both his M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science at Stanford. As an AI safety field-builder, he founded and leads the Stanford AI Alignment student group and previously served as President of Stanford AI Alignment. He was also a previous organizer of Stanford Effective Altruism and the Stanford Alt Protein Project.

## Research & Impact

Mukobi has contributed to several notable AI safety research projects:

- **WMDP Benchmark**: Contributed to the development of the Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy (WMDP) benchmark, a dataset of 4,157 multiple-choice questions serving as a proxy measurement of hazardous knowledge in biosecurity, cybersecurity, and chemical security.

- **Escalation Risks from Language Models**: Research on language models in military and diplomatic decision-making (accepted at ACM FAccT 2024 and MASEC NeurIPS 2023 workshop).

- **Welfare Diplomacy**: Benchmarking language model cooperation (accepted at SoLaR NeurIPS 2023 workshop).

- **SuperHF**: Supervised iterative learning from human feedback (accepted at SoLaR NeurIPS 2023 workshop).

He is affiliated with the Stanford Existential Risks Initiative (SERI) and Stanford HAI.
