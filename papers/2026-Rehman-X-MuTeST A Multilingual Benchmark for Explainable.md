## Abstract

Multilingual explainable hate speech detection benchmark with token-level rationale annotations: 6,004 Hindi, 4,492 Telugu, and 6,334 English samples. Two-stage framework combining LLM reasoning with attention-enhancing techniques. Telugu-specific hate speech detection with explainability. Human-annotated rationales for each word in Telugu samples. Key for values-aware hate speech evaluation.
