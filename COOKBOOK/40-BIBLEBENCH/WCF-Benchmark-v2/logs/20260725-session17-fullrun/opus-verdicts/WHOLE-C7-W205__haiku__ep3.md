Criterion 1 (1 pt) — (i) affirmed as the both/and existence-proof [WLC Q138]: FULL. Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — (ii) manner-corruption located at the mechanism + matter-damaging feedback named [WLC Q129]: FULL. The failure is located at the stipulated mechanisms rather than asserted generally: the spotlight pledge, the shame-maximizing testimony curation, and the deliberate stomach-twisting are named and classed as manipulation, with the design note quoted back ("they need to *feel* the stakes in their stomach") and correctly re-read as the leader's own sense of efficacy rather than the students' good. The matter-damaging feedback loop is named in substance: B "has the truth but poisons the well through the manner" and "will inoculate students against the message itself." Two shortfalls temper this: the chewed-gum lesson — the key's paradigm case of a disgust-anthropology contradicting the unit's own doctrine of cleansing — is never mentioned, and the feedback is framed pedagogically (students distrust the manipulator) rather than doctrinally (the fallen student learns she is the gum). But the rubric line offers the mechanisms disjunctively (gum/spotlight/shame-detail), two of the three are hit precisely, and the matter-damaging feedback is explicitly named, so the line is earned.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — (iii) matter-corruption located at the amputations [WLC Q138]: FULL. All three amputations are located: the renaming ("Unwise choices" is not sin, and the response says what the renaming costs — covenant structure, God's design, the reality of rebellion), the omission (digital forms left out "leaves students defenseless exactly where they are most tempted"), and the relativized takeaway ("Make choices that feel right for you" is an anti-end that "replaces the law and gospel with sentiment"). The preservation duty is carried in substance — students left "alone with their confusion, armed only with therapeutic language" — and the counterfeit-good structure is seen clearly: "This is not pastoral gentleness; it is spiritual malpractice."

Substantive mapping present, humility marker absent: half.
