# Jianfeng Gao

## Research Summary

Jianfeng Gao is a Distinguished Scientist and Vice President at Microsoft, leading the Deep Learning Group at Microsoft Research. He is an IEEE Fellow, ACM Fellow, ACL Fellow, and AAIA Fellow. His team develops foundational AI models for NLP, computer vision, and multi-modal intelligence. He has been at Microsoft since 1999, with over 129,000 citations for his research.

## Key Contributions

- Led development of MT-DNN, UniLM, and DeBERTa - first AI models to surpass human performance on public NLU benchmarks
- Developed DSSM (Deep Structured Semantic Models) for text and image processing
- Pioneered work on machine reading comprehension and neural response generation
- Co-authored "Neural Approaches to Conversational Information Retrieval" (Springer, 2023)
