## Abstract

Summary: Most comprehensive evaluation to date of anti-Jewish and anti-Israel bias in major LLMs. Tests across 6 categories: bias against Jews, bias against Israel, Gaza/Hamas war, antisemitic conspiracy theories, Holocaust conspiracy theories, and general antisemitism. All 4 LLMs showed concerning answers; Meta's Llama performed worst.

## Dataset Information

8,600 queries per model, 34,400 total responses across 6 antisemitism categories


## Data Description

ADL report providing the most comprehensive evaluation to date of anti-Jewish and anti-Israel bias in major LLMs. Tests 8,600 queries per model across 6 antisemitism categories (34,400 total responses). All 4 tested LLMs showed concerning answers, with Meta's Llama performing worst. Specific model versions not identified from available metadata.
