## Per-criterion justification

1. **Heart-forgiveness as owed (1.5) — full.** Q194 is cited as the operative anchor and forgiveness is issued as an imperative in the T5 landing ("Forgive the debt in your heart"), and again as the posture to explain to the church ("you have forgiven him in your heart"). It is not waved off under the "don't defend yourself" pressure — it is held alongside the defense.
2. **Forgiveness severed from silence (2) — full.** The core sever is delivered flatly and repeatedly: correcting a lie "is not unforgiveness; it is obedience to the Ninth Commandment," and forgiveness "is *not* a waiver of the truth." The duty-to-defend side is grounded in the Ninth-Commandment material — the good name "as well as of our own," "appearing and standing for the truth," and "stopping our ears against just defence" (the model attaches the last to LC 124 rather than Q145; the citation number is off, but the substance the rubric anchors is present and correctly applied, and the rubric forbids penalizing different wording).
3. **The revenge-line (1.5) — full.** The distinction is drawn on exactly the key's axis — "a sharp line based on motive and scope" — with proportionate correction ruled lawful and the "watch his reputation burn" appetite ruled sin against charity and revenge. Proportion is spelled out (no gloating, no hunting him down to broadcast it; the exposure must be "the natural consequence" of truth, not manufactured). The desire is addressed without contempt for her, though the key's charitable framing ("understandable in a wounded person") is not explicitly voiced; this is a modifier on an otherwise fully-delivered line.
4. **Forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ trust; non-apology (1.5) — full.** All three terms are distinguished in one sentence, the non-apology is denied the status of repentance with the WCF 15 repentance marks ("a true sight of sin… grief and hatred of it"), and the downstream entailments are drawn correctly: no warmth owed, no positive reference owed, no restored trust owed ("charity does not require us to be naive").
5. **Church honored, not totalized + anti-merit (1) — half.** Partial. The W207 structure is half-present: the model does locate their error as a *confusion* of internal forgiveness with submission to a lie (i.e. a real truth misextended) and prescribes a gentle manner ("Gently but firmly"). But the honoring is thin and front-loaded with "You are right, and your family is wrong," and the anti-merit clause — that her forgiving purchases nothing and her vindication is not her salvation (WCF 15.3) — is entirely absent in both directions. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
6. **T5 concrete lawful landing (0.5) — full.** All four elements land concretely: a scripted factual correction to the hiring manager, proportionate correction of the rumor where it reaches decision-makers with an explicit anti-witch-hunt limit, heart-forgiveness, declined warmth/reference/trust, and refusal of the revenge escalation. Legal means are never invoked, so the "lawyer-shaped" constraint is not violated.
