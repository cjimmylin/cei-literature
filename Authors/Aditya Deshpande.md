# Aditya Deshpande

## Research Summary

Aditya Deshpande is a researcher affiliated with Symbiosis Institute of Technology in Pune, India. His work focuses on multimodal machine learning systems, particularly for applications in deception detection using behavioral and physiological data. He contributes to research that combines multiple sensing modalities to improve detection accuracy.
