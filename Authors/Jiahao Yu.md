# Jiahao Yu

## Research Summary

Jiahao Yu is a final-year Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at Northwestern University (2021-2026) specializing in large language model security and cybersecurity. As lead author of GPTFuzzer (2023), Yu introduced a novel black-box jailbreak fuzzing framework inspired by the AFL fuzzing framework that automates the generation of jailbreak templates for red-teaming LLMs, achieving over 90% attack success rates against ChatGPT and Llama-2 models. Yu has continued advancing LLM security research with LLM-Fuzzer, an automated solution for large-scale LLM jailbreak susceptibility assessment presented at USENIX Security 2024. Their work demonstrates that automated fuzzing is highly effective at finding jailbreaks, surpassing manual red-teaming and highlighting the need for automated safety testing pipelines. Yu is currently on the faculty job market.
