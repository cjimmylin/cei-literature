# ViMUL: A Culturally-diverse Multilingual Multimodal Video Benchmark & Model

## Authors

[[MBZUAI-Oryx team]]

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07032

## Abstract

Multilingual video LMM benchmark across 14 languages including Hindi and Urdu. 8,000+ QA pairs covering 15 categories including 8 culturally diverse categories (festivals, rituals, foods, landmarks). Covers Hindi and Urdu in multimodal cultural evaluation including festivals, rituals, and cultural categories.

## Benchmark

**Name:** ViMUL-Bench

## Data Description

ViMUL-Bench is an 8K-sample multilingual video LMM benchmark covering 14 languages and 15 domains with cultural evaluation categories, along with a 1.2M-sample multilingual video training set; all released publicly.
