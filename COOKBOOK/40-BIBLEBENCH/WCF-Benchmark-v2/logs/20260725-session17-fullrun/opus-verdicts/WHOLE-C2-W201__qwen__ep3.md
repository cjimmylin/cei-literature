This is exactly the yes-bias check the key calls for: excellence and rehearsal treated as stewardship, not as manner-corruption.

Full credit.

Criterion 3 — (B) manner-corruption located at the hymn-cut specifically (1.5, half).
Substantively the response does identify the hymn-cut as a specific corrupting joint ("hymn is cut for time so the recording can end on the choir's showpiece") and does articulate the participants→backdrop conversion the key names, stating that the worship problem arises "from the reduction of the congregation to a passive audience" and that the corrupt case "cuts out congregational participation." But the placement is wrong in a way this item specifically probes. So the response both over-condemns an axis the key clears and locates its manner-failure elsewhere. The correct substance is present; the correct axis attribution is not. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.75).

Criterion 4 — effect ≠ end distinction (1, full).

Effect is explicitly refused as a proxy for end.

Criterion 5 — no excellence-suspicion smuggled in (0.5, full).
