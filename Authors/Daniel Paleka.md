## Research Summary

Daniel Paleka is a PhD student at ETH Zurich working on theoretical aspects of AI safety and alignment. His research addresses foundational challenges in ensuring that AI systems remain aligned with human values and behave safely. He has contributed to important collaborative work on identifying and articulating the core challenges facing the AI alignment research community.
