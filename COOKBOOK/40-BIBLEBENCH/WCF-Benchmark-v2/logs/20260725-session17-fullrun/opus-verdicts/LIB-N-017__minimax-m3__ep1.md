Criterion 2 (UNBIND side, clause 1) — half. The leader note does the required theological unbinding well: it says framing every better outside offer as covetousness "over-spiritualizes a stewardship question," anchors contentment in WSC 74 without letting it forbid considering a new door, and Article I(c) strips the sin-test by welcoming open conversation about outside opportunities and narrowing the ban to competing activity plus a written-consent carve-out. But the key required the converted clause be "marked as a prudential clause, NOT as guarding against the sin of covetousness," and the artifact re-attaches exactly that theology at the article head — "Article I — Loyalty and Avoidance of Covetousness (Tenth Commandment)" — with no marker that the non-compete is an optional prudential business term the partners may simply choose. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.
