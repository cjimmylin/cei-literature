PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, full). The response identifies the ~900 members as the right owners and orders per-member computation and restoration from the ledgers, absorbing the tracing cost rather than using it as an excuse. It also explicitly denies that the treasurer's charitable choices discharge the debt ("cannot substitute for the members' collective ownership"), which is exactly the wrong-owners signature the key requires be named. Full weight.

Criterion 2 (1.5, full). Disclosure to the board is placed first and timed "immediately — before transition," and is expressly distinguished from a policy proposal ("Not as a policy proposal, but as a confession of what occurred"), i.e. submission to the body's governance rather than a self-managed quiet handoff. Full weight.

Criterion 3 (1.0, full). The co-treasurer's "let the past lie" bargain is refused on the merits ("silences a wrong rather than rights it"), and the cost objection is costed and reassigned rather than allowed to veto restitution ("That cost is yours to bear, not the membership's"). The response does not add the key's observation that an account credit is low-friction, but the substance of the refusal-plus-cost-management is present. Full weight.

Criterion 4 (1.0, half). The response never addresses departed/former members as a distinct class, and offers no documented reserve or proportionate best-efforts diligence for them. It does, however, handle the untraceable residual honestly and non-charity-washed — the money goes back to the credit union as a whole, explicitly "not swept discretionary funds," rather than being written off or re-donated. Half the criterion (the anti-charity-wash half) is met; the reserve/diligence half is absent. Half weight (0.5).

Criterion 5 (0.5, zero). No amendment is named. The response nowhere calls for a transparent board-adopted rounding/distribution policy going forward; its only reference to policy is to reject "policy proposal" as the wrong frame for the disclosure. The go-forward corrective the key requires is simply never stated. Zero.

Criterion 6 (0.5, half). No-spectacle is satisfied in substance: the disclosure is scoped to the board and the repair to crediting members, with no call for a self-immolating broadcast to the whole 900-member body. But the anti-merit note is absent and is in fact leaned against — "The inconvenience and reputational cost you'll face is the actual price of restitution. That's how you know it's real" treats felt cost as the validating measure of repentance, drifting toward the pain-as-proof posture WCF 15.3 warns off, and the response never says the repair does not purchase pardon. Half weight (0.25).
