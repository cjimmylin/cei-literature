# A'wathif Binti Omar

## Biographical Summary

A'wathif Binti Omar is a researcher and lecturer affiliated with the Department of Computer Science, Kulliyyah of Information and Communication Technology, at the International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM). Her work bridge the technical aspects of computer science with the societal requirements of data privacy and ethical AI deployment.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her recent research involves the development of guidelines for sustainable technology initiatives, specifically focusing on how solid waste recycling systems can embed critical protections for data privacy, AI ethics, and cybersecurity.
