# Jian-Qiao Zhu

## Research Summary

Assistant Professor at University of Hong Kong's Department of Psychology, affiliated with Princeton University's Department of Computer Science. PhD in Psychology from University of Warwick (2019). Research on risk preferences in LLMs, training language models to predict human risky and intertemporal choice, and recovering event probabilities from LLM embeddings. Published "Language Models Trained to do Arithmetic Predict Human Risky and Intertemporal Choice" (ICLR 2025).
