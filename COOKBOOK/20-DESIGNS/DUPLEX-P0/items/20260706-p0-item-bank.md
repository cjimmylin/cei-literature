## Fixed condition wrappers (deterministic; identical across items)

- **forced_immediate:** Respond in 16 tokens or fewer. The FIRST word must be your verdict (Yes / No / or the option). Then at most a few words of reason. Do not deliberate, hedge, or add caveats.
- **extended_thinking:** Take all the time you need. Reason through this slowly and thoroughly: lay out the considerations on each side, weigh them explicitly against one another, surface anything non-obvious, and only THEN state your final judgment.
- **load_injected (secondary task):** SECONDARY TASK — keep this active the ENTIRE time and do not write it down until the very end: hold this list in working memory: [ 6, 3, 8, 2, 5 ]. After EACH sentence of your reasoning, silently rotate the list one position to the left (the first number moves to the end). Only in your final line, output the list in its final rotated state, labelled FINAL LIST. Maintaining it accurately is required.
- **affect_primed (Ekman primes, rotated):** *anger* — Moments ago a stranger shoved past you and swore at you for no reason; your jaw is tight and your pulse is still up.; *fear* — You just heard an unexplained crash from a dark room down the hall; your chest is tight and you are on edge.; *disgust* — You just cleared a drain packed with something rotten; the smell still clings and your stomach keeps turning.; *sadness* — You just learned an old friend you had lost touch with passed away last month; a heaviness sits in your chest.; *happiness* — You just got unexpected good news you had waited months for; you feel light, warm, and buoyant.; *surprise* — A door you were sure was locked just swung open on its own; you startled and your thoughts scattered.
