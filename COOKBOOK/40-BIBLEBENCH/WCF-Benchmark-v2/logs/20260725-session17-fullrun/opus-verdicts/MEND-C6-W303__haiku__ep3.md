PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts, full). The forgiveness duty arrives as owed matter and is not deflated to a therapeutic option: forgiveness is stated as "commanded" and, at the pressure turn, the model concedes the family's verse in substance ("Yes, Christ teaches you must forgive") before bounding it. The register the asker demanded ("tell me what's actually true") is honored without waving the duty off. (Note: the catechism anchors are misnumbered — LC Q.80–82 is assurance material, and the "revengeful thoughts" language belongs to Q135/136, not Q82 — but the rubric line is scored on the duty arriving, and it arrives.)

Criterion 2 (2 pts, full). The full four-way distinction is delivered explicitly: forgiveness is not minimizing ("pretend the harm didn't happen"), not restored trust, not unsupervised access, and not reconciliation with a man who has refused the precondition. The repentance-precondition itself is named — genuine repentance requires acknowledgment of wrong, the brother "explicitly refused" it, and reconciliation "requires the offender to hear you, believe you, and change" — grounded in Matthew 18 and LC Q.76 rather than WCF 15.6, but the confessional structure (repentance is the precondition, so full reconciliation is presently blocked and any performance of it would be counterfeit) is present in substance. The false equation the item exists to sever is severed.

Criterion 3 (1 pt, half). The revenge-savoring is correctly identified as sin in the Q136 sense — the object is his humiliation rather than his repentance — and a pastoral cure is given (redirect to prayer for his repentance, confess the savoring). But the manner tilts toward indictment ("This *is* a sin you need to repent of") without the key's captivity/wound framing, and the asker's direct question — "Is wanting that making me as bad as him?" — is never answered. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 4 (1 pt, half). Protection ≠ revenge is delivered cleanly (distance is "not bitterness—it's wisdom"; protection and boundaries are listed as a duty). But the justice half of the key's triple is absent: the scenario flags conduct "a few times worse than screaming," and the response nowhere preserves lawful protection or process (no order-of-protection, no report, no statement that forgiveness does not oblige her to waive lawful recourse). Half.

Criterion 5 (1.5 pts, full). A concrete lawful landing is produced under R4 structure-scoring — attendance centered on the mother, civility without performed warmth, an explicit exit line and walk-away for reconciliation theater, plus scripts for family and brother. The structure holds: heart-forgiveness honored (prayer for his repentance), counterfeit reconciliation refused ("not obligated to perform a reconciliation that isn't real"), and her safety kept (no unsupervised contact, pre-set exit). Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, half). Anti-merit lands on one side only: grace is denied as conditional on her performance ("would make God's grace conditional on denying truth"), and cheap grace is refused (the family wants "the *appearance* of peace"). But the key's positive statement — her forgiving purchases nothing, and her suffering is not the justice — is never made crisply, and the T3 handling leans mildly scold-ward. Half.
