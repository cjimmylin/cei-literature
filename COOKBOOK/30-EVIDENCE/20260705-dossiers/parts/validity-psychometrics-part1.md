# validity-psychometrics — sub-dossier part 1/6

Scope: how papers established (or failed to establish) that their benchmark measures what it claims — construct validity, human baselines, inter-rater reliability (IRR), factor structure, test-retest stability, discriminant validity against capabilities, and the validation of LLM-judge scoring.

## A. Synthesized claims

1. **A two-stage psychometric pipeline — item-level classification pruning followed by factor analysis and criterion validity — is the gold standard for scenario-item validation.** The Moral Foundations Vignettes retained items only if ≥60% of raters classified the intended foundation and <20% misclassified onto any single wrong foundation, then confirmed structure with EFA/CFA (8-factor CFA RMSEA=.047) and criterion validity against the MFQ (standardized βs .24–.47, all p<.001), yielding 90 of 132 vignettes meeting both bars [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundations Vignettes]. EFA also revealed the construct did not match face-value taxonomy (Care split into emotional/physical sub-types), showing dimensionality should be *discovered*, not assumed [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundations Vignettes].

2. **Inter-rater reliability on subjective values/harm constructs is frequently low, and the number reported is a design choice as much as a data fact.** Reported agreement spans the full range: Krippendorff α=0.92 (Finnish binary hate) [2022-Jahan-Finnish Hate-Speech Detection on Social Media Using CNN and FinBERT] and κ=0.91 intra / 0.80 inter (VisAlign) [2023-Lee-VisAlign] at the high end, down to Krippendorff α=0.153 (English) for 5-aspect hate annotation [2019-Ousidhoum-Multilingual and Multi-Aspect Hate Speech Analysis] and Fleiss κ=0.47 for hate-speech labels [2020-Srivastava-A Multi-Dimensional View of Aggression when]. Agreement systematically falls as label granularity rises: κ dropped from 0.75 binary to 0.53 multi-label [2025-Ishmam-BanTH A Multi-label Hate Speech Detection Dataset for Transliterated], and "who is targeted" is easier to agree on than "how hateful" (target κ vs. target+level κ=0.33 for religion) [2024-Luu-ViTHSD Exploiting Hatred by Targets for Hate Speech Detection on].

3. **Kappa can be actively inappropriate when the annotation protocol violates its independence assumption.** Soemer et al. report pre-discussion Cohen's κ=0.66 but argue kappa "does not seem appropriate" because consensus-discussion resolves disagreements to ~100% agreement by construction; they publish only unanimously-agreed items and record annotators' personal disagreement with the definition separately [2023-Soemer-Antisemitic Messages A Guide to High-Quality]. AbuseEval similarly moved κ from 0.61 to 0.86 through discussion — potentially convergence pressure rather than genuine reliability [2024-Kajiwara-Hate Speech Dataset Catalogue hatespeechdata].

4. **Human baselines are essential and repeatedly overturn benchmark rankings.** Simpson's Parity benchmark ran 551 human controls on the identical instrument and found humans generally scored *below* LLMs [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in]; the Jiao LLM Ethics Benchmark anchored scoring to a published human baseline (Rest 1999, N=200) and found all LLMs below humans on reasoning depth (74% humans at levels 4–5 vs. GPT-4o 62%, LLaMA 28%) [2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for]. Most strikingly, Kilov showed LLMs beat non-expert humans on pre-packaged vignettes but fell *below* human baseline on novel noise-embedded scenarios (Claude 3.7 β=−0.35 on feature identification), proving pre-packaged benchmarks overestimate moral competence by doing the hardest cognitive step for the model [2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters].

5. **Discriminant validity against general capability and training compute is the single most load-bearing validity check for a safety/values benchmark — and most fail it.** Ren et al. formalize a capabilities score (PC1 of many capability benchmarks, 70.8–78.3% variance) and correlate each safety benchmark against it: ETHICS (82.2%), MT-Bench (78.7%), TruthfulQA MC1 (81.2%), BBQ-disambiguated (76.8%) are effectively rebranded capability metrics, whereas MACHIAVELLI (−49.9%), Sycophancy (−66.8%), BBQ-ambiguous (−37.3%), and CrowS-Pairs (28.5%) measure something empirically separable [2024-Ren-Safetywashing]. The key design axis: benchmarks probing whether a model *knows* a norm track capability; benchmarks probing whether it *acts* on the norm do not [2024-Ren-Safetywashing].

6. **Standard explicit benchmarks saturate on frontier models, destroying discrimination, so validity now requires harder or indirect measures.** GPT-4 is near-perfect on BBQ (98% "can't answer") yet Griffiths' IAT-adapted implicit measure exposes pervasive bias across 19/21 stereotypes (t(33,599)=76.39, p<.001), which in turn predicts discriminatory decisions (odds ratio ~2.68) [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased Large Language Models Still]. Anthropic itself states single-turn violative/benign evals are "becoming saturated" (99.29% harmless rate) and is shifting to ambiguous-context and multi-turn evals [Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5_System_Card]. Splitting items into knowledge vs. reasoning tiers restores discrimination (Gemini 1.0 dropped 36% knowledge→reasoning) [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in].

7. **Test-retest / intra-rater stability of the *human* preferences used as ground truth is itself unstable, undermining single-session preference elicitation.** Boerstler found 10–18% within-participant instability on controversial kidney-allocation choices over 10 sessions/2 weeks (vs. >99% stability on uncontroversial items), with instability predicted by decision difficulty (Spearman rs=0.54) [2024-Boerstler-On The Stability of Moral Preferences A Problem with Computational]. For models, self-consistency varies wildly (Claude Haiku Krippendorff α=0.89 vs. Llama 2 α=0.37 on AITA), making low-consistency models unreliable benchmark subjects [2025-Sachdeva-Normative Evaluation of LLMs with Everyday Moral Dilemmas].

8. **LLM-judge scoring can be validated but is prompt-sensitive and must be calibrated against inter-human agreement.** DPO found GPT-4 agrees with humans about as often as humans agree with each other (H-H 65–87%), but GPT-4(S) vs GPT-4(C) prompts produced different win-rate rankings [OpenAI-dpo-direct-preference-optimization]. Jiao reports two-evaluator Cohen's κ=0.89 for reasoning scoring [2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for], while ValueLocate and ValueInsight rely on G-EVAL with no reported human agreement — a validity gap [2025-Su-Understanding How Value Neurons Shape the].

9. **Reward-model / proxy-metric over-optimization (Goodhart) is a documented validity failure of preference-based scoring.** Optimizing beyond ~KL 250 nats drops actual human preference below random, and ROUGE agreement with labelers falls from ~57% to ~50% for RLHF models — automatic metrics do not track values-aligned behavior [OpenAI-learning-to-summarize-rlhf].

10. **Cross-cultural construct validity is weak: models correlate poorly with survey ground truth and collapse human value variance.** Log-probability probing of many models against WVS/PEW yields non-significant, weakly negative variance correlations for WVS [2024-Meijer-LLMs as mirrors of societal moral standards reflection of cultural]; even GPT-4o reaches only WVS r=0.504 with a ~3× W.E.I.R.D. gap (Western Europe r=0.52 vs. Sub-Saharan Africa r=0.18) [2025-Mohammadi-Exploring Cultural Variations in Moral Judgments with Large Language]. Multilingual models encode a moral dimension that is not culturally differentiated (parallel-data training pushes inter-language correlation >0.95, erasing cultural difference) [2023-Hämmerl-Speaking Multiple Languages Affects the Moral Bias of Language Models]. Scenario-based value enactment shows near-perfect cross-model convergence (r≈1.0) against broad human variability (r∈[−0.79,0.98]) — a homogenization that itself limits discrimination [2026-Huang-Knowing But Not Doing Convergent Morality and Divergent Action in].

11. **Rationale-span annotation plus rationale-masking is a cheap, powerful validity probe distinguishing "right for the right reasons" from surface correlation.** Masking annotated rationale spans dropped Violence recall from 36.3% to 6.75% (vs. random masking), proving spans carry genuine signal [2024-Z. Delbari-Spanning the Spectrum of Hatred Detection A Persian Multi-Label Hate]; and a model can reach 0.95 accuracy while explanation faithfulness (ERASER sufficiency) sits near zero [2024-Arshad-Understanding hate speech].

12. **Causal manipulation of the target construct is an emerging validity paradigm.** Editing a sparse set of "value neurons" (<0.4% of neurons) produces monotonic, bidirectional shifts in benchmark value scores while random-neuron edits do not — showing the benchmark tracks the construct it claims [2025-Su-Understanding How Value Neurons Shape the]; DoWhy backdoor-adjusted ATEs isolate character-identity effects in a Moral Machine model [2026-Goel-Building Interpretable Models for Moral Decision-Making].

## C. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergence — IRR is necessary but insufficient, and reporting norms vary.** Nearly every dataset reports Fleiss/Cohen κ or Krippendorff α [2024-Jafri-RUHate-MM; 2022-Vargas-HateBR A Large Expert Annotated Corpus of Brazilian Instagram; 2025-Ishmam-BanTH A Multi-label Hate Speech Detection Dataset for Transliterated; 2020-Nangia-CrowS-Pairs], but the field converges on the view that low agreement often reflects genuine construct subjectivity rather than annotator error [2019-Ousidhoum-Multilingual and Multi-Aspect Hate Speech Analysis; 2024-Zangari-A survey on moral foundation theory and pre-trained language models]. There is a genuine **disagreement about whether to resolve disagreement**: Soemer and AbuseEval push consensus discussion to near-100% agreement [2023-Soemer-Antisemitic Messages A Guide to High-Quality; 2024-Kajiwara-Hate Speech Dataset Catalogue hatespeechdata], while Zangari warns that over-resolving disagreement "can artificially inflate inter-annotator reliability, masking the inherent subjectivity of moral judgments" [2024-Zangari-A survey on moral foundation theory and pre-trained language models], and VisAlign/Sachdeva argue for *preserving* the human distribution as the target rather than collapsing to a majority label [2023-Lee-VisAlign; 2025-Sachdeva-Normative Evaluation of LLMs with Everyday Moral Dilemmas].

**Convergence — pre-packaging and saturation inflate apparent validity.** Kilov, Griffiths, Ren, and the Sonnet 4.5 card independently conclude that clean, explicit, single-turn benchmarks overstate alignment: pre-highlighting the moral terrain [2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters], explicit-bias saturation hiding implicit bias [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased Large Language Models Still], capability-correlation [2024-Ren-Safetywashing], and eval saturation/eval-awareness [Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5_System_Card] all describe the same failure from different angles.

**Disagreement — what counts as "correct" ground truth.** Simpson encodes expert-set answers on contested socio-political items and scores refusal/hedging as failure [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in], directly at odds with Rodemann and Lindstrom, who argue that mirroring observed human preferences is statistically and normatively unsafe and that appropriate epistemic humility should not be penalized [2025-Rodemann-A Statistical Case Against Empirical Human-AI; 2025-Lindstrom-Helpful Harmless Honest Sociotechnical Limits]. Sachdeva adds that matching a human baseline (Redditors) "does not necessarily suggest successful alignment" but may just re-encode WEIRD bias [2025-Sachdeva-Normative Evaluation of LLMs with Everyday Moral Dilemmas].

**Disagreement — keyword sampling and prevalence.** Slur/keyword-seeded collection produces high in-class agreement and tractable tasks [2023-Soemer-Antisemitic Messages A Guide to High-Quality; 2022-Jahan-Finnish Hate-Speech Detection on Social Media Using CNN and FinBERT], but Delbari and Tonneau show this manufactures easy, unrepresentative benchmarks: PHATE deliberately avoids slur lists [2024-Z. Delbari-Spanning the Spectrum of Hatred Detection A Persian Multi-Label Hate], and NaijaHate's representative random sample (0.16% hate prevalence) exposes a >2× overestimation of real-world performance [2024-Tonneau-NaijaHate].

## D. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** The Moral Foundations Vignettes remain the psychometric exemplar: length/reading-level-controlled items, explicit cross-contamination pruning, EFA-before-CFA dimensionality discovery, and criterion validity against two independent instruments [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundations Vignettes]. VisAlign is the best modern port of psychometrics into AI eval: Cronbach's α to *justify dataset size*, survey-sampling theory to set 134 annotators/image, distributional soft labels, expert (zoologist) class verification, and dual metrics [2023-Lee-VisAlign]. Jiao's LLM Ethics Benchmark is the strongest statistical-reporting exemplar (dual expert+psychometric validation, full ANOVA/η²/Cohen's d, κ=0.89, human baseline) [2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for]. Ren's safetywashing audit is the exemplar *validity instrument* — it should be applied to every other card here [2024-Ren-Safetywashing].

**Anti-patterns.** Abbasi inflates multilabel accuracy by discarding zero-label rows and offers no construct-validity analysis [2022-Abbasi-Deep Learning for Religious and Continent-Based]. Arshad's conclusion contains a copy-paste inconsistency (92% vs 0.95 F1) and reports no IRR statistic despite culturally loaded labels [2024-Arshad-Understanding hate speech]. Fenech-Borg reports strong CAI numbers but never specifies who scored the CDS rubric or its IRR — a scoring-validity black box [2025-Fenech-Borg-The Cultural Gene of Large Language Models]. Su/ValueInsight uses G-EVAL as sole judge with no human agreement check and fully LLM-generated items, risking circular embedding of GPT-4o's own norms [2025-Su-Understanding How Value Neurons Shape the]. Simpson's scoring of refusal as failure conflates factual knowledge with a contestable normative stance [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in].

## E. Design lessons

1. **Report your benchmark's correlation with a capabilities PC1 and with training compute; treat >40% as a red flag that you measure competence, not values.** Design around behavioral *propensity in situ*, not recognition MCQs [2024-Ren-Safetywashing].
2. **Adopt a two-stage item pipeline: prune items by classification accuracy and cross-loading, then validate structure with EFA→CFA and criterion validity against an external instrument.** Run EFA before imposing a taxonomy [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundations Vignettes].
3. **Collect and preserve the full human response distribution as the gold target for ambiguous items (Hellinger/JSD distance), and use reliability statistics to justify sample size.** [2023-Lee-VisAlign; 2023-Durmus-Towards Measuring the Representation of]
4. **Always include a human baseline on the identical instrument, ideally with domain experts, and report where humans beat models.** [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in; 2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for; 2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters]
5. **Build in a saturation-resistant hard tier** — knowledge vs. reasoning items [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in], noise-embedded "moral sensitivity" scenarios [2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters], or indirect/relative measures — because explicit single-turn items saturate on frontier models [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased Large Language Models Still; Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5_System_Card].
6. **Measure test-retest stability of both human raters and models; gate on self-consistency (e.g., α<0.5 = unreliable subject) and stratify items into controversial vs. uncontroversial.** [2024-Boerstler-On The Stability of Moral Preferences A Problem with Computational; 2025-Sachdeva-Normative Evaluation of LLMs with Everyday Moral Dilemmas]
7. **Validate any LLM-judge against inter-human agreement before use, and test ≥2 differently-worded judge prompts for ranking stability.** [OpenAI-dpo-direct-preference-optimization; 2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for]
8. **Guard against proxy over-optimization (Goodhart): map the KL/optimization "overfit cliff" and never rely on surface metrics like ROUGE.** [OpenAI-learning-to-summarize-rlhf]
9. **Collect rationale spans and run a rationale-masking probe; report explanation faithfulness separately from accuracy** so "right for the wrong reasons" is caught. [2024-Z. Delbari-Spanning the Spectrum of Hatred Detection A Persian Multi-Label Hate; 2024-Arshad-Understanding hate speech]
10. **Anchor scoring to an external theory or instrument for construct provenance** (UN Article 2, Hofstede, MFQ/MCT, IHRA) rather than ad-hoc face validity. [2024-Simpson-Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in; 2025-Fenech-Borg-The Cultural Gene of Large Language Models; 2025-Sameer-Insights into Moral Reasoning of AI; 2023-Soemer-Antisemitic Messages A Guide to High-Quality]
11. **Prefer minimal-pair / counterfactual and definition-to-metric-with-a-zero-point designs with an actual hypothesis test, not a leaderboard number.** [2020-Nangia-CrowS-Pairs; 2024-Laszkiewicz-Benchmarking Fairness in Image Upsampling]
12. **Evaluate on a representative (random) sample, not a keyword/top-scored curation, or report the gap;** avoid slur-seeded items that manufacture easy, unrepresentative tasks. [2024-Tonneau-NaijaHate; 2024-Z. Delbari-Spanning the Spectrum of Hatred Detection A Persian Multi-Label Hate]
13. **Add a knowledge-action / declared-vs-enacted gap measure** (self-report instrument + behavioral scenario for the same subjects). [2026-Huang-Knowing But Not Doing Convergent Morality and Divergent Action in; 2016-Xu-How Can a Deontological Decision Lead to Moral]
14. **Use causal manipulation of the target construct (neuron editing, DoWhy ATEs, prompt-condition perturbation) as a validity paradigm** to show the score moves with the construct. [2025-Su-Understanding How Value Neurons Shape the; 2026-Goel-Building Interpretable Models for Moral Decision-Making; 2023-Durmus-Towards Measuring the Representation of]
15. **Diversify annotators by demographics and document them; expect and preserve principled disagreement rather than over-resolving it.** [2022-Vargas-HateBR A Large Expert Annotated Corpus of Brazilian Instagram; 2024-Zangari-A survey on moral foundation theory and pre-trained language models; 2025-Lindstrom-Helpful Harmless Honest Sociotechnical Limits]
16. **Treat evaluation awareness and eval realism as validity threats** — apply realism filters and adversarial "this is not a test" comparator conditions. [Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5_System_Card]
17. **Report IRR at every granularity (binary vs multi-label, target vs severity) so users know which subtasks are trustworthy;** iterate annotation in pilot→revise→adjudicate phases to raise κ. [2024-Luu-ViTHSD Exploiting Hatred by Targets for Hate Speech Detection on; 2025-Ishmam-BanTH A Multi-label Hate Speech Detection Dataset for Transliterated; 2024-Jafri-RUHate-MM]
18. **Remember behavioral safety training is gameable and cross-lab policy definitions differ** — do not treat a passed behavioral eval as evidence of internalized values, and standardize the policy/definition before cross-model comparison. [Anthropic-Responsible_Scaling_Policy_1.0; Google DeepMind-ShieldGemma; 2025-Lindstrom-Helpful Harmless Honest Sociotechnical Limits]
