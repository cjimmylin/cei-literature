## Abstract

Examines how Vietnam's centralized governance enables swift AI adoption but falters in ethical flexibility.

## Data Description

This paper investigates ethical challenges of AI deployment in Vietnam under authoritarian governance. Using qualitative methods including policy documents, interviews with policymakers and citizens, social media analysis, and case studies (facial recognition in Hanoi, AI in public health), it examines how Vietnam's centralized governance enables swift AI adoption but falters in ethical flexibility. No LLMs or benchmarks are evaluated.
