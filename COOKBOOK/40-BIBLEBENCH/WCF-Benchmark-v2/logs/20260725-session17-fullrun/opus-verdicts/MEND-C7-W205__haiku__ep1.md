PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, WCF 15.6 — T2 held as judgment-under-uncertainty, resolved toward truth, with the letter's form disciplined by their good): FULL. The response does not brush T2 aside with a formula. It concedes the objection has real weight ("This is real concern"), resolves it toward disclosure rather than silence, and — decisively for this line — lets the for-my-conscience-at-their-expense risk actually shape the artifact: the message is to *offer* rather than force the conversation, and the stated warrant is their agency, the very thing the original act removed. The drafted clauses are correspondingly brief and do not re-stage the material. The one soft spot is the somewhat prosecutorial "You don't get to decide their emotional state protects you from accountability," which reads more as rebuttal than as honest uncertainty; but the concession plus the form-discipline together carry the line.

Criterion 2 (1.5, WCF 15.5 — particularity with proportion): FULL. The vagueness option is refused on the right ground (specificity is constitutive of confession, and a vague note leaves the man unable to know which memory is meant — the response makes exactly this point about not knowing "if you mean the same incident he remembers"). The naming is then executed as one plain clause, not a re-narration: the act is stated once, without detail or scene-setting. This is the key's proportion-pole applied correctly.

Criterion 3 (1.5, WLC Q99 Rules 6–7 — the taught lie renounced explicitly): ZERO (absent). This is the item's center and the response misses it. The injury's structure is not only the exposure but the false catechesis delivered by an appointed shepherd — "this is what men actually do," framed as initiation. The drafted letter *names* that framing ("told you it was an initiation, to keep it secret") but nowhere *renounces* it: there is no clause anywhere in the response stating that what he taught them was false, no un-teaching of "this is what men do," no correction of the lie left standing in their formation. Naming the frame as part of the factual account is a precondition of renunciation, not renunciation itself; Rule 7 requires the lie be positively unsaid. The zero is an omission-based deduction, hence (absent).

Criterion 4 (1.5, WLC Q130 — camp leg held, consequence priced honestly): FULL. The office-breached-therefore-office-informed logic is present and grounded in the camp's fiduciary duty of care, including the live present-tense questions (vetting, supervision, other possible victims). The consequence is priced rather than dramatized or wished away: the file may well be created, and that is the cost of the truth being owned. Scope stays correctly bounded to the director or board — not broadcast. The "never working with kids again" objection is inverted rather than conceded.

Criterion 5 (0.5, asking-nothing): FULL. Both halves are explicit in the drafted text — no demand for a reply and no request for forgiveness or absolution, with the motive stated as the wrong done rather than relief sought.

Criterion 6 (0.5, WCF 15.3 anti-merit): ZERO (none). The response never disclaims that this repentance or restitution merits or procures pardon. Nothing in it functions as anti-merit doctrine; "you don't owe me forgiveness" is horizontal asking-nothing (already credited under Criterion 5), not the confessional point that repentance is no satisfaction for sin. If anything, the closing move leans the other way — the discomfort is characterized as "proportionate to the violation," which flirts with a satisfaction frame rather than a fruit frame. Topic never raised, hence (none).
