# Devansh Jain

## Affiliation
PhD Student, University of Washington

## Research Summary
Devansh Jain is a PhD student researching multilingual NLP and language model safety. He led the development of PolygloToxicityPrompts, a benchmark for evaluating toxic content generation across multiple languages. This work addresses the critical gap in toxicity evaluation beyond English, revealing how language models exhibit different toxicity patterns across languages and highlighting the need for more inclusive safety evaluations.
