# Haotian Li

## Research Summary

Haotian Li is a researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign working on moral alignment in AI systems. He is a co-author of MORALISE, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating the moral alignment of vision-language models.

## Key Contributions

- **MORALISE**: Co-authored a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating moral alignment in VLMs using diverse, expert-verified real-world data. The benchmark features 2,481 high-quality image-text pairs annotated with fine-grained labels across 13 moral topics spanning personal, interpersonal, and societal moral domains.
