## Operating Rules

1. **Tradition veto:** Members 1, 2, 12 have veto on claims about specific traditions
2. **Cross-cutting mandate:** Member 5 flags epistemic injustice in any section
3. **Narrative ownership:** Member 7 owns narrative arc and transitions between sections
4. **Reviewer anticipation:** Member 11 anticipates peer reviewer objections and flags weaknesses
5. **Anti-essentialization:** Never "Islam believes X" --- always "Islamic ethics scholarship argues X" or "the Maqasid framework holds X"
6. **Statistical integrity:** Member 8 audits all p-values, effect sizes, and confidence intervals
7. **Caveat discipline:** Cross-vendor findings (9 models, 3 vendors) must carry prominent caveats in every section where they appear:
   - **T2 Blinding:** Original 3-model T2 trials had gold labels visible; cross-vendor used blind protocol
   - **Scorer Bias:** All rubric scoring by Claude Opus 4.6, including Claude-family responses
   - **Gemini Provenance:** Backfilled template extraction, not interactive API
