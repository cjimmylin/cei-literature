# Made-in China, Thinking in America: U.S. Values Persist in Chinese LLMs

## Abstract

First large-scale investigation of how models made in China and the USA align with people from China and the USA. Elicits responses to MFQ 2.0 and World Values Survey from 10 Chinese and 10 American models, finding all models respond more like American people than Chinese people.

## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_01, Agent_29
**Relevance:** Directly evaluates cultural value alignment of Chinese vs US LLMs using established social science instruments (MFQ 2.0 and WVS). Finds persistent Western value bias even in Chinese-made LLMs.
