# Enmeng Lu

## Biographical Summary

Enmeng Lu is a Professor at Beijing Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of AI, cognitive science, and brain-inspired computing. Her research includes studying moral cognition and developing brain-inspired AI systems that can better understand and align with human values. She contributes to research on value alignment in AI systems, drawing on insights from cognitive science.

## Research Interests

- **Brain-Inspired AI**: Developing AI systems inspired by human cognitive processes
- **Moral Cognition**: Understanding how humans process moral information
- **Value Alignment**: Aligning AI systems with human values using cognitive insights
- **Computational Neuroscience**: Modeling neural processes computationally
