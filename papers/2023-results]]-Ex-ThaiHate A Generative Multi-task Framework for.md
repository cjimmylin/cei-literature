## Abstract

First benchmark hate speech corpus in Thai with four annotations: hate, sentiment, emotion, and rationales (explainability). Proposes GenX, a unified generative framework that redefines multi-task hate speech detection as text-to-text generation. First annotated Thai hate speech corpus with explainability. Includes rationale annotations showing which phrases justify hate speech classification. Directly relevant to Thai toxicity and values evaluation.
