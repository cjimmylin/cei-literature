# Alice Oh

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alice Oh is a Professor in the School of Computing at KAIST and the Director of the KAIST Institute for Artificial Intelligence. She holds an S.B. from MIT, an M.S. from CMU, and a Ph.D. from MIT. She is a global leader in natural language processing and computational social science, currently serving as the President of SIGDAT. She has held major leadership roles in the machine learning community, including Program Chair for ICLR and NeurIPS.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alice’s research sits at the intersection of NLP and computational social science. She focuses on using large-scale social media data to study human behavior and social dynamics. Her recent work emphasizes the multilingual and multicultural dimensions of LLMs, developing benchmarks for culturally aware AI evaluation and methods to mitigate social bias in language models.

### Significant Achievements
She has been instrumental in organizing the top venues in AI, serving as General Chair for NeurIPS 2023 and ACM FAccT 2022. She is a pioneer in "Socially Grounded NLP," advocating for AI systems that understand diverse cultural contexts and linguistic nuances. Her group at KAIST is globally recognized for its contributions to AI safety and multicultural empathy in AI.
