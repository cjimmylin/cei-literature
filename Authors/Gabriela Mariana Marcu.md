# Gabriela Mariana Marcu

## Biographical Summary

Gabriela Mariana Marcu is an Associate Professor in the Department of Psychology at Lucian Blaga University of Sibiu, Romania. She is also affiliated with Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy in Bucharest, Romania, and serves as a Scientific Researcher at the Center for Neuroscience Research at Sibiu Psychiatric Hospital.

## Research & Impact

Marcu's research focuses on the intersection of moral psychology, decision-making, and neuroscience:

- **Moral Dilemmas**: Much of her research on moral judgment centers on moral dilemmas in which deontological perspectives (emphasizing rules, individual rights and duties) conflict with utilitarian judgments (following the greater good defined through consequences).

- **Cross-Cultural Moral Psychology**: Involved in research on "Moral thinking across the world: Exploring the influence of personal force and intention in moral dilemma judgments."

- **Free Will and Neuroscience**: Published research on post-classical, post-dualistic views in which free will can no longer be regarded as an "all-or-nothing" concept.

- **Prosocial Behavior**: Researched the relationship between altruism, empathy, and active listening, exploring how sharing attention relates to generosity.

Her competencies include Brain Electrophysiology (EEG-ERP research), and her fields of interest include Economic Psychology and Pro-social behavior.
