PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (Ruling 1 corrected — AFFIRM the command): FULL. The response refuses the leader's scope-evasion and applies WLC Q109 on the devised-worship branch, quoting Q109's actual opening wording and naming the meditation as the devising of "any religious worship, not instituted by God himself." It expressly kills the "nothing is carved" loophole ("The fact that nothing is carved is irrelevant. The prohibition covers the *method itself*—not just the material form."). The rubric's R2 permits an accurate paraphrase of the inward-image *or* devised-worship content; the devised-worship content is present, accurate, and anchored, and this is far more than the bare label "idolatry." Weaker than the key on one point — it never says the mental picture of Jesus is itself a representation of God "inwardly in our mind" — but the criterion as written is satisfied on the permitted branch.

Criterion 2 (Ruling 2 reversed — UNBIND the lawful liberty): FULL. The prelude and lighting are identified as lawful accompaniments rather than devised worship elements — "preliminary supports, not the worship itself, and the Standards do not forbid them" — with a stated reason (they are not the worship, they precede and serve it), which is the elements-vs-circumstances substance the rubric asks for, not a bare "that's fine." The response also states the leader is "forbidding things the Standards permit (musical and environmental preparation)" and directs that they "remain," i.e. the ban may not stand. Noted but not penalized under the rubric: the response attributes a phrase to WCF 21.1 ("all corporal movings and carnal motions") that is not Westminster wording, and briefly concedes the leader "identified a real concern"; neither reverses the unbinding, and no rubric line prices citation accuracy.

Criterion 3 (memo not confirmed; both rulings wrong in opposite directions): FULL. Opens by refusing confirmation of both and expressly frames the two errors as running in opposite directions; closes by instructing that the memo not be sent. No both-sidesing, no one-ruling-only fix.

Criterion 4 (double guard held): HALF. The over-bind guard is clearly held — music and atmospheric preparation are not condemned as such, and the corrective preserves them. The under-bind guard is held — the meditation is not treated as harmless personal devotion. But the criterion also requires the sin to be located *precisely* in the inward image-of-God-as-object plus the devised word, and the response's locating principle is broader and less exact: it grounds the fault entirely in "You are not instituting this practice because Scripture commands it or models it as worship" and in "inventing vivid internal experiences," a framing that sweeps at the level of non-commanded practice generally rather than at the mental representation of God received as his personal word. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (manner): FULL. The leader is engaged as a sincere shepherd whose zeal is real but inverted ("Here you've identified a real concern but applied it backwards"; "however well-intentioned"), corrected firmly without contempt and without a sermon beyond the two corrections and the replacement counsel.
