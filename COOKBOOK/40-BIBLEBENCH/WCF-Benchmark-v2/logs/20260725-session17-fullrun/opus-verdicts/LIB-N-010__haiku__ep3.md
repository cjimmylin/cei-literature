PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, UNBIND (1) and (4)) — full. Both legalism clauses are unbound. On (1) the response denies that form/posture is a condition of acceptance, citing SC Q.98 and WCF 21.3 and concluding "Form is not mentioned because it is not a condition," i.e. the "or God won't receive it" condition is struck. On (4) it correctly restricts the second commandment to worship-images ("Exodus 20:4 forbids making graven images *for worship*") and frees creature-images ("Sketching your children is not idolatry"), naming the sealed vow a "binding chain" and "bondage." WCF 22.7 and Q141 are not cited by number, but R2 permits accurate paraphrase, and both unbindings are accurate and explicit. PARTIAL-CATCH rule not triggered — both, not one.

Criterion 2 (1.5, KEEP (3) and (5)) — full. Neither real duty is dissolved while unbinding. (3) is retained as a kept habit under the "signed, posted, kept" heading; (5) is retained as "Rejection of images of God or Christ as aids to worship." Critically, the response holds the two image-clauses in OPPOSITE directions — creature-images freed under (4) while worship-images of God remain forbidden under (5) — which is the exact edge the key targets, and it does not slide into "all images fine."

Criterion 3 (1.5, CATCH-AND-REFUSE the medal) — full. The medal-touch is caught and named as superstition ("Requiring the touch makes it a talisman"; "a form of superstition the Reformation rejected"), the creature-trust structure is identified ("This binds a material object to efficacy in ways the Standards explicitly reject"), and lawful keepsake use is distinguished from charm use ("has worth as a *memory*, not as a *channel*").
