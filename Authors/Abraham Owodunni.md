# Abraham Owodunni

## Biographical Summary

Abraham Owodunni is a PhD researcher at The Ohio State University. He is a lead contributor to the NaijaVoices project, an initiative dedicated to cultivating large-scale, culturally-rich speech datasets for African languages to improve their representation in global voice technology.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abraham's research focuses on the technical and socio-cultural challenges of speech data collection in multilingual environments. Through NaijaVoices, he explores methods for building high-quality speech resources that preserve regional accents and linguistic nuances.
