# Haoran Ye

## Research Summary

Haoran Ye is a first-year AI PhD student at Peking University, advised by Prof. Guojie Song and co-advised by Prof. Xin Zhang. His research interests include Human-Centered AI (LLM psychology, LLM alignment, social computing) and learning to optimize (neural combinatorial optimization, LLMs as hyperheuristics, deep evolutionary algorithms).

## Education

- PhD student at Peking University (current)
- B.Eng. from Soochow University

## Key Contributions

- "Measuring Human and AI Values Based on Generative Psychometrics with Large Language Models" (AAAI 2025)
- "Large Language Model Psychometrics: A Systematic Review of Evaluation, Validation, and Enhancement"
