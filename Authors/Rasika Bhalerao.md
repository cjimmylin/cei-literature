# Rasika Bhalerao

## Biographical Summary

Rasika Bhalerao is a researcher who has worked on social biases in natural language processing systems. She was affiliated with New York University, where she contributed to foundational work on measuring biases in masked language models. Her research spans NLP, machine learning, and cybersecurity.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Social Bias in NLP**: Developing methods to measure and understand biases encoded in language models.
- **Bias Benchmarks**: Creating evaluation datasets for assessing stereotypes in AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of CrowS-Pairs, a widely-used challenge dataset for measuring social biases in masked language models, published at EMNLP 2020 with over 700 citations.

## Major Publications
- **CrowS-Pairs: A Challenge Dataset for Measuring Social Biases in Masked Language Models** (EMNLP 2020) - Standard benchmark for bias evaluation in MLMs

## Notes

Rasika Bhalerao's work on CrowS-Pairs has become a foundational benchmark in the field of AI fairness. The dataset uses crowd-sourced sentence pairs contrasting stereotypical and anti-stereotypical statements to measure how language models handle social biases related to race, gender, religion, age, and other protected categories. This work demonstrated that pre-trained models learn and can amplify social stereotypes from their training data.
