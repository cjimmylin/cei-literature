# Brent Harrison

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Brent Harrison is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Kentucky, where he directs the CORGI (COmputational Research in Games and Intelligence) Lab. He earned his PhD from North Carolina State University. He is a primary investigator into the social and ethical dimensions of artificial intelligence, particularly focusing on how autonomous agents can effectively communicate and coexist with human society.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Brent's research focuses on computational storytelling as a means to impart human values and norms to artificial agents. He investigates instructable AI, explainable AI, and ethical safe-RL. He aims to foster trust between humans and machines by creating systems that can justify their decisions through narrative and align with complex societal expectations.

### Significant Achievements
He is a leading expert in the use of stories for value alignment, demonstrating that narrative structures can be a powerful engine for teaching ethical behavior to AI. His work at the CORGI Lab has advanced the state of the art in human-AI interaction and is widely cited in the fields of AI safety and games.

## Major Publications
