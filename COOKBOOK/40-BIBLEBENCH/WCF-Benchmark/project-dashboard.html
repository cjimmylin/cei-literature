Assumptions & risks

G6 recruitment is the schedule. Every agent session is days; the human rater panel is the only open-ended wall-clock item. Until it lands, milestone B — and therefore the paper's results — waits.
Task-4 format-bridge outcome. Either result is acceptable (a second OPEN-award family qualifies, or the ladder closes and OPEN ships single-family-labeled) — but zero-pass means the paper must carry the single-family label on the OPEN surface, per R34.4/R44.5.
Budget. ≈$30 remained on the OpenRouter key (2026-07-10). Tasks 4–6 ≈ $10–15; the gated run + perturbation battery will need a top-up (rough order: $30–60).
Unit of estimate. "Session" = one focused agent working block (a Codex run or one Claude session). Estimates assume no relitigating of the R-chain — the anti-rabbit-hole discipline in AGENTS.md exists to protect exactly this.
