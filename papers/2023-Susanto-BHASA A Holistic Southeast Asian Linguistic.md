## Abstract

Original version of SEA-HELM. Three components: NLP benchmark (8 tasks across NLU/NLG/NLR), LINDSEA linguistic diagnostic toolkit, and cultural diagnostics dataset probing cultural representation and sensitivity. Thai included in NLP benchmark. Foundational SEA cultural evaluation suite. Cultural diagnostics component directly evaluates cultural representation and sensitivity. Thai is one of the implemented languages.

## Benchmark

**Name:** BHASA

## Data Description

Holistic evaluation suite for LLMs in Southeast Asian languages comprising an NLP benchmark (8 tasks across NLU/NLG/NLR), LINDSEA linguistic diagnostic toolkit, and cultural diagnostics dataset for Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, and Tamil.
