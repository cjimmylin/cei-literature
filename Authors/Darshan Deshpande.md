## Research Summary
Darshan Deshpande is a PhD student researching AI safety and large language model evaluation. He has contributed to the development of benchmarks for assessing the reliability and safety of LLM reasoning, including work on the TRAIL benchmark which evaluates how well language models maintain logical consistency and avoid harmful reasoning patterns in extended contexts.
