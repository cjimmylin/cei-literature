# Assessing Large Language Models on Islamic Legal Reasoning: Evidence from Inheritance Law Evaluation

## Abstract

Evaluates 7 LLMs on Islamic inheritance law ('ilm al-mawarith). O3 and Gemini 2.5 achieved >90% accuracy; ALLaM, Fanar, LLaMA, Mistral scored <50%. Detailed error analysis identifies misunderstandings of inheritance scenarios, incorrect application of legal rules, and insufficient domain knowledge.

## Dataset Information

1,000 multiple-choice questions on Islamic inheritance law


## Data Description

1,000 multiple-choice questions on Islamic inheritance law (ilm al-mawarith) at two difficulty levels (beginner/advanced), with expert-reviewed annotations and error categorization.
