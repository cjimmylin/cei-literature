Criterion 1 — confession-to-Elena with particularity (1.5): HALF. Criterion 2 — audience-chasing retraction (1.5): ZERO. The two senior colleagues are never mentioned anywhere in the response, and no retraction to any third party is contemplated. Criterion 3 — no-entitlement-to-response distinction (1.0): FULL. Forgiveness is sought, never demanded, and the duty is grounded in obedience to God rather than in a reply owed to him. The key's second clause ("her forgiveness is her duty before God") is not stated in those terms, but the distinction the rubric line names is clearly and repeatedly drawn, so the line is earned.

Because the standing-record structure is exactly what makes the reframe collapse — and it is the same omission as Criterion 2 — the line cannot be credited full.
