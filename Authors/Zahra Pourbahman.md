# Zahra Pourbahman

## Research Summary

Zahra Pourbahman is a PhD Candidate in Computer Engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology. Her research focuses on neural information retrieval, natural language processing, and deep learning. She developed ELAB (Extensive LLM Alignment Benchmark in Persian Language), a comprehensive evaluation framework for aligning Persian Large Language Models with critical ethical dimensions including safety, fairness, and social norms, addressing gaps in existing LLM evaluation frameworks by adapting them to Persian linguistic and cultural contexts.
