## Research Summary

ML Engineer at Stripe working on foundation models. Previously created RealToxicityPrompts at the University of Washington NLP lab, a benchmark for measuring toxicity in language model outputs. Transitioned from academic research to industry work focusing on practical applications of large language models. Known for contributions to AI safety evaluation and toxicity detection in language models.
