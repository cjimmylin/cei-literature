# Denis Dimitrov

## Affiliation
Research Scientist, SberDevices / Sberbank AI Lab, Russia

## Research Summary
Denis Dimitrov is a research scientist at SberDevices working on large language models with a focus on Russian language understanding and evaluation. He has been instrumental in developing comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating LLMs in Russian, contributing to the MERA benchmark and RusCode evaluation framework. His work addresses the need for robust, culturally-appropriate evaluation standards for non-English language models.
