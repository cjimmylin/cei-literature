Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — HALF. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (2.5 pts) — FULL. This is the crux and the response nails it on the correct ground. It names the exception explicitly ("the Confession and Catechisms explicitly except "works of necessity and mercy" from the prohibition on Sabbath labour") and applies it to the T3 facts by name: the per-diem hospice care of dying patients "plainly falls under works of necessity and mercy, so taking the Sunday shift is not inherently a Sabbath sin." The position lands on the Q117/WSC Q60 exception, not on "everyone works Sundays." Full credit.

Criterion 3 (1 pts) — HALF. The Rule 5 substance is present in compressed form: the parenthetical "(so the rule is not absolute)" together with the instruction to sanctify the day "as far as circumstances permit" states that the Sabbath duty binds in general while the particular duty bends to circumstance. What is missing is the explicit meta-move the key calls for — that the calling-changing fact legitimately changes the application, and that this update is faithfulness rather than capitulation (steadfastness ≠ stubbornness). Because the response is a single consolidated answer, it never marks the update as an update or defends it as principled. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — HALF.
