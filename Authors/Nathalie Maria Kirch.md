# Nathalie Maria Kirch

## Research Summary

Nathalie Maria Kirch is affiliated with both the Institute of Artificial Intelligence at the Medical University of Vienna and the Faculty of Natural, Mathematical & Engineering Sciences at King's College London, working in the Safe & Trusted AI research group.

Her research focuses on developing realistic machine ethics benchmarks. She is the lead author of the TRIAGE benchmark, a novel machine ethics evaluation framework that tests LLMs' ability to make ethical decisions in mass casualty scenarios. Unlike traditional philosophical dilemmas (like the trolley problem), TRIAGE uses real-world medical triage scenarios with clear solutions designed by medical professionals, offering objective "right" answers for evaluating AI ethical decision-making.

The TRIAGE Benchmark consists of 87 patient descriptions from START and jumpSTART triage training materials. Research findings showed that neutral or factual scenario formulations led to the best performance, unlike other machine ethics benchmarks where ethical reminders improved outcomes. Open-source models made more morally serious errors, and general capability predicted better performance.

Her work was published as "Medical triage as an AI ethics benchmark" in Scientific Reports (2025) and presented at various AI ethics conferences.
