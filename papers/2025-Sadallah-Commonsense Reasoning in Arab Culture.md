## Abstract

Commonsense reasoning dataset in MSA covering cultures of 13 countries across Gulf, Levant, North Africa, and Nile Valley regions. 12 daily-life domains with 54 fine-grained subtopics reflecting social norms, traditions, and everyday experiences. Native speakers wrote and validated culturally relevant questions. Zero-shot evaluations show models up to 32B struggle with diverse Arab cultures.

## Data Description

ArabCulture: a manually constructed commonsense reasoning dataset in Modern Standard Arabic with 3,482 instances covering 13 Arab countries across 4 regions and 12 daily-life domains with 54 subtopics, built and validated by native speakers.
