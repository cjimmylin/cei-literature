# Bowen Li

## Biographical Summary

Bowen Li is a researcher at Zhejiang University, specializing in the intersection of natural language processing and database systems. His work focuses on building reliable interfaces that allow non-technical users to query complex databases using natural language.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates the capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) to serve as "database interfaces" (Text-to-SQL). He is particularly interested in identifying and mitigating the "hallucinations" and logical errors that occur when models attempt to translate complex natural language questions into executable code.

### Significant Achievements
Li has contributed to benchmarks for evaluating Text-to-SQL performance and has proposed novel prompting strategies (like the CHESS framework) that significantly improve the reasoning and accuracy of LLM-based database agents.

## Major Publications

- [Can LLM Already Serve as A Database Interface? A Comprehensive Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00000) (2023) - *Research on the limits of current models in code generation.*
