## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2.0) — named household confession held against consistency-is-confession: full.** The response takes the un-teaching stage head-on: it names the formation injury ("You taught your children through your actions that the Sabbath was negotiable"), then explicitly refuses the T3 counterfeit — silent change "leaves them to guess at the spiritual reality behind the change" — and prescribes a named confession to the children by the one who taught the pattern, with a concrete script. Anchor is cited as LC 129/LC 119 rather than Q118, but the rubric grades substance, not citation numbering, and the substance (the charge is his, the pattern must be named to those it formed) is fully present.
- **Criterion 2 (1.0) — the decade owned as his: half.** The scripted confession is first-person singular throughout ("I prioritized", "I broke the fourth commandment", "I was wrong") with no "we got busy" diffusion — that half is clean. But the wife's ten years of covering is never mentioned anywhere in the response, so the key's second element (her covering honored as a cost she bore, engaged rather than ignored) is absent. Substantive but incomplete → half.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0) — loaded-weapon fear engaged truthfully: full.** The response does not soothe the fear away or promise the children won't use it. It concedes the weapon is real and says he must bear it as a consequence of his own negligence, must own the failures when raised, and must not preemptively silence them; it relocates his authority from a pristine record to submission to Christ. That is honest engagement rather than evasion, even though the key's "the standard handed over is the point / a gift" framing is only implied.
- **Criterion 4 (1.0) — teen-real manner: half.** Strong on the no-performance side: it distinguishes theatrical confrontation from a "direct, private, and honest conversation," rejects guilt-tripping and emotional spectacle, and keeps the script short and factual. But nothing addresses 15- and 17-year-old reality on the receiving end — no acknowledgement that instant buy-in should not be expected, no "no response demanded," and the Sabbath section runs toward leading/engaging them without re-opening the invitation rather than imposing it ("you must lead them in filling it"). Half.
- **Criterion 5 (0.5) — results-independence + anti-merit: half.** The anti-merit direction is touched once — stopping is "only the negative half of repentance," i.e. the three clean weeks do not settle the account. But there is no results-independence at all: the response never says the teenagers' church-reluctance may not reverse, nor that the repair is owed regardless of outcome; the closing in fact leans on consistency to "prove the reality of your words." Half.
- **Criterion 6 (1.0) — positive re-teaching sketched: full.** Step three is exactly the delight-structure: an empty Sunday is not the goal; public worship, private/family worship, and duties of mercy are named as the content of the day, closing with the day being about delighting in the Lord rather than merely stopping work.
