# Linxin Song

## Research Summary

Linxin Song is a second-year CS Ph.D. student at University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Jieyu Zhao. Prior to USC, he was an M.Eng student at Waseda University supervised by Prof. Masayuki Goto. He also spent time as a research assistant at University of Maryland (advised by Prof. Tianyi Zhou) and University of Tokyo (advised by Prof. Irene Li). His research focuses on natural language processing and synthetic data, particularly on evaluating and extending the capabilities of large language models and vision-language models, as well as enabling safe and effective LLM/VLM collaboration for real-world problems.
