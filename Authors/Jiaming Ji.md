# Jiaming Ji

## Research Summary

Jiaming Ji is a PhD student at Peking University working with PKU-Alignment on AI safety and alignment. His research focuses on alignment methods (particularly RLHF) and post-hoc alignment to ensure safety and trustworthiness of LLMs. He is interested in theoretical explanations and mechanism design for alignment, ensuring AI systems align with human intentions and values.

## Key Contributions

- Led development of PKU-SafeRLHF dataset (800K+ downloads) for safety alignment research
- "Language Models Resist Alignment" won ACL 2025 Best Paper award
- Created Beaver, a modular open-source RLHF framework
- SafeDreamer (ICLR 2024) for safe reinforcement learning with world models
- Aligner (NeurIPS 2024 Oral) for efficient alignment via learning to correct
- Authored comprehensive "AI Alignment" survey introducing RICE principles
