# Tim Beyer

## Research Summary

Tim Beyer is a PhD student and researcher at the Technical University of Munich's Department of Computer Science, specifically in the Data Analytics and Machine Learning group. His research focuses on the robustness and safety of large language models, with particular emphasis on developing rigorous evaluation methodologies.

His notable 2025 work, "LLM-Safety Evaluations Lack Robustness," systematically analyzes the LLM safety evaluation pipeline, covering dataset curation, optimization strategies for automated red-teaming, response generation, and response evaluation using LLM judges. This critical meta-evaluation highlights that many 'state-of-the-art' safety results may be statistical noise due to intertwined sources of noise such as small datasets, methodological inconsistencies, and unreliable evaluation setups. The work was selected for an oral presentation at ICLR 2025.

Beyer's educational background includes an M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from TU Munich (2021-2024) and a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from TU Munich (2017-2021). His research addresses fundamental challenges in making AI safety research more reliable and comparable across studies.
