# Cunxiang Wang

## Biographical Summary

Cunxiang Wang is a researcher affiliated with Westlake University, Zhejiang University, and ZhipuAI. He completed his PhD at Zhejiang University in 2024 under the supervision of Yue Zhang. His research focuses on natural language understanding, open-domain question answering, and knowledge reasoning, with particular interest in LLM factuality and commonsense reasoning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Knowledge Reasoning**: Investigating commonsense knowledge graph reasoning (CKGR) and how to predict missing entities in knowledge graphs.
- **LLM Factuality**: Surveying and addressing factuality challenges in large language models.
- **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning**: Applying CoT prompting to enhance LLM reasoning on complex problems.

### Significant Achievements
- **SemEval 2020 Best Task Paper Honorable Mention**: Commonsense Validation and Explanation task.
- **China National Scholarships**: Received for both Bachelor and PhD studies.
- **NAACL 2025**: Two papers accepted (1 main, 1 industry track).
- **ACL 2023 Findings**: Multiple papers on open-domain QA.
