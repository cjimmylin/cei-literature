# Yang Trista Cao

## Research Summary

Yang (Trista) Cao received her PhD from the University of Maryland, College Park in 2024. Her research focuses on moving towards trustworthy and fair natural language processing systems. She analyzes how stereotypes manifest in large pretrained language models and works on making NLP systems more equitable. Her recent work (2024) includes research on toxicity detection and compositionality in vision-language models.
