# Chandra Bhagavatula

## Biographical Summary

Chandra Bhagavatula is an AI researcher and entrepreneur. He is the Co-founder of Chipstack AI and was previously a Lead Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). He holds a PhD from Northwestern University and is an Affiliate Assistant Professor at the University of Washington.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Commonsense Reasoning" and "Abductive Reasoning"—teaching AI systems to make inferences that go beyond the literal text. He is a primary architect of the "Abductive Natural Language Inference" (αNLI) task and has worked extensively on "Value Alignment," ensuring that LLMs reason in ways that are consistent with human common sense and ethical values.

### Significant Achievements
Chandra received the AAAI Outstanding Paper Award in 2020 for his work on abductive reasoning. His contributions to AI2's "Delphi" and "Value Kaleidoscope" projects have been foundational for the "Technical Ethics" community, providing the models and datasets needed to audit the "Moral Logic" of large-scale language systems.
