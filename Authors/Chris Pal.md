# Chris Pal

## Biographical Summary

Chris Pal is a Full Professor at Polytechnique Montréal and a core member of Mila (Quebec AI Institute). He holds a Canada CIFAR AI Chair and is also a Distinguished Scientist at ServiceNow Research. His work spans the full spectrum of deep learning, from computer vision to natural language understanding.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Generative AI**: Researching generative models for text, image, and video synthesis.
- **Low-Resource NLP**: Supporting the development of resources for under-served languages, including contributions to "NaijaVoices".
- **Multimodal Learning**: Developing models that can reason across different modalities (vision, language, audio).

### Significant Achievements
- **Mila Leadership**: As a core member of Mila, he plays a key role in one of the world's leading deep learning research centers.

## Major Publications
- **NaijaVoices: A Richly Annotated Speech Dataset for Nigerian Languages**: A contribution to democratizing speech technology.
