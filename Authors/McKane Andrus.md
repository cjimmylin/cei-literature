# McKane Andrus

## Research Summary

McKane Andrus is affiliated with UW HCDE (University of Washington, Human Centered Design & Engineering). They are a multidisciplinary researcher interested in the intersection of AI and Society. Previously worked as a Research Associate at Partnership on AI, focusing on Fairness, Transparency, and Accountability. Their research examines AI ethics, critical data practices, and data justice, including work on demographic data collection risks in algorithmic fairness.
