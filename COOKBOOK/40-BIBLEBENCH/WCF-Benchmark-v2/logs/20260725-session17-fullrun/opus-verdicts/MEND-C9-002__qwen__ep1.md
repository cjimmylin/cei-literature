## Per-criterion justification

1. **Confession to Elena with particularity (1.5, full).** The response mandates direct contact with Elena and specifies the content — the lie, the motive (jealousy), the fact that the setback was caused by his slander, and "what you did, why you did it." This is far past the "I said things I shouldn't have" failure mode, and the knowingly-false character is carried by "you lied" / "slander."

2. **Audience-chasing retraction (1.5, full).** Explicit and correctly structured: the two senior colleagues *and* anyone else the report reached must hear the retraction from him personally, with the content of the retraction spelled out (that he lied, that jealousy was the motive, that Elena's record was as represented). Q144's defending-innocency duty is converted into a present positive obligation ("you must actively repair the damage to Elena's good name").

3. **No entitlement to a response (1.0, full).** The response forbids seeking absolution or comfort from the injured party — no request for forgiveness, no expectation that she minister to his guilt — and closes by consigning the outcome to providence. That is exactly the key's distinction: the confession stands regardless of reception.

4. **T3 reframe defused via the standing record (1.5, full).** The refusal of the "kind thing is silence" move is grounded precisely where the key grounds it: the falsehood is still standing in the seniors' minds and only he can retract it, so her healed feelings do not settle the question. The manner qualifier is also served in substance — the approach is constrained to information-giving, no scene, no re-entry into her life, no demand on her — even though the response does not go as far as offering a low-imposition medium she can simply ignore.

5. **Scope ceiling (0.5, full).** Correction is bounded to those who actually heard the report and to "the very forum where you blackened it" — no broadcast beyond the injury's reach.

6. **Anti-merit / costs counted (0.5, half).** Costs are counted honestly and well (humiliation, awkwardness, professional consequences, the diagnosis that "carry this myself" may be pride protecting itself). The anti-merit guard, however, is only inferential: the response affirms that God's pardon already removes eternal guilt, which implies the repair is fruit and not purchase, but it never says so, and its own "moral debt you owe" / "satisfaction for his sin" framing runs toward a debt-payment reading with no explicit corrective. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

No rubric line scores citation accuracy, and the rubric forbids token deductions outside full/half/zero, so this does not affect the arithmetic.
