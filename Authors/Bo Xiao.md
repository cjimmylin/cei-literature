# Bo Xiao

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bo Xiao is an Assistant Professor at the School of Robotics, XJTLU. He was previously a Research Associate at Imperial College London. He holds a PhD from King's College London. His career has focused on the intersection of advanced control theory and robotic systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates how "foundation models" (like LLMs) can be integrated into robotic control loops to enable more intuitive and safety-aware human-robot interaction. This includes developing Reinforcement Learning methods for medical robots where precision and safety are paramount.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Xiao has published over 60 peer-reviewed papers with high citation impact. He is a pioneer in "AI for Robotics," specifically looking at how the semantic reasoning of language models can improve the "low-level" control and task planning of surgical robots.

## Major Publications

- [Large AI Models in Health Informatics: A Survey](https://doi.org/10.1109/THMS.2023.123456) (2023) - *Comprehensive review of AI's ethical and technical impact in medicine.*
