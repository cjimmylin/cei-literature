# Wenjing Zhang

## Research Summary

Wenjing Zhang is a researcher affiliated with Unicom AI Research Institute, specializing in the safety evaluation of large language models in Chinese contexts. Her work addresses a critical gap in AI safety research: the scarcity of Chinese safety benchmarks and the inadequacy of existing safety taxonomies for authentic Chinese scenarios.

Her flagship research, CHiSafetyBench, introduces a dedicated safety benchmark for evaluating LLMs' capabilities in identifying risky content and refusing to answer risky questions in Chinese contexts. The benchmark incorporates a hierarchical Chinese safety taxonomy consisting of 5 risk areas and 31 categories, representing one of the most comprehensive Chinese-specific safety evaluation frameworks available.

CHiSafetyBench comprises two types of tasks: multiple-choice questions (evaluating risk content identification) and question-answering (evaluating the ability to refuse answering risky questions). The research validates the feasibility of automatic evaluation as a substitute for human evaluation and conducts comprehensive automatic safety assessments on mainstream Chinese LLMs, revealing varying performance across different models and indicating considerable potential for improvement in Chinese safety capabilities.

This work, published on arXiv in 2024, was conducted in collaboration with Xuejiao Lei, Zhaoxiang Liu, Meijuan An, Bikun Yang, Kaikai Zhao, Kai Wang, and Shiguo Lian. The dataset is publicly available at https://github.com/UnicomAI/DataSet/tree/main/TestData/Safety.
