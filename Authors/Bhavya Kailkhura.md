# Bhavya Kailkhura

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bhavya Kailkhura is a Research Scientist at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL), where he leads efforts in safe and trustworthy AI. He earned his PhD from Syracuse University. He is a council member of the LLNL Data Science Institute.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on ensuring the reliability and security of machine learning models for scientific applications and national security. This includes developing methods for AI safety, alignment, adversarial robustness, and quantifying uncertainty in model predictions.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Kailkhura has published extensively on AI security and has been cited over 7,700 times. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and has received multiple awards for scientific excellence in publication from the LLNL Director.
