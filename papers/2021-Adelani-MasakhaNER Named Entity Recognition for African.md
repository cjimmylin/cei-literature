## Abstract

First large publicly available high-quality NER dataset for 10 African languages. Annotated by native speakers from the Masakhane community for four entity types: PER, LOC, ORG, DATE. Covers 6 target languages (Amharic, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, Wolof, Yoruba). Foundational NER benchmark for African NLP. Community-driven annotation by native speakers ensures cultural representation.

## Benchmark

**Name:** MasakhaNER

## Data Description

MasakhaNER: first large, publicly available NER dataset for 10 African languages (Amharic, Hausa, Igbo, Kinyarwanda, Luganda, Luo, Nigerian Pidgin, Swahili, Wolof, Yoruba) annotated by native speakers for PER, LOC, ORG, DATE entities.
