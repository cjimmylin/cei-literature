## State of the art
The pipelines are real and reaching production documents, but tradition-blind by construction — they reduce normative structure to opinion distributions (Front D open problem 1). CEI's unique asset is 14 traditions operationalized as corpora and item banks — precisely the input structure no current pipeline can consume. RP-08 CONCLAVE's first milestone is deliberately the **tradition-fidelity gate**: without it, tradition-agents are ventriloquism and the program self-kills (K1). Then a tradition-structured pipeline is tested against opinion-pooling on tradition-fidelity without majority loss (H1) — first on synthetic agents with caveats, then human panels.
