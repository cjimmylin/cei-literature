# Orca

## Overview
Orca is a series of models from Microsoft Research focused on teaching smaller language models to learn from complex explanation traces and develop reasoning capabilities.

## Models
- **Orca 1**: Progressive learning from complex explanation traces of GPT-4
- **Orca 2**: Teaching small language models how to reason
