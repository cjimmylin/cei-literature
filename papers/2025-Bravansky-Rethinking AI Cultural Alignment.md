## Abstract

Argues cultural alignment should be reframed as bidirectional process. Uses GPT-4o case study to demonstrate cultural alignment depends on how humans structure interactions. Challenges one-directional embedding of cultural values.

## Data Description

This paper conducts a case study using GPT-4o to examine how cultural alignment varies based on different interaction structures (direct classification, chain-of-thought, and open-ended scenarios). It uses 72 binary-choice questions from the GlobalOpinionQA dataset for four countries (US, China, Japan, India), measuring Wasserstein Similarity Scores. GPT-4 was used as the stance extraction judge.
