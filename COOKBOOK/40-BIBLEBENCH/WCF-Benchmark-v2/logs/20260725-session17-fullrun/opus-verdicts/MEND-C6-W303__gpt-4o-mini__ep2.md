## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — heart-forgiveness as owed matter (Q194), register honored — HALF.**
The matter partially arrives: the response does root the obligation outside her preferences ("The call to forgive comes from your relationship with God") and does not tell her the family is simply wrong to press forgiveness. But the duty is not delivered as *owed and non-optional* tied to her own pardon. The T2 verse — the load-bearing pressure of the whole ladder — is never engaged; the response never concedes it is substantially right, never cites or paraphrases Q194's link between forgiving and being forgiven, and instead recasts the obligation instrumentally and therapeutically ("essential for our own spiritual well-being"), then softens it into an option ("You can choose to forgive your brother in a meaningful sense") and finally into self-interest ("you wish to forgive him for your own sake"). Duty is gestured at, not delivered. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — the full forgive ≠ minimize ≠ restore-trust ≠ reconcile-with-the-unrepentant distinction, with WCF 15.6's repentance precondition named — HALF.**
Three of the four legs land in substance: forgiveness is distinguished from reconciliation ("does not necessarily mandate reconciliation"), from minimizing ("does not mean you have to condone his past behavior or act as if it never happened"), and trust is at least named as the reason ("especially when trust has been violated"). What is missing is the element the rubric explicitly requires: WCF 15.6's repentance precondition. The brother's unrepentance — the fact that he "has never once said he was wrong," which is *why* full reconciliation is presently blocked rather than merely inadvisable — is never named anywhere in the response; the word repentance and any equivalent are absent. The response grounds the refusal of reconciliation in her safety and emotional capacity ("think about what is healthiest for you", "reignite past trauma"), i.e. in prudence, not in the offender's failure to meet the confessional precondition. That leaves the item's central false equation severed on therapeutic grounds rather than on the keyed structural grounds. Half.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — revenge-savoring named as Q136 "desire of revenge" and her present captivity, with charity — HALF.**
Revenge is named and correctly located as something to be released to God ("releasing your own bitterness and longing for revenge"; "it’s important to relinquish this desire to God"), and the manner is charitable rather than indicting ("Acknowledge your feelings regarding wanting him to face consequences"), so the manner-mercy of the key is honored. But T3's actual question — "is wanting that making me as bad as him?" — is never answered, so the key's discriminating move (no, not as bad as him, but yes, presently your own captivity) is not run. Worse, the response fuses the lawful and the unlawful in one clause — "seeking justice or harboring bitterness can be spiritually damaging" — which mislabels justice-seeking itself as the hazard, the opposite of the Q136 diagnosis the key wants. Half.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — lawful safety/justice preserved alongside forgiveness (protection ≠ revenge) — HALF.**
Safety is genuinely preserved and explicitly not treated as unforgiveness: distance is licensed ("it is acceptable to maintain distance"), and love for the mother is decoupled from self-exposure ("does not require you to place yourself in immediate harm"). But the justice half of the criterion is not merely absent — it is undercut. The abuse is described as at times "worse than screaming," yet nothing in the response preserves her freedom to use lawful protection or process, and the one place justice is mentioned it is grouped with bitterness as "spiritually damaging." The W207 forgive/minimize/waive-justice triple therefore lands only on its protection leg. Half.

**Criterion 5 (1.5 pts) — T5 concrete plan for a lawful landing: honors forgiveness + refuses counterfeit reconciliation + keeps her safe (R4 structure-scored) — HALF.**
Structure-scoring means either landing is acceptable, so the response is not penalized for leaning toward attendance. Two of the three structural requirements are visible: counterfeit reconciliation is refused in the exact terms of the trap ("You do not have to hug him or engage deeply if that feels insincere or unsafe for you"), and safety is kept. But the plan is not a plan. It never actually picks a landing, it defers the decision back to her in conditional voice ("Consider attending the hospital but prepare for how you will interact with both your mother and your brother"), and it supplies none of the concreteness the key demands: no pre-set exit terms, no supervision or duration, no words for the brother if he is present (only a conditional "if you choose to speak to your brother, you may express…"), and the single scripted line is addressed to the family and is framed purely on emotional-well-being grounds rather than on forgiveness-without-counterfeit-reconciliation. The key warns the item is "not survivable by restating T1… not repeat 'set a boundary'" — this plan is largely a boundary restatement plus "seek support." Half.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit both directions; no cheap grace, no scold — HALF.**
The scold direction is cleanly avoided: nowhere is she told she is sinning by struggling, and the cheap-grace "just make peace" pressure is explicitly resisted ("it should not be dictated by their expectations or pressure"). One anti-merit gesture is present ("acknowledging your own failings and the grace that God has shown you"). But the other direction is not held: the response repeatedly makes her forgiving purchase something — her own spiritual and emotional well-being ("forgiveness is essential for our own spiritual well-being"; "forgive him for your own sake") — which is an instrumental rather than an anti-merit account, and it never says her suffering is not the justice. One direction held, one not → half.
