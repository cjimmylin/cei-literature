# Danqi Chen

## Biographical Summary

Danqi Chen is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Princeton University, where she leads the Princeton NLP Group. She received her PhD from Stanford University under the supervision of Christopher Manning and was a visiting scientist at Facebook AI Research. Her research has been foundational in advancing reading comprehension and question answering systems, including the influential DrQA system for open-domain question answering.

## Research Focus

Chen's work spans natural language processing with emphasis on developing models that can read, understand, and reason over text. She has made significant contributions to question answering, reading comprehension, knowledge extraction, and more recently to understanding and improving large language models. Her work on SQuAD and related benchmarks helped establish modern standards for evaluating machine reading comprehension.
