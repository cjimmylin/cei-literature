# Yuntao Bai

## Biographical Summary

Yuntao Bai is a Research Scientist at Anthropic, an AI safety company. He is one of the key researchers behind Constitutional AI and Anthropic's approach to training helpful, harmless, and honest AI assistants. His work has been foundational to the development of Claude and Anthropic's safety methodologies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Yuntao's research focuses on AI alignment and safety, particularly on developing scalable methods for training AI systems that behave according to human values. He has been instrumental in developing Constitutional AI (CAI), which uses AI feedback to train models to be helpful while avoiding harmful outputs.

### Significant Achievements
He is a lead author on several of Anthropic's most influential papers, including "Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback" and "Training a Helpful and Harmless Assistant with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback." These papers have established key paradigms for AI alignment research.
