# Jiayi Ye

## Research Summary

Jiayi Ye is a researcher focused on trustworthy AI and role-playing LLMs. Currently pursuing a B.S. in Cyber Science and working as a research intern at the University of Notre Dame (since July 2024), Ye has contributed to significant work on evaluating the trustworthiness of generative foundation models. Their research includes TrustGen, the first dynamic benchmarking platform designed to evaluate trustworthiness across multiple dimensions and model types including text-to-image, LLMs, and vision-language models.
