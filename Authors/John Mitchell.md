# John Mitchell

## Research Summary

John Mitchell is the Mary and Gordon Crary Family Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University. His research spans computer security, programming languages, privacy, and more recently, AI safety and trustworthiness. He has made significant contributions to web security, formal methods, and type theory.

He is known for foundational work on security protocols, web browser security, and privacy-preserving systems. His recent work addresses the trustworthiness and safety of large language models.
