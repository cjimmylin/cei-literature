# Drew A. Hudson

## Biographical Summary

Drew A. Hudson is a researcher at Stanford University specializing in visual reasoning and compositional AI systems. He completed his PhD at Stanford under the supervision of Christopher Manning, developing novel neural architectures for visual question answering and multimodal reasoning. His work on compositional attention networks has been highly influential in the vision-language community.

## Research Focus

Hudson's research focuses on building AI systems capable of compositional, multi-step reasoning over visual and linguistic inputs. He developed the MAC (Memory, Attention, Composition) network architecture and has contributed to major benchmarks for visual reasoning including CLEVR and GQA.

## Major Publications

- "Compositional Attention Networks for Machine Reasoning" (ICLR 2018) - Introduced MAC networks
- "GQA: A New Dataset for Real-World Visual Reasoning" (CVPR 2019) - Major visual QA benchmark
- "Learning by Abstraction: The Neural State Machine" (NeurIPS 2019)
