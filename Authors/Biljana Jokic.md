# Biljana Jokic

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Biljana Jokic is a psychologist and researcher at the Institute for Philosophy and Social Theory, University of Belgrade. Her research focuses on the social and psychological foundations of human belief systems and the mechanisms of moral reasoning in contemporary societies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research investigates how social identity and political orientation influence moral reasoning and the perception of truth. She is active in international collaborative projects that seek to map the cross-cultural landscape of human values.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Jokic contributed to the landmark "Situational Factors" study led by Bence Bago, which provided one of the most comprehensive datasets on human moral intuitions globally. Her work is crucial for understanding how "alignment" must account for regional and ideological differences in morality.

## Major Publications

- [Situational factors shape moral judgments in the trolley dilemma in 45 countries](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01387-z) (2022) - *Large-scale empirical study on global moral psychology.*
- [Psychological predictors of political beliefs](https://doi.org/10.1111/pops.12654) (2020) - *Research on the roots of ideological divergence.*
