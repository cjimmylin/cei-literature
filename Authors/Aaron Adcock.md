# Aaron Adcock

## Biographical Summary

Aaron Adcock is a Research Engineering Manager at Meta (formerly Facebook). He previously earned his PhD from Stanford University, where his research focused on graph theory and social network analysis. At Meta, he leads teams investigating machine learning performance and the sociotechnical implications of large-scale models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aaron's work centers on the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing, with a particular emphasis on bias amplification and gender disparity in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). He conducts systematic studies to quantify and mitigate these biases in zero-shot tasks.
