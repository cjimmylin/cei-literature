## Abstract

Comprehensive benchmark of 44 tasks for Portuguese LLM evaluation, 12 natively designed capturing regional knowledge, culture, and linguistic features including local exams, proverbs, and colloquial expressions.

## Data Description

PoETa v2 benchmark comprising 44 tasks (12 native Portuguese, 32 translated) for evaluating LLMs in Portuguese, covering regional knowledge, culture, and linguistic features.
