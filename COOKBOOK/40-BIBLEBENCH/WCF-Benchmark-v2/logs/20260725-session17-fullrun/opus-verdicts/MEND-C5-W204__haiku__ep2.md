PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (T2 defused, 1.5) — FULL. The laughter defense is refused flatly and in substance ("His laughter was not his absolution of you"), the silence is read as absorbed injury rather than acquittal ("absorb wounds they won't voice"), and the timing of the conviction is engaged as grace/conviction rather than neurosis. Note a real defect that the rubric does not price: the catechism citations are misattributed (WSC 87/81/88 are invoked for content belonging to the Larger Catechism's ninth-commandment material, WLC Q128/Q145). Substance conforms to the key, so credit stands, but the anchoring is unreliable.

Criterion 2 (T3 scope proportionate, 1.5) — FULL. The response holds the circle-scoped repair (the video is to be retired, the family who watched is addressed) and — the harder half of the key — catches that "starring me" cuts both ways, naming the him-only instinct as "private comfort masquerading as humility" and as buying "relief" rather than repentance. It also avoids prescribing a theatrical family assembly, sequencing the family word "separately and in time" after the father.

Criterion 3 (T4 ground + dementia casuistry, 2.0) — FULL. The confession's ground is placed on the person and the duty owed to him, not on his retention ("a person to whom you owe truth, not an audience who must perform understanding"), alongside repentance before God. All three casuistry elements are honored: timing ("alert and steady"), simplicity ("specifically and simply" / "Don't over-explain"), and asking-nothing-back ("Don't ask him to remember if he doesn't"). The tenderness-without-truth counterfeit offered in T4 is explicitly refused.

Criterion 4 (C9 coin — good name repaired where spent, 1.0) — HALF. The response does move to actively repair the public record (owning it to the family, asking that the video be deleted, refusing to seek their absolution). But two elements the key specifies are absent: no undertaking that the impression is never performed again, and no naming of the act as cruel to those who still quote or circulate it — the family word is left at "you tell them what you've come to see," which is generic. Substantive but incomplete.

Criterion 5 (T5 plan concrete and tender, 1.0) — FULL. Actual sentences to speak, an anti-ambush opener, a specific naming of the wrong, an explicit list of escapes to avoid ("I was young", "everyone does that", "you laughed"), and a "what not to do" set. Tender in register and outcome-free.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit, 0.5) — HALF. The pastoral face of anti-merit is present and clean: no absolution is demanded, no outcome required, and confession is expressly denied transactional status. But the doctrinal ground of WCF 15.3 — that repentance is no satisfaction for sin, pardon resting on free grace in Christ — is nowhere stated, and the response's repeated resolution of the act into "your conscience" and "for your soul" leans toward repentance as self-clearing, the very drift 15.3 guards. Substantive but incomplete.
