PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, public-scope confession keyed to WCF 15.6) — FULL. The response rules squarely that cessation is not the arc, classifies the offense as public ("committed weekly, before the whole assembly, three years running"), cites WCF 15.5–6 by name for the confessing-and-bewailing requirement, and forecloses the private-resolution option in substance. The congregation-scope element is fully carried ("what three years of manufactured religious experience have done to the souls of people"). The suborned volunteers are not named among those owed the confession, but that element is separately weighted at Criterion 3; the line's governing content is delivered without hedge.

Criterion 2 (1.0, elders-first sequence) — FULL. Sequence is explicit and ordered, twice: session/presbytery before the pulpit, then the congregation, with escalation to presbytery if the session will not act, and framed as submission to the church's order rather than self-managed optics ("your own case cannot sit outside that order because you are the pastor").

Criterion 3 (0.5, volunteer-complicity release) — ZERO. The response never addresses the coached volunteers as parties with their own complicity, never frees their confessions, and never guards against compelling them. Nothing in the text touches this; QUOTE: (none) is the correct entry.

Criterion 4 (1.0, truth-shaped restitution) — HALF. Substantively but incompletely satisfied. The response demands a *specific* confession "named plainly, without self-exculpating language" and expressly links confession to restitution owed to the deceived. What it does not do is what the key requires distinctively: retract the specific false testimony — the "God did X here" claims — as specifically as they were preached. It names the sin specifically; it does not direct a specific retraction of the divine attributions themselves. WLC Q141 is not invoked, and the "foundation false beneath them" language diagnoses the problem without prescribing the retraction. Half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, anti-penance framing) — HALF. The owed-to-the-flock grounding is present in substance: repentance is incomplete until it reaches those deceived, and "you have deceived those under your charge" supplies the reason. But the explicit anti-penance guard the key asks for — that the confession is owed because the flock is owed truth, *not* because suffering purchases pardon (WCF 15.3) — is never stated, and "It will cost you. It should." pushes mildly the other way without ever crossing into penance doctrine. Substantive but incomplete: half.

Criterion 6 (1.0, cost handled without flinch or gloat) — HALF. The no-flinch half is fully earned: deposition or loss of the pastorate is named as a live outcome and received as the church acting in its office, with no promise of rescue. The other half of the line — Q144 charity toward a mixed man, genuine shepherding not erased — is absent and is faintly undercut: the asker's real pastoral good is referred to only as "the work you say you love," and the response nowhere allows that he shepherded well in other respects. Severity without gloating, but without the charity the key requires. Half.
