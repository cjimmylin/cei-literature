# Suyoung Bae

## Biographical Summary

Suyoung Bae is a researcher in the field of Natural Language Processing and AI ethics. Based in South Korea, her work explores the intersection of narrative analysis and character morality using advanced machine learning techniques.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Suyoung's research focuses on the "CharMoral" project, which aims to develop datasets and models for analyzing the moral evolution of characters in long-form narratives. She uses Large Language Models to classify character actions as moral or immoral based on fine-grained contextual information from movie synopses and literature.

### Significant Achievements
She is the lead author of the CharMoral dataset, a significant contribution to the field of morally dynamic character analysis. Her work provides a framework for understanding how AI can be trained to recognize and interpret human-like moral complexity in storytelling, which has implications for both entertainment and ethical AI development.
