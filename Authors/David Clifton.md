# David Clifton

## Biographical Summary

David Clifton is a Professor of Clinical Machine Learning at the University of Oxford, where he leads research at the intersection of machine learning and healthcare. He holds the EPSRC Healthcare Technology Challenge Award and is a Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering. His work focuses on developing AI systems that can improve patient care through better monitoring, prediction, and decision support.

## Research Focus

Clifton's research applies machine learning to clinical problems, including patient monitoring in hospitals, wearable health sensing, and medical imaging. He has recently focused on the application of large language models in medicine, examining both their potential benefits and risks. His work emphasizes the importance of safety, reliability, and clinical validation in healthcare AI systems.

## Major Publications

- "A Survey of Large Language Models in Medicine: Principles, Applications, and Challenges" (2023)
- "Gaussian Processes for Classification: Mean-Field Algorithms" (2002)
- "Continuous Patient Monitoring with Wearable Sensors" (IEEE 2020)
- "Clinical Applications of Machine Learning in Cardiovascular Disease" (Nature Reviews Cardiology 2021)
- "Machine Learning for Healthcare: On the Verge of a Major Shift in Healthcare Epidemiology" (2019)
