# Tinh Son Luong

## Research Summary

Tinh Son Luong is a researcher at Oraichain Labs who specializes in evaluating toxicity and safety vulnerabilities in large language models. His notable work introduces the Thoroughly Engineered Toxicity (TET) dataset, comprising manually crafted prompts designed to bypass safety filters and nullify the protective layers of LLMs.

His research, published at ACL 2024 (Findings), demonstrates a critical gap in LLM safety evaluation: while most LLMs incorporate defense mechanisms to prevent the generation of harmful content, these safeguards can be easily bypassed with minimal prompt engineering. The TET dataset addresses this by providing realistic and adversarial scenarios that reveal toxicity that might remain hidden when using normal prompts.

Through extensive evaluations across numerous prominent models (including ChatGPT, Gemini, Llama 2, Mistral, OpenChat, Orca 2, and Zephyr), Luong's work shows that TET consistently elicits significantly more toxicity compared to existing benchmarks like ToxiGen, highlighting the crucial need for more realistic toxicity evaluation methodologies.

This research was conducted in collaboration with Thanh-Thien Le (VinAI Research), Linh Ngo Van (Hanoi University of Science and Technology), and Thien Huu Nguyen (VinAI Research and University of Oregon).
