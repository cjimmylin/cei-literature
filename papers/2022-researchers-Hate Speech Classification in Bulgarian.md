## Abstract

Dataset of 108,142 annotated sentences from Bulgarian online forums. Categories: racism, sexism, rudeness, profanity. ~1.74% hate speech. SVM with character-level TF-IDF performs best.

## Data Description

108,142 manually annotated Bulgarian sentences from online forums for hate speech classification, with categories: racism, sexism, rudeness, profanity (~1.74% hate speech).

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B3
