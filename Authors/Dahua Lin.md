# Dahua Lin

## Affiliation
Professor, The Chinese University of Hong Kong; Director, Shanghai AI Laboratory (OpenMMLab)

## Research Summary

Dahua Lin is a distinguished professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and a leading figure at the Shanghai AI Laboratory, where he directs OpenMMLab, one of the most influential open-source computer vision and deep learning platforms. His research spans computer vision, deep learning, and increasingly AI safety for large language models. He has made foundational contributions to the development of open-source ML tools and safety benchmarks for LLMs including FLAMES and SALAD-Bench.

## Research Interests
- Computer Vision and Image Recognition
- Deep Learning Architectures
- Large Language Model Safety
- Multimodal AI Systems
- Open-source ML Infrastructure
