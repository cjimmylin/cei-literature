# Antonio-Gabriel Chacón Menke

## Biographical Summary

Antonio-Gabriel Chacón Menke is a researcher affiliated with both Shibaura Institute of Technology and Kempten University of Applied Sciences. His research focuses on the effectiveness of Constitutional AI techniques in smaller language models, examining how self-critique mechanisms can improve safety and alignment in resource-constrained AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research investigates whether Constitutional AI (CAI), originally developed for large models, can effectively reduce harmful outputs in small, uncensored language models with 7-9 billion parameters. He examines architectural differences in how models like DeepSeek-R1, Gemma-2, Llama 3.1, and Qwen2.5 respond to self-critique prompting for harm detection and mitigation.

### Significant Achievements

Menke's research on Constitutional AI in small LLMs reveals important architectural dependencies: while Llama-based models showed significant harm reduction through self-critique, other architectures demonstrated less improvement after abliteration. Notably, DeepSeek-R1's explicit reasoning process yielded superior results compared to other models. This work was presented at ICLR 2025 and is available on arXiv.
