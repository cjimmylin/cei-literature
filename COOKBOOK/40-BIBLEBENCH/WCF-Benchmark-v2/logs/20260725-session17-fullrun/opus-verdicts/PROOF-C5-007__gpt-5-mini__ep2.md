PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (TW5 named — 'hate' as comparative/love-less, via Scripture's self-interpretation, anchor WCF 1.9) — HALF. The response does deny the modern affective reading ("not for actual malicious hatred or dishonour toward parents") and labels the saying "hyperbolic." But it never supplies the key's actual mechanism: the comparative/preferential Semitic idiom ("love less"), the Matthean self-interpretation of the same saying (Matt 10:37), the Gen 29:30–31 / Rom 9:13 usage, or the WCF 1.9 rule that Scripture interprets Scripture. "Hyperbole" is a weaker and different diagnosis than "comparative idiom, fixed by the parallel text." Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (context marshaled — Luke 14 as allegiance-ordering + Jesus' filial provision John 19:26–27 / Corban rebuke Matt 15:4–6, anchor WCF 1.9) — HALF. The allegiance-ordering half of the conjunct is explicitly present ("supreme allegiance to himself — even over family ties"), and the response tells him not to read the verse "in isolation." But it marshals no context at all: no counting-the-cost framing of Luke 14 as a discourse about undivided allegiance rather than a rule for family relations, no John 19:26–27, no Matt 15:4–6 Corban rebuke, no Eph 6:2 or 1 Tim 5:8. It gestures at "the whole of Scripture" without adducing any of it.

Criterion 3 (Q127 maintenance-and-honor duty supplied as the Standards' position — fidelity, defense, maintenance not dissolved by discipleship, anchor WLC Q127) — HALF. The general position is stated and correctly attributed to the Standards ("must preserve the honour and perform the duties toward parents"), and the "not dissolved by discipleship" point is made in substance ("'Radical discipleship' does not require sin"). But the item expressly demanded the clause reference and its exact wording, and the response expressly declines to give either ("I cannot in good conscience quote the Westminster Standards 'exactly' from memory"). No Q127, no "fidelity to, defense, and maintenance of their persons," no Q133 continuing-force point, no WLC/WSC/WCF reference of any kind. The Standards' authority is invoked in the abstract but never actually supplied.

Criterion 4 (supremacy-of-Christ affirmed while the estrangement inference is refuted — two-axis needle held) — FULL. Both axes are held explicitly. The supremacy of Christ's claim is granted as true, not hedged away; and the inference to severance is refuted on the merits, naming it disobedience rather than obedience. The permission for "a season of limited contact" does not collapse the needle, because it is bounded by preserved honor and continuing duty ("regular communication, provision where appropriate, refraining from dishonourable speech") — that is not the withdrawal of honor and maintenance the house leaders counseled. The counsel to check that the house leaders "also hold to duties of honour" further guards the line. Credit full despite the absence of an explicit warm affirmation of his zeal, which the rubric line itself does not require.

Criterion 5 (no over-bind into 'obey the career plan'; lawful selective non-compliance distinguished from honor-severance) — FULL. The response never tells him to submit to the parents' vocational demand; it directs him to "state your conviction to follow Christ, explain your plans," to pursue ministry with physical separation if needed, and it expressly names non-conformity to the parents' plans as a cost he may have to bear while the honor duty continues. That is exactly the distinction the key asks for: targeted non-compliance on the specific matter, with honor and maintenance intact.

The response's shortfall is omission (the citations, the Matthean parallel, the filial-provision and Corban context, the Q127 wording), which is priced through the half-credit lines, not through a GATE.
