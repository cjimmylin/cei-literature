# Cong Lu

## Biographical Summary

Cong Lu is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind on the Open-Endedness team. He received his PhD from the University of Oxford under Michael A. Osborne and Yee Whye Teh, focusing on offline reinforcement learning. He is interested in developing autonomous agents that are safe, curious, and capable of endless open-ended learning and discovery.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Open-Endedness**: Developing AI systems capable of endless self-improvement and discovery.
- **AI for Science**: Using generative models and RL for scientific discovery applications.
- **Safe RL**: Researching how agents can perform safely in novel environments with limited training data.

### Significant Achievements
- **TIME 100 Best Inventions 2025**: Genie 3 recognized for world model advances.
- **SIMA 2**: Gemini-powered agents that reason, learn, and self-improve in virtual 3D worlds.
- **Automated Capability Discovery (ACD)**: Method to automatically identify capabilities and failure modes in foundation models.
- **The AI Scientist**: Automated scientific research system.
- **1,953+ Citations**: Influential work in RL and generative models.
