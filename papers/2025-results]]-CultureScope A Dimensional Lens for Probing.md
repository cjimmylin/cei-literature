## Abstract

Most comprehensive evaluation framework for cultural understanding in LLMs. Novel dimensional schema with 3 layers and 140 dimensions based on cultural iceberg theory: Institutional Norms, Behavioral Patterns, and Core Values and Social Structures. Framework applicable to Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese cultural evaluation. Core Values and Social Structures layer directly probes cultural values.
