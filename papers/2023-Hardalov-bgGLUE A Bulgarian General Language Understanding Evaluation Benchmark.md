## Abstract

Bulgarian NLU benchmark with nine tasks covering NLI, fact-checking, NER, sentiment analysis, QA. First systematic evaluation of pre-trained language models for Bulgarian. Public leaderboard available.

## Data Description

bgGLUE benchmark: nine Bulgarian NLU tasks (NLI, NER, fact-checking, sentiment, QA, etc.) with curated datasets, standard splits, and a public leaderboard. All datasets released via HuggingFace Hub.
