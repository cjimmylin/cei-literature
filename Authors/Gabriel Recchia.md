# Gabriel Recchia

## Biographical Summary

Gabriel Recchia is a cognitive scientist working on the evaluation and alignment of large language models as the director of Modulo Research. He received his PhD in Cognitive Science from the University of Indiana in 2012. He has studied at Stanford University and the University of Memphis Institute for Intelligent Systems. At the University of Cambridge's Winton Centre for Risk and Evidence Communication, he led user testing research and evaluation of patient-friendly genetic reports and the prognostic tool Predict: Breast Cancer.

## Research & Impact

Recchia's research spans cognitive science, risk communication, and AI alignment:

- **AI Alignment**: Co-authored work identifying 18 foundational challenges in assuring the alignment and safety of large language models, organized into three categories: scientific understanding of LLMs, development and deployment methods, and sociotechnical challenges, along with posing 200+ concrete research questions.

- **Scalable Oversight**: His team released a dataset of expert-annotated valid and invalid solutions involving long-form reasoning intended to facilitate scalable oversight research.

- **Inverse Scaling**: Contributed to research on tasks where language model performance decreases with scale, published in Transactions on Machine Learning Research.

- **Industry Collaboration**: Has contributed to some of Anthropic's Frontier Red Team evaluation/demo projects as part of collaborations with Hidden Variable Limited.

His research concerns the communication of information in ways that support comprehension and informed decision-making, including distributional models and their applications in the cognitive and social sciences.
