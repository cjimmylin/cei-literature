# Percy Liang

## Research Summary

Percy Liang is an Associate Professor of Computer Science, Senior Fellow at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI, and Associate Professor, by courtesy, of Statistics at Stanford University. He is the director of the Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM). His research focuses on making foundation models (in particular, language models) more accessible through open-source and understandable through rigorous benchmarking.
