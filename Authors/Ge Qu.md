# Ge Qu

## Biographical Summary

Ge Qu is a researcher working on text-to-SQL parsing and database-grounded NLP tasks. Qu has contributed to influential benchmarks bridging natural language understanding and database systems.

## Research & Impact

Qu's primary research contributions are in text-to-SQL:

- **BIRD Benchmark**: Co-author of the BIRD benchmark, a large-scale text-to-SQL dataset containing 12,751 pairs of text-to-SQL data. BIRD emphasizes database values and highlights challenges of dirty database contents, external knowledge requirements, and SQL efficiency in massive databases. Even ChatGPT only achieved 40.08% execution accuracy compared to human performance of 92.96%.

- **BIRD-INTERACT**: Co-author of the BIRD-INTERACT benchmark (October 2025), which restores realism through a comprehensive interaction environment coupling databases with hierarchical knowledge bases, metadata files, and function-driven user simulators, enabling models to solicit clarifications, retrieve knowledge, and recover from errors.

This research addresses the gap between academic text-to-SQL studies and real-world applications where database schema alone is insufficient.
