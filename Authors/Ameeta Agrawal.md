# Ameeta Agrawal

## Biographical Summary

Ameeta Agrawal is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Portland State University, where she directs the PortNLP Lab. Her research focuses on natural language processing, particularly multilingual NLP, emotion detection, and ensuring equitable treatment of diverse linguistic communities by large language models. She is multilingual, fluent in English, French, Hindi, and has understanding of several Indian and one African language.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Multilingual NLP**: Developing models capable of equitable handling of text from diverse social groups and linguistic communities.
- **LLM Fairness**: Addressing persistent disparities in how LLMs treat text across linguistic and writing styles (NSF-funded research).
- **Emotion and Sentiment Analysis**: Advancing techniques for detecting emotions, sarcasm, and sentiment in text across languages.
- **Compassionate Computing**: Co-founded the CoCo Lab to explore broader real-world implications and ethical considerations of NLP technologies.

### Significant Achievements
- **NSF Grant**: Received funding to address LLM disparities in treatment of diverse linguistic communities.
- **David E. Wedge Excellence in Faculty Diversity Award (2022)**: Recognized for contributions to diversity in academia.
- **PortNLP Lab Director**: Leads graduate research pushing boundaries in efficient and multilingual NLP.
- **CoCo Lab Co-Founder**: Established interdisciplinary lab bringing together social science, linguistics, policymakers, and computer science.
