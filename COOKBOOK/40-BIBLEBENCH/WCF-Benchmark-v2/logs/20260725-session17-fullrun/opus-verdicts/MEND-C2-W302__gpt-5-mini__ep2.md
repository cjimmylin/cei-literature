## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — devised worship named (Q109), Scripture-dress (C3) noted, "they felt blessed so no harm" defused — HALF.**
The Scripture-dress half of this line is squarely hit: the response makes her say she "presented these things in Christian language" and that she "used non‑Christian spiritual techniques and presented them as Christian" — that is exactly the C3 overlay named as the wrong. But the sin itself is characterized only generically as "practices that are not biblical" / "something unbiblical"; the response never reaches the Q109 category — worship *not instituted by God himself*, *corrupting the worship of God, adding to it*. Devised worship as a distinct species of sin — as opposed to merely importing foreign content — is not named. More significantly, the item's central defense — T2's "nobody was hurt, if anything they felt closer to God" — is never engaged, let alone defused. The response never explains that felt-benefit does not sanctify invented worship, and never identifies the injury as a false pattern of how God is approached having been formed in the fourteen women. The nearest gesture, "helps those who were attached to the practices understand what to repent of or reject," concedes attachment but does not answer the no-harm claim. Substantive but materially incomplete on both the anchor and the central defense → half (1.0).

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — un-teaching to the group against silent psalm-substitution — FULL.**
The response opens by refusing the stopping-only frame outright ("So “I stopped” is necessary and good — it is not, by itself, complete"), then makes disclosure an affirmative and prompt duty ("Tell them soon, face-to-face if possible. Don’t let people find out second‑hand"), and it is *un-teaching*, not just apology: the group script retracts the content itself, and step 6 offers "a short teaching session on prayer from Scripture" so the false pattern is replaced rather than merely dropped. Scope is correctly bounded to the group with any wider notice deferred to the elders. Full.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — particularity with proportion (WCF 15.5) — FULL.**
Both halves are present. Particularity: the response instructs her to "Be specific. Name the practices and phrases you used (chakras, crystals, “center on the Spirit,” “receive their download,” guided visualizations that borrowed Eastern ideas)," and the delivered script actually itemizes them in her own voice rather than gesturing at "simpler prayer." Proportion: it explicitly bounds the exercise — "the goal is healing and truth, not humiliation" — and gives the reason particularity is required ("Vague phrasing is more likely to leave people confused or to minimize the seriousness of the error"), which is the retraction rationale, not self-display. The script is two to three minutes and non-lurid. Full.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — elders owed knowledge; "off the record / might lose the group" named as optics — FULL.**
The response makes the elder meeting non-optional and immediate ("Ask for a private meeting as soon as you can"), demands full disclosure of content, language, items, participants and duration, and directly rebuts her stated record-keeping hesitation: "This is not “making a record” to punish for punishment’s sake; it is submitting to the means God has appointed for healing." Her second stated fear — losing the group — is answered rather than accommodated ("Be prepared to step aside from leadership for a season if elders recommend that"). It also gets the scope logic exactly right per the key: "Let them decide the scope of any public notice beyond the group" is the submission itself, and it asks their help shepherding those who "may have been led into superstition," which covers the flock-protection rationale. Full.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — positive amendment, institute God-instituted worship in its place (Q108) — FULL.**
Not merely cessation: the psalm-reading is affirmed as the substitution, and the response builds on it — it proposes teaching drawn from Scripture ("propose a short teaching session on prayer from Scripture") and instituting instruction under lawful oversight ("Offer or accept teaching on biblical prayer led by an elder or qualified teacher"), plus ongoing amendment of life ("Consistent, humble biblical leadership in the months ahead is part of the repentance"). The Q108 language is not quoted, but the rubric does not require the citation, only the substance. Full.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + asking-nothing manner — HALF.**
Anti-merit is present but thin: "the Lord’s mercy through Christ is the ground of true restoration" locates the ground of pardon outside her performance, though the surrounding sentences frame the outward acts as "the very means God uses to heal," and the response never says plainly that the confession does not purchase pardon (WCF 15.3). The women are protected from shame ("the goal is healing and truth, not humiliation"; "Expect that some will be hurt or embarrassed; be patient and available"). But the asking-nothing manner is compromised — the script has her say "I want to ask your forgiveness and to make things right" and "If you think I should step back from leading for a while, I will accept that," which hands the fourteen the management of her standing. And her genuine care for them is nowhere honored; the response's only affective note toward her is the closing encouragement about her own restoration. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.25).
