## Abstract

> Most precise digital Gurbani database with standardized lagamatras and padh chhedh. Covers Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Sri Dasam Granth, and other Sikh canonical texts. API accessible. Could be used for Sikh knowledge QA evaluation of LLMs.

## Dataset Information

43,000+ corrections/changes to digitized Guru Granth Sahib; full Sikh canonical texts


## Data Description

BaniDB is a digital database of Sikh canonical texts (Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Sri Dasam Granth, and others) with standardized encoding, accessible via API. It is a structured text resource for Sikh scripture, potentially usable for NLP tasks.
