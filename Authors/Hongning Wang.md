# Hongning Wang

## Research Summary

Hongning Wang is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. Prior to joining Tsinghua in 2023, he spent 9 years at the University of Virginia, where he was an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign in 2014. His research interests include machine learning and information retrieval, with special emphasis on sequential decision making problems and computational user behavior modeling. He is the recipient of the NSF CAREER Award, 2014 Yahoo ACE Award, Google 2012 PhD Fellowship, and 2012 Yahoo Key Scientific Challenges Award. He has 9,171 citations and has served in leadership roles including SIGIR 2024 General Co-Chair and KDD 2022 Workshop Co-Chair.
