# Etsuko Ishii

## Biographical Summary

Etsuko Ishii is a PhD student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Her research focuses on natural language processing, particularly dialogue systems and the representation of human values in AI systems. She works on understanding how language models encode and express human values across different dimensions.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research examines how large language models represent human values, developing high-dimensional frameworks for characterizing value representations in AI systems. This work contributes to understanding the implicit value systems embedded in language models.

### Significant Achievements
Contributed to developing novel frameworks for measuring human value representation in language models, providing tools for more nuanced analysis of AI alignment with human values.

## Major Publications

- **High-Dimension Human Value Representation in Large Language Models** (2024) - Framework for value representation analysis
- Research on dialogue systems and conversational AI
