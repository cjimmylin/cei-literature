# Janel Thamkul

## Research Summary

Janel Thamkul drives legal strategy on AI as Deputy General Counsel at Anthropic, a frontier AI lab pioneering safe and responsible deployment of foundation models. She has built and currently oversees the legal teams counseling AI development, commercial deals, product deployment, regulatory/litigation, privacy, and more.

Prior to Anthropic, Thamkul was at Google where she led Google's Research product counsel team and advised Google's AI governance and ethics team. She has spoken at Stanford University's AI Symposium on trustworthy AI and participated in governance discussions including the Stanford event "A Conversation: Governance Options for Generative AI."

Thamkul earned her Bachelor's Degree in Fine Arts from UCLA, an Associate's Degree in Fashion Design from FIDM, and a J.D. degree from the University of California, Berkeley School of Law. Her unique career path went "from art student to fashion designer to litigator to Google Research and now Anthropic."
