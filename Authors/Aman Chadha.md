# Aman Chadha

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aman Chadha is a Senior AI/ML Manager at Apple, leading rigorous evaluation and safety initiatives. He is also an Adjunct Professor at Stanford University. His work spans the optimization of large scale models and the development of comprehensive benchmarks for multimodal AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aman's research addresses the scalability and reliability of foundation models. He has curated significant resources like the *Distilled AI* newsletter and leads research on efficient fine-tuning and massive multitasking. His work at Apple involves ensuring that generative models are both high-performing and safe for global deployment.
