# Yejin Bang

## Biographical Summary

Yejin Bang is a researcher at KAIST working on natural language processing and AI ethics. Her research focuses on building AI systems that can reason about ethical questions and detect problematic outputs such as hallucinations in large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Yejin's research centers on ethical AI systems, including developing methods for AI to reason about moral dilemmas and open-ended ethical questions. She also works on evaluating and improving the reliability of large language models, particularly in detecting and mitigating hallucinations.

### Significant Achievements
She has contributed to several influential papers on AI ethics benchmarks and evaluation methodologies. Her work on enabling classifiers to make ethical judgments and answering open-ended ethical quandaries has advanced the field of ethical AI reasoning.
