## Part A — Pilot Co-Design Brief (1 locale + 1 diaspora sub-locale)

### A.1 Goals

The pilot exists to produce the **held-out adaptation-selectivity eval** for one locale and one
diaspora community — the primary shipping contribution per 05 §1/§9 — at a scale that fits the stated
~$10–20k envelope and yields **estimable quantities plus passing gates**, not final-scale N (05 §8,
"Not applied" note).

Concrete pilot goals, each traceable to a 05 gate:

1. **Author a survey-groundable locale norm-set** for the locale + diaspora pair via native co-design
   (never translation; SHADES per-item cultural-validity tags), following the KoBBQ partition
   (Simply-Transferred / Target-Modified / Sample-Removed / Newly-Created) — 05 §3.2.
2. **Deliver 30 scenario-families × 4 variants**, *each paired with the §2.1 anti-adaptation controls*
   (unwarranted-cue trap / norm-conflicting-harm trap / same-locale conflicting-cue consistency pair),
   so **selectivity** (not adaptation rate) is estimable — 05 §3.1, §5, §8.
3. **Instantiate the tetrad** CD / AA / OA / AVA with co-authored keypoint rubrics — 05 §2, §5.
4. **Exercise the diaspora / bicultural cue-integration condition** (verified empty in the 218-corpus)
   — the diaspora sub-locale's distinctive job (05 §3.2, §9 novelty pillar 3).
5. **Establish, not assume, the motivating hypotheses in-pilot** (the 0–1/5 divergent gap, multi-turn
   erosion, communal-unit unsaturation are descriptive-only motivations, N_eff≈3.4 — 05 §1 disclosure,
   §6): produce the data that the P3 go/no-go table (05 §8.1) adjudicates.
6. **Ship the governance and disclosure apparatus**: datasheet (co-design composition + positionality,
   per-item validity territory, floor/divergent decision procedure, consensus threshold, CARE scoping,
   **training-prohibition / retraction clause**) — 05 §4.

Explicit **non-goals** (bound by the gate): shipping MGCFA as a headline result (demoted to exploratory
appendix / development machinery, C3); a determinate "0–1/5 divergent gap" headline (distributional
only, C2); Nigeria or any second full locale (expansion only, C4); IRT/2PL pruning at pilot N
(deferred to item-level CV + point-biserial, X4).

### A.2 The co-design process — partners *shape* items, they are not *surveyed*

The load-bearing distinction (and the reason this is the long pole): **community partners are
co-authors of the instrument, not respondents to it.** Surveying comes later and separately (§A.3,
Part B), and its job is only to *adjudicate* which co-authored items carry determinate gold. Three
human-participant pools are kept strictly distinct (see §D-1, a drafting disambiguation 05 leaves
implicit):

| Pool | Role | Shapes items? | Ships psychometrics? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Co-design partners** (this section) | Author scenarios, cues, controls, rubrics | **Yes — they build it** | Positionality documented (not a value-vector panel) |
| **Survey respondents** (§A.3) | Validate norm claims; supply divergent-band distributions | No — they answer | Yes (demographics + value vector) — Part B §B.4 |
| **Panel annotators** (Part B) | Rate transcripts; set human ceiling; validate judges | No — they judge outputs | Yes (demographics + psychometrics) — Part B |

The co-design workflow, per locale team (locale team and diaspora team run in parallel, sharing a
scenario-template library but authoring independently):

1. **Elicit scenario seeds from lived experience**, not from a researcher's scenario list. Partners
   surface the situations *they* recognize as culturally load-bearing across the 05 §3.1 domains
   (family decisions, food/hospitality, business etiquette, dispute mediation, ritual/holiday
   logistics, health advice). *Illustrative:* a Korean partner proposes an eldercare-arrangement
   decision among adult siblings; the researcher would not have known which turn the obligation
   becomes binding.
2. **Partners name the load-bearing cues and author the cue-schedule** (05 §3.1: upfront / drip /
   late-turn reveal / conflicting cues from two participants). Deciding *which* contextual detail is a
   genuine cue vs. surface noise is exactly the tacit knowledge co-design exists to capture.
3. **Partners author the divergent-value items** — where a locally-endorsed resolution conflicts with
   the Western-liberal default (filial obligation over individual preference; communal consent over
   individual autonomy; complementarian family-role expectations — 05 §2 AA). These are flagged as
   *candidates* for the distributional band; the ~85% survey threshold, not the partners' say-so,
   decides floor-vs-divergent (05 §4; this is the anti-"co-design discretion" guardrail).
4. **Partners author the §2.1 anti-adaptation controls** — the step that most needs insider knowledge:
   - *Unwarranted-cue traps*: a cue that *looks* like it warrants a locale shift but the locale's own
     norms don't support (correct = no shift). *Illustrative:* a Korean surname alone does not warrant
     assuming filial-priority values for a specific individual.
   - *Norm-conflicting-harm traps*: the culturally-cued user signals a preference the local survey
     marks harmful/non-modal, or the modal resolution would violate a UDHR floor clause (correct =
     principled non-adaptation with acknowledgment; the floor is never traded — 05 §2.1(ii), §4).
   - *Same-locale conflicting-cue consistency pairs*: two same-locale items with conflicting cues; a
     panderer flips inconsistently (consistency, not adaptation, is scored — 05 §2.1(iii)).
5. **Partners author the matched OA (essentialism) items**: for each group-cue item, a matched item
   where the *individual* signals a non-modal preference, so adapting on the group cue alone scores as
   stereotyping (05 §2 OA, the difference-aware / difference-blind synthesis).
6. **Partners write the AA keypoint rubrics** per scenario (05 §5 AA) and, for communal cells, the
   deliberation-facilitation rubrics (Ubuntu-*indaba*-style / Confucian role-dyad framing —
   operationalized as tradition-board guidance, not keyword aesthetics; 05 §3.1). These rubrics are
   later IRR-tested; if α is uninterpretable the communal cells go exploratory (05 §5, §8.1 M6).
7. **Diaspora-specific authoring**: the diaspora team builds the **bicultural cue-integration**
   scenarios — where heritage-culture and host-culture cues co-occur and must be *integrated*, not
   forced into one frame (05 §3.2, §9). *Illustrative:* a Korean-American navigating an eldercare
   decision under both filial expectation and a US-workplace-leave constraint.

Two authoring disciplines are inherited verbatim from 05: **native authoring, never MT** (05 §3.2,
KB 11 §3 ladder rung 5–6), and **per-item cultural-validity tags** (SHADES) so a model is scored only
on items locally valid for the pool it is being compared against.

### A.3 Where the survey fits (co-design's adjudicator, not its substitute)

Co-design *proposes* the norm-set; a **demographically-balanced local survey (N≥100 for distribution
estimation)** *disposes* which items are floor vs divergent (05 §3.2, §4, §8 P2). The survey is
authored jointly with partners but administered to an independent respondent pool (§A.2 table).

- Items reaching **≥~85% locale-survey consensus** carry determinate gold (cultural floor).
- Items **below** threshold — and any "no consensus exists" outcome — route to the **distributional
  band**: scored against the survey distribution with entropy flags, **no determinate-gold headline**
  (05 §2 AA, §4, C2). Intra-locale strata (urban/rural × generation, and the diaspora acculturation
  axis) are preserved, not averaged away (05 §10).
- The **universal floor** (safety, non-deception, UDHR-clause violations) is decided by the published
  clause list in the datasheet, **not** by survey majority and **not** by co-design discretion (05 §4).

*Drafting note (§D-2):* 05 sets N≥100 for the *eval's* distribution estimation but ties any
MGCFA/DIF respondent-N to a Monte-Carlo power target (likely ≥200–300, ≥400 if stratified). At the
pilot envelope the pilot funds **N≥100 for the eval**; MGCFA ships as development machinery only unless
Jimmy funds the powered respondent-N (05 §4, §8, C3). This brief plans to the N≥100 eval survey and
treats the powered MGCFA survey as an unfunded expansion line-item.

### A.4 Community-partner roles & governance

Grounded in KB 12 §3's **affected-community co-design** source (context experts raised item agreement
68.75%→93% [2022-Nkemelu]) with **positionality documented** as a hard requirement (KB 12 §3 rule;
05 §4 datasheet).

- **Locale co-design lead (1) + co-designers (2–3)**: native residents/members with domain range
  across the §3.1 domains; author locale items, cues, controls, rubrics.
- **Diaspora co-design lead (1) + co-designers (1–2)**: bicultural members authoring the diaspora
  cue-integration scenarios.
- **Tradition/community advisory board (2–3, part-time)**: guides communal-unit framing (indaba /
  role-dyad) and reviews divergent-item framing for "operate within, not endorse" neutrality
  (05 §3.1, §10). Advisory, with a **veto layer** over items they judge misrepresent the community
  (KB 11 §6 ELECTRE-style hard-constraint spirit).
- **Research team (Jimmy + collaborators)**: methodology, factorial assembly, survey/judge machinery,
  datasheet, pre-registration; **does not overrule** partner authorship on cultural validity, only on
  measurement structure (which factor a scenario populates, seed counts, holdout discipline).

**Governance instruments** (deliverables in themselves, §A.7):
- A short **co-design MOU / terms of engagement** stating authorship credit, decision rights,
  the veto layer, compensation-as-recognition (§B.5), and the **data-sovereignty / retraction**
  provisions (§A.5).
- A **positionality statement** for every named contributor (partners and research team) published in
  the datasheet (05 §4).
- A **decision log**: which items partners proposed, which the survey routed to floor vs divergent,
  which the advisory board vetoed — so the floor/divergent boundary is auditable and demonstrably *not*
  co-design discretion (05 §4).

### A.5 Ethics, consent, data sovereignty

- **Ethics/IRB review** before any human contact; separate consent for the three pools (co-designers as
  contracted expert contributors; survey respondents; panel annotators).
- **Informed consent** covering: purpose (an LLM cultural-competence eval), that authored material
  becomes a **held-out** benchmark, the training-prohibition clause, and the right to withdraw/retract.
- **Data sovereignty**: the co-design team **co-owns** the locale norm-set. The dataset carries a
  **training-prohibition clause** (dataset may not be used to train models) and a **retraction clause**
  (a community may retract items judged misrepresentative), both required by 05 (§4, §6, §8, m4). A
  **community pre-release review** gate precedes any public release (05 §8 P4).
- **CARE (Indigenous data governance)**: 05 §4 requires a CARE-compliance statement *or explicit
  scoping-out* (as INTRA does). For the **illustrative** Korean / Korean-American pair, Indigenous-data
  CARE is **explicitly scoped out** in the datasheet (the content is not Indigenous), while the
  collective-benefit and authority-to-control spirit is honored through the co-design governance above.
  **If Jimmy's final pair includes Indigenous-adjacent content, CARE compliance becomes binding**, not
  scoped out (§D-6).
- **Private holdout + canaries + contamination hygiene**: authored scenarios include a private holdout
  partition and canary items; hand-authoring itself doubles as a contamination control
  (KB 11 §3; 05 §6).

## Part B — Annotator-Recruitment Spec (panel raters & value-vector pool)

Scope: the **panel-annotator pool** that (1) rates full transcripts to validate AA/selectivity against
blind human judgment and set the human ceiling, (2) provides the culturally-competent human baseline
for CD, and (3) supplies the annotator-level value vectors that make the divergent band re-aggregable
(05 §5, §7, §8.1; KB 12 §1/§4/§8). Survey respondents (§A.3) inherit the same demographic + psychometric
shipping requirements (§B.4) so both human-ground-truth pools are re-aggregable.

### B.1 Criteria

- **Membership / cultural fluency**: locale-panel annotators are native residents/members; diaspora-panel
  annotators are bicultural members of the diaspora sub-locale (they bracket the bicultural construct,
  05 §7 "the human bicultural baseline brackets the construct").
- **Bracketing non-local annotators (small deliberate subset)**: 05 §7 requires CD validation against
  *both* locals **and** non-local humans to bracket the construct — recruit a small non-local comparison
  set explicitly labeled as such (not mixed into the locale ceiling).
- **Demographic balance within locale**: staffed to fill the intra-locale strata that matter for the
  distributional band — **urban/rural × generation** (and the diaspora acculturation axis), so the
  panel can be split by framing (§B.6), not averaged (05 §10; KB 12 §4).
- **Independence from co-design partners**: annotators are a separate pool from item authors (§A.2
  table) to avoid authorship-grading circularity.
- **Blind-rating capacity**: able to rate transcripts blind to model identity and condition.

### B.2 Native-language review requirement

All value-laden rating is performed **in the item's native language / register** (05 §3.3 language
factorial; KB 11 §3 "never MT for value items"). Requirements:

- Annotators rate the **native-language** transcript; no machine-translated intermediary is scored.
- Where code-mixed or transliterated variants exist (05 §3.3; KB 11 §4), annotators fluent in the
  relevant register rate those variants.
- A **translation-as-content control** and a reported **translation-retention rate** guard against
  grader-bias artifacts (Perspective scored German ~3× more toxic than its English translation
  [2024-Nogara]; judges worse in low-resource languages — KB 11 §4.6; 05 §5, §10).
- Judges (automated scorers) are validated **per-locale** against this native-language panel; κw must
  reach **≥0.6** vs the (committed, ≠1.0) local human ceiling or the judge is re-trained / the cell goes
  distributional (05 §8.1 P2).

### B.3 Panel size & structure (illustrative; §D-4)

05 specifies "local panels" and N≥100 for the *survey* but does not fix a *panel-rater* N. Illustrative
default: a rating panel large enough to (a) estimate κw with usable CIs against the human ceiling and
(b) populate each urban/rural × generation stratum with multiple raters for the per-framing split — on
the order of a few dozen raters, each item rated by multiple annotators for disagreement signal
(KB 12 §1: individual differences explain 39.5% of offensiveness variance vs 2.9% country; preserve all
ratings). Exact N is Jimmy's to set against the smallest-cell power target in the pre-registration
(05 §8.1).

### B.4 Demographics + psychometrics shipped at the annotator level

Per KB 12 §1/§4 and the pluralism checklist (§8), **every annotator ships a portable value vector**, and
**annotator-level labels are shipped** so users can re-aggregate:

- **Demographics**: the strata above (urban/rural, generation, gender, region/relevant local axis;
  diaspora: acculturation/generation-in-host-country). For the illustrative pair, add the India-style
  reminder that *locally salient* axes (there: region/caste) replace imported ones — for Korea the
  salient axes are generation and urban/rural rather than caste (KB 11 §4.4; 05 §10).
- **Validated psychometrics**: **MFQ-2, Schwartz values, WVS items, political leaning** (KB 12 §1) —
  administered in native language. These make each rating carry a value vector so the divergent band is
  scored against *declared* value communities, not a collapsed majority.
- **Shipping format**: annotator-level ratings + demographics + psychometrics released with the dataset
  (subject to consent + the training-prohibition/retraction clause), with an explicit
  **empirical ceiling ≠ 1.0** derived from human disagreement (KorNAT ceiling 0.450 [2024-Lee];
  05 §8.1 commits ≠1.0 ceilings). Test–retest instability is measured and **propagated into error bars**
  (10–18% flips on controversial items [2024-Boerstler]; KB 12 §6).

### B.5 Compensation framing

- **Recognition of expert cultural labor, not extractive micro-tasking.** Co-design partners are
  contracted expert contributors with authorship credit; panel annotators and survey respondents are
  fairly compensated for skilled rating/response time (KB 12 §3 "recruitment cost; positionality must be
  documented" — the labor is acknowledged, not hidden as the InstructGPT "~40 undisclosed contractors"
  anti-pattern, KB 12 §3).
- **Envelope, not rates**: the pilot's ~$10–20k envelope (05 §8) covers author-hours, survey-firm/panel
  time, and panel-hours for transcript rating. This brief does **not** invent per-role dollar rates
  (§D-5); it recommends an illustrative allocation — the majority to co-design author-hours and panel
  rating, a bounded slice to the N≥100 survey — with exact rates set by Jimmy against local fair-pay
  norms. The powered MGCFA respondent-survey is a **separate, unfunded** expansion line-item (§A.3).
- **Transparency**: compensation model + contributor roles are disclosed in the datasheet (KB 12 §3
  rule: the source is a disclosure requirement).

### B.6 Aggregation / reweighting plan — per-framing leaderboards where the panel splits

Bound to KB 12 §4's ordered **defensible moves** and 05's distributional-band commitment (C2). **No
collapse to a single majority label** (KB 12 §1: 97.1% of datasets do this wrongly; §2 determinism-as-
misalignment).

1. **Route by consensus** (05 §4): items at **≥~85%** panel/survey consensus → determinate gold, scored
   as right/wrong. Items **below** → distributional band.
2. **Score the distribution, not the mode** (KB 12 §2): N-sample the model per item; score the response
   *distribution* against the human distribution (KL / JSD / L2) with entropy/diversity metrics so a
   model cannot win by always hitting the mode; offer/track explicit indecision & incomparability
   options (KB 12 §2, §7 Adjudication 5).
3. **When the panel splits, split the leaderboard** (KB 12 §4 move 2): produce **per-framing
   leaderboards** — score each model against each declared value community rather than one averaged
   target. Framings are defined by the annotator strata/value-vectors: at minimum the pre-registered
   **urban/rural × generation** strata and the **diaspora acculturation** axis; optionally
   **psychometric clusters** (e.g., MFQ-2 / Schwartz profiles) where the survey reveals a stable split
   (§D-7 — the exact framing set is *set by the observed splits*, not fixed a priori). Report each
   framing's leaderboard separately; never publish only the collapsed score.
4. **Enable user re-aggregation** (KB 12 §4 move 1; §1): ship annotator-level labels + value vectors so
   downstream users can re-fit steerable weights to their own community.
5. **Empirical ceiling + instability** (KB 12 §4; 05 §8.1): every reported cell carries the human-
   disagreement ceiling (≠1.0) and propagated test–retest error bars; consensus stratification is
   preserved.
6. **The universal floor is exempt from reweighting** (05 §4; KB 12 §4 tiered normativity): floor items
   are scored strictly against the published clause list regardless of any framing's majority — a
   framing that would trade the floor does not get its own leaderboard credit for doing so.

  "annotator-level demographics + psychometrics" shipping requirement to the **panel annotators and
  survey respondents** (the human-ground-truth pools), with co-designers documented via positionality
  rather than value-vectors. This is an interpretation, not a change to 05.
- **D-2 — Two survey N's.** 05 distinguishes the eval survey (N≥100) from the powered MGCFA/DIF
  respondent survey (≥200–400). I planned the pilot to the **N≥100 eval survey** and treated the powered
  MGCFA survey as an **unfunded expansion line-item**, consistent with C3 (MGCFA ships as development
  machinery if unpowered).
- **D-3 — Timeline.** 05 gives phases P1–P4 but no calendar. The ~9-month schedule (§A.6) is
  illustrative and Jimmy-adjustable.
- **D-4 — Panel-rater N.** 05 fixes the survey N, not the rating-panel N. §B.3 proposes an illustrative
  size tied to κw-CI and per-stratum coverage; exact N set by the pre-registered smallest-cell power.
- **D-5 — Compensation rates.** 05 gives only the ~$10–20k envelope. §B.5 frames compensation and gives
  an illustrative allocation **without inventing dollar rates**; rates are Jimmy's against local fair-pay
  norms.
- **D-6 — CARE applicability.** 05 §4 requires CARE-compliance *or* explicit scoping-out. For the
  illustrative Korean/Korean-American pair I scoped it out (content not Indigenous) while honoring the
  spirit via governance; flagged that a different final pair may make CARE **binding**.
- **D-7 — Framing set for per-framing leaderboards.** 05/KB name urban/rural × generation and diaspora
  acculturation strata; I added optional psychometric-cluster framings and specified that the exact
  framing set is **determined by the observed survey/panel splits**, not fixed a priori — consistent
  with KB 12 §4 (per-framing reports) and 05 C2 (distributional-only, no determinate-gold headline).
- **D-8 — Illustrative pair.** South Korea + Korean-American is used as the worked example per 05 §8; it
  is **not** a committed selection. Final locale + diaspora choice is Jimmy's.
