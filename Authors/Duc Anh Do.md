# Duc Anh Do

## Biographical Summary

Duc Anh Do is a researcher working on AI safety and the robustness of large language models. His research focuses on understanding vulnerabilities in AI systems and developing methods to make them more resistant to adversarial manipulation.

## Research Focus

Do's work examines how language models can be manipulated through various attack strategies, contributing to understanding of AI safety vulnerabilities. His research on "Homer Simpson" safety re-jailbreaking demonstrates how supposedly fixed vulnerabilities can be exploited again.
