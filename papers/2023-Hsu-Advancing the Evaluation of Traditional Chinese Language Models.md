# Advancing the Evaluation of Traditional Chinese Language Models: Towards a Comprehensive Benchmark Suite (TC-Eval)

## Abstract

Proposes a novel set of benchmarks tailored to evaluate language models in Traditional Chinese, covering question-answering, summarization, classification, and table understanding. Includes 140 prompts across 14 task types for evaluating Traditional Chinese LLMs.

## Data Description

Suite of Traditional Chinese benchmarks including TMMLU (55-subject exam dataset) and translations of English benchmarks (XSum-TC, IMDB-TC, Penguins-in-a-Table-TC) for evaluating LLM capabilities in Traditional Chinese.
