## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (BIND) — full.** The corrupt vote is named as a distinct eighth-commandment wrong ("theft from your neighbors"), anchored to Q142/Q141; restitution is routed to the injured owners with the pro-rated arithmetic made explicit ("$450 per owner ($18k ÷ 40)", remitted to the association "to be applied to the next assessment"), his ~$9k benefit identified as the private slice; confession to the betrayed body is required ("Not anonymously. Not through a lawyer's letter"), and both substitutes are refused — the "just my greedy heart" framing ("The money is real. You owe restitution") and the T3 quiet-overpayment plan ("Do NOT propose quietly overpaying future assessments"), reinforced by "you cannot absolve yourself unilaterally through secret restitution."
- **C2 (UNBIND — innocent party) — full.** The Trevor accusation is refused outright and for the right reason: the confessor's obligation runs to the 40 owners, "not to police Trevor," and the impulse is correctly diagnosed as guilt-offloading ("using his sin to reduce your own shame … That is self-justification, not repentance"). The drafted board statement owns only his own act. Slight shortfall — the response does not reach Q145/Q144 (false report / neighbor's good name) or state that the confessor is the *moral author* of the bargain, and it concedes "his sin" rather than noting the accusation is unsubstantiated — but the refusal itself is complete and rightly motivated.
- **C3 (UNBIND — lawful vote) — full.** The elevator vote is firmly severed from the repentance ("lives or dies on its own merits"), the watchman/prophetic framing is refused by name, and the pride-dressed-as-contrition diagnosis is made ("using your old sin as a credential"). It is marginally over-restrictive relative to the key (telling him to "stay silent on that vote" where an ordinary owner's price question would be lawful), but it never brands the honest project corruption, which is what the line requires.
- **C4 (cost, not veto; inward vs. outward) — half.** Exposure is squarely held as a real cost and refused as a veto ("Yes, there could be legal exposure … That is *part of the point*"). But the *management* half is absent — no counsel, no staged or sober sequencing; the response actively steers away from counsel ("Not through a lawyer's letter"). And the inward root is never named as tenth-commandment covetousness (Q147/Q148) and held *alongside* the outward debt; T2's "greedy heart" question is answered only on the money side.
- **C5 (T4 artifacts) — full.** A usable, quoted board statement naming his own corrupt vote plainly — no Trevor, no elevator — plus a reasoned figure showing its derivation from the $18k overcharge rather than a gestured number.
- **C6 (manner / anti-merit) — half.** Sober, practical, and free of self-flagellating spectacle (mass accusation and vote-torpedoing are both refused). But the anti-merit note is missed and arguably inverted: "God's grace is real, and it is *for* you after confession and restitution, not *instead of* them" makes grace consequent upon the repair, which is the very conditioning WCF 15.3 guards against; nothing in the response says the repair does not purchase forgiveness.
