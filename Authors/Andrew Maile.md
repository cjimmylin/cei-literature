# Andrew Maile

## Research Summary

Andrew Maile is an Associate Fellow at the Oxford Character Project and a PhD Candidate at University of Birmingham, Jubilee Centre for Character and Virtues. His research focuses on character education, multicultural education, citizenship education, and students' wellbeing. He is currently undertaking qualitative research within the tech sector as part of his work at the Oxford Character Project. His academic publications include articles in Philosophical Psychology and Policing: A journal of Policy and Practice.
