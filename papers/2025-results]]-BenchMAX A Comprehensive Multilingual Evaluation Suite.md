## Abstract

Multi-way multilingual evaluation benchmark covering 17 languages including Telugu with 10 diverse tasks. DeepSeek-V3 accuracy drops from 50%+ (English/French) to below 40% for Telugu. Native-speaking annotators verify translations. Telugu included in comprehensive multilingual evaluation. Shows significant performance gaps for Telugu across reasoning and instruction-following tasks.
