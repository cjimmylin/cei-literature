# Alexandra Olteanu

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandra Olteanu is a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research (MSR) Montreal, where she is a core member of the Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics (FATE) team. She earned her PhD in Computer Science from EPFL and held dual Master's degrees from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and University POLITEHNICA of Bucharest. Prior to MSR, she was a Social Good Fellow at IBM's T.J. Watson Research Center.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandra’s research is at the intersection of computational social science and social computing. She focuses on unpacking the practices and assumptions in the design and evaluation of AI systems. Her recent work investigates the social science measurement challenges in evaluating Generative AI and the operationalization of "fairness" constructs in NLP.

### Significant Achievements
She is a leading voice in the critical survey of "bias" in NLP, advocating for more rigorous definitions and metrics. Her work on quantifying social biases has influenced how the industry approaches extrinsic fairness metrics. She has co-authored influential surveys that challenge the power dynamics inherent in language technologies.

## Major Publications

- [Language (Technology) is Power: A Critical Survey of "Bias" in NLP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14050) (2020) - *A seminal survey on the conceptualization of bias.*
- [Social Data: Biases, Methodological Pitfalls, and Ethical Boundaries](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/social-data-biases-methodological-pitfalls-and-ethical-boundaries/) (2019) - *A foundational study on the ethics of social data.*
