## Abstract

Korean cultural evaluation framework featuring datasets of cultural news, idioms, and poetry. Assesses cultural comprehension and reasoning at word, sentence, and paragraph levels. Addresses Western bias in translated benchmarks.

## Data Description

KULTURE Bench: an evaluation framework for Korean cultural comprehension, featuring datasets of cultural news, idioms, and poetry for word-, sentence-, and paragraph-level assessment.
