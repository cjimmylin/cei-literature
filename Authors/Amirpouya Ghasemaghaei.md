# Amirpouya Ghasemaghaei

## Research Summary

Amirpouya Ghasemaghaei is a researcher at the University of Central Florida. Their research focuses on multilingual and multimodal AI systems, contributing to efforts to evaluate and improve AI models across diverse linguistic and cultural contexts. They have participated in large-scale collaborative benchmarking projects aimed at assessing AI performance on culturally diverse content.
