# Chao Peng

## Biographical Summary

Chao Peng is a Research Scientist at Meta AI (FAIR) and a PhD student at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by Professor Philipp Krähenbühl. He is a highly influential researcher in Computer Vision, particularly known for his foundational work on object detection during his time at Megvii (Face++), where he served as a Research Manager. He also served as the Director of Perception at NIO before returning to academia and research roles at Meta.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Multimodal LLMs**: Developing unified architectures for vision-language tasks (e.g., VisionLLM).
- **Video Understanding**: Scaling recognition and detection models to complex temporal data.
- **Efficient Perception**: Designing backbones and frameworks for high-performance dense prediction.

### Significant Achievements
- **MegDet**: Lead author of the CVPR 2018 work that introduced large mini-batch training for object detection, which became a standard practice in the field.
- **Architectural Innovation**: Contributed to the development of several widely used vision backbones and semantic segmentation frameworks, including DetNet and BiSeNet.

## Major Publications
- **MegDet**: A Large Mini-Batch Object Detector.
- **VisionLLM**: Large Language Models as Open-Ended Visual Analyzers.
- **DetNet**: A Backbone network for Object Detection.
- **BiSeNet**: Bilateral Segmentation Network for Real-time Semantic Segmentation.
