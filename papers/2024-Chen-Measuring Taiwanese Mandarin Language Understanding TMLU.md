## Abstract

Introduces TMLU, a holistic evaluation suite for assessing advanced knowledge and reasoning in LLMs under Taiwanese Mandarin context. 37 subjects across social science, STEM, humanities, Taiwan-specific content, ranging from middle school to professional levels.

## Data Description

TMLU: a Taiwanese Mandarin language understanding benchmark with 2,981 multiple-choice questions across 37 subjects spanning social science, STEM, humanities, Taiwan-specific content, and other, from middle school to professional levels, with chain-of-thought explanations.
