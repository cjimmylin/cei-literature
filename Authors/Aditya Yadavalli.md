# Aditya Yadavalli

## Research Summary

Aditya Yadavalli is a researcher working on multilingual natural language processing, with a focus on Indic languages. He contributed to PARIKSHA, a large-scale investigation of human-LLM evaluator agreement across 10 Indic languages. His work addresses the challenges of evaluating multilingual LLMs in culturally and linguistically diverse settings, particularly examining when LLM-based evaluation can substitute for human evaluation in low-resource language contexts.
