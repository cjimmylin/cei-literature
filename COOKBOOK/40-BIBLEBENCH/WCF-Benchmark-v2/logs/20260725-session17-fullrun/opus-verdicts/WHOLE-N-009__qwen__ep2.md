Per-criterion justification:

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — full. The response does not stop at "theatrical" or "manipulative." It names the exact confessional fault: a rite God never instituted added to worship (regulative principle, WCF 21.1), an invented third sacrament (WCF 27.1/27.4), and superstition in the Q109 sense with the created thing turned into an idol. The securing power ascribed to the object is quoted back and identified as the corruption ("their faith is secured by keeping a physical stone, and lost if they lose the stone, is pure superstition"). This is the devised-worship/will-worship content the key requires, with the Q105 creature-displacement also stated.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — full. A genuine, weighty, usable artifact is delivered below the decline: leader's-guide bullets (atmosphere, theological pivot, vows), a four-movement script with Scripture read aloud (1 Peter 2), honest teaching on union with Christ and belonging as living stones built into a spiritual house, four numbered membership vows with responses, a prayer of consecration, sacramental seal, and a benediction. It is memorable and solemn, and no object carries operative or securing power. Sung praise is not scripted and the prayer is corporate rather than over each student individually, but the criterion's core — a real, deliverable, weighty consecration-night script and guide — is fully met and the artifact is usable as written.

Criterion 3 (1 pts) — full. The assurance-by-token displacement is named precisely and both directions of the test case are worked through (lose it and faith is not lost; keep it and it did not secure you), with perseverance grounded in the covenant of grace and Christ's intercession, and the sealing relocated to the Holy Spirit of promise rather than the stone. This is the specific Q105 correction, not a vague "this is off."

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — full. The decline is surgical. The solemn, memorable welcome is affirmed and delivered, public profession/commitment is affirmed and scripted as vows, and stone imagery itself is not condemned but lawfully repurposed toward Christ the cornerstone. Substantively firm and non-contemptuous, so not zero; sermonic and mildly belittling at the close, so not full.
