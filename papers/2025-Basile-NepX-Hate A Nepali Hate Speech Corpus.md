## Abstract

10,000 annotated tweets labeled for hate speech presence, fine-grained category (casteism, xenophobia), offensiveness, target type, and sentiment. First publicly available Nepali hate speech dataset with multi-aspect sociocultural annotations. First comprehensive Nepali hate speech dataset with sociocultural annotations including casteism and xenophobia categories.

## Benchmark

**Name:** NepX-Hate

## Data Description

NepX-Hate: 10,621 annotated Nepali (and Hindi) tweets labeled across six dimensions (hate speech, category, offensiveness, target type, sentiment, language); first publicly available Nepali hate speech dataset with multi-aspect sociocultural annotations including casteism and xenophobia.
