# Daniel Hershcovich

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, University of Copenhagen

## Research Summary

Daniel Hershcovich is an Assistant Professor at the University of Copenhagen, specializing in natural language processing with a focus on multilingual and cross-cultural aspects of language understanding. His research addresses how NLP systems can better serve diverse linguistic and cultural communities, including work on assessing cross-cultural alignment in language models. He has made significant contributions to semantic parsing and multilingual NLP.

## Research Interests
- Natural Language Processing
- Cross-lingual and Multilingual NLP
- Semantic Parsing
- Cross-cultural AI Alignment
- Universal Dependencies
