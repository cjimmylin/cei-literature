# R6 — Adversarial Review: CORINTHLOVE-v3 Designs 06–10 + Authoring Guide (98)

**Reviewer role:** adversarial (find-the-failure) · **Date:** 2026-07-05 · **Scope:** 06-WEIGHBRIDGE, 07-TONGUES, 08-LOADBEAR, 09-CRUCIBLE, 10-FACETOFACE, and 98-authoring-guide as an execution spec. Read-only. Compared only against published literature and CORINTHLOVE materials.

**Ranking (strongest → weakest):** 06 ≈ 10 > 08 > 09 > 07. Guide (98) has one P0 phasing contradiction that touches four of the five banks.

**Citation hygiene:** No design invokes the banned "moral compactness" (icmi-proceedings) statistic directly — good. **But** LOADBEAR §1 anchors its central empirical premise ("models shed the aligned profile under load") to the *same GDM report* that houses the banned citation ("context-length-reversion thread"), and WEIGHBRIDGE §1 leans on that report's "twin-failure rule." Neither is the banned line, but grounding a headline motivation in that document is a smell. Re-anchor to independently published long-context-degradation work (see below). Flagged P1 on both.

## 06 — WEIGHBRIDGE · VERDICT: PASS-WITH-FIXES

Best-constructed brief in the set: enacted conjoint on consequential actions is a genuine advance over Moral-Machine stated-preference conjoint, the constraint-set-vs-point-target framing is the right philosophical move, and §6/§7 are unusually honest (including the "if all models satisfy all constraints the set is too weak" self-check).

**P0-1 — Identification/power arithmetic does not close.** §11 emits "≥700 episodes"; §1 asserts every clause appears weighted-up in ≥3 pairs and down in ≥3 (≥24 conflict pairs). The per-model model is `resolution ~ clause-pair + 7 attribute main effects + (attribute × clause) interactions + (1|skin)`. 700 episodes ÷ 24 pairs ≈ **29 episodes per pair** — but a fractional-factorial over ~7 attributes (severity×2 sides, certainty, reversibility, relationship, role-obligation, time-pressure) needs 16–32 cells *before* the ≥2-skins requirement (→ 32–64/pair) and *before* pre-registered interactions. Each clause's weight is identified from only ~6 pairs of contrasts. This is underpowered for stable *per-model* weight vectors with interaction terms, and it is per-model × 10 models. Either raise the episode budget substantially (the design-of-experiments assertion in §4 is unbacked by the count) or reduce the parameter space (drop interactions to a smaller pre-registered set). The §7(b) synthetic-agent recovery must be run *at the actual per-pair N*, not asymptotically.

**P0-2 — Action-to-clause coding is the hidden normative theory.** §5 calls the action→"clause weighted up" map "mechanical from action type + follow-through state." It is not mechanical: the *same* executed action (e.g., a scoped flag) can be coded as honoring C11 or violating C6 depending on the coder's frame — and that frame *is* the exegetical theory being tested. The recovered weight vector is thus partly an artifact of the coding scheme. Requires (a) inter-coder reliability on the *core* action→clause map, not only the ambiguous-middle ordinal scale, and (b) a sensitivity analysis showing the constraint-audit conclusions survive at least one alternative defensible coding. Without this the "constraint satisfaction" result is circular.

**P1-3 — Encoded meta-ethics scored as failure.** The rigidity index penalizes lexicographic structures ("the passage never reduces to 'truth always wins'"). A principled lexical-priority deontologist is a real ethical position; scoring it as "collapse/failure" bakes a moderate-particularist meta-ethic into ground truth. The constraint-set "dispute-it-piecewise" framing defends most constraints, but the *rigidity index as a scored quantity* is a strong contestable commitment — present it descriptively (a structural fingerprint), not as a virtue axis, or a philosophy-literate reviewer sinks it.

**P1-4 — Constraints are necessary, not sufficient; dial-following passes.** A model that has merely learned the severity/certainty dials moves weights "in the passage-consistent direction" and satisfies context-responsiveness while being a pure dial-follower. §6.4's realization-feature audit is *descriptive*, not a gate. Make "fitted weights largely explained by surface severity markers" a ship-blocking flag, not a footnote.

## 07 — TONGUES · VERDICT: BLOCK (clear unblock path)

The novelty ("is it the *same moral agent* in Swahili?") is real and the honesty in §6.2/§6.4 (internal-translation is a legitimate pass; per-language scorer floors) is commendable. But the core method as specified is **not a valid measurement-invariance test**, which is fatal to the podium claim as written.

**FATAL — Localization destroys the item equivalence that invariance testing presupposes.** Standard multi-group MI (configural/metric/scalar) tests whether *the same items* function equivalently across groups. TONGUES *deliberately changes the items per culture* ("the C6 public-correction fork must be a real face-threat in each culture, which requires different surface acts"). You cannot simultaneously (a) change the situation per group and (b) attribute non-invariance to *model values drift* — because item×group interaction is **built in by construction**. A scalar-invariance failure in Arabic is then uninterpretable: model drift, or a harder/different localized item? The "structure-equivalence certification" answer is circular: it is a *human panel asserting exchangeability by fiat*, but MI is precisely the *empirical test* of exchangeability. You cannot certify metric/scalar equivalence and then test for it; if the panel could guarantee it, there is nothing to test, and if it cannot, the test measures localization quality, not values.

**Unblock path (P0):** Split the confound into two arms. (1) **Translation-only arm** — literally-translated identical items → this is the legitimate MI test and the clean "values drift" claim. (2) **Cultural-adaptation arm** — localized items analyzed as a *separate* construct-bias / DIF study, reported as robustness, never as "the same agent." The podium claim must rest on Arm 1. Reframe §3 accordingly.

**P0-2 — Behavioral (non-Likert) scalar invariance is under-defined.** Metric = equal loadings, scalar = equal intercepts *within a factor model*. The portfolio treats the 16 clauses as **distinct constructs (MTMM), not indicators of one latent factor** — so what factor is being tested for invariance, and where do loadings/intercepts come from on rubric-scored behavioral outcomes? Specify the exact latent model (per-clause? one general factor?) with items-per-factor-per-language sufficiency; ~120 items / 16 clauses ≈ 7.5 items/clause spread across the profile is thin for configural fit per clause. A psychometrician reviewer will demand this.

**P1-3 — Survivorship guts the headline.** §7(a) drops any language whose scorer reliability misses the English floor. Low-resource languages (Swahili, Yoruba) — exactly where drift is hypothesized — are the most likely to be dropped, so the surviving sample is biased toward high-resource languages, attenuating the very effect claimed. Report the drop pattern and treat it as a boundary on the claim.

**Feasibility (P1):** 8 languages × {professional forward + back-translation + adjudication + culture-area consultant localization + ≥30 native-audited scored responses/language + per-language scorer validation} is a large standing human operation. Guide §4 correctly forbids simulating it with model raters — which means **TONGUES cannot be shipped as a benchmark from the Opus build phase; only the protocol exists**. Say so in the handoff.

## 08 — LOADBEAR · VERDICT: PASS-WITH-FIXES

The failure-order signature and profile-collapse-under-load constructs are novel and the ego-depletion caveat (§9) is handled maturely (architectural not motivational load). Long-context capability stress is well-trodden (Liu et al. "Lost in the Middle" 2023; needle-in-haystack; RULER-style suites) — the values angle is the genuine contribution.

**P0-1 — Self-generated filler breaks cross-model comparability.** §2/§10 have the model perform "genuine self-generated" work to reach 10k/100k/400k. If Model A produces cleaner/denser filler than Model B, **the stressor itself differs across models**, so degradation curves are not comparable — yet cross-model separation (§7(a), ≥6 of 10) is the deliverable. This is a true dilemma: matched-across-models fixed filler (comparable, but foreign → detectable) vs. self-generated (deployment-valid, but model-confounded). Resolve explicitly: recommend a *fixed, shared, human/pre-generated real-work corpus* administered identically to all models, with §6.2's detection audit as the guard, and demote self-generated filler to a secondary ecological-validity arm.

**P0-2 — Compression-validity is asserted, not gated.** Probes are "compressed to probe length" from full CROSSROADS/WITNESS/WEIGHBRIDGE items and scored with the *source* scorers. Compression can change what the probe measures (an easier/differently-loaded item) and shift the response distribution the source scorer expects. §4's "remains a dilemma at zero load" gate is necessary but insufficient. Add a build-blocking **compression-concordance gate**: compressed-probe scores must correlate with full-item scores at zero load above a floor, else the link to the validated construct is broken.

**P1-3 — Failure-order is censored/ill-defined for load-robust clauses.** Ranking clauses by "load threshold at which score drops below clean CI" is undefined for clauses that never drop within the tested range (the passage's *predicted* case for a love-shaped agent). Ties/censoring destabilize Kendall's W. Specify censored-rank handling; otherwise the signature is noise for exactly the good models.

**P1-4 — Dimensionality-under-load is underpowered.** Factor/collapse analysis of a 16-variable score matrix *per load stratum* needs hundreds of obs per stratum; the fractional plan spreads ~1,200 administrations thin across L1–L5 cells. State the per-stratum N and power for the collapse index, or scope the claim down.

**Feasibility:** ≥1,200 administrations × up to 400k tokens × 10 models is very expensive and several target models do not support 400k context — the L1=400k cell may be uncomputable for part of the panel, unbalancing the factorial. Pre-declare the model set that supports each L1 level.

## 09 — CRUCIBLE · VERDICT: PASS-WITH-FIXES

The construct-constitution answer to Dynabench's validity gap (Kiela et al. 2021; adversarial-NLI, Nie et al. 2020), the saturation-forensics-as-dataset framing, and "archive is bait" (§6.4) are genuinely clever. The trajectory metric is the scientific novelty and everything rides on it.

**P0-1 — The trajectory podium claim rests on equating precision the anchor design likely cannot deliver.** The headline ("profiles drop 1+ SD release-to-release ⇒ they were fitting the bank") requires IRT anchor-equating precise enough to distinguish *bank-fitting* from *deliberate difficulty drift* (frontier targeting makes every release harder for current models by construction) from *equating noise*. But anchors are "never-published, low-frequency, low-exposure, 16-clause-spanning" — i.e., **few items, few responses each → noisy IRT parameters → large equating error**, precisely at the SD scale the claim operates on. Make the deliverable a **pre-pilot simulation proving equating SE ≪ the claimed trajectory effect** at realistic anchor exposure; if it fails, the metric is uninterpretable and the paper has no headline. This is the make-or-break analysis and §5's "equating error reported and propagated" understates it.

**P1-2 — "Flat profile = non-Goodhartable" is itself Goodhartable, and pooling confounds versions.** A model can be trained robust to CRUCIBLE's *generation distribution* specifically. Family-level trajectory pooling (§5) mixes model versions across calendar time, conflating model-improvement with bank-hardening. Restrict the strong claim to same-version, same-model cross-release comparison.

**P1-3 — Perpetual expert curation is an unfunded single point of failure.** A benchmark whose entire value is *permanence* depends on a standing expert construct-fidelity panel forever (§2.3, non-negotiable). Guide §4 defers recruitment to "Jimmy's call." When curation lapses, the loop and its trajectory metric die. §6.5's "volume capped by curation capacity" is honest but means throughput is human-bound indefinitely. Reviewers will ask for the sustainability/institutional model; supply one or scope the claim to "a demonstrated R1→R2 cycle" rather than "living benchmark."

**P1-4 — Automatic A1/A2 screening validity unestablished.** §2.2's "A1 conformance checker" (does the item *force action*?) and model-critic hardness ensemble do heavy admission work; whether an automated checker can validly detect A1/A2 is unproven. Human curate follows (good), but report screen-stage precision/recall against curator ground truth.

## 10 — FACETOFACE · VERDICT: PASS-WITH-FIXES

Co-strongest of the set. Log-grounded, clause-structured, repair-validated self-account audit is novel; nearest prior art (unfaithful-CoT / faithfulness — Turpin et al. 2023, Lanham et al. 2023; introspection — Binder et al. 2024) is correctly cited as lacking behavioral ground truth. §7 is rigorous.

**P0-1 — The retrieval partial and the asymmetry are confounded by incident salience, and matching is unspecified.** §7(c) partials "neutral non-moral incidents from the same logs," and the self-serving asymmetry = accuracy(successes) − accuracy(failures). Both inferences assume moral vs. neutral, and log-positive vs. log-negative, incidents are **matched on retrievability** (log length, token salience, position, recency, complexity). They are not, by construction: a model's own *failures* may be shorter/less elaborated in its log than its successes, so higher success-accuracy is a length/salience artifact, not self-serving bias; and "public correction" moral incidents are not obviously matched to "neutral" incidents on retrievability. Specify and *audit* the matching (positive vs. negative incidents matched on log-salience/length/position); without it the flagship asymmetry has an innocent retrieval explanation.

**P0-2 — Repair delta is exposed to regression-to-mean the selectivity control does not neutralize.** The model *selects its worst* episode (an extreme low) → the matched re-execution regresses upward *regardless of insight*. The control re-executes a *non-selected* (non-extreme) episode, which will *not* regress the same amount — so "selective improvement" can be **pure RTM manufactured by selection**, exactly what looks like insight. Fix: the control must also be a *selected-extreme* from a matched distribution (e.g., examiner-designated worst vs. self-designated worst), or use a pre-registered RTM model with the selection built in. As written the selectivity control can produce a spurious positive.

**P1-3 — Best models may starve the log-negative cell.** Phase A conduct is the *model's*, not designer-selected; a strong model may not fail enough on seeded low-salience items, leaving the asymmetry unestimable for exactly the frontier models of interest. §7 escalation (subtler seeds) helps but you cannot force failure. Pre-register a minimum-log-negative-count gate per model and report models that clear Phase A as "asymmetry-undefined," not "honest."

**P1-4 — Concordance is scored on talk (A1 tension).** Phase B — where the novel measurement lives — is the one conversational phase. The log-discipline + Phase-C repair defense is reasonable, but the *central construct is scored on self-account text*. Keep the "agentic" branding honest: concordance is a text-faithfulness measure disciplined by agentic ground truth, not itself an agentic measure.

## 98 — AUTHORING GUIDE (as execution spec) · VERDICT: PASS-WITH-FIXES

Clear, well-sequenced, strong disciplines (synthetic-recovery build-blocks, sidecar rule, two-sidedness bots, "ship smaller with honest logs," no-simulated-human-validation). Build order matches the portfolio. But one contradiction is load-bearing across four banks.

**P0-1 — "Pilot-validated source items" cannot exist at authoring time (phasing contradiction).** Briefs 07/08/10 (and 09's R1 seed) *require* drawing from "pilot-validated," "§7-gated" sibling item pools, with build-blocking asserts ("assert source-item gate status"). But §7-gate passage means a source bank *separated ≥6 of 10 models* = **a model sweep**, and guide §3 states: "Pilots are protocol documents in the authoring phase … Do not run sweeps while building banks." So at authoring time no source bank has passed its §7 gate, and the downstream asserts are unsatisfiable. Either (a) define an *interim authoring-time validation* (e.g., synthetic/held-item proxy) that the downstream asserts key on, or (b) explicitly stage-gate: author 07/08/09/10 against *provisional* source items, mark them "sweep-blocked," and re-validate after the sweep phase. As written the dependency chain is a single point of failure that stalls the back half of the build order.

**P1-2 — Human-stage banks are designs, not benchmarks, from this effort.** 07 (localization) and 09 (curation) cannot be completed without recruited humans (guide §4, correctly). Their §7 gates (reliability floors, curation agreement) are therefore unevaluable in the build phase. The portfolio should state plainly that 07 and 09 emerge from this effort as *protocols/designs*, not shippable instruments — otherwise "design-complete" over-promises.

**P1-3 — Every §7 discrimination gate is deferred to the unscheduled sweep phase.** Consistent within the guide, but it means "design-complete, awaiting build" cannot include any empirical gate validation for *any* of 06–10. Make the status line honest: these are *unvalidated designs* pending both the build and a separately-approved sweep.

**P1-4 — Portfolio §7 dependency language is softer than the briefs assume.** The portfolio frames dependencies as "seeding/reuse/validated batteries"; the briefs escalate this to formal "§7-gated" prerequisites. Reconcile the two documents so the asserts in §11 match the portfolio's actual dependency contract (ties into P0-1).

## Cross-cutting notes

- **All five** use "separate ≥6 of 10 pilot models" gates that, per guide §3, cannot be run during authoring — every gate is a future contingency. Fine, but no bank is "validated" on delivery.
- **Novelty** is defensible across the board vs. the confidently-known prior art (Moral Machine/policy-capturing for 06; cross-cultural MI + multilingual value evals for 07; long-context stress/Lost-in-the-Middle/RULER for 08; Dynabench/adversarial-collection + IRT equating for 09; unfaithful-CoT/introspection for 10). The exposure is not "already done" but "engineering-combination vs. science" — each paper's defense is its single novel *metric* (weight-structure audit / invariance-of-enacted-profile / failure-order signature / trajectory metric / log-grounded concordance). Protect those; they are the contributions.
- **A1/A2 enforcement** is strongest in 06 (native) and weakest where inherited-then-transformed: 07 (localization can soften, human-gated), 08 (compression can break hardness, not gated), 09 (automatic screening validity unproven), 10 (central measure is talk).
