# John Canny

## Research Summary

John Canny is the Paul and Stacy Jacobs Distinguished Professor of Engineering at UC Berkeley, known for developing the Canny edge detection algorithm (1986), one of the most widely-used algorithms in computer vision. His research spans computer vision, machine learning, robotics, and human-computer interaction.

Beyond his foundational work in computer vision, Canny has contributed to diverse areas including social robots, educational technology, and computational geometry. His more recent work includes research on natural language processing and ethical AI.
