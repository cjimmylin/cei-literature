# Jakob Foerster

## Research Summary

Jakob Foerster is an Associate Professor at the University of Oxford's Department of Engineering Science, where he leads the Foerster Lab for AI Research (FLAIR). He is one of the world's leading researchers in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), having helped bring deep MARL to the forefront of AI research.

### Career and Achievements

**Academic Positions:**
- Associate Professor, University of Oxford (current)
- Part-time position at Meta AI/FAIR (since September 2024) building a team investigating multi-agent approaches to universal intelligence
- Previous: (Senior) Research Scientist at Facebook AI Research (FAIR) in California

**Key Contributions:**
- Pioneered deep multi-agent reinforcement learning during his PhD at Oxford
- Developed the zero-shot coordination problem setting, crucial for safe human-AI coordination
- Led foundational research on emergent communication in multi-agent systems

**Awards and Recognition:**
- CIFAR AI Chair (2019)
- ERC Starter Grant (2023)
- JPMC AI Research Award for work on AI in finance
- Amazon Research Award for work on Large Language Models

**Recent Research (2025):**
- ARIA Funding: Leading the SAGEflex project developing AI-based frameworks for scalable coordination of net-zero power grids using MARL
- Multi-Agent Craftax: A new benchmark for open-ended multi-agent reinforcement learning at hyperscale

### Research Focus

FLAIR focuses on developing safe and scalable machine learning methods combining large-scale pre-training with RL and search, as well as foundations of meta-learning and multi-agent learning. The lab explores how agents can learn to cooperate, communicate, and coordinate in complex environments.
