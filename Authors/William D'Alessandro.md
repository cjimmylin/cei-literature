# William D'Alessandro

## Affiliation
Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Oxford (Faculty of Philosophy)

## Research Summary

William D'Alessandro is a philosopher specializing in the philosophy of mathematics, epistemology, and the ethics of artificial intelligence. His work on AI safety explores how deontological ethical frameworks can inform the design of safe and beneficial AI systems. He has made significant contributions to understanding mathematical explanation and the nature of mathematical understanding.

## Research Interests
- Philosophy of Mathematics and Mathematical Explanation
- Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
- Deontological Ethics and AI Safety
- Philosophy of Science
- Epistemology and Understanding

## Selected Publications
- "Deontology and safe artificial intelligence" (2024) - Explores Kantian approaches to AI alignment
