# Bojian Jiang

## Biographical Summary

Bojian Jiang is a researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, specializing in the empirical analysis of AI safety. His work investigates the vulnerabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) to various types of adversarial and non-adversarial failures.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focus is on developing comprehensive evaluation frameworks for LLM safety. This includes studying how models handle "boundary cases" where safety filters might fail or where the model might generate unintended harmful content.

### Significant Achievements
Jiang co-authored an influential paper on "Large Language Model Safety" that surveyed the landscape of vulnerabilities in current foundation models. This work has helped refine the red-teaming protocols used by many Asian AI labs.

## Major Publications

- [Large Language Model Safety: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00000) (2024) - *Comprehensive roadmap for LLM robustness and alignment.*
