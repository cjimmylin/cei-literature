# VLAIR Summary: Legal AI Benchmarking Report

## Overview

The Vals Legal AI Report (VLAIR) represents the first independent benchmarking study evaluating legal generative AI tools against lawyer baselines across seven critical tasks.

## Key Findings

**Top Performers:**
- Harvey Assistant dominated with highest scores on five tasks, achieving 94.8% on Document Q&A
- CoCounsel delivered consistent performance averaging 79.5% across four evaluated tasks
- Oliver excelled at complex EDGAR research using agentic workflows

**Task Performance Rankings:**
The study assessed accuracy across Document Extraction, Document Q&A, Document Summarization, Redlining, Transcript Analysis, Chronology Generation, and EDGAR Research. "Document Q&A produced the highest scores out of any task" with tools averaging 80.2% accuracy, substantially surpassing the baseline 70.1%.

## Methodology Highlights

**Dataset Quality:**
The research team compiled over 500 samples from Am Law 100 firms, representing authentic legal workflows rather than academic exercises. Eight law firms participated, with four identified by name: Reed Smith, Fisher Phillips, McDermott Will & Emery, and Ogletree Deakins.

**Evaluation Approach:**
Vals AI employed LLM-as-judge methodology for consistent, scalable assessment. Each tool underwent blind evaluation on identical tasks using reference answers with specific correctness criteria.

## Vendor Participation

Five vendors participated: Thomson Reuters (CoCounsel), vLex (Vincent AI), Harvey, Vecflow (Oliver), and LexisNexis (which withdrew from most tasks). Vendors could opt into specific skills matching their product capabilities.

## Speed Advantage

"The AI tools were six times faster than the lawyers at the lowest end, and 80 times faster at the highest end," making them valuable starting points for legal work even when accuracy closely matched human performance.

## Notable Limitations

The EDGAR research task revealed challenges with broad, unspecified searches. Future iterations will refine question formulation and expand to international jurisdictions and additional vendors.
