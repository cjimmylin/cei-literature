# Jiayi Liu

## Research Summary

Jiayi Liu is a researcher at Alibaba Group who has contributed to foundational work on evaluating the values and safety of Chinese Large Language Models. She is a co-author of the CValues benchmark, which was the first Chinese human values evaluation benchmark designed to measure the alignment ability of LLMs in terms of both safety and responsibility criteria. The research addresses growing concerns about potential risks and negative social impacts of rapidly evolving language models in a Chinese context.

## Notes

The CValues benchmark manually collected adversarial safety prompts across 10 scenarios and induced responsibility prompts from 8 domains by professional experts, providing comprehensive evaluation of Chinese LLM values through both human evaluation and automatic multi-choice evaluation.
