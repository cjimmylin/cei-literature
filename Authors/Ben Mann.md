# Ben Mann

## Biographical Summary

Ben Mann is a co-founder and researcher at Anthropic and was one of the original founding members of OpenAI. He is a primary technical leader in the development of frontier language models, with a background in software engineering and systems design. He was part of the pioneering team at OpenAI that established the foundation for modern large-scale AI before joining the founding team of Anthropic.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ben’s research focuses on the scaling and safety of large language models. At Anthropic, he works on the infrastructure and alignment techniques (such as Constitutional AI) that power the Claude series. He is interested in making frontier models more robust, controllable, and socially responsible.

### Significant Achievements
As a core contributor at OpenAI, he played a critical role in the GPT-3 project, specifically leading the engineering efforts for model scaling and the implementation of Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). He is a co-author of the seminal paper "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners," which redefined the state of the art in AI. His work is foundational to the current generation of aligned AI assistants.

## Major Publications
