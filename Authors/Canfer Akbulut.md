# Canfer Akbulut

## Biographical Summary

Canfer Akbulut is an AI researcher who has contributed to various safety and red-teaming efforts for Large Language Models. Based in Beijing and affiliated with Tsinghua University's research ecosystem, he works on the technical and governance challenges of deploying Frontier AI in a global context.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the "Vulnerability Analysis" of Large Language Models. He investigates how models can be manipulated via "jailbreaking" or "prompt injection" and develops methods to robustify model reasoning against such attacks. He is also interested in "Cross-Cultural Alignment"—how AI safety standards vary across different geopolitical regions.

### Significant Achievements
Akbulut has been involved in several large-scale red-teaming initiatives, providing datasets and analysis for the "Safety of LLMs" community. He has co-authored papers on the "Reasoning Capabilities" of models under adversarial pressure, helping model developers understand the limits of their current safety training (SFT/RLHF).

## Major Publications
