# Alipio Jorge

## Biographical Summary

Alípio Mário Guedes Jorge is an Associate Professor at the University of Porto (FCUP) and the Coordinator of the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence and Decision Support (LIAAD) at INESC TEC. He is a senior researcher in the Portuguese AI community with decades of experience in machine learning and data science.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Professor Jorge's research focuses on extracting structured knowledge from unstructured textual data, particularly through narrative extraction and recommendation algorithms. He is involved in multiple European projects aimed at improving the interpretability and utility of personalized AI systems.

## Major Publications
- [Text2Story: Extracting Narratives from Text](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-45442-5_57) (2020) - *Leading research on narrative structures in ML.*
