# Ian David Stephen

## Research Summary

Ian David Stephen is an Associate Professor in the Department of Psychology at Macquarie University, Sydney. His research examines how underlying physiology and psychology are reflected in human faces and bodies, how that information is extracted by observers, and how accurate the judgements people form from looking at others are. His work in evolutionary psychology suggests that perceptions of attractiveness may reflect aspects of underlying health and fertility. He has shown that facial skin colour reflects individuals' underlying health, and that enhanced facial skin yellowness, redness, and lightness are perceived as healthy. His research demonstrates that in humans, redder facial skin in men is interpreted as appearing more aggressive, dominant, attractive, and healthy.
