# Jie Yang

## Research Summary

Jie Yang is an Assistant Professor in the Web Information Systems (WIS) group at the Department of Software Technology, EEMCS faculty, TU Delft. His research focuses on human-in-the-loop approaches for trustworthy machine learning, especially for natural language processing systems. He studies human-centered computing for robust AI, combining human intelligence with machine scalability to solve complex tasks at scale.

Yang co-leads the research line on Crowd Computing & Human-Centered AI and co-directs the Delft Design@Scale AI Lab. He also co-leads GENIUS, an ICAI lab dedicated to the development and use of Generative AI. Previously, he was a machine learning scientist at Alexa Shopping, Amazon Research, and a senior researcher at the eXascale Infolab, University of Fribourg. He received his PhD from TU Delft in 2017.
