# Pieter Delobelle

## Affiliation
Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Computer Science, KU Leuven, Belgium

## Research Summary
Pieter Delobelle is a postdoctoral researcher at KU Leuven specializing in fairness and bias in natural language processing systems. His research focuses on measuring and mitigating social biases in large language models, with particular attention to multilingual settings and low-resource languages. He has contributed significantly to developing benchmarks and methodologies for evaluating bias in Dutch and other European languages, expanding bias research beyond English-centric contexts.

## Research Interests
- Fairness in NLP
- Bias in language models
- Multilingual NLP
- Social computing
- Language model evaluation

## Key Publications
- "SHADES: A Benchmark for Evaluating Bias in Text Generation" - evaluation of safety and demographic biases
- "Measuring Fairness with Biased Rulers" - analysis of bias in contextualized embeddings
- "RobBERT: A Dutch RoBERTa-based Language Model" - Dutch language model development
