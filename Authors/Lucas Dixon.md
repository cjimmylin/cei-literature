# Lucas Dixon

## Research Summary

Lucas Dixon is a Principal Research Scientist at Google DeepMind and co-lead of PAIR (People and AI Research). His work focuses on machine learning, interpretability, NLP, visualization, and graphical calculi. He specializes in visualization, interpretability and control of machine learning systems, particularly language models. The PAIR team conducts human-AI interaction research across computer science, HCI and design, focusing on explainability, interpretability, fairness, data visualization, human-centered AI, and ML for scientific discovery. He has contributed significantly to research on measuring and mitigating unintended bias in text classification and other fairness-related challenges in AI systems.
