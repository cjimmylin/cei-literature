# Guangzhi Sun

## Biographical Summary

Guangzhi Sun (also known as Brian Sun) completed his Ph.D. at the University of Cambridge supervised by Prof. Phil Woodland (advisor Prof. Mark Gales) on contextual knowledge integration in end-to-end neural-based conversational AI systems. In January 2024, he was awarded a Junior Research Fellowship at Trinity College, University of Cambridge starting from October 2024.

## Research Experience

He held research internships at Google Brain with Dr. Yu Zhang in 2019 and ByteDance with Dr. Wei Li in 2023, and has also collaborated with Poly AI Ltd working with Dr. Ivan Vulic and Dr. Pawel Budzianowski in 2023.

## Research Focus

His research focuses on safe and controllable multimodal large language models and contextual speech processing and understanding. He received the best student paper prize at Interspeech 2022.

## Notable Publications

- "CASE-Bench: Context-Aware SafEty Benchmark for Large Language Models" - accepted at ICML 2025
