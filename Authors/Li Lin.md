# Li Lin

## Research Summary

Li Lin is a PhD student in the Department of Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University, specializing in Responsible AI, Media Forensics, Deepfakes, Generative AI, and Computer Vision. As lead author of AI-Face (CVPR 2024), Lin introduced the first million-scale demographically annotated AI-generated face image dataset, including real faces, faces from deepfake videos, and faces generated by GANs and Diffusion Models, along with the first comprehensive fairness benchmark for AI face detectors. This pioneering work addresses critical fairness issues in deepfake detection, showing that current detectors exhibit biased performance across different demographic groups. Lin has continued advancing fairness in deepfake detection with publications at ACM Multimedia 2025 on "Rethinking Individual Fairness in Deepfake Detection" (co-authored with high school students and Professor Shu Hu) and "Preserving Fairness Generalization in Deepfake Detection" (CVPR 2024). Their research demonstrates the importance of ensuring detection systems perform equitably across all demographic groups.
