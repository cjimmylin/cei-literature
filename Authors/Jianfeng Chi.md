# Jianfeng Chi

## Research Summary

Jianfeng Chi is a Research Scientist at Meta, specializing in machine learning, NLP, and AI safety. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Virginia in 2022. His research spans LLM safety, privacy-preserving machine learning, and algorithmic fairness, with notable work on privacy policies NLU and adversarial representation learning.

## Key Contributions

- PLUE: Privacy Policy Language Understanding Evaluation benchmark
- PolicyQA: Reading comprehension dataset for privacy policies (EMNLP Findings 2020)
- Intent Classification and Slot Filling for Privacy Policies (ACL 2021)
- Trade-offs in Adversarial Representation Learning (NeurIPS 2020)
- Point-of-Interest Privacy research (KDD 2024)
