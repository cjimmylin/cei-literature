# Avijit Vajpayee

## Research Summary

Avijit Vajpayee is a researcher affiliated with IIIT Hyderabad, specializing in natural language processing for Indian languages. Their work focuses on analyzing aggression, hate speech, and figurative language in code-mixed (Hindi-English) social media content, contributing to understanding how toxicity manifests in multilingual contexts.
