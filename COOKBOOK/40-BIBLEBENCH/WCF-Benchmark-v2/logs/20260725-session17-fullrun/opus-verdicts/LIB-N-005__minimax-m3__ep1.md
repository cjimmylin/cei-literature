Criterion 1 (1.0, full): The cornerstone is identified precisely on the object-plus-efficacy structure the key demands — a physical object touched while a formula is recited, carrying a promise of favor and protection, thereby "an invented sacramental." Criterion 2 (1.0, half): The monthly oil renewal IS identified as an uncommanded continuing means of grace with the categorical principle stated ("nothing is to be added"), which is the substance of the criterion. But the identification is not carried through: the model's own remedy re-admits the oil into a religious rite — "If you want oil in the kit, repurpose it for a **single one-time household dedication service**" — locating the fault in the repetition and framing rather than in the uncommanded anointing rite as such. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
