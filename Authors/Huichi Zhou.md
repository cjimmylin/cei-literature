# Huichi Zhou

## Biographical Summary

Huichi Zhou is a researcher focusing on multilingual natural language processing and cross-cultural AI alignment. Their work addresses critical challenges in ensuring that large language models demonstrate consistent moral reasoning across diverse languages and cultures, particularly for low-resource languages.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Their research focuses on evaluating the moral reasoning abilities of large language models across typologically diverse languages and varying levels of contextual complexity. They investigate cross-linguistic inconsistencies in moral reasoning and the disproportionate impact of low-resource languages on multilingual AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
Zhou introduced the Multilingual Moral Reasoning Benchmark (MMRB), comprising 2,170 moral scenarios across five typologically diverse languages evaluated at three levels of contextual complexity: sentence, paragraph, and document. Their research revealed that moral reasoning performance degrades with increasing context complexity, particularly for low-resource languages such as Vietnamese, and that low-resource languages have a stronger impact on multilingual reasoning than high-resource ones. Benchmarking of five popular LLMs using MMRB demonstrated significant inconsistencies in moral reasoning across different languages, and fine-tuning experiments showed that low-resource languages have a disproportionately strong impact (both positive and negative) on multilingual reasoning performance.
