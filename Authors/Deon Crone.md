# Deon Crone

## Affiliation
Researcher, University of Southern California

## Research Summary
Deon Crone is a researcher in moral psychology and social cognition. He contributed to the development of the Socio-Moral Image Database (SMID), a comprehensive resource containing images rated for moral content and social relevance. This database enables research on how people perceive and process morally relevant visual stimuli, supporting experimental work in moral psychology, affective neuroscience, and computational modeling of moral judgment.
