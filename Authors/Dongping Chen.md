# Dongping Chen

## Affiliation
Researcher at Huazhong University of Science and Technology

## Research Summary

Dongping Chen is a researcher at Huazhong University of Science and Technology working on AI trustworthiness and foundation models. His research examines the reliability, safety, and trustworthiness of generative foundation models, including large language models and vision-language models. He has contributed to understanding the challenges and risks associated with deploying foundation models in real-world applications.

## Research Interests
- AI trustworthiness and reliability
- Generative foundation models
- AI safety and robustness
- Large language models
- Multimodal AI systems
