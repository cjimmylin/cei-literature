# Caiming Xiong

## Biographical Summary

Caiming Xiong is the Vice President of AI Research at Salesforce, where he oversees the integration of cutting-edge AI research into Salesforce products. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the State University of New York at Buffalo, followed by postdoctoral research at UCLA. Prior to Salesforce, he made significant contributions to deep learning at MetaMind.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the intersection of Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, specifically in building robust multimodal systems. He leads teams working on Large Language Models (LLMs), vision-language grounding, and the deployment of AI systems that can reason across different data types.

### Significant Achievements
Xiong is a highly influential researcher with over 72,000 citations. He has been instrumental in Salesforce's transition to an AI-first company, leading the development of foundational models and benchmarks that set industry standards for performance and ethics in enterprise AI.
