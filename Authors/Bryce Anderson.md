# Bryce Anderson

## Research Summary

Bryce Anderson is a researcher at Florida State University working on computational linguistics and AI alignment. His research examines how large language models may be influencing human language patterns. His work on model misalignment and language change analyzed 22 million words from podcasts to identify shifts in human spoken language that correlate with LLM usage patterns, raising important ethical questions about how AI may shape human communication and thinking.
