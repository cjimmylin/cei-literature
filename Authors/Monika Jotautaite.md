# Monika Jotautaite

## Research Summary

Monika Jotautaite is an AI safety researcher based in London who works as an independent researcher on LLM evaluations. She previously worked as an evaluations scientist and technical program manager with the ASET Benchmarks program at Arcadia Impact for the AISI (AI Safety Institute) bounty program.

Her notable research includes "From Stability to Inconsistency: A Study of Moral Preferences in LLMs" (2025), which introduces the Moral Foundations LLM dataset (MFD-LLM) grounded in Moral Foundations Theory. This dataset conceptualizes human morality through six core foundations to evaluate the consistency and stability of LLM moral preferences across varied dilemmas.

The research, conducted at Pivotal with collaboration from Mary Phuong at DeepMind, found that while LLMs exhibit homogeneous Western-liberal preferences, they are unstable and inconsistent when answering variations of the same moral dilemma, revealing a lack of robust moral reasoning. The work was supported by the Pivotal Research Fellowship.
