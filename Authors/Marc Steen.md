# Marc Steen

## Research Summary

Marc Steen is a Senior Research Scientist at TNO with MSc, PDEng and PhD degrees in Industrial Design Engineering from Delft University of Technology. He is an expert in Responsible Innovation, Human-Centred Design, Value-Sensitive Design, Applied Ethics of Technology, and Virtue Ethics. Author of "Ethics for people who work in tech" (Taylor & Francis/CRC Press, 2023).
