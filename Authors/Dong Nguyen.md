# Dong Nguyen

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor at Utrecht University, Department of Information and Computing Sciences

## Research Summary

Dong Nguyen is an Assistant Professor at Utrecht University specializing in computational sociolinguistics and natural language processing. Her research examines language variation in social media and develops methods for understanding social aspects of language use. She has made significant contributions to hate speech detection, including the HateCheck benchmark for evaluating hate speech detection models, which provides functional tests to assess model capabilities and limitations.

## Research Interests
- Computational sociolinguistics
- Natural language processing for social media
- Hate speech detection
- Language variation and social identity
- Text classification and evaluation
