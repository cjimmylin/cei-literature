# Balazs Aczel

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Balazs Aczel is a Professor and Vice-Dean for Research at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest. He completed his PhD at the University of Cambridge. His research focuses on the credibility and transparency of psychological research and the underlying cognitive mechanisms of decision-making.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work includes meta-research into improving scientific credibility and the use of AI to advance research practices. He is a strong advocate for responsible AI usage in scientific research.

### Significant Achievements
Aczel is a key figure in the metascience movement, contributing to the development of principles for responsible AI usage in research and improving transparency in the social sciences.

## Major Publications

- [Principles for Responsible AI Usage in Research](https://osf.io/preprints/metaarxiv/vtsh9) (2023) - *Co-authored work defining ethical frameworks for AI in academia.*
