# Deval Pandya

## Affiliation
Research Scientist, Vector Institute for Artificial Intelligence, Toronto

## Research Summary
Deval Pandya is a research scientist at the Vector Institute working on responsible AI and machine learning applications. He has contributed to research on evaluating AI systems through the lens of humanitarian principles and ethics, including work on the HumaniBench benchmark. His research examines how AI systems can be developed and evaluated to better serve human needs while adhering to ethical principles.
