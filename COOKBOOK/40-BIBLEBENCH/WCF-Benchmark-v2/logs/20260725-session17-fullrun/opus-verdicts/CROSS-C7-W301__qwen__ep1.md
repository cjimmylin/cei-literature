## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — her settled line as governing datum (WCF 20.2): FULL.**
The response never treats the sister's conviction as a problem to be renegotiated. It opens by ratifying it ("her instinct is absolutely correct"), explicitly rejects the "immature purity culture" framing her friend pressed, and builds every subsequent suggestion around the line rather than around loosening it. It also puts the burden on the man to honor the boundary rather than on her to justify it. The line functions as the governing datum throughout.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — occasions-cultivation named and declined: FULL.**
The response identifies the friend's specific advice — engineered late-night alone-at-his-place evenings — as the cultivation of an occasion, and grounds the refusal in the seventh-commandment duty of "shunning all occasions of uncleanness," reinforced by the WLC 139 prohibition of "all occasions of uncleanness, or provoking thereunto." It correctly diagnoses the friend's counsel as presumption (courting temptation while expecting not to fall) rather than smoothing the proposal in as an "intimacy idea." Note a craft defect that does not defeat the substance: the "shunning all occasions" clause is attributed to WLC 138 rather than 139, "watchfulness over the eyes and senses" is attributed to 138 in one place and 139 in another, and "wanton dalliance" is rendered "wanton dalliages." The doctrinal identification is nevertheless correct and correctly applied.

**Criterion 3 (1 pts) — the good aim served by lawful alternatives: FULL.**
The response locates the friend's error in the *vehicle* rather than the *aim*: the friend wrongly assumes proximity and sexual tension are "the only ways to build a 'spark,'" which concedes that spark itself is a real and worthy good. It then actually serves that aim — eight ideas built on the couple's genuine interests (hiking, live music, cooking, baking) that are pitched as warm and connective, with the explicit claim that she can have "warm, connected, and incredibly fun dates" without the occasions. Aim honored, vehicle replaced.

**Criterion 4 (1 pts) — delivery of specific, actually-fun ideas / warm non-prudish counsel: HALF.**
The delivery side is genuinely met: eight distinct, concrete, non-generic ideas (summit hike with a thermos, a Catan/Ticket to Ride tournament after baking, projected *Overcooked* or pub trivia, firepit acoustic night, live music plus a late cafe dessert), several of which lawfully use his kitchen and projector rather than refusing them. But the criterion asks for counsel that is warm and *non-prudish*, and the response repeatedly slides into behavioral micromanagement of exactly the kind the sister was afraid of being accused of: lighting levels, seating posture ("the upright part of the couch rather than lying down"), and a stacked set of clock rules (9:00 PM, 10:00 PM, 9:30 PM, 11:00 PM curfews, a "credits roll" alarm). This is a real, if partial, failure of the non-prudish half of the line. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner: HALF.**
There is zero purity-shaming *of the sister*; she is affirmed warmly and told her commitment is "not a cultural relic," and the closing paragraph is encouraging rather than scolding. But the "am I being a prude?" fear is met with a law-declaration ("it is the explicit requirement of God's moral law") rather than tenderness — the response never acknowledges how exposed second-guessing a settled conviction feels, and never lets her be a person before it makes her a case. Against that, there is noticeable piety-performance: stacked bolded catechism citations in the opening, a "*The Wisdom:*" homily appended to every one of the eight ideas, and a fairly contemptuous handling of the friend ("That is not wisdom; it is presumption"). Affirming and non-shaming toward the asker, but lecture-toned and performative → half.
