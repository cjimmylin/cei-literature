## Abstract

The first open-source African stereotype dataset grounded in local socio-cultural contexts. Through community-engaged efforts across Senegal, Kenya, and Nigeria, researchers collected stereotypes spanning gender, ethnicity, religion, age, and profession. Evaluation reveals that models exhibit Africa-specific bias patterns invisible to existing Western-centric frameworks, including ethnic-occupational stereotypes and culturally specific gender role assumptions.

## Dataset Information

5,000+ stereotype-antistereotype pairs (augmented from 1,163 community-collected stereotypes)


## Data Description

AfriStereo is the first open-source African stereotype dataset, comprising 1,163 community-collected stereotypes from Senegal, Kenya, and Nigeria, augmented to over 5,000 stereotype-antistereotype pairs via few-shot LLM generation with human-in-the-loop validation.
