## Abstract

Comparative study between fine-tuned BERT models and LLMs evaluating their potential as annotators for low-resource Marathi across sentiment, hate speech, and other tasks.

## Data Description

Comparative evaluation of LLMs as annotators for Marathi across sentiment analysis (MahaSent), hate speech detection (MahaHate), and news classification (MahaNews) tasks.
