## Abstract

First dataset annotated with ethnicity-targeted hate speech in Russian (5.5K texts). Constructed from 2.6M user messages mentioning ethnic groups with 12K annotated instances. Addresses differentiation of attitudes towards different ethnic groups.

## Data Description

Annotated dataset of 5,500+ Russian social media texts targeting ethnic groups, with 12,000 annotated instances for ethnicity-targeted hate speech detection across multiple classifiers.
