## Abstract

Benchmark datasets evaluating gender bias in MT for three low-resource languages. 2,400 gender-balanced sentences. Found 72% of Amharic and 93% of Afaan Oromoo sentences exhibited gender bias.

## Data Description

Gold Gender Bias Test Dataset (GGBTD) of 7,200 gender-balanced sentence pairs for Amharic, Tigrinya, and Afaan Oromoo machine translation evaluation; evaluates NLLB and Google Translate on gender bias.
