## Abstract

Defines model bias (from training) and inference bias (from query language). Two-phase evaluation with factual QA and geopolitically sensitive disputes. Manually curated dataset in four languages including Chinese.

## Data Description

Manually curated bilingual QA dataset covering 70 factual questions (translated into Korean, Chinese, Japanese, English = 280 samples) and 64 geopolitically sensitive dispute-related QA instances across four East Asian geopolitical disputes.
