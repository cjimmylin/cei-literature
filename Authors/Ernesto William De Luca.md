# Ernesto William De Luca

## Biographical Summary

Prof. Dr. Ernesto William De Luca is a Professor at the University of Potsdam and heads the department "Knowledge Technologies for the Social Sciences" at GESIS - Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences in Germany. His research spans information retrieval, digital libraries, semantic web technologies, and recently has extended to examining AI through philosophical lenses including virtue ethics and eudaimonia.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work combines technical information science with broader humanistic concerns about knowledge organization and access. Recent research has explored AI ethics from a virtue ethics perspective, examining how AI systems might be evaluated through the lens of eudaimonia (human flourishing).

### Significant Achievements
Has led major research groups in Germany focused on knowledge technologies and digital humanities. Pioneer in bridging technical information science with philosophical approaches to AI.

## Major Publications

- Research on digital library systems and information retrieval
- Work on semantic web technologies for social sciences
- "Through Eudaimonia's Lenses" - examining AI through virtue ethics
