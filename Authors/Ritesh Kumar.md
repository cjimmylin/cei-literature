# Ritesh Kumar

## Research Summary

Co-founder and Director of Council for Diversity and Innovation and Co-founder of UnReaL-TecE LLP. Previously Assistant Professor of Linguistics at Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar University, Agra (2013-2023). Research focuses on NLP for Social Good, including hate speech and aggressive language identification, and speech technologies and resources for minoritized and endangered Indian languages. Building an AI-based data management startup, Unreal Tece.
