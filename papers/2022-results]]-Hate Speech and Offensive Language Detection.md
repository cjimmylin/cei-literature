## Abstract

Deep ensemble framework for hate speech detection in Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada code-mixed text. Weighted ensemble achieved 0.802 and 0.933 weighted F1-scores for Tamil code-mixed datasets. Covers three Dravidian languages for hate speech detection. Ensemble approach establishes strong baselines for culturally specific offensive content.
