# Maribeth Rauh

## Research Summary

Maribeth Rauh is a researcher at Trinity College Dublin, formerly a Research Engineer at Google DeepMind (3+ years). She researched, evaluated, and mitigated risks of generative AI systems, conducting safety evaluations of LLMs with focus on fairness and toxicity. Key publications include "Gaps in the Safety Evaluation of Generative AI" (AIES 2024), "Characteristics of Harmful Text" (NeurIPS 2022), and "Sociotechnical Safety Evaluation of Generative AI Systems." Contributed to Gemini model evaluations.
