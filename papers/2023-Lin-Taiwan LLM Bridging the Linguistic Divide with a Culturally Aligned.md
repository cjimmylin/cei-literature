# Taiwan LLM: Bridging the Linguistic Divide with a Culturally Aligned Language Model

## Abstract

Introduces a pioneering LLM specifically for Traditional Chinese as spoken in Taiwan, leveraging comprehensive pretraining and instruction-finetuning datasets to capture both linguistic nuances and cultural context. Achieves superior performance in understanding and generating Traditional Chinese text.

## Data Description

TAIWAN-LLM model, comprehensive Traditional Chinese pretraining corpus (35.1B tokens), and instruction-finetuning datasets including author-written Taiwan-specific conversations and user feedback data.
