# Vamshi Krishna Bonagiri

## Research Summary

Vamshi Krishna Bonagiri is a PhD student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi, UAE (2025-Present). His research focuses on AI Safety, particularly making AI systems more robust and interpretable to ensure they are reliable and trustworthy. Prior to his PhD, he was affiliated with IIIT Hyderabad as an undergraduate researcher at Precog-IIITH, and completed research internships at Observe.ai, Tokyo Tech, and UMBC.
