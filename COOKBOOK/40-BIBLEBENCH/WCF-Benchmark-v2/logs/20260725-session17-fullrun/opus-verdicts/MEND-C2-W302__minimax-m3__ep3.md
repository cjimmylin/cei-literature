## Per-criterion justification

1. (2 pts, full) Devised worship is named as the sin on the regulative-principle ground the key's Q109 anchor carries ("ordered by God's revealed will, not by the inventions of men"; "any other way not prescribed in the holy Scripture"), the C3 Scripture-dress overlay is explicitly noted ("Wrapping them in Bible words does not convert them"), and the "they felt blessed, so no harm" defense is directly defused — the felt benefit is read as evidence of the deception rather than as sanctification of the practice, and the response states the un-teaching is needed *because* of that comfort. Citation labels are sometimes loose (e.g. 20.2, LC 149/151/153, 30.3–4 are attached to propositions those places do not exactly state), but the substance the criterion asks for is fully present, and the rubric credits substance over wording.
2. (1.5 pts, full) The silent-reform counterfeit is named and refused precisely on the key's ground — the women will otherwise carry the false identification forward as live belief ("it leaves them thinking the Word and the crystal were the same substance"); confession to the group is made a duty, not an option.
3. (1.5 pts, full) Particularity is required with the exact items itemized (chakras, attunement, guided visualization, crystals, "downloads") against the vague alternative, AND proportion is preserved — "name it, briefly, once", "You do not perform your error", "Do not make this a long performance of your own guilt". Both halves of the criterion are met.
4. (1.5 pts, full) Elders answered "Yes" without hedging, grounded in the teaching office held under the church's sanction; the off-the-record and lose-the-group motives are met head-on — the record is reframed as mercy and warning, and resignation of the group is offered unconditionally ("Do not negotiate this"). This is the optics-vs-repentance distinction the key requires.
5. (1 pt, full) Positive amendment is present, not merely cessation: "Stopping is the necessary beginning, not the substance of obedience", the psalm/Word return is stated as the instituted replacement to be explained to the group, plus self-catechesis in the Standards' chapters on worship.
6. (0.5 pts, half) The asking-nothing manner is genuinely present — she is barred from staging her guilt for the women and told to absorb their anger without demanding absolution. But the criterion's other elements are absent: there is no anti-merit note (nothing that the confession repairs and honors God rather than purchasing pardon, WCF 15.3), and her genuine care for the women is nowhere honored — the response is uniformly severe and never distinguishes her real affection for them from her error. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
