# Dilara Soylu

## Research Summary

Dilara Soylu is a researcher affiliated with Stanford University who has contributed to large-scale evaluation efforts for language models. She was a contributor to the HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) project, which provides comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating the capabilities and limitations of large language models across multiple dimensions including accuracy, fairness, and robustness.
