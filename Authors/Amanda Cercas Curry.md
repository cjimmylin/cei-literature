# Amanda Cercas Curry

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Amanda Cercas Curry is a researcher at CENTAI, working on applied NLP, fairness, and evaluation. Previously, she was a postdoctoral researcher at MilaNLP in Bocconi University. She completed her PhD at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh under Prof Verena Rieser. Her research lies at the intersection of NLP, HCI, and Feminism, with the goal of building more just AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Conversational AI Ethics**: Investigating how chatbots and voice assistants respond to abuse, harassment, and inappropriate requests.
- **Feminist NLP**: Drawing from philosophy, sociolinguistics, and psychology to develop more ethical AI systems.
- **Anti-Empathetic AI**: Arguing against uncritical implementation of "empathetic" AI that may cause harm.

### Significant Achievements
- **#MeTooAlexa Study**: Established how conversational systems respond to sexual harassment by analyzing the #MeTooAlexa corpus.
- **ConvAbuse Dataset**: Created first English corpus on abusive language toward conversational AI gathered "in the wild."
- **Gender & Anthropomorphism Research**: Examined discrepancy between tech companies' claims about non-gendered assistants and user perceptions.
- **"Computer says No" Paper**: Argued against empathetic conversational AI that validates emotions without real basis.
- **"Let's Chat Ethics" Podcast**: Co-hosts podcast on tech ethics.

## Major Publications
- **ConvAbuse: Data, Analysis, and Benchmarks for Conversational Abuse Detection** (EMNLP 2021) - Foundational abuse detection dataset
- **#MeToo Alexa: How Conversational Systems Respond to Sexual Harassment** - Analysis of chatbot responses to harassment
- **Gender and Anthropomorphism in the Design and Perception of Conversational Assistants** - Gender bias in AI assistants
- **Computer says "No": The Case Against Empathetic Conversational AI** - Critique of empathetic AI
