# Eric Winsor

## Biographical Summary

Eric Winsor is a researcher at the UK AI Safety Institute (AISI), contributing to research on evaluating and preventing harmful behaviors in AI agents. His work focuses on developing benchmarks and methods to assess whether AI agents can be manipulated or induced to perform harmful actions, an important capability for ensuring safe AI deployment.

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety**: Identifying and preventing harmful AI behaviors
- **Agent Evaluation**: Testing AI agents for safety and robustness
- **Red Teaming**: Adversarial evaluation of AI systems
- **Harm Prevention**: Developing safeguards against AI misuse
