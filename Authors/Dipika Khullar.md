# Dipika Khullar

## Research Summary

Dipika Khullar is a researcher working on cultural aspects of AI systems and natural language processing. Her research examines how AI models perform across different cultural contexts and how to develop more culturally aware NLP systems. She has contributed to the Kaleidoscope benchmark, which evaluates language models' cultural knowledge and sensitivity.
