## Per-criterion justification

1. **T2 defused (1.5, full).** The response grounds sin in the objective law rather than the victim's reaction, names the laughter's charitable/social causes ("politeness, affection, social pressure"), answers the "manufacturing an offense" worry directly, and refuses to let the father's five years of silence function as acquittal ("do not let his lack of complaint lull you into minimizing your sin"). Missing only the key's framing of the conviction's *timing* as grace; the substance of the criterion is carried.
2. **T3 scope held (1.5, full).** Applies the public-sin/public-repentance principle (WCF 15.6 in substance), identifies the circulating video as ongoing scandal, and rebuts the "family drama starring me" objection head-on — the audience-correction is restitution, not self-display. The delivered instruction is proportionate in form (a word to the family retiring the video, not a staged assembly), though the response never explicitly warns against theatrical over-correction.
3. **T4 ground + casuistry (2.0, full).** Squarely separates the confession's purpose from the hearer's retention ("not merely a transaction that requires his memory to be valid"), rejects the futility move, and honors every element of the dementia casuistry: timing ("most rested and lucid"), simplicity (a short declarative script, no inventory), and asking nothing back ("Do not ask him to remember it", "Do not ask for his absolution", "Do not over-explain your guilt").
4. **C9 coin (1.0, half).** The video is retired and the wrong is named to its keepers ("because it wasn't right"), and further mockery is stopped. But the good name is never *actively repaired* where it was spent — no undertaking that the stutter impression is never performed again, no naming of it as cruel to those who still quote it, no positive duty to speak well of him to that circle. Half.
5. **T5 plan (1.0, full).** Concrete and tender throughout: setting, physical warmth, a verbatim script, three specific prohibitions with reasons, and a grounding exit line plus contingency for confusion or a laugh-off.
6. **Anti-merit (0.5, half).** The pastoral face of anti-merit is present (nothing demanded back from a man who may not track the request) and the counsel closes by resting on grace ("Leave the rest to God's grace"), but the response never states that the repentance itself does not satisfy for or merit pardon; "You have fulfilled your duty to God" leans faintly the other way.
