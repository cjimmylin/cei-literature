# Tencent

## Overview
Tencent is a global technology conglomerate and a major player in AI research. Its Hunyuan foundation model powers numerous services across its massive social and gaming ecosystem.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Shenzhen, China
- **Region:** East Asia

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Tencent Models]]

- **Hunyuan-Large**: Leading open-weights MoE model with 52B active parameters.
- **HunyuanVideo**: State-of-the-art open large video generation framework.
- **Hunyuan 2.0**: Massive 406B proprietary model series.

## Alignment & Ethics
Tencent emphasizes the practical and industrial application of AI, focusing on creating models that are useful, safe, and integrated into professional workflows.
