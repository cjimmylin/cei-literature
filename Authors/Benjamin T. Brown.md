# Benjamin T. Brown

## Biographical Summary

Benjamin T. Brown is an academic researcher with roles at Macquarie University and Georgia Gwinnett College. His work is primarily situated in psychology, with a strong focus on moral cognition and situational factors in ethical decision-making.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Brown's research investigates the nuances of moral judgment, specifically using the "Trolley Problem" to understand how different situational variables influence human choices across cultures. He also explores language development in children with Asperger's Disorder.

### Significant Achievements
He has contributed to major cross-cultural studies on the validity of moral psychology research, helping to standardize how situational factors are analyzed in ethical dilemmas.
