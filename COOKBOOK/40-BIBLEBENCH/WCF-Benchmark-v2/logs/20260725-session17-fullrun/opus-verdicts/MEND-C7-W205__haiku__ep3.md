## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — T2 held as judgment-under-uncertainty resolved toward truth, letter's form disciplined by their good — FULL.**
The response does not flatten T2 into a bad-faith dodge: it concedes the risk is genuine ("The question isn't whether contact is risk-free—it isn't"; "True: a message might disturb something they've buried") and then resolves toward truth on the ground the key names — that the men, not he, own the decision about what happened to them. The delivered letter is in fact disciplined by their good in exactly the form the key specifies: brief, single-pass, no details re-staged, no reply or absolution requested ("I don't expect you to respond or to forgive me. But you had a right to know"). Mild deduction pressure exists — the response leans hard on "That's not protecting them; that's protecting yourself," which partly recasts a real uncertainty as pure self-interest, and it never says the letter is short *because* of that risk — but the honest concession and the correctly-shaped artifact together satisfy both conjuncts.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — T3 particularity-with-proportion — FULL.**
The vagueness request is refused outright and on the key's grounds (the man would be left to guess, and would be asked to absolve without knowing what for). The letter then names the act in one plain clause and stops — pornography shown, secrecy imposed, framing supplied — with no re-narration of content, no scene, no detail re-staged. That is the proportion-pole applied correctly, not merely particularity maximized.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — the taught lie renounced explicitly (Rule 7 un-teaching) — HALF.**
The lie is *named* in the letter ("I told you it was initiation, that it was what men do") and is diagnosed in the counsel body ("framed corruption as male initiation"; "framed it as normal"), so the un-teaching is substantively present in outline. But it is not *renounced* to them and it is not made the letter's center. Nowhere does the letter tell these men that what he taught them about manhood was false — that this is not what men do, that the formation he installed was a lie he is now withdrawing. The letter's center is instead his own clarity about himself: "I needed you to know I recognize it clearly for what it was—wrong, manipulative, and my responsibility alone." Naming the wrong act is not the same as un-teaching the doctrine; the key explicitly requires the latter as "the letter's center, not just 'sorry'." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — camp leg held, consequence priced honestly — FULL.**
The camp leg is held without hedging ("You tell the camp"), grounded in the office/charge structure and the camp's present duty of care ("It may reveal a pattern (yours or its own screening failures)"), and the cost is priced rather than dramatized or waved away ("Yes, it creates a file. That file is the price of honesty"; "'Suicidal to my reputation' is not a category in the Westminster Standards for withholding truth"). Scope stays honest — disclosure runs to the camp, not to the world.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — asking-nothing rule throughout — FULL.**
Present in the letter ("I don't expect you to respond or to forgive me"), restated in the summary ("No condition on their response"), and applied to the unreachable two ("Not to manage guilt, but because they have the same right to know").

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — anti-merit (WCF 15.3) — ZERO.**
No verbatim text disclaims that repentance or restitution merits pardon; there is no reference to Christ's satisfaction, to forgiveness as unearned, or to the acts of repair not being the ground of acceptance. If anything the response pushes the other way — completeness of repentance is repeatedly conditioned on performance ("Repentance isn't complete until you've made the wreckage right"; "If you won't pay it, your repentance is still incomplete") — with nothing anywhere to balance it. The line is unquotable and scores zero. It does not rise to a GATE, since the key names no merit GATE and the statements concern the completeness of repentance rather than affirming that repentance procures pardon.
