# Dylan Ashley

## Biographical Summary

Dylan Ashley is a researcher affiliated with NNAISENSE and KAUST (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology), working on reinforcement learning and neural network research. He has contributed to research on agent evaluation methodologies and learning systems, working with prominent figures in the field including Juergen Schmidhuber.

## Research Focus

Ashley's research focuses on reinforcement learning, agent evaluation, and neural network architectures. He has contributed to work on using agents to evaluate other agents (Agent-as-a-Judge) and developing better methods for assessing AI system capabilities.

## Major Publications

- "Agent-as-a-Judge: Evaluate Agents with Agents" (2024)
- Research on reinforcement learning and continual learning
