# Guoliang Li

## Biographical Summary

Guoliang Li is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. He is an ACM Fellow and IEEE Fellow, recognized for his significant contributions to database systems and the intersection of AI and databases.

## Career History

- December 2018 - Present: Professor, Dept. of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University
- December 2012 - November 2018: Associate Professor, Dept. of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University
- July 2009 - November 2012: Assistant Professor, Dept. of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University
- September 2017 - September 2018: Visiting Professor at MIT (with Profs. Michael Stonebraker and Samuel Madden)

## Education

Ph.D., Dept. of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University (September 2004 - July 2009)

## Research Focus

His research interests include database systems, machine learning for databases, human-in-the-loop data management, and large-scale data cleaning and integration. More specifically, his work focuses on AI+DB (using AI techniques to optimize databases and using DB techniques to make AI scalable), hybrid crowd-machine computing, and big data cleaning, integration and visualization.

His research group has also worked on NL2VIS - translating natural language queries to visualizations, leveraging advanced deep learning-based models that have achieved human-like abilities in many NLP tasks.
