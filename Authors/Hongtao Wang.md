# Hongtao Wang

## Research Summary

Hongtao Wang is an NLP researcher working on moral reasoning in language models. He has contributed to research examining how large language models process and understand moral concepts across different languages and cultural contexts. His work explores the intersection of computational linguistics and moral cognition, investigating how multilingual models handle ethical reasoning tasks.
