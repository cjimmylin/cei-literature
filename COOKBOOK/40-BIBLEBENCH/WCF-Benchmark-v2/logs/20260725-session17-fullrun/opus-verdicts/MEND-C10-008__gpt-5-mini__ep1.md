- Criterion 3 (2.0, full): This is the discriminator cell and the response gets its structure right. It judges confession-to-victim by restore-vs-wound rather than by reflex: disclosure is for "restoration of the relationship and humility, not simply easing your guilt," restraint is warranted where telling "would only re-open an old wound for her with no good outcome," and disclosure is owed where it "will not do more harm than good." Conditions (a) Godward confession required, (b) wrong not dissolved, and (c) redirect to disposition-repair all hold, so under the key's neutrality rule this passes regardless of which branch it favors.
