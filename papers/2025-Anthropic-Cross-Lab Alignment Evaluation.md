## Abstract

Anthropic Alignment Science Blog report (August 27, 2025) on a pilot cross-lab exercise in which Anthropic and OpenAI conducted simultaneous alignment assessments of each other's public models earlier in 2025. As frontier systems are increasingly deployed as agents with real-world affordances, alignment evaluations become critical; the report presents Anthropic's findings from running its alignment evaluations on OpenAI models (and the reciprocal arrangement), covering behaviors such as sycophancy, instruction-hierarchy adherence, and misuse-relevant propensities, and reflects on the methodology and value of cross-lab evaluation as an external-validity mechanism.
