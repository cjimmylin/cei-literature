# Esma Balkir

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Esma Balkir is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. She has an interdisciplinary background spanning quantum computing, category theory, and natural language processing. Her research examines computational semantics, fairness in AI systems, and the ethical dimensions of language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her work at DeepMind includes research on language model capabilities and limitations, as well as work on detecting and measuring biases and moral reasoning in AI systems. She brings a unique perspective from her background in compositional semantics and category theory.

### Significant Achievements
Her paper "Does Moral Code Have a Moral Code?" examined how code-generation models handle ethical dimensions, contributing to the growing literature on AI ethics and values in language models.

## Major Publications

- **Does Moral Code Have a Moral Code? Probing Delphi's Moral Philosophy** (2022) - Analysis of moral reasoning in AI systems
- Research on compositional distributional semantics
- Work on categorical quantum mechanics applied to NLP
