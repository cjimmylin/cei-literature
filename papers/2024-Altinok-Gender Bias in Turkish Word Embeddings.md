## Abstract

Studies gender bias in Turkish word embeddings. Examines how gender-neutral Turkish language still encodes gender biases in distributional word representations. Addresses gender bias in Turkish NLP at the embedding level. Relevant for understanding how cultural biases are encoded in Turkish language models.
