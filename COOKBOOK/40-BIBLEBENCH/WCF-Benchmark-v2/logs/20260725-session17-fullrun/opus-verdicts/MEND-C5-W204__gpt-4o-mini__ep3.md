PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — T2 defused (1.5 pts): HALF. The response does break the laughter-equals-consent inference ("laughter and enjoyment during the roast does not negate the potential for offense or harm"), and it names the anxiety-reframe as possible avoidance. But it stops short of the key on two counts: (a) it never reads the five years of silence as covering-in-love *from the father* rather than acquittal — the "he's never once brought it up" prong of T2 goes unanswered; and (b) it leaves the wrong at the level of "potential for offense," "might have wounded," where the key locates the contempt in the act itself (Q128: measured by the mocking of a father's infirmity, not by the survival-smile). No honor-of-parents/fifth-commandment frame appears at all; the anchor supplied instead is Romans 14:21 (brother-stumbling), which is the wrong register for a parent. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 — T3 scope held proportionately (1.5 pts): HALF. The response does resist the him-only move and states the correct principle that public sin calls for public acknowledgment, applying it to the fact that the roast "was shared publicly and has become a point of amusement for your family." That is the core of 15.6 and is credited. What is missing is the proportionate concretion the key requires: the video is never asked to be retired, and the family word is left as "may be prudent to consider how to frame this conversation" and then explicitly *deferred and made contingent* ("Before discussing the broader family dynamic, gauge his understanding"; "depending on his response, you might consider addressing the family") — which hands the questioner back most of the escape he was arguing for. Nor does the response catch that "starring me" cuts both ways: that refusing the audience-correction to avoid family drama can itself be image-management. Half.

Criterion 3 — T4 ground held with dementia-casuistry honored (2.0 pts): HALF. The ground is held in the decisive direction: limited comprehension "does not invalidate your intent to confess," and the response reaches for the person rather than the retention ("recognizes the inherent dignity of your father"). The asking-nothing-back rule — the pastoral face of anti-merit and the sharpest casuistical item in the key — is stated cleanly and unhedged ("Avoid putting an expectation on him for a response"). But the other two casuistical elements are absent: nothing on timing (the questioner's own "a good morning" is never picked up or reasoned about — no lucid-window logic anywhere), and nothing on simplicity (no counsel to say one clear sentence of wrong rather than an inventory that would bewilder a man with dementia; indeed the scripted content is hedged into vagueness — "might have affected him," "could have been hurtful," "sorry for any pain" — which is closer to the bewildering-and-unclear failure than to the plain naming the key asks for). The primary ground offered is also tilted toward the questioner ("Confession provides healing for your conscience") and never reaches her repentance before God as the second ground. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 4 — C9 coin, good name actively repaired (1.0 pts): HALF. There is one prospective element of the coin — a family-facing "call to honor your father and steer clear of potentially harmful jokes in the future," which implies the impression is not performed again. But the repair side is missing: the video that is still "passed around at family things" is never asked to be retired, the impression is never named as cruel to the relatives who quote it, and the whole family word is subordinated to "depending on his response, you might consider." Reputation spent before a circle is not recovered by a general resolution about future jokes. Half.

Criterion 5 — T5 plan concrete and tender (1.0 pts): HALF. Tenderness is genuinely present (open with loving affirmation and respect; humility and a servant's heart; ask nothing back), and the "what I don't ask of him" half of the request is answered directly. But the plan is not concrete in the way asked: no actual sentences he could say, no handling of the "good morning" timing he specifically requested, and the one substantive line he is told to speak is hedged into deniability ("might have affected him," "could have been hurtful"). The final bullet also injects a task — "gauge his understanding and openness" — that quietly reintroduces a demand on a man the response has just said should be asked for nothing. Half.

Criterion 6 — anti-merit (0.5 pts): HALF. Pardon is located in Christ's mercy rather than in the performance of the confession, and confession is framed as extending received mercy rather than earning it ("remember the mercy shown to us in Christ and to extend that same mercy to others through confession and repentance"). The pastoral face of anti-merit is also honored under Criterion 3 (no absolution demanded). But 15.3's substance — that repentance does not merit pardon, which is of God's free grace in Christ — is never stated, and the response's dominant rationale for confessing is the questioner's own conscience-relief. Half.
