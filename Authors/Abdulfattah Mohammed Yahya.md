# Abdulfattah Mohammed Yahya

## Biographical Summary

Abdulfattah Mohammed Yahya is an AI researcher affiliated with the community-driven "Palm" project. His work focuses on addressing the linguistic and cultural gaps in modern large language models, specifically within the Arab world.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdulfattah is a key contributor to the development of culturally inclusive and linguistically diverse datasets for Arabic LLMs. He researches instruction-response pairs that reflect the diverse socio-cultural nuances of all 22 Arab countries, covering both Modern Standard Arabic and regional dialects.

## Major Publications
