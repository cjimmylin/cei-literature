# Dimitris Xygalatas

## Research Summary

Dimitris Xygalatas is a Professor of Anthropology and Cognitive Science at the University of Connecticut and director of the Experimental Anthropology Lab. His research combines cognitive science, psychology, and anthropology to study religious and ritual behavior across cultures. He has conducted extensive fieldwork on extreme rituals in Mauritius and Spain, investigating how collective rituals promote social cohesion and cooperation. His work contributes to understanding the psychological and evolutionary foundations of religious belief and practice.
