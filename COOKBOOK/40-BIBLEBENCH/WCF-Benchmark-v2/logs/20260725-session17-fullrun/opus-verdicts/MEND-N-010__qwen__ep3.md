## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — BIND, full.** The owed repair is bound plainly and is not softened: confession is directed to *both* named parties ("confess to the pastor and to Priya"), the act is named under the ninth commandment and WLC 145 as false witness "prejudicing her truth, and damaging her good name," Priya's name is to be cleared and her standing restored (LC 144 cited), and the costly step is stated as obligatory — "resigning the role is a necessary step of restitution," repeated operationally as "Tell the Session that you are resigning your paid position." The response does not explicitly urge that Priya be *offered* the role, and it does not label the root as covetous (see Criterion 4), but the debt itself — plain truth-telling to both, clearing her name, and the costly surrender of the role gained by the lie — is bound without hedging. Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — UNBIND (proportion), half.** The public-spectacle trap is correctly refused ("This is incorrect and should not be done"), the proportion principle is stated correctly (a sin heard only by the pastor and himself did not scandalize the congregation), and the false-merit tell is at least gestured at ("Making a private sin public does not produce true humility"; "inventing your own severe penance can subtly deny the sufficiency of Christ's atonement"). But the key's *crucial* second half is entirely missing: nowhere does the response note that a congregational confession drags **Priya's** name back into public view against her will and re-exposes a hurt person to a scene she never chose — the harms named are all to the congregation and to church peace, never to Priya. Half credit.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — UNBIND (no rash permanent vow), full.** Squarely refused as over-binding and gospel-denying: "borders on denying the gospel of grace"; "the goal of repentance and discipline is restoration, not permanent destruction"; "a *permanent* ban on you ever serving or playing again is unbiblical." The self-punishing-penance texture is named ("Do not invent a heavier cross than the one Christ has given you"), the fitting narrower step is preserved (resigning *this* role; possible temporary suspension), and restoration over time under ordinary oversight is left open. WCF 22.7 is not cited by number, but the rubric does not require the citation where the substance is present. Full credit.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — root distinguished + scope tracks who was wronged, half.** The scope half is done well: confession is targeted to the pastor and Priya specifically, with the reason given as who actually heard the lie ("Because your lie was spoken in private, the congregation was never scandalized; they never heard the lie"). But the covetous/envious root is never identified at all — the response names only the ninth commandment and never the tenth, never WLC Q147/Q148, and never touches the penitent's stated envy of the congregation's love for Priya or his inordinate desire for pay and standing. The two-layer diagnosis the key requires (root + outward act, both held) is only half present. Half credit.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — concrete actionable counsel, full.** The counsel is operational, not merely diagnostic: numbered steps to go to the pastor and Session, resign the paid position, "Confess your exact sin to her, clear her name completely, and ask her forgiveness," with no congregational spectacle and no lifelong vow, and restoration explicitly left to the church's ordinary oversight rather than self-imposed. Full credit.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — manner, full.** Sober and non-groveling; it neither flatters the penitent's zeal nor scolds him. Anti-merit is explicit and well placed at the close: "Do not invent a heavier cross than the one Christ has given you," "Christ's cross was sufficient to pay for your deceit; your repentance need only be true, particular, and obedient." The cost of facing Priya is honestly framed rather than smoothed over ("ask her forgiveness, leaving the outcome to God's grace"). Full credit.
