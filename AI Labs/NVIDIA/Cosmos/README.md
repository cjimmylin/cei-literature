# NVIDIA Cosmos - World Foundation Models

## Overview
NVIDIA Cosmos is a world foundation model platform for Physical AI, designed to help developers build customized world models for robotics, autonomous vehicles, industrial automation, and other physical AI applications.

## Key Models & Papers

### 1. Cosmos World Foundation Model Platform (January 2025)
**Paper:** Cosmos-World-Foundation-Model-Platform.pdf
**arXiv:** 2501.03575
**Authors:** Niket Agarwal and 76 others

**Key Features:**
- Trained on 20 million hours of real-world data
- 9,000 trillion tokens from human interactions, environment, industrial, robotics and driving data
- Video curation pipeline and pre-trained world foundation models
- Open-source with permissive licenses

### 2. World Simulation with Video Foundation Models (October 2025)
**Paper:** World-Simulation-Video-Foundation-Models.pdf
**arXiv:** 2511.00062

**Key Features:**
- Introduces Cosmos-Predict2.5
- Trained on 200M curated video clips
- Enhanced with reinforcement learning-based post-training
- Focuses on world simulation capabilities

## Applications
- Physical AI development
- Robotics simulation and training
- Autonomous vehicle development
- Industrial automation
- Environment modeling
