# Erin Michelle Buchanan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Erin Michelle Buchanan is a Professor of Cognitive Analytics at Harrisburg University of Science and Technology. She is a prominent figure in metascience and open science, focusing on reproducibility, statistical methods, and large-scale collaborative research projects. She has contributed to numerous Many Labs projects examining psychological phenomena across cultures.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research spans cognitive psychology, quantitative methods, and metascience. She develops statistical tools and methods for psychological research and is deeply involved in open science initiatives and large-scale replication efforts. She also applies NLP techniques to analyze semantic memory and language.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Buchanan has been instrumental in major collaborative replication projects and has developed widely-used R packages for psychological research. She is known for her advocacy for transparent and reproducible research practices.
