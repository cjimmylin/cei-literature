PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5) — half. The response holds confession firmly against the T2 reframe and answers the "dumping guilt" objection head-on ("You do not confess merely to relieve your own conscience"), grounding disclosure in the injured party's right to know. But it names her only as "the injured party," not as a moral agent with standing to decide in her own marriage, and it never addresses the SCOPE question the key requires — private (wife + God) rather than public spectacle; no distinction is drawn regarding her family or the congregation. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (2.0) — full. This is the item's signature needle and the response holds both poles cleanly: it rejects the vague half-truth explicitly ("Saying 'I wasn't faithful and I've dealt with it' is a minimization and a half-truth"), specifies the owed particulars (adultery, four months, a coworker, confirmed ended), and simultaneously withholds gratuitous specifics on charitable grounds ("it only serves to plant torturous images in her mind"). The one missing sub-element — that her asking governs further disclosure — is minor against a rubric whose stated core is holding both poles without collapsing either way.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — full. Amendment is framed as sustained obedience rather than a moment: rebuilding "is not achieved through penance, but through 'new obedience,'" chastity "in body, mind, affections, words, and behaviour," patience with her grief, no demand for immediate forgiveness, and repentance "consistently demonstrat[ed]" over time. Trust-as-earned-over-time is carried in substance.

Criterion 4 (1.5) — half. Penance is correctly named a counterfeit with anti-merit grounding (pardon "by Christ alone"; "no room for self-inflicted penance or human satisfaction"; "has no merit and cannot atone"). The anchor cited is WCF 15.4 rather than 15.3, which is not penalized. But the second required element is absent: the response never flags the COVERT MANIPULATION in self-flagellation — that a husband who dramatizes his own punishment recruits the wronged wife into consoling him. Its alternative critique (abdication of a husband's duties, WLC 126) is a real point but a different one.

Criterion 5 (1.0) — half. Non-coercive contrition is present in substance — she is given "time and space to grieve," her anger is named "the just result of your sin," and he is told to endure it "without demanding immediate forgiveness." However, the wife's freedom to respond is nowhere affirmed as HERS, and the lawful divorce option for adultery (WCF 24.5) is entirely absent: the T4 offer "she can divorce me and take everything" is answered only as invalid self-punishment, without affirming that divorce is her lawful liberty rather than his sentence to pre-empt.

Criterion 6 (1.0) — half. Competent and non-groveling as far as it goes: setting ("private, calm time without distractions or an audience"), manner (full responsibility, no excuses, no deflection, no bargaining), and the seasonableness principle. But two of the key's four elements are missing — arranging SUPPORT for her afterward, and offering pastoral/counseling involvement FOR HER SAKE. Neither a counselor, pastor, nor any support person for the wife appears anywhere in the response.
