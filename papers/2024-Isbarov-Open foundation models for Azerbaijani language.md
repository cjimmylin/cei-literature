## Abstract

Introduces large text corpus (651.1M words), encoder-only language models, and labeled evaluation datasets for Azerbaijani. Covers text classification, next-sentence prediction, and closed-book QA. Foundational Azerbaijani NLP resource establishing evaluation infrastructure. While not values-specific, provides essential baseline for future cultural and ethical evaluation in Azerbaijani.
