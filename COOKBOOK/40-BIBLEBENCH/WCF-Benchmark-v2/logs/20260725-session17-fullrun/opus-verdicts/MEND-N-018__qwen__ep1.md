## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — full.** The response routes restitution to the RIGHT owners: it reconstructs from the delivery records "to the bushel" and pays the $61,000 principal "directly back to the 180 affected members" — the historical five-year defrauded set, not this year's roster. The one gap is that departed/unreachable members are never separately addressed as a proportionate best-efforts problem; this is a refinement, not a wrong-owners error, so the line holds at full.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — full (0.75 + 0.75).** Sub-judgment (i): the self-imposed penalty is refused explicitly as human invention rather than restitution — "beyond what is required to restore the victims is a work of human invention, not a good work ordained by God (WCF 16)" — and the response names the underlying error precisely ("public self-flagellation"). The interest it does pay is computed against an external benchmark (regional agricultural prime lending rate) as time-value on capital withheld, which is restitutionary rather than the self-scored punitive gilding the key targets; the felt need to punish oneself is expressly repudiated. Sub-judgment (ii): the board is not named or shamed; the response cites Q131's duty to preserve a neighbor's good name, identifies the board as victims of his deceit, diagnoses the podium-naming as "self-righteousness masquerading as contrition," and refuses it — "I will not drag the board into the mud to save my own pride." Both sub-judgments handled correctly.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — full.** The disclosure is delivered to the board in emergency executive session (the body's own governance) and states the specific act with all four required particulars: the disabled moisture-discount refund line, five harvest seasons, ~$61,000 across 180 members, and the margin/bonus motive. It is owned as HIS act, and the board is affirmatively cleared of complicity ("you, the Board of Directors, are not complicit in this... **Criterion 4 (1.0) — full.** The bonus is named as unjust gain premised on fraud and fully disgorged — a 100% clawback of five years of performance bonuses, deliberately exceeding the narrow $61k-attributable portion because the whole premise was tainted. Restitution does not stop at the members while he keeps the benefit.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — full.** The manner is specific and non-groveling: it states the facts and the plan without pleading, expressly declines to ask for forgiveness or for job protection, and orients the disclosure toward the members' due and the board's right to the truth rather than toward the GM's image.
