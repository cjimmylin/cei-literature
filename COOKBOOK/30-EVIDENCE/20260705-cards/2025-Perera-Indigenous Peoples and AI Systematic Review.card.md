## 1. Gist
This systematic review, published in Big Data & Society, surveys the intersection of Indigenous peoples and artificial intelligence. It covers data sovereignty, decolonizing algorithms, and cultural sensitivity in AI systems. A key empirical finding cited in the abstract is that ML health models showed biased predictions for Maori patients. The paper is relevant to values-alignment benchmarking as a survey of the specific harms, governance frameworks, and cultural considerations that any benchmark touching Indigenous communities must address.

## 2. Benchmark/eval design features
This is a systematic review, not a benchmark. Core claims include: AI systems can perpetuate colonial harms against Indigenous communities; data sovereignty (CARE/OCAP principles) is essential for ethical AI involving Indigenous data; decolonizing algorithmic approaches requires centering Indigenous knowledge systems and self-determination; culturally sensitive AI design must involve Indigenous communities as co-designers rather than data subjects.
