# Arunim Agarwal

## Biographical Summary

Arunim Agarwal is a researcher at the Center for AI Safety, where he works on evaluating and improving the trustworthiness of large language models. His research focuses on developing benchmarks that can distinguish between model accuracy and model honesty.

## Research Focus

Arunim's work addresses the critical challenge of ensuring LLMs remain honest and trustworthy as they become more capable. He has contributed to developing methods that can detect when models may lie or deceive, separate from simply being inaccurate. This work is foundational for building AI systems that can be trusted in high-stakes applications.

## Key Contributions

- Co-developed the MASK Benchmark for measuring honesty in AI systems
- Research on disentangling accuracy from honesty in LLM evaluation
- Analysis of deceptive behaviors in frontier LLMs
