## Abstract

Long-sequence story QA benchmark grounded in culturally diverse narratives from 9 Ethiopian regions. 571 training and 649 test examples from 244 stories. Reveals significant regional differences in LLM evaluation results. Directly evaluates cultural understanding in Amharic through Ethiopian regional narratives. Shows that within-language cultural variation significantly impacts LLM performance. Novel benchmark for culturally-grounded evaluation.
