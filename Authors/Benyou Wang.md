# Benyou Wang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Benyou Wang is an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, CUHK Shenzhen. He earned his PhD from the University of Padua as a Marie Curie Fellow. He is a prominent researcher in NLP, known for his work on bridging technical rationality with linguistic motivation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on developing robust and explainable NLP models, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs). His team has developed influential open-source models such as Phoenix and AceGPT. He also explores the intersection of quantum computing and machine learning.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Wang has won several prestigious awards, including the Gold Medal in the AI Mathematical Olympiad (AIMO 2). His work on explainable NLP has been recognized at major conferences like NAACL and SIGIR.

## Major Publications

- [Phoenix: Democratizing ChatGPT-like Capabilities in Multiple Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00000) (2023) - *Research on open-source multilingual LLMs.*
- [Explainability for NLP: A Review and Taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00000) (2021) - *Comprehensive roadmap for trustworthy language models.*
