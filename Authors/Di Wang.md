# Di Wang

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor, Division of Computer, Electrical, and Mathematical Sciences and Engineering (CEMSE), King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)

## Research Summary
Di Wang is an assistant professor at KAUST specializing in machine learning theory with emphasis on privacy and trustworthiness. His research spans differential privacy, statistical learning theory, and optimization for machine learning. Recent work explores how values and ethical considerations are encoded in neural networks, contributing to understanding the mechanistic interpretability of AI systems and how they represent human values.

## Research Interests
- Differential privacy
- Machine learning theory
- Trustworthy AI
- Statistical learning
- Optimization
