# Arvind Narayanan

## Biographical Summary

Arvind Narayanan is a Professor of Computer Science at Princeton University and the Director of the Center for Information Technology Policy (CITP). He is a leading researcher on the societal impacts of digital technologies, well-known for his critical perspectives on AI hype and his pioneering work on data privacy and algorithmic bias.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Arvind's research spans security, privacy, and the ethics of machine learning. He is particularly focused on de-anonymization, algorithmic fairness, and the intersection of technology and law. He co-authors the popular "AI Snake Oil" book and newsletter, which critiques overblown claims about the capabilities of artificial intelligence.

### Significant Achievements
His research was among the first to demonstrate how machine learning models can inherit and amplify societal biases from training data. He has testified before the U.S. Senate and advised various policy bodies on AI governance and data protection.

## Major Publications

- [AI Snake Oil: What Artificial Intelligence Can and Can't Do](https://www.aisnakeoil.com/book) (2024) - *A critical examination of AI's current state and limitations.*
