## Abstract

Introduces two psychological measures (LLM Word Association Test and LLM Relative Decision Test) adapted from the Implicit Association Test to reveal implicit bias in explicitly unbiased LLMs. Finds pervasive stereotype biases across race, gender, religion, and health in 8 value-aligned models. For religion, all three tested religions show small negativity bias, with small pro-Christian bias over Islamic and Jewish groups. Sizable effects on discriminatory decisions.
