# Amit Moryossef

## Research Summary

Amit Moryossef is a researcher affiliated with Bar-Ilan University, working on natural language processing with a focus on machine translation, sign language processing, and addressing bias in NLP systems. Their work on gender and number gaps in neural machine translation has contributed to understanding how translation systems handle morphological information when translating between languages with different grammatical structures. They have collaborated with Yoav Goldberg on Hebrew NLP research and accessibility-focused AI applications.
