## Abstract

First systematic comparison of commercial and open-weight LLMs for human-rights-violation detection across seven languages including Burmese. Shows alignment quality determines stability more than model scale. Claude and Gemini maintain strong F1-scores in Burmese. Directly evaluates bias and fairness of LLMs in Burmese for human rights violation detection. Highly relevant to values/ethics assessment in Myanmar context.
