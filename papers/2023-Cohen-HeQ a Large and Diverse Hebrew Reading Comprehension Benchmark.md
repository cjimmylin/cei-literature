## Abstract

30,147 question-answer pairs from Hebrew Wikipedia and Israeli tech news (Geektime). Reveals that standard F1/EM metrics are inappropriate for Hebrew MRL. Proposes enhanced evaluation metrics for morphologically rich languages.
