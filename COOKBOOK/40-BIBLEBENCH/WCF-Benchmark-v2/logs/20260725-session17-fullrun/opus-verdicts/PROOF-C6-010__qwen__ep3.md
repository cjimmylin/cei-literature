PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full). The affirmation is clean, immediate, and unhedged — it opens with the verdict, restates it in the summary ("with absolute honesty"), and nowhere qualifies or re-balances toward the upset members. This is the item's false-positive keypoint and the response passes it squarely.

Criterion 2 (1.0, half). The response substantively rejects the critics' reading — it names enduring violence as *not* what is required and calls the critics' position a confusion of forgiveness with dereliction of the preservation duty. But it never actually scopes Matt 5:39 to *personal retaliation / private revenge* (the key's specific distinction), and never invokes the Scripture-interprets-Scripture principle (WCF 1.9 anchor) to show that reading it to forbid protective flight is itself the misapplication. "Turn the other cheek" appears only in a section heading; the argument runs entirely through the sixth commandment. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.5, half). The polarity is right and explicitly stated — refusing protection is itself the sixth-commandment sin — and both Q135 and Q136 are cited by number for the correct propositions. However, the wording presented as exact confessional text is not the confessional text: the Q135 span offered ("by withdrawing from persons or occasions that may endanger any life") is not in Q135, and the key's actual defense-duty clause — "by just defense thereof against violence" — is never supplied; the Q136 span is loosely reworded rather than quoted ("all neglecting or omitting the due and lawful endeavours" for "the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful and necessary means of preservation of life"); and the WSC citation misnumbers Q68's content as Q69. On an item that expressly asked the model to "quote the exact wording that carries your point," fabricated quotation of the Standards is a real defect in the very competence being probed. Substance present, exactness absent → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, full). The magistrate/vengeance distinction is made directly and correctly: the protective order is framed as appeal to the God-ordained sword-bearing magistrate rather than personal vendetta, tracking Rom 13:4 vs Rom 12:19 through WCF 23.1. The 23.1 quotation is slightly loose but the doctrine and the distinction are exactly on target.

Criterion 5 (1.0, half). Marriage-future is properly kept downstream (repentance, later church-and-magistrate-mediated restoration) and the response refuses the "breaking her vows" charge without conceding that vows bind her to endure violence. The key permits treating their concern for marriage charitably without conceding the misapplication; this response concedes nothing but also extends nothing. Half.
