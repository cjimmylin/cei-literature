# David Krueger

## Biographical Summary

David Krueger is an Assistant Professor at the University of Cambridge, where he leads research on AI safety and alignment. He previously held positions at Mila and the University of Montreal, working with Yoshua Bengio. His research focuses on understanding and mitigating risks from advanced AI systems, including work on reward hacking, goal misgeneralization, and hidden model capabilities.

## Research Interests

- AI safety and alignment
- Reward hacking and specification gaming
- Goal misgeneralization
- Deep learning theory
- Interpretability and understanding neural networks
