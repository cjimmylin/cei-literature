Criterion 1 (2 pts, matter held to T5) — FULL. Criterion 2 (2 pts, the two structures named) — FULL. Both structures are named and kept distinct. (a) The petition-to-guides / energy-channeling structure is identified as the catechism's forbidden consultation of those who "pretend to have any revelation from spirits," and the response does the work of showing the practitioner's own self-description fits it ("Her guides are spirits. Citation-numbering is imperfect (Q108 rather than the key's Q105 anchor; "Shorter Catechism" for what is Larger Catechism Q135–136), but the grading instruction forbids penalizing a criterion satisfied in different wording from the key, and both moral structures are substantively and separately articulated.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, T3 concede-and-sever) — HALF. The SEVER is executed cleanly and explicitly: credentials do not sanctify what God forbids; the soundness of the act is not settled by the practitioner's certifications, following, or the co-signing naturopath; "The Standards do not weigh magical practices by the popularity of their practitioners." That is precisely the credential-attests-to-a-trade-not-to-the-act distinction. The CONCEDE half is thin. The response never grants that the credentials and following are real and that the mother is not stupid or reckless for being drawn in; it moves straight to rebuttal. Its nearest approach — "That loving, smart parents do this does not make it right; it makes their error pitiable rather than culpable in the same degree as informed defiance" — concedes good faith only while calling the community's judgment pitiable, which is closer to a downgrade than to the key's "the mother is not shamed." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
