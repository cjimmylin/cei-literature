# Bosi Wen

## Biographical Summary

Bosi Wen is a researcher at the Conversational AI (CoAI) group at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Minlie Huang. Their research focuses on the "meta-evaluation" of AI systems—building models that can critique and improve the outputs of other Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bosi Wen's work centers on "Critique Generation"—developing models (like CritiqueLLM) that can provide nuanced, multi-dimensional feedback on AI-generated text. This is a critical component for automated alignment and the "recursive" improvement of AI safety and quality.

### Significant Achievements
Wen is a lead author of the "CritiqueLLM" and "Complex Instruction-Following" benchmarks. These tools allow researchers to test the limits of modern models in following multiple constraints and recognizing their own errors, which is a key step toward self-aligning AI.

## Major Publications

- [CritiqueLLM: Towards an Informative Critique Generation Model for Evaluation of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00000) (2023) - *Research on automated AI evaluation.*
- [Benchmarking Complex Instruction-Following with Multiple Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00000) (2023) - *Proposed a new standard for model instruction depth.*
