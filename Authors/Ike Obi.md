# Ike Obi

## Research Summary

Ike Obi is a PhD Candidate at Purdue University whose research lies at the intersection of Neurosymbolic Reasoning, Robot Learning, Human Robot-Agent Interaction, and Safety Alignment. His groundbreaking research on "Value Imprint" has revealed significant imbalances in the human values embedded in AI training datasets. He introduced the Value Imprint framework for auditing and classifying the human values embedded within RLHF datasets, discovering that information-utility values (Wisdom/Knowledge, Information Seeking) were dominant while prosocial and democratic values (Well-being, Justice, Human/Animal Rights) were least represented.

## Key Contributions

- Developed Value Imprint, a systematic technique for auditing human values embedded in RLHF datasets
- Created a taxonomy of human values through integrated review of philosophy, axiology, and ethics
- Annotated 6,501 RLHF preferences and trained transformer-based models for automated auditing
- Audited major datasets including Anthropic/hh-rlhf, OpenAI WebGPT Comparisons, and Alpaca GPT-4-LLM
- Research selected as Spotlight presentation at NeurIPS 2024 Datasets & Benchmarks track
