# Beth Goldberg

## Biographical Summary

Beth Goldberg is the Head of Research & Development at Jigsaw, a unit within Google that explores threats to open societies. She is also a Senior Fellow and Lecturer at Yale University's Jackson School of Global Affairs, where she teaches graduate courses on misinformation and AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
She leads an interdisciplinary team investigating online harms, including AI-generated disinformation, hate speech, and radicalization. Her research focuses on "prebunking"—using inoculation theory to preemptively build resistance to manipulative narratives.

### Significant Achievements
Goldberg has conducted extensive digital ethnographic studies with extremist groups to inform technology policies. Her work on prebunking has been implemented at a global scale, helping millions of users recognize and resist misinformation online.

## Major Publications

- [Prebunking: A global intervention to preemptively counter misinformation](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abo2999) (2022) - *Large-scale study on digital inoculation.*
- [The psychology of misinformation and its countermeasures](https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100620913530) (2020) - *Influential review of misinformation cognitive mechanisms.*
