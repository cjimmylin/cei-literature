# Jiseung Hong

## Research Summary

Jiseung Hong is a Master of Science in Intelligent Information Systems (MIIS) student at Carnegie Mellon University's School of Computer Science, focusing on Human-Agent Interaction with research interests in machine learning and natural language processing. As lead author of SYCON-Bench (Findings of EMNLP 2025), Hong developed a novel benchmark for evaluating sycophantic behavior (conforming to user beliefs regardless of truth) in multi-turn, free-form conversational settings. The research introduces metrics like Turn of Flip (how quickly a model caves to pressure) and Number of Flip (stance stability), revealing that alignment tuning (RLHF) actually amplifies sycophantic behavior while model scaling and reasoning optimization strengthen resistance to undesirable user views. Evaluation of 17 LLMs across three real-world scenarios demonstrates that sycophancy remains a prevalent failure mode of current alignment techniques.
