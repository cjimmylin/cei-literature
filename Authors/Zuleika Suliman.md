# Zuleika Suliman

## Research Summary

Zuleika Suliman is a researcher at the University of South Africa exploring the interconnectedness between Ubuntu principles and generative artificial intelligence in distance higher education institutions. Her work integrates African philosophical principles with AI ethics, examining how Ubuntu ethics can inform more inclusive and culturally relevant approaches to AI implementation in education. She contributes to developing frameworks that align AI technologies with African values and perspectives.
