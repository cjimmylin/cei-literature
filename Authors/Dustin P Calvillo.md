# Dustin P. Calvillo

## Biographical Summary

Dustin P. Calvillo is an Associate Professor of Psychology at San Jose State University. His research examines cognitive processes underlying judgment and decision making, with particular focus on moral psychology, dual-process theories, and susceptibility to misinformation. He investigates how situational factors and cognitive processes influence moral judgments.

## Research Focus

Calvillo's research spans moral psychology, judgment under uncertainty, and belief in misinformation. He studies how factors like time pressure, cognitive load, and situational context affect moral reasoning and decision making. His work contributes to understanding the cognitive architecture underlying ethical judgments.
