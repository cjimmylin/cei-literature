# Christopher Kello

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Christopher Kello is Professor and Department Chair of Cognitive & Information Sciences at the University of California, Merced, where he heads the Cognitive Mechanics Lab. His research combines behavioral experiments with computational and mathematical modeling to understand the foundations of cognition and intelligence.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Cognitive Mechanics**: Building computational models to understand foundations of cognition and intelligence
- **Multiscale Dynamics**: Studying temporal hierarchies in speech, music, and animal vocalizations
- **Neural Networks and Complex Systems**: Investigating emergent properties of cognitive systems

### Significant Achievements
- Over 6,098 citations according to Google Scholar
- Served as Program Director at the National Science Foundation (Perception, Action, and Cognition Program)
- Former Associate Dean and Dean of Graduate Education at UC Merced
- Discovered remarkable similarities between whale songs and bird songs in terms of temporal hierarchies
- Research funded by NSF, NIMH, DARPA, Keck Foundation, Google, IBM, and Accenture
- Previously: Postdoctoral fellow at Carnegie Mellon (Center for Neural Basis of Cognition), faculty at George Mason University
