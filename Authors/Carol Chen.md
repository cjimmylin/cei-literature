# Carol Chen

## Biographical Summary

Carol Chen is a Member of Technical Staff at Anthropic, where she has been a researcher since 2022. She is known for her work on Constitutional AI and research investigating how language models represent global opinions and cultural perspectives. Before Anthropic, she interned at Shopify from ages 16-18 and is also a practitioner of aerial arts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Constitutional AI**: Contributing to methods for training AI systems to be helpful and harmless through self-improvement and AI feedback.
- **Global Opinion Representation**: Researching how LLM-generated responses compare to human survey responses across different populations, revealing biases toward certain regions (USA, Europe, South America).
- **Interpretability & Superposition**: Investigating polysemanticity in neural networks, demonstrating how models store sparse features in "superposition."

### Significant Achievements
- **Constitutional AI Paper**: Co-author on the influential 2022 paper establishing RLAIF (Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback) methodology.
- **Cross-Cultural AI Bias Research**: Contributed to defining metrics for measuring representation of subjective global opinions in LLMs.

## Major Publications
- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** (2022) - Foundational work on training harmless AI assistants
- **Towards Measuring the Representation of Subjective Global Opinions in Language Models** (2023) - Research on LLM cultural biases
- **Toy Models of Superposition** - Work on understanding polysemanticity in neural networks
