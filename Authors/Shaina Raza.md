# Shaina Raza

## Research Summary

Applied Machine Learning Scientist at Vector Institute specializing in Responsible AI (since January 2023). CIHR Health System Impact Fellow affiliated with University of Toronto's Dalla Lana School of Public Health. Postdoctoral fellow with fellowship co-funded by CIHR-IPPH as part of the Equitable AI and Public Health cohort and Public Health Ontario. Research focuses on large-scale application of named entity recognition to biomedicine and epidemiology, and developing equitable AI systems for public health applications.
