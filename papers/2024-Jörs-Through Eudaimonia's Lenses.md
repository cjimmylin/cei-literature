# Through Eudaimonia's Lenses: EudAImonic Well-Being Variables As Guiding Principles For AI Design

## Authors

[[Julian Marvin Jörs|Jörs, Julian Marvin]], [[Ernesto William De Luca|De Luca, Ernesto William]]

## Links



- **ResearchGate:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/380531535_Through_Eudaimonia's_Lenses_EudAImonic_Well-Being_Variables_As_Guiding_Principles_For_AI_Design

## Abstract

Psychological-philosophical perspectives on well-being have recently become relevant in the field of human-centered artificial intelligence (HCAI). A key challenge for HCAI involves analyzing how AI impacts well-being, specifically eudaimonic well-being. This paper explores eudaimonic well-being variables as guiding principles for AI design, proposing a framework to analyze AI's impact on human flourishing beyond simple user satisfaction or task performance.

## Data Description

This position paper proposes eudaimonic well-being variables (autonomy, environmental mastery, personal growth, purpose in life, positive relationships, self-acceptance) as guiding principles for human-centered AI design. It discusses how human-AI interaction affects these psychological well-being dimensions but does not create any dataset, benchmark, or evaluate any LLMs.
