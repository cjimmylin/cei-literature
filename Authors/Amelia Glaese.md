# Amelia Glaese

## Biographical Summary

Amelia Glaese is a Research Scientist at OpenAI, having previously spent five years at Google DeepMind where she led the development of Sparrow, an information-seeking dialogue agent trained to be more helpful, correct, and harmless. Her research focuses on aligning dialogue agents with human values using reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and rule-based reward models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Dialogue Agent Alignment**: Developing methods to train conversational AI systems that follow safety rules while remaining helpful and informative.
- **Rule-Based Safety**: Creating frameworks where AI safety requirements are decomposed into natural language rules that can be evaluated and enforced.
- **RLHF Research**: Advancing reinforcement learning from human feedback techniques for language model alignment.

### Significant Achievements
- **Sparrow Project Lead**: Led development of DeepMind's Sparrow chatbot, which demonstrated improved safety (only 8% rule violations when adversarially probed) while maintaining helpfulness.
- **Targeted Human Judgements**: Pioneered the approach of breaking down dialogue requirements into discrete rules for more efficient human evaluation.
- **Lasting Influence**: The Sparrow methodology influenced Google's subsequent safety work, with DeepMind noting that Gemini's model policy follows the approach established in Glaese et al., 2022.
