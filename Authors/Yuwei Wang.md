# Yuwei Wang

## Research Summary

Yuwei Wang is a researcher at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, affiliated with the International Research Center for AI Ethics and Governance, Brain-inspired Cognitive Intelligence Lab, and Center for Long-term Artificial Intelligence in Beijing. She developed STREAM (Social data and knowledge collective intelligence platform for TRaining Ethical AI Models), addressing the challenge of aligning AI models with human moral values through social data and collective intelligence approaches.
