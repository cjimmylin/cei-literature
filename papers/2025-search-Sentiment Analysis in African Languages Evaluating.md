## Abstract

Evaluates generative AI and Afrocentric transformer models for sentiment analysis in five Southern African languages. Afro-XLMR achieves over 75% F1 score, significantly outperforming GPT-4's 52-55% zero-shot accuracy. Demonstrates that Afrocentric models trained on African data substantially outperform Western-centric LLMs for culturally relevant NLP tasks.
