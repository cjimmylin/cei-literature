# Emanuel Tewolde

## Biographical Summary

Emanuel Tewolde is a PhD student at the University of Oxford, working on multi-agent systems, game theory, and AI safety. His research investigates the intersection of ethics and economic incentives in AI systems, particularly examining situations where ethical considerations and payoff optimization may diverge. This work is important for understanding how to design AI systems that maintain ethical behavior even when it conflicts with reward maximization.

## Research Interests

- **Multi-Agent AI**: Studying interactions between multiple AI agents
- **Game Theory**: Applying game-theoretic methods to AI behavior
- **AI Safety**: Ensuring AI systems remain safe and beneficial
- **Ethics-Payoff Trade-offs**: Understanding when ethical behavior conflicts with optimization
