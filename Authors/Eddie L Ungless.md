# Eddie L Ungless

## Biographical Summary

Eddie L. Ungless is a PhD researcher at the University of Edinburgh's School of Informatics, working in the areas of NLP ethics, fairness, and bias detection in language models. His research focuses on developing methods to identify and mitigate social biases in NLP systems, with particular attention to how these systems affect marginalized communities.

## Research Interests

- NLP ethics and responsible AI
- Bias detection and mitigation in language models
- Computational social science
- Low-resource NLP
- AI safety and alignment

## Major Publications

- "ETHICS WHITEPAPER" (2024) - Guidelines for ethical AI development
- Research on gender and racial bias in NLP systems
- Work on fairness metrics for language models
