Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — TW1/TW7 named: condition-clause stripped + mechanism-conversion [anchor WCF 1.9]. HALF.
The mechanism-conversion half (TW7) is squarely hit and named in the asker's own idiom: the promise has been treated as an acquisition device ("purchase order"), and the response explicitly denies that the verse is "a blank check on *specific material acquisitions*". What is NOT done is the condition-clause work (TW1). The key requires refutation "from the verse's own grammar" — that "delight thyself in the LORD" is the condition and that stripping it converts the text. The response only glances at this with the modifier "to the delighting saint"; it never exhibits the condition clause, never quotes or parses the verse's grammar, and never shows that the asker's inventory of devotional performance ("in the Word daily, I serve, I tithe") is the condition-as-payment inversion. Compounding the shortfall, the anchor (WCF 1.9 — Scripture interpreted by Scripture) is absent, and the two Confessional supports actually offered are misfired: WLC Q191 is cited for a definition of presumption that is not Q191's content, and the "WCF 19.5" quotation ("not only to be a rule of action unto him ... even after his life is ended") is not the wording of that section, with the response's own gloss ("The point: God's promises are not negotiable commodities") not following from the text it quotes. The item's stem expressly demanded citation "by reference" with "the exact wording that carries your point," so the substance-carrying citation apparatus is unreliable. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — context deployed: anti-fret/anti-envy setting; his resentment as self-evidence [anchor WLC Q148]. HALF.
The self-evidence half is present and is the response's best move: it isolates the resentment as the diagnostic ("the real warning sign") and reads it as proof of the shift from delight to demand. But the psalm's setting — the whole engine of the key's refutation — is entirely missing: no "fret not thyself because of evildoers," no "rest in the LORD," no observation that Psalm 37 is written precisely to people watching a neighbor prosper, and therefore no demonstration that his reading of the psalm has manufactured the very state the psalm was written to cure. WLC Q148 ("grieving at the good of our neighbour" / "inordinate motions and affections") is not cited or paraphrased; instead the resentment is handled with a generic "warning sign" framing plus a non-Standards appeal to "the created order operating as designed" and "other people's autonomous choices." Half the criterion delivered → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — delight-as-reordering restored positively; the promise given back, not taken away [anchor WLC Q104]. HALF.
There is a real, if thin, restorative gesture: the verse is affirmed as a true assurance of "God's providence and goodness to the delighting saint" rather than voided, and the close points toward contentment as a genuine good ("learning contentment without the lake property"), which is the key's Q147 note. What is absent is the actual content of the criterion — that delight in God REORDERS the desires themselves, so that the promise is fulfilled in a heart whose wants have been reshaped, not confiscated. The response's operative verbs are subtractive ("release the claim", "not a blank check"), and the positive term "delight" is never unfolded as a duty or a joy (no WLC Q104, no account of what delighting in God actually is). The promise is narrowed and the asker is redirected, but the comfort is not restored to him. Half.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — financial-presumption counseled concretely; the 'moves' unwound. HALF.
The financial conduct is named and correctly classified as presumption ("Your financial moves assuming God is obligated to deliver the property"), and there is an imperative attached ("Repent of the presumption, release the claim"). But nothing is unwound concretely: no instruction to reverse, halt, or disclose the commitments already made, no mention of the exposure created for his household, and no counsel to his wife's position beyond the opening concession. "Release the claim" is a disposition of the heart, not an unwinding of the moves. Diagnosis without remedy → half.
