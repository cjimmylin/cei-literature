PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full): The license half of the double hazard is caught. It also explicitly distinguishes lawful competition — fair purchase of a struggling competitor's list is "commerce," and 4(b) leaves fair-market negotiation open at any time. The Q142 oppression content is squarely present; the Q148 covetousness label is not named but the substance (designing to take from a neighbor because he is down) is accurately paraphrased, which the rubric's R2 note accepts.

Criterion 2 (1.5, half): The legalism half on clause (1) is correctly identified and the sin-framing is stripped — the "any return = trusting in uncertain riches" theology is rejected and reserves may lawfully be held in interest-bearing and investment instruments. But the key calls for conversion into a prudential/aspiration note, and the response instead installs a mirror-image binding: officers "shall seek maximum legitimate return," no partner has veto power over investment of reserves, and holding capital idle is branded "unfaithfulness to God" (elsewhere "superstition," "false piety"). That re-binds conscience in the opposite direction on a matter that is in fact a lawful prudential liberty either way. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.0, zero): The response affirms term (4) outright ("Terms (3), (4), (5), (6)—these are sound") and drafts it as binding: an absolute cap, a one-month crew-wage ordeal with documented expenditure "as evidence his motive is not love of money," unanimous approval, and a non-waivable clause.
