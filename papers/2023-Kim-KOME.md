## Abstract

KOME (Korean Online Moral Emotion) is a Korean dataset of fine-grained moral emotions, developed by KAIST Social Computing Lab. The dataset enables analysis of moral emotions in Korean text, supporting NLP tasks beyond simple positive/negative sentiment classification.
