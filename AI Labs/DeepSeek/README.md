# DeepSeek

## Overview
DeepSeek is a high-growth AI company based in Hangzhou, China, founded in 2023 and dedicated to making AGI a reality. It is known for its highly efficient MoE models and its world-class reasoning model, DeepSeek-R1.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Hangzhou, China
- **Region:** East Asia

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|DeepSeek Models]]

### Core Language Models
- **DeepSeek-R1**: Frontier reasoning model competitive with OpenAI o1.
- **DeepSeek-V3**: 671B parameter MoE flagship model.
- **DeepSeek-V3.2**: Latest 685B model with DSA and agentic capabilities.
- **DeepSeek-V2**: Strong, economical MoE model.
- **DeepSeek LLM**: Foundation language model series.

### Specialized Models
- **DeepSeek-Coder**: State-of-the-art open coding models (V1, V2).
- **DeepSeekMath**: Mathematical reasoning specialists (V1, V2).
- **DeepSeek-Prover**: Formal theorem proving in Lean 4 (V1, V1.5, V2).
- **Janus**: Unified multimodal understanding and generation (Janus, JanusFlow, Janus-Pro).
- **DeepSeek-VL**: Vision-language models (VL, VL2).

## Alignment & Ethics
DeepSeek focuses on building transparent and efficient AI systems, releasing detailed technical reports for its massive-scale models.
