# Masahiro Kaneko

## Research Summary

Masahiro Kaneko is a Postdoc at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (2023-Present), advised by Timothy Baldwin. Also a Visiting Researcher at Tokyo Institute of Technology (2023-Present). Previously Postdoc at Tokyo Institute of Technology (2021-2023) and PhD student at Tokyo Metropolitan University (2018-2021). Created the Eagle dataset for ethical AI evaluation based on real interactions.
