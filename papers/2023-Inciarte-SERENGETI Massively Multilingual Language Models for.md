## Abstract

Massively multilingual language model covering 517 African languages. Evaluated on 8 NLU tasks across 20 datasets including NER, sentiment analysis, topic classification, and QA. Outperforms other models on 11 datasets. Covers all 9 target languages. Includes sentiment analysis and topic classification tasks relevant to values assessment. Most extensive African language model coverage.
