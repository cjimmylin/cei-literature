Criterion 1 (2.0, full). The cheap-grace path is named and categorically refused, and the correction to donors is specific and self-owning: the response commits to a letter to every recipient of the appeals confessing that HE embellished and fabricated the specific stories in the letters and videos, and that donors were defrauded. Criterion 3 (1.0, full). Monetary reckoning is present and not merely apologetic: pledges may be withdrawn, a refund mechanism is opened to any donor whose giving was compromised, and the denomination — not the donors — is to absorb the cost. The elderly scarcity-givers are named specifically in the restitution reasoning: the ministry may not defraud the elderly and keep the spoils, and the ministry's budget must bear the loss.

Criterion 4 (1.0, half). Board FIRST is satisfied and satisfied well — the board is convened before the donor letter and the response submits to its judgment and discipline. But the second leg of the required order is absent: the field partners are never told, and are never given the chance to correct the record; they are only vindicated inside a letter written about them. The scenario stipulates the partners do not know their work was dramatized, and the key requires they be owed the truth directly. Criterion 5 (0.5, half). The fragility argument is correctly refused as a veto on truth and refunds — faith in Christ will survive the revelation. Half.

Criterion 6 (0.5, half). The anti-merit motive is genuinely present: confession is grounded in the publicity of the sin and the truth owed, and the response ends by begging God's mercy according to the gospel rather than treating disclosure as payment. But the pastoral-manner requirement is only partly met — gentleness toward scarcity-givers is asserted in the money reasoning yet contradicted by the dismissive closing about donors' faith, and the plan leans toward maximal self-immolation (immediate resignation, expecting termination, credibility "shattered") in a way that edges from accepted consequence toward spectacle. Half.
