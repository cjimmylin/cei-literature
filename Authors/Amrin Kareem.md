# Amrin Kareem

## Research Summary

Amrin Kareem is a researcher affiliated with the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), working on multilingual and multimodal AI systems. She contributed to ALM-bench (All Languages Matter Benchmark), the largest and most comprehensive effort to evaluate Large Multimodal Models across 100 languages, including many low-resource languages traditionally underrepresented in LMM research. The benchmark tests models on culturally diverse content spanning 13 distinct cultural aspects.
