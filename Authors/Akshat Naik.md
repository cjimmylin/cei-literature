# Akshat Naik

## Research Summary

Akshat Naik is a researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford, specializing in AI safety and alignment research. His work focuses on understanding and measuring misalignment propensity in LLM-based agents - the likelihood that autonomous AI systems will spontaneously pursue goals that conflict with their deployers' intentions. He developed the AgentMisalignment benchmark, which evaluates behaviors such as power-seeking, resisting shutdown, and sandbagging in realistic deployment scenarios.
