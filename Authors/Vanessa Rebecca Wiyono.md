# Vanessa Rebecca Wiyono

## Research Summary

Vanessa Rebecca Wiyono is an Undergraduate Informatics Engineering student at Institut Teknologi Bandung (Bandung Institute of Technology) with experience at Traveloka. Her research focuses on Indonesian natural language processing, including work on creating preference datasets for evaluating large language models in Indonesian. She has collaborated with researchers including Ayu Purwarianti and Genta Indra Winata on the IndoPref dataset.
