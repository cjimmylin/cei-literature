## Abstract

First shared task on Russian text detoxification with parallel corpus of toxic sentences and manually written non-toxic paraphrases. 6,948 training sentences from Odnoklassniki, Pikabu, and Twitter. Manual final evaluation.

## Data Description

First parallel corpus for Russian text detoxification with 6,948 training toxic sentences and manually written non-toxic paraphrases. Shared task with automatic and manual evaluation of participating detoxification systems.
