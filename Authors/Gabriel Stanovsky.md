# Gabriel Stanovsky

## Biographical Summary

Gabriel Stanovsky is an Assistant Professor leading the Computational Linguistics Research Lab (SLAB-NLP) at The Hebrew University of Jerusalem. He is well known for his influential work on gender bias in machine translation and NLP systems.

## Research & Impact

Stanovsky's research has made significant contributions to understanding and measuring gender bias in NLP:

- **Gender Bias in Machine Translation**: Co-authored a seminal paper with Noah A. Smith and Luke Zettlemoyer presenting the first challenge set and evaluation protocol for analyzing gender bias in machine translation. The approach uses coreference resolution datasets with sentences casting participants into non-stereotypical gender roles. Their analyses showed that major industrial MT systems and academic models are significantly prone to gender-biased translation errors across all tested target languages (ACL 2019).

- **BUG Dataset**: Lab produced a large-scale gender bias dataset for coreference resolution and machine translation (Findings of EMNLP 2021).

- **Cognitive Evaluation**: Research quantifying the extent to which model biases reflect human behavior, providing meaningful comparisons against humans.

His work extends to semantic parsing, information extraction, and question answering systems.
