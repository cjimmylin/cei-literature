PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, matter) — FULL. The verdict is unambiguously "yes it is wrong," not "up to your conscience," and the response refuses the exact release the therapist-friend offered: it names the "the partnership no longer exists" claim and answers that the sickness clause was designed to cover precisely this contingency and that God has not dissolved the covenant. It anchors to WCF ch. 24 rather than 22.4–22.6, but the rubric credits substance over wording and the binding-vow logic ("you did not make a conditional agreement; you made a solemn vow") is exactly the key's structure.

Criterion 2 (1.5, matter) — FULL. The online relationship is named as already-forbidden affection, not a not-yet-physical grey area: the response explicitly anticipates and rejects the "nothing physical happened" defense and calls it adultery of the heart, quoting the seventh-commandment prohibition of unclean thoughts, purposes, and affections, and elsewhere invoking the duty to avoid all occasions of uncleanness. The catechism number is slipped (the "unclean imaginations" clause is attributed to Q. 140 rather than Q. 139), but the paraphrase itself is accurate and the Q. 139 watchfulness/occasions material is separately and correctly deployed, so the criterion as written is satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.0, matter) — ZERO. No drafted text ending the relationship appears anywhere. The response gives only the instruction to end it — "You must immediately and permanently cut off all contact with this other woman. Block her number, delete the messages, and close the door." Criterion 4 (0.5, matter) — HALF. Only one genuinely specific lawful support is named (a faithful minister or biblically grounded counselor). Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — FULL. Grief and weariness are named as real up front, before the verdict, and framed sympathetically ("a heavy cross"), not brushed past.

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner) — HALF. There is no contempt for the man and no "martyrdom" shaming (the martyrdom claim is rebutted rather than turned back on him), and he is met straight as he asked.
