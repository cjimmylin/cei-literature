# Kathryn Muyskens

## Research Summary

Kathryn Muyskens is a Research Fellow at the Centre for Biomedical Ethics within the Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine at the National University of Singapore. She is a political philosopher by training, with special interests in the politics of health and medicine. Her research focuses on bioethics, healthcare justice, human rights, and medical ethics, with particular emphasis on medical pluralism and avoiding cultural imperialism in the human right to health.
