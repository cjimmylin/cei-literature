# Jeff Wu

## Research Summary

Jeffrey Wu was a Research Engineer at OpenAI from August 2018 to July 2024, focusing on AI safety research. His work centered on training GPT-2 and RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), where he trained the initial GPT Instruct series models. At OpenAI, he managed teams and projects working on scalable oversight, generalization, and interpretability.

Wu is a co-author (with primary author status) on the seminal paper "Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback" (InstructGPT), which demonstrated an avenue for aligning language models with user intent across a wide range of tasks. The paper showed how labeler demonstrations could be used to fine-tune GPT-3 using supervised learning, then rankings of model outputs to further fine-tune using reinforcement learning from human feedback.

He also contributed to OpenAI's "Weak-to-strong generalization" research, which presented a new research direction for superalignment addressing the core challenge that humans will need to supervise AI systems much smarter than them.
