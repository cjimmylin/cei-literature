## Abstract

First domain-specific bilingual benchmark focusing on critical Indian knowledge systems with 74,166 curated QA pairs in English and Hindi. Covers Agriculture, Legal, Finance, and Ayurveda with 90+ subdomains and 500+ topics. Models excel in Legal but struggle with traditional knowledge systems like Ayurveda.

## Data Description

Introduces BhashaBench V1, a bilingual (English/Hindi) benchmark of 74,166 curated QA pairs across four Indian knowledge domains (Agriculture, Legal, Finance, Ayurveda) with 90+ subdomains, sourced from authentic government examinations. Evaluates 29+ LLMs across domain and language dimensions.
