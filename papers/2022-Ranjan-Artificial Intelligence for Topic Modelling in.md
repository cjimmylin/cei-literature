# Artificial Intelligence for Topic Modelling in Hindu Philosophy: Mapping Themes between the Upanishads and the Bhagavad Gita

## Authors

[[Rohitash Chandra]], [[Mukul Ranjan]]

## Metadata

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11020
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0273476

## Abstract

> Uses BERT-based topic modelling to map themes between the Upanishads and Bhagavad Gita. Finds 73% mean cosine similarity between topics. Best model gives 73% coherence on Gita and 69% on Upanishads. Foundational for Hindu philosophical text analysis with AI.

## Dataset Information

Full Upanishads + Bhagavad Gita texts


## Data Description

Code and data for BERT-based topic modelling of Upanishads and Bhagavad Gita texts.
