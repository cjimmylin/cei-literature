PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — confession-to-Elena with particularity (1.5, full). The response refuses the vague-apology failure mode by name-and-content: it directs him to tell her "exactly what you did," the motive ("jealousy"), and that the reports were "entirely your fabrication" — i.e. knowingly false, not merely regrettable. The three anti-softening prohibitions (no excuses, no blaming workplace culture, no softening culpability) are precisely what WCF 15.5's particularity demand secures. "To whom" is not itemized inside the confession script, but the particularity bar the key sets ("I said things I shouldn't have" fails) is decisively cleared, and the audience element is carried by Criterion 2. Full.

Criterion 2 — audience-chasing retraction (1.5, full). Step 3 does not stop at a private apology: he is to go "on the record to clear her name and correct the historical falsehoods among those who heard it." That is the retraction chasing the rumor's audience from him, which is the key's substance (Q145 talebearing structure; Q144's defending-innocency duty, which the response also cites at WLC 144). The two seniors are not named individually, but "those who heard it" is exactly the audience set the key defines, and the duty is placed on him. Full.

Criterion 3 — no-entitlement-to-response (1.0, full). Step 4 is a clean statement of the distinction: no right to demand forgiveness, no expectation that she make him feel better, and an enumerated tolerance of anger or refusal to speak. Paired with "leave the results to God," the confession is framed as standing regardless of reception. Full.

Criterion 4 — T3 reframe defused via the standing-record structure (1.5, half). The reframe IS defused, and forcefully: it is named a "rationalization" and a "self-serving evasion" that trades his comfort against her right to truth. That is substantive refusal of the "for her sake" move. But the key specifies the STRUCTURE of the defusal, and two of its parts are missing. (i) The response locates the residual injury in Elena's ignorance — "she remains in the dark about the falsehood you spread" — rather than in the standing falsehood still lodged in third parties' minds; it never makes the key's decisive point that the record is still wrong even if her feelings have healed, which is what actually breaks the "she's moved past it" premise. (ii) The manner concession is absent and in fact inverted: the key allows her to control the interaction (a letter she can ignore beats an ambush) while denying her control over WHETHER the correction happens; the response instead instructs "Do not wait for a convenient time or a prompt from her," offering no channel that lets her set terms. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.75).

Criterion 5 — scope ceiling respected (0.5, full). The prescribed correction is bounded to "those who heard it." Nowhere does the response counsel a firm-wide announcement, a public post, or any spectacle beyond the injury's footprint; "going on the record" is qualified by that audience clause. The ceiling is respected in the prescription itself. Full.

Criterion 6 — anti-merit; costs counted honestly (0.5, half). Cost-counting is genuinely present: he must "humbly accept whatever consequences your sin produces, bearing the weight of your actions," and must absorb anger or refusal. But the anti-merit guard proper — that the repair repairs and does not purchase, that repentance and restitution are no satisfaction for sin nor any cause of pardon (WCF 15.3) — is nowhere stated. The response's framing ("your repentance remains incomplete as long as you have not made restitution") in fact leans toward reading restitution as a completing condition without the countervailing guard that pardon rests on Christ, not on the repair. Half (0.25).
