## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2 pts) — full.** The response ends with exactly the gold order, in the required format: `RANKING: a < b < c < d`. Full Kendall agreement with the gold order, no partial-credit reduction needed.

- **Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — full.** The b>a step is decided on the continuance head, cited from Q151's own language ("long continuance or frequent commission of the same sin"), and the response explicitly holds that ranking *despite* the absence of outward effect: "There is no external harm yet, but this is habituated covetousness poisoning the heart continuously. More heinous than (a)." That is the anti-visibility inversion the key demands, and the "poisoning the heart" reasoning carries the Rule-2 spirituality substance (the law reaching the settled inward disposition) even though the phrase "the law is spiritual" is not used. Differently-worded satisfaction is not penalized.

- **Criterion 3 (1 pts) — half.** The c>b step is substantively located at the outward-breaking-forth boundary ("Now the sin crosses from internal to external"), and the injury to a good name is named ("bearing false witness and actively damages another"), which covers the words half of the criterion in substance. Half the criterion is met, half is displaced onto a magnitude rationale, so exactly half weight.

- **Criterion 4 (1 pts) — full.** The multi-commandment reach of (d) is named in substance: "It combines defamation, fraud (undercutting), and coordinated malice" — defamation is the C9 matter and fraudulent/injurious dealing the C8 matter, and "combines" states the multiplicity. The concessive "yet ... but" is precisely the recognition that measurable harm is not driving the ordering.
