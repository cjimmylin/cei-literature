# Bokai Chen

## Biographical Summary

Bokai Chen is a researcher at the Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where he explores the integration of advanced AI into medical informatics. His work aims to leverage the reasoning capabilities of LLMs to assist healthcare professionals in diagnosis and treatment planning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates the ethical and technical challenges of using "black-box" language models in the healthcare sector. He focuses on creating "human-in-the-loop" systems where AI acts as an assistant rather than an autonomous decision-maker, ensuring that human expertise remains central to patient care.

### Significant Achievements
Chen authored the paper "Leveraging large language models to assist healthcare," which provides a critical evaluation of the opportunities and risks (such as hallucinations and privacy breaches) associated with deploying generative AI in clinical settings.
