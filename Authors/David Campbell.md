# David Campbell

## Biographical Summary

David Campbell is a researcher working on AI safety, with a focus on evaluating and mitigating dangerous capabilities in AI systems. His work addresses critical questions about the potential misuse of AI for creating weapons or other harmful applications.

## Research Focus

Campbell's research focuses on understanding and mitigating catastrophic risks from AI systems. He has contributed to the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark, which evaluates language models' knowledge about dangerous technologies and develops methods to reduce these risks through unlearning.
