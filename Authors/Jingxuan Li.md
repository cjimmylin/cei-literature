# Jingxuan Li

## Research Summary

Jingxuan Li is affiliated with the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) with email madili@g.ucla.edu. As lead author of Value-Spectrum (ACL 2025), Li developed a novel Visual Question Answering (VQA) benchmark for assessing vision-language models based on Schwartz's value dimensions. The research designed a VLM agent pipeline and constructed a vector database of over 50,000 short videos from TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels for quantifying VLM preferences via value decomposition in social media contexts. This work addresses the critical need to understand how AI systems interpret and prioritize human values when processing multimodal content from social media platforms. The Value-Spectrum paper was presented at the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics in Vienna, Austria.
