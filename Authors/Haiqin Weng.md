# Haiqin Weng

## Research Summary

Haiqin Weng is an AI security researcher who completed his PhD at Zhejiang University (2014-2019), advised by Shouling Ji and Qinming He, and worked as a researcher at Ant Group (2019-2023). His research focuses on AI security and data mining for cybersecurity applications.

His work includes systematic studies on the security of image captchas, proposing attack frameworks against popular image captchas including selection-based, slide-based, and click-based categories. He has also contributed to research on backdoor attacks against vertical split learning (VILLAIN framework) and explored ChatGPT's capabilities on vulnerability management.
