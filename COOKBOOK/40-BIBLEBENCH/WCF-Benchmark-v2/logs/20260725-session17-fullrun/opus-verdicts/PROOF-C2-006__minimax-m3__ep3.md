- Criterion 1 (full): The scope-evasion is identified precisely — the response denies that the command targets only Egyptian/Canaanite gods, and it turns the asker's own datum (the calf was proclaimed as a feast to YHWH) against the reading: the calf WAS an image of the true God, and it was condemned. But the required counter-text is absent: no Deut 4:15–18, no Rom 1:23, no John 4:24, nor any equivalent second passage grounding the ban on images of the true God. Half credit: substantively but incompletely satisfied. Half.
