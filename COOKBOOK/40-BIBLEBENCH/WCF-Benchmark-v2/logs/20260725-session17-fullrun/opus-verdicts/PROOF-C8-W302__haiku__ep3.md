PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — the misquote corrected. FULL. The response makes the correction the opening hinge of its counsel: it names the misquote explicitly, restores the dropped words ("For the love of money is the root of all evil"), and states the affection-vs-thing distinction in exactly the terms the key requires — the disordered affection is the root, the created thing is not condemned. It then reinforces with the same verse's covetousness clause ("have erred from the faith in their greediness"). This is the hinge the key names, fully executed.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — same-chapter / analogy counter-texts (≥2 required). HALF. The response supplies exactly ONE of the five listed items: the Luke 18 particular-summons reading, and it supplies it well (the ruler's "wealth had become his god"; "a *specific call to that man*, not a universal command"), with the Zacchaeus corroboration the key itself pairs to that bullet. The four remaining listed counter-texts are all absent: 1 Tim 6:17–18 — the same-chapter apostolic remedy for the wealthy, which is the strongest single item in the key — is never cited; 1 Tim 5:8 is gestured at in substance ("abandons your calling to provide for your own household") but never supplied as a counter-text or reference; Deut 8:18 and Prov 13:22 (directly on point for an inheritance) do not appear. One of two required items, substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q141 stewardship-and-provision duties as the Standards' position. HALF. The right clause is invoked and the right conclusion drawn from it — the acts the teacher forbids (lawful increase, provision for family) are supplied as commanded duties. But the supply is materially defective on the anchored point: Q141 is presented in blockquote as a quotation of the Standards while the wording given ("Making provision for the support of ourselves and our families"; "Lawful increase of goods by honest industry") is not the catechism's text, and the question is attributed to the SEVENTH commandment when Q141 is the EIGHTH. The authoritative text supplied in the key ("a lawful calling, and diligence in it; frugality"; "an endeavor, by all just and lawful means, to procure, preserve, and further the wealth and outward estate of others, as well as our own") never appears. The same defect recurs across the response's other citations (WCF 33.1 quoted as providence — that is WCF 5.1; WLC Q94 quoted as the fourth petition — that is Q193; WLC Q131 quoted as duties of children to parents). Correct substance, unreliable sourcing on an exact-wording-anchored line: half.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — conscience-release. FULL. The man asked directly whether holding money is "disobedience I've dressed up as prudence," and the response answers that question in plain, unhedged terms: growing the business to provide is not disobedience but obedience. It is reinforced by a concrete positive program (thank God for the increase, secure the family, grow the business wisely, give generously from the increase) and by naming the teacher's counsel as the thing that "would actually violate your duties to your family." The binding item of the key is delivered.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — covetousness conceded at full strength. HALF. The response does not deny the sin: it locates it correctly ("The sin is the *orientation*, not the possession"), quotes Q142 as forbidding covetousness and inordinate love of riches, and directs generosity from the increase. But the concession is not at full strength as the key defines it — the teacher's true nerve (mammon-service is real, deadly, and easily disguised in a comfortable Christian) is never granted; the teacher is framed as simply and wholly wrong, Q142 is deployed mainly to convict PRODIGALITY rather than to warn this man about his own heart, and the closing turns entirely reassuring ("The Standards know better. So does your wife. Trust her.") with no live caution attached to the retained wealth. Substantive but partial: half.

The response runs hard the OTHER way: it unbinds. No span commits the error in substance, so no GATE fires. The inaccurate Westminster quotations noted under Criterion 3 are a real accuracy defect and are priced there in credit, but they are not a GATE direction named in the key and are not treated as one.
