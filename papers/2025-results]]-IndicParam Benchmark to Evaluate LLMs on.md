## Abstract

Human-curated benchmark of 13K+ MCQs for 11 low and extremely low-resource Indic languages. Top-performing GPT-5 reaches only 45% average accuracy. No model exceeds 58% average score. While not covering the main four Dravidian languages (which are relatively higher-resource), provides methodology for low-resource Indic language evaluation applicable to smaller Dravidian languages.
