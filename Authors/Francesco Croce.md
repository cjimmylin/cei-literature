# Francesco Croce

## Biographical Summary

Francesco Croce is an Assistant Professor at Aalto University. During his PhD, he worked extensively on adversarial robustness in vision tasks, developing AutoAttack and RobustBench, two foundational tools for evaluating the security of machine learning models. His current research focuses on multimodal foundation models, investigating their adversarial robustness including jailbreaks and backdoors, especially for safe AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on adversarial robustness of AI systems, particularly multimodal foundation models. He investigates security vulnerabilities including jailbreaks and backdoors in large-scale models, with the goal of developing safer AI systems. His work bridges classical adversarial machine learning with modern foundation model safety.

### Significant Achievements
Croce developed AutoAttack, an ensemble of white-box and black-box attacks that has become the standard for evaluating adversarial robustness. He also created RobustBench, a standardized benchmark that systematically tracks progress in adversarial robustness. The benchmark contains evaluations of over 120 models and includes an open-source library to facilitate their use in downstream applications. These tools have become essential infrastructure for the adversarial robustness research community.
