# Giuseppe Russo

## Biographical Summary

Giuseppe "Peppe" Russo is a Postdoctoral Researcher at EPFL's Data Science Laboratory (DLAB). His research spans natural language processing, causal inference, responsible AI, and computational social science.

## Research & Impact

Russo's research addresses the gap between LLM moral reasoning and human moral pluralism:

- **The Pluralistic Moral Gap**: Lead author on research examining how LLMs align with human moral judgments. Introduced the Moral Dilemma Dataset (1,618 real-world dilemmas with human judgment distributions) and found that models reproduce human judgments only under high consensus - alignment deteriorates sharply when human disagreement increases.

- **Value Taxonomy**: Using a 60-value taxonomy built from 3,783 value expressions, showed that LLMs rely on a narrower set of moral values than humans, revealing a mismatch in both distribution and diversity of values.

- **Dynamic Moral Profiling (DMP)**: Introduced a Dirichlet-based sampling method that conditions model outputs on human-derived value profiles, improving alignment by 64.3% and enhancing value diversity.

This work frames alignment as a pluralistic distributional task rather than optimizing for a single correct answer.
