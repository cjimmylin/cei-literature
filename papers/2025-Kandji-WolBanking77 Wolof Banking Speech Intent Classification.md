## Abstract

Wolof banking speech intent classification dataset with 9,791 text sentences in banking domain and over 4 hours of spoken sentences. First dedicated Wolof speech and text benchmark for practical applications. First dedicated Wolof dataset for practical NLP applications. While not directly values-focused, establishes baseline for Wolof language AI capabilities needed for values assessment.

## Benchmark

**Name:** WolBanking77

## Data Description

WolBanking77 is a Wolof-language banking intent classification dataset containing 9,791 text sentences (77 intents) and ~4 hours of audio speech recordings (10 intents), the first dedicated Wolof dataset for both speech and text intent classification in the banking domain.
