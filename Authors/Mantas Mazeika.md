# Mantas Mazeika

## Research Summary

Mantas Mazeika is a Researcher at the Center for AI Safety (2024-Present). He completed his PhD in AI at the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign (2019-2024) under advisors David Forsyth and Bo Li. His research focuses on AI safety, ML safety, robustness, uncertainty estimation, out-of-distribution detection, alignment, and trojan detection.
