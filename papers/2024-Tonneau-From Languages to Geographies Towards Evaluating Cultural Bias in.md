## Abstract

Systematic survey of hate speech datasets showing geo-cultural bias. Compiles 29,855-post Spanish hate speech superset merging all available Spanish datasets. Shows hate speech datasets overrepresent more developed countries.

## Data Description

Preprocessed hate speech corpora for eight languages (Arabic, English, French, German, Indonesian, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish) with geocoding code, plus a 29,855-post Spanish hate speech superset merging all available Spanish-language datasets.
