# Gemini 1.5

## Overview
Introduced a breakthrough long-context window (up to 2M tokens) and significant improvements in reasoning and multimodal understanding.

## Technical Specifications
- **Release Year:** 2024
- **Version:** Pro/Flash
- **Context Window:** 1M - 2M tokens
