# Brandon Handoko

## Research Summary

Brandon Handoko is a researcher at Scale AI working on AI ethics and moral reasoning evaluation. He contributed to MoReBench, a benchmark for evaluating procedural and pluralistic moral reasoning in language models. His work focuses on understanding not just what decisions AI systems make, but how they reason through moral dilemmas, examining whether AI systems can reason under different ethical frameworks.
