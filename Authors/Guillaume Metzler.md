# Guillaume Metzler

## Biographical Summary

Guillaume Metzler is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at the University Lumiere Lyon 2 since September 2020. He is a member of the Data Mining and Decision Team (DMD) in the ERIC Laboratory located on the Campus of Bron.

## Education

Previously graduated from the University of Lyon in Applied Mathematics in Biology in Medicine, he completed a PhD in Computer Science at the Hubert Curien Laboratory (from January 2016 to September 2019) under the supervision of Prof. Marc Sebban, with the Blitz Business Services Company, focusing on Fraud Detection. After his PhD, he completed a 10-month post-doc at the Hubert Curien Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Amaury Habrard.

## Research Focus

He works in the field of Machine Learning, particularly on the following topics: Learning from Imbalanced Data, Fairness, Quantization of LLMs, and Survival Analysis.

## Notable Publications

- "HISTOIRESMORALES: A French Dataset for Assessing Moral Alignment" - published at NAACL 2025
- "When Quantization Affects Confidence of Large Language Models?" - published at NAACL-HLT (Findings) 2024
- "The Other Side of Compression: Measuring Bias in Pruned Transformers" - published at IDA 2023
- "SigBERT: Combining Narrative Medical Reports and Rough Path Signature Theory for Survival Prediction in Oncology" - published at ECML/PKDD 2025

He has also been involved in seminars on NLP in oncology, using consultation reports from cancer patients with Natural Language tools.

## Teaching

He teaches courses in Statistics, basics in Informatics, and Machine Learning for Bachelor and Master students at the Institute of Communication (ICOM) at Lyon 2 University, as well as Statistics and Data Analysis for Bachelor Students at EM Lyon Business School.
