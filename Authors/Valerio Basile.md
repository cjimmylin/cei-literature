# Valerio Basile

## Biographical Summary

Valerio Basile is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Turin, Italy. He is affiliated with the Content-centered Computing group and the HateSpeech research group. He holds a PhD in Computer Science and previously worked at INRIA Sophia Antipolis and the University of Groningen.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Valerio's research focuses on computational linguistics and NLP, with particular emphasis on hate speech detection, sentiment analysis, and the development of language resources. He is interested in subjective phenomena in language and how to model perspectivism in NLP systems.

### Significant Achievements
He was the lead organizer of SemEval-2019 Task 5 on Multilingual Detection of Hate Speech Against Immigrants and Women, which became a landmark shared task in hate speech research. He is also a co-author of HateBERT, a widely-used pre-trained language model specifically designed for hate speech detection.
