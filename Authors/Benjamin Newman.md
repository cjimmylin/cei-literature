# Benjamin Newman

## Biographical Summary

Benjamin Newman is a PhD student at the University of Washington's Allen School, advised by Yejin Choi. He was previously a Pre-doctoral Young Investigator at Semantic Scholar Research and a member of the Stanford NLP group. His research focuses on building reliable computational systems for human language and understanding their societal implications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work involves the systematic evaluation of large language models, particularly focusing on their transparency and reliability. He is a key contributor to the "Holistic Evaluation of Language Models" (HELM) project.

### Significant Achievements
As a co-author of HELM, Newman led the human evaluation of disinformation scenarios, providing critical insights into how language models can be misused or evaluated for harmful outputs.

## Major Publications

- [Holistic Evaluation of Language Models](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2211.09110) (2022) - *Comprehensive framework for evaluating LLMs.*
