## Abstract

Family of bilingual open-source instruction-tuned LLMs for Russian. Evaluated on MMLU, Ru-MMLU, MERA benchmarks. Vikhr-Nemo achieves 85.6% on safety benchmarks. Discusses bias and fairness concerns.

## Data Description

Bilingual Russian-English LLM adaptation pipeline producing Vikhr models, with Ru-MMLU and Ru-MMLU-pro benchmarks (translated via GPT-3.5/GPT-4), plus expanded Russian instruction-tuning datasets (Veles, ruFLAN, Saiga).
