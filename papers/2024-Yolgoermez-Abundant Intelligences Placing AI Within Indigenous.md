## Abstract

This paper describes the Abundant Intelligences Project, an Indigenous-led, Indigenous-majority international research program that imagines how to conceptualize and design AI based on Indigenous knowledge systems. It operates along three axes: Integrations (synthesizing Indigenous and mainstream AI frameworks), Imaginaries (speculative design), and Intelligences (translating human intelligences into machine intelligences). Key for NLP: it focuses on expanding linguistic structures NLP systems can handle, drawing on Indigenous storytelling and oral traditions.
