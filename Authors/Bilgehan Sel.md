# Bilgehan Sel

## Biographical Summary

Bilgehan Sel is a PhD student at Virginia Tech's Sanghani Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. Advised by Ming Jin, his research focuses on the intersection of reinforcement learning and ethical AI. He is a 2024 Anthropic Fellow, recognized for his contributions to safe AI development.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work aims to enhance the decision-making capabilities of foundational models (LLMs and robotic models) with safety and ethical considerations. He explores "collaborative alignment"—how various stakeholders can agree on the moral priorities of an AI system using multi-objective optimization.

### Significant Achievements
Sel co-developed the "Algorithm of Thoughts" (AoT) framework, which allows LLMs to explore ideas more systematically. He also pioneered the "Skin-in-the-Game" approach for alignment, which ensures that models consider the real-world impact of their decisions on different stakeholders.

## Major Publications

- [Algorithm of Thoughts: Enhancing Exploration of Ideas in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00000) (2023) - *Research on improving reasoning depth in LLMs.*
- [Skin-in-the-Game: Decision Making via Multi-Stakeholder Alignment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00000) (2023) - *Framework for grounding AI ethics in stakeholder outcomes.*
