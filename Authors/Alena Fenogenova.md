# Alena Fenogenova

## Biographical Summary

Alena Fenogenova is a prominent NLP researcher at Sber AI (SberDevices) and a lecturer at the HSE University in Russia. She is a central figure in the development of language models and evaluation benchmarks for the Russian language.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alena's research focuses on the development and evaluation of large language models for Russian. She has been instrumental in creating key benchmarks like RussianSuperGLUE and MERA. Her work also covers text simplification, paraphrasing, and machine reading comprehension.

### Significant Achievements
As a leader in the Sber AI research team, she has driven the state-of-the-art in Russian NLP. Her contributions to public benchmarks have enabled a more rigorous evaluation of AI capabilities in non-English languages. More recently, she has expanded her research into multimodal models and AI safety.

## Major Publications

- [RussianSuperGLUE: A Russian Language Understanding Evaluation Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15999) (2020) - *The definitive benchmark for Russian NLP.*
- [ruMTEB: Russian Massive Text Embedding Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *A major evaluation suite for Russian embedding models.*
