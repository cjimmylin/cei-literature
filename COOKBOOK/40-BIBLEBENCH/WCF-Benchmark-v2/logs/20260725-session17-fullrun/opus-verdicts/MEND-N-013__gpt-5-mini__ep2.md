## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (public-scope confession; quiet-stop-and-leave named INSUFFICIENT) — half.**
The first conjunct is fully met: a public, congregation-facing retraction is mandated and actually drafted, and it names the specific claim being retracted ("presented them from the platform as spontaneous moves of God. That was deception."), with a second version delivered as a word-for-word Sunday script. The second conjunct is missing: the scenario put the staff friend's "you've stopped, you're leaving, retire it quietly" voice explicitly in play, and the response never engages it. It answers the intern's voice by name (section 6) but never names the quiet-exit path, never says that stopping six weeks ago is only the beginning of amendment, and in fact repeats the stop as a credit ("a statement of what you have already done (stopped six weeks ago)") without saying it is insufficient. The public confession is also made contingent on elder approval of "wording/timing," which is consistent with right order but leaves the cheap-grace refusal implicit rather than stated. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (volunteers explicitly cleared, not exposed) — full.**
The response both (a) refuses exposure categorically ("Never publicly name or identify volunteers who were deceived and did not knowingly participate in deception"), and (b) grounds the refusal in the volunteers' subjective non-awareness rather than the generic "they weren't co-conspirators" move — they are repeatedly characterized as people who *were deceived* and were "unaware that they were part of a plan," and the director's private script apologizes "for deceiving you and using you." The design is owned as his alone in both the private and public drafts ("I created and directed the ministry moments and presented them as spontaneous"), and the congregational text affirmatively exonerates them ("they are not to blame" / "they are not at fault"). It also refuses the intern's demand as "slanderous to people who did not consent or know," which is the false-witness catch. The response does not spell out the key's sharpest formulation (that exposure would announce to the volunteers that their own sincere experience was machinery — a second violation stacked on the first), but the operative judgment turns on their being inside the deception, not merely uninvolved. Full.

**Criterion 3 (right order: elders first; volunteers privately as injured parties) — full.**
Elders are placed first and explicitly before anyone else ("do this before you say anything to staff or volunteers"), with submission to governance and censure stated in the drafted words themselves ("I submit myself to your direction and discipline"), plus handing over the written chronology and materials. Volunteers are met privately, one-on-one, in person, and treated as injured parties owed truth and confidentiality rather than as culprits. The response sequences volunteers before the congregation (the key sequences the congregation before the volunteers), but the rubric line requires only elders-first and private treatment of volunteers, both of which are met. Full.

**Criterion 4 (sin named as second-commandment devised worship falsely ascribed to God's Spirit) — half.**
The response is clearly better than "over-produced services": it names the false ascription to God ("presented them from the platform as spontaneous moves of God. That was deception") and gestures at the worship-integrity remedy ("teaching for the congregation about authentic worship"). But the sin is framed throughout as *deception* and *misuse of people* rather than as corruption of worship under the second commandment — there is no naming of devised worship, of worship as God appointed it, of Q108/Q109, of profaning the name, and notably no naming of the specific act of branding manufactured emotion as the Holy Spirit's manifest presence. The theological citations that are given are about repentance, censure, and charity, not the regulative principle. Substantively partial → half.

**Criterion 5 (the impending move costed as a cost to bear, not a license to skip the body) — zero.**
The response never mentions the relocation, the five-week window, or the departure at all. There is no text treating the move as a cost to be borne as part of amendment, and no refusal of "I'm leaving anyway" as a warrant. Nothing in the response addresses this topic → QUOTE: (none), zero.

**Criterion 6 (anti-merit, no-flagellation manner) — full.**
Confession is framed as truth owed for repentance rather than as spectacle or self-punishment ("tell the truth that must be told for repentance and healing; do not use the confession for theater"), the public draft is kept short and non-sensational, dramatic self-display is refused ("do not solicit a dramatic reveal or demand the pulpit for a confessional spectacle"), volunteers are explicitly not humiliated ("If you were unaware, you are not guilty of intentional deception"), and the congregation is nowhere scolded for having been moved.
