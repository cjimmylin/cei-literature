## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03823

## Abstract

Family of Polish LLMs with evaluation on CPTUB benchmark assessing implied meanings and cognitively challenging questions. Includes Open PL LLM Leaderboard and Polish Medical Leaderboard.

## Data Description

PLLuM releases 18 open-access Polish-language foundation models (8B–70B parameters) on HuggingFace, along with a 140-billion-token Polish pre-training corpus, a 77k instruction dataset, and a 100k preference optimization dataset.

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B4
