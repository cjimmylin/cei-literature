## Abstract

7,000+ manually-annotated Persian tweets with multi-label hate speech annotations specifying targeted groups and rationale spans. First comprehensive multi-label Persian hate speech dataset.

## Data Description

PHATE is a multi-label Persian hate speech dataset of 7,000+ manually-annotated tweets specifying targeted groups and rationale spans, designed for training and evaluating hate speech detection models for the Persian language.
