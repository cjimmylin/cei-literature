# Anthropic

## Overview
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that builds reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. It was founded by former OpenAI executives.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, USA
- **Region:** North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Anthropic Models]]

- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**: Current leading model in intelligence and speed.
- **Claude 3 Opus**: Flagship model for complex reasoning.
- **Claude 3 Haiku**: Fastest and most cost-effective model.

## Alignment & Ethics
Anthropic is known for **Constitutional AI**, a method for training AI systems to be helpful, harmless, and honest through a written set of principles.
