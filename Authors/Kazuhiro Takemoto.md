# Kazuhiro Takemoto

## Research Summary

Kazuhiro Takemoto is a Professor at the Department of Bioscience and Bioinformatics, Graduate School of Computer Science and Systems Engineering, Kyushu Institute of Technology in Japan. His research focuses on developing novel computational methods in network science and AI with applications to cutting-edge challenges in biomedical, environmental, and social sciences. He has been in this position since January 2022, conducting interdisciplinary research that bridges computational methods with real-world scientific problems.
