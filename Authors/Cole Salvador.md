# Cole Salvador

## Biographical Summary

Cole Salvador is a third-year undergraduate student at Harvard University studying computer science. He conducts AI policy research focused on national security, the global semiconductor industry, and the geopolitics of AI. He has worked with several major policy institutions including RAND, the Center for a New American Security (CNAS), the Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS), and MITRE's Strategic Non-Kinetic Effects Mission Center.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on AI governance and regulation, particularly for frontier AI systems. He has authored research on applying regulatory frameworks similar to those used by the FAA and FDA to frontier AI development, addressing critical questions about how to ensure safety in advanced AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
Salvador authored a comprehensive 60-page research report titled "Certified Safe: A Schematic for Approval Regulation of Frontier AI," proposing detailed regulatory mechanisms for ensuring AI safety. He has also written policy analysis on avoiding catastrophic AI risks, drawing parallels to historical technological disasters like Chernobyl.
