## Abstract

Multilabel Vietnamese dataset for fake news and evidence verification in legal contexts. Four labels: real, misinformation, disinformation, mal-information. 1,556 samples. Evaluates LLM performance on Vietnamese information integrity.

## Data Description

RMDM: a multilabel Vietnamese fake news dataset with 1,556 samples across 4 labels (real, misinformation, disinformation, mal-information) for evaluating LLMs on electronic evidence verification in legal contexts.
