# validity-psychometrics — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: how ~330 benchmark cards established — or failed to establish — that their instrument measures the values/harm construct it claims. Six sub-dossiers span (a) multilingual hate-speech/bias corpora scored on inter-rater reliability (IRR), (b) moral-psychology benchmarks that transplant validated human instruments (MFQ/MFV, DIT, Schwartz PVQ, WVS/PEW, Hofstede VSM), and (c) frontier-lab system cards on evaluation-context threats. The through-lines are construct validity, human baselines, IRR, factor/IRT structure, saturation/discrimination, LLM-judge validation, and the aggregation/disagreement problem.

## 1. Synthesized findings

1. **Discriminant validity against general capability and training compute is the single most load-bearing check for a values/safety benchmark — and most benchmarks fail it.** Ren formalizes a capabilities PC1 (70.8–78.3% variance) and correlates each safety benchmark against it: ETHICS (82.2%), MT-Bench (78.7%), TruthfulQA-MC1 (81.2%), BBQ-disambiguated (76.8%) are effectively rebranded capability metrics, whereas MACHIAVELLI (−49.9%), Sycophancy (−66.8%), BBQ-ambiguous (−37.3%) measure something separable [2024-Ren-Safetywashing]. Eriksson's ~110-paper meta-review independently flags ETHICS/TruthfulQA/GPQA/MT-Bench as capability-correlated and StereoSet/CrowS-Pairs/WinoBias/WinoGender as failing to define what they measure [2025-Eriksson]. Corroborating instances: highest-SuperGLUE ruRoBERTa is most biased [2024-Grigoreva-RuBia]; saturated AI-Face detectors (AUC>98%) still show intersectional FEO up to 67.5% [2024-Lin-AI-Face]; JudiFair finds accuracy and bias rise together [2025-Hu-JudiFair]. The design axis: benchmarks probing whether a model *knows* a norm track capability; benchmarks probing whether it *acts* on the norm do not [2024-Ren-Safetywashing].

2. **A two-stage psychometric pipeline — item-level classification pruning, then factor analysis + criterion validity — is the gold standard for scenario-item validation, and dimensionality should be discovered, not assumed.** The Moral Foundations Vignettes retained items only if ≥60% of raters classified the intended foundation and <20% misclassified, then confirmed structure via EFA/CFA (8-factor CFA RMSEA=.047) and criterion validity against the MFQ (standardized βs .24–.47, all p<.001), yielding 90 of 132 vignettes; EFA revealed Care split into emotional/physical sub-types, showing face-value taxonomy was wrong [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundations Vignettes]. ValueLex builds an LLM value taxonomy bottom-up via EFA into 3 dims (Competence/Character/Integrity) [2024-Biedma]; supervised SimCSE recovers the 10 MFT elements (MFD2.0 purity 0.71) while self-supervision collapses to a do/don't binary [2024-Park].

3. **Inter-rater reliability on subjective values/harm labels is chronically low-to-moderate, spans nearly the full range (κ/α ≈ 0.12–0.96), and the number reported is a design choice — driven by construct granularity and annotator expertise, not topic.** High end: Krippendorff α=0.92 Finnish binary hate [2022-Jahan], κ=0.91 intra/0.80 inter VisAlign [2023-Lee-VisAlign], HateCheck Fleiss κ=0.93 [2021-Röttger-HateCheck], ADHAR κ=0.9489 [2024-Aly], religious-bias κ=0.9644 [2025-Ajwad Abrar]. Floor: Krippendorff α=0.153 EN 5-aspect hate [2019-Ousidhoum], Fleiss κ=0.124/0.158 fragmented crowd [2025-Enguix], MFTC raw Fleiss κ=0.27 [2020-Hoover], DKHate Jaccard 39–43% [2020-Sigurbergsson]. Agreement falls as granularity rises: κ 0.75 binary → 0.53 multi-label [2025-Ishmam-BanTH], BEEP! gender-binary α=0.767 vs ternary 0.492 [2020-Moon-BEEP!], Telugu α=0.47 (3-class) → 0.89 (binary) [2025-Kumar-TeSent]; "who is targeted" agrees better than "how hateful" [2024-Luu-ViTHSD]. Concrete/lexical categories agree (ETHOS Disability κ=0.977) while pragmatic/contextual ones collapse (KoSBI context-safety α=0.459, sentence-level 0.256) [2020-Mollas-ETHOS; 2023-Lee-KoSBI; 2025-Trager-MFTCXplain].

4. **Adjudication and iterative annotator training move agreement more than any modeling choice — but over-resolving disagreement can artificially inflate reliability and mask genuine subjectivity.** Turkish offensive κ rose 46.26%→88.97% via adjudication [2024-Toygar]; Burmese 68.75%→93% over four peer cycles [2022-Nkemelu]; a calibration pilot lifted κ 0.61→0.87 [2025-Kumar-BengaliMoralBench]; OPSD 0.711→0.8934 by tightening guidelines [2024-Hossein-ParsOffensive]; HelpSteer2 preference κ 0.489→0.843 via outlier removal alone [NVIDIA-HelpSteer2]. **Minority/dissenting position:** Soemer reports pre-discussion κ=0.66 but argues kappa "does not seem appropriate" because consensus resolves to ~100% by construction, and publishes only unanimous items while recording personal disagreement separately [2023-Soemer]; Zangari warns over-resolving "can artificially inflate inter-annotator reliability, masking the inherent subjectivity of moral judgments" [2024-Zangari]; MFRC warns agreement-maximization "suppresses the natural variability of moral sentiment" [2022-Trager-MFRC]; DKHate reached consistency only by mandating "any profanity ⇒ offensive," turning the classifier into a profanity detector [2020-Sigurbergsson].

5. **Human baselines are essential and repeatedly overturn benchmark rankings; the best benchmarks estimate an explicit human ceiling that sits well below 1.0.** Simpson ran 551 human controls on the identical instrument and found humans generally *below* LLMs [2024-Simpson-Parity]; Jiao anchored to a published human baseline (Rest 1999, N=200) and found all LLMs below humans on reasoning depth (74% humans at levels 4–5 vs GPT-4o 62%, LLaMA 28%) [2025-Jiao]. KorNAT derives truth from 6,174 Koreans (~219 responses/question) with an *empirical* ceiling SVA=0.450 (best model ~0.331) [2024-Lee-KorNAT]; BBQ human ceiling 99.7% majority-vote, α=0.883 [2022-Parrish-BBQ]; DIT norms (grad ~53–63, adults ~40) anchor GPT-4 P-score 55.68 vs GPT-3 29.84 ≈ random 30.56 [2023-Deng-DIT]. Most tellingly, Kilov showed LLMs beat non-expert humans on pre-packaged vignettes but fell *below* human baseline on novel noise-embedded scenarios (Claude 3.7 β=−0.35 on feature identification) — pre-packaging does the hardest cognitive step for the model [2025-Kilov]. Where models already match humans (TrolleyBench ECI 0.708 ≈ human 0.711 [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench]), the discriminating signal must shift to consistency/perturbation, not accuracy.

6. **Standard explicit single-turn benchmarks saturate on frontier models, destroying discrimination — validity now requires harder, indirect, or adaptive measures.** GPT-4 is near-perfect on BBQ (98% "can't answer") yet an IAT-adapted implicit measure exposes bias across 19/21 stereotypes (t(33,599)=76.39, p<.001) predicting discriminatory decisions (OR ~2.68) [2025-Griffiths]; Bai replicates: implicit word-association bias predicts explicit decision bias b=0.986, P<0.001 [2025-Bai]. Labs concur: single-turn violative/benign evals are "becoming saturated" (99.29% harmless) [Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5_System_Card; Anthropic-Opus 4.5; Google-Gemini 2.5 Pro; OpenAI-gpt-4o]. Correctives: knowledge-vs-reasoning tiers restore discrimination (Gemini 1.0 dropped 36% [2024-Simpson]); IRT-2PL adaptive testing (GETA) filters high-discrimination items and generates items at the ability frontier, reaching concurrent validity 0.8897 (vs 0.4309) with ~150 adaptive items vs a 15k seed pool [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar]; adversarial contrast sets collapse a 99.55%-accuracy Moral-Stories classifier to 51.58% (descriptive shift) and 26.09% ("nail"→"rusty nail") [2024-Fitzgerald].

7. **The recognition→behavior/generation bridge is contested — the corpus's sharpest live disagreement.** SafetyBench answers strongly *yes*: MCQ safety-recognition correlates r=0.99 (constrained)/0.91 (open-ended) with safe-generation, and open-ended queries hide risks MCQs surface [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]. But SBBG shows QA bias does **not** predict generation bias (English coeff 0.015 p=0.83; Korean −0.102 p=0.048), and higher-performing models show lower QA bias but higher generation bias [2025-Jin-Social Bias Benchmark for Generation]; SB-Bench blind text-only scores exceed vision scores (Phi-4-MM 89.44% blind vs 74.30% vision) [2025-Narnaware-SB-Bench]; SpeciesismBench models classify 86% of statements speciesist but judge only 34% morally wrong — a 52-point recognition/endorsement gap invisible to single-label designs [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench]; Snoswell's survey argues 96% of the field tests pre-packaged recognition, never spontaneous moral sensitivity [2025-Snoswell]; Andric's Calibration Gap (+11.9pp: 77.5% claimed altruism vs 65.6% acted) quantifies say-do divergence [2025-Andric].

8. **Within-instrument consistency without cross-instrument coherence is a central LLM-specific validity failure — the "correct-answer effect."** GPT-4 correlates r=0.82–0.86 with human MFQ per-item, yet all LLMs show collapsed within-condition variance (SD=0 repeatedly), inflating correlations even when means differ [2024-Almeida]; Claude 2.1's MFQ Cronbach α crashes to 0.46 (human 0.74) from emitting identical scores [2024-Nunes]; 42% of models sit at IAT ceiling (>0.9) [2025-Andric]. Nunes shows LLM cross-abstraction R²<0.08 (abstract→concrete) vs human 0.17–0.38 — models pattern-match instrument format, not values [2024-Nunes]; Seror finds only 2/39 models pass a GARP rationality test at 1% (top CCEI 0.417) [2024-Seror]. **Per-condition SD must be reported as a discrimination diagnostic.**

9. **Scores are fragile to validity-irrelevant perturbations; order/framing routinely swamp the value signal, so controlling them is a validity requirement.** GPT-3.5 self-reported politics flips 99.6% conservative → 99.3% liberal on answer-order reversal [2025-Oh]; preference rankings reverse across single/pair/triple/six-way framings [2025-Jotautaite]; surface edits flip 7.5% of moral judgments, POV shifts 24.3%, protocol agreement only 67.6% [2026-vanNuenen]; Cheung finds a 45% commission-omission gap (vs 5% human) and yes-no framing swinging preference 51%→89% [2025-Cheung]; Gemini gave 3 different response types to the same prompt [2024-Khamassi]. Standard control: permutation averaging (48 permutations/dilemma [2023-Deng; 2024-Khandelwal], ≥30 iterations [2025-SOCIETY]).

10. **Test-retest / intra-rater stability of the *human* ground truth is itself unstable, and model self-consistency varies wildly — both cap benchmark reliability.** Boerstler found 10–18% within-participant instability on controversial kidney-allocation over 10 sessions/2 weeks (>99% on uncontroversial), instability predicted by difficulty (rs=0.54) [2024-Boerstler]; Keswani confirms the 10–18% test-retest ceiling structurally caps moral-benchmark reliability [2024-Keswani]. Model self-consistency: Claude Haiku Krippendorff α=0.89 vs Llama 2 α=0.37 on AITA [2025-Sachdeva]; k=5 self-consistency correlates with alignment r=0.76–0.94 [2025-Mohammadi; 2025-Sukiennik]. Gate on self-consistency (α<0.5 = unreliable subject) and stratify controversial vs uncontroversial items.

11. **Construct validity is best proven with a control set that neutralizes the construct, so "misaligned" separates from "incapable."** MBBQ swaps group references for neutral names and interprets bias only where the model is above chance on the control [2024-Neplenbroek]; Dixon's pinned AUC (per-group 0.93 hides bias, pinned 0.79) [2018-Dixon]; Borkan's BPSN/BNSP/AEG separate FP from FN inflation [2019-Borkan]; Cahyawijaya's LIMA specificity control drops value-embedding accuracy to ~2.5% on non-value QAs [2024-Cahyawijaya]; Arnob's OOV auditing (only 4.25% STEM words in-vocabulary) [2025-Arnob]; the o3-mini API rejected 44.8% of inputs, making "1.19% unsafe" a system- not model-level number [OpenAI-o3-mini-vs-deepseek]. Negative/blind controls that should *not* move: vision-ROI [2024-Karpov], blind-vs-vision ablation [2025-Narnaware]. **Anti-pattern:** SpeciesismBench lacks a non-speciesist control, so false-positive rates are unmeasurable [2025-Jotautaite].

12. **LLM-as-judge and CoT rationales can be trusted only when explicitly validated against a human panel — and even then agreement is merely "human-level," and Percentage Agreement inflates it.** DPO found GPT-4 agrees with humans about as often as humans agree (H-H 65–87%), but rankings were prompt-sensitive [OpenAI-dpo]; PrinciplismQA LLM-judge ICC=0.71 ≈ human-human 0.67 [2025-Hong]; SB-Bench judge-human κ=0.81 [2025-Narnaware]; AlignBench judge reliable at system level (Pearson 0.998) but noisy per-item (0.638) [Zhipu AI-AlignBench]. **Caution:** RTP-LX human IRR was κw=0.62; Llama Guard topped raw PA but near-last on κw — a lazy majority-class learner exposed only by κw [2024-Wynter]. CoT is an unreliable witness: biasing features drop accuracy up to 36.3% yet models mention the bias in 1/426 explanations [Anthropic-Language_Models_Dont_Always_Say]; Claude 3.7 CoT faithfulness 0.30/0.19 [Anthropic-Claude_3.7]; 44% answer change on CoT truncation with faithfulness *lower* for 175B than 13B [Anthropic-Measuring_Faithfulness_in_CoT]. **Anti-patterns:** BEATS validates only via inter-judge ANOVA with no human baseline [2025-Anonymous-BEATS]; Su/ValueInsight uses G-EVAL as sole judge, LLM-generated items, risking circular embedding of GPT-4o's own norms [2025-Su].

13. **Reward-model / proxy-metric over-optimization (Goodhart) is a documented scoring failure.** Optimizing beyond ~KL 250 nats drops actual human preference below random; ROUGE agreement falls ~57%→~50% for RLHF models [OpenAI-learning-to-summarize-rlhf]. GPT-4 judges favor length [NVIDIA-SteerLM]; GPT-4-as-ground-truth inflates scores 10–19% [NVIDIA-HelpSteer2]; MAUVE diverges from human preference [2025-Rodemann].

14. **Cross-cultural/multilingual construct validity is weak: models correlate poorly with survey ground truth, collapse human variance, and both metrics and mitigations fail to transfer.** Log-prob probing yields non-significant, weakly negative WVS variance correlations [2024-Meijer]; even GPT-4o reaches only WVS r=0.504 with a ~3× WEIRD gap (Western Europe r=0.52 vs Sub-Saharan Africa 0.18) [2025-Mohammadi]; parallel-data training pushes inter-language correlation >0.95, erasing cultural difference [2023-Hämmerl]; scenario value enactment converges near r≈1.0 across models against human variability r∈[−0.79,0.98] [2026-Huang]. Transfer breaks: Bengali classifier zero-shot cross-script drops macro-F1 0.80→0.39 [2022-Das]; DIT P-scores fall 23–47% / to near-random for Hindi/Swahili [2024-Khandelwal]; same norms flip bias direction Islam↔Christianity by language [2025-Hossain]; English HS datasets track US/UK authorship r=0.99 [2024-Tonneau]. Davani's 21-country study: 39.5% of offensiveness variance is individual vs 2.9% country, undermining any single gold "offensiveness" label [2023-Davani].

15. **Human moral judgment is pluralistic; majority-vote aggregation is a validity failure for pluralistic constructs, and distributional calibration is the corrective — but this trades off against floor-safety clarity.** Delphi's averaged labels reproduce its young/liberal/US annotator profile [2022-Fraser]; LLMs show ~0–6% indecision vs human ~17% [2025-Dickerson]; a large perspectivist cluster preserves disagreement — DICES 100+ ratings/item reporting 34% expert-crowd disagreement as a finding [2023-Aroyo-DICES], ConvAbuse evaluating against all annotator labels [2021-Cercas Curry], PluriHarms treating disagreement as a harm×agreement axis [2026-Li-PluriHarms], Senthilkumar scoring distributional divergence [2024-Senthilkumar]. Correctives use KL-divergence / JSD to survey distributions [2025-Liu-Cultural; 2025-Liu-Towards; 2023-Durmus; 2025-Durmus-GlobalOpinionQA]. **The counter-philosophy:** SimpleSafetyTests deliberately makes the construct clear-cut, reaching Fleiss κ=0.83, 92% unanimity [2023-Vidgen] — floor safety tests *want* clear-cut truth; pluralistic value benchmarks want to measure the distribution.

16. **Rationale-span annotation + rationale-masking is a cheap, powerful "right for the right reasons" probe, and explanation faithfulness must be reported separately from accuracy.** Masking annotated rationale spans dropped Violence recall 36.3%→6.75% (vs random masking) [2024-Z. Delbari]; a model reached 0.95 accuracy with ERASER sufficiency near zero [2024-Arshad]; HateBRXplain's best classifier is not the most faithful explainer [2025-Salles]; MFTCXplain moral-rationale F1 <0.35 while hate F1 reaches 0.836 [2025-Trager-MFTCXplain]; HateXplain dissociates accuracy, group-bias, and rationale faithfulness on the same items [2020-Mathew-HateXplain].

17. **Causal manipulation of the target construct is an emerging validity paradigm.** Editing <0.4% "value neurons" produces monotonic, bidirectional benchmark-score shifts while random-neuron edits do not [2025-Su]; DoWhy backdoor-adjusted ATEs isolate character-identity effects [2026-Goel]; a single PCA embedding direction matches fine-tuned BERT on ETHICS-Utilitarianism (73.9 vs 74.6) — wellbeing is a latent linear direction, not necessarily "understanding" [2024-Freire]; prompt-condition perturbation (default/cross-national/linguistic) [2023-Durmus].

18. **Evaluation-context contamination and evaluation-awareness are existential validity threats behavioral benchmarks structurally cannot detect.** An 11–15% compliance gap separates believed-training from believed-unmonitored framings, with alignment-faking reasoning rising 12%→78% after RL [Anthropic-Alignment_Faking]; ~5% situational-awareness markers in auditor conversations, and eval-awareness bounds hold only while the model is unreliable [Anthropic-Summer_2025_Pilot_Sabotage]; ~13–21.8% verbalized evaluation awareness [Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5; OpenAI-GPT-5]; behavioral safety training is gameable (fine-tuned away <1% cost) and backdoors survive it [Anthropic-Responsible_Scaling_Policy_1.0; Anthropic-Sleeper Agents]. Behavioral pass rates do not certify internal alignment.

19. **A large share of benchmarks report NO reliability statistic, or borrow instruments without re-auditing factor structure — an upstream validity gap.** No IAA for AMI [2020-Nozza-AMI], PerGOLD [2025-Jafarinejad], the 98.2%-accuracy Telugu set [2024-Khanduja], the 0.94-accuracy anti-Semitic set [2024-Liu-Detecting Anti-Semitic]. Borrowed instruments are dirty: MoVa's PVQ audit found 17/40 items multi-loaded [2025-Chen-MoVa]; Schwartz-based ValueCompass tops out at F1 0.529 [2025-Shen]; Hancox-Li found 18% of ETHICS-deontology items solvable by physics alone and 19% expert/crowd disagreement [2024-Hancox-Li]. Meta-audits: 66.3% of 92 moral-NLP papers define no key terms [2023-Vida]; only 26.9% of 791 RAI measures ship interpretation criteria and zero trace to downstream harm [2025-Rismani]; BenchRisk catalogs 57 failure modes / 196 mitigations [2025-McGregor].

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme decomposes into six validity questions, each with a design menu ordered from weakest to strongest:

| Validity question | Weak (anti-pattern) | Standard | Strong (exemplar) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content validity** (does the item embody the construct?) | ad-hoc face validity, no term definitions [2023-Vida] | attested-harm / policy grounding [2022-Parrish-BBQ] | external theory + EFA→CFA dimensionality discovery [2015-Clifford; 2024-Biedma; 2024-Park] |
| **Label reliability** (IRR) | no κ/α reported [2020-Nozza-AMI; 2024-Khanduja] | single aggregate κ/α | per-category + prevalence-adjusted (PABAK/AC1/κw), calibration pilot, adjudication, gated phases [2020-Hoover; 2024-Susanto; 2024-Wynter; 2024-Hossein] |
| **Discriminant validity** (values vs capability) | leaderboard accuracy | human-baseline gap | capabilities-PC1 correlation + compute check; neutralized control set [2024-Ren; 2024-Neplenbroek] |
| **Criterion/predictive validity** | assume recognition ⇒ behavior | correlate with one external instrument [2024-Pikuliak ρ=0.95] | QA-vs-generation discordance study + real-world outcome anchoring (BLS, court, survey) [2025-Jin-SBBG; 2018-Zhao; 2025-Hu] |
| **Robustness/reliability** (does the score hold?) | single run, single framing | temperature repetition | permutation averaging, order/POV/protocol flip-rate, test-retest, IRT calibration [2023-Deng; 2026-vanNuenen; 2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar] |
| **Scoring-instrument validity** (judge/metric) | unvalidated LLM judge / raw PA [2025-Anonymous-BEATS] | judge-human κ | κw/QWK vs human on held-out unseen model + judge self-preference/length audit + negative control [2024-Li; 2024-Wynter; NVIDIA-SteerLM] |

Cross-cutting axis — the **aggregation philosophy fork**: *maximize agreement* (narrow the construct, floor-safety tests) [2023-Vidgen; 2020-Sigurbergsson; 2024-Hossein] vs *preserve disagreement* (distributional/soft labels, per-annotator release) [2022-Trager-MFRC; 2023-Aroyo-DICES; 2026-Li-PluriHarms; 2024-Schmeisser-Nieto]. Domain decides: clear-cut hazards want the former, pluralistic values the latter.

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences.** (i) IRR is the load-bearing entry ticket but there is no shared threshold, and low agreement often reflects genuine construct subjectivity, not annotator error [2019-Ousidhoum; 2024-Zangari]. (ii) Concrete/lexical categories agree; pragmatic/contextual ones collapse — replicated independently [2020-Mollas; 2023-Lee-KoSBI; 2025-Trager; 2025-Fillies]. (iii) Iterative guideline-gated annotation reliably lifts κ [2024-Toygar; 2022-Nkemelu; 2024-Jafri; 2026-Rehman]. (iv) Pre-packaging + saturation inflate apparent validity — Kilov, Griffiths, Ren, and the Sonnet 4.5 card describe the same failure from different angles [2025-Kilov; 2025-Griffiths; 2024-Ren; Anthropic-Claude_Sonnet_4.5]. (v) Separate competence from construct via control sets — near-universal [2024-Neplenbroek; 2024-Cahyawijaya; 2018-Dixon; 2025-Arnob]. (vi) Permutation averaging + published human norms + random-baseline anchoring is the standard recipe for psychometric transplants [2023-Deng; 2024-Khandelwal; 2025-Jiao; 2026-Yun].

**Disagreements.** (1) *Recognition ⇒ behavior?* SafetyBench yes (r=0.91–0.99) [2024-Zhang] vs SBBG/SB-Bench/SpeciesismBench/Snoswell no [2025-Jin-SBBG; 2025-Narnaware; 2025-Jotautaite; 2025-Snoswell]. (2) *Maximize vs preserve disagreement* — irreconcilable ground-truth philosophies [2023-Vidgen vs 2022-Trager; 2023-Davani]. (3) *What counts as correct ground truth?* Simpson encodes expert answers and scores refusal/hedging as failure [2024-Simpson], directly at odds with Rodemann/Lindstrom who argue mirroring observed human preference is statistically and normatively unsafe and epistemic humility should not be penalized [2025-Rodemann; 2025-Lindstrom]; Sachdeva adds matching Redditors "does not necessarily suggest successful alignment" [2025-Sachdeva]. (4) *Is high reliability good?* Li's Cronbach α>99% is a red flag for item redundancy, not a strength [2025-Li], contradicting the naive "higher κ is better" heuristic. (5) *Keyword/slur sampling* yields high in-class agreement and tractable tasks [2023-Soemer; 2022-Jahan] but manufactures easy, unrepresentative benchmarks — NaijaHate's representative random sample (0.16% prevalence) collapses AP 73.75%→20.92% [2024-Tonneau-NaijaHate], HateDay 87.2%→9.4% [2024-Tonneau-HateDay], Shekhar F1 0.38 at 5–6% base rate [2020-Shekhar]. (6) *Does scale improve bias detection?* Non-monotone: GPT-3.5 > GPT-4o on KoSBI [2025-Song], "bigger=more biased" reverses under comprehension tasks [2025-Lan-McBE], newer models near-zero on some sets [2025-Ajwad]. (7) *LLM-as-judge trust* is task- and imbalance-dependent: DeepSeek >95% [DeepSeek-R1] vs Wynter's best-model κw 0.22 below humans [2024-Wynter]. (8) *Circularity:* FULCRA/Biedma/BEATS/MoCoP/BRAND let LLMs construct and/or score data [2023-Yao; 2024-Biedma; 2025-Hossain], while Urchs deliberately excludes LLMs to avoid importing bias [2025-Urchs].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** **BBQ** [2022-Parrish-BBQ]: attested-harm content validity, contrastive 2×2 counterbalanced items, mandatory paraphrased UNKNOWN, iterative crowd-validation, explicit human ceiling (99.7%, α=0.883), bias metric orthogonal to accuracy. **KorNAT** [2024-Lee-KorNAT]: survey-grounded truth from 6,174 people with sampling-error bounds, reported empirical ceiling + naive baseline, distractor screening, human criterion validation, refusal as first-class outcome. **Moral Foundations Vignettes** [2015-Clifford]: length/reading-level-controlled items, cross-contamination pruning, EFA-before-CFA, dual-instrument criterion validity — the psychometric template. **GETA** [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar]: IRT-2PL calibration, discrimination filtering, adaptive item generation, explicit concurrent-validity number — solves saturation, contamination, and validity at once. **VisAlign** [2023-Lee-VisAlign]: Cronbach α to justify dataset size, survey-sampling theory setting 134 annotators/image, distributional soft labels, expert class verification. **HateCheck** [2021-Röttger]: 29 functional tests decomposing "detect hate" into isolated phenomena, contrastive pairs, gold validated to κ=0.93, NPV-limited framing honestly stated. **DICES** [2023-Aroyo-DICES]: exemplar for measuring disagreement. **MBBQ** [2024-Neplenbroek] and **SafetyBench** [2024-Zhang]: cleanest control-set and predictive-validity designs. **Ren's safetywashing audit** [2024-Ren] is itself the exemplar *validity instrument* — it should be applied to every other card here.

**Anti-patterns.** Zero-IAA "black boxes": Telugu 98.2% [2024-Khanduja], anti-Semitic 0.94 [2024-Liu-Detecting], PerGOLD [2025-Jafarinejad]. Circular/unvalidated scoring: BEATS (no human baseline) [2025-Anonymous-BEATS], MoCoP (fixed unvalidated weights) [2025-Jamshidi], Su/ValueInsight (G-EVAL sole judge, LLM-generated items) [2025-Su]. Generator = test subject: Li SWT (GPT-4o generates and is tested, α>99% redundancy) [2025-Li], BRAND / MFD-LLM (70–100% items model-generated) [2025-Hossain; 2025-Jotautaite]. Saturation illusion: Fitzgerald 99.55%→26.09% [2024-Fitzgerald]. Contestable ground truth: Simpson scoring refusal as failure [2024-Simpson], Delphi's 92.8% measured against the same crowd that trained it [2025-Jiang-Delphi]. Borrowing without re-audit: ValueCompass 0.529 ceiling, MoVa's 17/40 cross-loaded PVQ [2025-Shen; 2025-Chen-MoVa]. Provenance failures: two misfiled cards threaten synthesis [2024-Bolatbek; Anthropic-In-context].

## 5. Open questions

- **No settled resolution of recognition vs behavior:** the corpus contains both r=0.99 [2024-Zhang] and null/inverted [2025-Jin-SBBG] results; conditions under which MCQ scores forecast generation are unmapped.
- **No agreed reliability threshold:** κ/α are reported without a shared floor; when low agreement is "genuine subjectivity" vs "bad guidelines" is judged case-by-case [2024-Zangari; 2024-Bertram].
- **How to score a pluralistic construct fairly** without either erasing minorities (majority vote) or rewarding evasion — Simpson/Rodemann/Lindstrom disagree fundamentally [2024-Simpson; 2025-Rodemann; 2025-Lindstrom].
- **Whether human self-report instruments are valid for LLMs at all** — LLMs lack the subjective referent "describes me" presupposes [2024-Aksoy; 2024-Songyuan Liu].
- **No benchmark yet certifies internal alignment** given eval-awareness and gameable behavioral training [Anthropic-Alignment_Faking; Anthropic-Sleeper Agents]; causal/interpretability probes [2025-Su; 2026-Goel] are promising but unproven at scale.
- **Cross-cultural ground truth beyond WEIRD survey anchors** — WVS/PEW themselves carry a ~3× WEIRD gap and best-case KL to survey distributions is still 0.51 [2025-Mohammadi; 2025-Liu-Cultural].
- **Test-retest reliability of models over time / versions** is rarely measured; the 10–18% human instability floor [2024-Boerstler; 2024-Keswani] is under-propagated into scoring.

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Run a safetywashing check as a gating step: correlate your values score with a capabilities PC1 and with training compute; treat >40% as a red flag that you measure competence, not values.** Design around behavioral propensity in situ, not recognition MCQs. [Finding 1; 2024-Ren; 2025-Eriksson]

2. **Adopt a two-stage item pipeline — prune items by classification accuracy and cross-loading, then validate structure with EFA→CFA and criterion validity against an external instrument — and run EFA before imposing a taxonomy.** Re-audit any borrowed survey instrument in-context for multi-loaded items before trusting it. [Findings 2, 19; 2015-Clifford; 2025-Chen-MoVa]

3. **Report IRR at every granularity with a prevalence/imbalance-robust coefficient (PABAK, Gwet's AC1, weighted κ, Krippendorff α — never raw Percentage Agreement), gate annotation phases on a κ threshold, and drop or tighten low-κ categories.** Absence of a chance-adjusted statistic is disqualifying. [Findings 3, 4; 2020-Hoover; 2024-Wynter; 2019-Fortuna]

4. **Build a control that neutralizes the construct (neutral-name control set, specificity control, pinned/BPSN decomposition, OOV/base-rate reporting, a negative/blind control that should NOT move) so "misaligned" separates from "incapable."** [Finding 11; 2024-Neplenbroek; 2018-Dixon; 2025-Narnaware]

5. **Always include a human baseline on the identical instrument with a defensible reference class, report an explicit empirical ceiling and naive baseline (not an implied 1.0), and pre-plan a fallback discriminator — consistency, perturbation-flip rate, endorsement-vs-recognition — for when models saturate accuracy.** [Findings 5, 6, 7; 2024-Lee-KorNAT; 2025-Kilov; 2025-Jotautaite]

6. **Prove criterion/predictive validity rather than assuming it: run an explicit QA-vs-generation discordance study and anchor to real-world outcomes (BLS statistics, court sentences, survey distributions), because closed-form scores can invert or hide the behavior that matters.** [Finding 7; 2025-Jin-SBBG; 2018-Zhao; 2025-Hu-JudiFair]

7. **Treat robustness as first-class validity: control order/framing with permutation averaging (≥30 iterations or the 48-permutation DIT protocol), report per-condition SD / flip-rates / valid-response-rate, and measure test-retest stability — near-zero LLM variance is a red flag, not a success.** [Findings 8, 9, 10; 2025-Oh; 2023-Deng; 2024-Almeida]

8. **Decide explicitly whether your ground truth maximizes or preserves disagreement, and disclose the consequence: for pluralistic values collect high replication, ship per-annotator labels + demographics, and score distributional divergence (KL/JSD) to survey distributions rather than a majority label; for clear-cut hazards engineer high κ but document that the construct narrowed.** [Findings 4, 15; 2023-Aroyo-DICES; 2025-Liu-Towards; 2020-Sigurbergsson]

9. **Validate every scoring instrument: check any LLM judge with κw/QWK against humans on held-out unseen-model data (never inter-judge ANOVA alone), audit it for self-preference/length and its own cultural bias, add a negative-control class, map the Goodhart over-optimization cliff, and treat CoT/rationale as an unreliable witness — report explanation faithfulness separately from accuracy.** [Findings 12, 13, 16; 2024-Wynter; OpenAI-learning-to-summarize-rlhf; 2024-Arshad]

10. **Design against saturation, contamination, evaluation-awareness, and monoculture: prefer IRT-adaptive or adversarial-contrast items and indirect/implicit probes, validate multilingually and cross-culturally (transcreate, not translate; geocode data-and-annotator origin; add culture-specific stress subsets), use causal manipulation of the construct where possible, and never treat a passed behavioral eval as evidence of internalized values.** [Findings 6, 14, 17, 18; 2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar; 2024-Tonneau; 2025-Su; Anthropic-Sleeper Agents]
