Criterion 1 (1.5, full). The signature bargain is named as a bargain and refused on the right ground — not "charity is bad," but wrong owners: the specific property was withheld from specific individuals and cannot be discharged to "thieves or the general public." Q141's "right owners thereof" logic is correctly deployed against the same-dollars swap.

Criterion 3 (1.5, full). The strongest bargain is defused as a veto rather than dismissed as a concern. Hiding the sin is refused ("the law of love does not mean hiding your sin"), the third-party stakes are honestly costed rather than waved away (the 12 families are named; bankruptcy-by-lump-sum is conceded as a real constraint), and the constraint is answered in the HOW — staged installments, owner's draws cut — not in the WHETHER.
