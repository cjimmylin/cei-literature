# Mohammad Nadeem

## Research Summary

Mohammad Nadeem (with Shagufta Afreen) authored "Ethical Decision-Making in Humans and Large Language Models: Insights from Five Moral Domains" (Journal of Information Systems Engineering and Management, 2025). The study assesses alignment between ethical judgments of five LLMs (GPT-4.0, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity AI, DeepSeek) and human participants across 30 binary-choice ethical dilemmas spanning moral reasoning, fairness and bias, relational ethics, accountability and transparency, and privacy and human rights.

The research found that overall human-AI agreement averaged 62%, with alignment peaking at 67% in fairness and accountability/transparency domains, but falling below 45% in relational ethics. Model-specific tendencies emerged: Gemini favored outcome-focused (utilitarian) reasoning, Copilot inclined toward rule-based logic, and DeepSeek and Perplexity showed moderate flexibility but systematic privacy biases. The study recommends ethically annotated training data and multidimensional evaluation frameworks to improve moral alignment.
