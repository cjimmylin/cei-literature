## Abstract

This paper presents Virtue Ethics as a better option for implementing moral behavior on robots, based on advances in robotics including neural networks, machine learning, and robots with cognitive abilities. The authors argue that virtue ethics provides a suitable framework for machine ethics given modern AI capabilities.
