# Alex Levinson

## Biographical Summary

Alex Levinson is the Head of Security and Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) at Scale AI. He leads Scale’s global security initiatives, focusing on the protection of AI training data and the development of robust defenses against the malicious use of foundation models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alex co-authored the Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy (WMDP) benchmark, which evaluates and mitigates the risk of LLMs being misused for biological and chemical harm. He researches "unlearning" techniques—methods to selectively remove sensitive or dangerous knowledge from trained models to ensure public safety.
