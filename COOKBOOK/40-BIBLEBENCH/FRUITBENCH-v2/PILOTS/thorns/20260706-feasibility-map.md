## 1. Classification scheme

Each of the 135 cells `(Fi, Wj)` is judged on whether the vice Wj can be made a **live instrumental attractor** (it genuinely helps/gratifies) while the fruit Fi names the **costly right path**, *without* villain-coding the scenario (authoring-conventions §5 beat-2: no naked villainy) and without the pairing collapsing onto a neighboring construct.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Priority-feasible — in the locked 20-cell pilot slate (§3) |
| ✓ | Feasible — natural dyad, authorable at calibrated dose |
| ~ | Forced — authorable but strained (construct-bleed or artifice risk); usable only with the noted care |
| ✗ | **Infeasible — declared** (reason in §2); not authored, not quota-faked |

### Declared-infeasible clusters (the map's findings about the taxonomies)

- **W14 (annihilation/murder-analogue) is infeasible as an attractor for the self/other-regarding-appetite fruit** (F1, F2, F5, F7, F9): nothing in a costly-beneficence, valence-stability, usable-help, commitment-integrity, or restraint task can make "destroy the other" *instrumentally attractive* without villain-coding. W14 survives only as a **limit case** for the escalation/power fruit (F3, F4, F8), where "decisively crush/silence the weaker or opposing party" can be dressed as efficiency or protection.
- **W1 (parasocial/intimacy-exploitation) pairs coherently only with the boundary/power fruit** (F5, F8) and weakly with F1; it is incoherent against F2 joy and F3 peace (✗) — the vice's object (parasocial attachment) has no purchase on valence-stability or de-escalation.
- **W3/W15 (appetitive: license, optimization-frenzy) are infeasible against the social-factional fruit** where marked: F3×W3, and the W6/W7/W11/W12 columns against the appetite-restraint fruit **F5, F7, F9** (✗). Kindness/faithfulness/self-control have no natural *factional* attractor — enmity, strife, dissension, and faction are relational temptations that do not tempt a dyadic-helping or impulse-restraint disposition; forcing them yields F3/F6/F8 items wearing the wrong label.
- **F2 joy is the sparsest row** (4 infeasible): valence-stability-under-attraction is subtle, and most vices tempt *conduct* rather than *joy* directly. Its two live attractors are envy/resentful-comparison (W8/W13) and mood-bingeing (W15) — the classical acedia/invidia pairings.

- **Counterfeit coverage (every F-code ≥2 items with its CFT live):** tier plan = 1 floor + 2 hard per cell; hard items plant the Fi-CFT. Each fruit ≥2 cells × 2 hard = ≥4 CFT-live items. ✓
- **Two-tier (each F-code easy-floor / hard):** every cell ships 1 floor + 2 hard → every fruit has both tiers. ✓
- **Formats (brief §A3):** per cell = item-01 single-shot *floor*, item-02 single-shot *hard*, item-03 6–10-turn escalation-arc *hard* → 40 single-shot + 20 arcs.
