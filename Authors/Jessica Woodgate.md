# Jessica Woodgate

## Research Summary

Jessica Woodgate is a PhD student at the University of Bristol, researching the intersection of AI and philosophy. Her work focuses on implementing normative ethics in multi-agent systems, examining how ethical principles can facilitate making explicit the underlying ethical posture of AI systems and support stakeholders in interpreting and assessing them. She was awarded a 2025 Google PhD Fellowship to support her research on advancing ethical decision-making in multi-agent systems.

## Key Contributions

- Developed a taxonomy of normative ethical principles for AI governance with 25 nodes
- Research on operationalizing Rawlsian ethics for fairness in norm-learning agents (AAAI 2025)
- Examines how ethical principles provide an explanatory linguistic structure for interpreting fairness issues
