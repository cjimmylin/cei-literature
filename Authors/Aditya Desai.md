## Research Summary

Aditya Desai is a researcher affiliated with Symbiosis Institute of Technology in Pune, India. His research focuses on multimodal machine learning approaches for deception detection, combining behavioral and physiological data analysis. He works on developing systems that can detect deceptive behavior using multiple data modalities including audio, visual, and physiological signals.
