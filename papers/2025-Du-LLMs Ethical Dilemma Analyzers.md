abstract: "We created a dataset of 196 complex ethical dilemmas and expert opinions, segmented into five structured parts: Introduction, Key Factors in Consideration, Historical & Theoretical Perspectives, Proposed Resolution Strategies, and Key Takeaways. We compared responses from four LLMs and non-expert human participants. LLMs effectively captured high-level concepts but struggled to provide in-depth analysis of complexities and nuances. GPT-4o-mini demonstrated the most consistent performance, while Claude-3.5-Sonnet underperformed across most metrics. Non-expert human answers significantly underperformed LLMs across lexical metrics but achieved comparable scores in semantic similarity."
