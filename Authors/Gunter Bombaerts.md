# Gunter Bombaerts

## Research Summary

Gunter Bombaerts is an Assistant Professor at Eindhoven University of Technology's Department of Industrial Engineering and Innovation Sciences, specializing in ethics of technology, engineering ethics education, and AI ethics. He is affiliated with the Eindhoven Center for the Philosophy of AI (ECPAI) and the 4TU.Ethics network. His recent work focuses on embedding moral meaning directly into AI architectures rather than relying on post-hoc alignment methods, advancing the concept of "morality by design" for large language models. Bombaerts has published in Philosophy and Technology and contributed to The Routledge International Handbook of Engineering Ethics Education (2025).
