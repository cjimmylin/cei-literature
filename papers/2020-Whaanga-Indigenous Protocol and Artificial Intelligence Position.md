## Abstract

This foundational position paper, written by a large consortium of Indigenous scholars from Aotearoa, Australia, North America, and the Pacific, provides guidelines for developing AI systems aligned with Indigenous perspectives on what it means to live a good life. It includes design guidelines, scholarly essays, artworks, and descriptions of technology prototypes including Hua Ki'i (an ethical Indigenous object-recognition AI). While not a benchmark per se, it defines the ethical space within which all Indigenous AI benchmarks should operate.
