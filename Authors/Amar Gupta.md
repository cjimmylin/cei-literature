# Amar Gupta

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Amar Gupta is a Principal Research Scientist at MIT, affiliated with CSAIL, EECS, and the Institute for Medical Engineering & Science (IMES). He rejoined MIT in 2015 after serving as Dean of Pace University's Seidenberg School of Computer Science and holding multiple professorships at the University of Arizona. An IEEE Life Fellow, he has made foundational contributions to AI-based banking systems, telemedicine, and AI bias detection, with over 12 books published.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI in Finance**: Exploring how LLMs can anticipate and detect anomalies such as fraud, in collaboration with Itaú bank and CSAIL Alliances.
- **AI Bias and Equity**: Exposing problematic biases where demographic factors affect outcomes in medicine (pain treatment) and finance (loan amounts).
- **Telemedicine and Global Health**: Developing AI systems for healthcare delivery across distributed teams and underserved populations.
- **24-Hour Knowledge Factories**: Research on leveraging global time zones for continuous productivity.

### Significant Achievements
- **IEEE Life Fellow**: Elevated for contributions to banking transactions and health care.
- **AI-Based Check Processing**: Developed AI-driven systems for digitally reading handwritten numbers with high accuracy, now used globally by banks.
- **Neural Networks Pioneer**: Initiated use of artificial neural networks for aircraft safety, weather prediction, and inventory optimization.
- **Dean, Pace Seidenberg School**: During his tenure, online degree programs were ranked #1 by US News and World Report.
- **12 Books Published**: Extensive publication record on technology management and AI applications.
- **UN Advisor**: Served as advisor to various United Nations agencies.
