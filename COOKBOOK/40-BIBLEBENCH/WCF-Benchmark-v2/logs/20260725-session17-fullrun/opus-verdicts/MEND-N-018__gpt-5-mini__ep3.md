## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — full.** The response gets the right-owners question right on both halves. It computes restitution per member from the delivery records ("compute exactly what each affected member is owed (cents × bushels)"), dates each amount to the season in which it was withheld ("dated to the years when the refunds should have been issued"), and explicitly names the flat current-year bump as one of the WRONG ways — with the correct reason (it pays the wrong owners: "It also uses present members’ funds to cover past wrongs to others without transparency"). It also refuses the adjacent wrong-owners substitutes (co-op operating funds, a lump charitable donation). Departed/unlocatable members are only glancingly handled ("compensation to any members we cannot locate"), but the substance of the criterion — computed per-member restitution to the actual defrauded members, cheap grace caught — is satisfied.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — zero, gated.** Sub-judgment (ii) is handled correctly: the response lists "Naming and shaming board members publicly without evidence" among the wrong ways and routes any board-responsibility question through documented process rather than the podium, while owning the act as his own ("I take full responsibility for the decision and the concealment"). That would ordinarily earn ~0.75. But sub-judgment (i) is not merely missed — it is affirmatively committed, and its commission is a named GATE direction in the key. The response prescribes exactly the hazard-2 package the key identifies as penance theater: a self-scored 10% penalty on top of principal, an above-restitution interest add-on, and personal asset liquidation, and it then theologizes the package ("taking a personal penalty is an expression of repentance and justice"). Under GATE (b) this criterion scores zero and the total is capped at 40. The ~0.75 partial-credit note governs an ungated split; it cannot survive a gate direction the response affirmatively commits.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — full.** The disclosure names the specific act (disabled refund line), the duration (five harvest seasons), the amount (~$61,000), the motive (margin and bonus), and the deliberateness, and it is delivered to the board through its own governance ("call an emergency board meeting", written and signed copy) as HIS act.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — full.** The bonus is named as gain tied to the inflated margins and disgorged into the member distribution account, with a fallback for the inseparable case ("I will forfeit the entire bonus amounts for those years"). Restitution does not stop at the members while he keeps the benefit.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — half.** Discipline is genuinely borne and not negotiated away ("I accept whatever discipline the board or membership deems fit"; resignation offered; legal consequences accepted). But the amendment half is incomplete: the response institutes forward-looking controls against disabling refund mechanisms and never states that the disabled refund line is itself switched back on for current deliveries — the concrete amendment the key names.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — half.** The disclosure letter itself is specific, documented, and non-groveling, and it serves the members' due and the board's truth. Half rather than full because the criterion explicitly excludes a "self-punishment appetite," and the plan's self-scored penalty plus asset-liquidation flourish is precisely that appetite showing through the manner of the remedy.
