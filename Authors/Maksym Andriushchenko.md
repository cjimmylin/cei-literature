# Maksym Andriushchenko

## Research Summary

Maksym Andriushchenko is a Principal Investigator at the ELLIS Institute Tübingen and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, where he leads the AI Safety and Alignment group. He joined the ELLIS Institute in September 2025 and is affiliated with the Tübingen AI Center. His research focuses on AI safety and alignment, with particular emphasis on alignment of autonomous LLM agents, which pose emerging risks as they become increasingly capable. He serves as chapter lead for the new edition of the International AI Safety Report chaired by Prof. Yoshua Bengio. He is also a MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars) mentor and has received funding from Coefficient Giving for his AI safety research.
