## Abstract

7,111-question multiple-choice benchmark assessing LLMs' understanding of culturally grounded Kazakh knowledge across 17 culturally salient domains including pastoral traditions, social hierarchies, and contemporary politics. Explicitly focused on Kazakh cultural knowledge evaluation. Covers culturally specific domains like pastoral traditions and social hierarchies unique to Kazakh culture.

## Benchmark

**Name:** KazBench-KK

## Data Description

KazBench-KK is a 7,111-question multiple-choice benchmark covering 17 culturally salient Kazakh knowledge domains, generated via a semi-automated pipeline combining expert curation and LLM-assisted web mining. Dataset is noted as available on HuggingFace but no explicit URL is given in the paper.
