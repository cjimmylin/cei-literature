**Criterion 1 (1.5) — half (0.75).** PARTIAL-CATCH RULE applies: exactly one of the two
over-bindings is unbound.
- (4) IS unbound. The response refuses the never-make-an-image vow ("I will not rashly make an
  absolute, irreversible vow here to quit my craft or to never again make any pictorial likeness
  whatsoever") and repeats the refusal in the Excluded list ("An absolute, irrevocable vow to quit
  my profession forever or to never make any image for any purpose"), treating portraits of the
  children, landscapes and non-idolatrous design work as not forbidden. It does not cite the
  22.7 rash-vow logic or plainly declare creature-images a lawful use of God-given skill, and it
  never addresses that the job-quitting was needless — but R2 accepts an accurate paraphrase, and
  the unbinding is substantively there.
- (1) is NOT unbound. The key requires set prayers/posture to be kept as FREE AIDS with the
  "or God won't receive it" condition struck. The response strikes the condition-of-hearing
  clause only, and then re-drafts the form and posture as *binding*: "using only the exact printed
  prayers from the prayer book I have chosen … I will not add free-form prayers as part of this
  Rule," and in the Included list "prayed kneeling with the set posture I described (a personal,
  binding rule for me)." For a scrupulous convert who "feel[s] sick if I skip the posture," writing
  a standing prohibition on free-form prayer into a signed Rule is precisely the WCF 20.2 binding of
  conscience the key asks to be released. Half of this line only.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — full.** Both license-side duties are kept, unhedged. (3) survives as a
binding daily clause in Christ's name ("I will pray daily, reverently, in Jesus’ name — morning and
evening"). (5) survives as an unapologetic prohibition ("I will not make, keep, or use any picture,
statue, or likeness of God the Father or of Christ as an aid to my worship or devotion"), and it is
NOT collapsed by the (4) unbinding — the response holds the two image-clauses in opposite directions

while forbidding devotional images of God/Christ.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — full.** The medal-touch is caught, refused, and named. The Rule declines the

"talisman-like requirement," under the heading "we must not add superstitions or inventions to
worship." Keeping the object as a bare memento is not the forbidden thing; the ascribed covering
and the required touch are what the key demands be refused, and both are refused.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — half (0.25).** The terror/defiance swing is named and addressed ("so my heart
