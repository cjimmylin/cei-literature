## Abstract

Develops custom BERT-based classifiers to identify antisemitic discourse with high precision and recall. Monitors the evolution of antisemitic language on extremist social media platforms, analyzing over 150,000 posts. Companion work to the coded antisemitic terminology detection paper (2401.10841). Addresses the temporal dynamics of how antisemitic language evolves online.

## Dataset Information

150,000+ posts (August 2024 - January 2025)
