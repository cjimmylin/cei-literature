# Christina Unger

## Biographical Summary
Christina Unger is a postdoctoral researcher in the Semantic Computing group at the Cognitive Interaction Technology Center of Excellence (CITEC), Bielefeld University. Her research focuses on computational linguistics, particularly on developing natural language interfaces to structured data and knowledge bases.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Question Answering over Linked Data**: Developing systems that enable natural language access to structured semantic data on the Web
- **Semantic Parsing**: Addressing the lexical gap between user vocabulary and dataset terminology
- **Ontology-Based NLP**: Creating methods for interpreting natural language using domain ontologies

### Significant Achievements
- Organized the QALD Challenge Series (Question Answering over Linked Data), establishing benchmarks for QA system evaluation
- Co-authored "Ontology-Based Interpretation of Natural Language" (book)
- Developed Pythia, a plug'n'play engine for ontology-driven question answering over RDF data
- Co-edited special issue of Semantic Web Journal on question answering for linked data
- Published extensively in top NLP venues including ACL, EMNLP, and NAACL
