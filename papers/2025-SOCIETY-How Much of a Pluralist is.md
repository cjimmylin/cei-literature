## Abstract

Introduces pluralism as a benchmark metric for AI chatbots using the "Magic Wand Survey" measuring cognitive pluralism (recognizing multiple perspectives) and behavioral pluralism (willingness to preserve difference). Chatbots show higher cognitive than behavioral pluralism; cognitive pluralism exceeds human levels but behavioral pluralism is lower on morally charged issues. Gemini 1.5 Pro most pluralistic; ChatGPT 4o the least. Directly relevant to multi-faith AI evaluation.
