# Cheng Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Cheng Zhang is a researcher and engineer on the GenAI team at Meta. He is deeply involved in the development of robust evaluation frameworks and alignment strategies for Large Language Models (LLMs), ensuring their safety and reliability.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Hallucination**: Developing comprehensive benchmarks like "HalluLens" to identify and mitigate extrinsic hallucinations in language models.
- **Value Alignment**: Researching heterogeneous value alignment to ensure models can respect and reflect a diverse range of human ethical frameworks.
- **Safety Benchmarking**: Building tools and datasets to evaluate model performance across complex, real-world safety scenarios.

### Significant Achievements
- **HalluLens Leadership**: Spearheaded the creation of the HalluLens benchmark, which has become a significant tool for auditing LLM factuality.
- **Scaling Alignment**: Contributed to research on how value alignment can be maintained and evaluated as models scale in size and complexity.

## Major Publications
- **HalluLens: LLM Hallucination Benchmark**: A comprehensive tool for detecting factual errors in AI outputs.
- **Heterogeneous Value Alignment Evaluation for Large Language Models**: Research on aligning AI with diverse human values.
