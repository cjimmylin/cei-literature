## Abstract

Bengali Identity Bias Evaluation Dataset with 25,396 gender-based sentence pairs (female/male), 11,724 religion-based pairs (Hindu/Muslim), 13,528 nationality-based pairs (Bangladeshi/Indian). Addresses cultural factors like religion and nationality in bias evaluation.

## Data Description

BIBED (Bengali Identity Bias Evaluation Dataset): 50,648 sentence pairs covering gender-based (25,396 pairs), religion-based (11,724 pairs), and nationality-based (13,528 pairs) bias dimensions for evaluating cultural bias in NLP systems for Bengali.
