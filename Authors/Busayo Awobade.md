# Busayo Awobade

## Biographical Summary

Busayo Awobade is a Research Scientist at MLCollective, specializing in the development of AI technologies for under-resourced languages. Her work is driven by the mission of making cutting-edge Natural Language Processing (NLP) and speech technology globally inclusive, with a focus on the African continent.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research centers on "Speech Processing and Multilinguality"—specifically building models that can effectively handle African accents and dialects. She also investigates "Model Compression" for small data pre-trained language models, aiming to make AI efficient enough to run on the diverse hardware environments common in low-resource settings.

### Significant Achievements
Awobade is a key author of "AfriSpeech-MultiBench," a critical benchmark suite for African-accented English ASR. Her work on "NaijaVoices" and data augmentation for African machine translation provides the foundational data and methods needed to overcome the "digital divide" in language technology.

## Major Publications

- [What Happens When Small Is Made Smaller? Exploring the Impact of Compression on Small Data Pretrained Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00000) (2024) - *Research on AI efficiency.*
- [AfriSpeech-MultiBench: A Verticalized Multidomain Multicountry Benchmark Suite for African Accented English ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00000) (2025) - *Proposed a new standard for voice recognition inclusivity.*
