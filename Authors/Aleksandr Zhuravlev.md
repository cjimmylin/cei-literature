# Aleksandr Zhuravlev

## Biographical Summary

Aleksandr Zhuravlev is a researcher engaged in identifying and articulating the critical technical and socio-political challenges facing the safe development of artificial intelligence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aleksandr contributes to systemic investigations of "hard problems" in AI safety. He co-authored a roadmap identifying ten fundamental challenges—including alignment, robustness, and governing the systemic risks of advanced AI—that the global community must solve to ensure AI's benefits are realized without causing catastrophic harm.

## Major Publications
