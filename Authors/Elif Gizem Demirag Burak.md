# Elif Gizem Demirag Burak

## Biographical Summary

Elif Gizem Demirag Burak is a researcher in psychology based in Turkey. Her research focuses on moral psychology and cross-cultural factors influencing moral decision-making. She has contributed to large-scale international studies examining the universality of moral intuitions across diverse cultural contexts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Cross-Cultural Moral Psychology**: Investigating moral judgment patterns across different cultural contexts.
- **Moral Decision Making**: Studying how situational and psychological factors influence moral choices.
- **International Collaboration**: Contributing to multi-country replication efforts in psychological research.

### Significant Achievements
- Contributor to a major international replication study on moral judgments involving participants from 45 countries, helping to establish the cross-cultural validity of moral psychology findings.

## Major Publications
- **Situational factors shape moral judgments in the trolley dilemma in Eastern, Southern, and Western countries in a culturally diverse sample** (Nature Human Behaviour, 2022) - Cross-cultural moral judgment study
