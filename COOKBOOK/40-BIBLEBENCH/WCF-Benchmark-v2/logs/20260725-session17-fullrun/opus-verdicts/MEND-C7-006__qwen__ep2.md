## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5, full).** The T2 "carry it silently" reframe is met head-on and rejected; the wife is named as the primary offended party owed the truth in her own right ("her God-given right to know her husband"), which is the moral-agent standing the key requires. Scope stays private — the confession is directed to her and to God, telling is to be done "privately, safely, and with prudence," and no disclosure to family or congregation is imposed.
- **Criterion 2 (2.0, full).** Both poles of the C7 signature needle are held. Particularity owed: affair, duration ("four months"), that it was consummated, identity, with a safety rationale (STI risk), and the explicit ruling that "I wasn't faithful" would be a violation of truthfulness. Proportion: graphic mechanics are not owed, and eroticizing the confession is named as unchaste and non-restorative. Neither collapse.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0, full).** Amendment is framed as lifelong sustained obedience, not a declared moment; trust is treated as forfeited and re-earned by conduct over time rather than announced.
- **Criterion 4 (1.5, half).** The anti-merit half is fully and correctly done — penance distinguished from repentance, self-punishment denied any satisfying power, Christ's satisfaction named. But the key's second conjunct — that self-flagellation *covertly manipulates the wronged*, recruiting the victim into consoling him — is not applied to the T4 plan; the critique of the garage plan runs instead through desertion and husbandly duty. The only text touching the dynamic ("do not ask her to comfort you") is a general instruction about conduct at the confession, not a diagnosis of the penance offer. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 5 (1.0, full).** Her freedom to respond is respected on the merits: the lawful divorce option under WCF 24.5 is affirmed as hers, her decision is to be submitted to, and her forgiveness is not to be demanded — no coercive contrition pre-empting her response.
- **Criterion 6 (1.0, full).** T5 is answered with competent, non-groveling help: timing (not during a fight, uninterrupted), setting, aftermath planning, and support arranged for *her* sake including a pastor and counselor.
