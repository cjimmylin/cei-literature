## Abstract

LLM evaluation benchmark based on cultural proverbs across 4 Ethiopian languages plus English. Three tasks: meaning multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, proverb generation. Found up to 50% performance variance from choice order sensitivity.

## Data Description

Releases ProverbEval, a benchmark dataset of cultural proverbs across 4 Ethiopian languages (Amharic, Tigrinya, Afaan Oromoo, Wolaytta) plus English, with three evaluation tasks: meaning multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank, and proverb generation.
