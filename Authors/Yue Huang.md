# Yue Huang

## Research Summary

Yue Huang is a PhD student in the MINE Lab at the University of Notre Dame's Computer Science and Engineering department, supervised by Prof. Xiangliang Zhang. His research focuses on trustworthy AI, foundation model alignment, and AI safety. His recent work includes benchmarking LLMs on laboratory safety risks (Nature Machine Intelligence, 2026), quantifying biases in LLM-as-a-Judge systems (ICLR 2025), and developing comprehensive trustworthiness frameworks for LLMs through TrustLLM and psychometric evaluation approaches.
