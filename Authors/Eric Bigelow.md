# Eric Bigelow

## Biographical Summary

Eric Bigelow is a Research Scientist at Anthropic, where he works on AI safety and alignment research. He is part of the broader AI safety research community working on foundational challenges in ensuring that advanced AI systems are safe and beneficial.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Safety Research**: Contributing to the theoretical and empirical foundations of AI alignment.
- **Foundational Challenges**: Identifying and addressing core scientific, methodological, and sociotechnical challenges in LLM safety.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of the comprehensive "Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of Large Language Models" paper, which identifies 18 foundational challenges and 200+ concrete research questions for the field.

## Major Publications
- **Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of Large Language Models** (TMLR 2024) - Comprehensive roadmap for AI alignment research
