# Daniel E. Ho

## Affiliation
William Benjamin Scott and Luna M. Scott Professor of Law; Professor of Political Science (by courtesy); Senior Fellow, Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI), Stanford University

## Research Summary

Daniel E. Ho is a leading scholar at the intersection of law, AI, and public policy at Stanford University. He directs the Regulation, Evaluation, and Governance Lab (RegLab), which focuses on using data science to improve government. His research addresses critical questions about AI fairness, discrimination in algorithmic decision-making, and the governance of AI systems. He has made significant contributions to understanding how AI systems can both perpetuate and help address discrimination.
