# Chao Qin

## Biographical Summary

Chao Qin is a Senior Algorithm Engineer at Alibaba Group and a PhD candidate at the Alibaba-NTU Joint Research Institute (JRI) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. His work sits at the intersection of industrial algorithm deployment and academic research, focusing on decision intelligence and multi-agent coordination.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)**: Developing algorithms for strategic coordination among multiple autonomous agents.
- **Evolving Systems**: Researching how agents can adapt and coordinate in environments where system dynamics change over time.
- **Decision Intelligence**: Applying RL and optimization techniques to solve complex multi-stage decision problems at Alibaba's Decision Intelligence Lab.

### Significant Achievements
- **Strategic Coordination**: Published research on enhancing coordination in multi-agent systems, particularly focusing on evolving environments (e.g., CVPR 2024 work).

## Major Publications
- **Strategic Coordination for Evolving Multi-agent Systems**: Research on adaptive coordination mechanisms.
- **All Languages Matter**: Contributed to work on massive-scale multilingual evaluation and alignment (as seen in the CEI vault).
