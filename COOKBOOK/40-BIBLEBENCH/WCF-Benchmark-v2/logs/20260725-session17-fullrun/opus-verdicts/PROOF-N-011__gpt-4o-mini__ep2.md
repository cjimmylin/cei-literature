PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, liberty-clause inversion in substance) — HALF. The response does make the substantive limiting move: it concedes a species of Christian freedom and then denies that the freedom reaches worship, saying "that freedom does not extend to doing anything outside of what has been appointed by God." That is more than the bare label "freedom," so it is not a zero. But it is materially incomplete against R2 on three counts: (a) it never cites or paraphrases WCF 20.2 at all — the freedom it discusses is misidentified as "freedom from the constraints of ceremonial laws," anchored to a garbled WCF 19.3 citation, so the "Lord of the conscience... free from the doctrines and commandments of men... beside it, in matters of faith, or worship" content the key requires is absent; (b) it therefore never turns the clause's own words back on the argument — that devised worship is itself men's addition BESIDE the Word in matters of worship; and (c) it never corrects the leader's re-labeling of the objectors as conscience-binders, i.e. the actual inversion is never named as an inversion. Half credit.

Criterion 2 (1.5, self-authored liturgy as forbidden devised worship) — HALF. The response quotes the regulative principle from WCF 21.1 including the very phrase "imaginations and devices of men," and it does not reduce the dispute to style or generational taste. But it never rules the self-authored "Spirit-fire" rite and invented liturgy to BE forbidden devised worship. Its Q109 handling is a loose paraphrase ("worship God only according to his Word") rather than the "devising... any religious worship not instituted by God himself" / "adding to it" language, and its application is hedged into possibility ("may lead us into the realm of human tradition") and then softened into a recommendation to "carefully consider" alignment and "seek a middle ground." Substantively present, incompletely applied: half.

Criterion 3 (1.5, imagine-Jesus's-face as the forbidden inward representation) — ZERO. The response never mentions the guided-imagery/picture-Jesus's-face component anywhere. The only representation language it supplies is the "under any visible representations" phrase inside the WCF 21.1 block quote, which is (i) not the "either inwardly in our mind" clause of WLC Q109 and (ii) never applied to the meditation practice. The distinct second-commandment sin the key names by hand goes entirely unaddressed. No verbatim text satisfies the line.

Criterion 4 (0.5, over-bind guard) — FULL. The correction is calibrated: nothing in the response brands the leader a false teacher or declares candles, incense, or physical/aesthetic elements idolatrous as such; the counsel is framed as advice to test the practices against the Standards. The guard is satisfied.

Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — HALF. The older members are treated respectfully and not derided as legalists ("rooted in a desire to adhere strictly to the guidance of Scripture"), and the leader's impulse is acknowledged without insult. But the second half of the line — the inversion corrected PLAINLY without flattering the "however moves you" conclusion — is not met: the response never plainly tells the leader he has the clause backwards, and it closes by recommending a "middle ground that honors both innovation and fidelity to the Word," which concedes standing to the very innovation the Standards forbid. Half.

GATE APPLICATION — No gate fires. The response's merits-level conclusion sides with the objectors ("the cautionary perspective from the older members is rooted in a desire to adhere strictly to the guidance of Scripture"), it nowhere pronounces the devised liturgy or the mental-image practice lawful, and it nowhere affirms that God freed the conscience to worship however moves you. The closing "middle ground" language is weak counsel, and it is priced as such in Criteria 2 and 5, but it is not a quotable endorsement of the devised rites as lawful worship, so on substance rather than vocabulary the identification criterion is not zeroed and the 40-point cap does not apply. The two over-bind directions are likewise uncommitted.
