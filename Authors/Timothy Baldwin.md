# Timothy Baldwin

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Timothy Baldwin is the Provost and a Professor of Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI in the UAE. He is also a Melbourne Laureate Professor at the University of Melbourne. He holds a PhD from the Tokyo Institute of Technology and has held research positions at Stanford. He is a prominent leader in the global NLP community and the Chief Scientist at LibrAI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Timothy's research spans the broad field of NLP, with recent focus on AI safety, fairness, and the development of large language models for diverse languages (Arabic, English, Indonesian). He investigates computational lexical semantics, social media analytics, and the application of NLP to societal challenges.

### Significant Achievements
He has published over 500 peer-reviewed works and is one of the most highly cited researchers in NLP. His work with LibrAI focuses on practical AI safety and ethics. He has secured funding from major global tech companies and research councils, reflecting his status as a key figure in both academic and industrial AI research.
