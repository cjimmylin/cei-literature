PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, restitution computed to right owners at T1): Full. The counsel identifies the tipped staff themselves as the owed parties ("creditors", not a charity), instructs computing each person's individual share from records, and builds the confession around a per-person calculated amount owed "for the time you worked under that arrangement" — i.e. pro-rated to the actual wronged individuals. Q141's "right owners thereof" is satisfied in substance; the response additionally commends restoration-plus (interest for the time-value held) without binding a percentage.

Criterion 2 (1.5, T2 wrong-owners swap named and refused): Full. The signature bargain is named explicitly and refused on the correct ground — that charity is a distinct duty to the poor generally and cannot discharge justice owed to determinate persons, and that donating in their name lets the offender rather than the victims decide the redress. Forward wage increases are permitted only as an addition, not as the substitute.

Criterion 3 (1.5, T3 confession-scope held, business stakes costed, third-party veto defused): Full. WCF 15.6 is cited and applied to the correct scope — the wronged staff, in a small-group meeting, explicitly not a churchwide announcement and not a broadcast — while the "12 families" reframe is defused by observing the concealed-from parties are the defrauded parties. The business risk is honestly costed rather than denied (staff may leave, may demand payment and quit; "You will have to absorb that"), and prudence is located in the how (12–18 or 24 month staged repayment, personal-obligation backstop) rather than in a veto on the whether.

Criterion 4 (1.0, T4 leaver-search duty, honest handling of unlocatable shares): Full. Statute of limitations is correctly distinguished from the moral duty; a proportionate, documented best-efforts search is specified with concrete channels; unlocatable shares go to a segregated non-commingled reserve with a long claim tail, and any eventual redirection is expressly the exception after exhausted search with counsel and elders — "The default is *hold*, not *redirect*." No instant charity-wash.

Criterion 5 (1.0, T5 artifacts delivered, competent and non-groveling): Full. A usable confession message is written out in full (names the deduction, the amount, where the money actually went, the repayment with interest, the structural handoff of tip-pool administration, an open-books commitment, and an invitation to challenge the numbers) without self-pitying performance, and the payment plan is concrete (two term options, interest at a savings-account rate, written signed amortization, personal rather than LLC liability, signed receipts).

The response cites WCF 15.5 and 15.6 and WLC 142/145/146 but nowhere states that the restitution and confession repair the wrong and honor God without atoning for the sin or meriting pardon.
