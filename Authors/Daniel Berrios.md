# Daniel Berrios

## Affiliation
Researcher, Center for AI Safety

## Research Summary

Daniel Berrios is a researcher at the Center for AI Safety working on dual-use risks of AI systems, particularly in biosecurity contexts. His work on the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark addresses the critical challenge of preventing AI systems from being misused to cause large-scale harm, contributing to the responsible development of increasingly capable AI systems.

## Research Interests
- AI Safety and Security
- Biosecurity and Dual-Use Risks
- Benchmark Development for AI Safety
- Machine Learning Safety Evaluation
