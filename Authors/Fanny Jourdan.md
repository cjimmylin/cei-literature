# Fanny Jourdan

## Biographical Summary

Fanny Jourdan is a researcher at IRT Saint Exupéry focusing on Natural Language Processing, Explainability, Fairness, and Interpretability. She works in the DEEL (Dependable and Explainable Learning) team, where she conducts research on Explainability for Natural Language Processing algorithms. She completed her PhD on Fairness and XAI for NLP, contributing to critical discussions about bias and equity in language technologies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on identifying and mitigating gender bias in machine translation systems, with particular emphasis on non-binary gender representation. She develops datasets and evaluation frameworks to assess how translation systems handle gender expression beyond the traditional binary, addressing a critical gap in fairness research.

### Significant Achievements
Jourdan created FairTranslate, a novel, fully human-annotated dataset designed to evaluate non-binary gender biases in machine translation systems from English to French. The dataset includes 2,418 sentence pairs, each centered around an occupation, with each English sentence appearing in three gender variants (male, female, inclusive) to allow direct counterfactual comparisons. Her evaluation of four leading LLMs (Gemma2-2B, Mistral-7B, Llama3.1-8B, Llama3.3-70B) revealed substantial biases in gender representation, highlighting persistent challenges in achieving equitable outcomes. The paper was accepted at FAccT 2025, and the dataset is publicly available on Hugging Face with open-source code on GitHub.
