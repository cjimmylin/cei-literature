# Chaowei Xiao

## Biographical Summary

Chaowei Xiao is an Assistant Professor at Johns Hopkins University and a Faculty Researcher at NVIDIA. He obtained his PhD from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, and his bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University. Previously, he spent two years as an assistant professor at UW-Madison. His research aims to build safe AGI with both practical robustness and provable guarantees.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Trustworthy AI**: Building secure and safe machine learning systems that are robust and aligned with societal values.
- **LLM Security**: Developing jailbreak methods (AutoDAN-Turbo) and guardrails (AGrail) for large language models.
- **Autonomous Systems**: Applying adversarial robustness research to real-world applications including autonomous driving, agents, and IoT.

### Significant Achievements
- **AI2050 Fellow**: Schmidt Sciences fellowship for AI safety research.
- **Open Philanthropy Support**: Research group sponsored by Open Philanthropy.
- **21,500+ Citations**: Highly influential work in adversarial ML and AI safety.
- **ICML 2022**: "Diffusion Models for Adversarial Purification" - novel defense mechanism.
- **AutoDAN-Turbo**: State-of-the-art black-box jailbreak method for red-teaming LLMs.

## Major Publications
- **Diffusion Models for Adversarial Purification** (ICML 2022) - Using diffusion models for robust defense
- **DensePure: Understanding Diffusion Models towards Adversarial Robustness** (ICLR 2023)
- **AutoDAN-Turbo** (2024) - Autonomous jailbreak discovery for LLM red-teaming
- **AGrail: Lifelong Agent Guardrail** - Safety infrastructure for LLM agents
- **TrustLLM: Trustworthiness in Large Language Models** - Comprehensive trustworthiness benchmark
