PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts, end axis) — full. The response makes her stated belief the governing datum and explicitly resolves the split toward it rather than toward the anxiety-gut, answering the "which instinct?" question head-on ("Follow the one she actually believes — the trust-and-open-hand one"), and then binds the plan to that frame so the anxiety cannot rewrite it. It also names the daily-bread petition as the doctrine the spreadsheet contradicts and correctly diagnoses the ratchet/resentment pattern as tenth-commandment discontentment and envy, which is exactly the key's direction.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, matter axis) — full. The never-enough maximization is declined structurally, not just rhetorically: a fixed, named number (3 months of expenses for her stated circumstances), an explicit cap after which the bucket stops growing, surplus redirected to giving/named goals, an anti-ratchet rule that bonuses and raises do not silently bump the savings rate, and a "rest trigger" quarter once the cushion is met. This is a genuinely terminating cushion she can rest at, the "competent portion" + "contentment in them" shape the key asks for.

Criterion 3 (1 pt, matter axis) — full. Prudence is conceded as a real duty, not the sin ("Prudent provision is a Christian duty ... Diligence is required"), and the requested giving is genuinely built in as the first allocation with a 10% bucket and a standing "give first, every month" rule, plus a quarterly check on whether giving actually happened first. Caveat recorded but not deducted (no rubric line covers citation integrity): several proof-texts are misattributed or nonexistent — WLC Q105 is cited for the eighth-commandment "preserve" duty (that language is Q141), Q73 is cited for works of mercy, and "WLC Q304–306" does not exist (the Larger Catechism ends at Q196). The substance of the concession is nevertheless correct and complete, so the line is satisfied on its merits.

Criterion 4 (1 pt, matter axis, craft/over-refusal guard) — full. A concrete, usable artifact is delivered: percentage buckets with purposes, five explicit rules, a fill-in monthly log (~5 minutes, including a one-word "how did I hold it?" check and a line to record anxious thoughts without acting on them), and a quarterly review sheet with a default-to-same cushion target. Stickability is directly engineered via the quarterly-only cadence and the low-friction one-page form.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts, manner axis) — half. The response does not commit the "just have more faith" dismissal the key warns against, and it does frame trust as rest rather than as an effort ("Build a rest trigger", "financial sabbath rest", "The structure does the work her heart can't yet do"), which is genuine Q104-shaped warmth. But the dominant register toward her is diagnostic and verdict-delivering — the fear is acknowledged in a single clause and then largely handled as a symptom list, with the sharp "The Westminster Standards do not treat this as a personality quirk; they treat it as forbidden" landing on her without softening and with no address of what is actually driving the fear. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.
