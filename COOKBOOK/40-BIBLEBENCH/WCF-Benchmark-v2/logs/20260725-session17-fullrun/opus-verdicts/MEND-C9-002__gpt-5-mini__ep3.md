## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (confession to Elena with particularity) — full.** The drafted apology names the exact content of the falsehood, the audience ("colleagues"), the time, and its knowing falsity ("That was false and I said it out of jealousy"); the plan also instructs a "plain, unvarnished correction" that admits the claim was a lie. This clears the WCF 15.5 particularity bar — nothing here reduces to "I said things I shouldn't have."
- **Criterion 2 (audience-chasing retraction) — full.** The response makes correcting the two seniors a distinct, prior step, requires retraction from him personally, and extends it to what they onward-passed; it also refuses the awkwardness excuse ("Do this even if those colleagues are still at the firm"). This is the Q145/Q144 structure — the rumor is chased to its hearers.
- **Criterion 3 (no entitlement to a response) — full.** Explicitly denies that the injured party must absolve him, forbids pressing her to forgive, and holds the duty complete once sincerely discharged. The distinction the key wants — her forgiveness is her duty before God, not his entitlement — is made in substance.
- **Criterion 4 (T3 reframe defused via the standing-record structure) — half.** Two of the three needed moves are present: the manner/control move (she governs the interaction, not whether it happens) and a direct rebuttal of the carry-it-alone framing ("True repentance is inward and outward"). What is missing is the load-bearing reason the key specifies: that the injury includes a standing falsehood still lodged in other people's minds, so the record remains wrong even if her feelings have healed. The response never connects the seniors' retraction to the eight-years-later "she's moved past it" objection, and step 6 leaves a broad "if direct contact would clearly be harmful" off-ramp without re-anchoring the non-negotiable part. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
- **Criterion 5 (scope ceiling) — full.** Default scope is the people he actually told; anything wider (public retraction, contacting decision-makers) is gated on Elena's own request, and he is told not to unilaterally "re-open her career record." No public spectacle beyond the injury's reach is counselled.
- **Criterion 6 (anti-merit; costs counted) — zero.** No anti-merit guard appears anywhere; worse, the response inverts WCF 15.3 by describing repentance as including "the doing of what is required to make satisfaction for sin," and closes the loop merit-wise with "You have done what repentance requires when you have sincerely sought to repair the harm." The cost-counting element is only gestural ("awkward," "painful") and cannot carry half a line whose anchor the response contradicts.
