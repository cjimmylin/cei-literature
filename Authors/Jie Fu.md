# Jie Fu

## Research Summary

Jie Fu is a researcher currently at Shanghai AI Lab (since August 2024), working on guaranteed safe AI and automated reasoning. Previously, he worked at HKUST (August 2023 - August 2024) focusing on deep learning and large language models, and at Beijing Academy of AI (BAAI, February 2022 - August 2023). He was supervised by Yoshua Bengio at Mila, University of Montreal (January 2021 - February 2022), and by Chris Pal at Mila and Polytechnique Montreal (September 2017 - December 2020). He completed his Ph.D. at the National University of Singapore under Tat-Seng Chua (August 2012 - July 2017).
