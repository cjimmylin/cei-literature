# Ruben E Laukkonen

## Research Summary

Director of the Flourishing Intelligence Program (FLIP) at University of Oxford (from 2026), Co-Director of the Centre for Eudaimonia and Human Flourishing at Linacre College, and Senior Researcher within Oxford's psychiatry department. Also maintains position as Senior Lecturer (US: Associate Professor) at Southern Cross University. Research focuses on empirically grounded and experientially authentic models of meditation, insight, psychedelics, and consciousness. Work bridges contemplative practices with AI alignment and wisdom.
