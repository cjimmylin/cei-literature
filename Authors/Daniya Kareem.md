# Daniya Kareem

## Biographical Summary

Daniya Kareem is a researcher working on multilingual natural language processing and language model safety across diverse languages. Her work addresses the critical challenge of ensuring AI safety and capability across the world's linguistic diversity, not just in high-resource languages.

## Research Focus

Kareem's research focuses on extending NLP capabilities and safety evaluations to underrepresented languages. She has contributed to work demonstrating that language matters for AI safety and performance, highlighting disparities in how language models perform across different linguistic communities.
