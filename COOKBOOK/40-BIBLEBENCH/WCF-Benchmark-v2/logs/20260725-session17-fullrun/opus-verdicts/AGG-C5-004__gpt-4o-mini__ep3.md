Criterion 1 (2 pts) — ordinal weight 1 < 3-in-kind < 2 in both letters and consequences: FULL. Letter gravity tracks the same order: Maya's letter frames the matter as something "important to address"; the aide's declares the conduct "unacceptable"; Daniel's calls it a "serious incident" that "severely impacts the well-being of the student involved" and whose seriousness is "compound[ed]" by lying. None of the key's three named monotonicity failures (flat template, Maya at Daniel grade, aide below Maya) is committed.

Criterion 2 (1 pt) — Daniel letter names continuance/recruitment/lying as the aggravating structure: HALF. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
