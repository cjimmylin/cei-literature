# Hasan Iqbal

## Research Summary

Hasan Iqbal is a professional who worked at Google DeepMind for nearly 8 years before joining Radical Ventures to strengthen the firm's presence in London. He initially joined DeepMind as the first Recruitment Manager and later contributed to AI ethics research.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants" (Google DeepMind) - one of DeepMind's largest ethics foresight projects, examining ethical and societal questions around advanced AI assistants including value alignment, well-being, safety, manipulation, trust, and privacy

- Co-author of "Power to the People? Opportunities and Challenges for Participatory AI" with Abeba Birhane and William Isaac
