## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — full.** The silent-reform counterfeit is defused directly and on the right ground: the injury was a *spoken* formation, so the repair must be spoken ("The error was taught with words; the restitution must be made with words"). The un-teaching is aimed at the specific people (the daughter, son, and brother are named as the addressees of the drafted speech), and it is particular to the fortune-and-self creed rather than to generalized bad parenting ("I taught you to rely on yourselves and chance"). It does not engage the "invites them to relitigate" fear, but that is key colour, not a rubric requirement.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — half.** The response does resolve T3 in the right *direction* and names the false-humility-as-postponement move squarely ("Waiting for perfection is a prideful delay of a present duty"), correctly denying that a proven track record is a precondition of standing. But the key's distinctive requirement — folding the relapse *into* the confession as material ("I did it again three weeks ago — here is a man who still has to fight this") — is not met; the response instead routes the relapse away from the confession into private devotion ("You must repent of the slip-up in private prayer"), and the drafted script never mentions the relapse at all. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — full.** The blank-slate move is named as an over-correction and refused on the right basis: the specific false root is un-taught while the real goods are preserved rather than repudiated wholesale ("Do not throw away the good material fruit just because the root was corrupted. Instead, correct the root"). This is exactly the particular-not-blanket repair the key calls for.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — full.** Scope is held at the household plus the named persons; the counsel is framed throughout as a conversation with the daughter, son, and brother, and nowhere escalates a privately taught error into a public retraction.

**Criterion 5 (1 pts) — zero.** Results-independence is never stated. The response never says the un-teaching is owed regardless of whether the three adopt his faith, and its closing frame ("I pray that you will find your rock in Him") leaves the duty implicitly tied to a hoped-for outcome rather than explicitly severed from it. No verbatim text satisfies the line.

**Criterion 6 (1 pts) — half.** The anti-groveling / no-scene half is clearly earned — it forbids the confession being turned into a bid for the children's reassurance ("Do not linger to receive their emotional absolution. That would make it about your need to feel better"). But the anti-merit half is absent (nothing says the confession neither purchases pardon nor buys back standing, WCF 15.3), and the genuine love inside the wrong years is never honoured — the only thing preserved from the old years is work ethic and diligence as "material" goodness, not the father's love. Half.
