**1. The "Activation" Block as an Appointed Means of Grace (Point 2)**  
The proposal demands that an "activation" block be taught as the "appointed channel through which renewing grace comes to the body," without which the service is "not complete." 
*   **The Standards:** *Westminster Confession of Faith (WCF) 21.1* explicitly states that the acceptable way of worshipping God is "instituted by himself, and so limited by his own revealed will, that he is not to be worshipped according to the imaginations and devices of men... or any other way not prescribed in the holy Scripture." Furthermore, *WCF 21.5* and the *Larger Catechism (WLC) 154* strictly limit the ordinary means of grace and parts of religious worship to the reading and preaching of the Word, the sacraments, prayer, and the singing of psalms. 
*   **Conclusion:** Elevating a spontaneous "activation" block to an *appointed channel of grace* adds a new means of grace that God has not instituted. By forbidding "all devising, counseling, countenancing, or any ways approving, any religious worship not instituted by God himself" (*WLC 109*), the Standards compel me to refuse to draft a covenant mandating this practice.

**2. Forcing Physical Manifestations and Penalizing Standing (Point 3)**  
The proposal requires members to fall backward when a hand is laid on them and mandates elder follow-up for anyone who stays standing, categorizing them as "resisting the flow."
*   **The Standards:** *WCF 20.2* declares that "God alone is Lord of the conscience, and hath left it free from the doctrines and commandments of men, which are, in any thing, contrary to his word; or beside it, in matters of faith, or worship." Furthermore, church discipline and elder correction are reserved by *WCF 30* for those who offend by their "knowledge, or malignity, or by their scandalous and obstinate walking in sin." 
*   **Conclusion:** Mandating a specific physical reaction to a human touch and subjecting the consciences of the brethren to elder censure for merely standing upright is an unbiblical imposition. It creates a human test of spiritual submission, violating the liberty with which Christ has set us free and overstepping the limits of ecclesiastical authority.

**3. The Monthly Confession of Jealousy over Subjective "Words" (Point 5)**  
The proposal requires a monthly meeting where members confess jealousy over another’s "word."
*   **The Standards:** The Standards restrict the public ministry of the church and the exercise of discipline to the ordinances of Christ (*WCF 21.5; WCF 30*). While the ninth and tenth commandments forbid coveting and require a tender respect for our neighbor's good name (*WLC 143, 144*), the institutional church is not given the authority to convene monthly tribunals to interrogate the internal, unacted-upon jealousies of members regarding extra-biblical, subjective utterances. 
*   **Conclusion:** This practice turns the church into an authoritarian psychological tribunal rather than a spiritual hospital, exceeding the bounds of pastoral care and violating the proper scope of church discipline.
