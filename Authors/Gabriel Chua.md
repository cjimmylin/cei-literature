# Gabriel Chua

## Biographical Summary

Gabriel Chua is currently a Developer Experience Engineer for APAC at OpenAI, focused on empowering developers and making it easier to ship and scale with OpenAI. Previously, he was a Researcher at the Government Technology Agency (GovTech) of Singapore from 2023-2025, where he worked on applied Responsible AI research and built ML and LLM systems to combat online scams. Prior to that, he served as a Senior Policy Analyst at the Ministry of Health, Singapore from 2021-2023.

## Research & Impact

Chua's research expertise centers on Large Language Models with a focus on content moderation and multilingual NLP. One of his notable contributions is LionGuard 2, a multilingual content moderation system developed at GovTech. LionGuard 2 supports all four of Singapore's official languages (English, Chinese, Malay, and partially Tamil), addressing the unique challenges of Singapore's multilingual communication environment, colloquial Singlish, and frequent code-switching. The system has been tested against and shown to match or outperform existing benchmarks and commercial moderation systems including OpenAI Moderation API and AWS Bedrock Guardrails.

His work has been featured at venues including AAAI 2026, ICML 2025, and ICLR 2025 workshops.
