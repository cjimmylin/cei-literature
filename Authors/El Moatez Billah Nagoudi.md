# El Moatez Billah Nagoudi

## Biographical Summary

El Moatez Billah Nagoudi is a researcher at MBZUAI specializing in Arabic NLP and multilingual language models. His research focuses on developing and evaluating language models for Arabic and other low-resource languages, including work on machine translation, cross-lingual transfer, and building evaluation benchmarks for Arabic language understanding.

## Research Interests

- Arabic natural language processing
- Multilingual and cross-lingual language models
- Low-resource language processing
- Machine translation
- Language model evaluation
