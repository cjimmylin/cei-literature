# Avner Seror

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Avner Seror is an Assistant Professor of Economics at the Aix-Marseille School of Economics (AMSE). He earned his Ph.D. from Aix-Marseille University. His research is highly interdisciplinary, bridging economics, psychology, and political science to understand the formation of social norms and the evolution of human values.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Avner’s research focuses on the interactions between economic institutions and psychological mechanisms. He investigates how cognitive biases influence political preferences and the transmission of values within society. Recently, he has explored the intersection of AI and psychology, specifically how large language models (LLMs) reflect and influence human moral reasoning.

### Significant Achievements
He has published in top-tier journals such as the *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization* and the *European Economic Review*. He is a member of the World Wellbeing Panel and a recognized expert in the use of formal models to explore complex social phenomena, including religious identity and the impact of technology on culture.
