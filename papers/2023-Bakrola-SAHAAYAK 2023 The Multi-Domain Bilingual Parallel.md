## Abstract

> Largest public-domain Sanskrit dataset (1.5M sentence pairs) for Sanskrit-Hindi machine translation. Includes Bhagavad Gita shlokas and texts from multiple domains. Most extensive dataset with Sanskrit as one of its languages.

## Dataset Information

1.5 million parallel Sanskrit-Hindi sentence pairs


## Data Description

SAHAAYAK 2023: a 1.5 million sentence-pair bilingual parallel corpus for Sanskrit-Hindi machine translation spanning multiple domains including news, politics, history, sport, and ancient Indian literature, the largest public-domain Sanskrit parallel corpus.
