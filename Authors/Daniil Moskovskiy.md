# Daniil Moskovskiy

## Biographical Summary

Daniil Moskovskiy is a researcher at SberDevices (part of Sberbank AI) in Russia, working on natural language processing and the development of language technologies for Russian. He has contributed to creating comprehensive evaluation benchmarks for Russian language models.

## Research Focus

Moskovskiy's research focuses on developing NLP resources and evaluation methodologies for Russian. His work on MERA (a comprehensive LLM evaluation benchmark for Russian) addresses the critical need for rigorous evaluation standards in non-English languages, ensuring that language model capabilities can be properly assessed across different linguistic contexts.
