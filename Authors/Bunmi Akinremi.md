# Bunmi Akinremi

## Biographical Summary

Bunmi Akinremi is a Machine Learning Engineer at Kochava and a passionate advocate for diversity in the AI community. She holds a BSc in Computer Science from Obafemi Awolowo University and is a MicroMasters candidate at MIT. She leads the Women in Data Science community and is committed to using AI for social good.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "Inclusive AI"—specifically building high-quality, culturally rich datasets for African languages. Her work on the "NaijaVoices" project aims to address the massive data gap in speech technology for the Global South, ensuring that voice-enabled AI is accessible to diverse populations.

### Significant Achievements
Akinremi is a co-author of the "NaijaVoices" dataset, one of the largest and most diverse speech datasets for Nigerian English. Her work combines technical engineering (building robust ML pipelines) with policy advocacy, focusing on how AI governance can better represent the needs of African societies.
