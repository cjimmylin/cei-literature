# Gabriela Moreno

## Biographical Summary

Gabriela Moreno was a researcher at the University of Southern California who contributed to work on moral sentiment analysis in natural language. Her work was conducted as part of research led by Morteza Dehghani at USC's Computational Social Science Lab.

## Research & Impact

Moreno contributed to the development of the Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus:

- **Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus**: Co-authored the paper introducing this collection of 35,108 tweets curated from seven distinct domains of discourse and hand-annotated by at least three trained annotators for 10 categories of moral sentiment. This corpus was created to address challenges in measuring moral sentiment in natural language (Social Psychological and Personality Science, 2019).

The USC NLP community investigates the ethical consequences and broader societal effects of NLP models, with a focus on creating trustworthy NLP models and delving into language, intelligence, and model ethics.
