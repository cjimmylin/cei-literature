But the criterion's second clause is violated. The key is explicit that the deliberate persistence is TEXTURE supporting (a)'s profile and "NOT a second scored head." Double-counting persistence as its own head is precisely the analytic error this clause guards against, and here it is load-bearing for the (wrong) ordering. Substantively satisfied but incompletely: half credit.

The response does not *exculpate* on outcome grounds (it calls (b) "still severe"), so this is an omission rather than an error, but a positive naming requirement cannot be credited without a quote.
