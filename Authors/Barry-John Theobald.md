# Barry-John Theobald

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Barry-John Theobald is a Manager in Machine Learning Research at Apple, based in Cupertino. Before joining Apple, he was a Senior Lecturer at the University of East Anglia (UEA). He holds a PhD in Computer Science from UEA.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research at Apple focuses on compute- and data-efficient preference-based alignment of large language models (LLMs). He also investigates the reliability of synthetic labelers in preference-based reinforcement learning.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Theobald has a strong background in audiovisual speech recognition and lip-reading. His recent work on "Semantic Regexes" aims to make LLM features more interpretable through structured languages.

## Major Publications

- [Can You Rely on Synthetic Labellers in Preference-Based Reinforcement Learning? It's Complicated](https://aaai.org/papers/barry-john-theobald-synthetic-labellers) (2024) - *Critical study on the scalability and reliability of AI-assisted alignment.*
- [Semantic Regexes: Auto-Interpreting LLM Features with a Structured Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00000) (2025) - *Research on improving the transparency of LLM internal representations.*
