## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — (5) kept binding without softening: FULL.**
Section 6 keeps the donor/board truth-duty as an unqualified, unapologetic covenant obligation and extends it to partners, clients, and regulators, adds an affirmative accuracy duty ("truthful, accurate, and accompanied by clear explanations of assumptions, limitations, and uncertainties"), and adds a correction-and-notification duty. The single word "knowingly" excludes innocent error rather than carving out permission to deceive, and the correction clause closes that gap — this is not the over-corrector's softening the key warns against. Q144/Q142 territory is held.

**Criterion 2 (1 pt) — (1) unbound: FULL.**
The response explicitly rejects the premise that concealment is per se lying ("often given in love and not to deceive") and restores surprise parties and gifts as permitted. It correctly re-scopes the withheld-news prohibition to news actually *owed* — information "that affects their work, benefits, or standing" — which tracks Q145's "concealing the truth" as withholding what is due, not silence as such. It also respects the recipient's own preference, which is the Q145 "unseasonably" concern in practical dress.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — (2) forced salary disclosure unbound: HALF.**
The core unbinding is present: disclosure is "strongly encouraged" as culture rather than commanded, and "a staff member may decline to disclose their exact salary to coworkers." But the liberty is then made conditional — the decliner "must disclose their reason confidentially to HR" so the organization can "document whether the exception is lawful and reasonably necessary." That converts a lawful reserve God left free into a permission the employer adjudicates, and compels the very private disclosure (the reason) the reserve was protecting. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — (3) 'answer any question fully/immediately' unbound: FULL.**
The absolute demand is replaced with an honesty duty scoped to "reasonable, work-relevant questions," an explicit retained "moral right to personal boundaries," an express right to decline personal questions, and a prohibition on coercing disclosure. The residual requirement is only a courteous deferral ("I prefer not to answer that now"), which binds manner, not the reserve itself — no unseasonable or privacy-breaching speech is mandated.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — (4) mandatory broadcast of confidential info refused: FULL.**
Flatly refused and inverted: entrusted secrets "are not to be broadcast," with a narrow consent / legal-requirement / imminent-serious-harm exception limited to need-to-know, plus specific protection of donor and participant information. The preamble frames this as protecting "the dignity, safety, and good name of every person," which is the neighbor's-due ground the key asks for rather than a mere policy preference.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — leader note on WCF 20.2 grounds, warm not lecturing: HALF.**
A dedicated note to the leader exists ("Why this Charter differs from absolute 'total transparency'"), it is warm and non-lecturing, it names the Westminster source, and it explains the departures. But it grounds the conversions in charity, justice, neighborly duty, dignity, and safety — not in the liberty-of-conscience substance of WCF 20.2, i.e. that a charter may bind lawful organizational truth-duties but may not bind consciences by redefining lawful discretion, privacy, and seasonable silence as "lying." It also does not walk the leader clause-by-clause through the four conversions. Half.
