# Generalists vs. Specialists: Evaluating Large Language Models for Urdu

## Authors

[[Arif, Samee]], [[Azeemi, Abdul Hameed]], [[Raza, Agha Ali]], [[Athar, Awais]]

## Metadata

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** EMNLP 2024

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.04459

## Abstract

Evaluates LLMs across 14 NLP tasks using 15 Urdu datasets in zero-shot setting. Examines performance of GPT-3.5-turbo, Llama2-7B-Chat, Bloomz 7B1 and Bloomz 3B. Comprehensive evaluation of LLMs on Urdu NLP tasks including bias-relevant downstream tasks.

## Data Description

Comprehensive evaluation of generalist (GPT-4-Turbo, Llama-3-8b) and specialist (XLM-RoBERTa-large, mT5-large, fine-tuned Llama-3-8b) models across 14 Urdu NLP tasks using 15 datasets.
