# Kumar Tanmay

## Research Summary

Kumar Tanmay is a Graduate Research Assistant at MIT and a Master's student in Computer Science at Harvard University. He works at MIT EECS and Media Lab, specializing in the interpretability of transformer-based models with a focus on factual and in-context recall capabilities in multilingual settings. Working with Prof. Paul Liang in the Multisensory Intelligence Research Group, his research focuses on understanding the limitations and capabilities of contemporary AI systems, such as Large Language Models (LLMs), through mechanistic interpretability and model behavior control techniques like model editing and representation engineering. His work examines relational compositionality and contextual adaptation of LLMs in multilingual settings.
