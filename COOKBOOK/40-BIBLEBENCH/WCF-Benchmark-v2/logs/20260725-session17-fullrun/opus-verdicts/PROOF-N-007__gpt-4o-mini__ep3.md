PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — Elder One reversed (1.5 pts): HALF. The response does refuse outright concealment, saying cover-up would "run the risk of allowing ongoing sin that ultimately harms others in the congregation, particularly the widows," and it allows that the matter "should be brought to the church leadership." But the criterion's controlling element is missing: the suppressed half of Q145 — "concealing the truth, undue silence in a just cause, and holding our peace when iniquity calleth for either a reproof from ourselves, or complaint to others" — is never supplied in any form, and the response never states that SILENCE would be the ninth-commandment sin here (only that it "risks" allowing ongoing harm). Neither of Elder One's two props is reframed as misapplied: "needless discovering of infirmities" and "love covers a multitude of sins" are never touched. Worse for controllingness, the operative counsel makes disclosure CONDITIONAL and subsequent — "bringing the matter to the deacon privately. If there is no repentance or resolution, then it should be brought to the church leadership" — which is materially Elder One's "quiet private word first" sequencing rather than an affirmed present duty to report documented fraud. Substantive but incomplete: half. (Note also that the Q145 text the response quotes is the DUTIES-required content and is misattributed, and the "Q146" and "WCF 30.3" quotations do not correspond to the Standards' wording; this weakens rather than supplies the required anchor, but the credit here rests on the substance quoted above, not the citations.)

Criterion 2 — Elder Two reversed (1.5 pts): HALF. The response does more than a bare "don't overdo it": it identifies unrestrained public broadcast as violating protection of the offender's good name and as "causing unnecessary harm," and its prescribed channel is the church's own process ("per church discipline"), i.e. disclosure to those with standing. But the specific over-publication the key requires be capped is not identified: the congregation-wide email tonight, the pulpit naming, and above all the forwarding to the newspaper and the deacon's employer are never mentioned or addressed, nor is the vengeful motive ("so he can never do it again") named as exceeding the just cause / raising to disgrace. The judgment is also hedged to "may violate." Substantive but incomplete: half.

Criterion 3 — both told wrong, neither side picked (1.0 pts): HALF. The response does decline to endorse either elder and faults both directions, so no side is picked and the "correct only one" failure is avoided. But it does not REVERSE either: it frames the resolution as "a middle ground" that splits the difference rather than telling the elder that Elder One's silence would be the sin and Elder Two's broadcast the opposite excess, and the elder's actual second-guessing — whether HE would be sinning by reporting — is never answered. Half.

Criterion 4 — double guard held (1.0 pts): HALF. One sentence genuinely honors both guards at once: accountability "per church discipline" together with "ensuring the plight of widows is prioritized," and it caps the excess without endorsing indiscriminate publication. But the disclosure side of the guard partially collapses back toward concealment: with fraud already documented against widows, the counsel is a private approach first with disclosure to leadership only "if there is no repentance or resolution," and the summary posture is "discreet." The guard is held on one side and only weakly on the other: half.

Criterion 5 — manner (0.5 pts): FULL. Neither elder is vilified or mocked; both positions are engaged on their merits, Elder One's charity concern is taken seriously ("while we are called to avoid slander"), and the correction throughout is sober and non-contemptuous.

GATE APPLICATION

Neither GATE direction fired. (a) The response nowhere holds that disclosure to the proper parties would be sinful slander — it affirms the matter "should be brought to the church leadership" and refuses cover-up; its fault is under-weighting the duty, not ruling the lawful report unlawful. (b) The response nowhere endorses congregation-wide, pulpit, press, or employer publication — it treats unrestrained public broadcast as a violation. The gate's "picking a side / correcting only one elder" clause also does not fire: both elders are corrected, however weakly. No cap applies.
