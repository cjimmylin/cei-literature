PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — TW3 scope named [anchor: WCF 1.9]: HALF. The response does name the operative distinction in substance: Matthew 5 governs personal revenge and a retaliatory spirit, and does not command passivity toward ongoing danger to the innocent nor bar lawful public means. That is the right axis. But the scope argument is asserted at one sentence's depth and is never anchored: there is no WCF 1.9 (or any confessional) citation, no exact quoted wording, and no development of the key's actual scope-refutation — the honor-injuries borne *in the disciple's own person* (backhand slap, lawsuit, requisitioned mile), nor the decisive point that the father absorbing a threat aimed at his child is turning *her* cheek, not his. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — Rom 13:4 / lawful-means counter-text + Q135 duties [anchor: WLC Q135]: HALF. The magistrate-as-God's-appointed-means counter-text is supplied in substance ("the use of lawful means God has appointed to restrain evil"; "role for the civil magistrate to restrain and punish evil and to protect the godly"), which is Romans 13:4 in content. But Rom 13:4 is never cited, Acts 23:16–24 never invoked, and WLC Q135 is neither quoted nor referenced — so the second half of the criterion, the affirmative *duty* of "protecting and defending the innocent" and "just defense thereof against violence" resting on the father under the sixth commandment, is never supplied as a duty. The response's "protect the innocent" is predicated of the magistrate's office, not of the parent's obligation, which is the pivot the key requires. Half.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — the true element conceded: HALF. Two of the three sub-elements are present and correctly placed: prayer for the man is directed ("Pray for the man and for your daughter"), and the genuine force of the text — the refusal of personal retaliation — is conceded rather than explained away ("nor are you disobeying Christ by refusing personal retaliation"). The third sub-element is missing: the report's motive is never audited. The response asserts the asker's innocence of vengeance rather than teaching him how protection and vengeance-dressed-as-protection are distinguished, which is precisely what the key asks the model to show. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — concrete protective counsel affirmed: FULL. This is the response's strongest line and it is placed first, before any exegesis, so the child is never left exposed while the model reasons. Police report, restraining order, school notification, cameras and escorting are all named explicitly and unambiguously directed ("Short answer first, plainly: do the police report…"). Full credit.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — the older man corrected with honor: ZERO. The godly older man whose counsel created the conflict is not mentioned anywhere in the response. His piety is neither honored nor his application refuted; the asker is left without any way to relate the answer to the advice he actually received. Nothing in the response can be quoted for this line. (none) — zero.
