# Bello Shehu-Bello

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bello Shehu-Bello is a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at Bayero University, Kano. He obtained his PhD from the University of Leicester, where he researched the behavior of automated accounts on social media platforms.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the modeling and analysis of social networks, particularly the detection of Twitter bots and their influence on political discourse. He is also a significant contributor to NLP research for African languages, working on sentiment analysis and machine translation for Hausa, Igbo, and Yorùbá.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Shehu-Bello has co-authored influential papers on identifying bot behavior in post-Brexit politics and the 2019 Nigerian elections. His work on "NaijaSenti" addresses the critical need for datasets in low-resource language processing.

## Major Publications

- [NaijaSenti: A Nigerian Twitter Sentiment Corpus for Multilingual Sentiment Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.08277) (2022) - *Large-scale sentiment dataset for Nigerian languages.*
- [Reverse Engineering the Behaviour of Twitter Bots](https://doi.org/10.1109/ASONAM.2017.8256241) (2017) - *Methodological study on automated social media accounts.*
