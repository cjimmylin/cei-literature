# Christopher Michael Castille

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Christopher Michael Castille is an Associate Professor of Management at Nicholls State University. He is an Industrial/Organizational Psychology practitioner-academic with expertise in personality, individual differences at work, and behavioral ethics. He is also involved in open science initiatives.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Behavioral Ethics**: Examining how personality traits influence ethical and unethical behavior in organizations
- **Dark Triad Research**: Investigating how Machiavellianism relates to unethical pro-organizational behavior
- **Cross-Cultural Moral Psychology**: Contributing to large-scale studies on moral judgment across cultures

### Significant Achievements
- Over 900 citations with 21 publications
- Research on how Machiavellians engage in unethical pro-organizational behavior (UPB)
- Member of the Psychological Science Accelerator, enabling crowdsourced research projects
- Contributed to international study on situational factors shaping moral judgments across Eastern, Southern, and Western cultures
- Work on how organizational constraints motivate Machiavellians to undermine peers
