# Valle Ruiz-Fernández

## Research Summary

Valle Ruiz-Fernández is a Junior Research Engineer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center, specializing in deep learning within the Language Technologies unit. Their research includes work on cognitive biases and task complexity in large language models, with a publication "Cognitive Biases, Task Complexity, and Result Intepretability in Large Language Models" accepted to COLING 2025.
