# Alexis Palmer

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexis Palmer is an Associate Professor of Linguistics at the University of Colorado Boulder. She is a prominent figure in the effort to bring natural language processing to low-resource and endangered languages, ensuring that AI technologies are inclusive of global linguistic diversity.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexis leads research on creating cross-lingual and multilingual datasets for underrepresented languages. She is a co-author of the *AmericasNLI* benchmark, which evaluates the performance of language models on indigenous languages of the Americas, highlighting the critical gap in current AI accessibility for these communities.

## Major Publications
- [AmericasNLI: Evaluating Zero-shot Natural Language Understanding of Indigenous Languages of the Americas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08726) (2021) - *Pioneering benchmark for indigenous language NLI.*
