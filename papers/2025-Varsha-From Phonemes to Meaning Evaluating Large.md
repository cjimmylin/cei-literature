## Abstract

First Tamil-specific linguistic evaluation benchmark with 820 questions from Sri Lankan school-level Tamil exams annotated by trained linguists under 5 linguistic categories and factual knowledge. Shows frequent errors in morphological agreement in LLMs. Tamil-specific linguistic and cultural knowledge benchmark. Tests culturally grounded knowledge beyond translation, revealing LLM limitations in morphologically rich Tamil.
