# Mohna Chakraborty

## Research Summary

Mohna Chakraborty is a Postdoctoral Researcher at University of Michigan. Her research examines real-world usage of GenAI techniques and develops approaches to improve LLMs' skills in handling social situations. Lead author of "Structured Moral Reasoning in Language Models: A Value-Grounded Evaluation Framework" (EMNLP 2025), which benchmarked 12 open-source models across four moral datasets using a taxonomy of prompts grounded in value systems, ethical theories, and cognitive reasoning strategies.
