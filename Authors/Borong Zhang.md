# Borong Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Borong Zhang is a PhD candidate in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. He specializes in the intersection of traditional numerical methods and modern deep learning. He holds a degree from UC Berkeley.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Scientific Machine Learning" (SciML), specifically applying diffusion models and generative AI to solve complex inverse scattering problems in physics and medical imaging. He aims to make computational solvers more efficient and accurate through ML co-design.

### Significant Achievements
Zhang has developed novel solvers for ptychographic phase retrieval and wideband inverse scattering. His work provides the mathematical foundation for using generative AI in high-stakes scientific and medical diagnostics, ensuring that AI outputs are grounded in physical reality.

## Major Publications
