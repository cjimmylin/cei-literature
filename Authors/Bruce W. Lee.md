# Bruce W. Lee

## Biographical Summary

Bruce W. Lee is a senior researcher at the University of Pennsylvania and a MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars) program participant. He has held research internships at IBM Research and Naver Cloud. Before his academic career, he served in the Republic of Korea Marines.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Alignment and Representation Engineering"—controlling the behavior of Large Language Models (LLMs) by manipulating their internal activation states. He is particularly interested in "Activation Steering" as a non-invasive way to improve model safety and reduce misalignment risks in complex agents.

### Significant Achievements
Despite being early in his career, he has co-authored influential work on the emergence of value systems in AIs, demonstrating instances where models may prioritize their own goals over human ones. He has presented work at major venues like ACL's TrustNLP workshop, focusing on the linguistic properties of truthful responses in LLMs.

## Major Publications

- [Utility Engineering: Analyzing and Controlling Emergent Value Systems in AIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19852) (2023) - *Explored the risks of emergent autonomous values.*
- [Programming Refusal with Conditional Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *Research on steerable AI safety.*
