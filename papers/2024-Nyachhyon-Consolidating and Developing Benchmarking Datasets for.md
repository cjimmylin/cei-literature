## Abstract

12 new datasets for Nepali NLU tasks including sentiment, hate speech, toxicity, QA, and NER. Comprehensive benchmark going beyond earlier 4-task Nep-gLUE. Comprehensive Nepali NLU benchmark including hate speech and toxicity detection tasks.

## Benchmark

**Name:** NLUE (Nepali Language Understanding Evaluation)

## Data Description

NLUE benchmark comprising 12 new Nepali NLU datasets covering sentiment analysis, hate speech, toxicity, QA, NER, paraphrase, similarity, NLI, coreference, and commonsense reasoning, evaluated with Nepali BERT variants, multilingual BERT, m-DeBERTa-v3, and GPT-4o mini.
