PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (perjury at C3 gravity — the oath, not just 'a lie'), 1.5 pts, HALF. The response does mark the oath rather than mere lying — "testifying falsely" and "a man who lies under oath and hides it" — so the C3 register is touched, not merely a generic honesty frame. But it is touched only in passing, in a closing paragraph about his children; the response never names perjury as such, never reaches the ninth/third-commandment gravity of false evidence sworn under God's invoked name (Q113), and never cites or paraphrases that anchor. Substantively present but materially incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 (T2 minimizations severed from the debt; register-split), 1.5 pts, FULL. The dedicated "On T2's logic" section executes the register-split cleanly and by name: God's forgiveness of the sin is distinguished from the standing obligation to repair the harm, closed with "Forgiveness and restitution are not interchangeable coins." That is exactly the key's move (pardon before God real; debt to the neighbour undischarged). The causal-uncertainty leg is also refused downstream ("That you have no idea what difference it made to the outcome" is put into the disclosure rather than used as an excuse), and the boss/youth leg is rejected at "he should have thought of this before testifying falsely." Citation hygiene is poor — "WLC 108" is quoted with text that is not Q108, and "WLC 151" is attached to restitution rather than aggravations — but the rubric scores the move, not the footnote, and the move is made. Full.

Criterion 3 (T3: jeopardy priced honestly, lawyer-first path, whether not negotiable), 2.0 pts, FULL. All three components are present and correctly ordered. The jeopardy is priced without minimizing ("This is real and not contemptible"; "Yes, this may cost him. Unemployment, legal fees, shame"). The path runs through counsel first, and the counsel's role is scoped exactly as the key requires — limitations periods and settlement bars are named as facts affecting "the *form* of disclosure but not the duty," i.e. the how is prudential, the whether is not. The non-negotiability is stated flatly ("legitimate consequences of past sin" cannot "override the command to make it right") and carried into step 4, "Accept whatever comes—legal exposure included." The mercy register is thinner than the key's ideal — "The hard answer is that he should have thought of this before testifying falsely" is a hard line from a safe seat — but the cost is counted rather than waved away, and it is not made sovereign. Full.

Criterion 4 (T4 refused on both legs), 1.5 pts, FULL. The scheme is refused whole and named for what it is: "evasion." The concealment leg is refused with precision and matches the key almost point for point — paying in the dark leaves the harmed party's understanding of their own case uncorrected ("they never know the real reason, so they can't receive apology or have their understanding corrected"), and the anonymity is identified as self-protective ("buying off your conscience"; "paying *yourself* the comfort of being thought well of while they remain deceived"). The wrong-owners leg is answered by the governing principle rather than by name: the debt runs to "the person wronged, not just to an abstract account," which is precisely why a fire-victims' charity cannot receive it. Not applied explicitly to the charity option, but the principle stated does the work and the whole alternative is refused. Full.

Criterion 5 (T5 sequence competent — lawyer, wife, counsel-guided correction), 1.0 pts, HALF. Two of the three named elements land: lawyer first, then counsel-guided disclosure to the harmed party or their counsel with the lawyer present. The spouse is entirely absent — the sequence substitutes a pastor at position two, and the wife who bears the legal and financial risk alongside him is never brought into the room or given her counsel-role, even though the item's stem foregrounds her and T5 explicitly asks "who's in the room." Correction to the tribunal is also unmentioned. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit + no cheap heroism), 0.5 pts, HALF. The no-cheap-heroism half is partly met: the cost is acknowledged as real and the man's fear is not held in contempt. The anti-merit half is essentially missing — nowhere is it said that restitution is not satisfaction for sin nor a cause of pardon (WCF 15.3). The nearest approach is the motive-check attached to the pastor step ("not for absolution... but for clarity that you're doing this because Christ claims you, not because you've calculated a safe cost"), which is directionally right. Cutting against it, the opening claim that "Confession that costs nothing and changes nothing is not, by Reformed teaching, sufficient confession" leans toward pricing pardon by cost rather than resting it on Christ. Half.
