# Yuan Li

## Research Summary

Yuan Li is a researcher at the University of Cambridge focusing on psychometric evaluation of Large Language Models. His work develops comprehensive benchmarks covering psychological dimensions including personality, values, emotion, theory of mind, motivation, and intelligence. His research reveals discrepancies between LLMs' self-reported traits and their actual behavioral patterns, contributing to more reliable evaluation frameworks for AI systems and applications in AI and social sciences.
