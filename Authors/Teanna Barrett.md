# Teanna Barrett

## Research Summary

Teanna Barrett is a researcher at the University of Washington working on AI fairness and bias in computer vision systems. Her research investigates how subjective factors affect the annotation and evaluation of AI systems, particularly examining skin tone annotation as a sociotechnical project. Her work challenges the assumption that skin tone can serve as an "objective" metric for fairness evaluation, demonstrating significant variability in how annotators perceive and categorize skin tone.
