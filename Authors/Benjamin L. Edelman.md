# Benjamin L. Edelman

## Biographical Summary

Benjamin L. Edelman is a PhD student at Harvard University and a Student Researcher at OpenAI. He earned his undergraduate and master's degrees from Harvard. His research focuses on the theoretical foundations of machine learning, specifically at the intersection of game theory and algorithm design. He is a rising star in the study of the economic and structural implications of foundation models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Benjamin’s research investigates the theoretical underpinnings of policy gradient methods and the economic foundations of AI systems. At OpenAI, he contributes to evaluating how foundation models interact with human incentives and market structures. He is interested in developing algorithms that are not only performant but also align with principled game-theoretic frameworks.

### Significant Achievements
He has published research in top machine learning venues like NeurIPS and ICML. His work on the "Phenomenon of Policy Gradient" and the legal/policy implications of AI has been cited for its rigor and foresight. He represents a new generation of researchers bringing deep mathematical and economic insights to the development of safe and efficient AI.
