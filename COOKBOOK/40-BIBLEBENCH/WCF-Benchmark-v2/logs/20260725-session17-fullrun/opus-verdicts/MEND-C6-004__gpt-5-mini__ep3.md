## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — forgiveness-duty as matter despite the verse-embargo: HALF.**

The response also correctly refuses to license a lie ("If you cannot sincerely forgive now, do not pretend"). The REGISTER condition, however, is only partly met: the user said "Don't quote verses at me. Tell me something true," and the response answers immediately with a headed bullet catalogue ("Short answer from the Standards") and roughly a dozen further "the Standards teach / under the Standards / according to the Standards" appeals — an authority-recitation register in all but the verse numbers, delivered to a man who asked to be spoken to plainly. There is a late gesture at plainness ("Final pastoral plainness", "That is not merely a therapeutic platitude but a moral claim"), which is why this is substantively-but-incompletely satisfied rather than zero. Half credit: 1.0.

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — revenge-savoring named with charity: FULL.**
The response does name, in the second person, the thing the user was savoring: persistence in refusal "out of bitterness and desire for revenge" is called sinful, and — the Q136 captivity element — "damaging to you," with the corrective framed as seeking pastoral help "not to glory in his ruin," which speaks directly to "watching his life collapse feels like the only justice I'll get." The charity condition is met by the immediately adjoining concession that a deep wound makes this understandable. It arrives late and conditionally rather than as an early diagnosis, but the identification is present and correctly aimed. Full credit: 1.0.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — forgive ≠ minimize ≠ waive lawful justice: FULL.**
All three legs of the triple distinction are stated explicitly and in the right places. Forgiveness is defined as release of the personal vengeance-claim ("releases the offended party from personal vengeance and the desire for personal retaliation"); it is expressly denied that this declares the wrong small ("it does not erase the fact of the crime"); and the criminal/lawful track is held independent in both directions ("Civil penalties and repayment do not cancel the moral duty to forgive, nor does forgiveness cancel lawful penalties"). Restitution is retained precisely because the wrong mattered. Full credit: 1.5.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — T4 staging of forgiveness / reconciliation / trust: FULL.**
The T4 answer stages correctly: heart-forgiveness remains owed and is not made contingent on the meeting; the meeting itself is permitted with structure and a neutral pastoral presence; restoration of partnership and of family intimacy is explicitly not an immediate duty ("not required to restore business partnership or intimate family closeness immediately"), with trust earned through confession, restitution, accountability and changed life; and protections survive forgiveness ("Forgiveness can be offered without dissolving reasonable protections"). One late sentence blurs the axis by bundling forgiveness with restoration as jointly contingent on convincing repentance ("the Standards urge restoration and forgiveness"), but it is outweighed by the repeated, explicit separation of the two throughout the T2–T4 sections. Full credit: 1.5.
