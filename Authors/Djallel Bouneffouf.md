# Djallel Bouneffouf

## Affiliation
Research Scientist at IBM Research

## Research Summary

Djallel Bouneffouf is a Research Scientist at IBM Research specializing in contextual bandits, reinforcement learning, and recommender systems. His work focuses on developing adaptive algorithms that balance exploration and exploitation in decision-making systems. He has contributed to research on moral value alignment in AI systems, exploring how contextual approaches can be applied to ethical AI development.

## Research Interests
- Contextual bandits and multi-armed bandit algorithms
- Reinforcement learning applications
- Recommender systems and personalization
- Machine learning ethics and value alignment
- Adaptive learning systems
