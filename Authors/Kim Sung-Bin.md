# Kim Sung-Bin

## Research Summary

Kim Sung-Bin is a Ph.D. student at POSTECH (Pohang University of Science and Technology), working in the fields of multi-modal learning and computer vision. Their research focuses on audio-visual synthesis, 3D facial animation, and video understanding. They have submissions to ICLR 2026 Conference and collaborate with researchers including Tae-Hyun Oh on various projects related to multi-modal AI systems.
