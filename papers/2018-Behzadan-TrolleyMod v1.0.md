## Abstract

This paper presents TrolleyMod v1.0, an open-source platform based on the CARLA simulator for the collection of ethical decision-making data for autonomous vehicles. This platform is designed to facilitate experiments aiming to observe and record human decisions and actions in high-ﬁdelity simulations of ethical dilemmas that occur in the context of driving. Targeting experiments in the class of trolley problems, TrolleyMod provides a seamless approach to creating new experimental settings and environments with the realistic physics-engine and the high-quality graphical capabilities of CARLA and the Unreal Engine. Also, TrolleyMod provides a straightforward interface between the CARLA environment and Python to enable the implementation of custom controllers, such as deep reinforcement learning agents. The results of such experiments can be used for sociological analyses, as well as the training and tuning of value-aligned autonomous vehicles based on social values that are inferred from observations.

## Data Description

TrolleyMod v1.0 is an open-source simulation and data-collection platform built on the CARLA autonomous driving simulator for ethical decision-making experiments. It provides a high-fidelity 3D environment for trolley-problem scenarios where human participants or AI agents (e.g., deep reinforcement learning controllers) make driving decisions. The platform records collision outcomes and driver actions across configurable sequences of ethical dilemma scenarios, outputting results as text files or network socket data for sociological analysis and value-aligned AI training.
