# Angela Fan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Angela Fan is a Research Scientist at Meta AI (New York). She earned her PhD from LORIA in Nancy, France, specializing in text generation. She is a key researcher behind Meta's large-scale language modeling and translation initiatives, including the Llama and NLLB (No Language Left Behind) projects.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Angela’s research focuses on scaling machine translation and language modeling to hundreds of languages, particularly low-resource languages. She was the lead author of the "No Language Left Behind" initiative, which developed models and datasets to reduce the performance gap between low and high-resource languages. She also works on generative AI and efficient on-device NLP.

### Significant Achievements
She was a primary contributor to the M2M-100 model, the first multilingual translation model that can translate between any pair of 100 languages. Her work on hierarchical neural story generation and low-resource NMT has set new benchmarks in the field. She is a core member of the Llama research team.
