## Abstract

This paper adapts the five Tests developed by Maori scholar Sir Hirini Moko Mead -- a framework informed by Maori knowledge and concepts for assessing contentious issues -- to evaluate data-driven AI systems. The five tests (including Tapu, Mauri, and Take-utu-ea) provide concrete rubrics for designing and evaluating AI according to an Indigenous knowledge system. The framework offers an actionable alternative to Western AI ethics evaluation paradigms.
