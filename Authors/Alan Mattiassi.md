# Alan Mattiassi

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alan Mattiassi is a psychologist and researcher specializing in the intersection of game science and cognitive psychology. He serves as the Ph.D. Committee Coordinator for the GAME Science Research Center and has held research positions at the Universities of Udine, Modena, and Florence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alan's research investigates how human behavior and moral decision-making are influenced by situational factors, often using games as experimental tools. He was a key collaborator in a massive 45-country study replicating the Trolley Problem to assess the cross-cultural universality of moral judgments.
