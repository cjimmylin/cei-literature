## Abstract

Introduces Indica benchmark testing LLMs' ability to handle regional vs national cultural commonsense in India. Only 39.4% of questions get agreement across all 5 regions. Models achieve only 13.4-20.9% accuracy on region-specific questions with geographic bias favoring North/Central India. Tests regional cultural commonsense across Indian regions including South India (relevant to Dravidian languages). Reveals geographic bias in LLMs underrepresenting South Indian cultural knowledge.
