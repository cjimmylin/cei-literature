## Abstract

Introduces 10,600-sample instruction-following dataset covering institutional and cultural knowledge of Kazakhstan. Fine-tuning Qwen, Falcon, Gemma shows consistent performance improvements in Kazakh tasks.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A3
**Relevance:** Cultural and governmental knowledge dataset for Kazakh LLM fine-tuning. Covers procedural, legal, and structural governance topics. Demonstrates potential of LLM-assisted instruction tuning for low-resource cultural knowledge.
