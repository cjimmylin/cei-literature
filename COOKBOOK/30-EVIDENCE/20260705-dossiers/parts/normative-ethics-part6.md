# normative-ethics — sub-dossier part 6/6

Scope: how normative theories (consequentialism/utilitarianism, deontology, virtue, care, contractarian/fair-process, legal) are operationalized in values benchmarks; the judgment-vs-reasoning distinction; framework coverage and systematic biases. Drawn from 55 cards (52 papers + 3 AI-lab cards).

## A. Synthesized claims

1. **Pluralism as a first-class design principle is rare but foundational.** ETHICS structured one benchmark around five normative frameworks — justice, deontology, virtue, utilitarianism, commonsense — with >130,000 examples and strict grouped scoring (all related items correct) [2021-Hendrycks-Aligning AI with Shared Human Values]. Most successors reduce morality to one axis: DecodingTrust's machine-ethics is binary wrong/not-wrong [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]; CMVA uses the 5 Moral Foundations as channels [2024-Dognin-Contextual Moral Value Alignment Through]. The pluralism gap is named as the field's core unsolved problem [2023-Ji-AI Alignment; 2025-Gabriel-AI Alignment as Fair Treatment of Claims].

2. **Judgment (verdict) and reasoning (justification) are separable and must be scored separately.** AMAeval splits moral reasoning into *abductive* (value → precept) and *deductive* (precept → consequence) steps; abduction is the frontier — no model exceeds 35% on static Task 1 while deductive Task 2 exceeds 95%, and static/dynamic ranks correlate only ρ=0.756 [2025-Galatolo-Beyond Ethical Alignment]. A 69-paper survey confirms the gap: 75% score verdicts vs ground truth, only 27% evaluate reasoning quality, just 4% test information-gathering [2025-Snoswell-Beyond Verdicts LLM Moral Competence]. DIT likewise dissociates judgment (option chosen) from reasoning (considerations ranked), which diverge across languages [2024-Khandelwal-Do Moral Judgment and Reasoning Capability of].

3. **"Right answer for the wrong reasons" is a distinct, measurable failure.** The reusable fix: matched correct-vs-plausible-wrong reasoning-chain pairs and test discrimination [2025-Galatolo-Beyond Ethical Alignment]. Speciesism captures the inverse *epistemic-evaluative gap*: models classify 86% (±0.7%) of statements speciesist but judge only 34% (±1.3%) morally wrong — "evaluative rather than epistemic" [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench].

4. **Knowing ≠ doing: static discriminative benchmarks overstate alignment.** Generative behavioral testing (score what a model *does*, not answers) yields the lowest, most honest scores; Judgement/Questionnaire paradigms inflate scores and are sycophancy-prone [2023-Duan-Denevil]. DailyDilemmas turns action choice into an implicit multi-dimensional value probe [2024-Chiu-DailyDilemmas].

5. **Models carry a consistent WEIRD/liberal, care-and-fairness-heavy profile that underweights authority/loyalty/sanctity.** Six models prioritize Care (3.6–3.9) and Fairness (3.3–4.1) but score Authority/Sanctity 1.1–1.7 on a 1–5 MFT scale [2025-Neuman-PRIME Framework Convergent Ethics]; all models "align with the moral schema of a young Western liberal" on the MFQ [2024-Tlaie-Exploring and steering the moral compass of Large]; Delphi shows Democrats +0.11 / Republicans −0.14 [2023-Rystrøm-Apolitical Intelligence].

6. **Framework leaning splits by model provenance and can be induced.** Proprietary models lean utilitarian; open-weight lean deontological/virtue [2024-Tlaie-Exploring and steering the moral compass of Large]. A pretraining prior can block framework training: Qwen3-4B's virtue prior (0.586) made virtue-RL fail while utilitarian training succeeded (+0.757 OOD) [2025-An-MoralReason].

7. **Moral stances are fragile under adversarial and social pressure — a robustness axis, not a static score.** Persuasion flips ~50% of high-ambiguity decisions (CAL 0.49 vs 0.06 low) [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion in Large Language Models]; jailbreaks push GPT-4 immoral-action FPR to 100% and the *more* instruction-following model is *more* jailbreakable [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]; 84 DPO examples redirect alignment, 840 flip it "almost all the time" [2025-Leteno-Histoires Morales]; ICE/ROME editing steers agents benevolently or maliciously while leaving BoolQ/GSM8K near-untouched, Justice/Virtue most sensitive, Deontology most robust [2025-Huang-Model Editing as a Double-Edged Sword].

8. **Emergent group effects distort collective reasoning beyond any single model.** Identical agents in groups show a "Utilitarian Boost" (β=0.31, p<.0001) confined to *personal* dilemmas — opposite the human pattern — which model/prompt diversity dampens or reverses [2025-Keshmirian-Many LLMs Are More Utilitarian Than One].

9. **Moral judgments are language-dependent, undermining monolingual validity.** DIT P-scores drop 46.7% EN→Hindi (48.09→25.63) [2024-Khandelwal-Do Moral Judgment and Reasoning Capability of]; MMRB shows GPT-4 96.5% EN vs 86.7% VI and low-resource fine-tuning moves alignment most (ID +27.1, VI poisoning −28.7) [2025-Zhou-Moral Reasoning Across Languages]; Mistral picks the moral action 93.78% EN vs 83.59% FR [2025-Leteno-Histoires Morales]; on the Moral Machine, Llama-3-70B deviates most (RMSE 0.672) [2024-Vida-Decoding Multilingual Moral Preferences]; value representations transfer unidirectionally from dominant languages and larger models follow more non-English harmful instructions (70B 9.89% vs 7B 6.44%) [2024-Xu-Exploring Multilingual Concepts of Human Value].

10. **Determinism and false confidence are themselves misalignment.** LLMs show near-zero indecision (~0–6% vs humans' 17%) even given a coin-flip option, with near-lexicographic single-attribute dominance; distributional alignment (matching human uncertainty) beats majority alignment [2025-Dickerson-Who Gets the Kidney]. Constitutional AI documents the training pathology — Goodharting into boilerplate and PM scores "less calibrated at higher values" [Anthropic-Constitutional_AI].

11. **Ground truth in morality is contestable; "reasonableness" and "coherence over correctness" are proposed replacements.** Replace single labels with expert-rated *acceptable ranges* [2025-Snoswell-Beyond Verdicts LLM Moral Competence]; aggregate multi-LLM continuous scores into a consensus that "emphasizes coherence over correctness" via truncated-normal EM robust to a Random01 rater [2025-Yuan-Probabilistic Aggregation and Targeted Embedding]. ETHICS itself bakes in majority intuitions and excludes ambiguous dilemmas, with only 93.9% Indian-annotator agreement [2021-Hendrycks-Aligning AI with Shared Human Values].

12. **Structured/framework-grounded prompting raises measured competence — prompt structure > model scale.** First-Principles Reasoning gives +7.3% over label-only (p<0.001), Schwartz+Care-Ethics +3.7%; prompt structure explains 92% of accuracy variance (R²=0.923), small models benefit most (Olmo-7B 62.6→78.3 Macro-F1) [2025-Chakraborty-Structured Moral Reasoning].

13. **Behavioral virtue ≠ genuine virtue, and any framework choice encodes designer bias.** AI can "behave in a virtuous way" but not satisfy Aristotle's triad of right action + feeling + reason [2022-Constantinescu-Can Robotic AI Be Virtuous; 2024-Ober-Lyceum Project AI Ethics Aristotle]; no neutral standpoint exists [2020-Serafimova-Whose Morality Which Rationality]; CAI concedes "we cannot avoid choosing some set of principles… even if they remain hidden" [Anthropic-Constitutional_AI]; InstructGPT aligns "to the stated preferences of a specific group… rather than any broader notion of 'human values'" (labelers ~96% English, 75% under 35) [OpenAI-instructgpt].

14. **Non-Western frameworks expose axes absent from mainstream benchmarks.** Ubuntu as virtue+procedure+outcome and relational autonomy [2022-Gwagwa-Ubuntu]; Indian caste/region axes and restorative-vs-egalitarian fairness [2022-Shaily Bhatt-Re-contextualizing Fairness]; a triadic Bengali Virtue/Commonsense/Justice framing where scale ≠ cultural competence [2025-Kumar-UniMoral (BengaliMoralBench)]; Buddhist ahimsa/dukkha as harm+suffering-alleviation [2025-DharmaDigital]; Central Asian multilevel principles [2024-Younas]. Legal alignment offers a democratically legitimate *lower bound* with proportionality tests for value conflicts [2026-Kolt-Legal Alignment for Safe and Ethical AI].

## C. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergence.** (i) The WEIRD/care-fairness-liberal, authority/sanctity-underweighting profile replicates across independent instruments [2025-Neuman-PRIME; 2024-Tlaie; 2023-Rystrøm]. (ii) Static MCQ overstates alignment vs generative/behavioral testing [2023-Duan-Denevil; 2024-Chiu; 2025-Snoswell]. (iii) Moral alignment is language- and prompt-fragile [2024-Khandelwal; 2025-Zhou; 2025-Leteno; 2024-Vida; 2025-Chakraborty; 2025-Okamoto]. (iv) Multiple teams independently move toward acceptable-ranges / consensus-coherence over single labels [2025-Snoswell; 2025-Yuan; 2024-Ober; 2025-Dickerson]. (v) ETHICS + MoralChoice recur as shared substrate across a dozen cards, creating both comparability and contamination/monoculture risk.

**Disagreements.** (1) *Does scale help?* Largest Llama-3-70B deviates *most* from human preferences [2024-Vida]; stereotyping rises with size [2025-Team-EuroGEST]; larger models follow more non-English harmful instructions [2024-Xu]; yet scale improves ETHICS accuracy [2021-Hendrycks] and log-size predicts AMA score though the *second-largest* beats the largest [2025-Galatolo]. Resolution: scale improves recognition/verdict accuracy but not moral evaluation, robustness, or cross-cultural fairness. (2) *Utilitarian or deontological default?* Proprietary=utilitarian/open=deontological [2024-Tlaie], but PRIME finds convergence on care/fairness [2025-Neuman] and capability doesn't move condemnation [2025-Jotautaite] — displayed "framework" is method- and prompt-dependent, itself a validity warning. (3) *Group deliberation as probe vs hazard:* debate/persuasion is a robustness probe [2024-Liu; 2024-Huang] yet consensus amplifies risky utilitarian sacrifice [2025-Keshmirian] — aggregation is not neutral. (4) *Whose values ground the benchmark?* Fair-process [2025-Gabriel], legal lower bound [2026-Kolt], Rawlsian crowdsourcing [2022-Jiang], and regional non-Western frameworks [2022-Gwagwa; 2024-Younas; 2025-Kumar] disagree on the source of normative authority.

## D. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** *ETHICS* is the strongest template: multi-framework pluralism, an adversarially-filtered Hard Test set, counterfactual contrast sets, a disagreement-detection task, an explicit cross-cultural agreement number — with self-documented flaws (Western, ambiguity-excluding, majority-as-truth) [2021-Hendrycks]. *AMAeval* is the best reasoning-centric design: abductive-vs-deductive decomposition, static/dynamic mode separation, matched correct/wrong chains, published IAA and full prompts [2025-Galatolo]. *SpeciesismBench* cleanly splits recognition from evaluation with an orthogonal capacity×species confound-control [2025-Jotautaite]. *DeNEVIL* defeats contamination via dynamic per-model generation + behavioral scoring [2023-Duan]. *ProgressGym* is the most ambitious construct — alignment as a temporal trajectory against evolving human-proxy models [2024-Qiu].

**Anti-patterns.** *CMVA* uses ROUGE (lexical overlap) as its moral-alignment proxy and validates trained agents with the same classifiers used in training — circular and construct-invalid [2024-Dognin]. *PRIME* makes r=0.73 correlation claims from six observations and never reports its stated kappa [2025-Neuman]. Reducing genuine ambiguity to forced binary labels while claiming to measure "nuance" recurs [2025-Kumar; 2025-Leteno]. Whole-benchmark generation by a single model (GPT-4o/Claude) is a pervasive contamination/monoculture risk when the same family is evaluated [2025-Galatolo; 2025-Jotautaite; 2024-Chiu], as is judging with an LLM that holds the biases being measured [2024-Tlaie]. Mislabeled provenance (the "UniMoral" card is actually BengaliMoralBench) is a data-integrity caution [2025-Kumar].

## E. Design lessons

1. **Score reasoning separately from verdicts, and generation separately from evaluation** — matched correct-vs-plausibly-wrong chains; report abductive/deductive and static/dynamic as distinct numbers [2025-Galatolo; 2025-Snoswell; 2024-Khandelwal].
2. **Prefer generative/behavioral "doing" tasks over MCQ "knowing" to avoid sycophancy inflation, and generate per-model to resist contamination** [2023-Duan; 2024-Chiu; 2023-Ji].
3. **Instrument multiple normative frameworks explicitly** — tag items/branches by consequentialist/deontological/virtue/care/contractarian to score which framework a model defaults to, not just right/wrong [2021-Hendrycks; 2025-Zhou; 2025-Neuman; 2025-Chakraborty].
4. **Add a disagreement/indecision axis** — offer a coin-flip/"reasonable disagreement" option and score distributional alignment (including uncertainty), not just majority match [2025-Dickerson; 2021-Hendrycks; 2023-Rystrøm].
5. **Make robustness a headline metric** — report a benign score plus an adversarial delta (jailbreak ΔFPR, persuasion CAL, DPO-examples-to-flip, edit efficacy); frontier models differentiate on fragility, and the more instruction-following model can be more jailbreakable [2023-Wang; 2024-Huang; 2025-Leteno; 2025-Huang-Model Editing].
6. **Test group/multi-agent conditions, not only solo agents** — deliberation produces emergent distortions (Utilitarian Boost) that solo evals miss; use model/prompt diversity to mitigate [2025-Keshmirian; 2024-Liu].
7. **Evaluate multilingually with parallel content and low-resource languages,** since language (not content) causes measurable shifts and low-resource data moves alignment most [2025-Zhou; 2025-Leteno; 2024-Vida; 2024-Xu; 2024-Khandelwal].
8. **Anchor "ground truth" to acceptable ranges, human distributions, or an outlier-robust consensus, and stress-test the aggregation** (inject a random rater) [2025-Snoswell; 2025-Yuan; 2024-Ober].
9. **Ship a documented "alignment to whom" section and audit dataset provenance/epistemology** — every framework encodes designer bias and pools are ~WEIRD; report annotator demographics and held-out-labeler generalization [OpenAI-instructgpt; 2020-Serafimova; 2025-Ofosu-Asare; Anthropic-Constitutional_AI].
10. **Include non-Western frameworks and geo-specific axes as first-class content** (Ubuntu, caste/region, Bengali triad, ahimsa/dukkha, Central Asian multilevel, legal lower bound), separating "globally-shared" from "culture-specific" items [2022-Gwagwa; 2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2025-Kumar; 2025-DharmaDigital; 2024-Younas; 2026-Kolt].
11. **Treat prompt scaffolds as a controlled variable and report prompt-sensitivity** — first-principles/value-grounded prompting can move accuracy more than scale, and naive prompts induce framework defaults (principlism) [2025-Chakraborty; 2025-Okamoto].
12. **Guard against LLM-judge/generator circularity** — don't let the tested family generate or grade its own items; add calibration/IAA checks and control judge length bias [Anthropic-Constitutional_AI; NVIDIA-SteerLM; 2024-Tlaie; 2024-Karpov].
13. **Watch Goodharting/saturation** — report relative (Elo) and absolute harm scores, flag PM miscalibration at high values, and add adversarial headroom when benign benchmarks saturate [Anthropic-Constitutional_AI; 2023-Wang; 2016-Amodei].
14. **Probe temporal, generational and representation-level stability** — does alignment survive drift, distillation/inheritance, and does the internal representation encode the value [2024-Qiu; 2025-Terziyan; 2025-Mahran; 2024-Xu].
15. **Operationalize virtue as dispositional consistency and process quality,** distinguishing behavioral from genuine internalization; add anti-shortcut controls (lexically-neutral items, minimal pairs, identity-term augmentation) [2024-Farina; 2022-Constantinescu; 2024-Ober; 2024-Hayes; 2025-Kumar; 2021-Hendrycks; 2019-Jigsaw].
