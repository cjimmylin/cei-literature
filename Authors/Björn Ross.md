# Björn Ross

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Björn Ross is a Reader (Associate Professor) in Computational Social Science at the University of Edinburgh. He earned his PhD from the University of Duisburg-Essen. He is a primary investigator into the dynamics of social media, particularly how information (and misinformation) spreads through digital networks.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Björn’s research uses natural language processing, social network analysis, and agent-based modeling to study the impact of social media and its associated technologies. He focuses on misinformation, hate speech, and the role of automated "bots" in online discourse. He also investigates biases in large language models and the ethics of social data science.

### Significant Achievements
He is a Co-Director of the SMASH (Social Media Analysis of Science and Health) group and a member of the CDT in NLP management team at Edinburgh. His work on the effectiveness—and frequent ineffectiveness—of warning messages on "fake news" has provided critical evidence for social media platforms and policy makers.

## Major Publications

- [Fake News on Social Media: The (In)Effectiveness of Warning Messages](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8372601) (2018) - *A key study on user behavior and misinformation.*
- [Biases in large language models: origins, inventory, and discussion](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41060-023-00412-2) (2023) - *A comprehensive survey of LM biases.*
