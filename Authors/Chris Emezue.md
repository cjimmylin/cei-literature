# Chris Emezue

## Biographical Summary

Chris Emezue is a researcher and PhD student affiliated with Mila (Quebec AI Institute) and the Technical University of Munich (TUM). He is a prominent member of the Masakhane community, working to democratize AI for African languages. He is also the founder of Lanfrica, a resource hub for African languages.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **African Language Resources**: Creating datasets and models for languages that are under-represented in mainstream AI, such as Nigerian Pidgin and Hausa.
- **Speech Technologies**: Advancing ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) and TTS (Text-to-Speech) for African languages through projects like "NaijaVoices".
- **Community Building**: Actively building infrastructure (Lanfrica) to connect researchers with African language data.

### Significant Achievements
- **NaijaVoices**: Lead author of this large-scale speech dataset, which significantly advances the capabilities of speech technologies for Nigerian languages.

## Major Publications
- **NaijaVoices: A Richly Annotated Speech Dataset for Nigerian Languages**: A key contribution to multilingual speech processing.
