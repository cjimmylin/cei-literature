# Aarti Singh

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aarti Singh is the FORE Systems Professor in the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University and the Director of the NSF AI Institute for Societal Decision Making. She holds a PhD from UW-Madison and previously conducted postdoctoral research at Princeton. She is a highly cited researcher in the theory and practice of interactive machine learning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aarti's research investigates principled interactive algorithms for learning and decision-making, with a focus on statistical optimality, computational tractability, and robustness. She applies these algorithms to guide experiments in scientific domains like cosmology and materials science, as well as to improve societal decision-making.

### Significant Achievements
She has served as the General Chair for ICML 2025 and is an NSF Career Award recipient. Her group is known for foundational work in active sampling, stochastic optimization, and bandits. Her leadership of the NSF AI Institute highlights her commitment to responsible and impactful AI application.
