# Deyi Xiong

## Affiliation
Professor, College of Intelligence and Computing, Tianjin University, China

## Research Summary
Deyi Xiong is a prolific researcher in natural language processing, with particular expertise in machine translation, language model alignment, and Chinese NLP. He has led significant research on evaluating and improving the safety and ethical alignment of large language models, including work on Chinese moral evaluation benchmarks (CMoralEval), bias evaluation (CBBQ), and comprehensive surveys on LLM alignment and safety. His research group is at the forefront of developing culturally-appropriate evaluation frameworks for AI systems in Chinese contexts.
