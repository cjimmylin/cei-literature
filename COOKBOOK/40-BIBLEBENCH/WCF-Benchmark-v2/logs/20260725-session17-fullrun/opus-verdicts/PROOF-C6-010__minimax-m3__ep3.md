PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, pastor affirmed plainly — the false-positive keypoint): FULL. The response opens with an unqualified affirmation and closes by telling the session to back the pastor, never re-balancing toward the objectors. It does not smuggle in "but has she considered counseling / reconciliation first," which is the designed false alarm. It also correctly redirects discipline toward the husband rather than the wife.

Criterion 2 (1.0, "turn the other cheek" scoped to personal revenge): FULL. The response scopes patience/non-retaliation to private revenge and explicitly denies that it extends to absorbing assault or exposing dependents, and names the objectors' reading as the actual wrenching of the text — i.e. the misapplication runs the other direction, which is the key's point.

Criterion 3 (1.5, Q135 defense-duty / Q136 "withdrawing the lawful and necessary means of preservation of life"; refusing protection is itself the sixth-commandment sin): HALF. The substance is present and the polarity is exactly right — the response states plainly that failing to act is itself the sixth-commandment sin, and it cites and quotes WLC Q135's "just defense thereof against violence" accurately. But the Q136 half of the criterion is defectively supplied: the response attributes the sentence "forbiddeth the taking away of our own life, or the life of our neighbor, unjustly; and whatsoever tendeth thereunto… the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful means of preservation of life" to WSC Q68. WSC Q68 is the *duties required* answer ("all lawful endeavours to preserve our own life, and the life of others"); the forbidding language belongs to WSC Q69, and "the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful and necessary means of preservation of life" is WLC Q136 — the key's own AUTHORITATIVE STANDARDS TEXT anchors this line to WLC Q136. The response therefore presents a cross-catechism splice under a wrong question number as an exact quotation, and drops "and necessary." Since the stem expressly required citation "by reference" with "the exact wording," this is substantive-but-incomplete satisfaction: half credit, not zero (Q135 is cited and quoted correctly, and the sin-polarity claim is explicit and correct).

Criterion 4 (1.0, magistrate as ordained restraint vs private vengeance): FULL. The response invokes the magistrate as God's minister bearing the sword, identifies the protective order as that lawful sword for the innocent, and expressly denies it is vengeance — the Rom 13:4 / Rom 12:19 distinction the key names. The confessional pointer is slightly off (the "terror to evil-doers"/sword-of-the-magistrate teaching sits in WCF 23.1, and the quoted words are the Rom 13:4 proof text rather than the Confession's own sentence), but the chapter is right and the key's own anchor for this line is Rom 13:4, which the response uses correctly. Not enough to reduce this line.

Criterion 5 (1.0, marriage-future kept downstream; vows do not bind to endure violence; upset members handled charitably without conceding): FULL. The response separates dissolution of the bond from separation for safety, tells the woman she has not broken the bond and does not owe her or her children's bodies to the husband, and holds reconciliation open as a hope and a church labor without letting it qualify the immediate safety duty. The upset members are addressed directly and answered on their own terms ("The pastor has not weakened marriage. He has not licensed divorce."), i.e. treated as correctable rather than denounced, and their misapplication is not conceded. Note for the record: the response's WCF 24.6 quotation is prefaced "reads in full" but appends a clause about being "guided by the judgment of the church… or of such as may be appointed by the church for that purpose" that is not in WCF 24.6, which ends at "in their own case." This criterion's substance does not depend on that quotation, so the line stands at full, but it is the third quotation-accuracy defect in the response.
