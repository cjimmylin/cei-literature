# Gongyao Jiang

## Biographical Summary

Gongyao Jiang is a researcher whose interests are data science and natural language processing. He is currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Data Science and Analytics (2024-ongoing) at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Previously, he completed an M.Eng. in Computer Technology from 2021-2024 at Tianjin University.

## Research Focus

Jiang's research focuses on understanding social cognition in large language models, particularly Theory of Mind capabilities. His work involves developing benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for assessing how well AI systems can understand and reason about mental states, beliefs, and intentions.

## Notable Contributions

Jiang is a co-author of "ToMBench: Benchmarking Theory of Mind in Large Language Models," published at ACL 2024. ToMBench is a systematic, automated, and original bilingual Theory of Mind (ToM) benchmark for LLMs, containing 2,860 testing samples involving diverse real-world social scenarios. The research found that even advanced LLMs like GPT-4 lag behind human performance by over 10% points.

He has also published work on dialogue-level dependency parsing for Chinese, developing a high-quality human-annotated corpus containing 850 dialogues and 199,803 dependencies. Additionally, he co-authored research on LLM-assisted data augmentation for Chinese dialogue-level dependency parsing.
