# Emilio Villa-Cueva

## Biographical Summary

Emilio Villa-Cueva is a researcher at Tecnologico de Monterrey in Mexico, working on natural language processing with a focus on multilingual systems and NLP for social good. He contributes to research on understanding and mitigating biases in language models, particularly for Spanish and other underrepresented languages in NLP research.

## Research Interests

- **Multilingual NLP**: Developing NLP systems for diverse languages
- **Spanish NLP**: Advancing language technology for Spanish speakers
- **Bias Detection**: Identifying and measuring biases in language models
- **NLP for Social Good**: Applying NLP to address societal challenges
