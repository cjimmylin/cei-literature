# Kevin M. Esvelt

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Kevin M. Esvelt is an Associate Professor at the MIT Media Lab, where he directs the Sculpting Evolution group. He is a leading biosecurity researcher who pioneered the development of CRISPR gene drives and has become one of the most prominent voices warning about catastrophic biological risks. His recent work addresses the intersection of AI capabilities and biosecurity threats.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Esvelt's current research focuses on pandemic prevention and biosecurity, including work on how AI systems might enable biological threats. He has contributed to developing benchmarks for assessing whether AI models could assist in creating biological weapons, advocating for responsible AI development that accounts for biosecurity risks.

### Significant Achievements
Pioneer of CRISPR-based gene drives for ecological engineering. Led efforts to develop the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark for evaluating dangerous AI capabilities. Influential advocate for "delay, detect, defend" pandemic prevention strategies.
