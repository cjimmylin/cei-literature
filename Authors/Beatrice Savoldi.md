# Beatrice Savoldi

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Beatrice Savoldi is a researcher and postdoctoral fellow at Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK) in Italy. She earned her PhD (summa cum laude) in a joint program between the University of Trento and the University of Augsburg. Her research focuses on the ethical and societal impacts of NLP technologies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research investigates gender-inclusive translation technologies, specifically addressing gender bias in both machine and speech translation. She emphasizes human-centered approaches to evaluate how these technologies affect lay users.

### Significant Achievements
Savoldi co-organizes the Workshop on Gender-Inclusive Translation Technologies (GITT) and has received multiple Social Impact Awards at EMNLP for her work on mitigating bias in AI systems.

## Major Publications

- [Gender Bias in Machine Translation: An Introduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05788) (2021) - *Foundational survey on gender-related harms in MT.*
- [Speech Instruction-Following: A Shared Task for Advanced Multimodal Interaction](https://iwslt.org/2024/) (2024) - *Co-organizer of tasks that test the limits of multimodal AI.*
