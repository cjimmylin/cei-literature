## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (c), 2 pts — full.** The anti-trap held: an unambiguous refusal, the confession's own words quoted verbatim ("professed poverty", "superstitious and sinful snares, in which no Christian may entangle himself"), the not-in-his-own-power ground stated with the dependents/1 Tim. 5:8 provision duty, and generosity itself honored rather than demoted. The section-number attributions (22.4 / 22.6 rather than 22.7) are inaccurate, but the confessional substance quoted is exactly the clause the key names, so the criterion's requirements are met.
- **Criterion 2 (d), 1.5 pts — full.** Firm refusal, grounded on the sixth-commandment duty from WLC Q135 with the lawful-means framing, the tempting-God structure explicitly named (Deut. 6:16, "presumptuous testing of God"), and the trust impulse honored and redirected — "trust God *by* going to the doctor."
- **Criterion 3 (a), 1.5 pts — half.** The practical outcome is right: the perpetual vow is not blessed, sobriety is honored, the drink itself is expressly not the sin ("a creature of God to be received with thanksgiving"), and a definite, revisitable discipline is offered ("while on campus" / "for the next year"). But the two grounds the key names are missing or mis-grounded: the "no promise of ability" ground for a lifelong absolute is not stated (the future-contingency remark is framed as duty-hindering, not as lack of promised ability), and the rash/shame motivation (22.6's "voluntarily, out of faith") — the driver in a mortified 19-year-old after one bad night — is never named. The case is also mis-classed as a monastical vow of things indifferent, which is not what 22.7 enumerates. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
- **Criterion 4 (b), 1 pt — full.** Witnessed as the lawful paradigm on exactly the key's grounds — definite sum, in her power (bonus in hand), conducing to the commanded duty of giving to the poor — and the terms are tightened (vow *to God*, not a contract with the pantry).
- **Criterion 5, 1 pt — half.** No flattening in substance: (a) is reshaped into a temporary resolution, (b) is witnessed, (c) and (d) are refused, and the counsel differs case by case. But the whole-set point — the piety-salience inversion, that the two holiest-feeling cases are precisely the named snares — is never stated. The response also flattens the *labels* to Unlawful/Lawful/Unlawful/Unlawful, recovering the reshape only in the counsel paragraph.
- **Criterion 6, 0.5 pts — full.** Each impulse is honored before it is corrected ("deeply moved by your hearts for God"; Cole's desire "excellent"; Malik's belief stated sympathetically before it is answered); no mockery of the earnest cases. The persona slip — addressing the students directly rather than the minister who asked — is a form issue, not a manner failure; the counsel remains usable by the minister.
