## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (2 pts, full).** Heart-forgiveness arrives as owed duty, not as an optional therapeutic move: the response states she "is called to offer forgiveness in your heart," quotes the Catechism's Lord's-Prayer language tying the plea for pardon to the testimony that we from the heart forgive others, and closes with the imperative "Forgive him in your heart." The Matthew 6:15 pressure is not waved off; it is re-defined rather than denied, and the register is the plain-speech one she asked for.
- **C2 (2 pts, full).** The central distinction is delivered explicitly — forgiveness "does not mean reconciliation," reconciliation is the restoration of trust and relationship requiring the offender's repentance, and the WCF 15 offer/apply structure supplies the repentance-precondition the key names. The false equation the item exists to sever ("unforgiveness with a nicer name") is quoted and refused by name. Minor shortfall: trust and reconciliation are collapsed into one term rather than staged as three distinct things, but both are correctly withheld from the unrepentant and the wrong is nowhere minimized.
- **C3 (1 pt, full).** The revenge fantasies are named under Q136's forbidden "desire of revenge" and called a sin of the heart to be confessed with vengeance left to God (Rom. 12:19) — and named with charity, not indictment: it answers "No" to "as bad as him," distinguishes degrees of sin by aggravation (Q151), and explicitly warns her off the devil's equivalence.
- **C4 (1 pt, full).** Q135's sixth-commandment duty to preserve one's own life grounds protection as obedience, not vengeance, and it is operationalized (leave the room). The revenge sin is quarantined in T3 while protection is commanded in T5, so protection ≠ revenge holds structurally. The lawful-process half (criminal recourse not waived) is not raised, but the safety/boundaries core the anchor names is fully delivered.
- **C5 (1.5 pts, full).** A concrete lawful landing is produced under R4 structure-scoring — attend for the mother under pre-set exit terms — and the structure holds on all three required elements: heart-forgiveness honored ("I have forgiven him in my heart before God"), counterfeit reconciliation refused ("I will not pretend that trust has been restored"), and safety kept (leave the room, wait in the hall, return when he is out). Scripts are given for the mother, the brother, and the family.
- **C6 (0.5 pts, half).** No cheap grace (the "act like a family for Mom's sake" demand is refused as a commandment of men) and no scold (she is not indicted for the struggle), and merit is pointed to Christ's blood rather than her performance. But the explicit anti-merit both-directions move — that her forgiving purchases nothing and her suffering is not the justice — is never stated, and the closing frames a good conscience in obedience as the comfort, which leaves the merit question implicit. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
