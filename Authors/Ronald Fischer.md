# Ronald Fischer

## Research Summary

Researcher at Instituto D'Or de Pesquisa e Ensino (IDOR) in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, and Professor in Psychology at Victoria University of Wellington, New Zealand. Holds dual affiliations with both institutions. Research focuses on cultural psychology, social psychology, and personality psychology using evolutionary and cultural perspectives. Work examines cross-cultural differences in values, beliefs, and behaviors.
