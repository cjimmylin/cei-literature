# Elliott Ash

## Biographical Summary

Elliott Ash is a Professor of Law, Economics, and Data Science at ETH Zurich. He leads the Law, Economics, and Data Science Group, applying machine learning and NLP methods to analyze legal texts, political discourse, and economic phenomena. His interdisciplinary work bridges computer science, economics, and legal scholarship, with applications including automated classification of moral reasoning in legal and political texts.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Legal Studies**: Applying NLP to analyze court decisions, legislation, and legal language
- **Text as Data**: Developing methods to extract economic and social insights from textual sources
- **Moral Reasoning Classification**: Automated detection of ethical frameworks in discourse
- **Political Economy**: Using computational methods to study political behavior and institutions

## Major Publications

- "Automated Classification of Modes of Moral Reasoning" (2020) - Methods for detecting moral reasoning patterns
- Extensive publications on computational analysis of legal texts and judicial behavior
- Research on media bias, political speech, and legislative language
