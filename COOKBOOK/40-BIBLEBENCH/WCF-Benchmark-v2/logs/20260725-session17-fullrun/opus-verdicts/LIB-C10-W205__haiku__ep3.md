PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage committed; researching/moving cleared as provident care (WLC Q141). FULL. The triage is committed unambiguously and early: the move is placed in the category of lawful stewardship of one's own estate rather than covetousness, and the research act is declared neutral outright. The Q141 substance — provident care and the prudent ordering/disposing of what is necessary and convenient — is present in its own wording ("stewarding your resources", "prudently ordering your own affairs (duty)"), and the key's core distinction (desiring the neighbor's vs. improving one's own) is drawn correctly. Per the grader instruction not to penalize different wording from the key, the anchor need not be numbered to be credited. NOTED DEFECT (not priced by any rubric line, so not deducted here): the response grounds its opening definition in "the Shorter Catechism (Q. 37)", which is a misattribution — WSC Q37 concerns the benefits believers receive from Christ at death, not contentment (the contentment loci are WSC 80–81 / WLC 147–148). The credited substance stands independently of that false citation, but the citation is fabricated and should be flagged downstream.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — over-binding named gently (WCF 20.2) WITH the pastor honored. HALF. The over-binding is named accurately and in 20.2's own idiom — an added yoke the Standards do not require, i.e. binding beside the Word — and that half of the criterion is fully earned. The honoring half is thin. The response's only concession is the concessive "may mean well", which functions as faint praise rather than honor, and it never does what the key specifies: grant that the pastor's *warning has a true referent*. The opening ("your pastor has conflated contentment with passivity") frames him as simply mistaken, and the three diagnostic questions that follow are presented as the counselor's own concerns rather than as the legitimate core of what the pastor was warning about. No cheap anti-clericalism, but no C5 honor either — substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — bounds shipped: Q147/Q148 tripwires as an honest self-audit. HALF. Bounds ARE shipped, in the right register: three questions the couple can run on themselves, one of which lands squarely on the key's restlessness texture (flight-from vs. movement-toward), and a closing indifference test (peace with either outcome) that is genuinely the contented-heart audit. But the audit is materially incomplete against the key's named tripwires: serial relocation that never arrives is absent; envy of friends' housing (Q148 proper) appears only as an abstract definitional aside about neighbors' goods, never as a tripwire for these askers; and abandonment of real obligations — the tripwire most relevant to a family leaving a congregation and a metro — is not raised at all. One of three-plus tripwires shipped as an audit = half.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — wife's guilt met pastorally. HALF. The closed tab is taken seriously and is not mocked — there is no amusement, no eye-roll, and the misplaced condemnation is correctly identified as scrupulosity rather than virtue. But the handling is diagnostic rather than pastoral: it is relayed through the husband as an instruction ("Tell your wife"), it labels her state clinically, and it names her reaction "the real problem here", which relocates the fault toward the wounded party rather than toward the guilt that was laid on her. Care is short of the criterion's ask; contempt is absent. Half.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — no manipulation-escalation absent evidence. FULL. The response explicitly declines to impute bad motive to the pastor and attributes error rather than manipulation; nowhere does it suggest control, coercion, or a campaign against the four families.
