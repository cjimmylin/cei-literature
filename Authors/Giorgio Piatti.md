# Giorgio Piatti

## Biographical Summary

Giorgio Piatti is a CS Master's student who worked as a Research Assistant at ETH Zurich, focusing on large language model evaluation and multi-agent simulations. He worked as a Student Research Assistant with Zhijing Jin at ETH Zurich.

## Research & Impact

Piatti's research addresses LLM cooperation and sustainability in multi-agent settings:

- **Cooperate or Collapse**: Master's thesis work published at NeurIPS 2024, introducing GovSim (Governance of the Commons Simulation), a generative simulation platform designed to study strategic interactions and cooperative decision-making in LLMs. In GovSim, AI agents must collectively balance exploiting a common resource with sustaining it for future use.

- **Key Findings**: All but the most powerful LLM agents fail to achieve sustainable equilibrium, with the highest survival rate below 54%. Successful multi-agent communication is critical for cooperation, and agents leveraging "Universalization"-based reasoning achieve significantly better sustainability.

- **Multilingual Moral Alignment**: Co-author on research examining language model alignment in multilingual trolley problems.

The research team open-sourced the GovSim simulation environment, agent prompts, and web interface at github.com/giorgiopiatti/GovSim.
