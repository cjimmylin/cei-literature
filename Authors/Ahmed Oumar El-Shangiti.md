# Ahmed Oumar El-Shangiti

## Research Summary

Ahmed Oumar El-Shangiti is a researcher contributing to Arabic natural language processing and culturally inclusive AI development. He is part of the PALM project, a year-long community-driven initiative covering all 22 Arab countries to develop culturally sensitive and linguistically diverse datasets for Arabic LLMs. His work contributes to ensuring AI systems are inclusive of Arab cultural contexts and dialectal Arabic variations.
