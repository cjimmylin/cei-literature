## Abstract

SOBACO (SOcial BiAs and Cultural cOmmonsense benchmark) is a Japanese benchmark evaluating social biases and cultural commonsense in LLMs in a unified QA format. It covers three categories: age, gender, and hierarchical relationships. The hierarchical relationships category captures culturally specific social norms rooted in Japanese Confucian-Shinto tradition. Key finding: debiasing methods that mitigate social biases degrade performance on cultural commonsense tasks by up to 75%, revealing a fundamental tension between Western-centric bias mitigation and preservation of Japanese cultural values.
