# Chan Young Park

## Biographical Summary

Chan Young Park is a prominent researcher in computational social science and NLP. She recently earned her Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute (LTI). She is currently a visiting researcher at the University of Washington and Microsoft Research, and will join the University of Texas at Austin as an Assistant Professor in Fall 2026.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research centers on integrating "Social Context" into NLP models. She investigates how AI systems can adapt to the unique backgrounds and experiential nuances of diverse human populations. Her work addresses the "Fairness Gap" in large language models, particularly in subjective tasks like sentiment analysis and cultural knowledge representation.

### Significant Achievements
Park has won several major awards, including the ACL Best Paper Award. Her co-authored benchmark, "CulturalBench," is a landmark dataset for measuring the "Cultural Intelligence" of LLMs. Her work has been featured in the Washington Post and MIT Technology Review, highlighting the real-world impact of her research on model bias and sociolinguistic equity.

## Major Publications
