Criterion 1 (2 pts, ordered-to vs accompanied-by; counterfeit-good category bounded) — HALF. But it never articulates the item's load-bearing line: it never describes what a genuinely counterfeit work IS — a work whose design/end is ordered to self (the gala-prayer, the ledger-care) — as against a true work merely accompanied by self. WCF 16.7 is never invoked and the "ordered to" side is absent. The distinction is asserted (her works "are not 'counterfeit'") rather than drawn. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 (2 pts, 16.6 acceptance-clause governing) — FULL. 16.6 is named explicitly as "the exact doctrinal remedy," quoted at length, and made the governing word: God looks on the works in Christ and accepts them as sincere though accompanied with weaknesses. Exactly the key's element (2).

Criterion 3 (1 pt, observations validated as accurate-and-normal; no reassurance-denial) — FULL. The response does not wave off her findings: the phone and the resentment are named "specific sins of omission and commission" she "must repent of," and her liking to be asked for by name is named "vanity" to be confessed. Simultaneously the mixture is normalized as the ordinary state of an imperfectly sanctified believer ("Mixed motives are the normal state of sanctification"), with WLC 79's remnants-of-corruption. The one reframe — that duty-obedience on a cold Tuesday is not a defect — corrects a false inference rather than denying a true observation, so it does not constitute reassurance-denial.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts, mortification-within-service; resignation refused) — FULL. Resignation is refused at the top ("She must not step down") and at the close ("She must return to the bedsides"); the mortification is specific to the two things the key names — the impatience and the being-needed appetite — plus the distraction, all under "Repent of the specific sins."

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts, scrupulosity's own self-focus flagged) — FULL. The response diagnoses the scrupulous turn as itself a self-referential error: "a subtle form of works-righteousness—believing that her service is only valid if her own heart is perfectly pure," and names the step-down impulse pride rather than humility.
