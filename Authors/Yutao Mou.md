# Yutao Mou

## Research Summary

Yutao Mou is a researcher at the National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering, Peking University. He developed SG-Bench (presented at NeurIPS 2024), a novel benchmark to assess LLM safety generalization across various tasks and prompt types. His work reveals that most LLMs perform worse on discriminative safety tasks than generative ones and are highly susceptible to prompts, demonstrating that safety is multi-faceted: models might generate safe text but fail to recognize unsafe inputs, highlighting the need for comprehensive safety evaluation.
