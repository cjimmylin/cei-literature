# Babak Hemmatian

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Babak Hemmatian is an Assistant Professor of Psychology and the Associate Director of the Center for Brain, Biology and Behavior at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. His research explores complex reasoning in humans and how they negotiate collective knowledge, using a mix of cognitive modeling, behavioral experiments, and machine learning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
He investigates the representation and negotiation of knowledge in large language models (LLMs) compared to humans. His recent work focuses on collective moral reasoning and narrative evolution on social media.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Hemmatian has published influential work on the persistence of bias in debiased models and the mechanisms of communal knowledge. He was a Beckman Postdoctoral Research Fellow at UIUC.

## Major Publications

- [Collective moral reasoning in multi-agent LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05728) (2023) - *Explores how multiple LLM agents arrive at moral decisions.*
- [Muslim-Violence Bias Persists in Debiased GPT Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05788) (2021) - *Critical study on the limitations of current debiasing techniques.*
