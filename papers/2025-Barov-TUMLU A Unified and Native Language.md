# TUMLU: A Unified and Native Language Understanding Benchmark for Turkic Languages

## Abstract

Comprehensive natively developed benchmark for 8 Turkic languages with 38,139 multiple-choice questions across 11 subjects. Includes TUMLU-mini balanced subset. Evaluates Claude, Gemini, GPT, LLaMA across languages, subjects, and alphabets. Covers ALL four target languages (Turkish, Azerbaijani, Kazakh, Uzbek) plus 4 more Turkic languages. Natively constructed (not translated) to capture cultural intricacies. ACL 2025 main conference paper.

## Benchmark

**Name:** TUMLU

## Data Description

TUMLU: a native multilingual benchmark of 38,139 multiple-choice questions across 8 Turkic languages (Azerbaijani, Crimean Tatar, Turkish, Uyghur, Uzbek, Karakalpak, Kyrgyz, Kazakh, Tatar) and 11 subjects; also releases TUMLU-mini (balanced 100-question subset per subject).
