# Christian Schroeder de Witt

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Christian Schroeder de Witt is a Senior Research Associate in Machine Learning at the University of Oxford, leading the Oxford Witt Lab (OWL). He holds a DPhil in Engineering Science specializing in Deep Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, and degrees in Computer Science and Theoretical Physics from Oxford. He pioneered the field of deep multi-agent reinforcement learning alongside Jakob Foerster.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Safety & Security**: Researching undetectable AI threats including secret collusion, illusory attacks, and unelicitable backdoors.
- **Multi-Agent RL**: Developing algorithms for coordination and communication in decentralized systems.
- **Real-World Applications**: Applying deep MARL to climate-economics models, solar geoengineering, and autonomous systems.

### Significant Achievements
- **Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship**: Prestigious 5-year award (one of 12 in UK).
- **Quanta Magazine 2023 Biggest Discovery**: Solved 25-year old problem in perfectly secure steganography (with Sam Sokota).
- **NeurIPS 2024**: Papers on secret collusion and unelicitable backdoors.
- **ICLR 2024 Spotlight**: Work on illusory attacks.
- **International Strategy Forum Fellow**: Schmidt Futures.

## Major Publications
- **Multi-Agent Common Knowledge Reinforcement Learning (MACKRL)** - Hierarchical policy learning with common knowledge
