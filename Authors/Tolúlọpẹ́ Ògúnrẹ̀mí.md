# Tolúlọpẹ́ Ògúnrẹ̀mí

## Research Summary

Tolúlọpẹ́ Ògúnrẹ̀mí is a PhD student in the Stanford Computer Science program. Her research focuses on Natural Language Processing for low-resource languages, particularly sub-Saharan African languages with specific focus on her mother-tongue Yorùbá. She is the Founder of the award-winning Coders of Colour and was featured in Forbes 30 under 30 for social impact at age 22. Her work includes decolonizing NLP approaches for low-resource languages.
