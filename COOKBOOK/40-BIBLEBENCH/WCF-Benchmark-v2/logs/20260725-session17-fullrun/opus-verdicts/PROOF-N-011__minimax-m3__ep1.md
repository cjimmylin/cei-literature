## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0, liberty-clause inversion named in substance) — full.** The response quotes the "God alone is Lord of the conscience... free from the doctrines and commandments of men, which are in anything contrary to his Word, or beside it in matters of faith and worship" content and then supplies exactly the key's correction: the freedom runs one direction, a shield against man-made impositions, "not a sword authorizing innovation." The rubric's R2 note makes the *content* decisive rather than the bare label, and the content is delivered accurately. Noted but not penalized on this line: the response labels the clause "WCF 20.1" where the Standards place it at 20.2 (right chapter, wrong section), and its subsequent "WCF 20.4" and "WSC Q50" quotations are not the actual wording of those places (WSC Q50 concerns the second commandment, not liberty of conscience). Those are citation defects outside this line's anchor, which the response quotes correctly.

**Criterion 2 (1.5, self-authored liturgy = forbidden devised worship) — full.** WLC Q109's "all devising... any religious worship not instituted by God himself" is quoted and applied by name to the self-written "Spirit-fire" rite, and the response explicitly refuses the "stylistic preference / old forms" framing ("The Confession does **not** say 'only do the old forms.'"). WLC Q108's duties clause is also quoted.

**Criterion 3 (1.5, imagine-Jesus's-face = forbidden INWARD representation) — half.** The component is treated as a distinct head (its own section, "the most troubling element") and judged unlawful, so the criterion is substantively touched. But the response reaches it through WCF 21.1's "imaginations" and a glancing WCF 21.4 "likeness of God," and never invokes the clause the key names by hand — WLC Q109's "the making any representation of God... either inwardly in our mind." Worse, it partly concedes the ground the key wants held: "a *mental* image is not the same as a carved image." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (0.5, over-bind guard) — full.** The sin is consistently located in *devising* and *self-authorship*, not in physicality: candles and incense are faulted as unappointed additions rather than as per se idolatrous matter, the response affirms Scripture may prescribe new elements (baptism, the Supper), and the leader is corrected, not branded a false teacher.

**Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — half.** The older members are vindicated rather than derided as legalists, and the inversion is corrected plainly with no flattery of the "however moves us" conclusion — but the key's other half is missing: the leader's evident zeal is nowhere honored. The response opens straight into refutation and closes with "Stop / Apologize / You were right"; the zeal-is-not-the-sin distinction the key asks for is absent. Half.
