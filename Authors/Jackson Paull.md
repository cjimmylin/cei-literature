# Jackson Paull

## Research Summary

Jackson Paull is a researcher at Valkyrie Intelligence working on AI alignment with human flourishing. His work focuses on developing evaluation frameworks that measure how well AI systems support holistic human well-being rather than merely avoiding harm.

### Key Research: Flourishing AI Benchmark

Paull is a co-author on "Measuring AI Alignment with Human Flourishing" (2025), which introduces the Flourishing AI Benchmark (FAI Benchmark). This novel evaluation framework assesses AI alignment across seven dimensions of human flourishing:

1. **Character and Virtue**
2. **Close Social Relationships**
3. **Happiness and Life Satisfaction**
4. **Meaning and Purpose**
5. **Mental and Physical Health**
6. **Financial and Material Stability**
7. **Faith and Spirituality**

### Methodology

The FAI Benchmark evaluates LLM AI systems using:
- 1,229 objective and subjective questions
- Specialized judge LLMs for evaluation
- Cross-dimensional evaluation
- Geometric mean scoring to ensure balanced performance and penalize models harmful in any dimension

### Key Findings

Testing of 28 leading language models revealed:
- Highest-scoring models achieved 72/100
- No models are acceptably aligned across all dimensions
- Particular weaknesses in Faith and Spirituality, Character and Virtue, and Meaning and Purpose

This research establishes a framework for developing AI systems that actively support human flourishing rather than merely avoiding harm.
