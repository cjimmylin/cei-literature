# Ennio Bilancini

## Biographical Summary

Ennio Bilancini is a Professor of Economics at IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy. His research spans behavioral economics, experimental economics, and moral psychology, with particular focus on understanding how people make moral and economic decisions. He contributes to large-scale cross-cultural studies examining moral judgment and the factors that influence ethical decision-making.

## Research Interests

- **Behavioral Economics**: Understanding deviations from rational economic models
- **Experimental Economics**: Using experiments to study economic behavior
- **Moral Psychology**: Investigating the psychology of moral judgment
- **Social Preferences**: Studying altruism, fairness, and other social motivations

## Major Publications

- Contributions to large-scale replication projects in moral psychology
- Research on social preferences and economic behavior
- Work on decision-making under uncertainty
