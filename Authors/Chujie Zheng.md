# Chujie Zheng

## Biographical Summary

Chujie Zheng (郑楚杰) is a Research Scientist in the Qwen Team at Alibaba Group. He received his doctoral degree in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in 2025, where he was advised by Professor Minlie Huang. His research focuses on building empathetic and emotionally supportive conversational AI systems, with applications in mental health support and social dialogue.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Emotional Support Conversation**: Pioneering the ESC task framework for training AI systems to provide effective emotional support following the Helping Skills Theory.
- **Empathetic Response Generation**: Developing models that can understand and appropriately respond to users' emotional states.
- **LLM Evaluation**: Researching the robustness and reliability of large language models for sensitive applications.

### Significant Achievements
- **ACL 2021**: First author on "Towards Emotional Support Dialog Systems" - established the ESC task, ESConv dataset, and Helping Skills-based framework.
- **ESConv Dataset**: Created a widely-used benchmark of 1,300+ emotional support conversations with rich strategy annotations.
- **Qwen Team**: Joined Alibaba's leading LLM research group post-PhD.

## Major Publications
- **Towards Emotional Support Dialog Systems** (ACL 2021) - Foundational work establishing the ESC task and dataset
- **AugESC: Dialogue Augmentation with Large Language Models for Emotional Support Conversation** (Findings of ACL 2023) - Using LLMs for data augmentation
- **CoMAE: A Multi-factor Hierarchical Framework for Empathetic Response Generation** (Findings of ACL 2021) - Empathy in dialogue systems
- **Crisp: Cognitive Restructuring of Negative Thoughts through Multi-turn Supportive Dialogues** (EMNLP 2025) - Mental health applications

## Notes

Chujie Zheng's work on emotional support dialogue systems represents an important application of NLP to mental health and well-being. His ESConv dataset and ESC framework, grounded in established psychological theory (Helping Skills), provide a principled approach to training AI systems that can genuinely help people in emotional distress rather than just generating plausible-sounding responses. This work is particularly relevant for AI alignment as it addresses how AI systems should interact with humans in emotionally sensitive contexts.
