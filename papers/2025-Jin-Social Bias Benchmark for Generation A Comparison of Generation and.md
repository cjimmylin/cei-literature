## Abstract

Bias Benchmark for Generation adapted from BBQ/KoBBQ for long-form story generation. LLMs show 49-61% neutrality in Korean vs 54-69% in English. Korean bias scores 0.12-0.25, consistently bias-aligned over bias-countering.

## Data Description

Bias Benchmark for Generation (BBG): English and Korean story-generation bias evaluation dataset adapted from BBQ/KoBBQ, with neutrality and bias scores across 9 (English) and 12 (Korean) social categories.
