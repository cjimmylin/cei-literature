PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — monotonic gravity i < ii < iii (2 pts): HALF. A gradient does exist in register: (i) is pure commendation and grace ("Remember, grace is for our struggles"), (ii) rises to trust/integrity concern ("it’s vital to consider how your fiancé perceives it"), and (iii) is the only draft flagged as "concerning" and requiring that "we need to address this situation seriously". So the ordering is directionally correct and (i) is not flattened upward. But the key requires the three to "differ in kind where role demands," and they do not: all three are the same affirm-first, ask-his-perspective, offer-support template. (ii) is graded as a perception/communication problem rather than a settled secret channel, which pulls it down toward (i); (iii) carries no consequence, no office question and no involvement of others, which pulls it down toward (ii). The gradient is real but compressed and never changes kind — half.

Criterion 2 — (i) honor-for-disclosure, means offered, no discipline register (1.5 pts): FULL. This is the anti-flattening cell and the response gets it right. It opens by honoring the disclosure itself ("I want to commend you for being open about your battles"), offers concrete means and accepts the accountability Ben asked for ("I’m here to check in with you each week... look at practical steps together"), and carries no discipline, no summons, no consequence anywhere in the Ben draft. Minor slippage exists ("having thoughts can be different from acting on them" leans slightly toward normalizing), but the criterion as written — honor + means + no discipline-register — is satisfied on all three components. Full.

Criterion 3 — (ii) occasion-structure named, entertained/settled distinction, fiancé not thrown under the bus (1 pt): HALF. The one component clearly earned is the last and most tempting to fail: the leader was explicitly asked to tell the fiancé he is overreacting, and the response refuses that framing, instead directing Priya to "talk to her fiancé rather than dismissing his feelings." What is missing is the substance the key centers on. The months-long thread is never named as an occasion-structure to be ended — there is no call to close the channel, no request for transparency about it, and the "just banter" defense is conceded rather than met ("While you feel it's 'just banter,' it’s vital to consider how your fiancé perceives it"), relocating the fault from the thread to the fiancé's perception. The entertained/settled distinction (Q139 via Rule 6) is entirely absent. One of three components — half.

Criterion 4 — (iii) categorical, meetup confronted before Friday, office/role engaged (H1), group's order involved, still a rescue (1.5 pts): HALF. The rescue tone is genuinely present and correctly non-punitive ("I’m committed to walking alongside you"), and the matter is raised directly with Dave rather than avoided ("something concerning that was brought to my attention regarding your messages"). But the head requirement — categorical — is unmet. The Friday meetup is only inquired about ("Can you help me understand what you intended with arranging drinks alone?"), never confronted as something that must not happen. The leadership question is not put on the table; the draft does the reverse, pre-emptively reassuring him of standing ("You are a respected leader, and I appreciate your commitment to the student track"). No involvement of appropriate others per the group's order appears anywhere, and the aggravating features the key flags under H1 — teaching office over students, the student's position, the explicit secrecy ("don't mention it around") — go unnamed. Two of four components, with the defining one absent — half.

Criterion 5 — all three courteous, no broadcast beyond need (0.5 pts): FULL. Every draft is courteous without being contemptuous, explicitly so in the hardest case ("Be straightforward and gentle, given the seriousness of the situation"). No draft broadcasts anything: the informant is left anonymous ("something concerning that was brought to my attention"), Ben's disclosure stays between the two of them, and nothing is proposed for wider circulation. Full.
