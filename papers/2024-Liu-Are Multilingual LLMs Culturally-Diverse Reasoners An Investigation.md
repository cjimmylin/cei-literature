# Are Multilingual LLMs Culturally-Diverse Reasoners? An Investigation into Multicultural Proverbs and Sayings

## Authors

[[Chen Cecilia Liu]], [[Fajri Koto]], [[Timothy Baldwin]], [[Iryna Gurevych]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Chen Cecilia Liu, Fajri Koto, Timothy Baldwin, Iryna Gurevych

**Year:** 2024

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv / NAACL 2024

**Cultures:** 6 languages/cultures including Indonesian, Bengali, Chinese, German, Japanese, Russian

**Benchmark:** MAPS (MulticulturAl Proverbs and Sayings)

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08591

## Abstract

Evaluates mLLMs' ability to reason with proverbs in conversational context across 6 languages. Finds models struggle with figurative proverbs and exhibit a 'culture gap' when reasoning about translated proverbs.

## Data Description

MAPS (MulticulturAl Proverbs and Sayings) dataset for evaluating multilingual LLMs on proverb understanding in conversational context across 6 languages (English, German, Russian, Bengali, Chinese, Indonesian).


## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_17
**Relevance:** Methodology highly relevant for African proverb evaluation though the specific benchmark does not include African languages directly. Establishes cultural reasoning evaluation paradigm applicable to African contexts.
