# Bence Bago

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bence Bago is an Assistant Professor of Social Psychology at Tilburg University. He earned his PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience in Paris. His research explores the cognitive and emotional underpinnings of human reasoning and belief formation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work focuses on dual-process theories of thinking, investigating how intuitive and analytic processes interact in decision-making and moral judgment. He also studies the psychological mechanisms that make people susceptible to "fake news" and misinformation.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Bago has published extensively on the "Trolley Problem" and other situational factors that shape moral judgments. His research has over 3,300 citations and contributes to the understanding of rationality and cognitive control.

## Major Publications

- [Situational factors shape moral judgments in the Real World](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspa0000294) (2022) - *Cross-cultural study on the validity of moral judgment research.*
- [False beliefs about COVID-19: A dual-process perspective](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797620939054) (2020) - *Influential research on belief formation during the pandemic.*
