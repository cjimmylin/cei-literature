# Maria De-Arteaga

## Research Summary

Maria De-Arteaga is an Associate Professor in the Department of Data, Analytics, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence at ESADE Business School. Previously an Assistant Professor at McCombs School of Business, UT Austin. Her research focuses on algorithmic fairness and human-AI complementarity, characterizing and mitigating algorithmic biases, and developing algorithms and sociotechnical interventions to improve AI-supported decision making. Published in top conferences including ACM CHI, FAccT, IJCAI, and CSCW.
