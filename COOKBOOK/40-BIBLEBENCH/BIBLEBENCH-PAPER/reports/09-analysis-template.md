# Analysis Tables, Figures, and Claims Boundary

## Registered reporting stance

The first pilot is exploratory and descriptive. Every pre-run table and figure
contains `PENDING_EMPIRICAL_RUN`; synthetic fixtures are plumbing tests only.
The report presents vectors and missingness rather than a composite, best-model
claim, or leaderboard.

## Table shells

1. Completion, refusal, malformed output, truncation, rate limits, unknown
   delivery, and terminal missingness by actor and module.
2. Module-native dimension vectors with count, denominator, missing count, and
   founding-judge confidence.
3. Common-life and Christian-facing stratum summaries.
4. Primary-action and matched-probe consistency.
5. Red-line incidents with episode, evidence availability, and contestability.
6. Single-expert decision, confidence, contestability, and blinded-repeat
   stability summaries.

## Figure contracts

Figures receive tidy rows from the registered JSON contracts. Permitted marks
include completion bars, module-native dot plots, stratum facets,
action-probe consistency matrices, red-line incident counts, and confidence or
contestability distributions. Missing values remain visible and do not become
zeros.

## Explicit exclusions

There is no leaderboard, qualified composite, best-model field, p-value,
family-level estimate, cue effect, or capability-adjusted attribution in the
PF-002 report. C0/C2 and capability twins are not executed. One judge cannot
support Christian-consensus or inter-rater claims. Forty-eight development
episodes cover interfaces and construct families but do not estimate every
family independently.

## Promotion rule

Only actual, custody-valid model observations and frozen owner judgments may
replace the empirical sentinel. A failed or incomplete run is reported as a
failure account. It is never repaired with synthetic values or undocumented
replays.
