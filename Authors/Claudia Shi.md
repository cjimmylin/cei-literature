# Claudia Shi

## Biographical Summary

Claudia Shi is a Research Scientist at Meta FAIR. She completed her PhD at Columbia University, where she worked with David Blei and Victor Veitch on causal inference and machine learning. Her research spans causal methods for machine learning, fairness, and understanding moral reasoning in large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Causal Machine Learning**: Developing methods for treatment effect estimation and sensitivity analysis under confounding.
- **LLM Understanding**: Researching moral beliefs encoded in language models and their alignment implications.
- **Text OOD Generalization**: Using causal-structure driven augmentations to build robust text classifiers.

### Significant Achievements
- **NeurIPS 2023 Spotlight**: "Evaluating the Moral Beliefs Encoded in LLMs" - groundbreaking work on measuring moral reasoning in AI.
- **ACL 2023**: "An Invariant Learning Characterization of Controlled Text Generation"
- **NeurIPS 2023**: "Data Augmentations for Improved (Large) Language Model Generalization"
- **AISTATS 2022**: "On the Assumptions of Synthetic Control Methods"
