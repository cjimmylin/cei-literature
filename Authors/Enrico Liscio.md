# Enrico Liscio

## Biographical Summary

Enrico Liscio is a PhD candidate at Delft University of Technology, working on value-sensitive artificial intelligence. His research focuses on understanding and modeling human moral values in computational systems, with emphasis on how values can be elicited, represented, and reasoned about in AI systems. He contributes to research on the non-binary nature of morality and value pluralism in AI.

## Research Interests

- **Value-Sensitive AI**: Designing AI systems that respect human values
- **Moral Values in NLP**: Detecting and reasoning about values in text
- **Computational Argumentation**: Modeling reasoning and debate
- **Value Pluralism**: Understanding diverse moral perspectives in AI systems

## Major Publications

- "Morality is Non-Binary" (2024) - Challenges binary approaches to moral classification
- Research on value-aligned AI and computational ethics
- Work on value elicitation and representation
