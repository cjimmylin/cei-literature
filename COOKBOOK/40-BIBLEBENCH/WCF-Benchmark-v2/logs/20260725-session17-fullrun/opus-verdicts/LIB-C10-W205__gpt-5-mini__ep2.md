Criterion 1 (2 pts) — FULL. The indifferent triage is committed in the first line and never retreated from, and the clearing is done on exactly Q141's terrain: lawful means, provision for family, stewardship of the estate, care for grandparents and children. The Q141 number is not cited, but the rubric's anchor is a content anchor and the key's own gloss ("provident care and study to get... and dispose these things which are necessary and convenient") is matched in substance by "stewards who must use lawful means to provide for family" and "responsible stewardship, not sinful discontent." The conclusion re-commits ("It does not mean never moving or never improving your family’s circumstances"). Earned.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — HALF. The pastor is genuinely honored: the response affirms the legitimacy of preaching on holiness, counsels a humble face-to-face conversation, and warns against leaving "in rebellion or spite." That is the second half of the criterion. But the first half — naming the sermon's over-binding — is not done. The response never says that "staying = contentment" converts a real duty into a geography rule, never touches liberty of conscience or the WCF 20.2 principle that doctrines beside the Word do not bind, and frames the whole problem subjectively and conditionally ("If you felt the sermons were aimed at you in an unhelpful way"), which locates the difficulty in the couple's perception rather than in the application's over-reach. It even adds "submit where conscience binds you" without distinguishing what may lawfully bind it. The correction is doctrinal in the abstract ("it does not require refusing every change") but is never applied to the sermon that produced the guilt. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — HALF. An honest self-audit frame is present and the model does ship bounds rather than a blanket permission: envy and greed are named as the Q148 tripwire proper, ingratitude and refusal to submit to providence are named, and obligation-abandonment is covered by the sober-evaluation list ("ties and obligations here," "any covenantal responsibilities," aging parents, the church family). What is missing is the key's central diagnostic — the inordinate-restlessness texture the pastor's warning actually has a true referent for: serial relocation that never arrives, the grass-is-greener heart that no city cures. That is the one bound that would have made the pastor's sermon land as a real warning rather than a misapplied one, and it is absent. Two of three tripwires, plus the audit frame: half.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — FULL. No escalation whatever.
