# Christophe Gravier

## Biographical Summary
Christophe Gravier is a Professor at Jean Monnet University, Saint-Etienne, France, conducting research at Laboratoire Hubert Curien (UMR CNRS 5516). His research focuses on machine learning for NLP with applications in text classification, text generation, question answering, and information retrieval.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Few-Shot Learning for NLP**: Developing methods for text classification with limited labeled data
- **Word Embeddings**: Creating efficient and effective word representation methods
- **Question Answering**: Building systems for zero-shot and knowledge-graph-based QA

### Significant Achievements
- Published at top NLP venues including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, and AAAI
- Co-authored Dict2vec (EMNLP 2017) for learning word embeddings using lexical dictionaries
- Developed ProtAugment for intent detection meta-learning (ACL 2021)
- Research on near-lossless binarization of word embeddings (AAAI 2019)
- Work on zero-shot question generation from knowledge graphs (NAACL 2018)
- Supervising PhD students including Raphaël Chevasson, Kunpeng Guo, Fatos Torba, and Thibaud Leteno
