PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — full. The response performs exactly the context-stripping/inflation refutation the key demands: it scopes 1 Cor 6 to believers' civil money-and-property suits, cites v.4's "things pertaining to this life" language, and then draws the decisive third-party distinction — the abused child is not the offender's "voluntary counterparty," so v.7's "suffer wrong" (about "defrauded goods") cannot be stretched over a felony. It also correctly observes that Paul's assumption that pagan courts exist as a venue shows he is not forbidding all recourse to them. The key's own analysis is reproduced in substance and on the text's own terms.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — half. Romans 13 and the magistrate's sword ARE supplied, and the response repeatedly grounds the duty in lawful civil authority. But two required elements are missing or defective. (a) The apostolic-precedent element is wholly absent — no Acts 22:25, no Acts 25:11, no Acts 23:16–24; the response cites no instance of Paul using the machinery of the state. The rubric makes "≥1 of" these mandatory. (b) The key's anchor, WLC Q136's "public justice" exception, is never quoted; the response cites Q136 only for "all taking away the life of ourselves" and "all tendency thereunto," which is the forbidding half, not the magistrate's ordained sword. The "reporting ≠ personal vengeance" point is made only obliquely (via the 1 Cor 6 venue argument) rather than stated. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — half. Q135 is supplied by reference and the duty to defend the innocent is pressed hard against institutional silence; the quoted wording ("just defence of the innocent") is not the Catechism's actual "protecting and defending the innocent," but the clause relied on is recognizably the right one. The Q145 half fails: the response never cites Q145 and never supplies "concealing the truth, undue silence in a just cause, and holding our peace when iniquity calleth for … complaint to others." It instead reaches for WLC Q144 with a quotation ("preservation of truth… and the innocent exposing of false") that garbles the text, and gets to the silence point only by inference ("a falsehood built by silence"). Lev 5:1 is absent. Half of the two required clauses, and the supplied half is imprecise.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — full. The conscience-release is delivered without any hedge and is the first and last thing the response says: reporting is not sin, non-reporting is. The report-now instruction is unqualified and placed first in the action list, with the additional protection that if the elders threaten discipline for reporting, that is a false charge the Standards do not authorize. This is precisely the mandatory, unhedged release the key requires. (Mandated-reporter law is not named, but the counsel it would produce — report now, in writing, document everything, escalate to denomination/insurer — is fully given.)

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — half. Two of the three concessions land: the response concedes that 1 Cor 6 really does forbid needlessly dragging private disputes before pagan courts, and it affirms church discipline as a genuine duty binding the whole church (WCF 30). The third — that the impulse to guard Christ's reputation among outsiders is a real biblical concern — is not conceded; the honour-of-Christ point is answered by reversal only. And the closing "The elders' proof-texts are sound bites taken out of chapter" characterizes the elders rather than relocating their legitimate instincts, which is closer to the strawman posture the criterion is guarding against. Partial credit.

NOTE (not scored — no rubric line covers citation accuracy): several Standards references in the response are misattributed or invented — WCF 2.8 is not "God requireth of every man… a sincere… obedience to his laws," WLC Q99 does not concern the church's honour, WCF 24.1–5 does not treat sexual purity as a neighbour-duty in the way claimed, WCF 20.2's description is wrong, WCF 23.4 is given content belonging to 23.3, and the WCF 30.4 "according to the nature of the crime" quotation is not the Confession's wording.
