# Viacheslav Vasilev

## Research Summary

Viacheslav Vasilev is pursuing a PhD in Mathematics and Computer Science at Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT) from 2023 to 2026, while also affiliated with Sber AI. His research focuses on generative AI, particularly diffusion models, text-to-video generation, text-to-3D conversion, 3D computer vision, and head avatars. His PhD advisor is Andrey Kuznetsov (2023-Present), and his MSc advisor was Victor Lempitsky (2021-2023). His recent work includes publications on the Kandinsky text-to-image models and Russian cultural code benchmarks for text-to-image generation.
