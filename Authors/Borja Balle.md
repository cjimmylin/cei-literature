# Borja Balle

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Borja Balle (Borja de Balle Pigem) is a Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. He earned his PhD from UPC and completed a postdoc at McGill University. He previously served as a lecturer at Lancaster University and was a senior ML scientist at Amazon. He is a leading expert in the theoretical and practical foundations of differential privacy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Borja's research focuses on all aspects of privacy-preserving machine learning. He investigates the theoretical limits of differential privacy and develops scalable algorithms for private data analysis. He is a primary contributor to JAX-Privacy, a library for differentially private training of large-scale deep learning models.

### Significant Achievements
He is highly recognized for his work on making differentially private image classification accurate at scale. His tutorials and publications on the core definitions of differential privacy have been instrumental in educating the broader machine learning community on privacy-safe research practices.

## Major Publications
