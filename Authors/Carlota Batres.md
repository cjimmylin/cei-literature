# Carlota Batres

## Biographical Summary

Carlota Batres is an Assistant Professor of Psychology at Franklin & Marshall College. She earned her PhD from the University of St Andrews, specializing in facial perception and the social judgments we make based on appearance. Her work explores how environmental factors and biological traits influence our aesthetic and social preferences.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on the "Psychology of Facial Perception." She investigates how humans judge traits like dominance, health, and attractiveness in faces. Recently, her work has expanded to explore how these human biases are replicated or amplified in "Generated Faces" by AI, and how humans perceive AI-driven avatars compared to natural human faces.

### Significant Achievements
Batres has published extensively on the evolutionary underpinnings of social perception. Her work is increasingly relevant to the "Digital Ethics" community as AI-generated media becomes indistinguishable from reality, posing new questions about how our biological hard-wiring for social trust can be "hacked" by synthetic entities.
