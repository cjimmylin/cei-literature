## Abstract

Multilingual benchmark covering 61 languages with NLU, commonsense reasoning, factual recall, knowledge-based QA, academic reasoning, and truthfulness evaluation. Notable performance drops in Burmese and Tagalog when normalized against English. Covers Thai and Burmese. Truthfulness evaluation dimension relevant to values assessment. Documents significant performance gaps in Burmese.

## Benchmark

**Name:** MuBench

## Data Description

MUBENCH is a multilingual benchmark covering 61 languages and 12 task categories (NLU, commonsense reasoning, factual recall, knowledge-based QA, academic reasoning, truthfulness) with ~3.9M aligned samples across languages, including code-switched variants.
