## Data Description

AraLingBench is a fully human-annotated benchmark of 150 expert-authored multiple-choice questions assessing Arabic linguistic competence across five categories (grammar, morphology, spelling, reading comprehension, and syntax), designed to isolate fundamental Arabic linguistic understanding in LLMs.
