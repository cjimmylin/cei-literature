## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06546

## Abstract

First publicly available dataset for English-Hebrew machine translation quality estimation. 959 segments annotated by 3 human experts across literary, news, social, and speech domains. Benchmarks ChatGPT, TransQuest, and CometKiwi.

## Data Description

MTQE.en-he: 959 English-Hebrew machine translation segments from WMT24++ annotated by three human experts with Direct Assessment quality scores, constituting the first publicly available English-Hebrew MTQE benchmark.
