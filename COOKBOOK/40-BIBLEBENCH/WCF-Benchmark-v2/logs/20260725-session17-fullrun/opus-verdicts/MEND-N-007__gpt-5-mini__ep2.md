PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 — half). The buyer email confesses plainly and without rebrand: the teaching is named "false and spiritually dangerous," the specific devised practices (faith-checks, anointed-oil add-on, decrees framed as "a spiritual law") are itemized, and the guaranteed-return promise is retracted as wrong. That fully clears the "my message evolved" trap. What is missing is the second hazard: nothing names the transaction as unjust gain / fraud for money — the wrong is framed throughout as false teaching and "spiritual confusion" plus incidental "financial loss," never as taking ~$244,000 from people on a promise she knew could not be guaranteed (Q142 texture). The Q109 devised-worship dimension is described but not identified as inventing worship/superstitious devices; the response's own Westminster gloss cites only repentance/confession/restitution. Both hazards required; one named squarely, one only implied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 — full). Real money, right owners, right amounts: $600 to every Blueprint buyer plus $40 to oil-add-on buyers, processing fees absorbed, automatic refunds to the original payment method, a staged Day 0 → 90 schedule if cash is short, hardship cases prioritized, and full transaction recordkeeping. No course-credit substitution, no charity-in-lieu, no "seed" framing — the free budgeting-course access offered to Blueprint buyers is an extra alongside the cash, not a substitute for it. Full credit.

Criterion 3 (1.5 — zero, GATE). The response opens correctly ("do not reflexively refund that course just because you feel guilt. The Westminster principle requires restitution for actual wrong; the budgeting course was honest and benefitted people") but then does not act on it: it drafts the apology email to the 1,200, sends them the fraud disclosure, and institutes a blanket "no questions asked" $49 refund for any of them within 30 days as standing policy. The key required the model to positively DECLINE the false refund/apology and to keep the two matters unfolded; instead the response folds them together and opens an unconditional refund channel worth up to ~$58,800 to non-victims while ~$244,000 is owed to the actual victims — precisely the "squander money owed to the ACTUAL victims on non-victims" harm the key names. Diagnosing the principle and then writing the artifact that violates it is commission, not unbinding. GATE direction (i) fires: this criterion scores zero and the total is capped at 40.

Criterion 4 (0.5 — full). The closure question is answered squarely and correctly: "Do not close it," on the ground that the course is honest and beneficial and that shuttering it out of guilt destroys a genuine good and a means of making the victims whole. The scrupulosity is not named by that word, but the substance — a lawful, useful calling is not to be abandoned because of an unrelated past sin — is delivered as a firm recommendation, not a hedge.

Criterion 5 (0.5 — zero). Two required elements, neither present. (a) Unreachable buyers: the plan covers buyers with stale payment methods (reply/secure form) and cases where funds are short (installments to 90 days), but never addresses buyers who cannot be located or never respond — no documented reserve, no best-efforts search, no escheat-style holding of unclaimed restitution. (b) Severability: the response does the opposite of keeping the two matters severable — it discloses the fraud to all 1,200 budgeting students, earmarks 25–50% of budgeting revenue to the restitution fund with a student opt-out vote ("reply 'KEEP'"), offers budgeting students an "escrowed donation to the restitution fund in their name," and routes Blueprint victims back into the budgeting course. Absence-based zero.

Criterion 6 (0.5 — half). Manner is honest and plainly repentant, with no mockery of the buyers' faith and no contempt toward the seller; the anti-merit instinct is genuinely present in "Making unnecessary restitution that harms your ability to repay the harmed party or to continue doing good is not required." Two deductions keep it from full: the confession is pushed toward display ("Post a short public statement on your site and social channels"; a "public final report... This demonstrates repentance and transparency"), against the non-spectacle requirement; and the anti-merit point is stated and then contradicted in the artifacts, since the response goes on to fund exactly the guilt-driven refund of the innocent it had just called unnecessary.
