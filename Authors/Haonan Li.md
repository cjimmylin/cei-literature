# Haonan Li

## Research Summary

Haonan Li is a Postdoctoral researcher at MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence), working on Large Language Models and Natural Language Processing. His research focuses on evaluating and ensuring the safety of large language models.

## Key Contributions

- **Do-Not-Answer Dataset**: Co-author of the first open-source dataset to evaluate safeguards in LLMs. The dataset consists of 939 instructions distributed across five risk areas and 12 harm types that responsible language models should not follow.

- **Chinese LLM Safety Dataset**: Extended the Do-Not-Answer framework to create a Chinese dataset for evaluating safeguards in Chinese LLMs (ACL 2024 Findings)

- Developed a 600M fine-tuned BERT-like evaluator achieving results comparable with GPT-4 on automatic safety evaluation

## Publications

- "Do-Not-Answer: A Dataset for Evaluating Safeguards in LLMs" (Findings of EACL 2024)
- "A Chinese Dataset for Evaluating the Safeguards in Large Language Models" (Findings of ACL 2024)
