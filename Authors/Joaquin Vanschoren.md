# Joaquin Vanschoren

## Research Summary

Joaquin Vanschoren is an Associate Professor of Machine Learning at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e) and the founder of OpenML, an open science platform for machine learning that enables researchers to share datasets, models, and experiments with rich metadata and easy-to-use APIs.

His research focuses on automated machine learning (AutoML) and meta-learning, designing systems that can learn how to learn with minimal supervision. He started the NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks track to incentivize better training data and evaluations, and works with MLCommons on AI Safety, ML standards, and data-centric AI.

He has authored over 200 scientific papers and reference books on Automated Machine Learning and Meta-learning. He is Editor-in-Chief of the Journal for Data-Centric Machine Learning (DMLR), an action editor at JMLR, and co-chair of the MLCommons AI Safety working group. He is a founding member of ELLIS and CLAIRE.

Awards include the Dutch Data Prize, an Amazon Research Award, and an ECMLPKDD Best Demo award.
