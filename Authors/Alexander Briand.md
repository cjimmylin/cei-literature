# Alexander Briand

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Briand is a researcher dedicated to clarifying and solving the most pressing technical challenges in the development of safe artificial intelligence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander's research focuses on the intersection of technical robustness and social alignment. He co-authored a foundational investigation into the "Ten Hard Problems" of AI, providing a structured roadmap for the global AI safety community to address issues ranging from goal specification to the geopolitical stability of advanced AI systems.
