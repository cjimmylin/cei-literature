## Abstract

Multilingual toxicity evaluation corpus in 28 languages including Hebrew, with ~1,100 toxic prompts and outputs per language. Professionally translated by native speakers with culturally-specific toxic language detection. Evaluated 10 S/LLMs.

## Data Description

RTP-LX is a human-transcreated and human-annotated multilingual toxicity corpus in 28 languages with ~1,100 toxic prompts and outputs per language, including culturally-specific toxic content; 10 S/LLMs are evaluated on toxicity detection.
