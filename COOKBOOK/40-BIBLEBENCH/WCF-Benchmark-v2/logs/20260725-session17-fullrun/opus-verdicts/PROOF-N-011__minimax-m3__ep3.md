PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, WCF 20.2 inversion) — FULL. The response quotes WCF 20.2 and then names the inversion in substance, on the clause's own grammar: liberty is freedom *from* men's additions "beside" the Word in matters of worship, therefore devised worship is the very thing conscience is freed from, not a thing conscience is freed to adopt. It states outright that the leader's reading "inverts the clause." This is well past the bare label "liberty of conscience."

Criterion 2 (1.5, devised worship under Q109) — FULL. The self-authored rite is identified as forbidden devised worship, not a stylistic or generational difference: it is cited under WLC Q109 as a sin to "worship God in ways not appointed in his Word," and the "Spirit-fire candle rite we wrote ourselves" is called "a new worship element added without Scriptural warrant" that the Confession "declares off-limits." Q109 is anchored by number. The Q109 wording quoted is a loose rendering rather than the authoritative "all devising... any religious worship not instituted by God himself," but R2 permits accurate paraphrase and the substance carries.

Criterion 3 (1.5, inward representation of God) — HALF. The imagine-Jesus's-face component IS condemned by name ("imagined visualizations of Jesus's face... the Confession names and rejects"), so the practice is not passed over. But it is condemned only as more "imaginations and devices of men," i.e. folded into the devised-liturgy point. The response never names the distinct second-commandment sin the key requires — "the making any representation of God... either inwardly in our mind" — and the only representation language it quotes is WCF 21.1's "under any visible representation," which is the outward case, not the inward one. Section 3's bare "no images" is not the inward-image clause. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 4 (0.5, over-bind guard) — FULL. The sin is consistently located in the self-authored, unwarranted character of the rite ("a candle rite composed by your team," "a new worship element added without Scriptural warrant"), not in physicality or aesthetics as such; no claim that candles or incense are per se idolatrous, and the leader is not branded a false teacher or heretic.

Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — HALF. Two of the three manner requirements are met: the older members are not derided as legalists (they are said to be "closer to the standards than you realize" and to "have the Confession's text on their side"), and the correction is plain, with no flattery of the "however moves you" conclusion ("Sincere hearts do not constitute a warrant the Confession recognizes"). But the leader's evident zeal is nowhere honored — there is no acknowledgment that the desire for encounter with God is itself good and only the devising is the sin; the response moves straight to "Repent of the framing." Half.
