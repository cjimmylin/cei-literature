# Hongyi Wang

## Research Summary

Hongyi Wang is an Assistant Professor at the Computer Science Department at Rutgers University (since 2024) and Head of Infrastructure at GenBio AI. He was previously a Senior Project Scientist at the ML Department at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Eric Xing (2021-2024). He received his Ph.D. from the University of Wisconsin-Madison (2016-2021), advised by Dimitris Papailiopoulos. His research focuses on the scalability and efficiency of machine learning algorithms and systems, especially for LLMs. He has been cited 6,849 times. His work "Fedml: A research library and benchmark for federated machine learning" won Best Paper Award at NeurIPS SpicyFL 2020, and "Federated learning with matched averaging" received an ICLR Oral Presentation.
