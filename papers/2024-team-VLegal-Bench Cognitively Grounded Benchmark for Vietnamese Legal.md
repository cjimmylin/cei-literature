## Abstract

First comprehensive benchmark for Vietnamese legal LLM evaluation with 10,450 expert-verified samples across 22 tasks at 5 reasoning levels. Includes ethical reasoning evaluation assessing fairness, impartiality, privacy protection, and bias detection in legal contexts.

## Data Description

VLegal-Bench is the first comprehensive Vietnamese legal reasoning benchmark, comprising 10,450 expert-verified samples across 22 tasks at 5 Bloom's taxonomy cognitive levels, evaluating LLMs on Vietnamese civil law comprehension, retrieval, reasoning, generation, and ethics/bias detection.
