# LaoPLM: Pre-trained Language Models for Lao

## Authors

[[Guilin Lin]], [[Jie Fu]], [[Weiwei Sun]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Guilin Lin, Jie Fu, Weiwei Sun

**Year:** 2021

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** ACL Anthology (LREC 2022)

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.05896

## Abstract

First transformer-based pre-trained language models for Lao (BERT-Small, BERT-Base, ELECTRA-Small, ELECTRA-Base). Evaluated on POS tagging and text classification. Constructs a Lao text classification dataset (2,968 news articles, 8 categories). Foundation Lao language model and first Lao NLP evaluation datasets. Relevant as infrastructure for future Lao values/ethics evaluation.

## Benchmark

**Name:** LaoPLM

## Data Description

First transformer-based pre-trained language models for Lao (BERT-Small/Base, ELECTRA-Small/Base) plus a Lao news classification dataset (2,968 articles, 8 categories).
