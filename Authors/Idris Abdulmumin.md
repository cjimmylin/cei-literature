# Idris Abdulmumin

## Research Summary

Dr. Idris Abdulmumin is affiliated with the Computer Science Department at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Kaduna, Nigeria. His research focuses on understanding how emotions are expressed across languages, which is vital for building culturally-aware and inclusive NLP systems. He actively collaborates with international research communities such as Masakhane, HausaNLP, LITHME, and the Open Language Data Initiative, and has contributed to several Semantic Evaluation shared tasks including AfriSenti 2023, SemRel 2024, and BRIGHTER 2025. He is passionate about mentoring students and early-career researchers, striving to integrate ethical, human-centric approaches into his work.

## Key Contributions

- Co-developed HausaNLP initiative, a curated catalog aggregating datasets, tools, and research works for Hausa
- Created hauWE (Hausa Words Embedding) for Natural Language Processing
- Co-authored the Hausa Visual Genome for multi-modal English to Hausa machine translation
- Contributed to HausaHate Corpus comprising 2,000 manually annotated comments for hate speech detection
- Authored systematic literature review on Hausa Natural Language Processing
