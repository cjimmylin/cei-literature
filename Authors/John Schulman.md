# John Schulman

## Research Summary

John Schulman is one of the most influential figures in modern AI, serving as a co-founder of OpenAI and the architect behind ChatGPT. He invented two foundational algorithms used in training large language models: Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO, 2017) and Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO, 2015). His work on reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) was fundamental to aligning large language models with human preferences.

Schulman received his PhD in Computer Science from UC Berkeley under Pieter Abbeel, focusing on robotics and reinforcement learning. He graduated from Caltech with a degree in physics in 2010. From 2022-2024, he co-led OpenAI's post-training team responsible for ChatGPT development.

In August 2024, Schulman joined Anthropic to focus on AI alignment research. In February 2025, he announced he was leaving to join Thinking Machines Lab as Chief Scientist.
