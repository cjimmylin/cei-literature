# Jason Ross Brown

## Research Summary

Jason Ross Brown is a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Cambridge working on AI alignment. His research interests include AI Safety, AI Alignment, Reward Learning, and Transformers.

Brown has made several notable contributions to AI safety research. He helped introduce the AgentMisalignment benchmark, a suite of realistic scenarios in which LLM agents have the opportunity to display misaligned behavior, addressing the gap in understanding how likely agents are to attempt misaligned behaviors in real-world settings.

His work on manipulation risk provides the first systematic methodology for integrating manipulation risk into AI safety governance, offering AI companies a concrete foundation to assess and mitigate these threats before deployment. The research presents a safety case framework structured around three core lines of argument: inability, control, and trustworthiness.

He also developed KL-regularised Q-Learning (KLQ), a new action-value RL method for LM-RLHF settings that addresses limitations in PPO's handling of KL-divergence constraints.
