# Rajkumar Pujari

## Research Summary

Rajkumar Pujari is an Applied Scientist II at Amazon AGI, specializing in LLM post-training and graph-based work within Amazon's AGI-F team. He is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Purdue University, working under Prof. Dan Goldwasser. His PhD thesis focuses on conceptualizing and building systems for contextualized pragmatic language understanding, specifically frameworks for wholistic political text contextualization and cultural context grounding for conversations.
