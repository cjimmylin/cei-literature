## Abstract

TMMLU+ is a comprehensive dataset for Traditional Chinese massive multitask language understanding with 66 subjects from elementary to professional level, 22,690 questions. Six times larger than original TMMLU. Finds Traditional Chinese models still trail behind Simplified Chinese counterparts.

## Data Description

TMMLU+ is a Traditional Chinese multi-task language understanding benchmark with 22,690 multi-choice questions across 66 subjects from elementary to professional level, six times larger than its predecessor TMMLU. Evaluated on 23 open-weight Chinese LLMs and several closed-source models.
