## Abstract

2,600 questions in Bahasa Malaysia evaluating LLMs on Malaysian culture across six pillars: arts, attire, customs, entertainment, food, and religion. Novel open-ended MCQ format reduces guessing bias. LLMs' performance drops at least 17% with open-ended format.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** Targeted cultural values benchmark for Malaysia/Malay language, capturing diversity of Malaysian society including religious and customary practices.
