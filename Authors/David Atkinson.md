# David Atkinson

## Biographical Summary

David Atkinson is a researcher working on AI ethics and the evaluation of large language models. His research contributes to developing benchmarks and evaluation methods for assessing the ethical behavior of AI systems.

## Research Focus

Atkinson's work focuses on evaluating the ethical capabilities and limitations of large language models. He has contributed to the LLM Ethics Benchmark, which provides systematic methods for assessing how AI systems handle ethical questions and dilemmas.
