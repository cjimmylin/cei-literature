## Abstract

ACLUE (Ancient Chinese Language Understanding Evaluation) is a benchmark with 15 tasks covering 5 categories (Vocabulary, Syntax, Semantics, Reasoning, Knowledge) derived from texts spanning from the Xia Dynasty (2070 BCE) to the Ming Dynasty (1368 CE). This range encompasses the core periods of Confucian and Taoist text composition and commentary. The benchmark is integrated into EleutherAI's lm-evaluation-harness, has a public leaderboard, and both code and data are fully available, making it highly implementation-ready.

## Dataset Information

15 tasks spanning phonetic, lexical, syntactic, semantic, inference, and knowledge domains; multiple-choice format
