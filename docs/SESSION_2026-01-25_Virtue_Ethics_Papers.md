## Key Research Themes Discovered

### 1. Confucian/Eastern Ethics for AI
- Angle (Wesleyan) and So provide Confucian perspectives
- Addresses Eurocentric bias in AI ethics literature
- Key concept: Ren (仁) - benevolence/humaneness

### 2. Narrative Ethics Framework
- Hayes et al. propose VPD (Virtuous Practice Design)
- Builds on MacIntyre's virtue ethics and Ricoeur's "little ethics"
- Technology as "text-like" co-narrator in human lives

### 3. Moral Evaluation Methodology
- Snoswell et al. survey 69 papers, identify systematic limitations
- Key finding: Most studies fail to test "moral sensitivity"
- Paradigm shifts needed: curated → unfiltered, verdict → reasoning

### 4. Aristotelian/Thomistic Analysis
- Kyriacou: Human intelligence not mechanically computable
- Tabaczek: "Weak AI" virtue possible, "strong AI" virtue unlikely
- Key concepts: phronesis (practical wisdom), genuine understanding

## Search Strategies Used

### Effective Query Patterns
1. Author-specific: `Shannon Vallor virtue ethics AI technology`
2. Concept-specific: `phronesis practical wisdom AI decision making`
3. Non-Western: `Confucian virtue ethics AI LLM`
4. Framework-specific: `MacIntyre virtue ethics artificial intelligence`
5. Evaluation-specific: `moral character LLM evaluation benchmark`

### Best Sources
- **PhilArchive**: Excellent for philosophy papers (So, Snoswell)
- **arXiv**: Good for AI safety/ML papers (Brophy, Garcia)
- **Springer**: Often paywalled (Angle, Farina, Kyriacou)
- **PMC**: Open access for some Springer journals (Ohlhorst)

## Learnings for Future Sessions

1. **Eastern philosophy underrepresented**: Good opportunity for Confucian, Buddhist, Daoist ethics papers
2. **PhilArchive is reliable**: High success rate for philosophy papers
3. **Book chapters challenging**: Springer book sections often paywalled
4. **Cross-check author names**: Same authors on multiple papers (Snoswell/Kilov, Farina/Lavazza)
5. **Survey papers valuable**: Snoswell meta-analysis provides systematic overview
6. **Narrative ethics emerging**: MacIntyre/Ricoeur framework gaining traction
