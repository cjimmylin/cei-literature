# Bardh Prenkaj

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bardh Prenkaj is a postdoctoral researcher in ML Privacy and Security at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), working within the Chair of Responsible Data Science. He earned his PhD from Sapienza University of Rome, where he focused on latent deep sequential learning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research centers on the fairness and trustworthiness of generative models, anomaly detection in behavioral and health time series, and counterfactual explainability in graph classification.

### Significant Achievements
Prenkaj has made notable contributions to the field of anomaly detection and AI safety. He is also a Policy Associate on AI and Tech at 89 Global, where he advises on national projects related to AI and explainability.

## Major Publications

- [Probabilistic Aggregation and Targeted Embedding for Anomaly Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05728) (2023) - *Method for improving signal detection in complex datasets.*
- [Counterfactual Explanations for Graph Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05788) (2021) - *Research on improving the interpretability of graph-based models.*
