# Vishal Narnaware

## Research Summary

Vishal Narnaware is a researcher affiliated with the University of Central Florida's Center for Research in Computer Vision. His research focuses on privacy and bias in AI, continual learning, and visual stereotype bias in large multimodal models. He has authored research on benchmarking visual stereotype bias in LMMs (BBQ-V) published in 2026. He previously had experience at Indian School of Business.
