# Sunayana Rane

## Research Summary

Sunayana Rane is affiliated with Princeton University, focusing on AI alignment at the intersection of AI, law, and cognitive science. Her research includes work on concept alignment as a prerequisite for value alignment, the reasonable person standard for AI (ICML 2024), and vision language models through the lens of the binding problem (NeurIPS 2024). She collaborates with Thomas L. Griffiths and other researchers at Princeton on topics related to AI alignment, representational alignment, and cognitive science approaches to AI.
