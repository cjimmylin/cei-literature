# Alexey Kurakin

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexey Kurakin is a software engineer and researcher at Google Research, Brain Team. He earned his PhD from ITMO University. He is a pioneer in the field of adversarial machine learning, widely recognized for his work on the robustness of deep learning models and the vulnerabilities of AI systems to intentional manipulation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexey’s research primarily focuses on adversarial examples—inputs specifically designed to deceive machine learning models. He investigates defense mechanisms against such attacks and the transferability of adversarial examples across different architectures. He is also a co-author of the "No Language Left Behind" (NLLB) project, contributing to the development of highly multilingual machine translation models.

### Significant Achievements
He is the lead author of the seminal paper "Adversarial Examples in the Physical World" (2016), which demonstrated for the first time that adversarial attacks could be successfully executed in real-world scenarios through sensors like cameras. This work transformed the field's understanding of AI security. His work on "Adversarial Machine Learning at Scale" has been foundational for developing robust and reliable production-grade AI systems.
