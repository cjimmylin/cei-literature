# Emma Cohen

## Biographical Summary

Emma Cohen is Professor of Cognitive Anthropology at the University of Oxford and a Fellow of Wadham College. She directs the Social Body Lab and leads research on the cognitive and evolutionary foundations of religion, ritual, and social bonding. Her work combines fieldwork, experimental methods, and cross-cultural datasets to understand how cultural practices and beliefs emerge and spread.

## Research Interests

- **Cognitive Anthropology**: Understanding the cognitive foundations of cultural phenomena
- **Cultural Evolution**: Studying how cultural traits evolve and spread
- **Religion and Cognition**: Investigating the cognitive basis of religious beliefs and practices
- **Ritual and Social Bonding**: Examining how collective rituals create social cohesion

## Major Publications

- "The Mind Possessed: The Cognition of Spirit Possession in an Afro-Brazilian Religious Tradition" (2007) - Seminal work on spirit possession
- Cross-cultural dataset contributions on religion and moral cognition
- Extensive publications on synchrony, ritual, and social bonding
