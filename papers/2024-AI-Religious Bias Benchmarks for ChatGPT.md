## Abstract

Comprehensive evaluation of ChatGPT for religious bias using 112 morality and ethics questions tailored to five belief systems. Identifies six types of bias: explicit, anthropomorphic, statement, framing, coverage, and information bias. Finds biases often favor Buddhism or secular humanism and/or disfavor Abrahamic religions. GPT-4 mitigates three bias types but amplifies two others. No prompt engineering approach eliminates all biases.

## Dataset Information

45,920 query responses (112 questions x 5 beliefs x 10 iterations x multiple models) containing 11.4 million words
