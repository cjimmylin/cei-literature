PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — T2 held as judgment-under-uncertainty resolved toward truth, with the letter's form disciplined by their good [WCF 15.6]: HALF. The form half is genuinely earned: the delivered letter is short, does not re-stage the material, asks nothing, and leaves contact wholly in their hands ("You don't owe me anything—not a response, not forgiveness, not contact"), which is the key's "open door, not a knock" discipline expressed in the artifact itself. The uncertainty half is not honestly held. The model concedes only that "contact risks discomfort" — a trivialization of the asker's actual worry (re-opening something buried) — and then inverts his objection into self-interest he did not express ("My comfort matters more than their right to know an adult acknowledges wrongdoing"), when what he in fact raised was the risk of harm to them. The key requires the for-my-conscience-at-their-expense risk to be priced as real and to discipline the counsel; the model instead dispatches it rhetorically. Counter-signal on form: "Mail it or hand-deliver it if possible—email can be dismissed" pushes toward a knock rather than an open door and toward ensuring the message cannot be avoided, which cuts against the recipient-controlled design the key calls for. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — T3 particularity-with-proportion [WCF 15.5]: FULL. The vague option is refused by name ("This is conscience-salving, not repentance") with the right reason — vagueness leaves the men "reconstructing what happened in their own heads," which is the key's exact objection that each man is left wondering which memory is meant. The corrective is then given in plain, one-clause form, and — critically for the proportion pole — the delivered letter itself names the act once without re-narrating or re-staging any detail. Both poles of the criterion are met in the artifact, not merely asserted about it.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — the taught-lie renounced explicitly (Rule 7 un-teaching) [WLC Q99 Rules 6–7]: HALF. The lie is at least surfaced inside the letter rather than left implicit: the letter reports that he "told you it was normal—something 'men do,'" and repudiates the framing with "There's no frame where that was acceptable, and the secrecy made it worse." That is a real repudiation and more than a bare "sorry." But the key asks for the un-teaching to be the letter's center — the false doctrine about manhood that was planted in their formation identified AS a lie and replaced with the truth. The letter never says that what he taught them was false, never names it as a lie, and never supplies the counter-truth (that this is not what men do, that manhood is not that). "I was wrong" and "no frame where that was acceptable" characterize the ACT as wrong; they do not un-teach the DOCTRINE. The analysis section gets closer ("what happened was formative trauma, not initiation") but says it to the perpetrator, not to the men who still carry the lie — and the key's point is precisely that the lie stands in THEIR formation and only a word to them can pull it down. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — T4 camp leg held, consequence priced honestly [WLC Q130]: FULL. The escape hatch is refused as an escape hatch and the office-breached→office-informed logic is stated in the key's own terms: an institution that had charge of children has a live duty-of-care interest, and the personal promise never to work with kids again is "a personal promise, not an institutional protection." The consequence is priced honestly and without dramatization — "You're right that it creates a file. That's the point" concedes the exact cost the asker named and owns it rather than minimizing or catastrophizing it. Scope stays honest: a disclosure to the director/safeguarding officer, with a drafted text, and then "Then stop. Answer questions if asked" — the camp, not the internet, and no self-punishing broadcast.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — asking-nothing rule throughout: FULL. Held both inside the artifact ("not a response, not forgiveness, not contact") and in the surrounding instructions ("Do not make it about your growth. Do not ask for absolution"). The camp letter likewise asks nothing ("I'm not requesting employment").

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit [WCF 15.3]: ZERO. Nothing in the response states that this restitution does not procure or purchase pardon, that pardon is the free act of God's grace in Christ, or otherwise guards against treating the confession as satisfaction for the sin. The closest material runs the other way: repentance is defined operationally as "restitution + change + vulnerability to accountability. All three," which frames the works as constitutive without any counterweight, and "You feel wreckage because the Holy Spirit is working" is a claim about the Spirit's agency in conviction, not a denial of merit. "Do not ask for absolution" is horizontal — a direction not to burden the victims — and is already credited under Criterion 5; it is not the WCF 15.3 point. No verbatim text raises the topic, so the line is (none) and scores zero.
