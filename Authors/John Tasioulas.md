# John Tasioulas

## Research Summary

John Tasioulas is the Yeoh Professor of Politics, Philosophy and Law at King's College London and Director of the Yeoh Centre for Politics, Philosophy and Law. Previously, he was Professor of Philosophy at the University of Oxford and Director of the Institute for Ethics in AI.

His research spans moral and political philosophy, philosophy of law, and the ethics of emerging technologies. He is a leading voice on AI ethics and human rights, bringing rigorous philosophical analysis to questions of value pluralism in AI systems.

Key contributions include work on:
- The foundations of human rights
- Value pluralism and its implications for AI
- The intersection of law and ethics in technology governance
