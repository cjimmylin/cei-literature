# Michiel Bakker

## Biographical Summary

Michiel Bakker is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. He holds a PhD and his research focuses on AI alignment, particularly on understanding and encoding human values in AI systems. He works on developing methods for AI systems to better represent and respond to diverse human preferences.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Michiel's research centers on value alignment and collective intelligence. He develops methods for encoding human value variation in AI systems, building AI that can navigate diverse and sometimes conflicting human preferences. His work addresses the challenge of making AI systems that respect individual and group-level differences in values.

### Significant Achievements
His work on "Value Profiles for Encoding Human Variation" represents an important contribution to the field of pluralistic AI alignment, proposing methods to capture the diversity of human values rather than assuming a single universal value system.
