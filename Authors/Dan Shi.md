# Dan Shi

## Biographical Summary

Dan Shi is a researcher affiliated with Tianjin University (TJU). He is the lead author of a comprehensive survey on large language model safety and maintains an active GitHub repository for LLM safety research papers, contributing to the broader AI safety research community.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on comprehensive analysis of LLM safety, covering four major categories: value misalignment, robustness to adversarial attacks, misuse, and autonomous AI risks. He investigates the safety implications of LLM agents, the role of interpretability in enhancing LLM safety, and technology roadmaps proposed by AI companies and institutes for LLM safety.

### Significant Achievements
Shi led the development of "Large Language Model Safety: A Holistic Survey" (December 2024), a comprehensive overview of the LLM safety landscape co-authored with 12 researchers including Tianhao Shen and Yufei Huang. The survey provides critical synthesis of the field, covering AI governance, international cooperation, policy proposals, and prospective regulatory directions. The team maintains a GitHub repository for LLM safety papers and actively welcomes contributions to keep the research community updated.
