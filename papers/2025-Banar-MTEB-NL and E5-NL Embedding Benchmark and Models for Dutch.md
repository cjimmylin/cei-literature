## Abstract

Massive Text Embedding Benchmark for Dutch with both existing and newly created datasets. Includes E5-NL embedding models demonstrating strong performance across multiple tasks for Dutch.

## Summary

This paper introduces MTEB-NL, a comprehensive text embedding benchmark for Dutch comprising 40 datasets across multiple task categories including retrieval, reranking, and classification. The authors compile a training mixture combining available Dutch retrieval datasets with synthetic data generated by LLMs to expand task coverage beyond retrieval. The paper releases a series of E5-NL models—compact yet efficient embedding models that demonstrate state-of-the-art performance among available Dutch embedding models and strong performance compared to multilingual alternatives.

## Data Description

Releases MTEB-NL, a massive text embedding benchmark for Dutch comprising 40 datasets across multiple tasks, plus a training mixture of Dutch retrieval and synthetic data, and E5-NL embedding models; all resources available on Hugging Face and GitHub.


## Detailed Summary

This paper addresses the underrepresentation of Dutch in text embedding resources by introducing three key contributions. First, MTEB-NL (Massive Text Embedding Benchmark for Dutch) comprises 40 datasets covering a wide range of tasks including retrieval, reranking, classification, and clustering, combining existing Dutch datasets with newly created ones. Second, the authors curate a training dataset compiled from available Dutch retrieval datasets (including mMARCO and BEIR-NL) complemented with synthetic data generated by LLMs to expand task coverage beyond retrieval. Third, they release a series of E5-NL models -- compact yet efficient embedding models based on the E5 family that achieve state-of-the-art performance among available Dutch embedding models. The work follows the principles of MMTEB (Massive Multilingual Text Embedding Benchmark) and builds on prior language-specific MTEB extensions for Chinese, French, German, Polish, Russian, and Scandinavian languages.

## Methods Summary

For the benchmark, the authors compiled human-annotated Dutch datasets and newly created evaluation sets following MMTEB quality principles, avoiding machine-translated datasets. For training data, they combined available Dutch retrieval datasets with synthetic data generated by LLMs to expand beyond retrieval tasks. The E5-NL embedding models were built on the E5 family architecture, trained using the curated Dutch training mixture. Evaluation follows the MTEB zero-shot evaluation protocol across all task categories. Resources are made publicly available through Hugging Face Hub and GitHub.

## Results Summary

E5-NL models achieve state-of-the-art results among available Dutch embedding models and demonstrate strong performance compared to multilingual alternatives. The benchmark reveals that the Dutch embedding landscape was previously scarce, with only DUMB (9 datasets), EuroEval (4 datasets), and BEIR-NL (retrieval-focused) available. MTEB-NL significantly expands evaluation coverage to 40 datasets. The synthetic data augmentation strategy proves effective for expanding task coverage beyond retrieval.

## Conclusions

The paper establishes MTEB-NL as a comprehensive evaluation suite for Dutch embeddings, filling an important gap in the Dutch NLP ecosystem. The E5-NL models demonstrate that compact, language-specific embedding models can compete with larger multilingual alternatives. All resources (benchmark, training data, models) are publicly released to encourage further development of Dutch embedding technology.
