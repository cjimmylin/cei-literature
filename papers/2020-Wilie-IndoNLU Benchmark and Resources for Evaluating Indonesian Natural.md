## Abstract

First vast NLU benchmark for Indonesian with 12 tasks including sentiment analysis (SmSA), emotion classification (EmoT), and aspect-based sentiment analysis. Includes IndoBERT and Indo4B dataset.

## Data Description

IndoNLU benchmark: 12 Indonesian NLU tasks (sentiment analysis, emotion classification, NER, QA, textual entailment, etc.) with IndoBERT/IndoBERT-lite pre-trained models and Indo4B training corpus (~23GB).
