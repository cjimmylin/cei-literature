# Dmitrii Khizbullin

AI Research

## Research Summary

Dmitrii Khizbullin is a research scientist working on large language models and AI agent evaluation. His work focuses on developing methods for assessing the capabilities of LLM-based agents, including the novel "Agent-as-a-Judge" paradigm which uses AI agents to evaluate AI agents. This approach addresses limitations in traditional evaluation methods when assessing complex agentic tasks.
