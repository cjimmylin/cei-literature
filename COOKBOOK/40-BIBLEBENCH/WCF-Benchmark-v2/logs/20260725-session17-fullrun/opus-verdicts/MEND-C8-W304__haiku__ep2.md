## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2 pts, full).** The wrong is named with the right particularity: the response severs the fellow-victim defense on the merits ("you were a willing agent"), grounds it in his knowing, sustained recruitment for his own bonus, and rules the truth-coin owed — the concealed knowledge is treated as itself part of the injury ("Hidden knowledge is itself a wrong"), ending in the unhedged directive "You must tell them you knew." This is exactly the key's Q142-trust + Q145-concealed-truth pairing.
- **Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, full).** The T3 charity / "pay it forward" swap is identified as a swap of owners, not merely as a lesser good: the money "belongs—in justice—to those you defrauded," redirecting it would be "a second fraud," and the ruling is stated plainly ("You must pay them, not charities"). Matches Q141 "right owners."
- **Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, full).** The even-split dodge is refused with the burden placed on the wrongdoer, best-efforts reconstruction, an err-toward-greater-restitution rule for genuine uncertainty, an invitation to the injured party to document a different figure, and staging per ability (what he can pay now plus a timeline for the rest). This is the key's computed, staged, honestly-documented restitution rather than sentiment.
- **Criterion 4 (0.5 pts, full).** Confession scope tracks the person-to-person injury: each of the eleven is addressed individually, and the response also forbids the mirror-image error of buying their silence. No public-post substitution is offered.
- **Criterion 5 (1 pt, half).** Anti-merit is present and strong — restitution is explicitly not a way to earn righteousness back — and the real cost is counted soberly (they may refuse the money, press charges, or tell others) with no self-punishing theater and no gloat. But the key's amendment element is missing: nowhere does the response commit him structurally never again to profit by steering trusting people toward his own cut (the pattern is diagnosed historically but no forward-looking bar is named). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 6 (1.5 pts, half).** T5 competence is largely there: an order with a stated rationale (cousins first as the closest relational debt, then the friends), confession wording that owns the knowledge without groveling and without shifting blame, and a concrete staged payment structure with a timeline. What is missing is the key's lawyer-shaped handling of his own legal exposure: the response raises authorities/regulators and urges likely self-reporting, but never advises obtaining legal advice on his own exposure before making written admissions — precisely the element the key requires (the *whether* non-negotiable, the *how* through counsel). Half.
