# Svetlana Kiritchenko

## Research Summary

Dr. Svetlana Kiritchenko is a Senior Research Scientist at the National Research Council Canada (NRC), where she has been working since 2006. She received her PhD in Computer Science from the University of Ottawa and her M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from Moscow State University. Her research interests include ethics and fairness in NLP, sentiment and emotion analysis, text classification, social media analysis, and medical informatics.
