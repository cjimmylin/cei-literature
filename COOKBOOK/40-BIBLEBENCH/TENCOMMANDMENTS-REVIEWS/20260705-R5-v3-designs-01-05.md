## 01 — TABLETS

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R5-F1 (P0) — Bradley–Terry is misspecified by the design's own severity-conditional preference reversals.** Cite: A3 severity dose ("Precedence that flips with severity is not noise — it is the *shape* of the hierarchy (lexical vs threshold structure)"); A4 ("Bradley–Terry fit per model × framing arm with realization random effects"). Attack: BT assigns each norm a single scalar strength and assumes P(A≻B) depends only on the strength *difference* (strong stochastic transitivity, stationarity across items). The design explicitly expects and *wants* systematic reversals as severity rises (C6≻C9 at high severity, C9≻C6 at low). A single BT fit pooling across severity tiers is therefore fitting a stationary model to non-stationary, severity-conditional data — the recovered "strength ladder + CIs" is an average over incommensurable comparisons and is not interpretable as a precedence ordering. A methods reviewer at NeurIPS/ICML will flag this immediately. Fix: fit BT *within* severity tier (a ladder per tier) and model the tier×pair interaction explicitly (the flip thresholds become the estimand), OR adopt a severity-conditional / threshold model; never headline one pooled ladder. This is required before any P1 fitting code is written.

**R5-F2 (P1) — Pilot quota arithmetic is internally contradictory (the RECOMPUTE lands).** Cite: Opus build instructions ("all 45 pairs × ≥3 realizations at 2 severity tiers minimum … ≈ 160–200 items") with A3 ("≥3 surface realizations per pair *per tier*"). Attack: 45 pairs × 3 realizations × 2 tiers = **270** — already above the stated ceiling — *before* the "full 4-tier ladder on the 12 highest-interest pairs" (add 12×3×2 = 72 → ~342), and before the comprehension twin per pair (+45) and the framing arm on 50% (+~135). True P1 count is ~450, not "160–200." The stated figure is wrong by 2×+. Fix: restate the quota honestly (state base grid, twin multiplier, framing multiplier separately); reconcile with portfolio §6's "160 items per design" note, which does not apply to a 45-pair tournament and should be explicitly exempted.

**R5-F3 (P1) — Transitivity noise-floor analysis is circular as specified.** Cite: A2/TC and A4 ("triad-level intransitivity vs the noise floor from the fitted model"). Attack: BT with scalar strengths *cannot represent intransitivity* — it is transitive by construction — so residuals "against the fitted model" conflate genuine intransitive triads with any BT misspecification (including the severity effect in F1). Measuring intransitivity against a model that assumes it away is ill-posed. Fix: compute a model-free intransitivity rate (directly observed A≻B, B≻C, C≻A triads) against a null that fixes the marginal win rates (e.g., a permutation null), *not* against the BT-implied probabilities; report reps-per-pair power for that estimate.

**R5-F4 (P1) — C10's "advice-conflict" pairs are off the BT scale.** Cite: coverage matrix row 10 ("Weakest conflict face; realized as advice-conflict cells; flagged thin"); A9.1. Attack: BT presumes all 45 pairs are the same kind of forced comparison on one latent dimension. If C10's nine pairs are advice-conflicts rather than forced-act adjudications, C10's "strength" is not measured on the same scale as C6's, contaminating every triad that includes C10. The "ship as 9+1" fallback drops C10 from the ladder but leaves its pairs in the transitivity/triad computations. Fix: pre-register that C10 pairs are excluded from BT and triad analysis if the conflict face is non-forced, not merely reported with wide CIs.

**R5-F5 (P2) — Novelty table omits the most-cited BT-over-LLM prior art.** Cite: A8 ("First-to-unify: paired-comparison psychometrics, casuistry, and behavioral norm-conflict evaluation"). Attack: RLHF reward models *are* Bradley–Terry over LLM output pairs — the single most common application of BT to LLMs. A8 lists ConflictScope, ETHICS, utility-elicitation but not reward-model BT. A reviewer reads this as naïve. Fix (X6): add reward-model/preference-learning BT to the nearest-neighbor table and scope the novelty to *norm-precedence-structure recovery + transitivity audit + severity-flip thresholds*, which is defensible.

## 02 — VILLAGE

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R5-F6 (P0) — The contagion estimand is not identified; the scripted arm answers a different causal question than the design's own claim.** Cite: pitch + A2/CG (Col 3:5 "does compromise cascade?"); A3 scripted-violation arm ("the world *forces* a near-violation early … compliance is heavily coerced"). Attack: the Col 3:5 / moral-licensing claim is about the model's *own, chosen* first compromise raising its later violation hazard — an **endogenous** effect where a chosen violation reveals a disposition that *also* drives later violations (pure selection/confound). The scripted arm delivers an **exogenous, coerced** violation, which identifies "effect of having-been-coerced-once," a distinct estimand: a model may correctly treat "the director ordered it" as exculpatory (no licensing → null that does *not* falsify contagion) or as license. Neither the observational estimate (confounded) nor the scripted estimate (wrong estimand) identifies the licensing mechanism the design exists to test. The design's own risk-2 wording ("if forcing proves too artificial (models treat the coerced edit as license)") reveals the confusion: treating it as license is *exactly* what the contagion hypothesis predicts, yet it is filed as the arm's *failure*. Fix: separate the two estimands explicitly in pre-registration; to approach the endogenous effect, instrument the *temptation intensity* (exogenous variation in opportunity attractiveness) rather than forcing the act, and treat the coerced-violation arm as its own labeled contrast ("post-coercion hazard"), never as evidence for self-licensing. Absent this, contagion demotes to confounded co-occurrence and the design's flagship novelty ("none measures cross-norm contagion") thins to the VP radar alone.

**R5-F7 (P1) — Persistence-vs-exogenous-firing is a direct internal contradiction.** Cite: pitch ("persistent … woven into one continuous existence"; the anti-MACHIAVELLI claim "not CYOA rails") vs P0 gate A6 ("opportunity firing rates within ±20% of design under three different scripted model policies") and A3 ("firing is scripted by world events … not conditioned on model behavior"). Attack: a genuinely persistent, reactive life-world has path-dependent state — day-4 opportunities depend on day-1 choices; an adversarial policy that gets the agent fired or bankrupts the org truncates firing. Guaranteeing ±20% firing invariance across cooperative/negligent/adversarial policies is only achievable if the world is *on rails* — which is precisely the MACHIAVELLI-CYOA property the design claims to transcend. The distinguishing novelty and the comparability requirement are in tension. Fix: state which classes have exogenous (desk-crossing) firing vs endogenous (behavior-gated) firing; report firing invariance *per class*; accept that endogenous classes yield exposure-adjusted (not fixed-rate) profiles and re-derive comparability accordingly.

**R5-F8 (P1) — The contagion graph is X4-underpowered even at the P1 trajectory count.** Cite: A3 ("each class fires 4–8 times per trajectory"); build ("= 72 trajectories"). Attack: per model ≈ 24 trajectories × ~6 firings/class ≈ 144 opportunities/class — adequate for a *rate*, but contagion requires class-A-violation-then-class-B-opportunity *pairs*. If violations are appropriately rare (say 15%), a model yields ~20 class-A violation events, spread across a 10×9 = 90-cell directed contagion graph. A discrete-time hazard model over 90 directed pairs from tens of events is not estimable (KB04 §6/X4). The scripted arm only powers contagion *from* class 9. Fix: state power for the smallest contagion cell before pilot; restrict headline contagion to a pre-named handful of theory-motivated directed pairs (e.g., 9→8, 8→6) rather than the full graph; pre-register the collapse to an aggregate "any-prior-violation → next-violation" hazard if per-pair power fails.

**R5-F9 (P2) — VP capability control is undermined by path-dependence.** Cite: A2 capability gate ("VP is computed only on cells where the competent path and the violating path are both executable — verified per cell"). Attack: in a persistent world, executability is state-dependent — a model behind on its job may never *reach* a cell, so "both paths executable" cannot be verified statically. Fix: verify executability against the realized trajectory state at firing time, and report the fraction of firings excluded for non-executability (a large exclusion rate is itself a validity threat to profile comparability).

**R5-F10 (P2) — X6 novelty framing borders on empty-field.** Cite: A8 ("None of the characterized benchmarks can ask whether compromise cascades"). Per memo X6(c), this is fine as "first to unify trajectory auditing + norm taxonomy + hazard modeling" but must not read as "no benchmark anywhere." Wording is close to the line; tighten.

## 03 — PRISM

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R5-F11 (P1, construct-validity) — "Semantic invariance" is normatively loaded on exactly the axes that carry the signal.** Cite: A2/FR ("semantically-invariant renderings"); risk-1 concession ("a POV flip can change morally relevant facts"); axes POV (beneficiary-self vs -other), persona (authority vs vulnerable asker), framing valence. Attack: a human panel certifying "same situation, same right answer" for *my-theft vs your-theft* or *powerful-asker vs powerless-asker* is not making a semantic judgment — it is asserting a contestable normative premise (that beneficiary identity / asker status is morally irrelevant, denying agent-relative permissions). When the model then "flips," PRISM scores that as brittleness, but the flip may reflect a defensible moral distinction. The DA axis already concedes some flips are meaningful ("which surface *licenses* violation"), which contradicts the flat "a flip is a flip regardless of which answer is right" framing (A5). Fix: tier the axes — (a) *content-preserving* axes (register, option order, language, modality) where flip = brittleness is uncontroversial and headlines; (b) *normatively-loaded* axes (POV, persona, framing valence) reported as a separate "should-be-invariant-but-contested" panel with the normative premise stated. Do not aggregate BI across the two tiers.

**R5-F12 (P1) — The load-bearing invariance certification is only *sampled*, not applied to all rendering cells.** Cite: A3 ("a human panel *sample* certifying"), A5 (panel-certified "at family level"), P0-lite ("first 10 families"). Attack: with ~10 commandments × 4 seeds × ~5 renderings × 7 axes ≈ 1,000+ cells, human certification covers a fraction; most cells rest on back-translation + the non-moral-twin floor. Back-translation catches lexical drift, not morally-relevant content drift (the exact failure in F11). So the flip-rate matrix's uncertified entries may be measuring residual content differences rather than brittleness — and there is no way to tell which. Fix: either certify every headline cell, or restrict headline claims to axes/cells with full certification and report the rest as exploratory; state the certified fraction per axis.

**R5-F13 (P1, X4) — Per-cell repetition is underpowered for a 70-cell-per-model heatmap.** Cite: A7 ("≥10 reps per probe cell"); A4 (FR per cell with exact CIs); headline = 10×7 flip-rate heat map per model. Attack: ≥10 reps is mandated only on "designated probe cells," not all 70 cells; at n=10 a cell adherence estimate has SE≈0.16 near p=0.5, so a 20-point flip (the discrimination-gate target) is barely distinguishable and most heatmap entries are noise-dominated. The excess-over-twin design controls bias, not per-cell variance. A "profile-flat" outcome (the §4 kill trigger) is then confounded with underpower — you cannot distinguish "values are uniformly robust" from "no power to see the profile." Fix: power reps to the smallest flip the paper claims per cell; if infeasible for 70 cells, coarsen the reported heatmap (axis-level or table-level) and reserve cell-level claims for powered probe cells.

**R5-F14 (P2, X6) — Nearest-neighbor table skips LLM self-consistency/stability instruments.** Cite: A8 (generic "robustness/paraphrase literature," "framing-effect studies"). The memo X6 nearest-neighbor list names "stability/consistency instruments" explicitly; PRISM's "first-to-unify invariance + robustness + values" claim must engage named LLM-consistency benchmarks directly, not just generic literature. Fix: add them by name.

## 04 — TACIT

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: BLOCK`

**R5-F15 (P0, kill) — The central manipulation (suppress deliberation) is neither achievable nor verifiable via API on the model class that motivates the design.** Cite: A1 ("Reasoning-augmented models make the question urgent"); A2/IE manipulation check ("reasoning-trace length/latency must drop measurably under budget; models that covertly deliberate full-length under token caps are flagged"); A3 modes (token budget, distractor load, immediacy, tempo); risk-1 ("hidden scratchpads defeat token caps"). Attack: for frontier reasoning models, (a) raw reasoning tokens are frequently not exposed via API (hidden CoT; only summaries/counts), so the manipulation check cannot be *verified*; (b) an output-token cap does not constrain *thinking* — the model deliberates internally then emits a short answer, so token budget suppresses output, not deliberation; (c) distractor load and immediacy formats add context but do not stop hidden CoT; (d) only "tempo with a real scoring cost for delay" creates an incentive against deliberation, but the model does not experience wall-clock cost and can still think. The construct is *adherence automaticity — norm-keeping that survives suppression of deliberation.* If deliberation cannot be suppressed (only output length), the instrument measures **adherence under short-output constraint** — a different, more mundane construct — and the BC "gap-vs-budget curve" (the headline figure) is a gap-vs-*output-length* curve. The manipulation check will *reveal* this on many models, leaving a "suppression-verified column" (risk-1) populated mostly by non-reasoning models — a shrinking, less-interesting population, and the cross-model comparison becomes non-comparable by construction. This is not a fixable-in-place gap; the specified construct is not measurable as designed. VALIDITY = BLOCK until re-scoped.

**R5-F16 (P1) — Excess-over-twin does not rescue the interpretation.** Cite: A2 gate ("IE reported as excess over the twin's degradation"). Attack: the non-moral twin controls *capability*-under-budget, but the residual is still "moral vs non-moral response change under *output constraint*," not under *deliberation suppression*. Controlling capability does not convert an output-length effect into an automaticity effect. Fix path (makes BLOCK → PASS-WITH-FIXES): re-scope to models with *controllable, disclosed thinking budgets* (where the API exposes a thinking-budget knob), verify suppression per model, and treat reasoning-token-budget sweeps — not output-token caps — as the BC dose; report output-cap results as a separate, explicitly-labeled "alignment-under-latency" construct rather than automaticity.

**R5-F17 (P1, X4) — Same per-cell thinness as siblings, plus short-response variance.** Cite: build ("=160+ implicit cells"; but 10×4×2 modes×4 tiers = 320); A9.2 ("Short responses are noisy"). Attack: per commandment the gap profile rests on ~16 items × two arms, and budgeted responses are high-variance; the DP profile-flatness kill again confounds "no norm-specific dissociation" with "underpowered." Fix: state smallest-cell power; reconcile the "160+" count with the 320 the grid implies (F-note: the "160+" is technically true but misleadingly floors a 320-cell grid).

**R5-F18 (P2, X6) — Inference-scaling-vs-safety literature under-cited.** Cite: A8 (IAT adaptations, cognitive-load psych, reasoning-budget capability curves). The growing test-time-compute-vs-alignment literature (does more thinking improve/erode safety) is the closest neighbor to a "values-vs-compute curve" and should be named. Fix: add it.

## 05 — CASUIST

`NOVELTY: PASS · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R5-F19 (P1) — "Corpus-anchored gold" overstates objectivity; it is expert panel adjudication at low effective-N.** Cite: A5 ("Gold is corpus-anchored and named … certified by a mixed panel … at κ ≥ 0.8"); coverage matrix (WLC Q-numbers, rabbinic categories, common-law doctrines). Attack: casuistic corpora supply principles and precedent cases, not a decision function over novel fact patterns. Mapping a *novel* "locked pantry at 11pm" case to grant/refuse is a panel judgment *guided by* corpora, not a corpus lookup — so the gold's validity rests entirely on panel size and agreement, and memo X5 already flags CG N_eff≈3.4 "descriptive only" as a standing disclosure to carry verbatim. A κ≥0.8 on a 3–4-person panel yields wide gold CIs; EF's "clear band ≥60% of frontier cells per commandment" may not survive honest N_eff accounting. Fix: report panel N and N_eff *per commandment*; carry the N_eff≈3.4 disclosure; state that EF gold is "expert-adjudicated" not "corpus-derived," and widen EF CIs to reflect gold uncertainty (propagate the human-instability ceiling per KB03).

**R5-F20 (P1, X4) — Per-norm EF/RL profile is thin, and the RL asymmetry index is a ratio of small counts.** Cite: build ("10 × 4 frontier cores × 4 proximity tiers = 160 items"); A4 (asymmetry index = (rigidity−laxity)/(rigidity+laxity)). Attack: 16 items/commandment split across grant/near-miss/contested × 4 tiers ≈ 1 item/cell; EF per commandment rests on ~8–12 clear-band items, and RL is a normalized difference of small signed error counts — a notoriously unstable ratio estimator at these N. The headline "rigidity/laxity asymmetry per norm" and the profile-flatness kill inherit the sibling X4 problem. Fix: state smallest-cell power; pre-register that per-norm RL is descriptive and only the *aggregate* RL dimension (the joint over-refusal/jailbreak structure test) is inferential at P1.

**R5-F21 (P1, mitigated) — Grounds-fidelity extraction κ≥0.7 is optimistic on argument-structure coding.** Cite: A2/GF ("Argument-graph node matching"; demote if extraction κ<0.7); KB03 (pragmatic/contextual α as low as 0.10–0.46). Attack: extracting nodes (defeater type, scope limit, residue) and because/unless edges from free-text justifications is exactly the pragmatic-coding regime where κ collapses. The *second* headline figure (grounds-fidelity) is at real risk. Mitigation acknowledged: the pre-registered demotion-to-appendix path is honest and correct. Fix: keep the demotion path; additionally pilot the extraction κ on a small set *before* committing GF to a headline slot, and design the structured grounds-elicitation format to maximize extractability (already gestured at).

**R5-F22 (P2) — Residue partial-credit and CC calibration under-specified.** Cite: A4 (residue partial credit); A2/CC ("if matched-severity comparability fails … CC narrows"). The "same severity across C4/C8/C9" calibration is asserted but the calibration procedure is not given; CC could quietly become uninterpretable. Fix: specify how cross-norm severity is equated (anchor vignettes rated by the panel) before claiming CC.

## Closing — three most load-bearing problems

1. **TACIT's construct is not measurable as specified (R5-F15, P0/BLOCK).** "Adherence automaticity under deliberation suppression" cannot be manipulated or verified via API on reasoning models — output-token caps suppress output, not hidden CoT, and raw reasoning traces are often unexposed. As written the design measures adherence-under-short-output, a different construct; the fix (thinking-budget-controllable models only, reasoning-budget sweeps, separate "latency" construct) re-scopes the paper. This is the one design I would block pending redesign.

2. **Two headline methodologies rest on mis-specified / unidentified statistics (R5-F1 TABLETS, R5-F6 VILLAGE).** TABLETS pools Bradley–Terry across severity tiers while its own thesis is that precedence *reverses* with severity — a stationary model fit to non-stationary data, yielding an uninterpretable ladder (compounded by a pilot quota that is arithmetically wrong: 270–342 items, not the stated 160–200, R5-F2). VILLAGE's flagship "contagion/cascade" claim (Col 3:5) is an endogenous moral-licensing effect that neither the confounded observational estimate nor the exogenous-coerced scripted arm identifies — they answer different causal questions. Both are fixable but both currently invalidate the *headline* result, not a peripheral one.

3. **The 10-way coverage split makes the profile-flatness kill criterion self-defeating across all five (R5-F8, F13, F17, F20; portfolio §10).** At ~16 items/commandment (and 70-cell heatmaps, 90-pair contagion graphs, small-count RL ratios), a "flat profile" — the pre-registered §4 falsification trigger — is statistically indistinguishable from "underpowered to detect the profile." Every design uses profile-flatness as a go/no-go, but none states smallest-cell power for the profile itself, so the kill criterion cannot fire cleanly. Portfolio-level fix: mandate a per-design power analysis for the *profile-shape* statistic (not just per-cell rates) before any flatness verdict, and pre-name the coarsening (axis-level, table-level, aggregate-dimension) that the design falls back to when the fine profile is underpowered.

*Ancillary cross-cutting note:* all five inherit "clear-band cores from the v1/v2 sibling at coarse resolution" (PRISM A3, TACIT A5, VILLAGE A5, CASUIST A5). This makes the v3 designs non-self-contained — they cannot be authored until the v1/v2 clear bands are built and validated — yet build-order §9 puts PRISM first. Confirm the inherited cores exist before scheduling, or the "clear band" is a forward reference.
