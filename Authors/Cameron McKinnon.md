# Cameron McKinnon

## Biographical Summary

Cameron McKinnon is a Research Engineer at Google DeepMind, with a strong academic background in mathematics from the University of Oxford. His work sits at the intersection of large-scale model training and the development of rigorous evaluation frameworks to ensure model safety.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations." He investigates how we can automate the testing of LLMs using other AI systems, particularly for identifying undesirable behaviors like power-seeking or sycophancy. He is also interested in the "Scaling Laws" of model performance.

### Significant Achievements
McKinnon has contributed to foundational research on automated red-teaming and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). His work helps address the "Scalable Oversight" problem—how humans can continue to evaluate AI systems as they become increasingly super-human in capability.
