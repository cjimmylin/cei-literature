# MATA (mata): Mindful Assessment of the Telugu Abilities of Large Language Models

## Authors

[[Kranti, Chalamalasetti]], [[Vajjala, Sowmya]]

## Abstract

Novel evaluation dataset with 729 curated MCQ and open-ended questions spanning diverse linguistic dimensions for Telugu. Evaluates 11 LLMs and compares LLM-as-a-judge with human evaluation for open-ended questions. Telugu-specific LLM evaluation benchmark. Shows how LLMs rely on superficial heuristics for MCQs and assesses LLM-as-judge reliability in low-resource Telugu.

## Benchmark

**Name:** MATA

## Data Description

MATA is a 729-question evaluation dataset for Telugu covering seven linguistic categories (grammar, factual knowledge, vocabulary, linguistic reasoning, riddles, idioms/proverbs) sourced from school textbooks and competitive exams; approximately 25% MCQ and 75% open-ended.
