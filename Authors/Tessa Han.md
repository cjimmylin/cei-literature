# Tessa Han

## Research Summary

Tessa Han is a Doctoral Student in the Bioinformatics and Integrative Genomics Program at Harvard Medical School and a member of the Trustworthy AI Lab at Harvard's Digital Data Design Institute. Her research focuses on trustworthy AI, explainable machine learning, and AI safety, with particular emphasis on the medical safety of large language models, data point vulnerability, and explainability in machine learning. She collaborates with Professor Himabindu Lakkaraju at Harvard.
