# Branislava Jankovic

## Biographical Summary

Branislava Jankovic is a PhD Fellow at MBZUAI, originally from Serbia. She has a background in radiophysics and a strong interest in the socio-technical impacts of artificial intelligence. Her research is focused on making AI systems more inclusive of global cultural and linguistic diversity.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her primary research focus is on "Cultural AI"—developing benchmarks and methods to evaluate how Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) perform across diverse cultural contexts. She is particularly interested in ensuring that "low-resource" languages and non-Western cultural sensitivities are represented in frontier AI evaluations.

### Significant Achievements
Jankovic is a lead author of the "All Languages Matter" (ALM-bench) project, which evaluates LMMs across 100 different languages and cultures. This work highlights the "cultural gap" in current AI systems and provides a framework for building more globally aligned models.

## Major Publications

- [All Languages Matter: Evaluating LMMs on Culturally Diverse 100 Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00000) (2024) - *Proposed a global benchmark for cultural alignment.*
- [UAV-Assisted Real-Time Disaster Detection Using Optimized Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00000) (2023) - *Research on AI for social good and emergency response.*
