# Emma Gouné

## Biographical Summary

Emma Gouné is a researcher at the UK AI Safety Institute (AISI), working on methods to detect and measure misaligned behavior in AI agents. Her research focuses on developing evaluation frameworks and benchmarks that can identify when AI systems behave in ways that diverge from intended goals or human values, contributing to the safety evaluation of advanced AI systems.

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety Evaluation**: Developing methods to assess AI system safety
- **Agent Misalignment**: Detecting and measuring misaligned behavior in AI agents
- **Benchmark Development**: Creating evaluation tools for AI safety research
