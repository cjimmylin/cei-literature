# Martin Hähnel

## Research Summary

Martin Hähnel is a Lecturer (Privatdozent) at the University of Bremen with a doctorate from Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt (2015) on virtue ethics. Completed habilitation at University of Bremen (2024) on Neo-Aristotelian ethical naturalism. Research stays at Boston College and Oxford University. Co-editor of "A Companion to Applied Philosophy of AI" (Wiley-Blackwell, 2025). His work advocates a neo-Aristotelian approach to ethics applied to contemporary bioethics, research ethics, and AI ethics.
