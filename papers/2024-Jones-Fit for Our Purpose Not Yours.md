## Abstract

Te Hiku Media created the first culturally appropriate benchmark for te reo Maori automatic speech recognition, arguing that mainstream benchmarks are irrelevant for low-resource Indigenous languages. The benchmark is based on the political history of the Maori community with thorough quality assurance, demonstrating that quality over quantity in curating a tailored benchmark is more beneficial than measuring against commonly used benchmarks. This is one of the most concrete, operational Indigenous AI benchmarks discovered.

## Dataset Information

Custom benchmark based on community-curated speech data; builds on 300+ hours of labeled speech data from Te Hiku Media's Papa Reo platform


## Data Description

This paper introduces Te Reo Irirangi o Te Hiku o te Ika's curated benchmark for the Maori language, the first of its kind. It critiques existing multilingual benchmarks (FLoRes-101, FLEURS) for their poor quality Maori translations, grammatical errors, and cultural irrelevance, and presents a methodology for Indigenous communities to develop high-quality, culturally appropriate benchmarks. The benchmark is designed for speech and NLP tools but no specific DATA_URL is provided.
