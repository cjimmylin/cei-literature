## Abstract

Multilingual guardrail for LLM safety in SEA languages. Constructs SEALSBench with 260,000+ prompts in ten languages (safe, unsafe, jailbreak cases). Improves Defense Success Rate by 48% over LlamaGuard for multilingual unsafe/jailbreak prompts. Covers ALL four target languages (Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese). Large-scale safety benchmark (260K+ prompts) addressing multilingual safety alignment gaps. Directly relevant to values/safety evaluation.
