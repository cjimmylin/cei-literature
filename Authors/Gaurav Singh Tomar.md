# Gaurav Singh Tomar

## Biographical Summary

Gaurav Singh Tomar is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on Generative Large Language Models research and applications. He holds a PhD in Computer Science from Georgia Institute of Technology and was an MS student at Carnegie Mellon University (2014-2016) with Carolyn Rose as his PhD advisor. He previously worked at Apple's Knowledge Platform team and was a gold medalist for holding first position in his B.Tech Information Technology batch.

## Research & Impact

Tomar's research spans NLP and dialogue systems:

- **Attribution in NLG**: Co-authored research on measuring attribution in natural language generation models (Computational Linguistics, 2023).

- **Paraphrase Identification**: Authored research showing that decomposable attention models with character n-gram representations achieve accurate performance for paraphrase identification while being simpler than competing neural architectures.

- **Dungeons and Dragons as Dialogue Challenge**: Co-authored work framing D&D as a dialogue system challenge, creating a gameplay dataset of nearly 900 games, 7,000 players, 800,000 dialogue turns, 500,000 dice rolls, and 58 million words.

- **FACTS Grounding Leaderboard**: Contributed to research on grounding evaluation for language models.

His research areas include Natural Language Processing, Language Technologies, and Artificial Intelligence in Education.
