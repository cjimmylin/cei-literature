## Abstract

Systematic critical synthesis analyzing 69 studies across five continents on digital technology projects for Indigenous sociocultural advancement. Identifies eight project types, three ideopolitical frames (cultural bridging, countering Euro-Western dominance, technical problem-solving), and tensions around outsider expert control over Indigenous data.
