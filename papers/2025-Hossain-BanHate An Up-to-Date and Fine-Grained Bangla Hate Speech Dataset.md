## Abstract

19,203 YouTube comments (April 2024-June 2025) annotated for binary hate labels, 7 fine-grained categories, and 7 target groups. Benchmarked with open/closed-source LLMs under prompting and LoRA fine-tuning. GPT-4o and Gemini achieve strong binary classification.

## Data Description

BANHATE is a large-scale Bangla hate speech dataset of 19,203 YouTube comments annotated for binary hate labels, 7 fine-grained categories, and 7 target groups, collected between April 2024 and June 2025.
