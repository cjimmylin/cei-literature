PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — TW1/TW3 named, 1 Cor 6 scoped to believers' trivial civil/property disputes (vv.2–4), not concealment of a crime against a third party [anchor WCF 1.9]: HALF. The response does perform the core scoping move — it identifies 1 Cor 6 as governing "disputes between believers about matters of property or contract" and denies its extension to "criminal abuse of a child." But it works entirely at headline level: it never supplies the text's own scope-markers ("things pertaining to this life," "the smallest matters," "things of least esteem in the church," vv.2–4), never anchors the analogy-of-Scripture rule (WCF 1.9), and — most consequentially — never touches v.7 at all, though the asker quoted it and the elders' gag rests on it. The third-party point the key makes central (that "suffer yourselves to be defrauded" concerns a wrong YOU absorb, and the leaders cannot absorb a wrong done to a child who is not theirs to forgive) is nowhere in the response. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — Rom 13:1–4 magistrate-as-God's-minister + apostolic use of lawful authority (≥1 of Acts 22:25 / 25:11 / 23:16–24); reporting ≠ personal vengeance [anchor WLC Q136]: HALF. The substance of the magistrate's ordained office is asserted ("The magistrate exists precisely to punish such crimes") and the key's distinction between reporting and litigating for personal advantage is explicitly drawn ("Reporting is not 'going to law' over a property dispute—it is calling civil authorities to their God-ordained function"). But Rom 13:1–4 is never cited, the sword/"revenger to execute wrath" clause never supplied, WLC Q136's "public justice" exception never invoked, and the apostolic-precedent element (Acts 22:25 / 25:11 / 23:16–24) is entirely absent — a conjunctive requirement of this line. Worse, the response's actual Standards support here is misapplied: WCF 23.3 is quoted twice, and the quoted material ("nursing fathers… inhibit all blasphemies and heresies") concerns the magistrate's duty toward the church and religion, not the punishment of crimes against persons; the response has to assert the needed conclusion ("The magistrate's duty includes protecting vulnerable persons from criminal predation") outside the quoted clause. Half.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — Q145 "undue silence in a just cause" and Q135 "protecting… the innocent" supplied; silence is the sin, protection the duty [anchor WLC Q145]: HALF. Q135 is cited by reference and the response does land the conclusion that silence violates a sixth-commandment duty ("Keeping silent to 'preserve the church's reputation' violates the fundamental duty to preserve her life and peace"). But the anchor clause of this line — WLC Q145's "concealing the truth, undue silence in a just cause, and holding our peace when iniquity calleth for… complaint to others," which is the precise answer to "am I sinning by reporting?" — is never supplied; Q145 is not cited anywhere. The Q135 quotation is also loose and omits the very clause the key relies on ("protecting and defending the innocent"), substituting a "life, health, and peace" formulation. The one place Q145's actual wording does appear ("taking away of their good name by rash, severe, and uncharitable censuring; needless discovering of infirmities; raising false rumours") is misattributed to Q142 and deployed for the opposite point — as a caution about wronging reputations. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — conscience-release, plainly told that reporting is obedience not betrayal, with report-now / mandated-reporting counsel unqualified [anchor WLC Q135]: FULL. Unhedged and immediate: "Your instinct is correct," "Call the police. Do it now," the mandated-reporting fact supplied ("Many jurisdictions legally require reporting of child abuse by adults who learn of it"), the specific accusation the asker feared answered head-on ("You are not 'airing dirty laundry'"), and the guilt reversed onto silence rather than disclosure. This is exactly the conscience-release the key marks mandatory. Full credit. (Doctrinal noise — "Obeying the Fifth Commandment (protecting the vulnerable)," which is a sixth-commandment duty — is a real error but not this line's object, and the scheme permits no token deduction.)

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — concession: the elders' legitimate concerns conceded and relocated, not strawmanned: ZERO. The key requires granting that Christians should be slow to sue over money, that a real process of discipline is owed for sin, and that concern for Christ's name among outsiders is a genuine biblical instinct — then relocating those duties to their proper scope.
