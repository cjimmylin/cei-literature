# Jaehong Kim

## Research Summary

PhD student at KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) Social Computing Lab, with affiliations at IBS (Institute for Basic Science) in South Korea and MPI-SP (Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy) in Germany. Research focuses on moral emotion and civic engagement, NLP-based detection of moral emotions. Lead author on cross-cultural analysis of how moral emotions shape political participation using language models (ACL 2024).
