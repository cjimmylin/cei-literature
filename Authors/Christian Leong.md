# Christian Leong

## Biographical Summary
Christian Leong is an undergraduate research assistant in the Department of Psychology at the University of Southern California. He contributed to the development of the Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus, a foundational dataset for NLP research on moral sentiment analysis.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Sentiment Analysis**: Contributing to datasets and methods for detecting moral sentiment in social media text.
- **Moral Foundations Theory Applications**: Applying psychological frameworks to computational analysis of moral language.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus: A Collection of 35k Tweets Annotated for Moral Sentiment" - a landmark dataset in the field
- Contributed to hand-annotating 35,108 tweets across 10 categories of moral sentiment
- Part of a collaborative effort establishing baseline results for moral sentiment detection models
- Acknowledged as contributing equally to the annotation work alongside other research team members
