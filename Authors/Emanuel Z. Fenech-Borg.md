# Emanuel Z. Fenech-Borg

## Biographical Summary

Emanuel Z. Fenech-Borg is a researcher affiliated with the University of Malta, working on understanding the cultural dimensions of large language models. His research examines how LLMs encode, reflect, and transmit cultural values and biases, investigating the "cultural gene" embedded in these systems and its implications for diverse user populations.

## Research Interests

- **Cultural AI**: Studying how AI systems reflect and transmit cultural values
- **LLM Analysis**: Investigating cultural biases and representations in language models
- **Cross-Cultural NLP**: Understanding how NLP systems perform across cultural contexts
