# Jiatao Li

## Research Summary

Undergraduate student at Peking University's Information Management Department (2022-2026), working in NLP with PhD advisor Xiaojun Wan. Affiliated with Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology. Lead author on "Analyzing Cognitive Differences Among Large Language Models through the Lens of Social Worldview" (arXiv 2025), introducing Social Worldview Taxonomy (SWT) grounded in Cultural Theory, examining LLMs' implicit socio-cognitive attitudes toward authority, equality, autonomy, and fate across 28 diverse models.
