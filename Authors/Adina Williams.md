# Adina Williams

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Adina Williams is a Research Scientist at Meta's Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) team in New York City. She holds a PhD in Linguistics from NYU. Her work focuses on the intersection of linguistics, cognitive science, and natural language processing, with a particular emphasis on responsible AI and dataset development.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adina's research explores computational morphology, natural language inference, and the evaluation of humanlikeness in large language models. She is a key contributor to work on measuring and mitigating social biases in generative AI models and improving the robustness of NLP benchmarks.

### Significant Achievements
She has been a central figure in the creation of large-scale NLP datasets and evaluations at Meta. Her work has bridge the gap between theoretical linguistics and large-scale machine learning, particularly in understanding how models process complex semantic and syntactic structures.

## Major Publications

- [MultiNLI: A Large-Annotated Corpus for Learning Natural Language Inference](https://aclanthology.org/N18-1101/) (2018) - *One of the most widely used datasets for evaluating NLI capabilities.*
- [Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110) (2022) - *Co-author of a major framework for comprehensive model evaluation.*
