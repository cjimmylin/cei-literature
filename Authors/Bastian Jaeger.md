# Bastian Jaeger

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bastian Jaeger is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Social Psychology at Tilburg University, where he also completed his PhD. His research is deeply rooted in social psychology, with a particular focus on the mechanisms of moral judgment and altruistic behavior.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work investigates how people form first impressions, the underlying cognitive processes of social biases, and the factors that influence altruistic decision-making. He is also a strong proponent of meta-science and rigorous research methodology.

### Significant Achievements
With over 3,100 citations, Dr. Jaeger has made significant contributions to the understanding of moral psychology. He explores complex topics such as meat consumption and the formation of social first impressions across different contexts.

## Major Publications

- [Situational factors shape moral judgments in the Real World](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspa0000294) (2022) - *Large-scale collaboration exploring the ecological validity of moral judgment research.*
- [First impressions of faces: The role of situational context](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jesp.2017.06.002) (2017) - *Influential study on social perception.*
