# Christian Demmler

## Biographical Summary

Christian Demmler is a Research Associate at the University of Leipzig, working within the AKSW (Agile Knowledge Engineering and Semantic Web) research group. His research focuses on the intersection of the Semantic Web and Natural Language Processing, particularly in the domain of Question Answering (QA).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **QA Benchmarking**: Developing frameworks to systematically evaluate Knowledge Base Question Answering (KBQA) systems.
- **Semantic Technologies**: Investigating how linked data and knowledge graphs can enhance information retrieval.

### Significant Achievements
- **QA Benchmarks**: Co-authored "Benchmarking question answering systems", helping to establish rigorous metrics for the KBQA community.

## Major Publications
