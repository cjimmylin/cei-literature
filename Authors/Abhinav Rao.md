# Abhinav Rao

## Biographical Summary

Abhinav Rao is a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Maryland, College Park, where he is advised by Prof. Yizheng Chen. He recently completed his Master’s at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU). His research is dedicated to making artificial intelligence more secure, ethical, and aligned with complex human values.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abhinav investigates the ethical reasoning capabilities of large language models and their vulnerability to "jailbreaks"—attempts to bypass safety filters. He develops frameworks for in-context ethical policies and studies how moral and cultural biases manifest in generative AI.

## Major Publications
