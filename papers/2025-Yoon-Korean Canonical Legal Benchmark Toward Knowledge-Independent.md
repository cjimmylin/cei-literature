## Abstract

Korean Bar Exam-based benchmark with question-level supporting precedents. KCL-MCQA (283 questions, 1,103 precedents) and KCL-Essay (169 questions, 550 precedents, 2,739 rubrics). Disentangles reasoning from parameterized knowledge.

## Data Description

Korean Canonical Legal benchmark (KCL) consisting of KCL-MCQA (283 multiple-choice questions with 1,103 supporting precedents) and KCL-Essay (169 open-ended questions with 550 precedents and 2,739 rubrics), derived from the Korean Bar Exam.
