## Key Research Themes

### 1. LLMs Exhibit Utilitarian Bias
- LLMs consistently favor **impartial beneficence** (helping the many) over **individual rights**
- Reject **instrumental harm** more strongly than humans
- Show "artificial moral compass" distinct from human lay populations

### 2. Humans Distrust Utilitarian AI
- Paradox: Humans expect AI to be utilitarian but distrust utilitarian AI advice
- AI perceived as high-competence/low-warmth (utilitarian profile)
- Humans perceived as high-warmth/low-competence (deontological profile)

### 3. Multi-Agent Utilitarian Boost
- Groups of LLMs become MORE utilitarian than individual LLMs
- Mechanism differs from humans: reduced norm sensitivity vs outcome sensitivity

### 4. Healthcare Concerns
- Medical trolley dilemmas: Some LLMs endorsed clearly unethical actions (amputation without consent) up to 80%
- Critical need for domain-specific ethical alignment

### 5. Moral Uncertainty is Critical
- Single ethical theory alignment is fragile
- Aggregation approaches (MEC, AMULED) more robust
- Hybrid deontological + consequentialist frameworks needed
