# Joris Baan

## Research Summary

PhD student at University of Amsterdam's Institute of Logic, Language and Computation. Research focuses on uncertainty, calibration, and human disagreement in NLP. Lead author on "Stop Measuring Calibration When Humans Disagree" (EMNLP 2022), addressing theoretical problems with calibration metrics in tasks with inherent human disagreement. Published "Interpreting Predictive Probabilities: Model Confidence or Human Label Variation?" (EACL 2024). Co-organized First Workshop on Uncertainty-aware NLP (UncertaiNLP).
