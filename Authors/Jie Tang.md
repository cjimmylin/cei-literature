# Jie Tang

## Research Summary

Jie Tang (born 1977) is a WeBank Chair Professor of Computer Science at Tsinghua University and Director of the Foundation Model Research Center (FMR) of AI Institute of Tsinghua University. He received his Ph.D. in computer science from Tsinghua University in 2006. He is known for building the academic social network search system AMiner (formerly ArnetMiner), launched in March 2006, which has attracted 30 million users from 220 countries/regions worldwide. His research interests include artificial general intelligence (AGI), data mining, social networks, machine learning, and knowledge graphs.

Tang is a leading figure in Chinese AI research, having authored the open-source GLM-serial models including ChatGLM-6B, which has achieved nearly 10 million downloads worldwide. His work on social network mining led to the SIGKDD Test-of-Time Award (10-Year Best Paper) for AMiner. He has published 400+ articles in major computer science conferences including IJCAI/AAAI (30+), NeurIPS/ICML (10+), and KDD (40+).
