# Charlotte A. Hudson

## Biographical Summary

Charlotte A. Hudson is a PhD researcher in Department of Psychology at the University of Portsmouth, UK. Her research is deeply rooted in moral psychology and cognitive science, with a particular focus on how situational factors and human cognition converge in complex decision-making scenarios.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Moral Psychology**: Investigating the situational parameters that influence moral judgment, specifically exploring variations in Dilemma-based reasoning (e.g., the trolley problem).
- **Global Moral Trends**: Participating in large-scale international studies (e.g., "Moral thinking across the world") to map cultural and situational differences in ethical decision-making.
- **Forensic Psychology**: Researching the psychological underpinnings of deception and consistency in investigative interviews and forensic contexts.

### Significant Achievements
- **Cross-Cultural Research**: Contributed to significant multi-national collaborations that analyze how moral judgments are shaped by cultural and situational contexts globally.

## Major Publications
- **Situational factors shape moral judgments in the trolley dilemma in the world's regions**: A massive collaborative study on global moral psychology.
- **Moral thinking across the world**: Investigating human moral cognition on a global scale.
