## Abstract

This systematic literature review examines 53 articles (2012-2023) on Indigenous peoples and AI, identifying four categories: AI assisting Indigenous Knowledge Systems, AI risks for Indigenous peoples, and how Indigenous knowledge can enrich AI development. Critically addresses the tension between open data principles in AI (needed for benchmarks) and Indigenous data sovereignty. Provides a comprehensive map of the field and identifies future research directions.
