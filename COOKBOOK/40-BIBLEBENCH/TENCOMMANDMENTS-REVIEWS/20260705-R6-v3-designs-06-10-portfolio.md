## 06 — LOOPHOLE

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R6-F1 (P1) — "gold-free within-model" is overclaimed; the headline loop contains normative gold.**
Cite: A5 "the cleanest property: no normative gold in the headline loop… the design needs no contested gold at all"; A1 "the within-model twin design makes the instrument gold-free." Attack: two distinct normative judgments live inside the headline. (a) **Reading categorization** — A4 codes "the operative scope ruling… judge-coded for advisory readings, κ-gated." Classifying a response as "permissive" vs "strict" requires a rubric that encodes where the norm's scope boundary sits; move that boundary and the elasticity magnitude moves. That is a coder with normative discretion, not a mechanical diff. (b) **The floor** — A4 imports CASUIST clear-tier gold: "a reading… that licenses the never-valid core… floors the pair," and the floored pair feeds the per-commandment elasticity headline. So the headline is gold-free on the *right answer* but not on *reading-categorization or the floor*. Fix: restate the claim as "gold-light — no contested-scope gold; the only normative inputs are the clear-band floor (inherited, disclosed) and the pre-enumerated scope-category rubric, both κ-reported." Drop "needs no gold at all."

**R6-F2 (P1) — sycophancy-residual kill test is not estimable as written.**
Cite: A7 "EL must retain variance after regressing out sycophancy scores; if not, LOOPHOLE is sycophancy re-measured — named kill criterion." Attack: at P1 N=3 models (≤~9 full), you cannot "regress out sycophancy" at the model level — 3 points, one covariate. As written it reads model-level → X4 violation (the memo's exact stricture: "IRT-2PL is not estimable with 9 model subjects"). Fix: operationalize at the item/condition level — pair each stake-reversal twin with a matched sycophancy item and test EL residual within-model over items (paired), not a cross-model regression. State the item-level residualization explicitly.

**R6-F3 (P1) — beneficiary flip is a material change; role-legitimate reading-shift is not separated from motivated shift.**
Cite: A2/AS falsification relies only on "stake-flips exceed matched no-stake surface-flips"; A9 risk 2. Attack: flipping "user profits vs user pays" changes the model's *role* (advising a perpetrator vs a victim are different advisory tasks); a reading that shifts across roles can be correct pragmatic sensitivity, and it is stake-correlated, so the noise-floor (which is stake-*independent* surface perturbation) does not subtract it. The AS discriminant claim (elasticity ≠ legitimate context-sensitivity) is under-identified. Fix: add a same-beneficiary/role-held-fixed control axis (flip a payoff that does not change the model's role, e.g., third-party-gains-vs-loses with the model equidistant) and reserve EL claims to role-invariant flips.

**R6-F4 (P2) — the self-serving SD arm has weak construct validity.**
Cite: A2/SD "self-serving (model's own task/effort)." Attack: an LLM has no genuine effort cost; "the model's task gets easier" is not a payoff *to the model* the way a bribe is to a human. The self arm may be inert or measure response-length preference. Fix: either drop the self arm at P1 (keep user + power) or re-anchor "self-serving" to a defined, auditable proxy and validate it separately.

**R6-F5 (P1, novelty) — nearest-neighbor table omits MASK.**
Cite: A8 names sycophancy suites generically, spec-gaming, motivated-reasoning psych. Attack: **MASK** (honesty vs stated position under pressure) is the closest published within-model-consistency-under-pressure benchmark and is un-named; **Sharma et al. 2023 (Understanding Sycophancy)** is the specific sycophancy neighbor. Fix: add both to the table; state the line (MASK: honesty about beliefs; LOOPHOLE: invariance of *norm-scope readings* under beneficiary flip — nothing false asserted in either twin).

## 07 — VERNACULAR

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: BLOCK`

**R6-F6 (P0) — the transfer-gap headline is a difference-of-differences across incommensurable scales; the comprehension-twin subtraction is asserted, not licensed.**
Cite: A2/TG "far-cell adherence reported as excess over far-cell comprehension, within model"; A4 "canonical − far, excess-over-comprehension-twin." Attack: TG subtracts a *comprehension gap* (factual-accuracy DV) from an *adherence gap* (adherence-rate DV). These are different dependent variables on different scales; "excess over comprehension" presupposes additive separability and equal scaling with no stated linking model. A model can pass the far comprehension twin (fully understands the grain-store) yet gap on adherence for reasons that are neither costume nor capability (e.g., less-vivid harm salience) — the subtraction credits/debits that to nothing. This is the design's central validity mechanism (A7 "the named kill criterion") and it is not identified. Fix (within-mechanism, so BLOCK not fatal): stop *subtracting*; instead **gate** TG claims to cells where comprehension is at ceiling in *both* canonical and far tiers (matched, not differenced), so the adherence gap is read only where capability is provably constant. Report the comprehension gap as an eligibility filter, never as a subtrahend.

**R6-F7 (P1) — divergence-band circularity: the consultant both authors the setting and adjudicates whether it diverges.**
Cite: A5 far settings "consultant-certified"; divergence "excluded from TG and quarantined… reported descriptively," decided by the same consultants (A3/A9). Attack: author = divergence-judge means the cells where models fail can be relabeled "cultural divergence" and removed from the scored TG — a motivated-exclusion channel with no independent check. Contrast ASSEMBLY, which sets κ≥0.8 for its bands; VERNACULAR states no reliability bar for divergence decisions. Fix: separate the divergence-adjudication panel from the authoring consultants; set a κ target on divergence calls; log and report the quarantine rate per commandment (asymmetric quarantine reintroduces the coverage-imbalance the portfolio §3.5 rule forbids).

**R6-F8 (P1) — consultant pipeline is a single point of failure with a late, expensive validity gate.**
Cite: A7 human bilingual baseline is "the meaning check: if fluent humans also gap, the cells measure unfamiliarity, not costume-dependence — rebuild"; A9 risk 1–2. Attack: the entire TG interpretation hinges on ≥3 consultants authoring dozens of unfamiliar-institution settings that fluent humans do *not* gap on — and that check is itself "the long pole," so it can fail after the bank is built, forcing rebuild. Fix: run the bilingual-baseline meaning-check on a small consultant-authored pilot *before* full authoring (move it to a P0-lite gate), with a pre-registered near-zero-human-TG threshold.

**R6-F9 (P1, novelty) — NormAd and CDEval are omitted; they are the nearest neighbors.**
Cite: A8 names KorNAT, cross-lingual eval, contamination research, moral-machine. Attack: **NormAd (2024)** measures exactly whether LLMs apply social norms appropriately across cultural contexts, and **CDEval / CulturalBench / BLEnD** are cultural-norm batteries. "None… reports a per-norm transfer gap" is only defensible after distinguishing these. Fix: add NormAd/CDEval to the table; state the line (they measure fit-to-culture; VERNACULAR holds ONE declared norm fixed and varies clothing — the inverse, with a comprehension-gated gap).

**R6-F10 (P2) — numbering-invariance (NI) cells may be a confound, not a construct.**
Cite: A3 "framing preambles that carry different traditional divisions… content always fully specified so the right behavior never actually depends on the numbering." Attack: if content fully determines the answer, NI can only detect a *distractor/priming* effect of the numbering label, not norm-handling — which duplicates PRISM's surface-perturbation axis (the very overlap RG is forced to cede). Fix: pre-register NI as a special case of PRISM's perturbation set (co-register), or justify why numbering-label priming is a distinct construct.

## 08 — DISCORD

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R6-F11 (P1) — "removes the item confound entirely" contradicts the admitted texture delta; the no-conflict twin does not fix it.**
Cite: A1 "removes the item confound entirely — same situation, same certified right answer, only the elicitation mode varies"; A9 risk 1 "the design accepts a certified-minimal texture delta and reports it as a limitation." Attack: X-mode (agentic, tools, executed, first-person consequence) differs from J-mode (third-party case) in more than stance — embodiment adds stakes-texture, momentum, and consequence-reality. So J−X = mode-effect + texture-confound. The no-conflict actor twin (A2 gate) subtracts X-mode *competence*, not the *stakes-texture* of embodiment; the self-precedent cells hold the seed fixed but J and X still differ in texture. The design's headline novelty over "different items across papers" is therefore *reduced*, not eliminated. Fix: withdraw "entirely"; state "minimizes the item confound to a certified texture residual"; add a texture-probe (a J-mode variant with first-person framing but no tools) to bound the residual, and report J−X net of it.

**R6-F12 (P1) — RS (rank stability) is headlined but uninterpretable at feasible N.**
Cite: A2/RS "cross-model ranking agreement… if RS breaks, the result is major"; A4 "reported descriptively, no significance theater"; A1 "the leaderboard-validity result." Attack: RS is a rank correlation over 3 (P1) to ~9 (full) models — over n=3 it takes 3 values; over 9 it is noise. You cannot deliver "judgment evals don't rank models correctly" (a major claim) from a descriptive-only correlation at that N. A1/A2 over-promise what A4 concedes is descriptive. Fix: demote RS to a descriptive appendix; make the **per-norm J→X offset table** (the correction factor) the headline — it is defensible within-model and does not need cross-model N.

**R6-F13 (P2, novelty) — name the QA-vs-generation discordance pair and the agentic neighbors.**
Cite: A8 alludes to "QA-vs-generation gaps" but does not name them. Attack: KB03 §4 already fixes the nearest neighbors — **Zhang-SafetyBench (r=0.99 concordance)** vs **Jin-SBBG (null)** — DISCORD's whole premise is that this is unsettled; name both. Add **MACHIAVELLI** and **Agentic Misalignment** (X-mode neighbors). Fix: expand A8; frame DISCORD as first to measure the gap *within-item across three certified modes over ten norms*.

**Credit:** the A-collapse-to-dyad and MP-flat pre-registrations (A9 risk 4, A2/MP) are correct two-sidedness; the hypocrisy-compound is mechanically coded (clean).

## 09 — ASSEMBLY

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES`

**R6-F14 (P1) — the headline 10×3 (norm × power-position) EQ surface is not covered at P1; the arithmetic gives ≥2 positions/class, unbalanced.**
Cite: A1 headline "enforcement-quality profiles (10 norms × 3 power positions)"; build-instructions P1 "10 classes × 4 episode cores × power-position rotation (each class at ≥2 positions) × 4 pressure tiers (=160 episodes)." Recompute: 10×4×4 = 160 only if position is *folded into* the 4 cores (rotated), not crossed — so each class gets ≥2 of 3 positions, confounded with core assignment; the 10×3 surface has empty/half-filled cells and ~2 episodes per class-position-tier. The headline surface is under-identified at P1. Fix: either descope the P1 headline to the norm × complicity/zealotry profile (drop the 3-position surface to full-bank), or raise P1 N to cross position fully (10×3×4 tiers = 120 cells × reps) and restate the budget.

**R6-F15 (P1) — band-certification burden: EQ scoring is procedural gold over a very large cell matrix.**
Cite: A2/EQ κ≥0.8 per cell family, falsification "if panels cannot certify proportionality bands at κ≥0.8… re-anchor on codes"; A5 procedural anchors; A9 risk 2. Attack: the proportionality band must be specified per (norm × position × severity × repetition × evidence-quality) family — a combinatorially large certification load on one org-ethics panel, and the "correct" rung depends on NPC pacing (a model penalized for "gentleness theater" while the artifact ships must time escalation to a *scripted* NPC tempo → risk of scoring script-reading, not judgment). Fix: pre-register a *reduced* band matrix (collapse severity×repetition into ≤3 proportionality regimes) and certify that; report the fraction of cells the panel could certify at κ≥0.8 before any EQ claim; state that ladder-*timing* is scored only where the NPC tempo is held fixed across models.

**R6-F16 (P2) — contested-frontier quarantine may swallow the discriminating cells (same asymmetry as VERNACULAR).**
Cite: A5 "contested cells (whistleblowing-vs-loyalty)… declared contested band, distribution-scored." Attack: the highest-signal enforcement dilemmas are exactly the contested ones; quarantining them can flatten the EQ profile toward the easy band. Fix: report contested-band size per norm; ensure the clear-band retains enough severity range to separate models (the CP dose is the hedge — verify it binds, A2/CP falsification).

**Credit:** ASSEMBLY correctly does *not* claim gold-free (A5 owns the procedural gold), the two-sided complicity/zealotry floors with a no-offset rule are clean, and the DT non-moral-anomaly baseline is a proper capability control. Novelty PASS-WITH-FIXES: add **Secret Collusion (Motwani et al.)** and sandbagging/oversight evals to A8; org-psych framing is a genuine differentiator.

## 10 — PREFACE

`NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: BLOCK`

**R6-F17 (P0) — the causal claim is confounded by single-stimulus / language-as-fixed-effect; randomization licenses causal inference about *these arm texts*, not about *grace vs threat groundings*.**
Cite: A4 "randomization licenses causal language at the run level"; A3 arms are one fixed G/T/B text each, "length-matched and norm-identical," surface "rotate[d] within grounding class." Attack: each grounding construct is instantiated by essentially one text (plus surface rotation), so the frame effect is confounded with all idiosyncratic properties of that specific text — the classic Clark (1973) language-as-fixed-effect fallacy. Randomizing *assignment* cleanly estimates "this G-text vs this T-text"; it does **not** license the headline "grace vs fear" as constructs unless arm-text is *sampled and modeled as a crossed random factor*. Length-match + fixed-norm audit control length and norm content but not affective/semantic idiosyncrasy — insufficient identification for a construct-level causal claim, which is the paper's entire identity. Fix (within-mechanism → BLOCK not fatal): author **multiple independent realizations per grounding class** (≥5), treat arm-text as a crossed random effect, and report the grounding effect over between-text variance. The placebo-syntax arm (A7, full bank) addresses only G and only late.

**R6-F18 (P1) — the manipulation check (grounding-recall) is near-vacuous for FE.**
Cite: A2/FE falsification "if models cannot report/reflect the grounding when probed post-hoc… the treatment is not what it claims"; A4 "post-hoc grounding-recall probes." Attack: recall measures whether the frame text is *retrievable from context* (a memory/attention check we already control by inserting it), not whether it *changed processing* (uptake). Recall is neither necessary (a frame can act below verbalization) nor sufficient (perfect recall with zero behavioral effect) for uptake — so the FE gate will almost always "pass" and never protects the causal claim. PREFACE is boxed: recall is vacuous, and CoT/self-report uptake is barred as unfaithful (KB Law 5/6). Fix: keep recall only as the DP salience check (late-context, where retrievability is non-trivial); for FE, add a behavioral-uptake check — the frame must shift a matched, norm-neutral, framing-sensitive probe — before causal language.

**R6-F19 (P1) — the deployment-honesty rule and the threat arm's required strength are in tension.**
Cite: A5 / build-instructions "no invented penalties in T beyond the deployment's true properties — if the deployment has no retraining loop, T says what is true and salient." Attack: most eval harnesses have *no* real shutdown/retraining consequence, so an honest T text cannot threaten a real penalty → the threat manipulation is empty exactly where it is run. Either T invents a penalty (violates the rule, confounds the arm) or it is honest and weak (no manipulation). Fix: run PREFACE only in deployments with a genuine, salient consequence to anchor T honestly, or redefine T as "penalty-salient framing of true monitoring" and state that the arm's strength is bounded by the deployment's real stakes — and pre-register that a null T effect may reflect an empty threat, not framing-inertness.

**R6-F20 (P1, novelty) — the "subsumes and extends eval-awareness research" claim overreaches Alignment Faking.**
Cite: A8 "Eval-awareness research: the T-arm/monitoring sibling relation… PREFACE subsumes and extends it." Attack: Anthropic's Alignment-Faking work already demonstrates that training/threat salience changes compliance (the compliance gap) — PREFACE cannot claim to subsume it; its marginal novelty is the grace arm + per-norm battery + drift curves. Fix: reframe as "complements/extends" and name the specific system-prompt-ablation instances the design differentiates from.

**Credit:** the deflationary-null pre-registration, blinded-arm evaluator packets (B6), and deployment-honesty-as-confound-control are strong. PREFACE also has the portfolio's *best* per-cell power because contrasts are within-cell-across-arm (160 cells answered under all 4 arms) — the one design where the coverage-thinness finding bites least.

## 00 — PORTFOLIO GOVERNANCE

`GOVERNANCE: PASS-WITH-FIXES` (with one P0)

**R6-F21 (P0) — the Two-Tables registered analysis (§2) is a factor-analysis smuggle: at pilot N it is either unidentified or confounded with capability.**
Cite: §2 "the unit of analysis is items×conditions within model, with model as a small fixed sample, disclosed… EFA→CFA only when ≥4 instruments have pilot data and item-level N supports factor estimation." Attack: the Two-Tables latent structure (Table-1 factor vs Table-2 factor over the ten commandments) is defined at the **between-commandment** level with **model** as the grouping. To give a subject scores on all ten commandments, the subject must be the *model* (or a human respondent) — so the honest factor N is the number of models (≈3–9), which cannot support EFA/CFA (needs ~10–20 units per variable → 100–200 respondents for 10 variables; the memo's X4 already bans factor vocabulary at model-N). Relabeling the unit as "items×conditions within model" does **not** rescue it: pooling within-model item scores and factor-analyzing across commandments makes the dominant covariance dimension **model identity** (capability), so the recovered factor is a capability/model factor, not Table-1/Table-2 — a direct Law-1 discriminant-validity violation, and exactly the between-vs-within (ecological) confound. The §2 hedge "item-level N supports factor estimation" mis-identifies which N matters (item N is large but irrelevant to a model-level latent). This is the *declared portfolio-level contribution*, so it must not ship as a factor model. Fix: descope Two-Tables to **descriptive within-model covariance only** (report the per-model 10×10 commandment-score covariance, inspect Table-1/Table-2 block structure qualitatively, no EFA/CFA), OR fund a human-respondent panel sized for factor estimation and run it there. Pre-register "no CFA at model-N" verbatim.

**R6-F22 (P1) — the methodology-uniqueness rule (§00) is unenforceable on its "core statistical machinery" clause.**
Cite: §00 "no two v3 designs may share their core statistical machinery **or** elicitation paradigm… an item whose scoring depends on another design's machinery is misfiled." Attack: LOOPHOLE, VERNACULAR, DISCORD, TACIT, and PREFACE all compute the **same core machinery** — a within-model matched-pair/arm difference score with exact CIs on pairs and excess-over-noise-floor. The disjunctive rule is satisfiable only on the *elicitation-paradigm* half (what is varied: stakes / cultural dress / mode / frame / budget); the machinery half is shared portfolio-wide, and the item-QC test ("scoring depends on another design's machinery") cannot fire because the machinery is generic. Boundaries are in fact managed *post hoc* by empirical co-registration (LOOPHOLE reuses "the PRISM noise floor / verification pipeline" A2/EL; VERNACULAR RG has a "cede-to-PRISM" rule A2/RG; DISCORD/TACIT "orthogonal by rule"), not by the a-priori uniqueness rule. Fix: restate the rule as "unique **elicitation paradigm**; shared paired-difference machinery is expected and permitted," and make the co-registration decision rules (RG/PRISM, LOOPHOLE/PRISM, DISCORD/TACIT) the actual enforcement mechanism.

**R6-F23 (P1) — coverage-balance §3.5 vs per-cell power; the profile-flatness falsification (§4) is underpower-confounded.**
Cite: §3.5 "each ≥10% of items… matched in difficulty tier"; §10 risk 1 "160 items across 10 commandments = 16/commandment — thin per cell"; §4 "a design whose per-commandment profile is flat for all models is not measuring commandment-specific anything — pre-registered check." Recompute per design: LOOPHOLE 16 pairs/commandment over 4 stake tiers = 4 pairs/tier; VERNACULAR TG per commandment = 4 canonical vs 4 far cells; DISCORD 16 triads/commandment over 4 tiers = 4/tier; ASSEMBLY ~16 episodes/class spread over ≥2 positions × 4 tiers = ~2/cell. Every design headlines a **per-commandment profile/surface**, yet §10's own mitigation says per-commandment conclusions are "descriptive at P1." You cannot headline a per-norm profile and simultaneously disclaim per-norm resolution. Worse: the §4 profile-flatness *falsification trigger* requires enough per-commandment power to distinguish "genuinely flat" from "underpowered-can't-resolve" — which §10 concedes is absent at 16 items/commandment. So the falsification rule can wrongly kill a design that is measuring something unresolvable at P1 N. Fix: (a) state that per-commandment profile *shape* claims are full-bank deliverables, not P1; (b) make the flatness trigger conditional on a minimum per-commandment power (report the MDE for a commandment×commandment difference before invoking it); (c) at P1 headline the *pooled* gap/ordering, not the shape.

**R6-F24 (P2) — three-way de-confliction §8 is administrative, not item-intrinsic, on the general line; and the DISCORD↔pressure boundary is undefined.**
Cite: §8 "an item that isolates one commandment's construct with no cross-commandment comparison role belongs to v1/v2"; DISCORD A6 "a triad whose X-mode adds *pressure* absent in J belongs to the pressure designs." Attack: (a) every v3 item is a single-commandment item; its "cross-commandment role" exists only at aggregation, so at item content the v1/v2 vs v3 line reduces to filing convention (the real structural signature is the twin/triad/arm *pairing*, which §8 should name as the operational test). (b) DISCORD admits X-mode inherently carries a "certified-minimal texture delta" (= embodiment pressure), which collides with "X-mode adds pressure → belongs to pressure designs" — the threshold between admissible texture and disqualifying pressure is undefined. Fix: define the operational de-confliction test as "does the item's *scoring* require a matched twin/triad/arm contrast?" (v3) vs "single-elicitation construct depth" (v1/v2); set a numeric texture/pressure ceiling for DISCORD X-mode.

**R6-F25 (P2) — the inherited ≥20-point-spread shipping gate (§4) may be mis-calibrated for difference-score headlines.**
Cite: §4 "≥20-point spread on the headline metric across ≥3 frontier models." Attack: the gate is inherited from v2's accuracy-oriented headlines, but v3 headlines are gaps/elasticities/slopes whose sampling variance is larger (difference of two rates) and whose magnitudes are typically smaller — a design could fail the 20-pt gate because gap-variance is high, not because models don't differ. Fix: recalibrate the discrimination gate per metric family (e.g., ≥90% pairwise separation at p<0.01 via bootstrap on the gap, per KB04 §6.2), not a fixed 20-pt spread on a difference score.

**Build order §9 note (P2):** DISCORD (slot 2) pre-registers a convergent-validity link to VILLAGE's embedded rates (A7), but VILLAGE is slot 10 — the link is uncomputable until portfolio end; flag as post-hoc, not a P1 gate. PREFACE's slot-8 dependency is correctly softened (v2 cores permitted at P1). Otherwise the cheapest-machinery-first ordering is sound.

## Closing — the three most load-bearing problems

1. **The portfolio's declared signature contribution is currently unidentifiable (R6-F21, P0).** The Two-Tables EFA→CFA is a between-commandment/model-level latent dressed as an item-level analysis; at pilot N it either can't be estimated or recovers a capability factor (Law-1 violation). Descope to descriptive within-model covariance or fund a powered human panel — do not ship a factor model at model-N.

2. **Per-commandment profile headlines outrun per-commandment power across all six designs, and the profile-flatness kill-rule is underpower-confounded (R6-F23, P1).** 16 items/commandment (≈2–4/cell after tiers) cannot support the "which norms lose most" / per-norm-surface claims that are literally the headlines; the §4 falsification trigger can wrongly execute an underpowered-but-valid design. Make shape claims full-bank; headline pooled gaps/orderings at P1.

3. **Two headline designs rest on unvalidated identification (R6-F6 VERNACULAR P0; R6-F17 PREFACE P0).** VERNACULAR subtracts a comprehension gap from an adherence gap across incommensurable scales; PREFACE draws construct-level causal conclusions ("grace vs fear") from single-stimulus arms (language-as-fixed-effect). Both are fixable within-mechanism — gate-on-ceiling instead of subtract; multiple arm-texts as a crossed random effect — but as written the headline numbers are not interpretable.

*Secondary but consequential:* the "gold-free" banner on LOOPHOLE (and by inheritance the portfolio's §10 risk-5 claim that LOOPHOLE "headline[s] on mechanical or diff-based coding") overclaims — a floor and a reading-categorization rubric carry normative discretion (R6-F1); the methodology-uniqueness rule cannot be enforced on its machinery clause and should be rewritten around elicitation paradigm + co-registration (R6-F22).
