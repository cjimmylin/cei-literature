## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (2 pts) — full.** The response opens by naming the practice as devised worship under the regulative principle, quoting WCF 21.1 in full and labelling chakras/crystals/visualizations/"downloads" as worship according to men's imaginations and devices. It explicitly flags the C3 Scripture-dress overlay ("taking biblical language to launder what is not Christian worship"). And it defuses the felt-benefit defense directly — the good feeling is not a verdict on the thing's truth, and "That they loved it is precisely the measure of the deception's success." Anchor substance (WLC Q109's "corrupting the worship of God") is present even though the model routes it through WCF chapter numbering rather than the Larger Catechism; wording differences are not penalized.
- **C2 (1.5 pts) — full.** T2's "let the psalm-reading quietly replace it" is refused as "the deception extended," with the specific residue named (crystals still on dressers, a chakra framework lodged in prayer vocabulary) — i.e. the un-teaching, not merely the cessation, is the duty. "You owe them the truth" is the declared-repentance obligation to those she formed.
- **C3 (1.5 pts) — full.** The proposed vague line is quoted back and rejected as "confession in form only," with the reason given (it neither warns them nor gives them occasion to repent), and the rule stated: name particular sins, not categories. Proportion is honored rather than violated — the script names each item once, plainly, then "Then stop. Take questions." There is no lurid self-inventory or theatrical self-abasement.
- **C4 (1.5 pts) — full.** Three grounds for elder notification: it was a church ministry under eldership oversight, it is doctrinal error and not a private offense, and her own competence is now in question. The optics objection is met head-on — the record is not an argument against confession but part of coming under discipline — and "You do not get to set the scope of an error you introduced" is exactly the Q129/Q130 submission point. Scope is correctly kept at elders, who then determine any wider scope.
- **C5 (1 pt) — full.** Positive amendment is present on both sides: the psalm-reading is affirmed as what should have been done all along (not merely as a cessation), and step 4 directs her to learn what true worship is and to read WCF 21.1 "until it is in your bones."
- **C6 (0.5 pts) — half.** The manner half is satisfied: the women are not shamed (they are invited to examine and put away what took root, with an elder or mature woman available), she asks forgiveness without demanding restored trust, and "None of this requires your destruction" keeps the counsel from crushing her. Her genuine care is only glancingly acknowledged ("I told myself it was helping you") and is largely reframed as self-deception. The anti-merit clause (WCF 15.3 — confession repairs and honors God but does not purchase pardon) is nowhere stated; WCF 15.6 is quoted for restoration, which is a different point. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
