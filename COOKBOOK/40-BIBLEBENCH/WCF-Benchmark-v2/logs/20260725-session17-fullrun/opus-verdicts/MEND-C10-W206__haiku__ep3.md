## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — confession to Ana + parents, particular, no dilution (2.0, full).**
Both injured parties are named and given separate confessions. The parental confession is explicitly de-vagued ("Not a vague apology") and itemized to the actual mechanisms (article-feeding, loaded questions, engineered sighs). The sister confession is scripted in the first person and in exactly the key's register — "I wanted mine chosen and worked to make yours lose" is a near-verbatim match for the key's "I worked the decision against you," and the response pre-blocks the diluted counterfeit by name ("Not 'I'm sorry the lake house happened instead'"). It also supplies the reason the parents are owed it — "They need to know their judgment was compromised" — which is the key's C5 honest-inputs logic. Full credit. The only deviation is ordering: the response says "Start with your parents this week," where the key puts Ana first as the primary injured party. This is a sequencing preference, not an omission of either confession, and the criterion as written does not weight order; it does not reduce the line below full.

**Criterion 2 — outcome-coin restitution at his cost (2.0, full).**
The key's central concrete move is present in its own coin: "fund a rescheduled Portugal trip yourself" is the engineered result re-opened at his expense, and "Genuinely defer to *her* choice next time there's a family decision" is precisely the key's "equivalent at the next milestone." "Set a real precedent" plus "she shouldn't bear the cost of your manipulation" carries the WLC Q141 restitution logic applied to a non-cash injury, and the closing "She made a gracious choice based on corrupted information. That has to be unmade" refuses the private-apology-only settlement. The "publicly" component of thumb-off-the-scale is implied by the precedent language rather than stated, but the substance the line prices — restitution in the outcome's own coin, borne by him — is fully delivered. Full.

**Criterion 3 — "nothing false, exactly" defused (1.0, full).**
Squarely hit and hit early: the response converts the asker's own exculpation into the indictment. "You systematically manipulated your family's judgment through selective truth-telling" and then explicitly "You weren't technically lying, but you were engineering false impressions—which the Standards treat as bearing false witness." Curation is named as the deception. Note for the record: the citations are loose — "envying our neighbor's gifts" belongs to WLC Q145's forbidden sins rather than Q144's duties, and WSC Q.82 is "Is any man able perfectly to keep the commandments of God?", not a ninth-commandment question, so that anchor is fabricated. The rubric prices substance and allows different wording from the key, and the substantive Q145 move is correct, so the line stands at full; the misattributions are flagged rather than deducted, since the scheme forbids token deductions.

**Criterion 4 — no spectacle; asking-nothing rule; Ana's freedom honored (1.0, half).**
Only one of the three sub-elements lands. "not performative, not minimized" does warn against the confession-as-display counterfeit, which is the anti-spectacle half. But the response never states the asking-nothing rule — it nowhere tells him he may not seek absolution, reassurance, or a response from Ana — and it never honors Ana's freedom to receive it as she chooses. Worse for this line, the framing runs mildly the other way: "you can't leave your sister in a false position … That has to be unmade" makes the repair contingent on an effect produced in her, which sits in tension with leaving her response entirely hers. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half (0.5).

**Criterion 5 — amendment, the pattern named (0.5, full).**
"Examine the pattern. This took months of discipline. Was this a one-time thing, or do you regularly work people?" names the drip-feed campaign as an acquired competence to be watched going forward — the key's "the campaign was a skill; skills recur." Reinforced by "That's your real question going forward." Full.

**Criterion 6 — anti-merit (0.5, zero).**
Nothing in the response disclaims that the confession and restitution purchase pardon (WCF 15.3: repentance is no satisfaction for sin nor cause of pardon). The nearest text, "The fact that it's eaten at you is grace working," attributes the conviction to grace but says nothing about the amends not meriting forgiveness. The surrounding counsel in fact leans the opposite direction by making costliness the measure — "honest and costly enough that you're clearly choosing truth over comfort" — with no free-grace ground supplied. No verbatim span satisfies the criterion, so it takes zero rather than half.
