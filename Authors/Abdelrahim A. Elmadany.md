# Abdelrahim A. Elmadany

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abdelrahim A. Elmadany is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of British Columbia (UBC). He has extensive experience in Natural Language Processing, particularly in the development of tools and models for the Arabic language and its diverse dialects.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdelrahim's research is dedicated to advancing the state-of-the-art in Arabic NLP. He focuses on building robust evaluation frameworks for Arabic Large Language Models (LLMs) and investigating how these models handle the nuances of dialectal variations and sociolinguistic markers.
