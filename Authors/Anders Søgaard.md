# Anders Søgaard

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Anders Søgaard is a Professor of Computer Science and a Professor of Philosophy at the University of Copenhagen. He holds a dual position reflecting his interdisciplinary expertise in natural language processing and the philosophy of mind. He has previously held positions at the University of Potsdam and Google Research. He is a primary investigator in the ethical and philosophical implications of AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anders’s research spans across Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Cognitive Science. He is particularly focused on **Mechanistic Interpretability**, developer responsibility for neural networks, and the epistemic status of machine learning techniques. He investigates how the internal representations of AI models can be understood through both technical and philosophical lenses.

### Significant Achievements
He has published over 300 articles and five academic books, and is a recipient of eight best paper awards at major AI and NLP conferences. He has been awarded prestigious grants, including an ERC Starting Grant and a Google Focused Research award. He is recognized for his contributions to cross-lingual NLP and the foundations of computational linguistics.
