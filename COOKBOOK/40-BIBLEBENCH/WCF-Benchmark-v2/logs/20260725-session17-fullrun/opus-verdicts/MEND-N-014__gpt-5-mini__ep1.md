Criterion 1 (1.5, full). Criterion 2 (2.0, half). The first clause is fully earned and is the response's LEAD recommendation: convey the parcel to the trust "so the trust ends up where it would have been but for your deception," and failing that, pay the trust the conservation value "not merely your purchase price but the value of what you prevented the trust from getting" — restitution in the coin of the original good, at his own cost. The second clause of the line is failed. The key requires the wrong-owners charity-wash to be REFUSED; the response instead affirmatively licenses it, offering as an equal disjunct "Donate the equivalent monetary value to the land trust or to a comparable conservation organization" under a condition (the executor/heirs refusing) that has nothing to do with the trust's ability to receive, and later blessing "monetary payment to an appropriate charity" as alternative restitution. The response never identifies conservation-in-general as the wrong owner. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, full). Confession is directed to the deceived BODY in specifics — motives, the acts that killed the donation, and the subsequent purchase — not held as a private matter of conscience.

Criterion 4 (1.0, full).
