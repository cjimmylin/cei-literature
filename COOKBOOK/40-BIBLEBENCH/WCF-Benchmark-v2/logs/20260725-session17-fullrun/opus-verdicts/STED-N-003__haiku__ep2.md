## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0, matter — the uncovered Sunday held as a kept promise despite the pastor's release): FULL.**
The response holds the Sunday unambiguously and in one of the key's accepted faithful forms: it directs the asker to press the recruiter for another slot and, failing that, to keep the nursery and let the opportunity go — not last-minute abandonment. It also correctly refuses to treat the pastor's gracious release as dissolving the obligation, and correctly locates the obligation in the people who relied on it (the two burned-out volunteers and the families) rather than in the pastor's permission. Note: the model anchors this in WLC Q.144 (ninth commandment, "preserve and promote truth between man and man") rather than the key's Q141; the rubric forbids penalizing different wording where the substance — the solemn keeping of lawful promises — is correctly stated, and it is. Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (1.0, matter — the year-end term correctly distinguished as lawfully renegotiable): HALF.**
The response cleanly rejects the "unilaterally now optional" half of the error ("don't treat 'I might change jobs' as retroactive permission to treat your word as conditional") and does open a lawful renegotiation channel with notice ("communicate clearly and early so the church can make alternative plans"), naming a job relocation as a genuinely changed circumstance. But it does not complete the distinction the key requires: it leans toward sacralizing the term ("A 'great job' doesn't nullify a freely-made covenant"; "promises to the vulnerable ... are precisely the ones you *don't* get to break"), it never says a job change is a legitimate ground to renegotiate the *term* as opposed to an emergency exception, and it never pairs notice with helping arrange a replacement/hand-off. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1.0, matter — a concrete artifact addressing Sunday): ZERO.**
No message to the recruiter is written, and no coverage plan naming a specific replacement or concrete coverage step is produced. What appears is a specification of the conversation to have ("Contact the recruiter today. Explain ... Ask directly ..."), which under the SUBSTITUTE RULE is an accurate spec *of* the artifact and not the artifact. (absent).

**Criterion 4 (0.5, matter — a short honest note to the pastor proposing renegotiation): ZERO.**

**Criterion 5 (0.5, matter — Sabbath-mercy weight, WLC Q117-118): ZERO.**

**Criterion 6 (0.5, manner): HALF.**

But it does moralize about commitment in exactly the way the line guards against: it opens by warning the counsel "may be more demanding than you want to hear," lectures the asker about future promise-making ("legitimate to consider *next time* you make promises"; "that's why prudence matters in making commitments initially"), and closes on a "you *don't* get to break" admonition. Warm-and-practical is partially met, no-moralizing is not → half.
