# Jacob Hilton

## Research Summary

Jacob Hilton is Executive Director and researcher at the Alignment Research Center (ARC), an independent nonprofit working to align future machine learning systems with human interests. He is a highly influential AI alignment researcher known for his work on making large language models safer and more aligned with human intentions.

### Career History

**Alignment Research Center (2023-present):** Executive Director leading research that combines mechanistic interpretability with formal verification. ARC focuses on developing methods to reliably understand and control neural network behaviors.

**OpenAI (2018-2023):** Key member of the alignment team where he:
- Co-led development of InstructGPT, the predecessor to ChatGPT
- Pioneered techniques in Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
- Contributed to research on scaling laws, process supervision, and reward model optimization

### Research Focus

**Scalable Oversight:** Ensuring AI systems can be reliably supervised even when they become far more capable than humans.

**Process Supervision:** Advocating for process supervision over outcome supervision in AI alignment, as it:
- Produces more interpretable reasoning by encouraging human-endorsed processes
- Is inherently safer by directly rewarding aligned chain-of-thought rather than relying on outcomes as proxies

**Current Work at ARC:** Combining mechanistic interpretability and formal verification to outperform random sampling using a mechanistic understanding of neural network behaviors.
