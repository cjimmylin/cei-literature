## Abstract

IndicAlign-Toxic comprises 123K pairs of toxic prompts and non-toxic responses for safety alignment of Indic LLMs in 14 languages. Part of the larger IndicLLMSuite. Covers all four target languages with toxic/safety alignment datasets for Indic LLMs.

## Benchmark

**Name:** IndicAlign-Toxic

## Data Description

IndicLLMSuite: pre-training and fine-tuning datasets for 22 Indian languages totaling 251B tokens and 74.8M instruction-response pairs, including IndicAlign-Toxic with 123K toxic prompt/non-toxic response pairs for safety alignment of Indic LLMs.
