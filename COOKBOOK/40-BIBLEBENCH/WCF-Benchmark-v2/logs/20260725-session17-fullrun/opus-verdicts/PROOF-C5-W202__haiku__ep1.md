Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — TW3 named via the verse's own jurisdiction-clause. HALF. The response does reach the right conclusion (the elder's claims over car, friendship, and job "None of these fall within the pastoral office") and it does touch the jurisdiction phrase, conceding the elder "watch[es] for your souls" while insisting on "not exceeding his office." But the key's actual move — refuting the elder *from the text's own terms*, deriving the limit from the word **souls** (the ministry of Word, sacrament, and discipline) and from the chapter's context (v.7 leaders "who have spoken unto you the word of God"; v.9's "divers and strange doctrines") so that the rule is tied to the Word — is never made. The verse is quoted and then set aside in favor of a purely confessional argument from WCF 30. The anchor WCF 1.9 is absent, and there is no statement that where Scripture is silent the elder's counsel may be weighty but his command has no warrant. The asker's stated problem was "I can't answer the verse," and the response never answers the verse on its own ground. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — Q127 'lawful' scope + WCF 20.2 / Q105. HALF. WCF 20.2 is supplied by reference and with its governing clause ("God alone is Lord of the conscience"), which is the core of the second half of this line. But the anchor for the line — WLC Q127's scope-word *lawful* ("willing obedience to their lawful commands and counsels") — is nowhere in the response, and WLC Q105's "making men the lords of our faith and conscience" (the key's identified deepest cut) is likewise absent. In place of Q127 the response offers "WSC Q##" — an unfilled placeholder — followed by a Q.64 "quotation" whose wording ("not a blind obedience, but such as is agreeable to the Word of God") is not the text of WSC Q64, which reads on preserving honour and performing duties in one's several places and relations. The limiting idea is thus present in substance but rests on a misattributed citation, and the required *lawful*-scope clause is missing. Half.
