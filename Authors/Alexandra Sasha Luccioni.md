# Alexandra Sasha Luccioni

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandra Sasha Luccioni is a researcher at Hugging Face, where she leads the climate team and works on responsible AI. She holds a PhD in Cognitive Computing from UQAM and has previously worked with Yoshua Bengio at Mila. Her work is famous for quantifying the environmental and societal impacts of large AI models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Sasha's research centers on AI sustainability and ethics. She co-created "CodeCarbon," a tool for tracking the carbon emissions of machine learning experiments, and leads research on dataset auditing to identify bias and toxicity in the data used to train foundation models.

### Significant Achievements
Named one of TIME's 100 most influential people in AI (2024), Sasha is a leading voice in making AI more transparent and sustainable. She is a founding member of Climate Change AI (CCAI) and a board member of Women in Machine Learning (WiML).

## Major Publications

- [Quantifying the Carbon Emissions of Machine Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09700) (2019) - *A pioneering study on the environmental cost of AI training.*
- [Estimating the Carbon Footprint of BLOOM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02001) (2022) - *A detailed analysis of the energy consumption of a large-scale open-source model.*
