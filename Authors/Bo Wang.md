# Bo Wang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bo Wang is a researcher at the Social Computing and Information Retrieval (SCIR) lab at Harbin Institute of Technology. His work is primarily focused on the development of conversational AI that is both helpful and safe for human interaction.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates "Dialogue Safety"—the process of ensuring that AI-generated responses are free from bias, toxicity, and harmful content. He is particularly interested in building "cross-domain" safety benchmarks that can evaluate models across different conversational contexts.

### Significant Achievements
Wang is a co-developer of "SafeDialBench," a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating the safety of Chinese and English dialogue models. His work at SCIR has contributed to the technical safeguards used in several major Chinese LLM projects.

## Major Publications

- [SafeDialBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Safety Evaluation in Dialogue Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00000) (2025) - *Proposed a new standard for conversational safety.*
- [Knowledge-grounded Dialogue Generation with Task-specific Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.00000) (2022) - *Research on improving the factual accuracy of AI responses.*
