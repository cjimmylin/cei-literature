# How Deep Is Representational Bias in LLMs? The Cases of Caste and Religion

## Abstract

Systematic audit of GPT-4 Turbo revealing deeply encoded representational biases for caste and religion in the Indian context. Prompted to generate stories about birth, wedding, and death rituals, GPT-4 consistently overrepresents culturally dominant groups far beyond their statistical representation. Representational bias has a "winner-take-all" quality more biased than training data distribution, and repeated prompt-based nudges have limited efficacy.

## Dataset Information

7,200+ generated stories about life rituals in India


## Data Description

Creates a dataset of 7,200+ stories about Indian life events (birth, wedding, death) generated by GPT-4 Turbo with varying diversity prompts, annotated for religious and caste representation; dataset and codebook link to be added post-review.
