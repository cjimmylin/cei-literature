# Alexandra Chouldechova

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandra Chouldechova is the Estella Loomis McCandless Associate Professor of Statistics and Public Policy at Carnegie Mellon University's Heinz College. She earned her Ph.D. in Statistics from Stanford University. She is a preeminent expert on algorithmic fairness and accountability, with a focus on high-stakes predictive systems in the public sector.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandra’s research investigates how predictive models introduce or amplify biases in domains like criminal justice and child protective services. She develops frameworks for vetting algorithms and advocates for transparency in data-driven decision-making. Her work often challenges the "accuracy vs. fairness" trade-off, demonstrating how technical metrics can hide deep societal harms.

### Significant Achievements
She is a founding member of the executive committee for the ACM FAccT conference. Her research has received significant support from the NSF and Amazon, focusing on human-algorithm collaborations. Her work on recidivism risk assessment and the fairness of child welfare algorithms has been influential in both academic and policy circles.
