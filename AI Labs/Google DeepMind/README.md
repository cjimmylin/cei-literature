# Google DeepMind

## Overview
Google DeepMind is a world-leading AI research laboratory formed by the merger of Google's Brain team and DeepMind. It focuses on AGI and scientific breakthroughs.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** London, UK / Mountain View, USA
- **Region:** Europe / North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Google DeepMind Models]]

- **Gemini 1.5 Pro**: Features industry-leading 2M token context window.
- **Gemini 2.0 Flash**: Next-gen multimodal model with real-time speed.
- **AlphaFold**: Breakthrough AI for protein structure prediction.

## Alignment & Ethics
Focused on "Socially Beneficial AI," DeepMind has dedicated teams for safety, ethics, and socio-technical research.
