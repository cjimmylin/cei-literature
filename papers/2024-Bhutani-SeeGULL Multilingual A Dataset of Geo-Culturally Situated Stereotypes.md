## Abstract

25K+ stereotypes in 20 languages with human annotations in 23 languages. Separate annotations for Spanish in Spain and Spanish in Mexico. Maximum overlap seen in Spain Spanish (13.2%). Published at COLM 2024.

## Data Description

Multilingual dataset of 25K+ geo-culturally situated stereotypes across 20 languages with human annotations from 23 regions, used to evaluate model safeguards against stereotype endorsement.
