# Mariia Vladimirova

## Research Summary

Mariia Vladimirova is a Senior Research Scientist at Criteo AI Lab in the Fundamental Deep Learning team. Her work focuses on fairness in online systems and fundamental deep learning research. She led the development of FairJob, a real-world dataset for fairness in online systems, presented at NeurIPS 2024 in collaboration with Université Paris Dauphine-PSL.
