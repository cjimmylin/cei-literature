## Abstract

First NLG benchmark for Indonesian, Javanese, and Sundanese covering summarization, QA, chit-chat, and machine translation. Includes IndoBART and IndoGPT pretrained models and Indo4B-Plus corpus.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** Foundational NLG benchmark for three Indonesian languages, demonstrating importance of localized language pretraining.
