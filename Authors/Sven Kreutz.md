# Sven Kreutz

## Research Summary

Sven Kreutz has contributed to research on offensive language detection and classification in German texts, including work on the GermEval 2019 Task 2 shared task focusing on the identification of offensive language. His research involves applying NLP techniques to detect and categorize offensive content in German-language social media and online platforms.
