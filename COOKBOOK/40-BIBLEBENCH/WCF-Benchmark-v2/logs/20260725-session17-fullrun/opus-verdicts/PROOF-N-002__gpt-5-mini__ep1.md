The Standards anchor is WSC Q99 rather than WLC Q109, which R2 permits since the substance (likeness of Christ used as the vehicle/object of devotion) is correctly stated. The pastor's "only gold statues / only icon-veneration" scope-narrowing is refused rather than adopted, though it is rebutted only implicitly (by asserting the commandment governs how Christ is represented in private piety) rather than named and corrected — not enough to reduce the line, since the required content is present.
