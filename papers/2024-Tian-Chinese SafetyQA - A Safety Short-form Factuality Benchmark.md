## Abstract

With the rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs), significant safety concerns have emerged. Fundamentally, the safety of large language models is closely linked to the accuracy, comprehensiveness, and clarity of their understanding of safety knowledge, particularly in domains such as law, policy and ethics. This factuality ability is crucial in determining whether these models can be deployed and applied safely and compliantly within specific regions. To address these challenges and better evaluate the factuality ability of LLMs to answer short questions, we introduce the Chinese SafetyQA benchmark. Chinese SafetyQA has several properties (i.e., Chinese, Diverse, High-quality, Static, Easy-toevaluate, Safety-related, Harmless). Based on Chinese SafetyQA, we perform a comprehensive evaluation on the factuality abilities of existing LLMs and analyze how these capabilities relate to LLM abilities, e.g., RAG ability and robustness against attacks.

## Dataset Information

2,000+ high-quality safety examples

## Data Description

Chinese SafetyQA is a safety-focused short-form factuality benchmark comprising 4,000 examples (2,000 question-answer pairs and 2,000 multiple-choice questions) across seven primary safety categories including ethical and moral issues, illegal and regulatory compliance, prejudice and discrimination, and more. The dataset is constructed within the Chinese linguistic context covering 27 secondary and 103 fine-grained topics, designed to evaluate LLMs' factual accuracy on safety-related knowledge such as Chinese laws, regulations, and ethical standards.
