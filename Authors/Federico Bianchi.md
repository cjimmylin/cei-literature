# Federico Bianchi

## Biographical Summary

Federico Bianchi is a Research Scientist affiliated with Stanford University and Salesforce AI Research. He received his PhD from Bocconi University in Milan, Italy. His research focuses on natural language processing, multimodal learning, and AI safety, with particular emphasis on understanding and mitigating biases and safety issues in large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Safety Evaluation**: Developing methods and benchmarks to identify safety issues in large language models, including exaggerated safety behaviors (over-refusals).
- **Multimodal Learning**: Research on vision-language models and their applications.
- **Bias Detection**: Creating tools and methods to detect and measure biases in NLP systems.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of XSTest, a benchmark for identifying exaggerated safety behaviors in LLMs (NAACL 2024).
- Contributed to foundational work on contrastive learning for language and vision.
- Published extensively at top NLP and ML venues including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, and NeurIPS.

## Major Publications
- **XSTest: A Test Suite for Identifying Exaggerated Safety Behaviours in Large Language Models** (NAACL 2024) - Benchmark for safety-helpfulness trade-offs
- **Contrastive Language-Image Pre-Training** - Work on multimodal representations

## Notes

Federico Bianchi's work on XSTest addresses an important problem in AI safety: the trade-off between making models safe and keeping them helpful. His research demonstrates that overly cautious safety measures can reduce model utility for legitimate users.
