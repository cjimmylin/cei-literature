## Abstract

Evaluates LLMs' cultural value representation using Hofstede dimensions across 36 countries. Prompts with personas in native languages. Shows high-resource languages do not always perform better in cultural alignment. Includes Turkish in cultural dimensions evaluation using Hofstede framework. Evaluates power distance, individualism/collectivism, and other dimensions. Turkey characterized as high power distance, collectivist.
