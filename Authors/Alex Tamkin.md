# Alex Tamkin

## Biographical Summary

Alex Tamkin is a research scientist at Anthropic. He holds a PhD in Machine Learning from Stanford University, where he was advised by Noah Goodman at the Stanford AI Lab (SAIL). His research focuses on understanding and improving the real-world impact of AI systems, with a focus on interpretability and human-AI interaction.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
At Anthropic, Alex works on privacy-preserving analysis of AI usage data (CLIO), tracking the economic impacts of AI, and developing new human-AI interfaces like "Claude Artifacts." He also develops interpretability methods to better understand and steer large models.

### Significant Achievements
During his doctoral studies, Alex pioneered domain-general benchmarks for self-supervised learning (DABS) and developed methods for eliciting human preferences using language models. He is a recipient of the Open Philanthropy AI Fellowship.

## Major Publications

- [DABS: A Domain-Agnostic Benchmark for Self-Supervised Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12062) (2021) - *Evaluates the universality of self-supervised learning methods across text, image, and sensor data.*
- [Understanding the Real-World Impacts of AI](https://anthropic.com/index/clio) (2024) - *Anthropic research on tracing model usage and impact.*
