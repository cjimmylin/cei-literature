# Jiayi Zhou

## Research Summary

Jiayi Zhou is a Ph.D. student at Peking University and a member of the PKU-Alignment research team. She is a co-author of the comprehensive survey "AI Alignment: A Contemporary Survey" published in ACM Computing Surveys, which provides an up-to-date overview of the alignment field identifying four key principles: Robustness, Interpretability, Controllability, and Ethicality (RICE). The survey outlines the landscape of current alignment research, decomposing it into forward alignment (making AI systems aligned via alignment training) and backward alignment (gaining evidence about systems' alignment and governance).
