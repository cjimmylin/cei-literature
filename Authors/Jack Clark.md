# Jack Clark

## Research Summary

Jack Clark is Co-Founder and Head of Policy at Anthropic, an AI safety and research company. He is one of the most influential figures in AI policy, bridging the gap between technical AI research and policy-making at the highest levels of government and international organizations.

Prior to co-founding Anthropic in 2021, Clark served as Policy Director at OpenAI (2016-2020), where he worked on strategy, communications, and AI policy. Before entering the AI field, he was a technology journalist covering distributed systems, quantum computing, and AI for The Register and Bloomberg News.

Clark is the author of Import AI, a widely-read weekly newsletter on AI research with over 70,000 subscribers. He has held numerous advisory positions including:
- Co-chair of the AI Index at Stanford University (2017-2024)
- Vice-Chair of the National AI Advisory Committee (NAIAC) (2021-2024)
- Expert member of the Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence
- Co-chair of the OECD's working group on AI and Compute
- Non-resident research fellow at the Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET) at Georgetown University

In 2023, Clark gave a briefing at the first United Nations Security Council meeting on AI's threats to global peace. He has advocated for developing standardized methods to test AI capabilities, potential misuses, and safety flaws.
