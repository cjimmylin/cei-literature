# Eve Fleisig

## Biographical Summary

Eve Fleisig is a PhD student in Computer Science at UC Berkeley. Her research focuses on the intersection of NLP and social impact, particularly examining fairness, bias, and the subjective nature of annotation tasks in machine learning. She is known for her work on developing more nuanced frameworks for evaluating fairness in NLP systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research examines how annotator perspectives and disagreement affect NLP system development, particularly for subjective tasks like toxicity and fairness assessment. She develops frameworks that better account for legitimate disagreement rather than treating it as noise.

### Significant Achievements
Lead author on FairPrism, a framework for understanding annotator rationales in fairness-related NLP tasks. Her work has been influential in moving the field toward more nuanced handling of subjective annotations.

## Major Publications

- **FairPrism: Evaluating Fairness-Related Harms in Text Generation** (2023) - Framework for fairness evaluation
- Research on annotator disagreement and its implications
- Work on toxic language detection and mitigation
