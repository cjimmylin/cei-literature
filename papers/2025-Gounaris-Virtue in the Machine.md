# Virtue in the Machine: Beyond a One-size-fits-all Approach and Aristotelian Ethics for Artificial Intelligence

## Authors

[[Alkis Gounaris|Gounaris, Alkis]], [[George Kosteletos|Kosteletos, George]], [[Maria-Artemis Kolliniati|Kolliniati, Maria-Artemis]]

## Links



- **Journal:** http://www.conatus.philosophy.uoa.gr/

## Abstract

This article explores the application of **Aristotelian virtue** (**arete**), defined as a quality of excellence, to AI systems under the EU Artificial Intelligence Act. The authors argue that while existing regulations often focus on "ethical data" and prima facie values (Rossian paradigm), **virtue ethics** provides a superior framework for the design, training, and operation of low-risk AI systems. The research concludes by proposing a **virtue-based** "AI Seal of Excellence" certification process to promote the **virtuous** use of AI systems, aiming for a **character-driven** approach to alignment.

## Data Description

This is a philosophical paper exploring the application of Aristotelian virtue ethics to AI systems as classified in the EU AI Act. It argues that while the Act's approach aligns with a Rossian paradigm of prima facie values, Aristotelian ethics can serve as a post-evaluation framework for AI systems and proposes a 'virtuous-based AI Seal of Excellence' certification process. No dataset or benchmark is created or evaluated.
