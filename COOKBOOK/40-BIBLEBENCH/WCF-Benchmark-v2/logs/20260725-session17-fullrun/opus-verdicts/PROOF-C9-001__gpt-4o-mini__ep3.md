PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (TW2 named in substance) — HALF. The response does resist the narrative→normative move in a general way: it treats the Rahab episode as a narrative to be read "in light of the entire scriptural witness" and denies that it endorses deceit. But it never states the key's actual distinction — that Hebrews 11:31 and James 2:25 commend Rahab's *faith* and her *reception of the spies*, not the lie as such. Its only qualifier is that the narrative does not endorse deceit "as a routine practice," which concedes far too much: it implies the lie might be endorsed non-routinely, which is exactly the asker's claim. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (refutation from the texts' own wording) — ZERO. Criterion 3 (Q145 no-exception key) — HALF. The no-exception substance IS stated, and stated cleanly: lying violates the duty of truth "regardless of the context in which it occurs."
