# Chris Alberti

## Biographical Summary

Chris Alberti is a Research Engineer at Google DeepMind (formerly Google Research). His work focuses on improving the reliability and trustworthiness of language models, with a specific emphasis on grounding, attribution, and fact verification.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Factuality in LLMs**: Developing rigorous benchmarks like "The FACTS Grounding Leaderboard" to measure how well LLMs ground their outputs in retrieved evidence.
- **Attribution**: Researching methods to ensure that model claims are supported by verifiable sources.
- **RAG Systems**: Improving retrieval-augmented generation to reduce hallucinations in open-domain question answering.

### Significant Achievements
- **The FACTS Leaderboard**: A primary contributor to this influential benchmark, which sets the standard for evaluating grounding in long-form generation.

## Major Publications
- **The FACTS Grounding Leaderboard: A Benchmark for Attribution in Long-Form Generation**: A key paper in the field of verifiable generation.
