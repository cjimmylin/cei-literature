# Cecilia Reyna

## Biographical Summary

Cecilia Reyna is a prominent social psychologist based in Argentina. She is an Adjunct Researcher at the National Scientific and Technical Research Council (CONICET) and an Assistant Professor at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba. She specializes in the psychological mechanisms underlying human decision-making and social behavior.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on the "Moral Dilemmas and Cultural Context"—specifically how situational factors influence moral judgments in complex scenarios like the trolley problem. She also works on psychometrics, validating psychological scales for the Argentinean population, and investigates the impact of global crises (like COVID-19) on social mindfulness and prosocial behavior.

### Significant Achievements
Reyna has contributed to massive, cross-cultural studies that define the "Global Baseline" of human moral reasoning. Her work is critical for identifying whether ethical principles touted as "universal" are actually shared across non-Western cultures, providing essential data for the "Cultural Alignment" of AI.
