# Matthew Pisano

## Research Summary

Matthew Pisano is an Artificial Intelligence Researcher and Software Engineer with a Master's degree in Computer Science from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. He worked as a Visiting Researcher at IBM Research (Yorktown facility) in Summer 2023, where he developed "Moral High Ground," a conversational alignment benchmark using text-based games to evaluate moral and ethical reasoning in large language models. His research focuses on model alignment using safety training on moral and ethical principles.
