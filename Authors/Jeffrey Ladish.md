# Jeffrey Ladish

## Research Summary

Jeffrey Ladish is the Director of Palisade Research, where they study the offensive capabilities of present-day AI systems to better understand the risk of losing control to AI systems indefinitely. He is also the Head of AI Insights for the Center for Humane Technology, whose mission is to align technology with humanity's best interests.

Ladish has over ten years of experience working at the intersection of cybersecurity and emerging technologies. Previously, he helped build out the information security program at Anthropic through his security consulting company, Gordian. He has helped dozens of tech companies, philanthropic organizations, and existential-risk-focused projects get started with secure infrastructure.

His research has included analyzing risks at the intersection of cybersecurity and AI, emerging biotechnology threats, and risks from nuclear war. He has helped advise the White House, Department of Defense, and other areas of government on risks from AI and emerging technologies.

Ladish is a mentor for MATS (ML Alignment Theory Scholars), a fellowship program that pairs junior AI safety researchers with senior researchers. His research contributions include "The Singapore Consensus on Global AI Safety Research Priorities" and investigations into the vulnerability of safety fine-tuning in large language models.
