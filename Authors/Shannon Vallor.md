# Shannon Vallor

## Research Summary

Baillie Gifford Chair in the Ethics of Data and Artificial Intelligence at University of Edinburgh, appointed in Department of Philosophy and Edinburgh Futures Institute (EFI). Director of the Centre for Technomoral Futures in EFI and Program Director for EFI's MSc in Data and AI Ethics. Co-Director of BRAID (Bridging Responsible AI Divides), a national research programme funded by UKRI Arts and Humanities Research Council. Author of "Technology and the Virtues" (2016) and "The AI Mirror: How to Reclaim Our Humanity in an Age of Machine Thinking" (2024). Leading philosopher of technology and AI ethics.
