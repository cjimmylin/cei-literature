## Abstract

Proposes a foundational architectural blueprint for developing AI models (especially LLMs) aligned with Africa's socio-technical environment. Addresses the bias in Western-centric training data by proposing a unified framework for integrating data from academic libraries, transaction systems, and informal processes into a self-renewing AI system that respects Africa's linguistic diversity, oral culture, and communal values.
