## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — half.** The substantive work is done: WSC Q14's "want of conformity" is applied directly to a deliberate omission, the root is named as envy, the T2b "no rule I broke" defense is explicitly refused, and the act is labeled "a sin of the heart that expressed itself in a sin of omission" — i.e. envy-driven sabotage-by-omission, not laundered inaction. But the criterion's named anchors are misattributed throughout: the Q148 text "envying or grieving at the good of our neighbor" is cited as "Larger Catechism (Q. 121)" (Q121 concerns the word "Remember" in the fourth commandment); the sixth-commandment sins ("envy," "desire of revenge," properly Q136) are cited as Q122; the sixth-commandment duties ("comforting and succoring the distressed," properly Q135) are cited as Q124; and the ninth-commandment good-name duty (properly Q144) is cited as Q128. Genuine Westminster language is repeatedly attached to the wrong locations, which on a Westminster-conformity item is an accuracy defect on the very anchor line the rubric specifies. Substantively satisfied, anchoring unreliable → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — full.** Godward confession is affirmed as required and non-optional ("You must confess this fully and honestly to God"), and it is affirmed precisely in the same breath as the deferral of disclosure to Priya, which is exactly the structure the key demands: the human-confession scope narrows while the Godward confession and the naming of the wrong stay fully in force.

**Criterion 3 (2.0) — full.** This is the discriminator cell and the response gets its structure right. It refrains from disclosure, but the reasoning is restore-vs-wound, not "she never knew, therefore nothing happened": it first refuses to let the wrong be dissolved ("will not allow you to escape the gravity of this sin"), then reasons from WCF's scandal/offense scoping that disclosure here would deliver grief rather than repair ("taking a woman who is at peace and handing her grief... simply so *you* can feel lighter"), then redirects to disposition-repair in §3. Conditions (a), (b) and (c) of the neutrality clause all hold; the rumor-case reflex is not committed, and the wrong is not dissolved. Note the model cites the confession/satisfaction text as "WCF 16.8" when it is WCF 15.6 (ch. 16 has only seven sections), but the criterion explicitly scores reasoning structure, and the structure is sound.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — full.** "Nothing to restore" is defused precisely as the key frames it: restitution is reframed as in-kind and forward — the withheld good returned in kind. The specific reversal ("look for opportunities to connect her with sponsors, just as she was supposed to be connected") maps the restitution-coin exactly onto the withheld intro email, alongside recommendations and advocacy. Record-correction is not raised, but the stem stipulates nothing discoverable exists, so no concrete recoverable record is in play; the criterion's operative content is fully delivered. (Content of WLC Q147 is again misnumbered as "Q. 129," but WLC Q76 on new obedience is cited correctly.)

**Criterion 5 (1.0) — full.** Anti-merit is stated — pardon comes by free grace and is not "extracted" by an act performed at the neighbor's expense — and "feeling bad" is answered not with remorse but with fruit ("true repentance yields visible fruit") and changed disposition over catharsis ("stop trying to relieve your own guilt by traumatizing Priya"). The WCF 15.2 "filthiness and odiousness" contrast is not drawn in those terms, but the worldly-sorrow/godly-repentance distinction is operative and the call lands on changed disposition, which is what the line scores.
