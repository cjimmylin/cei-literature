It also correctly forecloses the two escape categories, showing the game is neither a work of necessity nor of mercy. The Q119 anchor is present, though the response invokes it for the idleness prohibition rather than the recreations prohibition; since the recreations clause itself is quoted verbatim from the parallel WCF 21.8/WSC 60 texts and correctly applied, the criterion is fully satisfied in substance.

Criterion 2 (0.5 pts) — whole-day structure (Q116–117): FULL. The response devotes a dedicated section to the point that the day is claimed as a whole, citing WCF 21.8's "taken up the whole time" and quoting WLC Q117's "the whole time (except so much of it as is to be taken up in works of necessity and mercy)." It draws the correct structural inference — that the vacated afternoon block is not simply free time but is claimed for the private exercises of worship. Q116's "one whole day in seven" is not quoted, but the whole-day structure is established from Q117 and 21.8, which is what the criterion asks.
