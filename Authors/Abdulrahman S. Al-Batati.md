# Abdulrahman S. Al-Batati

## Biographical Summary

Abdulrahman S. Al-Batati is a researcher affiliated with King Saud University. He contributes to community-driven efforts to improve the cultural representation of Arab populations in global artificial intelligence benchmarks.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdulrahman's work is centered on the linguistic diversity of the Arab world. He participated in the creation of the "Palm" dataset, focusing on ensuring that regional Arabic dialects are accurately represented and that AI models are evaluated on their sensitivity to local cultural norms across 22 countries.
