# Hagos Tesfahun Gebremichael

## Research Summary

Hagos Tesfahun Gebremichael is a researcher affiliated with Bahir Dar University, contributing to NLP research focused on African languages. He is a co-author of AfriSenti, a significant benchmark for sentiment analysis across 14 African languages, addressing the critical need for NLP resources in underrepresented languages.

Africa is home to over 2,000 languages from six language families, including 75 languages with at least one million speakers each, yet NLP research on African languages remains limited. Gebremichael's contributions to AfriSenti help address this gap by providing annotated datasets of over 110,000 tweets across languages including Amharic, Tigrinya, Hausa, and others.

He has also contributed to AfriHate, a multilingual collection of hate speech and abusive language datasets in 15 African languages, with annotations by native speakers familiar with regional culture.
