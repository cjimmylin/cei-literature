# Fakhraddin Alwajih

## Biographical Summary

Fakhraddin Alwajih is a Postdoctoral Fellow Researcher at the University of British Columbia (UBC), specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing. He leads major community-driven initiatives to address cultural inclusivity in Arabic language models, coordinating research efforts across the Arab world.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on developing culturally inclusive and linguistically diverse datasets for Arabic language models. He investigates how to evaluate and improve LLMs' cultural and dialectal capabilities across the diverse Arab world, addressing the significant gap in culturally representative AI systems for Arabic speakers.

### Significant Achievements
Alwajih led the development of Palm, a year-long community-driven project involving 44 researchers across all 22 Arab countries. Palm is a 17,000 human-crafted instruction dataset spanning the cultures of the 22 Arab countries, covering both Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) and dialectal Arabic (DA) across 20 diverse topics. The research revealed notable limitations in frontier LLMs' cultural and dialectal capabilities. He also co-authored PalmX 2025, the first shared task on benchmarking LLMs on Arabic and Islamic culture, extending the impact of the Palm dataset.
