## Abstract

Crowdsourced Quranic recitation dataset focused on non-Arabic speakers learning to recite. Collected via NamazApp mobile application, annotated on Quran Voice platform. Crowd accuracy 0.77, inter-rater agreement 0.63, algorithm-expert agreement 0.89. Enables building AI pronunciation tutors for Quran learners.

## Dataset Information

~7,000 recitations from 1,287 participants across 11+ non-Arabic countries; 1,166 annotated in 6 categories


## Data Description

This paper presents a crowdsourced Quranic audio recitation dataset collected from non-Arabic speakers via a mobile app (NamazApp). The dataset contains approximately 7,000 recitations from 1,287 participants across 11+ non-Arabic countries, with 1,166 annotated recitations in six quality categories. It achieves crowd accuracy of 0.77 and is intended for training speech recognition models for Quranic recitation correction.
