# Bokai Xu

## Biographical Summary

Bokai Xu is a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. He is a member of the THUNLP (Tsinghua Natural Language Processing) group, where he is research-led by Professor Maosong Sun. He previously completed his undergraduate studies at Tsinghua University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bokai’s research focuses on the development and evaluation of Large Language Models (LLMs). He is a key contributor to the **GLM** (General Language Model) and **ChatGLM** series, which are among the most influential open-source bilingual (Chinese/English) foundation models. His work specifically targets pre-training methodologies, reasoning capabilities, and the integration of external knowledge into language models.

### Significant Achievements
He has co-authored several highly cited papers in major NLP and AI conferences (ACL, EMNLP, ICLR) regarding model efficiency and knowledge-augmented language processing. He is an active contributor to the open-source community, particularly within the Zhipu AI and Tsinghua ecosystem.

## Major Publications

- [GLM: General Language Model Pretraining with Autoregressive Blank Infilling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10360) (2021) - *A foundational Chinese LLM.*
- [ChatGLM: A Family of Self-aligned Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00000) (2024) - *Scaling and aligning bilingual LLMs.*
