# Abduragim Shtanchaev

## Biographical Summary

Abduragim Shtanchaev is a researcher at the Mohamed Bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi. His work bridges the gap between sophisticated vision systems and the need for global linguistic and cultural inclusivity in AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abduragim's research is focused on large-scale multimodal evaluation. He contributed to the "All Languages Matter" benchmark, which provides a framework for testing Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) across 100 languages, specifically evaluating their ability to respect local sensitivities and cultural contexts.

## Major Publications
- [All Languages Matter: Evaluating LMMs on Culturally Diverse 100 Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16508) (2024) - *A comprehensive benchmark for global linguistic inclusivity.*
