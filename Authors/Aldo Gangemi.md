# Aldo Gangemi

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aldo Gangemi is a Full Professor at the University of Bologna and an Associate Researcher at the Institute of Cognitive Sciences and Technologies (ISTC-CNR) in Rome. He coordinates the HumanisticAI branch of the Alma Mater Institute for Human-centred AI. He was previously the Director of ISTC-CNR and has been an invited professor at Stanford and Sorbonne Paris Nord.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aldo's research focuses on Neurosymbolic AI, integrating knowledge representation and reasoning with machine learning and cognitive science. He investigates knowledge patterns across data, ontologies, and natural language, developing hybrid methods for knowledge extraction and reasoning on knowledge graphs.

### Significant Achievements
He has authored over 300 peer-reviewed papers and is a co-designer of foundational ontologies like DOLCE. He founded the Semantic Technologies Laboratory (STLab) and has chaired major international conferences in the Semantic Web field. His work spans applications in cultural heritage, medicine, law, and robotics.
