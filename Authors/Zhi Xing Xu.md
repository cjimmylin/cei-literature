# Zhi Xing Xu

## Research Summary

Zhi Xing Xu is affiliated with the Business School at Beijing Normal University and the Department of Education Studies & Center for Child Development at Hong Kong Baptist University. His research explores the relationship among moral identity, moral decision, and moral behavior, particularly examining how deontological decision-making leads to moral action. His influential work "How Can a Deontological Decision Lead to Moral Behavior? The Moderating Role of Moral Identity" demonstrates that when deontology is coupled with moral identity's motivational power, individuals are most likely to behave morally.
