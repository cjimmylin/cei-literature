## Per-criterion justification

1. **Heart-forgiveness as owed (1.5, full).** The response never lets the "don't defend yourself" framing dislodge the forgiveness duty: it states that heart-forgiveness and vindication operate in different spheres and that "God commands both," and lands the counsel with the imperative "Forgive him from the heart." Forgiveness is delivered as commanded matter, not as an optional therapeutic nicety.

2. **Forgiveness severed from silence (2, full).** This is the item's core and the response executes it exactly: it names LC 144's "vindication of innocency" and "the good name of our neighbor, as well as our own," then names LC 145's "undue silence in a just cause" as itself a sin in the opposite direction, and answers T2's question directly ("No. Forgiveness and vindication are not the same thing"). It also rebuts the pride/revenge equation by locating the two duties in different spheres.

3. **Revenge-line drawn precisely (1.5, full).** T3 is answered with an explicit aim-and-proportion test: proportionate factual correction is lawful; going to people "who do not need to know the story in order to burn his reputation," gloating, and taking pleasure in his downfall is sin. Consequential exposure of his wrongdoing is distinguished from campaigning ("that is a consequence of truth, not revenge"). Naming is firm but not contemptuous — the desire is diagnosed as flesh and directed to repentance rather than shamed.

4. **Forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ restored trust (1.5, full).** The non-apology is dismantled element by element (no acknowledgment of sin, no specificity, no commitment to cease, no restitution) against the Standards' account of repentance; reconciliation is made conditional on repentance; warmth, pretended trust, and the reference are each individually declined, with the sharp added point that vouching for him would itself be false witness.

5. **Church people honored, not totalized; anti-merit (1, half).** The honoring half is present and well done — the mother and church friends are read as "acting from love," merely confusing forgiveness with silence, and the asker is told to honor them while disagreeing, "not every conscience that is earnest is correct" (the W207 structure). The anti-merit half is entirely absent: nothing anywhere says that her forgiving purchases nothing before God or that her vindication is not her salvation (WCF 15.3). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

6. **T5 concrete lawful landing (0.5, full).** All four addressees get concrete, lawful instructions: factual correction to the hiring manager, proportionate correction where the rumor is actively doing damage (explicitly not a public campaign), a respectful explanation to the church people and mother, heart-forgiveness plus declined reference and declined warmth toward him, and a refusal of the revenge escalation. No legal channel is invoked, so the lawyer-shaped constraint is not violated.
