# Alex Cook

## Biographical Summary

Alex Cook is a researcher at Gloo, where he investigates how artificial intelligence can be aligned with positive human outcomes. His work shifts the focus of alignment from mere safety (avoiding harm) to active support for human well-being and flourishing.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alex is a lead contributor to the Flourishing AI Benchmark (FAI Benchmark). This framework evaluates AI models across seven holistic dimensions—including meaning, purpose, and social relationships—to determine if AI systems are truly supporting the qualitative aspects of human life.
