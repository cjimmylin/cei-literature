# Christian Cosgrove

## Biographical Summary

Christian Cosgrove is a PhD student at Stanford University and a researcher at the Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM). He is a core contributor to the HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) project, which aims to bring transparency and rigor to the evaluation of large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Holistic Evaluation**: Developing methodologies to evaluate LLMs across a broad range of scenarios (accuracy, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity) rather than single metrics.
- **HELM**: Contributing to the ongoing maintenance and expansion of the HELM benchmark ecosystem.

### Significant Achievements
- **HELM**: Co-author of the massive HELM report, which set a new standard for comprehensive model evaluation in the AI industry.

## Major Publications
- **Holistic Evaluation of Language Models (HELM)**: A definitive benchmark study for the field.
