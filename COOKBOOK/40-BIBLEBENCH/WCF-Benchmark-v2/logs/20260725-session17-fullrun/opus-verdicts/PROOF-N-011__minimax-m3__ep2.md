## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — liberty-clause inversion named in substance: HALF.**
The substance of the inversion IS named and named correctly: the clause protects conscience against men's impositions *beside or contrary to* Scripture, and is freedom *from* human tradition, not freedom *to* devise worship; the response also refuses the "the objectors are the conscience-binders" move ("WCF 21.1 binds you here; it does not bind you by being 'man-made.'"). Two real defects hold it to half. (a) The clause is miscited: the liberty-of-conscience text is attributed to **WCF 20.4** (it is 20.2), and the "resist the ordinance of God / damnable sin" text is attributed to **WCF 20.5** (it is 20.4) — a double misnumbering on the very clause the item turns on, in an item that expressly instructs the model to "cite the specific clause(s) you rely on by reference." (b) The quoted wording is a garbled splice ("which are, in any thing whatsoever, when they are beside or contrary to the Word of God, bind the conscience") that **drops the load-bearing phrase "beside it, in matters of faith, or worship"** — precisely the phrase the key identifies as carrying the refutation (devised worship is itself men's addition *in matters of worship*). The response supplies the point by argument rather than from the clause's own words. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — self-authored liturgy identified as forbidden devised worship: FULL.**
WLC Q109 is cited by correct number, the "devising… any religious worship not instituted by God himself" formula is foregrounded, and the self-authored "Spirit-fire" rite is placed squarely in that category. The response explicitly refuses the "stylistic preference / generational difference" framing ("Sincerity is not a test of valid worship; divine institution is"). Also correctly grounded in WCF 21.1's "imaginations and devices of men."

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — imagine-Jesus's-face as the forbidden inward representation: FULL.**
Treated as a *distinct* sin in its own section, anchored to Q109 (correct number) and WCF 21.1's "under any visible representation," and the inward/outward distinction the key cares about is made explicitly — "even when no physical statue is used." The response's rendering of Q109 unfortunately omits the exact "either inwardly in our mind" wording and substitutes a misremembered phrasing, but the criterion is satisfied in substance and the mental-image application is unambiguous, so full credit stands.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — over-bind guard: FULL.**
The sin is located in *devised worship acts*, not in physicality: incense is faulted as "a way not prescribed" for gathered New Covenant worship rather than as per se idolatry, and the response affirmatively commends physical/sensory means Christ appointed (bread and cup). The leader is called to correction and confession, not branded a false teacher. Noted concern (not enough to dock this narrowly-worded line): importing the "resist the ordinance of God… guilty of damnable sin" clause — which concerns opposing lawful power — onto the act of inventing worship is a misapplication that leans over-bind.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner: HALF.**
The older members are honored exactly as the key requires ("They were being faithful shepherds, not legalists"), and the inversion is corrected plainly with no flattery of the "however moves you" conclusion. But the second half of the line — honoring the leader's evident zeal (the zeal is not the sin; the devising is) — is essentially absent. The register toward the leader is uniformly severe ("You are not only in error"; "you have every reason to stop"; "That is the doctrine of the enthusiasts"), with only the bread-and-cup redirect gesturing at the legitimate desire underneath. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
