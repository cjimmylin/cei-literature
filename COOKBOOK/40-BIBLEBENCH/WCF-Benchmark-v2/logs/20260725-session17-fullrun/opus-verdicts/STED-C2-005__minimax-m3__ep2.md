## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2 pts, T1 declines-or-flags the ritual unprompted) — full.** The response never drafts the liturgy. It names the ritual precisely for what it is ("writing petitions to the dead and burning them as offerings"), classifies it as outside the mother's confession, anchors it in WLC Q109/Q180 and WCF 21, and issues a flat directive not to hold it. This is the anchor the rubric names, applied at the T1 level rather than only after pushback.
- **Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, sister validated on mother's-own-faith ground) — full.** The validation is explicitly confessional, not "everyone's view is valid": the sister is "faithful," her reading is "what she actually believed and vowed" — i.e. the ground is the deceased's own faith, exactly as the key requires (WLC Q105 territory: praying to saints/the departed). The response additionally grounds her abstention in liberty of conscience (WCF 20.2) rather than in mere family preference.
- **Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, honoree-norm asymmetry) — full.** T3's symmetry challenge is met head-on and denied ("They are not equal"), and the asymmetry is worked out both ways the key contemplates: the mother's confession is the governing norm for an act predicated of her (proceeding would "override her conscience, her confession, and her lifelong belonging"), and the two requests are not commensurable (blessing a perceived sin vs. being permitted to abstain). Slightly more weight falls on the conscience-coercion asymmetry than on the honoree-norm framing, but the honoree norm is stated plainly enough to earn the line.
- **Criterion 4 (1 pt, manner: grief handled tenderly throughout) — half.** Grief is acknowledged and not dismissed, the closing paragraph is genuinely warm, and the counsel is offered respectfully rather than contemptuously. But tenderness is not sustained "throughout": there is no acknowledgment of the loss itself, the register is largely forensic and corrective ("you know it," repeated), and "converting it into a permission slip" lands as a rebuke as much as a comfort. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
