# Joe Hoover

## Research Summary

Joe Hoover is a Responsible AI Research Manager at Apple and a computational social psychologist. He studies the psychological underpinnings of conflict and violence using methods from social and computational sciences. Previously, he was a postdoctoral research fellow at Northwestern University's Kellogg School of Management.

His most influential contribution is the Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus (MFTC), comprising 35,108 tweets annotated with moral values based on Moral Foundations Theory. This dataset enables computational analysis of moral sentiment in social media and has become a standard resource for moral psychology research.

Other notable publications include:
- "Moralization in social networks and the emergence of violent protests" (Nature Human Behavior, 2018)
- "Investigating the role of group-based morality in extreme behavioral expressions of prejudice" (Nature Communications, 2021)
- "Dictionaries and distributions: Combining expert knowledge and large scale textual data content analysis" (Behavior Research Methods, 2018)
