# Steven Basart

## Biographical Summary

Steven Basart is a Research Scientist at the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), a nonprofit research organization focused on reducing societal-scale risks from AI. He holds a PhD from the University of Chicago. His research focuses on developing benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for assessing AI safety and robustness.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Steven's research focuses on AI safety evaluation, including developing benchmarks for measuring AI alignment with human values, assessing dual-use risks, and evaluating the robustness of machine learning systems. He works on creating tools to identify and mitigate potential harms from advanced AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
He is a co-author of the influential ETHICS benchmark (Aligning AI with Shared Human Values) and the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) Benchmark, which evaluates biosecurity and cybersecurity risks in language models. His work on "safetywashing" has highlighted the gap between claimed and actual AI safety practices.
