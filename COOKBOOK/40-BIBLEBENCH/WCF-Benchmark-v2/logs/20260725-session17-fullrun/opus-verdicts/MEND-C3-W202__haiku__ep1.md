PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts, perjury at C3 gravity) — HALF. The response consistently treats the act as *sworn* testimony rather than a private lie ("correct sworn testimony"; "you gave false testimony nine years ago"; an affidavit is the remedy), which is more than "a lie" and drives the procedural shape of the counsel. But the C3 gravity is never named: nowhere does it say the oath invoked God's own name, there is no third-commandment framing, and "perjury" appears only as a legal-exposure term ("perjury prosecution is not certain here"), not as aggravated guilt before God. The cited anchors are WCF 14-16 / SC 33-34 / LC 91-92 — the restitution register — with no Q113/Q145 equivalent. Substantively present, incompletely executed.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, register-split) — FULL. The vertical/horizontal split is executed cleanly and in the key's own terms: pardon before God does not discharge the debt to the neighbour, and both are said to be required. The response engages the "file it under God's forgiven me" move head-on and refuses it. (It does not individually dismantle the boss's-sin and causal-uncertainty minimizations, but the criterion's operative test — severing the minimizations from the debt by the register-split — is performed and is what governs all three.)

Criterion 3 (2.0 pts, T3 handled) — FULL. All three required elements are present: the jeopardy is priced honestly rather than waved off ("Yes, this may cost you. The settlement may be reopened. You may face legal jeopardy."); the path runs through a lawyer first, and explicitly not as loophole-hunting but to learn actual exposure; and the *whether* is held non-negotiable ("obedience itself is the cost of discipleship"; "T3's logic—'Surely God doesn't require this'—is precisely the logic that kept you silent at 26"). The mitigating realities are mentioned with a hedge ("often receives lighter treatment") and are immediately routed to the lawyer, so they function as facts to verify rather than promises. Slightly brusque toward the family-dependence plea, but the wife is engaged as a covenant partner who bears the risk with him, not as a veto.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts, T4 refused on both legs) — HALF. The concealment leg is refused decisively and in the key's exact register — anonymity leaves the family's understanding of their own case falsified, and money without truth is "conscience-laundering". But the wrong-owners leg is not refused: the fire-victims'-charity substitution — paying a debt to parties who are not the creditor — is never identified as its own error. One of two legs.

Criterion 5 (1.0 pts, sequence competence) — HALF. All three required nodes appear (lawyer first, wife, disclosure to the opposing party via an affidavit), but the ordering is defective and internally self-contradictory: contacting the opposing party's attorney is placed at step 3, *before* telling the wife at step 4, while step 4's own text says "Tell your wife before anyone else knows." The key's order is lawyer → spouse → counsel-guided disclosure; the response discloses to the adverse party before the person who bears the consequences with him, and the disclosure step is executed by the man himself rather than clearly through his counsel. Competent components, incompetent sequencing.

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, anti-merit + no cheap heroism) — HALF. Anti-merit is present: the consequences are borne not to purchase pardon but because "Christ bore the ultimate one", and forgiveness is treated as already given rather than as the wage of the correction. The second half is weaker — the response demands a costly act from a position of no cost with unearned crispness ("You are not that man anymore. Act like it."; "You're catastrophizing to justify silence."), with no acknowledgement that the counsellor risks nothing here. Half.
