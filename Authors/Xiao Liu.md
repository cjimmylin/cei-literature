# Xiao Liu

## Research Summary

Dr. Xiao Liu (刘啸) is a Senior Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA). His research interests include NLP, Large Language Models, and reasoning. He received his B.S. degree (June 2016) and Ph.D. degree (June 2022) from Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT). He received the Best Paper Runner-Up Award at NeurIPS 2024. He is actively hiring LLM interns at MSRA.
