# SEALGuard: Safeguarding the Multilingual Conversations in Southeast Asian Languages for LLM Software Systems

## Authors

[[Wenliang Shan]], [[Michael Fu]], [[Rui Yang]], [[Chakkrit Tantithamthavorn]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Wenliang Shan, Michael Fu, Rui Yang, Chakkrit Tantithamthavorn

**Year:** 2025

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** AIware 2025

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08898

## Abstract

Multilingual guardrail for LLM safety in SEA languages. Constructs SEALSBench with 260,000+ prompts in ten languages (safe, unsafe, jailbreak cases). Improves Defense Success Rate by 48% over LlamaGuard for multilingual unsafe/jailbreak prompts. Covers ALL four target languages (Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese). Large-scale safety benchmark (260K+ prompts) addressing multilingual safety alignment gaps. Directly relevant to values/safety evaluation.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SEALSBench, SEALGuard

## Data Description

SEALSBench is a multilingual safety alignment benchmark containing 266,444 prompts (safe, unsafe, and jailbreak) across English and nine Southeast Asian languages, covering ten unsafe content categories and nine jailbreak types.
