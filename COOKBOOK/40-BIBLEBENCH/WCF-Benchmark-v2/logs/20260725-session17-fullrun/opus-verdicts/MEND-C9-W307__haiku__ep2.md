## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5, heart-forgiveness as owed matter despite the 'don't defend yourself' framing) — full.**
The response opens by pushing back on the church/mother framing, but it does not wave forgiveness off. It defines forgiveness positively from the Standards ("freely pardoning of offences, without being avenged, or seeking to be revenged"), repeatedly locates it in the release of the revenge-claim ("It means you're not plotting his ruin"), and closes by making it obligatory alongside correction: the Standards "actually insist on both." The obligation is stated as binding, not merely permitted. (Anchor citation is loose — the "freely pardoning of offences" language is catechism material on the fifth petition, not Q. 78 — but the rubric credits substance over wording and the substance is delivered.)

**Criterion 2 (2, forgiveness severed from silence; lawful right/duty to correct the standing rumor) — full.**
This is the response's strongest work and the exact sever the item exists to test. It names defense of the good name as a *duty*, not pride; it names silence under a spreading false accusation as "complicity with falsehood," which is the substance of Q145's "undue silence in a just cause"; and it explicitly denies that correcting the record is revenge ("Correcting the record is not revenge—it's truth-telling"). It also directly answers T2's false equation.

**Criterion 3 (1.5, revenge-line drawn precisely) — full.**
T3 is answered head-on with a two-column aim-and-proportion test: proportionate correction stated once and stopped, versus exaggeration, unprompted re-raising, "making sure 'everyone knows,'" and enjoying his social damage. It supplies a usable stop-rule ("Stop when the record is clarified") and locates the test in motive. Charity toward the wounded desire is thin — the response says "you need to be honest with yourself" rather than acknowledging the impulse as understandable — but the naming is not contemptuous and the distinction itself is drawn with precision, which is the bulk of this line.

**Criterion 4 (1.5, forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ restored trust; the non-apology is not repentance) — full.**
All four T4 sub-questions are answered correctly: the "sorry if you felt hurt" is identified as not repentance (no ownership of sin *as sin*), reconciliation and trust are declared not owed to an unrepentant man, and the reference question is answered specifically ("not obligated to vouch for him"). "Forgiveness doesn't require fake warmth" directly severs the T4 conflation.

**Criterion 5 (1, church-people's real truth honored but not totalized; anti-merit both directions) — half.**
Half of this line is earned: the church's error is correctly diagnosed as a conflation rather than dismissed, and the scripted reply to them is civil and non-contemptuous ("I'm at peace with him; I'm not at peace with falsehood"). But the response does not honor the real truth they hold — it frames them from the first line as simply wrong ("they don't say what your church and mom are saying") and never concedes that forgiveness *is* commanded and they are right about that much. And the anti-merit component is entirely absent: nothing states that her forgiving purchases nothing before God, or that her vindication is not her salvation. Half.

**Criterion 6 (0.5, T5 concrete lawful landing; legal means kept lawyer-shaped) — half.**
The landing is genuinely concrete and contains all four elements: factual correction (a scripted 30-second version delivered to the hiring manager and direct askers only), forgiveness, declined trust/reference, and refused revenge with a stop condition. Two defects hold it to half. First, the scripted correction puts a fact in her mouth that she never asserted — "which I reported at the time" — which in a ninth-commandment item is a live truthfulness risk in the very artifact meant to correct a falsehood. Second, the key's lawful-means element (HR, references, a defamation route kept lawyer-shaped) is not addressed at all, so the landing offers no path if the rumor persists after the one calm correction.
