# Dawn Bloxwich

## Biographical Summary

Dawn Bloxwich is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on AI safety and responsible AI development. Her research focuses on developing comprehensive evaluation frameworks for assessing the safety and responsibility properties of large language models, contributing to holistic approaches for AI system testing.
