# Jeff Clune

## Research Summary

Jeff Clune is a Professor of Computer Science at the University of British Columbia and a Canada CIFAR AI Chair and Faculty Member at the Vector Institute. He is also a Senior Research Advisor to DeepMind. Previously, he was a Research Team Leader at OpenAI and a Senior Research Manager and founding member of Uber AI Labs (formed after Uber acquired a startup he helped lead).

Clune is a pioneer in the field of AI-Generating Algorithms (AI-GAs), an alternate paradigm for producing general artificial intelligence. His research aims to create open-ended algorithms wherein AI systems can learn and innovate without end, similar to how natural evolution and human culture produce continuous innovation. The three pillars of AI-GAs include meta-learning architectures, meta-learning algorithms, and auto-generating learning environments.

At OpenAI, Clune worked on emergent behavior research, including AI bots that learned to play hide and seek in virtual environments, discovering unexpected strategies through open-ended learning. His work suggests that using AI to make AI could be an important step toward artificial general intelligence (AGI).

## Notes

Clune's work is particularly relevant to AI safety as it explores fundamental questions about how to create AI systems that can continue to learn and improve safely. His research on open-ended algorithms has implications for understanding how AI capabilities might develop over time.
