## Abstract

Systematic evaluation of seven LLMs on tasks in Kazakh across six datasets covering QA, causal reasoning, math problems, translation, and spelling correction. GPT-4 shows best results. LLM performance on Kazakh tasks is lower than on parallel English tasks.

## Data Description

Three datasets prepared for this study: a Kazakh spelling correction dataset (created from scratch), a machine-translated COPA dataset for causal reasoning, and Kazakh math school problems scraped and post-processed from online sources.
