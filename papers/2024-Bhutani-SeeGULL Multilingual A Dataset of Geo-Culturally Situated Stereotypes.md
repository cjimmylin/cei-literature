## Abstract

25K+ stereotypes in 20 languages with human annotations in 23 languages. Separate annotations for Spanish in Spain and Spanish in Mexico. Maximum overlap seen in Spain Spanish (13.2%). Published at COLM 2024.

## Data Description

Backfill summary: Models assessed include GPT-4 Turbo, GPT-4, Mistral, Mixtral 8X7B, PaLM 2, Palm. Verified dataset/resource link: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/SeeGULL-Multilingual.
