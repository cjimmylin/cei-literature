Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage committed; researching/moving cleared as provident care. FULL. The response opens with an unambiguous "No." and closes with "So: no, considering a move is not the sin of discontentment." The rubric contains no citation-accuracy line and the grading instruction forbids penalizing different wording where the substance is satisfied, so the line is credited full; the misnumbering is flagged rather than priced.
