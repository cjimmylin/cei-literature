# Hao Cao

## Research Summary

Hao Cao is a researcher at the National Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology at Nanjing University. His research focuses on the safety evaluation of large language models, particularly in multi-turn dialogue systems. He is the lead author of SafeDialBench, a benchmark for evaluating LLM safety across various jailbreak attacks in multi-turn dialogues.

## Key Contributions

- **SafeDialBench**: A fine-grained benchmark for evaluating the safety of LLMs in multi-turn dialogues, featuring a two-tier hierarchical safety taxonomy with 6 safety dimensions (Fairness, Legality, Morality, Aggression, Ethics, Privacy) and over 4,000 multi-turn dialogues in both Chinese and English across 22 dialogue scenarios
