# Dong Xu

## Affiliation
Professor at University of Sydney, School of Electrical and Information Engineering

## Research Summary

Dong Xu is a Professor at the University of Sydney specializing in computer vision, deep learning, and transfer learning. His highly cited research spans visual recognition, domain adaptation, and applications of AI in healthcare. He has contributed to understanding how large AI models can be applied in health informatics, examining both opportunities and challenges in deploying AI for medical applications.

## Research Interests
- Computer vision and visual recognition
- Deep learning and neural networks
- Transfer learning and domain adaptation
- Medical AI and health informatics
- Pattern recognition
