# Paul Röttger

## Research Summary

Paul Röttger is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the MilaNLP Lab at Bocconi University and an Associate Member of Nuffield College at the University of Oxford (elected December 2024). His research focuses on evaluating and improving the safety of large language models (LLMs), as well as studying their societal impacts.
