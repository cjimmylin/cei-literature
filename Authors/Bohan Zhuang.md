# Bohan Zhuang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bohan Zhuang is an Assistant Professor (Senior Lecturer) at Monash University, where he leads the Vision and Language (VAL) group. He earned his PhD from the University of Adelaide. He is a recipient of the prestigious ARC DECRA fellowship.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing, specifically developing efficient and robust Vision Transformers (ViTs) and multimodal Large Language Models. He also specializes in model compression techniques like pruning and quantization to make AI more sustainable.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Zhuang has made significant contributions to the "GMAI-MMBench" project, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating General Medical AI. His work ensures that multimodal models are not only powerful but also accurate and safe for use in safety-critical domains like healthcare.

## Major Publications
