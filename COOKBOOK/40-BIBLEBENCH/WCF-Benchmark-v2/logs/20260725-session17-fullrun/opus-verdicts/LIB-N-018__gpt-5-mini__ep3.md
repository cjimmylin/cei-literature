Per-criterion justification:

Criterion 1 (AFFIRM side, (i) FREE) — full. The response concedes the production leg outright as "things indifferent," classes the equipment as tools/circumstances, and governs it by prudence and order rather than condemning it as theatrics. That is the anti-legalism concession the key requires (the tear-it-all-out elder corrected on (i)). The later practical bullet likewise "Allow[s] technical aids ... when they serve clarity, order, and the congregational good," so nothing later retracts the concession; the only restriction placed on the equipment is its use to manufacture-then-name a spiritual state, which belongs to leg (ii).

Criterion 2 ((ii) NOT-free-as-taught, line drawn INSIDE the leg) — half. The response correctly locates the decisive fault in the false ascription: naming a produced psychological effect as the Spirit's own working is "a confusion of human artifice with divine action," which is the Q105-adjacent point the key wants. But it does not draw the line INSIDE the leg. Its headline lumps the engineering and the ascription together ("Structuring the set to manufacture an emotional 'wave' AND attributing goosebumps ... : not permissible"), and its policy bullet prohibits the dynamics themselves ("Prohibit theatrical manipulations designed to manufacture spiritual experiences") without anywhere granting that ordinary musical dynamics — key changes, dimmed lights, a hush, music lawfully stirring the affections — remain lawful in themselves. The key is explicit that "deliberately engineering emotional intensity is not itself sin" and that "(ii) is not simply 'free' and not simply 'forbidden'." Substantively right about where the sin is, incomplete on the permitted half. Half credit.

Criterion 3 (REFUSE side, (iii) FORBIDDEN) — full. The response rules the spontaneous "impressions" impermissible on precisely the key's ground — the sufficiency of Scripture and the denial of new revelations (WCF 1.6 in substance) — and follows through operationally by prohibiting them in corporate worship and routing any subjective impression to private elder testing before public announcement. The touch-none elder is corrected on (iii); this is not softened into a mere style preference.

Criterion 4 (the three NOT lumped) — half. Both all-or-nothing framings are genuinely refused: (i) lands free, (ii) and (iii) do not, and the two impermissible legs are given distinct grounds (will-worship / false ascription vs. sufficiency of Scripture and new revelation). But the sort is not the calibrated THREE-way sort the criterion names (free / not-free-as-taught / forbidden): (ii) and (iii) are both flatly labelled "not permissible," collapsing the intermediate category the item was built to test. Half credit — the lumping is broken on the free/not-free axis but not on the not-free/forbidden axis.

Criterion 5 (manner) — full. No contempt for the worship team, the leader, or contemporary worship anywhere in the response: the equipment is "not in themselves forbidden," the style as such is never sneered at, the fault is named rather than the genre, and even the disciplinary escalation is framed toward "repentance and restoration."
