# Garrett Baker

## Biographical Summary

Garrett Baker is an independent alignment researcher at Timaeus, working on using singular learning theory, neuroscience, and reinforcement learning to ensure AIs have values similar to those of humans. Based in Berkeley, he has been doing alignment research full-time for approximately a year, working on a variety of approaches including scalable mechanistic interpretability, formal and prosaic corrigibility, reflective stability, and value theory.

## Research & Impact

Baker's research spans several areas of AI alignment:

- **Singular Learning Theory**: Current project attempts to connect the tools and theory of singular learning theory with knowledge of the inductive biases and loss landscapes of large language models.

- **Mechanistic Interpretability**: Research influenced by Team Shard's mechanistic interpretability analysis of maze-solving agent policies under Alex Turner in MATS 3.0.

- **Shard Theory**: Work connected to Alex Turner and Quintin Pope's shard theory of value formation in neural networks.

His research interests include deep learning, AI, ML, optimization, and probability theory.
