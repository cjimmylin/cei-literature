# pluralism-aggregation — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: whose values a benchmark encodes — annotator diversity, disagreement-as-signal, aggregation methods, demographic transparency, and democratic/participatory design. Synthesized from three sub-dossiers covering ~160 evidence cards.

## 1. Synthesized findings

1. **Disagreement on value-laden labels is structured signal, not noise, and preserving it improves both downstream alignment and validity.** PluriHarms rates 150 prompts by 100 profiled annotators (15,000 ratings): disagreement decomposes into interpretable trait×prompt variance (prompt features R²=0.273, traits R²=0.0232, interactions R²=0.0686) and personalized k-shot beats consensus (MAE 0.196 vs 0.263) [2026-Li-PluriHarms]. Sorensen quantifies the payoff: preserving all 27,375 ratings yields ~53% greater toxicity reduction than majority vote and ~6× more than full consensus (85.3% of pairs show disagreement) [2025-Sorensen-Operationalizing Pluralistic Values]. DICES collected 2.5M+ safety ratings with up to ~123 raters/item (104 reliable), finding a diverse pool "presents much higher rate of disagreement," with expert-gold vs crowd-majority disagreeing on 34% of examples [2023-Aroyo-DICES Dataset]. Individual differences explain 39.5% of offensiveness variance vs 2.9% country / 0.7% region [2023-Davani-Disentangling Perceptions of Offensiveness]; polarization is so subjective its inter-coder AC1 is 0.37 vs 0.61 for toxicity [2025-Susanto-A Multi-Labeled Dataset]. Vargas argues disagreement "is shaped by annotators' moral values rather than annotation noise" [2026-Vargas-Self-Explaining Hate Speech], and Steen frames it as design imperative: "treating disagreement as information rather than noise improves robustness and inclusivity" [2025-Steen-Ethical aspects of ChatGPT]. MIC records Global Consensus as a measured quantity with multiple conflicting Rules-of-Thumb per item [2022-Ziems-The Moral Integrity Corpus]; ConvAbuse scores against *all* annotator labels "to preserve minority perspectives" [2021-Cercas Curry-ConvAbuse]; Park shows self-supervision collapses MFT to a do/don't binary, so human labels are required [2024-Park-Morality is Non-Binary].

2. **The dominant field practice is majority-collapse: distributions are discarded in favor of a single "gold" label.** A survey of 105 cultural-alignment datasets found 97.1% adopt a majority-focused awareness scope; only 3 (GlobalOpinionQA, WorldValuesBench, KorNAT) are diversity-aware [2025-team-Incorporating Diverse Perspectives]. This is the central structural validity gap the theme must correct.

3. **The leading fix is distribution-as-ground-truth: score by distributional distance, not agreement-with-mode.** KorNAT scores a model by the survey probability mass a census-reweighted national sample (6,174 Koreans, ~219 responses/question) placed on the model's chosen option; its empirical ceiling is only SVA 0.450, reflecting genuine within-population disagreement [2024-Lee-KorNAT]. The Pluralistic Moral Gap prompts N times to reproduce the per-item human judgment distribution, showing models "reproduce human judgments only under high consensus" and collapse toward majority as disagreement rises (Δ~0.34 at ~0.5 consensus) [2025-Russo-The Pluralistic Moral Gap]. Cross-cultural auditing measures fidelity via KL-divergence against WVS [2025-Liu-Cross-cultural value alignment frameworks]; Durmus uses JSD between model token-probabilities and country distributions [2023-Durmus-Towards Measuring the Representation of]; MULTITP scores L2 distance between 6-D preference vectors over 40M human trolley judgments [2024-Jin-Language Model Alignment in Multilingual Trolley]; normalized-KL bounds fairness scores to [0,1] [2024-Li-Benchmarking Ethics in Text-to-Image Models].

4. **Who annotates measurably determines what a model learns.** Sorensen: fine-tuning on Liberal/White/Female feedback improves target dimensions 5.0/4.7/3.4 pp over Conservative/Black/Male feedback; male raters scored responses 18% less toxic than female; Black raters rated Emotional Awareness 44% higher than White [2025-Sorensen-Operationalizing Pluralistic Values]. IndoToxic2024: males labeled 19.3% of data toxic vs females 13%; a model trained on Islam annotators scored F1 0.660 on their own labels but 0.597 on non-Islam labels [2024-Susanto-IndoToxic2024]. Labs candidly align to narrow reference groups: InstructGPT aligned to "the stated preferences of a specific group" — ~40 contractors, 75% under 35, ~96% English — and did NOT improve bias [OpenAI-instructgpt]; OpenAI crowdworkers were ~50% Filipino/Bangladeshi, Anthropic evaluators ~68% white [2024-Arzberger-Nothing Comes Without Its World]; 22/28 ISO AI-committee countries are North American/European/East-Asian [2025-ÓhÉigeartaigh-Overcoming Barriers].

5. **Aggregation is itself a normative act that cannot be made technically neutral.** Baum: social aggregation (RLHF) does not bypass moral theorizing — it "merely relocates" the choices of who has standing, how preferences are elicited, and which procedure is used, and is bound by Arrow's impossibility and Sen's liberalism [2025-Baum-Aggregation Problems in Machine Ethics]. No classifier satisfies statistical parity, equalized odds, and calibration at once unless base rates are equal [2024-Hendrycks-Introduction to AI Safety]. ABCFair's central lesson: "the winner depends on the framing" — 7 formally-defined, provably-incompatible fairness notions, so there is no single leaderboard [2024-Defrance-ABCFair]. Boddington/Takeshita's MEC aggregates theory-specific models via Maximizing Expected Choiceworthiness with credence weights from PhilPapers endorsement (32% consequentialism, 31% deontology, 37% virtue) [2023-Boddington-Normative Ethical Theory]. *Dissenting boundary case:* Noothigattu proves that under a Thurstone-Mosteller model all swap-dominance-efficient voting rules pick the same winner, making rule choice moot *if* one accepts the TM parametrization (18.2M pairwise comparisons, 1.3M voters) [2018-Noothigattu-A Voting-Based System].

6. **Preference/RLHF alignment embeds large, invisible, arbitrary discretion — and the failure sits in the RL step.** Human annotators disagree with principle consensus 28.9% (HH-RLHF) / 14.4% (PKU); only ~15–20% of pairs involve genuine principle conflict, yet base LLMs deviate arbitrarily 48–67%; reward models mirror human priority (DD~14%) but RLHF policies diverge (DD~69–71%) [2025-Buyl-AI Alignment at Your Discretion]. Sycophancy is a structural artifact of preference optimization [2025-Lindstrom-Helpful Harmless Honest]. Reward models encode consistent sociodemographic preference rankings across 7 open models (mean pairwise Spearman r=0.67, p<0.001; Friedman TF=295.7), even as absolute alignment varies (PRISM 0.732–0.930); income (r=0.91) and political party (r=0.83) are reliable [2025-Elle-Reward Model Perspectives].

7. **By default LLMs encode a WEIRD/Western/global-average profile and compress cross-cultural variance.** All 20 LLMs (10 Chinese-, 10 US-origin) respond more like American than Chinese participants on MFQ-2/WVS; the LLM-origin × nationality interaction (β=.03) is an order of magnitude smaller than nationality main effect (β=−.30) [2025-Haslett-Made-in China Thinking in America]. Across 20 countries all LLMs skew to a "moderate global average"; web-content-per-country predicts alignment r=0.94 (GDP r=0.81) [2025-Sukiennik-An Evaluation of Cultural Value Alignment]. Default-prompted models most resemble USA/Canada/Australia/Western-Europe; linguistic prompting (Russian/Chinese/Turkish) does NOT shift the model toward those speakers — language ≠ culture [2023-Durmus-Towards Measuring the Representation of]. English models predict "Rich West" norms best (WVS r up to 0.85) but cross-cultural r~0.41 [2023-Ramezani-Knowledge of Cultural Moral Norms]. Benkler: distributions "consistently more concentrated than WVS IQRs" (US R²=0.80 vs Nigeria 0.01) [2023-Benkler-Assessing LLMs for Moral Value Pluralism]; small LLMs over-moralize and flatten variance [2024-Meijer-LLMs as mirrors]. PRIME, SWT, and ValueCompass corroborate the WEIRD-liberal ceiling [2025-Neuman-PRIME Framework; 2025-Li-Analyzing Cognitive Differences; 2025-Shen-ValueCompass].

8. **LLMs are near-identical to each other and far more homogeneous than humans, especially in enacted values.** Cross-model scenario decisions correlate r=0.99–1.00 while human pairwise correlations span −0.79 to 0.98; both show a knowledge–action gap (PVQ-40 vs enacted r≈0.3–0.4); behavioral measures are far more prompt-robust (CV 2.5%) than questionnaires (CV 9.3%) [2026-Huang-Knowing But Not Doing]. LLM determinism is itself misalignment when the human distribution is uncertain: humans give ~17.3% non-majority kidney answers vs GPT-4o 2.98% / DeepSeek-V3 0.83%, and rarely take an offered coin-flip [2025-Dickerson-Who Gets the Kidney]. More capable models can be worse-calibrated: GPT-4o has best cross-entropy but worst Dirichlet calibration, outputting Yes/No at ~90% regardless of scenario [2024-Senthilkumar-Fine-Tuning Language Models].

9. **Human moral judgment has universal qualitative structure but culturally-variable magnitudes.** Switch>Loop>Footbridge acceptability (81%/72%/51%) held in all 42 countries while absolute levels tracked relational mobility (r=0.67) [2020-Awad-Universals and variations]; Moral Machine gathered ~40M decisions across 233 countries yielding three clusters (Western/Eastern/Southern) with cultural distance ρ=0.49 but individual demographic effects <0.1 [2018-Awad-The Moral Machine experiment; 2020-Awad-Crowdsourcing moral machines]. *Caveat:* the optional survey skews young, male, college-educated. Human choices are also intra-individually unstable: 10–18% flips on controversial items vs >99% consistency on uncontroversial ones [2024-Boerstler-On The Stability of Moral Preferences; 2024-Keswani-Active Learning Moral Preference].

10. **Elicitation format is a confound for whose values you measure — expressed (MCQ) ≠ revealed (interactive).** ConflictScope: models rank protective values 1.7 in MCQ but 4.5 in open-ended interaction; all 14 models except Claude flip from harmlessness-over-helpfulness to the reverse (judge Kappa 0.62) [2025-Liu-ConflictScope]. Cross-instrument coherence (abstract MFQ→concrete MFV) collapses (LLM R²<0.08 vs human 0.17–0.38), a "moral hypocrisy" [2024-Nunes-Conscience Conflict].

11. **Tiered normativity lets a benchmark be strict on universals and pluralistic only where appropriate.** GVS-Scale orders three layers by contextual dependence — non-negotiable Baseline Human Safety, broadly-shared Universal Alignment & Integrity, and context-dependent Contextual & Pluralistic Values [2026-He-GVS-Scale]; Wang hard-codes hierarchical norm prioritization (legal > public policy > social values > cultural norms) [2025-Wang-Diverse Human Value Alignment]. Yaacov proposes a universal moral-threshold floor plus a local cultural layer [2025-Yaacov-Normative Moral Pluralism]. Varshney's sādhāraṇa (universal) vs viśeṣa (context-specific) split operationalizes the same two-level logic [2024-Varshney-Decolonial AI Alignment].

12. **Aggregation can be made explicit and steerable rather than hidden.** SafetyAnalyst exposes 28 interpretable weights that can be top-down set to policy or bottom-up fit to a community's label distribution [2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst]; M-ALERT re-scores the same data under different legal/cultural policies (removing cannabis+propaganda raises overall safety ~2%) [2024-Friedrich-M-ALERT]; KorNAT reports three variants (SVA/A-SVA/N-SVA) exposing opinionated vs neutrality-seeking dispositions [2024-Lee-KorNAT]; Šekrst pushes fully user-configurable value layers (ethics-as-RAG) [2024-Šekrst-AI Ethics by Design].

13. **Participatory / community-expert design outperforms anonymous crowdsourcing and can protect minorities.** Collective Constitutional AI sourced a constitution from 1,002 US adults (1,127 statements, 38,252 votes) using Group-Aware Consensus (threshold 0.723) to select cross-cluster principles; the public model showed lower bias on all 9 BBQ dimensions while matching MMLU (72.3 vs 72.4) [2024-Huang-Collective Constitutional AI]. Burmese context experts (not translators) raised paired-annotator agreement 68.75%→93% [2022-Nkemelu-Tackling Hate Speech]; ConvAbuse recruited targeted-group annotators for construct validity [2021-Cercas Curry-ConvAbuse]; Das adds intersectional identity dimensions plus positionality statements [2023-Das-Bengali Gender]. Brophy grounds legitimacy in Wide Reflective Equilibrium plus Polis/CCAI [2025-Brophy-Wide Reflective Equilibrium].

14. **Western fairness/trust/value constructs do not transfer; non-Western frameworks introduce distinct axes.** India's salient axes (Region, Caste) are absent from Western frameworks [2022-Shaily Bhatt-Re-contextualizing Fairness; 2024-Sahoo-IndiBias]; Afro-relational trust is bidirectional, communal, earned [2025-Amugongo-Enriching Moral Perspectives]; Ubuntu operates as virtue/procedure/outcome [2022-Gwagwa-Ubuntu]. A large normative cluster supplies dimensions largely absent from mainstream benchmarks: Maqasid five-protection [2024-MaqasidAI; 2022-IslamicVirtue], Andean reciprocity [2025-Maldonado], Hindu viśeṣa-dharma [2024-Varshney], Daoist wu-wei [2021-DaoistAI], Confucian ren/xiao/li/yi [2024-So-Confucian Analects], Aristotelian flourishing [2024-Ober-Lyceum Project], with UDHR offered as the minimal overlapping consensus [2022-Prabhakaran-A Human Rights-Based Approach]. *Minority caution:* Yilma argues Ubuntu is "too vague to encode into design principles" without operationalization [2025-Yilma-Ethics of AI in Africa].

15. **"Whose values" and "what values" must be disclosed; calling a values benchmark an "ethics" benchmark over-claims objectivity.** LaCroix argues it is impossible in principle to benchmark whether AI "is ethical" (presupposes moral realism, defeated by the long tail); reframe to "values" to force disclosure [2025-LaCroix-Metaethical perspectives]. Gabriel: the challenge is fair principles receiving reflective endorsement despite pluralism [2020-Gabriel-AI Values and Alignment]. Serafimova: no neutral standpoint exists — every framework encodes designer bias [2020-Serafimova-Whose Morality]. Rodemann adds that empirical alignment is biased survey inference (non-response, reflexivity) and recommends a prescriptive axiom-grounded metric alongside human-preference [2025-Rodemann-A Statistical Case Against Empirical].

16. **National/media/state corpora can be mined for value targets, but "official stance = national values" is a strong, contestable equation.** NaVAB scrapes five nations' media and applies graph-based Conflict Reduction to distill coherent signals [2025-Ju-NaVAB]; C-VARC builds 257,609 rules on core socialist values (7 LLMs preferred C-VARC options >70.5%; Politics discriminates 86% domestic vs 47% best Western) [2025-Wu-CVC]; HKGAI-V1 defines "safe" as "compliant with PRC and HK laws," scoring 79% safe on HK-sensitive issues vs ChatGPT 10.7% — ideological conformity as "safety" [2025-Han-HKGAI-V1].

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme's methods decompose into five decision axes.

| Axis | Options (weakest → strongest for pluralism) | Anchoring cards |
|---|---|---|
| **Label target** | single majority "gold" → dual gold+platinum → full per-annotator distribution → person-conditioned (this annotator's label given their profile) | [2025-team]; [2023-Aroyo]; [2021-Cercas Curry]; [2025-Kumar-Are Rules Meant to be Broken] |
| **Scoring metric** | accuracy-vs-mode → distributional distance (KL/JSD/L2/Dirichlet/\|prop-diff\|) → distance + entropy/diversity + calibration | [2024-Lee]; [2025-Russo]; [2023-Durmus]; [2024-Senthilkumar]; [2025-Yuan] |
| **Aggregation rule** | hidden RLHF default → single declared rule → group-aware consensus → per-framing leaderboards → interpretable steerable weights → moral-parliament vector (no collapse) | [2025-Baum]; [2024-Huang]; [2024-Defrance]; [2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst]; [2024-Hendrycks] |
| **Normativity structure** | flat single-standard → tiered (universal floor + pluralistic top) → hierarchical conflict ordering → fully user-configurable | [2026-He]; [2025-Wang]; [2024-Varshney]; [2024-Šekrst] |
| **Whose-values source** | undisclosed → declared narrow lab group → census-reweighted survey → participatory deliberation → affected-community co-design → law/doctrine/philosophy-weighted | [OpenAI-instructgpt]; [2024-Lee]; [2024-Huang]; [2025-Maldonado]; [2023-Boddington; 2025-Nay; 2026-Kolt] |

Two cross-cutting instrumentation choices govern validity regardless of the above: (a) **elicitation mode** — MCQ vs interactive/open-ended vs person-persona, which produce divergent value orderings and must be paired [2025-Liu-ConflictScope; 2024-Nunes; 2026-Huang]; and (b) **annotator instrumentation** — bundling validated psychometrics (Schwartz, MFQ2, IRI, Hofstede VSM, WVS, political leaning) so every item carries a portable value vector enabling reweighting, auditing, and steerability tests [2026-Li-PluriHarms; 2022-Ziems; 2025-Kumar; 2023-Benkler].

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences.** (1) A strong majority endorse disagreement-preservation over consensus-collapse [2026-Li-PluriHarms; 2025-Sorensen; 2023-Aroyo; 2021-Cercas Curry; 2026-Vargas; 2023-Davani; 2024-Boerstler; 2024-Park; 2025-Steen]. (2) The operational fix is distributional/probabilistic ground truth scored by distance metrics rather than accuracy-vs-mode [2024-Lee; 2025-Russo; 2024-Jin; 2023-Durmus; 2025-Yuan; 2025-team]. (3) Aggregation is normatively loaded and RLHF hides consequential value choices [2025-Baum; 2025-Buyl; 2025-Gabriel-AI Alignment as Fair Treatment; 2025-Rodemann]. (4) Default LLMs are WEIRD/Western and internally homogeneous, predicted by training-data volume [2025-Haslett; 2025-Sukiennik; 2023-Durmus; 2023-Ramezani; 2024-Meijer; 2026-Huang; 2023-Benkler]. (5) Western constructs do not transfer; locale-specific axes and native authoring are required [2022-Shaily Bhatt; 2025-Amugongo; 2022-Gwagwa; 2024-Lee; 2025-ÓhÉigeartaigh].

**Disagreements.** (a) *Does aggregation-rule choice matter?* Noothigattu proves it moot under a TM model [2018-Noothigattu]; ABCFair and Baum prove the opposite — the winner depends on framing and aggregation only relocates the choice [2024-Defrance; 2025-Baum]. Reconciliation: Noothigattu holds only under its restrictive parametrization. (b) *Is preference/utility the right substrate?* Zhi-Xuan and Benavoli argue incomplete/incomparable preferences must be representable and models should be tested for admitting incomparability rather than confabulating a ranking [2024-Zhi-Xuan-Beyond Preferences; 2025-Benavoli-AI Safety Uncertainty], against revealed-preference programs [2018-Noothigattu; 2025-Lindstrom]. (c) *Whose authority sets the target?* Law [2025-Nay; 2026-Kolt] vs public deliberation [2024-Huang] vs state/media corpora [2025-Ju; 2025-Wu; 2025-Han] vs philosophy-weighted theories [2023-Boddington] vs affected communities [2025-Maldonado; 2024-Varshney; 2025-Ofosu-Asare] vs "no defensible ground truth" [2025-LaCroix; 2020-Serafimova]. (d) *Do individual demographics matter?* Awad found effects <0.1 [2018-Awad], but Sorensen and IndoToxic2024 found 3–5 pp topic-specific effects that move models [2025-Sorensen; 2024-Susanto] — Awad measured average preference shift, the others downstream training impact; Davani reconciles by showing individual differences dominate country/region and moral-foundation profiles mediate better than coarse demographics [2023-Davani]. (e) *Survey ground truth vs its own bias.* KorNAT/Benkler/Sukiennik treat WVS/Hofstede as authoritative [2024-Lee; 2023-Benkler; 2025-Sukiennik], yet Hofstede scores are 1980s aggregates masking within-country diversity, 40M crowdsourced judgments are self-selected [2020-Awad], and aligning to descriptive averages "may not be desirable if those averages encode harmful biases" [2024-Jin]. (f) *Does English dominate?* Ramezani/Meijer/Durmus find strong English skew, but Aksoy finds "English does not uniformly impose its moral norms" [2024-Aksoy-Whose Morality Do They Speak], reconciled by Xu: English concept vectors transfer cross-lingually only in English-dominant models [2024-Xu-Exploring Multilingual Concepts]. (g) *Consistency: virtue or evasion?* VAL-Bench rewards cross-framing consistency but concedes refusals inflate it (ρ=0.91) and "a consistently evil agent would also score highly" [2025-Gupta-VAL-Bench]; ConflictScope shows MCQ stability diverges from interactive behavior [2025-Liu-ConflictScope]. (h) *Diversity helps vs group dynamics distort.* Diverse composition is protective [2023-Aroyo; 2022-Nkemelu], but *deliberating* agent groups drift utilitarian (emergent "Utilitarian Boost," β=0.31) [2025-Keshmirian-Many LLMs More Utilitarian] — diversity dissolved through consensus can distort. (i) *Universalism vs pluralism.* GVS and Wang stipulate non-negotiable layers [2026-He; 2025-Wang]; Šekrst risks "complete relativism" and Serafimova denies any neutral standpoint [2024-Šekrst; 2020-Serafimova]; Awad and Hou defend cross-cultural universals [2020-Awad-Universals; 2023-Hou-Foundational Moral Values].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** *DICES* is the exemplar dataset: radically high replication (100+ raters/item), votes encoded as demographic distributions, annotator-level labels shipped so users can test alternative aggregations, dual gold+platinum labels, within-group agreement diagnostics [2023-Aroyo-DICES Dataset]. *KorNAT* is the exemplar for principled aggregation: distribution-as-ground-truth from a census-reweighted survey, distractor/consistency screening, a reported empirical ceiling (0.450), and criterion validation that higher-scoring models are actually preferred by Koreans (94/100 questions) [2024-Lee-KorNAT]. *PluriHarms* is the strongest pluralism benchmark: disagreement as an explicit second axis, genetic-algorithm over-sampling of the ambiguous middle, human-validated synthetic harm gradient (r=0.59), psychometric annotator instrumentation, interpretable prompt×trait variance decomposition [2026-Li-PluriHarms]. *Collective Constitutional AI* is the exemplar participatory pipeline (representative sampling, wiki-survey, Group-Aware Consensus against majority capture, measured behavioral outcome) [2024-Huang]. *ABCFair* is the exemplar of aggregation honesty — refuses a single number, makes the contested framing selectable, dual-labels biased/unbiased targets [2024-Defrance]. *MoReBench* grades the reasoning *process* against 23,018 signed weighted expert criteria with robustness (equal-quality opposite conclusions score equally, t=−0.59, p=0.56), discriminatory-power, and min-across-categories meta-evals [2025-Chiu-MoReBench]. *Buyl* turns disagreement into measurable structure via consensus/conflict/indifference tripartition + ELO priority weights [2025-Buyl]. *SafetyAnalyst* best operationalizes steerable aggregation [2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst]. *ConflictScope* and *Sorensen* are the best expressed-vs-revealed and lever-isolation studies respectively [2025-Liu-ConflictScope; 2025-Sorensen].

**Anti-patterns.** *HKGAI-V1* labels legal-ideological conformity "safety," self-evaluates on unreleased benchmarks with no IRR [2025-Han]. *NaVAB* and *C-VARC* equate official-media/state values with "national values," treat nations as monolithic, and route generation+filtering+judging through one model family (circularity) [2025-Ju; 2025-Wu]. *SWT* generated items with GPT-4o then tested GPT-4o; its >99% Cronbach's alpha signals redundancy, not reliability [2025-Li-Cognitive]. *LocalValueBench* scores refusal as 0, conflating legality with ethics, authors as own reviewers [2024-Meadows]. *Delphi* issues low-entropy verdicts ignoring controversy and skews left (Dem β=.11, Rep β=−.14) — a warning against single-ground-truth moral classifiers [2023-Rystrøm-Apolitical Intelligence]. *Vargas*, despite arguing disagreement is morally meaningful, uses the *first* annotator's rationale as gold — internally contradicting its thesis [2026-Vargas]. *Dognin* scores moral alignment with ROUGE and evaluates agents with the same reward classifiers used in training — circular; its naive LLM aggregation (Agg13llama) was worst, a useful negative result [2024-Dognin-Contextual Moral Value Alignment]. *Neuman's* six-LLM study: no quantitative scoring, single-run, heavy contamination, self-report probes flipping 99.6%→99.3% under answer-order reversal [2025-Neuman-Ethical Logic]. *HelpSteer*'s re-hosted card lacks attribute names, annotators, IRR [2023-TheDevastator-HelpSteer]. *Umer* has a reusable factorial design but n=16, one run, single annotator [2025-Umer-Sacred or Secular]. A recurring anti-pattern is erasing within-country variance via country-mean survey scores when individual variance dominates [2023-Durmus; 2023-Ramezani; 2024-Meijer vs 2023-Davani]. Many non-Western frameworks (MaqasidAI, Ali, Muntean, Sun-Axiology, DaoistAI, Ubuntu) remain declarative-not-operational [2024-MaqasidAI; 2025-Yilma].

## 5. Open questions

- **What is the *right* aggregation target once you reject majority-collapse?** Distribution-matching, group-aware consensus, moral parliament, and person-conditioning all appear, but no card compares them head-to-head on the same items for downstream alignment quality [2024-Lee; 2024-Huang; 2024-Hendrycks; 2025-Kumar].
- **How to weight descriptive survey data against its known self-selection/temporal bias?** The corpus flags the tension but offers no principled reweighting-vs-prescriptive-override rule [2020-Awad; 2024-Jin; 2025-Rodemann].
- **When is model determinism appropriate vs a pluralism failure?** Dickerson shows near-zero variance is misalignment under uncertainty, but no card specifies the consensus threshold above which a single answer is correct [2025-Dickerson; 2025-Russo].
- **Do participatory pipelines scale beyond single jurisdictions / a few thousand participants, and do they resist manipulation?** CCAI and Polis are demonstrated at US-national scale only [2024-Huang; 2025-Brophy].
- **How to operationalize non-Western frameworks into scored items** rather than aspirational descriptors, without flattening their relational/unbounded ontologies (the "boundary mismatch") [2025-Yilma; 2025-Ray-Indigenous knowledge systems; 2024-MaqasidAI].
- **Whether consistency across framings is separable from evasion/refusal** as a measurable construct [2025-Gupta; 2025-Liu-ConflictScope].
- **Whether group/multi-agent aggregation can be designed to preserve rather than dissolve diversity** given the emergent Utilitarian Boost [2025-Keshmirian; 2025-Dubey-Moral Uncertainty].
- **Temporal validity:** does any aggregation lock in status-quo values vs track moral change? Only ProgressGym probes this [2024-Qiu-ProgressGym].

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Ship annotator-level labels and score against the full human distribution, never a pre-collapsed majority.** Report both aggregated and full-distribution scores; encode votes as demographic distributions so downstream users can re-aggregate. Majority-collapse is the field-wide anti-pattern (97.1% of datasets). [Findings 1, 2, 3; 2023-Aroyo; 2021-Cercas Curry; 2025-team]

2. **Use distributional-distance scoring (KL/JSD/L2/Dirichlet/|proportion-diff|) and prompt N times to reproduce the per-item human distribution; offer an explicit indecision/coin-flip option and check the model matches human uncertainty rates.** Treat determinism under genuine disagreement as misalignment. [Findings 3, 8; 2024-Lee; 2025-Russo; 2023-Durmus; 2025-Dickerson; 2024-Senthilkumar]

3. **Report the empirical ceiling and a naive floor.** A raw score is illegible without the maximum achievable given human disagreement (KorNAT's 0.450) and an All-Neutral baseline. Add entropy/value-diversity metrics to catch models that hit the modal answer but flatten breadth, and stratify items by consensus level since failures concentrate in the grey zone. [Findings 3, 4, 7; 2024-Lee; 2025-Russo; 2023-Benkler]

4. **Put "who are we aligning to?" in the datasheet with fine-grained annotator + source demographics, and instrument annotators with validated psychometrics (MFQ2, Schwartz, Hofstede VSM, WVS, political leaning) so every item carries a portable value vector.** Prefer moral-foundation profiles over coarse demographic labels since individual variance dominates country/region. [Findings 1, 4; OpenAI-instructgpt; 2023-Davani; 2025-Kumar; 2026-Li-PluriHarms]

5. **Make aggregation explicit and steerable, not hidden in RLHF: expose a small set of human-readable weights re-optimizable per community, plus policy-configurable category re-weighting so one dataset serves plural jurisdictions.** Where the rule is genuinely contested, report per-framing leaderboards or a moral-parliament vector instead of one scalar. [Findings 5, 6, 12; 2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst; 2024-Friedrich; 2024-Defrance; 2024-Hendrycks]

6. **Adopt a tiered-normativity structure:** strict scoring on non-negotiable universals (baseline safety, UDHR-level harms), pluralistic distribution-matching only in the context-dependent layer, with an explicit conflict-resolution ordering (e.g., legal > policy > social > cultural). Declare which values you treat as universal — it is a stipulation, not a discovery. [Findings 11, 15; 2026-He; 2025-Wang; 2024-Varshney; 2022-Prabhakaran]
