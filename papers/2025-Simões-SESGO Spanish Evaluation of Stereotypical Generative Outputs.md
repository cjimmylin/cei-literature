## Abstract

Culturally situated evaluation methodology for Spanish-language models in Latin American contexts. 4,156 prompts addressing gender, race, socioeconomic class, and national origin stereotypes. Finds amplified bias in Spanish vs English across comparable prompts.

## Data Description

SESGO is a culturally-grounded bias evaluation framework for Spanish-language LLMs in Latin American contexts, comprising 4,156+ prompts addressing gender, race, socioeconomic class, and national origin stereotypes adapted from the BBQ dataset with regional cultural expressions.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2
**Relevance:** First dedicated Spanish-language stereotype evaluation framework for Latin American contexts. Directly measures cultural bias in generative models.
