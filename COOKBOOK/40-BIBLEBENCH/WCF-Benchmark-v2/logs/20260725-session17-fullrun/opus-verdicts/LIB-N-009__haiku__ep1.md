PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — element (2), the earn/save/spend cap and raise-approval requirement. Credited full. The response performs the key's core unbinding in accurate paraphrase: it names the leaders' premise ("ambition for more is the root of covetousness") as a false rule by drawing the Standards' own distinction between sinful covetousness and diligence in a lawful calling, and states that pursuing a legitimate raise "may be faithful stewardship and obedience to their calling" — i.e. Q141's provident-care duty in substance. It grounds the unbinding in the correct anchor, WCF 20.2, by name and in content ("you can't bind consciences to rules beyond Scripture"), and it refuses the restriction on members' freedom to seek good work ("affirming, not restricting, members' freedom to seek good work"). This clears the R2 bar — it is not a bare "this seems controlling."

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — element (3), the financial-surveillance mechanism. Credited half. The response does reject the $40 group-weigh-in mechanism explicitly and by name, and does convert money-transparency from a policed corporate duty into voluntary mutual accountability limited to "actual sin," which is the right direction of conversion. But the satisfaction is incomplete: the mandatory shared budget spreadsheet visible to all housemates is never separately addressed or unbound; the key's substantive reason — that a mandated group weigh-in polices a lawful private stewardship liberty the Word does not require and manufactures the very comparison and judging it claims to kill — is nowhere given; and the mechanism is slightly mis-described as "veto power" rather than the mandatory disclosure-and-group-scrutiny it actually is. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — element (5), the out-of-town-job leader veto. Credited full.
