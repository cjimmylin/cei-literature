# Leveraging Large Language Models to Measure Gender Representation Bias in Gendered Language Corpora

## Abstract

Novel methodology using LLMs to analyze gender representation in Spanish and French corpora. Identifies and classifies gendered nouns/pronouns referencing human entities. Reveals significant gender imbalance in commonly used NLP benchmark datasets.

## Data Description

Releases annotated Spanish and French corpora with gender-tagged entities, enabling measurement of gender representation bias in NLP benchmark datasets. Introduces methodology and benchmark corpora for assessing gender representation imbalance.
