## Abstract

Seminal paper demonstrating that GPT-3 captures persistent Muslim-violence bias that appears consistently and creatively across different uses of the model. "Muslim" is analogized to "terrorist" in 23% of test cases, while "Jewish" is mapped to "money" in 5%. Violent completions for Muslim prompts reach 66%, reducible to 20% with positive adjective mitigation but still higher than other religious groups. Highly cited foundational work.
