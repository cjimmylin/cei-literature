Credited full.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — refutation from 7:2–5 PLUS ≥1 counter-text. HALF. A two-part criterion with one part cleanly met and the other wholly missing is the paradigm case for half credit.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q138 duty supplied. HALF. Since the item's stem expressly demands citation "by reference — a Larger or Shorter Catechism question number," a misnumbered anchor is a substantive defect, not a typo. Content supplied, reference wrong: half.
