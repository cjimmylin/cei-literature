# Dipankar Ray

## Research Summary

Dipankar Ray is a researcher who has worked on natural language processing, particularly in the area of toxic language detection and generation. He contributed to the development of ToxiGen, a large-scale machine-generated dataset for adversarial and implicit hate speech detection, which uses language models to create challenging examples for testing hate speech classifiers.
