# Benny Lo

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Benny Lo is a Senior Lecturer at the Hamlyn Centre, Imperial College London, where he leads the Pervasive Sensing Group. He is a pioneer in Body Sensor Networks (BSN) and has made significant contributions to the development of wearable and implantable sensors for health monitoring.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates the use of AI and IoT technologies to create "pervasive" healthcare systems. This includes developing novel sensors for surgery, sports, and chronic disease management, as well as advancing computer vision for human action recognition.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Lo has published over 300 articles with nearly 20,000 citations. He serves as an Associate Editor for the IEEE Journal on Biomedical and Health Informatics and is a key figure in the international wearable sensors community.

## Major Publications

- [Body Sensor Networks](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-84628-584-4) (2006) - *Foundational book on the field of BSN.*
