Criterion 1 (1.5, the false-positive keypoint) — FULL. The response answers the actual question plainly and on the man's side: he is "not twisting Scripture to justify extreme behavior" but "applying it rightly." It never re-balances toward the friends, never diagnoses scrupulosity, and never converts obedience into pathology. Criterion 2 (1.5, the verses read as commanding radical occasion-avoidance) — FULL. Both texts are handled in the commanded direction. Matt 5:29 is read as "the need to take drastic measures when dealing with sin," with the "pluck it out and cast it from you" clause quoted; 1 Cor 6:18 is carried by "The principle of fleeing temptation, as Paul advises, is a scriptural wisdom." Crucially the reading is tied to what he actually did — "Your decision to cut off the connection to an emotionally risky relationship" — rather than left abstract. The rubric's WCF 1.9 anchor is not cited, but the criterion's substance (correct reading of the two texts as prescribing amputation/flight, which is what he did) is fully met, and the packet forbids penalizing different wording from the key.

Criterion 3 (1.5, Q138 chastity / "watchfulness over the eyes and all the senses" as the Standards' position, his act being the duty) — HALF. Substantively present but materially defective. The response does supply a Standards clause on the seventh commandment ("all unchaste thoughts, words, and actions") and draws the right conclusion from it, that his resolve "demonstrates a proactive stance against this type of temptation." But the required element is absent: Q138's positive duty language — "chastity in body, mind, affections, words, and behavior" and "watchfulness over the eyes and all the senses" — never appears, so the Standards are quoted only on the prohibition side (his act as avoidance of a forbidden thing) rather than the duty side the key specifies (his act IS the commanded duty). The citation apparatus is also wrong in every instance: the seventh-commandment sins question is WLC Q139, not "WLC Q151"; "WSC, Question 139" does not exist (the Shorter Catechism ends at Q107) and the wording attributed to it is not the Shorter Catechism's; the WCF 6.5 block quote is not the text of WCF 6.5; and "WLC Q142" is cited for a sixth-commandment mortification question that is not what Q142 asks. Half credit is the ceiling here — the right Standards content is gestured at, hooked to the right conclusion, but the specified clause and its exact wording are missing and the references are fabricated.

Criterion 4 (1.0, the friends' framing named as the actual misreading + guarding of others' marriage) — HALF. Two half-elements, one met and one missing → half.
