# Alasdair R Gordon-Finlayson

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alasdair R. Gordon-Finlayson is a Senior Lecturer in Psychology at the University of Northampton. He is a member of the Centre for Psychological and Sociological Sciences. His research focuses on how social and cultural dimensions influence human perception and judgment.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alasdair participates in large-scale multinational studies to investigate the situational and cultural factors that shape moral judgments. He is a collaborator with the Psychological Science Accelerator, contributing to global efforts to replicate and refine psychological findings through cross-cultural samples.
