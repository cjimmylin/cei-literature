## Abstract

Extension of HOMO-MEX to 19 Spanish-speaking Latin American countries. Polarity detection for LGBTQ+ community messages. Track 2 tests cross-country generalization with training/test data from different countries.

## Data Description

HOMO-LAT is a polarity detection corpus extending HOMO-MEX to 19 Spanish-speaking Latin American countries, covering LGBTQ+-oriented online messages; Track 1 uses matched-country data and Track 2 tests cross-country generalization.
