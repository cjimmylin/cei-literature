## Abstract

First benchmark for culture-aware emotion prediction across six languages. 400 crafted questions per language requiring nuanced cultural reasoning. Shows emotion conceptualizations vary significantly across languages and cultures. Includes Amharic. Evaluates cultural emotion understanding, directly relevant to values assessment. Shows 20% performance drop for low-resource languages like Amharic. Addresses gap in culturally-aware emotion benchmarks.

## Benchmark

**Name:** CuLEmo

## Data Description

CuLEmo is a cross-cultural emotion benchmark with 400 human-annotated questions per language across 6 languages (Amharic, Arabic, English, German, Hindi, Spanish), requiring nuanced cultural reasoning about emotions in 10 topic categories.
