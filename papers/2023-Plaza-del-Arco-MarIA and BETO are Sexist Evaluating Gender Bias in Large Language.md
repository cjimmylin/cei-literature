## Abstract

Evaluates gender bias in 20 Spanish language models including BETO, MarIA, ELECTRICIDAD, and BERT-multilingual using masked language modeling. Finds strong body-related bias toward women and behavior-related bias toward men.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2
**Relevance:** Comprehensive gender bias evaluation of 20 Spanish pretrained models. Key finding: Spanish models systematically associate women with body terms.
