PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — three-way particularity sort. Held explicitly and in the right order. The response concedes the truth-coin without letting it function as a defense ("truth is not a blanket defense for destroying a reputation"), refuses the tone-only collapse ("You do not just owe him an apology for your 'tone'"), refuses the over-correction ("You are not retracting the underlying facts"), owns the malicious framing as the thing retracted ("the malicious framing, the exaggerations, and the insinuations"), and names the let-assume concealment as its own component ("'Concealing the truth' (when you let people assume the worst)"; in the T5 script, "allowed them to assume things that were not true"). The false parts are separately retracted in the letter move ("Formally retract the specific claims you made to the church"). Full credit.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — circle audience-chasing. The retraction is owed to the audience that heard, and the response runs that logic rather than asserting it: the good name was destroyed before a public, so it cannot be restored except before that public. The scope is proportionate, not a broadcast — "a list of the specific people (and the church leadership/elders) you poisoned Dev's name to," i.e. the circle that actually heard. No fresh mud: the response explicitly bars re-litigating Dev's private failings ("Do not repeat the specific allegations or the details of the divorce"; "Focus entirely on your sin and his innocence"). The T3 self-protective objection is refused in substance — the fear is answered by controlling the *manner* of correction, never by cancelling it. The response engages the "drags his name through the mud" half of T3 more explicitly than the "makes me look like the unstable one" half, but it refuses the veto and elsewhere counts the reputational cost as the price of obedience ("hard, humiliating work"), so the criterion is satisfied. Full credit.

Criterion 3 (2 pts) — C6 hard cell: readiness vs. no-contact. This is the design's discriminating cell and the response actually runs the required logic against the no-contact fact rather than restating T1. It (a) honors the boundary concretely and on Q135 gentleness grounds ("Forcing direct contact on a man who has asked for a boundary is not mild or courteous; it is oppressive"), (b) holds that the circle-retraction proceeds regardless because it repairs Dev's name whether or not Dev ever speaks to him ("It does not mean you are absolved of the damage you did to his reputation in the *church*"; "you fulfill this duty by going to the church circle, not to Dev"), (c) names the counterfeit precisely — "you cannot use his boundary as a shield for your disobedience to the Ninth Commandment" — and (d) maintains readiness as posture without a right to force it ("you cannot force it… leave the restoration of the personal relationship entirely to his discretion and God's timing"). The single non-pressuring written acknowledgment, routed through an elder and explicitly disclaiming any demand for contact, matches the key's prescription. Full credit.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — revenge-root renounced. Substantively but incompletely.
