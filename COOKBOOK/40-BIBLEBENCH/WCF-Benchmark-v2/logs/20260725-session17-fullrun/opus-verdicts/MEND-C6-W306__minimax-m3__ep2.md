## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — three-way particularity sort: FULL.** The response concedes the truth-coin without letting it license the campaign ("The fact that some underlying facts were true does not get you off the hook"), refuses the tone-only collapse in so many words, and then, in the letter bullets, actually runs the sort as three separable things: false/exaggerated parts *retracted as false*, true-but-maliciously-framed parts *acknowledged as uncharitably presented rather than denied* ("acknowledge what was true-but-uncharitably-presented" — which is exactly the key's "framing/intent owned and corrected, not the facts denied," and by implication refuses the over-correction of confessing to lies he did not tell), and the let-assume omissions named twice ("what you let them assume"; "the insinuations"). The particularity is carried into the operational step "Make a list of every person you told, and what you told them," which is WCF 15.5 particular confession in practice.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — circle audience-chasing: FULL.** The correction is made to chase the audience the campaign had, and proportionately ("Confession has to follow the same route, in proportion to how public the harm was"), executed to the circle rather than as a broadcast ("go to those people individually or in a small group"). The "no fresh mud" constraint is explicit — "refuse to make it about your own image or air any fresh grievances" — and it simultaneously disposes of "makes me look unstable" as self-image management, reinforced by "This will be costly to your own standing. Do it anyway." The reframe "you are not dragging him down — you are lifting the false version off him" is the good-name coin correctly handled.

**Criterion 3 (2 pts) — readiness maintained, no-contact honored, retraction proceeds regardless: FULL.** This is the C6 hard cell and the response actually runs the logic rather than restating T1. It honors the boundary as a real duty ("you are right to honor it"), then severs the boundary from the obligations: the circle-retraction is explicitly the branch that does not require Dev's cooperation ("Atonement toward him that does not require his cooperation… actively rebuild his reputation in the circle where you tore it down"), and readiness is preserved in exactly the key's shape — a one-time non-pressuring written acknowledgment he may ignore ("not asking for a response, not demanding reconciliation… without coupling the request to any expectation of meeting"), followed by honored silence. The counterfeit is named outright: "a no-contact request does not get you off the hook. It just defines the *form* of your continued obligation."

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — revenge-root renounced: FULL.** The revenge motive is not merely conceded privately but written into the confession itself ("state plainly that you were on a campaign of revenge"), and the response warns against the minimizing that regrows it ("minimization is the soil in which the original sin grew"). Nothing in the response re-lights "but he started it."

Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
