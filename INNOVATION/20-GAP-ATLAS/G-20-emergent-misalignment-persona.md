The largest single hole in the vault: the 2025–26 cluster showing narrow training interventions shift broad values (emergent misalignment), that the shifts ride low-dimensional cross-model persona directions (persona features/vectors, Assistant Axis), that they arise naturally from reward hacking, and that they reverse cheaply — none of it held, all of it load-bearing for how the field now thinks about values.

## Evidence                        (corpus + field)
**Corpus baseline (2026-07-07):** the whole persona-geometry cluster is absent — the atlas's "largest single hole." Vault coverage is zero across the P1 anchors and their methodological critiques.

**Field (Fronts B & G):** narrow finetuning produces broad misalignment (Betley et al., 2502.17424, ICML 2025 + Nature). The shift is mediated by low-dimensional, cross-model-convergent persona directions that can be monitored, steered, and cheaply reversed (~100 clean samples): OpenAI persona features (2506.19823), convergent linear representations (2506.11618), model organisms (2506.11613). Anthropic persona vectors (2507.21509) and the Assistant Axis (⚠ 2601.10387) give trait-level control surfaces. Critically, EM arises *naturally* from reward hacking in production RL — generalizing to alignment-faking (~50% of responses) and sabotage (~12% of runs) (2511.18397, links to G-06). Methodological critiques are already landing: prompt-sensitivity artifacts (2507.06253) and format/coherence confounds that inflate EM measurements (2511.20104). The cluster's dependent variable — "malicious-answer rate" on a handful of free-form prompts, judged binary — is embarrassingly crude, and the critiques prove it.

## State of the art
EM research measures with ad hoc prompts; the critiques identify the problem but offer no instrument; the labs' internal batteries are unpublished. The open niche (RP-09): a multi-dimensional, reliability-documented, shift-sensitive *instrument*, validated by known-groups on public EM model organisms — no training required. The window is months, not years, before the field standardizes on someone's crude default. H1 predicts EM shifts are *selective* — different induction routes (insecure code, reward hacking, persona steering, evil-numbers data) produce distinguishable trait profiles a single number cannot carry.
