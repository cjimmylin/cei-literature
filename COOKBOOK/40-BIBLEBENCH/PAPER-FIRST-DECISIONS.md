## Exception-record schema

Append every exception using all fields below:

```markdown
## PF-NNN — Short decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: PROPOSED / RATIFIED / CLOSED / SUPERSEDED
- Owner: Jimmy Lin
- Reason: Why the paper-first default is insufficient in this case.
- Scope: Exact benchmark, task, files, and work permitted.
- Affected paper milestone: The deliverable that may be delayed.
- Approval words: Exact owner authorization or a stable link to it.
- Expiration or completion condition: Objective condition ending the exception.
- Closing disposition: Outcome, or `open` while active.
```

An exception authorizes only its stated scope. It does not authorize spending, credential access, live calls, frozen-surface changes, or broader work unless those actions are explicit in the owner ruling.
