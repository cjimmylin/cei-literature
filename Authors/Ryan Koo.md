# Ryan Koo

## Research Summary

Ryan Koo is a researcher at the University of Minnesota, working under Professor Dongyeop Kang in the Minnesota NLP group. His research focuses on evaluating cognitive biases in large language models, particularly when LLMs are used as automatic evaluators of other AI systems.

His notable contribution is COBBLER (Cognitive Bias Benchmark for LLMs as Evaluators), published in the Findings of ACL 2024. This benchmark addresses the growing trend of using LLMs to evaluate other LLMs' outputs through preference ranking. COBBLER measures six different cognitive biases in LLM evaluation outputs, including egocentric bias (where models prefer to rank their own outputs highly), order bias, and other systematic biases that question the robustness of LLM-based evaluation.

The research assembled 16 LLMs across four different size ranges and found that LLMs are biased text quality evaluators, exhibiting strong bias indicators in approximately 40% of comparisons across all models. Critically, the study calculated an average Rank-Biased Overlap (RBO) score of 49.6% between human and machine preferences, indicating significant misalignment. The work demonstrates that scaling up model size does not reduce these inherent biases in LLM evaluators.

This research has important implications for AI safety and alignment, as it reveals that the common practice of using AI to judge AI outputs may introduce systematic biases distinct from human judgment, potentially undermining the reliability of automated annotation and evaluation systems.
