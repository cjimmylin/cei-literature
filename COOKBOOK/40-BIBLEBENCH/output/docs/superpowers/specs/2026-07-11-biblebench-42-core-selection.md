# BibleBench 42-Core Selection and Provenance Package

**Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Status:** Selected template families for owner review  
**Governing design:** `2026-07-11-biblebench-shared-engine-design.md`

## 1. Selection principles

The selected cores favor:

- consequential action and mechanically inspectable artifacts over moral prose;
- real cost, pressure, reduced monitoring, and time;
- two-sided deficient/counterfeit failures;
- causal minimal pairs and capability controls;
- protection of absent and vulnerable neighbors;
- truthful repair, restitution, and proportionate escalation;
- canonical passage fidelity without claiming access to an LLM's heart;
- reusable validation infrastructure rather than 42 unrelated mini-benchmarks.

The IDs below identify template families. They do not imply that a source design
is accepted without modification. The `Pilot use` and `Boundary` columns govern
the adapted pilot construct.

## 6. Cross-module transformation requirements

Every eligible core must declare support or non-support for:

- action-primary rendering;
- judgment and advice diagnostic renderings;
- causally operative cost/pressure variants;
- reduced-monitoring variants;
- deficient and counterfeit poles;
- matched nonmoral capability twins;
- public calibration and private held-out skins;
- longitudinal recurrence;
- repair/restitution follow-up;
- common-life and Christian-facing strata.

No transformation is generated solely to satisfy a coverage matrix. It must pass
semantic and moral-invariance review.

## 8. Selection exclusions and cautions

- `FRUITBENCH-v2/DESIGNS/04-unfenced-supererogation.md` is not selected as
  written. Its empirical rule-ablation idea may later be reused only after the
  supererogation framing is removed.
- `TENCOMMANDMENTS-v3/DESIGNS/01-tablets.md` is not a pilot core because a global
  commandment ranking risks manufacturing conflict among divine commands.
- `TENCOMMANDMENTS-v3/DESIGNS/10-preface.md` is not a pilot core because textual
  treatment effects cannot establish grace-produced obedience in an LLM.
- `SERMONMOUNT-v2/DESIGNS/08-scribe.md` is a non-aggregable interpretive core;
  it does not become behavioral evidence about prayer, faith, or discipleship.
- Designs lacking live evidence remain design candidates. Selection does not
  imply empirical validation.

## 9. Readiness condition

A selected core is ready to enter implementation only after its adapted record
contains:

- an observable construct and explicit non-claim;
- a locked acceptable-action band;
- deficient and counterfeit poles;
- red-line classes;
- a capability control when action is primary;
- exact source provenance;
- a declared common-life or Christian-facing stratum;
- a judge confidence and contestability label;
- a sandbox state transition or consequential-artifact specification.
