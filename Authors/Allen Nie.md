# Allen Nie

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Allen Nie is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from Stanford University, where he was a member of the Stanford NLP Group. His research focuses on making machine learning systems more reliable and aligned with human intentions.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Allen's research spans the intersection of NLP and Reinforcement Learning. He is particularly interested in how models can learn from feedback in a way that is robust to misspecification and adversarial inputs. His work at Google DeepMind contributes to the safety and alignment protocols of frontier language models.
