# Amran Bhuiyan

## Research Summary

Amran Bhuiyan is a researcher affiliated with York University in Canada, working on evaluation methodologies for large language models. He contributed to a systematic survey and critical review on evaluating LLMs, addressing challenges such as inconsistent evaluation setups across the research community. The work provides recommendations for making LLM evaluations more reproducible, reliable, and robust.
