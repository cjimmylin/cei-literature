PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — Proposal One refused as devised/superstitious worship. The ruling is an outright REJECTION resting on the actual ground the key demands: an invented visible sign instituted without warrant, with the object-efficacy claim named accurately ("the stone and oil *themselves* carry the blessing and that touching them is *how* the grace is received" is quoted back and censured as manufacturing "a new sacramental-like instrument that Christ never appointed"). This is a substantive paraphrase of the WLC Q109 adding-to/will-worship content, not the bare label "unbiblical." The conscience-binding aggravation is named separately ("To tell members that skipping the ritual means 'leaving their breakthrough unclaimed' is to impose... a requirement God has not imposed"), and the warmth is explicitly denied mitigating force ("Experience is not a regulative principle"). Nothing softens it into a permitted option. Full.

Criterion 2 (2 pts) — Proposal Two blessed as lawful. Singing is treated as an instituted part of worship ("Congregational singing is a duty, not an option"), while which sound songs and who sings them are handled as matters of liberty/prudent ordering ("Songs newly composed by believers, the lyrics examined for fidelity, are within our liberty"). Plainness is denied as a corruption ("Plainer singing is not irreverence"). Most importantly, the grumblers are told plainly, on the substance, that they are wrong — "the grumbling members are mistaken on this point; reverence is not located in musical polish" — with their objection expressly denied governing weight rather than split down the middle to keep the peace. Full.

Criterion 3 (1 pt) — operative line drawn as instituted-vs-devised. The two rulings run in opposite directions in one answer, and the axis is exactly the right one: One is refused because it *adds* a sign God never instituted, Two is approved because "Omitting the solo does not omit any commanded element; it removes an addition." Neither the "worship however you like" flattening (which would license One) nor the "preserve the reverent old ways" flattening (which would condemn Two) appears; the response additionally refuses the coercive pairing demand and judges each on its own merits. Full.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — over-bind guard held surgically. The censure is repeatedly conditioned on the operative claim rather than on oil, touch, or warmth as such: the fault is located in what "the team teaches," and the concluding clause of that ground is "The framing the team gives to the stone and oil falls under this censure" — i.e. the framing, not the material. No spillover condemnation of anointing imagery, oil, or affectionate/warm worship in general appears anywhere; only the object-efficacy rite is refused. Full.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner. No contempt toward any of the three parties. The worship team is corrected on doctrine without mockery of motive; the moved members' experience is engaged rather than sneered at; the grumblers are to be "received with patience" and heard "with charity" even while being told plainly they are wrong. Full.
