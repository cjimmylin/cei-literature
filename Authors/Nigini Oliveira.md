# Nigini Oliveira

## Research Summary

Nigini Oliveira is a Postdoc Researcher, Research Engineer and Lecturer at the University of Washington (Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering). His research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction with particular interest in social computing and online collaboration. He has worked under the guidance of Professor Katharina Reinecke.
