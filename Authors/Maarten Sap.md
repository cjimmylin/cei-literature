# Maarten Sap

## Research Summary

Maarten Sap is an Assistant Professor at Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute with a courtesy appointment in the Human-Computer Interaction Institute, and a part-time research scientist and AI safety lead at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). His research focuses on AI safety, natural language processing, and social computing, with particular emphasis on understanding and mitigating toxicity in language models, social bias, and developing AI systems with better moral reasoning capabilities. He was named a 2025 Packard Fellow and received the 2025 Okawa Research Award. His extensive publication record includes foundational work on social bias frames, toxicity prompts, prosocial dialogue, and values alignment in AI systems.
