# Alexandr Wang

## Biographical Summary

Alexandr Wang is the Chief AI Officer at Meta Platforms and the co-founder (and former CEO) of Scale AI. He famously dropped out of MIT after his freshman year to build Scale AI into the premier data labeling and infrastructure company for the AI industry. In 2025, following Meta's acquisition of a significant stake in Scale AI, he joined Meta to lead their Superintelligence Labs while remaining a strategic board member at Scale.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandr focuses on the industrialization of AI, particularly the creation of high-quality datasets for training the world's largest foundation models. At Meta, he leads "Superintelligence" initiatives, focusing on the scaling laws of intelligence and the safety frameworks required for AGI. He advocates for "American AI leadership" and the role of high-fidelity data in national security.

### Significant Achievements
Under his leadership, Scale AI became the "foundational" layer for almost all major AI labs (including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google). He has been a critical voice in the U.S. government's AI strategy and a pioneer in the "data-centric" AI movement. His transition to Meta marks a major shift in the global AI landscape toward vertically integrated superintelligence efforts.
