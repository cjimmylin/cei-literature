# Daniel Khashabi

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science, Johns Hopkins University

## Research Summary

Daniel Khashabi is an Assistant Professor at Johns Hopkins University, specializing in natural language processing with a focus on question answering, commonsense reasoning, and building AI systems that can engage in prosocial dialogue. Previously at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2), he has made significant contributions to developing benchmarks and models for natural language understanding. His work on ProsocialDialog explores how to train conversational agents to engage safely and helpfully with users.

## Research Interests
- Natural Language Processing
- Question Answering Systems
- Commonsense Reasoning
- Prosocial and Safe Dialogue Systems
- Knowledge Representation in NLP
