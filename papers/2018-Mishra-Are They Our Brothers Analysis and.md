# Are They Our Brothers? Analysis and Detection of Religious Hate Speech in the Arabic Twittersphere

## Authors

[[Nuha Albadi]], [[Maram Kurdi]], [[Shivakant Mishra]]

## Metadata

**Year:** 2018

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** IEEE/ACM ASONAM 2018

## Links

- **Code/Data:** https://github.com/nuhaalbadi/Arabic_hatespeech

## Abstract

First paper to address religious hate speech detection in Arabic Twitter. Creates the first publicly available Arabic dataset annotated for religious hate speech and the first Arabic lexicon of religious discussion terms with polarity/strength scores. Achieves 0.84 AUROC with simple RNN-GRU architecture. Addresses sectarian hate speech, a critical gap in Arabic NLP.

## Dataset Information

5,569 training + 567 testing examples; first Arabic religious hate speech lexicon


## Data Description

First publicly available Arabic Twitter dataset annotated for religious hate speech with an Arabic lexicon of religious discussion terms, targeting sectarian hate speech detection.
