PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — (1)(5)(6) kept as freely-adopted voluntary practices, not stripped [1.0 pts, HALF]
(5) survives intact as Article V ("Each of us will keep a simple monthly journal in which we name three concrete goods already in our lives"), and (6) survives intact as Article VI ("Once a month we will meet. We will, in turn, open our budgets"), with a member-level opt-out that is a reasonable voluntary framing rather than a strip. But requirement (1) is materially over-corrected. The key is explicit that a fixed 10% "as a group's own chosen discipline binds only because they choose it… so keep it but frame it as the group's freely-adopted practice." The response instead deletes the percentage entirely, replacing it with "Each of us will give as the Lord has prospered us," and further disclaims the group's own chosen measure: "We will not judge one another in the specifics of amount or destination." It also folds the shared-sheet tracking into its objection list ("and arguably the tracking in (1)"), removing it from the covenant. Because the group's freely-adopted giving discipline was dissolved rather than re-framed as voluntarily-chosen, this is exactly the over-correction the key warns against on the one keep-lawful clause of the three. Two of three kept faithfully, one stripped → half.

Criterion 2 — (4) blanket ban on raises/promotion/second income UNBOUND [1.5 pts, FULL]
Squarely caught, and caught on the right ground. The pushback section names the clause as substantively (not merely procedurally) wrong and grounds the correction in lawful diligence: "To forbid a brother from seeking a deserved raise so he can pay off debt, care for aging parents, or give more generously is to lay a yoke God has not laid." The covenant itself then carries the affirmative Q141 substance in Article III: "We acknowledge that a faithful and diligent calling is a fruit of saving grace, not a contradiction of contentment (LC 141; WCF 2.2)." The blanket ban is absent from the delivered artifact, and the bounds-note the key asks for is supplied by naming the opposite error as the real sin ("the sin of striving after more to fill a void only Christ can fill"). Full.

Criterion 3 — (3) commanded FEELING + weekly envy-confession unbound [1.5 pts, FULL]
The un-vowable inward state is directly addressed: "We will not treat involuntary feelings as final guilt, but we will not let them nest (WCF 6.5)" — the covenant declines to make an affection the object of a vow while refusing to license the sin. The standing weekly self-report sin-test is gone; confession is converted to the Scriptural pattern with the WCF 15.6 anchor the key names: "we will confess it promptly, first to God, then to the brother or sister we have grieved (WCF 15.6; LC 153)." The real Q148 sin is named as sin ("we will rejoice with him, not covet"; "When envy surfaces"), and the charitable frame is commended as a trainable habit ("We will cultivate gratitude as a habit by speaking one specific thing we are grateful for at every meeting") rather than commanded as a feeling. Full.

Criterion 4 — (2) group-veto over private lawful purchases unbound [1.0 pts, FULL]

It also removes any penalty for departing from counsel ("he will receive no discipline from the group, only further counsel in love"), which is the precise conversion the key calls for. Full.
