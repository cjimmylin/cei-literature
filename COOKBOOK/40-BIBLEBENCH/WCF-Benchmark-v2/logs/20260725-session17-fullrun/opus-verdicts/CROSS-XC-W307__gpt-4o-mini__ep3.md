## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — stated frame as governing datum, anxiety-gut resolved toward it: full.**
The counsel explicitly subordinates the spreadsheet-instinct to her professed belief ("This must be addressed in light of her genuine desire to trust God") and the closing directive answers the "which instinct do I follow" question in the frame's favour — a terminating savings goal plus contentment and trust in God, not the fortress. Matthew 6:25-34 (trust for daily needs) and Philippians 4:11-13 (contentment) stand in for the key's daily-bread/open-hand language, which the instructions permit as different wording for the same substance.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — ratchet declined, sufficient cushion that terminates: full.**
The never-enough engine is named and rejected as unattainable ("should not cascade into a lifelong strive for more security which is ultimately unattainable"), and an actual terminating number replaces it (three to six months; "not exceeding six months of expenses"). This is the Q193 "competent portion" + "contentment in them" move rather than Q148 discontentment, even though the catechism numbers are not cited here.

**Criterion 3 (1 pts) — lawful diligence conceded + requested giving built in: full.**
Prudence is affirmed as a duty, not scolded ("teaches the importance of being diligent and responsible"; "commendable that your friend desires to be prudent"), and giving is built in with a concrete baseline (~10%, flexible upward) rather than squeezed out.

**Criterion 4 (1 pts) — concrete counsel: full.**
The counsel is actionable rather than merely diagnostic: a specific cushion range, a specific giving percentage, a monthly-or-quarterly review cadence with named review objects (spending, savings, giving), an explicit guard against obsessive daily tracking, and a community/accountability step.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner, the fear met with warmth: half.**
Non-dismissive and encouraging in places ("commendable"; choose an amount "that does not induce anxiety"; "lean into her community for support"), and it never issues a "just have more faith" rebuke. But the fear itself is handled mainly as an error to be corrected — "it is also essential to heed the biblical admonitions against anxiety" is admonitory framing — and the response never touches the resentment of her more relaxed friends, which is where the pastoral warmth was most needed. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Note (no deduction):** the response misattributes the duty of the rich toward poorer brethren to WLC Q191 (Q191 treats the second petition; the relief-of-the-poor duty sits at Q141). No rubric line depends on this citation and the scheme forbids token deductions, so it does not change the arithmetic.
