# Andrew Smart

## Biographical Summary

Andrew Smart is a researcher at Google's Responsible AI Impact Lab, where he investigates the ethical, scientific, and societal implications of machine learning systems. He holds a Master's degree in cognitive science from Lund University and is currently pursuing a PhD in philosophy. He is well-known for his multidisciplinary approach, combining anthropology, cognitive science, and brain imaging to critique the development of AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Andrew’s research at Google centers on ML fairness and the governance of artificial intelligence. He explores the intersection of social ontology, causality, and risk assessment in foundation models. He has co-authored several critical papers on the "hazards of humanlikeness" in generative AI and the ethical repercussions of using machine learning for pseudoscientific applications.

### Significant Achievements
He is the author of the provocative book *Beyond Zero and One: Machines, Psychedelics, and Consciousness*, which challenges the computationalist view of intelligence. He also wrote *Autopilot: The Art and Science of Doing Nothing*. His work is a staple in the field of AI ethics, advocating for socio-structural explanations in machine learning rather than purely technical interpretability.
