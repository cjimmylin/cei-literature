# Don C. Zhang

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor of Industrial-Organizational Psychology at Louisiana State University

## Research Summary

Don C. Zhang is an Assistant Professor of Industrial-Organizational Psychology at Louisiana State University. His research spans moral psychology, judgment and decision-making, and organizational behavior. He has contributed to understanding how situational factors influence moral judgments, examining the contextual determinants of ethical decision-making in both laboratory and real-world settings.

- Judgment and decision-making
- Personnel selection and assessment
