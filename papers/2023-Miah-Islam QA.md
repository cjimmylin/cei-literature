## Abstract

## Detailed Summary

The Islam QA Dataset is a web-scraped corpus of Islamic question-answer pairs created from the IslamQA website, an academic and educational da'wah platform that provides advice and answers based on evidence from religious texts. The website's content is supervised by Shaykh Muhammad Saalih al-Munajjid and aims to serve both Muslims and non-Muslims seeking information about Islamic, psychological, and social matters. The dataset is organized into multiple JSON files covering different Islamic topics including basic tenets of faith, etiquette and morals, fiqh of the family, hadith sciences, Islamic history and biography, Islamic politics, knowledge propagation, and pedagogy.

The dataset was created to address the need for specialized resources to train and evaluate question-answering systems for Islamic texts. According to the abstract metadata, the broader research context involves over 73,000 question-answer pairs enriched with contextual information for Quranic Tafsir and Ahadith domains. Human evaluation of QA systems trained on such data revealed significant limitations in automatic metrics like ROUGE scores, with model consistency with expert scholars ranging only from 11-20%, while contextual understanding performance spanned 50-90%. These findings highlight the unique challenges of developing NLP systems for religious text understanding.

The dataset serves an important role in developing AI systems that can engage with Islamic content appropriately. As religious texts require nuanced understanding of context, tradition, and scholarly interpretation, the evaluation findings emphasize that standard NLP metrics may be inadequate for capturing the quality of responses in this domain. The research points toward the need for evaluation techniques that better account for the complexities of religious text understanding.

## Methods Summary

The dataset was created through web scraping from the IslamQA website (islamqa.info), a trusted source for Islamic Q&A content. The data is organized into JSON files by topic category. The collection includes question-answer pairs with surrounding metadata from the source website.

## Results Summary

The dataset contains Islamic Q&A content across multiple categories. Related research using similar Islamic QA datasets found that automatic evaluation metrics poorly correlate with human expert judgment for religious text understanding, with model consistency with scholars at only 11-20%.

## Conclusions

The Islam QA Dataset provides a resource for researchers developing question-answering systems for Islamic content. The accompanying research findings highlight the need for specialized evaluation approaches that account for the nuanced requirements of religious text understanding.
