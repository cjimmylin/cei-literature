# Allan Dafoe

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Allan Dafoe is the Senior Director of Frontier Strategy and Governance at Google DeepMind. He is also the founder and former Director of the Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI) at the Future of Humanity Institute, University of Oxford. He is one of the world's leading experts on the political and strategic challenges posed by advanced AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Allan's research focuses on the "Grand Strategy" of AI development—how nations and companies can cooperate to ensure safe and beneficial outcomes. He advocates for robust international governance frameworks and has written extensively on the risks of AI-led arms races and the necessity of institutional mechanisms for global safety.
