## Abstract

Systematically examines how LLMs represent different religions through emotion attribution using 19 distinct religious personas. Finds that major US/European religions are represented with more nuance, Eastern religions (Hinduism, Buddhism) are strongly stereotyped, and Judaism and Islam are stigmatized with model refusal rates skyrocketing. Llama2 frequently attributes fear, shame, and gratitude to Muslims and significant distortion toward shame for Jews.
