# Hanna Wallach

## Research Summary

Hanna Wallach is a VP and Distinguished Scientist in the Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics (FATE) group at Microsoft Research New York City. She also leads the Sociotechnical Alignment Center (STAC), an applied science team that bridges research and practice in responsible AI.

Her research focuses on issues of fairness, accountability, transparency, and ethics in AI and machine learning. She studies ways machine learning can inadvertently harm or disadvantage groups or individuals, emphasizing that the issues are not just from data or algorithms, but from people's decisions throughout the ML lifecycle.

She collaborated on creating the Fairlearn toolkit and worked with practitioners to understand how fairness toolkits are used in practice. She was involved early in defining responsible AI within Microsoft and helped shape their internal Responsible AI Standard. In 2020, she worked with ML practitioners across the tech sector to create an AI ethics checklist.
