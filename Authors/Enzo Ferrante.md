# Enzo Ferrante

## Biographical Summary

Enzo Ferrante is an Independent Researcher at CONICET (Argentina's National Scientific and Technical Research Council) and affiliated with the Universidad Nacional del Litoral. His research focuses on machine learning for medical image analysis, with growing interest in fairness and bias in AI systems, particularly in healthcare applications. He contributes to research on understanding cultural and demographic biases in AI systems.

## Research Interests

- **Medical Image Analysis**: Applying ML to medical imaging for diagnosis and treatment
- **Fairness in Healthcare AI**: Ensuring AI systems work equitably across populations
- **Computer Vision**: Developing visual recognition systems
- **Bias in AI**: Identifying and mitigating biases in machine learning

## Major Publications

- Extensive publications in medical image analysis (MICCAI, IEEE)
- Research on fairness and representation in AI systems
- Contributions to multimodal AI benchmarks
