# Bo Li

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bo Li is the Abbasi Associate Professor of Computer Science at UIUC, where she leads the Secure Learning Lab (SL2). She is also an associate professor at the University of Chicago and the CEO of Virtual AI. She is a globally recognized expert in the security and robustness of machine learning systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bo's research program focuses on bridging the "trustworthiness gap" in machine learning. She investigates adversarial attacks (evasion and poisoning) in both digital and physical environments and develops robust learning systems with formal guarantees. Her work is applied to computer vision, NLP, and safe autonomous driving.

### Significant Achievements
She has received some of the most prestigious awards in AI, including the IJCAI Computers and Thought Award and the Sloan Fellowship. Her work on stress-testing generative AI and implementing privacy safeguards is foundational to current AI safety standards. She is a member of several key research centers, including the Information Trust Institute (ITI).
