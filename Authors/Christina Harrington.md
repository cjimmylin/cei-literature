# Christina N. Harrington, Ph.D.

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Christina N. Harrington is an Assistant Professor in the Human-Computer Interaction Institute at Carnegie Mellon University, with a courtesy appointment in the School of Design. She is the Director of the Equity and Health Innovations Design Research Lab and a visiting faculty researcher at Google. Her work combines electrical engineering and industrial design backgrounds to focus on universal, accessible, and inclusive design.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Design for Health Equity**: Using design to support historically excluded groups (Black and LatinX communities, older adults, individuals with differing abilities) in maintaining health, wellness, and autonomy.
- **Community-Based Participatory Design**: Employing culturally tailored design to expand technology access and empowerment among marginalized communities.
- **Design Justice**: Broadening and amplifying participation in design by addressing barriers that corporate approaches have placed on seeing design as a universal language.

### Significant Achievements
- Recipient of the Computing Research Association's (CRA) Skip Ellis Early Career Award for significant research contributions and outreach to underrepresented populations
- Best Paper and Honorable Mention awards at CHI, CSCW, and Designing Interactive Systems conferences for work on equitable participatory design with Black communities
- Designed and evaluated household consumer products for people with physical and sensory disabilities
- Research examining tech design for health disparities among older African Americans
- Visiting Faculty Researcher at Google
