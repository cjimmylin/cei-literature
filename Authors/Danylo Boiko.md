# Danylo Boiko

## Biographical Summary

Danylo Boiko is a PhD student at Carnegie Mellon University working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and chemistry. His research explores how large language models can be applied to scientific discovery, with particular attention to the safety implications of AI systems with scientific capabilities.

## Research Focus

Boiko's research focuses on using AI systems, particularly large language models, for scientific applications in chemistry. His work on Kaleidoscope explores the capabilities and risks of AI systems with access to scientific knowledge and tools, addressing critical questions about AI safety in scientific domains.
