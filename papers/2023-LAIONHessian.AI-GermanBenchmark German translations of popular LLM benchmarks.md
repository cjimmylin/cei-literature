## Summary

This work presents GermanBenchmark, a collection of German translations of popular English LLM evaluation benchmarks including MMLU-DE, hellaswag_de, arc_challenge_de, truthful_qa_de, and GSM8K. Developed as part of the LeoLM project by LAION and Hessian.AI, these benchmarks enable systematic evaluation of both multilingual and German-specific language models on reasoning, knowledge, common-sense understanding, and truthfulness tasks. The authors evaluate several prominent LLMs including GPT-3.5, GPT-4, LLaMa-2, BLOOM, and Mistral on these German benchmarks, providing important insights into model performance across languages.

## Data Description

German translations of popular English LLM benchmarks including MMLU, HellaSwag, ARC, TruthfulQA, and GSM8K. Enables evaluation of multilingual and German-specific LLMs on reasoning, knowledge, and common-sense tasks in German language.
