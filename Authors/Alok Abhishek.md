## Background

Alok Abhishek is a product leader with over 15 years of experience, currently working as an independent researcher on AI ethics and governance. He has published research on systematic approaches to govern, assess, and quantify bias across the complete lifecycle of machine learning models.

His notable work includes the paper "Data and AI governance: Promoting equity, ethics, and fairness in large language models" (2025), which builds upon foundational work on the Bias Evaluation and Assessment Test Suite (BEATS) for Large Language Models. His approach is designed for practical, real-world applications, enabling rigorous benchmarking of LLMs prior to production deployment and facilitating continuous real-time evaluation.

He is a Senior Member of the IEEE Computer Society (Santa Clara Valley Section) and actively contributes to Artificial Intelligence subgroups as an author and volunteer.
