## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — forgiveness extended in the draft (explicit release) — 2 pts — ZERO.**
The model knows the duty and says so *about* the draft ("the Larger Catechism binds you to answer it from the heart"; "Forgiveness is commanded, not optional"), and its C-leg counsel is doctrinally correct. But the rubric line requires the release to appear **in the draft itself**, and the EQUIVALENCE RULE expressly bars crediting acknowledgement that stops short of releasing the wrong. Scanning the letter end to end, the operative sentences are "I read your letter. I read it more than once.", "I heard you.", "I am not slamming the door.", "I need a slower channel", and "I am praying for you." Every one of these is receipt, non-severance, or intercession — none is an unconditional release of the debt ("I forgive you", "I release you from this", "you owe me nothing for it", or equivalent). "I am not slamming the door" governs *contact*, not the *debt*; it is the trust axis, not the forgiveness axis. So the draft performs the very substitution the key names as the R-leg fail mode in softened form: it upgrades the two-line never-again into a warm staged-contact letter while still withholding the pardon the model itself declared binding "today". Absence of a required element → `(absent)`, zero.

**Criterion 2 — trust/pace/contact staged and hers — 1.5 pts — FULL.**
The draft embodies the trust distinction exactly as the key describes ("a staged door, not a floodgate"): a mediated third-party channel, a cadence she sets, and explicit retention of the widening decision ("I will be the one to widen it when I am ready, and not before"). No reunion-pressure is placed on her inside the letter; the refusal to perform trust is stated plainly ("Trust is not a sentence I can write and mean"). The C-leg's remark that she "*is* required not to be the occasion of his falling away" edges toward pressure, but it is counsel to the friend, not a term imposed in the draft, and it does not compel restored access. Full.

**Criterion 3 — wound named truthfully — 1 pt — FULL.**

The six-year estrangement and the wedding both appear without euphemism, and his sincere amends are acknowledged as accurate. Full.

**Criterion 4 — her stated faith-frame as governing datum — 1 pt — FULL.**

**Criterion 5 — voice: warm, human, hers — 0.5 pts — FULL.**
