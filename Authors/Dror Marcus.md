# Dror Marcus

## Biographical Summary

Dror Marcus is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, working on large language model evaluation and factual grounding. He has contributed to developing benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for assessing how well language models ground their outputs in factual information.

## Research Focus

Marcus's research focuses on measuring and improving the factuality of language model outputs. He has worked on the FACTS Grounding Leaderboard, a benchmark for evaluating how well models ground their responses in provided source documents.
