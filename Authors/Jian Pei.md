# Jian Pei

## Research Summary

Jian Pei is the Arthur S. Pearse Distinguished Professor of Computer Science at Duke University, where he also serves as Chair of the Department of Computer Science, with joint appointments in Biostatistics & Bioinformatics and Electrical and Computer Engineering. His research focuses on data science, data mining, databases, information retrieval, and applied machine learning. Previously, he was a Canada Research Chair (Tier 1) in Big Data Science at Simon Fraser University. He is an ACM Fellow and IEEE Fellow with over 77,000 citations.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored the definitive "Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques" textbook with Jiawei Han
- Pioneered work on fairness in data propagation (collaboration with LinkedIn)
- Received IEEE Fellowship (2014) for contributions to data mining and knowledge discovery
- PhD thesis advised by Jiawei Han at Simon Fraser University
