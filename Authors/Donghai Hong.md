# Donghai Hong

## Affiliation
Researcher at Peking University

## Research Summary

Donghai Hong is a researcher at Peking University working on AI alignment and AI safety. He has contributed to comprehensive survey work on AI alignment, examining the challenges and approaches for ensuring that AI systems behave in accordance with human values and intentions. This includes studying reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), constitutional AI, and other alignment techniques.

## Research Interests
- AI alignment
- AI safety and robustness
- Large language models
- Reinforcement learning from human feedback
- Value learning
