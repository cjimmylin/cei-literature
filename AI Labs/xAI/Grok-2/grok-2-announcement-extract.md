## Summary

xAI announced Grok-2 and Grok-2 mini as a significant step forward from Grok-1.5, featuring frontier capabilities in chat, coding, and reasoning.

## Key Features

### Model Variants
- **Grok-2:** Full-size model with frontier capabilities
- **Grok-2 mini:** Smaller, more efficient variant

### Performance Benchmarks

#### General Capabilities
- Competitive with **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** and **GPT-4-Turbo**
- Early version tested on LMSYS leaderboard under the name "sus-column-r"
- Outperformed both Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4-Turbo in leaderboard testing

#### Academic Benchmarks
Both Grok-2 and Grok-2 mini achieved competitive performance on:
- **GPQA:** Graduate-level science knowledge
- **MMLU:** Massive Multitask Language Understanding
- **MMLU-Pro:** Advanced version of MMLU
- **MATH:** Math competition problems

#### Vision Capabilities
Grok-2 excels in vision-based tasks with state-of-the-art performance:
- **MathVista:** Visual math reasoning
- **DocVQA:** Document-based question answering

### Advanced Features

#### Reasoning with Retrieved Content
- Improved reasoning with retrieved information
- Correctly identifies missing information
- Reasons through sequences of events
- Discards irrelevant posts/content

#### Tool Use Capabilities
Enhanced ability to use tools and external resources effectively

#### Image Generation
Integration with image generation capabilities on the X platform
