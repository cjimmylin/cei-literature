# Alexander Kapitanov

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Kapitanov is a machine learning researcher at SberDevices (Salute Devices), specializing in the development of multimodal systems and cultural representation in AI. Since joining the ML RnD department in 2020, he has focused on bridging the gaps in generative models for non-English speakers.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander lead researcher on the RusCode project, a unique benchmark designed to evaluate how well text-to-image models understand Russian cultural nuances. His work challenges the Western-centric bias of global foundation models, advocating for "culturally grounded" AI that can accurately represent diverse global heritage.

## Major Publications
- [RusCode: A Benchmark for Evaluating Russian Cultural Code in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00000) (2024) - *Addressing cultural bias in generative AI.*
