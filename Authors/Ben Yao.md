# Ben Yao

## Biographical Summary

Ben Yao is a Research Assistant at the Hong Kong Polytechnic University. His research focuses on the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and the nuances of human language, particularly subjective and ethical dimensions.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work investigates how LLMs handle subjective language, such as sarcasm and moral values. He is interested in the ethical considerations surrounding the deployment and understanding of these models.

### Significant Achievements
Yao has contributed to surveys on LLMs for subjective language understanding, highlighting "ethical considerations" as a critical open issue in the field.

## Major Publications

- [NurValues: A Multimodal Metric for Evaluating Moral Values in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00000) (2025) - *Research on evaluating moral alignment.*
- [Is sarcasm detection a step-by-step reasoning process in large language models?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-024-18345-z) (2025) - *Exploration of reasoning capabilities in LLMs.*
