# Laura Gustafson

## Research Summary

Laura Gustafson is affiliated with Facebook AI Research (now Meta AI Research). Her research focuses on artificial intelligence and computer vision, with particular emphasis on fairness in computer vision and object recognition systems. She has contributed to major projects including the Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM 2), a foundation model for solving promptable visual segmentation in images and videos. Her work addresses fairness issues in computer vision systems and has been highly cited, with over 20,676 citations to her publications.
