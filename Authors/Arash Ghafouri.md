# Arash Ghafouri

## Biographical Summary

Arash Ghafouri is a PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at the Iran University of Science and Technology, Tehran. His research focuses on natural language processing, information retrieval, and their applications in practical systems such as search engines and conversational AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research investigates computational approaches to language understanding, with particular emphasis on multilingual NLP challenges. He works on developing deep learning-based solutions for linguistic processing in Arabic and Persian, addressing the unique morphological and orthographic challenges of these languages.

### Significant Achievements

Ghafouri has published research on supervised deep learning approaches for bilingual Arabic and Persian spell correction, demonstrating expertise in handling the complexities of non-Latin script languages. His work contributes to making NLP technologies more accessible and accurate for Arabic and Persian speakers, addressing the linguistic diversity gap in AI systems.
