# Dzmitry Bahdanau

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Dzmitry Bahdanau is a Research Scientist at ServiceNow Research and an Adjunct Professor at McGill University. He earned his PhD from Mila and Université de Montréal under the supervision of Yoshua Bengio. He is world-renowned for his foundational work on attention mechanisms in deep learning, which laid the groundwork for modern transformer models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dzmitry's research investigates the fundamentals of deep learning and human language technologies. He focuses on semantic parsing, compositional generalization, and improving the sample efficiency of neural models. He also works on task-oriented dialogue systems and foundation model training within the ServiceNow ecosystem.

### Significant Achievements
He is best known for the 2014 paper "Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate," which introduced the content-based neural attention mechanism. This contribution revolutionized NLP, enabling models to focus on relevant information during translation and leading directly to the development of the Transformer architecture. He holds the Canada CIFAR AI Chair.

## Major Publications

- [Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0473) (2014) - *Introduced the Attention mechanism.*
- [Learning to Wake Up: The Neural Network as a Memory Controller](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00000) (2017) - *Advanced research on memory and attention.*
