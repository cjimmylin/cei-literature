# Amit Singhal

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Amit Singhal is an Indian-American computer scientist best known for leading Google's search team for 15 years. He earned his PhD from Cornell University under the mentorship of Gerard Salton, the "father of information retrieval." He joined Google in 2000 as its 176th employee and was eventually named a Google Fellow and Senior Vice President. He is a primary architect of the modern search engine.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Since retiring from Google in 2016, Amit has focused on philanthropy through the **Sitare Foundation**, which provides high-quality education to underprivileged children in India. He also founded Sitare University to cultivate the next generation of computer scientists from diverse backgrounds.

### Significant Achievements
Amit is credited with rewriting the original Google search algorithm (PageRank) to improve scalability and relevance. His team was responsible for the core search quality and ranking systems that powered Google's global dominance. He is a recipient of many of the highest honors in computer science, including being elected to the National Academy of Engineering.
