# Elias Stengel-Eskin

## Biographical Summary

Elias Stengel-Eskin is an Assistant Professor at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. His research focuses on vision and language understanding, compositional generalization, and building trustworthy AI systems. He completed his PhD at Johns Hopkins University, where he worked on semantic parsing and multimodal learning.

## Research Interests

- Vision and language integration
- Multimodal learning and reasoning
- Compositional generalization
- Semantic parsing
- AI trustworthiness and evaluation

## Major Publications

- "On the Trustworthiness of Generative Foundation Models" (2025)
- Research on compositional generalization in language models
- Work on visual question answering and semantic parsing
