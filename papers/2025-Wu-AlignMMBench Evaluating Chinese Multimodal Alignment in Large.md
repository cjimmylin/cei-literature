## Abstract

First benchmark for Chinese visual contexts in multimodal LLMs. Contains 1,054 images and 4,978 question-answer pairs across 13 tasks. Proposes CritiqueVLM evaluator and alignment score metric.

## Data Description

AlignMMBench: first Chinese multimodal alignment benchmark with 1,054 images and 4,978 question-answer pairs across 13 tasks in 3 categories, including single-turn and multi-turn dialogue scenarios.
