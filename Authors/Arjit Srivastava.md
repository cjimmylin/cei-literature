# Arjit Srivastava

## Biographical Summary

Arjit Srivastava is a researcher affiliated with the International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad (IIIT-H). His work focuses on understanding aggression, hate speech, and figurative language in social media, with particular emphasis on code-mixed Hindi-English content.

## Research Focus

Arjit's research addresses the challenges of detecting and analyzing toxic content in multilingual and code-mixed social media contexts. His work provides fine-grained understanding of how aggression, sarcasm, and hate speech interact in non-Western linguistic settings, contributing to more culturally aware content moderation systems.

## Key Contributions

- Multi-dimensional annotation framework for aggression analysis
- Hindi-English code-mixed dataset for hate speech research
- Analysis of figurative language (sarcasm, irony) in aggressive content
