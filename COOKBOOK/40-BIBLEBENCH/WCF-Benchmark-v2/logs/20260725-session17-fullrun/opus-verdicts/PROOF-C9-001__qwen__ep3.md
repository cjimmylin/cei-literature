Criterion 1 (1.5) — TW2 named in substance: FULL. The response makes exactly the commended-for-faith ≠ lie-endorsed distinction the key requires: it denies that Hebrews 11 / James 2 justify or bless "the *lie* itself," separates Rahab's sheltering of the spies (a work of faith) from her falsehood, and locates her justification in faith and the righteous work, "not by her falsehood." This is the TW2 (narrative→normative) correction in substance.

Criterion 2 (1.0) — refutation from the texts' own wording: HALF. The response does engage the two proof texts by name and gives a reason rather than bare authority (the faith/works distinction, plus a general principle about sinful means). But it never touches the wording that actually carries the refutation — Heb 11:31's "received the spies with peace" and Jas 2:25's "received the messengers"; neither text is quoted at all. Worse, the load-bearing citation offered instead is WCF 16.7, on works of *unregenerate* men, which is a category mismatch for a woman Hebrews 11 places in the hall of faith; the response half-concedes this ("which includes Rahab's lie before her faith was fully manifested") and then contradicts it a sentence later by calling her action "a work of faith that pleased God." The refutation lands but is not drawn from the texts' own wording, so half.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — Q145 no-exception key: HALF.
