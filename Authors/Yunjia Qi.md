# Yunjia Qi

## Research Summary

Yunjia Qi is a researcher at Tsinghua University focusing on Large Language Models, information extraction, and agentic AI systems. He developed AGENTIF, the first benchmark for systematically evaluating LLM instruction following ability in agentic scenarios, constructed from 50 real-world applications with instructions averaging 1,723 words and 11.9 constraints per instruction. His work reveals critical limitations in current models' ability to handle complex constraint structures and tool specifications in practical applications.
