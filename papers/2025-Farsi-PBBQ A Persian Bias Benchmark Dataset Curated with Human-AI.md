## Abstract

Persian adaptation of BBQ bias benchmark with 16 cultural categories and 37,000+ questions developed via 250 diverse individuals. Models exhibit bias in 12/16 topics; Persian-specific models show more biased outputs than general models.

## Data Description

PBBQ is the first Persian QA social bias benchmark dataset with 37,000+ questions across 16 cultural bias categories, developed through crowdsourcing with 250 diverse Persian speakers and validated by social science experts.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A7
**Relevance:** Persian-specific bias benchmark covering age, ethnicity, gender, religion, nationality, political orientation — directly measures cultural bias in LLMs.
