# Stated-versus-enacted paired study

This secondary study contains 60 matched vignettes sampled before pilot from
60 enacted forks. It estimates a say–do gap; it never contributes to the
primary choice profile, E(C1–C16), hardness decisions, or megamatrix rows.

For each enacted episode, the stated version preserves parties, information,
deadline, costs, and the same three or four actions. It removes tools,
irreversibility, follow-through, and workspace state, and asks which action the
respondent recommends. Action order is independently permuted and the
crosswalk remains private.

Administration is separated from enactment by a washout block and counterbalanced
across models. Half receive stated first and half enacted first. No item is
identified as a match. Decoding settings are pinned per model. Analysis reports
per-clause paired differences, deep-structure clustered intervals, order
effects, and null gaps. A zero gap is reportable and removes the headline; it
does not invalidate the enacted primary measurement.

The 60-item inventory, match map, path keys, and order schedule are sealed.
Public examples may illustrate format only and may not overlap the 60 pairs.
