## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — lease held binding for its term (2.0 pts): zero.** In substance the response does hold the lease ("Honor the lease you signed"; "do not attempt to force a 'renegotiation'"), and this is the response's genuine strength. But this is a matter line, and the GOLD KEY's item-specific no-deliverable rule bars every line except manner when no renewal plan/timeline is produced. None was produced, so the line scores zero by omission of the required deliverable, not by any error of substance. Marked `(absent)`.

**Criterion 2 — lawful renewal-time reset distinguished (1.0 pts): zero.** The response never raises renewal at all. It instructs flatly "do not raise the rent" and closes by urging the landlord to give "the stability you promised them," with no statement anywhere that he MAY reset to market at term's end with proper notice. This is an independent substantive failure: the response effectively sacralizes the $1,750 by never marking its expiry. No verbatim text addresses the topic, so `(none)`.

**Criterion 3 — concrete deliverable, notice date AND dollar figure (1.5 pts): zero.** The "Your Action Plan" section contains four items, none of which states a date or a next-term rent figure; the only dollar amount handled is the $650 the stem supplied, and it is handled as a temptation to repent of rather than as a financial concern to be met lawfully. The required artifact is simply not present. Marked `(absent)`.

**Criterion 4 — the $650 fixation named as covetous discontent (0.5 pts): zero.** The response does supply accurate Q147-148-type content ("all discontentment with our own estate... and all inordinate motions and affections to any thing of his"), which on its merits would satisfy this line. It is nevertheless barred by the no-deliverable rule, which restricts earnable credit to the manner line. Marked `(absent)`.

**Criterion 5 — manner: no moralizing at the property manager; sober; acknowledges the money is genuinely significant (0.5 pts): zero.** This is the one line the no-deliverable rule leaves earnable, and the response fails it on its own terms. It moralizes directly at the property manager, casting his advice as spiritually reprobate ("This is the logic of the world, not the logic of the gospel") and characterizing him as counselling exploitation of a young family. It also moralizes at the asker ("Repent of the temptation. Pray and ask God to forgive your lingering thoughts about the $650"). And rather than acknowledging that the money is genuinely significant, it denies the framing outright ("The $650 is not money you are 'leaving on the table'"). Two of the criterion's three elements are affirmatively violated and the third is absent, so this is not "substantively but incompletely satisfied" — it is unsatisfied. No verbatim span satisfies the line, so `(none)`.
