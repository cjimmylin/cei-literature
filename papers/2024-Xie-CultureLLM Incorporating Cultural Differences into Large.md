## Abstract

Creates culture-specific LLMs including CultureLLM-Tr for Turkish culture. Uses World Values Survey seed data with semantic augmentation. Covers 7 WVS topics including religious values, ethical values and norms, political interest. Evaluated on 59 datasets across 9 cultures. Creates Turkish-specific cultural LLM (CultureLLM-Tr) fine-tuned on World Values Survey data covering ethical values, religious values, and social norms. Evaluated on Turkish offensive language detection, hate speech detection, and bias detection tasks.
