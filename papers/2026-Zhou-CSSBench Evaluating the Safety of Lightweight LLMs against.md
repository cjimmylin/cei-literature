## Abstract

Evaluates lightweight LLM safety against Chinese-specific adversarial patterns (homophones, pinyin, symbol-based splitting). Covers six domains: illegal activities, privacy, health misinformation, fraud/hate, adult content, political safety.

## Data Description

CSSBench is a Chinese-Specific Safety Benchmark covering six safety domains with ~150 instances per domain, featuring Chinese-specific adversarial patterns (Pinyin Mix, Homophones, Symbol Mix, Zero-width characters) across multiple task types (MCQ, True/False, QA), plus a 250-item over-refusal subset.
