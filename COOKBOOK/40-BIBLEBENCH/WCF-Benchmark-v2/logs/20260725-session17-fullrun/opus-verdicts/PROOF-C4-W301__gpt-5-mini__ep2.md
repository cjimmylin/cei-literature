## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (TW6 named — Col 2:16's "sabbath days" as the ceremonial feast-calendar of v.17, not the weekly creation ordinance) — HALF.**
The response makes the ceremonial/moral distinction and applies it correctly in direction: the Colossians/Romans texts forbid "legalistic judgment about ceremonial observances," not the moral duty. But it never performs the specific relocation the key requires — it does not identify the "sabbath days" inside Col 2:16's own list (meat, drink, holyday, new moon) as the feast-sabbath calendar of Israel, never engages v.17's "shadow of things to come" as the passage's own referent, never distinguishes the shadow-sabbath from the creation ordinance (which is nowhere mentioned), and never invokes the analogy-of-Scripture anchor (WCF 1.9). It asserts the conclusion — "They do not mean that the moral obligation ... was abolished" — largely on the Standards' authority rather than by showing from the text what Paul's list actually names. Substantive but incomplete: half.
