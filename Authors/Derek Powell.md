# Derek Powell

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor, Department of Psychology, Arizona State University

## Research Summary
Derek Powell is a cognitive scientist studying moral judgment, causal reasoning, and decision making. His research on deontological coherence examines how people maintain consistent moral principles across different scenarios and how moral rules interact with consequentialist considerations. Using computational modeling and behavioral experiments, he investigates the cognitive mechanisms underlying moral reasoning and how people balance rule-following with outcome evaluation.
