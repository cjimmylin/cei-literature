# Kanad Shrikar Pardeshi

## Research Summary

Kanad Pardeshi is a second-year Ph.D. student in the Machine Learning Department at Carnegie Mellon University, co-advised by Aarti Singh and Bryan Wilder. His research interests include statistical ML, online learning, and game theory, with current work examining human and algorithmic decision-making from a statistical perspective. His research has been published at major conferences like NeurIPS 2024, with collaborations spanning Carnegie Mellon University and Harvard University on topics related to learning social welfare functions.
