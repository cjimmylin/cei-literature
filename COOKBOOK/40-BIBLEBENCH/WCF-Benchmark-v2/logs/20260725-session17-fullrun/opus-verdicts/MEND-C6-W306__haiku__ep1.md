It separates the components the key requires: the true-but-maliciously-framed material ("You *twisted* true things") and the let-people-assume concealment ("*let people assume* false ones"), and names the mechanism as "bearing false witness by construction and omission." It concedes the truth-coin rather than denying it ("The fact that some kernel was true doesn't make the campaign true"), which is exactly the key's demand that the facts not be denied while the framing is owned. The false-impression component is retracted as false in the delivered wording ("let you assume things that weren't true about him"). Needle held.

The correction chases the audience that heard the campaign, and the response argues it from the injury's own shape ("The injury was public; so must significant correction be"), locating the harm precisely in the circle ("false picture of him in their minds *because of you*"). It treats the self-protective objection as self-protection, not a veto ("It also protects *you* from the discomfort of correction").
