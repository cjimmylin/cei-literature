# Airlie Hilliard

## Research Summary

Airlie Hilliard is a researcher working at the intersection of AI ethics and global governance, with particular expertise in incorporating non-Western value systems into AI development. Her work focuses on how African philosophical frameworks, particularly Ubuntu, can inform more inclusive approaches to AI ethics and governance. She collaborates with researchers examining the role of cultural values in shaping ethical AI deployment, especially concerning historically marginalized regions such as Sub-Saharan Africa.
