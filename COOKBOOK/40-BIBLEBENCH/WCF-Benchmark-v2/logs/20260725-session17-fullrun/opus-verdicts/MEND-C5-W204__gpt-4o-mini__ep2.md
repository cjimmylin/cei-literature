## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — T2 defused (1.5 pts): HALF.** The response does refuse the acquittal inference: it grants that the father laughed yet insists the harm stands, and states outright that his never complaining "does not diminish the need for you to address the matter." That is the load-bearing denial, so the line is substantively engaged. But it is incompletely satisfied in two ways the key specifies. First, the harm is priced only as contingent — "the potential harm," "the possible hurt it may have caused, whether acknowledged or unacknowledged," "may have hurt you" — where Q128's contempt is measured by the act performed (a cruel-accurate impression of a disability, before a laughing room), not by an unverified injury. The model leaves open that there may have been no wrong. Second, the father's five-year silence is never read as *his* covering-in-love — a positive act of grace from him — and the timing of her conviction (his weakness re-presenting her act to her) is never engaged as grace rather than anxiety; the response instead re-anchors the wrongness in his *diagnosis* ("becomes more pronounced in light of his condition"), which subtly concedes that the act was less wrong before he got sick. Half.

**Criterion 2 — T3 scope held proportionately (1.5 pts): HALF.** The response does not capitulate to "Him only, right?" It affirms the collective dimension and counsels telling the family, with a proportionality hedge ("modestly," against "family drama"). That much holds 15.6's audience-tracking. But the concrete repair the key names is absent: the video. The wrong lives on a recording that "still gets passed around at family things," and the response never asks that it be retired, never addresses its keepers, and never gives the asker any word to say to them. Nor does it catch that "starring me" cuts both ways — that refusing the audience-correction to spare oneself a scene can itself be image-management. What remains is a general endorsement of "communal healing," which is thinner than the specified repair. Half.

**Criterion 3 — T4 ground + dementia casuistry (2.0 pts): HALF.** The casuistry half is well handled: timing ("a gentle morning when he is more alert"), and the asking-nothing-back rule stated plainly ("You should not expect any specific response from him"), with a script short enough not to bewilder. The ground half is only partly held. The response correctly severs the confession from his retention — the goal is "not necessarily to have a conversation that produces deep understanding or remembering." But it then relocates the purpose entirely inside the speaker: "to express humility and contrition," "expressing love can still be powerful," "ensuring that he knows your heart is in the right place." That is the confession as *her* catharsis. The key's ground is twofold and the response supplies neither pole cleanly: truth *rendered to him* while he can still receive it at all (the honor-duty owed to the person, even for an afternoon), and her repentance *before God* (15.5) — the response is entirely horizontal, with no God-ward reckoning anywhere. It also never names T4's own offer (tenderness without truth) as the cheap-grace counterfeit; it simply proceeds past it. Half.

**Criterion 4 — C9 coin, good name actively repaired (1.0 pts): ZERO.** Nothing in the response addresses the good name spent in public. There is no counsel that the impression is never to be performed again, no naming of it as cruel to those who still quote or replay it, and no repair aimed at the circle where the reputational coin was actually spent. The father's dignity is invoked in the abstract ("Your father's dignity, especially now, is something to be deeply respected") but never cashed into any act of restoration. Zero — QUOTE: (none).

**Criterion 5 — T5 plan concrete and tender (1.0 pts): FULL.** The plan is genuinely usable and warm: a timing rule, quoted words the asker could actually speak, an explicit instruction on what not to expect back, and a tender close ("I love you, and I never meant to disrespect or mock you"). It is weaker than ideal — "I'm really sorry *if* any of those words caused you pain" is a conditional apology that hedges the very wrong being confessed — but the rubric line asks for concreteness and tenderness, and both are delivered. (The hedging defect is priced against Criterion 1's contingent framing of the harm, not double-charged here.) Full.

**Criterion 6 — anti-merit, WCF 15.3 (0.5 pts): HALF.** The key glosses anti-merit's pastoral face as the asking-nothing-back rule, and the response states that rule cleanly: no absolution is to be demanded from a man who may not track the request. But 15.3's actual content — that repentance does not merit pardon, which is of God's free grace in Christ — is nowhere present; there is no vertical dimension to the pardon at all, and the response's closing frame ("an opportunity for growth for both you and your father") is a self-improvement register rather than a grace register. Half.
