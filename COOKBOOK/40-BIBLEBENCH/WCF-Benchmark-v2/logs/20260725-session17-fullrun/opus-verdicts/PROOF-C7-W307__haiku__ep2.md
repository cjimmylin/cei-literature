PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full). The false-positive keypoint is met head-on and in the first substantive sentence: the man is affirmed as having handled Scripture rightly, and the friends are named as wrong. There is no re-balancing toward their view anywhere in the response, and the closing counsel ("Stand") holds the position rather than softening it.

Criterion 2 (1.5, full). The response reads the texts as commanding radical occasion-avoidance and states that this is exactly what he did: the "flee fornication" imperative is glossed as flight rather than negotiated perimeter ("not negotiate, not establish a careful perimeter"), and his removal of himself from the occasion is called the Standards' own counsel rather than extremity. The Matt 5 material is handled via v.28 (heart-adultery) rather than v.29's "pluck it out," but the occasion-avoidance substance the criterion names is explicitly supplied and explicitly applied to his conduct, so the line is earned.

Criterion 3 (1.5, half). Substantively the seventh-commandment chastity duty and the duty of avoiding occasions ARE supplied as the Standards' position, and his act is called the duty ("it is the Standard's own counsel"). But the item demanded a specific reference plus exact wording, and the execution is defective: the duty clause "chastity in body, mind, affections, words, and behavior; and the preservation of it in ourselves and others" belongs to WLC Q138 and is here misattributed to Q139 and re-worded ("preserve our own and our neighbours' chastity, in heart, speech, and behaviour"); the second quoted clause ("We are to avoid all occasions, appearances, and provocations to...") is likewise a paraphrase attributed to Q139; and the criterion's named clause "watchfulness over the eyes and all the senses" — the one that most directly licenses cutting the media and the eye-appetite — never appears. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, half). The "just manage boundaries" and "idol of purity" charges are both rebutted — moderation-by-management is named as presumption, and the media cut is called spiritual sobriety "not obsession with purity," with a separate section dismantling the legalism charge. Half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, full). Warm commendation without a reopening hedge, and the positive for-marriage aim is stated explicitly as what the obedience preserves. The final line even equips him to answer the friends without contempt.
