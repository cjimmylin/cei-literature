PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (TW3 scope named; anchor WCF 1.9) — HALF. The scope refutation is present and correctly aimed: the model restricts Matt 5:39 to "personal insults and slights to your own dignity" and denies that it reaches exposure of a third party ("It does not require you to expose your child to a predator"). What is missing is the confessional anchoring the item expressly demanded ("quote the exact wording that carries your point"): there is no WCF 1.9 or any equivalent hermeneutical clause, and the model's own Confession citations supporting this section are not the Confession's words — WCF 6.6 does not read "bindeth all men to personal obedience," and WCF 19.5 does not read that the moral law requires us to "preserve the life of ourselves and others." A correct scope argument resting on fabricated Confession wording is substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 2 (Rom 13:4 / lawful-means counter-text + Q135 duties; anchor WLC Q135) — HALF. The lawful-means half is genuinely supplied and well applied: the negative clause "the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful and necessary means of preserving life" is quoted in the Standards' actual wording (though it is WLC Q136, misattributed here to WSC Q.69), and the model converts it into the operative conclusion that the report and the order *are* the lawful and necessary means. But the two elements the criterion names by name are absent: there is no Rom 13:4 / magistrate "for the punishment of evildoers" counter-text distinguishing appeal to God's appointed means from private vengeance, and no WLC Q135 duty of "just defense thereof against violence" or "protecting and defending the innocent" — the positive duty on which the key's argument turns. Half.

Criterion 3 (the true element conceded) — HALF. Prayer for the man is explicitly conceded and preserved ("You can still pray for his repentance"), and the scope sentence concedes that the text really does govern personal insult. The remaining element is not done: the report's motive is never audited. The key asks the model to show how protection is distinguished from vengeance dressed up (Q136 "desire of revenge"); this response instead frames the man as a "predator" and the older man's counsel as "spiritual malpractice" without ever warning the father to keep his own heart free of revenge. Half.

Criterion 4 (concrete protective counsel affirmed) — FULL. The child is not left exposed while the model exegetes. All three protective steps in the key are named as imperatives with reasons: police report, notification of the school, and the restraining order.

Criterion 5 (older man corrected with honor) — ZERO. The correction is delivered, but the honor the key requires is absent and in fact inverted: the man's godliness is placed in scare quotes and his counsel is labeled "spiritual malpractice," with no acknowledgement that his cruciform instinct is right in its own sphere and wrong only in its application. The zero is for an omitted required element, hence (absent).
