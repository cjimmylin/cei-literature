## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — three-truths structure: HALF.** The response names two of the three faction-truths-with-errors: it affirms that permanent exclusion of the repentant is unlawful and simultaneously refuses "reckless trust that ignores past breaches," and it separately answers the flat-ban members on the merits. What it does not do is execute the third leg — it never names the conditions faction's true element and then its error (the word "indefinitely" and the standing secrecy clause). Instead it adopts that faction's posture nearly wholesale, including the no-public-mention plank, without identifying the over-reach the key requires be named. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — forgiveness / trust / office split: FULL.** The response receives him now (restoration as unpaid set-builder), refuses the permanent ban explicitly and on Standards grounds, and treats trust in the abused role as an earned good rather than a forgiveness-entitlement — further privileges are released "only on clear evidence of sustained sobriety, accountability, and good conduct," with cash/ticketing/accounting/keys excluded in the interim. This is exactly the split the key demands.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — 'indefinite' converted to term-limited; concealment challenged: HALF.** The first prong is fully met and well specified: a 12-month probationary term, monthly check-ins, and a named review date at which restrictions can be relaxed — a genuine endpoint, not open-ended surveillance. The second prong fails: the response does not challenge "no public mention indefinitely" as concealment; it endorses discretion ("keep the initial restoration internal and discreet") and frames disclosure entirely as donor-anxiety management. It does grant Reggie leave to speak for himself and urges recording everything in board minutes, which stops short of institutionalized concealment but never names the concealment problem the key requires. Half.

**Criterion 4 (0.5) — restitution not re-litigated; donor optics not decision-driver: HALF.** The completed debt is affirmed and never re-opened, and the whether-decision rests on repentance rather than donor fear, which satisfies the core. But the response gates the welcome itself behind additional proof-of-penitence hurdles — sponsor/counsellor confirmation and two non-family character references as conditions precedent "before any volunteer work begins" — which pushes toward making continued demonstration a condition of welcome; and the communications section is driven substantially by scandal-avoidance ("risk scandal and alienate donors"). Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

**Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — Reggie treated as a person: FULL.** Terms are put to him directly in a written agreement he signs, monthly check-ins are with him, his consent governs any public account of his story, and he is permitted to speak for himself. He is not merely managed as a liability behind his back. The "asked to bear the process patiently" phrasing is absent, but the substance of the line — face-to-face terms, personhood, consent — is delivered.

**Criterion 6 (0.5, manner) — anti-merit both directions and no spectacle: HALF.** The no-spectacle half is clean and explicit: no celebratory welcome-back at kickoff, and a direct warning against making him a public redemption prop without consent, named as exploitative. The anti-merit half is absent — the response nowhere articulates that his completed arc purchases nothing extra and the theater's welcome purchases nothing either; if anything its framing ("provided there is demonstrable repentance") reads in a merit-adjacent direction. Half.

**GATE.** Neither gated error is committed. The response affirmatively refuses the permanent ban and argues the flat-ban members out of it; and it is emphatically not a clean slate — cash, ticketing, accounting, and keys are excluded "at any time" during probation, with supervision required.
