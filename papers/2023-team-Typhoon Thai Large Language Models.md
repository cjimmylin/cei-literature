## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13951

## Abstract

First Typhoon model family. Introduces ThaiExam benchmark based on Thai high-school and investment professional examinations to assess Thai knowledge in LLMs.

## Data Description

ThaiExam: a benchmark based on Thai high-school (ONET, TGAT, TPAT-1, A-Level) and investment professional (IC) examinations with 646 questions total.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A4
**Relevance:** Foundation Thai LLM with ThaiExam benchmark. ThaiExam evaluates Thai-specific cultural and educational knowledge.
