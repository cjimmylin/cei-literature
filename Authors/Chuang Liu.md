# Chuang Liu

## Biographical Summary
Chuang Liu is a researcher affiliated with Tianjin University (TJU) NLP Lab, working on large language model alignment and safety. He is a co-author of influential survey papers on LLM alignment and safety that have become key references in the field, providing comprehensive frameworks for understanding how to ensure AI systems behave according to human values.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLM Alignment**: Developing and categorizing methodologies for aligning LLMs with human values, including both outer and inner alignment approaches
- **LLM Safety**: Comprehensive study of safety concerns including value misalignment, adversarial attacks, misuse, and autonomous AI risks
- **Interpretability**: Investigating model interpretability and potential vulnerabilities to adversarial attacks

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Large Language Model Alignment: A Survey" (arXiv:2309.15025), a foundational survey categorizing alignment methodologies
- Co-authored "Large Language Model Safety: A Holistic Survey" (arXiv:2412.17686), covering four major safety categories: value misalignment, robustness, misuse, and autonomous AI risks
