## Problem

PRISMA 2020 does not strictly apply to this paper's corpus analysis (Study 1) or benchmark meta-evaluation (Study 2) — neither is a systematic *review* synthesizing evidence. Claiming "Modified PRISMA" risks PRISMA-washing: misrepresenting non-systematic-review research under PRISMA framing violates the standard's epistemic contract (exact counts, defined protocol, prospective design). Solution: rebrand as bibliometric-standard flowcharts with distinct labeling for cumulative (Study 1) vs systematic-search (Study 2) pipelines.

## Framing Decision

- **Study 1 figure:** "Corpus Assembly Flowchart" (bibliometric standard, Donthu et al. 2021; Zupic & Cater 2015 for cumulative review)
- **Study 2 figure:** "Benchmark Identification Flowchart" (PRISMA-adjacent, explicit "PRISMA 2020 reporting principles" language per Page et al. 2021)
- Both placed in `supplementary/supp-figures/` as separate single-column figures
- Two distinct captions clarify the methodological distinction

## Visual Design Spec

- **Tool:** matplotlib (`mpl.patches.FancyBboxPatch` for rounded boxes; `mpl.patches.FancyArrowPatch` for arrows). Rationale: reproducibility within Python environment, no external tool dependency.
- **Alternative:** graphviz dot (only if matplotlib pathway becomes too complex; ensure DOT source files committed for transparency).
- **Dimensions:** Single column (88mm = 3.46in)
- **Typography:** Sans-serif (Arial), 8pt body, 9pt node labels
- **Color scheme:** Okabe-Ito or strict grayscale
- **Arrow style:** Solid for "included" path; dashed for "excluded with reasons"
