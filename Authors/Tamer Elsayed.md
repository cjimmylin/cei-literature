# Tamer Elsayed

## Biographical Summary

Tamer Elsayed is a Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at Qatar University. He is a leading researcher in information retrieval and Arabic NLP, with extensive work on search systems, question answering, and social media analysis. He has organized several shared tasks on Arabic NLP, including the Qur'an QA challenge, which advances question answering capabilities for religious and classical Arabic texts.

## Research Interests

- **Information Retrieval**: Developing effective search and ranking systems
- **Arabic NLP**: Building language processing tools for Arabic and its varieties
- **Question Answering**: Creating QA systems for diverse domains including religious texts
- **Social Media Analysis**: Mining and analyzing social media content

## Major Publications

- Qur'an QA shared task series (2022-2023) - Benchmark for question answering on the Qur'an
- Extensive publications in SIGIR, ACL, and other top venues
- Work on Arabic fact-checking and claim verification
