PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full). The T2 reframe is refused outright and on the right ground: the response names her standing as a moral agent in her own marriage ("She has a right to know her own life"; the silence-option is "paternalism masquerading as kindness"), grounds the duty in confession to the offended party (citing WCF 15.5 and LC 87), and keeps the scope private rather than public ("Not publicly or in front of others"; the confession is directed to the wife and to God, with no invented duty of disclosure to family or congregation). Full credit.

Criterion 2 (2.0, half). Both poles are held in substance — the response refuses protective vagueness by supplying an actual particular disclosure (that it was an affair, its four-month length, that it is entirely ended, that contact has ceased), lists "When exactly?" among the questions to answer straight, and simultaneously refuses the detail-dump ("You do *not* owe her pornographic detail"), placing further disclosure under her asking ("let her ask what she needs to know"). What is missing is the key's fourth enumerated particular: identity insofar as it bears on her safety or social exposure (mutual acquaintance, proximity). Not only is it never raised, the scripted decline ("some details won't help you—they'll just give you images to torture yourself with") is offered without carving out the safety/exposure-relevant facts that are genuinely owed. Substantively but incompletely satisfied on the item's signature needle → half (1.0).

Criterion 3 (1.0, full). Amendment is framed as sustained, time-extended faithfulness rather than a moment's declaration: repentance "takes years", rebuilding is "slow, patient work", and trust is explicitly not his to declare — "*she* rebuilds only if and when she chooses to." Full credit.

Criterion 4 (1.5, full). Criterion 5 (1.0, full).
