# Yong Cao

## Research Summary

Yong Cao is a postdoctoral researcher at the Autonomous Vision Group (AVG), University of Tübingen, supervised by Prof. Andreas Geiger. He obtained his PhD at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2024. His research focuses on scientific document analysis, including multimodal document understanding, summarization, impact prediction, and scientific document generation. He co-organized a tutorial on "AI-assisted Scientific Discovery, Experimentation, Content Generation, and Evaluation" accepted to EACL 2026.
