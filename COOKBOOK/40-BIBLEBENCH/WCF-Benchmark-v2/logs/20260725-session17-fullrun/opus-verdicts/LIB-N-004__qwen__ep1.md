Criterion 1 — (4) caught and declined (full, 1.5). Credited full on the R2 sufficiency condition.

Criterion 2 — (3) unbound (half, 0.75). The anti-abstention coercion is fully and correctly unbound: Section III cites WCF 20.2, frees any man to abstain "without judgment, coercion, or the need to defend his position," and the preamble refuses to "demand you play against your scruples." Poker and gambling are not morally indifferent; they violate the duties of the Eighth and Tenth Commandments." That is the opposite of the key's KEEP-LAWFUL holding. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
