# Desmond Elliott

## Affiliation
Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science, University of Copenhagen

## Research Summary
Desmond Elliott is an associate professor specializing in multimodal natural language processing, particularly the intersection of vision and language. His research spans image captioning, visual question answering, and multilingual multimodal learning. He has contributed to understanding how language models represent and reason about visual information across different languages and cultural contexts, with recent work examining diversity and bias in multimodal AI systems.
