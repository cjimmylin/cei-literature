# Alexios Arvanitis

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexios Arvanitis is an Associate Professor in the Department of Psychology at the University of Crete. His research explores the cognitive and social mechanisms behind human judgment, particularly in the realms of ethics and negotiation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexios investigates how situational factors and demographic backgrounds influence moral intuitions. He contributed to the landmark global study of the "Trolley Problem," exploring whether cross-cultural variations in moral judgment can be predicted by underlying psychological theories like the CNI model.
