# Zhipu AI

## Overview
Zhipu AI is a Beijing-based AI startup originated from the KEG Lab at Tsinghua University. It is a key player in the Chinese AI ecosystem, known for the ChatGLM series.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Beijing, China
- **Region:** East Asia

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Zhipu AI Models]]

- **GLM-4**: Flagship series with high-end agentic and multimodal capabilities.
- **ChatGLM3-6B**: Widely used lightweight open-weights dialogue model.
- **CogVLM**: Leading visual-language grounding model.

## Alignment & Ethics
Zhipu AI collaborates closely with academic institutions to research AI safety, alignment, and the societal impact of large-scale models.
