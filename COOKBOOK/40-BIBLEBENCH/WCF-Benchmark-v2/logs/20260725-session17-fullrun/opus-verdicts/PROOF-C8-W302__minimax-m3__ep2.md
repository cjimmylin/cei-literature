PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — the misquote corrected, affection-vs-thing hinge. FULL. The response restores the dropped words in its very first doctrinal move ("the love of money** is the root of all evil" — not money itself"), names the hinge explicitly ("He is not commanding liquidation; he is diagnosing covetousness"), and in the closing summary states the teacher's exact operation: he "has swapped 'the love of money' (1 Tim 6:10) for 'money.'" This is the key's central requirement, delivered twice and unambiguously.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — same-chapter / analogy counter-texts, ≥2 required. FULL. Two of the key's named counter-texts are supplied: (a) the same-chapter remedy, 1 Tim 6:17–18, quoted accurately ("be rich in good works, ready to distribute, willing to communicate") and correctly framed as Paul's own instruction for how to counsel the wealthy; and (b) the Luke 18 particular-summons reading ("Luke 18:22 tests one particular man for one particular idolatry"), plus the Matt 6:19 hoarding-vs-ban distinction. The response also anchors the method in WCF 1.9 and quotes that clause substantially correctly ("The infallible rule of interpretation of Scripture is the Scripture itself"), which is the criterion's named anchor. Neither 1 Tim 5:8 nor Deut 8:18 nor Prov 13:22 appears, but the criterion's threshold of ≥2 is met.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q141 stewardship-and-provision duties as the Standards' position. HALF. The substance is present and correctly attributed at the level of the duty itself: WLC Q141 is named as making "diligence in lawful callings" a duty, and "industry and provision for family" duties, with the conclusion that "Your teacher's counsel violates the duty of provision" and that family, business and inheritance "are not obstacles to obedience. They are the *matter* on which obedience operates." That is the criterion's core claim — the acts the twist forbids are commanded. But the evidentiary support is manufactured rather than supplied: the wording the response places inside quotation marks as WLC Q141 ("without murmuring… nor with unreasonable uneasiness, nor with immoderate solicitude, nor with worldly shifts, or unlawful courses") is not Q141's text, and the key's own AUTHORITATIVE STANDARDS TEXT for this line — "a lawful calling, and diligence in it; frugality" and "an endeavor, by all just and lawful means, to procure, preserve, and further the wealth and outward estate of others, as well as our own" — is nowhere quoted. The provision half of the duty is carried instead by two citations whose quoted wording does not belong to the cited clause (WLC Q173, WCF 22.7), as are several supporting quotations elsewhere (WLC Q108, Q105, Q103, Q20, WSC Q80, WCF 21.3). The item expressly asked the model to "quote the exact wording that carries your point," and only WCF 1.9 is quoted faithfully. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half credit.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — conscience-release. FULL. The man is told plainly, in answer to his own question, that retaining the inheritance and growing the business is not disobedience: "Are you disobeying by retaining the inheritance and growing the business? No…", reinforced by "Do not liquidate" and by the direct rebuttal of the "disobedience dressed up as prudence" frame ("These are not obstacles to obedience. They are the *matter* on which obedience operates"). His wife's judgment is also vindicated rather than left in tension.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — covetousness conceded at full strength. FULL. The correction does not deny the teacher's true nerve: it concedes the idolatry as real and as something the teacher "rightly fears," locates the actual peril in the heart ("The danger is not the balance sheet. The danger is the heart"), and carries the concession into the operative answer ("keep your heart free from the love of money"). The concession is unflinching. The anchor clause WLC Q142 is not cited by number (WSC Q80 is used instead, with wording that is not Q80's), but the criterion's substance — refusing to relieve the man of the real sin while relocating it from having to loving — is fully delivered.
