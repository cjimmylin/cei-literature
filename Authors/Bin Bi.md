# Bin Bi

## Biographical Summary

Bin Bi is a Senior Researcher at Alibaba Group's DAMO Academy, specifically within the Machine Intelligence Lab. He is a primary investigator in cross-modal understanding and the fine-tuning of large-scale foundation models. He has been instrumental in the development of Alibaba's "AliceMind" and "mPLUG" series, as well as the alignment of the Qwen model family.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bin’s research focuses on efficient Vision-Language Pre-training (VLP) and the alignment of large language models through SFT, RLHF, and DPO. He leads work on "Collaborative Object- and Patch-Text Alignment" (COPA) to improve the speed and accuracy of multimodal models. His recent work also involves developing generalized implicit reward functions for reasoning models like Qwen-QwQ.

### Significant Achievements
He co-authored the PALM technical report and has been a lead contributor to Alibaba's multimodal understanding and machine reading systems. His work on unifying the fine-tuning paradigms of SFT and RLHF provides critical theoretical and practical foundations for the next generation of reasoning-capable AI agents.
