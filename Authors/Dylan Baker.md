# Dylan Baker

## Biographical Summary

Dylan Baker is a researcher affiliated with the University of Southern California, working on natural language processing and computational social science. His research focuses on understanding subjective aspects of NLP tasks, particularly how different annotators perceive and evaluate offensive content.

## Research Focus

Baker's work addresses the inherent subjectivity in NLP annotation tasks, especially for content like offensiveness that depends on individual perception. He has contributed to research on disentangling different factors that influence how people perceive offensive language.
