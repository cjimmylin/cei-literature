# Hilla Merhav-Fine

## Research Summary

Hilla Merhav is a Product Manager for NLP Data at Webiks in Tel Aviv, Israel, with extensive experience in linguistics and NLP research. She previously worked as a Linguist at the Israeli Association for Human Language Technology (IAHLT) and held positions at Tel Aviv University, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, and Unit 8200 (Israeli Intelligence Corps). She received her Bachelor of Arts degree from The Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Her research contributions include co-authoring HeQ (Hebrew QA), a large and diverse Hebrew reading comprehension benchmark featuring 30,147 question-answer pairs derived from Hebrew Wikipedia articles and Israeli tech news. The work addresses challenges posed by Hebrew's morphologically rich nature through novel guidelines, controlled crowdsourcing protocols, and revised evaluation metrics.
