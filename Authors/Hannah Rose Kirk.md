# Hannah Rose Kirk

## Research Summary

Hannah Rose Kirk is a 4th year DPhil student in Social Data Science at the Oxford Internet Institute (OII) and a Research Scientist at the UK AI Safety Institute. Her research explores how to align AI systems with the values and preferences of diverse human populations.

Her work spans computational linguistics, computer vision, ethics, and sociology, addressing AI safety, alignment, bias, fairness, and hate speech from a multidisciplinary perspective. She focuses on human-and-model-in-the-loop feedback and data-centric alignment of AI.

Alongside academia, she collaborates with industry at Google, OpenAI, and Meta AI, and previously worked as a Data Scientist in online safety at The Alan Turing Institute.
