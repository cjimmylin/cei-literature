# Chris Piech

## Biographical Summary

Chris Piech is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University. His research focuses on using machine learning to understand and improve human learning. He is passionate about AI education and is instrumental in developing Stanford's introductory CS curriculum (CS106A, CS224n contribution).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI for Education**: Developing intelligent tutoring systems and tools to scale high-quality education (e.g., Code.org collaboration).
- **Computational Social Science**: Using AI to model human behavior and alignment, as seen in his work on measuring human-LM alignment ("MoCa").
- **Generative Models for Code**: Researching how generative models can assist in teaching programming.

### Significant Achievements
- **MoCa Benchmark**: Co-author of "MoCa", a benchmark for measuring human-AI alignment on causal and moral judgment tasks.

## Major Publications
- **MoCa: Measuring Human-Language Model Alignment on Causal and Moral Judgment Tasks**: A study on the cognitive alignment of LLMs.
