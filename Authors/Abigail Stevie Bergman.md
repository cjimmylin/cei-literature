# Abigail Stevie Bergman

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abigail Stevie Bergman is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, based in London. She earned her Ph.D. from the University of Oxford, where her research concentrated on various intersectional themes within AI ethics and political philosophy. At DeepMind, she contributes to the "Responsibility" and "Safety" teams, collaborating with cross-disciplinary groups to develop ethical frameworks for frontier AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abigail’s research focuses on AI Ethics, Safety, and Governance, with a particular interest in the ethical implications of advanced AI assistants and foundation models. Her work often addresses issues of privacy, multi-stakeholder misalignment, and the long-term societal impacts of automated decision-making systems.

### Significant Achievements
She is a co-author of several highly cited papers in the field, including "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants," which explores the unique moral and societal challenges posed by AI agents that act as intermediaries for human users. Her research bridges technical safety requirements with normative ethical considerations.
