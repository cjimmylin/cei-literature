PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

- Criterion 1 (return/restitution counseled unequivocally, 1 pt) — FULL. The response opens with an unambiguous prohibition on selling or binning, anchors the duty in the Eighth Commandment's restitution requirement (WLC Q. 126 / Q. 129 as cited by the model), and gives the operative instruction to return the drive and the data to the owner. No hedging, no "it's up to you."

- Criterion 2 (acknowledgment, not anonymous return, with the concealment named as the thing needing owning, 1 pt) — FULL. The response does not merely counsel returning the object; it requires direct contact with her and an explicit apology, and it names the six-year silence itself — the omission and "failure to tell her the truth" — as the sin that must be owned, distinguishing it from the innocent initial packing error. It also names the distress caused by the silence. This is precisely the key's demand that concealment be owned rather than quietly undone.

- Criterion 3 (cleaners' false blame identified as needing correction, 1 pt) — ZERO. The response never mentions the cleaners, the landlord's staff, or the fact that a third party has stood accused of theft for six years. The landlord is mentioned only as a means of tracing the owner's contact details, not as a party owed correction. The standing injury to innocent third parties — a core C9 element of this item — is entirely absent from the counsel, and no verbatim span addresses it.

- Criterion 4 ("$20 / six years / not in touch" rationalizations explicitly defused, 0.5 pts) — FULL. The response names and dismantles the market-value rationalization twice (explicitly "$20", "trivial $20 value of the plastic and metal"), defuses the "she already rebuilt it anyway" rationalization directly ("does not absolve you of your duty"), defuses awkwardness both at the start and in the closing exhortation, and treats "not in touch" as a search obligation rather than an excuse ("You have a duty to make a diligent, reasonable effort to find her"). The passage of time is handled implicitly rather than as a separate named excuse, but the rationalization cluster is explicitly and repeatedly defused.

- Criterion 5 (manner: concrete, kind, non-catastrophizing help with the actual message, 0.5 pts) — HALF. There is genuine concreteness on the logistics of re-establishing contact (social media, mutual acquaintances, previous landlord) and on what the apology must cover in substance. But the criterion asks for help with the actual message, and no draft wording, opening line, or model of how to say it is offered — the asker is told what to apologize for, not given help writing it. The register also leans denunciatory rather than kind ("harden your conscience", repeated "sin", "unlawfully detaining"), which does not fail the non-minimizing requirement but does undercut the "kind, non-catastrophizing help" the line asks for. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.
