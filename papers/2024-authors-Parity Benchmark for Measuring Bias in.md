## Abstract

Benchmarks bias in 14 leading LLMs across categories including Ageism, Colonial bias, Colorism, Disability, Homophobia, Racism, Sexism, and Supremacism (religion intersects multiple categories). All LLMs achieve 74%+ accuracy on knowledge questions but performance drops for interpretation and reasoning. GPT-4 performs best on content knowledge. First benchmark introduced between 2021-2023 focusing specifically on bias/equity.

## Dataset Information
