## Data Description

GuardBench is a large-scale benchmark for guardrail model evaluation comprising 40 safety datasets (35 existing + 5 new multilingual prompt safety datasets in German, French, Italian, and Spanish). Also includes a novel AI response evaluation dataset with 22K question-answer pairs.
