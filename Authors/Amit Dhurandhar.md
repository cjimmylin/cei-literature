# Amit Dhurandhar

## Research Summary

Amit Dhurandhar is a Principal Research Scientist at IBM Research. His research focuses on explainable AI, trustworthy machine learning, and AI ethics. He has made significant contributions to the field of interpretable machine learning, developing methods for understanding and explaining AI model decisions. His recent work includes developing frameworks for evaluating moral reasoning capabilities in large language models, contributing to the responsible development of AI systems.
