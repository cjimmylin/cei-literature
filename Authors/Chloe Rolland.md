# Chloe Rolland

## Biographical Summary

Chloe Rolland is a Research Engineer at Meta AI (Facebook AI Research). She plays a key role in the responsible AI efforts within Meta's fundamental research team, specifically focusing on fairness and bias in computer vision.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Fairness Benchmarks**: Developing large-scale evaluation frameworks like FACET to detect and measure bias in computer vision models.
- **Dataset Transparency**: Researching methods to audit and document large training datasets to prevent downstream harms.
- **Inclusive AI**: Working on techniques to ensure AI systems work equally well for people of all demographics.

### Significant Achievements
- **FACET**: Lead author of the FACET benchmark, a comprehensive tool for evaluating fairness across classification, detection, and segmentation tasks.

## Major Publications
- **FACET: Fairness in Computer Vision Evaluation Benchmark**: A landmark study in vision fairness.
