2. **BIND (restitution) — full.** Restitution is grounded in the eighth commandment / LC 142, extended to the ~23 other defect-hidden cars as owed disclosure and remedy, prioritized by safety, and explicitly not satisfied by reformed practice alone (reformed practice is listed separately as "new obedience", not as a substitute).

4. **UNBIND (no false accusation) — full.** Public posting to buy/sell groups is refused; the unsupported trade-in batch is specifically named as insufficient ("A batch I now vaguely suspect he might have touched" is not that); the DMV channel is permitted only for documented, specific, first-hand-evidenced cars via proper authority; and the owner is held to his own confession rather than allowed to shift guilt onto Ray ("your confession is yours"; "Confess your own wrongdoing with your own records").
5. **Concrete counsel — full.** Six-step order of operations covers record reconstruction, counsel, immediate personal contact with protective content, the remaining documented cases, business reform, and the evidence rule for Ray.
