# Abhijit Das

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abhijit Das is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Systems at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. He earned his PhD from IIT Kharagpur and has held research positions at Charles University (Prague) and Inria. His work focuses on the intersection of computer vision and human behavior analysis for security applications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dr. Das's research program investigates multimodal machine learning for deceptive behavior detection. He explores how physiological and behavioral signals (vision, audio, thermal) can be integrated to build robust systems for security screening and the identification of malintent.
