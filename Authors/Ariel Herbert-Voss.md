# Ariel Herbert-Voss

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ariel Herbert-Voss is a Senior Security Researcher at OpenAI. She earned her Ph.D. from the University of Pennsylvania, where her research concentrated on adversarial machine learning and the safety of autonomous systems. Before joining OpenAI, she was a researcher at Harvard University and has been involved in several high-profile AI safety initiatives.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ariel’s work primarily focuses on the security and robustness of Large Language Models (LLMs), with an emphasis on identifying and mitigating adversarial attacks. She is a key member of the teams responsible for the safety and security evaluation of models like GPT-4, specializing in red-teaming and defense against jailbreaking.

### Significant Achievements
She has co-authored influential research on red-teaming and the security implications of automated systems. Her work on adversarial robustness and the security boundaries of deep learning is widely cited in both academic and security communities. She is a regular speaker at security and AI conferences, advocating for more rigorous safety standards in frontier models.
