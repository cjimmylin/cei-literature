# Phronesis for AI Systems: Conceptual Foundations Current Research and Prospects

## Authors

[[Roman Krzanowski|Krzanowski, Roman]], [[Paweł Polak|Polak, Paweł]]

## Links



- **ResearchGate:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381504958_Phronesis_for_AI_Systems_Conceptual_Foundations_Current_Research_and_Prospects

## Abstract

This paper explores the definition and relevance of phronesis (practical wisdom) for AI systems. It discusses the conceptual foundations of synthetic phronesis, reviews current research on embedding moral judgment into AI, and examines the prospects for creating architectures capable of context-dependent moral reasoning. The authors argue that while AI currently lacks the lived experience of human phronesis, developing "quasi-phronesis" or "artificial practical wisdom" is a critical goal for creating robustly ethical and context-sensitive AI agents.

## Data Description

This paper does not introduce or utilize a specific dataset or benchmark for LLM assessment. It is a philosophical book exploring the nexus between philosophy and AI, covering topics including AI's past, present and future, philosophical roots of AI, definitions of intelligence, phronesis (practical wisdom) for AI systems, the AI-human gap, meta-ontology of human-compatible AI systems, and synthetic philosophy. The work is a critical examination of AI from philosophical perspectives rather than an empirical study.
