# Binhang Yuan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Binhang Yuan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he leads the Relaxed System Lab. He earned his PhD and Master's degrees from Rice University and conducted postdoctoral research at ETH Zurich in the systems group. He is a specialist in the architectural foundations of large-scale machine learning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Binhang’s research focuses on building scalable distributed systems for the efficient training and inference of foundation models. He leads research into algorithmic and systems-level innovations for trillion-parameter models, specifically targeting high-performance hardware and decentralized training environments. He is an expert in optimizing the memory and compute efficiency of deep learning workloads.

### Significant Achievements
He is a lead creator of **FlashAttention** related projects, including Flash Sparse Attention (FSA), which significantly improves the efficiency of attention mechanisms in LLMs. His work on "Decentralized Training" has enabled collaborative model training across heterogeneous environments. He has received prestigious recognition from top-tier venues like VLDB and SIGMOD.

## Major Publications

- [Decentralized Training of Foundation Models in Heterogeneous Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01288) (2022) - *A key paper on distributed scaling.*
