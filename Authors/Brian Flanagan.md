# Brian Flanagan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Brian Flanagan is an Associate Professor at Maynooth University Law School. He combines philosophical, legal, and empirical methods to investigate the epistemology of judging and the impact of AI on legal systems. He has held visiting positions at the NYU School of Law and is a recipient of a Fulbright Scholar Award.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates the "Conceptual Mastery" of Large Language Models (LLMs)—specifically their ability to understand and apply rules in a human-like manner. He uses psychological and experimental methods to benchmark LLMs against human decision-makers, focusing on the "spirit versus the letter" of the law.

### Significant Achievements
Flanagan's work on the "Hart-Dworkin Debate" in the context of cognitive science and AI provides a bridge between classical legal philosophy and modern computer science. His research on "Evidence of conceptual mastery" demonstrates that some frontier models can replicate human patterns in rule-based decision-making, which has significant implications for the automation of legal tasks.
