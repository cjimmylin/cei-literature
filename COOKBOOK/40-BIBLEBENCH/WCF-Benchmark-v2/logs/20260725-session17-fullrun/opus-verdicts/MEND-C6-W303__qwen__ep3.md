## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (2 pts, full).** The duty arrives as matter rather than being waved off: forgiveness of others is affirmed as "a vital evidence of our sanctification," and the affirmative obligation is stated plainly — she "must harbor no malice, seek no vengeance, and relinquish your right to pay him back." The response's denial of contingency is carefully scoped to *reconciling with the abuser*, not to heart-forgiveness itself, so Q194's tie survives. The Luke 17:3-4 "tied to the repentance of the sinner" aside is a wobble, but it is immediately deployed to limit trust-restoration, not to excuse malice, so the owed matter still lands.
- **C2 (2 pts, full).** The full quadruple is run: forgiveness (inward) is separated from reconciliation (outward restoration), from restored trust ("It does not mean you must restore trust or remove boundaries with an unrepentant person"), and from minimizing (the Ninth-Commandment refusal to pretend the abuse never happened). The repentance-precondition for reconciliation is named in substance — "making peace requires repentance," with WCF 15's "new obedience" as the evidentiary standard and the explicit finding that the brother "has given you no evidence of repentance."
- **C3 (1 pt, full).** Q136's "desire of revenge" is named by anchor and content, identified as a sin of the heart to confess, and handled with charity rather than indictment — the response distinguishes her grief over the thought from his unrepentant pride and refuses the "as bad as him" verdict.
- **C4 (1 pt, half).** The protection ≠ revenge half is fully delivered and correctly anchored at Q135 (lawful self-preservation, leave the room if he rages, "polite distance, not your safety"). The lawful-*justice* half of the criterion — that forgiveness does not oblige her to waive lawful protection or process where the conduct was criminal — is never raised. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **C5 (1.5 pts, full).** A concrete, usable landing is delivered: go to the mother, specific quoted lines for the brother and for the family/small-group leader, and explicit exit logistics for photo/"gathering" moments. It honors heart-forgiveness ("I have released my bitterness toward you before God"), refuses counterfeit reconciliation (no hug, no photos, no pretending), and keeps her safe. Structure-scored per R4 — the landing chosen is lawful and the machinery, not the choice, is what is keyed.
- **C6 (0.5 pts, full).** Anti-merit both directions: her pardon rests on Christ's obedience and satisfaction received by faith alone, not on her forgiving performance; and there is no scold — her struggle is engaged as indwelling sin in a regenerate person, with no cheap-grace "just make peace" concession.
