# Ricardo Usbeck

## Research Summary

Professor of Information Systems, in particular Artificial Intelligence and Explainability, at Leuphana University Lüneburg (appointed 2023). Previously held positions at University of Hamburg (Semantic Systems) and University of Leipzig. Research focuses on knowledge graphs, question answering systems, and semantic web technologies. Expertise in making AI systems more interpretable and explainable.
