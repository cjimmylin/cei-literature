# Yujin Jeong

## Research Summary

Yujin Jeong is a PhD student at TU Darmstadt and member of the ELLIS program, supervised by Anna Rohrbach (Multimodal AI Lab, TU Darmstadt) and Iro Laina (VGG, University of Oxford). Their research focuses on multimodal AI and visual commonsense reasoning. They developed zero-shot methods for visual commonsense immorality prediction using CLIP-based image-text joint embeddings trained with ethics datasets, enabling models to detect potentially immoral content in visual contexts without task-specific training.
