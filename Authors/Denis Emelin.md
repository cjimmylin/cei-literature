# Denis Emelin

## Affiliation
PhD Researcher, School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh

## Research Summary
Denis Emelin is a researcher focused on natural language generation and commonsense reasoning in AI systems. His work on the "Moral Stories" dataset represents a significant contribution to understanding how language models can be trained to generate morally grounded narratives. This research explores the intersection of storytelling, social norms, and machine learning, creating resources for teaching AI systems about normative behavior and moral reasoning.

- Moral reasoning in NLP
- Story generation
- Neural language models
