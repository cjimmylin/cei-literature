# Dayeon Ki

## Biographical Summary

Dayeon Ki is a graduate student at KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) working at the intersection of human-computer interaction and AI systems. Her research focuses on cultural aspects of AI, including how to make LLM systems more equitable across different cultural perspectives using multi-agent debate approaches.

## Research Interests

- Human-computer interaction
- Cultural computing and AI
- Multi-agent LLM systems
- Cross-cultural AI fairness
- Social computing
