# Jianchao Ji

## Research Summary

Researcher at Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey. Research interests include large language models, recommendation systems, information retrieval, data augmentation, knowledge graph embedding, and graph neural networks. Lead author on MoralBench (published July 2025), the first comprehensive dataset specifically curated to probe the moral dimensions of LLM outputs and measure moral reasoning capabilities.
