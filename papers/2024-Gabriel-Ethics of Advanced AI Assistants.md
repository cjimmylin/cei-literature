## Abstract

The paper examines opportunities alongside ethical and societal challenges posed by advanced AI assistants—defined as "artificial agents with natural language interfaces, whose function is to plan and execute sequences of actions on behalf of a user." Key topics covered include value alignment, safety, user-level concerns like manipulation and privacy, societal impacts including misinformation and equity, and evaluation frameworks. The work concludes with actionable recommendations for multiple stakeholder groups.

## Topics Covered

1. **Value Alignment**: How to align AI assistants with human values
2. **Safety**: Preventing harmful outputs and behaviors
3. **User-Level Concerns**: Manipulation, persuasion, anthropomorphism, trust, privacy
4. **Societal Impacts**: Misinformation, equity, labor displacement
5. **Evaluation Frameworks**: How to assess AI assistant ethics
