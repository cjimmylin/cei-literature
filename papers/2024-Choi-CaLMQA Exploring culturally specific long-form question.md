## Abstract

51.7K culturally specific questions across 23 languages. Collected translation-free from community forums and native speakers. LLMs make more factual errors on culturally specific questions than culturally agnostic ones. Includes Wolof among low-resource languages. Evaluates culturally specific knowledge which directly relates to values assessment. Translation-free methodology ensures cultural authenticity.
