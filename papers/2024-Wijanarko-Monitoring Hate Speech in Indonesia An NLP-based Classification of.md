## Abstract

NLP-based hate speech classifier for texts targeting vulnerable groups in Indonesia (Shia, Ahmadiyyah, Christians, LGBTQ+, Chinese Indonesians, people with disabilities). Deployed as monitoring dashboard for Indonesian elections.

## Data Description

IndoToxic2024 is a multi-label hate speech dataset of 27,228 annotated Indonesian social media texts targeting seven vulnerable groups, constructed via keyword-based scraping and annotated by 19 annotators including minority group members.
