# Nihar Sahoo

## Research Summary

Nihar R. Sahoo is a PhD student at IIT Bombay, India, advised by Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharyya. His research focuses on Bias and Fairness in NLP, Ethical AI, and Explainability. His PhD is funded by the Prime Minister Research Fellowship. He has served as a reviewer for EMNLP 2022, ICON 2022, EMNLP 2023, AAAI 2023, and LREC-COLING 2024.
