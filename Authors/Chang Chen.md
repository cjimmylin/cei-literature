# Chang Chen

## Biographical Summary
Chang Chen is a researcher working on multilingual AI safety and natural language processing. He is a co-author of significant research on the multilingual safety of large language models, collaborating with researchers at Tencent AI Lab, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and other institutions.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Multilingual AI Safety**: Investigating how safety alignment in LLMs fails to transfer across languages, with particular attention to non-English language safety.
- **Cross-lingual Generalization**: Developing methods to improve safety alignment transfer across languages.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "All Languages Matter: On the Multilingual Safety of Large Language Models" (ACL Findings 2024)
- Contributed to building XSafety, the first multilingual safety benchmark for LLMs, covering 14 safety issues across 10 languages
- Research demonstrated that LLMs produce significantly more unsafe responses for non-English queries
- Co-developed prompting method that reduces unsafe responses by 42% for non-English queries
