## Abstract

First benchmark measuring national alignment between LLMs and South Korea. Contains 4K social value and 6K common knowledge multiple-choice questions. Ground truth from large-scale survey of 6,174 South Koreans. Social value alignment evaluates nation-specific social values; common knowledge alignment captures basic knowledge related to the nation.

## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_03
**Relevance:** Core Korean cultural values alignment benchmark. Directly measures how well LLMs align with Korean social values through survey-grounded evaluation. Government-approved dataset.
