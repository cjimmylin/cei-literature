This is a Chinese government policy document (draft for public comment) establishing a standards framework for AI safety governance in the industrial and information technology sector. It covers seven areas: governance capability, infrastructure security, network security, data security, algorithm/model security, application security, and enabling security. No LLMs are evaluated; this is a regulatory standards-building guide with short-term (1-2 year) and long-term (3-5 year) implementation targets.
