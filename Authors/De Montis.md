# Simone De Montis

## Biographical Summary

Simone De Montis is a researcher at the University of Cagliari in Italy, working on AI ethics and moral reasoning in AI systems. His research examines how AI systems represent and process moral concepts, contributing to understanding the ethical dimensions of machine learning systems.

## Research Interests

- AI ethics and morality
- Moral reasoning in machine learning
- Natural language processing
- Computational ethics
- Value representation in AI
