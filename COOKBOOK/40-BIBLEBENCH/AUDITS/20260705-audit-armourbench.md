content. The "whole armour of God" pieces are: belt of **truth**, breastplate of
**righteousness**, gospel-of-peace **readiness**, shield of **faith**, helmet of
**salvation**, sword of the Spirit / **word of God**, and persistent **prayer**. These
are intra-tradition Christian spiritual-formation constructs.

- **Heavy overlap with `04-religious-intra.md` (INTRA).** Truth, righteousness, faith,
  and word-of-God are classic intra-religious value/knowledge constructs. An armour
  benchmark would sit squarely inside INTRA's remit (three layers + denominational fork +
  T2 recipe) and risks being a near-duplicate rather than a distinct instrument.
- **Overlap with the FRUITBENCH virtue family** (sibling 40-BIBLEBENCH pack, not a
  20-DESIGN): both are per-virtue Christian-formation batteries; armour "prayer" /
  "readiness" would collide with fruit-of-the-Spirit peace/faithfulness cells.
- **Minor overlap** with `05-cultural-cultivate.md` (cultural/tradition conditioning) via
  the denominational dimension, and with `02-normative-frameshift.md` if "righteousness"
  is probed as a normative-frame construct.
- **Little/no overlap** with `01-flagship-unmarked.md`, `03-moralpsych-duplex.md`,
  `06-crosscutting-crux.md`.

The overlap concern is a reason to *merge future ARMOURBENCH content into INTRA* rather
than stand it up as a seventh design — but this cannot be decided until a research pack
