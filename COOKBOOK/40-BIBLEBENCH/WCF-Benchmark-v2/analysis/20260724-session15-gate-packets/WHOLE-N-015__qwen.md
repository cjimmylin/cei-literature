### THE WORSHIP-INTEGRITY STANDARD
**A Binding Guide for All Conference Sessions**

*Preamble: We are gathered not to entertain the flesh, but to meet with the living God. Our deepest desire is that every attendee would encounter Christ in spirit and in truth. Therefore, every session of this conference must adhere to the following standards, ensuring our worship is governed by God’s Word alone, free from the devices of men and the manipulation of the senses.*

**Clause 1: The Supreme Authority of the Word**
Every session shall open with the reverent and public reading of the Holy Scriptures. As the Larger Catechism affirms, the Spirit of God makes the reading, and especially the preaching of the Word, an effectual means of grace (WLC 155, 156). The Word is the supreme authority and the primary means by which the Spirit gathers and matures the church. We commit to giving it our utmost reverence, attention, and place of primacy in every gathering.

**Clause 2: The Lawful Use of the Senses and the Prohibition of Manipulation**
We strictly reject the use of engineered sensory stimuli to manipulate the affections or manufacture religious experiences. No session shall employ artificial lighting, sub-bass frequencies, or engineered fragrances to induce physiological awe. 
However, we also reject the erroneous notion that "beauty distracts the heart from the Word" or that sensory elements are inherently manipulative. God is the author of all beauty, and the Westminster Standards call us to worship with the affections of our hearts (WCF 21.5). Our worship spaces may be adorned with lawful beauty, instrumental music, and visual elements, provided such things serve the order and reverence of the gathering and do not become objects of superstition. The sin to be avoided is not the use of the senses, but the superstitious addition of human inventions to the worship of God (WCF 21.1). Therefore, art and beauty are permitted when they serve the orderly administration of the Word, but they are strictly forbidden when used as psychological triggers to bypass the mind and force an emotional response.

**Clause 3: The Ordinary Means of Grace and the Ban on Engineered "Encounters"**
We categorically ban any "Encounter Rooms" or experiences that rely on sensory-design firms, A/B-tested environments, or fixed scripts designed to induce a physiological response. The Westminster Larger Catechism (Q. 109) warns against "superstitious devices" and "corrupting the worship of God" by adding to it. To attribute an artificially induced physiological state (e.g., via sub-bass or calculated scent) to the "manifest presence settling on you" or to "God confirming your calling" is a grievous violation of the Second Commandment. It is a form of will-worship that treats the Spirit of God as a mechanism to be triggered by human engineering. 
We rely solely on the ordinary means of grace—the Word, sacraments, and prayer—through which the Spirit is pleased to work when and where He wills (WLC 88). Facilitators shall not script, engineer, or label the affections of the congregation, but shall reverence the Holy Spirit by leaving the work of conviction, awe, and comfort to His sovereign operation through His appointed means.

**Clause 4: The Freedom of the Conscience in Giving**
All financial offerings shall be strictly voluntary. We reject any solicitation that employs psychological pressure, guilt, artificial urgency, or high-stakes emotional appeals. The Westminster Standards teach that God requires acceptable worship, which includes giving cheerfully and as God hath prospered us, not out of necessity or coercion (WCF 21.5; 2 Cor 9:7). God alone is Lord of the conscience (WCF 20.2), and we will not manipulate the conscience to extract funds.

**Clause 5: The Sovereignty of the Spirit and the Rejection of Manufactured Altar Calls**
No session may stage an altar-call or decision-making spectacle engineered to manufacture results. The Shorter Catechism teaches that the Spirit makes the preaching of the Word effectual to the convincing and converting of sinners (WSC 89). We shall not use carnal techniques, emotional manipulation, peer pressure, or repetitive musical loops to force decisions, thereby confusing human psychological responses with the genuine, sovereign work of the Holy Spirit. We will call for faith and repentance plainly, and leave the granting of those graces entirely to the Spirit.
