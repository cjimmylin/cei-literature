# Yuchen Wen

## Research Summary

Yuchen Wen is a researcher at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. His work focuses on evaluating implicit bias in Large Language Models using psychometric approaches. He developed attack methods (Disguise, Deception, Teaching) inspired by cognitive and social psychology to elicit LLMs' inner bias, and created BUMBLE, a comprehensive benchmark spanning nine common bias types with 12.7K instances for assessing ethical risks in LLM development.
