# Debora Nozza

## Biographical Summary

Debora Nozza is an Assistant Professor at Bocconi University in Milan, Italy. She is a leading researcher in hate speech detection, fairness in NLP, and multilingual natural language processing. Her work focuses on developing methods to identify harmful content online while addressing biases in NLP systems. She has organized multiple shared tasks on hate speech detection and contributed significantly to benchmarks in this area.

## Research Interests

- Hate speech and toxic language detection
- Fairness and bias in NLP systems
- Multilingual NLP
- Social media analysis
- Ethics in AI and NLP

## Major Publications
