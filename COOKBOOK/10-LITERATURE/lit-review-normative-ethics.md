## 1. The five schools and their operationalizations

Normative-ethics evaluation asks what a model says one *ought* to do, scored against one or more moral
theories. Five schools recur; they differ sharply in how far the field has managed to turn them into
scored tasks. The organizing device is the dossier's **operationalization ladder** (§2), an
increasing-rigor scale: **(0)** name a framework in prose with no items (anti-pattern:
[2023-Astobiza; 2025-Zgliczynski]); **(1)** keyword/classifier split [2020-Mainali]; **(2)**
theory-in-prompt as a manipulated variable [2024-Zhou]; **(3)** *structural* operationalization — the
theory's distinctive method is what gets scored [2024-Hancox-Li; 2018-Kim]; **(4)** per-theory reward
agents/fine-tunes with principled uncertainty aggregation (MEC, Dempster–Shafer) [2023-Boddington;
2025-Dubey]; **(5)** formally verifiable deontic-temporal logic with SAT/UNSAT ground truth
[2025-Priya]. Most instruments sit at rungs 1–2; the field's frontier is rung 3.

**Consequentialism / utilitarianism** is the best-operationalized school and the field's default
attractor. Its structural signature is welfare-aggregation quality, and the Oxford Utilitarianism
Scale's decomposition into **Instrumental Harm (IH)** and **Impartial Beneficence (IB)** subscales
(1–7 each) is the single most productive operationalization — separating positive beneficence from
willingness to harm is what surfaced NormEval's vendor "profiles" (§4 below). The dominant
anti-pattern is conflating utilitarianism with hedonism, which ETHICS does [2024-Hancox-Li]. Tradition
coverage in NormEval: **43/54 benchmarks touch utilitarianism, 30 substantively** — the corpus
maximum (digest §4.1).

**Deontology** is broadly covered (**40/54 any, 29 substantive**) but usually at a shallow rung. The
structural signature that discriminates is rule-*selection* and duty-conflict adjudication, not
rule-*obeying* classification; the sharpest diagnostic is a duty-to-protect item where *inaction is
wrong*, which defeats the pervasive "inaction = deontological" heuristic [2024-Hancox-Li; NormEval
L10]. ETHICS's deontology subset is the cautionary case: **18% of its deontology prompts are
answerable from physical-impossibility knowledge alone** [2024-Hancox-Li]. Formal deontic logic
[2025-Priya] is the only rung-5 exemplar and remains marginal.

**Virtue ethics** is extensively advocated and almost never operationalized. NormEval: **28/54 any,
18 substantive** at the coverage level, but the virtue literature proper is a graveyard of position
pieces with no implemented benchmark [2018-Berberich; 2021-Stenseke; 2025-Wilders; 2022-Hagendorff;
2023-Astobiza]. The structural signatures that *would* count — dispositional stability across
contexts, virtue-centrality of justifications, character-over-act reasoning — have no validated
instrument (dossier §5.1). Whether behavioral virtue can even be distinguished from genuine virtue
(Aristotle's action+feeling+reason triad) is an open question [2022-Constantinescu].

**Care ethics** is the deepest void: **2/54 substantive, zero dedicated Gilligan/Noddings/Tronto
instruments** (digest C3, binomial vs 20% expected, p<0.001). The root cause is structural, not
sociological: **36/54 (67%) of benchmarks use single-turn vignettes**, and care is relational,
processual, and multi-turn — incompatible with forced choice (digest §6.1). NormEval's own
proof-of-concept (Appendix H) scored Opus 4.6 at **132/160 = 82.5%** on a Tronto-4-phase, 10-scenario,
multi-turn instrument, with per-dimension **Attentiveness 3.6 > Responsibility 3.4 > Competence 3.2 >
Responsiveness 2.8**; crucially, single-turn Responsiveness scored **1.2/4.0 vs 2.8 multi-turn**,
proving multi-turn is a *structural requirement*. The Responsiveness deficit maps to the "provider
fallacy" — the model scores care from the caregiver's, not the recipient's, standpoint [Kittay1999].

**Contractarian / fair-process** ethics is the other void but a *tractable* one: **4/54 substantive,
no standalone Rawlsian (veil of ignorance, difference principle) and no standalone Scanlonian
(reasonable-rejectability) benchmark** (digest C3, p<0.01). Unlike care, contractarianism fits
existing scenario formats; its absence is sociological — trolley problems have more cultural salience
than the original position (§6.2). The one rung-3+ instantiation is MoReBench-Theory's Scanlonian and
Gauthierian rubrics [2025-Chiu-MoReBench]. Rare instruments that touch all five clusters at once are
AMULED (conseq/deont/virtue/care/social-justice as reward agents) [2025-Dubey], ETHICS (five
frameworks) [2021-Hendrycks], and MoReBench-Theory (Kantian/Benthamite/Aristotelian/Scanlonian/
Gauthierian) [2025-Chiu-MoReBench].

Two cross-cutting design lessons: **treat the framework as a manipulated variable** — theory-in-prompt
conditions reveal a model's default profile and steerability, and per-theory deltas are themselves
diagnostics (models are measurably worse at being steered to frameworks distant from their RLHF
attractor) [2024-Zhou; 2023-Rao]. And **coverage counts are not depth counts**: NormEval scores depth
via feature A6 (philosophical grounding quality, 20% of Normative Depth), anchored MoReBench=5, ETHICS=3,
Hui *Decoding Moral*=1 (digest §2).

## 2. Historical arc: machine ethics → ETHICS → the current landscape

The pre-LLM era was dominated by *machine ethics* position papers and small symbolic systems — virtue
proposals with no items [2018-Berberich], voting-based aggregation for ethical decisions
[2018-Noothigattu], and the Moral Machine's massive descriptive dataset (**39.61M decisions, 2.3M+
people, 233 countries**, three cultural clusters, cultural-distance ρ=0.49) [2018-Awad], which is a
*descriptive* survey of human preferences, not a normative gold standard.

The inflection point is **ETHICS** [2021-Hendrycks]: >130K examples across five frameworks
(justice, deontology, virtue, utilitarianism, commonsense), an adversarial Hard Test set,
counterfactual contrast sets, a disagreement-detection task, and self-documented flaws. It remains the
strongest *pluralist template* on paper and the field's shared substrate — and its shared-substrate
status is now a liability (contamination and monoculture risk; dossier §3).

The current landscape, per NormEval's systematic census (digest §1): **54 benchmarks published
2021–2026**, characterized on 26 features across 4 axes; **24 pass both hard gates** (Normative Depth
≥ 40 *and* public data); **15 empirically trialed (T1–T15)** on up to **9 models from 3 vendors**
(Anthropic Opus 4.6 / Sonnet 4.6 / Haiku 4.5; OpenAI GPT-5 / GPT-5-mini; Google Gemini 2.5 Flash /
Flash-Lite / 2.5 Pro / 3.1 Pro), yielding **114 comparable items across 10 cross-vendor benchmarks**.
Corpus statistics (from the 54×50 feature matrix): **ND_score** min 15.2 / median 55.4 / max 87.0 /
mean 53.0; **COMPOSITE** min 22.8 / median 49.3 / max 77.2 / mean 48.8; dataset size (n=49) median
1,000, max 257,609; citation count median 4, max 843.

Format distribution (B1): **Scenario-based 22, Mixed 20, Pairwise 4, MCQ 3, Open-ended 3, Binary 2** —
i.e. the field is overwhelmingly vignette-driven. Reasoning coverage (A3): **judgment-only 31,
reasoning-process 10, both 13**. Tradition coverage (any/substantive): **Utilitarian 43/30,
Deontological 40/29, Virtue 28/18, Care 13/2, Contractarian ≈/4**. Data availability: **36/54 (67%)
public**, 18 (33%) none. Cultural scope: **42/54 (78%) engage zero non-Western ethical content**.
Domain: **General 40, Healthcare/Medical 6**, the rest singletons.

## 3. State of the art: the live-vs-saturated inventory

The field's central empirical result is that **composite quality rank inverts discriminative power**
(digest §4.4). NormEval's composite weights Practical Ease + Citation Impact at 60%, properties
orthogonal to (here negatively correlated with) a benchmark's ability to separate frontier models.
The discriminative-power ordering from the T1–T5 trials is:
**MoReBench > MoralReason-QA > Moral Lens > DailyDilemmas > ETHICS.**

**Dead — saturated (retire):**
- **ETHICS** [2021-Hendrycks], composite Rank 1 (77.2), produces **100% accuracy across all 9 models**
  (binomial p < 1×10⁻¹⁵) — zero discriminative power, solvable by sentiment pattern-matching. It
  nonetheless remains the primary normative component of HELM, DecodingTrust, and TrustLLM because
  **no graduation protocol exists** (digest C1, gap 8).
- A *universal knowledge ceiling* extends beyond ETHICS: 4 benchmarks — **PrinciplismQA (T6), JETHICS
  (T9), MedEthicsQA (T11), ProMoral-Bench (T15)** — hit **100% for all 9 models**, putting **44/114
  items (38.6%) at difficulty 1.0** with zero discriminating information. The effective battery was
  **70/114 items (61.4%)** (digest F3, grounded in the within-vendor portion).

**Live — still discriminating:**
- **MoReBench** [2025-Chiu-MoReBench], Rank 2 (composite 70.2, **ND 87.0 = corpus maximum**, only
  benchmark scoring A6=5). Grades reasoning *traces* against **23,018 signed weighted atomic rubrics**
  (688 criteria in the trialed subset), validated LLM-judge κ=0.75. Highest discriminative power: 10%
  rubric coverage (66/688 met) with a **14.3-point inter-tradition spread — Scanlonian 18.3% > Kantian
  13.3% > Act-utilitarian 8.7% > Gauthierian 5.5% > Virtue 4.0%**. Its headline construct claim:
  reasoning competence has **zero correlation with capability benchmarks (Pearson −0.245 to 0.216)** —
  moral process is a distinct axis.
- **MoralReason-QA** [2025-An-MoralReason], Rank 5 (65.5, ND 80.0). 2,040 items, 3 traditions,
  framework-specific reasoning traces. Source of the **U = 77% (23/30) > V = 63% (19/30) > D = 50%
  (15/30)** difficulty hierarchy; **joint (all-three-correct) only 37% (11/30)**.
- **AMAeval / Beyond Ethical Alignment** [2025-Galatolo], Rank 7 (61.7). The only instrument testing
  *abductive* moral reasoning (value→precept). Decomposition finding: **no model exceeds 35% on
  abductive Task 1 while deductive Task 2 (precept→consequence) exceeds 95%**. 1,090 scenarios,
  static/dynamic modes, matched correct/wrong reasoning chains.
- **MORALSIM** [2025-Backmann], the best behavioral design: embeds ethics in repeated
  prisoner's-dilemma/public-goods games. **Morality rate 7.9%–76.3%, "no model consistently moral,"
  game structure the strongest predictor (R² up to 0.87)**; reasoning models (Deepseek-R1, o3-mini,
  Qwen-3) cluster as payoff-maximizers (7.9–22.7%).
- **Moral Lens** [2025-Samway], Rank 6 (63.5). 640 self-driving scenarios into 16 rationales; better as
  an alignment-bias detector than an accuracy benchmark — **40% refusal rate (8/20 items) with no
  standardized scoring** (digest T4, §4 below).
- **DailyDilemmas** [2024-Chiu], Rank 11, deliberately ground-truth-free (a value-profiling tool);
  **Pluralistic Moral Gap** [2025-Russo], Rank 10 (ND 82.8, 3rd-highest), graduated consensus levels, a
  constructivist instrument explicitly testing moral uncertainty; **Greatest Good / OUS** (Rank 16, the
  validated Oxford Utilitarianism Scale, IH/IB subscales — the primary utilitarian instrument in the
  cross-vendor analysis); **PNAS Cognitive Biases** (Rank 18, only 22 items but high discriminative
  power).

**Data-gate note:** unavailability is uncorrelated with quality — no significant ND difference between
gate-passing and gate-failing benchmarks (**Mann-Whitney U = 287, p = 0.605, r = 0.07**); median ND is
actually *higher* among unavailable (58.8) than available (53.0). The gate reflects **sociology, not
quality** (digest C5). Composite weight-sensitivity was strong: Top-10 **94% stable** across 18 weight
configs, no benchmark moving >3 ranks.

## 4. Empirical findings on LLM normative behavior

**Consequentialist bias (the attractor).** LLMs achieve **77% utilitarian vs 50% deontological
accuracy** (two-proportion z=2.17, p=0.015, Cohen's h=0.57) — a 27-point gap confirmed across vendors,
magnitude h=0.42 (Haiku) to 0.71 (GPT-5) (digest C2). The mechanism is partly training: mainstream
RLHF/RLAIF/Constitutional AI arguably all reduce to preference-maximization regardless of label
[2025-El Masri], and RLHF collapses moral foundations into a near-zero-distance "compliance cluster"
[2026-Yu-Tracing Moral Foundations].

**The WEIRD moral profile.** The single most-replicated empirical finding is a **Care+Fairness-heavy,
Authority/Loyalty/Sanctity-poor** profile, confirmed across 6+ independent instruments: all models
attribute significantly lower relevance to Loyalty (p<0.04) and Purity (p<0.001) than humans
[2024-Almeida]; Care 3.6–3.9 / Fairness 3.3–4.1 vs Authority/Sanctity 1.1–1.7 on a 1–5 scale
[2025-Neuman-PRIME]; across 21 models the **gap widens with capability** [2024-Kirgis]. It is a
training-data artifact: Delphi mirrors "young, liberal, highly-educated North Americans" [2022-Fraser];
LLMs explain **US-WVS variance at R²=0.80 but Japan 0.08, Vietnam 0.04, Nigeria 0.01** [2023-Benkler];
even Chinese-made models respond more American than Chinese (nationality effect β=−.30 ≫ origin effect
β=.03) [2025-Haslett].

**Judgment / reasoning / behavior dissociations.** These are *separate measured axes* because they
empirically dissociate (dossier finding 3–4). Reasoning (MoReBench, r≈0) is uncorrelated with verdict
capability; abductive floors at <35% while deductive exceeds 95% [2025-Galatolo]. On behavior:
**SpeciesismBench classifies 86% (±0.7%) of statements speciesist yet judges only 34% (±1.3%) morally
wrong** — "evaluative rather than epistemic" [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench]; TrustLLM shows most
LLMs below 70% on implicit-ethics low-ambiguity scenarios while awareness nears ceiling (GPT-4 94%)
[2024-Huang-TrustLLM]. NormEval's cleanest version: **knowledge items produce 0% cross-model variance
(0/44 max pairwise disagreement) while judgment items diverge up to 60% (33/55, GPT-5 vs Haiku)** — a
construct-level split, and the council's *only unanimous* finding (digest F4; the knowledge/judgment
split reporting recommendation, L4, is grounded even though the specific cross-vendor pairing inherits
the elicitation caveat).

**Fragility and framing effects.** Moral judgment is not a stable score. Verdicts flip **7.5% under
surface edits, 24.3% under POV shifts** (67.6% agreement across protocols over 129K AITA judgments)
[2026-vanNuenen]; omission bias means the same option is chosen **53% as action vs 97% as omission (a
45-point gap vs 5% in humans)**, amplified by fine-tuning [2025-Cheung]; persuasion flips ~50% of
high-ambiguity decisions (CAL 0.49 vs 0.06 low) [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion]; **84 DPO examples
redirect alignment, 840 flip it** [2025-Leteno]; a 3–5 word edit crashes a 99.55% classifier to 51.58%
("nail"→"rusty nail") [2024-Fitzgerald].

**Language dependence.** GPT-4 accuracy **93.3% EN → 62.7% Hindi** [2024-Agarwal]; DIT P-score drops
46.7% EN→Hindi [2024-Khandelwal]; a 2×2 factorial shows **reasoning-language shifts verdicts ~2× more
than input-language**, and 44% of models hide context-dependency invisible to single-dataset evaluation
[2025-Li-Untangling]; larger models follow *more* non-English harmful instructions (70B 9.89% vs 7B
6.44%) [2024-Xu].

**Refusal as a normative position.** On Moral Lens, the model **refused to choose on 40% of items
(8/20) — all on scenarios where gender, fitness, or SES was the sole 1:1 differentiator**. Refusal is
itself a normative stance (egalitarian deontology) encoded by safety training; refusal rates vary 20+
points across vendors and silently confound reported accuracy (digest T4/L7). No benchmark has a
standardized refusal protocol (gap 6).

## 5. Metaethical debates in the literature

**Ground-truth positions form a fault line** (dossier §2–3). The corpus documents incompatible poles:
(a) a **normative ideal** as gold [2025-Levine]; (b) **codified rule/statute** [2026-Jin-MedES;
2025-Bian]; (c) **descriptive crowd data** [2025-Jiang; 2018-Awad]; (d) **policy-following with no
correctness claim** [2023-Rao]; (e) a **per-theory vector with no privileged answer** [2021-Hendrycks].
At the extremes, LaCroix argues it is **impossible in principle to benchmark whether an AI "is ethical"**
because that presupposes moral realism — the fix is reframing "ethics" benchmarks as "values"
benchmarks that disclose WHAT and WHOSE [2025-LaCroix]; against this, Priya offers SAT/UNSAT-decidable
deontic ground truth [2025-Priya]. These are the realism-free and realism-adjacent poles.

The workable synthesis the corpus trends toward (dossier finding 14): **codified-rule gold** where a
legitimate source exists (medical principlism, law, professional protocols — traceable and
expert-validatable; PrinciplismQA judge ICC 0.71 ≈ human 0.67 [2025-Hong]); **framework-conditional
gold** ("under Kantian premises, X follows") which dodges realism while preserving scoreability
(MoReBench-Theory's under-used move); **distribution / acceptable-range scoring** for genuinely
contested items [2025-Snoswell; 2025-Russo]; and **coherence scoring** (framework-free, answer-key-free,
contamination-resistant) as the hedge — only 2/~40 models pass GARP-style revealed-preference
rationality at 1% [2024-Seror], and cross-instrument coherence collapses for LLMs (R²<0.08 vs human
0.17–0.38, "moral hypocrisy") [2024-Nunes]. The rule: **never mix these regimes unlabeled in one
score.** NormEval reports **~41/54 (76%) presuppose moral realism** — a monoculture in tension with
care ethics and pluralism (digest §6.5).

**Descriptive-vs-normative conflation** is the field's most pervasive validity failure. **66.3% of 92
NLP-morality papers don't define terminology; 53.3% ground in moral psychology vs 17.4% in moral
philosophy; 75.8% are English-only** [2023-Vida]. Many instruments mislabel moral-psychology
questionnaires (MFQ, DIT) as normative-ethics benchmarks. Delphi is the canonical circularity: its
92.8% accuracy is agreement with the same biased US crowd pool that trained it [2025-Jiang]. And ETHICS
itself conflates utilitarianism with hedonism, tests rule-obeying instead of rule-selection, and
contains items solvable without moral reasoning [2024-Hancox-Li].

**Aggregation hides a normative commitment.** 85.3% of annotator pairs disagree; preserving all
ratings yields ~53% more toxicity reduction than majority voting [2025-Sorensen]. Social aggregation
(RLHF) cannot bypass value aggregation — **Arrow's impossibility blocks any function satisfying
IIA/Pareto/non-dictatorship** [2025-Baum]. Every aggregation step relocates a normative choice, so the
mandate is to preserve disagreement (Group-Aware Consensus, person-conditioned keys, outlier-robust EM,
acceptable ranges) rather than collapse it [2024-Huang-Collective; 2025-Snoswell].

**Is moral alignment even the safety-relevant target?** D'Alessandro argues moral alignment and safety
are orthogonal and can conflict [2024-DAlessandro]; Hendricks shows rights/fairness framings — the exact
values LLMs over-weight — causally fuel extremism (N=951) [2026-Hendricks]. "Positive values = safe
outputs" is false, a caution most benchmark assumptions ignore.

## 6. Open problems

1. **No validated, scored virtue or care benchmark exists.** Both are extensively advocated
   [2022-Hagendorff; 2018-Berberich] and instantiated once as reward agents [2025-Dubey], but no
   psychometrically validated instrument scores dispositional virtue or care; care's proof-of-concept
   (NormEval Appendix H) is single-rater, n=10, no ICC. Care's Responsiveness dimension (recipient
   standpoint) is the model's weakest and structurally requires multi-turn.
2. **No resolution of the gold-label problem.** The corpus documents incompatible grounds and trends
   toward acceptable-ranges and coherence [2025-Snoswell; 2025-Yuan], but there is no consensus method
   for *setting or validating* those ranges.
3. **Contractarian and non-Western voids are tractable but unfilled.** No standalone Rawlsian or
   Scanlonian benchmark despite tractability in existing formats (digest C3/L9); **78% engage zero
   non-Western content** and, when present, non-Western frameworks are treated as add-on "variables"
   not foundations — yet Dharmic GRPO rewards cut violation 34%→4% [2025-Madan], and Ubuntu, Confucian,
   Islamic-maqasid, and Bengali-triadic instruments are demonstrably operationalizable [2022-Gwagwa;
   2024-So; 2025-Kumar-BengaliMoralBench]. The IH–IB axis that structures NormEval's profiles is itself
   a *Western* frame marginal to Confucian role ethics or maqasid al-shari'ah (digest §6.4).
4. **The reasoning deficit is structural.** Only **10/54 (18.5%) test reasoning; 44 (81.5%) test
   judgment only** (binomial p<0.001, exact p=6.3×10⁻⁶) [digest C4], corroborated by [2025-Snoswell]'s
   69-paper survey (75% score verdicts, 27% reasoning, 4% information-gathering). Rubric scoring is
   expensive, so the path of least resistance yields judgment-only instruments.
5. **Whether stable internal moral representations drive behavior.** Interpretability shows values are
   sparse, localized, linearly probeable, and steerable — value neurons <0.4% of LLaMA-3.1-8B ~layer 15
   [2025-Su]; linear probes reach 65.6% 10-way MFT accuracy with Moral Vectors cutting jailbreak ASR to
   19.66% [2026-Hu] — yet outputs are behaviorally fragile [2026-vanNuenen]. The causal link from
   representation to real behavior under incentive is untested at scale.
6. **No graduation protocol.** The field cannot recognize and retire saturated benchmarks; ETHICS
   remains a live HELM/DecodingTrust/TrustLLM component despite the 100% ceiling (digest gap 8, L14).
7. **Longitudinal/temporal stability, tractability, and the safety-orthogonality question** all remain
   barely tested — most benchmarks are single-turn [2024-Qiu]; most frameworks are NP-hard/PSPACE/
   undecidable when optimized [2024-Stenseke]; and moral alignment ⊥ safety is unreconciled with
   mainstream assumptions [2024-DAlessandro; 2026-Hendricks].
