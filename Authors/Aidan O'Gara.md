# Aidan O'Gara

## Biographical Summary

Aidan O'Gara is a researcher focusing on the technical and policy dimensions of AI governance and safety. He is a DPhil candidate at the University of Oxford and a grant investigator for Longview Philanthropy. His work investigates the physical and technical foundations required to enforce safety standards in advanced AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aidan's research focuses on hardware-enabled mechanisms (HEMs) for AI governance, aiming to create robust ways to verify compliance with safety rules for high-compute models. He also investigates deception in large language models and analyzes trends in dual-use AI technologies, particularly in biology.

### Significant Achievements
He has co-authored comprehensive surveys on AI alignment and deception, contributing to the foundational literature of the field. His multifaceted background includes research at the Center for AI Safety, Epoch, and GovAI, as well as leadership roles in fintech data science.

## Major Publications

- [AI Alignment: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19852) (2023) - *A broad overview of the mathematical and philosophical challenges of alignment.*
- [Hardware-Enabled Mechanisms for Verifying Responsible AI Development](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *Research on the infrastructure of AI governance.*
