# Filipino Benchmarks for Measuring Sexist and Homophobic Bias in Multilingual Language Models from Southeast Asia

## Authors

[[Gamboa, Lance Calvin Lim]], [[Lee, Mark]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Lance Calvin Lim Gamboa; Mark Lee

**Year:** 2025

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv / ACL Anthology (LoResLM @ COLING 2025)

**Languages:** Filipino/Tagalog

**Benchmark:** Filipino CrowS-Pairs / Filipino WinoQueer

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07303

## Abstract

7,074 challenge pairs adapted from CrowS-Pairs and WinoQueer for Filipino. Assesses sexist and anti-queer biases in PLMs. Found considerable bias in multilingual models including SEA-pretrained ones.

## Data Description

Filipino CrowS-Pairs and Filipino WinoQueer: 7,074 new Filipino-language challenge pairs culturally adapted from English bias benchmarks to assess sexist and homophobic biases in multilingual PLMs.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** First Filipino-specific bias benchmarks for gender and LGBTQ+ bias, culturally adapted rather than translated from English.
