## Abstract

WenMind is a comprehensive benchmark dedicated to evaluating LLMs in Chinese Classical Literature and Language Arts (CCLLA), covering Ancient Prose, Ancient Poetry, and Ancient Literary Culture. The benchmark's "Ancient Literary Culture" domain specifically tests knowledge of Confucian and Taoist philosophical traditions, literary commentary traditions, and cultural concepts embedded in classical texts. Even the best-performing model (ERNIE-4.0) achieved only 64.3 points, indicating significant room for improvement. This is a high-quality NeurIPS-accepted benchmark with publicly available code and data.

## Dataset Information

4,875 question-answer pairs spanning 42 fine-grained tasks, 3 question formats, 2 evaluation scenarios (domain-oriented and capability-oriented)
