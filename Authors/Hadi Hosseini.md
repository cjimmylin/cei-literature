# Hadi Hosseini

## Research Summary

Hadi Hosseini is an Assistant Professor of Artificial Intelligence in the College of Information Sciences and Technology at Penn State University. His research focuses on problems at the interface of computer science and economics, studying different aspects of self-interested players in multiagent systems and developing algorithms with provable fairness and societal guarantees.

His work on fair division provides computational and mathematical frameworks for the allocation of indivisible goods, addressing fairness concepts essential for ensuring social acceptability and practical implementation of resource allocation algorithms. He introduced the novel concept of "multi-layered cake-cutting" to achieve fairness when distributing access time over multiple resources.

Prior to joining Penn State in Fall 2020, Hosseini was an assistant professor at Rochester Institute of Technology. He is an associate director of the Center for Artificial Intelligence Foundations and Engineered Systems (CAFE) and was elected to the IFAAMAS Board of Directors for a six-year term.
