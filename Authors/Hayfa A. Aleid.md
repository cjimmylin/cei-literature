# Hayfa A. Aleid

## Biographical Summary

Hayfa A. Aleid is a researcher specializing in Arabic natural language processing with a focus on developing AI systems for Islamic religious contexts. Her work addresses the underdevelopment of Arabic-language systems for religious question-answering, particularly for Hajj pilgrimage-related inquiries. She collaborates with Aqil M. Azmi on this research.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on developing specialized question-answering systems for Islamic religious contexts, particularly for fatwas (non-binding Islamic legal opinions) related to the Hajj pilgrimage. She investigates how to capture the unique linguistic and jurisprudential characteristics of pilgrims' inquiries to enable accurate, domain-specific responses.

### Significant Achievements
Aleid introduced Hajj-FQA, a benchmark Arabic dataset specifically designed to develop HajjBot, a specialized chatbot for fatwas QA during the annual Hajj pilgrimage. The high-quality, human-annotated dataset is specifically tailored for Hajj-Fatwa question answering, addressing a critical gap in resources for Arabic natural language processing and religious computing. Her research provided extensive benchmarking of different LLMs on the dataset, showcasing their capabilities and limitations in understanding and processing Arabic religious texts. The work was published in the Journal of King Saud University Computer and Information Sciences through Springer Nature.
