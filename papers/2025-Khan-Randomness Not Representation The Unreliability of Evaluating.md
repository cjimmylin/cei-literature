## Dataset Information

Systematic robustness testing of survey-based cultural alignment evaluations; tests stability, extrapolability, and steerability across multiple dimensions

## Data Description

This study evaluates cultural alignment in five LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Llama 3.1 405B, Mistral Large 2411) using survey-based assessments including the Value Survey Module (24 questions across 65 countries), Global Opinion Q&A dataset (180 questions across 15 countries), and a job hiring sandbox with 813 culturally variant cover letters. The data examines stability, extrapolability, and steerability assumptions in cultural alignment evaluation.
