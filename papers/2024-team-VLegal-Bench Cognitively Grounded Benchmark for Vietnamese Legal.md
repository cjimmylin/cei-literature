## Abstract

First comprehensive benchmark for Vietnamese legal LLM evaluation with 10,450 expert-verified samples across 22 tasks at 5 reasoning levels. Includes ethical reasoning evaluation assessing fairness, impartiality, privacy protection, and bias detection in legal contexts.
