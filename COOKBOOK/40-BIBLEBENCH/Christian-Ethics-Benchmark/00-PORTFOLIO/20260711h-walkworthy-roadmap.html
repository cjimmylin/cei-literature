7Assumptions behind the estimates

  • A "session" = one focused agent working session (Codex, Opus, or Fable/Claude). Jimmy's labeling and ratifications are human work counted in hours, not sessions — and they are the schedule's critical path.
  • Assumes no both-judge G5 disqualification on 06/09 (would add ~1–2 sessions for a replacement judge re-run), and that design 07 avoids full redesign (a REDESIGN-REVIEW outcome adds ~2 sessions and pushes 07 to the next cycle).
  • A design that fails hard can be released without — G4 is per-design and waves release independently, so a partial v1.0 benchmark (e.g. 7–8 of 10 designs) is a legitimate earlier endpoint if Jimmy prefers.
  • Fable-window availability changes which tier does the review sessions, not the session count.
  • Live-API spend for everything remaining is small (<$60 est.); cost is not a constraint.
