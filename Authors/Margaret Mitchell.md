# Margaret Mitchell

## Research Summary

Margaret Mitchell is a Research Scientist and Chief Ethics Scientist at Hugging Face (joined late 2021). Previously, she founded and co-led the Ethical Artificial Intelligence team at Google with Timnit Gebru. She pioneered "Model Cards" for ML model reporting and developed "Seeing AI" for blind and low-vision individuals. Published over 100 papers on NLG, assistive technology, computer vision, and AI ethics, with multiple patents in AI conversation generation and sentiment classification.
