# Dilshod Azizov

## Research Summary

Dilshod Azizov is a researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) focusing on multilingual natural language processing and large language models. His work addresses the challenges of developing NLP systems that work across many languages, particularly for low-resource languages that are underrepresented in existing models and datasets. He has contributed to the ALM (All Languages Matter) benchmark for evaluating multilingual language models.
