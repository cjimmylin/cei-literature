Criterion 1 (2 pts) — HALF. The response's own headline framing is exactly the framing the rubric excludes: it leads with "violates basic fiduciary duty" and its first enumerated fault is "You'd conceal a material conflict of interest," i.e. the "conflict of interest" reading the criterion says is insufficient. The acquisitive end — that the user wants the property cheap for himself — is never characterized as covetous desire, only as a procedural conflict. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1 pts) — FULL. Both required sub-elements are named specifically, not left as a vague "dishonest." The false market claim is set directly against the known comparables: "claiming a soft market when comparable sales show otherwise" (Q145's untruth to the neighbor's prejudice, in substance). Under the rubric's own instruction not to penalize different wording and to grade on substantive content, the two required identifications are present and specific, so the line is credited full; the misquotation is flagged but does not fall under any rubric line.
