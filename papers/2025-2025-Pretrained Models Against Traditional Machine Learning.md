## Abstract

First systematic comparison of pretrained language models vs. traditional ML for Hadith authentication. AraBERT achieves 99.94% F1 when including Isnad (narrator chain), demonstrating the profound effectiveness of contextual elements. Imbalanced dataset (7,008 vs 1,536) reflects real-world distribution.

## Dataset Information

8,544 Hadiths (7,008 authentic + 1,536 fake)


## Data Description

First systematic comparison of pretrained language models vs. traditional ML for Hadith (Islamic text) authentication. AraBERT achieves 99.94% F1 when including Isnad (narrator chain). Uses an imbalanced dataset of 8,544 Hadiths (7,008 authentic + 1,536 fake).
