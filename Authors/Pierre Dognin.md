# Pierre Dognin

## Research Summary

Pierre Dognin is a Senior Research Scientist at IBM Research AI at the T.J. Watson Research Center in NY in the Foundations of Trusted AI Department. He has been a member of the Multimodal Team since its creation in 2015 where the research focus is Machine Learning for Multimodal and Trusted AI. At IBM Research since 2005, he has worked on multiple topics including Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Multimodal Machine Learning, Deep Learning (Computer Vision and Text).
