# Hitoshi Iyatomi

## Research Summary

Hitoshi Iyatomi is a Professor at Hosei University's Department of Applied Informatics in Tokyo, Japan. He received his BE and ME degrees in electrical engineering and PhD in science for open and environmental systems from Keio University (1998, 2000, 2004). From 2016-2017, he was a visiting scholar at Johns Hopkins University. He leads the Intelligent Information Processing Laboratory (IIPL), which focuses on machine learning, computer vision, medical engineering, and NLP. His research includes developing automated diagnosis systems for crops (plant disease detection), computer-aided diagnosis for skin and gastric cancers, and brain MRI analysis including OpenMAP-T1 for rapid whole-brain parcellation. He has 175 publications with 5,629 citations.
