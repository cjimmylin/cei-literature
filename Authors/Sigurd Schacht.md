# Sigurd Schacht

## Research Summary

Prof. Dr. Sigurd Schacht is Director of COAI RESEARCH and Professor of Applied AI at the University of Applied Sciences Ansbach (Hochschule Ansbach) in Germany. His research focuses on AI ethics, particularly ethical alignment in large language models, moral reasoning circuits in LLMs, deception in LLMs, and the study and mitigation of bias in AI systems.
