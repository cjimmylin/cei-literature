# Barbara Plank

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Barbara Plank is a Professor and the Chair for AI and Computational Linguistics at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität (LMU) Munich, where she leads the MaiNLP lab. She is also a Visiting Professor at the IT University of Copenhagen. She earned her PhD cum laude from the University of Groningen. She is a highly influential figure in the NLP community, currently serving as the Vice President-elect of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Barbara’s research centers on making language technologies more robust, inclusive, and fair. She focuses on transfer learning, cross-lingual NLP, and human-inspired data-centric approaches. Her work investigates how models can be effectively adapted to new domains and languages with limited data, and how to handle label noise and human disagreement in dataset creation.

### Significant Achievements
She has received numerous prestigious grants, including an ERC Consolidator Grant and a DFF Sapere Aude Research Leader grant. She is an ELLIS Fellow and has served in leadership roles for major NLP conferences. With over 10,000 citations, she is a leading voice in the European NLP landscape, particularly in the areas of low-resource and robust machine learning.
