# Alison Lentz

## Biographical Summary

Alison Lentz is a Research Product Manager at Google DeepMind, specializing in Responsible AI and Human-AI Interaction. She has a background in HCI and is a key figure in Google's efforts to integrate human values into the development of large-scale AI models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alison's research focuses on developing methodologies for "Moral Imagination" in AI safety—helping researchers and designers anticipate and mitigate potential societal harms before they manifest. She works on the operationalization of ethical principles in model development and evaluation.
