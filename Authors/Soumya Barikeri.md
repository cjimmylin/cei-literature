# Soumya Barikeri

## Research Summary

Soumya Barikeri is a Senior Data Scientist NLP at LangTec based in Bengaluru, India. She completed her education at the University of Mannheim and has held previous positions at BASF, SAP, Ribbon Communications, and IIT Bombay. Her research includes work on bias evaluation and debiasing of conversational language models, including the publication "RedditBias: A Real-World Resource for Bias Evaluation and Debiasing of Conversational Language Models" at ACL/IJCNLP 2021.
