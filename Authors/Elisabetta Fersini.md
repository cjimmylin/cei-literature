# Elisabetta Fersini

## Biographical Summary

Elisabetta Fersini is an Associate Professor at the University of Milano-Bicocca in the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication. She is a leading researcher in hate speech detection and sentiment analysis, having co-organized several influential shared tasks at SemEval and EVALITA on detecting misogyny, hate speech, and abusive language in social media.

## Research Interests

- Hate speech and misogyny detection
- Sentiment analysis and opinion mining
- Natural language processing
- Machine learning for text classification
- Social media analysis

## Major Publications

- "SemEval-2019 Task 5: Multilingual Detection of Hate Speech Against Immigrants and Women in Twitter" (2019)
- "AMI @ EVALITA: Automatic Misogyny Identification" (shared task organizer)
- Research on sentiment analysis in social media
- Work on abusive language detection
