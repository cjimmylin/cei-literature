# Alexander Rusnak

## Research Summary

Alexander Rusnak is a researcher working at the intersection of AI ethics and Christian virtue ethics. His research explores how traditional virtue ethics frameworks, particularly from the Christian tradition, can inform the development and deployment of AI systems. He has proposed the "EudAImonia" framework, which integrates virtue ethics into AI training through reinforcement learning from AI feedback (RLAIF), aiming to cultivate practical wisdom and moral character in AI systems rather than relying solely on rule-based approaches.
