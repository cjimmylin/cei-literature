# Franziska Hopp

**NOTE: This profile may contain a name discrepancy. Web sources indicate the researcher's name is "Frederic Hopp" (male), not "Franziska Hopp". The vault metadata should be verified.**

## Biographical Summary

Frederic Hopp is currently Junior Professor for Big Data in Psychology at the Leibniz Institute for Psychology (ZPID) in Germany. He previously served as Assistant Professor at the Amsterdam School of Communication Research, University of Amsterdam from 2021-2024. His research bridges moral psychology, communication science, and computational methods.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research primarily investigates how morality permeates human communication and how moralized messages are cognitively processed and motivate behavior. He studies neural activation patterns that give rise to moral decisions and examines how large language models align with humans' moral judgments. His work spans moral psychology, neuroscience, and computational social science.

### Significant Achievements
Hopp developed the Dutch Moral Foundations Stimulus Database, adapting and validating moral vignettes and socio-moral images for Dutch populations. The database includes translations of 120 Moral Foundations Vignettes (MFVs) and 120 images from SMID, validated by 586 Dutch crowd-workers providing over 38,460 individual judgments. He also developed the extended Moral Foundations Dictionary (eMFD), a crowd-sourced approach to extracting moral intuitions from text, published in Behavior Research Methods (2020).
