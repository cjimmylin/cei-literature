# Geng Hong

## Biographical Summary

Geng Hong is an Assistant Professor at Fudan Development Institute and the School of Computer Science at Fudan University. He is a faculty member of the System Software and Security Group. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science at Fudan University in June 2022, advised by Prof. Min Yang.

## Research & Impact

Hong's research focuses on cybersecurity, cybercrime, and novel security threats:

- **Mobile Security**: Research on mobile gambling scams with ground-truth data analysis (IEEE S&P Oakland 2022) and cryptojacking in real-world systems (ACM CCS 2018).

- **LLM Security**: Research on RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) security, demonstrating that RAG-Thief can extract over 70% of information from private knowledge bases within customized RAG applications, highlighting privacy vulnerabilities.

- **Deception Detection**: Contributed to OpenDeception research examining deception in AI systems.

- **Internet Security**: Research on device search engines, email security, and software version identification at top venues including NDSS 2025/2026 and USENIX Security 2025.

He has been awarded the Fudan Policy Advisory Research Support Program.
