# Utkarsh Agarwal

## Research Summary

Utkarsh Agarwal is a PhD student in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence), working with Dr. Monojit Choudhury. He previously worked as a Research Fellow at Microsoft Turing and completed his undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bombay (2018-2022). His research focuses on ethical reasoning and moral value alignment in large language models.
