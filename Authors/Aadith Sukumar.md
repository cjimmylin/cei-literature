# Aadith Sukumar

## Biographical Summary

Aadith Sukumar is an AI/ML engineer and researcher currently working as an Associate Software Engineer at Maersk. He is completing his undergraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Symbiosis Institute of Technology. His work focuses on the intersection of AI safety, ethics, and cybersecurity.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aadith's research interests include building robust deep learning models and developing techniques for adversarial debiasing in machine learning, particularly within network security contexts. He has worked on multimodal neurophysiological frameworks for behavior analysis and the integration of Generative AI in software tools.

### Significant Achievements
As a research intern at the Symbiosis Center for Applied Artificial Intelligence (SCAAI), he led projects funded by the Government of India. He is actively involved in the open-source community through GitHub and contributes to the discussion on staying updated with AI advancements and security trends.
