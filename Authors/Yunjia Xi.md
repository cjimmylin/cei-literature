# Yunjia Xi

## Research Summary

Yunjia Xi is a researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University working on agentic information systems and retrieval-augmented generation. He developed InfoDeepSeek, a benchmark for evaluating Agentic RAG systems that move beyond static retrieval to dynamic information seeking in web environments. His work measures utility and compactness of autonomous agents' search behaviors, revealing that current LLMs struggle with efficient information seeking and often retrieve redundant or irrelevant data despite eventually answering questions.
