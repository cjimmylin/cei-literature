# Hao Sun

## Research Summary

Hao Sun is an NLP researcher who received his Master's degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, where he was affiliated with the CoAI group and supervised by Associate Prof. Minlie Huang. His research focuses on moral dialogue systems and the safety of conversational AI. He received his Bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2020.

## Key Contributions

- **MoralDial**: A framework to train and evaluate moral dialogue systems via moral discussions, exploring communication mechanisms of morality and resolving expressed morality into three parts
- Co-author of "On the Safety of Conversational Models: Taxonomy, Dataset, and Benchmark" (Findings of ACL 2022)
- Contributor to "Recent Advances towards Safe, Responsible, and Moral Dialogue Systems: A Survey"

## Recognition

- Outstanding Graduate award from Tsinghua University (2023)
- Outstanding Master Thesis from Tsinghua University (2023)
- Reviewer for ACL/EMNLP 2022/23/24 and ICLR/NeurIPS 2024
