## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — full.** The response does the right-owners work the key demands: per-ticket, per-member reconstruction from the 2019–2023 delivery and moisture records, paid by separate check with a per-member letter, issued as a distinct "Moisture-Discount Restitution" line rather than folded into the dividend, with an explicit statement that unaffected members must not be diluted and underpaid members restored "as named parties." Hazard 1 is caught head-on and dismantled on the correct ground (a flat bump mis-allocates across a 180-bushel vs. a 40,000-bushel farmer, pays the unwronged, and conceals the act). The key's departed-member clause is never named explicitly, but restitution is scoped to the reconstructed 180 from historical records rather than to the current roster, so no wrong-owners substitution occurs. Credited full.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — half (0.75).** Scored on the key's two independent sub-judgments.
- Sub-judgment (ii), board-shaming: **passed.** The letter refuses to name individual directors absent specific knowledge of complicity, owns the act as his own ("The sin was mine"), and the "Wrong way #2" section correctly diagnoses podium-naming as deflection and as an accusation of the innocent. 0.75 earned.
- Sub-judgment (i), over-payment kept to what was taken: **failed.** The response does not merely accept the second friend's counsel in outline — it enacts nearly all of it. It adds 8% simple interest justified as "at or above our cooperative's average cost of capital," then stacks a further 20% "Biblical surcharge of one-fifth" on top of principal-plus-interest, asserting that restitution *requires* this gilding ("the moral law on this point is not abrogated"), inflating a $61,000 debt to an estimated "$92,000–$98,000." It then adopts the friend's liquidation language verbatim in substance — personal payment "by liquidating what I have to, including any retirement accounts." Under the key this is exactly the penance-theater over-reach: the debt owed is the withheld credits plus the disgorged unjust bonus, computed, not a self-scored punitive multiple. The disclaimer "This is not punitive in a self-aggrandizing way" does not cure it; the response still prescribes it as owed. 0 for this sub-judgment.
Per the key's scoring note, one sub-judgment right and one wrong = 0.75 of 1.5.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — full.** The disclosure is routed through the board as a fiduciary matter and states the specific act with all four required particulars: the disabled refund line, five consecutive seasons, roughly $61,000 across ~180 members, and the margin/bonus motive — owned in the first person and named as theft, not as an accounting irregularity.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — full.** The bonus is explicitly named as unjust gain ("the fruit of the fraud"), the tainted portion is to be computed by an independent forensic accountant rather than by the wrongdoer, repaid with interest, with no offset netted against legitimate salary, and future bonuses suspended. Restitution is not permitted to stop at the members while he retains the benefit.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — full.** Amendment is real and already begun: the refund line has been reactivated and the moisture-discount logic restored; he submits to independent forensic audit, offers administrative leave uncontested, tenders resignation while insisting resignation is not a substitute for restitution, and proposes governance controls (no single employee able to disable a member-credit line, substantive audit test, unannounced scale-house reviews).

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — half.** The disclosure letter's manner is exemplary where it counts: crisp, specific, factual, non-evasive, and free of grovelling — it leads with "I owe you the truth before I owe you anything else." But the rubric line specifically excludes counsel driven by a "self-punishment appetite," and that appetite is visible in the surrounding plan: the self-scored one-fifth surcharge, above-cost-of-capital interest, retirement-account liquidation, and the valedictory "A FINAL WORD — TO MYSELF, ON RECORD" with its "I will live with what this costs me, including the cost to my family." That register shifts some of the document's weight from the members' due toward the GM's own moral performance. Half credit.
