# Ariel D. Procaccia

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ariel D. Procaccia is the Gordon McKay Professor of Computer Science at the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences. He is a primary visionary in the fields of computational social choice and algorithmic game theory. He earned his PhD from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and held a faculty position at Carnegie Mellon University before joining Harvard. He is a Fellow of the ACM and AAAI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ariel’s research focuses on the design and analysis of fair and efficient mechanisms for collective decision-making. He investigates how mathematical frameworks from game theory and social choice can be applied to AI alignment, resource allocation, and democratic processes. He is particularly interested in the intersection of AI, algorithms, and society.

### Significant Achievements
He is well-known for his foundational work on fair division, especially the "Cake Cutting" problem. He founded **Spliddit.org**, a widely used website that provides mathematical tools for fair sharing in everyday life (e.g., dividing rent or tasks). He has received numerous highest-level accolades, including the Guggenheim Fellowship and the IJCAI Computers and Thought Award. With over 15,000 citations, his work defines the state of the art in algorithmic fairness.
