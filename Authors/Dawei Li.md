# Dawei Li

## Biographical Summary

Dawei Li is a researcher working on natural language processing and large language models. His research focuses on model editing techniques and understanding how modifications to language models can affect their behavior and safety properties. He has contributed to research examining the potential risks and benefits of model editing approaches for LLMs.

## Research Interests

- Large language model editing
- Knowledge manipulation in neural networks
- AI safety and robustness
- Natural language processing
- Model interpretability
