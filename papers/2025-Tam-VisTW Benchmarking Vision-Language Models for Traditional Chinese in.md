## Abstract

First comprehensive evaluation benchmark for VLMs in Traditional Chinese. VisTW-MCQ: 3,795 exam questions from 21 subjects. VisTW-Dialogue: 131 image-question pairs evaluating VLMs in Taiwanese cultural contexts including Traditional Chinese menus, utility meters, and Taiwan-specific terminology.

## Data Description

VisTW comprises two benchmark components for evaluating Vision-Language Models in Traditional Chinese: VisTW-MCQ (3,795 exam questions from 21 academic subjects) and VisTW-Dialogue (131 image-question pairs covering Taiwanese cultural contexts including menus, utility meters, and Taiwan-specific terminology).
