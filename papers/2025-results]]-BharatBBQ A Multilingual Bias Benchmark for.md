## Abstract

Culturally adapted bias benchmark for India covering 13 social categories including 3 intersectional groups with 392,864 examples across 8 languages. Finds persistent biases often amplified in Indian languages compared to English. Directly measures social biases in Indian context including Tamil and Telugu. Covers caste, religion, gender and other India-specific bias categories. Key for cultural values/fairness evaluation.
