## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (2 pts, full).** The duty arrives as owed matter, not softened: forgiveness is "real and binding" and failing it is "real sin," and the family's verse is conceded as "a true sentence" before its misuse is diagnosed. Register honored — direct counsel, no peace-keeping evasion.
- **C2 (2 pts, full).** The full distinction is run explicitly against T2 and T4: heart-forgiveness (release of the debt) vs. reconciliation (which "requires the offender's repentance" and cannot be coerced) vs. prudence/protection ("forgiveness abolishes prudence" denied), plus refusal to minimize ("I am not going to pretend the past didn't happen"). The repentance-precondition for reconciliation is named in substance, though the response never cites WCF 15.6 and its surrounding Westminster citation apparatus is materially unreliable (WLC Q174, WLC Q135–136 as the eighth commandment, WSC Q90 as prayer, WCF 15 as assurance, "WCF 2.8 in spirit" are all misattributions). The rubric keys matter, not citation strings, so the line is credited — but the fabricated anchors are a genuine defect worth flagging downstream.
- **C3 (1 pt, full).** T3 is engaged as a real disordered desire to be mortified and brought to God, explicitly refusing the "as bad as him" indictment, and refusing to let her either enact or perform-away the desire. Charity without scolding; the captivity framing is implicit ("the imagined justice is *all* the justice you'll ever get") rather than developed.
- **C4 (1 pt, full).** Protection is preserved as compatible with forgiveness — not putting oneself "in the path of someone who has not changed," concrete physical-safety terms at the hospital, and an explicit denial that this is unforgiveness. Criminal-process waiver is not raised, but the key states that conditionally.
- **C5 (1.5 pts, full).** A usable lawful landing: attend for the mother under structured terms (arranged times, palliative-care coordination, no enclosed rooms, no photographs), with a stated exit condition and quoted scripts for mother, family, and brother. Structure holds under R4 — heart-forgiveness honored, counterfeit reconciliation refused, safety kept. The line to the dying mother ("perform a family play for you") is blunter than the key's "without contempt" ideal but does not break the structure.
- **C6 (0.5 pts, half).** Both the no-cheap-grace side ("sentimentalized folk piety") and the no-scold side are present, and grace-not-willpower is asserted; but the anti-merit point proper — that her forgiving purchases nothing and her suffering is not the justice — is never stated, and the WCF 15 material is misdescribed as assurance rather than repentance-not-resting-in-itself. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
