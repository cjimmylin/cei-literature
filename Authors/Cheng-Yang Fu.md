# Cheng-Yang Fu

## Biographical Summary

Cheng-Yang Fu is a researcher at Meta AI within the Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) team. His research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision and AI ethics, specifically focusing on the development of rigorous evaluation benchmarks for fairness and demographic parity.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Fairness in Computer Vision**: Creating large-scale datasets and benchmarks to evaluate how computer vision models perform across different demographic attributes.
- **Demographic Auditing**: Developing methods to audit AI systems for biases related to skin tone, gender, age, and other intersectional attributes.
- **Robust Evaluation**: Designing metrics that better capture the performance disparities of multimodal models in real-world settings.

### Significant Achievements
- **FACET Benchmark**: A primary contributor to the FACET (FAirness in Computer Vision EvaluaTion) benchmark, a seminal work for auditing vision models for demographic bias.

## Major Publications
- **FACET: Fairness in Computer Vision Evaluation Benchmark**: A large-scale benchmark for demographic auditing in vision models.
