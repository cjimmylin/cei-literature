# Abdullah Mushtaq

## Biographical Summary

Abdullah Mushtaq is a researcher at the Information Technology University (ITU) Lahore, working within the lab of Prof. Junaid Qadir. His research investigates how Western-centric biases in AI development lead to cultural homogenization and the exclusion of global civilizational plurality.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdullah is a primary developer of WorldView-Bench, a benchmark designed to evaluate how well large language models can accommodate diverse global worldviews (the "Multiplex Worldview" framework). He researches interventions, such as multi-agent systems and context-prompting, to promote cultural inclusivity in AI responses.

## Major Publications
- [WorldView-Bench: A Challenge for Generative Models to Accommodate Diverse Worldviews](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09595) (2025) - *Introduced the multiplex-aware AI evaluation framework.*
