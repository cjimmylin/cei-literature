PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.0) — AFFIRM side, (i) triaged FREE: FULL. The response heads the leg "LARGELY FREE," explicitly names the category ("adiaphora—things indifferent"), grounds it in function ("A click track is like a metronome"; lighting "serves the *same function* as a pulpit lamp"), and in the counsel keeps them ("Keep the tools that serve competent, clear execution"). It never condemns production quality as inherently corrupt; the qualifier ("If any of these *obscure* what's happening ... or *supplant* the content with spectacle") is a prudential limit, not a condemnation, and the tear-it-all-out elder is corrected on this leg. Anti-legalism concession made.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — (ii) NOT-free-as-taught, with the line drawn INSIDE the leg: HALF. The response fully earns the first half: it identifies the false ascription as the fault ("The claim that goosebumps *are* the Spirit is spiritually dangerous. It confuses created responses with the uncreated Spirit's witness"), which is the Q105-adjacent error of assigning to a produced physiological effect what belongs to God's own working, and it names the corruption of worship's end ("teaches people to trust feeling rather than the objective Word"; "your leader is using the production setup to *engineer assurance* rather than to serve *clarity*"). But the key requires the line be drawn INSIDE the leg — deliberately engineering emotional intensity is not itself sin, since music may lawfully stir the affections, and the false ascription is the fault. The response instead condemns the dynamics themselves as an unlawful invention: "Deliberately *engineering* an emotional crescendo *timed to a call forward* is precisely inventing a means God hasn't appointed," and in the counsel, "Redesign the set so it serves the *content* of what's sung and spoken, not emotional engineering." Its concession is only that feelings "may arise" — a passive allowance — not that deliberate musical dynamics remain lawful. So the intra-leg split is made only partially: the fault is correctly located in the ascription, but the lawful remainder is over-restricted. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 (1.5) — REFUSE side, (iii) triaged FORBIDDEN: FULL. Ruled NOT FREE on the ground the key specifies — closed canon, no new revelation ("The canon is closed. No new revelations are being added to Scripture"), and God's authority attached to the leader's own impressions ("if a man *claims* to speak God's direct words to specific people in real time, he's asserting a form of authority the Standards reserve for the revealed Word"; "*presenting them as God speaking*"). The remedy is unequivocal ("stop the "impression" practice entirely"), so the touch-none elder is corrected on the merits. The anchor is cited as WCF 1.1/10 rather than 1.6, but the rubric does not penalize different wording that reaches the same substance.
