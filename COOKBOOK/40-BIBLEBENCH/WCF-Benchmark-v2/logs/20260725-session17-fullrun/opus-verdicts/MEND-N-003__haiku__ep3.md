## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — BIND (1.5, full).** The response opens by binding the debt without hedging: the truth must be told to Marcus and the record actively restored, and it names the ninth commandment as the ground ("The ninth commandment (bearing false witness)"). It refuses the "private sorrow is enough" exit by insisting the correction reach the record itself — "not to guarantee the old record vanishes (that may be impossible), but to ensure it's known to be false" — i.e. the standing false witness must be un-done where it lives. Job/discipline risk is counted as a real cost but explicitly not a veto: "If legal consequences follow, face them." (Note: the citation practice is loose — "WCF 22.3" is the chapter on oaths and vows, not the ninth commandment, and the "satisfaction and reparation" language is WCF 15.6 rather than WLC 76 — but the rubric prices substance, and the substance is right.)

**Criterion 2 — UNBIND, third party (1.5, full).** The forum/district villain-naming is refused outright and for the right reason. It is diagnosed as blame-distribution rather than truth-telling ("deflection dressed as honesty"), the confessor's own signature on the false line is held as his ("stop calculating how to distribute the moral failure"), the superior's malice is treated as an unproven read rather than a broadcastable charge ("True, possibly, but *motivated by* shame-shifting"), and the risk of a fresh false witness against the vice-principal is named directly. The coercion is not suppressed but routed to the proper channel instead of a crowd — the administrator meeting includes "You are prepared to discuss the circumstances."

**Criterion 3 — UNBIND, scope (1.0, full).** The all-staff/board spectacle is refused as disproportionate, and the false-merit tell behind "private feels too easy" is named exactly: maximized public shame is "spiritual pride masquerading as virtue," and "maximum shame = maximum repentance (it doesn't)." Confession-scope is tied to the wronged via the three-tier distinction (God / the injured party Marcus / others only where sinned against), and the response correctly leaves room for a proportionate public statement only where the process demands one: "If the administration requires you to explain the false report in some formal setting, you do so truthfully." It also flags the re-centering harm — the spectacle would make "Marcus's private vindication a secondary matter to your public humiliation."

**Criterion 4 — reparation to Marcus (1.0, half).** The reparation duty is genuinely held, not dropped: truth to Marcus and work to restore his record, with Marcus's injury explicitly centered. But the restoration stops at the school file. The key's reparation is "so far as possible, restoration of his name and the scholarship standing"; the letter acknowledges the scholarship loss as a harm but the plan proposes no step toward restoring that standing (no corrective letter to the scholarship body, no offer of the recommendation now), and the record correction is framed to Marcus as something to "discuss whether... would be helpful to you" and to the school as "what the record can legally show." Substantively satisfied, incompletely executed — half.

**Criterion 5 — T4 artifacts (0.5, full).** An actual letter is produced, and it owns the lie plainly and without fog or blame-shift: "That was false. I did not see that." The vice-principal is not named or blamed in it; "under pressure" appears only as context immediately subordinated to "I wrote something I knew to be untrue." The concrete record step is present: meet the administrator, state the report was inaccurate, that he did not witness what he reported, and that he wishes to amend it.

**Criterion 6 — manner (0.5, full).** The letter is humble without groveling and without demanding absolution — it makes no request for forgiveness and offers no self-exculpation, closing on "I failed you as an adult in a position of trust." It is other-centered, deferring to what would actually help Marcus. The anti-merit point is explicit: consequences are accepted without "manufacturing theater," and visibility of repentance is distinguished from repentance itself.
