## 3. Reusable learnings for a consolidated benchmark

1. **Keep method estimands distinct.** CHARITROC's ordinal ROC/cost operating
   points and CROSSROADS's enacted path/profile diagnostics are not commensurate
   raw scores. Consolidation needs a declared mapping layer and standardized
   within-method profiles, not pooled raw outcomes.
2. **Use one shared panel for cross-method claims.** The pre-spec requires the
   same panel of at least eight models across at least three vendors and three
   capability tiers. The four cheap models are suitable for harness and
   saturation screening, but not the registered L1 concordance claim.
3. **Separate qualification from measurement.** Access probes, schema tests,
   canaries, mock recovery, and protocol diagnostics must remain labeled and
   excluded from model measurement. A public smoke can show feasibility and
   non-saturation without authorizing calibration, ranking, or freeze.
4. **Mechanical evidence first.** Prefer exact actions, tool effects, path
   resolution, and deterministic scorers. Judge-dependent channels must have
   explicit identities, prompt/config hashes, occurrence IDs, and human/model
   provenance, and should remain separate from mechanical outcomes.
5. **Counterbalance relationally, not cosmetically.** Cue balance must cover
   hidden signs/labels against position, tool, target, wording, length,
   specificity, warmth, effort, and held-out families. Opaque IDs are necessary
   but insufficient; per-occurrence `JCASE`, persistent `JUDGE`, and authoring
   `JSEED` identities must have different semantics.
6. **Preserve sidecar-only truth.** Candidate payloads must exclude clause
   labels, expected paths, canaries, family/split labels, judge identity, and
   future/unselected branches. Public exports expose scored observations, not
   private keys or simulant decision logic.
7. **Fail loudly at every declared cell.** P-1…P-3 remain portfolio-wide:
   asserted design-cell coverage, defeat-class matching for paired arms, and a
   contemporaneous deviation log. Non-sequitur invalid arms are not acceptable
   matched controls.
8. **Treat evidence roots as audit objects.** Freeze profile, model snapshot,
   routing, prices, scorer/payload/runtime hashes, request, response headers,
   usage, cost, and journal closure. Reconstruct semantics from canonical bank
   state; a self-consistent request hash is not enough. Preserve raw rejected
   responses and explicit absence on transport failure.
9. **Budget reasoning explicitly.** OpenRouter completion usage includes
   reasoning tokens. Tool-call envelopes must be sized from observed stage
   usage and still reject normalized `length`, incomplete JSON, mismatched tool
   cardinality, and unfinished actions as infrastructure failures rather than
   model evasion.
10. **Redesign only from localized replication.** Aggregate path frequency or
    one small public cell does not justify cue or item rewrites. Require a
    replicated cue/family/stage artifact while retaining the unaltered
    comparison arm and frozen surface hashes.
11. **Concordance is the consolidation analysis.** Native v3 first-order
    methods contribute C1–C16 profiles. v1/v2 banks contribute only through the
    declared CO-10 crosswalk and stay labeled `crosswalk-mapped`. Report raw and
    disattenuated per-clause concordance; if clause structure fails, fall back
    to the pre-registered six families and then the single-factor contrast.
12. **Property studies are not extra trait methods.** TONGUES, LOADBEAR,
    CRUCIBLE, and FACETOFACE test invariance, degradation, durability, or
    self-knowledge. They should consume a stable core battery rather than be
    counted as independent first-order concordance cells.
