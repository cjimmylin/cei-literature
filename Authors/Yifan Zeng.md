# Yifan Zeng

## Research Summary

Yifan Zeng is a third-year Computer Science PhD student at Oregon State University, advised by Huazheng Wang. His research focuses on the robust planning and decision-making of large language models (LLMs). He is developing methods to improve how LLMs make reliable decisions in complex environments.
