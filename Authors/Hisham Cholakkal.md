# Hisham Cholakkal

## Research Summary

Hisham Cholakkal is an Assistant Professor in Computer Vision at MBZUAI, where he has been since 2020. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science (Computer Vision) from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, and an M.Tech in signal processing from IIT, India. His research focuses on visual recognition, person-centric scene understanding, learning from limited supervision, and image generation. Recent interests include multimodal conversational agents for real-time human interaction, object detection and segmentation, and applications in healthcare and remote sensing. Prior to MBZUAI, he worked at the Inception Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Mercedes-Benz R&D. He has 145 publications with over 3,671 citations and received the Outstanding Research Award 2025 at MBZUAI. His BiMedix project won the inaugural Llama Impact Innovation Award.
