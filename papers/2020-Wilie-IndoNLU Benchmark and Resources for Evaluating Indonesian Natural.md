## Abstract

First vast NLU benchmark for Indonesian with 12 tasks including sentiment analysis (SmSA), emotion classification (EmoT), and aspect-based sentiment analysis. Includes IndoBERT and Indo4B dataset.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** Foundational Indonesian NLU benchmark including sentiment and emotion tasks relevant to cultural value expression.
