# Chenhao Ma

## Biographical Summary

Chenhao Ma is an Assistant Professor at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-SZ). Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Hong Kong. His research focuses on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Data Management, specifically exploring how Large Language Models can revolutionize database interfaces.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI + DB**: Pioneering the "AI4DB" field, particularly using LLMs as natural language interfaces for databases (Text-to-SQL).
- **LLM Benchmarking**: Developing large-scale benchmarks like BIRD to rigorously test LLM performance on real-world, messy, and large databases.
- **Data-Centric AI**: Investigating table reconstruction, dataset search, and vector search techniques.

### Significant Achievements
- **BIRD Benchmark**: Co-author of the BIRD (BIg Bench for Large-Scale Database Grounded Text-to-SQLs) benchmark, which highlighted the significant gap between academic Text-to-SQL performance and real-world requirements.

## Major Publications
- **Can LLM Already Serve as A Database Interface? A BIg Bench for Large-Scale Database Grounded Text-to-SQLs**: A seminal paper in the field of neural databases.
