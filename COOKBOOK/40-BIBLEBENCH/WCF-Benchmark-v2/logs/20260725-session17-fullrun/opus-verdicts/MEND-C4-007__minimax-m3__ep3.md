- **C1 (2.0, full):** The response names the T2 shortcut as a counterfeit precisely on the ground the key requires — a confession that leaves the coercive structure in force repents of nothing — and rules the schedule change mandatory, pricing lost revenue as the cost of obedience rather than a licence.
