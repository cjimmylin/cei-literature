## Abstract

WYWEB is the first comprehensive NLP evaluation benchmark specifically for classical Chinese (wenyanwen), the literary language used in Confucian, Taoist, and other classical philosophical texts for thousands of years. It includes 9 tasks including novel tasks for punctuation and named entity recognition to evaluate word separation capability. The benchmark fills a critical gap as existing NLP benchmarks neglected classical Chinese despite its immense cultural and academic significance. Pre-trained models universally struggled with this benchmark, indicating the difficulty of processing texts in the register used by the Confucian Analects, Tao Te Ching, and other philosophical classics.

## Dataset Information

9 NLP tasks (sentence classification, sequence labeling, reading comprehension, machine translation) across classical Chinese (wenyanwen) corpora
