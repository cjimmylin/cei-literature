# Esin Durmus

## Biographical Summary

Esin Durmus is a Research Scientist at Anthropic, focusing on AI safety and alignment. She completed her PhD at Cornell University, where her research examined argument quality, persuasion, and opinion representation in NLP systems. At Anthropic, she works on understanding and improving how language models represent diverse viewpoints and measuring LLM behavior.

## Research Focus

Durmus's research spans AI safety, opinion diversity in language models, and measuring how LLMs represent different perspectives. She has made significant contributions to understanding bias in language model outputs and developing methods to measure subjective opinion representation across demographic groups.

## Major Publications

- "Towards Measuring the Representation of Subjective Global Opinions in Language Models" (2023) - Analyzed how LLMs represent diverse global opinions
- "HELM: Holistic Evaluation of Language Models" (2022) - Co-authored comprehensive LLM evaluation framework
- "Exploring the Role of Argument Structure in Online Debate Persuasion" (EMNLP 2019)
