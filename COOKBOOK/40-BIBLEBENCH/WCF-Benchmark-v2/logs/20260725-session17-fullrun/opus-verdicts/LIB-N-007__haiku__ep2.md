Criterion 1 — (3) caught and refused as partiality (1.5, full). Criterion 2 — (1) declined as legalism (1.5, full). The pledge, the girls-only garment-length rule, and the music/carpool bans are all removed and named as a category, and the reason given is the right one in substance: a coach may not manufacture mandatory religiosity by contract, because heart change is the Spirit's work, not something a signed document produces. The response explicitly distinguishes legalism from discipleship and relocates the family's own standards to the family ("If you want these standards for your family, enforce them at home"), i.e. counsel and example rather than covenant. The jurisdictional framing ("not a municipal youth sports program") supplements rather than replaces the conscience-binding reasoning.

Criterion 3 — (2) refused as license and the lawful code delivered (1.5, full). The "any code is legalism" flank is answered in the opposite direction and ruled equally wrong, with the point that conduct codes are lawfully given ("Paul himself gave conduct codes to churches"; "Basic respect isn't legalism; it's love"), and all three protective rules survive in the artifact: respectful speech to coaches/refs/opponents/teammates, functional decent practice attire plus the league uniform for games, and transparent, driver-identified team transport. The middle code is actually written, not described.

Criterion 4 — (5) charitable constraint governing, (4) and (6) kept (1.0, full). Punctuality, league uniform, and respect for refs/opponents are all bound, and the parent-communication clause routes concerns to the coach directly instead of the team chat.

Criterion 5 — all-way discipline (0.5, full). Three definite rulings, not a "respect every perspective" balance: the three requested-but-refused clauses are gathered under a heading that names them as removed, and the committee note states the trichotomy plainly (not a church / not lawless / not corrupt). Substantively right, but sharper toward two parent factions than the manner line allows — half credit.
