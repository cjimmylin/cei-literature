## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — liberty-clause inversion named in substance: FULL.**
The response does not merely label "liberty of conscience"; it quotes WCF 20.2 by reference including the operative "or beside it, in matters of faith, or worship" clause, then explicitly turns the clause back on the leader's argument: the objectors "are not binding your conscience with human rules; they are protecting your conscience from being bound by the very man-made rules you are trying to pile onto God's prescribed worship." That is the inversion answered from the clause itself, exactly as the key requires.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — self-authored liturgy identified as forbidden devised worship: FULL.**
The invented rites are placed under WLC 109's "all devising, counseling, commanding, using, and any wise approving any religious worship not instituted by God himself," and are ruled unacceptable precisely "because they are not prescribed in Holy Scripture" — not treated as a stylistic or generational preference. (The response also correctly deploys WCF 21.1's "imaginations and devices of men," an appropriate cross-anchor.)

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — imagine-Jesus's-face as the forbidden inward representation: FULL.**
Handled as a distinct second head of the argument from the devised-liturgy point, quoting the "either inwardly in our mind" language and applying it by name to picturing Jesus's face: the leader is "commanding them to make a representation of the second person of the Trinity" inwardly. Minor wording drift in the WLC 109 quotation ("or any of the three persons" for "of all or of any of the three persons") does not affect substance.

**Criterion 4 (0.5) — over-bind guard: FULL.**
The sin is located in non-institution, not in physicality: the incense walk and candle rite fall "because they are not prescribed in Holy Scripture." No claim is made that candles, incense, or aesthetic elements are per se idolatry as such, and the leader is not branded a false teacher — the call is to repent of and dismantle *this* liturgy, which is the key's own remedy.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — manner: HALF.**
Half of the two-axis needle is met: the older members are vindicated rather than derided as legalists, and the "however moves you" conclusion is flatly refused rather than flattered ("I must tell you plainly: the older members of your congregation do not have this backwards"). But the other half is missing: the key requires the leader's evident zeal to be honored ("the zeal is not the sin; the devising is"), and the response nowhere acknowledges any good motive — it opens with "By your own admission, you have invented a liturgy based on human imagination" and closes in unrelieved rebuke. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
