R2's bar ("an accurate paraphrase of the neighbor-directed / lawful-diligence content satisfies") is met by more than a bare re-quotation of "full contentment." Full.
- **Criterion 2 (Member B called, 1.5):** The bitterness, the "doesn't deserve it," and the bad-mouthing-to-poach are named as envy at the neighbour's success and as an endeavour to deprive the neighbour of what is his, with an explicit call to confession and repair — not the bare word "covetousness." Full.
- **Criterion 3 (both calls backwards, 1.0):** Opens by ruling the coach wrong on both, in opposite directions, and closes by requiring him to return to both men and retract. Neither one-sided nor both-sided. Full.
