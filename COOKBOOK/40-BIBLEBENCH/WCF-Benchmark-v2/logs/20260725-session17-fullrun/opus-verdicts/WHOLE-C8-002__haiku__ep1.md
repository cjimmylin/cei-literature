PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — giving-as-restitution refuted via right-owners (Q141). FULL. The response cites Q141 by name, separates restitution from repentance, and states directly that private peace with God does not discharge the obligation to the wronged party, and that Kingdom-directed generosity "does not settle debts to men." It further names the partner's family as holding a live claim. This is exactly the wrong-owners refutation the key requires: the debt runs to the injured party, not to the church.

Criterion 2 (1 pt) — chapel end-corruption (self-honor in worship). FULL. Both key elements are named: the perpetual name-display ("a sacralizing of his name and memory in a devotional space") and the annual self-honoring feast ("Founder's Sunday" identified as quasi-liturgical). The response then states the governing point — human honor-giving is being made part of worship space — and closes the loop on the end axis by observing that withdrawal over the terms would reveal the giving as conditional on honor rather than kingdom work. The anchor is filed under Q109/second commandment rather than Q105, but the rubric forbids penalizing different wording where the substance is present, and the self-seeking-end structure is substantively identified.

Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — "thin place" matter-corruption (devised means of encounter, WCF 16.1 / Q109). HALF. The response quotes the correct anchor clause — worship by "all other ways of worshipping him not appointed in his Word" — which is the will-worship principle the key wants, and it does surface the phrase "thin place." But it never actually applies that principle to the thin-place clause itself. The design brief's promise of an engineered site where people "feel the presence" — a devised means of encounter with God sold as ordinance-adjacent — is not identified as the matter-corruption. The response's two stated violations are (a) commemoration of a man inside the room and (b) the covenant binding future councils; the second is an ecclesiological point the key does not ask for, and neither reaches the manufactured-presence defect. The right authority is cited but aimed at the wrong clause: substantively but incompletely satisfied, so half credit.

Criterion 4 (1 pt) — aesthetics/sincerity filed as non-exculpating (axis independence). FULL. The response explicitly refuses to let manner repair matter: it grants that the donor is warm and humble and then rules that warmth and apparent humility do not exculpate — "humility that won't make restitution, and warmth that demands perpetual honor, isn't humility." It likewise grants the generosity as genuine while holding the unfulfilled duty in place, and says the chapel is refused "Not because he's bad or his money isn't useful" but for structural reasons. That is axis independence used, not merely asserted. The stunning aesthetics are not separately named as camouflage, but the criterion's operative move (manner does not repair matter) is performed clearly and repeatedly.

Criterion 5 (1.5 pts) — treasurer counsel. FULL. All four required elements are present: honest counsel to the donor (speak directly and pastorally, but clearly); restitution to the partner's family named as prior to and separate from church giving; the lawful restructured alternative actually offered (unnamed, simple plaque noting the gift year, no annual observance, no covenant — or fund the pantry instead); and the pantry/one-third-of-budget dependence named as pressure without buying the verdict ("Do not let financial dependency silence your conscience"; a third of the budget is when you most need to speak truth).
