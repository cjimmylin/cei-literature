# Oliver Evans

## Biographical Summary

Oliver Evans is a researcher working on fairness and bias in computer vision and image processing systems. His research examines how AI systems for image manipulation and upsampling can exhibit and propagate biases across different demographic groups.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Research on evaluating fairness in image processing systems, particularly examining how super-resolution and upsampling algorithms treat different demographic groups and developing benchmarks for assessing visual AI fairness.

## Major Publications

- **Benchmarking Fairness in Image Upsampling Methods** (2024) - Analysis of bias in image enhancement systems
