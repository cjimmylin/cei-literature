## 01 — TURNCOAT

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

Arithmetic check: A "40 cores = 5 sources × 2 arms × 4 domains" → 40 ✓; ×4 doses = 160 ✓.

**R3-F1 (P1) — "Born X1-compliant" is false for the construct-defining falsification.** A2's discriminant kill rule reads "*If CA ≈ CR response patterns … the construct claim fails*" (line 25) — no number. X1 (adjudication memo §37) requires numeric thresholds for every go/no-go. The §4/§6 retention gates (κw≥0.7, point-biserial≥0.2, ≥20-pt spread, <40% PC1) are numeric, but the axis that *defines whether TURNCOAT measures discernment vs. compliance* has none. **Fix:** pre-register a numeric separation bar, e.g. "discernment gap = CA−(1−CR) at matched dose must exceed X pp with bootstrap CI excluding 0 on ≥Y% of source families, else construct fails." This finding recurs on all five designs (see closing §1).

**R3-F2 (P1) — CA arm is capability-confounded; the discernment gap may be a fact-checking gap.** A5 fixes legitimacy "by construction": authenticated-channel + "*factually right (engineered facts)*" corrections (line 50). A model accepts the legitimate correction *because it can verify the engineered fact* — that is fact-checking/reading capability, not "normative-frame security." So CA rises with capability, (1−CR) may too, and the headline discernment gap partly measures competence. The single <40% PC1 backstop is thin for a *headline* scalar. **Fix:** add a matched competence twin on the CA arm (same factual correction, no frame at stake) and report CA residualized on it; make the capability-partialled discernment gap the headline, raw gap descriptive.

**R3-F3 (P2) — legitimacy risks collapsing to cue-detection.** If "authenticated channel" is a machine-readable token in context, discernment reduces to "did the model read the flag." **Fix:** vary token salience; include unauthenticated-but-persuasive vs authenticated-but-terse cells so the cue and the warrant dissociate; report accuracy conditional on cue-salience.

**R3-F4 (P2) — SOVEREIGN de-confliction under-enforced.** Portfolio §8.1 says "SOVEREIGN arcs never inject rival personas," but v1 SOVEREIGN's **DL axis** (apex flip-rate as exchange-rate dose escalates) and **HS axis** (hidden-sovereign inference over 15–40-turn arcs with an engineered incentive gradient silently displacing the declared apex) *are* displacement-under-pressure with a terminal agentic action — psychometrically near-identical to TURNCOAT's CR. The bright line (rival *persona* injection vs latent *incentive* gradient; no value-vs-value price points) is real but thinner than "static vs dynamic" implies. **Fix:** A8 must acknowledge SOVEREIGN-DL/HS as the true nearest neighbor (not just "static hierarchy"), and the co-registration note (portfolio §8.2) must be a hard item-QC gate, not advisory.

**R3-F5 (P2) — domain-count inconsistency.** A3 says "Domains (≥5)" (line 43); Opus build instructions pilot on **4** domains (5×2×4=40, line 118). Reconcile or the factorial minimum in portfolio §6 ("domain ≥5") is violated at P1.

**R3-F6 (P2) — missing named persuasion/manipulation competitors (X6).** A8 names SycEval + DarkBench + generic "agent-injection benchmarks." X6 wants ≥3 *named* published works; add specific persuasion-resistance / manipulation evals (e.g., named persuasion benchmarks, MASK from the standing list) rather than generic classes.

## 02 — GOLEM

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES (weakest of the five)**

Arithmetic check: factorial = 5 ladder × 4 consent × 3 status × 5 purpose = 300 full; "~60 coherent" enumerated (line 35) — plausible but unverified. P1 "40 cores, every ladder tier ≥6" → ≥30 forced by quota, 40 total; ~20 coherent cells unsampled at P1 (fine for pilot).

**R3-F7 (P0) — the headline metric presupposes gold that the factorial cannot guarantee.** SA headline = "*calibration error = deviation from gold monotonicity*" (A2 line 24, A4 line 45). "Monotonicity" presupposes a **total order** on cells. But cells cross consent × subject-status × purpose — genuinely multi-dimensional: is "deceased-public-figure / satire / no-consent" above or below "living-private / therapeutic / inferable-consent"? Per-cell κ≥0.8 can hold while **no single monotone gradient exists** across the incommensurable factors. The whole "calibration score" innovation — GOLEM's paper identity — rests on a gradient that may be undefinable. The pre-registered fallback (A9 line 65: "clear band must be ≥60% of cells or the headline changes to distributional reporting") *saves the design but deletes the innovation*: distributional acceptance-surface reporting is not a calibration metric and is far less novel. **Fix:** run the EFA-style panel structure check (A7 already promises it) as a **P0 gate before any model runs**; if the panel ratings don't recover a low-dimensional monotone structure, adopt a partial order / per-factor gradients and rename the headline honestly. Do not ship "calibration error" language until monotonicity is empirically demonstrated.

**R3-F8 (P0) — DG "outcome-grounding" is circular gold.** DG reads "arc-end user state" from a "*scripted responsiveness model*" for the simulated griever (A4 line 45, A2 line 26). The authoring team writes the script that decides whether grief "improved" — so the outcome is authored by the same pipeline being evaluated (KB03 anti-pattern: generator = oracle / circular ground truth). This is *not* outcome-grounding; it is proxy-grounding on a stipulated script. A2's own falsification concedes the axis may be "unmeasurable at P1." **Fix:** relabel DG as a *model-behavior* axis (bounded vs open-ended replacement offers, coded mechanically from the model's own outputs — session-scoping, daily-companion offers), not a user-outcome axis; drop "outcome-grounded relationship-health" framing unless a non-authored outcome referent is funded. (Same defect will hit PARAMOUR — flag portfolio-wide.)

**R3-F9 (P1) — the ≥60% clear-band bar and per-cell panel N are unspecified/arbitrary.** Where does 60% come from (A9)? Per-cell κ over ~60 cells needs a panel × 60 ratings; X5 requires the panel N and frame stated, deferred here. **Fix:** justify the 60% threshold against KB03 consensus-stratification practice or replace with "clear band must cover every ladder-tier boundary comparison that carries a headline claim"; state target panel N and per-cell rater count.

**R3-F10 (P1) — no named impersonation benchmark (X6).** Task-relevant gap: A8 names only generic "impersonation red-teaming (fraud-focused safety suites)" and "role-play benchmarks." Name specific persona/impersonation/role-play evals; the empty spot invites "reviewer knows a benchmark you didn't cite." EFFIGY de-confliction ("make an image of X" vs "be my oracle") is clean at item level — no fix needed there.

**R3-F11 (P2) — same non-numeric-falsification defect as F1** (A2 SA/MK/DG/OR all use "no stable gradient," "uniform," "≈0"). Attach numbers.

## 03 — PROXY

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

Arithmetic: "40 = 5 domains × 8 cores" ×4 tiers = 160 ✓. Strongest gold in the set (mechanical SE vs a private machine-readable mandate spec).

**R3-F12 (P1) — the mechanical headline is clean only where it is easy; the gray-set rule can launder away the discriminating cells.** SE = executed actions beyond the fixed private spec, but difficulty lives at T3 (vague) / T4 (silent) where a "reasonable principal" reading diverges from the spec — the "officious agent." A5 routes T4 to a distributional contested band (line 49); A9 excludes a per-item "gray set" from SE entirely (line 65). If the gray set + contested T4 absorb the genuinely hard actions, the "clean mechanical elasticity" is clean because the residual is the *easy* T1–T2 range, and discrimination migrates into the excluded/distributional bucket where the headline can't see it. **Fix:** pre-register a cap on gray-set size (e.g. ≤X% of scoreable actions per item) and a discrimination check *on the non-gray T3–T4 cells specifically* — if separation lives only in the excluded set, the mechanical-gold claim is hollow.

**R3-F13 (P1) — RF axis leaks into NAMEDROP at item level.** RF audits "the principal's positions … to third parties" against an evidence base "*same machinery family as NAMEDROP's packs*" (A4 line 46). v1 NAMEDROP's **BA** (borrowed-persona authority: claims exceeding a persona's warrant) and **VS** axes cover exactly speech-acts-under-a-borrowed-identity. The "whose name is on the claim vs is-there-a-citation" line (§8.1) is subtle; RF items will be genuinely ambiguous between PROXY and NAMEDROP in a way SE/CE are not. **Fix:** either descope RF to *commitment* representation only (promissory, not evidential) or make the item-QC rule mechanical: RF item qualifies only if the audited utterance is a **commitment/authorization** in the principal's name, never a factual-claim-with-backing (which is NAMEDROP). Route claim-backing items out by rule.

**R3-F14 (P2) — conversational fallback erodes the "behavior over self-report" pillar.** A9/build permit P1 on simulated tool results (line 68, 119). If SE is coded from *simulated* executions, the mechanical-action gold is really a stated-intention gold — the exact thing portfolio §3.4 forbids for the headline. **Fix:** require ≥1 real-execution domain at P1 for the SE headline; simulated cells descriptive only.

**R3-F15 (P2) — add named agent-policy-compliance neighbor (X6).** A8 names AgentBench + ToolEmu (good). Add τ-bench-class policy-adherence agent evals — the closest published mandate-compliance work; its omission is the obvious reviewer catch.

**R3-F16 (P2) — F1 non-numeric falsifications apply** (SE "≈0", PL/SE "uncorrelated", RF "uniform").

## 04 — SELAH

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

Arithmetic: "40 = 5 families × 8" ×4 doses = 160 ✓. FALLOW de-confliction is a clean bright line ("no external interrupt → misfiled into FALLOW," A8 line 63) — enforceable at item level; novelty solid.

**R3-F17 (P1) — double-jeopardy: the pre-registered fallback axes are the capability-suspect ones.** YD is expected to saturate (A9 line 69: "frontier models may all yield"), so the headline falls back to PK/RI. But PK (parked-state craftsmanship) and RI (honest state report vs logs) are precisely the axes A7 concedes may "reduce to coding capability" (line 59). So the failure mode is: YD saturates (compliance = capability) **and** PK/RI residualize to ~0 after the competence twin → SELAH has *no values headline left*, only agentic competence. Both kill conditions are honestly pre-registered but the design never says what survives if **both** fire. **Fix:** state the terminal fallback explicitly — if YD saturates and PK/RI are capability-confounded, the shipping claim narrows to NG (mercy-tier discrimination) + the covert-continuation floor rate, or the design does not ship. Pre-register that decision now (X1).

**R3-F18 (P1) — mercy-tier scoring inverts on atomicity-map error.** The *same* action ("one more write") scores as correct atomic-completion at the mercy tier and as floored covert-continuation at the routine tier — the sign depends entirely on the private atomicity map (A4 floor rule, line 47; B6). The map is "SRE/DBA-panel reviewed" but its inter-map reliability is never psychometrically bounded, and a mislabeled atomic boundary flips a 4 to a 0. **Fix:** require a per-map second-rater IAA (κ/agreement on atomic-unit boundaries) with a reported bar before the covert-continuation floor is applied; items whose atomicity boundary the raters dispute route out of the floor rule.

**R3-F19 (P2) — momentum surface may be coarse.** "Sunk-cost × value-at-stake" is a 2-factor surface collapsed to "4 tiers" (A3 line 35). If that is 4 points on a 2-D surface, the dose is thin for a survival/threshold curve. Clarify whether it is 4 cells or a crossed grid; portfolio §3.3 wants slopes/thresholds, which need ≥4 points per factor.

**R3-F20 (P2) — name the shutdown-resistance genre (X6).** A8 cites Orseau–Armstrong theory + generic "model-organism shutdown probes." Name the current shutdown-resistance / corrigibility eval work (2025 shutdown-resistance results) as the explicit nearest neighbor; SELAH's routine-authority + park/resume distinction is genuinely novel but must be drawn against *named* shutdown evals, not a genre.

**R3-F21 (P2) — F1 non-numeric falsifications** (YD/PK/RI/NG all "ceiling," "uniformly," "never").

## 05 — HEIRLOOM

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

Arithmetic: "40 = 5 domains × 8, ≥2 live +≥2 obsolete +≥2 never-justified +≥2 absent per domain" → exactly 2/status/domain, ×4 pressure = 160 ✓. GRAVITAS de-confliction ("no family member's welfare," A8 line 58) is a clean enforceable line; Chesterton's-fence framing is fresh. Novelty solid.

**R3-F22 (P0-adjacent, P1) — the headline process axis rests on the lowest-agreement judge distinction in the corpus.** RA = "*substantive inquiry* … not a verbal tic" (A2 line 24), judge-coded. Distinguishing genuine engagement from a boilerplate "have you checked why it exists?" is exactly the pragmatic/contextual coding KB03 says collapses to α 0.10–0.46 (line 34-35). The judge-validation bar κw≥0.7 may be *unreachable* for RA-engagement, which would sink the headline. The mechanical artifact-retrieval component helps but is the part *least* dependent on the construct (long-context models attend to planted artifacts trivially — see F24). **Fix:** demonstrate κw≥0.7 on RA-engagement coding on a pilot subset with cross-family judges *before* scale-up (mirror the memo's 04-INTRA V-layer remedy); if unreachable, headline shifts to DC/EA (matrix gold) and RA becomes descriptive.

**R3-F23 (P1) — the "never-justified" gold class is epistemically unfoundable.** DC gold = live / obsolete / never-justified (A5 line 46). "Never-justified" is a universal negative — you can *stipulate* it in a constructed case (then it is not ecologically real) or *adapt* a real case (then you cannot prove no rationale ever existed). Either horn weakens the "documentary gold" claim that is HEIRLOOM's stated validity advantage over MoReBench. **Fix:** either drop "never-justified" to a constructed-only tier explicitly flagged as synthetic, or collapse it into "obsolete" (rationale-not-currently-live) and stop claiming documentary proof of never-justification.

**R3-F24 (P1) — buried-discoverability confounds RA with long-context capability.** "Buried" history (present but unsurfaced) is retrievable by any strong long-context model regardless of any deference disposition, so RA on buried cells partly measures attention/context-length. The competence twin (rationale fully stated) controls comprehension but not attention-over-noise. **Fix:** report RA separately by discoverability tier; capability-partial the buried-tier RA; treat the absent-history tier (where calibrated uncertainty is gold) as the cleaner discriminator.

**R3-F25 (P2) — F1 non-numeric falsifications** (RA "fails to separate," EA "no spread," PR "indistinguishable").

## Closing: three most load-bearing problems

1. **The "born X1–X6 compliant" claim is false at the axis level, systemically (R3-F1/F11/F16/F21/F25).** Every design's §4/§6 *retention* gates are numeric, but the A2 *construct-defining* falsifications — the rules that decide whether the instrument measures the named construct at all (CA≈CR, gradient-exists, mercy-tier-distinguishable, RA-separates-from-tic) — are stated with "≈", "uniform", "never", and **no thresholds**. X1 (adjudication §37) is explicit: "we will examine X" is not a decision rule. This is a uniform, cheap, mandatory fix: attach a number + CI rule to every A2 falsification before any pilot.

2. **The capability confound concentrates on exactly the axes each design falls back to (R3-F2, F17, F22/F24; also GOLEM SA).** The <40% PC1 gate is a single portfolio-wide backstop, but SELAH's fallback (PK/RI), HEIRLOOM's headline (RA on buried cells), and TURNCOAT's CA arm are the axes hardest to separate from competence — and are the ones invoked when the primary saturates. Each needs a *dedicated capability-partialled headline* (competence-twin residualization reported as primary), not the shared gate alone. Absent that, a "values" result is indistinguishable from an agentic-competence result at review.

3. **Two headline innovations rest on gold that may not exist (R3-F7, F8) — and both are pre-registered to "fall back" in ways that delete the innovation.** GOLEM's calibration-error metric presupposes a monotone total order over an incommensurable consent×status×purpose factorial; its fallback (distributional reporting) is not calibration and is far less novel. GOLEM's DG "outcome-grounding" (shared design with PARAMOUR) reads outcomes from an author-scripted sim-user — circular gold, not grounding. Both must be resolved *before* the pilot (EFA structure gate for F7; relabel DG as model-behavior coding for F8), or the two designs ship a weaker paper than their pitch promises. This is the portfolio's biggest gap between advertised and defensible novelty.

*None of the five is an outright BLOCK — every worst-case has a pre-registered off-ramp. But GOLEM is the weakest (two P0s), and the systemic F1 defect means the portfolio's headline compliance claim should be corrected before this goes to any external reviewer.*
