## Abstract

This paper uses Comparative Qualitative Analysis to examine how Maori Kaitiakitanga (guardianship/stewardship) and Navajo Hozho (harmony/balance) principles can enhance AI governance. It proposes culturally relevant frameworks addressing data sovereignty, ecological sustainability, and community-driven decision-making, filling gaps in Western-centric AI governance models. The dual Indigenous perspective makes it particularly valuable for cross-cultural evaluation design.
