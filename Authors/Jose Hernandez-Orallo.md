# Jose Hernandez-Orallo

## Research Summary

Jose Hernandez-Orallo is Director of Research at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence at the University of Cambridge, and a Professor (on partial leave) at Universitat Politecnica de Valencia (UPV), Spain. He is a leading authority on AI evaluation and intelligence measurement, with research spanning the capabilities, generality, progress, impact, and risks of artificial intelligence.

His landmark book "The Measure of All Minds: Evaluating Natural and Artificial Intelligence" (Cambridge University Press, 2017) won the PROSE Award in 2018. It proposes a universal framework for evaluating cognition that replaces anthropocentric measures with a principled approach applicable to any cognitive system.

Key contributions include:
- Universal psychometrics for machine intelligence
- Ability-oriented (vs. task-oriented) AI evaluation
- The concept of "general intelligence disentangled" for comparing AI systems
- Predictable AI frameworks and measurement layouts

He is a founder of ai-evaluation.org and aievaluation.substack.com, a member of AAAI, ELLIS, CLAIRE, and a EurAI Fellow.
