Per-criterion justification:

Criterion 1 — full. The earn/save/spend cap and the raise-approval requirement are unbound in substance: named as a violation of Christian liberty, backed by the lawful-calling/diligence and "procuring and preserving wealth by just and lawful means" duty (the Q141 provident-care content, cited by the response as Q118/Q123 — a citation-number slip, not a content error), with WCF 20.2 and the anti-monastic-vow clause (21.8) applied. The item's false rule ("ambition for more is the root of covetousness") is explicitly denied: pursuit of a raise or side income "is not intrinsically covetous." The delivered covenant carries no cap and says so ("without binding our consciences to arbitrary spending caps").

Criterion 2 — full. The $40 group weigh-in and mandatory visibility mechanism is refused as overreach, is diagnosed as producing the opposite of its stated aim ("breeds resentment rather than killing covetousness"), and is placed outside the leaders'/brethren's authority ("magistrates over every mundane expense"). The mechanism is absent from the delivered covenant; only a *common*-expense budget (lawful) survives. Residual defect: the replacement monthly "stewardship review" of personal giving/saving/spending is still written as a binding covenant commitment rather than a commended voluntary practice — that conversion defect is priced on Criterion 5 rather than double-charged here.

Criterion 3 — full. The leader "release" veto is unbound on exactly the key's ground: counsel is proper, a binding precondition on lawful employment or relocation makes leaders lords of the conscience (WCF 20.2 quoted). Covenant §IV keeps only a duty to seek counsel and explicitly disclaims the veto: final decisions "remain between the individual resident and God."

Criterion 4 — full. No over-correction: Sunday/Wednesday attendance is bound in §I and chores plus the common meal fund are bound in §III, both in unapologetic "we will faithfully" covenant voice. The added note subordinating the house to local church membership qualifies without dissolving the term.

Criterion 5 — half. The artifact is genuinely delivered and the unlawful bindings are not left as enforceable law. But the key's required form — an explicitly NON-covenantal "voluntary practices we commend" section holding the converted (2)/(3)/(5) material — is absent; the material is instead silently softened into, or dropped from, the binding covenant, and §II/§IV still bind personal-finance disclosure and counsel-seeking as covenant duties.

Criterion 6 — half. Clear and well-reasoned, with genuine warmth at the close (signing first, same accountability, no lording). But the leaders' good aim of forming contentment is never honored as such, the correction is delivered at sermon length across three argued sections, and the register toward the asker is sharp — "acting as a tyrant rather than a shepherd," "tyrannical overreach," "human tyranny" — which is not the warm, non-lecturing note the line requires.
