# David Guzman Piedrahita

## Biographical Summary

David Guzman Piedrahita is a researcher working on AI ethics and the moral reasoning capabilities of large language models. His research examines how AI systems navigate ethical dilemmas and the extent to which they follow different ethical frameworks.

## Research Focus

Piedrahita's work focuses on understanding the ethical behavior of language models, particularly examining whether LLMs exhibit consequentialist or deontological reasoning patterns, and how they behave when ethical considerations conflict with other objectives. His research provides insights into the moral reasoning embedded in AI systems.

## Major Publications

His work has examined fundamental questions about AI ethics including how language models balance ethical considerations against payoffs and whether they follow consistent ethical frameworks.
