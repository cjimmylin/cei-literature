# Shibani Santurkar

## Research Summary

Researcher at OpenAI working on building safe and reliable machine learning models. Previously completed postdoctoral work at Stanford University. Research focuses on adversarial robustness, understanding model behaviors, and developing evaluation frameworks for language models. Known for work on holistic evaluation of language models and investigating whose opinions language models reflect. Expertise in ML safety and alignment research.
