## Abstract

Introduces HateBRMoralXplain, a Brazilian Portuguese benchmark dataset with 7,000 Instagram comments annotated with hate labels, moral categories, token-level moral rationales, and socio-political metadata. Proposes SMRA framework for supervised moral rationale attention grounded in Moral Foundations Theory.

## Data Description

HateBRMoralXplain is a Brazilian Portuguese hate speech benchmark dataset of 7,000 Instagram comments annotated with hate labels, moral categories (based on Moral Foundations Theory), token-level moral rationales, and socio-political metadata. Code, models, and inter-annotator disagreements are also released.
