## Abstract

Vietnamese multimodal benchmark with 2,548 questions across 7 tasks. Tests visual understanding and reasoning in Vietnamese contexts including driving test scenarios (traffic safety) and cultural knowledge. Proprietary models achieve only 66% accuracy.

## Data Description

Releases VMMU, a Vietnamese multimodal benchmark of 2,548 questions spanning 7 domains (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, Driving Test, IQ Test), collected from official Vietnamese government exam sources and made available with code at vmmu-bench.github.io.
