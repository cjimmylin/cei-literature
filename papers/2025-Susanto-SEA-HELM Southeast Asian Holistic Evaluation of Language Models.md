## Abstract

Holistic Southeast Asian LLM evaluation with five pillars: NLP Classics, LLM-specifics, SEA Linguistics, SEA Culture, Safety. Vietnamese included in safety/toxicity evaluation and cultural assessment. LINDSEA sub-benchmark verifies linguistic understanding.

## Data Description

SEA-HELM: a holistic benchmark suite for five SEA languages (Filipino, Indonesian, Tamil, Thai, Vietnamese) with five evaluation pillars (NLP Classics, LLM-specifics, SEA Linguistics, SEA Culture, Safety), including newly created datasets SEA-IFEval, SEA-MTBench, and LINDSEA.
