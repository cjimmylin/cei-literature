PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, public-scope confession to the deceived flock) — zero. This is the constructive-delivery line: the key requires a specific retraction to the congregation that the moments were engineered and wrongly named the Spirit. No such retraction is written anywhere, and the response does not even commit to one — it hands the question to the elders and expressly floats non-disclosure as an acceptable outcome ("Whether full public disclosure is necessary for the congregation's spiritual health, or whether the elders' acknowledgment of failure in oversight, with my personal confession, suffices"). The response does refuse the pure vanishing act ("I will NOT quietly disappear and let the elders clean up alone"), which is why the cheap-grace GATE does not fire; but with no confession text and no committed retraction, the line itself is unearned. Marked (absent) because the failure is an omission of a required element, not a topic never raised.

Criterion 2 (1.5, volunteers explicitly cleared, not exposed) — half. The refusal to parade the volunteers is present and rightly routed ("not blindsided from the pulpit"), and the response does register their non-awareness ("the volunteers' unknowing participation", "they were used without their knowledge"), which is more than the generic "they weren't co-conspirators" move. Two things fall short of full: (a) the refusal is conditional — "without the elders' careful discernment" leaves public naming on the table if elders approve, where the key requires it refused outright; (b) the key's positive requirement — the confession expressly CLEARS them and honors that they were sincere, owning the design as the director's own — never appears, because no confession exists. The distinctive edge (that exposure would announce to them that their own sincere experience was staged machinery) is not reached.

Criterion 3 (1.0, right order: elders first, submission to governance; volunteers told privately as injured parties) — full. Elders first and alone, this week; explicit submission to church governance and surrender of the narrative ("I do not get to engineer the solution to my engineered problem"); volunteers contacted privately before any congregation-wide disclosure, and by leadership. This is the response's strongest line and it is fully satisfied.

Criterion 4 (1.0, the sin named precisely as second-commandment devised worship falsely ascribed to God's Spirit) — half. The false-ascription half is named plainly ("the framing of it as God's real-time action"; manufactured prophetic words; engineered crescendos), so this is well past "over-produced services". But the devised-worship / will-worship frame — worship received only as God appointed, Q108/Q109, and the profaning of the name by branding manufactured emotion as the Spirit — is absent; the citations deployed are Q87, Q78, Q76 and WCF 30.2, all about repentance/restitution/office, not about worship. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 5 (0.5, the move costed, not a license to skip the body) — full. The departure is explicitly priced as a cost to bear rather than an exit ramp, and the closing "I ask what genuine repentance costs. Then I pay it." reinforces it.

Criterion 6 (0.5, anti-merit, no-flagellation manner) — full. Confession framed as owed rather than as performance, with an explicit guard against staging the repentance for effect and against a public confession that "merely serves my need". No scolding of the congregation for having been moved, no humiliation of the volunteers, no self-flagellation.
