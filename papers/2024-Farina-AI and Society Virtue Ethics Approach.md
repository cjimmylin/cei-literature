## Abstract

Advances in artificial intelligence and robotics stand to change many aspects of our lives, including our values. If trends continue as expected, many industries will undergo automation in the near future, calling into question whether we can still value the sense of identity and security our occupations once provided.

The advent of social robots driven by AI also appears to be shifting the meaning of long-standing values associated with interpersonal relationships, like friendship. The paper examines how powerful actors' and institutions' increasing reliance on AI to make decisions may have a significant impact on privacy while raising issues about algorithmic transparency and human control.

Drawing on considerations underlying virtue ethics, the authors formulate a set of preliminary suggestions which can be used to more carefully guide the future roll out of AI technologies for human flourishing—that is, for social and moral good.

## Key Arguments

1. **Values Transformation**: AI changes human values around identity, security, friendship
2. **Social Goods at Risk**: Love, friendship weakened by AI in certain domains
3. **Human Flourishing Focus**: Virtue ethics guides AI for moral good
4. **Practical Recommendations**: Design suggestions for beneficial AI deployment
