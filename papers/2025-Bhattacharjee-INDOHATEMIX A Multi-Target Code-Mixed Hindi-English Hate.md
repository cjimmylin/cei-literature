## Abstract

Diverse code-mixed Hindi-English hate speech dataset from Koo platform capturing religious and political targeting with transliteration. LLMs significantly outperform traditional transformers. Hindi-English code-mixed hate speech with religious and political targeting; culturally contextualized.

## Benchmark

**Name:** INDOHATEMIX

## Data Description

INDOHATEMIX is a diverse Hindi-English code-mixed hate speech dataset from the Koo platform capturing multi-target (religious, political) hate with transliteration, designed as a benchmark to evaluate model robustness in multilingual hate speech detection.
