# Seth Lazar

## Research Summary

Professor of Philosophy at Johns Hopkins University (as of 2026), previously at Australian National University. Leads the Machine Intelligence and Normative Theory (MINT) Lab working on AI safety, governance, and resilience. Visiting faculty researcher at Google DeepMind, nonresident scholar of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, and distinguished research fellow at the University of Oxford Institute for Ethics in AI. Research bridges political philosophy, ethics of war, and AI ethics.
