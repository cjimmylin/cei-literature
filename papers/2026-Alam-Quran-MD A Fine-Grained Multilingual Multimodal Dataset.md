## Abstract

First holistic multimodal Quranic dataset integrating textual, linguistic, and audio data at verse and word levels. Includes Arabic text, English translations, phonetic transliterations, and verse-level audio from 32 reciters. Supports ASR, tajweed detection, Quranic TTS, multimodal embeddings, semantic retrieval, and personalized tutoring.

## Dataset Information

114 surahs, 6,236 ayahs (~77.8K words), audio from 32 reciters


## Data Description

Quran-MD is a fine-grained multilingual multimodal dataset combining Quranic text and audio data. The dataset is designed for research on Quranic text processing and speech recognition. This is a dataset contribution paper and does not evaluate any LLMs on the data.
