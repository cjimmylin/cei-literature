# Clara Vania

## Biographical Summary

Clara Vania is an Applied Scientist at Amazon Alexa, where she works on natural language understanding and machine learning. Her research focuses on applying ML techniques and exploring linguistic resources to help models understand the world's languages, with particular interest in low-resource and multilingual NLP.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Natural Language Understanding**: Building robust NLU systems for voice assistants and conversational AI.
- **Low-Resource NLP**: Developing methods that work well for under-resourced languages, particularly Indonesian.
- **Information Extraction**: Research on faithful and robust information extraction from web data.

### Significant Achievements
- **ACL 2023**: Co-authored "WebIE: Faithful and robust information extraction on the web"
- **Indonesian NLI Dataset**: Created challenging test-bed for Indonesian natural language inference with 18K sentence pairs
- **SEALP2023 Workshop**: Co-organized workshop on South East Asian Language Processing
- **2,145+ Citations**: Significant impact in NLP research

## Major Publications
- **WebIE: Faithful and robust information extraction on the web** (ACL 2023)
- **CrowS-Pairs: A Challenge Dataset for Measuring Social Biases in Masked Language Models** (EMNLP 2020)
- Indonesian NLI research incorporating numerical reasoning, idioms, and spatial/temporal reasoning
