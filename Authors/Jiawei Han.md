# Jiawei Han

## Research Summary

Jiawei Han is the Abel Bliss Professor of Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where he founded the Data Mining Group (DMG). He is one of the most influential figures in data mining, having co-authored the seminal textbook "Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques" which defined the field. He is an ACM Fellow and IEEE Fellow with numerous awards including the 2009 IEEE McDowell Award (highest technical award from IEEE).

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored "Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques" (3rd ed., 2011) - the definitive textbook in the field
- Created core data mining algorithms used worldwide
- 2004 ACM SIGKDD Innovations Award
- 2005 IEEE Computer Society Technical Achievement Award
- 2009 IEEE McDowell Award
- Founding Editor-in-Chief of ACM Transactions on Knowledge Discovery from Data
