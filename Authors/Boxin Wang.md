# Boxin Wang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Boxin Wang is a Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where he leads research on developing practical and scalable large language models while closing the trustworthiness gap. He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where he was supervised by Prof. Bo Li in the Secure Learning Lab. During his doctoral studies, he completed research internships at Google, NVIDIA, and Microsoft.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research vision centers on developing trustworthy large language models that are both practical and scalable. At NVIDIA, he has contributed to major projects including Nemotron-H, Cosmos-Reason1, and NVLM multimodal models, focusing on ensuring these systems are safe, reliable, and aligned with human values.

### Significant Achievements

Wang has made significant contributions to AI safety and trustworthiness research, with expertise in adversarial machine learning, model robustness, and secure deployment of large-scale AI systems. His work at NVIDIA on flagship LLM projects has helped advance the state of the art in building production-ready AI systems that maintain high standards of safety and reliability.
