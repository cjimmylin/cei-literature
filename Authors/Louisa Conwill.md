# Louisa Conwill

## Research Summary

Louisa Conwill is a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame. Her research focuses on human-computer interaction (HCI) and technology ethics. She co-authored the book "Virtue in Virtual Spaces: Catholic Social Teaching and Technology" published by Liturgical Press in September 2024. Her work examines the intersection of technology, ethics, and human values, with recent publications including "Do We Need Subsidiarity in Software?" scheduled to appear in the Proceedings of the ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems in April 2026.
