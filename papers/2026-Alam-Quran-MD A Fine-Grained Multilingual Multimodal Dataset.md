## Abstract

First holistic multimodal Quranic dataset integrating textual, linguistic, and audio data at verse and word levels. Includes Arabic text, English translations, phonetic transliterations, and verse-level audio from 32 reciters. Supports ASR, tajweed detection, Quranic TTS, multimodal embeddings, semantic retrieval, and personalized tutoring.

## Dataset Information

114 surahs, 6,236 ayahs (~77.8K words), audio from 32 reciters


## Data Description

QUR'AN-MD is a comprehensive multimodal dataset of the Quran integrating textual and audio dimensions at verse and word levels. It contains 114 surahs, 6,236 ayahs, approximately 77,800 words, verse-level audio from 30 reciters totaling roughly 665 hours, and word-level audio of approximately 22 hours. The dataset supports ASR, tajweed detection, text-to-speech synthesis, linguistic analysis, and digital Islamic studies applications.
