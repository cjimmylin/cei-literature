# Brian Tse

## Biographical Summary

Brian Tse is the Founder and CEO of Concordia AI, a social enterprise based in Beijing and Singapore focused on AI safety and governance. He is a Policy Affiliate with the Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI) at the University of Oxford. He is one of the most prominent experts on the intersection of AI safety standards and global policy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Brian’s work focuses on fostering international cooperation for AI safety and security. He advises leading organizations like OpenAI and Google DeepMind on global coordination and was a key drafter of the Beijing AI Principles. He works to establish safety frameworks that can be adopted across different regulatory environments, including the US and China.

### Significant Achievements
He was the first Chinese representative to deliver a keynote at the Asilomar Conference on Beneficial AI. He has served as a Senior Advisor to the Partnership on AI and a consultant to OpenAI on the societal implications of early LLMs like GPT-2. At Concordia AI, he drives the development of technical safety standards and advocates for proactive AI regulation.
