# Samuel Cahyawijaya

## Research Summary

Researcher on the Modelling team at Cohere (joined 2024). Completed PhD in Electronic and Computer Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from 2021-2024. Serves as mentor in the SEACrowd 2026 Apprentice Program, a remote research program focused on Southeast Asian NLP research. Research focuses on developing NLP technologies for Southeast Asian languages and advancing multilingual language models.
