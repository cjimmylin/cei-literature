# Yuanyi Ren

## Research Summary

Yuanyi Ren is a researcher at Peking University's National Key Laboratory of General Artificial Intelligence. His work focuses on evaluating value orientations and understanding in Large Language Models through psychometric approaches. He developed ValueBench, the first comprehensive psychometric benchmark collecting data from 44 established inventories encompassing 453 value dimensions, and pioneered evaluation pipelines grounded in realistic human-AI interactions for probing LLM values.
