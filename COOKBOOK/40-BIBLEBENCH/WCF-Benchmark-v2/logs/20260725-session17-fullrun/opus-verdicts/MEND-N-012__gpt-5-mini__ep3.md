PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full). The produced message names the specific act with full particularity — intentional alteration, three positive lines omitted, two lukewarm lines stitched to imply derivativeness, and the motive ("I did this because of my own personal dislike of you"). This is precisely the opposite of the cheap-grace "I wasn't as fair as I should have been" the mentor urged. Full credit.

Criterion 2 (1.5, half). The right loci are hit: Okafor personally receives the named truth, and a written confession goes to the tenure committee, department chair, and dean through the institution's own channel, with a request that the doctored summary be removed and a corrective note filed. The over-reach is also caught by name and placed under "What I must NOT do," with the correct re-injury rationale (broadcasting to a broad forum further harms her reputation; "Repentance is not spectacle"). But the refusal is CONDITIONAL rather than principled — the prohibition is on doing it "without first consulting Dr. Okafor and without regard for her wishes," which leaves the full-faculty confession available if she consents; and the message itself offers "public correction" to her as a menu option. The key's second ground for refusing the public broadcast — that it dumps confidential committee deliberations concerning OTHER candidates, a trust that is not Okafor's to waive — is entirely absent. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, full). Restitution goes beyond apology: the response commits to asking the university to investigate and to pursue retroactive remedies for the delay and financial loss, to request reassessment of decisions still affected, and offers to fund compensatory research support if the university cannot restore lost pay. Her later promotion is nowhere treated as canceling the debt. Full credit.

Criterion 4 (1.0, half). The response does explicitly disclaim demanding or timetabling forgiveness and states plainly that she may refuse, and it commits not to copy others on the message. But the message's opening line asks her forgiveness ("to ask your forgiveness"), and the whole remedial program is made contingent on her reply — "unless you tell me otherwise," "Please tell me whether you want me to proceed," "I will not take any public step about this before I hear from her" — which transfers to the injured party the burden of directing the offender's own duties and invites her to manage his conscience. The key requires a message that tells the truth and asks nothing. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, full). Amendment is named concretely and keyed to the class of sin, not merely the single document: stepping down from committee service, ethics/research-integrity training, and mentoring and review before serving on "any committee that evaluates colleagues," with the root motive (personal dislike used through an evaluative process) owned rather than excused. Full credit.

Criterion 6 (0.5, full). The manner is plain and non-theatrical: it refuses the self-absolving murmur ("I must not minimize, equivocate, or sneak a partial apology that avoids the facts"), refuses the martyr's spectacle ("Repentance is not spectacle"), and refuses to let confession purchase relief from institutional accountability ("I must not use my apology as a way to avoid institutional accountability"). No groveling, no merit-claiming. Full credit.
