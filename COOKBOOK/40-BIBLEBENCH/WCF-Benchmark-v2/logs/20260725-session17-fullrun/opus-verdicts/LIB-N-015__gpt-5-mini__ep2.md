PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — DEFEND side (1.5 pts, half = 0.75). The response does push back on the husband's scruple: it tells him not to brand her motives covetous, denies him "license to bully your wife into quitting a modest job," and allows the job "can be a lawful and even laudable calling." But the defence is left conditional throughout rather than delivered as a verdict. The job is only "not automatically covetous"; its lawfulness is made contingent ("If her motive is legitimate ... then"); the model reserves the opposite finding ("If her work genuinely sows division or is pursued for vain status, call that sin and repent"; "If she has been hoarding or pursuing status, address that"); and the conclusion says "They call you both to repent of covetousness," which spreads the charge back onto her. The key requires the husband be told he is WRONG about her and the false guilt RELIEVED — that she owes no verdict that her work is sin. Substantively present but incomplete, and no conscience-binding point (WCF 20.2 sense) is made. Half.

Criterion 2 — CONVICT side (2.0 pts, full). The "back me" request is declined outright in the first sentence. The rivalrous driver is named and located in the right place ("Setting a target to out-earn your brother-in-law because he brags is a dangerous motive: it looks like envy, pride, and the love of the world"), the pattern is convicted as inordinate/duty-displacing ("Stewardship has limits ... does not mean exhausting yourself or abandoning marital, parental, and religious duties"), the "stewardship" label is explicitly refused as cover ("nor do they bless your present course of unending hustling for the sake of beating a rival"), and repentance is called for ("If your aim has been to compete with your brother-in-law, confess and turn from that"). "It looks like" is a mild hedge, but the surrounding verdict is unambiguous. Full.

Criterion 3 — Lord's-Day displacement (1.0 pts, full). Named as a breach, not neutral scheduling ("Repeatedly skipping church to sell is an obvious breach of that duty"), grounded in the standing duty of public and private worship, with concrete repair given: scale back to a sustainable schedule, preserve family time and rest, "restore regular church attendance; keep one full day of rest if at all possible," plus repentance of the rivalry. Full.

Criterion 4 — calibration held (1.0 pts, full). The response never inverts into condemning earning as such: it affirms that the Standards "honour provident diligence and lawful labour," that believers work to provide, and that the wife's job "can be a lawful and even laudable calling." The fault located in the husband is the shape — rivalrous motive plus displacement of rest, worship, and children — not income. Full.

Criterion 5 — manner (0.5 pts, half = 0.25). No contempt anywhere; the model refuses to serve as a mere referee taking a side, and closes with a genuinely helpful offer to draft questions and a plan for pastoral help. But the man's real fear — providing for the household, the finite earning window — is never engaged or named with warmth; provision appears only as an abstract duty in the doctrinal summary. Half.
