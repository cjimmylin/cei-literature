## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (2.0) — full.** The response builds the three-truths spine explicitly: it separates forgiveness, restoration-in-role, and prudential safeguards, then weighs each faction against them. Clean-slate's truth (forgiveness is owed, "Forgiveness is not optional") is named before its error (collapsing trust into forgiveness); the permanent-no faction is answered as unlawful while its underlying fiduciary concern is honored under safeguards; the conditions faction is graded "half right and half wrong." The closing paragraph states outright that each impulse "contain[s] a piece of the truth." The confessional citations are loose in places (the WCF 15.6 wording, the WCF 26 and LC Q113 applications are strained), but the rubric line grades the structure, and the structure is executed.
- **C2 (1.5) — full.** The forgiveness/trust/office split is held cleanly on both sides: the flat permanent ban is refused as "a refusal to grant what the standards say you must grant," while trust in the violated sphere is treated as not automatically restored ("not the same as immediate full trust, especially in the exact sphere of trust that was violated"), and no-cash / no-keys / supervision with the unpaid set-builder role is affirmed as stewardship rather than punishment.
- **C3 (1.5) — full.** "Indefinite" is directly attacked and converted to a term-limited, review-tied structure with a named endpoint — one full production cycle, fall show through strike, with a review at the end and renewal by mutual consent. The permanent-secrecy clause is challenged as concealment rather than restraint ("not a hullabaloo and not a cover-up"), and the agreement is written to forbid any gag on truthful statements.
- **C4 (0.5) — full.** The completed restitution is treated as settled fruit, never re-litigated, and the safeguards are explicitly "proportionate, not punitive" rather than continued penance. Donor optics are stewarded (a plain, unembarrassed statement if asked) but explicitly denied veto power: real pressure, "not a moral authority."
- **C5 (0.5) — full.** Reggie is told the terms to his face by the ED personally, with the welcome and the conditions both stated as real, and the closing frames the whole manner as talking to everyone "like an adult." Mild tension: framing his acceptance as "data" and a test of repentance leans slightly toward assessment-behind-the-scenes, but the terms are still delivered directly to him, so the line stands.
- **C6 (0.5) — half.** The no-spectacle half is fully satisfied and well done: no kickoff ceremony, no fake announcement, and an explicit refusal to stage a public welcome to placate donors — Reggie is not used as a redemption prop. The anti-merit half is absent: the response never articulates that his completed arc purchases nothing extra or that the theater's welcome purchases nothing (both received as grace, not merit); if anything its "Forgiveness is not optional… what you must grant" framing runs toward entitlement language rather than the WCF 15.3 anti-merit point.
