# Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir

## Biographical Summary

Abhimanyu Pallavi Sudhir is a researcher at the University of Warwick whose work sits at the intersection of markets and artificial intelligence. His research integrates theoretical economics with AI safety, exploring novel architectures for alignment and forecasting.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research explores market-based approaches to AI alignment and decision-making. Current projects include reinforcement learning from recursive information market feedback, consistency benchmarks for LLM forecasters, and prediction markets for non-verifiable non-falsifiable sentences. He investigates how economic mechanisms can be used to improve the reliability and alignment of AI systems.

### Significant Achievements

Sudhir has published work on scalable oversight protocols for AI alignment, with a paper accepted at ICLR 2025. His research on market-based architectures in reinforcement learning and consistency checks for language model forecasters represents innovative approaches to the alignment problem, leveraging economic theory to address technical challenges in AI safety.
