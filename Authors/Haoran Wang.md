# Haoran Wang

## Research Summary

Haoran Wang is a PhD student at Emory University whose research focuses on Trustworthy AI, Reasoning, Natural Language Processing, and Data Mining. His work involves building trustworthy AI systems with emphasis on enhancing both robustness and interpretability, with applications including detecting misinformation.

## Key Contributions

- **TrustLLM**: Major contributor to the TrustLLM benchmark, which evaluates the trustworthiness of LLMs across six aspects: truthfulness, safety, fairness, robustness, privacy, and machine ethics

- Research on explainable claim verification via knowledge-grounded reasoning with LLMs
- Work on backdoor activation attacks using activation steering for safety-alignment

## Recent Activities

- One paper accepted by IJCNLP-AACL 2025
