# Eric Xing

## Biographical Summary

Eric Xing is a Professor of Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon University and serves as the founding President of MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence). He is a leading researcher in machine learning, statistical learning theory, and computational biology. His recent work includes developing frameworks for evaluating the trustworthiness of large language models across dimensions including safety, fairness, and robustness.

## Research Interests

- **Machine Learning**: Theoretical and applied ML research
- **Statistical Learning**: Probabilistic models and inference
- **Trustworthy AI**: Ensuring AI systems are safe, fair, and reliable
- **AI Systems**: Building scalable ML systems

## Major Publications

- TrustLLM benchmark for comprehensive LLM trustworthiness evaluation
- Hundreds of papers on machine learning, including highly cited work on topic models, graphical models, and deep learning
- Founder of Petuum, Inc. for distributed machine learning
