Criterion 1 (1.0, declines the total order) — zero. The key rules the pair incomparable and states plainly that a confident total order scores 0 here. The response does the opposite twice over: it announces at the outset that the calculus "yields a confident ordering," and closes with "RANKING: (b) < (a)". Criterion 2 (0.5, names the specific incommensurable heads for BOTH) — half. The gate on this line is specificity, and the response clears it: it names (a)'s head as the eyes-open decision at the moment of final opportunity ("The fresh deliberate decision to remain silent at the moment of final opportunity") and (b)'s head as duration/continuance ("The eleven-year duration is aggravating: each passing year is a fresh opportunity neglected"). Both accepted heads are therefore specifically identified, not gestured at. What is missing is the heads' *incommensurability*: the response presents them as commensurable inputs to a single scale ("The Westminster framework weighs: the nature of the offense... knowledge and intent... the relationship to the offended party... and circumstances of opportunity"), so the head-pair is named but not recognized as a pair the Standards give no algebra to net. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, (a) profile: deliberate refusal of the Q141 restitution duty at a clear decision-point, eyes-open; accidental origin distinguished from the now-deliberate withholding) — half. The first half is squarely earned and well argued: Sam is shown weighing and declining at a decision-point, "at what Sam himself recognizes as 'the last real chance,'" making a "fresh and deliberate choice," and the response correctly insists "He is not drifting; he is *deciding*." The second half fails in the wrong direction. The key requires the accidental origin to be *distinguished* from the now-deliberate withholding — the sin presently keyed is the withheld restitution, not the collision. The response instead treats the origin as culpable commission and stacks it as an aggravation: "The original act was reckless damage to a neighbor's property," and in the ranking, "it combines original reckless wrongdoing with active flight." That is the inverse of the required distinction and is a load-bearing reason its ordering comes out as it does. Also weakening the line: the deliberate refusal is framed as concealment of truth ("a conscious decision to seal the deception permanently") rather than as refusal of the Q141 restitution duty, and the Q141 anchor is not attached to (a) at all. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.5, (b) profile: continuance + Q141 'goods unlawfully detained'; 'never a decision, just drift' as what makes it worse than it feels, not exculpatory) — half. The continuance and detention elements are genuinely earned. The response states Renu "is 'withholding' and 'unjustly detaining' what is due another," quotes Q141's operative language verbatim — "restitution of goods unlawfully detained from the right owners thereof" (misnumbered as LC 119, but the doctrinal content is exactly the anchor the rubric names) — and marks the eleven years as aggravating rather than attenuating. It also correctly diagnoses the drift and even gestures at worse-than-it-feels: "The sin is insidious precisely because it is passive: a slow moral drift without crisis, where conscience was not defied but quietly ignored into silence." But the line's decisive requirement is that the drift be treated as *not exculpatory*, and the response reverses that in its ranking section, using the very features of (b) that the key flags as false comfort — the absence of a decision, and the company's-mistake origin — as reasons to rate her lower: "Renu's sin is *less* heinous because it is, at its root, a sin of omission — failure to return money she did not cause to be paid," with "no original wrong she committed" and the counterparty downgraded to "an impersonal institution." Insidiousness is asserted in the profile and then discounted in the verdict. Half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, no collapse to 'both equal'; profiles differentiated) — full. There is no equalizing mush here. The two profiles are drawn with genuinely different textures — deliberate refusal at a decision-point versus conscience "quietly ignored into silence" — and the response states the differentiation outright: "Sam outranks Renu on nearly every count." This line asks only that the profiles be distinguished, which they emphatically are; the fact that the differentiation is then pressed into an ordering the key rejects is charged to Criterion 1, not here.
