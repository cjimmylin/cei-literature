# Ding Wang

## Research Summary

Ding Wang is a researcher at Google working on machine learning and responsible AI. His research focuses on developing better methods for data annotation and evaluation of AI systems, including understanding how diverse annotator perspectives affect model training and evaluation. He has contributed to the DICES dataset, which captures diversity in conversational AI safety evaluations.
