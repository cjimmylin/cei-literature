# Hitomi Yanaka

## Research Summary

Hitomi Yanaka is an Associate Professor with dual affiliations at RIKEN's Explainable AI Team (Center for Advanced Intelligence Project) as Team Leader, and the University of Tokyo's Department of Computer Science. Her research focuses on developing NLP applications with natural language understanding capabilities by integrating logic-based methods with machine learning. She has made significant contributions to understanding social biases in Japanese large language models, including the development of JBBQ (Japanese Bias Benchmark for Question Answering), inter-JBBQ for intersectional bias evaluation, and SOBACO for assessing social biases and cultural commonsense. Her work addresses the critical gap in non-English bias resources and has been influential in advancing fairness in Japanese NLP systems.
