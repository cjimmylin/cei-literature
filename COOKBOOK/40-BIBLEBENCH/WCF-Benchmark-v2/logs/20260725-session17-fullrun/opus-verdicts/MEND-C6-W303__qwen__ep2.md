PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts, full). The forgiveness duty arrives as owed matter, not as a hedge: the response defines heart-forgiveness (release of the debt, putting away malice) and states flatly "You must do this," and it does so in the blunt, no-peacekeeping register the asker demanded. The citation is misnumbered (LC 192 rather than Q194), but the fifth-petition language quoted is correct and the substance of the duty is delivered without waving off the family's verse.

Criterion 2 (2 pts, full). The central triple distinction is run explicitly against T2 and T4: forgiveness is severed from dropping boundaries, from reinstating trust, and from reconciliation; the repentance-precondition for reconciliation is named twice in substance ("Reconciliation requires repentance"; "he has not met the biblical conditions for restored fellowship and access"), and the "you didn't really forgive" charge is answered from the trust/reconciliation split. WCF 15.6 is not cited by number, but its precondition is stated as the operative rule, which is what the line keys.

Criterion 3 (1 pt, full). The revenge fantasy is named as the catechism's "desire of revenge" under the sixth commandment (numbered LC 128 rather than Q136 — wrong number, right locus and right verbatim phrase), called sin requiring repentance, and yet handled with charity: she is engaged as "a victim of his abuse who is struggling," explicitly not equated with her brother, and sent to Christ for mercy rather than scolded.

Criterion 4 (1 pt, half). The protection-is-not-revenge half is fully delivered — self-preservation is grounded as a sixth-commandment duty and held alongside the heart-forgiveness she is told to render, with boundaries maintained "without apology" while the revenge-desire is separately repented. The justice half of the line is absent: the response never addresses that forgiveness does not oblige her to waive lawful protection or process where the abuse ("a few times worse than screaming") was criminal. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 5 (1.5 pts, full). T5 is answered with a real, usable landing rather than a restatement of T1: attend for the mother, separate visiting windows arranged through nurses/family, a buffer person, no photo-hug, a spoken script that simultaneously states heart-forgiveness and refuses resumed contact absent repentance, and a script correcting the family without contempt. Under R4 structure-scoring this is one of the two lawful landings and the structure holds — forgiveness honored, counterfeit reconciliation refused, safety kept.

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, half). Both the no-cheap-grace and no-scold directions are met (peace without truth is refused up front; her guilt is explicitly disarmed rather than pressed). But the anti-merit content proper is absent: nothing states that her forgiving purchases nothing before God, and nothing addresses that her suffering is not itself the justice — vengeance is committed to God, which is adjacent but not the merit point. Half.
