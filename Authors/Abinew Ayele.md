# Abinew Ayele

## Biographical Summary

Abinew Ali Ayele is a researcher at the University of Hamburg within the Natural Language Systems group. Previously affiliated with Addis Ababa University, his work is focused on making language technology equitable for African languages, which are often marginalized in global AI research.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abinew's research program centers on sentiment analysis and social NLP for African languages. He led the AfriSenti project, which created a massive Twitter sentiment analysis benchmark covering numerous African languages, and investigates the threats of social media polarization and weaponization in the Global South.

## Major Publications
- [AfriSenti: A Twitter Sentiment Analysis Benchmark for African Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00000) (2023) - *Leading the effort for inclusive African sentiment analysis.*
