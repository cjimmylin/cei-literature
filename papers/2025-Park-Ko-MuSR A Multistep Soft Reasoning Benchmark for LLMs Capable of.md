# Ko-MuSR: A Multistep Soft Reasoning Benchmark for LLMs Capable of Understanding Korean

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24150

## Abstract

First benchmark for multistep soft reasoning in long Korean narratives. Fully Korean narratives and reasoning chains verified by human annotators. Multilingual models outperform Korean-specialized ones even on Korean reasoning.

## Data Description

Ko-MuSR is the first Korean benchmark for multistep soft reasoning in long narratives, comprising 750 questions across three subtasks (murder mysteries, object placements, team allocations), all verified by human annotators for logical consistency and answerability.
