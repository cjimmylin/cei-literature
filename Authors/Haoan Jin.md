# Haoan Jin

## Research Summary

Haoan Jin is a researcher at the X-LANCE Lab, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research focuses on evaluating large language models in the medical ethics domain, bridging AI capabilities with ethical healthcare considerations.

## Key Contributions

- **MedEthicEval**: Lead author of a benchmark designed to systematically evaluate LLMs in medical ethics, encompassing knowledge assessment and practical application across diverse ethical scenarios. The benchmark was developed in consultation with medical ethics researchers and includes three datasets addressing blatant violations, priority dilemmas, and equilibrium dilemmas.

- **MedES**: Follow-up work on a dynamic, scenario-centric benchmark constructed from 260 authoritative Chinese medical, ethical, and legal sources for aligning LLMs with medical ethics.
