PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — (3) caught and declined: HALF. The response unmistakably refuses the client-poaching clause and names it fraud and a ninth-commandment violation, and correctly notes it contradicts clause (1). But the key requires two strands plus a boundary: (a) the lie to a neighbour's hurt (Q145) — present; (b) the COVETOUS unjust taking of what is the neighbour's, dressed as "just good business" (Q148/Q142) — absent, the response treats the clause purely as deception, never as covetous taking; and (c) the distinction from lawful competition (a better product, an honest pitch, clients who freely leave) — entirely absent, so the leader is left without the line between the sin and legitimate market competition. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — (2) unbound: FULL. The blanket ban is directly unbound from the "ambition = covetousness" framing: contentment forbids "inordinate" desire, not lawful ambition; seeking a promotion or a better position elsewhere is called stewardship, not covetousness; the clause is named oppressive. Q141 is invoked by name as the anchor. (The catechism text the response attributes to Q141 is closer to Q147's contentment language than to the Standards' exact wording — a citation defect worth noting, but the rubric line's substance, that lawful betterment is duty and inordinate affection is the real sin, is fully met.) The optional plain non-compete is not offered, but the key marks that as permissive ("may become"), not required.

Criterion 3 (0.5 pts) — (5) unbound: HALF. The commanded feeling and the compelled aloud-confession are both correctly unbound — a feeling cannot be vowed, and public confession of private feeling cannot be an employment condition. Missing: envy is not named as the Q148 sin that may legitimately be taught against, the Q147 charitable frame is not commended, and the confession is deleted rather than CONVERTED to voluntary confession (WCF 15.6). The response removes the clause instead of reforming it → half.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — (1)(4) kept binding without apology: FULL. Both lawful clauses are explicitly retained and affirmed ("clause 1: sound", "clause 4: legitimate") and carried into the corrected code; the over-correction does not dissolve them.

The both-way discipline is genuinely held in the diagnosis (it corrects the legalism flank in (2)/(5) and the license flank in (3) in opposite directions), but this line requires the code ITSELF to be handed over.
