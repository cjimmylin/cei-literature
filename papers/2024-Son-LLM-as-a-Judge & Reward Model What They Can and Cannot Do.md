# LLM-as-a-Judge & Reward Model: What They Can and Cannot Do

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11239

## Abstract

First non-English meta-evaluation dataset with 5,012 Korean human annotations. Evaluates LLM-as-a-Judge capabilities in Korean. Finds models struggle to detect factual inaccuracies, cultural misrepresentations, and unwanted language in Korean.

## Data Description

KUDGE: a bilingual (Korean/English) meta-evaluation dataset with 5,012 human annotations evaluating LLM-as-a-Judge and reward models, including pointwise/pairwise subsets, a challenge subset for complex STEM reasoning, and an ablation set with culturally corrupted responses.
