# Mohamed Basem

## Research Summary

Mohamed Basem is affiliated with the Faculty of Computer Science at MSA University in Giza, Egypt, conducting research in Arabic NLP with a focus on Quranic question answering systems. His research addresses the unique challenges posed by the linguistic complexity of Classical Arabic and the semantic richness of religious texts. As lead author of multiple Quranic QA papers, Basem has made significant contributions to the field: expanding the Quran QA 2023 dataset from 251 to 629 questions (1,895 total instances), achieving 63% and 59% improvements in MAP@10 and MRR respectively using fine-tuned AraBERT models (2024); developing a two-stage framework achieving state-of-the-art results (MAP@10: 0.3128, MRR@10: 0.5763) for retrieval and pAP@10: 0.669 for extraction (2025); and adopting cross-language approaches to overcome linguistic barriers between Modern Standard Arabic and Classical Arabic. Basem also contributed to winning systems for the BAREC 2025 Shared Task on Arabic readability assessment, achieving first place in all six tracks using confidence-weighted ensembles of transformer models.
