# Laura Weidinger

## Research Summary

Laura Weidinger is a Staff Research Scientist at DeepMind (Google DeepMind), focused on evaluating AI systems for ethics and safety. She developed a widely used taxonomy of ethical and social harms from generative AI and leads work on red teaming and other methods for safety evaluation. Her research takes a sociotechnical approach to AI safety evaluation, with emphasis on risks to people and early risk assessment methods aimed at making AI systems safer and creating a more accountable AI innovation ecosystem. She has contributed to DeepMind flagship work on Gopher, Sparrow, YouTutor and Gemini.
