# Scott Clifford

## Research Summary

Professor of Political Science at Texas A&M University. Research focuses on public opinion and political psychology, with emphasis on moral psychology and how moral foundations influence political attitudes and behaviors. Co-editor of the Journal of Experimental Political Science alongside Jennifer Jerit from Dartmouth College. Expertise in experimental methods and survey research in political science.
