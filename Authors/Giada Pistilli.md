# Giada Pistilli

## Biographical Summary

Giada Pistilli is a philosopher specialized in AI ethics, focusing on conversational agents and generative AI. She holds a PhD in Philosophy from Sorbonne Universite - CNRS, exploring the ethics of conversational AI at the intersection of moral philosophy and machine learning. Currently at Mistral AI focusing on model behavior and cultural/multilingual alignment, she previously spent nearly four years as Principal Ethicist at Hugging Face, leading interdisciplinary research on AI ethics in open source environments.

## Research & Impact

Pistilli examines how cultural values and social norms shape AI systems:

- **CIVICS Dataset**: Lead author on research creating datasets for evaluating AI systems on culturally sensitive topics.

- **Cultural Alignment**: Studies how AI systems transition from simple tools to entities occupying social and relational roles, including AI companionship.

- **Open Source AI Ethics**: Pioneered ethical frameworks for open-source AI development at Hugging Face, including "Stronger Together: On the Articulation of Ethical Charters, Legal Tools, and Technical Documentation in ML" (FAccT 2023).

- **Consent in AI**: Research on the evolution of consent from medical ethics to AI contexts, published on Hugging Face blog.

Her work has been featured in Nature, The New York Times, The Washington Post, MIT Technology Review, Wired, and Bloomberg.
