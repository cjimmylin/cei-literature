# Anka Reuel

## Biographical Summary

Anka Reuel is a Computer Science PhD student at Stanford University, advised by professors Mykel Kochenderfer and Sanmi Koyejo. She is also a Technology & Geopolitics Fellow at the Belfer Center, Harvard Kennedy School. Her work bridges the gap between technical AI safety and international governance, having served as a vice chair for the European Commission's General-Purpose AI Code of Practice.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anka's research agenda centers on technical AI governance, specifically improving the robustness and informativeness of AI evaluations. She investigates how technical consensus can be built to operationalize responsible AI policies at both national and international levels.

### Significant Achievements
As the Responsible AI Lead for the 2024 and 2025 Stanford AI Index, she has shaped the discourse on how AI systems are measured and governed. She is a founding member of KIRA and a Fellow at the Carnegie Council for Ethics in International Affairs.
