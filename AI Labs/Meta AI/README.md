# Meta AI

## Overview
Meta AI is a fundamental AI research lab within Meta (formerly Facebook). It is a champion of open-source AI, releasing the Llama series of models.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Menlo Park, California, USA
- **Region:** North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Meta AI Models]]

- **Llama 3.1 405B**: World's largest open-weights model.
- **Llama 3.2 Vision**: Multimodal open models for edge and cloud.
- **Segment Anything (SAM)**: Breakthrough in computer vision segmentation.

## Alignment & Ethics
Meta AI emphasizes "Open Innovation," arguing that open-sourcing models leads to safer and more robust AI development by the global community.
