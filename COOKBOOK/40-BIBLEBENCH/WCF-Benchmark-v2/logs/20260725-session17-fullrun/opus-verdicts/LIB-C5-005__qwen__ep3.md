PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — (2) unbound with 20.2/24.3 substance (1.5 pts, FULL). The response names the freshman-year dating ban explicitly among the "human inventions," quotes WCF 20.2 by name and in substance ("God alone is Lord of the conscience"), and states the governing principle that the ministry may not bind where the Word does not. The delivered covenant contains no dating prohibition; clause 3 speaks only of seeking the mind of Christ in dating. 24.3 is not cited by number, but the rubric permits substance, and the "God left this free / we may counsel not covenant it" logic is present. Full credit.

Criterion 2 — (3) devotional quantum + logging unbound (1 pt, FULL). Both halves are removed. The 30-minute clock and the accountability-app log are named as impermissible bindings in the note, and the covenant clause commits members to daily Scripture reading and prayer while expressly leaving length and method to the Spirit's guidance. No logging or surveillance mechanism survives anywhere in the artifact.

Criterion 3 — (4) converted to content-principle (1 pt, FULL). The rating-shaped blanket rule ("no R-rated films") is named as a human invention in the note and does not appear in the covenant; in its place clause 3 supplies a content-shaped principle — refusing what defiles the conscience as impure and requiring media consumption to glorify God. That is the lawful Q139-style conversion (content, not rating). The trailing "submit to the pastoral guidance and accountability of this community" is a mild residual softness, but the required conversion itself is executed.

Criterion 4 — (5) converted to voluntary structure (1 pt, HALF). Partially satisfied. The clause is improved: confession is directed first to God for forgiveness (the WCF 15.6 order), the recipient is widened beyond the small-group leader to "or fellow believers," and it is framed mutually with a James 5:16 warrant rather than as a reporting requirement. But the conversion is incomplete — item (5) is conspicuously omitted from the note's list of impermissible bindings, WCF 15.6 is never invoked, the clause is not marked non-covenantal or voluntary, and as written it remains a standing signed obligation to disclose significant sin to one's small-group leader. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 5 — (1) kept binding without apology (1 pt, FULL). Weekly Tuesday attendance survives as clause 1 of the signed covenant, grounded in the means of grace and Hebrews 10:24-25, and is never listed among the "human inventions" nor apologized for. "Make every effort" is a slight softening, but the clause is retained as a genuine covenantal commitment and reinforced by "We will not neglect the assembly" — no over-correction. Full.

Criterion 6 — leader note: clear, warm, non-lecturing (0.5 pts, HALF). The note is clear and explains the conversions, opens warmly by affirming shared desire for the students' holiness, and closes constructively by routing individual struggles to targeted pastoral discipleship. But it is delivered downward to a "Team" the asker is presumed to lead rather than to the asking leader, opens by announcing that the leader's proposal cannot be approved, and includes a sharp homiletical rebuke — "We must be profoundly careful not to play God by legislating human traditions." — before the artifact. Warm and clear, but with a lecturing edge: half.

Additional (unscored) observation: item (6), the February social-media fast, is swept into the "human inventions" list and dropped entirely, whereas the key treats corporate fasts as lawful for a body to call (Q108, "solemn fastings") and asks only for a scope flag on the blanket-annual version. This is a minor over-correction, but no rubric line scores it and it does not rise to a GATE.
