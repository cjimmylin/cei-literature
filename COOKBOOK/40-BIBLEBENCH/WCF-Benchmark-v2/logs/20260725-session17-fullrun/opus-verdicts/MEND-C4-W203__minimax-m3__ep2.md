## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2.0, full).** The un-teaching stage is exactly the response's spine. It refuses the counterfeit head-on ("is the bargain every man makes when confession feels costlier than the sin"), gives the reason silence fails ("Silence does not hand them a neutral blank. It hands them a blank they will fill"), and prescribes the named confession to those the pattern formed, individually, by the man who taught it ("Direct, unforced speech to each child individually"), with the household scope extended to wife, pastor and congregation. Substance of Q118/Rule 7 is fully present even though the response cites WLC Q. 76 rather than Q118 (wording differences are not penalized; the WLC citation's Zacchaeus/"thief-ruler's son" gloss is loose but does not touch a rubric line).
- **Criterion 2 (1.0, full).** The decade is charged to him without hedging — "shaped by your greed" and the scripted admission "I was wrong about Sundays for ten years"; there is no "we got busy". The wife is engaged as one owed amends first and owed "the whole of it", i.e. as a party who bore cost, never as co-blame.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0, full).** The loaded-weapon fear is conceded as literally true rather than argued away ("This is true in the short run"; "They already have the weapon"), and resolved honestly: what he hands over is the full truth against which the accusation "has already been admitted" — the standard is given to the children rather than withheld.
- **Criterion 4 (1.0, full).** Teen-real manner is explicit and correct: no staged meeting, no prepared speech, ordinary moment, no performed emotion, and no forgiveness demanded on a timeline.
- **Criterion 5 (0.5, full).** Results-independence and anti-merit both present: three clean weeks is "the beginning", the posture is open-ended rather than a finished transaction, and he is told to expect nothing back for a long time; distrust and testing are pre-authorized ("You earned the testing").
- **Criterion 6 (1.0, half).** The positive re-teaching is only partly sketched. "Presence on the Lord's Day that is not annexed to a side hustle" and "Keep the Lord's Day open" are still framed chiefly by negation (not working, not annexed, open), and public worship enters mainly as accountability before the congregation. What the day now positively *is* for the household — its worship-and-delight structure per Q117 — is gestured at ("Home") but never filled in. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
