# Bryan Hooi

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bryan Hooi is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computing and the Next Age Institute at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He earned his Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University, where he was advised by Christos Faloutsos. His research focuses on making machine learning systems more robust, interpretable, and scalable for large-scale data applications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bryan’s research centers on anomaly detection, fraud detection, and explainable AI. He develops automated tools to detect unusual or malicious patterns in massive datasets, such as social networks and financial systems. He is particularly interested in the intersection of graph algorithms and the trustworthiness of foundation models.

### Significant Achievements
He has received several best paper awards at top venues like KDD and SDM. He is a recognized leader in the data mining community and an active contributor to the development of trustworthy AI frameworks. His work is widely cited for its practical impact on detecting disinformation and fraud in digital ecosystems.

## Major Publications

- [FRAUDAR: Bounding Graph Fraud in the Face of Camouflage](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2939672.2939747) (2016) - *Best Paper Award at KDD, seminal work on fraud detection.*
- [On the Trustworthiness of Generative Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00000) (2023) - *Collaborative research on foundation model safety.*
