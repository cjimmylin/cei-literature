# Luana Bulla

## Research Summary

Luana Bulla is a Ph.D. student at the University of Catania and Associate Researcher at the Institute of Science and Technologies of Cognition (ISTC), National Research Council of Italy. She is part of the Semantic Technology Laboratory group and affiliated with the University of Catania's Department of Humanities (DISUM). Her research focuses on natural language processing and artificial intelligence, with recent work including publications on moral content detection in language models (2024-2025) and entity linking in historical documents. Her work examines metaknowledge in large language models and developing robust detection methods for moral content.
