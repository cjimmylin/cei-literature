# Ece Kamar

## Biographical Summary

Ece Kamar is a Principal Researcher and Research Manager at Microsoft Research in Redmond, Washington. Her research focuses on building reliable AI systems that work effectively with humans, studying human-AI complementarity, and understanding AI system failures. She has made significant contributions to crowdsourcing, citizen science, and AI fairness, including work on the Galaxy Zoo project and research on algorithmic fairness in machine learning systems.

## Research Interests

- Human-AI collaboration and complementarity
- AI fairness, reliability, and failure analysis
- Crowdsourcing and citizen science
- Multi-agent coordination
- AI safety evaluation and testing
