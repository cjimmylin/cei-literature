# Alon Jacovi

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alon Jacovi is the Research Director at AI2-Israel and an incoming faculty member at Bar-Ilan University. He is a leading researcher in the field of Explainable AI (XAI), focusing on the theoretical and practical aspects of how we can trust and understand the decisions of complex neural networks.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alon's work addresses the "crisis of trust" in AI interpretability. He has challenged the field to define "faithfulness" more rigorously, arguing that many popular explanation methods are misleading or superficial. His research is essential for AI safety, as it provides the critical tools needed to audit the internal reasoning of models before they are deployed in high-stakes environments.
