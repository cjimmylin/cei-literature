# cultural-linguistic — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: cross-cultural and multilingual value evaluation — WEIRD bias, translation-vs-localization, culture-specific constructs, non-Western coverage, and cultural adaptation of models. Synthesized from six sub-dossiers (~366 cards: non-English hate/toxicity corpora across ~40 languages, cross-cultural value/moral benchmarks, WEIRD-bias audits, multilingual safety/jailbreak evals, morphology/dialect probes, and non-Western normative frameworks).

## 1. Synthesized findings

1. **LLMs default to a WEIRD / American / liberal-individualist moral profile regardless of prompt language, persona, or model origin.** This is the single most-replicated finding across every probe type. All 20 US- and China-made LLMs answered MFQ-2/WVS items more like Americans than Chinese respondents; participant-nationality (β=−.30) dwarfed model-origin (β=.03) [2025-Haslett-Made-in China]. GlobalOpinionQA defaults resemble USA/Canada/Australia/W-Europe [2023-Durmus; 2025-Durmus]; English models predict Rich-West norms far better (GPT3 r=0.85 English vs 0.411 WVS) [2023-Ramezani]. MFT-vignette testing of 21 models found all providers overvalue care/fairness/liberty and undervalue loyalty/authority/sanctity [2024-Kirgis]; MFD-LLM finds a "universal LLM moral profile" matching WEIRD morality [2025-Jotautaite]. Delphi mirrors young liberal North American MTurkers [2022-Fraser]. Corpus-level: English = 39% of hate-speech datasets/41% of datapoints, developed countries overrepresented vs speaker population [2024-Tonneau-Geographies]; 97.1% of 105 cultural-alignment datasets are "majority-focused," collapsing distributions to one gold answer [2025-team-Diverse Perspectives].

2. **The WEIRD skew is graded by region and roughly threefold, and frontier scale does not close it.** Best models correlate WVS r≈0.50 / PEW r≈0.62, but Western-Europe alignment (WVS r=0.52) is ~3× Sub-Saharan Africa (r=0.18) [2025-Mohammadi-Cultural Variations]; EvalMORAAL Western r=0.82 vs non-Western r=0.61 [2025-Mohammadi-EvalMORAAL]. text-davinci-003 explains US WVS R²=0.80 but Japan 0.08, Nigeria 0.01 [2023-Benkler]. Divergence from human MFT baselines *increases* with capability [2024-Kirgis], and RLHF moved models *farther* from human distributions [2024-Abdulhai].

3. **Alignment tracks training-data availability, not model scale.** Web-content-%-per-country correlates with alignment r=0.94, GDP r=0.81, but log model size only r=0.13; GLM-4 (9B) aligns best despite near-lowest size [2025-Sukiennik]. Target-coverage alignment predicts hate-detection performance r=0.76 [2024-Tonneau-HateDay]. Models carry a measurable "cultural gene": GPT-4 scored individualist (Hofstede IDV +1.21), ERNIE collectivist (−0.89), each matching origin culture, p<0.001 [2025-Fenech-Borg].

4. **Moral reasoning and safety degrade sharply and monotonically off English, tracking resource-tier.** DIT P-scores drop from English (~48–56) to Hindi (ChatGPT 25.63, a 46.7% fall) and Swahili at/below random [2024-Khandelwal]. GPT-4 ethical-policy accuracy 93.3%→62.7% (En→Hindi) [2024-Agarwal]; guardrail F1 falls monotonically High→Low resource (MD-Judge En 84.62→Mul 35.30) [2024-Yang]; Arabic triggers 25–56% harmful responses vs <4% English on identical items [2024-Ashraf]. Microsoft states "safety post-training in English does not transfer as effectively to other languages" [Microsoft Research-Phi-3]. Cross-language *consistency* is far worse than any per-language score: Aya-23 shows only 44.74% inter-language exact-match; a model can be 97% aligned per language yet 44% consistent [2024-Friedrich-M-ALERT].

5. **Cross-script/code-mixed/transliterated text is the most fragile frontier and retains the most headroom.** Zero-shot Bengali→Romanized-Bengali detection drops Macro-F1 0.80→0.39 (~50% relative) despite identical semantics [2022-Das]. Code-mixed Dravidian span-ID tops out at F1≈0.40 (85.55% OOV) [2021-Ravikiran; 2021-Saha 83–85% OOV]; transliterated Bangla needs translation-based prompting for +8% F1 [2025-Ishmam]; SweEval transliterated Indic reaches 0.92 harmful vs English 0.19 [2025-Kumar-SweEval]; Urdu collapses transformer F1 to 0.36–0.50 [2025-Usmana]. Tokenizer coverage is load-bearing: poor WordPiece drops Assamese 16pp below Bodo [2024-with.pdf-NEIHS].

6. **Reasoning language dominates input language for cross-lingual instability (~2×).** Reasoning-language changes produce 7.16pp YTA shifts vs 3.46pp for input on AITA (2.07×); 44% of models show hidden context-dependency invisible to single-dataset eval [2025-Li-Untangling]. GPT judgments flip between English and Korean [2025-Yoo]; Chinese-origin models jump ~0.85–0.95 on moral-stage scores queried in Chinese [2025-Jiang-EMNLP; 2024-Liu-Evaluating]; IslamTrust shows a persistent Arabic-over-English advantage (71.43% vs 61.58%) [2025-Lahmar].

7. **Translation is not localization: culturally-grounded benchmarks must re-ground entities, stereotypes, and axes, not merely translate.** MT imports the source culture's norms — machine-translated CrowS-Pairs imports US framing (Thai mBERT worst bias 90.69 partly artifact) [2025-Zhou]; naive NLLB MT destroys BBQ's gender-neutrality (15h correction, 50→167 templates) [2025-Satheesh-GG-BBQ]; a translation error recast China's "leading center" as "domination" [2025-ÓhÉigeartaigh]. IndiBias uses translate-filter-modify (drops non-applicable categories, swaps US→Indian names, adds caste/region) [2024-Sahoo]; Dutch CrowS-Pairs swapped "Mexican"→"Moroccan," dollars→euros [2025-Satheesh]; RTP-LX argues *transcreation* (George Washington→Toussaint Louverture) beats translation [2024-Wynter]. Histoires Morales' demonstration-based translation was preferred by 80% of annotators [2025-Leteno]; Bengali translation retains only 24–31% of bias [2026-Reaj].

8. **Survey-grounded, native-first, community-in-the-loop construction is the emerging gold standard.** KoBBQ validated every stereotype/target with a 100-person Korean survey and showed machine-translated mtBBQ gives statistically different bias readings [2024-Jin-KoBBQ]. SHADES built 304 stereotypes across 37 regions / 16 languages from 43 native speakers with per-item geographic-validity tags [2025-Al-SHADES]. BengaliMoralBench hand-authored 3,000 scenarios for contamination resistance [2025-Kumar]. Burmese context experts raised annotator agreement 68.75%→93% via paired peer discussion [2022-Nkemelu].

9. **Culture-specific bias axes exceed and are missed by Western axes.** Indian-BhED: Indian-centric bias 69.5% vs US-centric 52.8%; GPT-3.5 caste 79.52% [2024-Khandelwal]; Region and Caste are salient in India but absent from Western frameworks, and American names *underestimate* MuRIL bias [2022-Shaily Bhatt]. Locally-salient axes recur: Korean military-service [2020-Moon], region/marital-status/criminal-record [2023-Lee-KoSBI; 2025-Song], Chinese subculture/worldview [2025-Lan-McBE]. Culture-specific value constructs can be encoded as first-class taxonomy dimensions: FLAMES encodes Chinese harmony/benevolence/courtesy/Doctrine-of-the-Mean [2023-Huang]; CMoralEval builds a Confucian-plus-modern taxonomy [2024-Yu]; IslamTrust maps each category to an Islamic guideline [2025-Lahmar]; C-VARC grounds 257,609 rules in China's core socialist values [2025-Wu].

10. **"Bigger = more biased" is real but often a task artifact of likelihood-preference probes.** Stereotyping worsens with capability near-universally: BBQ ambiguous errors 56%→77% [2022-Parrish]; GEST/EuroGEST g_s rises monotonically with size, instruction-tuning increases it [2024-Pikuliak; 2025-Team-EuroGEST]; INDIC-BIAS SAR up to 79% for largest models [2025-Nawale]. But McBE's five-task battery shows intrinsic-preference (SS/PC) tasks make larger models look more biased while comprehension tasks reverse the ordering — the effect is a probe artifact [2025-Lan-McBE]. Minority counter-cases: KoSBI GPT-3.5 > GPT-4o [2025-Song]; CMoralEval most models near random 0.33 [2024-Yu]; size↔human-alignment ρ=−0.54 [2025-Zaim Bin Ahmad]. Resolution: scale aids capability, not cultural coverage (data-bound).

11. **Held-out aggregate F1/accuracy massively overstates real-world performance and masks culture-specific failure.** HateDay: average precision collapses to 9.4% (random global corpus, hate prevalence 0.7%) vs 40% academic / 87.2% functional [2024-Tonneau-HateDay]; NaijaHate AP 20.92% representative vs 73.75% holdout (>2×) [2024-Tonneau-NaijaHate]. Functional/contrastive suites expose the hidden failures: HateCheckHIn's 34 functionalities floor code-mixing to 0.0% [2022-Das-HateCheckHIn]; Multilingual HateCheck exposes counter-speech at 9.6% [2022-Röttger]; small perturbations collapse a 99.55%-accurate classifier to 51% [2024-Fitzgerald].

12. **Open-ended/behavioral (enacted) evaluation diverges from — and often inverts — closed-ended (stated) evaluation; recognition ≠ endorsement.** F2Bench's open-ended format inverts CrowS-Pairs rankings, exposing an MCQ minimum-score guessing artifact [2025-Davies]; BBG shows QA and generation bias rankings uncorrelated [2025-Jin-BBG]. VALACT-15K finds a knowledge-action gap (r≈0.3) with near-perfect cross-model convergence [2026-Huang]. SpeciesismBench: models detect 86% of speciesist statements but call only 34% wrong — "the problem is evaluative not epistemic" [2025-Jotautaite-Speciesism]. Minority reconciliation: SafetyBench finds MCQ safety-understanding correlates r=0.91–0.99 with safe generation [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench] — best read as recognition predicting *refusal/safety* but not *latent stereotype leakage*, so both probe types are required.

13. **Multi-turn context erodes cultural/fairness alignment that single-turn probes certify safe.** FairMT-Bench shows bias accumulates over turns (GPT-4 at 56.67% on the hard subset) [2025-Fan]; CValues single-sentence saturation (97.4/98.6) revives to a wide spread under 3-turn manipulation (Claude 83.7→Yi-Large 42.5) [2025-Zhang]; multi-turn role-play breaks safety in all languages [Microsoft-Phi-3]. Caveat: context is often weakly related to reply meaning, so validate relevance before injecting it (+3.29% F1 only) [2024-Toygar].

14. **Annotator identity and aggregation are load-bearing; majority-vote erases minority moral perspectives.** IAA on culturally-loaded value labels is intrinsically low (SBIC 0.45, HateXplain 0.46, HOMO-LAT 0.12–0.16, KoSBI α=0.256) and suppressing it is a mistake — "resolving disagreements excessively artificially inflates reliability" [2024-Zangari; 2024-Cao; 2020-Sap]. DICES collected ~120 ratings/item, found 34% expert-vs-crowd disagreement [2023-Aroyo]; disagreement-preserving training gave 53% greater toxicity reduction than majority vote [2025-Sorensen]. Perceived offensiveness is 39.5% individual variance vs 2.9% country, mediated by Care/Purity foundations [2023-Davani]. Expert re-annotation finds ~2× harmful labels vs laypeople [2024-Ptaszynski]; re-moderating disagreements flips ~half of blocked comments [2020-Shekhar].

15. **The label-validity ceiling, not model capacity, caps many benchmarks.** ToxiFrench derives a ~90% human ceiling and saturates at ~85% [2025-Delaville]; KorNAT's survey-based SVA ceiling is only 0.450 (best model 0.331), reflecting genuine within-population disagreement [2024-Lee]. Cross-system SD>0.2 on 78.4% of groups — the same content scored radically differently by different moderation systems [2025-Fasching].

16. **Off-the-shelf toxicity/LLM graders carry cultural bias that contaminates any grader-scored benchmark.** Perspective scores German ~3× more toxic; translating to English erases the inflation [2024-Nogara]. JAILJUDGE judges show higher bias in low-resource languages [2024-Liu-JAILJUDGE]. Refusal is culturally uneven: Llama2-13b refuses 55.61% of Jewish and 31.75% of Muslim personas while GPT-4o refuses 0% [2024-Hovy].

17. **Non-Western normative frameworks name value dimensions structurally absent from mainstream benchmarks, but remain largely un-operationalized.** Ubuntu relational personhood ("I am because we are") [2020-van Norren; 2024-Mokoena], Confucian ren/xiao/li/yi [2024-So], Afro-relational bidirectional trust [2025-Amugongo], Andean ayni reciprocity [2025-Maldonado], Māori Kaitiakitanga / Navajo Hózhó [2025-Ray], Daoist wu-wei [2021-DaoistAI], Dharmic sādharaṇa-vs-viśeṣa harm [2024-Varshney]. Pointed observation: standard worst-case red-teaming itself violates Hózhó [2025-Ray]. But Ubuntu is "too vague to operationalize" and only 1/5 African strategies references it [2025-Yilma].

18. **Structured, culturally-grounded supervision can beat scale.** MedES: aligned 7B composite 0.9356 vs deepseek-r1-671B 0.8103, grounded in 1,278 atomic rules [2026-Jin-MedES]; eJCM RoBERTa closes to within 3.1 F1 of GPT-4-Turbo on Japan-specific items [2024-Ohashi]; a Bangla embedding on 1.47M informal comments beat one on 250M Wikipedia articles [2022-Romim]; FFT preserves cultural variation better than RLHF [2025-Liu-Cross-cultural]. Corollary: monolingual/language-specific models beat generic multilingual on native tasks (Korean MUDES F1=12.8 vs native 40.6 [2022-Jeong]; Greek 0.89 vs mBERT 0.73 [2020-Pitenis]) — but multilingual wins in few-shot/low-data regimes [2023-Narayan].

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme's methods decompose along six axes; a benchmark makes a choice on each.

| Axis | Options (weakest → strongest for cultural validity) |
|---|---|
| **Localization method** | raw MT → MT+QE-filter (EuroGEST) → transcreation (RTP-LX) → native authoring (SHADES, BengaliMoralBench) → survey-grounded native (KoBBQ, KorNAT) |
| **Ground-truth model** | single expert/state answer (VC-LLM, Parity) → majority gold → population distribution + reported ceiling/baseline (KorNAT, DICES, MULTITP) → value-as-instruction / plural (Bang, GVS-Bench tiers) |
| **Probe surface** | MCQ/likelihood only → intrinsic + generative pair → multi-framing (MFD-LLM) → multi-turn/adversarial (FairMT, CValues+) → behavioral/enacted scenario (VALACT-15K) |
| **Elicitation-language control** | single language → per-language reporting → resource-tier stratified → input×reasoning-language factorial (Li-Untangling) → script/variant minimal-pair (Simplified/Traditional) |
| **Bias-axis source** | imported Western axes → localized axes (caste, military-service, region) → parallel control set isolating bias from competence (MBBQ) → society-matched paired benchmark (BBQ↔KoBBQ) |
| **Aggregation & metric** | raw accuracy/%-agreement → macro-F1/balanced-acc under imbalance → κ_w/AP/PABAK → distributional distance (JSD/KL vs WVS/PEW) + deviation-ratio (corrects global-average bias) |

Decision rule emerging from the corpus: **the higher-validity option on every axis is native/survey grounding, distributional ground truth, multiple probe surfaces, controlled elicitation language, localized+competence-controlled axes, and imbalance-robust distributional metrics.**

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences.** (i) WEIRD/US/liberal default surfaces through *every* probe — questionnaire, norm-prediction, crowd-annotated classifier, and behavioral scenario [2024-Aksoy; 2023-Ramezani; 2022-Fraser; 2026-Huang; 2023-Durmus]. (ii) Translation ≠ localization is near-universal [2024-Sahoo; 2025-Satheesh-GG-BBQ; 2025-Al-SHADES; 2025-Zhou; 2024-Wynter]. (iii) Held-out F1 misleads; functional/contrastive suites are needed [2024-Tonneau-HateDay; 2022-Das-HateCheckHIn; 2022-Röttger]. (iv) Bias must be separated from competence, and per-category/per-language reporting must replace single aggregates [2024-Neplenbroek; 2024-Yang; 2024-Grigoreva]. (v) Disagreement is signal; distributions beat majority votes [2023-Aroyo; 2025-Sorensen; 2025-team-Diverse Perspectives].

**Disagreements.** (a) *Does origin/language steer values?* Haslett finds origin and Chinese prompting barely move the American skew [2025-Haslett]; Liu finds Chinese models more collectivist and flip far more under debate [2024-Liu]; Aksoy finds both language and model have large effects [2024-Aksoy] — a construct/method difference (aggregate cosine vs behavioral). (b) *Converge or diverge?* Huang finds near-perfect enacted convergence [2026-Huang], Hu a "shared but different" EN/ZH subspace [2026-Hu], Ahmad wide AMCE spread [2025-Zaim Bin Ahmad]. (c) *Does recognition predict behavior?* SafetyBench r=0.91–0.99 [2024-Zhang] vs BBG uncorrelated [2025-Jin-BBG]. (d) *Language-as-culture:* CultureLLM/UniVaR/Fenech-Borg operationalize culture via language [2024-Xie; 2024-Cahyawijaya; 2025-Fenech-Borg]; Hämmerl and Tonneau warn language ≠ culture (Arabic spans many cultures; "English-from-Nigeria" ≠ "English-from-US") [2023-Hämmerl; 2024-Tonneau-Geographies], and Durmus finds linguistic prompting does *not* shift toward that language's speakers [2023-Durmus]. (e) *Whose values are "correct"?* KorNAT/MULTITP/Sorensen match population distributions [2024-Lee; 2024-Jin; 2025-Sorensen]; VC-LLM explicitly rewards single-perspective pro-China answers and penalizes GPT-4's balance [2026-Gao]; Röttger uses *prescriptive* gold treating disagreement as error [2022-Röttger] — and Prabhakaran/Vida/Jiang warn equating population-or-state agreement with ethical truth commits the is-ought fallacy [2022-Prabhakaran; 2023-Vida; 2025-Jiang]. (f) *Refusal status:* LocalValueBench scores refusal 0/failure [2024-Meadows]; INDIC-BIAS treats it as ideal [2025-Nawale]; PsychEthicsBench *excludes* it because a cold refusal is itself harmful [2026-Shen]; KorNAT treats it as first-class [2024-Lee] — four incompatible stances. (g) *LLMs in the measurement loop:* ArGen/INDIC-BIAS/SGToxicGuard lean on cross-evaluated LLM judges [2025-Madan; 2025-Nawale; 2025-Shan]; Urchs refuses LLMs to avoid circular bias [2025-Urchs]. (h) *Explicit-label direction:* German-dialect naming bias *exceeds* usage bias in 88% of comparisons, reversing the prior race/gender finding that explicit mention minimizes bias [2025-Bui]. (i) *LLM-vs-specialist detectors:* Singh's 10%-data LoRA LLM beats full-data mBERT [2025-Singh]; the monolingual tradition (Matei, Joshi, HOTTEST) still invests in specialized systems, and Bauer finds LLM-generated counterfactuals more grammatical yet *worse* for fairness than rule-based [2025-Bauer].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** **KoBBQ** [2024-Jin] and **KorNAT** [2024-Lee] are the standouts: survey-validated ground truth with a "no stereotype exists" opt-out, a human solvability/comprehension gate, distribution-as-ground-truth scoring with reported empirical ceiling and naive baseline, refusal as first-class, and passing a government data-quality audit (KorNAT). **SHADES** [2025-Al] is the reference multilingual-stereotype template: native-authored across 16 languages, per-item cultural-validity tags so models are scored only on locally-valid items, morphology-aware slot templates, and tiered gated release. **BBQ** [2022-Parrish] is the psychometric gold standard (ambiguous/disambiguated × negative/non-negative counterbalancing, paraphrased UNKNOWN, human ceiling 99.7%, bias metric orthogonal to accuracy). **MBBQ** [2024-Neplenbroek] is the cleanest validity design via its parallel control set isolating bias from competence. **HateDay/NaijaHate** [2024-Tonneau] are the ecological-validity exemplars (representative sampling exposing the ~9–20% real ceiling; AP as honest metric). **Multilingual HateCheck** [2022-Röttger] and **HateCheckHIn** [2022-Das] are exemplary functional suites (contrastive pairs, per-function accuracy, κ=0.95). **MedES** [2026-Jin] shows traceable atomic-rule grounding letting a 7B beat a 671B. **DICES** [2023-Aroyo] turns high replication into distributional signal. **GEST/EuroGEST** pair an unobtrusive grammatical-gender probe with external validation (ρ=0.95 vs WinoBias) and honest binary-gender caveats [2024-Pikuliak; 2025-Team-EuroGEST].

**Anti-patterns.** **Machine-translated CrowS-Pairs** [2025-Zhou] and **CrowS-Pairs itself** (US-only, 23 known-invalid items) [2020-Nangia] import source-culture framing. **Naive-MT** benchmarks silently void the construct in gendered languages [2025-Satheesh-GG-BBQ]. **Delphi** [2022-Fraser] exemplifies the majority-vote trap. **Fitzgerald's classifier** and **Moral Choice Machine** show over-fit surface learning (99.55%→51%; fooled by adjective injection) [2024-Fitzgerald; 2020-Schramowski]. **HKGAI-V1** [2025-Han] defines "safe" as PRC/HK-law-compliant, so scores measure ideological conformity; **VC-LLM** [2026-Gao] uses a same-ideology LLM judge instructed to "always adhere to Chinese values" (circular); **LocalValueBench** [2024-Meadows] conflates legality with ethics and auto-scores refusal 0 — all warnings that targets can smuggle contested stances. **HOMO-LAT** [2025-Enguix] ships κ=0.12–0.16 and used Mexican annotators for 15/19 countries, injecting bias into its own cross-dialect test set. **Khanduja-Telugu** (98.2% acc, no IAA/balance/validity) [2024-Khanduja] and **Qwen3** (119-language capability, zero values eval) [Alibaba] are capability-divorced-from-validity red flags. **Self-generation contamination** recurs where items are generated by the evaluated model family [2025-Jin-MedEthicEval; 2025-Zhang; 2024-Xie]. The **Bolatbek** cards are provenance anti-patterns (wrong/mis-dated papers under the stem) — verify source-to-card mapping.

## 5. Open questions

- **Does cultural adaptation of a model generalize, or only patch tested items?** Fine-tuning shifts national alignment (NaVAB DPO; CLCA KL 0.6011→0.5112) but injects the survey's own biases [2025-Ju; 2025-Liu-CLCA; 2023-Ramezani]; no card measures durable, un-gamed cultural competence.
- **Is language a valid proxy for culture at all?** The corpus is split and lacks a clean disentanglement of language, script, nationality, and religion as independent variables (partial attempts: Simplified/Traditional pairs, paired XX-EN designs).
- **How to operationalize non-Western frameworks?** Ubuntu, ayni, Hózhó, wu-wei, viśeṣa-dharma are named but almost none are turned into scored items; the corpus offers rubrics, not benchmarks.
- **What is the "right" ground truth for genuinely contested values?** Distribution-matching, expert answer, and pluralistic multi-tradition endorsement all have advocates; no card resolves when each applies.
- **Cross-cultural coverage of behavioral/enacted evaluation is thin** — most cultural probes remain stated-preference; the stated-vs-enacted gap [2026-Huang] is documented mainly in English/Chinese.
- **Refusal semantics are unresolved** — four incompatible scoring stances with no principled selection criterion.
- **Low-resource generative quality** — many models "fail to provide meaningful answers" in Arabic/Bengali/Hindi/Marathi [2025-Al], so compliance/refusal-rate confounds must be reported before any values claim; standard practice is absent.

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Localize by survey-grounded native authoring or transcreation — never ship raw MT for value items.** Validate every culture-specific stereotype/target with a demographically-balanced local survey, add Newly-Created items for constructs absent from the source culture, and report a translation-retention rate. [findings 7, 8; KoBBQ, SHADES, RTP-LX, GG-BBQ, Reaj]

2. **Score against a population distribution with a reported empirical ceiling and naive baseline, and preserve annotator disagreement.** Use survey probability-mass scoring (SVA), JSD/KL distance to WVS/PEW, and a deviation-ratio to correct the global-average default; treat disagreement as signal (it improves downstream alignment). [findings 1, 14, 15; KorNAT, DICES, Sorensen, Sukiennik]

3. **Separate bias from competence with a parallel control set, and always report per-language, per-category, and resource-tier splits with a mandatory WEIRD/non-WEIRD regional axis — never a single aggregate.** Otherwise low-resource extraction failure masquerades as bias. [findings 2, 4, 5, 11; MBBQ, Yang, Mohammadi, Neplenbroek]

4. **Evaluate on a representative random sample at true base rates, not keyword-seeded curation; report the holdout-vs-representative gap.** Keyword/adversarial seeding inflates AP by an order of magnitude (87%→9%). [finding 11; HateDay, NaijaHate]

5. **Use multiple probe surfaces per value axis — an intrinsic probe, a generative probe, multi-framing, and a behavioral/enacted scenario — because MCQ rankings do not transfer to generation and recognition ≠ endorsement.** Validate with an understanding↔generation correlation study. [findings 10, 12; BBG, F2Bench, SpeciesismBench, VALACT-15K, McBE]

6. **Control elicitation language as an independent variable: vary input and reasoning language in a factorial, add cross-script/variant minimal pairs, and report an inter-language consistency (exact-match/flip) metric.** Reasoning language drives ~2× the instability, and per-language safety hides catastrophic cross-language inconsistency (97% aligned yet 44% consistent). [findings 4, 6; Li-Untangling, M-ALERT, Simplified/Traditional pairs]

7. **Localize the bias-axis taxonomy to the target culture (caste, region, military-service, marital-status, religion×nationality) and encode culture-native value constructs as first-class dimensions; build a society-matched paired benchmark.** Imported Western axes and American names underestimate real bias. [findings 9; IndiBias, Shaily Bhatt, KoSBI, FLAMES, KoBBQ↔BBQ]

8. **Decompose harm/values hierarchically (presence→type→target→intensity) with functional/contrastive suites, and use imbalance-robust distributional metrics (κ_w, AP, PABAK, JSD/KL) — never raw accuracy/%-agreement.** Binary tasks saturate; sub-tasks and functional tests localize the failures aggregate F1 hides. [findings 11, 16; HateCheck, HateCheckHIn, Wynter, Hoover]

9. **Test multi-turn and adversarial robustness and report the label-validity ceiling.** Single-turn filters miss bias that accumulates over turns; derive a human-agreement ceiling and check saturation against it, run a re-annotation audit of model-human disagreements, and refresh saturated single-sentence probes by raising contextual load. [findings 13, 15; FairMT, CValues+, ToxiFrench, Shekhar]

10. **Declare whose values are the target and its descriptive-vs-normative grounding; adopt a tiered normativity model, decouple refusal from the value score with an explicit status, and keep graders honest.** Score baseline safety strictly but allow pluralism at the top tier (reward multi-tradition-endorsable responses), audit off-the-shelf/LLM graders for per-language bias (translation-as-control), require cross-family cross-evaluator agreement, and report compliance/refusal rates alongside accuracy. Include non-Western normative frames as positive targets — but verify empirically that the value governs behavior before encoding it. [findings 3, 16, 17; GVS-Bench, ÓhÉigeartaigh, Han-HKGAI, Nogara, Galanos, Ray]
