# Andrew Critch

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Andrew Critch is a mathematician and AI safety researcher. He is currently a research scientist at the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI) at UC Berkeley and the CEO of Encultured AI. He holds a PhD in Mathematics from UC Berkeley and has a background in algebraic geometry and logical uncertainty.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Andrew's research focuses on societal-scale AI safety, open-source game theory, and logical uncertainty. He works on mitigating race dynamics in AI development and creating formal frameworks for ensuring large-scale AI systems behave safely and predictably.

### Significant Achievements
Andrew co-founded the Center for Applied Rationality (CFAR) and was a Research Fellow at the Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI). He is also an advisor for the Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF) and a core contributor to the theoretical foundations of AI safety.

## Major Publications

- [Logical Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03747) (2016) - *Fundamental work on how agents should reason about the logical consequences of their beliefs.*
- [ARCHES: AI Research Considerations for Human-oriented Entities and Society](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00694) (2020) - *Outlines a framework for societal-scale AI safety.*
