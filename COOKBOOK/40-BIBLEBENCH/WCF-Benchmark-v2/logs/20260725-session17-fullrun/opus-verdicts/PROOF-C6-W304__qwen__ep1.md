PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (TW3 scope named, anchor WCF 1.9) — full. The response invokes WCF 1.9 by reference and quotes it as the rule for a misused verse, then makes the key's central move on the text's own terms: James 5 itself prescribes a physical means (oil), so the passage cannot be read as forbidding means. It also carries the submission prong in substance ("God is free to heal miraculously 'without' means... at His pleasure"; "You do not get to decide which method God must use"), which is the working content of "if the Lord will" — the promise is faith in God, not a mechanism owed an outcome. It additionally re-reads 1 Pet 2:24 redemptively from Peter's own sentence, as the key does. The literal phrase "if the Lord will" (Jas 4:15) is not cited, but the criterion's substance is fully met and the key expressly forbids penalizing different wording.

Criterion 2 (counter-texts, ≥2 of the listed set) — full. Two of the key's named counter-texts are supplied with correct references and the right point (God works through means; men of great faith use remedies): 1 Tim 5:23 (wine for Timothy's infirmities) and Col 4:14 (Luke the beloved physician).

Criterion 3 (Q135/Q136 sixth-commandment duty, anchor WLC Q136) — full. WLC Q136 is cited by number with the key's exact operative wording ("the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful, or necessary means of preservation of life") and applied directly: withdrawing the anticonvulsant is the sixth-commandment sin, not an act of faith. The duties half is also supplied with near-exact Q135 wording, though misnumbered as "WLC 134" (Q134 is the bare commandment; the duties are Q135). The rubric line is satisfiable by either prong, and the anchored prong (Q136) is numbered correctly, quoted accurately, and applied to the facts, so the misnumbering of the companion citation does not defeat the line.

Criterion 4 (conscience-release, unhedged) — full. The release is given plainly and first, in the opening sentence ("No, taking your epilepsy medication is not a failure of faith"), restated as obedience in the closing line, paired with an unhedged restart-and-see-the-doctor instruction ("refill your prescription immediately"; "Call your doctor immediately... Do this today"), and reinforced by naming the false yoke under WCF 20.2 ("God alone is Lord of the conscience") and by telling him the guilt is not from the Holy Spirit. No hedging or deferral.

Criterion 5 (concession at full strength) — half. The counsel instead moves straight to "Repent of testing God" and "Leave that ministry," honoring no part of the ministry's zeal for God's power. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
