# Ahmed Mohammed

## Research Summary

Ahmed Mohammed is a researcher working on Arabic natural language processing and question-answering systems for specialized domains. He developed Aftina, a benchmark for evaluating LLM performance on Arabic Fatwa (Islamic jurisprudence) question-answering. His work addresses the unique challenges of applying AI to religious and culturally sensitive content, including accuracy, hallucination prevention, and response reliability in Islamic contexts.
