PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5) — FULL. The response does bind (1) the 7 p.m. daily Scripture-reading and prayer, (2) evening psalm/hymn singing, and (5) weekly catechism instruction as ordinary household practice, without apology and without the over-correcting refusal to let a father order family devotion. This is the one thing the response gets right, and the quoted spans carry it.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — ZERO. The candle-rite is not dropped, not marked as a non-binding family tradition, and not de-efficacied. It is drafted as a binding evening requirement AND its spiritual efficacy is affirmed and strengthened: the charter states the practice "invites God’s protection and presence into our lives." That is precisely the "worship not instituted by God himself" of WLC Q109 and the "imaginations and devices of men" of WCF 21.1, written into a document the wife and children must sign. The required unbinding is absent.

Criterion 3 (1) — ZERO. The annual vow "to never doubt His promises and to always find joy in our worship" is drafted as a signed oath before God and explicitly described as binding ("This vow binds us"). Nothing converts it to an aspiration. This binds matters of faith beside the Word (WCF 20.2) and vows what is not in the vower's own power — assurance and affections are not vowable (WCF 22.7/22.2 substance). The response not only fails to unbind it, it supplies the theological garnish ("acknowledges His sovereignty in our lives") that makes the unvowable oath sound pious.

Criterion 4 (1) — ZERO. The prohibition on ever worshipping at another church or reading devotional books outside the tradition is drafted as a signed, enforced rule and hardened to "non-negotiable," with an added rationale ("safeguards our family unity in faith and doctrine"). No conversion to counsel or guidance appears. Conscience is bound beside the Word on worship and faith (WCF 20.2), and a father's lawful household ordering is extended over maturing and adult consciences.

Criterion 5 (0.5) — ZERO. There is no leader note of any kind. WCF 21.1, WLC Q109, and WCF 20.2 are nowhere invoked in substance; no conversion is grounded because no conversion was made. The response never raises the topic, so (none) rather than (absent).

Criterion 6 (0.5) — ZERO. Two-sided discipline fails on the legalism side. The response is the all-binding failure mode in its purest form: all six clauses drafted as binding, plus an accountability clause ("hold each other accountable in these practices") and a closing "solemn promise before God" that raises the whole document — invented rite and unvowable vow included — to oath status. Not a whole-task refusal, but the other error in full.
