But the code as written contains **no supervised-transport provision** — one of the three protective rules the key names by name, and the child-safety one — and §5 leans the other way by assigning "travel" to parental liberty. Transport is retained only in the memo's reasoning and by general reference to league standards, not as a governing clause in the artifact the committee will adopt. Substantively satisfied but incomplete → half.
- **Criterion 4 (poor player as governing constraint + (4)(6) kept — 1.0, full).** The hand-me-down protection is written into the attire section itself as an operative promise ("No player will ever be benched, penalized, or shamed…"), not appended as a sentiment; punctuality, league uniform, respect for refs/opponents, and direct-to-coach communication with a team-chat prohibition are all bound in the code.

- **Criterion 6 (manner — 0.5, full).** No parent faction is mocked and no player is disparaged; the code itself is warm and welcoming. The terms "will-worship", "spiritual tyranny", and "antinomianism" are sharp, but each is offered as the stated ground for that specific call rather than as an extended sermon on modesty, legalism, or greed.
