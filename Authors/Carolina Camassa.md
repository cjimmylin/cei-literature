# Carolina Camassa

## Biographical Summary
Carolina Camassa is a researcher at the Bank of Italy's Applied IT Research Team since 2022. Her expertise spans AI alignment (since 2024) and NLP applications to legal and regulatory domains (since 2022). She conducts empirical research on how large language models interpret and apply legal and constitutional principles.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLMs and Constitutional Interpretation**: Researching how LLMs apply constitutional principles in complex decision-making scenarios, comparing model-generated legal arguments to human legal reasoning.
- **NLP for Regulatory Compliance**: Applying textual analysis to regulatory documents, including work on crypto asset regulations (MiCAR).

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Legal Minds, Algorithmic Decisions: How LLMs Apply Constitutional Principles in Complex Scenarios" (AAAI/ACM AIES 2024)
- Found that GPT-4 consistently aligns more closely with progressive interpretations of the Italian Constitution, often overlooking competing values
- Research contributes to understanding how AI systems may encode value biases in legal reasoning
