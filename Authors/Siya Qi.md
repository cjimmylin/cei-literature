# Siya Qi

## Research Summary

Siya Qi is a PhD student in the Department of Informatics at King's College London. Her research focuses on Natural Language Processing, including text generation, summarization, and text factual consistency. She has published work on scientific paper summarization, including extractive summarization methods and heterogeneous graph-based approaches for scientific documents.
