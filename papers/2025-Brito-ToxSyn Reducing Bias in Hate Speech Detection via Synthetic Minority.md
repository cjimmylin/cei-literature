## Abstract

First Portuguese publicly available corpus for hate-speech classification across multiple minority targets, comprising 53,274 LLM-generated samples annotated for toxicity, discourse type, and target group covering nine protected communities.

## Data Description

ToxSyn: first large-scale Portuguese corpus for multi-label hate speech detection across nine protected minority groups, comprising 53,274 LLM-generated samples with discourse-type and target-group annotations; released on HuggingFace (no specific URL in paper).
