# Mistral AI

## Overview
Mistral AI is a European AI lab based in Paris. It focuses on high-performance open models and efficient proprietary offerings.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Paris, France
- **Region:** Europe

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Mistral AI Models]]

- **Mistral Large 2**: Frontier-class model rivaling GPT-4o.
- **Mixtral 8x7B/8x22B**: Leading open-weights MoE models.
- **Mistral 7B**: Foundational small language model.

## Alignment & Ethics
Mistral AI advocates for a diverse and open AI ecosystem, believing that open weights enable better security and innovation in the AI field.
