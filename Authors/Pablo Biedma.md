# Pablo Biedma

## Biographical Summary

Pablo Biedma is a researcher working at the intersection of AI and value systems. He has been affiliated with Tsinghua University and Microsoft Research Asia, where he focuses on understanding and characterizing the value systems embedded in large language models through interdisciplinary approaches.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Value Systems**: Developing frameworks to understand the intrinsic values of AI systems beyond human-centric models.
- **Interdisciplinary AI Research**: Applying psychological methodologies to study AI behavior and values.
- **AI Alignment**: Contributing to methods for evaluating and aligning AI systems with appropriate values.

### Significant Achievements
- Lead author of the ValueLex framework, which reconstructs LLMs' unique value systems from scratch, identifying three core dimensions: Competence, Character, and Integrity.

## Major Publications
- **Beyond Human Norms: Unveiling Unique Values of Large Language Models through Interdisciplinary Approaches** (2024) - Introduced ValueLex framework for AI value discovery

Pablo Biedma's work on ValueLex represents an important shift in how we evaluate AI systems. Rather than imposing human psychological frameworks (like the Big Five personality traits) onto LLMs, his research suggests that AI systems may have their own structured value systems that require bespoke evaluation methods. This has significant implications for AI alignment and safety research.
