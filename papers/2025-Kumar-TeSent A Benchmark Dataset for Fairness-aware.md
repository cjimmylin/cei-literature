## Abstract

Comprehensive benchmark for fairness-aware sentiment classification in Telugu with 21,119 sentences from social media and news. Includes TeEEC (Equity Evaluation Corpus) for evaluating gender and religion biases with human-annotated rationales. First Telugu-specific fairness benchmark. Evaluates gender and religion biases in sentiment analysis with explainability. Key for values/fairness evaluation in Telugu.

## Benchmark

**Name:** TeSent / TeEEC

## Data Description

TeSent is a Telugu sentiment classification benchmark of 21,119 sentences from social media and news with human-annotated sentiment labels and rationales, plus TeEEC (Equity Evaluation Corpus in Telugu) for evaluating gender and religion fairness biases. All trained models and datasets are released on HuggingFace.
