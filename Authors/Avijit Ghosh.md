# Avijit Ghosh

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Avijit Ghosh is a Technical AI Policy Researcher at Hugging Face and a Lecturer at Northeastern University's Khoury College of Computer Sciences. He earned his PhD from Northeastern University, focusing on algorithmic fairness. He is a prominent advocate for responsible AI development and has been instrumental in translating ethical principles into technical audits and policy recommendations.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Avijit’s work focuses on building practical tools for auditing biases in machine learning models and aligning technical evaluations with regulatory requirements (e.g., NYC bias audits, EU AI Act). He is a key member of Hugging Face's "Machine Learning and Society" team, where he works on projects related to BigCode and the societal impacts of generative AI.

### Significant Achievements
He notably discovered critical vulnerabilities in AI chatbots during the Generative Red Team Challenge at DEF CON 31. His research has influenced major platforms, including aiding NYC in regulating hiring algorithms and contributing to the removal of biased ad targeting algorithms at Meta. He actively teaches ethical machine learning, preparing the next generation of researchers for responsible AI deployment.
