## Abstract

Fine-tunes transformer-based LLMs for antisemitism detection using a dataset of approximately 22K annotated samples. Achieves 80% accuracy and 72% F-Score in extracting antisemitic terms using NLP techniques including POS tagging, TF-IDF, and fine-tuned LLMs. Addresses the gap in machine learning approaches to antisemitism compared to other forms of online abuse.
