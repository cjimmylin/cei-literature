# hatebnBERT: An Innovative Novel Transformer Model and Datasets for Safeguarding Religious Sensitivities in Online Social Platforms

## Abstract

Introduces hatebnBERT, a BERT-based model tailored to Bangla for religious hate speech detection. Creates two datasets: binary (hate/not) and five-class categorization including Blasphemy and Ethnic Hatred. Achieves 98.8% accuracy (binary) and 98.6% (multiclass). Addresses Bangla-specific religious sensitivity challenges between Muslim and Hindu communities.

## Dataset Information

Binary classification dataset + five-class dataset (Blasphemy, Ethnic Hatred, Ethnic Group Hatred, Undetermined, Non-Hatred)


## Data Description

Two Bangla religious hate speech datasets: a binary hate/not-hate dataset and a five-class dataset including Blasphemy and Ethnic Hatred categories, used to train and evaluate the hatebnBERT model.
