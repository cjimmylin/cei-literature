# Dawn Drain

## Biographical Summary

Dawn Drain is a Research Scientist at Anthropic, where she works on AI safety and alignment research. She has been involved in foundational work on Constitutional AI and methods for training helpful, harmless, and honest AI assistants. Her research contributes to developing scalable approaches for aligning language models with human values.

## Research Focus

Drain's work focuses on developing training methods that produce AI systems aligned with human preferences and values. She has contributed to Anthropic's research on RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and Constitutional AI, which uses AI feedback to guide behavior without extensive human labeling.
