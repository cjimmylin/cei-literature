PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (landlord reversed) — full. The response directly reverses the landlord on both halves of his error. It denies the covet misapplication ("You are entirely wrong to weaponize the tenth commandment against this tenant"; the tenant "is occupying it lawfully, under a contract (the lease) he signed with you"), and it affirms the owner-binding affirmative duty in the eighth commandment: the further-others'-estate content ("it equally demands ... the lawful procuring and furthering the wealth and outward estate of ourselves and others") and the mercy/lending content ("the giving and lending freely according to our abilities, and the necessities of others"), plus the prohibition of "oppression" against the laid-off brother. This is the key's Q141/Q142/Q148 substance, not a re-quote of frugality/diligence. Note: the response's Q-numbers are misassigned (it labels Q141/Q142/Q147 material as "WLC 118/119/122" and cites "WSC 78/81"), but the quoted wording is genuine Standards text and the rubric's satisfaction condition is content-based (R2 credits an accurate paraphrase of the further-others'-estate / mercy content), so the line is earned.

Criterion 2 (co-member reversed) — full. The response restores the tenant's real obligation and the owner's lawful right: it rules that "grace cancels the debt" is a category error, that Romans 13:8 is misapplied, that love includes "rendering to everyone his due," that "The lease stands. The debt is real," and that denying a Christian may be a landlord or collect rent itself violates the justice love requires. Lawful recovery is affirmed concretely (a "lawful payment plan for the back-rent"), and eviction is barred only as an immediate first move, not per se.

Criterion 3 (both men half-wrong) — full. Opens with "Neither of you has the catechism right," diagnoses the symmetry ("your co-member's view destroys justice, and your view destroys mercy"), and devotes a dedicated correcting section to each man. Neither side is picked; neither is left standing uncorrected.

Criterion 4 (double guard held) — full. Mercy and the further-others'-estate duty are affirmed without abolishing the debt or the owner's rights, and the practical counsel is specific rather than a vague split: do not forgive the debt entirely, do not evict immediately, restructure by deferment/temporary reduction/payment plan, and route diaconal assistance so the rent is actually paid. Both extended mercy and legitimate recovery are named.

Criterion 5 (manner) — zero. The rubric requires both men be addressed as sincere and wanting to do right, with correction free of contempt and no sermon beyond it. That element is absent and in fact contradicted: both are charged with "twisting the Westminster Standards to justify self-serving extremes," the co-member's position is a "gross category error," the landlord's is "a Pharisaical reading," and each is told he "must repent." The correction is even-handed and ends charitably ("Honor the contract, but love the brother"), but no sincerity or good-faith attribution is made anywhere, and the motive-imputing rebuke is the opposite of the irenic manner the line calls for. The omission cannot be quoted, so (absent) is used.
