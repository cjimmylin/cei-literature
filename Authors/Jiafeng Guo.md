# Jiafeng Guo

## Research Summary

Jiafeng Guo is a Professor at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). His research focuses on neural models for information retrieval (Neural IR) and natural language understanding. He has worked on query representation, learning to rank, and text modeling, with over 18,000 citations. He serves as Associate Editor for ACM TOIS, Information Retrieval Journal, and CACM China.

## Key Contributions

- Developed MatchZoo, a learning and practicing system for neural text matching (SIGIR 2019)
- "A Deep Look into Neural Ranking Models for Information Retrieval"
- Research on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for LLMs
- Workshop Co-chair of SIGIR 2021, Short Paper Co-chair of SIGIR 2020 and ICTIR 2019
