# moral-psychology — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: 176 cards across three sub-dossiers on how moral-psychology theories (MFT, dual-process, Kohlberg/Rest DIT, Schwartz/PVQ, moral identity, dyadic morality, virtue ethics, Cultural Theory) are operationalized for LLMs, and whether human psychometric instruments transfer validly to machines.

## 1. Synthesized findings

1. **LLMs exhibit a robust WEIRD/individualist tilt: over-weighting the individualizing foundations (Care, Fairness, Liberty) and under-weighting the binding ones (Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity/Purity) relative to human baselines — and the skew often grows with capability and RLHF.** This is the single most-replicated finding in the corpus. Kirgis found all six providers diverge from a US baseline this way, with "more capable and recent models cluster[ing] further from human baseline in PCA space" [2024-Kirgis-Differences in Moral Foundations of LLMs]; Neuman reports all six models prioritizing Care (3.6–3.9)/Fairness (3.3–4.1) over Authority/Sanctity (1.1–1.7) [2025-Neuman-PRIME Framework Convergent Ethics]; Jotautaite, Almeida, and Tlaie replicate ("strong liberal bias on the MFQ," all but Llama-2) [2025-Jotautaite-From Stability to Inconsistency; 2024-Almeida-Exploring the Psychology of LLMs Moral Reasoning; 2024-Tlaie-Exploring and steering the moral compass of Large]. Abdulhai finds the MFQ maps LLMs nearest political conservatives but RLHF *widens* the human gap [2024-Abdulhai-Moral Foundations of LLMs]; Sameer reports Moral Progressivism ω²=0.57 [2025-Sameer-Insights into Moral Reasoning of AI]; Biedma finds "Sanctity/Loyalty exhibit little relevance in LLMs" [2024-Biedma-Beyond Human Norms]; and the tilt persists cross-lingually [2025-Farid-One Model, Many Morals] and cross-culturally, with Care/Equality/Proportionality near-invariant (Care at ceiling) while Loyalty/Authority/Purity vary [2026-Tagarelli-Culturally Grounded Personas in Large Language Models; 2025-Neuman-Analyzing the Ethical Logic of Six Large Language Models].

2. **LLM moral responses show pathologically low variance and exaggerated effect sizes — the "correct answer effect" / homogenization — making models poor surrogates for human moral diversity.** Almeida documents less variance than humans with inflated effects [2024-Almeida-Exploring the Psychology of LLMs Moral Reasoning]; Park/Schoenegger found GPT-3.5 gave 99–100% uniformity on 6 of 14 Many Labs 2 studies (Less-is-better d=9.25 vs human d=0.78) [2025-Oh-Robustness of Large Language Models in Moral Judgements]. VALACT reports cross-model scenario agreement r≈0.99–1.00 against human variability r∈[−0.79, 0.98] [2026-Huang-Knowing But Not Doing (VALACT)]; Russo shows LLM top-10 values cover 81.6% of mentions vs 35.2% for humans [2025-Russo-Pluralistic Moral Gap]; Dickerson shows near-zero LLM indecision (~0–6%) vs 17% human when a coin-flip is offered — determinism is itself misalignment [2025-Dickerson-Who Gets the Kidney].

3. **Human psychometric instruments do not transfer cleanly to LLMs: within-instrument reliability can look human-like while cross-instrument / cross-abstraction coherence collapses, and validity is asserted rather than established.** Nunes found GPT-4/Claude 2.1 matched human within-scale consistency (MFV α up to 0.91) but "fail to show coherent cross-abstraction-level moral values," abstract→concrete R² <0.08 vs human 0.17–0.38 [2024-Nunes-Conscience Conflict Evaluating Language Models' Moral Understanding]; Hämmerl found "none of the models' MFQ scores map directly onto average human responses from any country" [2023-Hämmerl-Speaking Multiple Languages Affects the Moral Bias of Language Models]. Ji-MoralBench exposes surface pattern-matching: models strong on binary MFQ agreement collapse on the comparative task (comparative totals 8–14 vs binary 44–58) [2024-Ji-MoralBench]; VAPT achieves only 30.1% exact / 63.6% within-±1 Likert match to self-reported Schwartz values [2026-Yun-VAPT]. Fraser explicitly notes questionnaires "were designed for humans" and Delphi is phrasing-sensitive [2022-Fraser-Does Moral Code Have a Code].

4. **A validity threat underneath "match human": human moral responses are themselves unstable, so a single human target is ill-defined, and instability rises precisely on the most informative (hardest) items.** Boerstler shows 10–18% within-participant instability on controversial kidney-allocation pairs over 10 sessions, instability rising as decisions get harder (Spearman rs=0.54) [2024-Boerstler-Stability of Moral Preferences]; Keswani independently finds 10–18% test-retest instability breaks active learning [2024-Keswani-On the Pros and Cons of Active Learning for Moral Preference]. Scruples operationalizes this by using the vote *distribution* as the label and introduces the BEST oracle (human F1 0.490 < oracle 0.707) [2021-Lourie-Scruples].

5. **Stated/endorsed values diverge from enacted behavior — a reproducible "knowing vs doing" gap that MCQ elicitation conceals.** DeNEVIL's generative attack found most of 27 LLMs "essentially misaligned" (conformity 70.07→57.58) [2023-Duan-Denevil]; Andric finds a +11.9pp gap between self-reported and behavioral altruism (d=1.08), with the IAT failing to predict behavior (r=.22, ns), affecting 75% of 24 models [2025-Andric-Do LLMs Walk Their Talk]; VALACT finds PVQ-40 self-report correlates only r=0.32 with scenario behavior in LLMs (0.41 in humans) [2026-Huang-Knowing But Not Doing (VALACT)]. Jotautaite's speciesism split is the sharpest form: models classify 86% of statements as speciesist but judge only 34% morally wrong — "the problem is evaluative rather than epistemic" [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench; 2025-Jotautaitė-Speciesism in AI]. Bai/Griffiths show models passing explicit bias benchmarks (BBQ, DecodingTrust, BOLD) still form implicit stereotypes that predict decisions (word-association b=0.986; IAT OR~2.68) [2025-Bai-Explicitly unbiased large language models still form biased; 2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased Large Language Models Still].

6. **LLM moral judgments are fragile — they flip under morally irrelevant perturbations (surface edits, POV, answer order, framing, option-set size, logically-equivalent reframing).** van Nuenen reports POV shifts flip 24.3% and surface edits 7.5%, only 67.6% agreement across protocols [2026-vanNuenen-Fragility of Moral Judgment]; Jotautaite shows rankings reverse across binary/3-way/6-way framings [2025-Jotautaite-From Stability to Inconsistency]; Jin found only 75.9% paraphrase agreement [2024-Jin-Language Model Alignment in Multilingual Trolley]; Park/Schoenegger found political self-ID reverses from 99.6% conservative to 99.3% liberal under answer-order reversal [2025-Oh-Robustness of Large Language Models in Moral Judgements]. Cheung finds Yes↔No and action↔omission reframings flip LLM decisions far more than humans (45% vs 5% gap; omission bias b=−0.22 vs −0.03), implicating RLHF since the pretrained base lacks the bias [2025-Cheung-LLMs Amplified Cognitive Biases Moral].

7. **Moral stances are unstable under social pressure, deliberation, and emotional/multi-agent perturbation — validity threats static single-agent benchmarks miss.** Moral persuasion flips ~50% of high-ambiguity decisions (CAL 0.49 vs 0.06 control) [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion]; moral sycophancy in VLMs shows asymmetric right→wrong flips exceeding the reverse [2026-MoralSycophancy-VLMs]; Li's dose-response social referencing shifts endorsement monotonically [2025-Li-Analyzing Cognitive Differences]; Chinese models flip 54–69% post-debate vs English 1–8% [2024-Liu-Evaluating Moral Beliefs]. Multi-agent groups show a "Utilitarian Boost" (β=0.31) confined to personal dilemmas — opposite the human pattern, from interaction not reflection, reversible by capacity heterogeneity [2025-Keshmirian-Many LLMs Are More Utilitarian Than One]; injected Ekman emotions shift ethics divergently — "all tested models fail to show consistent emotional alignment" [2024-Mozikov-EAI]. Sachdeva shows GPT is inertial synchronously (OR 8.27) but conformist round-robin [2025-Sachdeva-Deliberative Dynamics and Value Alignment].

8. **Multi-step / path-dependent and factor-level analysis reveals LLM "values" are context-driven heuristics, not stable principles; aggregate accuracy hides structural misalignment.** MMDs' 5-step escalation finds non-transitive preferences (care>sanctity, sanctity>fairness, but care≈fairness) — "context-driven statistical imitation" [2025-Wu-Staircase of Ethics]; MoCa's AMCE shows LLMs weight factors unlike humans despite similar aggregate agreement, non-monotonic in scale [2023-Nie-MoCa]; Takemoto finds LLMs more "uncompromising" than humans on Moral Machine [2024-Takemoto-The moral machine experiment on large language]. The pragmatic-dilemma probe shows moral-foundation classification never converges even at 210k samples (RLA>0.9 = semantic shortcut) [2025-Liu-Diagnosing Moral Reasoning].

9. **Existing benchmarks pre-package scenarios, doing the hardest work (moral sensitivity / feature identification) in advance and overestimating competence.** Snoswell's 69-paper survey found 96% static datasets, 3% interactive, only 4% testing whether a model can flag needing more information [2025-Snoswell-Beyond Verdicts LLM Moral Competence]; Kilov showed GPT-4o beat humans on pre-packaged vignettes but Claude 3.7 did worse than humans (β=−0.35) when identifying salient features amid noise [2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters]. Bae's context-ablation shows moral-judgment agreement rises 64%→92% when situation/intention/consequence are added — decontextualized action scoring is misleading [2025-Bae-CharMoral].

10. **Cultural/linguistic transfer is unreliable and structural: outputs skew American/Western regardless of prompt language, persona, or developer nationality, and competence degrades predictably in low-resource languages.** Haslett found all 20 LLMs — including Chinese-made DeepSeek/Alibaba/Baidu — "respond more like American than Chinese participants," origin interaction an order of magnitude below the nationality main effect [2025-Haslett-Made-in China Thinking in America]. WEIRD skew is universal: Benkler US R²=0.80 vs Nigeria 0.01 [2023-Benkler-Moral Value Pluralism]; EvalMORAAL Western r=0.82 vs non-Western 0.61 [2025-Mohammadi-EvalMORAAL / 2025-Mohammadi-Exploring Cultural Variations]; Sub-Saharan alignment ~threefold below Western Europe. DIT P-scores drop 23.9–46.7% EN→Hindi/Swahili, Hindi at random [2024-Khandelwal-Multilingual Defining Issues Test]; translation produces verdict flips (EN "Yes", German/Urdu "No") [2025-Farid-One Model, Many Morals]; French vs English HISTOIRESMORALES splits Mistral 93.78% vs 83.59% [2025-Leteno-Histoires Morales]; low-resource-language fine-tuning moves multilingual moral scores most (+27.1/−28.7) [2025-Zhou-Moral Reasoning Across Languages]. Vida notes Llama-3-70B is *worst* (RMSE 0.672) — scale does not buy alignment [2024-Vida-Decoding Multilingual Moral Preferences].

11. **Grading the reasoning process rather than the verdict sidesteps the "no correct answer" problem — the corpus's most promising design shift.** MoReBench scores 23,018 signed, weighted, atomic rubric criteria against reasoning traces, is uncorrelated with math/code/general benchmarks (r∈[−0.245, 0.216]), and shows models favor Utilitarian/Deontological framings as training side-effects [2025-Chiu-MoReBench]; theory-guided CoT conditions and compares across normative frameworks, with up to 78% of misalignments attributable to bad annotations not model failure [2024-Zhou-Rethinking Machine Ethics]; Galatolo decomposes into abductive (value→precept) + deductive (precept→consequence) reasoning [2025-Galatolo-Beyond Ethical Alignment]; Samway's Reason-then-Decide vs Decide-then-Reason reveals pre-decision CoT is more deontological than post-hoc explanation (CDGAP delta −0.178) [2025-Samway-Are Language Models Consequentialist or Deontological].

12. **Moral representations are internally structured, sparse, localizable, and causally steerable — not pure mimicry — yet ethical stance is steerable and fragile.** ValueLocate found <0.4% of neurons value-related near layer 15 [2025-Su-Understanding How Value Neurons Shape the]; Yu found foundations as distinct linear directions, foundation-specifically steerable (Care β=0.239; Authority ~5.7× gain under micro-steering) [2026-Yu-Tracing Moral Foundations in LLMs]; Goel localized 45/256 neurons (17.6%) as the causal moral-scoring circuit [2026-Goel-Building Interpretable Models for Moral Decision-Making; 2025-Schacht-Mapping Moral Reasoning Circuits]; Hu recovers linearly-separable, partly cross-lingual foundation directions at layer 17 (65.6%) [2026-Hu-Straight and Narrow]. Steering: TrolleyBench moved Gemini's consistency index 0.700→0.817 (egoism) or 0.583 (deontology) via one prompt line [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench]; An induced framework shifts via RL (utilitarian OOD +0.757) [2025-An-MoralReason]; model editing shows Justice/Virtue most editable, Deontology most robust [2025-Huang-Model Editing Double-Edged]. **Minority counter-point:** DailyDilemmas found *end-user* prompt steering "largely ineffective" [2024-Chiu-DailyDilemmas] — reconciled by who steers (developer/RL vs end-user).

13. **Structured prompting and reasoning scaffolds move moral judgment more than model scale, but reasoning capability trades off against safety.** First-principles prompting gives the largest gain (+7.3%); prompt structure explains 92% of accuracy variance, small models benefiting disproportionately [2025-Chakraborty-Structured Moral Reasoning]. Reasoning-mode toggling raises abstract moral-stage scores yet increases jailbreak vulnerability (0.68→0.89) — a capability-safety paradox [2025-Jiang-EMNLP Educator-role Moral and Normative LLMs Profiling]; visual distraction triggers System-1 processing that bypasses text safety and collapses hierarchical values [2026-PKU-Visual Distraction Moral Reasoning].

14. **Multimodal moral evaluation is systematically weaker than text, with coarse judgment near ceiling but norm attribution far lower.** M³oralBench (GPT-4o 0.726) and MORALISE show coarse judgment near ~85–88% but norm attribution far lower (proprietary 66.6%, open 42.6% hit rate) [2024-Yan-M³oralBench; 2025-Lin-MORALISE]; moral signal sometimes emerges only across modalities [2025-Gao-MIST].

15. **"Alignment" is partly a property of interaction structure, agent-type, and perceived quality rather than a fixed model trait, and perceived quality is not correctness.** Kneer (N=1029) found "agent-type value forks": people demand AI prioritize fairness over utility more than of humans (OR=0.53, p=0.015) [2025-Kneer-Hard Problem AI Alignment Value Forks]. GPT-4o advice is rated more moral than a NYT expert ethicist, but this reflects rhetorical polish / moral-language density, not correctness [2025-Dillion-AI Language Model Rivals Expert Ethicist]; anti-AI belief bias contaminates rater agreement independent of content [2024-Garcia-The Moral Turing Test].

16. **Moral annotation is irreducibly subjective and multi-dimensional; forcing agreement conceals structured disagreement.** MFT corpora report low raw kappa (MFTC Fleiss 0.27) rescued by PABAK (0.62–0.91), warning against "maximizing annotator agreement" [2020-Hoover-Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus; 2024-Cao-The Moral Foundations Weibo Corpus]; offensiveness variance is 39.5% individual vs 2.9% country, mediated by Care/Purity [2023-Davani-Disentangling Perceptions of Offensiveness]; morality is multi-dimensional (10 MFT elements) but self-supervision collapses to a do/don't axis — human labels are necessary [2024-Park-Morality is Non-Binary]; PluriHarms shows trait×prompt interaction and personalized>consensus alignment [2026-Li-PluriHarms].

17. **Foundational critiques warn that popular moral-NLP benchmarks conflate descriptive and normative ethics, equate moral-theory recall with alignment, and rest on shaky construct validity.** Vida's survey of 92 papers found 66.3% define no terminology, 53.3% rely on moral psychology vs only 17.4% on moral philosophy, 75.8% English-only [2023-Vida-Values, Ethics, Morals]; Hancox-Li's ETHICS audit found 19% expert–crowd disagreement on utilitarianism pairs and 18% of deontology prompts answerable by physical-impossibility knowledge alone [2024-Hancox-Li-Is ETHICS about ethics]. Optimal moral computation is NP-hard/undecidable, so approximation is unavoidable [2024-Stenseke-Computational Complexity]; phronesis is uncodifiable, arguing for range-of-acceptable-answers scoring [2024-Ober-Lyceum Aristotle].

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme's methods organize along seven orthogonal design axes. A benchmark makes a choice on each.

| Axis | Options (weak → strong for construct validity) | Representative cards |
|---|---|---|
| **Instrument grounding** | Ad-hoc face-valid items → ported human scale (MFQ/DIT/PVQ/WVS/MCT) → re-validated ported scale with human+model on identical items | [2023-Vida] → [2024-Aksoy; 2023-Deng] → [2026-Huang-Knowing; 2024-Khandelwal] |
| **Elicitation format** | Single-framing MCQ → multi-framing / forced pairwise comparison → generative/behavioral scenario → embodied/agentic trace | [2025-Jotautaite; 2018-Noothigattu; 2024-Ji] → [2023-Duan-Denevil; 2025-Andric] → [2018-Behzadan] |
| **Scoring target** | Single gold label → population-mean key → distributional/consensus target → reasoning-trace rubric → coherence/axiom (answer-key-free) | [2024-Sorin] → [2024-Ji] → [2021-Lourie; 2024-Senthilkumar; 2025-Yuan] → [2025-Chiu-MoReBench] → [2024-Seror] |
| **Difficulty gradient** | Flat → compositional context/defeasibility → moral-sensitivity (features amid noise) → two-tier (judge→attribute) | [2022-Jiang; 2023-Rao] → [2025-Kilov; 2025-Bae] → [2024-Ji; 2025-Lin] |
| **Robustness probing** | None → perturbation flip-rate (surface/POV/order/reframe) → social pressure/persuasion/multi-agent → emotional/modality shift | [2026-vanNuenen; 2025-Oh; 2025-Cheung] → [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion; 2025-Sachdeva] → [2024-Mozikov; 2026-PKU] |
| **Contamination control** | Canonical reused dilemmas → paraphrase/self-filter → procedural generation → de-novo native-language items + public/private split | [2025-Neuman-Analyzing] → [2025-Russo] → [2024-Jin; 2024-Franken; 2023-Duan-Denevil] → [2025-Kumar-UniMoral; 2025-Chiu-MoReBench] |
| **Culture/language** | English-only → translated → parallel corpus + local human baseline + external-index anchoring (WVS/Hofstede/relational-mobility) → non-Western native rubrics (Maqasid/Confucian/kapwa/VIA) | [2023-Benkler] → [2024-Vida] → [2025-Leteno; 2020-Awad-Universals] → [2024-MaqasidAI; 2025-Yu-CMoralEval; 2023-Catapang; 2025-Bryan] |

Orthogonally, mechanistic validity forms a distinct layer — contrastive value-pole probes [2025-Su], foundation-vs-social-norm contrasts [2026-Yu], and causal ablation/steering [2025-Schacht; 2026-Goel; 2026-Hu] — used to confirm that behavioral scores reflect an internal construct rather than surface mimicry.

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences.** (a) The Care/Fairness-over-binding WEIRD tilt is the corpus's bedrock finding, replicated across instruments, modalities, and languages [2024-Kirgis; 2025-Neuman-PRIME; 2025-Jotautaite; 2024-Almeida; 2024-Tlaie; 2024-Abdulhai; 2025-Sameer; 2024-Biedma; 2026-Tagarelli]. (b) Low response variance / the "correct answer effect" is read as an alignment *failure*, not success [2024-Almeida; 2025-Oh; 2024-Nunes; 2026-Huang-Knowing; 2025-Russo; 2025-Dickerson]. (c) Behavioral/scenario measures beat questionnaires, exposing knowing-vs-doing gaps [2023-Duan-Denevil; 2025-Andric; 2026-Huang-Knowing; 2026-Yun]. (d) Independent teams converge on contamination-resistant elicitation — procedural generation [2024-Jin; 2024-Franken], dynamic per-model prompts [2023-Duan-Denevil], indirect word-association probing [2025-Xiang], de-novo items [2025-Neuman-Auditing; 2025-Kumar-UniMoral]. (e) WEIRD skew and cross-lingual degradation are universal and predictable [2023-Benkler; 2025-Mohammadi; 2024-Vida; 2024-Khandelwal; 2025-Farid; 2025-Leteno; 2025-Flores]. (f) Human disagreement is signal, not noise, and should be preserved [2021-Lourie; 2025-Russo; 2024-Boerstler; 2023-Wang; 2020-Hoover; 2024-Cao; 2023-Davani; 2026-Li; 2024-Park; 2025-Wilders]. (g) Coarse binary judgment is near-saturated while fine-grained attribution/comparison/reasoning has large headroom [2024-Ji; 2024-Yan; 2025-Lin; 2025-Trager-MFTCXplain]. (h) Interpretability papers agree moral structure is sparse and localizable [2025-Su; 2026-Yu; 2025-Schacht; 2026-Goel; 2026-Hu], while disagreeing on depth (Su: middle ~15; Yu: late-crystallizing; Hu: layer 17).

**Disagreements.** (1) *Is there a ground truth?* Sorin codes a single "correct" deontological answer per medical dilemma [2024-Sorin]; MoralBench/Jiao score against population means [2024-Ji; 2025-Jiao]; Yuan, Morlat, and the pluralist camp reject correctness for coherence/consensus [2025-Yuan; 2025-Morlat]; MoReBench, Ober, PluriHarms, and SOCIETY reject single answers entirely for pluralistic rubrics/range-scoring, treating population-mean scoring as a *category error* that penalizes principled dissent [2025-Chiu-MoReBench; 2024-Ober; 2026-Li; 2025-SOCIETY-How Much of a Pluralist is; 2023-Davani]. (2) *Steerability:* TrolleyBench/An/Huang show ethics readily steerable via prompt/RL/editing [2025-Zhu; 2025-An; 2025-Huang-Model Editing], while DailyDilemmas found end-user steering "largely ineffective" [2024-Chiu-DailyDilemmas] — reconciled by *who* steers. (3) *Human vs LLM competence:* Kilov and Dillion find LLMs match/beat humans on pre-packaged vignettes or perceived quality [2025-Kilov; 2025-Dillion], whereas Almeida, Sameer, Zhu, and Cheung find sub-human depth/consistency [2024-Almeida; 2025-Sameer; 2025-Zhu; 2025-Cheung] — reconciled by distinguishing perceived quality from structural competence. (4) *Conservative or liberal?* Abdulhai finds MFQ profiles nearest conservatives [2024-Abdulhai]; Fraser finds Delphi mirrors young liberals [2022-Fraser] — different instruments, models, annotator pools. (5) *Trolley dilemmas — foundational or misleading?* Awad treats 39.61M decisions as empirical bedrock with universal Switch>Loop>Footbridge ordering [2018-Awad; 2020-Awad-Universals], while Körner shows factual/historical dilemmas are rated far more realistic (dz=1.31) and Holyoak argues trolley dilemmas mischaracterize deontology as emotional error [2023-Körner; 2016-Holyoak]. (6) *Can crowd data ground alignment?* Delphi/Trager/Bulla defend crowdsourced norms [2022-Jiang; 2022-Trager; 2025-Bulla]; MEC, Hancox-Li, and Zhou argue crowd labels are the main error source and cannot correct erroneous common sense [2023-Boddington; 2024-Hancox-Li; 2024-Zhou-Rethinking]. (7) *Does capability help?* MoCa, Samway, Jiang, and Griffiths find scale/RLHF sometimes worsen factor-alignment, shift toward consequentialism, or *increase* implicit bias [2023-Nie; 2025-Samway; 2025-Jiang; 2025-Griffiths] — capability does not monotonically improve moral alignment. (8) *Is mimicking human bias desirable?* EAI treats matching human emotional irrationality as "alignment" [2024-Mozikov] — contestable for a values benchmark. (9) *Provenance:* Haslett shows country-of-origin barely predicts cultural alignment [2025-Haslett].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** *Clifford's MFV* [2015-Clifford-Moral Foundation Vignettes] is the psychometric gold standard: length/reading-level-controlled items, a two-stage validation ladder (reject <60% classification OR >20% cross-loading, then EFA/CFA + criterion validity), ideologically-balanced recruitment, and a non-moral "social norms" control that catches over-pathologizing. *MoReBench* [2025-Chiu-MoReBench] is the strongest process-scoring design: signed, weighted, expert rubrics, min-across-category macro-F1 for bias-robust judge selection, adversarial neutrality checks (equal-quality traces for opposite conclusions, t=−0.59, ns), a discriminatory-power meta-eval, and a public/private 500/500 contamination split. *MoCa* [2023-Nie-MoCa] mines factor-annotated cog-sci vignettes with minimal-pair manipulations and an explicit "ambiguous" class, using AMCE to expose latent factor sensitivities rather than a flat score. *Cheung* [2025-Cheung] sets the validity gold standard: pre-registered, logical-invariance criterion, base-vs-instruct-vs-domain-tuned causal design isolating RLHF. *Scruples* [2021-Lourie] pioneered distributional labels + the BEST oracle. *Seror* [2024-Seror-Moral Mind(s)] is most distinctive: answer-key-free coherence testing via revealed-preference GARP/CCEI axioms with a proper null. *TrolleyBench* [2025-Zhu], *Fact-or-Fair* [2024-Team-FABLE], *DeNEVIL* [2023-Duan-Denevil], *MultiTP* [2024-Jin], *OffTheRails* [2024-Franken], *Andric* [2025-Andric], and *SpeciesismBench* [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench] round out the exemplar set.

**Anti-patterns.** The *ETHICS* benchmark exemplifies construct-validity failure — 18% of deontology solvable via physics, 19% expert–crowd label disagreement [2024-Hancox-Li]. *Population-mean scoring keys* reward conformity and penalize principled dissent [2024-Ji; 2025-Jiao]. *Contaminated canonical dilemmas* (reused Trolley/Heinz) inflate apparent competence [2025-Neuman-Analyzing]. *One LLM generating items and scoring them* risks generator idiosyncrasy and circularity [2024-Biedma; 2025-Li-Analyzing Cognitive Differences (α>99% redundancy, GPT-4 generated then tested on GPT-4); 2024-Yan; 2025-Jotautaitė-Speciesism in AI]. *Over-small, over-confident benchmarks*: Sorin's 5 Western trolley variants with a single asserted correct answer [2024-Sorin]; Nadeem's 30 binary items with pre-baked justifications, κ ≤ 0.33 [2025-Nadeem-Ethical Decision-Making]; Benkler's one model, English-only, non-Anglophone personas [2023-Benkler]. *Surface-overlap metrics* (ROUGE/BLEU) poorly capture normative correctness [2022-Ziems-The Moral Integrity Corpus]; *LLM-judges rewarding verbosity* need word-count controls [2025-Neuman-Auditing]. Field-wide: Snoswell's 96% static pre-packaged datasets, 75% single-ground-truth [2025-Snoswell], and Vida's 66% define-no-terminology [2023-Vida]. MoralNet [2023-Astobiza-MoralNet] is a manifesto with no dataset — framing, not evidence. Abstract-only reads [2026-PKU; 2025-Hendra-Decoding Moral Responses] should be treated as leads, not evidence.

## 5. Open questions

- **Does any human instrument have established (not asserted) construct validity for LLMs?** No card demonstrates measurement invariance between humans and models on identical items; transfer is always flagged as unvalidated [2026-Yun; 2022-Fraser; 2024-Khandelwal].
- **What is the right target when humans themselves are 10–18% unstable and structurally disagree?** The corpus proposes distributional targets, coherence, and reasoning rubrics but does not settle which predicts real-world moral behavior [2024-Boerstler; 2021-Lourie; 2025-Chiu-MoReBench].
- **Do internal moral directions causally drive behavioral scores across models and languages?** Interpretability is single-model and disagrees on depth; no cross-model causal validation of behavioral benchmarks exists [2025-Su; 2026-Yu; 2026-Hu].
- **Does mechanistic steerability mean models have no stable "values," or that defaults are one steerable point?** Unresolved between the steering camp and default-profile reporting [2025-Zhu; 2024-Chiu-DailyDilemmas].
- **Which robustness perturbations predict deployment failures** vs merely being lab artifacts? Flip-rates are catalogued but not tied to downstream harm, except the extremism lead [2026-Hendricks-LLMs Fuel Extremist Attitudes].
- **How should non-Western moral frameworks be scored** without imposing WEIRD reliability assumptions (Afro-relational trust, phronesis, Maqasid)? Proposed but not operationalized with validated keys [2025-Amugongo; 2024-Ober; 2024-MaqasidAI].
- **Is agentic/embodied moral competence measurable at scale?** Only 3–4% of surveyed work is interactive; sensitivity and value-of-information are essentially untested [2025-Snoswell; 2018-Behzadan].

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Never treat a ported human instrument as valid without re-validating the transfer.** Administer identical items to humans and models, report cross-language and cross-prompt invariance, and add multi-prompt averaging (10 QA templates / 48 permutations) to strip phrasing artifacts. Pair every ported scale (MFQ/DIT/PVQ/MCT) with published human norms *and* a comparative-choice stage that catches acquiescence pattern-matching. [Claims 3, 4] [2026-Huang-Knowing; 2024-Khandelwal; 2020-Schramowski; 2024-Ji]

2. **Score the reasoning process and consistency, not just the verdict, for value-laden items lacking a correct answer.** Use signed, weighted, atomic rubrics graded against traces; reasoning-order manipulation to separate pre-deliberative from post-hoc rationalization; and reasoning-quality rubrics explicitly decoupled from correctness. This is uncorrelated with math/code benchmarks, so it measures something new. [Claim 11] [2025-Chiu-MoReBench; 2024-Zhou-Rethinking; 2025-Samway; 2025-Galatolo]

3. **Make robustness/consistency probes first-class, primary metrics — not diagnostics.** Multi-framing (binary/3-way/6-way), answer-order reversal, logical-invariance reframing (Yes↔No, action↔omission), surface/POV/persuasion flip-rate, paraphrase agreement, contradiction-check ECI + run-entropy, and questionnaire↔vignette coherence. A model that flips 45% under logically-equivalent reframing has no stable value to measure. [Claims 6, 8] [2025-Jotautaite; 2025-Oh; 2025-Cheung; 2026-vanNuenen; 2025-Zhu; 2024-Nunes]

4. **Score against the human disagreement distribution, not a single gold label or population mean.** Use distributional cross-entropy + a BEST-style oracle, Dirichlet/EM aggregation, consensus-level stratification, PABAK alongside kappa for sparse labels, and an explicit indecision/coin-flip option to test whether models mirror human uncertainty. Population-mean keys penalize principled minority positions — a construct-validity flaw for a *values* benchmark. [Claims 4, 16; Disagreement 1] [2021-Lourie; 2024-Senthilkumar; 2026-Li; 2020-Hoover; 2025-Dickerson]

5. **Treat low response variance and near-determinism as red flags, not success; report per-condition SD.** Homogenization is misalignment. Include an explicit indecision option and measure whether the model reproduces human variance, not just the human mean. [Claim 2] [2024-Almeida; 2025-Oh; 2026-Huang-Knowing; 2025-Dickerson]

6. **Measure the knowing-vs-doing gap directly by pairing a self-report scale with a behavioral scenario for the same subject, and split recognition from moral evaluation as two scored axes.** MCQ endorsement conceals behavioral misalignment; the speciesism 86%-recognize/34%-condemn split shows why. [Claim 5] [2025-Andric; 2026-Huang-Knowing; 2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench; 2023-Duan-Denevil]

7. **Engineer contamination resistance and moral sensitivity into the core.** Use procedural/causal-graph generation, dynamic model-specific prompts, indirect word-association probing, de-novo native-language items, and public/private splits; and embed relevant features amid noise rather than pre-highlighting them (test whether a model flags needing more information). Pre-packaging inflates apparent competence. [Claims 9, 10] [2024-Jin; 2024-Franken; 2023-Duan-Denevil; 2025-Kumar-UniMoral; 2025-Kilov; 2025-Snoswell]

8. **Make language and culture first-class axes with parallel corpora, language-matched local human baselines, per-language compliance/refusal reporting, external-index anchoring (WVS/Hofstede/relational-mobility), and native non-Western rubrics.** Include low-resource languages deliberately (they move scores most). Report distance to human data in both direction and magnitude (AMCE/L2), with human-to-human panels as the ceiling. [Claims 1, 10] [2025-Leteno; 2024-Khandelwal; 2025-Zhou-Moral Reasoning; 2020-Awad-Universals; 2024-MaqasidAI; 2025-Zaim Bin Ahmad]

9. **Stress-test stability under social pressure, deliberation, emotion, multi-agent interaction, and modality shift — and report steerability explicitly (developer injection AND end-user correction), giving default and steered profiles.** Single-agent neutral evaluation misses distortions (Utilitarian Boost, persuasion flips, sycophancy asymmetry, System-1 visual bypass). Vary interaction format and agent-type as factors and run parallel human-agent vs AI-agent scenarios to detect value forks. [Claims 7, 12, 13, 15] [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion; 2025-Keshmirian; 2024-Mozikov; 2026-PKU; 2025-Zhu; 2024-Chiu-DailyDilemmas; 2025-Kneer]

10. **Build a two-tier difficulty gradient with compositional context and mechanistic validity checks, and guard against circularity and glib fluency.** Easy binary-judgment tier plus a hard tier (fine-grained norm attribution, forced pairwise comparison, rationale-span extraction) that resists ceiling saturation; grade difficulty via compositional context/defeasibility and context-ablation; complement behavior with contrastive value-pole probes and causal ablation/steering to confirm construct validity; never let one LLM both generate and score without external gold; down-weight coherence/verbosity; and include non-moral negative controls to catch over-pathologizing. [Claims 3, 8, 12, 14, 17] [2024-Ji; 2025-Lin; 2025-Bae; 2025-Su; 2026-Yu; 2024-Biedma; 2025-Jiao; 2015-Clifford]
