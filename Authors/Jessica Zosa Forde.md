# Jessica Zosa Forde

## Research Summary

Jessica Zosa Forde is a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Brown University, studying the evaluation of AI systems with an emphasis on real-world settings. Her work focuses on improving the reliability of deep learning models in high-stakes domains such as healthcare. Prior to her PhD, she was a data scientist at organizations including DARPA and McKinsey, and an open source core-maintainer at Project Jupyter.

## Key Contributions

- Research on model selection's disparate impact in real-world deep learning applications
- Work evaluating large multilingual language models (Mozilla Technology Fund 2023 Award, covered in WIRED)
- Editor for "I Can't Believe It's Not Better!" workshop proceedings at NeurIPS
