# Bertie Vidgen

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bertie Vidgen is the CEO and co-founder of Rewire, a tech startup specializing in socially responsible AI for online safety (acquired by ActiveFence in 2023). He is also a Research Fellow at The Alan Turing Institute and a visiting researcher at the University of Oxford. He earned his PhD from the Oxford Internet Institute, focusing on Islamophobic hate speech on social media.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bertie’s research and commercial work focus on the detection and mitigation of online harms, including hate speech, misinformation, and extremism. He leads efforts to build robust, socially aware AI models for content moderation. He is also a primary investigator in AI alignment and the development of safety frameworks for large language models.

### Significant Achievements
He has served as a specialist adviser to the UK Parliament for the Online Safety Bill and is a member of the World Economic Forum's Expert Advisory Group on Digital Safety. He has published over twenty peer-reviewed papers on computational social science and AI safety. His startup, Rewire, has been instrumental in providing production-ready safety guardrails for the AI industry.
