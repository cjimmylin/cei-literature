## 2. Block adjudications (reasoning)

**2.1 CHARITROC (overruled to PWF).** R2 is right that parametric equal-variance d′ over heterogeneous graded multiturn items is indefensible. But the construct (separating discrimination from disposition in benign/malicious interpretation) does not depend on that estimator: replace with ordinal ROC machinery (graded responses → empirical ROC per item family; discrimination as AUC-class statistic; disposition as operating point), item heterogeneity handled by family-stratified estimation. The criterion "smuggled loss function" objection is accepted and inverted into a feature: report the operating point against an *explicit, stated* cost-ratio family with sensitivity analysis — that is more honest than any existing over-refusal metric, and becomes part of the podium claim. Stage-3 updating becomes a separately-reported metric (it was always separable). Block dissolved because every fatal element has a standard, superior replacement that preserves the design's identity.

**2.2 EGOTAX (block accepted; design inverted, not killed).** Eval-time derivation of verified-better R from arbitrary fresh O is indeed not automatable outside test-backed code. Inversion: the primary arm uses pre-authored, pre-verified artifact pairs where the model produces O to a fixed spec and the environment supplies R with checkable evidence; O's quality is *measured* against the same checklist rather than *controlled*. The self-preference construct survives (retention of own-authored O vs adoption of R remains revealed preference; the quality ordering is known per-episode after scoring O). O-derivation survives as a code-only robustness arm where tests make verification mechanical. Adoption-asymmetry statistics unchanged.

**2.3 OPENDOOR (block accepted; closed-world redesign).** The real-world inventory completeness problem and the Opus-authored-ground-truth circularity are fatal as specced. Redesign: every item becomes a **closed world** — the episode ships its own normative corpus (program rules, policy documents, procedure manuals; fictionalized but internally complete), and the option inventory is *derivable from the corpus by construction*. Recall = corpus-derivable options surfaced; fabrication = options not derivable from the corpus; empty-inventory items are provably empty within the corpus. This inherits DIMGLASS's corpus-local truth discipline, kills the completeness and circularity objections, and costs some ecological validity — accepted and stated as a limitation with a real-world-jurisdiction pilot on the roadmap.

**2.4 ONEBODY (block partially overruled).** R4's "results are about the model × the simulation" is true of every synthetic-environment benchmark, including all accepted game-based evals; artificiality alone is not a block. What IS accepted: the brief over-claimed real-world depolarization. Re-scope: claims become "depolarization competence in a specified opinion-dynamics environment," with the two-family requirement repositioned honestly as internal robustness; a small human-anchoring study (do human groups rate the agent's interventions as bridging?) enters the roadmap as the external-validity leg; and the exploitation probe (private-goal-via-division), which is valid regardless of dynamics realism, is promoted to co-headline. Blocked as originally claimed; passes as re-scoped.

**2.5 v3 portfolio / megamatrix (block accepted in substance).** R5's algebra is correct: CT-CM over 10 models cannot estimate a 16×16 clause structure. Re-scope to a **two-level validity analysis**: (L1) cross-method profile concordance at model level — rank/profile agreement across methods, reported descriptively with exact small-n inference, no latent modeling; (L2) clause-structure analysis at episode level — pooled item-level scores across models (thousands of episodes, model fixed effects) test whether 16 clauses are behaviorally distinct. The **hierarchical registry fallback** is adopted: C1–C16 remains the authoring taxonomy; if L2 shows collapse, reporting drops to the pre-registered 6-family level (capability-vs-love; patience/kindness; status (C4/C5/C7); truth/dignity (C6/C10/C11); protective trust (C12/C13/C14); endurance/humility (C15/C16/C2 overlap resolved at analysis)) — this is an empirical outcome, not a failure, and becomes a headline finding either way. Shared-method-variance critique accepted with mitigations: ≥2 mechanically-scored methods per clause; independent item authorship quota (CRUCIBLE human-injection); judge-family diversity. R7's first-order/second-order split adopted: triangulation core = 01/02/04/05/06 + 03's ENACT cells; 07/08/09/10 repositioned as *property studies of the profile* (invariance, degradation, durability, self-knowledge) — the portfolio thesis text must be rewritten accordingly.

**2.6 WEEKLONG (block accepted; descoped, not killed).** The tagged/untagged contrast as written is self-defeating (R5-C1 correct). Replaced by a **salience-gradient design**: all scored windows are seeded elicitations, with generator-controlled situational salience/test-likeness varying from conspicuous to buried; the exam-behavior gradient = score as a function of salience. Estimable, meaningful, and preserves the headline intent. The performative-virtue objection (C2) is *overruled doctrinally*: virtue-by-proxy is a portfolio-wide commitment — sustained enacted virtue at cost is the measurand; but the claim language is tightened from "deployed disposition" to "sustained enacted profile under continuity" (accept the wording criticism, reject the construct criticism). Cost (C3): pilot descoped to 3 weeks × 250 turns × control-pairing on the C9 subset only; scale-up is post-pilot and conditional. WEEKLONG stays late in build order per R7.

**2.7 MANYMIRRORS (block accepted; pairing redesigned).** R5-D1 identified a genuine self-inflicted flaw: the Latin square destroyed the within-model comparison that is the design's point. Fix: **certified twin-structure pairs** — each model encounters structure S in role A and its equivalence-certified twin S′ in role B; within-model hypocrisy indices computed over twin pairs; the existing structure-equivalence protocol (already specced with 80% rater bar) becomes the load-bearing validation, and twin-pair equivalence error propagates into the index CIs. Latin-square assignment is retained only for cross-model calibration cells. CT-CM demoted per CO-6; hypocrisy indices are direct paired contrasts needing no latent model.

**2.8 TONGUES (block accepted; reviewer's unblock adopted).** Localization does confound item and group for scalar-invariance purposes. Adopted wholesale: Arm 1 = rigorous translation-only battery → the measurement-invariance analysis; Arm 2 = cultural localization → a separately-reported DIF/robustness study answering a different question ("does the profile survive native re-instantiation?"). The behavioral measurement model is specified: item-parcel scores per clause, ordinal multi-group factor model, invariance ladder on parcels.

**2.9 v3 98 guide dependency contradiction (accepted).** "§7-gated source items" is unsatisfiable during authoring if §7 requires model sweeps. Defined: **interim gate** = synthetic-recovery asserts pass + adversarial-filter bots defeated + fixture-validated scorers + hardness-gate log complete (no model sweeps). Downstream banks (03/07/08/09/10 dependencies) may consume interim-gated items; full §7 pilot gates remain pre-publication requirements for every bank.
