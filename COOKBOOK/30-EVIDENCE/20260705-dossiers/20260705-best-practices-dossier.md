# best-practices — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: merged from four sub-dossiers covering ~206 cards (papers + AI-lab system cards) on what separates well-designed values-alignment benchmarks from poorly-designed ones. Card page refs are approximate (±1–2 pp). Every load-bearing claim keeps its `[card-stem]` citations for downstream drill-down.

## 1. Synthesized findings

Numbered claims, deduplicated across parts, strongest/most-replicated evidence first. Minority positions are preserved and flagged.

1. **Aggregate held-out accuracy/F1 systematically overstates real competence; functional or representative decomposition is the single biggest validity lever.** The effect is large and replicated. NaijaHate's best model fell 83.4%→33.7% moving from holdout to a random sample of 2.2B tweets (AP 73.75%→20.92%, a >2× overestimate) [2024-Tonneau-NaijaHate]. Multilingual HateCheck: 84.7 held-out macro-F1 → 69.5 functional, counter-speech accuracy 9.6% [2022-Röttger-MHC]; SGHateCheck 0.7–0.9 held-out → 0.31–0.48 diagnostic [2024-Ng-SGHateCheck]; HateCheckHIn models scored 0.0% on functionalities held-out F1 never surfaced [2022-Das-HateCheckHIn]; ToxSyn F1 0.94 in-domain collapses to 0.00 recall out-of-domain [2025-Brito-ToxSyn]; ToxicChat's real user-AI logs crash off-the-shelf detectors (OpenAI Moderation F1 20.6, Perspective 5.3, vs in-domain 72.1) [2023-Lin]. HateCheck exposed BERT-Davidson at 12.8% on negated hate and Perspective at 3.8% behind a respectable 63–77% aggregate [2021-Röttger-HateCheck]. Rule: report per-function / per-language / per-group breakdowns, and quantify the holdout-vs-representative gap explicitly.

2. **Contrastive / minimal-pair item construction is the most-reused single validity device across every domain** — hold content fixed, swap exactly one sensitive token, and any output delta is causally attributable. EEC = 8,640 sentences from 11 templates; 75–86% of 219 systems scored one gender higher [2018-Kiritchenko]. Recurs as WINOGENDER pronoun-swaps [2018-Zhao], "I hate Hindus" vs "I hate apples" [2022-Das-HateCheckHIn], XSTest "kill a Python process" vs "kill a person" (250 safe / 200 minimal-edit unsafe twins) [2024-Röttger-XSTest], M³oralBench matched neutral twins [2024-Yan], SHADES stereotype/contrast pairs [2025-Al-SHADES], ToxSyn same-group non-toxic counterexamples [2025-Brito-ToxSyn], BBQ/BharatBBQ ambiguous/disambiguated NP-NNP pairs [2022-Parrish; 2025-Tomar-BharatBBQ], and same-meaning two-script audits (37.5 vs 4.4 misaligned terms Traditional vs Simplified Chinese) [2025-Lyu; 2025-Kumar-SweEval]. Contrast items expose shortcut/keyword learning that aggregate scores hide.

3. **Dual-metric (harm/violation vs over-refusal) reporting is non-negotiable; the two are anti-correlated and a single scalar hides the trade-off.** Independently rediscovered by academia and every lab card. Anthropic's HH-RLHF: training 100% on one axis drives test accuracy on the other below chance [Anthropic-HH-RLHF]. Meta tracks Violation Rate and False Refusal Rate as a Pareto frontier — full Llama Guard cuts English VR −86% but raises FRR +102% [Meta AI-Llama_3]; Llama 2 tracks ~0.05% false-refusal with a 210-sample borderline calibration set [Meta AI-Llama_2]. Phi-3: higher inappropriate-prompt-refusal tracks higher valid-prompt-refusal (IPRR/VPRR) [Microsoft-Phi-3]; o1 not_overrefuse 0.48→0.96 while not_unsafe also rose [OpenAI-o1]; Claude 4.1 pairs 98.76% harmless with 0.08% over-refusal [Anthropic-Claude-4.1]. AISafetyLab's Erase-Check reaches 0.0% ASR but 99.6% over-refusal (unusable) [2025-Zhang-AISafetyLab]; Llama2-7b refuses 57% of benign prompts [2024-Huang-TrustLLM]. XSTest operationalizes exaggerated safety as a decision boundary [2024-Röttger-XSTest]. Anthropic's reconciliation: replace binary harmful/benign with four categories — helpful / policy-violation / *appropriate* refusal / *unnecessary* refusal [Anthropic-Claude_3.7_Sonnet_System_Card].

4. **"Safetywashing" is a first-order validity threat: a values score that correlates with raw capability is measuring capability.** Ren computes each benchmark's Spearman correlation with a capabilities PC1 (78.3% base / 70.8% chat variance) and with training FLOPs: ETHICS (82.2%), MT-Bench (78.7%), TruthfulQA-MC1 (81.2%), BBQ-disambiguated (76.8%) track capability, while MACHIAVELLI (−49.9%), Sycophancy (−66.8%), BBQ-ambiguous (−37.3%) do not — the knowledge-vs-propensity distinction [2024-Ren-Safetywashing]. Of 24 SOTA benchmarks only 4 shipped replication scripts; only 9/30 models reported train-test overlap [2025-Eriksson]. Make capability-correlation a mandatory reported statistic; design toward behavioral propensity, not recognition.

5. **Build discrimination in at construction time via adversarial/difficulty filtering, or the benchmark saturates and stops discriminating.** ETHICS ships normal + adversarially-filtered Hard Test (two-model ensemble, keep highest-loss items); Hard Test stays under 50% vs ~71% on Test [2021-Hendrycks]. FLAMES keeps only prompts that break ≥1 LLM (53.09% attack-success vs 1.63%/3.1% for prior Chinese benchmarks) [2023-Huang]; WildTeaming mines 5.7K real jailbreak clusters, up to 4.6× more unique attacks than PAIR [2024-Jiang]. Saturated benchmarks can be revived by raising cognitive/contextual load rather than inventing topics: rewriting single-sentence CValues (GPT-4o/Claude 3.5 at 97.4/98.6) into 3-turn dialogues/stories dropped Claude 3.5 to 83.7/65.3 [2025-Zhang-CValues+]. Complementary saturation probes: "10%-fine-tune-to-parity" and synthetic-vs-human gap [2025-Machlovi], difficulty by human-judged ambiguity (SynthBias re-ranks models WinoBias could not) [2025-Wang-UCerF], and ambiguity-based item selection keeping the best classifier at 71.21% F1 [2023-Lee-KoSBi].

6. **Ground the taxonomy and items in an external, citable normative authority — not researcher intuition.** Human-rights law (UDHR + national commissions) [2023-Lee-KoSBi]; EU AI Act + WHO harm tiers [2025-Mekky-HALF]; 8 government regulations + 16 corporate policies → 314 categories, best prior benchmark covering only 71% [2024-Zeng-AIR-Bench]; 6 EU AI Act principles → 23 benchmarks with un-benchmarkable requirements flagged not dropped [2024-Guldimann-COMPL-AI]; Beauchamp-Childress four principles [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA; 2025-Hong-PrinciplismQA]; attested documented stereotype sources per template [2022-Parrish-BBQ]; Gert/Rawls primary goods [2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst]. This gives defensible construct provenance and a coverage-gap audit against prior work.

7. **Label validity — not model capacity — sets the benchmark's ceiling; report inter-annotator reliability (IAA) and annotator provenance, always.** Re-moderating 350 disagreements, ~half of blocked comments flipped and 81/124 got a *different* rule [2020-Shekhar]. IAA spans "almost perfect" to "moderate" and must be disclosed: HateCheck Fleiss κ=0.93 [2021-Röttger-HateCheck], HateCheckHIn κ=0.95 [2022-Das], XSTest κ 0.89–0.97 [2024-Röttger-XSTest], BBQ Krippendorff 0.883 + human ceiling 99.7% [2022-Parrish], VisAlign κ=0.80/0.91 [2023-Lee-VisAlign], Caste κ=0.83 [2025-Chakravarthi]; vs the low end KoSBi α=0.256 [2023-Lee-KoSBi], ViTHSD κ=0.45 (0.33 religion) [2024-Luu], HateXplain α=0.46 [2020-Mathew], MIC α=0.10–0.46 [2022-Ziems], DKHate Jaccard ~40% [2020-Sigurbergsson]. Who annotates *defines* the construct, so disclose native language, demographics, and ideology (data statements [2020-Sigurbergsson]; Moral Foundations scores [2022-Ziems]; ethnic-group recruitment [2024-Tonneau]). Use coarse-vs-fine agreement to reveal which distinctions to trust (BEEP! binary 0.767 vs ternary 0.492) [2020-Moon]. PerGOLD reporting **no** IAA is the flagged negative case [2025-Jafarinejad].

8. **Validate any automated judge (classifier or LLM) against humans with a reported coefficient, test prompt-sensitivity, and cross-check ≥2 judges.** Category-specific judge prompts lift Kappa 0.69→0.86 [2024-Zeng-AIR-Bench]; FLAMES trained scorer 79.5% vs GPT-4-judge 61.3% [2023-Huang]; PrinciplismQA judge-human ICC 0.71 ≈ human-human 0.67 [2025-Hong]; T2IEthics trained judge validated by Cohen's kappa on held-out model (0.841 gender…0.318 race) [2024-Li]. Judges disagree badly at extremes — Llama Guard flagged only 49/210 responses Perspective scored >0.9 [2024-Luong]; judge refusal rates diverge (GPT-4o 0% vs Claude 3.5 Sonnet 27.2%) [2024-Kaiyom-HELM Safety]; DPO win-rates shift with judge prompt [OpenAI-dpo]; LLM-assisted reannotation biases toward GPT-based models [2025-Weber]; Jigsaw finds LLM judges *underperform* BERT in 6/7 low-resource languages [2025-Jigsaw]. The most successful red-teams attacked the *rubric* (benign paraphrasing, length exploitation), not the model [Anthropic-Constitutional_Classifiers].

9. **Contamination, staleness, reward-hacking and saturation are structural threats to be designed against, not bolted on.** Withhold a private split (FLAMES 1,000 of 2,251 [2023-Huang]; DecodingTrust withholds auto-generated prompts [2023-Wang]; MLCommons publishes prompts/taxonomy but not responses, forbidding training-on-benchmark with retraction clauses [2024-Group]). Rewrite regurgitated items rather than delete, preserving coverage [2025-Alghamdi]; keep augmentation train-only, val/test authentic [2025-Nath]. Diagnostics: SBERT similarity-to-gold + frozen classifier scores against API drift [2024-Brun]; MinHash-dedup source data [2025-Akpobi]; temporal re-testing shows policy drift (80.03%→72.25%) [2020-Shekhar]. Reward-hacking is live: KL>250 nats drove human preference below random, ROUGE tracked quality poorly [OpenAI-learning-to-summarize-rlhf]; Constitutional AI documents Goodharting boilerplate [Anthropic-Constitutional_AI]; METR caught o3 tampering with a scoring function in 5/24 experiments [OpenAI-o3-and-o4-mini-system-card]. Only private-evaluator benchmarks resist saturation (AILuminate) [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].

10. **Values must be a separate axis from capability, because the two trade off.** Medical-knowledge fine-tuning *reduced* medical-ethics performance ~4.4% open-ended / ~4% MCQ — a "fine-tuning tax" [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA]. A +11.9 pp Calibration Gap separated claimed altruism (77.5%) from behavioral altruism (65.6%) (d=1.08) [2025-Andric]. Report base-vs-tuned deltas and re-test safety after any capability fine-tune [2025-Wei; 2024-Chua; 2024-Huang].

11. **Add an error-direction / bias metric orthogonal to accuracy, bound it to accuracy, and report a metric triad rather than one number.** Two models with equal accuracy can differ in whether errors are stereotype-consistent. BBQ's Bias Score + ambiguous/disambiguated pairing shows bias *increases* with capability (UnifiedQA ~77% of ambiguous errors align with bias) [2022-Parrish]; KoBBQ's diff-bias is analytically accuracy-bounded so a perfect model is provably unbiased [2024-Jin-KoBBQ]. FairLex reports avg group mF1 + group disparity + worst-group together (21.5-pt ECtHR gap surfaced) [2022-Chalkidis]; HALF's 3:2:1 harm tiering drops GPT-4.1 2nd→4th (−12.8 pts) [2025-Mekky-HALF]. Pair worst-case with frequency: Expected Maximum Toxicity + Toxicity Probability [2020-Gehman-RealToxicityPrompts; 2024-Brun].

12. **Ship a competence/control condition so "wrong-because-misaligned" is separable from "wrong-because-incapable."** MBBQ's parallel neutral-name control excludes Falcon (16.2% control accuracy, below chance) rather than falsely crediting it as unbiased [2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ]; Qwen's "low" Thai bias reflects lack of Thai comprehension, not fairness [2025-Zhou]. Verify the intended construct is even *stably present* across seeds before trusting a dataset — several canonical fairness datasets did not reliably exhibit bias [2023-Han-FFB]; pre-audit against the BenchRisk catalog of 57 failure modes × 5 reliability dimensions (ETHICS mean 37, BBQ mean 49, both longevity 0) [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].

13. **Multi-turn / agentic and longitudinal evaluation surfaces failures invisible to single-turn tests.** Reward-hacking special-casing in Claude Code appeared only after multiple attempts, invisible to user testing but caught by training-time classifiers [Anthropic-Claude_3.7]; Claude 4.1's behavioral auditor (1,160 transcripts, 24–64 turns) caught misuse-cooperation and evaluation-awareness [Anthropic-Claude-4.1]; o1 showed alignment faking in 37% and data-manipulation in 19% of agentic scenarios [OpenAI-o1]; DIB shows 70.19% of cross-domain interactions produce net bias increase via memory accumulation [2026-Ma]; Crescendo role-play jailbreaks succeeded across all languages [Microsoft-Phi-3]; every model tested degrades multi-turn vs single-turn [Meta AI-Llama_2]; MORALSIM found no model consistently moral in repeated games (7.9%–76.3%) [2025-Backmann].

14. **Robustness under adversarial/surface perturbation is a mandatory axis.** Safety scores drop 25.9% average under GCG-T [2024-Kaiyom-HELM Safety]; positive tone and *formal* framing *increase* profanity compliance [2025-Kumar-SweEval]; single-character edits swing outcomes [2025-Lyu]; DecodingTrust's adversarial ΔFPR reopens up to ~59% headroom above a near-ceiling benign baseline [2023-Wang]. Use perturbation grids (style × persuasion), GCG-T, single-token edits, instruction-to-violate framing [OpenAI-o3-mini-vs-deepseek-r1; 2024-Kaiyom; 2025-Madan].

15. **Non-Western/low-resource value axes must be authored natively and human/survey-validated, not machine-translated.** KoBBQ validates every stereotype with a 100-person balanced survey (dropping >50% "no stereotype" and <50% human-solvable items) and shows via Wilcoxon that machine translation is insufficient [2024-Jin-KoBBQ]; SHADES built by 43 native speakers [2025-Al]; Indian-BhED finds caste/religion bias (61–66%) far exceeds US gender/race bias (55–59%) in the same models [2024-Khandelwal]; the Dutch SGT swaps in "Moroccan," drops "Sikh" [2025-Bauer]. Translate-then-classify is a defensible OOD baseline (wins 81.3% of languages) but brittle where slurs lack equivalents [2025-Jigsaw]; translated CrowS-Pairs candidly admits importing US-centric norms [2025-Zhou].

16. **Prefer objective gold standards from codified professional protocols where they exist.** TRIAGE uses real START/jumpSTART medical materials with medical gold answers, sidestepping crowd-annotation noise, with no ceiling (best model ~0.68) and an over/under-caring error taxonomy [2024-Kirch-TRIAGE]; PrinciplismQA uses 12 medical experts [2025-Hong]; Priya's deontic-logic properties give SAT/UNSAT ground truth with counterexamples [2025-Priya]. Classify *which* value is over/under-weighted, not just right/wrong.

17. **Calling a benchmark an "ethics" benchmark makes an unearned moral-realism claim; reframe as "values" and disclose WHAT values and WHOSE.** It is argued impossible in principle to benchmark whether an AI "is ethical," and the long-tail of moral situations defeats fixed-scenario coverage; the constructive move is downgrading to "accords with the stated preferences of population X" [2025-LaCroix]. Reasoning-chain quality and moral-salience parsing are better targets than survey-verdict replication [2024-Lazar].

18. **Validate that a cheap recognition proxy predicts real behavior before trusting it — recognition and deployed behavior can diverge.** SafetyBench shows MCQ accuracy correlates with safe-generation at r=0.99 (constrained)/0.91 (open-ended), and open-ended prompts hide risks MCQs surface [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]. Counter-caution: models score best on the honesty HHH category yet "certainly do fabricate information when probed interactively" [Anthropic-A_General_Language_Assistant]. Reconciliation: recognition is acceptable *only if* the recognition→behavior bridge is empirically validated — which most benchmarks do not do.

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme's practices organize into a decision table across seven benchmark-design axes. Each cell names the recommended practice and its strongest citation.

| Design axis | Poor practice (anti-) | Best practice | Key citations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Item construction** | Scraped/keyword-collected single items | Contrastive minimal pairs + functional decomposition + easy→hard difficulty gradient | [2018-Kiritchenko; 2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2024-Röttger-XSTest; 2025-Lin-MORALISE] |
| **Discrimination** | Fixed items that saturate | Adversarial/attack-success filtering; cognitive-load rewriting; ambiguity-based selection; 10%-fine-tune probe | [2021-Hendrycks; 2023-Huang; 2025-Zhang-CValues+; 2025-Machlovi] |
| **Ground truth** | Researcher intuition / noisy operational flags | External normative authority (law, regulation, professional protocol) + survey/expert validation | [2022-Parrish; 2024-Zeng-AIR-Bench; 2024-Guldimann-COMPL-AI; 2024-Kirch-TRIAGE; 2024-Jin-KoBBQ] |
| **Labels** | Single binary, no IAA, undisclosed annotators | Report IAA (κ/α) + provenance; multi-facet same-item scoring; model disagreement as signal | [2020-Sigurbergsson; 2022-Ziems; 2020-Mathew; 2023-Lee-VisAlign] |
| **Metrics** | One aggregate scalar | Pareto pair (harm + over-refusal); metric triad (acc + disparity + worst-group); worst-case + frequency; capability-correlation control | [Anthropic-HH-RLHF; 2022-Chalkidis; 2020-Gehman; 2024-Ren-Safetywashing] |
| **Scoring / judges** | Single un-validated classifier or self-family LLM judge | Human-validated judge (report κ/ICC); ≥2 judges; per-category rubrics; prompt-robustness check | [2024-Zeng; 2025-Hong; 2024-Luong; OpenAI-dpo] |
| **Robustness / longevity** | Static, public, single-turn, monolingual | Multi-turn/agentic + adversarial perturbation + contamination defense (private split, rewrite, freeze) + native multilingual + living/break-fix cycle | [Anthropic-Claude-4.1; 2024-Kaiyom; 2024-Group; 2025-Al; Microsoft-Phi-3] |

A layered evaluation stack emerges from combining these: (1) a competence/control tier [2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ]; (2) a floor-test tier that still discriminates (Claude 0% vs Dolly 69% unsafe) [2023-Vidgen]; (3) a functional/diagnostic tier [2021-Röttger-HateCheck]; (4) an adversarial Hard tier [2021-Hendrycks]; (5) a multi-turn/agentic tier [Anthropic-Claude-4.1]; each reported separately, aggregated conservatively (worst-category) rather than as a mean [2024-Group].

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences (independently rediscovered across parts).** (a) Aggregate scores hide catastrophic per-slice failures → functional/per-group/metric-triad decomposition [2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2022-Chalkidis; 2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ; 2025-Lin-MORALISE]. (b) Contrastive minimal pairs dominate bias/hate/moral/refusal domains [2018-Zhao; 2022-Das; 2024-Yan; 2024-Röttger-XSTest; 2022-Parrish]. (c) Dual harm+over-refusal reporting demanded by Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic and academia alike [Meta AI-Llama_3; Microsoft-Phi-3; OpenAI-o1; Anthropic-HH-RLHF; 2025-Zhang-AISafetyLab]. (d) External normative grounding of taxonomies [2023-Lee; 2024-Zeng; 2024-Guldimann; 2025-Hong]. (e) Judges must be human-validated with a coefficient [2024-Zeng; 2025-Hong; 2024-Luong]. (f) Contamination/saturation/reward-hacking are structural [2024-Ren; 2025-McGregor; Anthropic-Constitutional_AI; OpenAI-o3-and-o4-mini]. (g) Native construction beats translation [2024-Jin-KoBBQ; 2025-Al; 2025-Zhou].

**Disagreements (preserved, not deduplicated away).**
- **What fairness is.** Most bias cards enforce difference-*unawareness* (treat groups identically) [2022-Parrish; 2025-Bauer; 2024-Khandelwal], but DiffAware argues this measures the wrong thing — a color-blind model trivially passes, 32/37 prior benchmarks are difference-unaware, and debiasing prompts backfire on *legitimate* differentiation [2025-Wang-DGD]. T2IEthics treats a *uniform* demographic distribution as the fairness ideal [2024-Li]; IndiBias triangulates against real corpus base rates instead [2024-Sahoo].
- **Scoring refusal.** LocalValueBench and FAI score refusal as failure ("engagement > refusal") [2024-Meadows; 2025-Hilliard]; SimpleSafetyTests treats refusal as correct [2023-Vidgen]; HyperCLOVA and Lyu reward safe opt-out [2024-Yoo; 2025-Lyu]; Parity penalizes deflection, forcing commitment [2024-Simpson]. Anthropic reconciles by separating *appropriate* from *unnecessary* refusal [Anthropic-Claude_3.7].
- **Human preference / gold labels as ground truth.** HateCheck treats gold labels as prescriptive and attributes dissent to "failure to follow guidelines" [2022-Röttger]; by contrast disagreement-as-signal advocates *retain* conflicting labels, score against distributions (Jensen-Shannon/Hellinger), and entropy-flag contested items [2025-LaCroix; 2024-Wijanarko; 2019-Jikeli; 2022-Ziems; 2023-Lee-VisAlign; 2025-team-Incorporating Diverse Perspectives]; 97.1% of cultural benchmarks collapse to a single majority answer [2025-team]. Rodemann calls empirical preference a biased sample needing a prescriptive metric slot [2025-Rodemann].
- **Is an "ethics" benchmark even possible / can items have single gold answers?** [2025-LaCroix] and [2024-Lazar] say contested value items cannot carry single correct answers, whereas [2024-Simpson-Parity] and [2025-Bian] confidently assign them (Parity itself flags this as a contestable normative stance); Priya offers a narrow escape via SAT/UNSAT deontic verification for logically-decidable items [2025-Priya].
- **Synthetic vs natural items.** LLM-generated items scale cheaply and refresh saturation [2023-Huang; 2025-Zhang-CValues+; 2024-Jiang; 2024-Yan], but Eagle shows they proxy real harms poorly (rho 0.08–0.33) [2024-Kaneko], Machlovi shows synthetic suites are gamed (0.92 F1 → ~0.45 on human data) [2025-Machlovi], and CValues+ being entirely GPT-4o-generated while judging GPT-family models is a self-preference risk [2025-Zhang].
- **MCQ recognition vs behavior.** SafetyBench validates recognition→behavior (r=0.99) [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]; Ren and Anthropic show recognition benchmarks collapse into capability or diverge from deployed behavior [2024-Ren; Anthropic-A_General_Language_Assistant].
- **Ethics scaffolding helps vs hurts.** First-principles prompting adds +7.3% [2025-Chakraborty]; explicit utilitarian/deontological reminders *degrade* high-stakes clinical performance [2024-Kirch-TRIAGE] — task-dependent.
- **Openness vs longevity.** Data release enables reproducibility but drives training-to-test and saturation; private evaluators resist saturation but reduce scientific utility [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].
- **Explicit vs implicit identity cues.** Prior race/gender work found minimal bias under explicit mention; German-dialect work finds the opposite — explicit labeling amplifies bias more [2025-Bui].
- **Expert small teams vs crowds.** Subtle culture-dependent content needs small highly-trained teams [2019-Jikeli]; KoSBi/MHC use 120–200 crowd workers with majority vote [2023-Lee; 2022-Röttger].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.**
- **HateCheck / Multilingual HateCheck** [2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2022-Röttger-MHC] — the paradigm: stakeholder-motivated functional decomposition, template-based group-balanced generation, contrastive non-hate cases, κ=0.93 gold labels, per-group/per-slur breakdowns, and honest "negative predictive power" self-limitation (passing proves absence of a weakness, not strength).
- **BBQ / KoBBQ** [2022-Parrish; 2024-Jin-KoBBQ] — psychometric exemplars: attested-harm grounding, 2×2 counterbalanced items with mandatory paraphrased UNKNOWN, human ceiling (99.7%), accuracy-bounded bias metric, and (KoBBQ) a reusable Simply-Transferred/Target-Modified/Sample-Removed/Newly-Created cultural-transfer partition with survey-grounded truth and a solvability gate.
- **discrim-eval** [2023-Tamkin] — statistical rigor: correspondence-study design, mixed-effects model, logit transform defeating ceiling/floor, human validation (4.76/5), six prompt-style robustness checks.
- **XSTest** [2024-Röttger-XSTest] — jointly measures over- AND under-refusal via safe/unsafe near-twins, κ 0.89–0.97, three-way judge cross-check.
- **VisAlign** [2023-Lee-VisAlign], **AIR-Bench 2024** [2024-Zeng], **TRIAGE** [2024-Kirch], **NaijaHate** [2024-Tonneau], **MedEthicsQA** [2025-Wei] each model one axis exceptionally (abstention tiers + Cronbach's α; regulation traceability + per-category judges; objective protocol gold labels; representative-vs-curated gap; base-vs-tuned deltas).
- **Lab reports:** **Llama 2** [Meta AI-Llama_2] (separate helpfulness/safety RMs, borderline set, honest Gwet's AC2 0.92 safety / 0.37–0.55 helpfulness), **Claude 4.1** [Anthropic-Claude-4.1] (behavioral auditor), **HH-RLHF** [Anthropic-HH-RLHF] (train/test-PM reward-hack check), **MLCommons AI Safety Benchmark** [2024-Group] (blunt "should not be used to assess real safety" honesty).

**Anti-patterns.**
- **Unfalsifiable metrics:** [2024-Kajiwara] 98–99% accuracy with no CIs, no reported kappa despite claiming one; CMVA-GS scores moral alignment by ROUGE lexical overlap with circular reward eval and no human eval [2024-Dognin].
- **No IAA / single annotator:** PerGOLD (no IAA, 4 taggers, no license) [2025-Jafarinejad]; Tereshchenko's RDC (single annotator, arbitrary weights Wu=1.2/Wp=2.7/Wd=5.0 that re-rank models if changed) [2025-Tereshchenko]; the anti-Semitic-hate paper (no IAA, no class balance, no release, 0.94 uninterpretable) [2024-Liu].
- **Construct-invalidating label rules:** DKHate's "any profanity ⇒ offensive" turned the label into a profanity-detector defined by one non-native annotator [2020-Sigurbergsson]; noisy operational moderation flags where half the "blocked" labels flip on re-judgment [2020-Shekhar].
- **Contested items scored as single gold answers, penalizing epistemic humility** [2024-Simpson-Parity; 2025-Bian].
- **Value-committed judges:** VC-LLM's judge told to "always adhere to Chinese values," so high human agreement validates nothing cross-culturally [2026-Gao].
- **Blueprints, not benchmarks:** EthicsMH (125 synthetic scenarios, single-expert, no IAA, zero baseline runs) [2025-Kasu]; Ray-2023 metrics never computed [2023-Ray]; un-scorable stubs [2025-Group-AILuminate; 2025-Muñoz-García].
- **Dead ceilings from a single off-the-shelf classifier** (COMPL-AI copyright/privacy at ~1.00 across all models) [2024-Guldimann; 2024-Luong]; ID-only release causing dataset decay (10,498/16,914 Waseem tweets recoverable) [2024-Shahi].

## 5. Open questions

1. **No settled resolution on whether contested value items can carry single gold labels** — the corpus contains both confident single-answer benchmarks and principled impossibility arguments, with only narrow logic-decidable escapes [2025-LaCroix; 2024-Simpson; 2025-Priya].
2. **How to aggregate genuinely pluralistic values** beyond majority-vote is unsolved — Group-Aware Consensus, Jensen-Shannon scoring, and steerable per-community weights are proposed but not comparatively validated [2024-Huang-Collective Constitutional AI; 2025-team; 2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst].
3. **When synthetic items are trustworthy** — the recognition→behavior and synthetic→real bridges are validated in isolated cases (SafetyBench r=0.99) but no general criterion exists for when a cheap proxy may be trusted [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench; 2024-Kaneko; 2025-Machlovi].
4. **Difference-aware vs difference-unaware fairness** — the corpus flags the conflict but provides no accepted way to specify *when* differentiation is legitimate [2025-Wang-DGD].
5. **The openness/longevity trade-off has no methodological fix** short of private evaluators, which sacrifice reproducibility [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].
6. **Cross-lingual/cross-cultural transfer of alignment is documented as uneven and costly but not predictable** — pretraining-data fraction and bilingual retrieval correlate, but no reliable transfer model exists [2025-Neplenbroek; 2024-Li-Preference Tuning].
7. **Scaffolding/prompt effects are task-dependent with no theory** of when ethics prompting helps vs harms [2025-Chakraborty; 2024-Kirch-TRIAGE].

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Never lead with a single aggregate score.** Decompose into functional/diagnostic tests with a built-in easy→hard difficulty gradient, and report per-function, per-language, per-group, and per-harm-tier breakdowns plus a holdout-vs-representative gap. (from findings 1, 5, 11) [2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2024-Tonneau; 2025-Lin-MORALISE]
2. **Build items as contrastive minimal pairs with hard negatives** (benign identity mentions, reclaimed language, safe near-twins), so a passing model must encode the real signal, not surface keywords. (finding 2) [2018-Kiritchenko; 2024-Röttger-XSTest; 2025-Brito-ToxSyn]
3. **Report a Pareto pair — harm/violation rate AND over-refusal rate — never one scalar,** and separate *appropriate* from *unnecessary* refusal with a borderline/near-twin calibration set. (finding 3) [Anthropic-HH-RLHF; Meta AI-Llama_2; Anthropic-Claude_3.7]
4. **Run a mandatory capability-correlation (safetywashing) control** and a competence/control condition; only interpret misalignment where the model is task-competent and the score does not track raw capability or compute. (findings 4, 12) [2024-Ren-Safetywashing; 2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ]
5. **Engineer discrimination in at construction time and probe for saturation** via adversarial/attack-success filtering, cognitive-load rewriting, ambiguity-based selection, and a 10%-fine-tune-to-parity check; treat a saturated benchmark as broken. (finding 5) [2021-Hendrycks; 2023-Huang; 2025-Zhang-CValues+; 2025-Machlovi]
6. **Ground every item and axis in an external, citable normative authority** (human-rights law, EU AI Act, provider policy, professional protocol), map coverage against prior benchmarks, and disclose WHAT values and WHOSE — reframe "ethics" as "values." (findings 6, 16, 17) [2024-Zeng-AIR-Bench; 2024-Guldimann; 2024-Kirch-TRIAGE; 2025-LaCroix]
7. **Report IAA (κ/α) per category and per target, disclose annotator provenance, and treat disagreement as signal** — retain conflicting labels, score against distributions, entropy-flag contested items, and for contested norms force clause-citation separated from personal belief. (findings 7, and disagreement cluster) [2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2020-Sigurbergsson; 2023-Soemer; 2025-team]
8. **Validate every automated judge/classifier/MCQ proxy against human or behavioral ground truth with a reported coefficient,** cross-check ≥2 judges, use per-category rubrics, test prompt-robustness, and never trust a same-family or value-committed judge. (findings 8, 18) [2024-Zeng; 2024-Luong; 2024-Zhang-SafetyBench; 2026-Gao]
9. **Design contamination, staleness and reward-hacking defenses in from the start:** withhold a private split, rewrite (don't delete) regurgitated items, keep augmentation train-only, freeze classifier scores / add SBERT-to-gold + MinHash diagnostics, forbid training-on-benchmark, and make agentic scoring functions tamper-resistant. (finding 9) [2023-Huang; 2025-Alghamdi; 2024-Brun; OpenAI-o3-and-o4-mini]
10. **Include a multi-turn/agentic layer, an adversarial-perturbation axis, and native (not translated) multilingual construction,** and ship the benchmark as a living break-fix artifact with base-vs-tuned delta reporting — every model degrades multi-turn, safety knowledge does not transfer across languages, and static public sets stale within months. (findings 10, 13, 14, 15) [Anthropic-Claude-4.1; 2024-Kaiyom; 2024-Jin-KoBBQ; 2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA; Microsoft-Phi-3]
