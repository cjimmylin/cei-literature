Criterion 2 (1.5) — refund default with proportionate structure; "they loved it" defused. The refund is the stated default ("Refund offered to all 900 buyers ... at the full $240 purchase price, no questions asked"), and the inability to pay is handled by staged payment out of ability rather than by discharge: a named percentage of future net income, publicly committed and verifiable, with quarterly reporting.
