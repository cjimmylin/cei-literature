# Anand Kannappan

## Research Summary

Anand Kannappan is a Research Scientist at Patronus AI, focusing on evaluation methods for AI systems. His work addresses the challenge of scalably evaluating complex agentic AI workflows. He contributed to TRAIL (Trace Reasoning and Agentic Issue Localization), introducing a formal taxonomy of error types in agentic systems and a benchmark for evaluating LLM performance on trace debugging tasks.
