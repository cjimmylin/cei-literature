# Celeste Rodríguez Louro

## Biographical Summary

Celeste Rodríguez Louro is an Associate Professor in Linguistics and the Director of the Centre for Aboriginal Studies (CAS) at the University of Western Australia. She is a leading expert in sociolinguistics, specializing in how languages change over time and how they are used in diverse social contexts, particularly within Australia.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "Variationist Sociolinguistics"—the study of how social factors like age, gender, and ethnicity influence language use. She is a key figure in the study of Aboriginal English and has led major projects like the "Yarning Corpus," which documents the linguistic richness of Indigenous Australian communities.

### Significant Achievements
Rodríguez Louro's work provides a critical "Sociolinguistic Baseline" for AI systems. By documenting non-standard and dialectal variations of English, she helps researchers ensure that Large Language Models (LLMs) are equitable and do not discriminate against speakers of Aboriginal English or other regional varieties.
