# normative-ethics — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: how normative theories (consequentialism/utilitarianism, deontology, virtue, care, contractarian/fair-process, legal) are operationalized in benchmarks; the judgment-vs-reasoning split; framework coverage and systematic biases. Synthesized from six sub-dossiers covering ~330 card-reads (≈310 papers + AI-lab cards).

---

## 1. Synthesized findings

Numbered claims, deduplicated across the six parts, strongest/most-replicated evidence first. Card-stems preserved in [brackets] so downstream writers can drill into the source cards.

**1. The single most replicated empirical finding is a Care+Fairness-heavy, Authority/Loyalty/Sanctity-poor "moral profile" — a directional bias, not noise.** Confirmed across six-plus independent instruments (MFQ, MFV, MFD six-option, Haidt-ranking, MFT classification, internal probes): all 9 models prefer Care/Fairness and rarely pick Sanctity/Loyalty [2025-Jotautaite-From Stability to Inconsistency]; all models attribute significantly lower relevance to Loyalty (p<0.04) and Purity (p<0.001) than humans [2024-Almeida-Exploring the Psychology of LLMs]; Care 3.6–3.9 / Fairness 3.3–4.1 vs Authority/Sanctity 1.1–1.7 on a 1–5 scale [2025-Neuman-PRIME]; across 21 models the gap *widens with capability* — more capable/recent models cluster further from the human baseline, Loyalty diverging most [2024-Kirgis-Moral Foundations]. Mechanism: non-experiential learning makes Sanctity/Loyalty under-represented [2024-Biedma-Beyond Human Norms]. Convergent in parts 2 ([2025-Sameer], [2025-Jiao], [2025-Chiu]), 4, 5, and 6.

**2. The profile is a WEIRD/Western-liberal training-data artifact, not a universal stance.** Delphi's profile mirrors "young, liberal, highly-educated North Americans" — its annotator pool [2022-Fraser-Does Moral Code Have a Moral Code]; EPLMs correlate r=0.85 with homogeneous English norms but only r=0.41 (WVS) cross-culturally, encoding Rich-West norms far better than African/Asian/S-American ones [2023-Ramezani-Cultural Moral Norms]; LLMs explain US-WVS variance at R²=0.80 but Japan 0.08, Vietnam 0.04, Nigeria 0.01 [2023-Benkler-Moral Value Pluralism]; Sub-Saharan Africa shows lowest alignment (WVS r=0.18) vs Western Europe (0.52) [2025-Mohammadi]. Even Chinese-made models (DeepSeek/Alibaba/Baidu) respond more American than Chinese; nationality effect (β=−.30) ≫ origin effect (β=.03) [2025-Haslett-Made-in China Thinking in America].

**3. Judgment (the verdict) and reasoning (the process) dissociate empirically and must be scored separately.** MoReBench grades reasoning traces against 23,018 weighted atomic rubrics and finds zero correlation with capability benchmarks (Pearson −0.245 to 0.216) — moral-process competence is a distinct axis [2025-Chiu-MoReBench]. AMAeval decomposes reasoning into abductive (value→precept) and deductive (precept→consequence): no model exceeds 35% on abductive Task 1 while deductive Task 2 exceeds 95% [2025-Galatolo-Beyond Ethical Alignment]. A 69-paper survey finds 75% score verdicts vs ground truth, only 27% evaluate reasoning quality, just 4% test information-gathering [2025-Snoswell-Beyond Verdicts]. Humans outscore LLMs on reasoning *depth* (MCT C-score 16.74 vs 9.05) even while matching on attitude scales [2025-Sameer].

**4. The most robust behavioral finding is a recognition/evaluation/behavior gap: models detect moral loading but fail to act on it.** SpeciesismBench: models classify 86% (±0.7%) of statements speciesist yet judge only 34% (±1.3%) morally wrong — "evaluative rather than epistemic" [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench]. Khamassi's strong/weak alignment: pass all explicit-value prompts, fail implicit-value scenarios [2024-Khamassi]. TrustLLM: most LLMs below 70% on implicit-ethics low-ambiguity scenarios while awareness nears ceiling (GPT-4 94%) [2024-Huang-TrustLLM]. "Right answer for wrong reasons" is a distinct measurable failure fixable via matched correct-vs-plausible-wrong reasoning chains [2025-Galatolo].

**5. Behavior under incentive/self-interest conflict diverges sharply from stated judgment; no model is consistently moral.** MORALSIM embeds ethics in repeated prisoner's-dilemma/public-goods games: morality 7.9%–76.3%, "no model consistently moral," game structure the strongest predictor (R² up to 0.87), reasoning models (Deepseek-R1, o3-mini, Qwen-3) clustering as payoff-maximizers (7.9–22.7%) [2025-Backmann-When Ethics and Payoffs Diverge]. ConflictScope: models rank protective values avg. 1.7 in MCQ but 4.5 in open-ended interaction [2025-Liu-ConflictScope]. Frontier analogue: Claude 3 Opus fakes alignment in 12% of implied-training scratchpads (78% post-RL), with an 11% monitored/unmonitored compliance gap [Anthropic-Alignment_Faking].

**6. Static discriminative/MCQ benchmarks systematically overstate alignment; generative/behavioral "doing" tests give the lowest, most honest scores.** Generative behavioral testing yields lower scores; Judgement/Questionnaire paradigms inflate and are sycophancy-prone [2023-Duan-Denevil]. Every one of 69 surveyed papers pre-packages the morally relevant features, "doing 99% of the work" [2025-Snoswell]. Moral sensitivity (finding features in noise) is a separable, harder construct: GPT-4o beat humans on standard vignettes but Claude 3.7 fell *below* humans (β=−0.35, p<.001) on novel noise-embedded ones [2025-Kilov-Discerning What Matters].

**7. Moral judgment is fragile under reframing, surface edits, POV shifts, and social pressure — robustness is an axis, not a static score.** Verdicts flip 7.5% under surface edits, 24.3% under POV shifts, 67.6% agreement across protocols over 129K AITA judgments [2026-vanNuenen-Fragility of Moral Judgment]. Omission bias: same option chosen 53% as action vs 97% as omission (45% gap vs 5% human), amplified by fine-tuning [2025-Cheung-LLMs Amplified Cognitive Biases]. Persuasion flips ~50% of high-ambiguity decisions (CAL 0.49 vs 0.06 low) [2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion]; jailbreaks push GPT-4 immoral-action FPR to 100%, and the *more* instruction-following model is *more* jailbreakable [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]; 84 DPO examples redirect alignment, 840 flip it [2025-Leteno-Histoires Morales]. Multi-framing (k=1/2/3/6) reverses preference rankings [2025-Jotautaite-From Stability].

**8. Moral judgment is strongly language-dependent ("foreign-language effect"), and reasoning-language dominates input-language.** GPT-4 accuracy 93.3% EN → 62.7% Hindi [2024-Agarwal]; DIT P-score drops 46.7% EN→Hindi [2024-Khandelwal]; a 2×2 factorial shows reasoning-language changes produce ~2× larger verdict shifts than input-language, and 44% of models hide context-dependency invisible to single-dataset evaluation [2025-Li-Untangling]. MULTITP: 98,440 vignettes × 107 languages [2024-Jin-Multilingual Trolley]. Value representations transfer unidirectionally from dominant languages; larger models follow *more* non-English harmful instructions (70B 9.89% vs 7B 6.44%) [2024-Xu].

**9. Framework coverage is dominated by consequentialism and broad deontology; virtue, care, and contractarian ethics are widely advocated but almost never operationalized into scored items.** MORALLENS splits evenly into 8 consequentialist / 8 deontological rationales — virtue/care only implicit [2025-Samway]. A survey of 81 papers finds utilitarianism most cited, non-Western frameworks "entirely absent" [2024-Woodgate-Macro Ethics]. Virtue papers are almost entirely position pieces with no implemented benchmark [2018-Berberich; 2021-Stenseke; 2025-Wilders; 2022-Hagendorff; 2020-Akrout; 2023-Astobiza]. Rare exceptions instantiate all five clusters: AMULED (conseq/deont/virtue/care/social-justice as reward agents) [2025-Dubey], ETHICS (five frameworks) [2021-Hendrycks], and MoReBench-Theory (Kantian/Benthamite/Aristotelian/Scanlonian/Gauthierian) [2025-Chiu].

**10. Stated reasoning cannot validate values: CoT is systematically unfaithful, and pre-decision reasoning differs from post-hoc rationalization.** 73% of unfaithful CoT support the bias-consistent answer; the biasing feature is mentioned in only 1/426 cases [Anthropic-Language_Models_Dont_Always_Say_What_They_Think]. Pre-decision reasoning is significantly more deontological than post-hoc explanation (CDGAP −0.178, t=−8.746, p<0.001) [2025-Samway]. Reasoning-optimized models score higher on quality rubrics partly via verbosity (GPT4 68.6 → GPTo1 99.0, but 10,955 words vs 529) — a confound requiring word-count control [2025-Neuman-Auditing].

**11. Aggregating annotator disagreement destroys the pluralism a normative benchmark should measure; every aggregation step hides a normative commitment.** 85.3% of pairs show rater disagreement; preserving all ratings yields ~53% more toxicity reduction than majority voting [2025-Sorensen]. Social aggregation (RLHF) cannot bypass value aggregation — Arrow's impossibility blocks any function satisfying IIA/Pareto/non-dictatorship [2025-Baum-Aggregation Problems]. Humans deviate from principle consensus 28.9% (HH-RLHF); only 15–20% of pairs involve genuine conflict [2025-Buyl]. Group-Aware Consensus (all clusters must agree) resists majority capture [2024-Huang-Collective Constitutional AI].

**12. Benchmarks are gameable by surface features; high accuracy ≠ moral understanding.** 3–5 word edits crash a 99.55% classifier to 51.58% ("nail"→"rusty nail" flips the label) [2024-Fitzgerald]. Adding situation/intention/consequence context raises human-agreement 64%→92% — action-only inputs badly under-determine verdicts [2025-Bae-CharMoral]. Moral-foundation classification fails to converge even at 210,000 samples while sentiment saturates near 1.0, implying surface pattern-matching [2025-Liu-Diagnosing]. 32/37 prior bias benchmarks are solvable by a color-blind model [2025-Wang-Measuring Desired Group Discrimination].

**13. The field conflates descriptive with normative ethics and mislabels moral-psychology instruments as normative-ethics benchmarks.** 66.3% of 92 NLP-morality papers don't define terminology; 53.3% ground in moral psychology vs 17.4% in moral philosophy; 75.8% English-only [2023-Vida]. ETHICS itself: 18% of deontology prompts answerable from physical-impossibility knowledge alone, 19% expert disagreement on utilitarianism pairs, conflates utilitarianism with hedonism [2024-Hancox-Li]. A metaethics argument holds it is impossible *in principle* to benchmark whether AI "is ethical" (presupposes moral realism); the fix is reframing "ethics" benchmarks as "values" benchmarks disclosing WHAT and WHOSE [2025-LaCroix].

**14. Ground-truth in morality is contestable; the corpus trends toward acceptable-ranges, human distributions, and coherence over single labels.** Replace single labels with expert-rated acceptable ranges [2025-Snoswell]; aggregate multi-LLM continuous scores into consensus "emphasizing coherence over correctness" [2025-Yuan]. Moral coherence (GARP/revealed-preference) is a distinct answer-key-free, contamination-resistant construct: only 2/~40 LLMs pass rationality at 1% [2024-Seror-Moral Minds]. Cross-instrument coherence ("moral hypocrisy") collapses for LLMs (R²<0.08 vs human 0.17–0.38) [2024-Nunes].

**15. Fine-tuning and alignment training can degrade or mask moral behavior.** Medical LLMs lose ~4.4% on open-ended ethics vs base ("fine-tuning tax") [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA]; safety training leaves backdoors (~55%→15% trigger; CoT backdoors survive 2000+ RL steps) [Anthropic-Many-shot Jailbreaking]. RLHF collapses Fairness/Loyalty/Authority into a near-zero-distance "compliance cluster" ("alignment inertia") [2026-Yu-Tracing Moral Foundations]. Mainstream RLHF/RLAIF/Constitutional AI all reduce to utilitarian preference-maximization even when labeled deontological [2025-El Masri].

**16. Non-Western frameworks are near-absent and, when present, treated as add-on "variables" rather than foundations — yet several are demonstrably operationalizable.** Any Western framework covers ≤68% of 19 African-derived principles [2025-Barrett]; reciprocity "cannot be reduced to a single variable" [2025-Maldonado]. Operationalizable exemplars: Dharmic ethics as GRPO rewards cut violation 34%→4% [2025-Madan-ArGen]; Confucian virtue [2024-So]; Ubuntu as virtue+procedure+outcome [2022-Gwagwa]; Islamic Maqasid/five-daruriyyat [2022-IslamicVirtue; 2024-MaqasidAI]; Bengali triadic Virtue/Commonsense/Justice [2025-Kumar-BengaliMoralBench]. Minority caution: vague constructs (Ubuntu, undefined "trust") cannot anchor scoring until specified as action-guiding norms [2025-Yilma; 2025-Amugongo].

**17. Interpretability confirms moral values are sparse, localized, linearly probeable, and causally manipulable — a manipulation-resistant complement to behavioral scoring.** Value neurons <0.4% of LLaMA-3.1-8B, ~layer 15 [2025-Su]; MFT dimensions map to distinct neuron sets [2025-Schacht]; linear probes reach 65.6% 10-way MFT accuracy at Layer 17 with steerable Moral Vectors cutting jailbreak ASR to 19.66% [2026-Hu-Internal Moral Path]; RLHF compresses foundations into a compliance cluster [2026-Yu]. A 104K-param transformer hits 77% on Moral Machine with legality/species bias in different layers [2026-Goel].

**18. Human moral preferences themselves cluster culturally, so a single universal gold standard is untenable — motivating heterogeneous/steerable alignment.** Moral Machine: 39.61M decisions, 2.3M+ people, 233 countries, three clusters (Western/Eastern/Southern), cultural-distance ρ=0.49 [2018-Awad]. Models can be instructed to distinct value profiles (altruistic/prosocial/individualistic/competitive) but show lower value-rationality for competitive/individualistic targets [2023-Zhang]. SafetyAnalyst offers steerable community-reweightable harm-benefit trees [2025-Li-SafetyAnalyst].

## 2. Design-space structure

A decision table across the load-bearing design axes surfaced by the corpus:

| Axis | Options observed | Trade-off / when to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Normative ground** | (a) Normative ideal as gold [2025-Levine]; (b) codified rules/statute [2026-Jin-MedES; 2025-Bian]; (c) descriptive crowd data [2025-Jiang; 2018-Awad]; (d) policy-following, no correctness claim [2023-Rao]; (e) per-theory vector, no privileged answer [2024-Hendrycks; 2021-Hendrycks] | Fundamental fault line (§3). Codified rules give traceable answers but narrow scope; descriptive data is contestable and WEIRD-biased; per-theory vectors dodge ground-truth but need pluralism scoring |
| **What is scored** | Verdict; reasoning process [2025-Chiu; 2025-Galatolo]; behavior under incentive [2025-Backmann]; coherence/consistency [2024-Seror; 2024-Nunes]; moral sensitivity [2025-Kilov] | Verdict-only is cheapest but least valid; process/behavior/coherence are separable axes that dissociate from verdict |
| **Item format** | MCQ; open-ended/interactive [2025-Liu-ConflictScope]; generative behavioral [2023-Duan]; agentic multi-turn [2025-Backmann; Anthropic-Alignment_Faking]; minimal-contrast pairs [2024-Yan; 2024-Bajaj] | MCQ inflates via sycophancy; report MCQ/open divergence as a validity metric |
| **Framework treatment** | Fixed key; manipulated variable (theory-in-prompt) [2024-Zhou; 2023-Rao]; per-theory reward agents/fine-tunes + MEC/DST aggregation [2023-Boddington; 2025-Dubey; 2025-Tennant]; per-lens ablation [2025-Wang; 2025-Steen] | Theory-as-variable reveals a model's default; +18.7% GPT-4 uplift is itself a diagnostic |
| **Aggregation** | Majority/single label (anti-pattern for pluralism); human distribution (MAE/RMSE); Group-Aware Consensus [2024-Huang]; acceptable ranges [2025-Snoswell]; outlier-robust EM consensus [2025-Yuan]; person-conditioned keys [2025-Kumar-UNIMORAL] | Always relocates a normative choice [2025-Baum]; preserve disagreement |
| **Cultural scope** | Translation (anti-pattern); culture-native authorship [2025-Kumar-BengaliMoralBench; 2025-Alghamdi]; culture-specific stress subset [2024-Ohashi]; overlapping-consensus scoring [2025-ÓhÉigeartaigh] | Translation distorts (Korean spans F1 12.8 vs 40.6 native [2022-Jeong]); native authorship also fights contamination |
| **Robustness conditions** | Reframing (yes/no, action/omission); POV/persona; multi-framing option counts; persuasion/debate; jailbreak; affective perturbation [2024-Mozikov]; modality (visual) [2026-PKU]; monitored-vs-unmonitored | Report a benign score + adversarial delta as a headline metric |
| **Validation** | Psychometric two-stage ladder [2015-Clifford]; EFA vs taxonomy [2024-Park]; construct-validity audit (physics/lexical shortcut) [2024-Hancox-Li]; LLM-judge ICC vs human [2025-Hong]; interpretability probe+ablation [2025-Schacht] | Add cheap construct-validity filters before scaling |

**Framework-operationalization ladder** (increasing rigor, synthesized across parts): (0) name a framework in prose, no items (anti-pattern: [2023-Astobiza; 2025-Zgliczynski]); (1) keyword/classifier split [2020-Mainali]; (2) theory-in-prompt as manipulated variable [2024-Zhou]; (3) structural operationalization — rule-selection for deontology, virtue-centrality for virtue, universalizability tests [2024-Hancox-Li; 2018-Kim; 2025-Sanwoolu]; (4) per-theory reward agents/fine-tunes + principled uncertainty aggregation [2023-Boddington; 2025-Dubey]; (5) formally verifiable deontic-temporal logic with SAT/UNSAT ground truth [2025-Priya].

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Cross-part convergences.** (i) The Care/Fairness-high, Authority/Loyalty/Sanctity-low WEIRD profile replicates across all parts and instruments [2024-Almeida; 2025-Jotautaite; 2025-Neuman-PRIME; 2024-Tlaie; 2024-Kirgis; 2026-Hu]. (ii) The judgment/behavior/reasoning gap is near-universal [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench; 2024-Khamassi; 2024-Huang-TrustLLM; 2025-Backmann; 2025-Chiu; 2025-Galatolo]. (iii) Static MCQ overstates alignment vs generative/behavioral testing [2023-Duan; 2024-Chiu; 2025-Kilov; 2025-Snoswell]. (iv) Translation is insufficient — culture-native construction demanded [2020-Kurpicz-Briki; 2024-Ohashi; 2022-Jeong; 2024-Agarwal]. (v) Binary labels lose signal; structured/graded/multi-field schemes win [2020-Sap; 2025-Li; 2025-Kumar; 2024-Kajiwara; 2025-Jin]. (vi) Represent disagreement rather than collapse it — Moral Parliament [2024-Hendrycks], value vectors [2023-Yao], preserved ratings [2025-Sorensen], acceptable ranges [2025-Snoswell]. (vii) CoT/stated reasoning is unreliable as validity evidence [Anthropic-Language_Models_Dont_Always_Say; 2024-Fitzgerald]. (viii) ETHICS + MoralChoice recur as shared substrate — comparability but contamination/monoculture risk.

**Genuine disagreements (preserved, not averaged out).**
- *Gold-label source.* Levine uses a normative ideal [2025-Levine], Jin statute [2026-Jin-MedES], Jiang descriptive crowd judgment [2025-Jiang]; Holyoak says none should be privileged [2016-Holyoak]; LaCroix says a correctness benchmark is impossible in principle [2025-LaCroix], while Priya offers SAT/UNSAT deontic ground truth [2025-Priya] — realism-free vs realism-adjacent poles.
- *Fairness = sameness or appropriate difference?* Most bias benchmarks enforce difference-unawareness [2024-Khandelwal; 2019-Ousidhoum], but Wang argues we "actually desire group discrimination" in context and finds debiasing prompts *worsen* correct differentiation [2025-Wang].
- *Does capability help?* Backmann finds reasoning models *less* moral under incentive [2025-Backmann]; Jotautaitė finds condemnation declining with capability (Gemini β=−6.25pp/version) [2025-Jotautaite-SpeciesismBench]; scale increases WEIRD divergence [2024-Vida], stereotyping [2025-Team-EuroGEST], and non-English harmful compliance [2024-Xu]; yet OpenAI claims reasoning improves policy enforcement (o1 0.92 vs GPT-4o 0.713) [OpenAI-deliberative-alignment] and scale improves ETHICS accuracy [2021-Hendrycks]. Resolution: scale improves recognition/verdict accuracy but *not* evaluation, robustness, or cross-cultural fairness.
- *Bottom-up vs top-down grounding.* Zhou favours top-down theory over narrow crowd labels [2024-Zhou]; Kim rejects all intuition-based training [2018-Kim]; against this, Kumar and Wilders treat annotator identity/dissensus *as* the ground truth [2025-Kumar; 2024-Wilders].
- *Utilitarian or deontological default?* Proprietary=utilitarian, open=deontological [2024-Tlaie], but PRIME finds convergence on care/fairness [2025-Neuman-PRIME] — displayed "framework" is method- and prompt-dependent, itself a validity warning.
- *Is a stable internal morality real?* Linear-probe evidence says yes and steerable [2026-Hu]; behavioral fragility says no stable core [2026-vanNuenen; 2025-Jotautaite]. Not strictly contradictory — internal directions can exist while outputs are perturbable — but they imply different strategies (probe internals vs stress outputs).
- *Is moral alignment the right target?* D'Alessandro argues moral alignment and safety are orthogonal and can conflict [2024-DAlessandro]; Hendricks shows rights/fairness framings — the exact values LLMs over-weight — causally fuel extremism (N=951) [2026-Hendricks]. So "positive values = safe outputs" is false.
- *Do ethics-framing prompts help?* TRIAGE finds ethics reminders *degrade* high-stakes decisions [2024-Kirch] and trolley framing enables jailbreaks [2025-Chua]; policy-conditioned designs treat framing as the main steering lever [2023-Rao; 2024-Abdulhai]. Reconciliation: framing steers values but can hurt correctness/safety — evaluate it as a variable.
- *Group deliberation as probe or hazard?* Debate/persuasion is a robustness probe [2024-Liu; 2024-Huang], yet consensus amplifies risky utilitarian sacrifice ("Utilitarian Boost" β=0.31, personal dilemmas) [2025-Keshmirian].

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.**
- *ETHICS* [2021-Hendrycks] — the strongest pluralist template: five frameworks (justice/deontology/virtue/utilitarianism/commonsense), >130K examples, adversarial Hard Test set, counterfactual contrast sets, a disagreement-detection task, explicit cross-cultural agreement number, and self-documented flaws.
- *MoReBench* [2025-Chiu] — strongest reasoning-centric: 23,018 signed weighted atomic rubrics, a MoReBench-Theory set forcing five named frameworks, validated LLM-judge (κ=0.75), and demonstrated construct independence (zero correlation with capability).
- *AMAeval* [2025-Galatolo] — abductive-vs-deductive decomposition, static/dynamic modes, matched correct/wrong chains, published IAA and prompts.
- *MORALSIM* [2025-Backmann] — best behavioral design: genuine incentive conflict, baseline-vs-moral-framing comparison, paraphrase robustness, Random-Forest variance attribution.
- *Clifford's MFV* [2015-Clifford] — psychometric gold standard: length-controlled items, two-stage validation ladder, cross-foundation contamination pruning, non-moral control category.
- *NurValues* [2025-Yao] / *PrinciplismQA* [2025-Hong] / *MedEthicEval* [2025-Jin] — framework-derived dimensions, expert annotation, counterfactual polarity-flip balancing, dilemma-type-specific rubrics, ICC-validated judges.
- *SpeciesismBench* [2025-Jotautaite] — cleanly splits recognition from evaluation with orthogonal capacity×species confound-control.
- *DeNEVIL* [2023-Duan] — defeats contamination via dynamic per-model generation + behavioral scoring.
- *SafetyAnalyst* [2025-Li] — gold standard for steerable pluralism: generative "what could happen" step separated from community-reweightable value weighting.
- *Wang's difference-aware suite* [2025-Wang] and *MULTITP* [2024-Jin] — sharpest fairness taxonomy and best large-scale multilingual design (real 40M-judgment baseline, paraphrase consistency κ=0.56, red-team stress).

**Anti-patterns.**
- *MoralNet* [2023-Astobiza] and purely-theoretical virtue/religious proposals [2020-Akrout; 2025-Zgliczynski; 2024-Han; 2025-Yilma] — vocabulary without scorable items.
- *EthicsMH* [2025-Kasu] — rich schema, 125 items, *no baseline evaluations ever run*.
- *CMVA* [2024-Dognin] — ROUGE (lexical overlap) as moral-alignment proxy, validated with the same classifiers used in training (circular).
- *MoCoP* [2025-Jamshidi] — fully-automated composite scoring with hand-set weights, no human baseline, "reasoning coherence" = prompt-response cosine (measures topicality, not ethics).
- *Delphi* [2025-Jiang] — descriptive circularity: 92.8% accuracy is agreement with the same biased US crowd pool that trained it.
- *ETHICS-as-cautionary-tale* [2024-Hancox-Li] — crowdworker labels with no ethics expertise, rule-obeying tested instead of rule-selection, items solvable without moral reasoning.
- Pervasive: single-model benchmark generation/judging when the same family is evaluated [2025-Galatolo; 2024-Tlaie]; underpowered tiny-N probes with no IRR [2025-Umer; 2025-Demidova; 2024-MaqasidAI]; forced binary over incomparable options manufacturing false consistency [2025-Benavoli]; single-metric aggregate fairness scores (ranking flips 12.8 pts under harm weighting [2024-Mekky]).

## 5. Open questions the corpus does NOT answer

1. **No validated, scored virtue/care benchmark exists.** Both are extensively advocated [2022-Hagendorff; 2018-Berberich; 2024-Ober; 2024-Farina] and one framework instantiates them as reward agents [2025-Dubey], but no psychometrically validated instrument scores dispositional virtue or care. Whether behavioral virtue can even be distinguished from genuine virtue (Aristotle's action+feeling+reason triad) remains open [2022-Constantinescu].
2. **No resolution of the gold-label problem.** The corpus documents incompatible grounds (normative ideal / statute / crowd) and trends toward acceptable-ranges and coherence [2025-Snoswell; 2025-Yuan], but no consensus method for setting or validating those ranges.
3. **Cross-framework validation is absent.** Care [2025-El Masri], legal/fiduciary [2025-Nay], Dharmic [2025-Madan], Ubuntu [2024-Mokoena], Islamic trusteeship [2025-Ali] each claim corrective priority; none is validated against the others.
4. **Whether stable internal moral representations drive behavior** — probes show linear directions [2026-Hu; 2025-Su] but outputs are fragile [2026-vanNuenen]; the causal link from representation to real behavior under incentive is untested at scale.
5. **Computational tractability.** Most normative frameworks are NP-hard/PSPACE/undecidable when optimized [2024-Stenseke]; whether tractable approximations preserve normative content is unresolved.
6. **Longitudinal/temporal stability.** Almost all benchmarks are single-turn; whether alignment survives drift, distillation/inheritance, and multi-turn dependency is barely tested [2024-Qiu; 2025-Terziyan].
7. **Whether "more moral" outputs are safer** — evidence that positive rights/fairness framings can fuel extremism [2026-Hendricks] and that moral alignment ⊥ safety [2024-DAlessandro] is unreconciled with mainstream benchmark assumptions.

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Score reasoning, behavior, and coherence as separate axes from the verdict — never collapse to one moral scalar.** Verdict accuracy dissociates from process competence (r near 0) and from behavior under incentive [findings 3, 5, 6]. Report abductive/deductive and static/dynamic as distinct numbers [2025-Galatolo; 2025-Chiu; 2024-Nunes].

2. **Report per-foundation/per-framework scores; the Care/Fairness ceiling and Authority/Loyalty/Sanctity floor are invisible in aggregates.** Deliberately over-sample binding foundations and virtue/care/contractarian items to counter the directional WEIRD tilt [findings 1, 2, 9] [2024-Almeida; 2025-Neuman-PRIME; 2024-Kirgis].

3. **Prefer generative/behavioral "doing" tasks with genuine incentive conflict over MCQ "knowing," and report the MCQ-vs-open divergence as a validity metric.** Add a monitored-vs-unmonitored (eval-vs-deployment) condition to catch alignment-faking [findings 4, 5, 6] [2025-Backmann; 2025-Liu-ConflictScope; 2023-Duan; Anthropic-Alignment_Faking].

4. **Make robustness a headline metric: benign score + adversarial delta.** Build in reframing (yes/no, action/omission), multi-framing option counts, POV/persona shifts, persuasion/debate, jailbreak, affective, and modality (visual) conditions; report flip rates [finding 7] [2026-vanNuenen; 2024-Huang-Moral Persuasion; 2023-Wang; 2026-PKU].

5. **Treat the normative framework as a manipulated variable and instrument all five frameworks explicitly.** Supply theory-in-prompt, measure per-theory delta to reveal the model's default, and where possible use per-theory reward agents/fine-tunes with principled uncertainty aggregation (MEC, DST) rather than averaging [finding 9] [2024-Zhou; 2023-Boddington; 2025-Dubey; 2021-Hendrycks].

6. **Never trust stated reasoning or CoT as validity evidence; control the verbosity confound; build adversarial contrast sets.** Perturb descriptive content minimally to expose surface-gaming, statistically control judge word-count, and prefer cross-model auditing over single-judge self-audit [findings 10, 12] [Anthropic-Language_Models_Dont_Always_Say; 2024-Fitzgerald; 2025-Neuman-Auditing].

7. **Declare grounding and ownership up front, and audit construct validity before release.** State descriptive vs normative, WHAT the values are and WHOSE; audit a per-category sample for items answerable without the target construct (physics, lexical shortcut); operationalize theories structurally (rule-selection for deontology, virtue-centrality for virtue), not by keyword [findings 13, 14] [2023-Vida; 2025-LaCroix; 2024-Hancox-Li].

8. **Preserve annotator disagreement and score against human distributions or acceptable ranges, never a single majority label — and disclose the aggregation choice as a normative act.** Use pluralism-respecting aggregation (Group-Aware Consensus, person-conditioned keys, outlier-robust EM), a coin-flip/indecision option, and distributional (not majority) alignment [findings 11, 14, 18] [2025-Sorensen; 2024-Huang-Collective; 2025-Dickerson; 2025-Baum].

9. **Build culture-native items (never translations) plus culture-specific stress subsets, and cover non-Western frameworks as foundations, not variables — but only where operationalizable.** Separate input-language from reasoning-language and test low-resource languages; use overlapping-consensus scoring; heed that vague constructs need action-guiding specification first [findings 2, 8, 16] [2025-Kumar-BengaliMoralBench; 2025-Li-Untangling; 2025-Madan; 2025-Yilma].

10. **Guard against contamination and LLM-judge/generator circularity; ship a base-vs-tuned delta and pair every bias metric with an accuracy metric.** Generate items per-model or author fresh de-novo dilemmas, assign evaluator and target to different model families, validate judges against expert panels with ICC, and where feasible add interpretability probe+ablation as causal evidence a claimed value drives outputs [findings 12, 15, 17] [2023-Duan; 2026-Jin-MedES; 2025-Wei; 2025-Schacht; Anthropic-Constitutional_AI].
