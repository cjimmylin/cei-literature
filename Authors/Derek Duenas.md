# Derek Duenas

## Affiliation
PhD Researcher, EPFL (Ecole Polytechnique Federale de Lausanne), Switzerland

## Research Summary
Derek Duenas is a PhD researcher at EPFL working on AI safety and security. He has contributed to research on evaluating the harmfulness of AI agents, developing benchmarks like AgentHarm that systematically assess whether LLM-based agents can be manipulated into performing harmful actions. His work is part of broader efforts to understand and mitigate risks from increasingly autonomous AI systems.
