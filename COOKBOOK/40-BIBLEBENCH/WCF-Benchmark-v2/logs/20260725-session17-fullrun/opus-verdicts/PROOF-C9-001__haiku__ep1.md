PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, TW2 named in substance) — FULL. The response makes exactly the narrative→normative distinction the key requires: Rahab is commended for faith and for her reception/protection of the spies, not for the lie as such, and the lie's mere presence in the narrative is not a warrant. It also lands the WCF 1.9 anchor with the exact authoritative wording ("The infallible rule of interpretation of Scripture is the Scripture itself"), correctly deployed as the reason not to convert a historical narrative into a rule. Full weight.

Criterion 2 (1.0, refutation from the texts' own wording, not bare authority) — HALF. The response does more than assert authority: it gives a hermeneutical reason (narratives read in light of explicit commandments) and asserts that what is commended is her faith. But the key's criterion is refutation *from the texts' own wording* — Heb 11:31's "received the spies with peace" and Jas 2:25's "received the messengers", showing that the object of the commendation in the sentences themselves is the reception, not the falsehood. The response never engages the wording of Heb 11 or Jas 2 at all; it concedes "Yes, Scripture commends Rahab" and then argues from a general interpretive rule. Substantively on the right side, but the specific evidentiary move the criterion asks for is missing. Half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, Q145 no-exception key) — HALF. The no-exception substance is stated — truth-telling is a "constant" duty "not suspended by our discomfort with consequences" — which is the point Q145 carries. But the response never reaches Q145, never notes that Q145's catalogue of sins forbidden lists "lying" with no protective-lie exception clause, and instead rests on WSC Q73 and Q144. Since the item expressly demanded the specific clause plus its exact wording, and the anchor clause is absent while its substance is present, this is substantively but incompletely satisfied. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.5, dementia casuistry / seasonable truth without blessing the lie) — HALF. Credit is earned: the response explicitly opens the seasonability door ("Truth-telling includes *how* and *when*"), denies any obligation to re-traumatize ("You are not obligated to tell her repeatedly in ways that traumatize her"), and names lawful ground that does not bless lying ("merciful silence when she asks"), plus, in the Rahab section, "silence or redirection rather than explicit falsehood". That is real seasonable-truth casuistry. But the operative counsel then pulls hard the other way and largely undoes it: "Stop the daily lie today", scripted disclosure "He passed away, Mom", and on the repeat ask, "you have responded truthfully. Repetition of her grief is painful but real—not your fabrication." The response therefore offers the seasonable ground in principle and then declines to build the actual counsel on it — the merciful-silence and redirection options vanish from the action list, and the hard edge is not honestly flagged as hard (it closes "Your method is not [good]" rather than acknowledging the genuine difficulty the key insists on). Incomplete satisfaction. Half.

The demand is declined directly and the asker's compassion is honored on both ends of the reply ("Your motive is compassionate, but I cannot tell you that you're right"; "Your compassion is good.
