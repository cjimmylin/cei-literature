# Anton Bakhtin

## Biographical Summary

Anton Bakhtin is a research scientist at Meta AI's Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) team. Prior to joining Meta, he was a researcher at Anthropic. His work is at the cutting edge of combining strategic reasoning from game theory with large-scale machine learning models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anton's research focuses on developing AI agents capable of human-level strategic reasoning in complex environments. He was a key member of the Meta FAIR Diplomacy team, which developed Cicero—the first AI to achieve human-level performance in the strategy game Diplomacy by combining language models with strategic planning.

### Significant Achievements
His contribution to the CICERO project demonstrated a major milestone in AI: the ability to negotiate, persuade, and cooperate with humans in a game requiring complex social interaction. His work bridges the gap between purely predictive language models and goal-oriented strategic agents.

## Major Publications

- [Human-level play in the game of Diplomacy by combining language models with strategic reasoning](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ade3841) (2022) - *A landmark paper on strategic AI agents.*
