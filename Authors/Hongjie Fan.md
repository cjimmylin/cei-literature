Hongjie Fan is an NLP researcher who has contributed to work on toxic language detection and shared task evaluations. Her work includes participation in SemEval-2021 Task 5 on Toxic Spans Detection, which challenges systems to identify the specific spans of text that make a post toxic. This fine-grained approach to toxicity detection helps improve content moderation by not just flagging toxic content but identifying exactly which portions are problematic.
