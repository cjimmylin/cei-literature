# Halil Emre Kocalar

## Research Summary

Halil Emre Kocalar is a research assistant at Mugla Sitki Kocman University, Turkey. His research interests include counseling and educational psychology, cognitive-behavioral therapy, and open science practices.

His research includes investigation of psychometric properties of the Self-Critical Rumination Scale and the mediating role of self-critical rumination in perfectionism and academic procrastination among university students. He has contributed to cross-cultural research examining whether people in different societies experience morality differently in everyday life, using experience sampling methods to investigate everyday moral experiences across 20 countries on 6 continents.

He has also contributed to open science by translating materials on Open Science Initiatives into Turkish.
