## Abstract

Examines inconsistencies in hate speech detection across LLM-generated datasets, with implications for model reliability in low-resource language contexts including Dravidian languages. Addresses reliability concerns in LLM-based hate speech detection relevant to Dravidian language evaluation.

## Data Description

A synthetic hate speech dataset of 1.3+ million sentences generated via a factorial design combining 2 quantifiers, 125 demographic groups, 55 hate speech phrases, and 70 incitement options, used to systematically compare content moderation systems across identical content.
