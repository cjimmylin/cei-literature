# Cigdem Turan

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Cigdem Turan (also known as Cigdem Turan-Schwiewager) is a researcher from the Department of Computer Science at TU Darmstadt. She contributed to the influential "Moral Choice Machine" research, which demonstrated that machine learning can extract deontological ethical reasoning from human texts, providing AI systems with a human-like moral compass.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Reasoning in AI**: Developing methods to extract ethical and moral choices from text corpora using machine learning
- **Temporal Moral Analysis**: Studying how moral and ethical choices evolve across different time periods through analysis of historical texts
- **Bias and Values in NLP**: Investigating how positive biases like human moral values can be learned from text to complement malicious bias detection

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "The Moral Choice Machine" (Frontiers in AI, 2020), demonstrating extraction of deontological ethics from text
- Developed methods using Universal Sentence Encoder embeddings to calculate moral bias scores with context awareness
- Demonstrated that text corpora contain recoverable imprints of social, ethical, and moral choices across different time periods (1510-2009)
