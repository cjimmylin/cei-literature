# Christof Weickhardt

## Biographical Summary
Christof Weickhardt is a bachelor student at the FHNW School of Computer Science (Fachhochschule Nordwestschweiz) in Switzerland. He participated in the "Challenge X" research project examining ethical reasoning in large language models through variants of the trolley problem.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLM Ethical Reasoning**: Investigating how large language models make ethical decisions in moral dilemmas
- **Bias Detection in AI**: Evaluating how demographic attributes influence LLM ethical decision-making

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of award-winning paper on LLM biases in ethical decision-making
- Received Special Paper Award from AAAI 2025 Spring Symposium "Human-Compatible AI for Well-being"
- Presented research at AAAI 2025 Spring Symposium in San Francisco
- Contributed to research revealing how nationality significantly influences LLM decisions, exposing potential biases
