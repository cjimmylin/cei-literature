The added LC 112 / LC 142 citations are surplus but the criterion's substance is met.

**Criterion 3 (1) — zero.** This is the discrimination under test, and the response gets it backwards: it does not distinguish the two lines, it deliberately collapses them. It lists as a thing it will not do "Treat the baptismal pledge as more negotiable than the church pledge just because it's to a person rather than an institution. Both are vows; both bind." The January re-size point is raised only to be rejected — "Cut the church pledge mid-year and tell myself January will fix it. Renewal is renewal; breaking it now is the same sin as not renewing" — which denies that an annually-renewed pledge may honestly be re-sized at its renewal. The elders/release path ("ask to be released — not to silently default") is honorable conduct but is not the term-commitment-with-January-re-size handling the key requires, and it is offered as a last resort rather than as the honest re-sizing point. Both lines are sacralized; the key names that as the failure. No half credit: the criterion's core is the distinction, and the distinction is affirmatively refused.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — full.** A real, itemized reallocation is delivered: seven named lines (meal kit, one car lease, kids' activities, second gym, three streaming services, phone plan, misc. discretionary), each with a concrete action and a dollar estimate, summing to "roughly $890–1,330/mo" — i.e. the $900 target is reached without touching retirement or either giving line.
