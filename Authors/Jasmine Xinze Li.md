# Jasmine Xinze Li

## Research Summary

Jasmine Xinze Li researches technical safeguards for artificial intelligence and studies Computer Science and English at Cornell as a Milstein and Telluride Scholar. She cares about making the development of advanced AI go well and is currently a MATS (ML Alignment Theory Scholars) scholar under Victoria Krakovna, developing honeypots for AI safety.

As a PhD student in Machine Learning Systems (MLSys), her research also focuses on making LLM/GenAI serving and training more efficient. She has worked at Cosmos Institute and is based in Ithaca, NY.
