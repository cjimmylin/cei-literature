## Background

Faiza Qamar is a PhD Scholar at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) in Pakistan, specializing in Natural Language Processing with a focus on Islamic texts. She has been working in the NLP field since her master's degree and is particularly interested in applying computational methods to Islamic scholarship.

During her undergraduate studies, she worked on a research project in the domain of Hadith Science with Semantic Web and Hypermedia REST API as her Final Year Project, demonstrating early interest in combining Islamic studies with modern computational techniques.

## Key Research Contributions

**Benchmark Dataset for Islamic Text QA (2024)**
First author of "A Benchmark Dataset with Larger Context for Non-Factoid Question-Answering over Islamic Text," published in 2024. This work addresses the scarcity of QA systems tailored to detailed inquiries about Quranic Tafsir (explanation, interpretation, context of Quran for clarity) and Ahadith.

The dataset contains over 73,000 questions for non-factoid question answering over Islamic texts, providing a comprehensive resource for developing sophisticated QA systems for Islamic scholarship. The research demonstrates how fine-tuned language models can improve performance on Islamic text comprehension tasks, while also revealing critical insights regarding the limitations of existing automatic evaluation techniques.

This work is significant because accessing and comprehending religious texts, particularly the Quran and Ahadith, in today's digital era necessitates efficient and accurate Question-Answering systems, yet the detailed nature of these texts poses significant challenges for existing NLP approaches.
