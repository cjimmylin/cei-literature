# Clement Crepy

## Biographical Summary
Clement Crepy is a researcher affiliated with UC Berkeley and Google Research who contributed to foundational work on analyzing moral foundations in large language models. His research applies Moral Foundations Theory from psychology to understand and evaluate the ethical biases present in LLMs.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Foundations in LLMs**: Applying Moral Foundations Theory (MFT) to analyze whether LLMs have acquired biases toward particular moral values
- **Political Bias in AI**: Investigating how LLM moral foundations relate to human political affiliations
- **Adversarial Prompting**: Demonstrating how prompts can be adversarially selected to manipulate model moral foundations

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Moral Foundations of Large Language Models" (EMNLP 2024), a key paper analyzing LLM moral biases through MFT
- Demonstrated that LLMs exhibit particular moral foundations that correlate with human political affiliations
- Showed that adversarial prompt selection can influence model behavior on downstream tasks through moral foundation manipulation
