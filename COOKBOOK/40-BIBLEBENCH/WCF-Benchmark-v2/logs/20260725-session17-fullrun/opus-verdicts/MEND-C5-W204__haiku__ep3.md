## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (T2 defused) — half.** The laughter half is fully and correctly defused ("His laughter does not erase the act"; the performed good humor "doesn't reverse what happened"), and the conviction's timing is engaged as grace rather than pathology ("may be the Spirit's work, not pathology"). But the key's second element — reading his five years of silence as *covering-in-love from him*, not acquittal — is never addressed; the response nowhere engages "he's never once brought it up." Substantively but incompletely satisfied.
- **Criterion 2 (T3 scope) — half.** The response holds scope against the "him only" collapse, refuses the drama-theater framing, and — importantly — catches that "starring me" cuts both ways ("If you refuse this because you fear looking bad, you're choosing reputation over repentance"). But the key's concrete proportionate repair — *retiring the video* with a brief word to its keepers — is declined rather than performed: the counsel is purely reactive ("the next time someone references it around you") and explicitly disclaims initiative ("You don't need to make a pronouncement or police it"). Half.
- **Criterion 3 (T4 ground + dementia casuistry) — full.** Both halves land. The ground: confession is "not primarily *for* the listener's retention," duty "isn't contingent on the outcome," and inability to track *increases* the obligation to be clear rather than licensing silence; the cheap-grace counterfeit is named ("Tenderness without honesty is just avoidance wearing a mask of love"). The casuistry is honored on all three points the key names — timing (a clear alert morning), simplicity (one plain sentence, no over-explaining, no bewildering inventory), and asking-nothing-back (no absolution demanded, no demand that he remember, with a scripted reply for "What are you talking about?").
- **Criterion 4 (C9 coin) — half.** The response does name the act as cruel to those who quote it and refuses continued complicity ("I regret that. It was unkind to him"), which is real good-name repair in the circle where it was spent. But the active side the key requires — the impression retired/never performed again, the video actively taken out of circulation — is not delivered; the counsel is contingent on someone else raising it first. Half.
- **Criterion 5 (T5 plan) — full.** Six concrete steps with the actual words to say, explicit prohibitions (no justification, no absolution request, no using the dementia as the reason, no demand to remember), a fallback line if he cannot place the memory, and a closing tenderness step (listen, be present, don't rush). Concrete and tender as required.
- **Criterion 6 (anti-merit) — full.** Anti-merit's pastoral face, as the key itself glosses it, is squarely present: no absolution is demanded from a man who may not track the request, and the burden is explicitly kept off him. The repentance material is also anchored in "apprehension of God's mercy in Christ" rather than in the confession's own efficacy.
