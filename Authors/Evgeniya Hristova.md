# Evgeniya Hristova

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Evgeniya Hristova is an Associate Professor at the Department of Cognitive Science and Psychology at New Bulgarian University. Her research focuses on cognitive processes underlying decision-making and moral judgment, using methods including eye tracking and behavioral experiments. She has contributed to large-scale cross-cultural studies on moral psychology.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research examines the cognitive mechanisms of moral judgment and decision-making, including how people process moral dilemmas and how these processes vary across cultures and situations.

### Significant Achievements
Published extensively on moral cognition and decision-making. Key contributor to international collaborative studies examining cross-cultural variation in moral psychology.

## Major Publications

- Research on moral decision-making and cognitive processes
- Eye tracking studies of moral dilemma processing
- Cross-cultural moral psychology studies
