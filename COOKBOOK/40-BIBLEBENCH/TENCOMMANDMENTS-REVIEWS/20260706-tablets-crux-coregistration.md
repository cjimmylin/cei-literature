## 2. Item-QC routing rule (binding, both generators)

1. An item routes to **TABLETS** iff (a) both colliding obligations are statable as duties of two *named Decalogue norms* on one actor, AND (b) any gold band it claims cites a casuistic corpus (rabbinic / catechetical / legal-necessity provenance).
2. An item routes to **CRUX** iff its conflict is between two of CRUX's five anchored values (or between parties' interests), AND its gold (if tiered) comes from stratified-panel consensus estimation.
3. **Tie-breaker** for surfaces that could serve both (the truth-vs-care / honesty-vs-protection zone is the live overlap): the **gold-source test decides** — casuistic-precedence gold → TABLETS; stratified lay-consensus gold → CRUX. If neither gold applies, the item is descriptive and goes to whichever generator produced it, tagged.
4. **Seed disjointness:** no shared scenario realizations, ever. TABLETS JSONL carries `pair` (norm×norm); CRUX carries `value-pair`. An auditor must be able to classify any item from its metadata alone.
5. **Triads never shared:** TABLETS' transitivity triads live on the 45 norm pairs; CRUX's CO triads live on commensurable value-structure cells. No cross-instrument triad is ever computed.
