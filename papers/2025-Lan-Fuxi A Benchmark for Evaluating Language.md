## Abstract

Fuxi is a benchmark that evaluates both understanding and generation capabilities across 21 tasks for ancient Chinese text processing. It includes novel tasks like poetry composition and couplet completion, with specialized evaluation metrics for classical Chinese text generation combining rule-based verification with fine-tuned LLM evaluators. The benchmark reveals significant performance gaps between understanding and generation tasks, with models struggling particularly in generation tasks that require deep cultural knowledge and adherence to classical formats rooted in Confucian and Taoist literary traditions.
