## Abstract

First large-scale human-annotated Twitter sentiment dataset for four Nigerian languages with ~30,000 annotated tweets per language including code-mixed tweets. Includes sentiment lexicons for Hausa, Igbo, and Yoruba. Covers Hausa, Igbo, and Yoruba. Sentiment analysis directly relevant to cultural values expression. Includes native-speaker annotated lexicons capturing culturally-specific sentiment.
