# Jie Zhou

## Research Summary

Jie Zhou is a researcher affiliated with Tsinghua University who works on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Graph Neural Networks. He is a co-author of the highly influential paper "Graph Neural Networks: A Review of Methods and Applications" (Arxiv 2018), along with Ganqu Cui, Shengding Hu, Zhengyan Zhang, Cheng Yang, and Zhiyuan Liu. His research interests include graph neural networks and their applications on text and knowledge graphs.

Zhou was a Master student at the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University (2018-2021), supervised by Prof. Zhiyuan Liu at THUNLP (Tsinghua NLP Lab). He has collaborated extensively with other researchers at THUNLP on topics related to knowledge graphs, relation extraction, and pre-trained language models.
