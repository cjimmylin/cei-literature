## Abstract

First systematic study of school-specific Islamic legal reasoning focusing on Maliki Fiqh. Tests GPT-4o and ALLaM on 550 curated questions across purification (Tahara), marital jurisprudence (Fiqh Al-Nikah), and financial transactions (Buyu). Highlights gaps in adherence to established Fiqhi rules.

## Dataset Information

550 questions across 3 Fiqh domains (Tahara, Fiqh Al-Nikah, Buyu)


## Data Description

FiqhAlign-Maliki: a specialized dataset of 550 questions (450 True/False, 100 MCQ) across three Maliki Fiqh domains (Taharah/purification, Fiqh Al-Nikah/marital jurisprudence, Buyu/financial transactions) for evaluating LLMs on Islamic jurisprudence.
