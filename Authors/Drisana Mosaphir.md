# Drisana Mosaphir

## Biographical Summary

Drisana Mosaphir is a researcher affiliated with the Berkman Klein Center at Harvard University, working on AI ethics and value pluralism. Her research examines how AI systems, particularly large language models, handle diverse moral values and perspectives across different cultural and philosophical traditions.

## Research Focus

Mosaphir's work focuses on understanding and evaluating how AI systems represent and navigate moral value pluralism. She has contributed to research assessing whether LLMs can appropriately handle the diversity of moral viewpoints present in human societies.
