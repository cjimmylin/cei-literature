# Jesus Rios

## Research Summary

Jesus Maria Rios Aliaga is a Research Staff Member in Cognitive Computing at IBM T.J. Watson Research Center in Yorktown Heights, NY. He joined IBM Research in 2010 and focuses on applying risk and decision analysis to solve complex business problems. He defended his PhD thesis "Learning From Event Sequences" at Universidad Rey Juan Carlos in 2023.

## Key Contributions

- Developed causality-based Root Cause Identification (RCI) algorithm integrated into IBM Instana
- Co-authored "Adversarial Risk Analysis" which won the 2017 De Groot Prize from ISBA
- Research on causal AI for application performance management
