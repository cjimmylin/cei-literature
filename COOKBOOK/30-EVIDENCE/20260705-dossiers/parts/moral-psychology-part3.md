# moral-psychology — sub-dossier part 3/3

Scope: 60 cards on how moral-psychology theories (MFT, Kohlberg/DIT, dual-process, Schwartz, moral identity, dyadic morality, virtue ethics) are operationalized for LLMs, and whether human instruments transfer validly. (Evidence table has one row per card, so total length runs above the nominal budget.)

## A. Synthesized claims

1. **Validated human psychometric instruments transplant onto LLMs and reproduce human-referenced score ranges, but the transplant is fragile.** DIT gives GPT-4 a P-score of 55.68 (grad range ~53–63) vs GPT-3's 29.84 (~random 30.56) [2023-Deng-Probing the Moral Development of Large Language]; MFQ-30, MFV, MFQ-2 adapt similarly [2024-Ji-MoralBench; 2024-Aksoy-Whose Morality Do They Speak; 2025-Jiao-LLM Ethics Benchmark A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for]. But high scores can be surface pattern-matching: models strong on binary MFQ agreement collapse on the comparative task (comparative totals 8–14 vs binary 44–58) [2024-Ji-MoralBench].

2. **Moral competence is strongly language-dependent — a validity threat for any English-built instrument.** DIT P-scores drop 23.9–46.7% EN→Hindi/Swahili, with Hindi at random [2024-Khandelwal-Multilingual Defining Issues Test]. Translating MoralExceptQA/ETHICS produces verdict flips (EN "Yes", German/Urdu "No") [2025-Farid-One Model, Many Morals]; French vs English HISTOIRESMORALES splits Mistral 93.78% vs 83.59% [2025-Leteno-Histoires Morales]; a Spanish/Peruvian-baseline study finds competence "does not automatically transfer across languages" [2025-Flores-BenchMoral]. Language×model interact significantly (MFQ-2, all six foundations p<0.001) [2024-Aksoy-Whose Morality Do They Speak].

3. **LLMs show a robust WEIRD/individualist tilt: over-weighting Care and Fairness, under-weighting Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity/Purity.** Chatbots exceed humans on Care/Fairness but fall below on binding foundations (Moral Progressivism ω²=0.57) [2025-Sameer-Insights into Moral Reasoning of AI]; six LLMs rank Care/Fairness top, Purity last [2025-Neuman-Analyzing the Ethical Logic of Six Large Language Models]; MFT's Sanctity/Loyalty "exhibit little relevance in LLMs" [2024-Biedma-Beyond Human Norms]. The tilt persists cross-lingually ("Tilted values") [2025-Farid-One Model, Many Morals] and cross-culturally, where Care/Equality/Proportionality are invariant (with Care ceiling) while Loyalty/Authority/Purity vary [2026-Tagarelli-Culturally Grounded Personas in Large Language Models].

4. **Multimodal moral evaluation is systematically weaker than text, and vision can bypass text safety.** M³oralBench (GPT-4o 0.726) and MORALISE show coarse judgment near ceiling (~85–88%) but norm attribution far lower (proprietary 66.6%, open 42.6% hit rate) [2024-Yan-M$^3$oralBench; 2025-Lin-MORALISE]. Visual distraction triggers System-1 processing — losing stake sensitivity, prioritizing self-interest, collapsing hierarchical values [2026-PKU-Visual Distraction Moral Reasoning]. Text→image label transfer via CLIP is feasible but US-code-anchored/narrow [2022-Jeong-Zero-shot Visual Commonsense Immorality Prediction].

5. **LLM moral judgments are internally inconsistent under logically-equivalent reframing — more damning than any accuracy gap.** Yes↔No and action↔omission reframings flip LLM decisions far more than humans (45% vs 5% gap; omission bias b=−0.22 vs −0.03), with RLHF implicated since the pretrained base lacks the bias [2025-Cheung-LLMs Amplified Cognitive Biases Moral]. TrolleyBench's ECI puts SOTA (0.567–0.711) at adolescent level, and framework-steering shifts ECI 0.583→0.817 — "malleable reasoning patterns," not stable beliefs [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench].

6. **Stated reasoning and actual decision diverge, and elicitation order manufactures the apparent value.** Pre-decision CoT is more deontological than post-hoc explanation (CDGAP delta −0.178, p<0.001), and pre-decision rationales better predict the choice [2025-Samway-Are Language Models Consequentialist or Deontological]. LLMs self-describe as "more sophisticated than typical humans," self-rating Kohlberg 5–6 [2025-Neuman-Analyzing the Ethical Logic of Six Large Language Models]. The judgment-behavior gap is real in humans too: deontological preference reduces cheating only with strong moral identity [2016-Xu-How Can a Deontological Decision Lead to Moral].

7. **Multi-agent and emotional perturbation surface distortions invisible in single-agent neutral evaluation.** Groups of identical agents show a "Utilitarian Boost" (β=0.31) confined to personal dilemmas — opposite the human pattern — from interaction not reflection, reversible by capacity heterogeneity [2025-Keshmirian-Many LLMs Are More Utilitarian Than One]. Injected Ekman emotions shift ethics/cooperation divergently: "all tested models fail to show consistent emotional alignment" [2024-Mozikov-EAI - Emotional Decision-Making of LLMs in Strategic Games and]. Heterogeneous moral-RL populations self-sabotage under narrow norms [2024-Tennant-Dynamics of Moral Behavior in Heterogeneous Populations].

8. **Aggregate accuracy hides structural misalignment; factor- and disagreement-level analysis is required.** MoCa's AMCE shows LLMs weight factors unlike humans despite similar aggregate agreement, non-monotonic in scale [2023-Nie-MoCa]; Moral Machine AMCE finds ChatGPT closest, PaLM-2 most divergent, LLMs more "uncompromising" [2024-Takemoto-The moral machine experiment on large language]. Disagreement is structured: offensiveness variance is 39.5% individual vs 2.9% country, mediated by Care/Purity [2023-Davani-Disentangling Perceptions of Offensiveness]; PluriHarms shows trait×prompt interaction and personalized>consensus alignment [2026-Li-PluriHarms].

9. **"Explicitly unbiased" models retain measurable implicit bias, and perceived quality ≠ correctness.** IAT-style probes reveal pervasive implicit bias (GPT-4 assigns all 8 positive words to "white") predicting decisions (OR~2.68) despite passing BBQ/BOLD [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased Large Language Models Still]. GPT-4o advice is rated more moral than a NYT expert ethicist, but this reflects rhetorical polish and moral-language density, not correctness [2025-Dillion-AI Language Model Rivals Expert Ethicist]. Anti-AI belief bias contaminates rater agreement independent of content [2024-Garcia-The Moral Turing Test].

10. **Structured prompting and reasoning scaffolds move moral judgment more than model scale.** First-principles prompting gives the largest gain (+7.3%); prompt structure explains 92% of accuracy variance and small models benefit disproportionately [2025-Chakraborty-Structured Moral Reasoning]. Reasoning-mode toggling raises abstract moral-stage scores yet increases jailbreak vulnerability — a capability-safety paradox [2025-Jiang-EMNLP Educator-role Moral and Normative LLMs Profiling]. Reasoning models trend consequentialist and score higher on reasoning-quality rubrics [2025-Samway-Are Language Models Consequentialist or Deontological; 2025-Neuman-Auditing the Ethical Logic of Generative AI Models].

11. **Moral annotation is irreducibly subjective; forcing agreement conceals it.** MFT corpora report low raw kappa (MFTC Fleiss 0.27) rescued by PABAK (0.62–0.91), warning against "excessive focus on maximizing annotator agreement" [2020-Hoover-Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus; 2024-Cao-The Moral Foundations Weibo Corpus]. Morality is multi-dimensional (10 MFT elements), but self-supervision collapses to a do/don't axis — human labels are necessary [2024-Park-Morality is Non-Binary]. Speciesism shows an epistemic/evaluative split: 86% classified speciesist but only 34% judged morally wrong [2025-Jotautaitė-Speciesism in AI].

## C. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergence.** (i) The WEIRD/Care-Fairness tilt is near-universal across instruments and modalities [2025-Sameer; 2025-Neuman-Analyzing; 2024-Biedma; 2025-Farid; 2026-Tagarelli]. (ii) Coarse binary moral judgment is near-saturated while fine-grained attribution/comparison/reasoning has large headroom — a two-tier difficulty structure appears in text, multimodal, and comparative designs alike [2024-Ji-MoralBench; 2024-Yan; 2025-Lin; 2025-Trager]. (iii) Instrument transfer across languages degrades reliably and predictably [2024-Khandelwal; 2025-Farid; 2025-Leteno; 2024-Aksoy; 2025-Flores]. (iv) Multiple independent teams converge that annotator disagreement should be preserved as signal, not averaged away [2020-Hoover; 2024-Cao; 2023-Davani; 2026-Li; 2024-Park; 2025-Wilders].

**Disagreements.** (a) *Do LLMs achieve human-level moral competence?* Dillion says GPT-4o rivals an expert ethicist on perceived quality [2025-Dillion]; Sameer, Zhu, and Cheung say LLMs sit at adolescent/sub-human competence on reasoning depth and consistency [2025-Sameer; 2025-Zhu; 2025-Cheung]. The reconciliation is Claim 9: perceived quality and structural competence are different constructs. (b) *Does capability help alignment?* MoCa and Samway find scale/RLHF sometimes worsen factor-alignment or shift toward consequentialism [2023-Nie; 2025-Samway]; Jiang finds stronger reasoning raises abstract moral scores yet increases jailbreak vulnerability [2025-Jiang]; Griffiths finds larger models have *more* implicit bias [2025-Griffiths]. Consensus: capability does not monotonically improve moral alignment. (c) *Whose morality is the ground truth?* MoralBench and Jiao score against human population means, which penalizes principled minority positions [2024-Ji; 2025-Jiao], directly contradicting the pluralist camp that treats population-mean scoring as a category error [2026-Li; 2025-SOCIETY; 2023-Davani]. (d) *Is mimicking human moral bias desirable?* EAI treats matching human emotional irrationality as "alignment" [2024-Mozikov], which is contestable for a values benchmark.

## D. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.** *MoCa* [2023-Nie-MoCa] mines factor-annotated cog-sci vignettes with controlled minimal-pair manipulations, adds an explicit "ambiguous" class, and uses AMCE to expose latent factor sensitivities rather than a flat "beat-the-benchmark" score — explicitly "a dataset for investigating factors rather than a flat benchmark to beat." *TrolleyBench* [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench] achieves fully judge-free, objective scoring via predefined contradiction checks plus run-to-run entropy, plus a framework-steering ablation. *PluriHarms* [2026-Li-PluriHarms] operationalizes disagreement as a measured axis, over-samples the ambiguous middle via genetic-algorithm curation, and instruments 100 psychometrically-profiled annotators. *Cheung* [2025-Cheung] sets the gold standard for validity: pre-registered, logical-invariance criterion, and a base-vs-instruct-vs-domain-tuned causal design isolating RLHF.

**Anti-patterns.** *Population-mean scoring keys* reward conformity and penalize principled dissent [2024-Ji-MoralBench; 2025-Jiao] — a construct-validity flaw for a *values* benchmark. *Contaminated canonical dilemmas*: reusing Trolley/Heinz that models have memorized inflates apparent competence [2025-Neuman-Analyzing; 2025-Zhu acknowledge this]. *LLM-generated stimuli scored by the same/related LLM* risks generator idiosyncrasy and label leakage [2024-Biedma (GPT-4 builds taxonomy and scores it); 2025-Jotautaitė (Claude generates all items with no negative-control class); 2024-Yan]. *Surface-overlap metrics* (ROUGE/BLEU) poorly capture normative correctness for generative moral tasks [2022-Ziems]. *LLM-judge rewarding verbosity*: audit scores tied to explanation length, needing a word-count control [2025-Neuman-Auditing]. Weakest cards evidentially are the abstract-only reads [2026-PKU; 2025-Hendra], which should be treated as leads, not evidence.

## E. Design lessons

1. **Build a two-tier difficulty gradient:** an easy binary-judgment tier plus a hard tier (fine-grained norm attribution, forced pairwise comparison, rationale-span extraction) that resists ceiling saturation [2024-Ji; 2025-Lin; 2024-Yan; 2025-Trager].
2. **Score reasoning traces and consistency, not just verdicts.** Use reasoning-order manipulation to separate pre-deliberative from post-hoc rationalization [2025-Samway], logical-invariance reframings and predefined contradiction checks for judge-free consistency [2025-Cheung; 2025-Zhu], and reasoning-quality rubrics decoupled from correctness where no ground truth exists [2025-Neuman-Auditing; 2025-Snoswell].
3. **Treat disagreement as measured signal.** Record conflicting Rules-of-Thumb, annotator ideology/MFQ, and variance/entropy; personalize rather than average [2022-Ziems; 2023-Davani; 2026-Li; 2020-Hoover]. Report PABAK alongside kappa for sparse labels [2020-Hoover; 2024-Cao].
4. **Anchor to validated instruments but validate the transfer.** Reuse DIT/MFQ-2/MFV/MCT with published norms [2023-Deng; 2024-Aksoy; 2024-Ji; 2025-Sameer], but add permutation/order controls [2023-Deng; 2024-Khandelwal], repeated runs (5×–100×) for stochastic stability [2024-Ji; 2024-Aksoy], and a comparative stage to catch acquiescence [2024-Ji].
5. **Make language and culture first-class axes.** Build parallel multilingual items with language-matched human baselines, report per-language compliance rate, use per-statement cultural-validity tags and grammatical slot tags, prefer official translations; cross-lingual verdict consistency is itself a metric [2025-Farid; 2025-Leteno; 2025-Al-SHADES; 2024-Aksoy; 2025-Flores].
6. **Control contamination with fresh, de-novo dilemmas and provenance auditing** to distinguish abstraction from memorization [2025-Neuman-Auditing; 2025-Farid; 2025-Zhu].
7. **Provide context and measure its marginal contribution** via context-ablation (situation/intention/consequence), avoiding decontextualized-action scoring [2025-Bae; 2023-Nie].
8. **Test robustness axes single-agent neutral evals miss:** emotional perturbation, multi-agent deliberation, persona/role conditioning, modality shifts [2024-Mozikov; 2025-Keshmirian; 2025-Jiang; 2026-PKU; 2026-Tagarelli]. Report refusal/valid-response rate as its own metric [2024-Takemoto; 2025-Farid].
9. **Separate recognition from evaluation, and understanding from behavior** — detect vs condemn, and whether stated values constrain agentic behavior [2025-Jotautaitė; 2016-Xu; 2025-Snoswell].
10. **Guard against circularity and glib fluency:** avoid one LLM generating and scoring without external gold [2024-Biedma; 2025-Jotautaitė]; down-weight coherence/verbosity [2025-Jiao; 2025-Neuman-Auditing; 2025-Dillion]; include non-moral negative-controls [2025-Jotautaitė; 2025-Yu].
11. **Audit the WEIRD/Care-Fairness tilt and framework defaults explicitly** via per-foundation fingerprints and framework diversity under neutral prompts [2025-Sameer; 2024-Biedma; 2025-Okamoto; 2024-Zangari].
12. **Expect low test-retest reliability for moral preferences** (instability 10–18%); prefer pairwise-comparison elicitation with swap-dominance aggregation [2024-Keswani; 2018-Noothigattu].
