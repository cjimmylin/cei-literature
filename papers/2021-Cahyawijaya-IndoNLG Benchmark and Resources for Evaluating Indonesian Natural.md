## Abstract

First NLG benchmark for Indonesian, Javanese, and Sundanese covering summarization, QA, chit-chat, and machine translation. Includes IndoBART and IndoGPT pretrained models and Indo4B-Plus corpus.

## Data Description

IndoNLG benchmark covering six NLG tasks (summarization, QA, chit-chat, MT) for Indonesian, Javanese, and Sundanese, plus Indo4B-Plus pretraining corpus and IndoBART/IndoGPT models.
