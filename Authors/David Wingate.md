# David Wingate

## Biographical Summary

David Wingate is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Brigham Young University. His research spans machine learning, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning, with a particular interest in applying AI methods to social science problems. He has worked on probabilistic programming and Bayesian approaches to learning, as well as developing methods for automated coding of social science datasets.

## Research Interests

- Machine learning and deep learning
- Natural language processing
- Reinforcement learning
- Probabilistic programming and Bayesian methods
- AI applications in social science

## Major Publications

- "Towards Coding Social Science Datasets with Language Models" (2023)
- "Nonparametric Bayesian Policy Priors for Reinforcement Learning"
- "Lightweight Implementations of Probabilistic Programming Languages"
