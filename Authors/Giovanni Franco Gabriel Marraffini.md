# Giovanni Franco Gabriel Marraffini

## Biographical Summary

Giovanni Franco Gabriel Marraffini is a researcher working on AI ethics and moral evaluation of language models. His work focuses on measuring and understanding the moral alignment of LLMs, particularly from utilitarian perspectives.

## Research & Impact

Marraffini's research examines the ethical reasoning capabilities of AI systems:

- **The Greatest Good Benchmark**: Lead author on this benchmark for measuring LLMs' alignment with utilitarian moral dilemmas, published at EMNLP 2024 in Miami, Florida. The research addresses the question of how to make decisions that maximize the well-being of all persons - highly relevant for designing beneficial and harmless language models.

- **Key Findings**: Analysis across 15 diverse LLMs reveals consistently encoded moral preferences that diverge from established moral theories and lay population moral standards. Most LLMs show marked preference for impartial beneficence and rejection of instrumental harm, revealing an "artificial moral compass" in LLMs.

- **Ethics by Design for AI**: Has also worked on top-down approaches for Artificial Moral Agents using the Beliefs, Desires, and Intentions (BDI) model, proposing hybrid solutions applying both utilitarian and deontological ethical frameworks.
