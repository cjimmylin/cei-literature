# Erchin Serpedin

## Biographical Summary

Erchin Serpedin is a Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University. He is a highly cited researcher with expertise spanning signal processing, machine learning, and biomedical applications. His research includes contributions to AI systems for healthcare, communication systems, and more recently, evaluation of hallucination in large language models.

## Research Interests

- **Signal Processing**: Developing advanced signal processing techniques
- **Machine Learning for Healthcare**: Applying ML to biomedical problems
- **AI Evaluation**: Assessing capabilities and limitations of AI systems
- **Communications**: Research on communication system design and optimization

## Major Publications

- Highly cited work in signal processing and communications
- IEEE Fellow with extensive publication record
- Recent contributions to LLM evaluation research
