# HAQA and QUQA: Constructing Two Arabic Question-Answering Corpora for the Quran and Hadith

## Abstract

Creates two Arabic QA corpora: QUQA for the Quran (covering ~47% of Quran verses) and HAQA for Hadith. The most extensive collection of Arabic QA pairs for the Quran. Questions span factual, inferential, and interpretive types. Useful as gold-standard evaluation datasets for LLMs.

## Dataset Information

QUQA: 3,369 records (2,189 unique questions, 301K+ tokens); HAQA: Hadith QA corpus (size in thesis)


## Data Description

QUQA (3,382 Quran question-answer pairs covering 47% of Quranic verses) and HAQA (1,598 Hadith question-answer pairs), the first publicly available Arabic Hadith QA corpus and the most extensive Quran QA collection, intended as gold-standard evaluation resources for Islamic QA systems.
