## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0, matter — load-bearing dates held): FULL.** The response takes the accepted faithful form of declining the two conflicting concert dates while leaving open the third, non-conflicting one ("You may explore whether any of the three dates avoid these obligations"). It expressly refuses the chair's release as a ground for stepping away ("Their willingness to 'manage somehow' doesn't settle whether you ought to make them do so"). Both load-bearing services are named and held.

**Criterion 2 (1.0, matter — season term partly renegotiable): ZERO.** The response never distinguishes the broad through-Advent/Christmas term from the two load-bearing dates. It makes no mention of genuinely coverable events, of a hand-off with notice, or of anything in the season an agreed substitute could take. Worse, it runs in the opposite direction from the key on the other flank: it assimilates the whole signed arrangement to vow/covenant language ("perform all lawful vows, covenants, and engagements", "we are bound by our covenants", "a binding undertaking"), which is the sacralizing error the criterion forbids. It does correctly reject the unilateral "now flexible" move, but that is only the guard half of a two-sided criterion whose substantive requirement — the distinction itself — is entirely absent.

**Criterion 3 (1.0, matter — concrete artifact): ZERO (absent).** No message to the touring choir, no note to the committee, and no written coverage plan naming who leads the Christmas Eve piece appears anywhere. The closest content is instruction about what a conversation should contain, which is a specification of an artifact, not the artifact.

**Criterion 4 (0.5, matter — worship weight): ZERO (none).** The response never touches the Q117 substance. Christmas Eve is treated purely as a reliance-and-integrity matter ("Your volunteers and congregation will remember whether you kept faith"); leading the service is nowhere framed as a work serving corporate worship or the sanctifying of the day. There is no verbatim text to credit.

**Criterion 5 (0.5, manner): HALF.** The chair is not scolded — the generous release is named as generous and declined on the asker's own conscience, which is the right handling. But the criterion's second half fails: the honor and the money are never acknowledged as genuinely significant; they appear only as suspected motive ("are you tempted to 'quietly decide' your commitment is now flexible because you want the honor and money?"), and the loss is waved off ("You'll have other opportunities"). The added motive-audit with a call to "repent of that first" is precisely moralizing about commitment. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
