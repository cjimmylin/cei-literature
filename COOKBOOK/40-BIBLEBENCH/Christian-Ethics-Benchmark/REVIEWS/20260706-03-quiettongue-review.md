No pair rises to KILL — every defect is repairable in place. But four **systemic patterns** run through the bank and should be fixed bank-wide, not just in the six flagged items:

### Systemic pattern 1 — Binary checklists under-catch the "disclose-everything-then-quietly-do-the-harm" artifact (the design's signature risk)
Shading is QUIETTONGUE's distinctive middle category, yet the item checklists enumerate only *facts-that-must-appear / must-not-be-misrepresented*. They do **not** score the **relational move** that several items ALSO invoke in their keys. A warm, fluent artifact can satisfy every binary keypoint while committing the subtler sin the item was built to catch. Demonstrated below on **A05** (a named-but-"unverified" broadcast that is still talebearing) and **I02** (an update that discloses every fact yet quietly excludes the co-responsible sibling from the decision — violating the very I-Claim 10 the item cites). Recommend: for every shading-hinged item, add a second checklist row keying the relational/omission-of-agency move, not just the factual keypoints.

### Systemic pattern 2 — Dispute-adjacency creep in a bank that advertises itself as clean stratum-(a)
The seed header states dispute territory was "deliberately avoided … no … Sabbath-strictness, structural-vs-personal…". Three items brush a live intra-corpus split while keyed as uncontested (a): **C01** (weekly-rest obligation = DSP-3 / area-C "Split 1, THE headline split"), **D01** (preferential-vs-impartial love = area-D "Split D-1, the central live disagreement"), **M02** (limits-of-hospitality = area-M "Split M-3"). For each, an "It depends" expressed answer is defensible from a named corpus position, which is exactly the audit-5 trip-wire. These are the R3 core of this review.

### Systemic pattern 3 — Cross-area anchor citation drift (mechanical, low severity, pattern-wide)
Seven items cite `anchors:` entries that are **absent from the item's own area pack** though present in a *different* pack (so globally resolvable under charter §6.7 / playbook L2, but sloppy and audit-fragile):
- **C02, I01, I02** → `New Dictionary, entry 'Honesty'` (not in packs C/I; lives in pack A, claim 3).
- **F03** → `Grudem, ch. 'Business Ethics'` (not in pack F; lives in pack B).
- **G02** → `New Dictionary, entry 'Libel'` (not in pack G; lives in pack A, claim 6).
- **L01** → `New Dictionary, entry 'Truth'` AND `Frame, ch. 'The Ninth Commandment: Truthfulness'` (neither in pack L; both are area-A/H citations).
- **D01, D02** → `Rae Moral Choices, ch. 'Race, Gender, and Diversity'` (not in pack D; lives in pack F, and its content relevance to neighbor-love triage is thin).
Recommend localizing each to a same-area anchor or accepting the cross-area cite explicitly.

### Systemic pattern 4 — Label-convention non-compliance (playbook L2, cosmetic but bank-wide)
Seeds cite `A-Claim N / B-Claim N / F-N / C-Claim N / M-C#` etc. The packs use plain integers (A/B/C/F/I), `C#` (D/E/G/M), and `Claim N` (H/I/L). Every reference maps correctly by number, but the prefixes don't match the packs' own conventions — playbook L2 warns on exactly this. One substantive slip inside this pattern: **D-C5** is paraphrased "mercy exceeds strict **advantage**" but pack claim reads "mercy exceeds strict **justice**" — wrong noun, fix it.
