## Abstract

Open-source Thai-centric LLM based on Llama-2 (7B/13B/70B). Evaluated on Thai Exam Benchmark, M3Exam, and additional OpenThaiGPT benchmarks. Performance approaches Claude Sonnet level on Thai benchmarks. Major open-source Thai LLM with Thai-specific evaluation. Thai Exam Benchmark evaluates Thai cultural and educational knowledge.
