# Geoff Keeling

## Biographical Summary

Geoff Keeling is a Staff Research Scientist and philosopher at Google DeepMind, working on the ethical and societal impacts of artificial general intelligence including questions around AI alignment, welfare and consciousness. He is also an Associate Fellow at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence at the University of Cambridge. He holds a PhD in Philosophy from the University of Bristol, an MA in Philosophy from Bristol (Distinction, ranked 1st), and a BSc in Philosophy, Logic, and Scientific Method from the London School of Economics (First Class, Joint 1st). Previously, he was a postdoc at Stanford University between the Center for Ethics in Society and HAI.

## Research & Impact

Keeling's research addresses fundamental questions in AI ethics:

- **AI Ethics Framework**: Led the Google DeepMind report on "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants" with Iason Gabriel and Arianna Manzini, receiving 100+ citations in its first year with coverage in WIRED, The Verge, and The Information.

- **AI Agents Ethics**: New paper in Nature titled "We need a new ethics for a world of AI agents" with Iason Gabriel, Arianna Manzini, and James Evans.

- **AI Welfare**: Book on AI welfare forthcoming with Cambridge University Press, co-authored with Winnie Street.

- **Machine Sentience**: Collaborated with Jonathan Birch's lab at LSE on Google's first empirical study on machine sentience.

His philosophical interests include alignment, manipulation, trust, digital minds, and human-AI relationships.
