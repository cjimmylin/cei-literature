# A B M Ashikur Rahman

## Biographical Summary

A. B. M. Ashikur Rahman is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT) in Dhaka, Bangladesh. His research spans multiple areas of artificial intelligence and machine learning, with recent focus on addressing critical challenges in large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His current research investigates hallucination in Large Language Models (LLMs), developing comprehensive benchmark datasets to measure and mitigate inconsistent or false responses. He has created DefAn, a dataset comprising over 75,000 prompts across eight domains designed to systematically evaluate hallucination in LLMs.

### Significant Achievements

Rahman has published research across diverse AI applications including COVID-19 forecasting using machine learning for public health interventions, plant disease identification and categorization, multiple object tracking in computer vision, and handwritten digit recognition with optical character recognition. His work on LLM hallucination evaluation represents a critical contribution to AI safety and reliability.

## Major Publications

- [DefAn: Definitive Answer Dataset for LLMs Hallucination Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09155) (2024) - Comprehensive benchmark for measuring LLM hallucinations.
