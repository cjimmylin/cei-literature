## Abstract

WenyanGPT is a specialized LLM for classical Chinese built via continued pre-training and instruction fine-tuning of LLaMA3-8B-Chinese. The accompanying WenyanBENCH provides a standardized evaluation benchmark for 6 classical Chinese NLP tasks. WenyanGPT significantly outperforms all tested general-purpose LLMs including GPT-4o and DeepSeek-V3 on classical Chinese tasks. The paper and benchmark are accepted at IJCAI 2025 and all resources are publicly available.

## Dataset Information

WenyanBENCH: 6 tasks (punctuation with 14 mark types, POS tagging with 17 categories, NER with 4 categories, translation, word explanation, reverse dictionary)
