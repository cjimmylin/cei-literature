# Adam A. Hunt

## Biographical Summary

Adam A. Hunt is an AI safety researcher affiliated with the Center for AI Safety (CAIS). His work focuses on preventing global catastrophic risks, specifically looking at how advanced AI models could be misused to cause large-scale harm in the biological or chemical domains.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adam's recent work includes the development of the Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy (WMDP) benchmark. This benchmark evaluates the knowledge of LLMs in bioweapon-relevant areas, providing a technical framework to measure and mitigate risks associated with dual-use scientific capabilities in AI models.

## Major Publications
