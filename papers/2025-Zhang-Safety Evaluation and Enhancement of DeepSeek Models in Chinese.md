## Abstract

Extends CHiSafetyBench evaluation to all six DeepSeek-R1 distilled models. Applies supervised fine-tuning for safety enhancement while preserving reasoning capabilities. Open-sources all safety-enhanced models.

## Data Description

CHiSafetyBench is a Chinese safety evaluation benchmark used to test DeepSeek-R1 series models (6 distilled variants plus the full R1-671B model). The study evaluates safety across multiple Chinese-specific risk categories and proposes enhancement methods for improving safety in DeepSeek's reasoning models. The safety-enhanced models and code are available on GitHub.
