# Tolga Bolukbasi

## Research Summary

Tolga Bolukbasi is affiliated with Google DeepMind and has connections to Boston University (verified bu.edu email). He is well-known for his influential 2016 NeurIPS paper "Man is to computer programmer as woman is to homemaker? debiasing word embeddings" co-authored with Kai-Wei Chang, James Y Zou, Venkatesh Saligrama, and Adam T Kalai. His current work at Google DeepMind focuses on large language models, interpretability, machine learning, and training data attribution.
