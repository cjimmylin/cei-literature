# Daniel L. Chen

## Affiliation
Professor of Law and Economics, Toulouse School of Economics; Director, Toulouse Institute for Artificial Intelligence (ANITI)

## Research Summary

Daniel L. Chen is a Professor at Toulouse School of Economics and directs research at the Toulouse Institute for Artificial Intelligence. His pioneering work applies machine learning and data science to legal studies, examining judicial behavior, moral reasoning in legal contexts, and the computational analysis of legal texts. He has made significant contributions to understanding how moral frameworks influence legal decision-making and how these patterns can be automatically classified.
