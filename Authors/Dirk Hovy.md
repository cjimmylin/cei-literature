# Dirk Hovy

## Research Summary

Dirk Hovy is a Professor of Computing Sciences at Bocconi University in Milan, where he directs the MilaNLP lab. His research bridges computational linguistics and social science, focusing on how demographic and social factors influence language and how AI systems can perpetuate or mitigate biases. He has made significant contributions to understanding bias in NLP systems, developing ethical frameworks for AI, and creating benchmarks for evaluating language model safety and political bias. He is known for his work on the social impact of NLP technologies.

## Major Publications

- Work on demographic bias in NLP systems
- XSTest benchmark for safety evaluation
- Research on political bias in language models
- The Pluralistic Moral Gap study
