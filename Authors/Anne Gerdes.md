# Anne Gerdes

## Biographical Summary

Professor Anne Gerdes is affiliated with the University of Southern Denmark, located at Universitetsparken 1, 6000 Kolding, Denmark, in the Department of Design and Communication. Her research explores the philosophical and ethical dimensions of AI, particularly the role of large language model chatbots in providing moral guidance and their impact on human understanding and interaction.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

Gerdes investigates whether LLM chatbots can serve as ethical guides, drawing on philosophical traditions including virtue ethics and the work of Søren Kierkegaard. Her research examines both the possibilities and limitations of AI systems in supporting ethical reflection, emphasizing that while AI can provide structured guidance, genuine ethical decision-making requires human consciousness and moral agency.

### Significant Achievements

Her notable publication "Exploring LLM Chatbots as Potential Guides to Virtue Ethics" uses a case study on euthanasia to examine how Kierkegaard's critique of clergy and his imagined "machine sermons" relates to contemporary debates about AI-mediated ethical guidance. She has also conducted research on emoji use in LLM chatbot conversations, analyzing how interpersonal resources are deployed in human-AI interaction from a social semiotic perspective.
