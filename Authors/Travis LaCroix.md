# Travis LaCroix

## Research Summary

Travis LaCroix is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Philosophy at Durham University. He is also a faculty affiliate at the Schwartz Reisman Institute for Technology and Society (University of Toronto) and an IMH fellow at the Institute for Medical Humanities (Durham University). His research includes interrelated projects in ethically-aligned artificial intelligence, social dynamics and norms, and the philosophy of autism. Previously, he was a postdoctoral fellow at Mila - Québec Artificial Intelligence Institute and received his PhD from UC Irvine in 2020.
