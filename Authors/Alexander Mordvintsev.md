# Alexander Mordvintsev

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Mordvintsev is a researcher at Google Research in Zurich, widely recognized as the creator of the **DeepDream** algorithm. Originally from Russia, he graduated from ITMO University and worked in commercial computer vision before joining Google. His accidental discovery of "algorithmic pareidolia" while visualizing neural networks sparked a global fascination with AI-generated art and provided deep insights into the interpretability of deep learning models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander's research focuses on the visualization and interpretation of deep neural networks, combining machine learning with computer graphics. He is a pioneer in **Neural Cellular Automata (NCA)**, investigating how living organisms self-organize through local interactions. His work on "Growing Neural Cellular Automata" explores the robustness and self-regeneration of bio-inspired systems, bridging the gap between biology and artificial intelligence.

### Significant Achievements
DeepDream remains one of the most culturally significant AI algorithms, offering a rare look into the internal representations of convolutional neural networks. His subsequent work on morphogenesis and self-organizing systems has pushed the boundaries of generative modeling and complex systems theory. His art and research have been exhibited in major venues internationally, influencing both the scientific and creative communities.
