# Nathan Moss

## Research Summary

Nathan Moss has contributed to research on algorithmic fairness and bias in AI systems, with particular focus on cross-cultural consistency in human-annotated labels for recommendation systems. His work examines how labels are conceptualized differently across annotators from different demographics and cultural backgrounds, addressing the challenge of homogeneous annotator pools being applied to heterogeneous global user populations.

His research on video game label annotation (surveying 5,174 gamers) identified inconsistently conceptualized labels and provided both cultural and linguistic explanations for cross-country differences. The work demonstrates that predictive models perform better on global training sets compared to homogeneous single-country datasets, and provides a generalizable framework for auditing data annotation processes.

Referenced in work emphasizing that addressing societal implications of unfair algorithmic decision-making requires more than technological solutions (Sloane and Moss, 2019).
