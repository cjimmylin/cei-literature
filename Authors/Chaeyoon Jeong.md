# Chaeyoon Jeong

## Biographical Summary

Chaeyoon Jeong is a researcher at Samsung Research and a graduate student at the KAIST School of Computing. Her work sits at the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Social Psychology, focusing on how language patterns reflect and influence human emotional and political states.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on the "Quantification of Moral Emotions." She developed methods to analyze how emotions like outrage, pity, and pride shape online political participation across different cultures. By using context-aware NLP models, she seeks to understand the social dynamics of digital activism and the role of language in collective action.

### Significant Achievements
Jeong's work has been published in top-tier venues like ACL (Findings), where her cross-cultural analysis of online petitions provided new insights into the "Universal Emotional Drivers" of political engagement. Her research is essential for building "Emotionally Aware" AI that can navigate the nuances of human sociopolitical discourse without inciting polarization.
