# Artem Karpov

## Biographical Summary

Artem Karpov is an independent researcher working on AI alignment, specifically investigating how to induce human-like moral reasoning patterns in large language models. His work is funded by Good Ventures Foundation through the "Early-career funding for individuals interested in improving the long-term future" program by Open Philanthropy, and is conducted with support from AI Safety Camp.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research examines the alignment (measured by BrainScore) of large language models fine-tuned for moral reasoning on behavioral data and/or brain data from humans performing the same task. He investigates whether training AI systems on both behavioral patterns and neural activation data can produce more human-compatible moral reasoning.

### Significant Achievements

Karpov co-authored "Inducing Human-like Biases in Moral Reasoning Language Models," which fine-tuned several LLMs (BERT, RoBERTa, DeBERTa) on moral reasoning behavioral data from the ETHICS benchmark and fMRI data from Koster-Hale et al. (2013). His contributions included implementation of fine-tuning, experimental design, data processing, infrastructure setup, code reviews, and significant portions of the written analysis. The paper was published on arXiv in November 2024.
