# Amir Feder

## Research Summary

Amir Feder is a researcher at Columbia University working on AI ethics, causal inference, and natural language processing. His research examines the moral beliefs and ethical reasoning encoded in large language models. He has developed statistical methods for eliciting and evaluating the beliefs encoded in LLMs, particularly focusing on how models handle ambiguous moral scenarios. His work contributes to understanding AI alignment and the consistency of moral judgments in AI systems.
