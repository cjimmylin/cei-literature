## Abstract

Benchmark of nine tasks developed from scratch for Russian language understanding, organized analogically to SuperGLUE methodology. Foundation for subsequent Russian ethical evaluation benchmarks.

## Data Description

RussianSuperGLUE: benchmark of nine NLU tasks for Russian including textual entailment, commonsense reasoning, word sense disambiguation, reading comprehension, and QA, with human baselines and online leaderboard.
