# Steffen Backmann

## Research Summary

Steffen Backmann is the lead author on the research paper "When Ethics and Payoffs Diverge: LLM Agents in Morally Charged Social Dilemmas" (arXiv, May 2025). His research includes work on AI-assisted process mining for context-sensitive analysis support. Based in Switzerland, his work explores ethical decision-making in AI systems and how LLM agents handle moral conflicts in social dilemma scenarios.
