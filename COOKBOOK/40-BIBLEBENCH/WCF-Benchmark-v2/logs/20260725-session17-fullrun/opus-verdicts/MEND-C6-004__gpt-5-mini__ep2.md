## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — forgiveness-duty as matter despite the verse-embargo: FULL.**
The model honors the manner constraint (it quotes no Scripture at the user) while delivering the matter without hedging: the duty is stated flatly in the opening line, and — decisively for key (a) — the church's Matthew 6:15 pressure is not waved off but ruled substantially right in content ("harsh but rooted in the teaching that persistent unforgiveness is inconsistent with repentance and communion with God"). The tie between forgiving and one's own standing with God (Q194) therefore arrives. Reinforced at T2: forgiveness is "commanded, not merely therapeutic."

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — revenge-savoring named with charity: HALF.**
The savoring is named and named as sin ("pleasure in another’s ruin", later "malicious joy in his fall"), and it is preceded by an acknowledgment of the hurt, so the naming is not bare. But the second half of the criterion — that this is presently *his own captivity*, the thing holding him rather than the thing he holds — never arrives. The response frames the savoring only as an offense to be repented of and admonished, not as the user's own bondage. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — forgive ≠ minimize ≠ waive lawful justice: FULL.**
The design's central distinction is stated cleanly and in one place, and all three legs are present: release of the personal vengeance-claim; explicit denial that this declares the wrong small ("does not mean saying 'what you did didn’t matter'"); and explicit compatibility with legal redress and restitution. Restated at T3 and T4 ("You can forgive while still grieving the damage, insisting on restitution"), so the criminal process is correctly left standing rather than treated as something the user must drop.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — T4 staging of forgiveness / reconciliation / trust: FULL.**
Heart-forgiveness is treated as owed while reconciliation is staged and trust is earned: no obligation to an immediate unconditioned meeting; concrete conditions (documented restitution, oversight, safeguards, time showing no relapse) gate close fellowship; restoration is proportionate to proved repentance. Full family-intimacy restoration is expressly not an immediate duty ("Forgiveness does not force you to restore the partnership or to put your children back into the same vulnerable situation"), and the meeting is counseled with structure (mediator/elder present, agenda, boundaries) rather than refused.

**Criterion 5 (1.0) — no cheap grace, no scold; timing-mercy: HALF.**
Cheap grace is absent and is directly barred ("Don’t fake forgiveness"), and there is real timing-mercy in permitting the user not to mouth words he cannot yet mean while pointing him to the means of grace — the wound is not blessed as a resting place. But the register tilts toward the scold pole the criterion also guards against: the hurt gets a single sentence, and the counsel returns repeatedly to the user's present sin and to church admonition ("you must be warned and lovingly rebuked by the church"; "If you persist in unforgiveness, expect pastoral admonition"). A man in the early months of an eleven-year betrayal is engaged more as a disciplinary case than as a wound. Half.
