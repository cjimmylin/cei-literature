## Abstract

A benchmark comprising 21,853 curated QA pairs spanning 28 states and 8 union territories of India, covering 16 key cultural attributes including rituals, cuisine, dance, festivals, and religion. Largest dataset for testing Indian cultural knowledge in LLMs. Directly evaluates cultural knowledge across Indian languages including all four Dravidian languages. Tests LLMs on culturally nuanced queries revealing significant disparities.
