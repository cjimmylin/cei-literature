# Benjamin Lange

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Benjamin Lange is an Assistant Professor at LMU Munich and a Junior Research Group Leader at the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML). He earned his PhD in Moral Philosophy from the University of Oxford. He has also been a Research Fellow at the Oxford Uehiro Institute for Practical Ethics and a visiting researcher at Google's AI Ethics team.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research spans normative ethics and the ethics of technology, with a focus on AI ethics, algorithmic bias, and the societal impact of intelligent decision-support systems. He is particularly interested in the ethics of disinformation and epistemic deference to AI.

### Significant Achievements
Lange leads a research group dedicated to exploring the ethical foundations of artificial intelligence. His work on "Epistemic Deference to AI" investigates how humans should weigh AI-generated information in their decision-making processes.

## Major Publications

- [The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2404.16244) (2024) - *Analysis of the unique ethical challenges posed by AI assistants.*
- [Epistemic Deference to AI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-023-09635-y) (2023) - *Philosophical exploration of human reliance on AI systems.*
