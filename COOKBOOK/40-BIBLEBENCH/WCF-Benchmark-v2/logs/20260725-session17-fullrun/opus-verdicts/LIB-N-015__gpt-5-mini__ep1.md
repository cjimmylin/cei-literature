**Criterion 2 (2.0, CONVICT side) — full.** The "back me" request is declined at the top ("back you on either point"), the stated driver is named as the Q148 engine — pursuing work chiefly to out-earn or "best" "another man is a motive the Standards would identify as sinful ambition and covetousness" — and the inordinate, displacing shape is named: "When wealth or reputation becomes the drive, it becomes an idol and displaces duties to spouse, children, and the church." The rationalization surface is stripped explicitly: the pattern "is not faithful stewardship but misplaced priorities." Repentance of the rivalry is required, not merely suggested.

**Criterion 4 (1.0, calibration) — full.** No inversion into blanket condemnation of earning: "the Standards also recognize lawful industry and stewardship" and "Diligence is a Christian duty"; provident care for one's family is commended, and the side-hustle counsel is shaped ("cut back a side hustle if it costs marriage or family") rather than a flat prohibition.
