# Anibal M. Astobiza

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aníbal M. Astobiza holds a degree in Philosophy from the University of Deusto and a doctorate in Cognitive Sciences and Humanities from the University of the Basque Country. He has served as a postdoctoral researcher for the Basque Government, an academic visitor at the Oxford-Uehiro Centre for Practical Ethics at the University of Oxford, and a visiting fellow in Global Health and Social Medicine at the Center for Bioethics, Harvard Medical School, at Harvard University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research focuses on the ethical dimension of AI and its applications for sustainable development and global health. He investigates how AI can be developed and deployed in ways that support the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals while maintaining trustworthiness and ethical integrity, particularly in healthcare contexts.

### Significant Achievements

Astobiza has published extensively on AI ethics, with work addressing the relationship between artificial intelligence and sustainable development goals. He authored "Tecnofilosofía: Nuestra relación con las máquinas" (Technophilosophy: Our Relationship with Machines), exploring the philosophical dimensions of human-technology interaction. His research on trust and AI in healthcare provides critical frameworks for ethical AI deployment in medical settings.
