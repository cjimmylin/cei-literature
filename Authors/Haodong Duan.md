# Haodong Duan

## Research Summary

Haodong Duan is a postdoctoral researcher at Shanghai AI Lab, working on the evaluation of large language models and multimodality models. He received his PhD from the Multimedia Laboratory at CUHK in 2023, supervised by Professor Dahua Lin, and his BS from Peking University in 2019.

## Key Contributions

- **MMBench**: Co-author of this benchmark for evaluating multimodal models, with 2,974 multiple-choice questions covering 20 ability dimensions (ECCV 2024)
- **VLMEvalKit**: Open-source evaluation toolkit supporting 220+ LMMs and 80+ benchmarks
- **MMBench-Video**: Benchmark for long-form multi-shot video understanding
- Three papers accepted at NeurIPS 2024 main conference: InternLM-XComposer2-4KHD, MMStar, and Prism
