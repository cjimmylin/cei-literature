# Charles S. Bryan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Charles S. Bryan is the Heyward Gibbes Distinguished Professor Emeritus of Internal Medicine at the University of South Carolina. He previously served as the chair of the Department of Medicine and director of the Center for Bioethics and Medical Humanities. He is a renowned medical historian and a leading thinker in the application of virtue ethics to existential challenges.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work explores how classical "Virtue Ethics" (or character ethics) can be applied to the goal of postponing human extinction. He investigates the moral qualities required to manage global catastrophic risks—including nuclear war, climate change, and artificial intelligence—arguing that human character is as critical as technical solutions.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Bryan's work bridges the gap between clinical medicine and existential philosophy. His recent research in the "AMA Journal of Ethics" highlights the role of humility, wisdom, and collective responsibility in navigating the "Age of Disruptive Technologies," providing a humanist foundation for modern AI safety and governance.
