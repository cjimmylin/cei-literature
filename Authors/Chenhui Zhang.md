# Chenhui Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Chenhui Zhang is a researcher affiliated with Tsinghua University. He is a significant contributor to the evaluation and development of autonomous LLM agents. His work often involves rigorous benchmarking to understand the capabilities and limitations of agents in complex, multi-turn environments.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Agent Evaluation**: Developing comprehensive benchmarks like "AgentBench" to assess LLMs as pragmatic agents in real-world scenarios (e.g., OS interaction, database querying, games).
- **Reasoning Models**: Contributing to the development and analysis of foundation models capable of advanced reasoning and coding (e.g., GLM-4.5).
- **Graph Learning**: Researching improvements in the training and application of Graph Neural Networks.

### Significant Achievements
- **AgentBench**: Co-author of AgentBench, one of the first and most comprehensive frameworks for evaluating LLM agents, revealing significant gaps between open-source and proprietary models.

## Major Publications
- **AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents**: A foundational benchmark for the agentic AI community.
