# Kehua Feng

## Research Summary

Kehua Feng is a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, working in the areas of Natural Language Processing, Language Models, and AI for Science. Their research includes work on knowledge preference optimization for controllable protein generation and sample-efficient human evaluation of large language models. Recent publications appear at ACL 2025 and other major NLP conferences, with collaborations spanning researchers at Zhejiang University including Huajun Chen and others.
