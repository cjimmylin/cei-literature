# Dinura Dissanayake

## Research Summary

Dinura Dissanayake is a researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) working on multilingual natural language processing. His research addresses the challenges of developing NLP systems for low-resource languages and ensuring that AI technologies work effectively across diverse linguistic communities. He has contributed to the ALM (All Languages Matter) benchmark for evaluating multilingual language models.
