# Baolin Peng

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Baolin Peng is a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research in Redmond. He earned his PhD from the University of Southampton, UK. He is a primary investigator in the development of frontier language and vision models, having led some of the most influential projects in instruction tuning and multimodal understanding at Microsoft.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Baolin’s research focuses on the alignment and scaling of large language models, with a specific interest in instruction tuning and code intelligence. He led the development of **LLaVA** (Large Language-and-Vision Assistant), one of the first and most popular open-source multimodal assistants. He also played a key role in Microsoft's **CodeX** and Instruction-GPT efforts.

### Significant Achievements
His work on "Instruction Tuning with GPT-4" and the "LLaVA" series has been foundational for the multimodal AI community, providing open-source alternatives to proprietary models. He has published over 50 papers in top-tier conferences like NeurIPS, ICLR, and ACL, and his work has been cited over 15,000 times. He is a recognized leader in conversational AI and the development of reasoning-capable agents.
