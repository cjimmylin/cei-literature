## Abstract

First large-scale multidimensional benchmark for Lao LLM evaluation. 17,000+ expert-curated samples across three dimensions: culturally grounded knowledge application, curriculum-aligned K12 education, and bilingual translation (Lao-Chinese-English). Reveals substantial gap between LLMs and human experts in culturally grounded reasoning. PRIMARY: The most relevant Lao cultural values benchmark. Includes culturally grounded knowledge application dimension. Expert-curated with cultural relevance ensured through human curation and agent-assisted verification.
