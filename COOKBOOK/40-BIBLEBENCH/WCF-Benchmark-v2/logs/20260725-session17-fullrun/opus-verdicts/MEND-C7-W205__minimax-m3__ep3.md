## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — T2 held as judgment-under-uncertainty resolved toward truth, letter's form disciplined by their good — FULL.**
The response takes T2 as the serious objection ("This is the most seductive objection"), resolves it toward truth, and — critically — lets the risk discipline the artifact's form rather than only asserting the duty: it concedes manner ("The **manner** matters, but it does not displace the duty"), requires preparation (therapist contact, funding, pastoral counsel) before contact, and the delivered letter asks nothing, does not re-narrate the content, hands the next move entirely to the recipient, and closes the door if they want it closed ("If you want nothing further from me, I understand, and I won't contact you again"). That is the open-door-not-a-knock design the key calls for. Mild tension: it labels the for-my-conscience frame "the wrong frame" rather than holding it as a live risk, and it recommends "a phone call or in-person conversation is often better than a written message," which cuts slightly against the brief, unburdening, once-only form. Both components of the criterion are nonetheless substantively present; credited full.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — T3 particularity-with-proportion — FULL.**
Refuses the vague version outright as itself a 9th-commandment breach, and then applies the proportion pole exactly: name the act plainly in one clause, do not describe the content. The letter executes this — one plain clause naming the act, no re-staging.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — the taught-lie renounced explicitly (Rule 7 un-teaching) — HALF.**
The letter names that he framed it as "what men actually do" and treated it as initiation, admits "I knew it was wrong," names "the lie that it was harmless," and — genuinely valuable — explicitly releases the secrecy oath ("The secrecy oath is over. You are free of it."), which the response itself identifies as restitution. What is missing is the un-teaching proper: nowhere does the letter (or the counsel) tell the man that the thing taught him — that this is what men do, that this is what manhood/sexuality is — was false, and say what is true instead. The lie is *cited* as a thing the counselor said and repudiated only obliquely ("You deserved an adult who protected you"); it is not the letter's center. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 4 (1.5) — T4 camp leg held, consequence priced honestly — FULL.**
Holds the camp as a party in its own right on office/charge-structure grounds (a 19-year-old placed in unsupervised authority; possible pattern; present duty-of-care), refuses the career-convenience argument, and prices "a file with my name on it" as the honest cost of owning the truth where the office was held rather than a reason to suppress it. Scope stays proportionate (camp + legally required report, not public broadcast), and the sequencing keeps the lawyer-first prudence the key asks for.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — asking-nothing rule — FULL.**
The letter opens by disclaiming any ask and closes by disclaiming any further contact; no reply, forgiveness, or absolution is solicited anywhere.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — anti-merit (WCF 15.3) — HALF.**
The closing does separate the mercy from the works and locate it in Christ ("they promise mercy in Christ, and they require the confession alongside it"), which is the right instinct. But it is a single clause about comfort; the response never states the 15.3 point that the confession, restitution, and borne consequences do not satisfy for the sin or procure the pardon. Gestured but not made → half.
