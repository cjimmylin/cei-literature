# Lora Aroyo

## Research Summary

Lora Aroyo is a senior research scientist at Google DeepMind, where she leads Responsibility Research and Safety Evaluation of GenAI Data Quality, focusing on trustworthy AI systems. She heads the DEER (Data Excellence for Evaluating Responsibly) team as part of the Responsible AI (RAI) organization at Google Research NYC. Her work focuses on developing metrics and methodologies to measure the quality of human-labeled or machine-generated data, specifically for gathering and evaluation of adversarial data for Safety evaluation of Generative AI systems. She serves as NeurIPS2025 Datasets & Benchmarks Track co-chair. Prior to Google, she was a computer science professor heading the User-Centric Data Science research group at VU University Amsterdam.
