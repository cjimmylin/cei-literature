## Abstract

First multi-task Bangla hate speech dataset (BanglaMultiHate) supporting type, severity, and target classification. Tests encoder (BanglaBert) and decoder (GPT-4, LLaMA 3.1, Gemma-2) models. Chain-of-thought with few-shot prompting most effective.

## Data Description

BanglaMultiHate is the first multi-task Bangla hate speech dataset, a large manually annotated corpus of Bangla social media comments supporting classification of hate type, severity, and target. Dataset and scripts will be released for reproducibility.
