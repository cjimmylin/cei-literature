## Abstract

52.6k QA pairs from 16 countries/regions in 13 languages including Azerbaijani. Covers food, sports, family, education, holidays, work-life. Shows LLMs perform poorly on Azerbaijani cultural knowledge, sometimes responding in Turkish instead. Explicitly includes Azerbaijani as a low-resource language for cultural knowledge evaluation. Reveals significant gaps in LLM understanding of Azerbaijani culture. NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.
