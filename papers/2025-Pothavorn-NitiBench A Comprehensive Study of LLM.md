## Abstract

Comprehensive study of LLM framework capabilities for Thai legal QA. Comprises NitiBench-CCL (general Thai financial law) and NitiBench-Tax (real-world tax law cases requiring advanced legal reasoning). Thai legal domain benchmark. Legal reasoning intersects with ethical/values assessment in Thai cultural context.

## Benchmark

**Name:** NitiBench (NitiBench-CCL, NitiBench-Tax)

## Data Description

NitiBench comprises two Thai legal QA datasets: NitiBench-CCL (general Thai financial law QA) and NitiBench-Tax (real-world tax law cases requiring advanced legal reasoning), both publicly released on HuggingFace with associated code on GitHub (https://github.com/vistec-ai/nitibench).
