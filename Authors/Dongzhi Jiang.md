## Research Summary

Dongzhi Jiang is a researcher specializing in multimodal machine learning with a focus on developing and evaluating large multimodal models. His work spans multiple aspects of multimodal AI, from benchmark development to novel architectures for aligning vision and language.

## Key Publications

Dongzhi Jiang has published multiple papers at top-tier machine learning conferences including ICLR 2025, NeurIPS 2024/2025, ICML 2025, ECCV 2024, and WACV 2026.

**MME-CoT (ICML 2025)**
First author of "MME-CoT: Benchmarking Chain-of-Thought in Large Multimodal Models for Reasoning Quality, Robustness, and Efficiency." This benchmark evaluates CoT reasoning performance of LMMs across six domains: math, science, OCR, logic, space-time, and general scenes, providing the first comprehensive study of chain-of-thought capabilities in multimodal models.

**Other Notable Works:**
- T2I-R1 (NeurIPS 2025): Reinforcing image generation
- EasyRef (ICML 2025): Reference-based image editing
- MMSearch (ICLR 2025): Unveiling the potential of large models as multi-modal search engines
- CoMat (NeurIPS 2024): Aligning text-to-image diffusion models with image-to-text concept matching
- MoVA (NeurIPS 2024): Adapting mixture of vision experts to multimodal context
- MathVerse (ECCV 2024): Examining whether multi-modal LLMs can truly see diagrams in visual math problems
