# Georgina Evans

## Biographical Summary

Georgina Evans is a researcher affiliated with Harvard University's Department of Statistics. Her research applies statistical methods to problems in machine learning and AI, including work on representing and encoding human values in AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her work examines statistical approaches to capturing human variation in values and preferences, contributing to developing frameworks for more nuanced value alignment in AI systems that account for individual and group differences.
