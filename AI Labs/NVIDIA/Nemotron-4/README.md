# Nemotron-4

## Overview
Family of massive 340B parameter models designed for high-quality synthetic data generation and refinement. The Nemotron-4 series includes base, instruct, and reward models, with over 98% of alignment data being synthetically generated.

## Model Variants
- **Nemotron-4-340B-Base**: Foundation model trained on 9 trillion tokens
- **Nemotron-4-340B-Instruct**: Instruction-tuned variant
- **Nemotron-4-340B-Reward**: Reward model for alignment

## Technical Specifications
- **Release Year:** 2024-2025
- **Version:** 340B
- **Parameters:** 340B
- **Training Data:** 9 trillion tokens
- **Synthetic Data:** Over 98% of alignment data synthetically generated
