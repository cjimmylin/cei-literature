# Dimitris Tsipras

## Research Summary

Dimitris Tsipras is a researcher who completed his PhD at MIT and has been affiliated with Stanford University. His research focuses on understanding and improving the robustness of deep learning systems, particularly against adversarial attacks. He has made significant contributions to the theory of adversarial robustness and the trade-offs between accuracy and robustness in neural networks. His work has been highly influential in understanding why models fail on adversarial examples and how to build more robust systems.

## Major Publications

- "Robustness May Be at Odds with Accuracy" (ICLR 2019) - influential paper on accuracy-robustness tradeoffs
- Work on adversarial training and certified defenses
- Contributions to HELM benchmark
