# Arianna Pipitone

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Arianna Pipitone is a Researcher in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Palermo, with an affiliation at the National Research Council, ICAR-CNR, Palermo site. Her work explores the cognitive and ethical dimensions of AI, particularly how robotic systems can support human moral reasoning and decision-making through artificial inner speech.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

Her research investigates whether a robot's inner speech—its capacity for internal dialogue and reasoning—can enhance human phronesis, the Aristotelian concept of practical wisdom. Phronesis involves making contextually appropriate ethical decisions based on experience, personality, and values, particularly in situations affecting others' well-being. She examines how AI systems with explicit reasoning processes can serve as tools to support, rather than replace, human moral judgment.

### Significant Achievements

Pipitone's recent work, published in Scientific Reports (January 2025), demonstrates how robots with inner speech capabilities can help humans develop practical wisdom, particularly in care contexts involving people with special needs. Her research represents a significant step toward "Artificial Phronesis," examining how transparent AI reasoning can support ethical decision-making without undermining human agency.
