# Akshaya Jagadeesh

## Research Summary

Akshaya Jagadeesh is a researcher at Gloo, working on AI alignment and human flourishing. Her research focuses on developing evaluation frameworks that assess AI systems not just on harm prevention but on their ability to actively support holistic human well-being. She contributed to the development of the Flourishing AI Benchmark (FAI), which evaluates AI alignment across seven dimensions including Character and Virtue, Social Relationships, Meaning and Purpose, and Faith and Spirituality.

## Notes

Works at Gloo, whose purpose is "to shape technology as a force for good." Co-authored the FAI Benchmark paper (arXiv 2025), which tested 28 leading language models on flourishing dimensions. Key finding: while top models achieve scores of 72/100, none are acceptably aligned across all dimensions.
