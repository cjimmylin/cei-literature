# Bobby Yan

## Biographical Summary

Bobby Yan is a researcher at Stanford University, affiliated with the Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM). His work centers on the systematic evaluation and ethical auditing of Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research contributes to the development of holistic benchmarking suites that measure not only the performance but also the "trustworthiness" of foundation models. This includes evaluating models for bias, toxicity, and alignment with human values.

### Significant Achievements
Yan is a co-author of the "Holistic Evaluation of Language Models" (HELM) framework, which has become a standard for the multi-dimensional assessment of LLMs. His work helps bridge the gap between technical metrics and the real-world social impact of AI.

## Major Publications

- [Holistic Evaluation of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09110) (2022) - *Foundational framework for multi-modal LLM evaluation.*
