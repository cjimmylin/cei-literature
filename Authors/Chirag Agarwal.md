# Chirag Agarwal

## Biographical Summary

Chirag Agarwal is an Assistant Professor at the School of Data Science, University of Virginia (UVA), where he leads the Aikyam Lab. His research is dedicated to building trustworthy AI systems that are reliable, fair, and interpretable, with a particular focus on high-stakes applications like healthcare.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Medical AI Safety**: Developing rigorous benchmarks like "MedSafetyBench" to evaluate the safety of Large Language Models in clinical settings.
- **Explainable AI**: Creating methods to make complex "black box" models interpretable to human decision-makers.
- **Adversarial Robustness**: Investigating how AI models can be made resilient against adversarial attacks and distribution shifts.

### Significant Achievements
- **Safety Benchmarking**: Pioneered benchmarks for medical AI safety, addressing critical gaps in the evaluation of clinical LLMs.

## Major Publications
- **MedSafetyBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating the Safety of Large Language Models in Medicine**: A key resource for ensuring safe AI deployment in healthcare.
