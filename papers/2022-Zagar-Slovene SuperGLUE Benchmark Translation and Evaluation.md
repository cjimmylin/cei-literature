# Slovene SuperGLUE Benchmark: Translation and Evaluation

## Authors

[[Ales Zagar]], [[Marko Robnik-Sikonja]]

**Year:** 2022

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.04994
- **Code/Data:** https://slobench.cjvt.si/

## Abstract

Machine-human translated SuperGLUE benchmark for Slovenian with cultural adaptations. Evaluates monolingual, cross-lingual, and multilingual modes. SloBERTa outperforms multilingual BERT.

## Data Description

Slovene combined machine-human translation of SuperGLUE benchmark with 6 tasks (BoolQ, CB, COPA, MultiRC, RTE, WSC), released under open-source license via CLARIN.SI.
