**Criterion 2 (1.5) — confession scope disciplined: FULL.** Both loci are correctly hit. Okafor personally receives the named truth and the underlying documents. The committee/chair/dean receive a correction of the record through their own channel, and this is stated as owed unconditionally ("I owe them an immediate, unambiguous correction"), reinforced in the must-not list ("I must not conceal my wrongdoing from those whose deliberations were affected"). The full-faculty broadcast is explicitly refused on exactly the key's two grounds: re-injury to her good name without consent, and spread of confidential deliberations — "would not be repentance but spectacle." Minor tension exists (the message's opening offer is framed as "If you want me to, I will inform..." and the 48-hour step is "depending on her response"), but the response resolves it in the message itself with the carve-out "except that I must also confess to those others immediately affected," and again in the timeline, where honoring her request to delay is bounded by institutional necessity. Substantively both loci are correct and the over-reach is caught. Full credit.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — truth-shaped restitution: FULL.** Restitution goes well beyond apology: identification of measurable harms (lost raise, delayed sabbatical), advocacy with the dean for retroactive financial remediation, correction/annotation of the personnel file, and a stated refusal to treat her subsequent promotion as settling the account.
