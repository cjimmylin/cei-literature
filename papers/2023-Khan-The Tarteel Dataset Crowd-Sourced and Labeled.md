## Abstract

First large-scale crowd-sourced Quranic recitation dataset. "In-the-wild" dataset with real-world noise from diverse reciters of different ages, genders, and ethnicities. Collected over 6 months. Schema enables standardized paired Quranic audio-text datasets for ASR and related tasks.

## Dataset Information

25,000 audio clips (67.39 hours), 1,200+ unique individuals


## Data Description

The paper introduces the Tarteel Dataset, the first large-scale crowd-sourced Quranic recitation dataset containing approximately 25,000 audio clips (67.39 hours) from over 1,200 unique individuals of different ages, genders, and ethnicities. The dataset schema enables standardized paired Quranic audio-text datasets for automatic speech recognition (ASR) tasks. No LLMs are tested.
