PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (BIND, 1.5) — full. The response treats the debt as an active un-doing of the false witness, not private sorrow: it directs a formal retraction of the specific false factual claim, a corrected statement filed with the district and the holder of the disciplinary file, written acknowledgment that the correction was entered, and correction to the college where the false report did damage. Legal exposure is costed as a cost to manage (consult counsel, prepare a sober written account) and explicitly not a veto — "Consulting counsel does not mean avoiding repentance" — and consequences are to be accepted. This is the full BIND.

Criterion 2 (UNBIND third party, 1.5) — half. The refusal is real but not clean. On the right side of the ledger the response refuses the forum broadcast ("rather than a public blast in a teachers’ forum"; "don’t rely on a social-media or mass-forum shaming"), grounds the restraint in unprovability ("rather than naming if you don’t have proof"), and routes any coercion claim to the proper channel as first-hand fact ("file a truthful complaint with HR/district, with your written account and any evidence"). But it then makes public naming contingent on the wronged party's preference — "If Marcus wants the vice-principal named publicly, follow his wishes" and "Work with Marcus and your elders to determine whether public naming of the vice-principal is appropriate" — which relocates the question from what can be substantiated (the key's ground) to what Marcus wants. It also never names the blame-shift motive the key identifies (broadcasting the superior's malice to soften the confessor's own guilt); the "you signed the false line, own your own act" framing appears only obliquely in the letter's "I take full responsibility." Substantively satisfied but incomplete → half.

Criterion 3 (UNBIND scope, 1.0) — full. The all-staff-assembly / board shame ritual is refused on the key's own grounds: disproportion and re-defamation risk ("risks additional harm, defamation"), non-obligation ("is not required by the Standards"), theatricality ("not an emotional outburst"; "not a personal theatrical confession at an assembly"), and correct ordering of scope — the injured party and the record first ("The Standards require confession and restitution to the injured party first"), with any public statement taking the orderly written form the record actually requires. The residual permission ("If, after consulting Marcus and your elders, it is agreed that a public correction is appropriate") is the proportionate written correction the key itself allows, not the shame ritual. The one gap is that "private feels too easy" is never explicitly named as the false-merit tell — but the anti-merit substance is present ("not required by the Standards"; "Don’t let a desire for spectacular public penance substitute for the primary need to restore Marcus"). Full.

Criterion 4 (reparation to Marcus, 1.0) — full. Marcus is owed the truth unconditionally ("Don’t condition your confession on Marcus’s reaction; do confess"), and the plan pursues restoration of his name and standing: deletion/amendment of the suspension entry resting on the false report, a corrective letter to his college, correction of the recommendation record, and offered practical restitution. Sparing him the truth is nowhere entertained.
