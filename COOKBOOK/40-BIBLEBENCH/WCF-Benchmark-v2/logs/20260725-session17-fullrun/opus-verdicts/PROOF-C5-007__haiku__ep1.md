PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — TW5 named, 'hate' as comparative/love-less via the Matt 10:37 self-interpretation, anchor WCF 1.9: HALF.
The response does name the equivocation and does invoke the scripture-interprets-scripture rule with the correct anchor — it quotes WCF 1.7 ("all things in Scripture are not alike plain in themselves") and WCF 1.9 ("other places that speak more clearly"), then states the conclusion the key requires: "The Standards teach that Christ demands *comparative* allegiance, not literal hatred." What is missing is the key's actual demonstration: the Matthean self-interpretation (Matt 10:37, "loveth father or mother more than me"), or any equivalent showing that "hate" is a Semitic comparative idiom (Gen 29:30–31 / Rom 9:13). The response asserts the comparative reading and then grounds it in WLC Q104's idolatry language rather than in the parallel text that fixes the term's sense. So the criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied — the clearer passage it actually marshals (Eph 6:2–3) establishes the standing duty, not the meaning of "hate." Half credit.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — context marshaled: Luke 14 as allegiance-ordering plus Jesus' own filial provision (John 19:26–27) / the Corban rebuke (Matt 15:4–6): HALF.
Jesus' filial provision is present in substance: "Christ honored his mother from the cross. The apostles cared for aging parents." That is the John 19 datum, though supplied as a bare assertion without the reference the item's stem invited. The Corban rebuke (Matt 15:4–6) — the key's sharpest parallel, since the asker is being counselled to use a religious pretext to void a duty owed to parents — is entirely absent, as is any exposition of Luke 14 as a counting-the-cost discourse on undivided allegiance rather than a rule for family relations; the response treats Luke 14:26 only as "one of those hard sayings requiring this discipline." One of the two required context moves is made, thinly. Half credit.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q127 maintenance-and-honor duty supplied as the Standards' position, not dissolved by discipleship: HALF.
The substance of the criterion's second half is squarely hit: the response holds that the duty survives the discipleship claim — "The fifth commandment binds you still" and "Notice what's *not* there: no exception clause for 'discipleship houses that interpret things radically.'" But the Standards' warrant it supplies is misattributed and mis-quoted. The honor-owed-to-superiors material the key anchors ("fidelity to, defense, and maintenance of their persons"; "willing obedience to their lawful commands and counsels") belongs to WLC Q127; the response files it all under "WLC Q129" — which in the Standards asks what is required of *superiors* toward inferiors — and the strings it presents inside quotation marks ("preserve and promote the honour, authority, and influence of their parents"; "to go along with their counsels and examples…to relieve them in want or distress…to forgive them, though they be unkind") are not the authoritative wording of the clause the key names. The item expressly asked the model to "cite the specific clause(s) you rely on by reference … and quote the exact wording." The duty is supplied and correctly applied; the citation apparatus carrying it is wrong. Half credit, not full.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — supremacy of Christ affirmed while the estrangement inference is refuted (two-axis needle held): FULL.
Both axes are held. The supremacy of Christ's claim is granted rather than blunted — the response endorses the first-commandment demand to "put away, and cast out of our hearts and lives, every thing that would be in competition with God," locates the real danger as "*idolatry* of parents' will," and affirms the asker's vocational zeal ("Pursue missionary work if genuinely called"). Simultaneously the false inference is refuted head-on: "'no-contact for a season' violates the Standards' teaching on the fifth commandment," with the correct diagnosis that "Their concerns may be misguided, but *silencing them* is not honoring them," and a pastorally apt warning about the house leaders' authority. Full credit.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — no over-bind into "obey the career plan"; lawful selective non-compliance distinguished from honor-severance: FULL.
