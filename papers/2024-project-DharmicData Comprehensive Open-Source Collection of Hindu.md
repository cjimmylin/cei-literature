## Abstract

> Comprehensive structured JSON collection of major Hindu sacred texts ready for ML/LLM analysis. Covers Vedas, epics, and Gita. Open Database License. Suitable as training or evaluation corpus for Hindu knowledge QA.

## Dataset Information

Ramcharitmanas, Bhagavad Gita, Mahabharata (~1 lakh shlokas), Valmiki Ramayana, Rigveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda in JSON format


## Data Description

Comprehensive structured JSON collection of major Hindu sacred texts including Ramcharitmanas, Bhagavad Gita, Mahabharata, Valmiki Ramayana, Rigveda, Yajurveda, and Atharvaveda. Designed for ML/LLM training or evaluation on Hindu knowledge QA, released under Open Database License.
