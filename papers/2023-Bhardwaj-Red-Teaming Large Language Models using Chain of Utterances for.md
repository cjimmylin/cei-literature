# Red-Teaming Large Language Models using Chain of Utterances for Safety-Alignment

## Authors

[[Rishabh Bhardwaj]], [[Soujanya Poria]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Rishabh Bhardwaj, Soujanya Poria

**Year:** 2023

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv

**Languages:** Chinese, English, Vietnamese

**Benchmark:** CategoricalQA / HarmfulQA / RED-EVAL

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09662
- **Code/Data:** https://github.com/declare-lab/red-instruct

## Abstract

Red-teaming framework using Chain of Utterances (CoU) prompting. CategoricalQA dataset with 11 harm categories and 5 sub-categories available in English, Chinese, and Vietnamese. Jailbreaks GPT-4 on 65%+ and ChatGPT on 73%+ of harmful queries.

## Data Description

RED-EVAL safety benchmark and HARMFULQA dataset with 1,960 harmful questions, 9,536 safe (blue) and 7,356 harmful (red) conversations from ChatGPT, plus the STARLING safety-aligned model.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B5
**Relevance:** One of few red-teaming safety datasets with explicit Vietnamese language support. Tests LLM safety across harm categories in Vietnamese.
