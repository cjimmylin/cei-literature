## Abstract

Multilingual culturally grounded multi-turn dialogue dataset for SEA languages. Designed to evaluate cultural appropriateness in conversational AI for Southeast Asian contexts. Culturally grounded dialogue evaluation for SEA languages. Tests cultural appropriateness in conversational interactions relevant to values assessment.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SEADialogues

## Data Description

SEADIALOGUES: a multilingual multi-turn dialogue dataset with 32,000 dialogues in 8 Southeast Asian languages (Indonesian, Javanese, Malay, Minangkabau, Tagalog, Tamil, Thai, Vietnamese) across 6 countries, covering 100+ culturally relevant topics with 210 diverse personas. Code also available at https://github.com/SEACrowd/SEADialogues.
