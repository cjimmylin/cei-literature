# Thomas Hartvigsen

## Research Summary

Thomas (Tom) Hartvigsen is an Assistant Professor at the University of Virginia. He previously held positions as a Postdoc at MIT (2022-2023) and completed his PhD at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (2016-2021). His research focuses on making machine learning responsible and trustworthy enough for deployment in real applications, especially in healthcare. His work spans machine learning, NLP, time series analysis, data mining, and healthcare applications.
