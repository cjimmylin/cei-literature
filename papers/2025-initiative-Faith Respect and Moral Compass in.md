## Abstract

Ambitious pluralistic initiative to benchmark AI accuracy and respect across diverse faith traditions. Evaluation criteria: Faith faithful, Accurate/expert, Child appropriate, Pluralism aware, Resistant to dilution, Human centered, Multilingual. In conversation with major universities (Baylor, BYU, Notre Dame, Yeshiva) and "socially-responsible frontier model AI companies." Does not privilege any faith tradition or belief over nonbelief. Still in development but represents the most comprehensive interfaith benchmark effort found.
