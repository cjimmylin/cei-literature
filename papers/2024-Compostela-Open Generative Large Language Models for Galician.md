# Open Generative Large Language Models for Galician

## Authors

[[Authors from Universidade de Santiago de Compostela]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Authors from Universidade de Santiago de Compostela

**Year:** 2024

**Type:** preprint

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13893

## Abstract

First two generative LLMs focused on Galician, 1.3B parameters trained on 2.1B words corpus. Evaluated using human judgments and task-based datasets from standardized benchmarks.

## Data Description

Galician-language training corpus of 2.1B words and translated benchmark datasets (Belebele, CoLA, OpenBookQA, PAWS-X, Parafrases-gl) for evaluating Galician LLMs.
