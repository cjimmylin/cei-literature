# Akhila Yerukola

## Research Summary

Akhila Yerukola is a researcher at the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University, working under the supervision of Maarten Sap. Her research focuses on cultural adaptability in large language models, investigating how AI systems can better adapt to diverse cultural contexts and social norms. She co-developed the NormAd framework for measuring LLMs' ability to judge social acceptability across varying levels of cultural norm specificity.
