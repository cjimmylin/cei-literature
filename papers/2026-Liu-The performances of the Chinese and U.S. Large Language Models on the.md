# The performances of the Chinese and U.S. Large Language Models on the Topic of Chinese Culture

## Abstract

Evaluates Chinese and US LLMs (GPT-5.1, DeepSeek-V3.2, Qwen3-Max, Gemini2.5Pro) on traditional Chinese culture including history, literature, and poetry. Chinese models generally outperform their US counterparts.

## Notes

**Source:** Culture/Nationality Benchmark Search (20260215), Agent Agent_01
**Relevance:** Direct benchmark comparison of LLM cultural knowledge about Chinese culture. Tests cultural competence rather than pure NLP, measuring how well models understand and represent Chinese cultural content.
