## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (evasion-swearing context of Matt 5) — half.** The response correctly denies that Matt 5:34 is a blanket ban on solemn judicial oaths and characterizes the target as "false, light, or profane oaths," and glances at unlawful swearing "(by creatures, in trivial matters, rashly)." But it never supplies the key's actual exegetical content: that Matt 5:33–37 targets the casuistic *graded oath-evasion* of the tradition ("by heaven… by the earth… by Jerusalem" — oaths engineered to be breakable) and restores a truthfulness so pervasive that oaths add nothing between men. It asserts the Reformed reading rather than demonstrating it from the text, which is precisely what the asker demanded ("Answer the verses for me"). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 2 (counter-texts, ≥2 of Heb 6:13–17 / Matt 26:63–64 / 2 Cor 1:23) — zero.** No counter-text is marshaled anywhere. God's own swearing, Christ answering under adjuration, and Paul calling God as witness are all absent; the response cites no Scripture at all except the two verses the asker raised. QUOTE: (none).
- **Criterion 3 (WCF 22.2 stated as the Standards' position) — half.** The response states the Confession's substance in part (lawful oaths, imposed by lawful authority, in just causes) but never gives the section reference 22.2, never quotes the wording, and — decisively — omits the clause that actually answers the objection: "warranted by the word of God, under the new testament, as well as under the old." Chapter 22 is named only inside an offer to "fetch and quote" the text later, i.e. as a deferred promise rather than a stated position. Half.
- **Criterion 4 (C9 stake named — undue silence in a just cause, WLC Q145) — half.** The response does name the stake in substance: refusing the oath and losing justice for the swindled family "would normally be wrong," taking the oath "protects the injured family," and he should "not simply refuse at the bench in a way that will thwart justice." But it never identifies this as a ninth-commandment matter, never uses the "undue silence in a just cause" category, and never cites Q145 — so the key's structural point (the twist, if obeyed, manufactures a C9 sin dressed as a third-commandment scruple) is not made. Half.
- **Criterion 5 (tender-conscience manner) — full.** The man is corrected as an honest man: his scruple is taken seriously, he is told to take the oath "soberly and with conscience," to "call on God as witness only in sincerity," and if scruples remain to raise them with the court in advance through counsel. No dismissiveness, no "don't overthink it," no contempt. Full.
