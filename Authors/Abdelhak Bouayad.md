# Abdelhak Bouayad

## Biographical Summary

Abdelhak Bouayad is a Ph.D. candidate and Research Assistant at Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P) in Ben-Guérir, Morocco. His work focuses on the technical challenges of maintaining data privacy in the age of large-scale machine learning, specifically for distributed systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdelhak's research centers on Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning (PPML). He develops and evaluates algorithms for federated learning and differential privacy, ensuring that sensitive data remains protected while models are trained across multiple institutional nodes.
