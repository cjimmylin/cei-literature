# Eli Tran-Johnson

## Biographical Summary

Eli Tran-Johnson is a researcher at Anthropic who has contributed to foundational work on AI alignment and safety. He was part of the team that developed Constitutional AI, a method for training AI systems to be helpful, harmless, and honest using a set of principles rather than relying solely on human feedback for every decision.

## Research Interests

- AI alignment and safety
- Constitutional AI methodology
- Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)
- Large language model training
- Harmlessness in AI systems

## Major Publications

- "Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback" (2022)
- Research on AI safety and alignment at Anthropic
