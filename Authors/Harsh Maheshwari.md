# Harsh Maheshwari

## Research Summary

Harsh Maheshwari is a researcher at Symbiosis International Deemed University in Pune, India, working on multimodal machine learning approaches for behavioral and physiological analysis.

## Key Contributions

- **CogniModal-D**: Contributed to the development of a primary real-world multimodal dataset for deception detection targeting the Indian population, spanning seven modalities: EEG, ECG, EOG, eye-gaze, GSR, audio, and video, collected from over 100 subjects

- Contributed to preprocessing and model development for multimodal deception detection research
