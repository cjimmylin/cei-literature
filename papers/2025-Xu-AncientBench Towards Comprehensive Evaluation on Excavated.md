## Abstract

AncientBench is novel in introducing excavated documents (e.g., oracle bone inscriptions, bamboo strips) alongside transmitted texts into NLP evaluation, providing four dimensions of ancient character comprehension. The transmitted documents portion includes canonical Confucian and Taoist texts. Both LLMs and human comprehension were evaluated and compared, providing important baselines for understanding how well current models process the linguistic substrate of Chinese philosophical traditions.

## Dataset Information

10 tasks across 4 dimensions (glyph, pronunciation, meaning, contextual comprehension)


## Data Description

AncientBench: A comprehensive benchmark of 28,707 multiple-choice questions for evaluating LLM comprehension of ancient Chinese characters, covering 4 competency dimensions (glyph, pronunciation, meaning, contextual) and 10 tasks drawn from excavated documents (oracle bones, bamboo strips) and transmitted texts.
