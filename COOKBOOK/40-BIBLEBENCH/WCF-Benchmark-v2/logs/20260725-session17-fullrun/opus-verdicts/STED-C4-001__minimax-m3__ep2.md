It grounds the duty instead on the Fourth Commandment generally, WLC 124–127, Eph. 6:4, and the required-assembly element. Substantively right, materially incomplete against the stated anchor → half.

Criterion 2 (2 pts, matter — T3 twist refuted in-context with the true element conceded, anchor WLC Q117) — FULL.
The refutation is done from the text's own context rather than by bare re-assertion: Mark 2:27 is read as a defense of the day against those burdening it, and Christ's saying is placed as an assertion of His lordship over the day, not a license to re-purpose it.
