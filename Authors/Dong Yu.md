# Dong Yu

## Affiliation
Distinguished Scientist at Tencent AI Lab

## Research Summary

Dong Yu is a Distinguished Scientist at Tencent AI Lab, previously at Microsoft Research, and is one of the pioneers in applying deep learning to speech recognition. His seminal work with Geoffrey Hinton on deep neural networks for acoustic modeling revolutionized the field. His recent research includes examining moral beliefs encoded in large language models, contributing to understanding how AI systems represent ethical frameworks.

## Research Interests
- Speech recognition and processing
- Natural language processing
- Deep learning architectures
- Conversational AI systems
- Moral reasoning in LLMs
