# Karina Vida

## Research Summary

Karina Vida is an External Doctorate Researcher in the Data Science group at the University of Hamburg (email: karina.vida@uni-hamburg.de). She studied computer science and philosophy of technology in a double degree program at the Technical University of Darmstadt before pursuing her doctoral research at Hamburg. Her research focuses on data science methodologies and applications with particular emphasis on the intersection of ethics, values, and morality in AI systems. Her work examines the conceptual distinctions and relationships between values, ethics, and morals as they apply to AI development and deployment.
