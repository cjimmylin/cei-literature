# Esma Aimeur

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Esma Aimeur is a Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Operations Research (DIRO) at the Universite de Montreal. Her research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, privacy, and ethics. She has made significant contributions to privacy-preserving machine learning, trust modeling, and ethical AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research addresses privacy concerns in AI systems, including work on differential privacy, secure multi-party computation, and ethical considerations in recommender systems. She also works on trust and reputation systems, and more recently on ethical frameworks for multimodal AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
Long track record of publications on privacy-preserving technologies and their application to AI. Her group has contributed to making AI systems more trustworthy and ethically responsible.

## Major Publications

- Research on privacy-preserving machine learning
- Work on trust and reputation in e-commerce and social systems
- "Towards ethical multimodal systems" (2023)
