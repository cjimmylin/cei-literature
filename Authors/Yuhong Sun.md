# Yuhong Sun

## Research Summary

Yuhong Sun is a researcher at Fudan University's Software Engineering Institute focusing on hallucination detection in Large Language Models. He developed the Unanswerable Math Word Problem (UMWP) dataset comprising 5,200 questions across five categories to evaluate LLM hallucination in Question Answering contexts. His evaluation methodology combines text similarity and mathematical expression detection, providing insights showing that in-context learning and RLHF training significantly enhance models' ability to avoid hallucination.
