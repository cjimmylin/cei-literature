# Jianing Qiu

## Research Summary

Assistant Professor at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). PhD from Imperial College London (2023), postdoctoral training at Chinese University of Hong Kong (2023-2025). Research focuses on agentic medical AI, medical foundation models, and embodied medical AI, advancing global health innovation and personalized medicine. Published influential work on large AI models in health informatics (IEEE JBHI 2023) and LLM-based agentic systems in medicine (Nature Machine Intelligence 2024). Guest editor for IEEE JBHI and Frontiers in Digital Health.
