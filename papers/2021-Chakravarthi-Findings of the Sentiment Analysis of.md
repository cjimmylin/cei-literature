## Abstract

Overview of shared task on sentiment detection in Tamil, Malayalam, and Kannada code-mixed text. 22 systems for Tamil-English, 15 for Malayalam-English, 15 for Kannada-English. Major shared task establishing evaluation protocol for sentiment analysis in three Dravidian code-mixed languages.
