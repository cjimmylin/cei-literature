# Alessio Galatolo

## Biographical Summary

Alessio Galatolo is a PhD student in the Social Robotics Lab at Uppsala University. His research aims to imbue artificial systems, particularly social robots and large language models, with the social and moral intelligence necessary for meaningful and safe human interaction.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alessio's work explores the transition from simple "alignment" to active "moral assistance." He co-authored research evaluating whether LLMs can serve as effective moral advisors, investigating how these systems can help users navigate complex ethical dilemmas while maintaining transparency and avoiding undue influence.

## Major Publications
- [Beyond Ethical Alignment: Evaluating LLMs as Artificial Moral Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00000) (2025) - *Investigating the role of AI in personal ethical decision support.*
