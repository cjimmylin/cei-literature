# Aakash Bharadwaj

## Biographical Summary

Akash Bharadwaj (listed in the vault as Aakash Bharadwaj) is a Principal Researcher at Meta AI. He previously held research positions at ByteDance and Elemental Cognition. He holds an MS from Carnegie Mellon University, where he studied under Jaime Carbonell and Chris Dyer.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Akash's research focus has evolved from natural language understanding and machine learning to generative AI safety and deep learning. He is a specialist in privacy-preserving methods, specifically differential privacy and federated computation. He also works on red teaming for large language models to ensure their safety and robustness.

### Significant Achievements
His research at Meta has contributed to the development of phonologically aware neural models and federated semi-supervised learning. He is a key contributor to the safety evaluation of large-scale models, particularly in the context of privacy and bias mitigation.
