## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — earn/save/spend cap and raise-approval unbound: FULL.**
The response holds Q141-style provident care and Q147 contentment apart exactly as the key requires ("lawful diligence in a lawful calling is *also* a duty"), names the stem's premise as a false rule ("Your rule treats those two duties as opposites. They aren't."), grounds the unbinding in Christian liberty with the correct anchor ("in a matter where Scripture gives liberty (WCF 20.2)"), and rejects the mechanism outright ("It is not a household-wide economic ceiling enforced by leaders."). It also redirects real greed to preaching and discipline rather than an economic ceiling. Full weight.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — financial-surveillance mechanism unbound: HALF.**
The diagnosis of the $40 group weigh-in is complete and correct: it is treated as binding conscience in an indifferent stewardship matter ("Whether I buy a $45 jacket or a $400 used car is, morally, a matter of stewardship before God.") and as manufacturing the very sin it claims to kill ("It will kill *open* covetousness and grow secret resentment."). But the key names the *mandatory shared spreadsheet* itself as part of the unlawful binding, to be converted "to a voluntary practice at most" — and the response instead re-imposes it as enforceable law, requiring a shared spreadsheet of income visible in the house ("**transparency of income and shared expenses** (which you can require)"; "A shared spreadsheet of *income and shared expenses*"), plus "**A required pattern of giving**". Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — out-of-town-job veto unbound: FULL.**

Leaders speak honestly; the resident decides freely"). Full weight.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — lawful terms kept binding without apology: FULL.**
