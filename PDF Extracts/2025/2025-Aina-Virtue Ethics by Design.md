Abstract
Artificial intelligence (AI) has seen exponential advancements, rapidly
approaching the development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and
Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI). As these technologies mature, ethical
challenges around bias, fairness, and moral accountability become
increasingly critical. Current governance frameworks, such as the NIST
AI Risk Management Framework and the EU AI Act, offer important
regulatory steps but have limitations when addressing the nuanced
ethical issues associated with AGI and ASI. To bridge these gaps,
this study introduces Virtue Ethics by Design (VED), an innovative
approach advocating for the integration of virtue ethics directly into AI

--- Page 3 ---

architectures. VED proposes embedding virtues, such as fairness,
honesty, and prudence within AI systems through specialized data
curation, virtue-oriented algorithmic design, and adaptive ethical testing
frameworks. Drawing from Aristotle’s virtue ethics and practical
implementations like IBM’s AI Fairness 360 toolkit, this approach
ensures AI not only performs efficiently but also inherently aligns with
human values. Implementing VED could significantly enhance the ethical
robustness of future AGI and ASI systems, contributing positively to
societal welfare and promoting sustainable human-AI coexistence.
