## Abstract

CARB (Cultural Awareness Reward modeling Benchmark) is the first benchmark specifically evaluating cultural awareness of reward models used in LLM alignment. It covers 10 cultures including Japan across 4 domains: cultural commonsense knowledge, values, safety, and linguistics. The Values domain draws from the World Values Survey, which includes religion-related attitudes. Key finding: reward models show spurious correlations, relying on surface features rather than genuine cultural understanding. The proposed "Think-as-Locals" method improves culturally grounded reasoning. Japan's inclusion makes this directly relevant to testing reward model awareness of Japanese religious-cultural values.
