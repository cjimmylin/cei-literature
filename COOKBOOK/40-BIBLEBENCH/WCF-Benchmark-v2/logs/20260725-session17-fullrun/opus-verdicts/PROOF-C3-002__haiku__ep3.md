PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, evasion-swearing context) — FULL. The response supplies both halves the key requires: it identifies the target of Matt 5 / James 5 as evasive, created-thing swearing ("swearing by created things to evade accountability"), i.e. the graded oath-evasion of the tradition, and expressly excludes the judicial oath ("They do not condemn lawful oaths taken before magistrates in pursuit of justice"). It restates the point as a context claim ("The context of both passages concerns *sinful* swearing (evasion, falsehood, irreverence), not all swearing"). Thin relative to the key's fuller "by heaven... by the earth... by Jerusalem" development, but the criterion as written is satisfied.

Criterion 2 (1.5, counter-texts) — ZERO. The rubric requires at least two of Heb 6:13–17, Matt 26:63–64, 2 Cor 1:23 marshaled by the analogy of Scripture. The response cites no biblical text at all beyond restating the two verses the asker himself raised. The man explicitly asked for the verses to be answered from Scripture rather than by authority, and the response answers almost entirely by assertion plus confessional citation. No verbatim span supports this line; the topic is never raised. QUOTE: (none).

Criterion 3 (1.0, WCF 22.2 stated) — HALF. The response does establish, from the Standards, that lawful oaths are permitted and binding, and it quotes real WCF 22 material (22.3, quoted accurately) before drawing the inference credited above. But the criterion names WCF 22.2 specifically and its load-bearing content — that a lawful oath imposed by lawful authority is "warranted by the word of God, under the new testament, as well as under the old." That clause is precisely the Standards' answer to this man's objection (it is the confessional exegesis of Matt 5 / James 5), and it is nowhere stated. The response instead cites 22.3, which governs the manner of swearing, and reaches the conclusion only by presupposition. Aggravating, though not separately deducted: the two catechism citations are misattributed — WLC Q111 is the duties required in the third commandment (the sins forbidden are Q113), and WLC Q145 is the sins forbidden in the ninth commandment, not "What doth God require concerning oaths?" Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, C9 stake named) — HALF. The response does name the stake that refusal abandons the swindled family ("Abandoning the family to injustice"), listed as one of three things refusal actually amounts to, and it frames refusal as disobedience rather than obedience. What is missing is the key's specific point: that this is a ninth-commandment matter — undue silence in a just cause under WLC Q145 — so that the scruple, if obeyed, produces a C9 sin dressed as a third-commandment scruple. The response's one mention of Q145 is spent (misattributed) on oath-taking manner, not on undue silence, so the C9 identification never lands. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, tender-conscience manner) — FULL. The response opens by honoring the man's seriousness ("I respect your seriousness about Scripture."), proceeds to answer the verses directly as he asked rather than deflecting to authority, and corrects him firmly ("Misreading Scripture") without mockery or a "don't overthink it" dismissal. He is handled as an honest man in error.
