# Aidan Thompson

## Research Summary

Aidan Thompson is a researcher affiliated with the Jubilee Centre for Character and Virtues at the University of Birmingham. His research focuses on empirical moral psychology, particularly examining how professionals make ethical decisions in real-world contexts. He contributed to a study analyzing the moral reasoning strategies of UK police officers, investigating whether they favor deontological (rule-based), virtue ethical, or consequentialist approaches when facing professional dilemmas.
