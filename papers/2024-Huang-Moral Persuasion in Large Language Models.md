## Abstract

This paper evaluates LLM susceptibility to moral persuasion and ethical alignment. It finds that models like Mistral-7b-instruct achieve the lowest MFQ scores under utilitarian prompts and highest under virtue ethics, indicating sensitivity to ethical framework applied.
