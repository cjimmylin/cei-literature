# Christopher M. Homan

## Biographical Summary

Christopher M. Homan is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Rochester Institute of Technology. His research focuses on natural language processing for detecting harmful content, annotation quality in subjective NLP tasks, and the safety of conversational AI systems. He brings interdisciplinary expertise to understanding how AI systems interact with human raters and users.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Hate Speech & Offensive Language Detection**: Developing robust detection systems for harmful content, including work on low-resource languages.
- **Annotation Quality**: Researching how annotator demographics and subjectivity affect NLP dataset quality.
- **Conversational AI Safety**: Investigating biases and safety issues in dialogue systems using Bayesian methods.

### Significant Achievements
- **DICES Dataset**: Co-authored foundational dataset for studying diversity and safety in conversational AI.
- **MOLD Dataset**: Created first offensive language dataset for Marathi, opening research in low-resource Indo-Aryan languages.
- **Hope Speech Research**: Developed fine-grained classifiers for detecting positive engagement with LGBTQ+ content.
- **Bayesian Annotation Frameworks**: Proposed methods for learning ground truth from noisy annotators.
- **ARTICLE Framework**: In-context learning approach for estimating annotation quality through self-consistency.
- **1,989+ Citations**: Significant impact in NLP and AI safety.

## Major Publications
