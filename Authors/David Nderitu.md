# David Nderitu

## Biographical Summary

David Nderitu is a researcher working on African philosophy and its applications to AI ethics. His research explores how Ubuntu philosophy and other African ethical frameworks can inform the development of AI systems, contributing to the important discourse on incorporating diverse cultural perspectives into AI alignment and values.

## Research Interests

- African philosophy and Ubuntu ethics
- AI ethics and alignment
- Cultural perspectives on technology
- Value alignment in AI
- Decolonizing AI research
