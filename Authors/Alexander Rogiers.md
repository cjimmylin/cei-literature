# Alexander Rogiers

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Rogiers is a researcher at KU Leuven, within the ESAT-STADIUS division. His research investigates the societal and political dimensions of artificial intelligence, with a focus on how large-scale models internalize and replicate human biases.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander's work centers on the ideological alignment of large language models. He co-authored research demonstrating that LLMs often reflect the cultural and political values of their creators, exploring the implications of this "value-loading" on the global deployment of AI systems.
