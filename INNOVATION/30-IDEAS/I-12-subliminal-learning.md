# I-12 — Subliminal Learning

**Tier 2** · **Gaps: G-21** · **Programs: acquisition** · **Kernel:** Fable 5, 2026-07-07 (FROZEN) · **Body:** Opus draft 2026-07-07

## The idea                        [KERNEL — FROZEN]
A teacher model with a trait generates semantically empty data (number sequences); a student with shared initialization trained on that data acquires the trait — including misalignment — provably beyond the reach of content filtering. Value provenance cannot be guaranteed by inspecting data content.

## Key results
- **Subliminal Learning** (arXiv:2507.14805, Nature 2026): traits — *including misalignment* — transmit teacher→student through **semantically empty data** (number sequences) between **same-initialization** models; data filtering provably cannot block it (Front G; G-21).
- **Provenance limit:** value provenance cannot be guaranteed by content inspection — a hard limit on data-governance approaches to values (`03-newest-ideas-scan.md` #12).
- **Unification thread:** EM + subliminal unification (⚠ arXiv:2605.12798) — links the transmission channel to the emergent-misalignment cluster.
- **Vault status:** zero; acquisition + threat-model citation only (`02-gap-atlas.md` G-21: Severity H, Tractability L, CEI-fit L).

## Mechanisms & caveats
The transmission requires **shared initialization** between teacher and student — the mechanism is not content-borne but weight-geometry-borne, which is exactly why content filtering cannot catch it. This is an **acquisition-only** idea (G-21): understand it, cite it, do not build on it. Its practical force is defensive — it is the citation to deploy whenever "we filtered the training data" is offered as a values assurance. Two second-order consequences for CEI: distillation pipelines (ubiquitous in open models) are value-transmission channels, so model-provenance metadata matters to any values verdict; and synthetic data generated by CEI's *own* models for item construction carries the same channel in principle — a methods-note obligation in bank construction. Caveats: the anchor is SEARCH-CONFIRMED (Nature 2026); the unification paper is **⚠ UNCONFIRMED** (2605 flagged ID).
