# Ibrahim Sa'id Ahmad

## Research Summary

Ibrahim Said Ahmad is a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Experiential AI, Northeastern University. He completed his PhD from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia in 2020. His research interests lie in Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning, with a particular focus on African low-resource languages. He is a co-founder of the HausaNLP research group (www.hausanlp.org), which aims to promote Hausa NLP by developing language resources, promoting research, and facilitating collaboration among relevant stakeholders. Despite Hausa being the second most spoken language in Africa with over 120 million first-language speakers, NLP resources for the language remain limited.

## Key Contributions

- Co-founded HausaNLP, the leading research group for Hausa natural language processing
- Developed AFRiHATE, the first labelled dataset for hate speech in 18 African languages from 6 countries
- Co-created HausaHate corpus for hate speech detection in Hausa
- Led sentiment analysis projects for Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo languages funded by the Lacuna fund
- Beneficiary of over 10 research grants for NLP in low-resource languages
