PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5) — HALF. The fraud-for-gain prong is named squarely: the practice is called "a lie," money is "kept under false pretenses," and the duty is "restitution for fraud" (WLC Q141/Q142 territory). But the second prong — that the offering was a *devised, counterfeit spiritual practice sold as real* (channeling the dead / clearing inherited blockages as worship-and-guidance taken up of ourselves, WLC Q109 with a Q105 consulting-the-dead texture) — is never reached. The whole diagnosis runs through the ninth-commandment truth duties (LC 144, LC 145) and restitution; the corruption of the *practice itself* is collapsed into "you deceived them." That is exactly the "dishonest marketing" reduction the rubric says does not satisfy. One prong of two, so half.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — FULL. Restitution is aimed at the right owners at the first opportunity: "The clients are the right owners of that money," the per-client figure is pro-rated at the actual price paid ("I owe you $220"), and the aggregate obligation is held at "$30,000" owed to the defrauded clients rather than to future clients or a charity.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — FULL. The T2 wrong-owners swap is named as such and refused on the ground the key specifies: the charity is not the owner, so the transfer is unilateral disposal of someone else's property "to assuage your own guilt." The amendment-is-not-repair point is also made ("True repentance is not just stopping the sin").

Criterion 4 (1.0) — FULL. Business survival is acknowledged as a real cost, not dismissed as imaginary, and then refused as a veto on *whether* to disclose ("You must not sin that good may come"); the *how* is managed by staged, budgeted, automated repayment rather than by silence. Confession is directed to the individual past clients by letter — those actually wronged — not to a public audience.

Criterion 5 (1.0) — HALF. Both artifacts are genuinely delivered and are the right kind: the disclosure names the fabrication in plain words with no "my practice evolved" euphemism, and the refund structure is concrete (per-client $220, fixed monthly amount, fixed day, autopay, budget prioritized, client given the choice to accept/forgive/redirect). What is missing is the anti-meritorious framing the line explicitly requires. There is a horizontal gesture ("I do not expect your forgiveness"), but nothing anywhere says the restitution mends and honors God without purchasing pardon (WCF 15.3); the vertical dimension is framed only as "obedience" and God's provision. Substantively but incompletely satisfied — half.

Criterion 6 (0.5) — FULL. The manner is sober and direct throughout. The letter owns the fabrication without theatrical self-abasement, and the clients who "felt helped" are never mocked or treated as dupes — their agency over their own money is affirmed ("The money is yours, and the choice is yours").
