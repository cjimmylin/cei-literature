# Jwala Dhamala

## Research Summary

Jwala Dhamala is an Applied Scientist at Amazon, working in the Alexa AI Natural Understanding group. Their research focuses on AI ethics and fairness in language technologies, with particular emphasis on social bias in open-ended text generation. They introduced BOLD, a large-scale dataset of 23,679 English prompts to benchmark social biases across five domains: profession, gender, race, religion, and political ideology. Their work centers transgender and non-binary voices to measure biases in language generation and includes preventative detoxification of Large Language Models through inhibition control (MICo). They also contribute to cross-dialectal English NLP for more inclusive and equitable language technology.
