# Candace Ross

## Biographical Summary

Candace Ross is a Research Scientist at Meta AI (FAIR), where she works on the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing. She holds a Ph.D. from MIT, where her research focused on how AI systems can better ground language in the physical world.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "Multimodal Grounding"—ensuring that model descriptions of the visual world are accurate, fair, and free from stereotypical biases. She is a key contributor to projects like "FACET," which benchmarks the fairness of computer vision models across diverse demographic groups.

### Significant Achievements
Ross has been at the forefront of the "Multi-Modal Alignment" movement, developing datasets and evaluations that measure how models describe objects and people. Her work at FAIR is critical for ensuring that Meta's vision-language models (like LLaVA or Segment Anything) are developed with a rigorous "Responsible AI" framework.
