# Usman Anwar

## Research Summary

Usman Anwar is a PhD student in the Computational and Biological Learning lab at the University of Cambridge, supervised by David Krueger. He is funded by the Open Phil AI Fellowship and Vitalik Buterin Fellowship on AI Safety. His research focuses on AI Safety and Alignment, and he led an agenda paper on "Foundational Challenges in Assuring Safety and Alignment of Large Language Models" with 35+ co-authors and advisors from the NLP, ML, AI Safety, and AI Ethics communities.
