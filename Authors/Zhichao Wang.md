# Zhichao Wang

## Research Summary

Zhichao Wang is a researcher at Salesforce who authored the comprehensive survey "A Comprehensive Survey of LLM Alignment Techniques: RLHF, RLAIF, PPO, DPO and More." This influential work, completed with nine co-authors, provides an extensive overview of LLM alignment techniques including reward models, preference feedback mechanisms, and various alignment methods such as Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), RLAIF, PPO, and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), serving as a critical reference for AI safety research.
