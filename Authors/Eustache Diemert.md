# Eustache Diemert

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Eustache Diemert is a Principal Research Scientist at Criteo AI Lab, where he works on machine learning applications for online advertising and recommendation systems. His research focuses on developing scalable, fair, and causally-grounded machine learning methods for large-scale ad systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research addresses the intersection of machine learning, causal inference, and fairness in the context of recommendation and advertising systems. He works on ensuring that ML-powered systems make fair decisions across different user groups.

### Significant Achievements
Has contributed to influential work on fairness in job recommendation systems, developing methods to reduce bias in ML-driven employment platforms.

## Major Publications

- **FairJob: A Real-World Dataset for Fairness in Job Recommendations** (2024)
- Research on causal inference in recommendation systems
- Work on large-scale machine learning for ad tech
