## Abstract

Benchmark dataset labeling each post with both hate speech and sentiment information for Thai language. Uses a dual-channel deep learning approach (FastText + BERT) with capsule network. Achieves 89.67% accuracy/89.79% F1 for hate speech detection. Thai-specific hate speech detection benchmark with sentiment analysis. Addresses the gap in Thai language hate speech research. Relevant to toxicity and fairness evaluation in Thai.
