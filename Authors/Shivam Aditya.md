# Shivam Aditya

## Biographical Summary

Shivam Aditya is a researcher focusing on the development of global ethical frameworks for artificial intelligence. He investigated how diverse cultural and regional perspectives should be integrated into standardized AI governance models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Shivam’s research centers on AI sovereignty and the creation of regulatory frameworks that address the unique challenges of different global regions. He advocates for a more inclusive approach to AI ethics that goes beyond Western-centric paradigms.

### Significant Achievements
He is the first author of the influential paper "Toward global ethical frameworks for AI," which explores the landscape of international AI policy and the need for culturally grounded safety standards.
