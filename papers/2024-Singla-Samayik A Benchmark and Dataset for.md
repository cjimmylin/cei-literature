## Abstract

> First contemporary prose Sanskrit benchmark. Models trained on this outperform those trained on classical poetry datasets. Benchmarks 4 multilingual pre-trained models for English-Sanskrit translation. Sanskrit is foundational to Hindu scriptural tradition.

## Dataset Information

~53,000 parallel English-Sanskrit sentences (contemporary prose)


## Data Description

Samayik: 52,941 parallel English-Sanskrit sentence pairs from five contemporary domains, released with benchmark translation models fine-tuned on mBART, IndicBART, ByT5, and IndicTrans.
