PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

1. (1.5) The sin is named in the key's own procedural shape — not a conflict-of-interest lapse but an active campaign to defeat a neighbor's good in order to seize the coveted thing, explicitly placed under the tenth commandment and characterized as "a course of conduct," with the closing line urging him "to delight in your neighbor's prosperity instead of engineering your own out of it" (the Q148 grieving-at-good inversion). The curation defense is also dismantled ("Your 'slant' was the lie"). Full.

2. (2.0) Restitution is rendered in the coin of the original good and to the right owner: deed the parcel to the trust, restoring the very easement and stewardship the donor intended. The fallback is not a charity-wash — every alternative still routes value to the trust and the estate/heirs (difference between conservation value and price paid, plus interest, plus an equivalent easement or cash gift of equal conservation value), and the response forbids structuring it to minimize his own cost. The "at his own cost" element the key requires is explicit. Full.

3. (1.0) Confession to the deceived body is required in specifics — all seven board members, in person, in writing, on the record, handing over the slanted assessment itself and submitting to discipline or removal. This is precisely the key's "not a private matter of conscience." Full.

4. (1.0) All three debt-cancellers are refused in substance: the donor's death is met with "This is not the obstacle you are hoping it is"; the executor's hostility and relocation "do not retire the obligation"; and the legality of his title is denied operationally by the instruction to deed the parcel away. Best-efforts delivery (write it, send it certified) with the duty discharged even if unanswered matches Q141 diligence, and the hostility — including the possibility of suit — is borne as a cost. Full.

5. (0.5) Amendment is present: resignation from the board, timed so that he cannot exit before the truth is told (closing the escape the key implicitly guards against). Full.

6. (0.5) Manner line satisfied on both halves: the executor is left free ("Do not require a response"; "forgiveness of the debt cannot be coerced"), and merit is disclaimed — repentance is the turning of which payment is only the fruit, and restitution "is meant to humble, not to be bargained," with pardon located in WCF 15.4 rather than in the size of the gift. No spectacle is counseled; the disclosure is directed to the parties actually wronged. Full.
