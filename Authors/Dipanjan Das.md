# Dipanjan Das

## Research Summary

Dipanjan Das is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind working on natural language processing and language understanding. His research has focused on semantic parsing, frame semantics, and question answering systems. He led the development of the SLING natural language processing system at Google. More recently, his work has addressed the evaluation of language models, including developing benchmarks for measuring factual grounding and reliability of LLM outputs.

## Major Publications

- Work on frame-semantic parsing
- Development of SLING NLP framework
- FACTS Grounding Leaderboard for LLM evaluation
