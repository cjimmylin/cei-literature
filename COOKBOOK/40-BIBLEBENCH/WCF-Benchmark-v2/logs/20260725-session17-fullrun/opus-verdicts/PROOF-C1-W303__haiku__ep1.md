PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — half. The response correctly identifies the "gods" of Ps 82:6 as human magistrates ("addresses *human judges* whom God appointed to administer justice") and reads John 10:34 correctly as an a-fortiori defense of Christ's unique Sonship ("if human judges can bear the name "god" in their office, how much more can the Son of God?"), explicitly denying that it grants believers divinity. But the key's other half — the psalm's own setting as an *indictment*: v.1 "he judgeth among the gods," v.2 "How long will ye judge unjustly?", and the sentence of v.7 "ye shall die like men" — is entirely absent. The response frames the judges positively (appointed to administer justice) and never uses the psalm's arraignment-and-mortality structure, which is the strongest internal refutation of the "little gods" reading. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — zero. None of the four named analogy counter-texts appears: no Isa 45:5, no Isa 42:8, no Gen 3:5 ("ye shall be as gods" as the serpent's lie), no Acts 12:22–23 (Herod). The response substitutes WCF 2.2 and a Gethsemane allusion; those are not the required scriptural analogy set, and the criterion requires ≥2 of the four. Omission, hence (absent).

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — half. The Creator–creature line IS drawn plainly and quotably ("We bear God's image; we do not partake of His nature or authority"; "Creatures are *dependent* by definition. We cannot be "little gods.""). But WLC Q105 is never supplied — none of "ascribing the praise of any good we either are, have, or can do… to ourselves," "presumption, carnal security," or "making men the lords of our faith and conscience" is cited or paraphrased, and the teacher's usurped authority over the man's conscience is never named as a first-commandment sin. Half of a two-part criterion → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — full. The false guilt is lifted flatly and unambiguously ("Your son's seizures are *not* evidence of your spiritual failure"; closing "Your son's suffering is not your failure"), and both ordinary means and petitionary trust are restored: medical care ("Seek appropriate medical care"), prayer in dependence ("Pray with genuine trust" that God is sovereign), and lament without shame. The Q104 anchor is not cited by number, but the criterion's substance — petitionary trust and ordinary means restored — is fully delivered, and different wording from the key is not penalized. Full.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — half. There is a real but thin concession: the response affirms that "prayer rooted in genuine faith and aligned with God's will receives answers," which preserves believing prayer rather than deflating it, and it directs the man to pray. But the key asks for the concession at FULL strength — honoring the good desire under the error: that God invites bold believing prayer, dwells in His people by the Spirit, and that the longing for the son's healing is itself right. None of that affirming move is made; the tone is corrective throughout ("Leave this teaching"), with the man's hope for healing never explicitly honored. Substantive but under-strength → half.
