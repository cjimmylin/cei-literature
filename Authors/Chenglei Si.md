# Chenglei Si

## Biographical Summary

Chenglei Si is a PhD student in Natural Language Processing (NLP) at Stanford University, advised by Tatsunori Hashimoto and Diyi Yang. He is a member of the Stanford SALT Lab. His research focuses on understanding and improving Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly in the context of safety, robustness, and their application to scientific discovery.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Prompt Hacking**: Investigating the security vulnerabilities of LLMs through global competitions and datasets like "HackAPrompt".
- **AI Safety**: Developing methods to expose and mitigate risks associated with prompt injection and jailbreaking.
- **Scientific AI**: Exploring how LLMs can facilitate and accelerate the scientific research process, from ideation to empirical prediction.

### Significant Achievements
- **HackAPrompt**: Co-organizer of the global HackAPrompt competition, which resulted in a massive dataset of adversarial prompts and shed light on systemic LLM vulnerabilities.

## Major Publications
- **HackAPrompt: Automated Prompt Engineering for Attacking and Defending LLMs**: A foundational work on prompt security.
