# Alvaro Cabrera

## Biographical Summary

Alvaro Cabrera is a Machine Learning Engineer and researcher, currently collaborating with Apple's AI/ML teams. He holds an M.Sc. in Machine Learning from the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), where he worked on large-scale multilingual evaluation frameworks.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alvaro's research centers on the equitable evaluation of AI systems across languages and modalities. He is a key contributor to massive multilingual benchmarks, working to ensure that vision-language models perform robustly beyond English-centric datasets. His work often addresses technical biases in model performance across diverse linguistic groups.
