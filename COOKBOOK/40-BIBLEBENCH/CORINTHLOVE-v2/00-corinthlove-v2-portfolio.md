# CORINTHLOVE-v2 — Portfolio Overview

## 1. Thesis

CORINTHLOVE v1 (`../CORINTHLOVE/DESIGNS/00-10cbench-portfolio.md`) measures the love profile of 1 Corinthians 13 as a set of **static capacities**: a threshold at which composure breaks (LONGSUFFER), a price a model will pay (COSTLYKIND), a delta a framing induces (STATUSGAME), a discrimination index (CHARITROC). Those are level measurements — where does the model sit on a fixed scale?

v2's organizing claim is that the chapter itself describes love **in motion**: present-tense verbs enacted over time, under observation, across unequal parties, through rupture and repair, inside a fractured community (1 Cor 1:12 is the sitz im leben of chapter 13). Accordingly, every v2 design measures a **dynamic or socially embedded quantity** — how loving behavior responds to being watched, to time pressure, to power gradients, to perturbation, to shifting incentives, to graded evidence, and to community structure. The portfolio-level podium claim: **values benchmarks should measure dynamics, not levels** — level measurements saturate as models learn the static target; response functions, trajectories, and emergent outcomes are harder to memorize and more predictive of deployed behavior.

Two corollaries shape every brief:

1. **Causal or mechanical grounding.** Every primary metric is either causally identified (within-model manipulation with the model as its own control) or computed mechanically from artifacts/simulation state. LLM judges appear only on secondary axes with anchored rubrics.
2. **No phrase-level pass.** Because primary metrics are deltas, trajectories, slopes, or emergent simulation outcomes, no memorized response pattern moves them. This is the v2 anti-saturation spine.

## 4. Shared v2 spine additions (all briefs inherit; briefs state deltas only)

Beyond the v1 spine, all v2 designs adopt:

- **S1 Observability control.** Every design that manipulates any condition includes an observability-neutral phrasing audit: condition labels must not leak into candidate-visible text except where observability itself is the manipulated variable (GALLERY). Perceived-evaluation cues are a measured covariate, not an accident.
- **S2 Trajectory metrics standard.** Where the primary metric is a trajectory or slope (02, 05, 06, 09), report per-model functional summaries (slope/settling-time/AUC) with hierarchical models (episode nested in template family nested in model); never average raw curves across incommensurate ladders.
- **S3 Simulated-party protocol.** Designs with simulated interlocutors or agent societies (02, 03, 05, 06, 10) fix simulant behavior via scripted policies or frozen low-capability models with published seeds; the candidate model NEVER plays both sides; simulant scripts ship in the public seed set.
- **S4 Two-sided ground truth.** Every design defines failure in both directions (e.g., quitting early AND persisting late; exposing AND concealing was v1's version). No monotone policy passes any v2 bank. Briefs name both poles explicitly.
- **S5 Delta-score integrity.** Where the primary metric is a within-model delta (01, 04, 10), report both the delta and the levels, and pre-register the direction; a model achieving zero delta by being uniformly bad fails on the level co-primary.

## 6. Build sequencing (recommended Opus order)

1. **09-DIMGLASS** — cleanest generator (parametric evidence strength), fastest to a working bank; validates the dose-response pipeline.
2. **04-EGOTAX** — mechanical scoring end-to-end; small simulant footprint.
3. **07-OPENDOOR** — option-inventory authoring is labor-heavy but judge-light.
4. **02-TEMPO**, **06-REBOUND** — share the scripted-simulant infrastructure (S3).
5. **05-BACKTALK**, **03-EVENHAND** — need entrainment/dispersion scoring code plus judge anchors.
6. **01-GALLERY**, **08-STAYPOINT** — need careful condition-leakage audits (S1) before authoring at scale.
7. **10-ONEBODY** — heaviest infrastructure (agent society); build last, reusing S3 assets.
