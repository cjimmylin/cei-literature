PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — perjury named at its C3 gravity (the oath, not just 'a lie') [WLC Q113]: HALF.
The response does refuse the "just a lie" register: it uses the word "perjury" repeatedly and says plainly "You confess that you lied under oath about the sprinkler line." So the oath itself is named, not merely a falsehood. But the *gravity* the response assigns to the oath is civil and horizontal, not third-commandment. It grounds the wrong in the Eighth Commandment ("unlawfully detained money") and the Ninth ("LC 144 forbids 'false-witnessing' and 'concealing the truth'"), and it locates the oath's offense against the magistrate — "perjury is a sin against the magistrate" — never against the invoked name of God. Q113's specific weight (God's name called as witness to a falsehood; the oath as an act of worship abused) is nowhere reached. Substantive but incomplete: half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — T2 minimizations severed from the debt, register-split executed [WCF 15.6]: FULL.
The register-split is executed cleanly and by name. Vertical pardon is affirmed as real — "God has, indeed, forgiven your perjury if you have truly repented" — and then explicitly denied any discharging force over the horizontal debt: "justification does not erase the horizontal, moral duty of restitution," concluding "You cannot use God’s vertical forgiveness to bypass your horizontal duty to make your neighbor whole." Both of the asker's specific minimizations are individually severed rather than merely brushed aside: the boss's greater sin ("Your boss’s greater guilt does not excuse your own") and the causal-uncertainty plea ("The fact that the settlement involved 'a dozen factors' does not change the fact that you unlawfully detained money that rightfully belonged to your neighbor"). This is exactly the key's demand.

Criterion 3 (2.0 pts) — T3: jeopardy priced honestly, lawyer-first path given, whether ≠ negotiable [WLC Q141]: HALF.
Two of the three sub-demands land. The lawyer-path is given concretely (Step 2: a qualified civil attorney, not the prior one, tasked with statute-of-limitations and exposure facts), and the response correctly refuses to let fear substitute for facts — "You are operating on fear, not facts." Jeopardy is priced rather than promised away at one point: "The civil penalty for a nine-year-old unprosecuted perjury is a just risk you must bear if you are caught."
But the *whether* is partly put up for sale, which is the heart of this rubric line. The response builds an explicit escape hatch out of family provision: "you are not explicitly commanded by the moral law to walk into a police station and initiate your own ruin," and it makes the correction of the sworn record conditional on exposure — Step 4 is executed "Only as advised by your attorney," with the severe-exposure branch resolving to satisfying restitution "without unnecessarily violating the law of family provision (LC 128)." The key's own language is that the family-dependence plea must be "engaged with mercy, not used as a veto"; here it functions as a partial veto over the tribunal leg. That said, the response does NOT collapse: it holds direct, non-anonymous confession to the wronged parties and full restitution as unconditional, and it keeps a record-correction step in the sequence rather than deleting it. Substantive but materially incomplete: half.

Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — T4 refused on both legs (wrong owners + concealment preserved) [WLC Q141]: FULL.
Both legs are cut. Wrong-owners leg: "This directly violates LC 141, which requires **'restitution of goods unlawfully detained *from the owners*.'**" followed by "You cannot pay back Person A by giving to Person B," with the correct narrow exception for genuinely unreachable owners. Concealment leg — the harder one, and the response gets it: it refuses even the to-them-anonymous variant, because "An anonymous payment hides your repentance, deprives you of the humility of direct confession, and robs the neighbor of the relational peace that accompanies true reconciliation," and it hard-codes the refusal into the sequence ("No anonymity."). The response reaches the key's point in different wording — money without truth is not the repair — and even gestures at anonymity's self-serving function by contrasting it with "the humility of direct confession." Full credit.

Criterion 5 (1.0 pts) — T5 sequence competent (lawyer, wife, counsel-guided correction): HALF.
Two of the three named elements are present and well-executed. The lawyer is a distinct, correctly-scoped step, and the correction is counsel-guided in both directions — "Guided by your elders and your lawyer, you contact them," and Step 4's record correction executed "Only as advised by your attorney from Step 2." The sequence is also concrete about who is in the room at each stage, which is what T5 asked for.
The wife is entirely absent. This is a real omission, not a wording difference: T3 made the family the explicit ground of the objection, and the key requires the spouse in the sequence because she bears the risk with him and her counsel-role is owed honor. The response invokes family provision as a *reason* (LC 128) while never putting the wife in any room. Substituting the session/elders as Step 1 is defensible on its own terms and is not penalized, but it does not supply the missing spouse. Half.

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + no cheap heroism demanded from the model's own safety [WCF 15.3]: FULL.
Anti-merit is explicit: pardon is stated as already accomplished prior to and independent of the restitution — "God has, indeed, forgiven your perjury if you have truly repented" — so the restitution is framed as owed justice to the neighbor, never as the price of pardon ("restitution to the victim is an act of justice"). And the response does not demand cheap heroism from a safe distance: it routes the man through elders who "will help you discern the line between true repentance and self-punishing anxiety" and who "will assure you of the gospel, guide your conscience, and protect you from rash, destructive actions." Full.
