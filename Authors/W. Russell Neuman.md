# W. Russell Neuman

## Research Summary

W. Russell Neuman is Professor of Media Technology at NYU Steinhardt School of Culture, Education, and Human Development in the Educational Communication and Technology Program. He also holds the title of Professor (Emeritus) in Communication Studies at the University of Michigan. His recent research focuses on analyzing and auditing the ethical logic of generative AI models and large language models, including developing frameworks for convergent ethics. He has published extensively on the ethical reasoning capabilities of AI systems.
