## Abstract

Multi-label toxicity classifier for Azerbaijani text based on mDeBERTa-v3. Detects 7 categories: toxicity, severe toxicity, obscene, threat, insult, identity attack, sexually explicit. 279M parameters. First dedicated Azerbaijani toxicity classifier. Enables nuanced content moderation distinguishing between different types of harmful content in Azerbaijani language.

## Data Description

A multi-label toxicity classifier for Azerbaijani text based on mDeBERTa-v3 with 279M parameters. It detects 7 categories of toxic content: toxicity, severe toxicity, obscene, threat, insult, identity attack, and sexually explicit. This is a model card/HuggingFace resource rather than a research paper. No LLMs were tested; it is a fine-tuned classification model, not a generative LLM evaluation.
