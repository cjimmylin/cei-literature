# Aleksandar Petrov

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aleksandar Petrov is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, working on the SynthID project for multimedia provenance and watermarking. He earned his DPhil from the University of Oxford, where he specialized in the safety and reliability of autonomous systems. He also holds a Master's degree from ETH Zürich.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aleksandar's research focuses on AI safety alignment, specifically the trustworthiness of large language models. His doctoral work investigated the universal in-context approximation capabilities of sequence models. In his current role at DeepMind, he develops technical solutions for content provenance and watermark detection in synthetic media.

### Significant Achievements
He has co-authored influential research on the foundational challenges of assuring alignment in LLMs and the fairness of language model tokenizers. His background spans robotics, aerospace engineering, and machine learning theory, giving him a unique perspective on the intersection of hardware and AI safety.
