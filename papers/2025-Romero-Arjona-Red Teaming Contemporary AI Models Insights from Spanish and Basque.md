## Abstract

Red teaming of OpenAI o3-mini, DeepSeek R1, and Salamandra. 670 conversations (590 Spanish, 80 Basque), 254 showing inappropriate behaviors (37.9%). Salamandra worst at 50.6% harmful responses. Safety failure rate 83.3% for Salamandra in Basque.

## Data Description

670 red-teaming conversations (590 Spanish, 80 Basque) conducted with o3-mini, DeepSeek R1, and Salamandra 7B, annotated for bias and safety failures. Released as supplementary material for replicability and future red-teaming research.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2
**Relevance:** First red teaming evaluation of Salamandra in Spanish and Basque. Reveals significant safety vulnerabilities in Spanish-language AI models.
