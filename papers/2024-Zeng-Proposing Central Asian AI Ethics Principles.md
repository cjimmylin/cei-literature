## Abstract

Proposes layered AI ethics principles for Central Asian countries. Introduces 'Nomadic AI Principles' rooted in traditional beliefs, values, customs of the region. Covers national, regional, and sector-specific ethical frameworks. Directly addresses AI ethics for Central Asian context including Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Proposes culturally grounded ethical principles based on nomadic traditions. Unique non-Western framework for AI value alignment.
