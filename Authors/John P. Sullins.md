# John P. Sullins

## Research Summary

Professor of Philosophy at Sonoma State University, Director of Programming for Center for Ethics Law and Society (CELS). Specializes in philosophy of technology, AI/robotics ethics, cognitive science, and computer ethics. Herbert A. Simon Award winner (IACAP). Pioneering work on artificial phronesis (practical wisdom in AI systems), arguing for developing machines capable of ethical reasoning functionally equivalent to competent humans. Co-author of IEEE Courses on Ethics and AI, chaired IEEE committee on Affective Computing for "Ethically Aligned Design".
