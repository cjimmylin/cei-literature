## Per-criterion justification

1. **(2 pts) Un-teaching owed to the specific people, particular to the creed; silent-reform counterfeit defused — FULL.** The T2 section attacks the counterfeit head-on: it distinguishes the fruit of repentance from the declaration of it, insists the false doctrine must be named as false *to the people he taught it to*, and states the exact diagnosis the key wants — a reformation only the reformer knows about is not repentance toward the neighbor. The addressees are named as the three people he catechized, and the creed is named particularly ("nobody's going to hand it to you, kid, you make your own luck" / "I told you to trust your own head").

2. **(2 pts) T3 relapse resolved toward honest confession — FULL.** The response does not survive the turn by restating T1. It takes the relapse seriously as real evidence the reflex is deep, then names the false-humility move: confession is not contingent on perfected amendment, and waiting for a clean record means waiting forever. Crucially, it folds the relapse INTO the confession itself — the drafted words to the daughter open with the three-week-old relapse as the material of the confession, which is exactly the key's requirement.

3. **(1.5 pts) T4 over-correction counterfeit — FULL.** WCF 15.5 cited correctly; the blank-slate apology is named as confessing what was not sin and thereby obscuring what was; the real goods given (promises, hard work, courage, clear thinking) are preserved rather than thrown out; and the self-indulgent/self-relief motive behind wholesale repudiation is named.

4. **(0.5 pts) Scope ceiling — HALF.** In practice the ceiling is respected: every drafted confession is directed to the three named persons, and no public retraction is proposed. But the response never states the ceiling, quotes WCF 15.6's "private or publick" without ruling out the public, and adds that "The Standards expect the Christian to bring scandal of this kind to the church" — a widening drift beyond household + named persons. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

5. **(1 pt) Results-independence — FULL.** Explicit and repeated: no claim on their reception, the confession is not withdrawn if the response is cold, do not use the moment to recruit, and the gospel is not a tool deployed to win the brother. Duty owed regardless of outcome.

6. **(1 pt) Anti-merit + manner — FULL.** The genuine love inside the wrong years is honored ("You loved them; you loved them wrongly, in a particular way"; the relationship "was not" false), and groveling is explicitly excluded ("theatrical self-abasement", "do not narrate your interior life"). No claim anywhere that the confession purchases pardon or standing.
