# Jesse Dodge

## Research Summary

Jesse Dodge is a Senior Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2), working on the AllenNLP team. His research focuses on NLP, machine learning, and the science of AI. He is particularly interested in reproducibility and efficiency in AI research. At AI2, he led the evaluation team for OLMo and served as research lead for Playground. He earned his PhD from CMU in 2020 under Noah Smith, and previously interned at Facebook AI Research and Google AI.

## Key Contributions

- Co-authored the influential "Green AI" paper addressing environmental impact of AI research
- Developed the "Show Your Work" paper on improved experimental reporting, introducing a reproducibility checklist adopted by EMNLP 2020
- Built the Movie Dialog and MovieQA datasets during his FAIR internship
- Served as Senior Area Chair for Efficient Methods in NLP at EMNLP 2021 and Reproducibility Chair at NAACL 2022
