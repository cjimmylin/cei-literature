# Jared Mueller

## Research Summary

Jared Mueller is a researcher who contributed to Anthropic's Constitutional AI work, which introduced a method for training AI assistants to be helpful, harmless, and honest using AI feedback rather than human feedback for the harmlessness component.

Constitutional AI represents a significant approach to AI alignment where the model is trained to follow a set of principles (a "constitution") and uses self-critique to improve its responses, reducing the need for human labelers to evaluate potentially harmful outputs.
