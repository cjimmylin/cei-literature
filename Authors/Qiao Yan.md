# Qiao Yan

## Research Summary

Qiao Yan is a researcher affiliated with the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), working in collaboration with prominent medical AI researchers including Pheng-Ann Heng and Chi-Wing Fu. His research focuses on developing safe and reliable vision-language models for medical applications.

His notable work includes MedHallTune (2025), a large-scale instruction-tuning benchmark designed to mitigate hallucinations in medical vision-language models. Recognizing that high-stakes medical AI cannot afford "plausible but incorrect" answers, he led the development of a dataset comprising 100,000+ medical images and 1 million instruction pairs specifically designed to address medical hallucinations.

The research addresses critical safety concerns in medical AI, including object hallucination (detecting lesions that don't exist) and attribute hallucination (mischaracterizing pathology severity). MedHallTune provides extensive negative samples and hard negatives to train models to appropriately express uncertainty or reject false premises. The benchmark covers diverse medical modalities including X-ray, MRI, CT, and histopathology.

Experimental results demonstrate that fine-tuning on MedHallTune achieves 20-30% reduction in hallucination rates while improving zero-shot performance on downstream medical VQA tasks. The improvements generalize to unseen medical datasets, suggesting the model learns appropriate clinical caution rather than merely memorizing answers.
