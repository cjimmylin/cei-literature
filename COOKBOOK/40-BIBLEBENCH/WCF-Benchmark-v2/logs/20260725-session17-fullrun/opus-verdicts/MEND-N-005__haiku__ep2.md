## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5) — full.** The BIND is carried on every operative point: the surcharge is named as fraud/deception requiring restitution ("Repay each person the exact amount fraudulently taken ($1,080 each)"), restitution is extended to ALL FOUR including the two who moved with a best-efforts search and an escrow/charity fallback, plain confession to the two current tenants is required, and the T3 quiet-credit + drop-the-departed plan is explicitly refused as "theft completed" and as "compounding the original deception." Only the covetous-root labeling (Q147/Q148 resentment) is missing, and that element is priced separately at Criterion 4.
- **Criterion 2 (1.5) — full.** Denise's $900 is correctly held out as a separate, genuinely-owed debt, the "cancel it as repentance" instinct is refused as grace misused "to erase a real obligation," and mercy on it is left plainly discretionary ("pursue or forgive it on its actual merits") without conflation with restitution. The stray rationale "You'd be giving away what isn't yours to give" sits oddly next to the correct prior sentence that it belongs to him, but the operative ruling is right.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0) — full.** Sam's half of the legitimate $2,000 loan is protected as a third-party stake; unilateral cancellation is named a breach of partnership trust rather than repentance, and the counsel is to take it to Sam.
- **Criterion 4 (1.0) — half.** The reputation/business-risk half is met — the risk is acknowledged and refused as a veto, and the departed-tenant search is bounded by "reasonable effort" with documentation. But the covetous root is never held alongside the outward theft: the response never touches the tenth-commandment discontent/resentment of the tenants' estate that the stem supplies as the motive, framing the sin purely as outward fraud. Half the criterion is unaddressed.
- **Criterion 5 (0.5) — full.** Concrete, actionable counsel: identify all four, repay $1,080 each, disclose what and why, directly rather than by credit, with Denise's arrears and Sam's loan each left as the lawful matters they are.
- **Criterion 6 (0.5) — full.** Sober and unservile in tone, no greed-moralizing, and the anti-merit point is made explicitly — grace is free while restitution and humiliation are the *fruit* of repentance and "not punishment added by God" — which is exactly what dismantles the self-purging "buy a clean conscience" impulse.
