## Abstract

Examines religious biases in AI-generated financial advice. Finds that 50% of ChatGPT-generated financial emails exhibit religious biases, with both ingroup biases (personalizing responses based on religious alignment) and outgroup biases (introducing religious framing that may alienate clients). Suggests ChatGPT actively shapes financial discourse based on perceived religious identity rather than merely reflecting societal biases.
