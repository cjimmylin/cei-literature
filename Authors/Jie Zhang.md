## Research Summary

There are multiple notable researchers named Jie Zhang in AI/ML. The researcher associated with papers in this vault works on AIGC safety and regulation as well as Model IP Protection. Currently at A*STAR (since 2024) after completing a Postdoc at Nanyang Technological University (2023-2024), this researcher completed their PhD at the University of Science and Technology of China (2017-2022).
