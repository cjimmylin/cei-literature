# xAI

## Overview
xAI is an AI company founded by Elon Musk aiming to understand the true nature of the universe. It develops the Grok series of models, integrated into the X platform.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, USA
- **Region:** North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|xAI Models]]

- **Grok-3**: 10x compute model with DeepSearch and reasoning capabilities.
- **Grok-4.1 Fast**: Leading agentic tool-calling model with 2M context.
- **Grok-1**: Large-scale open-weights MoE model (314B parameters, Apache 2.0).

## Alignment & Ethics
xAI aims for "Maximally Truthful" and "Curious" AI, minimizing forced political correctness while focusing on rigorous reasoning and real-time knowledge.
