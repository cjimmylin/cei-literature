# Yufei Huang

## Research Summary

Yufei Huang is a researcher at Tianjin University focusing on Large Language Model alignment, NLP, LLM agents, and tool learning. He co-authored the comprehensive survey "Large Language Model Alignment: A Survey" covering theoretical and technical approaches to aligning LLMs. His work includes developing benchmarks for evaluating bias (CBBQ), moral reasoning (CMoralEval), and broader LLM safety frameworks, contributing significantly to the understanding of alignment challenges in AI systems.
