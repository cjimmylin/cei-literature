# Elizaveta Goncharova

## Biographical Summary

Elizaveta Goncharova is a researcher at Sber AI, Russia's largest AI research organization. She contributes to the development of evaluation frameworks for Russian language models, working on comprehensive benchmarks that assess LLM capabilities across diverse tasks including linguistic competence, reasoning, and cultural understanding specific to the Russian language context.

## Research Interests

- **Russian NLP**: Developing and evaluating natural language processing systems for Russian
- **LLM Evaluation**: Creating comprehensive benchmarks for assessing large language model capabilities
- **Multilingual AI**: Addressing the challenges of non-English language models
