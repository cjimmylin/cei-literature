# Charith Peris

## Biographical Summary

Charith Peris is a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon AGI (formerly AWS AI), where he leads research initiatives focused on the controllability, safety, and reliability of Large Language Models (LLMs). His work is central to developing more steerable and ethical AI systems that can be safely deployed in diverse applications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Steerability**: Developing techniques to ensure that language model outputs align with specific stylistic, topical, or safety-related attributes.
- **Responsible AI**: Innovating on methods for attribute-controlled fine-tuning and evaluation to mitigate biases and improve model safety.
- **Controlled Generation**: Researching how to effectively guide natural language generation through fine-grained constraints.

### Significant Achievements
- **Steerability Research**: Author of foundational work on the steerability of LLMs, exploring how models can be effectively directed through prompting and fine-tuning.

## Major Publications
- **On the Steerability of Large Language Models**: A key study investigating the mechanics of model control.
- **Controlled Text Generation**: Contributions to the field of attribute-aware language modeling.
