# Alan W Black

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alan W Black is a Professor Emeritus in the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University. He earned his PhD from the University of Edinburgh and has held positions at ATR in Japan. He is a world-renowned expert in speech synthesis and language technologies, having retired from CMU in 2022 to Alaska.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alan's research has focused on the practical implementation of computational theories of speech and language. He is a primary author of the Festival Speech Synthesis System and created the FestVox Voice Building tools and the Flite synthesizer. His work aims to make synthetic speech as natural and efficient as human communication.

### Significant Achievements
He was a pioneer in unit selection speech synthesis, which transitioned the field toward data-driven approaches. He co-founded Cepstral, a speech translation company, and initiated the Blizzard Challenge for evaluating speech synthesis. His open-source contributions through the Festvox project remain foundational to the linguistics and AI communities.

## Major Publications
