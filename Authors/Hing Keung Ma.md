# Hing Keung Ma

## Research Summary

Hing Keung Ma is a Professor and Director of the Centre for Child Development at Hong Kong Baptist University's Faculty of Social Sciences. He holds a Ph.D. from the University of London. His research focuses on the psychology of moral development, going beyond the cognitive-focused theories of Piaget and Kohlberg to include affective aspects. He has proposed a 7-stage development theory for moral development in Chinese people and a 4-stage developmental model of citizenship. He served as Head of the Department of Education Studies (1994-2002) and Associate Dean (Research) at the Faculty of Social Sciences (2010-2013). His work includes extensive research on the Project P.A.T.H.S. positive youth development program in Hong Kong.
