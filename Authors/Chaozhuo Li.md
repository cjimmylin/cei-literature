# Chaozhuo Li

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Chaozhuo Li is a researcher who received his Ph.D. in Engineering from Beihang University in 2020. During his doctoral studies, he was a visiting scholar at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) from 2017 to 2019. He worked at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) as a Lead Researcher from 2020 to 2024.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLM Security**: Research on security aspects of large language models, including fairness and alignment.
- **Knowledge Graph Alignment**: Integrating knowledge from multiple sources for improved AI reasoning.
- **Social Network Analysis**: User identity linkage across different social platforms.

### Significant Achievements
- Published over 100 research papers, including 60+ CCF-A tier conference/journal papers
- 2,405+ citations and 155 publications
- Best Paper Nomination at WSDM 2023
- Best Paper Nomination at PAKDD 2023
- Research spans user identity linkage, image inpainting with GANs, knowledge graph alignment, and unsupervised graph representation learning
