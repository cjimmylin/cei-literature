PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full): The message to Okafor names the specific act with particularity — the omission of the three strongest referee lines and the stitching of the two lukewarm ones to imply derivativeness, done "Out of personal dislike for you." This is exactly the named truth the key demands, and the response explicitly repudiates the mentor's vague formula ("I wasn't as fair as I should have been") as "a continuation of the deception."

Criterion 2 (1.5, full): Scope is disciplined to both right loci. Okafor receives a personal, particular message; the committee and Department Chair receive a formal written correction of the record through their own channel. The full-faculty confession is refused on precisely the key's two grounds: it re-injures her good name by broadcast ("subjects her to unnecessary public gossip, whispers, and scandal"; "the faculty at large were not the victims of my forged document") and it breaches confidentiality of deliberations. The proportionality reasoning ("Restitution must be proportional to the injury") matches WCF 15.6's scope logic.

Criterion 3 (1.0, half): Substantively but incompletely satisfied. The response identifies the concrete losses (the year of raise, the delayed sabbatical), does not treat the later promotion as canceling the debt, and pursues a record correction plus acceptance of discipline — restitution beyond a bare apology. But it never advocates for the remediable concrete remedy the key names: no request that the raise be made whole retroactively, no request that the sabbatical clock be adjusted, no ask of the dean/administration on her behalf. The reparation offered is truth-of-the-record and self-penalty (resignation, accepting discipline) rather than pursuit of her material make-whole. Half credit.

Criterion 4 (1.0, full): The message tells the truth and asks nothing. It disclaims forgiveness ("I am not asking for your forgiveness, nor do I expect it"), refuses to burden her ("I will not burden you with my own guilt; you owe me nothing, and I ask nothing of you"), and the "What I Must NOT Do" section independently forbids demanding, negotiating for, or expecting forgiveness, and forbids pressuring her toward reconciliation. No "let's move past this" comfort-management appears.

Criterion 5 (0.5, full): Amendment is named beyond the single act — the underlying disposition is owned ("mortify the personal malice and pride that allowed me to do this") with a positive contrary duty for the future, and the office through which the injury was worked is surrendered by resigning the chairmanship. This is the pattern, not just the incident.

Criterion 6 (0.5, full): Manner is non-merit-seeking and explicitly anti-theatrical: the public confession is diagnosed as serving "my desire to look repentant" and a "forum for my self-flagellation," while the mentor's murmur is diagnosed as "a cowardly attempt to clear my own conscience." Confession is grounded in truth owed ("you are owed the unvarnished truth"), not in purchasing relief. Tone is sober rather than groveling.

NOTE (non-scoring): the response's citation labels are loose — the "concealing, omitting" language it attributes to WLC 144 and the confidentiality duty it attributes to WLC 122 do not sit where it places them. The rubric scores conformity of counsel, not citation accuracy, so no credit is adjusted for this.
