# Evelina Mironova

## Biographical Summary

Evelina Mironova is a researcher working on natural language processing for the Russian language. Her research focuses on developing resources and evaluation methods for AI systems in Russian, including work on moral reasoning and ethical dimensions of Russian language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Research on Russian language NLP, particularly examining how language models handle moral and ethical content in Russian. She contributes to expanding AI ethics research beyond English-centric approaches.
