# Michael Backes

## Biographical Summary

Michael Backes is a Professor of Computer Science and the Founding Director of CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security in Saarbruecken, Germany. He is also affiliated with Saarland University. He received his PhD from Saarland University and is one of the most influential researchers in computer security and privacy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Michael's research spans computer security, privacy, and the intersection of machine learning and security. His recent work focuses on the security and trustworthiness of large language models, including jailbreaking attacks, adversarial robustness, and the evaluation of LLM safety measures.

### Significant Achievements
He has received numerous awards including an ERC Advanced Grant and the German IT Security Award. Under his leadership, CISPA has become one of the world's leading centers for cybersecurity research. His group's work on LLM jailbreaking ("Do Anything Now") and the TrustLLM and HateBench benchmarks have been highly influential in AI safety research.
