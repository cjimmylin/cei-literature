# Haixin Liu

## Research Summary

Haixin Liu is a researcher with connections to Meta and Carnegie Mellon University. His work includes developing high-performance kernel libraries for quantized inference on CPUs (fbgemm) and designing inference accelerators for ML workloads with unique characteristics such as sparse memory accesses, large model sizes, and high compute/memory/network bandwidth requirements.

At Meta, he contributed to fbgemm, achieving greater than 2x performance gains through fusing common quantization operations with high-performance gemm implementations. He has also co-authored work on optimizing LLMs for personalized emotional support.
