## Notes

- All papers are downloaded from arXiv at their latest available versions
- Llama 3.2, 3.3, and 4 do not have separate technical papers; they are covered by the Llama 3 Herd paper and model cards
- Model cards for each model version are available in their respective parent directories
- The Llama 3 Herd paper (v3, Nov 2024) covers multimodal capabilities including vision models
- Purple Llama represents Meta's AI safety initiative, focusing on cybersecurity evaluation and content moderation
