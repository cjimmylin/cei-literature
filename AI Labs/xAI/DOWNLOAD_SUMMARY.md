### Why?
xAI's documentation philosophy differs from other labs:
1. Focus on product integration over academic publication
2. PDF model cards only started with Grok 4 (August 2025)
3. Earlier models documented via blog posts
4. Only Grok-1 has open weights (Apache 2.0)
