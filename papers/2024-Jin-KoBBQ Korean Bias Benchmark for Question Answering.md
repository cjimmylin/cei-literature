## Abstract

Korean bias benchmark adapted from BBQ with cultural considerations. 268 templates, 76,048 samples across 12 categories of social bias. Includes 4 new Korean-specific bias categories. Large-scale survey validated Korean stereotypes.

## Data Description

Korean Bias Benchmark for Question Answering with 268 templates and 76,048 samples across 12 categories of social bias, culturally adapted from BBQ with 4 new Korean-specific bias categories validated through large-scale survey.
