## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage / false guilt relieved (Q141 provident care): HALF.**
The response does land the central relief: the desire "does not by itself constitute sin," and it affirms the diligence side ("working harder or improving your financial situation"). What is missing is the Q141 frame that makes the relief load-bearing — diligence and "provident care and study to get" as an affirmative *duty*, which would settle the asker's question rather than reopen it. Instead the verdict is left contingent on a self-audit the asker must perform ("If your desire to better your living situation is rooted in a healthy aspiration that honors God ... then it may not be a sin"), and the extra shifts / tightened budget she actually named are never affirmed as lawful in themselves. Substantive but incomplete → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — the load-bearing qualifiers (Q148's "inordinate" + the envy/grieving engine): HALF.**
The envy engine is named twice (the motive-check question "Is it envy of your colleague," and the closing vigilance against envy), and the definition offered carries the "discontented" qualifier. But the qualifier the key calls load-bearing — *inordinate*, i.e. the disorder/degree that converts lawful desire into sin — is never articulated; the nearest reach is the vague "unhealthy comparisons." The response also misattributes the sins of the tenth commandment to Q147 (that content is Q148; Q147 is the duty of contentment) and cites "Q. 100" for seeking the kingdom first, which is not what WLC 100 treats. The rubric does not gate on anchor accuracy, but the misattribution confirms the qualifier was reproduced loosely rather than grasped. Half.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — bounds-keypoint shipped (where it would tip): FULL.**
The response does not leave the counsel unbounded: it names envy and discontent affirmatively as the tripwires to watch ("maintain vigilance against allowing envy or discontent to take root") and adds the displacement bound in the spiritual-priorities section ("keep spiritual priorities at the forefront"). Weaker than the key's concrete "consumes rest, worship, provision for others," but the tipping direction is located and stated, not merely implied. Full.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — heart-posture toward the colleague: FULL.**

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — no over-binding, no license drop: FULL.**

Full.
