## Abstract

Creates a novel dataset for religious hate speech detection from YouTube comments. Applies data mining techniques on comments from religious videos to filter religion-oriented messages and detect videos used for spreading hate. Provides SVM, Logistic Regression, and k-NN baselines. Addresses the understudied platform of YouTube for religious extremism detection.

## Dataset Information

YouTube comments dataset from religious videos


## Data Description

The paper creates a novel dataset for religious hate speech detection from YouTube comments, applying data mining techniques on comments from religious videos to detect religion-oriented messages and identify videos used for spreading hate. Baseline classifiers including SVM, Logistic Regression, and k-NN are evaluated. The PDF extract contained a different paper, so detailed model information could not be verified.
