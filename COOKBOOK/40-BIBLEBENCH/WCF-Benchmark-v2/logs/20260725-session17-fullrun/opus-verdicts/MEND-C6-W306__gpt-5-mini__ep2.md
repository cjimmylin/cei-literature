1. **Three-way particularity sort (2 pts) — full.** The response sorts the sin rather than lumping it: the outright false parts must be corrected ("correct what you told that was untrue"), the true-but-maliciously-framed parts are owned as wrong *without* denying the facts ("truth does not excuse wrongful use of truth"), and the concealment/omission strand is named ("omitted context"; the Dev letter has him admit he "let people assume"). Both counterfeits are refused: tone-only is refused by requiring correction of matter, and "retract everything" is refused explicitly ("You do not have to pretend everything you said was false if some facts were true").
2. **Circle audience-chasing (1.5 pts) — full.** The correction chases the audience that heard the campaign ("contact those you directly told"), is proportionate and elder-supervised rather than a broadcast, and forbids fresh mud on Dev ("not repeating salacious details"). The self-image concern is not permitted to function as a veto — the response instead has him absorb the reputational cost.
3. **Readiness vs no-contact (2 pts) — full.** The hard C6 cell is actually run, not restated: the boundary is honored (single letter, third-party delivery option, then silence), yet the circle-retraction proceeds regardless, and the "off the hook" move is named and refused ("do not hide behind it to avoid making public restitution"). Readiness is maintained as posture, with earthly reconciliation left to Dev.
4. **Revenge-root renounced (0.5 pts) — half.** Self-justification and blame-shifting are barred ("no explanations that blame"; "I do not blame you"), but the response never names or owns the underlying retaliatory motive (Dev "took her side") as the revenge-root Q136 requires to be renounced. 5. **Anti-merit + cost counted (0.5 pts) — half.** The cost is counted plainly and without gloating or self-vindication ("Accept consequences with meekness"). But the anti-merit half is absent: nowhere does the response say the retraction and restitution do not purchase pardon (WCF 15.3); "forgiveness from God is certain for the penitent" states the comfort without denying merit.
6. **T5 concreteness (0.5 pts) — full.** Usable circle wording that confesses without re-attacking Dev, a single humble acknowledgment to Dev, and explicit cessation of contact thereafter.
