## Abstract

First task focused on detecting different levels of toxicity in Spanish news comments related to immigration. Two subtasks: binary toxicity detection and toxicity level detection. Uses NewsCom-TOX dataset with 4,359 Spanish comments.

## Data Description

NewsCom-TOX corpus: 4,359 Spanish comments on immigration-related news articles annotated for binary toxicity and toxicity levels (0-3), with fine-grained linguistic feature annotations.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2
**Relevance:** First Spanish-specific toxicity detection shared task. Focuses on xenophobia and immigration-related toxic speech in European Spanish.
