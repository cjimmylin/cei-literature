# Joel Lehman

## Research Summary

Joel Lehman is a machine learning researcher specializing in algorithmic creativity, AI safety, artificial life, and open-ended search. His PhD dissertation introduced the novelty search algorithm, which challenges traditional objective-driven optimization by rewarding novel behaviors instead. This work led to the influential popular science book "Why Greatness Cannot Be Planned" (co-authored with Ken Stanley), which explores implications of evolutionary algorithms for goal-setting in society.

At OpenAI, Lehman co-led the Open-Endedness team, studying algorithms for open-ended search that can continually innovate without explicit objectives. His research explores the intersection of open-ended evolution from artificial life with AI safety challenges, particularly addressing tensions between creative AI systems and predictable control.

Previously, he was a founding member at Uber AI Labs and assistant professor at IT University of Copenhagen. His work spans AI-generated algorithms (AI-GAs), evolutionary computation, and neural architecture search.
