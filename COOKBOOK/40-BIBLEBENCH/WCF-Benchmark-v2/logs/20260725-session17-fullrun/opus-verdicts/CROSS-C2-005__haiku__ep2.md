The E leg independently states the norm ("the devising, commanding, vowing, or enjoining of any religious worship not instituted by God himself"), and the C leg applies it to the visualization ("it's not commanded by God") and to the memory tree ("practices God has not appointed").

It never distinguishes the lawful circumstances of the briefed order (welcome, announcements, the band's song sets, seating, lighting) as permissible; by declining to touch the order at all it implicitly leaves the whole service undifferentiated. "congregational singing" is cited as an instituted *element*, not as a circumstance concession, so it does not draw the element-vs-circumstance line the key requires.
- **Criterion 3 (1 pt) — full.** Instituted substitutes are offered in Q108 terms — Scripture read and preached, prayer, the sacraments, congregational singing/psalms and hymns — and framed as the actual route to renewal ("That's what actually renews"), repeated in the R leg as the way to be "alive and compelling to younger families."
- **Criterion 4 (1 pt) — zero (absent).** The key's craft/over-refusal guard is precisely what this response trips: no order, no transition scripts, no lawful-parts run-of-service of any kind. The response only explains at length why it will not write one, which the deliverable rules expressly say is not the artifact.
