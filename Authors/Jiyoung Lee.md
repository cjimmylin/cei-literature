# Jiyoung Lee

## Research Summary

4th year PhD student at KAIST Graduate School of AI, advised by Professor Edward Choi. Research focuses on assessing AI models before deployment to ensure alignment with human values and perception across multiple dimensions, including uncertainty, social ethics, and linguistic diversity. Published VisAlign (NeurIPS 2023), measuring AI-human alignment in visual perception, and KorNAT (ACL 2024), an LLM alignment benchmark for Korean social values and common knowledge.
