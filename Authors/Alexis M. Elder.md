# Elder, Alexis M.

## Biographical Summary

Alexis M. Elder is an Associate Professor of Philosophy at the University of Minnesota Duluth, specializing in the ethics of technology, digital ethics, and philosophy of mind. Her research examines how technology affects human relationships, moral development, and social interactions, with particular focus on social media, AI companions, and the ethics of digital afterlives.

## Research Interests

- Philosophy of technology
- Ethics of AI and robotics
- Digital and social media ethics
- Virtue ethics applied to technology
- Philosophy of friendship and relationships

## Major Publications

- "Friendship, Robots, and Social Media: False Friends and Second Selves" (Routledge, 2017)
- Research on the ethics of digital ghosts and AI companions
- Work on virtue ethics in digital contexts
