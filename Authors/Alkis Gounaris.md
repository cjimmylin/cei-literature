# Alkis Gounaris

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alkis Gounaris is a Research Fellow at the Applied Philosophy Research Laboratory of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA) and a Senior Member of the PPE (Philosophy, Politics, and Economics) Lab. His academic background bridges philosophy and cognitive science, with a particular focus on the ethical implications of autonomous systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alkis's research explores the philosophical foundations of AI ethics, specifically regarding cognitive agency and the ethical boundaries of embodied cognition. He is involved in developing frameworks for applied ethics in AI, focusing on how human-AI interaction can be grounded in robust philosophical principles.
