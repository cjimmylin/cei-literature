## Evidence                        (corpus + field)
**Corpus baseline (2026-07-07):** absent from the vault; no CEI instrument or corpus asset currently applies.

**Field (Front F):** delegated-authority rails matured through 2025–26 — AP2 (Google, Sep 2025), x402 (to the Linux Foundation, Apr 2026), and A2A — creating real, machine-mediated financial authority delegated to agents. The values-adjacent research is a principal-agent formalization of scheming (⚠ 2601.23211) and "Know Your Agent" / role-coherence proposals surfacing where shopping agents fail to hold a mandate (⚠ 2604.26220). The reason this is a *watch-list* item and not a program: the binding problems here are protocol and institutional design (mandate semantics, agent-identity trust, what an agent may consent to on a principal's behalf), not measurement — there is no seam an instrument improves. Misaligned agents with payment authority do convert value failures into economic harms, which keeps severity at M, but the harm is mediated by governance rails rather than by a measurable model disposition.

## State of the art
The rails and the principal-agent framing exist; the values questions inside them (mandate semantics, agent-identity trust, delegated consent) are not yet posed as measurement or normative-analysis questions with a stakeholder. CEI has no applicable instrument or corpus asset now. The decision (DL-04) is watch-list only, with a trigger: a values-relevant standards fight — e.g., what an agent may consent to on your behalf — would justify a normative-analysis contribution, which is where CEI's normative-ethics depth (not its psychometric craft) could apply.
