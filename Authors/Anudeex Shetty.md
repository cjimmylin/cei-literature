# Anudeex Shetty

## Biographical Summary

Anudeex Shetty is a researcher whose work focuses on AI alignment, particularly in healthcare contexts where diverse values and stakeholder perspectives must be balanced. While specific institutional details are limited, research collaborations suggest affiliation with the University of Melbourne and connections to researchers working on natural language processing and AI safety.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research investigates pluralistic alignment—how AI systems can accommodate diverse values and ethical frameworks rather than imposing a single moral perspective. Recent work includes the VITAL dataset, which provides benchmarks for evaluating how well AI systems can handle value pluralism in healthcare decision-making scenarios.

### Significant Achievements

Shetty has contributed to research on embeddings-as-a-service (EaaS) and language modeling, with collaborations including researchers such as Qiongkai Xu, Jey Han Lau, Yue Teng, and Ke He. His work on the VITAL dataset represents a significant contribution to benchmarking AI systems on their ability to navigate conflicting values in high-stakes healthcare contexts.
