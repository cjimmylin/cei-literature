# Abeba Birhane

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abeba Birhane is a cognitive scientist who explores the dynamic relationship between ubiquitous technologies, personhood, and society. She serves as a Senior Advisor in AI Accountability at the Mozilla Foundation and an Adjunct Assistant Professor at Trinity College Dublin. Her interdisciplinary work is recognized for uncovering systemic biases in large-scale machine learning datasets.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dr. Birhane's research program focuses on AI accountability, investigating how computational models and datasets shape human experience through the lenses of complexity science and critical data studies. She is particularly interested in the social implications of AI systems and the challenges of creating equitable algorithmic governance.

### Significant Achievements
Named one of TIME's 100 most influential people in AI (2023), she has also served on the UN Secretary-General’s AI Advisory Body. Her research on large image datasets has led to the removal of thousands of harmful labels in widely used computer vision repositories.

## Major Publications

- [The Algorithmic Colonization of Africa](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3351095.3375662) (2020) - *Critically examines AI through an anti-colonial lens.*
