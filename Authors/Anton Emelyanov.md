# Anton Emelyanov

## Biographical Summary

Anton Emelyanov is a researcher at Sber AI, contributing to the development of evaluation frameworks and benchmarks for Russian language models. His work focuses on creating comprehensive assessment tools that measure LLM capabilities across various linguistic and reasoning tasks in the Russian language context.

## Research Interests

- **Russian Language Models**: Developing and evaluating NLP systems for Russian
- **LLM Benchmarking**: Creating comprehensive evaluation suites for language models
- **Machine Learning**: Applied ML research for natural language understanding
