# Atoosa Kasirzadeh

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Atoosa Kasirzadeh is an Assistant Professor at Carnegie Mellon University and a part-time Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. She holds two doctoral degrees, one in Philosophy of Science and Technology and another in Applied Mathematics. Her work investigates the societal and philosophical impacts of AI through an interdisciplinary lens combining philosophy, systems theory, and machine learning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Atoosa's research program focuses on AI safety, specifically sociotechnical value alignment for generative models, algorithmic fairness, and the governance of AGI. She explores how philosophical frameworks can be operationalized to ensure AI systems align with human values and justice.

### Significant Achievements
A 2024 Schmidt Sciences AI2050 Early Career Fellow, she also serves as a council member of the World Economic Forum's Global Future Council on AGI. She has held prominent positions at the University of Edinburgh's Centre for Technomoral Futures and The Alan Turing Institute.

## Major Publications

- [In conversation with AI: Aligning language models with human values](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17548) (2023) - *Explores the challenges of value alignment in LLMs.*
- [Algorithmic fairness and structural injustice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.10301) (2021) - *Critically examines the limitations of mathematical fairness metrics in capturing systemic inequality.*
