## Abstract

Evaluates cultural values in GPT-4o, YandexGPT, GigaChat, Claude Sonnet, and Gemini using Hofstede's framework. YandexGPT and GigaChat show collectivist tendencies; Gemini and Claude lean individualistic Western values. First study including underrepresented Russian models.
