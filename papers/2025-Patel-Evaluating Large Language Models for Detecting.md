## Abstract

First study exploring LLM capabilities in detecting antisemitism using the IHRA definition as policy. Designs novel Guided-CoT prompt that injects domain-specific reasoning, increasing performance and reducing refusals across all models. Llama 3.1 70B outperforms fine-tuned GPT-3.5. Demonstrates effectiveness of in-context definition injection for specialized hate speech detection.
