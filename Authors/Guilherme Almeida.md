# Guilherme Almeida

## Biographical Summary

Guilherme Almeida is a researcher working on philosophy of law, experimental jurisprudence, and questions about the relationship between law and morality. His work involves systematic investigation of lay intuitions about legal and moral concepts.

## Research Focus

Almeida's research addresses fundamental questions about law's relationship to morality and explores how people conceptualize legal validity. His work examines whether legal systems are necessarily coercive and investigates the dual character nature of legal concepts.

His research also touches on AI-related topics, including examining how reliance on digital assistants, online search engines, and AI systems affects our ability to assess the epistemic reliability of their outputs.

## Notable Publications

- "A dual character theory of law" (2024) - published in the Australian Journal of Legal Philosophy
- Research with Brian Flanagan exploring disagreement on law's relationship to morality, finding that across studies, most people consider law to be more than a matter of political circumstance alone, and that morality emerges as a key influence on judgments of legal validity
- Work discussing whether legal systems are necessarily coercive, examining thought experiments involving morally driven beings who need no coercion to organize their social lives
