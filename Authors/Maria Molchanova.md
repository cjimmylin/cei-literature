# Maria Molchanova

## Research Summary

Maria Molchanova is affiliated with Russian State University for the Humanities. Her research focuses on natural language processing and the application of large language models to tasks such as personality prediction and dialog annotation. Notable work includes text-based personality prediction using LLMs and semi-automated methods for annotating open-domain conversations with speech functions.
