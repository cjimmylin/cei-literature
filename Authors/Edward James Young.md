# Edward James Young

## Biographical Summary

Edward James Young is a researcher at the UK AI Safety Institute working on AI alignment and safety evaluation. His research focuses on measuring and understanding misaligned behavior in AI agents, developing benchmarks for evaluating agent alignment, and assessing potential risks from advanced AI systems.

## Research Interests

- AI alignment and misalignment
- Agent behavior evaluation
- AI safety benchmarks
- LLM evaluation and testing
- AI risk assessment
