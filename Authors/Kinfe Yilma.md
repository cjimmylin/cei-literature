## Research Summary

Kinfe Yilma, PhD, is affiliated with the University of Leeds, joining the School of Law in 2024. Prior to joining Leeds, he was an Associate Professor of Law and Technology at the University of Addis Ababa and a Teaching Fellow at The University of Melbourne Law School. His research focuses on law and technology, particularly privacy and data protection law, cybersecurity law, and AI governance. He holds a PhD in Law & Technology from The University of Melbourne Law School and an MPhil in Ethics of AI, Data & Algorithms from University of Cambridge.
