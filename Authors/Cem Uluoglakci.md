# Cem Uluoglakci

## Biographical Summary

Cem Uluoglakci is a PhD student at Middle East Technical University (METU) in Turkey. His research focuses on the technical challenges of deploying Large Language Models, particularly in the areas of truthfulness and reliability.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Hallucinations in Large Language Models"—identifying when and why models generate factually incorrect information. He is a key contributor to the "HypoTermQA" benchmark, which uses hypothetical terms to stress-test the tendency of LLMs to invent information rather than admit ignorance.

### Significant Achievements
Uluoglakci's work on hallucination tendency provides critical tools for the "AI Safety" community. By developing datasets that target the limits of model knowledge, he helps researchers create more "Groundable" and trustworthy AI agents that can distinguish between fact and fiction.

## Major Publications
