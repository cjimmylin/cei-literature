# Brenda Odero

## Biographical Summary

Brenda Odero is a legal researcher and ethicist based at Strathmore University in Nairobi and the University of KwaZulu-Natal. She is a specialist in the ethical governance of digital technologies in Africa, with a particular focus on how indigenous philosophies can inform modern AI regulation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Odero's research centers on "The Ubuntu Way"—a framework for integrating the African philosophy of Ubuntu (communalism, interconnectedness) into the ethical review of AI in healthcare. She investigates how this "relational" approach to ethics provides a necessary alternative to the individualistic Western models of bioethics and AI governance.

### Significant Achievements
She has developed practical guidelines for Kenyan Research Ethics Committees (RECs) to better evaluate AI-driven health research. Her work advocates for "community-oriented" decision-making in AI, ensuring that the benefits and risks of technology are shared equitably across African societies.
