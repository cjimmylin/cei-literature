# Anne Lauscher

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Anne Lauscher is an Associate Professor of Data Science at the University of Hamburg. She earned her PhD summa cum laude from the University of Mannheim, specializing in language representations and computational argumentation. She has held research positions at Bocconi University, Grammarly, and the Allen Institute for AI. She is a prominent figure in the AI ethics community, recognized as one of the "100 Brilliant Women in AI Ethics."

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anne’s research group focuses on building "Conversational AI" systems that are fair, inclusive, and sustainable. She investigates the integration of demographic factors into language processing to improve system fairness and reduce sexist or racist biases. She also works on computational argumentation and developing more energy-efficient AI models.

### Significant Achievements
She has made major contributions to the study of bias in NLP and the development of ethical AI frameworks. Her work on computational argumentation is widely cited, and her focus on the intersection of language, ethics, and sustainability represents a critical frontier in modern AI research.
