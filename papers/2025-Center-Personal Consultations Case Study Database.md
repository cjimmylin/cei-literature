# Personal Consultations Case Study Database

## Abstract

A case study database containing records of personal consultations for ethics and values research. The database provides real-world examples of ethical decision-making scenarios and consultations that can be used to study how individuals and organizations approach moral dilemmas and value conflicts in practice.

## Authors

[[Center]], [[NCBC Bioethics]]

## Detailed Summary

The National Catholic Bioethics Center (NCBC) has developed a database of anonymized case studies from their Personal Consultations Department, primarily designed for use in university courses and professional training in Catholic bioethics. These case studies are derived from real ethics consultation requests that the Center has received, providing authentic scenarios that help students develop listening skills, critical thinking abilities, and appreciation for patient experiences while enhancing Catholic medical-moral education.

The database contains de-identified consultation questions sourced from actual ethics requests. Each case study included in the database was created with explicit permission from the original consultation requester, who also participated in developing the anonymized materials. This ensures both ethical sourcing of the content and appropriate privacy protections. Educators have noted the particular value of these materials because students know they represent genuine ethical dilemmas encountered in real healthcare and life situations.

Access to the database is restricted to qualified educators. The intended users are professors and mission integration officers working in higher education or professional training contexts. Prospective users must complete an application providing their name, institution, academic field, Google email address, and an explanation of how they plan to use the cases. For those outside academic settings who wish to engage with NCBC case study materials, the Center offers alternative resources including case studies in the revised Handbook on Critical Life Issues, research-related cases in A Catholic Guide to Ethical Clinical Research, and various free online resources and journal articles.

## Methods Summary

The case study database was developed through the NCBC's Personal Consultations Department by anonymizing and de-identifying actual ethics consultation requests. Each case included explicit consent from the original requester, who participated in creating the anonymized version. The database is maintained as a controlled-access resource requiring educator applications.

## Results Summary

The database provides a collection of real-world bioethics case studies for educational purposes. Specific metrics on the number of cases or topic distributions are not publicly available, as the actual content requires authorized academic access.

## Conclusions

The NCBC Case Study Database represents an educational resource specifically designed to support Catholic medical-moral education through authentic, real-world ethical dilemmas. By basing cases on actual consultations with proper consent and anonymization, the resource offers students and professionals realistic scenarios for developing ethical reasoning skills within a Catholic framework.
