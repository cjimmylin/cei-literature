## Abstract

This paper proposes using role-playing games riddled with moral dilemmas to bridge the gap in engineering artificial virtuous agents. It shows how a systemic role-playing game can be designed to develop virtues within an artificial agent, using affinity-based reinforcement learning and explainable AI.
