# Neel Guha

## Research Summary

Neel Guha is a sixth year JD-PhD student in Computer Science at Stanford University, advised by Chris Ré. He is affiliated with Hazy Research Lab, the Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models, RegLab, and liftlab. His research focuses on AI and law at the intersection of both fields, including building and evaluating AI systems for legal applications, expanding access to justice through AI, and examining challenges in AI governance and regulation. He is currently on the legal academic job market (PhD 2020-2026, JD 2020-2023).
