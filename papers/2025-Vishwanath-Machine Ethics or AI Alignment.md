## Abstract

AI, in its current form, can be argued to be utilitarian (a form of consequentialism), by design. In supervised learning, an objective function is optimized to maximize accuracy or minimize error in predictions, while in reinforcement learning, a reward function is maximized. This paper examines the relationship between Machine Ethics and AI Alignment fields and their approaches to ethical AI.
