## Research Summary

Chaoyu Li is a PhD student in Computer Science at Arizona State University, specializing in multimodal large language models for video understanding. His research addresses critical challenges in how MLLMs process and reason about video content, with particular focus on hallucination detection and accessibility.

## Key Publications

**VidHalluc (CVPR 2025)**
Lead author of "VIDHALLUC: Evaluating Temporal Hallucinations in Multimodal Large Language Models for Video Understanding," accepted to CVPR 2025. VidHalluc is the largest benchmark for evaluating hallucinations across action, temporal sequence, and scene transitions in video understanding. The work also proposes DINO-HEAL, a training-free method that mitigates hallucinations by integrating spatial saliency from DINOv2.

**VideoA11y (CHI 2025)**
Co-author of "VideoA11y: Method and Dataset for Accessible Video Description," accepted to CHI 2025. VideoA11y leverages multimodal large language models and video accessibility guidelines to generate descriptions tailored for blind and low vision (BLV) individuals, addressing the critical need for accessible video content.
