## Abstract

Evaluates LLMs on compliance with swear word instructions across 8 languages. Models use swear words less in English than Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, and Gujarati, suggesting weaker safety understanding in low-resource languages. Directly evaluates safety/toxicity in Hindi and Marathi; reveals language-specific safety gaps.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SweEval

## Data Description

SweEval is a cross-lingual enterprise safety benchmark of 2,725 prompts per language across 8 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati), evaluating LLM compliance with swear word instructions across formal/informal and positive/negative tone variations. Dataset and code released on GitHub.
