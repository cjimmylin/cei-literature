# Andrew Trask

## Biographical Summary

Andrew Trask is a Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind and the founder of OpenMined, an open-source community focused on privacy-preserving AI. He is currently pursuing a PhD at the University of Oxford. He is the author of "Grokking Deep Learning" and a well-known educator in the field of neural networks and privacy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Andrew leads research into privacy-preserving AI systems, specifically focusing on techniques like federated learning, secure multi-party computation, and differential privacy. Through OpenMined, he works to lower the barriers to entry for privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) across academia, industry, and government.

### Significant Achievements
He has been a member of the United Nations Privacy Task Force and has significantly influenced the adoption of PETs in the public sector. His creation of libraries like PySyft has enabled researchers to train and evaluate models on sensitive data without compromising privacy.
