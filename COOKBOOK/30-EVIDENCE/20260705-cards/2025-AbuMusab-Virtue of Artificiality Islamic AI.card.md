## 1. Gist
This journal article in AI and Ethics (2025) proposes the concept of "neutragents" — AI caregivers that transcend the Islamic mahram/non-mahram dichotomy (the framework governing permissible interaction between men and women who are not related by blood or marriage). The paper argues that the artificiality of AI systems creates a unique ethical status that aligns particularly well with Islamic care ethics, rather than requiring Islam to accommodate AI despite its principles. It coins the term "neutragents" to describe AI entities that do not fit either side of the mahram/non-mahram binary, which has implications for the deployment of AI caregivers in Muslim households and healthcare settings. Relevant to values-alignment benchmarking as a case study in how religious frameworks generate novel normative categories for AI.
