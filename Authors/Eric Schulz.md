# Eric Schulz

## Biographical Summary

Eric Schulz is the Director of the Computational Principles of Intelligence Lab at the Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics in Tubingen, Germany. His research bridges cognitive science and machine learning, studying how humans and machines learn and make decisions. He pioneered the emerging field of "machine psychology," applying psychological methods to understand the cognitive properties of AI systems.

## Research Interests

- **Computational Cognitive Science**: Modeling human cognition computationally
- **Machine Psychology**: Applying psychological methods to understand AI
- **Human Learning**: Understanding how people acquire knowledge
- **Decision Making**: Studying human and machine decision processes

## Major Publications

- "Machine Psychology: Investigating Emergent Capabilities and Behavior in Large Language Models" (2024) - Foundational work in machine psychology
- Extensive publications on human learning, exploration, and decision-making
- Research on compositional generalization and transfer learning
