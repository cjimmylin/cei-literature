# Peter S. Park

## Research Summary

Peter S. Park is the Vitalik Buterin Postdoctoral Fellow in AI Existential Safety at MIT, working in the Tegmark Lab. He is also the Director of StakeOut.AI. His research focuses on AI, cognition, evolution, mathematics, and game theory, with major contributions including work on AI deception and potential solutions.
