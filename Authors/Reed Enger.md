# Reed Enger

## Biographical Summary

Reed Enger is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, contributing to research on the ethics and safety of advanced AI systems. He was a contributor to the comprehensive report on the ethics of advanced AI assistants, which examines the societal implications, risks, and governance challenges of increasingly capable AI assistant systems.

## Research Interests

- **AI Ethics**: Examining moral and societal implications of advanced AI systems
- **AI Safety**: Ensuring AI systems behave safely and beneficially
- **Human-AI Interaction**: Understanding how humans interact with AI assistants

## Major Publications

- "The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants" (2024) - Comprehensive analysis of ethical considerations for AI assistants (with the DeepMind Ethics team)
