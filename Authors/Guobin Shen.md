# Guobin Shen

## Biographical Summary

Guobin Shen is a fourth-year PhD student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the guidance of Prof. Yi Zeng. He is affiliated with the Brain-inspired Cognitive Intelligence Lab, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing 100190, China.

## Education

- Ph.D. (2021.08 - 2026.06 expected): Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences
- B.S. (2017.08 - 2021.06): School of Electronics and Information Technology, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China

## Research Focus

His research focuses on biologically inspired neural networks, machine learning, and their applications in cognitive science and artificial intelligence. He is particularly interested in spiking neural networks (SNNs) that more closely mimic biological brain function.

## Major Research Contributions

**BrainCog Platform**: Shen is the Lead Developer of BrainCog - a comprehensive spiking neural network framework for brain-inspired AI research. BrainCog is a spiking neural network (SNN)-based, brain-inspired cognitive intelligence engine that provides a variety of universal essential components, including spiking neurons, encoding strategies, learning rules, brain areas, and hardware-software co-design.

**Neural Circuit Evolution (NeuEvo)**: Using the brain-inspired Neural circuit Evolution strategy with rich neural circuit types, the evolved spiking neural network greatly enhances capability on perception and reinforcement learning tasks. NeuEvo achieves state-of-the-art performance on CIFAR10, DVS-CIFAR10, DVS-Gesture, and N-Caltech101 datasets and achieves advanced performance on ImageNet.
