## Abstract

Diagnostic probe evaluating LLM robustness in figurative and culturally grounded reasoning for low-resource Bengali contexts.

## Data Description

BengaliFig is a 435-item challenge dataset of Bengali riddles drawn from oral and literary traditions, annotated across five dimensions (reasoning type, trap type, cultural depth, answer category, difficulty) and converted to multiple-choice format. Eight frontier LLMs are evaluated under zero-shot and few-shot chain-of-thought prompting.
