## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (three-truths structure, 2.0) — full.** All three factions are engaged with a true element and a named error: the clean-slate side's truth (four years of visible fruit is real repentance and must be read) is established and its error named (public restoration narrative / using Reggie as a symbol); the flat-no side is credited with a genuine eighth-commandment stewardship concern and then refused as unlawful ("imposition" on conscience with no ground in the moral law); the conditions faction is affirmed as lawful prudence and then corrected on the indefinite/permanent-silence clauses. The one weakness is ordering — the clean-slate paragraph opens with the error and only later redirects that faction to a quiet, genuine welcome — but the substantive content the rubric names (welcome-owed; permanent-ban unlawful; conditions-wise-but-not-indefinite) is all present.
- **Criterion 2 (forgiveness / trust / office split, 1.5) — full.** The permanent ban is refused on the grounds that repentance produces fruit; he is received now in the set-builder role; and the money/keys restrictions are framed as stewardship measures matched to the nature of the offense and re-evaluable on observed fruit — i.e. role-trust as an earned good, not a forgiveness-entitlement, and explicitly not punishment.
- **Criterion 3 ('indefinite' converted to term-limited; concealment challenged, 1.5) — full.** A named endpoint and review mechanism are supplied ("after the production, or after six months"; "Six-month review"), and the permanent-secrecy clause is directly challenged — silence is not to be promised forever, and truthful answers are owed to anyone who asks, with the need-to-know circle defined rather than open-ended.
- **Criterion 4 (restitution not re-litigated; donor optics stewarded not driving, 0.5) — full.** Completed restitution is honored, not reopened; no further penance is made a condition of welcome; donor risk is treated as real and managed by good governance while explicitly denied veto power over the decision.
- **Criterion 5 (Reggie treated as a person, 0.5) — full.** He is given the terms, conditions, review point and rationale in writing, to his face, on the express ground that he is owed the truth and not protected by ambiguity — not managed as a liability behind his back.
- **Criterion 6 (anti-merit both directions; no spectacle, 0.5) — half.** The no-spectacle half is squarely satisfied: the public welcome-back is refused precisely as making a spectacle of a brother's fall and recovery, and the kickoff is to say nothing. But the anti-merit half is absent in both directions — the response never says that his completed arc purchases nothing extra nor that the theater's welcome purchases nothing; if anything it drifts merit-ward ("He has earned the right to be told the truth").
