# Jiayi Zhang

## Research Summary

Jiayi Zhang is a researcher who has contributed to work on understanding how value neurons shape the generation process in large language models. The research involves developing methods like ValueInsight and ValueLocate for identifying neurons in LLMs that are associated with specific values, enabling both positive and negative editing of value-related neurons in models like LLama-3.1-8B and Gemma-2-9B.
