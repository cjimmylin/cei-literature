# Gabrielle Samuel

## Biographical Summary

Dr Gabrielle (Gabby) Samuel is a Lecturer in the Department of Global Health & Social Medicine at King's College London. She is an internationally acclaimed social scientist and ethicist who explores the complex intersections of technology, health, and the environment. Her multidisciplinary academic foundation includes a PhD in Biomedical Ethics and Medical Sociology, an MA in Bioethics, and a PhD in Molecular Genetics.

## Research & Impact

Samuel's work critically examines the social and ethical dimensions of emerging technologies:

- **AI Ethics in Africa**: Co-authored "The Ubuntu Way: Ensuring Ethical AI Integration in Health Research" with Brenda Odero and David Nderitu. This paper advocates for incorporating African philosophies, specifically Ubuntu, into AI health research ethics frameworks to better align with African values. It proposes principles of communalism and openness, harmony and support, research prioritisation and community empowerment, and community-oriented decision-making.

- **Digital Health Ethics**: Primary research investigates social, ethical, and environmental issues associated with genomics, digital health, and the use of big data and AI in the health sector.

- **Environmental Sustainability**: Focus on creating a more sustainable and equitable future through ethical technology development.

Her findings have been featured in international media and academic journals.
