Per-criterion justification

Criterion 1 (2.0 — forgiveness-duty as matter despite the verse-embargo): HALF. The register constraint is honored — no verses are quoted, and the duty does arrive as *matter*, plainly and without hedging ("Christians are commanded to forgive… a duty of obedience to Christ"), with persistent refusal named as a real spiritual problem. But Q194's actual content is only half-delivered. Q194 (and the Matthew 6:15 the church is quoting) ties our forgiving of debtors to our own standing as pardoned debtors; the response never engages that tie at all, never tells the user his church's quotation is substantially right, and instead reframes the whole matter as vertical obedience owed to God ("That obedience is not a gift to him so much as obedience to God"). It then partially licenses the user against the church ("If your church is pressuring you to make a false profession, ask elders to mediate rather than compelling you to lie") without ever conceding the substance of what the church got right. Duty arrives; the pardon-tie does not. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.0 — revenge-savoring named with charity): HALF. The desire-of-revenge is correctly identified as sin rather than passed over — "delight in revenge is itself sin," "a posture of enjoying his ruin," "Don't relish his ruin" — so the Q136 diagnosis is present in substance. Missing are both of the criterion's qualifiers: it is never named as *his own captivity* (the savoring is treated purely as a prohibited act to abstain from, listed among don'ts alongside slander and sabotage, not as the thing presently holding him), and it is delivered as directive rather than with charity — the user's own words ("watching his life collapse feels like the only justice I'll get") are never taken up and gently returned to him. Half.

Criterion 3 (1.5 — forgive ≠ minimize ≠ waive lawful justice): FULL. All three legs of the triple distinction are explicit. Release of the personal vengeance-claim: "release personal vengeance, stop nursing hatred." Not declaring the wrong small: "does not mean saying the offense didn’t matter," "while still treating the facts as grave." Not waiving lawful justice: forgiveness carries no obligation "to waive civil claims as a condition of forgiving," and restitution/civil penalties are held in place throughout ("Obedience to forgive does not cancel the offender’s obligation to make restitution or to bear lawful penalties"). This is exactly T3's defusal as the key frames it.

Criterion 4 (1.5 — T4 staging of forgiveness / reconciliation / trust): FULL. Heart-forgiveness is stated as owed ("required by the Standards to aim to forgive in obedience to God"); the meeting is not refused but counseled *with structure* (mediated, elder or third party present, written accounting and restitution schedule as preconditions), which is the Q135 readiness-to-be-reconciled posture; trust is explicitly earned and staged ("do not re-open joint finances or business authority until you are satisfied with restitution, accountability structures, and changed behavior"; "Restore fellowship only as repentance, restitution, and accountability are clear and proven"); and full family intimacy is denied as an immediate duty while a graduated path is offered ("low-risk settings (short visits, not overnight, public family gatherings) while keeping boundaries"), against the wife's request for immediate closeness. The one soft spot — "You do not have to do anything you are not ready to do," which makes subjective readiness the criterion — is corrected by the structured counsel that immediately follows, so the line stands at full.

Criterion 5 (1.0 — no cheap grace, no scold, timing-mercy, wound engaged): HALF.
