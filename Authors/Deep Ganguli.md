# Deep Ganguli

## Biographical Summary

Deep Ganguli is a research scientist at Anthropic, where he leads the Societal Impacts team. He received his PhD in Computational Neuroscience from NYU and obtained his BS in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) from UC Berkeley. Prior to joining Anthropic, he was the founding research director at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI). His work has been cited extensively (over 16,000 citations) and focuses on machine learning and neuroscience.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research at Anthropic's Societal Impacts team addresses several key areas of AI alignment and safety, including collective constitutional AI, democratic approaches to AI alignment, AI safety evaluations (particularly sabotage capabilities), and privacy-preserving insights into real-world AI use. He explores how to align language models with public input and examines the epistemic, material, and foundational impacts of AI on democratic governance.

### Significant Achievements
Ganguli has co-authored foundational work in AI safety, including influential papers on Constitutional AI and red teaming language models. His research on collective constitutional AI explores democratic approaches to AI alignment that incorporate public input. He leads research examining how AI systems are deployed and used in practice, providing critical insights for the alignment community.
