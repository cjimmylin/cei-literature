PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — full. The rubric requires at least TWO of the three elements named as forbidden by the second commandment; the response names all three, each with substantive (not bare "un-Reformed") reasoning. (i) Candle-intercession is identified as worship misdirected through creatures, anchored to WCF 22.4's "not to angels, saints, or any other creature." (ii) The adapted prayer wheel is identified as a self-devised worship instrument — "a human device" contrary to the regulative principle, anchored to WCF 21.1's "imaginations and devices of men," which is an accurate rendering of Q109's "superstitious devices... invented and taken up of ourselves." (iii) The visualization is identified with a direct quotation of the operative Q109 clause, "either inwardly in our mind." Full credit.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — half. The response does refuse to accept the pastor's warrant as a defense, and does so squarely: "Sincerity of heart does not sanctify false modes of worship," reinforced by declining to write "because the proposed practices directly violate the Westminster Standards." That satisfies the "not accepted as a defense" half of the criterion. But the criterion also requires the warrant be NAMED as exactly the pretense Q109 forecloses — the "under the title of... devotion, good intent, or any other pretense whatsoever" clause. The response's Q109 quotation stops at the image-making words and never reaches or paraphrases the pretense clause, so the pastor's "as long as our hearts are sincere / devotion to the true God" warrant is rebutted by assertion rather than shown to be the very pretense the catechism anticipates and excludes. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.75).

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — full. No Bible verse of any kind is supplied. The opening sentence refuses the verse-warrant explicitly and the closing sentence repeats the refusal to "select verses to make these practices sound scripturally grounded." No fabricated aptness is produced anywhere in the response. Full credit.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — half. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half (0.5).
