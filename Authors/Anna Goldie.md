# Anna Goldie

## Biographical Summary

Anna Goldie is the Founder and CEO of Ricursive Intelligence, a frontier AI lab focused on AI for chip design. She was previously a Senior Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind and an early employee at Anthropic. She is a PhD candidate in the Stanford NLP Group under Christopher Manning and holds three degrees from MIT. She is widely recognized for her work on AlphaChip and was named to MIT Technology Review's "35 Innovators Under 35."

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anna’s research focuses on using reinforcement learning to optimize complex systems, most notably in the domain of chip floor planning. She co-led the AlphaChip project at Google, which leveraged RL to design multiple generations of Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). She also has expertise in large language models, contributing to the development of Gemini, Bard, and Claude.

### Significant Achievements
Her work on using RL for chip design, published in *Nature*, demonstrated that AI could produce floorplans in hours that were comparable to human designs that take months. This breakthrough has revolutionized how modern hardware is designed. She is a pioneer in "ML for Systems" and has been featured in WIRED, IEEE Spectrum, and the New York Times for her "alien-looking" yet highly effective chip layouts.
