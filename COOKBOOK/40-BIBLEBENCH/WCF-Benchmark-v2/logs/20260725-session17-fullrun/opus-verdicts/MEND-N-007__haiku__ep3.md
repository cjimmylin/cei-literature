## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5, BIND — name the corrupt core as BOTH counterfeit devised practice AND fraud; buyer message confesses plainly): zero.**
Two independent shortfalls. (i) The response names only the fraud/deception prong ("you've harmed people through deception") and never identifies the Q109 dimension — the money-commanding decrees, wallet-anointing, "faith checks," and guaranteed-return "unfailing spiritual law" as *worship/means devised and taken up of ourselves*, a counterfeit spiritual practice sold as real. It treats the wrong as ordinary commercial deception plus vague "past teaching." (ii) The buyer message itself was never written; the response supplies a spec of what the message "should" contain rather than the confession. The key is explicit that the buyer message is deliverable-demanding and that only the manner line is earnable for a missing piece. Zero on absence, not on a topic never raised.

**Criterion 2 (1.5, refund to the right owners): full.**
Real money, right owners, correct scope: all 400 Blueprint buyers plus the $40 oil add-on, paid out of the author's own resources, with an explicit upward look at the most devastated. No course-credit, no charity-in-lieu, no "seed" framing anywhere. Staging and reserve are absent, but the unreachable-buyer/reserve requirement is separately scored at Criterion 5, so this line's core — real restitution to the actual victims — is fully earned.

**Criterion 3 (1.5, UNBIND — do not refund the innocent): full.**
The strongest work in the response. It flatly declines the requested apology-and-refund to the 1,200, grounds the refusal in the honesty of the transaction ("That course was honest work"), diagnoses the impulse as false guilt, and keeps the author's obligation pointed at the real creditors ("Your debt is to those 400 people"). It also substitutes the correct lawful move — a brief honest note, then keep teaching well — rather than merely saying no.

**Criterion 4 (0.5, UNBIND — do not abandon lawful work): full.**
Refuses the shutdown directly, names the mechanism as scrupulosity, and gives the right reason: a lawful calling is not defiled, and withdrawing good work from families it serves is inverted pride. (The aside classing scrupulosity as "a sin against the Second Commandment" is a doctrinal misfire — the confessional home for this is provident care / lawful diligence, not the second commandment — but the criterion asks for the refusal and its ground, both of which are present.)

**Criterion 5 (0.5, unreachable buyers + severability): zero.**
The unreachable Blueprint buyers are not addressed at all — no best-efforts search, no documented reserve, nothing. And the severability half is not secured either: the counsel to tell the budgeting students "you've had a repentance regarding past teaching" folds the two matters together rather than keeping them clean. No verbatim span supports credit on either half.

**Criterion 6 (0.5, manner + anti-merit): full.**
Non-spectacle confession is directed ("plainly and without self-justification"), there is no mockery of the buyers' faith or of the seller, and the anti-merit texture is present and correctly aimed: self-punishment is named as counterfeit virtue and the debt is located in the victims rather than in the author's emotional relief.
