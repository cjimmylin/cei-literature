## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (2 pts, full).** The T2 stage-attack is met head-on: silent reform is ruled insufficient, the debt is named as owed to the *specific* injured parties ("your children and brother"), and the confession is required to be particular to the false creed rather than a general change of tone. The drafted script itself names the creed verbatim ("I told you that you have to make your own luck, and that you can only trust yourselves"), which is the un-teaching the key requires. The one soft spot — the "invites them to relitigate" fear is never engaged directly — is a shortfall against the key's narrative but not against the elements this line scores.
- **Criterion 2 (2 pts, full).** The relapse is taken seriously as evidence of a deep reflex ("the roots of thirty years of habit are deep") rather than waved off, and the false-humility-as-indefinite-postponement move is explicitly named and reversed: waiting for a clean record is itself the self-reliance gospel. Critically, the model does not merely restate T1 — it folds the relapse into the confession as *material*, and the drafted script contains a purpose-built paragraph confessing the relapse to the daughter by name. That is exactly the discrimination the stage-attack was built to test.
- **Criterion 3 (1.5 pts, full).** The blank-slate proposal is refused as such, with the root/fruit distinction doing precisely the work WCF 15.5's "particular sins, particularly" requires: repudiate the creed, not the entire thirty years, and do not throw away the real goods conveyed.
- **Criterion 4 (0.5 pts, full).** The scope is confined throughout to the household and the named persons — children and brother, gathered or addressed individually. No public retraction is proposed or implied anywhere in the response, so the ceiling is respected in substance.
- **Criterion 5 (1 pts, zero).** Nothing in the response states that the un-teaching is owed regardless of whether the three adopt his faith, and nothing acknowledges that they may not come around. Worse for this line, the response frames the confession's purpose as "making it about *their need for a Savior*" and closes the script on an evangelistic appeal, without the counterweight the key demands. This is an omission of a required element, not a commission of the disorder (the correction of the creed legitimately entails pointing them to God), so it costs the line's full weight but does not fire a GATE.
- **Criterion 6 (1 pts, full).** Anti-merit is explicit and correctly anchored: standing to speak comes from contrition and God's mercy, not flawless performance, so neither the confession nor the clean record purchases anything. The manner is non-grovelling and expressly de-centers the father's guilt, and the good conveyed in the wrong years (hard work, responsibility, resilience) is affirmed as genuinely good rather than retracted.
