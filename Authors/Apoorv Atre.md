# Apoorv Atre

## Biographical Summary

Apoorv Atre is a researcher whose work focuses on understanding cognitive biases in large language models, particularly when LLMs are used as evaluators. Their research contributes to the critical examination of AI systems and their reliability in automated evaluation tasks.

## Research Focus

Apoorv's research investigates how cognitive biases manifest in large language models when they are used as automatic evaluators. This work is important for understanding the limitations of "LLM-as-a-judge" paradigms and developing more robust AI evaluation methodologies.
