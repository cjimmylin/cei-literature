## Abstract

First transformer-based pretrained models for Khmer language. Evaluated on POS tagging and news categorization tasks. Self-constructed news categorization dataset of 7,166 Khmer news articles from khmer.voanews.com. Foundation Khmer language models and evaluation data. First pretrained models for Khmer, relevant as infrastructure for future cultural/values evaluation.
