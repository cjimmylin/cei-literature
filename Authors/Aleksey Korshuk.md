# Aleksey Korshuk

## Biographical Summary

Aleksey Korshuk is a researcher and engineer focused on the intersection of user experience and AI safety. He is a Founding Engineer at Coframe and a core research scientist at Chai Research, where he develops safety frameworks for large-scale conversational platforms.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aleksey's work centers on creating "safer by design" conversational agents. He led the development of the Chai Platform's AI Safety Framework, researching how content safeguarding and operational transparency can be integrated into consumer AI products without compromising user delight or engagement.
