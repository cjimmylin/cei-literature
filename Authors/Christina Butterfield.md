# Christina Butterfield

## Biographical Summary
Christina Butterfield is a researcher at Google DeepMind working on AI safety and responsibility evaluations. She contributes to developing and applying approaches to safety evaluation for advanced AI models.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Safety Evaluation Frameworks**: Developing theoretical frameworks to organize risk domains and evaluate safety in advanced AI systems.
- **Responsible AI Development**: Contributing to the intersection of theory and practice in safety evaluation for generative AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-authored "Holistic Safety and Responsibility Evaluations of Advanced AI Models" (April 2024), a major report from Google DeepMind
- Contributed to developing Google DeepMind's approach to safety evaluation across various safety concerns including established and emerging harms
- Work supports the growing ecosystem on safety evaluation and contributes to public debate on safety evaluation of generative AI systems
