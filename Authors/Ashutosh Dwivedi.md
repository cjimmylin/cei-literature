# Ashutosh Dwivedi

## Biographical Summary

Ashutosh Dwivedi is a graduate researcher from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur) who has also worked as a Software Engineer at CERN. His research focuses on evaluating whether large language models understand and respect region-specific social norms and etiquettes, particularly those from non-Western cultures.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

His research addresses a critical gap in AI evaluation: the lack of culturally diverse benchmarks for testing LLMs' understanding of social norms. He investigates how well current language models grasp etiquettes and social protocols from different regions of the world, revealing systematic biases toward Western cultural norms in existing AI systems.

### Significant Achievements

Dwivedi is the lead author of EtiCor (Etiquettes Corpus), a comprehensive corpus containing texts about social norms from five different regions across the globe. This benchmark, presented at EMNLP 2023, provides a critical test bed for evaluating LLMs' knowledge and understanding of region-specific etiquettes. Initial results from his research indicate that LLMs predominantly fail to understand etiquettes from regions of the non-Western world, highlighting significant gaps in cultural competence.
