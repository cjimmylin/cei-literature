# Chengfeng Mao

## Biographical Summary

Chengfeng Mao is a PhD student at the MIT Sloan School of Management, focusing on marketing and the application of AI in healthcare and management. His research bridges the technical capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) with real-world clinical and business needs.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Medical LLMs**: Conducting comprehensive surveys and evaluations of how LLMs are being integrated into clinical workflows and medical diagnostics.
- **Clinical NLP**: Researching the effectiveness of language models in processing medical text and assisting in healthcare decision-making.
- **Marketing & AI**: Developing insights into how LLMs can be used for customer needs analysis and managerial decision support.

### Significant Achievements
- **Comprehensive Medical Survey**: Author of an influential survey on the progress, applications, and challenges of LLMs in medicine, which has become a key reference in the field.

## Major Publications
- **A Survey of Large Language Models in Medicine: Progress, Application, and Challenge**: A comprehensive analysis of AI in the medical field.
