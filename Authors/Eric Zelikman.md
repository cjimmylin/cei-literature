# Eric Zelikman

## Biographical Summary

Eric Zelikman is a PhD student in Computer Science at Stanford University, working with Percy Liang and Nick Haber. His research focuses on improving reasoning capabilities in large language models, particularly through self-improvement and chain-of-thought methods. He is known for his influential work on STaR (Self-Taught Reasoner) and other techniques for eliciting and enhancing model reasoning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Eric's research centers on developing methods for language models to improve their own reasoning abilities. His work on STaR demonstrated that models can bootstrap better reasoning by learning from their own correct solutions, a significant advance for AI self-improvement. He also works on evaluation methodologies for large language models.

### Significant Achievements
His STaR paper has been highly influential in the reasoning and self-improvement research communities. He has contributed to the HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) benchmark project at Stanford, helping establish comprehensive evaluation standards for foundation models.

## Major Publications

- **STaR: Bootstrapping Reasoning With Reasoning** (NeurIPS 2022) - Seminal work on self-improvement of reasoning in LLMs
- **Parsel: Algorithmic Reasoning with Language Models** - Work on structured reasoning approaches
- **Holistic Evaluation of Language Models** (TMLR 2023) - Co-author on comprehensive LLM benchmark
