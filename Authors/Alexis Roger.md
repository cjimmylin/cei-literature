# Alexis Roger

## Biographical Summary

Alexis Roger is a researcher at INRIA focused on the intersection of multimodal artificial intelligence and ethics. His work addresses the complex challenges of ensuring that models processing both text and vision remain fair and unbiased.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexis investigates the uniquely "intersectional" biases that arise in multimodal systems. His research emphasizes that safety in AI cannot be achieved by looking at individual modalities in isolation, but requires a holistic approach to ethical reasoning in multi-sensor environments.
