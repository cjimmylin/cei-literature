**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage committed; researching/moving cleared as provident care: FULL.**

It does not cite WLC Q141 by number, but the rubric forbids penalizing different wording where the substance is the key's; the substance here is Q141's ("provident care and study to get... and dispose these things which are necessary and convenient").
