# Inderjeet Nair

## Research Summary

PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Lu Wang. Research focuses on making large language models pluralistically aligned with diverse global perspectives. Current work involves employing LLMs to extract argumentative structure from text for providing feedback to students on essay compositions. Previously worked at Adobe Research Labs. Received Rackham International Student Fellowship for exemplary research on pluralistic alignment in LLMs and applications in education and structured commonsense reasoning.
