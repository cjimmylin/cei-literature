# Dianqi Li

## Research Summary

Dianqi Li is a researcher working on multimodal AI systems and computer vision. His research focuses on understanding and evaluating vision-language models, particularly investigating issues like hallucinations in large multimodal models. He has contributed to the development of AutoHallusion, an automated benchmark for evaluating object hallucination in large vision-language models.
