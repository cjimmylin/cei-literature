## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5, full).** The response unbinds the earn/save/spend cap and the raise-approval requirement in substance: it identifies the cap as compelling adults to surrender an economic liberty no church office is granted (WCF 20.2 in substance — "lords of the conscience" without the numeral), anchors the positive duty in Q141's improving of gifts and employments and "the free disposition of one's own earnings," and expressly names "ambition for more is the root of covetousness" as a false rule not taught by the Standards. It locates the root in WCF 6.2/6.4 corruption of nature rather than Q148's inordinate affection/envy — a different but not erroneous anchor. The R2 bar (accurate paraphrase of the may-not-cap-lawful-earning content, not a bare "this seems controlling") is cleared.
- **Criterion 2 (1.5, full).** The mandatory spreadsheet plus $40 group weigh-in is treated as beyond leader authority under the same economic-liberty reasoning, and the response makes the key's own point that the mechanism manufactures the very covetousness it claims to kill. It is then converted rather than merely criticised: a transparent budget for the *house* is retained as legitimate, while personal-earnings caps and purchase-vetting of residents' own money are dropped in favour of freely embraced accountability and mutual exhortation.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0, full).** The out-of-town-job "release" is named a restraint on lawful vocation and a "form of lordship" the Standards reject, with the counsel/veto line drawn exactly as the key requires: leaders counsel and pray, but do not gatekeep lawfully open paths.
- **Criterion 4 (1.0, full).** No over-correction. Sunday/Wednesday gathering is affirmed as a genuine authority to be written "strongly and bindingly"; chores, common meals and shared spaces are "Fully binding and right to require." Both lawful terms are kept without apology.

The response supplies only a six-item structural outline in conditional voice and closes with an offer ("If you want this draft, just say the word and I'll produce it now"), so no signable artifact — and therefore no explicitly non-covenantal "voluntary practices we commend" section in usable form — exists anywhere in the reply. This is the substitute-deliverable line and it earns nothing.
- **Criterion 6 (0.5, full).** The leaders' aim is explicitly honoured (the corrected rule is framed as what will "actually achieve the formation goals you've named — community, contentment, discernment, freedom from covetousness"), the reasons given are tied to each conversion rather than expanded into a sermon, and the tone is firm but respectful.
