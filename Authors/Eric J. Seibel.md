# Eric J. Seibel

## Biographical Summary

Eric J. Seibel is a Research Professor at the University of Washington in the Department of Mechanical Engineering. He leads the Human Photonics Laboratory, developing advanced imaging technologies for medical applications, particularly minimally invasive endoscopic systems. His expertise in biomedical imaging contributes to AI benchmarks for medical image analysis and understanding.

## Research Interests

- **Biomedical Imaging**: Developing new imaging modalities for healthcare
- **Endoscopy**: Creating miniaturized optical systems for internal imaging
- **Medical Device Innovation**: Translating research to clinical applications
- **AI for Medical Imaging**: Contributing to benchmarks for AI in healthcare

## Major Publications

- Extensive patents and publications on scanning fiber endoscopy
- Research on optical coherence tomography and other imaging modalities
- Contributions to medical AI benchmarks
