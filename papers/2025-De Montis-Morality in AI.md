## Abstract

This paper proposes a novel approach to integrate moral meaning directly into the architectural mechanisms and frameworks of transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs), moving beyond traditional fine-tuning methods. The authors argue that embedding **virtue ethics** and other moral frameworks at the architectural level is essential for creating AI systems with genuine **moral character**. The paper discusses the limitations of post-hoc alignment methods and advocates for "morality by design" in LLM development, where ethical principles like **wisdom**, **justice**, and **responsibility** are structurally encoded rather than retrofitted.
