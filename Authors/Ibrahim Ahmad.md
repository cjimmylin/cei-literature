# Ibrahim Ahmad

## Research Summary

Ibrahim Ahmad is a researcher who has contributed to African language NLP research, particularly in the area of sentiment analysis for African languages. He is a co-author on the AfriSenti project, which focuses on developing sentiment analysis resources and models for African languages. This work addresses the significant gap in NLP resources for African languages, which represent a substantial portion of global linguistic diversity but are severely underrepresented in NLP research and resources.
