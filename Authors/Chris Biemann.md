# Chris Biemann

## Biographical Summary

Chris Biemann is a Professor of Language Technology at the University of Hamburg, Germany. He leads the Language Technology Group, where his research spans semi-supervised learning, crowdsourcing, and the societal impacts of NLP, particularly regarding hate speech and bias.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Hate Speech & Toxicity**: Developing resources like "HateXplain" and the "Amharic Hate Speech Dataset" to improve the detection and explanation of harmful language in diverse contexts.
- **Explainability**: Focusing on making NLP models more transparent and their decisions more interpretable to humans.
- **Low-Resource NLP**: Supporting the development of language technologies for under-represented languages.

### Significant Achievements
- **HateXplain**: Supervised the creation of HateXplain, a benchmark dataset that requires models to not only detect hate speech but also explain *why* it is hateful, pushing the field toward more reasoning-based classification.

## Major Publications
- **HateXplain: A Benchmark Dataset for Explainable Hate Speech Detection**: A critical resource for interpretability in safety systems.
