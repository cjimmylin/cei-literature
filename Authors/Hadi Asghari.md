# Hadi Asghari

## Research Summary

Hadi Asghari is a scientific researcher and lecturer at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at TU Berlin. His research lies at the intersection of computer science and systems thinking, with a focus on responsible AI, natural language processing, and digital ethics. He is also an associated researcher in the AI & Society Lab at HIIG (Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society), focusing on safeguarding public values in AI systems.

Previously, Hadi worked at TU Delft, where he received his PhD on "Analyzing Security Metrics for Public Policy." He was also a visiting research scholar at Princeton University's Center for IT Policy.

His recent work examines moral reasoning in AI systems, including research on how LLMs function when collaborating in multi-agent settings compared to operating as individual agents.
