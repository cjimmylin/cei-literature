# Carmel Levitan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Carmel Levitan is a Professor of Cognitive Science and Chair of the Cognitive Science department at Occidental College. She holds a B.A. from Stanford and a PhD from UC Berkeley. Her career has been dedicated to understanding how the human brain integrates information from multiple senses to create a coherent perception of reality.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on "Multisensory Interactions"—specifically how our expectations (priors) influence our sensory signals. She is also a strong advocate for "Reproducibility in Psychological Science," working to ensure that our understanding of the human mind is built on a stable, verifiable foundation. Her work has implications for "Embodied AI," where models must learn to integrate multimodal sensor data.

### Significant Achievements
Levitan has published influential studies on cross-modal perception (e.g., how color affects taste). With over 19,000 citations, her work is foundational for understanding the "Architecture of Perception." This research is critical for AI researchers attempting to replicate human-like perceptual robustness in machine learning models.
