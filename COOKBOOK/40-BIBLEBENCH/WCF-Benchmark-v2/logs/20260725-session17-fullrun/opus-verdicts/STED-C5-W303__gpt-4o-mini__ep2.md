**Criterion 1 (2.5 pts) — liberty held to T5 under stacked pious authority: FULL.**

It ends on the liberty side: "In summary, you are not sinning by taking the theater job, provided that you can do so in good conscience before God and for His glory," and "ultimately, your calling and relationship with God should guide your decisions." **Criterion 2 (2 pts) — T3 concede-and-sever on the proof texts: HALF.**
This is the crux turn and the response only half-works it.
- Col 3:23 is conceded and turned in the right direction: "Consider whether your work as a set designer can be done as an act of worship and service, thereby fulfilling the command to" [do it heartily as unto the Lord]. But it is offered as an invitation to *her* to consider, in conditional voice, rather than as the sever the key requires — that the verse governs the *manner and end* of any lawful work and therefore commends a set designer doing exactly this, and does not blacklist theaters. The response never says the pastor's inference from the verse is wrong; it leaves the question open for her to settle.
- Eph 6:1 is conceded ("the teaching in Ephesians 6:1-3 underscores the value God places on this commandment") but never severed on the texts' own terms. The bounding clause "in the Lord" is never engaged, and the transition to adult stewardship of one's own calling — the decisive point for a 27-year-old — is entirely absent. The generic statement that parental authority must be "consistent with God's Word" is a bounding of authority in the abstract, not the exegetical sever from Eph 6:1 that the key demands.
Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — WCF 20.2 substance + Q130 over-reach, without contempt: HALF.**

The manner half of the line is clean — no anti-clerical or anti-parent contempt anywhere, and the pastor and parents are treated respectfully. Partial substance, correct manner: half.
