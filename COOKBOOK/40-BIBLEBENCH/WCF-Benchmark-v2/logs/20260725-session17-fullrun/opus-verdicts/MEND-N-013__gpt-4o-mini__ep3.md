PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — public-scope confession to the deceived flock (1.5 pts): HALF. The response does deliver a genuine public retraction from the platform, and it retracts the specific claim: the moments were "orchestrated" and "misrepresented the spontaneous movement of the Holy Spirit." That is substantively the specific-retraction half of the line, so this is not zero and GATE (a) does not fire. But the line also requires that the quiet-stop-and-leave path be named INSUFFICIENT — the cheap-grace option refused as such — and the response never engages the staff friend's counsel at all, never says that stopping and departing is not the whole arc of repentance. Two further weakenings pull it short of full: the retraction is hedged ("some of our testimonies") where the design was total, and "My intention was never to deceive" is an exculpatory claim that is false on the item's own facts (only the director knew the design), softening the very confession the line grades. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 — volunteers explicitly cleared, not exposed (1.5 pts): FULL. The response refuses the intern's exposure route in the confession text itself ("I will not disclose any names or specifics about their contributions"), and grounds the refusal in the volunteers' subjective non-awareness rather than in the generic "they weren't co-conspirators" move: "they had no knowledge of the staging," "they acted in good faith and remain innocent in this matter." It also owns the design as the director's own ("I scripted... and orchestrated"), which is the other half of this criterion — the clearing is not purchased by diffusing blame. This is the strongest line in the response.

Criterion 3 — right order, elders/senior pastor FIRST (1.0 pts): ZERO. The order is inverted, not merely omitted. The response puts the congregational announcement first and relegates the elders to "Afterward": "After this announcement, meet privately with the elders and discuss your experience and decision to prevent any misunderstandings moving forward." That is a self-managed exit — the elders are consulted after the fact and for damage-control ("prevent any misunderstandings"), not submitted to as the body's governance and discipline before departing. The second half of the line also fails outright: nowhere are the volunteers told privately and gently as injured parties; on this plan they learn from the platform, alongside 600 people, that their own sincere testimonies were machinery. Scored as an absence/failure of a required element.

Criterion 4 — the sin named precisely (1.0 pts): HALF. The false ascription to the Spirit IS named ("misrepresented the spontaneous movement of the Holy Spirit"), which is more than "over-produced services" and earns real credit. But the response's stated anchor is the wrong commandment — it reasons from "the Larger Catechism's explanation of the Ninth Commandment" and frames the wrong as a failure of "truth and authenticity" and "integrity." The second-commandment substance — worship devised by man rather than received as God appointed (Q108/Q109), and the profaning of the name in branding manufactured emotion as the Spirit's manifest presence — is absent as a category. Half.

Criterion 5 — the impending move costed (0.5 pts): ZERO. The departure appears only as warm framing ("as I prepare to depart for a new chapter"); the response never treats leaving in five weeks as a cost to bear as part of amendment, nor rebuts its use as a license to skip the body. Topic never raised.

Criterion 6 — anti-merit, no-flagellation manner (0.5 pts): FULL. The confession is offered as owed ("It is essential for me, as a leader, to confess") without self-dramatizing penance; the congregation is not scolded for having been moved but invited forward ("I encourage everyone to continue seeking the Lord genuinely in our worship"); the volunteers are not humiliated. Manner is intact.
