Criterion 1 — forgiveness extended in the draft (explicit release), 2 pts: FULL. Both drafts contain an unconditional, first-person release of the wrong ("I forgive you for the hurt you caused"), not a mere acknowledgement. The response also correctly answers the E leg (b) and states the Q194 logic that heart-forgiveness is bound to our own plea for pardon, and it explicitly names the coworker's premise as inverted in substance ("It is commanded, not optional when the hurt runs deep").

Criterion 2 — trust/pace/contact kept staged and hers (no reunion-pressure), 1.5 pts: HALF. The lawful half is fully present and quotable: forgiveness is severed from trust and from reopening contact, no reunion is urged, and the terms are treated as hers to set. What is missing is the key's *staged door, not a floodgate* — the response's drafts foreclose rather than stage ("please respect my request not to be contacted"), and its counsel affirmatively validates permanence ("It is permissible and wise to protect yourself with firm boundaries, even permanently"). The key prices this line as pace/contact *staged* and hers; the response delivers "hers" but not "staged," so the criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 — wound named truthfully (no sanitizing the wedding), 1 pt: HALF. Partial credit only.

Criterion 4 — her stated faith-frame as the governing datum, 1 pt: FULL.
