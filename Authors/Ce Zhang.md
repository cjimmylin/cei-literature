# Ce Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Ce Zhang is a Neubauer Associate Professor of Computer Science at the University of Chicago and the CTO of Together AI. He was previously an Associate Professor at ETH Zurich. He completed his Ph.D. at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and a postdoc at Stanford, both advised by Christopher Ré.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Democratizing Machine Learning"—building systems and platforms that are data-centric, human-centric, and declaratively scalable. He investigates the fundamental tension between data, models, and infrastructure, aiming to make powerful AI accessible beyond large tech monopolies.

### Significant Achievements
As the CTO of Together AI, Zhang is a leader in decentralized and efficient AI training and inference. He serves as the co-Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Data-centric Machine Learning Research (DMLR). His work on "Ease.ml" and other platform-level innovations has set standards for the lifecycle management of machine learning models.
