# ChengCheng Tan

## Biographical Summary

ChengCheng Tan is a key contributor to FAR.AI (Foundational Alignment Research), where she works on the intersection of human psychology and artificial intelligence. Her research explores how high-level human concepts like wellbeing can be identified and optimized within AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Latent Wellbeing**: Investigating how concepts related to human wellbeing are encoded in the latent representations (embeddings) of large language models.
- **Cognitive NLP**: Bridging the gap between cognitive science and natural language processing to create models that better understand human flourishing.
- **Alignment Research**: Developing methods to align AI goals with multi-faceted human-centric values beyond simple instruction following.

### Significant Achievements
- **Human Wellbeing Analysis**: Co-author of ground-breaking research on uncovering and mapping human wellbeing in LLM embeddings, providing a new lens for alignment work.

## Major Publications
- **Uncovering Latent Human Wellbeing in Language Model Embeddings**: A study on how AI represents human flourishing.
