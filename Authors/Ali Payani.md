# Ali Payani

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ali Payani is a Senior Research Scientist at Cisco Research. His career spans academic and industrial research, with a focus on making AI systems more interpretable, robust, and controllable through techniques like model editing and neuro-symbolic reasoning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ali's research addresses the "double-edged sword" of model editing—how correcting factual errors in a model can inadvertently introduce new safety risks or degrade other capabilities. He works on developing robust evaluation suites for these editing techniques to ensure that modifications to a model's knowledge do not undermine its alignment.
