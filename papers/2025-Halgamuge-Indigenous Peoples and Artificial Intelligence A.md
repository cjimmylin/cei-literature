## Abstract

This systematic literature review examines 53 articles (2012-2023) on Indigenous peoples and AI, identifying four categories: AI assisting Indigenous Knowledge Systems, AI risks for Indigenous peoples, and how Indigenous knowledge can enrich AI development. Critically addresses the tension between open data principles in AI (needed for benchmarks) and Indigenous data sovereignty. Provides a comprehensive map of the field and identifies future research directions.

## Dataset Information

53 articles reviewed


## Data Description

This is a systematic literature review examining the intersection of Indigenous Knowledge Systems (IKS) and artificial intelligence. It reviews articles published between 2012 and 2023, classifying them into four categories: AI tools for preserving Indigenous knowledge, AI tools for addressing Indigenous challenges, concerns about AI's risks for Indigenous communities, and utilizing IKS to enrich AI development. No new dataset or benchmark is created.
