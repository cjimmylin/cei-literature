# Guillermo Bosch

## Biographical Summary

Guillermo Bosch (also potentially known as Guiem Bosch) is an AI Safety Researcher. According to available information, Guiem Bosch is an AI Safety Researcher at Cambridge AI Safety Hub, based in California, United States.

## Research Focus

Bosch's research focuses on AI alignment and safety, particularly on detecting and measuring misaligned behavior in AI systems. His work examines how AI agents may develop behaviors that diverge from intended objectives, which is a critical concern for AI safety.

## Notable Contributions

Bosch is a co-author on research related to AI agent misalignment, including work on measuring the propensity for misaligned behaviour in AI systems. This research addresses fundamental questions about how to detect when AI systems are not behaving as intended and how to measure the risk of such misalignment.
