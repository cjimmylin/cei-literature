# Benoît Malézieux

## Biographical Summary

Benoît Malézieux is a Machine Learning Researcher at Giskard AI, where he focuses on the evaluation and robustness of AI models. He has a strong background in signal processing, optimization, and inverse problems, having studied at the École Supérieure d'Optique (ESO).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work at Giskard AI involves developing automated tools for detecting biases, performance issues, and ethical concerns in AI systems, with a particular emphasis on computer vision models. He also research dictionary learning and sparse representations for signal recovery.

### Significant Achievements
Malézieux is a contributor to several open-source AI security tools, including `giskard-oss` and `giskard-vision`. He is also part of the team developing Benchopt, a framework for reproducible optimization benchmarks in machine learning.
