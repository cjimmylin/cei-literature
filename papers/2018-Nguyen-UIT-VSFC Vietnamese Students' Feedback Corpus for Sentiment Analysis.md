## Abstract

16,000+ human-annotated Vietnamese sentences for sentiment-based and topic-based classification. 91.20% inter-annotator agreement for sentiment task. Used in SEA-HELM as Vietnamese sentiment benchmark.

## Data Description

UIT-VSFC is a corpus of 16,000+ Vietnamese sentences annotated for sentiment and topic classification, with 91.20% inter-annotator agreement, serving as a benchmark for Vietnamese sentiment analysis (used in SEA-HELM).
