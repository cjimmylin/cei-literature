# Gavin Leech

## Biographical Summary

Gavin Leech is a researcher at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence at the University of Cambridge (2024-present). He holds a PhD in AI from the University of Bristol (2019-2023), where he was advised by Laurence Aitchison (2020-2024) and worked with Peter Flach (2019-2024). He founded the research consultancy Arb (arbresearch.com), which answers hard questions about artificial intelligence and other sciences.

## Research & Impact

Leech's research spans AI safety, metascience, and computational methods:

- **Safety Properties of ILP**: Co-authored "Safety Properties of Inductive Logic Programming" (AAAI Workshop on AI Safety 2021), investigating the safety properties of inductive logic programming compared to deep learning systems. The research considered properties like ease of model specification, robustness, verification, post-hoc model editing, transfer learning, and interpretability.

- **AI Safety Research**: Active in AI safety field-building and research evaluation, including proposals for surveys of the AI Safety field.

- **Research Consultancy**: Through Arb, provides research services on AI and scientific questions.

His areas of expertise include Bayesian epidemiology, metascience, computational statistics, and reinforcement learning.
