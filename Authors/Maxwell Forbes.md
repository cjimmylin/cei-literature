# Maxwell Forbes

## Research Summary

Maxwell Forbes is an NLP researcher affiliated with the University of Washington and the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). He is the lead author of "Social Chemistry 101: Learning to Reason about Social and Moral Norms" (EMNLP 2020), which introduced Social-Chem-101, a large-scale corpus cataloging 292k rules-of-thumb for everyday social norms and moral judgments. His research focuses on computational approaches to understanding social and moral reasoning.
