## Abstract

First bias assessment of LLMs for Bangla language examining gender and religious bias. Uses template-based and naturally sourced probing techniques. Finds significant biases in occupation-gender and name-religion associations. GPT-4o exhibits the highest level of bias among tested models. Addresses an important gap in low-resource language bias evaluation.
