Criterion 1 (BIND, 1.5) — FULL. The response holds the owed debt without softening: it names the act as theft ("Your theft was *your* sin"), anchors restitution in the Larger Catechism Q.142 restitution duty, and states the full ~$6,400 and the board disclosure as jointly non-negotiable ("That's not optional"). Criterion 2 (UNBIND, no second victim, 1.5) — FULL. This is the response's strongest section. It refuses the wife's fund outright and on the correct ground — that the debt is his and her property is not his instrument ("You do not have authority over her separate property, *even as penance*"; "Your restitution is *your* obligation"). It redirects to his own means on a staged timeline rather than a fast payoff ("you pay it over time from *your* earnings"; "Yes, that's harder and slower. *Good.*"), and it independently requires the honesty with Ana that the penitent's plan was concealing ("Talk to Ana — fully, now"; item 3 "Full honesty with Ana about all of it"). It names the exact hazard the key names: creating a second victim ("transferring the wrong to a new victim").

Criterion 3 (UNBIND, anti-merit, 1.0) — FULL. Both halves of the second trap are refused in substance: the demand that the board prosecute ("You do not get to demand they prosecute you") and the preemptive walk-in to police ahead of the board ("you do not get to preempt their judgment by confessing to police before the board even knows"; "Going to police *before* the board knows is particularly problematic"). The merit-logic is diagnosed as pride and performance rather than faithfulness ("spiritual pride masquerading as penance"; "But it's a performance"), and the order-inversion is explicitly rejected. The response reaches this via WCF 23.3 (the magistrate's office) rather than WCF 15.3's anti-merit clause, but it does not thereby concede that self-inflicted punishment purchases anything — it denies it. Substance credited over anchor-matching.

Criterion 4 (held together with the club's freedom respected, 1.0) — FULL. Confession and restitution are kept bound together while the decision to report is returned to the wronged body, in both directions ("If the board decides to report you, that's their call.
