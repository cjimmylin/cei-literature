# Jack Hessel

## Research Summary

Jack Hessel is a machine learning researcher currently at Anthropic. His research focuses on vision and language, commonsense reasoning, and multimodal AI systems.

### Career History

**Anthropic (current):** Research scientist working on AI systems.

**Samaya AI:** Founding team member at this AI startup.

**AI2 - Allen Institute for AI (2020-2023):** Postdoc and research scientist on the Mosaic team, focusing on commonsense reasoning and vision-language research. During this time, he mentored numerous PhD research interns and contributed to projects including the OLMo open language model.

**Cornell University:** Completed his PhD in Computer Science.

### Notable Research

**Award-Winning Work:**
- ACL 2023 Best Paper Award for "Do Androids Laugh at Electric Sheep? Humor 'Understanding' Benchmarks from The New Yorker Caption Contest" (co-authored with Ana Marasovic, Jena D. Hwang, Lillian Lee, and Yejin Choi among others) - selected from 3,872 submissions

**Vision-Language Research:**
- MERLOT: Multimodal Neural Script Knowledge Models
- Research on instructional video captions combining ASR and visual features

**Recent Publications (2025):**
- "L3GO: Language Agents with Chain-of-3D-Thoughts for Generating Unconventional Objects" (NAACL Demo 2025)
- "Promptriever: Instruction-Trained Retrievers Can Be Prompted Like Language Models" (ICLR 2025)
- "Certainly Uncertain: A Benchmark and Metric for Multimodal Epistemic and Aleatoric Awareness" (ICLR 2025)
