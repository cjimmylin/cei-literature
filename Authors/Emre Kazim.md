# Emre Kazim

## Biographical Summary

Emre Kazim is Co-Director of the Digital Ethics Center at University College London and co-founder of Holistic AI, a company focused on AI auditing and governance. His research spans AI ethics, governance, and the integration of diverse philosophical perspectives into AI development. He has contributed significantly to incorporating African philosophical traditions, particularly Ubuntu, into global AI ethics frameworks.

## Research Interests

- **AI Ethics**: Developing ethical frameworks for AI development and deployment
- **AI Governance**: Creating governance structures for responsible AI
- **African AI Ethics**: Integrating Ubuntu philosophy into AI ethics
- **AI Auditing**: Methods for evaluating AI systems for ethical compliance

## Major Publications

- "The role of the African value of Ubuntu in global AI ethics" (2022) - Influential work on incorporating Ubuntu philosophy
- Co-author of numerous AI ethics and governance papers
- Research on AI auditing methodologies
