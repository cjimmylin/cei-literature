# Yuru Jiang

## Research Summary

Yuru Jiang is a researcher at Zhejiang University focusing on collective alignment methods for Large Language Models. She developed SPARTA ALIGNMENT, a novel algorithm enabling multiple LLMs to form a "sparta tribe" that collectively improves through competitive combat and peer evaluation. This game-theoretic approach demonstrates 7.0% average improvement over self-alignment baselines across 10 of 12 tasks, showing that collective adversarial self-alignment among diverse models can generalize better to unseen tasks without human supervision.
