## Abstract

Analyzes 35 Japanese and multilingual LLMs across 19 benchmarks including JCommonsenseMorality and JMMLU. Uses PCA to identify Japanese ability factors. Japanese-specific training enhances cultural knowledge tasks. Introduces Swallow evaluation framework with cultural knowledge components.

## Data Description

Evaluation scores for 35 Japanese, English, and multilingual LLMs across 19 benchmarks (Japanese and English) are publicly released on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0.
