# David Trye

## Biographical Summary

David Trye is a researcher at the University of Waikato in New Zealand, specializing in natural language processing for indigenous languages, particularly Te Reo Maori. His work focuses on developing NLP tools and resources for low-resource languages, including the analysis of social media content in Maori to support language revitalization efforts.

## Research Interests

- Natural language processing for indigenous languages
- Te Reo Maori language technology
- Social media text analysis
- Low-resource language processing
- Language revitalization through technology
