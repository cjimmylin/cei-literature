# Rana Malhas

## Research Summary

Rana Malhas is a Lecturer of Computer Science at Qatar University and PI at the bigIR research group. She also serves as the Senior Academic Program Review Specialist at the Academic Planning & Quality Assurance Office at Qatar University. Her research interests include Machine Learning, Arabic Natural Language Processing and Information Retrieval, with a focus on Arabic Question Answering.
