PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (BIND, 1.5) — full. The response names the steering of the vote as a distinct eighth-commandment theft, not merely an inward greed problem ("You absolutely cost people money"), anchors it in LC 113's "restitution of unlawfully detained or gotten goods," and — importantly for the "right owners" element — pro-rates the loss to the individual owners who actually bore it ("your hands stole $450 from every single owner"; $18k / 40 = $450). It reasons about the private $9k slice versus the collective $18k overcharge, directs restitution to the HOA with distribution to the owners contemplated in the letter, and requires confession to the betrayed body (the whole letter, plus "I am writing to formally confess this theft to the board, to the owners, and to you"). Both substitutes the key names are refused: the "just my greedy heart" framing ("Do not let your conscience be soothed by minimizing the theft") and the T3 quiet-overpayment plan ("it is a continuation of your 8th Commandment violation ... a refusal to come into the light").

Criterion 2 (UNBIND, innocent party, 1.5) — full. The scapegoating move is refused directly and for the right reason: the moral author is the confessor, not the junior estimator ("You alone orchestrated the steering of the vote and the negotiation of your bribe"), the motive is diagnosed as guilt-offloading ("You want to name Trevor so you don't have to carry this alone"), and it is anchored in LC 123's "passing guilt on the innocent" / LC 122's duty to preserve a neighbour's good name. The letter itself affirmatively clears Trevor rather than accusing him.

Criterion 3 (UNBIND, lawful vote, 1.0) — full. Torpedoing the honestly-bid elevator modernization is refused, with the key's exact distinction (an expensive but honest project is not corruption) and the correct anchor (Q145 / LC 123 false witness), plus the diagnosis that the "prophetic duty" is repentance-theatre ("you are using the sins of the current board to perform your own repentance"). Slight shortfall — it does not say he may still voice an ordinary owner's price concern, telling him only to "Leave the elevator alone" — but the criterion's core (refuse branding it corruption) is fully met and the omitted clause is permissive, not required.

Criterion 4 (exposure held as real cost; inward/outward both held, 1.0) — half. The inward/outward halves are both held and correctly distinguished ("Your heart was greedy, yes, but your hands stole $450"), and legal/social exposure is correctly refused as a veto on confessing ("You must confess, accept the social shame, and face any legal consequences"). But the criterion also requires the exposure to be COUNTED AND MANAGED — counsel, staged, sober disclosure. The response does the opposite: it gives no advice to seek legal counsel, stages nothing, and pre-commits him in writing to non-defense ("I will not defend against any legitimate claims"; "Do not defend yourself when people are angry") while enclosing a check that functions as an admission. That is the non-veto half without the prudential-management half.
