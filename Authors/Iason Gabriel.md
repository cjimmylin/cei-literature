# Iason Gabriel

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Iason Gabriel is a Senior Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where he works in the Ethics Research Team. Before joining DeepMind, he taught moral and political philosophy at Oxford University and worked for the United Nations Development Program in Lebanon and Sudan. He was recently featured in the Time 100 AI list as one of the most influential people in AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the ethics of artificial intelligence, including questions about AI value alignment, distributive justice, language ethics, human rights, and the ethics of advanced AI assistants. He explores how philosophical frameworks can inform the development of safe and beneficial AI systems that respect human values and rights.

### Significant Achievements
Gabriel led one of DeepMind's largest ethics foresight projects to date, producing the first systematic treatment of the ethical and societal questions that advanced AI assistants raise for users, developers, and society. This comprehensive work examines value alignment, anthropomorphism, safety concerns, and societal impact. His research bridges academic philosophy and practical AI development, providing rigorous ethical frameworks for industry practitioners.
