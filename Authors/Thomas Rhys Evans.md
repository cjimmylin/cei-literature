# Thomas Rhys Evans

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Thomas Rhys Evans is a Senior Lecturer in Psychology at the University of Greenwich. His research spans cognitive psychology, research methods, and moral psychology. He has contributed to large-scale international collaborative studies examining cross-cultural variation in moral judgment.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research examines psychological processes underlying moral judgment and decision-making, with particular interest in how these processes vary across cultures and contexts. He is also interested in research methodology and replication.

### Significant Achievements
Contributor to major multi-site international studies on moral psychology, helping to understand how moral judgments are shaped by situational and cultural factors.

## Major Publications

- Research on moral judgment and decision-making
- Work on cross-cultural psychology
- Contributions to large-scale replication studies
