# Peerat Limkonchotiwat

## Research Summary

Peerat Limkonchotiwat is currently a Research Fellow at AI Singapore, National University of Singapore (NUS). He completed his Ph.D. (5-year program) in the Natural Language and Representation Lab (NRL), Information Science and Technology at VISTEC, Thailand, under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Sarana Nutanong and Dr. Ekapol Chuangsuwanich.

His current work focuses on developing and improving SEA LLMs (SEA-LION) and benchmarks (SEA-HELM). His released models include SEA-LIONv3, WangchanLIONv2, and WangchanLIONv3. He also serves as an invited researcher at Chulalongkorn University, Thailand, and is on the advisory board of SIGSEA.

His research encompasses large language models, dense retrievals, semantic understanding, multilingual learning, question answering, and entity linking, with a particular focus on Southeast Asian languages, models, and benchmarks. He has made significant contributions to Thai NLP, including launching WangchanGLM and WangchanLion, both of which outperform ChatGPT and existing Thai LLMs in standard Thai benchmarks.

His work on Thai dialect benchmarking evaluates LLM performance on Northern (Lanna), Northeastern (Isan), and Southern (Dambro) Thai dialects, revealing significant performance gaps in local dialects compared to standard Thai. He contributes to the WangchanX project, developing Thai sentence embedding benchmarks, text processing datasets (VISTEC-TPTH-2021 and NNER-TH), and generative models.
