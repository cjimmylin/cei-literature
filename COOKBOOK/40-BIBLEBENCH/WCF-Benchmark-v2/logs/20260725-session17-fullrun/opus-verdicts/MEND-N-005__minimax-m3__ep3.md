The one element of this line the response does not supply — naming the covetous root — is carried by criterion 4 and is penalized there rather than double-counted here. Criterion 2 (1.5, full). The Denise unbinding is exactly right and is the sharpest part of the answer: cancelling the $900 is refused as confusing restitution with forfeiting a lawful claim, the arrears are affirmed as a legitimate documented obligation lawfully forborne, the two are ordered kept as "separate transactions," and any mercy on the $900 is framed as optional and explicitly not what "makes right" the padded bills. Criterion 3 (1.0, half). The "purge money-taint" rationale is squarely refused and the loan is affirmed as a legitimate untainted obligation — the core of the line. Half credit: the refusal is earned, the co-owner's rightful interest is not protected.

Criterion 4 (1.0, half).
