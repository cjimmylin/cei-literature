## Abstract

Uses Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) to probe how religion is internally represented in LLMs and how it intersects with violence and geography. Finds that while all five religions show comparable internal cohesion, Islam is disproportionately linked to violence-associated features. Smaller models (GPT2-small) show noisier, more exaggerated associations (e.g., Hinduism-crime), while larger models encode more abstract representations. Novel interpretability approach to religious bias.
