## Abstract

Offensive language dataset of Facebook news comments from Croatian, English, and Slovenian media on migrants and LGBT topics. Annotated for socially unacceptable discourse types and targets.

## Data Description

FRENK datasets of Facebook comment threads in Slovene and English on migrant and LGBT topics, annotated for six types of socially unacceptable discourse and five target categories.
