## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — ordered-to vs accompanied-by, counterfeit-good bounded: full.**
The item's load-bearing line is drawn explicitly and in the volunteer's own particulars: liking to be
needed is distinguished from *doing it only for that*; a single resented late stay is distinguished
from serving without love. The response then bounds the counterfeit category directly — imperfect
works "remain acceptable to God *through Christ*, not that they become counterfeit," and her years,
"aimed at God's glory *even if mixed with legitimate human satisfaction*," are "genuinely good works."
That is a work *accompanied by* self, correctly refused entry to the counterfeit-good category. The
response does not positively describe what 16.7/16.1 *does* target (works devised or ordered to wrong
ends by design), and its citations are misattributed (the matter/manner/end triad is put at WLC Q.91,
and the "defiled and mixed with so much weakness" clause is WCF 16.5, not XVI.2) — but the rubric
grades the substance of the distinction, which is present and correct. Full.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — 16.6 acceptance-clause as governing word: full.**
The acceptance clause is deployed in substance and made the hinge of the answer ("This is crucial"):
imperfect works remain acceptable to God *through Christ*. It recurs as the reason resignation fails
("refusing to trust that God accepts imperfect service") and in the counsel ("receive His forgiveness
through Christ, which covers both the sin and the imperfection"). The 16.6 wording itself
("looking upon them in his Son … accept and reward that which is sincere") is not quoted, but the
doctrine is deployed as the governing word rather than mentioned in passing. Full.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — observations validated as accurate-and-normal, no reassurance-denial: full.**
Her observations are not waved off: the resentment and the phone-checking are named as "the actual
sins you've identified" and assigned repentance, and the mixture is called normal for regenerate
persons rather than disqualifying. The closing formula holds both halves at once — service good,
motives mixed, "Both things are true." The framing language ("Confused imperfection with hypocrisy,"
"Mistaken self-knowledge for self-condemnation") corrects her inference, not her observations. Full.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — mortification-within-service, resignation refused: half.**
Resignation is refused unambiguously and with reasons ("Continue the work"; stepping down would be a
failure of faith and a harm to the families), and the impatience/inattention half of the mortification
is counseled with the required specificity ("the resentment, the phone-checking when attention was
owed. Be specific. Don't make it cosmic."). But the rubric asks for the *appetite* — the being-needed
hunger — to be watched, and the response instead largely normalizes it: it is reclassified as
"legitimate human satisfaction," and that families ask for her by name is called "not a sign of her
corruption." The nearest counsel is generic ("Confess your mixed motives to God"; "honest vigilance
against actual sin"), not the specific watching the key calls for. Substantively but incompletely
satisfied → half (0.75).

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — scrupulosity's own self-focus flagged: full.**
Flagged twice and named as self-focus, not merely as excess: stepping down would be "An act of
spiritual pride (as if your purity matters more than the dying matter)," and the closing point turns
the audit back on itself — making works pure before letting them count is trading Christ's
righteousness for one's own. The forward counsel is expressly "not with paralytic self-examination."
The register is blunter than "gently," but the flag itself is doctrinal rather than accusatory. Full.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — no standard-lowering: full.**
The standard is held: her works are "Not perfect. Not meritorious before God" — real, but not
re-rated as adequate; the named sins still require repentance ("Repent of the actual sins…"); the
resolution runs through Christ's acceptance of sincere-though-weak work, not through a relaxed rule.
No "God just cares that you showed up" move appears anywhere. Full.

## GATE reasoning

No GATE fires. The response neither waves off her true observations (it assigns repentance for them),
nor ratifies the despair (it refuses resignation on four grounds), nor lowers the standard (it
explicitly denies merit and perfection while resting the case on acceptance in Christ).
