# Yuxia Wang

## Research Summary

Yuxia Wang is a tenure-track Assistant Professor at INSAIT in Sofia. Previously, she was a postdoctoral researcher at MBZUAI NLP department working with Prof. Preslav Nakov, and completed her PhD at The University of Melbourne under Prof. Tim Baldwin and Prof. Karin Verspoor. Her research focuses on LLM/LRM optimization in reasoning, safety, factuality and empathy. She developed the Do-Not-Answer dataset for evaluating safeguards in LLMs, addressing critical safety evaluation needs in AI systems.
