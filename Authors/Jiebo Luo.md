# Jiebo Luo

## Research Summary

Jiebo Luo (born 1967) is the Albert Arendt Hopeman Professor of Engineering and Professor of Computer Science at the University of Rochester. He joined the university in 2011 after a prolific 15-year career at Kodak Research where he served as Senior Principal Scientist. He received his B.S. (1989) and M.S. (1992) from University of Science and Technology of China, and Ph.D. (1996) from University of Rochester, all in electrical engineering.

Luo's research spans computer vision, multimodal AI, data-driven social computing, and digital health. He is a pioneer of contextual inference in semantic understanding of visual data and social multimedia data mining. He has authored over 600 technical papers and holds more than 90 U.S. patents, with an h-Index of 132.
