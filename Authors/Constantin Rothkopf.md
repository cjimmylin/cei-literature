# Constantin A. Rothkopf

## Biographical Summary
Constantin Rothkopf is a full professor at TU Darmstadt's Institute for Psychology with a secondary appointment in Computer Science. He is the founding director of the Center for Cognitive Science and a founding member of hessian.AI (Hessian Center for Artificial Intelligence). His research focuses on understanding the interrelationship between perception and action in humans through computational modeling and behavioral experiments.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Computational Cognitive Science**: Understanding human behavior through cognitive computational models using algorithms similar to AI
- **Sequential Decision Making**: Developing probabilistic models of human decision-making under uncertainty
- **Active Vision**: Studying the relationship between 'looking' and 'seeing' in goal-directed behavior
- **ACTOR Project**: ERC-funded project on computational accounts of natural sequential behavior

### Significant Achievements
- ERC Consolidator Grant (2022) for the ACTOR project
- Founding director of TU Darmstadt's Center for Cognitive Science
- Founding member and executive board member of hessian.AI
- Member of ELLIS (European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems)
- Co-speaker of "The Adaptive Mind" and "Whitebox" collaborative projects
