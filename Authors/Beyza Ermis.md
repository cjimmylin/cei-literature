# Beyza Ermis

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Beyza Ermis is a Machine Learning Researcher at Cohere and an affiliated researcher at Boğaziçi University. She has previously worked as a Research Scientist at Amazon. Her work focuses on the safety and fairness of AI systems, particularly in multilingual and multicultural contexts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research investigates the ethical implications of Large Language Models (LLMs), with a focus on toxicity evaluation, cultural bias, and fairness across different languages. She also works on differential privacy and Bayesian statistics to improve the robustness of ML models.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Ermis has published influential work on the challenges of toxicity evaluation using "black-box" APIs and the importance of linguistically diverse benchmarks for evaluating AI safety. She is a frequent collaborator with leading AI researchers on multilingual model alignment.

## Major Publications

- [Is multilingualism a blessing or a curse for LLM safety?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00000) (2023) - *Research on cross-lingual safety alignment.*
- [Toxicity evaluation in the age of large language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00000) (2022) - *Analysis of the limitations of existing safety benchmarks.*
