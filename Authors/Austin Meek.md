# Austin Meek

## Research Summary

Austin Meek is a researcher at the University of Delaware with interests in moral reasoning and AI alignment. Their work explores whether fine-tuning language models on human behavioral and brain data can improve alignment with human moral intuitions, using metrics like BrainScore to measure neural alignment.
