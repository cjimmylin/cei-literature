# Charlotte Laclau

## Biographical Summary

Charlotte Laclau is an Associate Professor in the Department of Image, Data, and Signal at Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris. She is also a member of the LIG Laboratory. Her research is at the forefront of machine learning, with a specific focus on making algorithms more equitable and efficient.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Algorithmic Fairness**: Developing methods to identify and mitigate bias in machine learning models, particularly for relational data and graph-based systems.
- **Representation Learning**: Researching how to learn useful and robust representations from complex, high-dimensional data.
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Innovating in clustering and co-clustering techniques for data discovery without explicit labels.

### Significant Achievements
- **Ethical AI**: Contributed to significant research on "Histoires Morales" (Moral Stories), exploring how ethical considerations can be integrated into technical frameworks.

## Major Publications
- **Histoires Morales**: Collaborative work on the intersection of storytelling, morality, and machine learning.
- **Fairness in Graphs**: Research on ensuring equitable outcomes in networked data environments.
