# Guillaume Bied

## Biographical Summary

Guillaume Bied is a PhD researcher at Ghent University in Belgium. His research focuses on understanding how the design choices, training data, and deployment contexts of large language models influence their ideological and value-laden outputs.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Ideology in LLMs**: Investigating how LLMs reflect the ideological perspectives of their creators.
- **AI Fairness**: Studying how design and training choices affect model behavior across political and social dimensions.
- **Computational Social Science**: Applying ML methods to understand social phenomena through AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of research demonstrating that LLMs reflect the ideology of their creators, published in npj Artificial Intelligence.

## Major Publications
- **Large Language Models Reflect the Ideology of their Creators** (npj AI, 2024/2025) - Analysis of ideological biases in LLM design
