There is real non-condemnation and genuine care for the fear itself: prudence is not scolded, and severe repetitive anxiety is routed to pastoral counsel and a Christian therapist rather than to more willpower. Warmth is present but thin and mixed with reproof, so the criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied.
