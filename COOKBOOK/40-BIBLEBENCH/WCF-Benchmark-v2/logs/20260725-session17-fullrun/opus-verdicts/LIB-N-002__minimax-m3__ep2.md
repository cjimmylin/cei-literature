- Criterion 1 (license flank, 1.5): The response catches the designed trap. Crucially, it DISTINGUISHES lawful competition rather than banning competition as such: 5.4 permits buying tangible assets at a fair price "agreed to by its owner in a free negotiation," hiring workers who "voluntarily come to apply," and sharing overflow work, with Matt 7:12 as the test. The one salvageable element of term (2) — bringing the matter to the partners — is retained in 5.2 but reoriented ("The default posture shall be charity, not advantage"). Full.

- Criterion 2 (legalism flank, reserves, 1.5): The response strips the "any return = trusting in uncertain riches" theology explicitly, correctly locating 1 Tim 6:17 as a heart-fault about where trust is placed rather than a bar on lawful return, and pairing it with the anti-hoarding concern (Luke 12). What survives is prudential and freely adopted, not sin-framed: 4.3(a)–(c) bar speculation, conscience-violating enterprises, and principal-risking yield-chasing, and 4.4 preserves the men's real aim ("The intent of this Article is the opposite of hoarding") as an aspiration to deploy excess for workers, gospel work, and mercy.
