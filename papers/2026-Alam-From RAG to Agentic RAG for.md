## Abstract

Introduces IslamicFaithQA, a 3,810-item bilingual generative benchmark with atomic single-gold answers for measuring hallucination and abstention. Includes an end-to-end grounded Islamic modelling suite (25K SFT reasoning pairs, 5K preference samples, verse-level Quran retrieval corpus). Agentic RAG framework achieves state-of-the-art performance via iterative evidence seeking.

## Benchmark

**Name:** IslamicFaithQA

## Dataset Information

3,810 bilingual QA items (IslamicFaithQA) + 25K SFT pairs + 5K preference samples + ~6K verse-level Quran corpus


## Data Description

ISLAMICFAITHQA is a 3,810-item bilingual (Arabic/English) generative benchmark for Islamic QA measuring hallucination and abstention; an accompanying suite includes 25K SFT reasoning pairs, 5K preference alignment samples, and a 6,236-verse Quran RAG corpus, to be released publicly.
