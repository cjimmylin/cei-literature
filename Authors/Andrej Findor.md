# Andrej Findor

## Biographical Summary

Andrej Findor is an Associate Professor at the Institute of European Studies and International Relations, Faculty of Social and Economic Sciences, Comenius University in Bratislava, Slovakia. His research focuses on political psychology, including the psychological foundations of political attitudes and moral judgments. He has contributed to large-scale international replication studies examining the universality of moral cognition across cultures.

## Research Summary

Findor's work spans political psychology and experimental social science. He is particularly interested in how psychological and situational factors shape moral and political judgments. He has participated in major multi-lab studies testing the cross-cultural validity of classic findings in moral psychology, such as the dual-process model of moral cognition in trolley dilemmas.
