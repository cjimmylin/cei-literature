# Biljana Gjoneska

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Biljana Gjoneska is a Research Associate at the Macedonian Academy of Sciences and Arts. She is a cognitive psychologist whose work bridges the gap between individual psychological mechanisms and aggregate social behavior. She holds a PhD from Sapienza University of Rome.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on the situational and individual factors that shape moral judgments and pro-social behavior. She is particularly interested in how cultural context and cognitive biases influence decision-making in ethical dilemmas.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Gjoneska is a key collaborator in the "Situational Factors" project—a massive multi-national study (spanning 45 countries) that investigated the universality and cultural variation of moral judgments. This work provides the empirical baseline for aligning AI with diverse human values.

## Major Publications

- [Situational factors shape moral judgments in the trolley dilemma in 45 countries](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01387-z) (2022) - *Large-scale empirical study on moral psychology.*
