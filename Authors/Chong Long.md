# Chong Long

## Biographical Summary

Chong Long is a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. He is a member of the CoAI (Conversational AI) Lab, where he conducts research on the safety and reliability of Large Language Models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Safety Evaluation**: Developing comprehensive benchmarks like "SafetyBench" to assess LLMs across a wide taxonomy of safety risks (ethics, bias, toxicity, etc.).
- **Adversarial Attacks**: Investigating how LLMs handle adversarial inputs and "jailbreak" attempts.
- **Robust Alignment**: Researching methods to train models that preserve safety guardrails even under stress.

### Significant Achievements
- **SafetyBench**: Lead author of SafetyBench, a widely cited benchmark that provides a systematic evaluation of LLM safety in Chinese and English contexts.

## Major Publications
- **SafetyBench: Evaluating the Safety of Large Language Models**: A comprehensive safety evaluation framework.
