## Research Summary

Dr. Katleho K. Mokoena is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Learning Information Networking Knowledge (LINK) Centre at the University of the Witwatersrand. He completed his PhD at the University of Pretoria in the Faculty of Theology and Religion, focusing on Ubuntu ethics and frontier technologies in the African context. His research experience includes digital and AI policy research and analysis through involvement with the Center for AI and Digital Policy (CAIDP), All Tech is Human, and Research ICT Africa (RIA).
