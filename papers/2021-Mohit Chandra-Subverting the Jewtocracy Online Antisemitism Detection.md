## Abstract

First work on automated multimodal detection of online antisemitism. Collects and labels two datasets from Twitter and Gab with both text and image modalities. Detects presence of antisemitic content and its specific antisemitism category using multimodal deep learning. Addresses the gap that online antisemitism has not been studied from a machine learning perspective compared to other forms of abuse.

## Dataset Information

3,102 Twitter posts + 3,509 Gab posts (multimodal: text + images)
