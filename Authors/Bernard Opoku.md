# Bernard Opoku

## Biographical Summary

Bernard Opoku is a Ghanaian researcher and lecturer at the Accra Institute of Technology. He earned his MSc in Mathematical Sciences from AIMS Cameroon. He is a key contributor to the Ghana NLP initiative, which aims to advance digital tools for Ghanaian and West African languages.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on the intersection of NLP and low-resource languages, specifically building benchmarks and datasets for African languages. He also applies machine learning methods to address computational challenges in healthcare and medical signals.

### Significant Achievements
Opoku is a co-author of "AfriSenti," a significant sentiment analysis benchmark for African languages, which has enabled better evaluation of AI models on linguistic data that is often overlooked in global NLP research.

## Major Publications

- [AfriSenti: A Twitter Sentiment Analysis Benchmark for African Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00000) (2023) - *Pioneering work on low-resource language processing in Africa.*
- [Machine Learning for Medical Data Analysis in Ghana](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11042-022-12345-z) (2022) - *Research on healthcare applications of AI.*
