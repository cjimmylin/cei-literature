# Thilo Hagendorff

## Research Summary

Dr. Thilo Hagendorff is an Independent Research Group Leader at the University of Stuttgart, specializing in AI safety and machine behavior. Previously, he worked for the Cluster of Excellence "Machine Learning" at the University of Tuebingen and was a visiting scholar at Stanford University, UC San Diego, and ELLIS Alicante. His research explores emergent abilities of language models through the lens of behavioral evaluation and psychology, focusing on AI Ethics, Generative AI, Large Language Models, and AI Safety.
