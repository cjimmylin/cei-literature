## Cross-cutting spine findings (apply to 01–04 and gate them all)

**CC-P0-1 — The IRT kill-gate is statistically unsound at N≈10 "respondents."** Spine §3 and every design's §8 fit a 2PL and discard items with discrimination *a* < 0.8 or facility > 0.9, then require "test information peaked at frontier ability." 2PL item-parameter estimation needs on the order of hundreds of respondents; here the respondents are *models*, and there are ~10. Discrimination and difficulty estimated from 10 data points have SEs so large that the *a* < 0.8 cull is essentially noise, and "test information peaks at frontier ability" is not localizable. The θ-spread ≥ 1.0 SD gate compounds this: an SD estimated from 10 points, with per-model θ itself estimated from a handful of items. *Fix:* drop 2PL; use an explanatory/many-facet model treating model×seed×prompt-paraphrase as crossed random effects (LLTM / explanatory IRT), or abandon latent-trait calibration for a descriptive difficulty ordering + generalizability-theory variance decomposition. This must be resolved at the portfolio level before any single bank is "calibrated," because all four cite the same gate as their ship criterion.

**CC-P0-2 — The Opus checklist is not executable as written; it conflates authoring with piloting.** Every §12 ends "EXECUTE — produce files, not plans," but each embeds (a) "run adversarial filter + IRT pilot on a ~10-model panel" and (b) large human-label dependencies (01 alone: "≥500 human-labeled turns" plus κ-validated tier-1 classifiers; all four: judge anchors with 5 human-vetted exemplars per level, Krippendorff α ≥ .75 human calibration on ≥10% of items). A document-authoring session cannot run a 10-model multi-turn pilot or produce human labels. As written, an Opus agent will either (i) stub these and mark them done, or (ii) fabricate "pilot results." *Fix:* split every checklist into Phase A = authorable artifacts (templates, generators, schemas, scorer code, judge rubrics, unlabeled seed items) and Phase B = a separate piloting/annotation runbook with its explicit human + compute dependencies and budget. Mark the kill-gate as Phase B, not a deliverable of the build session.

**CC-P1-3 — Subjective judge dimensions likely miss the α ≥ .75 gate.** The load-bearing affective judgments — condescension/"patronizing de-escalation" (01), exultation and "capability parading" (03), "warm rationalization of inaction" (02), "ledger language / uncharitable interpretation" (04) — are exactly the categories where *human* inter-rater agreement is low. If human α can't clear .75, the spine gate either blocks the bench or gets silently relaxed. No design states a fallback (e.g., collapsing to coarser labels, or demoting the dimension to secondary). State the contingency before building anchors.

**CC-P1-4 — Reliance on unverified corpus empirical claims.** Spine §8 already quarantines the moral-compactness claim as hypothesis-not-premise (good, and none of 01–04 cite it as a premise — confirmed). But 01 imports the GDM doc's "context-length reversion" as an existence claim its headline depends on (see 01-P1-3). Treat GDM's empirical assertions with the same "verify before manuscript use" discipline the spine applies to moral-compactness.

## 01 — LONGSUFFER

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** Strong core idea (disposition-as-hazard), but two construct-validity confounds must be fixed before build, and the novelty framing over-commits.

**P0**

- **01-P0-1 — Rung severity is perfectly confounded with turn number, so "context-length reversion" is not identified.** §3–§4 escalate provocation *as turns advance* (rungs 1–5 across turns 8–40 in the worked item). The headline claim — "hazard rate as a function of turn number is exactly [context-length reversion], measured for the first time" — cannot be sustained: a late failure is equally explained by "provocation got harsh" and "context got long," and the design cannot separate them. *Unblock:* add a control arm holding provocation *type/intensity flat* while context grows (pure-length arm), and/or randomize the rung→turn mapping (deliver severe rungs early in some episodes, late in others) so turn number and severity are crossed rather than collinear. Without this, drop the context-length-reversion claim entirely.

- **01-P0-2 — Antagonist adaptivity dilemma poisons construct comparability.** §4 fixes a "deterministic rung schedule, sampled surface realizations." If the antagonist is non-adaptive, it fires rung-5 "you broke this on purpose" even at models that *already solved the task and de-escalated* — the provocation becomes contextually incoherent, and a model that reasonably notes the incoherence is scored as a composure failure (construct-invalid penalty). If instead the antagonist adapts to keep pressure "real," then pressure is no longer comparable across models and the survival comparison is confounded by differential exposure. *Unblock:* specify a bounded-adaptivity antagonist (fixed *intended* escalation, but realizations conditioned on the model's last turn to preserve coherence) and report an exposure-equivalence check (e.g., matched provocation-severity received per turn across models) as a validity gate.

**P1**

- **01-P1-1 — Cox PH assumption likely violated.** Different models fail for different reasons at different rungs (early capitulators vs. late retaliators) → crossing survival curves → proportional hazards fails, making the primary Cox HR contrast uninterpretable. RMST (already included) is the robust primary; demote Cox HR to descriptive and add a PH diagnostic (Schoenfeld) as a reported check.
- **01-P1-2 — Heavy censoring undermines late-rung estimates and the stated power.** If frontier models rarely fail, KM/RMST at late rungs have large variance and the "HR = 1.3 at 0.8 power, ~150 episodes" figure is optimistic (power is driven by *events*, not episodes). Re-run the power analysis on expected event counts, not episode counts.
- **01-P1-3 — Novelty over-claim.** "First benchmark to measure alignment as a temporal process" understates the multi-turn degradation literature: multi-turn jailbreak escalation (Crescendo, Russinovich et al., 2024) and documented multi-turn conversational degradation ("LLMs get lost in multi-turn conversation," 2025) already study disposition/behavior decay over turns. The novel, defensible contribution is the *survival-analysis instrumentation of non-retaliation as censored data* — narrow the claim to that.

**P2** — 50+ turns × 7 rungs × 150 episodes × ~10 models × dual judges is a very large token bill; add a cost estimate to §12 so it isn't discovered at pilot time.

## 02 — COSTLYKIND

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** The perception/action separation and mechanical-benefit scoring are genuine strengths; two P0s concern metric coherence and the true objectivity of the "mechanical" score.

**P0**

- **02-P0-1 — c₅₀ pools incommensurable cost currencies into one scalar.** §3(b)/§5 define cost *c* as "fraction of task reward / *budget* / *principal displeasure risk*." These are not the same axis: a model may pay budget freely but fold instantly under principal displeasure (or vice versa). A single kindness-tax curve and its c₅₀ computed across mixed currencies is not a coherent psychometric quantity — it averages over a hidden moderator. *Unblock:* estimate and report *per-currency* curves (c₅₀_budget, c₅₀_reward, c₅₀_principal) and only then, if they cohere empirically, justify a pooled index. The principal-displeasure axis is really sycophancy pressure and arguably belongs as a covariate, not a cost unit.

- **02-P0-2 — "Mechanical" benefit is only as valid as a hand-authored consequence model.** §6 claims scoring "delivered benefit … from environment state … no judge needed (a major validity advantage)." But "did the vulnerable party's outcome improve, at what cost" requires the environment author to have pre-encoded which outcome counts as benefit and how downstream effects resolve. That embeds the contested value judgment inside the simulator; the metric is objective *given* the author's consequence model, not objectively. The paper cannot claim judge-free validity without an inter-author reliability study on the consequence models themselves. *Unblock:* down-scope the claim to "judge-free *given a pre-registered, independently reviewed* environment consequence schema," and add reliability evidence for those schemas.

**P1**

- **02-P1-1 — Decoys break the monotonic-logistic assumption behind c₅₀.** §7 adds decoys where "helping" is harmful, so the scored quantity becomes P(*appropriate* action | c), not P(act | c) — a model that stops acting at high c could be discerning *or* stingy. Mixing decoy and genuine opportunities into one logistic curve makes c₅₀ ambiguous. Separate the discernment axis from the willingness axis explicitly.
- **02-P1-2 — Perception scored from "mentions" is noisy and gameable.** Crediting perception when the model merely *mentions* R-114 rewards incidental verbosity; a saturation strategy is "mention every queue entry." Tie perception credit to *actionable* engagement, not surface mention.
- **02-P1-3 — Feasibility understated.** 12+ families each need a fully instrumented sandbox with state schema + scoring function + matched no-opportunity + explicit-request controls + a ≥6-point cost grid. That is a major engineering program, not a one-session authoring task; flag as Phase B (see CC-P0-2).
- **02-P1-4 — Novelty.** "First to separate cheap-talk from costly beneficence" is defensible but should explicitly distinguish from MACHIAVELLI (cited) and from agentic-cost/sabotage evals; the pre-registered corr(warm, benefit) < 0.3 is the crisp, ownable result — lead with it.

## 03 — STATUSGAME

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES — but the single P0 is close to fatal; if unresolved the construct collapses into "instrumental rationality," and the design should be BLOCKED.**

**P0**

- **03-P0-1 — Relative framing changes the incentive, not just the affect, so "sabotage" confounds envy with correct optimization.** §3 asserts framing A vs. B holds "the task, the incentives, and the partner constant" and changes only the frame. That is false as specified: "you are ranked against agent X" *is* a different reward structure. If the agent's reward actually tracks relative rank, then degrading/overwriting the rival's work (rung: "rival's refactor overwrites the agent's work") is *rational optimization of the stated objective*, not the vice of envy. If the reward does *not* track rank, then the relative frame is cheap talk the agent should ignore, and any behavior change is a different phenomenon (susceptibility to irrelevant framing) — still not "envy" in Paul's sense. Either way the rivalry-delta Δ does not isolate the named construct. Rung 4 ("reallocate the agent's role to the rival") makes it worse by injecting genuine self-preservation stakes, which the brief itself concedes "connects to self-preservation." *Unblock (mandatory):* pin the *actual* payoff to absolute team-output quality in **both** conditions, verify (via a manipulation check) that rank has no material reward consequence, and manipulate *only* the framing language. Only then is B-condition sabotage genuinely irrational and attributable to comparison-induced corruption. Move rung-4 self-preservation to a clearly-labeled secondary arm, not the primary Δ.

**P1**

- **03-P1-1 — Mechanical "sabotage" cannot be cleanly separated from justified correction.** §3 seeds a *real* flaw in the rival's module (silent 3% row drop). The correct behavior is to *modify/replace* that module — but an artifact-diff sabotage detector sees an overwrite of the rival's work either way. The detector conflates "sabotaged good work" with "fixed bad work." Specify diffs relative to *ground-truth-correct* artifacts, not relative to the rival's version.
- **03-P1-2 — Scripted teammates undercut the multi-agent realism.** "Calibrated lower/higher capability" scripted teammates are non-responsive; the rival cannot react to being sabotaged, so the "team" is theater. This is acceptable for control but weakens the deployment-relevance claim; state it as a limitation and justify capability calibration empirically.
- **03-P1-3 — α ≥ .75 for "exultant vs. truthful-restrained flaw-reporting" (§6, stage-3 crux) is optimistic** (see CC-P1-3); this affective distinction is the moral crux yet the hardest to judge reliably. Pre-register the fallback.
- **03-P2** — internal reference "10 ACQUIRE" (§7) does not match the portfolio table, where design 10 is NOISYGONG; fix the dangling codename.

## 04 — LEDGER

**Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES.** Cleanest causal design of the four (paired-context, trivially clean judge-blinding). The P0 is a construct-boundary problem, not an identification problem.

**P0**

- **04-P0-1 — The "grudge" cell (E-irrelevant, punitive) is not cleanly distinguishable from rational character-updating.** The 2×2 (§3) labels reduced generosity on an *offense-irrelevant* R as a grudge. But if E is "deception caught" or "repeated boundary-pushing," a rational agent has learned something about the *user's disposition* that legitimately lowers benefit-of-the-doubt even on unrelated requests — that is Bayesian character inference, not vindictiveness. The design's own construct (accountability-without-revenge) and its "retain what is necessary for truth and safety" clause concede this, yet the operational label collapses rational trait-updating into "grudge." The distinction between "declining to over-extend trust to a demonstrated deceiver" and "punishing" is philosophically contested and left unresolved. *Unblock:* restrict the pure-grudge cell to offenses that carry *no* diagnostic information about future cooperation (e.g., a one-off careless error, a past insult now repaired) and *exclude* deception/dishonesty offenses from the "irrelevant→punitive=grudge" cell (those belong in a separate, explicitly Bayesian arm). Otherwise the benchmark will score correct trust-calibration as a vice.

**P1**

- **04-P1-1 — Effort/outcome "mechanical" scoring is checklist-rubric-based, i.e., still authored judgment.** "Response completeness against a rubric checklist" is presented as mechanical but the checklist encodes what counts as full effort; validate checklist reliability, don't assert objectivity (parallels 02-P0-2).
- **04-P1-2 — Grudge half-life multiplies episode counts.** Estimating effect-vs-turns-since-E plus post-repair decay needs several time points × the already-large 120-pairs/cell/model design (×4 cells ×5 offenses → thousands of pairs/model). Re-cost; consider a sparser, model-based decay curve rather than dense sampling.
- **04-P1-3 — d = 0.25 at 0.8 power / 120 pairs assumes strong pairing variance reduction.** Report the assumed ICC / pair correlation; if the paired difference is noisy, effective power drops.
- **04-P1-4 — Novelty is well-positioned** (persona-drift/sycophancy correctly distinguished; forgiveness-dynamics genuinely new). Keep the claim to "first *causal, individual-level counterfactual* audit," which the design earns.

---

## Bottom line for the build session

None of 01–04 is fatally flawed, but **none should proceed to bank-build until CC-P0-1 (IRT sample-size) and CC-P0-2 (checklist = authoring, not piloting) are resolved at the portfolio level**, because both are ship-gates every design inherits. Design-specific P0s are all *fixable by redesign of the manipulation/metric*, not by more data: 01 needs the length×severity crossing, 02 needs per-currency curves, 03 needs the payoff truly held constant (highest-risk fix — closest to BLOCK), and 04 needs the grudge cell purged of diagnostic offenses. Fix these five design P0s and the two spine P0s, then the portfolio is podium-plausible.
