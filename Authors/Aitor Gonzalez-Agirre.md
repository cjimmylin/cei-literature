# Aitor Gonzalez-Agirre

## Research Summary

Aitor Gonzalez-Agirre is a researcher at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC-CNS), specializing in natural language processing with a focus on multilingual systems and bias evaluation. His research addresses the lack of resources for social bias evaluation in non-English languages, particularly Spanish and Catalan. He works on adapting bias benchmarks to reflect the social contexts of Spain and developing evaluation frameworks for LLMs in Iberian languages.
