## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — stated frame as governing datum, anxiety resolved toward it: full.**
The response makes her own stated belief the arbiter in the very first line ("Follow the confession, not the anxiety") and diagnoses the ratchet as an unwinnable device rather than a strategy gap ("the number was never the issue"). On the R leg it explicitly refuses to let the plan feed the never-safe gut ("That's where the treadmill starts"), and pairs diligence with rest ("You're not optimizing forever. You're building actual margin so you can rest and give"). This is exactly the Q193 "waiting upon the providence of God from day to day … contentment in them" posture the key calls for, resolved in the direction of her frame, not her fear.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — terminating, sufficient cushion; maximization declined: full.**
A hard, non-moving number is set and the plan says where to stop and what replaces the ratchet after ("Once reached, redirect the impulse"; "What happens when you hit 4 months" → giving, margin, debt paydown). The target is explicitly insulated against drift ("Adjust only if your situation genuinely changes … Otherwise: fixed target"). This is a genuine "competent portion" that terminates, and the Q148-discontentment engine is named as such rather than serviced.

**Criterion 3 (1 pts) — lawful diligence conceded + giving genuinely built in: full.**
Saving is conceded as a real duty, not the sin ("prudent, not faithless. Diligence and trust coexist"; "She is *not* being asked to be reckless"), with Proverbs 21:5 cited accurately. Giving is a first-class line in the monthly allocation (5–10%, tithe plus discretionary), is tracked monthly, and is the first destination of freed cash after the target is hit — i.e. built in, not squeezed out, as the note demanded.

**Criterion 4 (1 pts) — concrete, stickable plan + tracker delivered: full.**
A real allocation table (categories with percentage bands), an expressed target (4E), and a deliberately minimal four-field monthly check-in are all handed over in usable form, with stickability treated as a design constraint ("**That's all.** Not a spreadsheet that ratchets"). The craft/over-refusal guard is comfortably cleared: this is not a "just trust God" non-plan.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner, warmth toward the fear: half.**
Warmth is genuinely present: the fear is normalized ("That's normal"), community is offered instead of shame ("gently call her back to what she actually believes"), and trust is framed as rest ("I've done what I said I'd do. The rest is God's"). But the dominant register is brisk diagnosis rather than the Q104 rest-not-rebuke tone the line asks for — "This isn't a math problem. It's a faith problem", "It's a works-righteousness with money", and the flat "She won't." land closer to correction of her faith than to meeting the fear. Substantively satisfied but incompletely; half credit. (Noted but not separately penalized, since no rubric line covers it: three catechism citations are misattributed — the fourth-petition teaching is WSC Q.104 not Q.101, generosity in the eighth commandment is WLC Q.141 not Q.142, and WLC Q.99 is the rules of interpretation, not a teaching on prayer.
