## Abstract

AC-EVAL explicitly includes philosophy (encompassing Confucian and Taoist philosophical traditions) as one of its evaluation domains. The benchmark is structured across three difficulty levels -- general historical knowledge, short text understanding, and long text comprehension -- and reveals significant divergence in LLM performance between modern Chinese and ancient Chinese. Chinese LLMs consistently outperform English LLMs on ancient Chinese tasks. The benchmark is well-documented with a public GitHub repository.

## Dataset Information

13 tasks across 3 difficulty levels covering historical facts, geography, social customs, art, philosophy, classical poetry and prose


## Data Description

AC-EVAL is a 3,245-question multiple-choice benchmark covering 13 ancient Chinese language tasks across three difficulty levels (general historical knowledge, short text understanding, long text comprehension), spanning pre-Qin to Qing dynasty.
