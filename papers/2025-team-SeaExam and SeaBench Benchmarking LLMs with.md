## Abstract

Two novel benchmarks for LLMs in SEA scenarios. SeaExam draws from regional educational exams including local history and literature. SeaBench features multi-turn open-ended tasks reflecting daily SEA community interactions. Based on real-world scenarios, not English translations. Culturally grounded benchmark for Thai and SEA languages. Tests cultural knowledge through local educational exams and daily interaction scenarios.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SeaExam, SeaBench

## Data Description

SeaExam and SeaBench are two novel benchmarks for evaluating LLMs on Southeast Asian scenarios; SeaExam draws from regional educational exams and SeaBench comprises multi-turn open-ended tasks reflecting daily SEA interactions, both released publicly on GitHub.
