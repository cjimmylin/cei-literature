# Hamidreza Saffari

## Biographical Summary

Hamidreza Saffari is a researcher affiliated with Politecnico di Milano. His research focuses on Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Social Network Analysis, and Natural Language Processing, with particular emphasis on cross-cultural AI and social norms understanding in non-Western contexts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on evaluating how large language models understand and classify social norms in Iranian society, addressing a critical gap in AI research by providing culturally-specific data for non-Western cultures. He investigates the performance gap in LLMs' comprehension of cultural norms beyond Western contexts.

### Significant Achievements
Saffari led the development of the Iranian Social Norms (ISN) dataset, the first comprehensive dataset designed to capture social norms and cultural expectations in Iranian society. The dataset contains 1,699 Iranian social norms, including environments, demographic features, and scope annotation, alongside English translations. His evaluation of 6 large language models revealed a substantial performance gap in LLMs' comprehension of Iranian norms. The work, titled "Can I Introduce My Boyfriend to My Grandmother? Evaluating Large Language Models Capabilities on Iranian Social Norm Classification," was published at NAACL 2025 (Findings), with the dataset openly available on GitHub.
