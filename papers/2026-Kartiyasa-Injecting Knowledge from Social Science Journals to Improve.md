## Abstract

Novel dataset of journal article passages from 151 open-source Indonesian social science journals. Demonstrates recipe for injecting Indonesian cultural knowledge into LLMs via RAG, achieving 81.4% on IndoCulture benchmark.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** Addresses Western bias in LLMs for Indonesian cultural understanding. Uses IndoCulture benchmark for evaluation across eleven provinces.
