# Erik Jenner

## Biographical Summary

Erik Jenner is a PhD student at UC Berkeley's Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI), advised by Stuart Russell. His research focuses on AI alignment and safety, particularly understanding how AI systems generalize their goals and how to ensure they remain aligned with human intentions. Prior to Berkeley, he studied Mathematics and Computer Science at Oxford.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Erik's research addresses foundational challenges in AI alignment, including goal misgeneralization, interpretability of learned objectives, and formal approaches to ensuring AI systems remain aligned. He works on both theoretical and empirical aspects of these problems.

### Significant Achievements
He has contributed to influential surveys and position papers on AI alignment challenges, collaborating with other leading researchers in the AI safety community.

## Major Publications

- **Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of Large Language Models** (2024) - Major survey of alignment challenges
- Work on goal misgeneralization and mesa-optimization
- Research on interpretability methods for understanding model objectives
