# Chenchen Yuan

## Biographical Summary

Chenchen Yuan is a researcher at the University of Tübingen, Germany, working in the Department of Computer Science. Her research focuses on the intersection of probabilistic modeling and moral reasoning within Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Collective Moral Reasoning**: Developing frameworks for probabilistic aggregation of moral judgments across multiple LLMs to reach a consensus.
- **Embedding Optimization**: Innovating in targeted embedding optimization to align deviating models with ethical standards.
- **AI Alignment**: Researching methods to ensure that LLM outputs reflect coherent and human-aligned values.

### Significant Achievements
- **ACL 2025 Contributions**: Lead author of research on probabilistic aggregation and targeted embedding for moral reasoning, addressing the challenge of synthesizing diverse model perspectives.

## Major Publications
- **Probabilistic Aggregation and Targeted Embedding Optimization for Collective Moral Reasoning in Large Language Models**: A key study on ethical consensus in AI.
