# Bohan Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Bohan Zhang is a Member of Technical Staff at OpenAI. He earned his Master of Computational Data Science from Carnegie Mellon University and his B.S. in Computer Science from Peking University. He is a specialist in the intersection of machine learning and data systems, having co-founded OtterTune, an automated database optimization service that was spun out of CMU's database group. He has also worked at Databricks and Striim.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bohan’s research and engineering efforts focus on building highly efficient, large-scale data systems to support frontier AI models. He has extensive experience in using machine learning to optimize the performance of databases like Postgres and MySQL. At OpenAI, he brings his expertise in distributed systems and data engineering to the development of next-generation foundation models.

### Significant Achievements
He co-developed "Grape," a parallel graph computation system that was commercialized and later acquired by Alibaba. His work on OtterTune demonstrated that ML-driven configuration tuning could significantly improve database throughput and latency. He is a key expert in making large-scale machine learning systems performant and cost-effective.
