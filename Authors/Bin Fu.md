# Bin Fu

## Research Summary

Bin Fu is a researcher at the Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences. His research focuses on medical artificial intelligence, particularly in the development and evaluation of vision-language models for clinical applications. He contributed to the GMAI-MMBench benchmark, a comprehensive evaluation framework for medical AI systems.
