# Christina Park

## Biographical Summary
Christina Park served as an undergraduate research assistant in the Department of Psychology at the University of Southern California. She contributed to foundational research in computational moral psychology, particularly in creating annotated datasets for moral sentiment analysis.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Sentiment Annotation**: Work on annotating social media content for moral foundations categories

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of the Moral Foundations Twitter Corpus, a collection of 35,108 tweets annotated for moral sentiment across 10 categories
- Contributed to advancing research at the intersection of psychology and natural language processing
