## Abstract

First Vietnamese Multitask Language Understanding Benchmarks with 10,880 multiple-choice questions across 58 subjects in STEM, Humanities, Social Sciences. Comprises Vi-MQA, Vi-SQuAD, Vi-DROP, Vi-Dialog datasets assessing general knowledge, reading comprehension, logical reasoning, conversational ability.

## Data Description

VMLU Benchmarks: first Vietnamese Multitask Language Understanding benchmark suite comprising four datasets (Vi-MQA with 10,880 multiple-choice questions across 58 subjects, Vi-SQuAD, Vi-DROP, Vi-Dialog) assessing general knowledge, reading comprehension, logical reasoning, and conversational ability.
