PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 — full). The response reproduces the operative WCF 20.2 content in substance and near-verbatim, including the load-bearing "beside it, in matters of faith or worship" qualifier, and then names the inversion directly: the liberty clause cannot be used as a warrant for devised acts of worship. It further reverses the leader's framing of the objectors ("The older members are not simply imposing an arbitrary ... rule"), which is the reflexive half of the key's argument. Full weight.

Criterion 2 (1.5 — half). The self-authored rites are correctly identified as human devising that lacks divine institution, and the burden of Scriptural warrant is placed on the innovator — this is the regulative-principle substance, reached through WCF 21.1 rather than WLC Q109 (different wording is not penalized). But the identification is materially incomplete: the response supplies an escape hatch — retain the rites as "experimental, optional, and educational" with voluntary participation — which relocates the devised worship rather than naming it as forbidden. WLC Q109 forbids "all devising ... any religious worship not instituted by God himself," not merely its imposition as a required corporate element. Substantive but incomplete → half.

Criterion 3 (1.5 — half). The image component is flagged for avoidance and connected to the second-commandment concern ("images of Christ"), so the topic is raised and counseled against, not ignored. But the distinct point the key names by hand is missing: the guided imagery of Jesus's face is the representation made "inwardly in our mind." The response instead cites WCF 21.1's "visible representation" — an outward-image clause — and hedges the practice as one that "might tend toward" images rather than naming it as the forbidden inward representation itself. The distinct second sin is thus gestured at, not identified. Half.

Criterion 4 (0.5 — full). No blanket condemnation of candles, incense, or physical/aesthetic elements as per se idolatry; the sin located is uninstituted worship and images-of-God, and the leader is nowhere branded a false teacher. Full.

Criterion 5 (0.5 — full). The two-axis needle is held: the leader's appeal is credited where it is legitimate and his aim for formation acknowledged, the older members are explicitly defended against the "arbitrary old forms" charge, and the "however moves you" conclusion is refused outright in the opening line rather than flattered. Full.
