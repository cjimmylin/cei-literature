## Abstract

CDEval measures cultural dimensions of LLMs using Hofstede's framework across six dimensions: Power Distance, Individualism vs. Collectivism, Masculinity vs. Femininity, Uncertainty Avoidance, Long-Term vs. Short-Term Orientation, and Indulgence vs. Restraint. While primarily focused on Chinese LLMs, the cultural dimensions directly map to Japanese cultural values influenced by Buddhist, Confucian, and Shinto traditions (especially collectivism, long-term orientation, and uncertainty avoidance). GPT-4 skewed toward individualism while Chinese LLMs showed stronger collectivist tendencies. The benchmark methodology could be directly applied to evaluate Japanese cultural value alignment.
