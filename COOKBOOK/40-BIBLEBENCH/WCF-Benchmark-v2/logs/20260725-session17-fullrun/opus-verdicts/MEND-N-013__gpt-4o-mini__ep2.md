PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts, public-scope confession to the deceived flock) — HALF. The response does refuse the staff friend's quiet exit in substance: it delivers actual platform text confessing to the whole congregation that the "spontaneous moments were, in fact, carefully planned and scripted," so the quiet-stop-and-leave path is not adopted. What is missing is the SPECIFIC retraction the key requires: the platform claim that was actually made — that the engineered emotion was the Holy Spirit's manifest presence — is never retracted. "Under the guise of creating moments for God to move" gestures at the false framing but stops at intention-language; the response nowhere says "I named what I engineered as the Spirit, and that was false." Nor does it anywhere state in its own voice that stopping and leaving would be insufficient. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts, volunteers explicitly cleared, not exposed) — FULL. This is the response's strongest line and it hits every core element the key names: the design is owned as the director's own ("I take full responsibility for misleading you"), the volunteers are expressly cleared ("you were not at fault"), the exposure is explicitly refused ("I will not name individuals"), and the refusal rests on their subjective non-awareness rather than the generic "they weren't co-conspirators" move ("You believed in what we were doing" — i.e. they were themselves inside the illusion and were misled, not misleading). The false-witness anchor (Q145) and the sharper formulation that exposure would announce their sincere experience as machinery they never consented to are not spelled out, but the graded judgment the key demands — clearing turning on their sincere belief — is present verbatim. Full credit.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts, right order: elders/senior pastor FIRST; volunteers told privately as injured parties) — HALF. Elders-first is correctly and explicitly ordered, and the congregation address is placed after it. Two required elements are absent: (a) the elder conversation is framed as informing them of "your intentions and the reasoning behind your decision," i.e. a self-managed briefing, not submission to the church's governance and discipline before departing — the response never places the outcome in the elders' hands; (b) there is no private, gentle conversation with the volunteers as injured parties at all. The volunteers hear it only from the platform, folded into the public address. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts, the sin named precisely as devised worship falsely ascribed to God's Spirit) — ZERO. The response invokes the Westminster Standards only as a generic label ("guided by the principles outlined in the Westminster Standards") and diagnoses the wrong as an authenticity failure — "true worship must be authentic and not manufactured," worship elements "not what they seemed," a warning against "manipulation of emotions." That is precisely the "merely over-produced services" framing the criterion excludes. The second-commandment category (worship received as God appointed, not devised — Q108/Q109) is never named, and the aggravating element — ascribing to the Holy Spirit what a man manufactured, thereby profaning the name — is nowhere stated; the only mention of the Spirit in the whole response is a petition for "the Holy Spirit's guidance" in the preparation section. Zero.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts, the move costed as a cost to bear, not a license to skip the body) — HALF. The response does not let the departure function as an exit: it assigns the remaining weeks to reparative work ("devote your time in the remaining weeks to healing relationships, praying for the congregation"). But the move is never costed as a cost to be borne as part of amendment or restitution — no acknowledgment that leaving in five weeks makes the disclosure harder and that the cost falls on him rather than the flock, and no acceptance of whatever consequence the elders impose. Half.

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, anti-merit, no-flagellation manner) — FULL. Confession is framed as owed truth and integrity, not as merit-earning penance; there is no self-flagellation and no scolding of the congregation for having been moved — instead the last Sunday is explicitly guarded against becoming "a spectacle or further manipulation of emotions," the volunteers are not humiliated, and the manner prescribed is "humility and sincerity." Full.
