# Albina Akhmetgareeva

## Biographical Summary

Albina Akhmetgareeva is a data scientist and researcher specializing in artificial general intelligence (AGI) and natural language processing at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT). She also collaborates with SberDevices on their AGI NLP initiatives.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Albina is a core contributor to the MERA (Multimodal Evaluation for Russian) benchmark, which serves as a comprehensive suite for evaluating LLMs on the Russian language. Her work addresses the critical gap in non-English evaluation frameworks, focusing on the few-shot and zero-shot reasoning capabilities of foundational models.

## Major Publications
- [MERA: A Comprehensive LLM Evaluation in Russian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00000) (2024) - *Standardizing Russian language model evaluation.*
