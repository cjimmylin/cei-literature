# Bumjin Park

## Biographical Summary

Bumjin Park is a PhD student at KAIST, working in the Statistical Artificial Intelligence Lab. He earned his MS from KAIST in 2023. He is an emerging researcher in the field of AI safety and interpretability, focusing on the internal mechanisms of large language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bumjin’s research explores mechanistic interpretability—the study of how neural networks represent and process information internally. He investigates how large language models make normative judgments and the impact of modal expressions on model behavior. His work also covers multi-agent reinforcement learning and the source identification of generated text.

### Significant Achievements
He has published innovative work on "Deontological Keyword Bias," analyzing how specific linguistic cues influence the ethical reasoning of AI systems. Despite being early in his career, his research at KAIST's Statistical AI Lab is contributing critical insights into the safety and reliability of complex AI agents.

## Major Publications

- [Deontological Keyword Bias: The Impact of Modal Expressions on Normative Judgments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00000) (2023) - *A key study on the ethical reasoning of LMs.*
- [Identifying the Source of Generation for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00000) (2022) - *Research on AI-generated content detection.*
