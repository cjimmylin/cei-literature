# Adam Gleave

## Biographical Summary

Adam Gleave is the co-founder and CEO of FAR.AI, a research non-profit dedicated to ensuring advanced AI systems are safe and beneficial. He holds a PhD from UC Berkeley, where he was advised by Stuart Russell at the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI). His work focuses on technical alignment and securing AI against adversarial threats.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adam's research agenda centers on securing advanced AI systems through red-teaming, auditing for hidden behaviors, and developing robust evaluation methodologies. He is a primary maintainer of major open-source libraries for reinforcement learning and imitation learning, including Stable Baselines3 and `imitation`.

### Significant Achievements
As CEO of FAR.AI, Adam has led significant research into "sleeper agents" and deceptive behaviors in LLMs. His doctoral work at Berkeley laid foundational techniques for quantifying reward function differences and improving the trustworthiness of machine learning systems.

## Major Publications
