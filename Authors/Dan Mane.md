# Dan Mane

## Affiliation
Software Engineer, Google DeepMind (formerly Google Brain)

## Research Summary

Dan Mane is a software engineer at Google DeepMind, previously with Google Brain. He has contributed to foundational work on AI safety and is known for co-authoring the influential paper "Concrete Problems in AI Safety," which helped establish the research agenda for AI safety as a field. He has also worked on TensorBoard, the visualization toolkit for TensorFlow, contributing to making machine learning systems more interpretable and debuggable.
