# Ernie Chang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ernie Chang is a researcher working at the intersection of natural language processing and AI systems evaluation. He has worked at Amazon and has connections to Saarland University. His research focuses on language generation, dialogue systems, and methods for evaluating large language models, including novel approaches using AI agents as evaluators.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His recent work examines how AI agents can serve as judges and evaluators of other AI systems, exploring paradigms like "Agent-as-a-Judge" for more comprehensive evaluation of complex AI behaviors beyond simple benchmark metrics.

### Significant Achievements
Contributed to influential work on LLM evaluation methodologies and agentic AI systems.

## Major Publications

- **Agent-as-a-Judge: Evaluate Agents with Agents** (2024) - Novel framework for AI evaluation
- Research on dialogue systems and natural language generation
- Work on controllable text generation
