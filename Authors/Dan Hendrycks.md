# Dan Hendrycks

## Biographical Summary

Dan Hendrycks is the director of the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), a nonprofit organization based in San Francisco focused on AI safety research. He received his PhD in Computer Science from UC Berkeley in 2022, where his research focused on machine learning safety, machine ethics, and robustness. He also serves as a safety advisor to xAI (Elon Musk's AI company) and Scale AI, receiving symbolic one-dollar salaries to avoid conflicts of interest.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on catastrophic AI risks and developing practical tools for AI safety. He works on machine learning safety, machine ethics, and robustness, with particular emphasis on identifying and mitigating existential risks from advanced AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
Hendrycks has authored influential work on AI alignment and safety, including seminal papers on aligning AI with shared human values and analyzing existential risks from AI research. He is the author of "Introduction to AI Safety, Ethics, and Society" (Routledge, 2024), one of the first comprehensive textbooks in the field. As director of CAIS, he leads a major research organization dedicated to reducing societal-scale risks from AI.
