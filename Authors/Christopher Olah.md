# Christopher Olah

## Biographical Summary

Christopher Olah is a Co-Founder of Anthropic and one of the pioneers of mechanistic interpretability, an emerging scientific field dedicated to reverse-engineering neural networks into human-understandable algorithms. He was named one of TIME's 100 Most Influential People in AI 2024. Previously, he led interpretability research at OpenAI, worked at Google Brain, and co-founded Distill, an influential machine learning journal focused on clear explanations.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Mechanistic Interpretability**: Leading Anthropic's efforts to reverse-engineer how trained neural networks work, treating them as binary programs to understand and make safer.
- **Circuits Research**: Developing mathematical frameworks for understanding transformer architectures, including work on in-context learning and induction heads.
- **Superposition Theory**: Investigating how neural networks store multiple features in overlapping representations.

### Significant Achievements
- **TIME 100 Most Influential People in AI 2024**: Recognized for pioneering interpretability research.
- **Feature Visualization**: Developed foundational techniques for visualizing what neural networks learn.
- **Deep Dream**: Co-authored the influential visualization technique at Google Brain (2015).
- **Distill Journal**: Co-founded the interactive ML research journal promoting clarity in scientific communication.
- **105,000+ citations**: One of the most cited researchers in deep learning interpretability.
