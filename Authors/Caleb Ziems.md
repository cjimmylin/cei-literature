# Caleb Ziems

## Biographical Summary

Caleb Ziems is a PhD student at Stanford University, previously affiliated with Georgia Tech. His work bridges the gap between linguistics and machine learning, focusing on how Large Language Models (LLMs) reason through complex social and cultural contexts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Computational Social Science" and the evaluation of LLMs on tasks involving pragmatics, sociolinguistics, and ethical reasoning. He is particularly interested in identifying "Model Misalignment"—where models fail to capture the nuances of human social behavior despite their high performance on technical metrics.

### Significant Achievements
Ziems has co-authored several high-impact benchmarks, including work on "Can Large Language Models Reason about Real-World Social Contexts?" His work provides critical tools for the AI safety community to measure "behavioral alignment" beyond simple factuality.

## Major Publications
