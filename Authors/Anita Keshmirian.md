# Anita Keshmirian

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Anita Keshmirian is an Assistant Professor in Psychology and Data Science at Forward College, Berlin. She also holds an affiliation with Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU) and the Munich Center for Neurosciences - Brain & Mind. Her doctoral research, completed in 2021 at the Graduate School of Systemic Neuroscience (GSN) at LMU, focused on the cognitive facets of group decision-making within social and moral contexts.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus

Her research examines how group dynamics influence moral decision-making, with particular attention to how AI systems (specifically large language models) exhibit similar patterns to human group behavior. She investigates the psychological mechanisms that drive utilitarian versus deontological judgments in both humans and AI.

### Significant Achievements

Keshmirian is the lead author of "Many LLMs Are More Utilitarian Than One," which demonstrates that AI models, when operating as a group, exhibit a "Utilitarian Boost" similar to humans. However, the research reveals that while humans become more utilitarian due to heightened sensitivity to decision outcomes, LLM groups show either reduced sensitivity to norms or enhanced impartiality—revealing fundamental differences in how humans and AI process moral trade-offs.
