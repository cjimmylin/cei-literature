# Alexandra Birch

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandra Birch is a Personal Chair and Professor of Multilingual Natural Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh. She is a world-renowned expert in machine translation, having developed several foundational techniques in Neural Machine Translation (NMT).

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
In addition to her work on translation, Professor Birch is a leading voice in AI governance. She co-authored the *Ethics Whitepaper on Large Language Models*, providing a comprehensive guide for researchers to navigate the ethical, privacy, and safety challenges inherent in developing and deploying massive language models across diverse linguistic contexts.

## Major Publications
