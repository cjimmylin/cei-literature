# Masashi Takeshita

## Research Summary

Masashi Takeshita is a post-doctoral fellow at Nagoya University, Graduate School of Informatics. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing and Applied Ethics, especially AI Ethics and Animal Ethics. He researches the ethics of non-human entities from both engineering (NLP) and philosophy perspectives. Created JETHICS, a Japanese dataset (78K examples) for evaluating ethics understanding of AI models. His work includes theory-based moral AI and examining speciesism in NLP research.
