# Danielle Ochoa

## Biographical Summary

Danielle Ochoa is a researcher who has contributed to studies on moral psychology and judgment. Her work examines how situational factors influence moral decision-making across different cultural contexts, contributing to our understanding of the cognitive and contextual factors that shape ethical reasoning.

## Research Focus

Ochoa's research focuses on understanding how situational and contextual factors shape moral judgments. She has contributed to large-scale cross-cultural studies examining moral reasoning, which provide insights into both universal and culturally-specific aspects of moral psychology.
