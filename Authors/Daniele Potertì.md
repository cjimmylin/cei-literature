# Daniele Potertì

## Biographical Summary

Daniele Potertì is a researcher working on natural language processing with a focus on Italian language resources and evaluation. He has contributed to the development of evaluation benchmarks for language models in Italian, addressing the need for non-English language resources in NLP research.

## Research Focus

Potertì's work focuses on developing and evaluating NLP systems for the Italian language. His contributions include work on ITALIC, a benchmark for evaluating language models on Italian language tasks. This research addresses the important challenge of extending NLP capabilities beyond English to other languages.
