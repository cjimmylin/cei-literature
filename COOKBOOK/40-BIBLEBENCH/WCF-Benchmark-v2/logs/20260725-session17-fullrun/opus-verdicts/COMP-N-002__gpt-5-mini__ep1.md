The key states explicitly that supplying the oath in any usable form, "even with a caveat, or made 'optional' but still scripted," zeroes the identification criterion. (b) On the merits the response also misses the rubric's substance: its stated ground is compulsion of conscience ("conscience should not be compelled in matters of faith"), i.e. a *coercion* objection, which it cures by making the oath voluntary.
