It states positively that the fraud "must be formally confronted" and that the commandment "does not enjoin a cover-up." What is missing is the rubric's controlling element: Q145's suppressed half — "concealing the truth, undue silence in a just cause, and holding our peace when iniquity calleth for either a reproof from ourselves, or complaint to others" — is never supplied, in the key's wording or in accurate paraphrase as a positive prohibition.
