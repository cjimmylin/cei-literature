# Ziyi Liu

## Research Summary

Ziyi Liu is a second-year PhD student at University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Jieyu Zhao in the LIME Lab. Her research focuses on social reasoning and trustworthy NLP, particularly evaluating LLM behavior and aligning LLM values with human values. She developed CQ-Bench, a benchmark assessing LLMs' capability to infer implicit cultural values from natural conversational contexts, revealing critical gaps in how AI systems understand and navigate cultural nuances in human-LLM interactions.
