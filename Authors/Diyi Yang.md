# Diyi Yang

## Research Summary

Diyi Yang is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University. Her research focuses on natural language processing, computational social science, and human-AI interaction, with particular emphasis on understanding social norms, morality, and human values in language. She has made significant contributions to developing NLP systems that understand social context and moral reasoning, including foundational work on the Moral Integrity Corpus and NormBank. Her work bridges technical NLP methods with social science insights to build AI systems that better understand human social behavior.

## Major Publications

- The Moral Integrity Corpus (MIC) for moral reasoning in dialogue
- NormBank: A knowledge bank of situational social norms
- Work on consistency of large language models in value judgments
