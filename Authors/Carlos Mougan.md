# Carlos Mougan

## Biographical Summary

Carlos Mougan is an AI researcher and technical specialist at the European Commission's AI Office (A3 AI Safety). He earned his PhD in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Southampton, specializing in model monitoring. He has held prestigious fellowships and previously worked as a Principal Investigator at the Alan Turing Institute.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Model Monitoring in the Absence of Labeled Data"—developing methods to detect when AI systems are drifting from their intended behavior. He is also a pioneer in "Philosophically Grounded Alignment," specifically exploring how Kantian deontology can be integrated into fairness metrics to create more morally robust AI systems.

### Significant Achievements
Mougan's work on "Kantian Deontology Meets AI Alignment" provides a rigorous ethical framework for machine learning, moving beyond purely utilitarian or statistical definitions of fairness. His role at the EC involves implementing the "AI Act," ensuring that Frontier AI models meet the technical safety standards required for deployment in the EU.
