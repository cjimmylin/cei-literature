# Giulio Corsi

## Biographical Summary

Giulio Corsi is a Research Associate at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence at the University of Cambridge, where he works on the AI: Futures and Responsibility (AI:FAR) Project. He also serves as a Senior Researcher at CARMA (Center for AI Risk Management & Alignment), focusing on offense/defense dynamics and balances generated by AI capabilities. He holds an MPhil and PhD from Cambridge, with his doctoral research (funded by ESRC and Cambridge Trust) examining how online activism can counteract climate change disinformation.

## Research & Impact

Corsi's research focuses on AI risks, disinformation, and epistemic security:

- **Epistemic Security**: Currently leads the Epistemic Security project at Cambridge, exploring how technology (particularly AI) influences information circulation and decision-making. Research investigates how epistemic threats can be countered through peer-produced approaches enabling online community participation in information moderation.

- **AI Risk Assessment**: Developed novel techniques for assessing risks in AI-mediated environments, particularly threats from synthetic media circulation. Published in EPJ Data Science and Harvard Kennedy School Misinformation Review.

- **Foundational Challenges**: Co-author of "Foundational Challenges in Assuring Alignment and Safety of Large Language Models."

- **Singapore Consensus**: Contributed to "The Singapore Consensus on Global AI Safety Research Priorities."
