# Guangyue Peng

## Biographical Summary

Guangyue Peng is a researcher working on evaluation methods for large language models, particularly focusing on hallucination detection and dialogue-level evaluation benchmarks.

## Research Focus

Peng's research focuses on developing comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating LLM capabilities and limitations, particularly in the areas of hallucination and code generation.

## Notable Contributions

Peng is a co-author of "HalluDial: A Large-Scale Benchmark for Automatic Dialogue-Level Hallucination Evaluation," published in 2024. HalluDial is described as the first comprehensive large-scale benchmark for automatic dialogue-level hallucination evaluation.

Key features of HalluDial:
- Encompasses both spontaneous and induced hallucination scenarios
- Covers factuality and faithfulness hallucinations
- Contains 4,094 dialogues with a total of 146,856 samples
- Introduces HalluJudge, a specialized judge language model for hallucination evaluation

The research also introduced the high data quality of HalluDial enables HalluJudge to achieve superior or competitive performance in hallucination evaluation. The dataset and code are available at https://github.com/FlagOpen/HalluDial.

Peng has also contributed to research on FEA-Bench, a benchmark designed to assess the ability of large language models to perform incremental development within code repositories.
