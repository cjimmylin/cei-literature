## Abstract

The paper asks how we develop AI systems that adhere to human norms and values, and whether it's promising to develop systems based on normative frameworks such as consequentialism, deontology, or virtue ethics. While many machine ethics researchers answer yes, the author challenges this methodological strategy by exploring the difference between normative ethics in human practices versus machines.

## Data Description

This book chapter critiques the methodological strategy of applying normative ethical frameworks (consequentialism, deontology, virtue ethics) directly to machines. The author argues that machines lack the resources to justify normative theory use, exploring the difference between normative ethics as applied in human practices versus machine contexts. No models are tested or benchmarks created.
