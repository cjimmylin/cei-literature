## 1. Gist
This 2022 journal article in "Discover Artificial Intelligence" proposes an Islamic virtue-based framework for AI ethics grounded in Maqasid al-Shariah (the higher objectives of Islamic law). The overarching goal of the framework is tazkiya (spiritual self-purification), and it positions this as an alternative to Western ethical frameworks (which tend toward secular consequentialism or deontology). For values-alignment benchmarking, the paper matters because it provides a worked example of how a non-Western religious ethical system can be translated into principles actionable for AI system design — offering a template for deriving benchmark dimensions from Islamic moral philosophy.

## 2. Benchmark/eval design features
This is a normative theory paper. Core claims:
1. Islamic virtue ethics, centered on tazkiya, provides a coherent and comprehensive framework for AI ethics.
2. The Maqasid framework's five essential protections (protection of faith, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth) can structure evaluative criteria for AI systems.
3. Existing AI ethics frameworks are dominated by Western secular assumptions that fail to accommodate Islamic moral priorities.

The paper does not present a benchmark, evaluation, or empirical study.
