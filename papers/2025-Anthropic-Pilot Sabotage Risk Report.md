## Abstract

Anthropic Alignment Science Blog report (October 28, 2025) assessing the level of misalignment-related sabotage risk posed by Anthropic's deployed models as of Summer 2025. Under the Responsible Scaling Policy's future commitments for misalignment risks that initiate from a model's own behavior, the report pilots a partial 'affirmative case' — identifying the most immediate risks from models pursuing misaligned goals and explaining mitigations (monitoring and safeguards). The authors conclude the level of risk is very low but not fully negligible, and note that complete affirmative cases of this kind are uncharted territory for the field.
