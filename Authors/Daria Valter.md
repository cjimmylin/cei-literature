# Daria Valter

## Biographical Summary

Daria Valter is a researcher working on the intersection of moral psychology and artificial intelligence. Her research examines how large language models encode and express moral values, contributing to our understanding of value alignment in AI systems.

## Research Focus

Valter's work focuses on understanding the moral foundations embedded in large language models. She has contributed to research examining how LLMs reflect different moral values and frameworks, which has implications for developing AI systems that can navigate ethical considerations appropriately.
