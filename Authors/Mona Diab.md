# Mona Diab

## Affiliation
Professor, Language Technologies Institute, Carnegie Mellon University (previously Meta AI)

## Research Summary
Mona Diab is a distinguished researcher in natural language processing with particular expertise in multilingual and Arabic NLP. Her research spans computational semantics, word sense disambiguation, and cross-lingual transfer learning. She has made significant contributions to understanding semantic representations across languages and developing NLP tools for under-resourced languages. Her recent work includes research on fairness, bias, and responsible AI, examining how NLP systems can make more nuanced and contextually appropriate judgments.
