# Diana Acosta-Navas

## Research Summary

Diana Acosta-Navas is a researcher affiliated with Stanford University working at the intersection of philosophy and artificial intelligence. Her research focuses on the ethical dimensions of AI systems, including issues of fairness, accountability, and the social implications of machine learning technologies. She has contributed to major evaluation efforts for language models, including the HELM (Holistic Evaluation of Language Models) project.
