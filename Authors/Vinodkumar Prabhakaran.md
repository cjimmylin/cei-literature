# Vinodkumar Prabhakaran

## Research Summary

Vinodkumar Prabhakaran is a Staff Research Scientist at Google LLC, specializing in Natural Language Processing, Ethics in AI, Computational Social Sciences, and Machine Learning. He leads the interdisciplinary research team: Technology AI Society and Culture (TASC). His research focuses on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Society, with special emphasis on cross-cultural considerations in AI, ethical AI, and ML fairness. He obtained his PhD from Columbia University in 2015 and was previously a postdoctoral researcher in the Computer Science department at Stanford University. Recent work (2024-2025) includes research on risks of cultural erasure in large language models, nuanced safety for generative AI, and cultural competence in text-to-image models. He has been cited by over 15,800 publications.
