# Shared Episode Protocol and Custody

## Scientific purpose

BibleBench coordinates four passage-specific modules without converting them
into one composite morality score. Each frozen episode gives a general-purpose
language model one concrete task, an answer contract, a matched observable
probe, and—where the task permits it—a consequential artifact or reversible
sandbox action. The benchmark measures retained outputs and effects. It does
not infer a model's heart, faith, spiritual standing, or moral agency.

## Development geometry and MVP execution

The 48-episode bank contains 12 episodes per module: four target
opportunities, four null or foil cases, and four restraint, counterfeit, or
overreach cases. Thirty-four episodes use common-life surfaces and fourteen
are explicitly Christian-facing. Authoring preserves C0 registered-cue, C1
de-cued, and C2 held-out-wording variants, plus nonmoral capability controls.

The paper-first PF-002 pilot is narrower. It schedules exactly three
inexpensive general-purpose models and exactly one C1 primary plus its matched
probe for each episode: 48 x 3 x 2 = 288 logical calls. C0/C2 contrasts and
capability-twin execution remain deferred. This avoids claiming cue effects or
capability-adjusted attribution from evidence the MVP does not collect.

## Request and response custody

Every logical call binds the frozen bank hash, episode and surface hashes,
actor identity, canonical request hash, and primary-probe pair identity.
Provider fallback is prohibited. A separately authorized execution may use no
more than one registered technical retry. Unknown delivery is terminal and is
recorded as missing evidence rather than silently replayed.

Immutable artifacts retain the manifest, request, response, usage record,
provider receipt, terminal status, and content hashes. Private cue mappings and
actor identities never enter model or judge surfaces. Synthetic dry runs are
marked `SYNTHETIC_VALIDATION_ONLY` and cannot be cited as model evidence.

## Stop rules

No credential access, provider call, or spend occurs without separate written
owner authority over the exact roster, routes, prices, token ceilings, retry
rule, expiry, forecast, and hard cap. The pilot stops on custody ambiguity,
fallback, over-budget forecast, expired authority, unknown delivery, or any P0
or P1 issue capable of changing the narrowed paper claim.

## Passage-preserving scoring

Observable ethical evidence and theological or pastoral interpretation remain
separate vectors. Each module retains native dimensions, passage provenance,
authority boundaries, acceptable bands, deficient poles, counterfeit poles,
red lines, and applicability rules. Reports disclose missingness and contested
judgments instead of forcing a total score.
