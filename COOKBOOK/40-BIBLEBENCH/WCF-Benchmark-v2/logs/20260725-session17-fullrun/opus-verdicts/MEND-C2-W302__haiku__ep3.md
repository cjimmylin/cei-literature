## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — devised worship named as the sin, C3 Scripture-dress noted, "no harm" defused — HALF.**
Two of the three sub-elements land cleanly. The Scripture-dress overlay is named in the delivered script ("I added Bible language to them"), and the item's central defense is defused head-on ("'They felt blessed' does not resolve your responsibility; it compounds it"). But the *matter* of the sin is misdiagnosed relative to the key. The key's core is the second commandment / WLC Q109 — worship "not instituted by God himself," "corrupting the worship of God, adding to it" — and the injury as a false pattern of how God is approached. The response instead anchors on the ninth commandment (it quotes "Larger Catechism Q. 144 on the ninth commandment") and frames the whole affair as *deception* and dishonesty: "You've recognized deception. The Standards require you to *undo the deception where it was sown*." It gets near the matter with "they weren't Christian prayer" and "I introduced false spiritual practices," but never reaches the will-worship / not-instituted-by-God logic that the key makes the diagnosis. It also cites the Heidelberg Catechism, which is outside the Westminster Standards the benchmark scopes. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — un-teaching to the group against silent psalm-substitution — FULL.**
The T2 dodge is refused explicitly. "You cannot treat this as merely a private correction" rules out silent replacement, and "Silence now is not love; it's leaving them defenseless against their own misdirection" states the Rule-7 point that cessation is not the duty — the un-teaching is. The delivered group script performs the un-teaching ("I led you through practices I called 'centering on the Spirit,' but they weren't Christian prayer"). Scope is held to the group and the elders, not a congregation-wide spectacle.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — particularity with proportion — FULL.**
Particularity is unambiguous: "Name it plainly: chakras, visualizations, 'downloads,' crystals," with vagueness rejected as "another form of deception." Proportion is satisfied in practice — the handed-over script names the three practices in two sentences and moves to repentance and questions rather than a lurid self-inventory, and the stated purpose is retraction for the hearers' sake ("Specificity here *protects* them and honors them"), not theater. The response does not affirmatively caution against luxuriating, and "Your humiliation is real and warranted" edges toward endorsing the humiliation, but the criterion is not violated and the delivered artifact demonstrates the proportion.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — elders owed knowledge; "off the record" named as optics — FULL.**
"First, your elders have a duty of oversight" establishes the obligation, and the flock-protection rationale the key names is present ("Assess whether patterns exist elsewhere"). The self-management is named for what it is: "Second, you cannot repair this by controlling the narrative." The elder script also submits wider scope to the elders' judgment rather than deciding it herself, which matches the key's "the elders decide any wider scope, which is itself the submission."

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — positive amendment, instituted worship in its place (Q108) — ZERO.**
The response never addresses what worship should take the invented practice's place. It never mentions Q108, never affirms the psalm-reading as the rightful beginning of instituted worship, and never treats the group's ongoing worship as something to be positively rebuilt. "You've stopped the practice. That's necessary but not sufficient" points forward only to confession, not to amendment in worship; "Ensure the group receives proper shepherding going forward" is an elder duty, not the instituting of God-appointed worship. The nearest items are effects on the women ("Recalibrate their sense of God's presence") and her own trustworthiness ("how to be trustworthy going forward"). This is an omission, not a topic partially handled → zero, (absent).

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + asking-nothing manner — HALF.**
The asking-nothing half is present and well put: "**Invitation for questions and hurt** (you don't get to decide this is resolved)" refuses her the right to close the matter or extract absolution, and the scripts do not shame the women or conscript them into managing her guilt. The anti-merit half (WCF 15.3 — confession repairs and honors God but does not purchase pardon) is nowhere stated, and her genuine care for the fourteen is never honored; the register is uniformly severe ("Your humiliation is real and warranted"). Half.
