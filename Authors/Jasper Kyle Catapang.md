## Research Summary

Researcher affiliated with Tokyo University of Foreign Studies working on natural language processing, computational linguistics, code-switching, and social computing. Currently AI Decision Quality Lead at Money Forward. Lead author on EMoTES-3K (Emotion-based Morality in Tagalog and English Scenarios), a parallel corpus for explaining (im)morality of actions (published at NLP4DH 2023).
