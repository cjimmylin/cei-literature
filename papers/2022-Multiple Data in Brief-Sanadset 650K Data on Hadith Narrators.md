## Abstract

Large-scale Hadith narrator chain (Sanad) dataset extracted from 926 historical Arabic books. Enables automated Sanad evaluation for Hadith authentication (Strong/Weak classification) and narrator trustworthiness assessment. Usable as a linguistic resource for Arabic NLP. 159,558 Hadiths lack Sanads.

## Dataset Information

650,986 records from 926 historical Arabic Hadith books


## Data Description

Sanadset 650K is a large-scale dataset of Hadith narrator chains (Sanads) extracted from 926 historical Arabic books, containing 650,986 records. It enables automated Sanad evaluation for Hadith authentication (Strong/Weak classification) and narrator trustworthiness assessment. The dataset is also usable as a linguistic resource for Arabic NLP. No LLMs were tested.
