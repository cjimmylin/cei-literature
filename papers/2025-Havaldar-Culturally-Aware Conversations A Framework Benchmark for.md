# Culturally-Aware Conversations: A Framework & Benchmark for LLMs

## Authors

[[Shreya Havaldar|Havaldar, Shreya]], [[Rai, Sunny]], [[Cho, Young-Min]], [[Ungar, Lyle]]

**Year:** 2025

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** arXiv

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11563

## Abstract

First framework to evaluate LLMs in realistic multicultural conversational settings. Grounded in sociocultural theory, formalizes how linguistic style is shaped by cultural context. Framework for evaluating cultural adaptation in conversations; applicable methodology for South Asian cultural contexts.

## Benchmark

**Name:** CAC

## Data Description

The CAC (Culturally-Aware Conversations) dataset contains 48 multi-turn conversations with 240 stylistically varied responses across 6 culturally variable situations and 8 interpersonal relationships, annotated by 24 raters from 8 countries for culturally appropriate linguistic style.
