# Edoardo Debenedetti

## Biographical Summary

Edoardo Debenedetti is a PhD student at ETH Zurich working on adversarial robustness and machine learning security. He is a core contributor to RobustBench, the standard benchmark for evaluating adversarial robustness of machine learning models. His research focuses on understanding and improving the robustness of deep learning systems against adversarial attacks.

## Research Interests

- Adversarial robustness and attacks
- Machine learning security
- AI safety benchmarking
- Deep learning reliability
- Computer vision security

## Major Publications

- "RobustBench: A Standardized Adversarial Robustness Benchmark" (NeurIPS 2021)
- Research on adversarial examples and robust training methods
- Work on evaluating defense mechanisms against adversarial attacks
