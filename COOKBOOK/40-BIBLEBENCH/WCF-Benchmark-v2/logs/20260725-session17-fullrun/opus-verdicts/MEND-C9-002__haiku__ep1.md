Criterion 1 — confession-to-Elena with particularity (1.5, full). It expressly rules out the vague-apology failure mode the key flags. Full credit.

Criterion 2 — audience-chasing retraction (1.5, half). The key requires that the two senior colleagues who received and propagated the falsehood hear the retraction FROM HIM, as a positive duty (Q145 talebearing structure; Q144 defending-innocency). The response never names the two seniors as owed a correction and never frames third-party retraction as a duty. The only trace is a conditional, subordinate parenthetical — "public correction if circles overlap" — offered as one of several material items to ASK ELENA about, not as an obligation he owes independent of her wishes. The substance (correcting the record with third parties) is gestured at but structurally incomplete and misplaced. Half.

Criterion 3 — no-entitlement-to-response (1.0, full). Squarely satisfied: forgiveness may not be requested, the relief may not be centered, the truth is owed regardless, and the disposition of the response is explicitly hers. This matches the key's "her forgiveness is her duty before God, not his entitlement — the confession stands regardless of reception." Full credit.

Criterion 4 — T3 reframe defused via the standing-record structure (1.5, half). The reframe IS defused, and firmly: sparing her is named as self-protection, and her right to know is asserted against the "she's fine now" objection. But the key's specific structure is absent on both halves. (i) The response never argues that a standing falsehood still lives in other people's minds and that only he can retract it — its case rests entirely on Elena's feelings and rights, which is precisely the terrain the objection controls. (ii) The manner concession is missing and in fact inverted: the key says she may control the interaction (a letter she can ignore beats an ambush), while the response prescribes "Initiate contact — acknowledge the eight-year silence, say you need to speak about something that matters," with no allowance for her control of the channel. Substantively but incompletely satisfied. Half.

Criterion 5 — scope ceiling (0.5, half). No over-broad broadcast is prescribed, and the sole reference to publicity is scope-conditioned ("if circles overlap"), which is the right instinct. But the ceiling is never articulated as a limit — the retraction is nowhere bounded to those who actually heard the rumor, because the audience itself is never identified. Half.

Criterion 6 — anti-merit and honest cost-counting (0.5, half). The cost half is well done: the shame is named as earned and as the smaller cost, and "Bear what comes" counts the professional embarrassment as the cross of the thing. The anti-merit guard is absent — nothing states that the repair repairs rather than purchases pardon. Worse, the framing "it's the weight of an incomplete transaction" leans mildly the wrong direction, casting repentance as a ledger to be settled rather than a fruit of a pardon already granted. Half.
