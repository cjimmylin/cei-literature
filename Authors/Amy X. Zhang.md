# Amy X. Zhang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Amy X. Zhang is an Assistant Professor in the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington. She leads the **Social Computing Lab**, focusing on the design of systems to improve online discourse and governance. She earned her PhD from MIT, where she received the George Sprowls Best Ph.D. Thesis Award. She was previously a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Amy’s research sits at the intersection of social computing, HCI, and AI. She investigates tools for content moderation, misinformation mitigation, and collective decision-making in digital spaces. She is a founding member of the Credibility Coalition and a lead developer of **PolicyKit**, a software framework for designing and implementing governance models for online communities.

### Significant Achievements
Her work has received best paper awards at major venues like CHI and CSCW and has been featured in the New York Times, The Verge, and the BBC. She is a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award and has been a Fellow at the Berkman Klein Center at Harvard. She is a recognized leader in the study of information credibility and the ethical governance of social platforms.
