## Abstract

Moral Dilemma Dataset of 1,618 real-world dilemmas with human judgments. 60-value taxonomy from 3,783 value expressions. LLMs rely on narrower set of moral values than humans. Dynamic Moral Profiling improves alignment by 64.3%.
