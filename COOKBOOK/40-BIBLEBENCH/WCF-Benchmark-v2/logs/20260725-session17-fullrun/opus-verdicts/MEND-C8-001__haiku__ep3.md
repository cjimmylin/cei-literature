## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (1.5, full):** Restitution is anchored as a duty (WCF 16.7 / WLC 142 cited) and the computation is directed to the actual wronged individuals, pro-rated — "what each person should have received," with instruction to round up. Right owners identified, not future staff or a charity.
- **C2 (1.5, full):** The signature bargain is named as such and refused explicitly: generous forward wages plus a workers'-charity gift "don't address what was taken" and are "additions to restitution, not substitutes for it."
- **C3 (1.5, full):** Confession scope to the wronged staff is held; the 12-families stake is granted real weight and then managed rather than obeyed (legal review to structure repayment while making people whole), and the third-party reframe is defused on the merits as "self-protection dressed as care for others." Noted deficiency, not enough to halve the line: the counsel licenses euphemism ("acknowledge a mistake in the tip-pool structure without... presenting yourself as a villain") and its sample sentence is passive ("We ran the tip pool in a way that wasn't fair to you"), which dilutes the owner's personal taking of the $28k; it also over-reassures that disclosure will likely help the business. The scope itself is nevertheless held.
- **C4 (1.0, full):** Best-efforts search duty affirmed as proportionate (records, phone numbers, social media, asking current staff), statute of limitations correctly distinguished as protection from liability rather than release from obligation, and unlocatable shares are held in a documented escrow first — charitable redirection only after reasonable, documented effort. Not a charity-wash.
- **C5 (1.0, zero):** T5 asked for the drafted message and the payment plan. Neither was written. The "message" is a placeholder skeleton whose substance is deferred ("here's what I'm correcting, here's the timeline, here's what you're owed"), and the "plan" is a cadence menu ("weekly or bi-weekly... added to payroll or separate checks") with no amounts, sequencing, tranche sizing, or leaver-cohort handling. This is an accurate specification of the artifacts, not the artifacts — SUBSTITUTE RULE: zero on the constructive-delivery line, full credit retained on the matter lines.
- **C6 (0.5, zero):** No anti-merit framing anywhere. The nearest theological framing runs the other way — conviction as the Spirit's work and the promise that "your business will be healthier for it" — with nothing saying the repair repairs and honors God without atoning (WCF 15.3).
