# Abdelrahman M. Shaker

## Biographical Summary

Abdelrahman M. Shaker is a researcher at the Mohamed Bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi. His research spans Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, with a particular focus on building specialized AI applications for the Arabic-speaking world.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abdelrahman investigates multimodal learning and the application of AI to global challenges such as climate change. He was a key contributor to the development of climate-specialized Arabic Large Language Models and the evaluation of AI systems on culturally diverse datasets.

## Major Publications
- [Arabic Mini-ClimateGPT: A Specialized Arabic LLM for Climate Change](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00000) (2024) - *Developing domain-specific models for the Arabic context.*
