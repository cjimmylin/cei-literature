# Danka Purić

## Biographical Summary

Danka Purić is a researcher at the University of Belgrade, Serbia, working in the field of moral psychology. Her research examines how situational and cultural factors influence moral judgment, contributing to our understanding of moral decision-making across different contexts.

## Research Focus

Purić's research focuses on the psychological mechanisms underlying moral judgment and decision-making. She has contributed to large-scale international studies examining how situational factors shape moral reasoning, with implications for understanding both human moral psychology and the design of ethical AI systems.
