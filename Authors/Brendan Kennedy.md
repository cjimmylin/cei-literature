# Brendan Kennedy

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Brendan Kennedy is a Data Scientist at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL). He earned his PhD from the University of Southern California (USC), where he worked in both the Department of Psychology and the Computer Science Department. He is a specialist in applying NLP and machine learning to computational social science and AI ethics.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Brendan’s research focuses on measuring and reducing bias in NLP systems. He investigates "upstream bias mitigation" (UBM) and its transferability across different tasks like hate speech detection and toxicity prediction. He also works on interpretable NLP methods and the moral language of hate, analyzing how value-laden speech contributes to online influence campaigns.

### Significant Achievements
During his doctoral research, he compiled influential datasets for hate speech detection and conducted novel studies on the relationship between moral language and online hate. His work at Twitter's content understanding team and his current role at PNNL involve high-stakes applications of AI for social good and toxicity mitigation.
