# Rohit K. Dubey

## Research Summary

Postdoctoral researcher heading the Zurich team for the "Semantic Urban Elements" project at the Computational Social Science chair at ETH Zurich. PhD from ETH Zurich. Previously served as team lead and postdoctoral researcher at Technical University of Munich. Research focuses on LLM, agentic AI, and AI for urban planning, particularly adaptive traffic infrastructure. Active research in computational social science since 2022.
