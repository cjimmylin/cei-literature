# Keith J. Holyoak

## Research Summary

Keith J. Holyoak is a Distinguished Professor of Psychology at UCLA, a position he has held since joining the department in 1986. His research focuses on human reasoning, learning, decision making, and problem solving, with much of his work concerned with the role of analogy in thinking. Recent work includes examining human versus artificial creativity, including a 2026 publication on comparative creativity in poetry. He is affiliated with the UCLA Brain Research Institute and runs the UCLA Reasoning Lab.
