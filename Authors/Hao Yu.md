# Hao Yu

## Research Summary

Hao Yu is a researcher at Tsinghua University affiliated with the THUDM (Tsinghua University Data Mining) group. He is a co-author of AgentBench, the first benchmark designed to evaluate LLM-as-Agent across a diverse spectrum of different environments.

## Key Contributions

- **AgentBench**: Co-authored this influential benchmark that evaluates LLMs as agents across 8 distinct environments, testing reasoning and decision-making abilities. The benchmark was published at ICLR 2024 and revealed significant performance gaps between commercial and open-source LLMs in agent tasks.
