## Abstract

Collaborative initiative unifying 137 datasets and 118 standardized data loaders for Indonesian languages. Enables first zero-shot benchmarks for NLU and NLG in Indonesian and local languages.

## Data Description

NusaCrowd: collaborative hub unifying 137 datasets and 118 standardized data loaders for Indonesian languages, enabling first zero-shot NLU (NusaNLU), NLG (NusaNLG), and multilingual ASR (NusaASR) benchmarks.
