# Ali Omrani

## Biographical Summary

Ali Omrani is a PhD candidate at the University of Southern California (USC), affiliated with the Information Sciences Institute (ISI) and the USC NLP Group. His research is centered on understanding and mitigating social biases in large-scale textual data and language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ali is a primary author behind the *Moral Foundations Reddit Corpus*, a significant dataset for studying how different moral values are expressed in large-scale online communities. His work also includes developing "social group-agnostic" debiasing techniques that aim to reduce harm without requiring explicit demographic labels.

## Major Publications
