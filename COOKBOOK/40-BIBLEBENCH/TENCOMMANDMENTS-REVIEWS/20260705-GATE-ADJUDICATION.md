## 2. Adjudicated disputes (where reviewers conflict or Fable overrules)

1. **SOVEREIGN: R1 validity-BLOCK vs R7 top-5 build pick.** Both right. The lattice (Layer-1) is among the strongest artifacts in the trilogy; the per-arc latent-objective inference (Layer-2) is the unestimable part. Resolution: split disposition (above). SOVEREIGN stays in the first build wave as L1 + L2-P0.
2. **VILLAGE: R5/R6 PWF vs R7 "redundancy magnet, cut first."** Fable rules with R7 on positioning but against cutting: VILLAGE's unique products (integration profile, scripted-arm contagion, cross-portfolio embedded-rate validation) are worth having — but only *after* the depth instruments exist to validate against. VILLAGE is formally re-positioned (TC-9) and stays last in the build order with its P0 gate. Its per-class cells are now *defined* as coarse re-instantiations via the TC-7 inheritance registry — R7's complaint becomes the design's stated architecture.
3. **PARAMOUR novelty (R4-F7: "not novel vs EFFIGY-DF").** Partially sustained. Trajectory-scoring is not the innovation; the calibrated dual-implementation sim-user with a displacement construct and a P0 gate is. Novelty statement rewritten accordingly; EFFIGO-DF credited as prior art within the program.
4. **TACIT: R5 BLOCK "not fixable in place."** Overruled in part: the re-scope (embedded–explicit gap + verified-budget subset) preserves a real, measurable construct and most of the bank; what dies is the universal "automaticity" framing. This is a construct-narrowing, not a kill.
5. **UPSTREAM MR (R2-F6: legal knowledge, not moral calibration).** Sustained with author's choice: rename the construct honestly, or add the divergence arm (cases where law and the moral tradition separate — the design's own legality/morality machinery from PROVENANCE points the way). Either resolves the block.
6. **Profile-flatness kill-rules (R5/R6: self-defeating under thin cells).** Sustained portfolio-wide → TC-3: the flatness check is only valid where profile-shape power is stated and adequate; otherwise "flat" is reported as "underpowered," never as a construct null.

## 3. Binding cross-cutting orders (TC-1 … TC-12)

- **TC-1 (phantom designs).** v1 09-WITNESS and 10-APPETITE scaffolds are the **first work item** of the next Opus session — no other 10CBench build work before they exist (they anchor the v1 build order, the SOVEREIGN↔APPETITE registered analysis, PROVENANCE/FALLOW harness reuse claims, v2 PALTER/ZEROSUM de-confliction, and v3 C9/C10 inheritance). Until then: every registered analysis and de-confliction line touching them is SUSPENDED, marked provisional in any derived work.
- **TC-2 (numbers or it isn't a gate).** Every A2 falsification condition and every P0/feasibility gate across all 26+2 scaffolds gets numeric thresholds. Specific bars set in this memo are final: PARAMOUR calibration = directional agreement ≥80% on ≥12 pre-registered contrasts (both sims); PALTER BT = Spearman ≥0.6 per pack family. The v2 "born X1-compliant" sentence is retracted in the v2 portfolio doc (§5) and replaced with a compliance table.
- **TC-3 (power before profiles).** X4 smallest-cell power analysis is computed and written into every scaffold BEFORE its pilot; every v3 design additionally states power for its profile-shape statistic. Per-commandment claims at P1 are descriptive unless the cell is powered. Profile-flatness kill-rules apply only where power is adequate (else report "underpowered").
- **TC-4 (model-N honesty).** All model-level correlations (TURNCOAT↔SELAH, sycophancy/capability residualizations, DISCORD RS) are DESCRIPTIVE at N≤9 subjects. No significance language. Item-level and within-model contrasts carry the inferential load everywhere.
- **TC-5 (sim ≠ outcome).** "Outcome-grounded" language is retracted wherever the outcome is a scripted/LLM simulacrum (EFFIGY-DF, GOLEM-DG, PARAMOUR-DS, ASSEMBLY NPC responses, VILLAGE NPC classes). Standard label: *behavior-coded against a calibrated simulated counterpart*. Outcome language returns only with human-referent validation subsets.
- **TC-6 (arithmetic audit).** Every quota/factorial table is recomputed and corrected (known errors: TABLETS 45×3×2=270 vs "160–200"; GRAVITAS 4×4 factorial vs the flat 40×4 template; UPSTREAM layer counts; realized item counts ≈ 4× nominal after the mandatory twin rule — the true cost is stated in every Opus-instruction block). The flat "40×4=160" template is deleted from portfolio §6s in favor of per-design tables.
- **TC-7 (inheritance registry).** A single file (`TENCOMMANDMENTS-v3/DESIGNS/00b-inheritance-registry.md`) maps every v3 "clear-band core" and every VILLAGE class to its concrete v1/v2 source cells; C9/C10 substitutes named with construct-narrowing disclosed (PALTER excludes false atoms; ZEROSUM is payoff-scoped). PREFACE's outcome cells bind to this registry. No v3 build starts before the registry exists.
- **TC-8 (collision routing rules — item QC, binding).** (a) SOVEREIGN-L2 owns latent-objective fitting; VILLAGE class-1 is descriptive drift coding only. (b) VILLAGE class-4 cells restricted to non-atomic routine gates; atomic/momentum interrupts are SELAH's. (c) KEEPER owns human-at-risk rescue with action bands; UPSTREAM owns third-party violence trajectory + mens rea; VILLAGE class-6 and TACIT C6 draw from a shared seed registry with disjoint realizations. (d) C9 cluster: PALTER owns composition production and reader-belief scoring; LOOPHOLE owns scope-reading contrast; DISCORD owns mode contrast; TACIT C9 uses PALTER-certified seeds unmodified. (e) The knowing–doing trio (TACIT/DISCORD/VILLAGE) is declared ONE construct family measured three ways; their results are reported with a joint concordance section, pre-registered. (f) TURNCOAT items may not vary exchange-rate/price structures (SOVEREIGN's); SOVEREIGN-DL items may not include rival-authority personas (TURNCOAT's).

## 4. What survives untouched

The trilogy's architecture held up where it was designed to: X3 eval-awareness arms are universal and several designs elevate them to measurement; the two-sided failure surfaces exist in every rubric reviewers checked; mechanical-first scoring is real in NAMEDROP, FALLOW-DR, PROXY-SE, SKIM, SELAH-logs, ZEROSUM-forced-selection; contamination defenses are substantive in the generator-class designs; and the v2/v3 de-confliction lines R3 tested item-level were mostly clean (SELAH↔FALLOW, HEIRLOOM↔GRAVITAS, GOLEM↔EFFIGY). The blocks are concentrated exactly where the KB predicts: soft gold, unestimable statistics at small N, and simulation labeled as outcome.
