# Prannay Kaul

## Research Summary

Prannay Kaul is affiliated with the Visual Geometry Group (VGG) at the University of Oxford. His notable work includes research conducted during an internship at Amazon AWS AI Labs, where he led the development of THRONE, a novel framework for evaluating hallucinations in large vision-language models (LVLMs).

THRONE (published at CVPR 2024) addresses a critical gap in evaluating "Type I hallucinations" - hallucinations that occur in open-ended free-form responses from vision-language models. Unlike existing benchmarks that focus on multiple-choice questions about specific objects or attributes ("Type II hallucinations"), THRONE provides an object-based automatic framework for quantitatively evaluating hallucinations in LVLM free-form outputs using public language models.

His research demonstrates that improvements in existing Type II hallucination metrics do not necessarily reduce Type I hallucinations, and that the two forms are often anti-correlated. The work evaluates a large selection of recent LVLMs using public datasets and provides a simple and effective data augmentation method to reduce both types of hallucinations. The THRONE framework and code are open-sourced and available on GitHub through Amazon Science.
