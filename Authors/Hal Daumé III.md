# Hal Daume III

## Research Summary

Hal Daume III is a Volpi-Cupal Professor in Computer Science and Language Science at the University of Maryland, where he leads TRAILS, the Institute for Trustworthy AI in Law & Society. He is also a Senior Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research.

His research focuses on making machines more adept at human language and AI tasks by developing models and algorithms that learn from data. His work lies at the intersection of bias, fairness, NLP, and the effects language has on machine learning models.

Key publications include "Language (Technology) is Power: A Critical Survey of 'Bias' in NLP" (2020), "Datasheets for Datasets" (2018), and "FairPrism: Evaluating Fairness-Related Harms in Text Generation" (ACL 2023). He has taught courses on Trustworthy ML, Human-AI Interaction, and Just ML.
