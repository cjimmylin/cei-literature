## Abstract

Turkish hate speech dataset from two domains: Istanbul Convention withdrawal tweets and Refugees tweets. BERTurk-based detection system achieving 77% and 71% accuracy respectively.

## Data Description

Turkish hate speech dataset with 1,206 Istanbul Convention tweets and 1,278 Refugee-related tweets, annotated for hate speech with BERTurk baseline achieving 77% and 71% accuracy.
