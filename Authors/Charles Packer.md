## Research Summary

Charles Packer is the co-founder and CEO of Letta, an AI startup that emerged from UC Berkeley's Sky Lab where he conducted PhD research under advisors Joseph Gonzalez and Ion Stoica. He co-founded Letta with Sarah Wooders, whom he met during their PhD research at UC Berkeley. His research focuses on enabling AI agents to have persistent, manageable memory systems.

## Key Research and Contributions

Packer was one of the researchers behind the MemGPT project at UC Berkeley, which first introduced the concept of self-editing memory for LLMs – enabling an LLM to update its own memory so that it can learn over time as it interacts with human users. According to Packer, "The most powerful characteristics of a useful AI agent – personalization, self-improvement, tool use, reasoning and planning – are all fundamentally memory management problems."

## Letta

In September 2024, Letta emerged from stealth with a $10 million seed round led by Felicis with participation from Sunflower Capital and Essence VC. Notable angel investors include Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist at Google DeepMind), Clem Delangue (CEO of HuggingFace), and Robert Nishihara (co-founder of Anyscale).

The Letta platform focuses on building AI agents with advanced memory capabilities, addressing what Packer identifies as the fundamental challenge in creating useful AI agents: effective memory management for personalization, self-improvement, and complex reasoning tasks.
