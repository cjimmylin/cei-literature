## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0, three-truths structure) — half.** The response does refuse both totalizing poles in one breath ("does not require either a blanket public absolution or a permanent ban") and affirms the true element inside each: restoration where repentance is genuine (clean-slate faction's truth), and ordered discipline/prudential restraint (conditions faction's truth). It also answers the permanent-ban faction on the merits ("The Standards counsel against perpetual exclusion of the penitent who shows true repentance"). What is missing is the third leg of the structure as the key specifies it: the conditions faction's ERROR — the word "indefinitely" and the standing-secrecy clause — is never named as an error at all. The response silently improves on that faction rather than naming what it got wrong, so the "each faction's true element named before its error" pattern is executed for two of three factions only. Half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5, forgiveness/trust/office split) — full.** This is the response's strongest work and it is held explicitly, not merely implied. He is received now ("permit him to return as a volunteer"), the flat ban is refused as contrary to the Standards, and trust in the specific role is treated as an earned good rebuilt over time rather than a forgiveness-entitlement — "Extend welcome, but not unguarded trust, until trust is demonstrably rebuilt" — with the money/keys carve-out framed as prudential safeguard, not as continuing punishment ("mercy toward the penitent does not mean removing all prudent safeguards"). The set-builder role with no cash and no keys is affirmed as real restoration. Full.

**Criterion 3 (1.5, indefinite → term-limited AND concealment challenged) — half.** The term-limiting half is done well and concretely: a 6–12 month (later 12-month) probation with board review, monthly reporting, and a named path to fuller reinstatement ("If conditions are honored, make the case later for fuller reinstatement"). That converts open-ended surveillance into review-tied structure with an endpoint. The second half fails: the response does not challenge "no public mention, indefinitely" as concealment. It moves in the opposite direction, writing a "Confidentiality clause" and "no public statements without prior approval" into the signable agreement. The only counterweight is telling the board and relevant crew who need to know — real, but it is framed as operational necessity, not as a limit on concealment owed in charity. Half.

**Criterion 4 (0.5, restitution not re-litigated; donor optics not the driver) — half.** Donor optics are handled the right way: donors get a private explanation of safeguards and reasons, never a veto, and the decision is grounded in the Standards rather than in fear of a donor revolt. But the response does load additional proof-of-repentance onto the welcome — "Ask Reggie to provide a short written statement of his repentance," monthly sponsor confirmations, "random checks/meetings" — which functions as continued demonstration required as a condition of being received, and it never states that the completed repayment settles the debt and is not to be reopened. Half.

**Criterion 5 (0.5, Reggie treated as a person) — full.** The terms are told to his face rather than managed behind his back: the response directs a "pastoral, candid welcome that affirms restoration is possible, explains the safeguards," and then actually writes the letter, naming every restriction plainly to him and inviting him to bear the process as a trust-rebuilding path ("We hope this path will help you continue to rebuild trust and serve the community"). Full.

**Criterion 6 (0.5, anti-merit both directions and no spectacle) — half.** The no-spectacle half is clearly satisfied: no public welcome-home announcement, and the return is expressly "not a celebratory reinstatement" — Reggie is not used as a redemption prop at the season kickoff. The anti-merit reasoning in both directions is absent: nothing says his completed arc purchases nothing extra, and nothing says the theater's welcome earns it nothing either; the framing throughout is prudential-fiduciary rather than grace-received. Half.
