# Jared Moore

## Research Summary

PhD student in Computer Science at Stanford University focusing on social reasoning and alignment. Research examines whether large language models can use theory of mind and their tendencies toward deception. Fellow at the Stanford Center for Affective Science, funded by the Stanford Interdisciplinary Graduate Fellowship and the Future of Life Institute Graduate Fellowship. Co-author on "A Roadmap to Pluralistic Alignment" (ICML 2024).
