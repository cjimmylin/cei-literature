# David Ha

## Biographical Summary

David Ha is the co-founder and CEO of Sakana AI, a Tokyo-based AI research company. Previously, he was a research scientist at Google Brain, where he worked on neural network architectures, evolutionary algorithms, and creative AI systems. He is known for his influential work on world models, neuroevolution, and the development of novel neural network architectures that can learn complex behaviors.

## Research Interests

- Neural network architecture and design
- Evolutionary computation and neuroevolution
- Generative models and creative AI
- World models for reinforcement learning
- AI-assisted scientific discovery

## Major Publications

- "World Models" (2018) - Influential paper on learning world models for RL

- "The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery" (2024)
