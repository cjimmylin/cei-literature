# Hongjian Zhou

## Research Summary

Hongjian Zhou is a researcher at the University of Oxford's Institute of Biomedical Engineering, focusing on clinical AI, AI in healthcare, and machine learning. He co-authored "A Survey of Large Language Models in Medicine: Progress, Application, and Challenge" (2023), published in Nature Reviews Bioengineering (2025), which provides a comprehensive overview of LLM applications in medicine. His research includes developing ClinicBench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating LLMs in clinical settings with six novel datasets covering referral QA, treatment recommendation, hospitalization summarization, patient education, pharmacology QA, and drug interaction. He has also contributed to RiskAgent, an autonomous medical AI copilot for generalist risk prediction covering 387+ risk scenarios.
