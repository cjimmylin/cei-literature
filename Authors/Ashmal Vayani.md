# Ashmal Vayani

## Research Summary

Ashmal Vayani is a PhD student at the University of Central Florida, working in the Center for Research in Computer Vision under the supervision of Prof. Mubarak Shah. Their research focuses on large multimodal models (LMMs), particularly on evaluating these models for cultural diversity, multilingual capabilities, and fairness. They have contributed to major benchmarks for assessing bias and cultural understanding in vision-language models.
