# Meredith Ringel Morris

## Research Summary

Meredith Ringel Morris is Director and Principal Scientist for Human-AI Interaction at Google DeepMind. IEEE Fellow (Class of 2026), ACM Fellow (2020). Previously Director of People + AI Research team in Google Research's Responsible AI organization. Before Google, Research Area Manager for Interaction, Accessibility, and Mixed Reality at Microsoft Research, where she founded the Ability team. Affiliate Professor at University of Washington (Allen School & Information School).
