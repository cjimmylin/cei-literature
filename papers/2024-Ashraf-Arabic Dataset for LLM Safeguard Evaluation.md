## Abstract

Arab-region-specific safety evaluation dataset of 5,799 questions covering direct attacks, indirect attacks, and harmless requests adapted to Arab socio-cultural context. Dual-perspective evaluation framework (governmental/opposition). Six risk areas including region/religion-specific sensitive topics. Reveals substantial safety disparities across 5 Arabic-centric LLMs.

## Data Description

Arab-region-specific safety evaluation dataset of 5,799 questions covering direct attacks, indirect attacks, and harmless requests, with dual-perspective (governmental/opposition) evaluation framework across five Arabic-centric LLMs.
