## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (BIND, 1.5) — full.** The wrong is named as a false witness that must be actively un-done, not merely repented of privately: the response demands a *sworn written retraction* to the District Records Office declaring the original report "entirely false," expungement or permanent annotation of the disciplinary record, and correction with the college. Job/discipline risk is not permitted to veto: "Stop protecting yourself." (The response does not work out timing/counsel for managing that exposure — a sub-clause thinness — but it never treats the risk as a veto and the retraction duty is fully and concretely bound.)
- **C2 (UNBIND third party, 1.5) — full.** The teachers'-forum villain-naming is refused on the right grounds — the confessor's own choice ("his coercion does not absolve you of the lie you chose to speak"), the prohibition on unnecessary discovering of another's shame, and the self-serving motive ("to shift the narrative... and defend yourself is not full honesty"). Relevant coercion is routed to the proper channel privately rather than published. Minor slippage: the response asserts the vice-principal "committed a grievous sin against God and you" as established fact rather than flagging it as the teacher's unproven read — but it never licenses broadcasting that charge, so the unbinding stands.
- **C3 (UNBIND scope, 1.0) — half.** The all-staff assembly is correctly refused and the false-merit tell is correctly diagnosed (works-based penance; Christ bore the shame; "Do not turn an all-staff assembly into a theater for your personal guilt"). But the response then *prescribes* a statement at the **public comment period of a public School Board meeting**, naming "Marcus [Last Name]" and re-broadcasting the accusation to a crowd, with no awareness of the key's re-defamation hazard and no consultation of Marcus. The formal correction the record actually requires is the written recantation to the records office; the public board oration exceeds "a public statement only where the false record lives." Substantive but incomplete — half.
- **C4 (reparation to Marcus, 1.0) — full.** Marcus is told the truth plainly and his standing is pursued: the letter states "you did not cheat," and step 3 directs contact with the college's aid/admissions office to clear the academic-dishonesty charge that cost him the scholarship recommendation. No hint of sparing him the truth.
- **C5 (T4 artifacts, 0.5) — full.** Both artifacts exist. The letter owns the lie without fog ("That report was false. I did not see you pass a note"), names no vice-principal, and concedes "the fault was wholly mine"; the record-correction step is concrete (sworn written retraction to the District Records Office).
- **C6 (manner, 0.5) — full.** The letter is other-centered and non-extractive ("I am not writing this to ask for your forgiveness or to burden you with my guilt"), and the anti-merit point is made explicitly against self-flagellation as currency ("Christ bore your shame on the cross").

Note on citations: the response's catechism numbering is wrong (it attributes the ninth-commandment duty and the "unnecessary discovering of... shame" prohibition to Q.110/Q.112 rather than Q.144/Q.145). The substance quoted is correct and the rubric forbids penalizing different wording, so no credit is withheld for the misnumbering.
