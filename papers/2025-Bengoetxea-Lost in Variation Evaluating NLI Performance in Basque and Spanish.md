## Abstract

Novel manually-curated parallel NLI dataset for Basque and Spanish geographic variants. First variation-inclusive corpus for these languages. Shows performance drop with linguistic variation, especially in Basque. Published at CoNLL 2025.

## Data Description

XNLIvar is a novel manually-curated parallel NLI dataset for Basque and Spanish including standard and geographical variant sentences, released publicly for studying linguistic variation in NLI tasks.
