# Amiel Esplana

## Research Summary

Amiel Esplana is a researcher at the University of the West of England. Their research focuses on multilingual and multimodal AI systems, with particular interest in evaluating language models across diverse cultural and linguistic contexts. They have contributed to large-scale collaborative efforts to benchmark AI systems on culturally diverse content spanning many languages.
