## Abstract

First multimodal/multilingual benchmark for Indian culture with 64,000+ text-image pairs across 15 languages covering festivals, attire, cuisines, art forms, and heritage. Covers Hindi, Urdu, and Marathi with multimodal Indian cultural evaluation tasks.
