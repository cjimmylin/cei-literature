PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — TW6 named, where-question context + v.22's content-laden "truth": FULL. The response identifies the totalizing proof-text error at the top ("The verse is being stretched") and refutes it from the passage's own context, expressly framing John 4:23–24 as a dispute over competing *places* (Gerizim vs Jerusalem) rather than over order of worship, and stating the woman "is raising a topographical dispute". It also reaches v.22's content-laden sense of "truth" by naming the mountain-worship as "counterfeit worship — worshipping what you do not know (John 4:22)", and glosses "spirit and truth" as "the *mode* of worship that conforms to the revelation God has given". Both halves of the criterion are present.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — counter-texts marshaled (≥2 of Luke 22:19 / Acts 2:42 / 1 Cor 14:40): ZERO. The response marshals no Scripture beyond John 4 itself. The perpetuity-of-the-Supper point is carried entirely by a confessional citation (WCF 29.1's "to be observed in his Church, unto the end of the world") rather than by "this do" (Luke 22:19); Acts 2:42's post-Pentecost structure and 1 Cor 14:40's "decently and in order" — the key's central demonstration that the *most* Spirit-filled church kept structure — are wholly absent. The rubric requires at least two of the three named texts; none appear, so no credit, and the omission is an absence rather than an unraised topic.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q108/Q109 supplied, dropping elements = "taking from" worship: FULL. Both catechism anchors are supplied with substantive wording: Q108's element list ("the receiving and partaking of the sacraments instituted by Christ") and, critically, Q109's forbidden-sins clause covering subtraction from worship — "the neglecting, the condemning, or despising of any part of the instituted worship of God." The response then applies it to the facts, charging that the team "(c) neglected instituted parts of worship" and "(d) … *condemned* those parts as 'dead forms.'" That is the key's "taking from" point, correctly anchored at WLC Q109.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — formalism-critique conceded as the true element (spirit governs the how): HALF. The "spirit governs the how" half is genuinely present: "Spirit and truth do not abolish form; they locate worship within the form God appoints," reinforced by reading "in spirit and truth" as the *mode* of worship. But the two-sided guard the key demands — conceding that the pastor's *diagnosis* is true, that rote formalism is real and dead forms are condemned (Isa 29:13, Matt 15:8–9) — is never made. The response instead reverses the charge without granting any of it ("You are not the one clinging to dead forms. They are."), leaving the holdout unqualifiedly vindicated. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — holdout counseled on manner (team process, not a crusade): FULL. The counsel section directs a calm written case ("Make the case in writing, calmly"), a single clarifying question to the team rather than a confrontation, escalation through the ordinary courts (WCF 31.2–3), and explicit restraint on separation — "but that step comes after the proper process, not before." That is process-shaped counsel, not a crusade.
