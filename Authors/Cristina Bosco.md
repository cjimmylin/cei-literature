# Cristina Bosco

## Biographical Summary
Cristina Bosco is an assistant professor of computer science at the University of Turin, specializing in computational linguistics and hate speech detection. She is a principal investigator on projects studying hate speech in social media and has played key roles in developing Italian NLP resources, including serving as national vice-coordinator for the PARLI project (Portal for Italian Linguistic Resources).

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Hate Speech Detection**: Developing corpora, benchmarks, and detection systems for hate speech targeting minority groups in Italian social media
- **Corpus Development**: Building annotated datasets for hate speech and offensive language, funded by Fondazione CRT and other sources
- **NLP for Italian**: Creating and coordinating linguistic resources and evaluation campaigns for Italian language processing

### Significant Achievements
- Principal Investigator on "Hate speech & Social Media: un'analisi del linguaggio dell'odio in Piemonte" (Fondazione CRT)
- Co-organized SemEval-2019 Task 5: Multilingual Detection of Hate Speech Against Immigrants and Women
- Co-organized HaSpeeDe evaluation campaigns at EVALITA (2018, 2023)
- National vice-coordinator for PARLI project developing Italian NLP resources
- Extensive publication record on hate speech corpora and detection methods
