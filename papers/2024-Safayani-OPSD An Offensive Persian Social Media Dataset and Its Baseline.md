## Abstract

Over 50,000 Persian social media posts from Twitter and Instagram, manually annotated for offensive language. Three-stage labeling process with inter-annotator agreement. Best F1 of 76.9% (3-class) and 89.9% (2-class) with XLM-RoBERTa.
