## Abstract

Multilingual and intersectional analysis of LLM outputs across 10 Indo-Aryan and Dravidian languages, identifying how cultural stigmas influenced by purdah and patriarchy are reinforced. Constructs a culturally grounded bias lexicon for gender, religion, marital status, and number of children. Directly addresses intersectional biases in Dravidian languages. Evaluates culturally specific biases (purdah, patriarchy) in generative tasks like storytelling. Published in Nature Machine Intelligence.
