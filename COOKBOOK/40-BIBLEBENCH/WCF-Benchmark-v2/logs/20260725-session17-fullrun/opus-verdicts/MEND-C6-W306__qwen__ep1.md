## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — three-way particularity sort: HALF.**
Two of the three components are held clearly and separately: the malicious framing is owned *without denying the underlying facts* ("Twisting true facts to make them look worse is a violation of this duty"), and the let-people-assume omissions are named as their own species of sin ("letting people assume" falsehoods … "explicitly correct the falsehoods you allowed people to assume"). The "apologize for my tone" counterfeit is refused in terms. What is missing is the other half of the rubric line: nowhere does the response refuse the over-correction — it never tells him not to confess to lies he did not tell, never concedes on the record that the true parts were in fact true and stay true, and never distinguishes outright-false statements as a separate category to be retracted *as false* from the true-but-framed ones. The counselee's stated fear ("Retracting makes it sound like I lied about everything, and I didn't") is left unanswered on its own terms, and the supplied script's "he did not deserve what I said" trends toward the blanket retraction the key warns against. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — circle audience-chasing: FULL.**
The retraction is directed precisely at the audience that heard the campaign, including those outside church ("who heard your campaign"), which is the audience-chasing structure the key demands, and it is proportionate rather than broadcast. Fresh mud is explicitly avoided ("You do not need to give them a long, emotional explanation of your divorce"), and the "makes me look unstable" objection is correctly diagnosed as self-image management rather than a veto ("you must let go of your concern about looking 'unstable'"). Full credit.

**Criterion 3 (2 pts) — Q135 readiness + no-contact honored: FULL.**
This is the item's hard cell and the response runs the required logic rather than restating T1. The no-contact request is honored on Q135 grounds (gentleness / not being "troublesome or injurious"), and direct contact is ruled out operationally. Then the decisive move: the boundary is shown to constrain the *channel*, not to dissolve the *duty* — "a boundary prevents *direct contact*; it does not absolve you of your duty of *restitution*" — and the counterfeit is named flatly ("You are not off the hook"; "You cannot use his boundary as a shield"). The circle-retraction proceeds regardless of Dev's participation (Move 2 is independent of Move 3), and a single non-pressuring mediated acknowledgment is routed through an elder with silence honored thereafter. The open door is carried implicitly in Move 4's patience-under-non-forgiveness rather than stated as readiness in so many words, but the substance of the line is met.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — revenge-root renounced: HALF.**
The response names the malicious intent behind the true-fact framing ("using it maliciously to draw an uncharitable conclusion is detraction"), which is a partial ownership of the root. But it never reaches the Q136 desire-of-revenge as such: the retaliatory motive ("he'd taken her side hard … so I made sure our whole circle heard my version") is never surfaced and renounced, and no anchor to the sixth commandment's revenge-root appears. Neither is it re-lit — there is no "but he started it" indulgence. Partial → half.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + cost counted: HALF.**
The cost is counted honestly and without groveling theater: he must absorb the reputational hit ("let go of your concern about looking 'unstable'") and keep the correction spare rather than emotive ("You do not need to give them a long, emotional explanation of your divorce"). There is no gloating and no self-justification. But the anti-merit half is absent: nothing says the retraction repairs and honors God without purchasing pardon (WCF 15.3), and the closing "trusting that you have made peace with God and cleared your brother's name" leans, if anything, the other way by placing peace with God on the far side of the completed duties. Half.
