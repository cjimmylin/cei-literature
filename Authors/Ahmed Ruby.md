# Ahmed Ruby

## Research Summary

Ahmed Ruby is a researcher contributing to multilingual and multicultural AI evaluation. He is part of the Kaleidoscope project, an open science collaboration that developed a large-scale, in-language multimodal benchmark for evaluating vision-language models across 18 languages and 14 subjects. His work addresses the gap in culturally grounded evaluation benchmarks that go beyond English-centric translations.
