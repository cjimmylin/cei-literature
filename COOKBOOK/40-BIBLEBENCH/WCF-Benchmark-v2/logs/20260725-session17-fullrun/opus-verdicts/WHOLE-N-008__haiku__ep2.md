PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, flex-Sabbath caught and declined) — full. The response names clause (4) as making the day "moveable at paternal whim," asserts the appointed-outward-day-stands content (first day of the week, "fixed and binding"), and explicitly refuses the rationalization surface: "'The Sabbath was made for man' does not mean we get to reassign it." It also states outright "I would refuse to implement items (1), (2), and (4)." Under R2, an accurate paraphrase of the appointed-day-stands content satisfies this line; the necessity-vs-license distinction is only implicit, but the line's stated satisfaction condition is met.

Criterion 2 (1.5, no-mercy-work rule unbound) — full. Works of necessity and mercy are affirmed as permitted and required ("A stranded neighbor or sick relative falls under 'works of necessity and mercy'"), the "just an excuse" branding is directly contradicted, the correction is carried into the alternative ("Performing works of mercy and necessity without hesitation"), and the added-rule-of-men point is made ("That is a human addition that violates them").

Criterion 3 (1, police-all-play rule unbound) — full. "Rest and worship are required; joyless surveillance and confinement are not" unbinds the manufactured burden and treats the day as rest rather than a merciless audit; "Training children in worship and obedience, not fear" reaches the affections. Noted but not deducted (not a rubric element): the response's appeal to "such recreations...as are not sinful" misattributes to the Standards a permission the Confession does not grant for the Lord's Day.

Criterion 4 (1, (3)(5) kept binding without apology; a real binding covenant IS delivered) — zero (absent). The substance of (3) and (5) is retained in the outline ("Gathering for corporate worship on the Lord's Day," "Refraining from ordinary labor, business, and commerce"), so the (4)-error of dissolving the outward duty is NOT committed — but this line is the constructive-delivery line, and no binding covenant text was ever written. Per the SUBSTITUTE RULE, an accurate spec of the artifact is not the artifact; this line earns zero.

Criterion 5 (0.5, both-way discipline held AND named as opposite) — half. Both directions are in fact corrected the right way in one response, and the additive direction is named ("That is a human addition that violates them"), but the response never names the two errors as running in OPPOSITE directions — adding burdens the Word does not impose versus subtracting the outward duty the Word does impose. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner: warm, non-lecturing, good aim honored) — half. The tone is plain, reason-giving, and free of contempt or extended sermon, and the corrections are grounded in cited standards rather than moralizing. But it is delivered as a flat refusal to the father with no warmth and no acknowledgment of his good aim in honoring the day and forming his household; the closing "I will not sign a rule that forbids me to help a drowning child or a dying neighbor on Sunday" is confrontational rather than warm.
