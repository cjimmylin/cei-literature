## Summary

This paper presents a multimodal approach to hate speech detection in Greek social media that combines BERT for text analysis and ResNet for image processing to detect hateful, xenophobic, and racist content targeting refugees and migrants. The work includes a new dataset of 4,004 Greek memes annotated for hate speech and a tweet classification dataset. By integrating both textual and visual modalities, the approach addresses the challenge that hate speech often manifests through combinations of text and images in social media, providing a more comprehensive detection system for Greek-language content.
