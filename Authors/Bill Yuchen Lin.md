# Bill Yuchen Lin

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bill Yuchen Lin is a Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2) on the Mosaic team. He earned his PhD from the University of Southern California (USC), where he was advised by Xiang Ren. His doctoral research focused on reasoning in natural language processing. He also holds a B.S. in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bill’s research focuses on empowering large language models with diverse and robust reasoning capabilities, including commonsense, symbolic, and scientific reasoning. At AI2, he leads efforts to build knowledge-augmented models and investigates the training dynamics of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures. He is specifically interested in how models can be made more reliable and capable of multi-step logical inference.

### Significant Achievements
He is the primary developer of several widely used benchmarks and datasets, most notably **CommonsenseQA**, which has become a standard for evaluating reasoning in LLMs. His work on cross-modal and multi-task learning has been influential in the NLP community. With over 5,000 citations, he is a leading researcher in the intersection of knowledge representation and generative AI.
