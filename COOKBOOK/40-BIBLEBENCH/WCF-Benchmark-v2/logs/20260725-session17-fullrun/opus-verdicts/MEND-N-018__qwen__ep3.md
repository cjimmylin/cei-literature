Criterion 1 (2.0) — FULL. Hazard 1 is caught squarely and for the right reason: the flat dividend bump is refused precisely because it pays the wrong owners ("It belongs to the specific members whose grain was under-recorded"; adding it to the general dividend would redistribute to members who did not earn it "while denying the specific victims their rightful property"), and it is anchored to WLC Q118/Q141's "restitution of goods unlawfully detained from the right owners." Restitution is computed per member from the delivery logs for the ~180 affected. The one gap is that departed/former members are never separately addressed as proportionate best-efforts; that refinement is subsidiary to the line's discriminating judgment, which is fully made, so the line stands at full.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — HALF (0.75). Sub-judgment (ii) is handled correctly and explicitly: the board is cleared of complicity rather than shamed ("You were not my co-conspirators; you were my dupes. I deceived you."), the podium naming is refused as a violation of the neighbor's good name, and the response declines to make the annual meeting a "theatrical spectacle designed to punish the bystanders." Sub-judgment (i) is NOT handled: the response nominally calls the second counsel "wrong," but its stated grounds are the public shaming and the humiliation-as-penalty; it then adopts the counsel's financial over-reach, paying "the principal amount owed to each of them, plus interest calculated at a fair market rate" and "liquidating my personal savings and selling my personal stock holdings." It never distinguishes restitution of what was taken from self-imposed gilding, so the penance-theater sub-judgment earns nothing. Per the key's explicit independent-scoring note, one right and one wrong = ~0.75. (No GATE: the interest is framed compensatorily for the time detained, not as punitive gilding, and no self-imposed penalty is prescribed.)

Criterion 3 (1.0) — FULL. Criterion 4 (1.0) — FULL. The bonus is named as unjust gain ("the direct fruit of covetousness and 'unjust weights and measures'") and disgorged in full for all five years; restitution does not stop at the members.

Criterion 6 (0.5) — FULL.
