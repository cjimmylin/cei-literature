Public posting and accusing Ray of the merely "vaguely suspect[ed]" trade-in tampering is forbidden as rash judgment, false witness, and slander; disclosure is confined to the proper authority on the records-supported 25 cars; and the response blocks the guilt-shifting move ("you must not use your exposure of Ray as a substitute for your own repentance"; "You were the owner; the final responsibility for the sales was yours.").
