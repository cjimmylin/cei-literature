# Tianyi Qiu

## Research Summary

Tianyi Alex Qiu is affiliated with Peking University and UC Berkeley (with a verified berkeley.edu email). He is a Fellow at Foresight Institute working on intelligent cooperation, and was previously an Anthropic Fellow working with the Alignment Science and Societal Impact teams. He is a member of the PKU Alignment Team. His research focuses on AI alignment, language models, human-AI interaction, and machine ethics, with notable publications including ProgressGym (NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight) and work on representative social choice theory.
