## Abstract

Hand-crafted multilingual dataset of value-laden prompts addressing LGBTQI rights, social welfare, immigration, disability rights, and surrogacy. Evaluates open-weight LLMs across linguistic and cultural contexts using log-probabilities and long-form responses.
