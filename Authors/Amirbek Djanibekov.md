# Amirbek Djanibekov

## Research Summary

Amirbek Djanibekov is a researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). Their research focuses on multilingual and multimodal AI systems, with particular emphasis on evaluating language models for cultural inclusivity and stereotype assessment. They have contributed to major benchmarking efforts for evaluating LLMs across diverse languages and cultures, including work on culturally-specific stereotypes in multilingual settings.
