## Abstract

Multicultural intent detection and slot-filling dataset for 16 African languages, with utterances generated by native speakers across domains (banking, travel, home, dining). Addresses Western-centric bias in existing benchmarks. Covers all 9 target languages. Specifically designed to address Western-centric bias with culturally relevant African utterances. Demonstrates advantage of African-cultural over Western-centric data for cross-lingual transfer.
