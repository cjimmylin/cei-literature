# Brendon Boldt

## Biographical Summary

Brendon Boldt is a PhD student at Carnegie Mellon University's Language Technologies Institute (LTI). He holds a Master of Language Technology from CMU and a BS in Computer Science and Philosophy from Marist College. His work is situated at the intersection of linguistics, machine learning, and the philosophy of science.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Emergent Language"—using computer simulations to study how communication systems evolve in multi-agent environments. He investigates the meta-scientific issues of these simulations, aiming to understand the information-theoretic properties of AI-generated languages and their alignment with human linguistic structures.

### Significant Achievements
Boldt has developed "XferBench," a data-driven benchmark for evaluating emergent language and its transferability. His work provides a rigorous framework for studying how AI agents can discover and refine their own communication protocols, which is a key component for modular and scalable AI systems.
