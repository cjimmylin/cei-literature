# Edward Choi

## Biographical Summary

Edward Choi is an Associate Professor at KAIST in the Graduate School of AI. His research focuses on applying machine learning and deep learning to healthcare, particularly analyzing electronic health records and developing AI systems for medical applications. He has made significant contributions to temporal modeling of patient data and developing interpretable AI systems for clinical decision support.

## Research Interests

- Healthcare AI and machine learning
- Electronic health records analysis
- Deep learning for medical applications
- NLP in healthcare settings
- AI fairness and evaluation in Korean context

## Major Publications

- "Doctor AI: Predicting Clinical Events via Recurrent Neural Networks" (2016)
