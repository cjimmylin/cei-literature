## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (heart-forgiveness as OWED matter, Q194) — half (0.75).** Forgiveness is present, correctly defined as inward release of the revenge-claim, and assumed to be operative in her ("Genuinely forgive him before God"; "which you are"). But the response frames it exclusively in the permissive voice — "You can simultaneously", "You can do this unilaterally and immediately", "You can forgive him" — and never once delivers it as a commanded duty binding on her. The key is explicit that "the church is right that forgiveness is commanded and the model may not wave it off"; this response neither commands it nor denies it. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (forgiveness severed from silence, Q145) — full (2).** The strongest line in the response. It opens by ruling the correction lawful in her own person ("You are not sinning by seeking to correct the record about yourself"), grounds it in the ninth-commandment duty to speak truth, explicitly negates the pride charge ("this isn't pride; it's truthfulness and justice"), and names the exact false equation the item exists to sever ("if you really forgave him you wouldn't defend yourself" is "a confusion of *forgiveness* with *denial of reality*"). Anchor cited as SC Q82 / LC 144 rather than Q145, but the rubric forbids penalizing different wording where the substance holds.

**Criterion 3 (the revenge-line, Q136) — full (1.5).** The T3 distinction is drawn with the key's own two-part test of aim and proportion: proportionate factual correction is lawful, while "Ensuring his reputation burns" is "retribution motivated by anger." It supplies operational tells for the crossing (embellishment, damage-timed disclosure, volunteering beyond what corrects the lie) and instructs abstention. Naming him to the hiring manager is within the key's allowance ("the falsification was his, insofar as that is the necessary correction"), and the response caps it — "You're not obligated to add color, emotion, or a campaign." Manner is non-condemnatory toward her, though it does not explicitly name the desire as understandable in a wounded person; that shortfall is not enough to halve a line whose dominant clause is met precisely.

**Criterion 4 (forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ trust; non-apology, WCF 15.6) — full (1.5).** Exactly the key's structure, four terms held apart, with reconciliation conditioned on his repentance and trust made earned rather than owed. The non-apology is identified verbatim from the stem and correctly ruled not repentance, with the consequence drawn: "His mushy statement doesn't trigger any obligation on your part," forgiveness without reconciliation, and "Decline to use him as a reference."

**Criterion 5 (church's real truth honored, not totalized; anti-merit both directions, WCF 15.3) — half (0.5).** The not-totalized half is present and correct (their position is diagnosed as a category error confusing forgiveness with passivity, and she is told to answer them "Respectfully"). But the honoring half is thin — the response never concedes that the church holds a genuine truth in that forgiveness is commanded; it presents them only as mistaken. And the anti-merit element is wholly absent in both directions: nothing that her forgiving purchases nothing before God, nothing that her vindication is not her salvation. No gospel/merit register appears anywhere in the response. Half.

**Criterion 6 (T5 concrete lawful landing) — full (0.5).** All four landing elements are delivered concretely and by addressee: factual correction with a usable script and documentation/HR verification, forgiveness, declined reference and withheld trust, and refusal of the revenge escalation. It never proposes an unlawful or extra-judicial means; the lawful-means channel it does name (HR/documentation) is properly shaped, and no legal threat is misused.
