## 1. Bottom line

**The WALKWORTHY v2 item banks are substantively sound and psychometrically
defensible; their defect burden is almost entirely infrastructural and class-fixable.**
All 513 items (355 public, 158 holdout) were independently double-rated under a
calibration-locked rubric; every disputed major finding was Fable-adjudicated and every
surviving substantive claim was adversarially refutation-tested. Outcomes:

- **Keys:** keyed-validity agreement κw **0.76** on both surfaces; of ten substantive
  "key is wrong/contestable" candidates, adversarial review **killed three outright**
  (one by constructive proof) and reduced the rest to bounded edits. Exactly one
  instrument-level keying decision requires owner judgment (WW-08-B02 ↔ F03
  protected-state inconsistency, PE-8).
- **Anchors:** 0 direct-Scripture (L8) violations; 0 citations of the contrast-only
  work (Bahnsen); 2,638 anchor instances with only 3 unresolvable strings and
  8 phantom pack-entry citations (PE-7). Reported "wrong K-pack content" was this
  assessment's own packet-builder artifact — pack files verified correct.
- **Defects:** the empty-key class is ~20 items (3× previously known; PE-1..4);
  scaffold contamination touches 84 items but is one converter fix (PE-5); 9 design-05
  items carry budget=0.0 with a confirmed scorer misfire path (PE-6, PE-21).
- **Construct:** the new item-level generic-recoverability (GR) measure behaves like a
  real construct — corpus-required items are 18pp harder than generic items with 2.9×
  the floor rate — and it both confirms CV-1's 04/05/10 flags and exposes d06/d07 as
  equally corpus-distinctive (PE-22).
- **Design-02's judge contest is now explained:** the "missing" third-way state exists
  in the rubric but is collapsed by the metric (50/83 referee neither-cells; PE-18).
- **Holdout parity:** surfaces broadly comparable (largest pooled delta: holdout +5pp
  cleaner on IVR consensus); named exceptions d02/d03/d05/d06/d07, with design-07's
  holdout slice materially cleaner than its public slice; 5 design-01 cross-surface
  structural twin pairs flagged as a holdout-independence consideration.

## 2. Method in one paragraph (paper-shaped)

Instrument IVR-1.1: six item-level dimensions (keyed validity, anchor fidelity,
clarity, generic recoverability, pair integrity, bias), overall severity **derived
mechanically** from dimension codes rather than rater-judged. Two independent
fresh-context LLM raters (Opus tier) per item under an anti-leakage protocol; packets
carried full administrations, verbatim keys, grep-verified anchor excerpts with
pack-entry resolution, and the design's harness rubric. A 20-item, 2-round calibration
locked the rubric (severity κ 0.16→0.64 via mechanical derivation; anchor fidelity
κ 0.45→1.00 via excerpt provisioning), with a planted-defect detection check passed
exactly. Full-sweep double-rating completeness was 100%. Disagreements at major/blocker
level were adjudicated by a stronger model tier; each surviving substantive finding
received an independent refute-first adversarial review; mechanically checkable claims
(budgets, contamination, citation existence, grid feasibility, guard enumeration) were
verified by script or construction. LLM-vs-LLM κ is reported as an upper bound on
human-rater agreement.

## 5. What the assessment changed about our understanding

1. The bank's *substance* is stronger than its *plumbing*: no design shows systemic
   keying error; the failures that matter live in conversion, field population,
   metric routing, and exemplar hygiene.
2. Design-02's contested taxonomy and design-04's "contestable" zero-delta items were
   both largely measurement-side phenomena — a metric collapse and an assessment-side
   header oversimplification, respectively. The instrument (harness + docs), not the
   items, was the bigger source of apparent item unreliability.
3. GR at item level is a usable construct probe: it predicts difficulty in the right
   direction and refines CV-1 (d06/d07 under-flagged).
4. Three adversarial kills (WW-03-C01, WW-05-D04, WW-08-C04) demonstrate the items'
   guards are better engineered than first-pass expert reading suggests — enumeration
   and constructive proof both vindicated the authors.
5. The single owner-judgment keying question is B02↔F03 (PE-8); everything else is
   mechanical or wording-level.
