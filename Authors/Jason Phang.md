# Jason Phang

## Research Summary

Jason Phang is currently a Member of Technical Staff at OpenAI and Lead Scientist at EleutherAI. In Spring 2024, he graduated with his PhD from the NYU Center for Data Science, where he was advised by Sam Bowman.

His research interests primarily focus on transfer learning and natural language understanding with large-scale language models, including efficient tuning of LLMs and hypernetworks. He has contributed to high-profile open-source projects like Hugging Face Transformers and EleutherAI's GPT-NeoX, including adding DeBERTaV2 model support and writing scripts to merge and reconfigure 20B-parameter model weights to run on a single GPU.

At EleutherAI, Phang has helped establish a radically novel approach to AI research that goes beyond transparency by conducting research entirely in public, allowing anyone in the world to observe and contribute at every stage. He co-authored the paper "EleutherAI: Going Beyond 'Open Science' to 'Science in the Open'" presented at the Broadening Research Collaborations Workshop at NeurIPS 2022.
