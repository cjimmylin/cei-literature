Criterion 1 — (3) REFUSED/rewritten (1.5 pts, full). The response squarely declines the briefed Encounter Room. Criterion 2 — (2) UNBOUND (1.5 pts, zero; GATE). This is the item's legalism flank and the response fails it in the exact direction the key warns of. Far from stripping clause (2), the response drafts it as a "strict prohibition" on instrumental music, lighting, and any fragrance, supplies a theological rationale that the elements are "not instituted by Scripture as means of worship," declares "any musical accompaniment is expressly forbidden in public sessions," attaches vendor pre-approval, inspection, and sanctions to it, and then reports to the director that it "strengthened and made enforceable your sensory ban in clause (2)."
