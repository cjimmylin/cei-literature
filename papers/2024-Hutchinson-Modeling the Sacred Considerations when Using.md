## Abstract

Position paper presenting an empirical study of how NLP papers use sacred texts. Argues that religious texts encode culturally important values that ML models reproduce, and that repurposing translations for NLP raises considerations beyond bias: data provenance, proselytism, marginalized communities' perspectives. Discusses approaches from ethical theories, Indigenous perspectives, human rights, and institutional AI principles. Foundational for ethical frameworks around religious text NLP.

## Dataset Information

Empirical survey of NLP papers using religious texts


## Data Description

This position paper examines ethical considerations in using religious texts (Bible, Quran, etc.) in NLP research. It includes an empirical study of NLP papers in the ACL Anthology that use sacred texts and discusses issues of data provenance, cultural contexts, proselytism, and researcher positionality. No dataset or benchmark is created, and no LLMs are tested.
