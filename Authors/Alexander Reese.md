# Alexander Reese

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Reese is a researcher at Google Research focused on the ethical implications of advanced AI systems. His work frequently involves cross-functional collaborations to determine how agentic models should be designed to respect human agency and avoid deceptive behaviors.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander's research specifically addresses the shift from static AI models to advanced AI assistants. He co-authored work defining the ethical boundaries for agents capable of high-level planning and execution, emphasizing the need for robust safety frameworks in the next generation of consumer AI products.
