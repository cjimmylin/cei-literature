## Abstract

> Large-scale parallel corpus of 93K Sanskrit shlokas and English translations from the Ramayana and Mahabharata. Standard translation models perform poorly (7-8 BLEU), emphasizing complexity. Foundational dataset for Hindu epic text NLP.

## Dataset Information

93,030 parallel Sanskrit-English sentence pairs


## Data Description

Large-scale parallel corpus of 93,000 Sanskrit shloka-English translation pairs from the Ramayana and Mahabharata, with train/dev/test splits used to benchmark Moses SMT and BERT-based Transformer seq2seq NMT models (trained from scratch).
