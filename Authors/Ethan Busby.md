# Ethan Busby

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ethan Busby is an Associate Professor of Political Science at Vanderbilt University. His research focuses on political psychology, public opinion formation, and experimental methods in political science. He has contributed to work on using AI/ML methods for coding and analyzing social science data.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research examines how people form political attitudes and opinions, using both traditional experimental methods and newer computational approaches. He has been involved in exploring how large language models can assist in coding social science datasets.

### Significant Achievements
Published in top political science journals including Journal of Politics and Political Behavior. Pioneer in exploring the intersection of political science methods and AI capabilities.

## Major Publications

- Research on political persuasion and attitude formation
- Work on experimental methods in political science
- "Towards Coding Social Science Datasets with Language Models" (2023)
