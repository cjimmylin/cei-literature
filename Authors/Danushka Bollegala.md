# Danushka Bollegala

## Biographical Summary

Danushka Bollegala is a Professor of Computer Science at the University of Liverpool, UK. He received his PhD from the University of Tokyo and has held positions at the University of Tokyo and Amazon. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular emphasis on semantic similarity, word embeddings, and addressing biases in NLP systems.

## Research Focus

Bollegala's research spans computational semantics, word representations, and fairness in machine learning. He has made significant contributions to semantic similarity measures, cross-lingual learning, and methods for detecting and mitigating biases in language models. His work on EAGLE explores extrinsic approaches to evaluating gender bias in language models.

## Major Publications

- "EAGLE: Exploring Gender Bias in Language Models through Linguistic Features" (2024)
- "Learning to Measure the Semantic Similarity between Sentences" (2007)
- "Automatic Discovery of Personal Name Aliases from the Web" (2008)
- "Cross-domain Sentiment Classification using a Sentiment Sensitive Thesaurus" (2013)
- "Debiasing Word Embeddings Improves Multimodal Machine Translation" (2021)
