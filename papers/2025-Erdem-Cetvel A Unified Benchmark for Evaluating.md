## Abstract

Comprehensive benchmark for Turkish LLMs covering 23 tasks in 7 categories, including tasks rooted in Turkish history and idiomatic language. Evaluates 33 open-weight LLMs. Addresses gaps in cultural content representation in existing Turkish benchmarks. Explicitly evaluates cultural capacity of LLMs for Turkish, including culturally grounded question answering. Demonstrates that Turkish-centric instruction-tuned models often underperform multilingual models.
