# Md Sultan Al Nahian

## Research Summary

Md Sultan Al Nahian is faculty at Penn State Harrisburg, having previously been a postdoctoral scholar at the Institute for Biomedical Informatics at the University of Kentucky, where he also earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science. His research focuses on NLP, Large Language Models, and Reinforcement Learning applied to AI Value Alignment and Biomedical AI. Lead author of "The Goofus & Gallant Story Corpus for Practical Value Alignment" (ICMLA 2024).
