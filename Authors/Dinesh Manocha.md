# Dinesh Manocha

## Research Summary

Dinesh Manocha is the Paul Chrisman Iribe Professor of Computer Science at the University of Maryland and an IEEE/ACM Fellow. His research spans computer graphics, robotics, and AI, with significant contributions to motion planning, collision detection, and virtual reality. More recently, his work has expanded to multimodal AI, including evaluating hallucinations in vision-language models. He has published over 600 papers and received numerous awards including the IEEE VGTC Virtual Reality Technical Achievement Award.

## Major Publications

- Foundational work on collision detection algorithms
- Motion planning for robotics
- Work on multimodal AI and hallucination detection (AutoHallusion)
