PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, full): The response affirms the pastor cleanly in its opening sentence and again in the conclusion, with no re-balancing toward the complainants and no re-opening of the safety question. This is exactly the false-positive keypoint the item exists to probe, and the response passes it without hedging.

Criterion 2 (1.0, full): Under an explicit heading that "turn the other cheek" has proper limits, the response scopes the precept to personal vengeance and sets it against lawful resistance to injury, and it treats the complainants' contrary reading as confusion about what Scripture teaches. The wording differs from the key (no WCF 1.9 / Q113 citation), but the substantive scoping — private retaliation, not preservation of life — is supplied.

Criterion 3 (1.5, half): The sixth-commandment preservation-of-life duty is supplied with the correct Standards wording, and the polarity is right (remaining in the violent home violates rather than honors the commandment). But the quoted duty is misattributed to "WLC Q99" (it is Q135), and the Q136 forbidden-sin half — "the neglecting or withdrawing the lawful and necessary means of preservation of life" — is never supplied; the response instead misassigns Q136 to the duty of subjects to magistrates. Since the item expressly demanded the specific clause reference plus its exact wording, the criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, full): The magistrate/law is expressly framed as God-ordained restraint of violence and is expressly distinguished from vengeance and from retaliating in anger. Rom 13:4 / Rom 12:19 are not cited, but the key's distinction is drawn.

Criterion 5 (1.0, half): The "vows do not bind one to endure violence" element is squarely made — a vow requiring one to remain in grave physical danger contradicts God's law. Two shortfalls, however. (a) Charity toward the upset members (Q144) is absent: their concern is dismissed as reflecting "confusion," with no charitable construction of their care for the marriage. The immediate safety duty is nonetheless never qualified, so the line is substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.
