# Yehor Tereshchenko

## Research Summary

Yehor Tereshchenko is a researcher located in Tampere, Pirkanmaa, Finland. He has co-authored research with Mika Hämäläinen, publishing at the 5th International Conference on Natural Language Processing for Digital Humanities in 2025. His research focuses on analyzing ethical and safety gaps in Large Language Models, contributing to the understanding of AI ethics and safety challenges.
