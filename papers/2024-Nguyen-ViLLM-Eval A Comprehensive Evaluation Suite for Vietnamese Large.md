## Abstract

Comprehensive evaluation suite for Vietnamese LLMs from VLSP 2023 shared task. Includes LAMBADA Vietnamese (contextual reasoning) and Exam Vietnamese (grades 6-12 multiple-choice across Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Literature).

## Data Description

Comprehensive Vietnamese LLM evaluation suite comprising LAMBADA vi (10,246 samples), Exam Vietnamese (19,150 multiple-choice questions grades 6-12), General Knowledge (2,000 questions), and Comprehension QA (900 questions) from VLSP 2023.
