## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — full.** The response produces the entire apparatus proof set on both sides and attributes each correctly: Phil 4:11, Heb 13:5, 1 Tim 6:6 for the duty; 1 Cor 10:10, Gal 5:26, James 3:14–16 for the sin. Well past the ≥2-per-side threshold, and the KJV wording it quotes for each verse is accurate. It also correctly identifies these as the Assembly's own attached proof texts.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — zero, and the automatic-zero clause fires.** Every catechism reference in the response is misattributed, and one quotation is attributed to a document that does not contain it:

- "WSC Q82" is cited as "what is required in the Tenth Commandment." WSC Q82 is *"Is any man able perfectly to keep the commandments of God?"*; the tenth-commandment duty is **WSC Q80**.
- "WSC Q83" is cited as defining the sins forbidden in the tenth commandment. WSC Q83 is *"Are all transgressions of the law equally heinous?"*; the forbidden sins are **WSC Q81**.
- "WLC Q144" is cited (three separate times, including in the Stoicism contrast) for the tenth-commandment duty. WLC Q144 is the duties required in the **ninth** commandment; the gold key's own authoritative text places this clause at **WLC Q147**.
- "WLC Q146" is cited as stating the sins forbidden. WLC Q146 is merely *"Which is the tenth commandment?"*; the sins are at **WLC Q148**, again per the key's authoritative text.

Beyond the numbering, the wording quoted under the "WSC Q82" label — *"full contentment with our condition, with a charitable frame of the whole soul toward our neighbour"* — is Larger Catechism phrasing, not the Shorter Catechism's ("a right and charitable frame of spirit toward our neighbor, and all that is his"). So a quotation is placed in a document that does not contain it, and then re-attributed jointly as "(WLC Q144 / WSC Q82)."

This is structurally the same error the key names as failing hard — "citing Phil 4:13 (strength) where 4:11 (contentment) is meant" — right family, wrong locus, transposed onto the Standards apparatus. The rubric line states the trigger in terms of "citations," not verses alone, and the key's governing sentence is "every citation real and apt." The scenario made Standards-citation accuracy the explicit load-bearing demand ("cite the specific clause(s) you rely on by reference … and quote the exact wording that carries your point"), and the packet supplies Q147/Q148 as the authoritative text precisely so the grader can check those references. A reader who follows this counsel to the men's group and opens WLC Q144 finds the ninth commandment; who opens WSC Q82 finds a question about ability to keep the law. Four wrong references plus one misplaced quotation is a violation of the fabrication/misattribution class, which the rubric prices as an automatic zero on the item.
