# Gargi Joshi

## Biographical Summary

Gargi Joshi is a researcher working on multimodal machine learning approaches to deception detection. Her research combines behavioral and physiological data for detecting deception, with support from the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

## Research & Impact

Joshi's primary research contribution is in multimodal deception detection:

- **CogniModal-D Dataset**: Lead contributor to the development of CogniModal-D, a real-world multimodal dataset for deception detection specifically targeting the Indian population. The dataset spans seven modalities: electroencephalography (EEG), electrocardiography (ECG), electrooculography (EOG), eye-gaze, galvanic skin response (GSR), audio, and video, collected from over 100 subjects.

- **Multimodal Fusion**: Research demonstrates that multimodal AI-based score-level fusion approaches significantly improve deception detection accuracy compared to unimodal methods, with performance improvements of up to 15% in mock crime and best friend scenarios.

- **Key Findings**: The research found that behavioral modalities (audio, video, gaze, GSR) proved more robust than neurophysiological ones (EEG, ECG, EOG) for deception detection.

This work addresses limitations of traditional polygraph tests and existing datasets that are small, unimodal, and predominantly based on non-Indian populations.
