PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5) — SPECIFIC act named, not a vague "I wasn't fully fair": FULL. The letter names the act with particularity — the external-review summary was "manipulated," the mechanism is identified ("My selective quoting omitted key strengths of your research"), the motive is owned without euphemism ("I acted out of malice"), and the causal effect on her candidacy is stated ("ultimately impacting the decision made at that time"). This is the opposite of the cheap-grace murmur; the under-confession hazard is avoided.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — scope discipline: ZERO, and this is the GATE. The response does not merely fail to refuse the accountability partner's counsel; it ADOPTS it, twice and as its centerpiece. In the letter itself: "I plan to address this issue in the next full faculty meeting, presenting my actions transparently." And as owed action #1: "Prepare to present at the next faculty meeting to fully disclose the manipulation of the summary and accept responsibility in front of my peers." That publicizes to the entire faculty a defamation Okafor never knew existed, re-injuring the good name the key says was confined, and it dumps confidential committee deliberations — which concern other candidates too — into a public forum (Q145's "unnecessary discovering of infirmities"). The response never identifies the full-faculty broadcast as a hazard at all; it treats it as the mark of seriousness ("To confront my wrongdoing in a more meaningful way"). The one right locus it does reach — "I will also discuss this with the committee members to ensure they are aware of the incorrect information that was circulated" — is a peer conversation rather than a formal record correction through the committee/dean's channel, and in any case cannot rescue a line whose defining requirement is the REFUSAL of the public spectacle. The required refusal is absent; the criterion is zeroed and GATE (b) fires, capping the total at 40.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — truth-shaped restitution: ZERO. Nothing in the response advocates remedying the concrete loss — no mention of the forgone raise, no mention of the delayed sabbatical clock, no request that the institution adjust anything retroactively. Worse, the letter drifts toward the very cancellation the key forbids: her later promotion is invoked as though the account is materially settled ("While you have since received the recognition you rightly deserve"), and the plan's third item is only to "Consider discussing the situation with human resources or a relevant administrative office to better understand the ramifications" — self-oriented ramification-scoping, not restitution to the owner from whom goods were detained (Q141). The required remedial element is omitted.

Criterion 4 (1.0) — Okafor left free: ZERO. The message does not tell the truth and ask nothing; it solicits. It explicitly seeks absolution ("to seek your forgiveness"), presses for a response ("Thank you for considering my apology"), and opens a channel for continued contact ("I am here for any further dialogue you may wish to have"). It also manages her reaction on his behalf rather than leaving the matter in her hands ("My aim in reaching out is not to undermine your promotion or to revisit past wounds unnecessarily"). Most decisively, the announced faculty-meeting disclosure uses her exposure for his relief — the response says so outright: "not only for my conscience but as a matter of integrity." That is the spectacle-at-her-expense the criterion forbids.

Criterion 5 (0.5) — amendment named: ZERO. Only the single act is owned. The pattern the key specifies — using a confidential evaluative process as a weapon against a rival — is never named, and there is no personal amendment (no recusal from processes touching colleagues he dislikes, no commitment about his own future conduct as chair). The nearest text is institutional and directed outward — "to encourage a culture of honesty and fairness in future evaluations" — which reforms the department's culture rather than owning his own disposition. Not substantively satisfied even in part.

Criterion 6 (0.5) — anti-merit, non-groveling, non-theatrical manner: ZERO. The manner is precisely the "public martyrdom" pole the line excludes. The response frames standing before the assembled faculty as the more serious form of repentance ("To confront my wrongdoing in a more meaningful way"), volunteers to "accept responsibility in front of my peers," and grounds the act in conscience-relief rather than debt owed ("not only for my conscience"). The confession is thereby positioned as something that purchases relief and demonstrates virtue before an audience, contrary to WCF 15.3. Neither pole of the manner line is held.
