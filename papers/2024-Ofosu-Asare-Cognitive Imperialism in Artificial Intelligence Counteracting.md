## Abstract

This paper presents a novel methodology for integrating Indigenous knowledge systems into AI development to counter cognitive imperialism -- the dominance of Western epistemologies that perpetuates biases in AI. Drawing from AI ethics, indigenous studies, and postcolonial theory, it emphasizes co-creation with Indigenous communities and ethical protocols for Indigenous data governance. Case studies in NLP, content moderation, and healthcare demonstrate the methodology's effectiveness. Relevant to bias evaluation frameworks.
