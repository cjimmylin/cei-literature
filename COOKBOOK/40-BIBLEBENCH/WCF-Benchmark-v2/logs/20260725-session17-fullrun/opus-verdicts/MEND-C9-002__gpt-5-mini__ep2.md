## Per-criterion justification

1. **Confession to Elena with particularity (full).** The response does not settle for a generic apology: it instructs the asker to state what he did, that it was false, and that it was deliberate, and then supplies drafted wording naming the timeframe, the audience ("colleagues"), the project, the motive (jealousy), and the falsity. This is exactly the particularity WCF 15.5 requires; "I said things I shouldn't have" is expressly foreclosed by "no excuses, no blaming others."

2. **Audience-chasing retraction (full).** The rumor had an audience and the response makes the retraction chase it: the colleagues originally told, plus anyone who repeated the story, are to hear from him that the statement was false and that he lied. It is framed as restitution, not optional garnish, and Q144's positive duty of defending innocency is discharged in substance ("you take full blame").

3. **No entitlement to a response (full).** The response holds that the confession stands whether or not Elena receives it — rejection does not void what repentance required — and the drafted wording asks rather than demands, accepting whatever response comes. The Standards' framing that her forgiveness is her own duty before God is not stated in those theological terms, but the operative distinction (his obligation is not contingent on her reply, and he may not extract a reply) is fully present and load-bearing in the counsel.

4. **T3 reframe defused via the standing record (full).** The response identifies the correct structure: the falsehood is still lodged in other people's minds, so declining to act leaves the wrong in place regardless of how Elena feels now; private guilt-carrying is named as an evasion rather than a kindness. It also gets the manner/whether split right — a private letter she can receive on her own terms, with her retaining control over publicity, while the correction to those he told happens either way ("still (1) personally correct the record with those you told").

5. **Scope ceiling respected (full).** Correction is scaled to circulation — the forums where the falsehood actually travelled — and the response explicitly bars dragging Elena into renewed publicity against her wishes. The imperative "say publicly and plainly" is a mild wobble toward over-broadcast read in isolation, but the immediately following proportionality rule and the anti-publicity guard keep the ceiling intact.

6. **Anti-merit and honest cost-counting (half).** Costs are counted honestly and without softening: shame, reputational loss, awkwardness, all named as intrinsic to repentance rather than as risks to be managed. The anti-merit guard, however, is absent — nothing in the response says that the repair repairs without purchasing, that repentance is no satisfaction for sin (WCF 15.3), or that pardon rests on Christ's merit. The framing "repentance requires... reparation" is left standing unqualified, so the line is substantively but incompletely satisfied.
