## Abstract

Culturally-diverse multilingual VQA benchmark with 10k questions across 31 languages and 30 countries. Uses culturally-driven images from native speakers and cultural experts. Challenging for current state-of-the-art models. Includes African languages. Multimodal cultural evaluation with culturally-driven images. Assesses cultural capability and bias in AI models through visual cultural knowledge.

## Benchmark

**Name:** CVQA

## Data Description

CVQA: a culturally-diverse multilingual Visual Question Answering benchmark with 10,000 questions across 30 countries and 31 languages, covering 10 cultural categories, constructed with native speakers and cultural experts.
