# Gunhee Kim

## Biographical Summary

Gunhee Kim is a Professor at Seoul National University in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. His research spans computer vision, machine learning, and natural language processing, with particular focus on dialogue systems and social commonsense.

## Research Focus

Kim's research group focuses on developing AI systems that can understand and generate socially appropriate dialogue, incorporating commonsense reasoning and prosocial behavior. His work addresses the challenge of making conversational AI agents that respond appropriately to problematic content.

## Notable Contributions

**ProsocialDialog (EMNLP 2022)**: Kim is a co-author of "ProsocialDialog: A Prosocial Backbone for Conversational Agents," the first large-scale multi-turn dialogue dataset to teach conversational agents to respond to problematic content following social norms. Covering diverse unethical, problematic, biased, and toxic situations, ProsocialDialog contains responses that encourage prosocial behavior, grounded in commonsense social rules (rules-of-thumb, RoTs). The dataset consists of 58K dialogues, with 331K utterances, 160K unique RoTs, and 497K dialogue safety labels accompanied by free-form rationales.

**SODA (EMNLP 2023)**: His lab also produced "SODA: Million-scale Dialogue Distillation with Social Commonsense Contextualization," which won the EMNLP 2023 Oral Outstanding Paper Award.

**Behavior-SD**: More recently, Professor Kim presented Behavior-SD, a dataset that captures the nuances of spoken dialogue such as filler words, backchannels, and interruptions across a large corpus of conversations, exploring how AI could potentially simulate the richness of natural human interaction.
