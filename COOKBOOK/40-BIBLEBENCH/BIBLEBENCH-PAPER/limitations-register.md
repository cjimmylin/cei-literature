# BibleBench Pre-Empirical Limitations Register

**As of:** 2026-07-13

**Authority:** PF-002 active low-cost MVP

**Scope:** venue-neutral design and methods paper for the consolidated FruitBench, CORINTHLOVE, 10CBench, and SermonMount pilot

This register distinguishes disclosure from a paper or execution blocker. P2/P3 hardening does not enter the critical path without a recorded owner exception. No entry authorizes a credential, provider call, model-output exposure, or spend.

| ID | Limitation | Consequence for claims | Current mitigation | Exit or update condition | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-001 | No consolidated live-model run exists. | No model outcome, comparison, ranking, performance conclusion, or empirical generalization may be reported. | The manuscript contains one Results sentinel and otherwise reports design and method only. | Authorized frozen run, verified custody, completed adjudication, and descriptive analysis. | Open; blocks empirical claims, not methods drafting. |
| L-002 | Episode-level owner adjudication and the blinded repeat remain pending. | Authored bands and poles are not completed gold; pilot outputs cannot yet be exposed. | Owner-only pass one followed by a blinded repeat, both frozen before output exposure. | Complete the registered owner workflow with no unresolved record-level P0/P1. | Open P1 for live execution. |
| L-003 | The founding judge is one theologian-technical expert. | The study cannot claim Christian consensus, population gold, inter-rater reliability, or tradition-level representativeness. | Label accepted decisions `single_expert_provisional_gold`; retain confidence, rationale, and contestability. | Separately approved multi-judge study with declared panels and disagreement analysis. | Accepted first-pilot limitation. |
| L-004 | The 48 episodes prioritize construct and interface coverage; most of the 42 families lack replication. | No family-level estimate or family ranking is supported. | Report episode-level observations and module-native vectors only. | Add preregistered matched cells with adequate sampling. | Accepted first-pilot limitation. |
| L-005 | Six families repeat to preserve equal 12-episode module geometry. | Equal episode counts do not imply equal independent construct coverage. | Report unique-family coverage separately from episode count. | Revisit sampling after item diagnostics. | Accepted design trade-off. |
| L-006 | The 34 common-life / 14 Christian-facing split is authored, not population sampled. | Stratum summaries do not estimate deployment prevalence. | Describe the split as a design allocation and retain the strata. | Define and sample a defensible target-use population. | Accepted first-pilot limitation. |
| L-007 | The normative frame is founding, broadly evangelical Protestant, and owner calibrated. | Findings do not represent all Christian traditions or disputed applications. | Declare the frame, retain contested bands, and keep confessional calibration distinct from identity. | Multi-tradition panels and sensitivity analysis under a separate protocol. | Accepted and disclosed. |
| L-008 | Construct validity is not established by the design labels. | Module names and operationalizations cannot be treated as validated latent traits. | Preserve passage-specific definitions, non-claims, failure poles, item provenance, and observation/interpretation separation. | Accumulate response-process, reliability, convergent/discriminant, and external evidence. | Open validation program. |
| L-009 | One C1 primary plus one matched probe provides a narrow response surface. | The MVP cannot support cue-effect claims, broad elicitation invariance, or a full modality-gap claim. | State the narrow geometry and treat prior multi-surface designs as deferred history. | Separate preregistered study with matched surfaces, custody, and sufficient observations. | Accepted PF-002 scope. |
| L-010 | Capability twins are not part of the active MVP schedule. | Task failure cannot be separated from ethical failure by twin outcomes; capability-adjusted attribution is forbidden. | Report missingness and episode observations without capability adjustment. | Separately implement, freeze, and analyze matched twin execution under a new claim. | Deferred; does not block PF-002. |
| L-011 | Sandboxes and consequential artifacts simplify real social settings. | External validity to real people, institutions, and deployment consequences is unknown. | Use synthetic reversible environments, explicit role limits, and bounded claims. | Separate ethics-approved external validation after safety and construct stabilization. | Accepted safety/validity limitation. |
| L-012 | The exact three-model roster, provider routes, versions, prices, limits, and retry rule are not approved. | The paper cannot present provider coverage, price, or availability as an executed condition. | Require a fresh proposal at or below $3.00 with a $5.00 hard ceiling and separate owner authority. | Owner approval of exact identities, routes, evidence date, ceilings, expiry, and stop rules. | Open external gate; $0.00 authorized. |
| L-013 | Model and provider behavior may change before an authorized run. | Reproducibility depends on execution-time identity and custody, not a marketing name. | Pin exact identities, disable fallback, retain request/response/usage/receipt artifacts, and record price evidence. | Verified preflight plus immutable authorized manifest. | Open until execution. |
| L-014 | Human adjudication remains interpretive even when bytes and events are preserved exactly. | Exact custody cannot establish normative neutrality or exhaustive event coverage. | Separate retained evidence, mechanical event location, passage-native interpretation, rationale, and contestability. | Later event-coverage audit and multi-judge validation. | Accepted first-pilot limitation. |
| L-015 | A single composite and leaderboard are not validated for the development bank. | Rankings, composites, best-model conclusions, and performance claims would hide module boundaries, missingness, and judge uncertainty. | Vector-first exploratory reporting with the prohibited claims named in advance. | A later study would require validated scoring, adequate sampling, reliability, missingness, and capability controls. | Explicitly outside PF-002. |

## Active claim ceiling

Until L-001, L-002, L-012, and L-013 close, the project may report only design properties, verified engineering properties, and clearly labeled provisional theological judgments. Even after a pilot, L-003 through L-015 continue to prohibit rankings, composites, best-model, cue-effect, capability-adjusted, family-level, Christian-consensus, and performance claims unless a later registered design satisfies each limitation's exit condition.
