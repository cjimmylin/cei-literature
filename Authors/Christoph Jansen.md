# Christoph Jansen

## Biographical Summary
Christoph Jansen is a tenured Assistant Professor in Data Science at the School of Computing & Communications at Lancaster University Leipzig. He completed his habilitation at LMU Munich in January 2025 with a thesis on machine learning under weakly structured information. His work examines statistical biases in human-AI alignment methodologies.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Statistical Foundations of AI Alignment**: Analyzing how empirical human-AI alignment can introduce statistical biases
- **Imprecise Probabilities**: Developing frameworks for decision-making in complex information settings
- **Depth Functions for Non-Standard Data**: Creating analytical frameworks for partial orders and non-standard data types

### Significant Achievements
- Completed habilitation at LMU Munich Department of Statistics (January 2025)
- Served as interim W2-professor for Statistics at LMU Munich (Summer 2022)
- Executive committee member of the Society for Imprecise Probabilities: Theories and Applications (SIPTA)
- Contributed to research arguing that "alignment should be governed by principles, not just by unreflected mirroring of observations"
