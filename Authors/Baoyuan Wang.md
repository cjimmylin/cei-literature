# Baoyuan Wang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Baoyuan Wang is a Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research, working in the Graphics and Multimedia Group. He earned his PhD from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He is a specialist in the integration of visual information with language models, with a background in high-performance computer graphics and computer vision.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Baoyuan’s research focuses on the intersection of computer vision, computer graphics, and machine learning, specifically in the area of **Vision-to-Language** (V2L) and multimodal foundation models. He leads projects on 3D Digital Humans, neural rendering, and the unified modeling of visual and linguistic information. His work aims to create more intelligent and creative AI applications capable of deep multimodal understanding and synthesis.

### Significant Achievements
Throughout his career at Microsoft, he has made significant contributions to the state of the art in image and video synthesis and human-centric computer vision. His research is widely integrated into Microsoft products and services. With over 10,000 citations, he is a highly influential figure in the fields of computer graphics and vision, regularly publishing in top-tier venues like CVPR and SIGGRAPH.
