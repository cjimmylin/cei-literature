## Abstract

Curated dataset of 226 Burmese hate speech posts from Facebook collected during the 2020 Myanmar national elections. Develops process for coordinating ML work in low-resource language settings with context experts. 873 posts categorized with toxicity scores. PRIMARY Burmese hate speech dataset. Directly relevant to toxicity and values evaluation in Myanmar context. Addresses real-world hate speech challenges during critical political events.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Burmese Hate Speech Facebook Dataset

## Data Description

The paper presents findings from a remote study on automatic detection of hate speech in Myanmar using Burmese-language Facebook data. A dataset of 226 Burmese hate speech posts was curated from Facebook via CrowdTangle, annotated by 8 context experts. Classification models tested include SVM, Balanced Random Forests, and FastText using n-gram and TF-IDF features, with FastText (with oversampling) performing best (F1=0.87). No LLM models from the canonical list were tested.
