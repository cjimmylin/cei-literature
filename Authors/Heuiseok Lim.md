# Heuiseok Lim

## Research Summary

Heuiseok Lim is a Full Professor at the NLP & AI Lab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, Seoul, South Korea. His research focuses on natural language processing with particular emphasis on Korean language technologies, including machine translation, named entity recognition, and instruction-following capabilities of large language models. He has published more than 500 papers in international journals and conferences, registered 143 domestic and international patents, and has 55 technology transfer agreements. His lab has made significant contributions to Korean cultural heritage document processing and cross-lingual information retrieval. He also contributed to "The Handbook of East Asian Psycholinguistics" published by Cambridge University Press.
