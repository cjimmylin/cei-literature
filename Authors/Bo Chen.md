# Bo Chen

## Biographical Summary

Bo Chen is a graduate researcher at the Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG) at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Jie Tang. His research focuses on the intersection of knowledge representation and generative AI, specifically building more reasoning-capable language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be better aligned with human reasoning processes. This includes developing benchmarks for evaluating the "internal knowledge" and reasoning depth of models like DeepSeek and GLM.

### Significant Achievements
Chen has contributed to several high-impact studies on LLM evaluation, including the analysis of "info-leakage" in benchmarks and the development of more robust evaluation protocols for open-source foundation models.

## Major Publications
