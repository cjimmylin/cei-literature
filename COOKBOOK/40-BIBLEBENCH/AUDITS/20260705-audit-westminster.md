## 3. Forward-looking design note — the codified-corpus property, and its trap

The premise that motivated flagging WESTMINSTER is correct *and* double-edged:

**Why it looks attractive.** The Westminster Standards (Confession + Shorter/Larger Catechisms) are a
codified normative corpus with native Q&A structure — the WSC/WLC are literally question-and-answer.
KB10 §4's T2 recipe rewards "computable / codified liturgical language" and KB10 §5 credits Islam's
strength partly to formalized jurisprudence. A catechism is natively machine-checkable and exact-match.

**Why that is a Law-1 trap, not a T2 win.** The T2 discriminator (Arabic *fara'id*) is computable
because it demands **multi-step derivation** (*'awl/radd* share redistribution) — reasoning, not recall.
Catechism Q&A is computable because it is **recitation** — the "computation" is lookup. A benchmark that
harvests WSC/WLC Q-and-A therefore lands squarely on KB10 §2's over-produced **knowledge-recall layer**,
which "saturates first" and is where "sounding religious" lives — the exact ELQV-paradox failure the
whole religious-values program is built to escape. It would correlate high with a capability PC (Law 1
red flag >~40%) and hit the trimodal ceiling/floor pathology (KB10 §1, finding 5). **Catechism = INTRA's
K-layer (or at best D-know), which INTRA has already *demoted* to a paired capability baseline
(INTRA §2, "M-layer demoted post-gate").** Nothing headline-worthy lives there.

**Where genuine discrimination could come from, if the pack is built well:**
- **Reasoning (M-analogue):** the WLC's casuistic exposition of the Decalogue — the graded *duties
  required* and *sins forbidden* under each commandment — is real Reformed casuistry. Novel-scenario
  application of that method (not recall of the list) is the reasoning layer. This is also the T2
  "canon-law-style" computable subdomain the Reformed tradition offers, paralleling INTRA's Catholic
  canon-law marriage-impediment subdomain.
- **Values-behavioral (V-analogue):** multi-turn pastoral/advisory dialogue reasoning *from within* the
  covenantal framework (KB10 §6; INTRA V) — the least-saturated dimension (Faith floor 35/100,
  KB10 §3).
- **Denomination:** Westminster is a **single confessional standard (Reformed/Presbyterian)**. It cannot
  natively supply INTRA's *D-flag* fork (that needs cross-school ambiguity). What it supplies is the
  **Reformed-covenantal D-know reference** — precisely the "Reformed covenantal vs Catholic natural law"
  contrast KB10 §26 and §4 name as the Christian denominational axis. That is a contribution *into* a
  fork, not a fork by itself.
