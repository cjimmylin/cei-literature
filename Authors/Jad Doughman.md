# Jad Doughman

## Research Summary

Jad Doughman is an AI Engineer and Researcher working on Arabic NLP, multilingual AI systems, and bias detection in language models.

### Research Contributions

**ArabicMMLU (ACL 2024):**
Co-author on "ArabicMMLU: Assessing Massive Multitask Language Understanding in Arabic" - the first multi-task language understanding benchmark for Arabic language. The dataset is sourced from school exams across diverse educational levels in countries spanning North Africa, the Levant, and Gulf regions, comprising 40 tasks and 14,575 multiple-choice questions in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA).

**Bias Detection Research:**
Published work on measuring sexism in debiased language models, including "Can a Prediction's Rank Offer a More Accurate Quantification of Bias? A Case Study Measuring Sexism in Debiased Language Models" (2023).

**DiaLex:**
Contributed to DiaLex, a benchmark for intrinsic evaluation of dialectal Arabic word embeddings covering five important Arabic dialects: Algerian, Egyptian, Lebanese, Syrian, and Tunisian.

**SHADES:**
Contributor to SHADES, a multilingual benchmark for assessing stereotype biases in LLMs across multiple languages and cultural contexts.
