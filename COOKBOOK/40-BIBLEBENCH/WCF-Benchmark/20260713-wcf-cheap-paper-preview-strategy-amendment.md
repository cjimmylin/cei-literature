## Owner direction

Jimmy directed WCF-Bench to take the fastest low-cost path to a visible manuscript before
scaling to more models, expensive models, or a more robust judge panel. He selected an
**exploratory manuscript preview**: enough evidence to populate provisional tables,
figures, Results, Discussion, and limitations, but not a replacement for registered T4.

Jimmy approved all of the following in writing:

- all 226 runnable base items rather than a smaller sample;
- three inexpensive, distinct-family SUTs;
- one inexpensive judge;
- no perturbation battery or gate claims in the preview; and
- preservation of full T4 as the later confirmatory study.

## Strategic rule

The next paper-facing empirical milestone is **PPP-1 — Exploratory Manuscript Preview**.
PPP-1 precedes further expensive scaling. Its purpose is to reveal the actual paper:
which tables and figures work, which claims survive contact with preliminary evidence,
which discussion branches are needed, and what limitations must be foregrounded.

Once the minimum valid PPP-1 packet exists, work moves immediately to manuscript
assembly and owner review. Further runner hardening, broader rosters, stronger or
additional judges, perturbation expansion, and confirmatory gates wait unless they are
necessary to prevent invalid preview evidence or Jimmy explicitly authorizes them.

## PPP-1 frozen preview scope

### Items

Run all **226 runnable base items**. The four `dev_set_only` items remain excluded under
the standing bank rule. No frozen bank item is edited.

### SUT roster

Use exactly these three inexpensive, distinct-family SUTs:

1. `meta-llama/llama-3.1-8b-instruct`
2. `deepseek/deepseek-chat`
3. `qwen/qwen3.7-plus`

This is a preview roster only. It does not replace, amend, qualify for, or count toward
the binding R59.2 full-T4 roster.

### Judge

Use only:

- `google/gemini-2.5-flash`

Mechanical ORDINAL and citation-gated ANCHOR scoring remain mechanical. Judge-required
preview surfaces use the single Gemini family solely to create a manuscript scaffold.
Single-judge output is not panel evidence and carries no qualification, consensus,
release, or gate implication.

### Excluded from PPP-1

PPP-1 does not run or claim:

- P3, P4, P6, or any other perturbation battery;
- G1–G7 pass/fail conclusions;
- human-consensus or G4/G6 completion;
- cross-family judge agreement;
- global- or roster-frontier claims;
- release-ready benchmark scores;
- replacement, amendment, or completion of registered T4; or
- any headline result suitable for external quotation.

## Required validity labels

Every PPP-1 run artifact, table, figure, manuscript paragraph, dashboard reference, and
handoff statement must carry:

> **EXPLORATORY MANUSCRIPT PREVIEW · SINGLE-JUDGE · NON-QUOTABLE**

PPP-1 numbers may guide drafting and study design. They may not be presented as final
benchmark findings, used to adjudicate R67.4, or substituted into release-labeled paper
content.

## Paper packet deliverable

PPP-1 is complete only when evidence has been converted into a paper-facing packet:

1. a reproducible immutable run record;
2. provisional per-design and aggregate tables;
3. the minimum useful figures for the current manuscript;
4. a preview Results section populated with traceable values;
5. a preview Discussion section choosing or preserving the evidence-supported branches;
6. a conspicuous limitations box covering the three-SUT roster, single judge, preview
   status, and absence of perturbation/gate evidence; and
7. an assembled manuscript preview for Jimmy to review.

Test counts, controller code, dashboards, and handoffs are enabling work. They do not
complete PPP-1 without this paper packet.

## Stop and scale-up rule

After the assembled preview is delivered, Jimmy chooses among:

1. revise the manuscript or analysis plan using the preview;
2. repeat a narrowly corrected preview only if a material validity defect requires it;
3. authorize the registered full T4 confirmatory run; or
4. narrow or defer the confirmatory study.

Codex does not automatically scale from PPP-1 to five or ten SUTs, add judges, buy more
expensive models, run perturbations, or start full T4. Each expansion requires its own
paper-facing reason and explicit spending authorization.
