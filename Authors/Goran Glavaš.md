# Goran Glavaš

## Biographical Summary

Goran Glavaš is a Professor at the University of Würzburg, holding the Chair for Natural Language Processing (Informatik XII) at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, and is a member of the Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (CAIDAS).

## Career History

Previously, he worked as an Assistant Professor at the University of Mannheim, Data and Web Science Group (September 2017 to September 2021) and as Interim Associate Professor at LMU Munich, Center for Information and Language Processing (October 2021 to March 2022). Before that, he was a researcher at the Text Analysis and Knowledge Engineering Lab, University of Zagreb, where he completed his doctorate in 2014 under the supervision of Jan Snajder.

## Research Focus

His main interests are in computational semantics and multilingual and low-resource representation learning. He also works toward democratization of language technologies: fair and sustainable NLP as well as NLP applications in social sciences and humanities.

His research group (WuNLP) emphasizes deep learning and representation learning techniques to precisely model the meaning of natural language across various languages, prioritizing multilingual representation learning and the transfer of models for different NLP tasks. The group addresses challenges in the field by striving for equitable, socially responsible, and sustainable language technology through sustainable, modular, and sample-efficient models, fair and unbiased NLP practices, and multilingual NLP, particularly targeting low-resource languages.
