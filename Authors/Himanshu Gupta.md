# Himanshu Gupta

## Research Summary

Himanshu Gupta is a machine learning researcher currently working as an Applied Scientist at Amazon (Stores Foundation AI team), where he focuses on mid-training, post-training optimization, synthetic data creation, and benchmarking of large foundation models such as Rufus. Previously, he was an Applied Scientist at Microsoft. He is also a graduate student at the Language Technologies Institute (LTI) at Carnegie Mellon University and holds a CS degree from BITS Pilani. His research interests span Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models, with work on toxicity detection and bias in language systems.
