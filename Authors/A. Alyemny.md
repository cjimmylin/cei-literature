## Key Research

Ohoud Al-Yemni (also spelled Alyemny) is a faculty member at King Saud University who has made significant contributions to Arabic NLP and Islamic studies. Her most notable work is the development of "Fatwaset," the first public Arabic dataset of Islamic fatwas, collected from 13 trusted websites across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Jordan, Qatar, and Syria, containing 130,182 records with rich metadata.

The Fatwaset dataset enables various NLP tasks including language modeling, question answering, author attribution, topic identification, text classification, and text summarization. This work was published in Data, 8(10), 155 (2023) with co-authors Hend Al-Khalifa and Abdulrahman Mirza from King Saud University.
