# Delong Chen

## Affiliation
PhD Researcher, Department of Computer Science, Hong Kong Baptist University

## Research Summary
Delong Chen is a PhD researcher working on multimodal machine learning and large language models. His research explores the intersection of vision and language understanding, with a focus on how AI systems can better capture and represent human values. He has contributed to research on value alignment and the development of high-dimensional representations for capturing human preferences and cultural variations in AI systems.

## Research Interests
- Multimodal learning
- Large language models
- Vision-language models

- Human values in AI
