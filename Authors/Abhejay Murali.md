# Abhejay Murali

## Biographical Summary

Abhejay Murali is a researcher at the University of Texas at Austin, where he is a member of the Urban Information Lab (UIL). His research focuses on the intersection of urban science, computer science, and ethics, specifically investigating the moral reasoning capabilities of advanced AI models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abhejay contributes to the development of systematic benchmarks for AI ethics. He co-developed a three-dimensional assessment system that evaluates Large Language Models (LLMs) on foundational moral principles, the robustness of their reasoning, and the consistency of their ethical outputs.
