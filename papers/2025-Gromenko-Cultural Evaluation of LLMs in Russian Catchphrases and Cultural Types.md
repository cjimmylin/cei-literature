## Abstract

Introduces structured framework with 8 Cultural Types (e.g., Spiritual Practitioner, Soviet Intellectual) and 5 catchphrase groups. 400-question evaluation dataset probing 10 multilingual LLMs on Russian cultural knowledge and linguacultural understanding.

## Data Description

A 400-question Russian cultural evaluation dataset structured around 8 Cultural Types and 5 catchphrase groups, in multiple formats (MCQ, gap-fill, matching, answer extraction). Used to probe 10 multilingual LLMs for cultural awareness in a zero-shot setting.
