## Abstract

Alif-1.0-8B-Instruct incorporates Urdu-native chain-of-thought reasoning, cultural relevance, and ethical safety alignments. Outperforms leading multilingual LLMs on Urdu benchmarks. Urdu LLM with explicit cultural relevance and ethical safety alignment features.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Alif

## Data Description

The paper releases Alif-1.0-8B-Instruct, an Urdu-English LLM trained on Urdu-Instruct, a high-quality multilingual synthetic dataset of Urdu chain-of-thought reasoning, translation, cultural relevance, and safety tasks generated via a modified self-instruct technique.
