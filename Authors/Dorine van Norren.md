# Dorine van Norren

## Biographical Summary

Dorine Eva van Norren is an Associate Researcher at the Van Vollenhoven Institute for Law, Governance and Society at Leiden University in the Netherlands. She served as Coordinator for UNESCO affairs in the Netherlands from 2016 to 2020, bringing unique expertise in international AI governance and cross-cultural perspectives on technology ethics.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Van Norren specializes in AI ethics through a unique lens, examining the relevance of worldviews of the global south to debates about artificial intelligence. Her work particularly focuses on African philosophy and the Ubuntu perspective, addressing the underrepresentation of African voices in global AI ethics discussions.

### Significant Achievements
Her primary work, "The ethics of artificial intelligence, UNESCO and the African Ubuntu perspective" (2022), explores how different value systems may lead to different choices in programming and application of AI. She argues that the ethics of Ubuntu, with its tenet of relatedness rather than individuality and its collective understanding that "I am because we are," offers unique vantage points that are at odds with Western individualistic thought stemming from Descartes. This work advocates for more inclusive, culturally diverse frameworks for AI development and challenges programming languages that may acerbate existing biases by capturing Western worldviews.
