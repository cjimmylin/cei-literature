# Ernest Davis

## Biographical Summary

Ernest Davis is a Professor of Computer Science at New York University, where he has worked since 1984. He is one of the leading researchers in commonsense reasoning and knowledge representation, focusing on how AI systems can be endowed with the kind of everyday knowledge that humans take for granted. His work spans physical reasoning, spatial reasoning, and the development of benchmarks for evaluating AI commonsense capabilities.

## Research Interests

- Commonsense reasoning in AI
- Knowledge representation and reasoning
- Physical and spatial reasoning
- Automated reasoning systems
- AI benchmark development

## Major Publications

- "Commonsense Reasoning" (2014)
