## Abstract

Dataset compiled from YouTube, Telegram, and VK using keyword-based crawling for destructive speech in Kazakh. Covers cyberbullying, racism, religious extremism, nationalistic extremism.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A3
**Relevance:** Kazakh-specific hate speech detection dataset addressing culturally relevant categories of harmful content including nationalistic extremism and religious extremism specific to Kazakhstan.
