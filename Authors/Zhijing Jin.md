# Zhijing Jin

## Research Summary

Zhijing Jin is an incoming Assistant Professor at the University of Toronto and Research Scientist at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems working with Bernhard Schoelkopf. She completed her PhD under the co-supervision of Bernhard Schölkopf (Max Planck Institute) and Mrinmaya Sachan (ETH Zurich). Her research focuses on causal LLMs for social good, including multilingual AI ethics. She developed the MULTITP dataset examining moral dilemmas across 100+ languages, revealing significant variances in LLM moral alignment across cultures. Her work won Best Paper Award at NeurIPS 2024 Workshop on Pluralistic Alignment.
