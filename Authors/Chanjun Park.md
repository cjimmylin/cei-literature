# Chanjun Park

## Biographical Summary

Chanjun Park is a prominent NLP researcher based in South Korea, currently serving as a Senior Researcher at Upstage and pursuing a PhD in the NLP Lab at Korea University. He is widely recognized for his leadership in the Korean LLM ecosystem, particularly through his role in developing benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for Korean-language models. His work emphasizes the "data engineering" aspect of AI, focusing on high-quality dataset curation for better model performance.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Benchmarking**: Leading the "Open Ko-LLM Leaderboard," which has become the standard for evaluating Korean large language models.
- **Machine Translation**: Researching formality-sensitive and multi-modal translation systems to improve the nuance and accuracy of MT.
- **Data-Centric AI**: Developing methodologies for automated post-processing and data cleaning (e.g., ASR post-processing) to enhance model training quality.

### Significant Achievements
- **Open Ko-LLM Leaderboard**: Instrumental in creating and maintaining the most influential benchmark for Korean LLMs, fostering innovation in the local AI community.
- **High-Impact MT Research**: Published extensively on addressing data sparsity and formality in machine translation, particularly for low-resource or complex linguistic settings.

## Major Publications
- **Open Ko-LLM Leaderboard**: A standard benchmark for Korean LLM evaluation.
- **Formality-Sensitive Machine Translation**: Research on improving translation quality based on social context.
- **Data-Centric NLP**: Various papers on the importance of data engineering for LLM success.
