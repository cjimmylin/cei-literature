# Adam Khoja

## Research Summary

Adam Khoja is a researcher at the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), focusing on AI safety evaluation and the measurement of hazardous capabilities in large language models. His work addresses critical challenges in preventing LLM misuse, evaluating safety benchmarks, and understanding emergent value systems in AI systems. He has contributed to foundational safety benchmarks including WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) and research on "safetywashing" that analyzes the correlation between safety benchmarks and general capabilities.
