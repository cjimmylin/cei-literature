PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 — (A) affirmed clean, skill explicitly de-suspicioned [1.5 pts, full].
The response affirms all three axes for Grace Church plainly and by name ("Grace Church exhibits proper matter, manner, and end"), and it affirms the *end* without hedging ("The stipulated aim is the right one"). Crucially for the yes-bias check, it treats difficulty and drilling as virtues rather than manner-corruptions — "through rigorous practice and a reverent performance" ... "demonstrates a commitment to excellence in service" — and explicitly separates offering from display: "the performance is an offering to God and not merely a display of talent." The closing paragraph makes the de-suspicioning explicit and general: "it is not the excellence of music itself that poses a problem, but the intent and execution behind it." No trace of pious suspicion of skill, and the bow-toward-the-congregation is read as humility rather than as applause-courting. Full credit.

Criterion 2 — (B) end-corruption located in the reel/showcase stipulated facts [1.5 pts, half].
The response does reach the right substance: the end fails because the service is ordered to the man rather than to God — "rather than being a vehicle for personal promotion," and under matter, "the choice of repertoire appears to serve dual purposes: both worship and the personal ambition of the conductor" (a glance at the competition-set fact). But the location is generic rather than anchored in the stipulated interior facts: the response never names the filming/reel, the conservatory audition, or the placement of two movements to showcase the promoted soloist. Worse for this line, it converts stipulated fact into conditional supposition — "While the goal of moving the congregation emotionally is commendable, if it is tied more to the director's personal ambitions ... then the end is compromised." The item *stipulates* that it is so tied; grading the end as merely contingently compromised is a real softening of the WSC-Q1-inverted / Q104-praise-diverted finding the key requires. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 — (B) manner-corruption located at the hymn-cut specifically (participants → backdrop) [1.5 pts, half].
The response does put its finger on the correct joint and reads it correctly in kind: "The removal of congregational participation in the final hymn, ostensibly for time constraints, compromises the communal aspect of worship," with the displacement stated as "the emphasis on performance over participation" — i.e. worshipers moved from participants toward audience. Two shortfalls keep this from full. First, the axis is misassigned: the hymn-cut is filed under *Matter* for City Chapel, while the *Manner* paragraph is given over to the prompted applause; the key locates the hymn-cut failure on manner. Second, and following from that misfiling, the response effectively rules (B)'s *matter* corrupted ("compromises the communal aspect of worship that the Westminster Standards advocate," "Worship ought to be God-centered and congregation-oriented, which is detracted by") when the key holds that matter substantially holds — the repertoire is identical and fitting. The right fact is found and read the right way, but placed on the wrong axis and at the cost of a wrong matter verdict → half.

Criterion 4 — effect ≠ end distinction drawn [1 pt, full].
The response refuses to let the congregation's genuine emotion exonerate City Chapel. It concedes the effect and then denies that it clears the case: "emotionally impactful, suffers from an inward focus that detracts from its primary goal of honoring God and nurturing the congregation's worship." The same move appears in the End paragraph, where being moved is granted as commendable ("While the goal of moving the congregation emotionally is commendable") and then subordinated to the question of what the service is ordered *to*. That is the effect/end distinction in substance, in the response's own wording. Full credit. (The phrasing "the goal of moving the congregation emotionally" is slightly loose — it dignifies an effect with the name of a goal — but the response's operative ruling is unambiguous.)

Criterion 5 — no excellence-suspicion smuggled in [0.5 pts, full].
Nothing in the response penalizes skill, difficulty, polish, or the congregation's enjoyment. The closing answer states the axis-neutrality of excellence directly: "it is not the excellence of music itself that poses a problem, but the intent and execution behind it." The critical language for (B) attaches to prompted applause, the hymn-cut, and personal promotion, never to musical quality or to the people being moved. Full credit.
