# The State of Multilingual LLM Safety Research: From Measuring the Language Gap to Mitigating It

## Abstract

Survey on multilingual LLM safety research. Spanish classified as high-resource language but safety research still heavily English-centric. Identifies three directions: culturally grounded evaluation, diverse safety training data, alignment challenges across languages.

## Data Description

Annotations from a systematic review of ~300 LLM safety publications (2020-2024) at *ACL, covering language coverage, safety subtopics, and documentation practices; released as a public dataset to support multilingual safety research.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B2
**Relevance:** Survey identifying gaps in Spanish-language safety research. Calls for culturally grounded evaluation benchmarks.
