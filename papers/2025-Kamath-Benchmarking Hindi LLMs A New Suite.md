## Abstract

Suite of five Hindi LLM evaluation datasets created with translate-and-verify methodology ensuring linguistic and cultural relevance. Presented at BHASHA 2025. Comprehensive Hindi-specific LLM evaluation suite designed for cultural and linguistic relevance.

## Benchmark

**Name:** IFEval-Hi, MT-Bench-Hi, GSM8K-Hi, ChatRAG-Hi, BFCL-Hi

## Data Description

A suite of five Hindi LLM evaluation datasets (IFEval-Hi, MT-Bench-Hi, GSM8K-Hi, ChatRAG-Hi, BFCL-Hi) totaling ~10,566 samples, created via translate-and-verify plus human annotation methodology for instruction-following, conversational, math, RAG, and function-calling tasks in Hindi.
