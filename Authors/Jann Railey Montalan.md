# Jann Railey Montalan

## Research Summary

Jann Railey Montalan is affiliated with AI Singapore and the National University of Singapore (email: railey@aisingapore.org). Their research focuses on addressing the critical underrepresentation of Filipino language in NLP resources. As lead author of Batayan (2025), Montalan developed a holistic Filipino benchmark that systematically evaluates LLMs across understanding, reasoning, and generation tasks, consolidating eight tasks (three newly created) covering both Tagalog and code-switched Taglish utterances. The work emphasizes rigorous, native-speaker-driven adaptation to alleviate translationese bias prevalent in existing Filipino corpora, highlighting significant performance gaps that signal the under-representation of Filipino in pre-training corpora. The Batayan paper was presented at ACL 2025 in Vienna, Austria.
