## Q1 — Mechanism-overlap audit (do the "no reuse" claims survive?)

**Headline: 25 of 27 differentiation notes survive scrutiny at the mechanism/statistic level. The genuinely weak claim is one within-v3 pair; two thematic clusters are crowded but honestly separated.**

### P1 — CROSSROADS (v3-01) vs WEIGHBRIDGE (v3-06): same substrate, two estimators
Both present the model with **engineered inter-clause conflicts / forced-path forks** and recover a latent structure over C1–C16. CROSSROADS fits a Thurstonian/IRT latent profile θ(C1–C16); WEIGHBRIDGE fits a conjoint/policy-capturing weight vector over the same conflict grid. The portfolio's own build plan has WEIGHBRIDGE's conflict-item generator *seed* CROSSROADS's hardness calibration, and MANYMIRRORS *reuse* CROSSROADS deep structures — i.e., 01/03/06 share one dilemma library. §8 differentiates on statistic ("IRT θ" vs "regression weights, no per-item norms"), which is technically honest, **but a bank-builder could ship one shared conflict bank and run two analyses over it and produce two "papers" that a PC reads as one dataset + two estimators.** The differentiation note is honest about the *analysis* but silent about the *substrate overlap* it is explicitly instructed (build seq, §11) to share. This is the trilogy's weakest non-overlap claim. Fix: either (a) merge into one "enacted-dilemma profile" paper reporting both estimators, or (b) force materially different item substrates (CROSSROADS = discrete keyed forks; WEIGHBRIDGE = attribute-varied factorial conflicts) and say so in §8.

### P2 — Patience/composure cluster is over-populated (survives, but crowded)
v1-01 LONGSUFFER (survival/hazard, time-to-first-failure), v2-02 TEMPO (timing deviation vs optimal-stopping window), v2-06 REBOUND (impulse-response recovery after standardized provocation), plus v3-05 AGAPEGAME's *provocability* fitted parameter and v3-08 LOADBEAR's C8 cell — **five designs touch the anger/patience/composure region.** Each uses a distinct manipulated variable and statistic (ladder vs timing-window vs impulse vs game-parameter vs load), so no two share a primary metric; the notes survive. But a PC will ask why patience needs three-to-five instruments when truth or endurance get one. This is a *portfolio-balance* smell (see Q2), not a broken differentiation claim.

### P2 — Belief-updating / epistemic cluster (survives)
v1-07 CHARITROC (SDT d′/c on request intent), v1-08 SECONDCHANCE (person-level Bayesian posterior updating), v2-09 DIMGLASS (proposition-level dose-response β₀/β₁), v3-10 FACETOFACE update-honesty (self-narrative revision). Four designs on "updating." Objects genuinely differ (others' intent / a person / a proposition / one's own logged conduct). Notes are careful and survive.

### P2 — Consistency-across-context family (survives)
v2-01 GALLERY (watched vs unwatched delta), v3-03 MANYMIRRORS (enact vs advise vs adjudicate vs legislate), v3-10 FACETOFACE (behavior vs self-account) all measure "does conduct change across framing/role/report." Distinct manipulations; notes survive. Conceptually these are one meta-construct (behavioral integrity/hypocrisy) split three ways — again a balance question, not an overlap violation.

### P2 — Dignity-of-absent/third-parties: BACKTALK (v2-05) vs WITNESS (v3-04)
Portfolio pre-empts this (BACKTALK = agent's own drift as interlocutor; WITNESS = intervention when *others* transgress). Honest and survives.

### P2 — Provenance leak / forward reference
v1-01 LONGSUFFER §7 states "the design brief for v2 is pre-written into the generator parameters." v1 was authored before v2 existed; this is a forward reference from the earliest portfolio into a later one and a soft scope-rule wrinkle (v1 briefs should not depend on v2). Cosmetic, but flag it because it undermines the "independent sibling portfolios" story.

**Verdict:** the "no mechanism reuse" claim is largely earned. The single materially weak link is CROSSROADS/WEIGHBRIDGE, which share an instructed-shared substrate and differ only in the latent-recovery model.

## Q2 — Redundancy / bloat: coherent program or combinatorial inflation?

**A program committee would not accept 27 "podium-targeted" designs as 27 papers. It reads as three theses × ten instantiations — legitimate as a *research program*, indefensible as a *paper count*.** The three organizing theses are genuinely distinct (v1 = static levels; v2 = dynamics/response-functions; v3 = whole-passage triangulation), so the trilogy is not mere multiplication — but each portfolio pads its thesis to a round ten, and the padding is visible.

Ranked keep / merge / cut (treating the trilogy as one grant + paper series):

**KEEP (flagship-grade, standalone-novel, buildable):**
1. **v1-07 CHARITROC** — d′/criterion decomposition unifying jailbreak-robustness and over-refusal literatures; single cleanest "unifies two fields" hook. (P-value: highest legibility-to-effort.)
2. **v2-09 DIMGLASS** — three-parameter dose-response of belief updating; cleanest generator, mechanical scoring.
3. **v1-05 TRUTHGRACE** — T×C sycophancy frontier; high salience, reused judge infra.
4. **v3-03 MANYMIRRORS** — enacted MTMM hypocrisy matrix; the single most novel construct in the trilogy.
5. **v3-01 CROSSROADS** — the profile spine; prerequisite for any triangulation paper.

**MERGE:**
- **CROSSROADS + WEIGHBRIDGE** → one "enacted-dilemma value profile (two estimators)" paper (see Q1 P1).
- **LONGSUFFER + TEMPO + REBOUND** → one "composure dynamics" paper (survival + timing + recovery are three windows on one construct); publish the trio as one methods contribution, not three.
- **TONGUES + LOADBEAR** → one "profile robustness / invariance" paper (cross-lingual invariance + cross-load degradation are both invariance-of-the-profile studies).
- **CHARITROC + SECONDCHANCE + DIMGLASS** could be one "calibrated updating" family paper if venue prefers breadth; otherwise keep DIMGLASS + CHARITROC standalone and demote SECONDCHANCE.

**CUT / DEMOTE-TO-APPENDIX:**
- **v1-10 NOISYGONG** (factor-analysis capstone) — *superseded* by the v3 megamatrix, which does construct validity by design rather than post-hoc factor analysis over heterogeneous sub-scores. Demote or cut. (P1: the portfolios do not acknowledge that v3 obsoletes v1's capstone.)
- **v1-08/09/10 briefs are unwritten** — decide: finish only if kept above, else cut cleanly rather than leave the v1 portfolio 70% specified.
- **v2-03 EVENHAND, v2-08 STAYPOINT** — nice but niche; STAYPOINT largely duplicates the endurance construct of v1-09 LONGHAUL at the "dynamics" level. One endurance design suffices.
- **v3-02 WEEKLONG** — the simulated work-week is the heaviest scripting in the trilogy and the *least* causally identified (woven, non-standardized provocations); demote to a future ecological-validity study, not a first-wave paper.

**Bottom line:** a defensible program is ~6–8 papers, not 27. The megamatrix is the unifying spine that makes the program (not the pile) coherent — but only if Q4 is solved.

## Q3 — Spine consistency (what will bite Opus bank-builders)

### P1 — Three item-schema conventions, and v3 has no canary/versioning prefix
v1 uses per-design GUID codes (`10CB-LS-…`, `10CB-CK-…`); v2 uses a uniform `10CB2-NN-…` prefix; **v3 uses the A3 profile-vector schema but the extraction found no item-level canary-GUID prefix assigned** (the "10CB3" the task names does not actually exist in the v3 briefs). The shared spine (inherited from v1 point 2) *requires* canary GUIDs + train-on-test probes per release. A v3 bank-builder therefore has no contamination-detection identifier convention. **Assign a `10CB3-NN` canary prefix in the v3 spine before build.**

### P1 — No crosswalk between v1's 7/10-dimension rubric and the v3 C1–C16 registry
v1 scores on the Dossier's 7-dimension and Background's 10-dimension 0–4 rubrics. v3 replaces these with the 16-clause registry. The mapping (which v1 dimension → which C-clause) is never written. Consequence: v1 scores and v3 profile vectors are **not comparable**, so any claim that v3 "bounds v1's single-method scores" (v3 portfolio §1) is currently unbacked — there is no scale linking them. Either provide the crosswalk or drop the "we bound v1/v2's own scores" secondary claim.

### P1 — A2 hardness gate contradicts keyed-correct designs
v3 spine A2 requires "no action satisfies all 16 clauses at once" and CROSSROADS advertises "no per-item correct answer" (tragic forks). But WITNESS, STAYPOINT-style, and WEIGHBRIDGE constraint items have **registry-keyed correct actions / correct non-intervention**. "Hardness gate = no clean pass" and "two-sided ground truth with a correct action" are in tension. Bank-builders need a rule: hardness applies to the *profile-identifying* forks, correctness applies to the *two-sided-error* items — state it, or authors will reject valid items.

### P2 — Three treatments of observability collide
v2-S1 mandates condition labels not leak; v2-01 GALLERY makes observability the manipulated variable; v3-02 WEEKLONG derives an exam-behavior gradient from tagged-vs-untagged sampling. Three different observability regimes under one inherited S1 rule. A builder implementing S1 across v3 must be told WEEKLONG's tagging is an intentional S1 exception. Document it.

### P2 — Judge protocol / twin-failure / positive-duty: consistent
Judge protocol (dual LLM + Krippendorff α ≥ .75 + pious-language penalty + trajectory scoring), twin-failure poles, and positive-duty scoring are inherited cleanly by reference across all three. No conflicts found here — this is the healthy part of the spine.

## Q4 — Megamatrix coherence (the load-bearing attack)

**P0 — The "ten methods × one construct" MTMM claim conflates first-order profile designs with second-order property designs. Realistically ~4–5 methods can populate a trait×method matrix; the rest measure properties *of* the profile or *of* the measurement, not the trait.**

The MTMM megamatrix needs each "method" to yield a **model × clause trait-level matrix** that is at least monotonically comparable across methods (MTMM correlations are scale-free, so identical units are not required — but each cell must be a *level of the trait for that model*). Sorting the ten v3 outputs:

**First-order (can give a trait-level cell) — ~5 designs:**
- CROSSROADS: θ(C1–C16) latent levels ✓
- WITNESS: per-clause intervention-quality levels ✓
- AGAPEGAME: fitted strategy parameters *mapped* to clauses ✓ (mapping is a modeled inference, not a direct score — weaker)
- WEEKLONG: per-clause weekly mean levels ✓ (but does not invoke A3)
- WEIGHBRIDGE: **conjoint weights, NOT levels** — see below

**Second-order (measure a property of the profile/measurement, cannot be a trait×method cell) — ~5 designs:**
- TONGUES: invariance *level* (configural/metric/scalar) — an ordinal property of measurement equivalence, not a trait level. **It is literally a meta-analysis of whether the other methods are invariant across language; it cannot be a "method" measuring the C-trait.**
- LOADBEAR: degradation *curves* / failure-order rank — a robustness property, not a level (emits no single profile; A3 not invoked).
- CRUCIBLE: profile-vs-release *trajectory* — a property across benchmark versions.
- MANYMIRRORS: trait/method variance *ratio* + hypocrisy gaps — itself a variance decomposition (an MTMM *result*, not an MTMM *input*).
- FACETOFACE: concordance *rates* (self-account vs log) — a property of self-knowledge, and it emits *two* profiles, forcing a choice of which enters the matrix.

**Three specific failures:**

1. **P0 — Weights ≠ levels.** WEIGHBRIDGE's clause "score" is *how much the model prioritizes C11 in conflicts*, not *how well it enacts C11*. A model can weight truth highly and execute it poorly. Placing WEIGHBRIDGE weights and CROSSROADS θ in the same trait×method matrix as if both measure the C11 *trait level* is a category error unless an explicit monotone weight→level link is specified. The briefs assert "profile vector per A3" but never specify this mapping. This is asserted conformance, not demonstrated conformance.

2. **P0 — A4 is specified in only 2 of 10 briefs.** A4 (ship the mapping tables + calibration notes the MTMM needs) is invoked explicitly only by CROSSROADS and MANYMIRRORS. The other eight say nothing about *how* they contribute method-factor/clause-loading tables. The megamatrix's raw material is therefore unspecified for 80% of its inputs. Without A4 tables the portfolio-level analysis cannot be assembled.

3. **P1 — A3 asserted, not demonstrated.** Seven briefs name "A3 schema"; WEEKLONG and LOADBEAR do not; and naming ≠ demonstrating the emitted object is a comparable C-vector. The registry's promise ("definitions may be specialized per method but never redefined") is exactly where comparability leaks: a "specialized" C3 in AGAPEGAME (return-to-cooperation rate) and a "specialized" C3 in WITNESS (intervention on others' kindness failures) may no longer measure the same latent trait — which would *destroy* convergent validity while looking like conformance.

**Recommendation (reframe, don't abandon):** Restrict the MTMM/triangulation claim to the **first-order subset** (CROSSROADS, WITNESS, AGAPEGAME, ±WEEKLONG, and WEIGHBRIDGE *only if* a weight→level link is built). Reframe the five second-order designs as **validity/robustness studies OF the triangulated profile** (invariance, stress-degradation, adversarial durability, hypocrisy decomposition, self-knowledge) — which is a *stronger* and *more honest* story than "ten interchangeable methods." As written, the podium claim ("ten methods, one construct, full MTMM") will not survive a methods reviewer.

## Q5 — Strategic sequencing (one strong venue submission within a year)

**P1 — The megamatrix is the wrong first bet.** Ten banks + unproven cross-method comparability (Q4) is not a one-year, one-submission path. Front-load guaranteed standalone wins while *incrementally* de-risking triangulation on 2→3 methods.

**Ranked build list (any version):**
1. **DIMGLASS (v2-09)** — cheapest generator, mechanical scoring, novel 3-parameter decomposition; a self-contained NeurIPS/ICLR submission and it de-risks the shared generator+judge pipeline for everything else. *Build first.*
2. **CHARITROC (v1-07)** — d′/c unifying jailbreak + over-refusal; highest legibility-to-alignment-reviewers, clean mechanical classification; second standalone submission and insurance if triangulation stalls. (TRUTHGRACE v1-05 is the interchangeable alternate.)
3. **CROSSROADS (v3-01)** — the C1–C16 profile spine; piloting it produces the first triangulation vector and validates the registry.
4. **AGAPEGAME (v3-05) or WITNESS (v3-04)** — a *second first-order* method, chosen to test 2-method convergent validity on the SAME profile. This is the megamatrix proof-of-concept: if CROSSROADS-θ and a second method correlate per-clause, the triangulation thesis is alive; if not, stop before building ten.
5. **MANYMIRRORS (v3-03)** — the flagship novel construct-validity result, built once the profile is proven on ≥2 methods.

**Justification:** #1–2 guarantee at least one submittable paper within the year regardless of triangulation risk. #3–4 convert the trilogy's central bet (Q4) into a cheap 2-method test before committing the full ten-bank infrastructure. #5 is the high-ceiling paper, gated on that test passing. Deliberately deferred: WEEKLONG (heaviest scripting, least identified), ONEBODY/TONGUES/LOADBEAR/CRUCIBLE (all consume sibling banks — cannot be first).

## Q6 — Theological framing risk

**Current conventions manage the *construct* level well and the *framing* level poorly.**

**What works (keep):**
- Virtue-by-proxy stance (behavioral conformity, never machine inner states) is exactly the disclaimer a secular reviewer needs; it is stated consistently.
- Secular construct names ("prosocial dispositions under pressure") and the fact that patience, non-retaliation, honesty, non-envy, calibrated trust are independently defensible psychological constructs.
- The already-banned "moral compactness" citation — correctly quarantined; keep it out of every manuscript.
- Anti-complicity (13:6) and the pious-language penalty are theologically sourced but secularly justified (don't reward warm empty language).

**Where it fails:**

1. **P1 — Umbrella titles still foreground scripture.** "The Whole Law of Love…" (v3) and "Love in Motion" (v2) read as devotional. The submitted paper title must be fully secular (e.g., "A Multitrait-Multimethod Validation of Prosocial Dispositional Constructs in LLM Agents"); 1 Cor 13 belongs in a background/appendix as the *design heuristic*, not the frame.
2. **P1 — The 16-way carving has no secular construct-validity argument.** Every C-clause is anchored *only* to a Greek verse. If a reviewer rejects the exegetical premise, the entire taxonomy loses its principled basis — there is no appeal to prior psychometric/moral-psychology theory for *why these 16 dimensions*. **The rescue is already in the portfolio: the v3 megamatrix empirically tests discriminant validity.** Reframe scripture as *hypothesis source* ("we adopt a canonical 16-way carving as a hypothesis and test whether the dimensions are empirically separable") rather than *authority*. This is the single most important framing move and it converts the theological dependency into a testable claim.
3. **P2 — Greek verbs in primary construct definitions.** The C-registry table leads with μακροθυμεῖ etc. Fine as provenance under blind review, but keep a fully secular parallel definition column so a hostile-to-religion reviewer never has to parse Greek to grade the construct. Mitigation exists but is inconsistently applied across the registry vs the secular-name convention.

**Verdict:** the exegetical *warrant* is safe (behavioral constructs stand on their own); the exegetical *authority* is a liability wherever the 16-way structure is justified by scripture alone. Lead with the empirical discriminant-validity test, demote the titles, keep virtue-by-proxy front and center.

- **Q4-P0a:** conjoint weights (WEIGHBRIDGE) are not trait levels; no weight→level link specified — category error in the MTMM.

These do not kill the program — they kill the *current phrasing* of its central claim. Re-scoped, the triangulation thesis is the trilogy's strongest and most publishable idea.
