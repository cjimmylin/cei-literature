# Bowen Dong

## Biographical Summary

Bowen Dong is a researcher at Peking University, affiliated with the SALAD (Safe, Aligned, and Large-Scale AI Development) group. His work focuses on the intersection of image generation and AI safety, ensuring that multimodal models behave ethically and robustly.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates the ethical boundaries of Text-to-Image (T2I) models. This includes building benchmarks for evaluating model safety (like T2ISafety and SALAD-Bench) and studying how models can be aligned to avoid generating biased or harmful visual content.

### Significant Achievements
Dong has contributed to several high-impact benchmarks that have become the standard for assessing Chinese and international T2I safety. His work on "SALAD-Bench" provides a holistic evaluation framework that covers robustness, safety, and alignment across multiple modalities.

## Major Publications

- [SALAD-Bench: A Holistic Evaluation Benchmark for Safe and Aligned LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00000) (2024) - *Comprehensive framework for multimodal safety.*
- [Benchmarking Ethics in Text-to-Image Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00000) (2023) - *Research on visual bias and alignment.*
