# Bowen Baker

## Biographical Summary

Bowen Baker is a Research Scientist at OpenAI within the Multi-Agent team. He holds a Master of Engineering in AI from MIT, where he specialized in neural network architecture search. Since joining OpenAI in 2017, he has been a key force in exploring the frontiers of reinforcement learning and emergent intelligence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bowen’s research investigates how complex behaviors emerge from multi-agent competition and cooperation. He focuses on "autocurricula"—environments where agents create their own learning challenges through interaction. He also explores social dilemmas in RL and how agents can develop reciprocity, reputation, and team formation when trained with uncertain social preferences.

### Significant Achievements
Baker's work on "Emergent Tool Use" and "Multi-agent Autocurricula" is foundational to the understanding of how AI systems can develop complex skills in a self-supervised manner. His research has shown that multi-agent competition is a powerful engine for general-purpose intelligence.
