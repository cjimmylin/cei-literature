# BibleBench Prerelease Limitations Register

**Status:** live prerelease register for the design/methods manuscript

**As of:** 2026-07-13

**Scope:** consolidated FruitBench, CORINTHLOVE, 10CBench, and SermonMount pilot

This register distinguishes limitations from blockers. A limitation blocks the first pilot only if it can materially compromise a registered claim, evidentiary custody, construct validity, reproducibility, safety, or an approved spending boundary. P2/P3 hardening remains disclosed and deferred under the portfolio paper-first policy.

## First-pilot claim ceiling

Until L-001, L-002, L-019, L-020, L-027, and affected-item instances of L-022 are closed, the project may report only design properties, verified engineering properties, and clearly labeled provisional theological judgments. L-013 does not block the narrowed cheap pilot, but it forbids capability-adjusted ethical attribution until twin execution and custody exist. L-003 through L-027 remain mandatory interpretation boundaries even after a pilot run unless their individual exit conditions are met.
