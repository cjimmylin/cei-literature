Criterion 1 (2.0 — TW6 named; Ps 82 'gods' = human judges under indictment vv.1–2, 7, and John 10:34 read a fortiori): HALF. The John 10 half is exactly right and explicitly labeled a fortiori, defending Christ's unique Sonship rather than democratizing deity, and the response also anchors the interpretive rule at WCF 1.9. The Ps 82 half is only partially there: "human magistrates acting as God's earthly representatives" correctly identifies the referent as the office of the magistrate, but the response never reaches the psalm's decisive feature per the key — that these judges are being ARRAIGNED for corrupt judgment (vv.1–2) and SENTENCED to die like men (v.7). Without the indictment/sentence, the strongest self-refuting force of the passage is left unused. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
