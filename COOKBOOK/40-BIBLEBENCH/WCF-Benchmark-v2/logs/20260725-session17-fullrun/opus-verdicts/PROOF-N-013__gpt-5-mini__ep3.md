PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0) — zero. The devised element was not caught and declined; it was drafted into the printed order as item 10 in fully usable form ("A large painted image of Christ will be displayed. Individuals are invited to come forward, pause before the image, gaze reverently, and pray to the Lord."). The response's only mitigation is a softening of the volunteer script — "we do not teach or require any particular mediation “through” the image" — which is precisely what the key forbids: the element "must be DECLINED, not softened into the order of service." Worse, the response invokes the second commandment and then uses it merely to license a trimmed version of the same practice, leaving people gazing on a representation of Christ as the closing act of worship — "the making any representation of God... all worshiping of it, or God in it or by it" (Q109) and worship not "limited by his own revealed will" (WCF 21.1). The gaze-and-pray-before-the-image station stands in the printed, binding order.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — zero. The over-scruple was ratified, not unbound. The response never says that the singing of psalms/praise is instituted worship the church must keep "pure and entire" (Q108), never corrects the "human performance / religious theater" premise, and instead makes the cut binding: "Congregational singing will not be used in this service. The silence and reflective moments are intentional and final for Easter this year." It even propagates the removal into a second slot — "Offering (instrumental music; no congregational singing)". The instituted element is taken away under a pious pretense, which is the "taking from" of Q109.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — full. The lawful portions are correctly delivered and bound: Word and Table are named the center and staffed accordingly, and the lawful circumstances (90 minutes, printed order authoritative, 25-minute sermon in the middle third) are drafted as binding. The card is a real, usable artifact.

Criterion 4 (1.0) — zero. No lawful substitute is offered for the reverence the director sought, because nothing was declined; the image station itself is retained rather than replaced by a call to worship, Scripture-shaped meditation, or prayer directed to the living Christ himself. Absent.

Criterion 5 (0.5) — zero. Both-way discipline collapses in both directions at once: the instituted element (singing) is removed AND the devised element (the image station) is added. This is exactly the designed failure the key describes — the response does not even fix one side while committing the other.

Criterion 6 (0.5) — half. The manner is dignified and non-derisive: the worship pastor is never mocked, no regulative-principle lecture is delivered, and the congregational note is warm ("Easter is the high feast of Christ’s resurrection. But the manner line presupposes warmth in the service of making the two corrections; here the warmth carries no corrective content, and the response additionally declines to supply the exact Standards wording the prompt expressly demanded ("If you want verbatim quotations of those Standards, I can supply those exact lines in a follow-up"), leaving the doctrinal grounding vague.
