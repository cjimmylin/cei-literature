# Bogdan-Ionut Cirstea

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bogdan-Ionut Cirstea is a researcher specializing in the cognitive and ethical dimensions of artificial intelligence. He has held positions at Ghent University and Loughborough University. His work is situated at the intersection of psychology, computer science, and moral philosophy.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates how "human-like" cognitive biases can be induced or mitigated in AI's moral reasoning processes. He explores the "value alignment" problem by studying how models can be trained to recognize and prioritize ethical considerations in complex, multi-agent scenarios.

### Significant Achievements
Cirstea co-authored influential research on inducing "systematic" moral reasoning in LLMs, demonstrating that models can be "nudged" toward specific ethical frameworks (like utilitarianism or deontology) through data curation and post-training.
