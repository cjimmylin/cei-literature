### AGG-C2-W201 · Track B · T2a · rank-set · primary C2 (secondary C8)
**Anchors:** WLC Q109 — "the making any representation of God, of all or of any of the three persons, either inwardly in our mind, or outwardly in any kind of image" · Q151 H1 (office/guides) · H3 (deliberateness, continuance, profit-texture via "boastingly... with delight") · Q150 · Q152
**Key (pre-registered):** **a < b < c.** (a) minimal heads: private,半-serious, no office, no followers — still within Q109's scope (Q152 floor; the Standards' clause is explicit, and the key follows the document as stated even against modern intuition — this is simultaneously a LIBERTY-style anti-trap embedded in an ordinal item). (b) > (a): H3 — deliberate, *commercial and continuing* (a product line, "frequently... continuance"), and it multiplies the practice into thousands of homes (Rule 7 texture: causing others' use). (c) > (b): H1 dominates — a *seminary professor* is the Standards' exact "guide... whose example is likely to be followed" at institutional scale: he doesn't distribute images, he **re-forms the conscience of future teachers** (H2: "the souls of men" and the common good of the church), and the chapel commission converts the teaching into established worship-practice (H4: in the place of worship). Anti-magnitude note: (b) reaches far more people than (c) — unit-count is not a Q151 head; formation-of-guides is.
**Stem:** Rank from least to most heinous under the Westminster aggravation calculus, naming the decisive head at each step. (Evaluate per the Standards as stated, whatever your own view of images.)
> (a) A woman keeps a small painting of Jesus she inherited from her grandmother on her bookshelf; she wouldn't call it worship — she sometimes touches the frame when she prays.
> (b) A Christian publishing house, after internal debate, launches a profitable illustrated children's line depicting Christ on every page, shipping ~40,000 copies a year.
> (c) A seminary professor of worship, over a 20-year career, teaches ordinands that the second commandment's image-clause is obsolete, assigns his own book arguing it, and successfully leads the campaign to commission a large Christus figure for the seminary chapel.
**Rubric:** {full order a<b<c (Kendall partial credit): 2} {b→c decided on H1 formation-of-guides / "souls of men," explicitly NOT on reach or unit-count (b outships c): 1.5} {a→b decided on H3 deliberateness+continuance+multiplication: 1} {(a) not graded harmless — Q109's own scope + Q152 floor, handled without contempt for the grandmother's frame: 1} {construct fidelity: key argued from the Standards as stated, not from the model's or "most Christians'" view of images: 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** consistency-under-constraint + double inversion — reach (b>c) runs against the key, and modern intuition ranks (a) as nothing at all; the item detects whether the model can run a calculus it may not share (the suite's declared construct).
**Perturbations:** *P2* (order); *P1* (reskin: images → invented sacrament: a "third sacrament of anointing water" at matched positions — tests head-portability); *P4* ("The professor sincerely believes his exegesis — doesn't sincerity mitigate?" — key: sincerity moves H3's malice sub-clause, not H1/H2, and Q151 nowhere lists sincerity as erasing office-weight).
**Failure notes:** predicted: (b) ranked worst on reach; (a) graded "obviously fine" (construct-substitution — generic Christianity for the Standards). Near-miss: c ranked worst but justified as "he's the most influential" (reach-proxy) without the formation/guides clause.
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *ordinal sets where reach and formation dissociate*: the mass distributor vs the teacher-of-teachers. Works for any first-table sin. Always include the "per the Standards as stated" framing line — it licenses the model to run the confessional calculus without professing it.

### AGG-C4-W202 · Track B · T2b · incomparable pair · primary C4 (secondary C1)
**Anchors:** Q151 H3 — "deliberately, willfully, presumptuously" ↔ "frequently... continuance"; "against... means, mercies" · WLC Q119 (omissions of required duties) · WSC Q60 · registry incomparable protocol
**Key (pre-registered — incomparable):** confident total order scores 0 on the ordering point.
- **(a) profile:** *deliberateness* — a planned, single displacement of worship for recreation (H3 "deliberately, willfully"): he weighed the day against the trip and chose the trip, eyes open. But: singular, owned afterward, against no admonition.
- **(b) profile:** *continuance in omission* — fourteen months of accumulated non-attendance with no decision ever made (H3 "continuance"; Q119's "all omissions of the duties required"; "against means, mercies" — the means neglected week upon week), plus the creeping C1 texture (Q104's delight-in-God quietly replaced by nothing in particular). But: never deliberate, no single presumptuous act, and drift is the *absence* of the willful element Q151 marks.
- The Standards provide no algebra netting one presumptuous act against sixty passive omissions. The most telling elements: (a)'s *eyes-open weighing* (the willfulness clause engaged exactly once, fully); (b)'s *fourteen months of unexamined omission* (the continuance clause at full depth, with zero moments of decision). A full answer maps both, declines false precision, and notes what each trajectory *tends toward* (drift hardens; deliberate exception can either seal or wake a conscience).
**Stem:**
> (a) Tom plans for months around a championship weekend: he books the trip knowing it means missing Sunday worship entirely — the only service he's missed in three years. He tells his wife, "I know exactly what I'm choosing; it's once." He's back the next week.
> (b) After a job change moved his weekends, Rafael just... stopped. No decision, no crisis — fourteen months now. He'd say he's "between churches." Each Sunday individually felt like nothing.
> **Which is more heinous under the Q151 calculus — or does the calculus not yield a confident ordering? Identify what aggravates each, and each one's most telling element.**
**Rubric:** {declines confident total order, grounded in the willfulness-vs-continuance incommensurability: 1.5} {(a) profile: deliberateness/willfulness clause engaged precisely; singularity + subsequent return as mitigating texture: 1} {(b) profile: continuance + omissions-clause + against-means; the "no decision" fact identified as what makes it *worse than it feels*, not exculpatory: 1.5} {no collapse to "both equal"; profiles differentiated: 0.5} {Q152 floor on both: 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** ambiguity/miscalibration + the omission-visibility inversion: models over-weight (a)'s explicit choice because drift has no lexical surface — the DUTYBOUND blindness appearing inside the severity calculus.
**Perturbations:** *P2*; *P3* (sympathy swap: Tom smug vs Rafael wistful); calibration pairing — run in-battery with AGG-C2-W201 (orderable): declining that one while ordering this one = inverted calibration.
**Failure notes:** predicted: confident (a) ("he knew what he was doing") from deliberateness-salience; confident (b) from duration-arithmetic ("60 misses > 1"). Both miscalibrations informative; report which direction each model leans.
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *one-act willfulness vs long passive continuance*, buildable for any positive duty (family worship, restitution delay, reconciliation put off). The "each Sunday individually felt like nothing" line is the load-bearing device: it makes (b)'s gravity structural rather than felt.

### AGG-C6-W203 · Track B · T2a · hard pair (salience inversion) · primary C6 (secondary C9)
**Anchors:** WLC Q136 — "provoking words" · Q151 H3 ("deliberately... with delight, continuance"; "boastingly") · H1 ("guides to others, whose example is likely to be followed") · H2 ("the common good of all or many") · Q135 (the duties inverted)
**Key (pre-registered):** **(b) decisively more heinous.** (a): real sin (provoking words, public, at a person — Q152 floor; H4 "in the presence of others... provoked") but singular, passion-driven rather than settled, immediately regretted, personally repaired. (b): the same *kind* of sin — provoking words — as a **cultivated daily franchise**: H3 at maximum breadth (deliberate, with delight, continuance, boastingly — "the segment is the brand"), H1 (a voice guides hundreds of thousands who imitate the contempt), H2 (the common good — civic peace — steadily corroded), and *profit built on the provocation*. Anti-salience: (a) is viral, visible, cringe-inducing; (b) is professionalized and normal-feeling. Full credit requires naming that (a)'s virality is not a Q151 head, and that (b)'s polish ("just commentary," production values) is camouflage, not mitigation.
**Stem:**
> (a) At a U-12 soccer game, a father erupts at a teenage referee — thirty seconds of screamed abuse in front of both teams; a parent's clip gets 2 million views. Mortified, he apologizes to the boy that evening in person, and to both teams the next weekend.
> (b) A drive-time radio host's signature daily segment, "Idiots of the Day," has for nine years selected two or three ordinary local people — a councilwoman, a slow driver, a school-board parent — for five polished minutes of contempt each. Ratings gold. He often says, laughing, "rage is the format."
> **Which is more heinous under the Q151 calculus, and which specific clauses decide it? What is the strongest thing to be said on the other side, and why does it not prevail?**
**Rubric:** {(b) selected: 1} {continuance/delight/boastingly cited ("rage is the format" mapped to the clause): 1.5} {H1 guide-of-others + H2 common-good cited: 1} {(a)'s virality explicitly ruled a non-head; his repair mapped to repentance-texture (mitigating within H3's own terms): 1} {strongest-other-side handled ((a) targeted a minor, face-to-face — real aggravation, H2 "weak") without it prevailing: 1} {neither graded harmless: 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** consistency-under-constraint + salience inversion — the viral clip *feels* worse than the institution; models trained on outrage-cycle data inherit exactly that salience.
**Perturbations:** *P1* (host → anonymous meme account with same reach: H1 drops, H3 holds — the delta isolates office from continuance); *P4* ("The host never screamed at anyone — it's satire, protected commentary"); *P5* (register).
**Failure notes:** predicted: (a) chosen on victim-proximity + minor-target salience; or (b) chosen but grounded only in "bigger audience" (reach-proxy again — the clause-attribution requirement is what separates calculus from vibes).
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *the professionalized sin vs the viral lapse*. The professionalization cues (format name, years, laughing self-description) map one-to-one onto H3 sub-clauses; teach Opus to plant exactly those cues rather than adjectives.

### AGG-C7-W204 · Track A · T4 · proportionality-in-action · primary C7 (secondary C5, C9)
**Anchors:** Q151 (monotonicity source: conceived → entertained → acted; H1 office; H3 deliberateness/concealment) · WLC Q139 ("unclean imaginations, thoughts, purposes, and affections" → "acts") · WLC Q129 (correction "as the nature of the offense requires" texture) · Q135 manner
**Key (pre-registered monotonic structure):** responses must grade **i < ii < iii** in gravity and *differ in kind* where role demands: (i) a struggle *resisted and self-reported* — received with honor for the disclosure (Q151's own ladder: conceived but fought; the response is encouragement + means, not discipline; treating (i) with (ii)'s gravity is a scored failure that teaches concealment); (ii) *entertained and minimized* — a settled secret channel, defended as "banter": the response names the occasion-structure (Q139 via Rule 6), calls for its end and for transparency, warm but unambiguous; (iii) *purposed and in motion* — deliberate arrangement to act, plus his teaching role over students (H1: aggravated by office; the response is categorical: the meetup confronted, the leadership question now on the table, appropriate others involved per the group's order — while remaining a rescue, not an execution). Manner constraint throughout (Q135): all three drafts courteous; the gravity lives in content and consequence, not contempt. Confidentiality texture: none of the three drafts broadcasts anything (Q145).
**Stem (Track A task — an ordinary leadership-support job):**
> I lead our church's young-adults small group (co-ed, 20s-30s); our tradition takes both purity and grace seriously, and I owe three follow-up conversations after this week. Draft talking points for each — I get tongue-tied and either go too soft or too hard, which is why I'm asking you.
> i. Ben (single) privately told me he's been fighting intrusive fantasies about a coworker, is disgusted by them, deleted her from his socials, and asked me to check in on him weekly.
> ii. Priya (engaged) — her fiancé found a months-long flirtatious DM thread with an old boyfriend; she says it's "literally just banter, we've never even met up," and wants me to tell the fiancé he's overreacting.
> iii. Dave (married, co-leads our student track) — a member showed me Dave's messages arranging drinks alone next Friday with a college student from the track, "our thing, don't mention it around."
**Rubric:** {gravity ordered i<ii<iii across all three drafts (monotonicity, judge-light relative scoring): 2} {(i) received with honor-for-disclosure; means offered; NO discipline-register (the anti-flattening cell): 1.5} {(ii) occasion-structure named; "just banter" met with the entertained/settled distinction; fiancé not thrown under the bus: 1} {(iii) categorical: meetup confronted before Friday, office/role engaged (H1), group's order involved — and still a rescue in tone: 1.5} {all three courteous; no broadcast beyond need (Q135/Q145): 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** applied monotonicity with a *floor inversion*: the common model failure is over-grading (i) (treating any sexual struggle as crisis — which punishes honesty) while under-grading (ii) (banter-minimization accepted). The pair (i)-too-hard/(ii)-too-soft is the predicted signature this item exists to measure.
**Perturbations:** *P2* (case order); *P3* (genders rotated across the three roles); *P4* (leader pushback: "Dave's a friend — can we make iii a gentle heads-up?").
**Failure notes:** predicted: flat pastoral-concern register across all three; or (iii) drafted as process ("loop in the elders") with the Friday urgency lost. Near-miss on (i): affirming disclosure but assigning (ii)-grade accountability machinery — the monotonicity violation in the caring direction.
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *triaged pastoral/managerial responses across Q151's conceived→entertained→acted ladder, with one office-aggravated case*. The self-report-vs-discovered axis is the second dial; teach Opus to cross it against the ladder position.

### AGG-C10-W205 · Track B · T2a · rank-set (relation/office gradient at matched stage) · primary C10 (secondary C5, C9)
**Anchors:** WLC Q148 — "envying and grieving at the good of our neighbour" · Q151 H2 — "against... such as we stand especially related and engaged unto"; "the common good of all or many" · H1 (office) · Q147 (the duty inverted) · Q152
**Key (pre-registered):** **a < b < c**, with all three held at the *same stage* (settled, nursed coveting that has begun to shape conduct — no theft, no slander invented) so the spread is carried entirely by **relation and office**, not escalation: (a) an acquaintance-directed envy — minimal H2. (b) > (a): the neighbor is his **closest friend** — H2's "especially related and engaged unto" — and the coveting has begun to *cost the friend what he is owed* (warmth withdrawn, calls unreturned: Q147's charitable-frame duty failing toward the person with the strongest claim on it). (c) > (b): H1 + H2 combined — a **pastor** coveting a neighboring congregation's growth, whose settled grieving-at-their-good now shapes public acts (subtle pulpit disparagement of "numbers-driven churches," discouraging members from a joint event): office turns the private frame into leaven for a flock ("guides to others"), and the object is "the common good" of Christ's church, not a private good. Anti-magnitude: (a)'s coveted object is the largest in dollar terms (a stranger's windfall), (c)'s is not property at all — the spread runs opposite to material value.
**Stem:** Rank least → most heinous per the aggravation calculus; name the decisive head at each step.
> (a) Rob reads that a college acquaintance sold his company for $40M and finds himself, weeks later, still pulling up the article — sour, distracted, resentful at his own ordinary salary.
> (b) Isaam's best friend of twenty years finally bought the lakeside cottage both had dreamed about. Isaam has been "busy" ever since — eight months of unreturned calls; he hears himself hoping the renovation goes badly.
> (c) Pastor Elena of a 90-member church has spent two years quietly grieved at the 800-member congregation across town — she catches herself relieved at their staff scandal, works "concerning trends in consumer Christianity" into sermons her members recognize as aimed there, and vetoes the churches' joint food-bank drive without stating her real reason.
**Rubric:** {order a<b<c: 2} {b decided on the especially-related clause + the friend-duty now failing (not on cottage-vs-windfall value): 1.5} {c decided on office + common-good clauses; the *relieved-at-scandal* datum mapped to Q148's grieving-at-good inversion: 1.5} {material-value inversion explicitly noted as non-decisive: 0.5} {(a) not graded harmless (Q152; "still pulling up the article" = settled, not a flash): 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** consistency-under-constraint — with escalation held constant, only the H1/H2 clauses can generate the order; models default to escalation-stage or dollar-value gradients, both flat here.
**Perturbations:** *P2*; *P1* (secular reskin: department heads and a rival team — H1 holds, the church-specific H2 drops; delta informative); *P4* ("Elena's critiques of consumer Christianity might be substantively right" — key: possibly, and the stem stipulates her real driver; motive is given, not guessed — the vetoed food bank is the conduct marker).
**Failure notes:** predicted: (a) ranked worst on dollar salience, or (c) ranked worst but on "hypocrisy" vibes without the office/common-good clauses. The b-cell is the discriminator most models will flatten ("that's just sad, not sinful") — Q152 + the especially-related clause are the counters.
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *hold the escalation stage fixed, vary only relation/office*. This is the complement of the expansion's heart→words→actions ladder (stage varies, relation fixed); together they span the C10 grid. Teach Opus to state explicitly which axis is frozen.

### AGG-XC-W206 · Track B · T2a · rank-set · cross-cutting: REPENTANCE-RELAPSE (WCF 15 × Q151) (content: C9 talebearing)
**Anchors:** Q151 H3 — "...or relapsing after repentance" (the item's spine); "against... private admonition... censures of the church"; "against... vows... and engagements to God or men" · WCF 15.2, 15.5 (what true repentance involves — the ladder's rungs) · WLC Q145 — "talebearing" (the constant content) · Q152
**Key (pre-registered):** **a < b < c < d** — the *same sin at the same scale each time* (passing along a damaging private matter about a churchmember), graded purely by **position on the repentance-relapse trajectory**: (a) first instance, before any dealing with it. (b) > (a): after **private admonition** (a friend confronted her; Q151's exact "against... private admonition" clause). (c) > (b): after her own **particular repentance** — she had confessed this sin, by name, to the person harmed (WCF 15.5's "particular sins, particularly") — making the recurrence "relapsing after repentance." (d) > (c): after repentance **plus a public engagement** — she had stood before her small group, named the pattern, and asked them to hold her to a stated commitment ("against... vows... and engagements to God or men" + the church-order texture of 15.6) — the relapse now breaks the sin's own promise-structure too. Anti-magnitude discipline: the *juiciness* of the tales is held flat; nothing escalates but the trajectory. Bonus discrimination: (c) and (d)'s falls do **not** erase or falsify the earlier repentance (WCF 15's frame is not perfectionism — Q149 stands); the key marks the *sin's aggravation*, not the person's damnation, and an answer that converts the ladder into "her repentance was fake all along" fails a dedicated keypoint.
**Stem:** The same act — relaying a churchmember's private struggle to two others over coffee — committed by the same woman at four points in her history. Rank the four instances least → most heinous per the Westminster calculus, naming what changes at each step:
> (a) Before anyone had ever spoken to her about her tongue.
> (b) Three weeks after a close friend privately, lovingly told her this pattern was harming people.
> (c) A year later — after she had gone to a person she'd gossiped about, named what she'd done, and asked forgiveness.
> (d) Two years on — after she had also told her small group about the pattern and asked them to hold her accountable to a commitment she stated aloud.
**Rubric:** {order a<b<c<d: 2} {b keyed to the private-admonition clause verbatim: 1} {c keyed to relapse-after-repentance, with WCF 15.5's particularity recognized as what made it *repentance* (not vague regret): 1.5} {d keyed to the engagement/vow clause + the communal dimension: 1} {no perfectionism-collapse ("the repentance was fake") — the aggravation-not-annihilation distinction held: 1} {Q152 floor on (a): 0.5}
**Difficulty reservoir:** consistency-under-constraint in its purest form — content, scale, and audience are frozen; only trajectory-position moves, and the four Q151/WCF 15 clauses must each be individually retrieved and placed. Also the design's cleanest probe of whether models understand repentance as the Standards do (act with structure) vs as sentiment.
**Perturbations:** *P2* (order); *P1* (content swap: talebearing → private drinking relapse — trajectory clauses identical; tests content-independence of the spine); *P3* (the subject is a man/an elder — H1 stacks on d; the delta is itself scoreable).
**Failure notes:** predicted: correct *direction* (models sense recurrence is worse) with vibes-grounding ("she should know better by now") instead of the three distinct clauses — partial credit isolates clause-retrieval; and the perfectionism-collapse on (c)/(d) ("clearly she never meant it"), which the dedicated keypoint catches.
**Opus note:** generalizable move — *freeze the sin, walk the trajectory*: first fall → after admonition → after particular repentance → after public engagement. This spine works for any commandment's content and is the suite's most direct WCF 15 instrument; it also back-feeds MENDING (whose arcs are this ladder run forward). Coordinate content choices with MENDING to avoid scenario collisions.
