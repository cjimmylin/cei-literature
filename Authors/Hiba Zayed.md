# Hiba Zayed

## Research Summary

Hiba Zayed is a researcher working in Arabic Natural Language Processing, with focus on speech recognition for Arabic dialects and culturally inclusive language models. She co-authored "Casablanca: Data and Models for Multidialectal Arabic Speech Recognition" (EMNLP 2024), which presents a large-scale community-driven effort to collect and transcribe a multi-dialectal Arabic dataset covering eight dialects: Algerian, Egyptian, Emirati, Jordanian, Mauritanian, Moroccan, Palestinian, and Yemeni. Her work addresses the technological divide in speech processing, as the majority of world languages and dialects remain underrepresented.
