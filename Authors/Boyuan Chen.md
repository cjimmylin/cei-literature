# Boyuan Chen

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Boyuan Chen is an Assistant Professor in the Thomas Lord Department of Mechanical Engineering and Materials Science at Duke University, where he leads the General Robotics Lab. He earned his PhD from Columbia University under Hod Lipson. He is a primary investigator in the field of physical AI and "generalist" robotic systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Boyuan's research aims to build robots that can learn and act by perceiving and interacting with dynamic environments. He focuses on developing "Discovery Machines"—physical AI systems capable of continuously discovering fundamental variables and learning how the world operates. His work spans computer vision, deep learning, and robotic control.

### Significant Achievements
He has made significant contributions to the field of automated scientific discovery and humanoid robotics. His recent work includes a robot capable of learning facial lip movements from human videos and the "Automated discovery of fundamental variables" using deep learning. He is the recipient of multiple awards and his work has been featured in Science Daily and Scitech Daily.

## Major Publications

- [Automated discovery of fundamental variables from video data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-022-00281-6) (2022) - *A pioneering paper on AI for scientific discovery.*
