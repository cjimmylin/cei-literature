# Anton Korinek

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Anton Korinek is a Professor in the Department of Economics and the Darden School of Business at the University of Virginia. He is the Faculty Director of the Economics of Transformative AI (EconTAI) Initiative and a Visiting Scholar at the Brookings Institution. He serves as the Economics Lead at the Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI) and is a member of Anthropic's Economic Advisory Council. He was named to the 2025 TIME100 AI list.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anton’s research focuses on the economic implications of AI, particularly its impact on economic growth, labor markets, and global inequality. He investigates how transformative AI might lead to structural shifts in the economy and advises on the governance of these risks. He is a primary investigator into the "Economics of Transformative AI."

### Significant Achievements
He has been a key advisor to international bodies, including the G7 Panel of Experts on AI, and has testified before the U.S. Senate on AI's workforce impact. He co-edited "The Oxford Handbook of AI Governance" and is a leading figure in using generative AI for economic research. His work bridges the gap between technical AI progress and macroeconomic policy.
