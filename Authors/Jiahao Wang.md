# Jiahao Wang

## Research Summary

Researcher at Trustworthy Technology and Engineering (TTE) Laboratory, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. Proposed novel ethical reasoning paradigm for LLMs inspired by ethical decision-making models, enhancing diverse human value alignment through deliberative ethical reasoning. Framework includes structured five-step process: contextual fact gathering, hierarchical social norm identification, option generation, multiple-lens ethical impact analysis, and reflection. Published at AAAI/ACM Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society (October 2025).
