## Abstract

3B parameter LLM adapted for Egyptian and Moroccan communities, incorporating language, cultural heritage, and values. Uses controlled synthetic data generation and retrieval-augmented pre-training for Moroccan Darija and Egyptian Arabic. Outperforms existing Arabic LLMs on cultural and values alignment benchmarks (Palm benchmark scores: 5.72 EGY, 5.86 MOR).

## Data Description

NileChat is a 3B-parameter LLM built on Qwen-2.5-3B, fine-tuned for Egyptian and Moroccan Arabic dialects. The paper includes NileBench, a culturally grounded benchmark covering Egyptian and Moroccan Arabic with tasks spanning dialectal understanding, cultural knowledge, and code-switching. Multiple Arabic-focused LLMs are benchmarked against NileChat. Code, models, and benchmark are available on GitHub.
