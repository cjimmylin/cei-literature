## Abstract

First large-scale crowd-sourced Quranic recitation dataset. "In-the-wild" dataset with real-world noise from diverse reciters of different ages, genders, and ethnicities. Collected over 6 months. Schema enables standardized paired Quranic audio-text datasets for ASR and related tasks.

## Dataset Information

25,000 audio clips (67.39 hours), 1,200+ unique individuals


## Data Description

The first large-scale crowd-sourced Quranic recitation dataset collected over 6 months, featuring in-the-wild audio with real-world noise from diverse reciters of varying ages, genders, and ethnicities, enabling ASR and related tasks.
