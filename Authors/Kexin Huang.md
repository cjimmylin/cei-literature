# Kexin Huang

## Research Summary

Kexin Huang is a PhD student at Stanford Computer Science, advised by Prof. Jure Leskovec and affiliated with Stanford AI Lab. They have dual affiliations with Stanford University and Harvard Medical School's Department of Biomedical Informatics. Their research focuses on AI applications in biomedicine, particularly foundation models for drug discovery and biomedical research. They are also a co-founder at Phylo AI, a startup building AI-native workspace for biologists. Recent work includes developing foundation models for clinician-centered drug repurposing and biomedical AI agents.
