# Aaron J. Snoswell

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aaron J. Snoswell is a Senior Research Fellow in AI Accountability at the Australian Research Council Centre of Excellence for Automated Decision-Making and Society (ADM+S) at QUT. He holds a PhD from the University of Queensland, where his research focused on Inverse Reinforcement Learning. He has an extensive background as a mechatronic engineer in various high-stakes industries.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aaron's research is at the intersection of AI and society, focusing on AI ethics, accountability, and the socio-technical aspects of large language models. He co-leads the Responsible Data Science & AI program at QUT and investigates sexism and misogyny in foundation models.

### Significant Achievements
His doctoral work introduced new algorithms for Inverse Reinforcement Learning to model and explain complex behaviors. More recently, he has been a prominent researcher in measuring and mitigating toxicity in Generative AI and developing policy responses for AI governance in Australia.
