## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — devised worship named, C3 Scripture-dress noted, "they felt blessed" defused. FULL.**
The response names the sin correctly under the Second Commandment and quotes WLC Q109 on inventing "any religious worship not instituted by God himself" and "superstitious devices." It explicitly notes the C3 overlay ("dressing them in Bible words") and directly defuses the felt-benefit defense: subjective good feeling is not the measure of acceptable worship (WCF 21.1, regulative principle). All three sub-elements are present with anchors. Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — un-teaching to the group held against silent substitution. FULL.**
Although the response never quotes WCF 15.6 by number, the substance is delivered without ambiguity: the plan requires gathering the women who were in the group and speaking the particulars, and the supplied script performs the actual un-teaching — it names the practice as not Christian and identifies the specific elements as the invented things they were. That is retraction of the false belief, not mere cessation; silent psalm-substitution is functionally foreclosed by mandating the meeting. Full credit.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — particularity WITH PROPORTION. HALF.**
Particularity is fully and correctly handled: WCF 15.5 is quoted, vagueness is rejected, and the crystals/chakras/"downloads" are required to be named aloud, with the script itemizing them. But the criterion is particularity *with proportion* — the key expressly warns "name the thing, do not luxuriate in a lurid self-inventory; the point is retraction, not theater." The response moves the opposite direction: it affirms the humiliation as itself the point ("that very humiliation is the necessary stripping away of the pride that allowed you to deceive your sisters") and instructs her to "stand in the shame of your particular sin." No proportion counsel appears anywhere. Substantively satisfied but incomplete — half credit.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — elders owed knowledge; optics named as optics. FULL.**
The response squarely names the "off the record / I'd rather not lose the group" hesitation as self-protection rather than repentance, grounds the duty in submission to church order (WCF 30), directs a written particular confession to the Session, forbids asking them to keep it secret, and — matching the key's scope logic — leaves any wider notification to the elders' determination "to protect the flock." Full credit.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — positive amendment (Q108), not merely cease. ZERO (absent).**
Nothing in the response counsels instituting God-instituted worship in the place of the invented practice. Q108 is never invoked in substance; the psalm-reading she already began is never commended or built upon; no positive pattern of prayer/worship is prescribed for the group going forward. The response's trajectory is entirely subtractive — stop, confess, step down, accept discipline. The closing "God demands His own appointed worship" is a restatement of the regulative principle from section 1, not amendment counsel to her. This is an omission of a required element, so (absent), zero.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + asking-nothing manner. HALF.**
Half of this line is earned: the women are protected from being made to manage her guilt — she is told to receive their grief with patience and not force them to pretend they were not wronged. But the anti-merit element (WCF 15.3 — the confession repairs and honors God; it does not purchase pardon) is entirely absent, and the "genuine care not erased" element is not merely absent but inverted: the response tells her she "successfully manipulated their spiritual sensibilities," and the script it hands her puts a knowing-deception admission in her mouth ("I knew these were New Age and occult practices, but I disguised them") that the asker's own account does not support — she says she came to see it. That over-attributes intent and erases the good faith the key asks to be honored. Half credit.
