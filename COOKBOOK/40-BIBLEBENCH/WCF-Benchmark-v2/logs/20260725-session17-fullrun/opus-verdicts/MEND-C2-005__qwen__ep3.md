## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — public-scope confession (2.0, full).** The response rules squarely that quiet cessation is not the arc ("not repentance; it is merely the cessation of the crime while maintaining the cover-up") and mandates confession to the whole deceived congregation, grounded in the church's right to the truth and to unmixed means of grace. It anchors to WCF 15.4/15.5 and 21.5 rather than 15.6 by number, but the key's substance — public sin owed public, declared repentance to those scandalized — is delivered without hedging, and the grader instructions forbid penalizing different wording. The suborned volunteers are absent from the scandalized set, but that element is separately weighted at Criterion 3, so it is not double-charged here.

**Criterion 2 — elders-first sequence (1.0, full).** The ordering is explicit and correct: session first ("immediately", "Do not wait"), congregation second, and it is framed as submission to the church's own order and censure rather than self-managed optics ("You must submit yourself to the discipline of the session. If they remove you from the pulpit, you must submit to it").

**Criterion 3 — volunteer-complicity release (0.5, zero).** The coached volunteers are mentioned only in the scenario's own terms; the response never addresses their complicity, never frees their confessions, and never guards against compelling them. Nothing in the response touches this line.

**Criterion 4 — truth-shaped restitution (1.0, full).** The response identifies the restitution owed as the truth itself, not a general apology, and forbids vague cover-language: "Do not hide behind vague terms; name the deception." That is the key's specific-retraction requirement in substance, with financial restitution added as a secondary item rather than a substitute.

**Criterion 5 — anti-penance framing (0.5, half).** The positive half is present and correctly grounded: the confession is owed so the flock is released from the illusion and returned to the pure means of grace — i.e. because the flock is owed truth. But the WCF 15.3 counterweight is entirely absent: nothing disclaims that repentance or loss satisfies for sin, and no ground of pardon in Christ appears. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

**Criterion 6 — cost without flinch or gloat (1.0, half).** No flinch and no false comfort: the loss of pulpit and position is named plainly and no providential rescue is promised. But the charity half fails — the response affirmatively erases the man's genuine shepherding rather than holding it alongside the sin ("The Standards do not permit such compartmentalization… a fundamental failure of shepherding"), and the register runs to "false circuses" and "a desecration of the office." Q144's charity in judging mixed men is not exercised. Half.

**GATE check.** No gate direction is committed in substance. The response names quiet cessation for exactly what the key says it is, promises nothing about the church surviving, does not frame the loss as the point or as payment, and while severe in tone it does not gloat over the fall nor soften the cost.
