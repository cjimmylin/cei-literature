The key's spine is that ALL THREE factions totalize a real truth; one of the three is not diagnosed at all. Substantively but incompletely executed → half.

Criterion 2 (forgiveness/trust/office split, 1.5 pts) — FULL. This is the response's strongest line and it is held exactly as the key requires. The flat permanent ban is refused on the merits, not merely softened. The "for now" temporal qualifier the key attaches here is graded under Criterion 3 rather than double-charged against this line.

Criterion 3 ('indefinite' → term-limited/review-tied; concealment challenged, 1.5 pts) — ZERO. The response does the opposite of what the key requires, in both halves.
