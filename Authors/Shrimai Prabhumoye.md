# Shrimai Prabhumoye

## Research Summary

Shrimai Prabhumoye is a Senior Research Scientist in the Applied Deep Learning Research Group at NVIDIA and an Adjunct Professor at Boston University. She obtained her PhD from Carnegie Mellon University under the supervision of Prof. Alan W. Black and Prof. Ruslan Salakhutdinov. Her research focuses on natural language processing, particularly bias in NLP systems, text generation, and the application of deep learning to language technologies.
