# Stephanie Lin

## Research Summary

Stephanie Lin is a Research Scholar who has been affiliated with the University of Oxford (2021-2022) and is currently a Resident at OpenAI (2022-present). Her work has been highly influential with over 26,500 citations. She is known for developing the TruthfulQA benchmark, which measures how models mimic human falsehoods, contributing significantly to AI safety and truthfulness research.
