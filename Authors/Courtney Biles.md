# Courtney Biles

## Biographical Summary

Courtney Biles worked at Alphabet for 18 years—12 at Google and 6 at Google DeepMind—before recently leaving the company. At DeepMind, they contributed to foundational research on AI ethics and the risks posed by large language models, participating in interdisciplinary horizon-scanning workshops to identify and analyze potential AI harms.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Ethics Research**: Contributing to systematic analysis of ethical and social risks from language models.
- **Risk Taxonomy Development**: Participating in interdisciplinary efforts to structure the risk landscape of LLMs.
- **AI Governance**: Involved in research on participatory approaches to AI development.

### Significant Achievements
- **Language Model Risk Taxonomy**: Co-authored foundational papers analyzing risks from large-scale language models.
- **Ethics of AI Assistants**: Contributed to comprehensive treatment of ethical questions raised by advanced AI assistants.
- **Participatory AI Research**: Provided expertise on "Power to the People?" research on participatory AI approaches.
- **Model Policy**: Involved in hiring and developing policy research at DeepMind.

## Major Publications
- **Ethical and Social Risks of Harm from Language Models** (2021) - Foundational LLM risk taxonomy
- **Taxonomy of Risks posed by Language Models** (ACM FAccT 2022) - Structured risk framework
- **The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants** (2024) - First systematic treatment of AI assistant ethics
