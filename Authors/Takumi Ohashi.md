# Takumi Ohashi

## Research Summary

Takumi Ohashi is an NLP researcher associated with Tokyo Institute of Technology (Institute of Science Tokyo). His research includes work on Japanese commonsense morality datasets and NLP techniques. He has co-authored several papers with Professor Yuki Arase, including work on distinct label representations for few-shot text classification (ACL-IJCNLP 2021), tiny word embeddings using globally informed reconstruction (COLING 2020), and text classification with negative supervision (ACL 2020).
