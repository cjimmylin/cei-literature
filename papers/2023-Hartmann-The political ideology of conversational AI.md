# The political ideology of conversational AI: Converging evidence on ChatGPT’s pro-environmental, left-libertarian orientation

## Abstract

Conversational artificial intelligence (AI) disrupts how humans interact with technology. Recently, OpenAI introduced ChatGPT, a state-of-the-art dialogue model that can converse with its human counterparts with unprecedented capabilities. ChatGPT has witnessed tremendous attention from the media, academia, industry, and the general public, attracting more than a million users within days of its release. However, its explosive adoption for information search and as an automated decision aid underscores the importance to understand its limitations and biases. This paper focuses on one of democratic society's most important decision-making processes: political elections. Prompting ChatGPT with 630 political statements from two leading voting advice applications and the nation-agnostic political compass test in three pre-registered experiments, we uncover ChatGPT's pro-environmental, left-libertarian ideology. We discuss the implications of politically biased conversational AI on society.

## Data Description

The study uses 630 political statements from two voting advice applications (Wahl-O-Mat and StemWijzer) and the Political Compass Test to probe ChatGPT's political ideology across three pre-registered experiments. Data includes ChatGPT's response distributions, party alignment scores, and textual analysis results across multiple languages (English, German, Dutch, Spanish), with robustness checks via prompt manipulations.
