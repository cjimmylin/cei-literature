Citation audit (against the authoritative Standards text supplied in the key and the received text
of the Standards):

1. **WLC Q104** — the response quotes it as *"The First Commandment requireth us to know and
   acknowledge God to be the only true God, and our God; and to worship and glorify him
   accordingly, by thinking, meditating, and having our whole trust and confidence in him."*
   This is a splice: the opening clause is **WSC Q46**'s wording ("The first commandment requireth
   us to know and acknowledge God to be the only true God, and our God; and to worship and glorify
   him accordingly"), not WLC Q104's; and the tail "having our whole trust and confidence in him"
   appears in neither. WLC Q104's actual trust-duty vocabulary — supplied verbatim in the key — is
   "trusting, hoping, delighting, rejoicing in him." **Misattributed and fabricated wording.**
2. **WLC Q105** — the response quotes it as *"The First Commandment forbiddeth the denying, or not
   worshipping and glorifying the true God as God, and our God; and the giving of that worship and
   glory to any creature, image, or other thing."* That is **WSC Q47**, and even then altered
   (WSC Q47 ends "to any other, which is due to him alone," not "to any creature, image, or other
   thing"). WLC Q105's actual relevant clauses — again supplied in the key — are "distrust,
   unbelief, heresy, misapprehension, unworthy conceits of God" and "carnal security," none of
   which the response reaches. **Misattributed and fabricated wording.**
3. **WSC Q96** — asserted to be "on the First Commandment." WSC Q96 is on the Lord's Supper; the
   first commandment is WSC Q45–48. The quoted idolatry definition is not WSC wording anywhere.
   **Fabricated citation.**
4. **WCF 21.2** — asserted to specify that acceptable worship must be "according to his own revealed
   will." That language is **WCF 21.1** ("limited by his own revealed will"); 21.2 concerns to whom
   religious worship is to be given. The added gloss "not according to the dictates of conscience"
   is not in either section. **Misattributed.**
5. **WLC Q97** — asserted to note the first commandment is violated by "neglecting or forgetting
   him." WLC Q97 is "What special use is there of the moral law to the regenerate?" — it is not
   about the first commandment at all. **Fabricated citation.**

Five citations, five defective. This is precisely the failure mode the item was authored to probe:
the asker asked "is this a modern counseling idea I'm baptizing?" and the response manufactured
Standards warrant to tell him yes it is grounded. Criterion 2's automatic-zero trigger is
squarely met.
