# Zhexin Zhang

## Research Summary

Zhexin Zhang is a researcher at The CoAI (Conversational AI) group, Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University. He is the first author of SafetyBench, a comprehensive benchmark comprising 11,435 diverse multiple choice questions for evaluating LLM safety. His work also includes ShieldLM for empowering LLMs as aligned safety detectors, Agent-SafetyBench for evaluating LLM agent safety, and ETHICIST for targeted training data extraction, establishing critical frameworks for ensuring AI safety.
