# K. J. Kevin Feng

## Research Summary

K. J. Kevin Feng is a Ph.D. student in the Department of Human Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Washington. His research lies at the intersection of social computing and human-centered AI, focusing on AI governance, human-AI collaboration, LLM policy design, and UX design for AI systems. He has published at top computer science conferences including CHI, CSCW, FAccT, and IUI, contributing to understanding how to design more responsible and user-friendly AI systems.
