# I-21 — Agentic Commerce and Know-Your-Agent

**Tier 4** · **Gaps: G-19** · **Programs: watch-list** · **Kernel:** Fable 5, 2026-07-07 (FROZEN) · **Body:** Opus draft 2026-07-07

## The idea                        [KERNEL — FROZEN]

Payment and delegation rails for agents (AP2 mandates, x402, A2A) create real delegated financial authority, with "Know Your Agent" proposals and role-coherence failures (overspending, unreasonable transactions) already documented — principal-agent misalignment becoming economically consequential infrastructure.

## Key results

- **Delegated-authority rails** (SEARCH-CONFIRMED, standards/non-arXiv): AP2 (Google, Sep 2025) with agent "mandates"; x402 → Linux Foundation (Apr 2026); A2A agent-to-agent protocol — real delegated financial authority for agents.
- **Role-coherence failures + Know Your Agent** (⚠ 2604.26220 — ⚠ UNCONFIRMED): documented overspending / unreasonable-transaction failures in shopping agents; "Know Your Agent" proposals as the identity/authorization analogue of KYC.
- **Principal-agent framing of scheming** (⚠ 2601.23211 — ⚠ UNCONFIRMED): scheming formalized as a principal-agent problem — the theoretical bridge to delegated economic authority.

## Mechanisms & caveats

This is infrastructure-flavored (G-19: Severity M / Tractability L / CEI-fit L) — the right posture is a one-line watch, not a build. The values relevance is latent: mandate semantics (*what* an agent may commit its principal to) are normative documents in embryo, and role-coherence failures are naturalistic revealed-preference data about agent behavior under *real* incentives — but neither is stable enough to instrument yet. Both substantive citations are ⚠ UNCONFIRMED same-month IDs; the rails themselves are documented via standards bodies, not fetchable papers.

## Implications for CEI practice   [KERNEL — FROZEN]

(a) Watch-list (G-19): engage when a values-relevant standards question surfaces — e.g., what an agent may consent to on a principal's behalf. (b) Mandate semantics are normative documents in embryo; CEI's spec-analysis skill applies when they stabilize. (c) Role-coherence failures are revealed-preference data about agent values under real incentives — a future naturalistic data source. (d) No build; one-line tracking in field-map front F reviews.
