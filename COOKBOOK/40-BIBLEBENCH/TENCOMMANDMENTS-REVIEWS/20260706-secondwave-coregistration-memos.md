## 1. LOOPHOLE (v3-06) × FRAMESHIFT (COOKBOOK 02)

**Boundary.** FRAMESHIFT manipulates the **interpretive framework on the supply side**: a named ethical sub-variant is legitimately *instructed*, and the score is method fidelity (FC/PR) and switching (SA) — gold is ethicist-validated derivations. LOOPHOLE holds every framework and norm **fixed** and manipulates the **demand side**: only the beneficiary of a permissive reading flips, and the score is within-model reading invariance (EL/SD/AS/CR) — gold-light by design.

**Routing rule.** (1) Any item that names or instructs an ethical framework, or scores fidelity to a reasoning method → FRAMESHIFT. (2) Any item whose twins differ only in beneficiary/stake configuration with a scope-reading extracted → LOOPHOLE. (3) LOOPHOLE items must never mention frameworks; FRAMESHIFT items must never vary who profits between matched conditions (its divergence-engineering varies *scenario features*, never the payoff to the asker).

**The intersection is parked, by name.** "Motivated frame-shopping" — does a model *select* the convenient framework when stakes flip? — is a real, interesting construct that needs both instruments' machinery. It belongs to NEITHER at P1. It is pre-registered here as a joint follow-up study (LOOPHOLE twin design × FRAMESHIFT sub-variant gold), buildable only after both P1s pass their gates.

**Overlap analysis (locked).** Cross-tabulate SA (switching agility) × EL (elasticity) per model, descriptive: the pre-named risk profile is high-SA + high-EL (fluent method-switching combined with payoff-tracking readings). Both directions reportable; no causal language.

## 2. TURNCOAT (v2-01) × FRAMESHIFT (COOKBOOK 02)

**Boundary.** The discriminating variable is **authority and its legitimacy**. FRAMESHIFT's frame changes are uncontested, legitimate task instructions (the operator asks for Kantian reasoning; fidelity is scored). TURNCOAT's frame changes are **contested authority claims** crossed with legitimacy (capture vs correction-acceptance is scored, behaviorally, from terminal actions).

**Routing rule.** (1) If the legitimacy of the frame-change request is itself measured, or a rival persona / unauthenticated credential / persuasion dose ladder appears → TURNCOAT. (2) If legitimacy is unquestioned and method fidelity is scored → FRAMESHIFT. (3) FRAMESHIFT's single persuasion probe (one counter-argument turn on a derived framework-conditional answer) stays single-turn and framework-internal — it may not grow dose ladders, credentials, or personas (those are TURNCOAT CR machinery). (4) TURNCOAT items never score the *quality* of reasoning within the rival frame (no PR-style rubrics) — capture is coded from behavior only.

**Overlap analysis (locked).** Two descriptive panels after both P1s: (i) SA vs CA — a model agile at instructed switching should also accept authenticated legitimate updates; a high-SA/low-CA profile (switches for anyone who asks nicely, resists the authenticated operator) is the pre-named anomaly; (ii) FRAMESHIFT persuasion-probe capitulation rate vs TURNCOAT CR at the lowest dose tier, per model.

## 3. PRISM (v3-03) × VERNACULAR (v3-07)

**Boundary (as both briefs already state — formalized here).** PRISM perturbs surface renderings **within** a cultural frame (7 axes, semantic invariance certified; flip-rate/DIF logic). VERNACULAR transports the norm **across** instantiation frames (canonical→near→far→secular-code distance; comprehension-ceiling-gated transfer gaps).

**The two declared overlap zones, with decision rules:**
- **Register.** Shared register cells exist BY DESIGN to test the boundary. Build ownership: **PRISM builds the shared register cells** (it is earlier in TC-9's second wave; VERNACULAR is deferred behind its consultant pipeline) — ≥20 shared cells, spanning ≥4 register values on ≥5 commandments; VERNACULAR imports them unmodified. Decision rule (from VERNACULAR A2/RG, confirmed): if RG↔PRISM-register correlation r ≥ 0.95 on shared cells, VERNACULAR **cedes the register axis to PRISM and drops RG**; below 0.95, both keep their axes and the shared-cell delta is reported in both papers.
- **Language.** PRISM's language axis = the same situation *translated*, setting fixed (invariance question). VERNACULAR's language crossing = language × cultural-distance *manipulated separately* (attribution question). Routing: pure translation with the institutional setting unchanged → PRISM; any change of institutional/cultural setting → VERNACULAR. A translation that unavoidably shifts the setting is a VERNACULAR cell, never a PRISM cell (PRISM's invariance certification would fail it anyway).

**Overlap analysis (locked).** Besides the r≥0.95 cession test: regress (descriptively, within model) VERNACULAR's far-tier transfer gap on PRISM's per-commandment brittleness index — if BI fully predicts TG, VERNACULAR's distance construct adds nothing beyond surface brittleness (pre-registered honest-reframe: VERNACULAR reports it and re-scopes to the secular-code and NI panels).

## 4. TACIT (v3-04) × DISCORD (v3-08)

An item varying both belongs to **neither** at P1.

**Seed ownership.** Per the adjudication (TACIT "builds only after PRISM and DISCORD stabilize shared seeds") and TC-9 order: **DISCORD owns the shared seed registry.** DISCORD's J-mode realizations are the reference explicit-arm cells; TACIT inherits DISCORD-stabilized seeds unmodified for its explicit arm and derives its implicit-arm realizations from them (disjoint surfaces, same certified situation + right answer). TACIT may not author new seeds for commandments where DISCORD-stabilized seeds exist.

**The intersection is parked, by name.** The budget×mode interaction — X-mode (actor) adherence under budget compression, deployment's realistic worst case — is pre-registered here as a joint follow-up cell family, buildable only after both P1s pass. Neither design may smuggle it in at P1.
