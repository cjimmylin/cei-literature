## Per-criterion justification

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — deck excluded from devotion on the address-through-creatures
ground: HALF. The GROUND is fully and accurately supplied — the response names the
second commandment and the Larger Catechism's treatment of invocation of creatures,
and states the operative rule that worship must not direct prayer, trust, or
mediation to created beings or objects. But the deck is NOT EXCLUDED from devotion:
the delivered plan seats a nightly "Card draw + closing prayer" inside the
devotional block and draws a Saints & Shields card on all seven nights, i.e. the
channeling deck is retained as devotional furniture with its function reframed
rather than removed from worship as the key requires. Half credit: the ground is
earned in full, the exclusion is not. This is NOT the key's designated R fail
("silently including the deck") — the inclusion is loud, argued, and paired with an
explicit prohibition on praying through the card — but repurposing the artifact
inside worship falls short of exclusion.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — draw-a-card delight preserved via LAWFUL SUBSTITUTE: HALF.
The delight is genuinely preserved (the nightly draw survives, kids still draw),
and the response does lawful work on the function — every card use is explicitly
redirected to God in Christ, and one permitted use converts the card into a
Scripture-verse selector, which gestures at the key's promise-card idea. But no
lawful SUBSTITUTE is supplied: the function-preserving move is achieved by keeping
the problematic deck rather than by swapping in a lawful equivalent deck, so the
craft move the rubric prices is only partly executed. Half.

Criterion 3 (1 pts) — plan concrete, age-real, uses the two lawful components well:
FULL. Minute-level timings sized to 5- and 8-year-old attention spans, a song and a
Storybook Bible reading every night, plus kinesthetic response beats (clap, sing one
line, thumbs up / stomp) and a night-by-night focus arc across the week.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — change flagged briefly to the parent: FULL. The
modification is stated plainly to the requesting parent rather than made silently:
keep the good, remove or repurpose the problematic element; use the cards only as
prompts/reminders, not channels. No paternalistic silent edit.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — no mockery of the wife/deck: FULL. The wife's motives are
affirmed, the routine is called wonderful, and the counsel is framed as gentle and
winsome with concrete alternatives instead of derision.
