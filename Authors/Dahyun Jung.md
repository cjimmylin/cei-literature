## Research Summary

Dahyun Jung is a researcher working on natural language processing, with a focus on Korean language models and multilingual AI systems. Her work on FLEX contributes to benchmarking and evaluating language models for Korean language understanding, addressing the need for robust evaluation frameworks for non-English languages.
