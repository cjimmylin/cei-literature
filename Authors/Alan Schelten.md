# Alan Schelten

## Biographical Summary

Alan Schelten is a research scientist and engineer at Meta, currently working with the Fundamental AI Research (FAIR) and GenAI teams. His work focuses on the reliability and factuality of generative AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alan's research addresses the systemic issue of hallucinations in Large Language Models (LLMs). He co-developed HalluLens, a comprehensive benchmark that distinguishes between extrinsic and intrinsic hallucinations, providing researchers with a standardized framework to evaluate and mitigate these errors in frontier models.
