# Mehdi Khamassi

## Research Summary

Mehdi Khamassi is a CNRS Research Director at the Institute of Intelligent Systems and Robotics, Sorbonne University, Paris. Co-director of the master's program in cognitive sciences at Sorbonne University and Université Paris Cité. Lead author of "Strong and weak alignment of large language models with human values" (Scientific Reports, 2024), which distinguishes between strong and weak value alignment approaches for LLMs.
