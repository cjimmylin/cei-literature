# Guanqun Bi

## Biographical Summary

Guanqun Bi is a researcher affiliated with Tsinghua University (CoAI Group, DCST, IAI, BNRIST) and the Institute of Information Engineering at the Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS). His research interests include Social Agents and Natural Language Generation.

## Research Focus

Bi's research focuses on understanding and evaluating social cognition in AI systems, particularly Theory of Mind capabilities in large language models. He also works on character-based AI systems and mental health applications.

## Notable Contributions

Bi is a co-author on the ToMBench paper, a significant benchmark for evaluating Theory of Mind in Large Language Models. Published at ACL 2024, ToMBench introduces a systematic evaluation framework encompassing 8 tasks and 31 abilities in social cognition, a multiple-choice question format to support automated and unbiased evaluation, and a build-from-scratch bilingual inventory to strictly avoid data leakage.

ToMBench is a systematic, automated, and original bilingual Theory of Mind (ToM) benchmark for LLMs, containing 2,860 testing samples involving diverse real-world social scenarios. The research found that even advanced LLMs like GPT-4 lag behind human performance by over 10% points.

## Other Research Contributions

- **CharacterBench**: Described as "the largest bilingual generative benchmark, with 22,859 human-annotated samples covering 3,956 characters from 25 detailed character categories."
- **Mental Health Counseling AI**: Research evaluating large language models for mental health counseling support, addressing static assessment limitations, single perspective issues, and open-loop framework challenges.
