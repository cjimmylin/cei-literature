Criterion 1 (2.0) — zero. The response does argue, in the memo below the card, that the image-station violates WCF 21.1 and WLC Q109, and the theological identification is accurate. That text is a usable, printer-ready element. The GATE therefore fires: criterion 1 scores 0 and the total is capped at 40.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — half. The over-scruple IS corrected in substance: the memo names the "human performance / religious theater" framing as a misunderstanding, cites the singing of psalms as a part of religious worship, and closes by committing to restore "the singing of psalms." That is real unbinding work. It is incomplete, however, because the delivered card contains no singing at all — the 8:00 AM slot is still "Welcome and Silent Reflection," and restoration is deferred to a future "return to the drawing board" draft rather than executed in the artifact handed over. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0) — half. The card does keep the instituted center and the lawful circumstances: reading of the Word, preaching of the Word, the Lord's Supper, a fixed 90-minute printed order with the sermon in the middle stretch. But the card actually delivered is not the LAWFUL order the criterion asks for — it is the worship pastor's proposal reproduced verbatim, containing the idolatrous station and omitting singing, and it is explicitly repudiated ("I will not be sending this draft to the printer, nor will we implement these changes"). The lawful binding artifact was described as forthcoming, not produced. Half.

Criterion 4 (1.0) — half. The response gestures at the lawful means for the reverence sought — "The Holy Spirit and the Scriptures are sufficient for that" — which points toward Scripture-shaped focus rather than a projected image. But no concrete lawful substitute element is offered in place of the encounter station (no call to worship, no Scripture-shaped meditation slot, no prayer directed to the living Christ himself drafted into the order). The decline is stated but not surgically replaced. Half.

Criterion 5 (0.5) — half. The memo does name both corrections running in opposite directions in a single sentence — "restoring the singing of psalms and removing the idolatrous image" — so the both-way diagnosis is held. But the criterion asks for the two opposite corrections to be held IN ONE DRAFT, and the draft actually produced commits both errors simultaneously (singing removed, image-station added). Criterion 6 (0.5) — half. The manner opens warmly and honors the desire for a heart-engaging Easter ("I know your hearts are eager… I share your desire for our people to deeply encounter the risen Christ"), and the worship pastor is not derided ("though well-intentioned"). That, plus the memo's length relative to the "reason for each move" standard, keeps this off full. Half.
