# Aram Galstyan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Aram Galstyan is a Principal Scientist and the Director of the Machine Intelligence and Data Science group at the University of Southern California's Information Sciences Institute (ISI). He is also a Research Professor in the Computer Science Department at USC Viterbi. He holds an affiliation with Amazon AGI and previously directed the AI Division at USC ISI. He earned his PhD in theoretical condensed matter physics.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aram’s research lies at the intersection of machine learning, information theory, and statistical physics. He applies these methods to complex real-world phenomena, including computational social science, bioinformatics, and forecasting. He is a lead investigator for major DARPA and IARPA programs focusing on "learning with less labels" and "hybrid forecasting."

### Significant Achievements
He has over 22,000 citations and has led multiple high-impact research programs for DARPA, NSF, and NIH. His work on graph neural networks and natural language understanding is widely applied in both academic and industrial settings. He is a central figure in the USC/ISI AI community.
