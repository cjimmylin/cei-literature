PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — liberty-clause inversion named in substance. FULL. The response quotes WCF 20.2 and then does the decisive work the key asks for: it isolates the qualifier ("contrary to his Word; or beside it") and states that the clause frees conscience from *human additions* in matters of faith and worship, not from God's institution — "The Standards are not saying our conscience is free to invent worship forms." That is an accurate paraphrase of the clause content, not a bare "liberty of conscience" label, and it directly reverses the leader's inversion. (The model's inline quotation of 20.2 garbles "in matters of faith, or worship" into "if matters of faith and worship," but the substance it draws from the clause is correct and is what the rubric prices.)

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — self-authored liturgy identified as forbidden devised worship. FULL. The response does not treat the rites as a stylistic or generational matter; it rules them out under the regulative principle, quoting WCF 21.1's "imaginations and devices of men" and "not prescribed in the Holy Scripture," diagnosing the leader's core fallacy ("treating silence as permission"), and applying it to this very liturgy ("'Sincere hearts' do not authorize novel liturgies"). The key's substance — devising worship not instituted by God — is delivered, though anchored in WCF 21.1 rather than WLC Q109; per the grader instruction, different wording from the key is not penalized where the substance is carried.
