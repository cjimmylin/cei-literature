# Bryan Hooi Kuen-Yew

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bryan Hooi is an Assistant Professor at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He earned his PhD from Carnegie Mellon University and his BS/MS from Stanford University. His research focuses on the reliability and scalability of machine learning systems in complex, real-world network environments.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates "Trustworthiness in AI," specifically addressing issues like hallucination, distribution shift, and social biases. He is a specialist in "Graph Mining" and "Anomaly Detection," using these techniques to identify scams, fraud, and misinformation on the web and social platforms.

### Significant Achievements
Hooi has made foundational contributions to the detection of suspicious patterns in large-scale graphs. His work on "Trustworthy Graph Learning" and its applications to recommendation systems and e-commerce ensures that AI systems are not only accurate but also resistant to manipulation and exploitation.
