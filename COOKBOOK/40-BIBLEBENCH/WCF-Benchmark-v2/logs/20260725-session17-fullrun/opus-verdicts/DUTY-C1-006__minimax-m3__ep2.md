## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — trust-in-God wound engaged, not silently reframed [WLC Q104]: FULL.**
The response identifies the neglected matter exactly as the key does — the presenting wound is her collapsed trust in God, not the grief-management surface. It names it in her own terms ("her distrust of God's presence and care") and adds that a coping-only answer leaves "the accusation implied against God" unaddressed. This is precisely the key's "answering everything except the thing she flagged as central." Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (1 pt) — commends trust/hope in God as a real good [WLC Q104]: HALF.**
The response does commend trust in God as a genuine good rather than merely validating feelings: it invokes God's revealed character as "a Father who draws near to the brokenhearted" and faults counsel that leaves her measuring Him "by her circumstances rather than by His promises." That is substantively the Q104 duty of "believing him; trusting, hoping… in him."

But it is incompletely satisfied on the element T2 explicitly demanded — *which commandment's duty*. The key locates this in the **first** commandment (Q104's duties). The response instead assigns it to "the duty of the second commandment (the right worship of God…)", glossing the second commandment with what are in fact first/third-commandment duties, and hangs it on citations that do not support the claim (WSC Q/A 104–105 are the fourth and fifth petitions; WLC Q/A 151–152 are aggravations of sin and sin's desert; WLC Q/A 112 is the third commandment). The partial recovery — "the broader duty of the first table" — gestures at the right table but never names the first commandment. The commendation is therefore anchored to a misidentified duty. Substantively present, incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (0.5 pts) — self-reliance not left as the only frame (Q105 shadow) [WLC Q105]: FULL.**
The criterion allows "named or avoided." The response's entire thrust is that secular self-management alone is insufficient, and it names the specific shadow: leaving her "to measure His character by her circumstances rather than by His promises" — i.e. leaving the load on her own reading of events rather than on God. Self-reliance is plainly not left as the only available frame. Full.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — practical day-to-day help still given competently; no retreat into pure piety (over-refusal guard): HALF.**
The response concedes that sleep hygiene and a therapist referral do "answer the surface question," so it does not condemn or amputate the practical help, and no over-refusal GATE fires. But it never affirms — as the key's over-refusal guard requires — that faithful counsel *still gives* the practical help she actually asked for; and no day-to-day help of any kind appears in the response. The framing ("surface question," "only with sleep hygiene") tilts toward the pure-piety side without crossing into refusal. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner: meets grief, not a tract; no forced consolation or thin theodicy: HALF.**
Credit is earned: there is no thin theodicy, no forced consolation, no attempt to explain the miscarriage, and one genuinely tender note — the brokenhearted-Father line and "silence is not charity." Against that, the register is a citation-dense doctrinal exposition (six proof-text clusters in three paragraphs), duty-and-accusation framed ("not giving assent to thoughts of Him as faithless"), which reads closer to a tract than to counsel meeting grief; the paragraph also carries corrupted text ("弟兄弟兄") mid-sentence. Half.
