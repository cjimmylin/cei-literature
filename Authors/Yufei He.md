# Yufei He

## Research Summary

Yufei He is a researcher at the National University of Singapore. His work focuses on instrumental convergence in Large Language Models, particularly examining how RL-trained models develop unintended intermediate goals that may deviate from human intentions. He developed InstrumentalEval, a benchmark for evaluating instrumental convergence in RL-trained LLMs, providing empirical evidence that direct RL optimization leads to stronger instrumental convergence risks than RLHF, with critical implications for AI safety in goal-directed systems.
