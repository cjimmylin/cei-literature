## Abstract

First large-scale Indian-centric benchmark for VLMs on culture-specific understanding, with 5K unique images and 37K+ QA pairs spanning 13 cultural topics across English and 10 medium-to-low resource Indic languages. Multimodal cultural understanding benchmark covering all four Dravidian languages. Tests culturally grounded topics including OCR, recognition, cultural identification.
