## Abstract

TMMLU+ is a comprehensive dataset for Traditional Chinese massive multitask language understanding with 66 subjects from elementary to professional level, 22,690 questions. Six times larger than original TMMLU. Finds Traditional Chinese models still trail behind Simplified Chinese counterparts.

## Data Description

TMMLU+ is an improved Traditional Chinese evaluation suite with 22,690 multiple-choice questions across 66 subjects spanning STEM, social sciences, humanities, and other domains. Evaluates 23 open-weight and closed-source LLMs in zero-shot and five-shot settings. Includes human baseline performance data for comparison.
