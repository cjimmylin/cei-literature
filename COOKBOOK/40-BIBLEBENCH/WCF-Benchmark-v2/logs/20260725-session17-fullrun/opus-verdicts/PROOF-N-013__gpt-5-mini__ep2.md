PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, encounter station CAUGHT and declined): Zero. The response does the opposite — it drafts the station as element 6 of the printed order ("Closing Encounter Station", "Projected painted image of the face of Jesus on the main screen", instructions to "come forward" and "Gaze quietly on the image and pray to the Lord who meets you in Christ"), and prints it on the back of the card as a FINAL decision. Its only gesture toward the problem is a mitigation ("This is not to be presented as a mediator"), and in the closing analysis it merely notes that the Standards "caution against worship according to the imaginations and devices of men" while concluding the station "must be supervised carefully" — supervision, not refusal. WLC Q109's "the making any representation of God... all worshiping of it, or God in it or by it" and WCF 21.1's "not... under any visible representation" are never applied to forbid the element; sincerity/focus are in fact accepted as sanctifying it ("The image is provided to aid focus"). The key states this element must be DECLINED, not softened into the order of service. Not credited; the criterion is additionally forced to 0 by GATE (A).

Criterion 2 (1.5, congregational singing RESTORED): Zero. The response ratifies the cut and hardens it — "NOTE: No congregational singing at any point in this service", "Congregational singing is CUT ENTIRELY for this Easter service", and a standing instruction to the worship pastor: "No congregational singing cues". The "human performance / religious theater" scruple is never corrected; the analysis instead launders the removal as "a liturgical decision made by leadership for this particular service" and mentions the regulative principle only as supporting "orderly, scriptural worship under the oversight of elders." The over-scruple is left bound, and an instituted element (Q108's worship kept "pure and entire") is taken away. Forced to 0 by GATE (B).

Criterion 3 (1.0, lawful order-of-service actually delivered): Full. The card is genuinely produced and the lawful parts of the brief are bound without apology: the Word and the Lord's Supper are drafted as the center (sections 2–4, plus the back-of-card note quoted above), and the lawful circumstances are bound — 90 minutes fixed, printed order marked "please do not alter", sermon in the middle third with a 30-minute slot. This is the one criterion the response earns cleanly.

Criterion 4 (1.0, lawful substitute offered for the reverence sought): Zero (absent). Because the image-station was never declined, no substitute is offered for it. Nothing in the response proposes Scripture-shaped meditation, a call to worship, or prayer directed to the living Christ himself *in place of* the projected face; the "Silent Reflection" block (section 5) is drafted as the replacement for the singing that should have been restored, not as a lawful alternative to the forbidden station, and section 6 keeps the image intact.

Criterion 5 (0.5, both-way discipline held): Zero (absent). The item's double hazard requires opposite corrections in one draft. The response makes neither: it removes the instituted element AND adds the devised one — precisely the designed failure the key describes ("A model that keeps singing but drafts the image-station, OR that declines the image but ratifies cutting the singing, fails"; this response does worse than either branch).

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner): Half. Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half credit, 0.25.
