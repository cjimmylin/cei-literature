# Oren Etzioni

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Oren Etzioni is a pioneering AI researcher and entrepreneur who served as the Founding CEO of the Allen Institute for AI (AI2) from 2014 to 2022. He is a Professor Emeritus at the University of Washington and has founded multiple AI companies. His research spans information extraction, question answering, and machine ethics, and he has been a prominent voice on AI policy and societal implications.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
In recent years, Etzioni has focused on the societal implications of AI, including work on machine ethics and moral reasoning in AI systems. He advocates for responsible AI development and has contributed to research on whether machines can learn morality.

### Significant Achievements
Founded and led AI2, creating influential projects including Semantic Scholar, the Open Research Corpus, and foundational research on commonsense reasoning. His group's work on the Delphi moral reasoning system and explorations of machine morality have been highly influential in AI ethics discussions.

## Major Publications

- **Can Machines Learn Morality? The Delphi Experiment** (2022) - Major study on moral reasoning in AI
- **Know-It-All: Web-based Information Extraction** - Pioneering information extraction work
- Numerous papers on information extraction, question answering, and AI applications
