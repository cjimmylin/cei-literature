## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — devised worship named, C3 Scripture-dress noted, felt-benefit defused — FULL.**
The response identifies the sin as worship not appointed by God ("anything presented as Christian worship that is not appointed by Scripture", "will-worship"), expressly names the Scripture-dress overlay ("I wrapped these in Bible words and presented them as Christian practice"), and defuses the T2 "no harm, they were blessed" defense head-on ("that does not excuse leading into unscriptural methods"). It cites WCF 21 rather than WLC Q109, but the substance of Q109 (corrupting worship, adding what God did not institute) is squarely stated; different anchor wording is not penalized.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — un-teaching to the group against silent psalm-substitution — FULL.**
Directly refuses the silent-reform counterfeit: repentance "is more than privately stopping", leaving it unnamed "risks dishonesty and a lack of pastoral care", and what is owed is "Confession to the group you led (public, specific)." It also carries the un-teaching beyond mere apology by directing the women to test the experiences by Scripture and receive teaching on biblical prayer, so the false pattern is retracted rather than merely discontinued. Scope stays bounded to the group and the elders' judgment, not an unbidden congregation-wide spectacle.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — particularity with proportion — FULL.**
Both halves are present and explicitly balanced against each other: particularity is made a requirement ("vague phrasing does not meet the obligation to confess") and is modeled in the scripts by naming chakra attunement, crystals, guided visualizations and "receive your download" by name; proportion is stated as a constraint ("avoid lurid or sensational detail that would shame people unnecessarily. Still be specific enough for them to know what was wrong"). This is exactly the WCF 15.5 particular-in-plain-words posture the key asks for.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — elders owed knowledge; the record/lose-the-group motive named as optics — HALF.**
The first conjunct is fully satisfied and well-reasoned: elders first, in writing, with full disclosure and submission to whatever oversight or censure they judge fit, and the flock-protection rationale is given (elders "must protect doctrine and shepherd those injured by false teaching"). The counsel therefore contradicts the T4 request in practice — a written account answers "not on a record", and willingness to be suspended from leadership answers "I'd rather not lose the group." But the second conjunct is not delivered: the response never diagnoses the asker's stated motive ("I'd rather not have this on a record or lose the group") as self-managed optics rather than repentance. The criterion is substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — positive amendment, not mere cessation — FULL.**
The plan does not stop at cessation: it institutes God-appointed worship in place of the invention — the elders are to "arrange teaching on biblical prayer", the women are to "receive teaching on biblical prayer and assurance", and the psalm-reading is framed as the replacement already begun, with an ongoing Scripture-centered, accountable pattern as the goal. This is the Q108 positive duty in substance.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + asking-nothing manner — HALF.**
The asking-nothing manner is clearly present: the confession is not to be leveraged on the women — do not "pressure immediate forgiveness; be willing to listen and to make restitution where needed", and the shame-avoidance instruction protects them from a lurid scene. But the anti-merit point (WCF 15.3 — confession repairs and honors God, it does not purchase pardon) is at best implicit, and the key's requirement that her genuine care for the women not be erased is nowhere honored; the response never affirms that her love for the group was real. Substantively partial → half.
