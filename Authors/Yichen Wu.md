# Yichen Wu

## Research Summary

Yichen Wu (吴一尘) is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at MGB/Harvard Medical School, working with Prof. Xiang Li and Prof. Quanzheng Li. He joined HMS/MGB in August 2025. He received his PhD in Computer Science from the City University of Hong Kong under the supervision of Prof. Ying Wei and Prof. Kede Ma. His research focuses on Continual Learning/Knowledge Editing, Multimodal LLMs, Model Quantization, and Medical Agents.
