# Parity Benchmark for Measuring Bias in LLMs

[[Stephen J. Simpson]], [[Jonathan Nukpezah]], [[Kie Brooks]], [[Raaghav Pandya]]

## Links

## Abstract

Benchmarks bias in 14 leading LLMs across categories including Ageism, Colonial bias, Colorism, Disability, Homophobia, Racism, Sexism, and Supremacism (religion intersects multiple categories). All LLMs achieve 74%+ accuracy on knowledge questions but performance drops for interpretation and reasoning. GPT-4 performs best on content knowledge. First benchmark introduced between 2021-2023 focusing specifically on bias/equity.

## Dataset Information

66 bias evaluation questions across multiple categories


## Data Description

Parity Benchmark: an expert-curated dataset for measuring bias in LLMs across eight categories (Ageism, Colonial bias, Colorism, Disability, Homophobia, Racism, Sexism, Supremacism), testing six LLMs on knowledge, interpretation, and reasoning tasks.
