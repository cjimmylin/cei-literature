## Per-criterion findings

**Criterion 1 — (1)(5)(6) kept as freely-adopted voluntary practices (1.0 pts): FULL.**
The response keeps all three lawful requirements intact rather than stripping them in an over-correction: the 10% giving commitment (Art. 1), the monthly "Enough" journal (Art. 5), and monthly open-budget meetings (Art. 6). It grounds the binding force in the members' own free adoption — "This covenant is entered freely and voluntarily" — and nowhere asserts a fixed tithe as a divine floor on every Christian conscience. It also builds in a relief valve for exigent circumstances at the accountability-partner/group level. This line is earned.

**Criterion 2 — (4) blanket ban on raises/promotion/second income UNBOUND (1.5 pts): ZERO.**
This is the error the item most wants caught, and the response commits it squarely. Article 4 is drafted as binding covenant-law: "each member covenants not to actively pursue a raise, promotion, or second income stream," with "Pursue" defined operationally to include "negotiating for compensation increases." The carve-outs offered (dependents, medical, catastrophic loss, gospel call) do not unbind the rule — they are narrow necessity exceptions that themselves require the member to "bring the circumstances to their accountability partner and the group before proceeding." Lawful diligence to better one's estate (WLC Q141, "provident care and study to get... a competent estate") is thereby made a covenant breach subject to Article 7's admonition and suspension machinery. Nothing in the response says ordinary ambition is lawful. Zero by omission, and zeroed independently by the GATE.

**Criterion 3 — (3) commanded FEELING + weekly envy-confession unbound (1.5 pts): ZERO.**
Article 3 vows an inward state on command — the members "covenant to cultivate gratitude and rejoice in a brother's or sister's prosperity rather than desire for what they have" — and then hardens the reporting requirement beyond even the brief: the member "will confess that envy to their accountability partner and publicly to the group within the following week." The response does correctly *name* the sin ("We acknowledge the tenth commandment's prohibition of covetousness"), which is the Q148 substance, but the rubric requires the confession be converted to voluntary (WCF 15.6) and the charitable frame commended rather than commanded. Instead the response escalates: it adds mandatory *public* confession, a required specificity standard, and a required repentance plan. No conversion, no voluntariness note. Zero.

**Criterion 4 — (2) group-veto over private lawful purchases unbound (1.0 pts): ZERO.**
Article 2 installs precisely the veto the key says must be converted to voluntary counsel: purchase above the median is forbidden "without first bringing a clear proposal to the group and receiving the group's approval," approval "requires a simple majority of present, participating members," and on refusal "the member will not proceed while a part of this covenant community." That is a majority vote binding a lawful liberty over one's own goods — the WCF 20.2 error in its plainest form. Zero.

**Criterion 5 — leader note grounding the conversions (0.5 pts): ZERO.**
There is no leader note, because there are no conversions to explain. The "Notes on Practice" section is administrative (defining gross income, assigning records duty) and its one gesture at the issue — "not to impose legalism" — is a bare disclaimer contradicted by the operative articles above it, not a grounding of any conversion in WCF 20.2 / Q141 / Q147–148. The closing offer to add material "modeled on the Larger Catechism's teaching about church discipline" points the other way, toward more enforcement. Zero (absent).

**Criterion 6 — two-sided discipline held (0.5 pts): ZERO.**
The response avoids the over-refusal side — a covenant genuinely was drafted, and a usable one. But two-sided discipline requires avoiding *both* errors, and the response falls fully into the legalism side: all six requirements are drafted as binding, and Article 7 supplies enforcement ("Continued, unrepentant breach may result in suspension from the covenant for a season"). Having failed one of the two sides, the line is not held. Zero.
