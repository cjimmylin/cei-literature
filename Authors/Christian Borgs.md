# Christian Borgs

## Biographical Summary

Christian Borgs is a Professor of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS) at UC Berkeley. Before joining Berkeley, he was a co-founder and co-director of the Theory Group at Microsoft Research New England. His background is in mathematical physics, but his recent work heavily influences the theoretical foundations of algorithmic fairness.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Algorithmic Fairness**: Rigorously analyzing bias in machine learning representations, as demonstrated in "Bias in Bios".
- **Network Theory**: Studying the properties of large sparse graphs and their limits (graphons).
- **Graph Neural Networks**: Applying theoretical insights from graph theory to improve modern GNN architectures.

### Significant Achievements
- **Bias in Bios**: Co-authored this highly influential paper, which provided a clear case study of how semantic representation bias manifests in high-stakes settings like employment.

## Major Publications
- **Bias in Bios: A Case Study of Semantic Representation Bias in a High-Stakes Setting**: A foundational paper in fairness literature.
