# Jianfei Cai

## Research Summary

Jianfei Cai is a Professor at the Faculty of IT, Monash University, where he previously served as inaugural Head of the Data Science & AI Department. Before Monash, he was a full professor at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). He is an IEEE Fellow (2021) for contributions to multimedia transmission and content analysis, with over 300 publications. His research spans computer vision, deep learning, vision-language understanding, and 3D vision.

## Key Contributions

- IEEE Fellow for contributions to multimedia transmission and content analysis
- Research.com Computer Science in Australia Leader Award (2023, 2024, 2025)
- Editorial board member of TPAMI and IJCV
- Work on 3D Gaussian Splatting and panoramic video generation
