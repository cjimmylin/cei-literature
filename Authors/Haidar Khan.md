# Haidar Khan

## Research Summary

Haidar Khan's research focuses on building efficient (small/fast) models efficiently (without breaking the compute bank) for NLP tasks. He is also excited about ZeroSumEval, a project to scale evaluation of LLMs with compute and capabilities.

Prior to his current work, he was a senior applied scientist at Amazon Alexa AI conducting research in NLP topics including semantic parsing and efficient modeling. He helped build Alexa Teacher Models (AlexaTM 20B), a 20 billion parameter multilingual seq2seq model that achieves state-of-the-art performance on 1-shot summarization tasks.

He completed his PhD at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) with the Data Science Research Center, working with Prof. Bulent Yener on methods for classifying, predicting, and analyzing medical data. He worked with doctors from Mount Sinai Hospital Epilepsy Center on patient care applications.

In September 2024, he founded ai.astrolabe, a venture to fill the gap in alignment data for low-resource languages and dialects.
