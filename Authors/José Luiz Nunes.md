# José Luiz Nunes

## Research Summary

PhD Candidate in Informatics at Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), conducting research at Center for Technology and Society at FGV Direito Rio. Interdisciplinary research at intersection of machine learning ethics, machine behavior, and quantitative empirical legal studies. Published "Evidence of conceptual mastery in the application of rules by Large Language Models" (March 2025), leveraging psychological methods to investigate LLMs' rule-based decision-making, finding LLMs replicate human patterns across diverse scenarios.
