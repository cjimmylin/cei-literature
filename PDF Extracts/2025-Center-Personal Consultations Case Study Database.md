# NCBC Case Study Database Overview

## Purpose

The National Catholic Bioethics Center's Personal Consultations Department has developed a database of anonymized case studies primarily for use in university courses and professional training. These real-world scenarios help students develop listening skills, critical thinking, and appreciation for patient experiences while enhancing Catholic medical-moral education.

## Database Content

The collection contains de-identified consultation questions sourced from actual ethics requests. As one educator noted: *"They are so helpful for the students—especially in that they know these are real questions."*

## Access Requirements

- **Intended Users**: Professors and mission integration officers in higher education or professional training
- **Application Process**: Educators must complete a form providing their name, institution, academic field, Google email address, and explanation of how they plan to use the cases
- **Participant Consent**: Every case study included explicit permission from the original consultation requester, who also participated in developing the anonymized materials

## Alternative Resources

For those outside academic settings, the NCBC offers:
- Case studies in the revised *Handbook on Critical Life Issues*
- Research-related cases in *A Catholic Guide to Ethical Clinical Research*
- Extensive free online resources and accessible journal articles
