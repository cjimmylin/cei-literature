# Ibrahim Farouq

## Research Summary

Ibrahim Sambo Farouq is a researcher at the World Bank in Washington, D.C., working in the Cluster of Social Cohesion and Violence Prevention. He holds a PhD and has contributed significantly to research on hate speech detection in Nigerian contexts. His work on NaijaHate introduced the first dataset annotated for hate speech detection containing a representative sample of Nigerian tweets, addressing the critical issue that HSD systems are typically developed on datasets from the United States and fail to generalize to English dialects from the Majority World. His research demonstrated that content moderators would need to review about ten thousand Nigerian tweets flagged as hateful daily to moderate 60% of all hateful content.

## Key Contributions

- Co-created NaijaHate, the first representative hate speech detection dataset for Nigerian Twitter
- Demonstrated that HSD evaluated on biased datasets overestimates real-world performance by at least two-fold
- Contributed to developing NaijaXLM-T, a pretrained model tailored to the Nigerian Twitter context
- Developed methods for characterizing demographics of social media users at scale
