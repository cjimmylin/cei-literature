# Boyang Li

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Boyang Li is an Associate Professor at the College of Computing and Data Science, NTU Singapore. He was previously a Senior Research Scientist at Baidu Research USA and a Group Leader at Disney Research. He earned his PhD from Georgia Tech. He is a leading expert in the intersection of multimodal AI and human-like reasoning.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Multimodal Learning" and "Data-centric AI." He investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be integrated with visual and auditory perception to create more capable and intuitive AI agents. He is also a pioneer in "Computational Narrative Intelligence"—studying how AI can understand and generate complex stories.

### Significant Achievements
He has held leadership roles in major industrial labs (Disney and Baidu) and is recognized for his work on "Computational Narrative Intelligence," which aims to imbue AI with the human-like ability to process and reason about stories. His research has been featured in major technology media like New Scientist and TechCrunch.
