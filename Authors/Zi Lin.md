# Zi Lin

## Research Summary

Zi Lin is a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego. He is the first author of ToxicChat (EMNLP 2023), a novel benchmark based on real user queries from open-source chatbots for toxicity detection in real-world user-AI conversations. ToxicChat reveals hidden challenges in current toxicity detection models, particularly in identifying nuanced toxic phenomena that emerge in actual conversational contexts, advancing the development of safer AI interaction systems.
