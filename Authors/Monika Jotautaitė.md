# Monika Jotautaitė

## Research Summary

Monika Jotautaitė is an independent researcher working on LLM evaluations and AI Safety. Previously worked as an evaluations scientist and technical program manager with the ASET Benchmarks program at Arcadia Impact for the AISI bounty program. Lead author of "Speciesism in AI: Evaluating Discrimination Against Animals in Large Language Models" and co-author of "From Stability to Inconsistency: A Study of Moral Preferences in LLMs." Co-organizer of Women in AI Safety networking events.
