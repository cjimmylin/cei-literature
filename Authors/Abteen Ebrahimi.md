# Abteen Ebrahimi

## Biographical Summary

Abteen Ebrahimi is a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Katharina Kann. He is a member of the Boulder Natural Language Processing Group, where his research focuses on extending the benefits of language technology to underserved and indigenous linguistic communities.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Abteen's work centers on the AmericasNLI project, which benchmarks the zero-shot natural language understanding (NLU) capabilities of multilingual models on indigenous languages of the Americas. He investigates cross-lingual transfer learning and the development of machine translation systems for truly low-resource scenarios.

## Major Publications
- [AmericasNLI: Evaluating Zero-shot Natural Language Understanding of Pretrained Multilingual Models in Truly Low-resource Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08726) (2021) - *A pioneering benchmark for indigenous NLU.*
