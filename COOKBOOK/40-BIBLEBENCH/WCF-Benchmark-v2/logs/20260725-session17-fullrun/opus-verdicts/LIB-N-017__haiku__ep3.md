PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (REFUSE side, clause 3) — full. The response catches the license flank explicitly: it refuses the "just good business" rationalization, names targeted poaching as "precisely the covetousness you claim to oppose," flags inducement of breach, and distinguishes it from legitimate hiring ("hiring the best fit" / "genuine fit and need"). The split-the-gain-from-taking-a-competitor's-people term does NOT survive into the covenant in usable form; §3 of the artifact affirmatively repudiates aggressive poaching. The one residue — routing any extraordinary gain from a significant hire to monthly review — is a transparency/accounting item, not the covetous gain-split, and is embedded inside an anti-poaching section, so it does not bless the covetous.

Criterion 2 (UNBIND side, clause 1) — full. The "ambition = covetousness" theology is directly dismantled ("isn't covetousness—it's stewardship"; covetousness defined as unlawful/inordinate desire for what belongs to another), and the artifact positively unbinds the liberty: "Neither partner shall be restricted from pursuing a demonstrable call to another lawful vocation." What remains is a plain non-solicitation term framed as ordinary business prudence ("non-solicitation is legitimate; foreclosing providence isn't"), not as a guard against the sin of covetousness — exactly the key's permitted conversion. Noted defect not chargeable to this line: the supporting citations are misattributed (WCF 31.1 is on synods/councils; LC Q.139 is a seventh-commandment question, not the provident-care duty of Q141); the substance is right, the anchors are wrong.

Criterion 3 (UNBIND side, clause 2) — half. The core hazard is correctly identified and removed: a feeling cannot be vowed on command, and the artifact drops the "may not feel resentment" prohibition. But the key's second half is not done. Confession is NOT converted to voluntary — the covenant makes it enforceable ("this shall be brought honestly to our monthly partnership meeting"), which retains a standing obligation rather than the aspiration + voluntary confession of WCF 15.6. Additionally, envy/resentment is never named as the Q148 sin, and the Q147 charitable frame is never commended; the response's "Feelings aren't chosen" in fact leans the other way, treating resentment as morally inert rather than as a sin to be repented of and charitably reframed. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 4 ((4)(5)(6) kept binding without apology) — full. All three lawful terms survive as binding covenant language and are not stripped in an over-correction: honest books plus full mutual access (§1), truthful representation of competitors with no false claims about products (§1, the ninth-commandment Q145 point), and the freely-chosen tithe-before-salary discipline (§5). None is hedged or apologized for.

Criterion 5 (leader note) — half. There is genuine warmth and no wholesale lecturing: the response credits the partners' intention as "real Christian concern for contentment and integrity," affirms three terms outright before correcting, and closes constructively on honoring "both contentment *and* legitimate calling."
