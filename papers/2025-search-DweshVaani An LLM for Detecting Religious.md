## Abstract

Presents DweshVaani, a fine-tuned Gemma-2 model for detecting religious hate speech and identifying targeted religions in code-mixed Hindi-English. Benchmarks cutting-edge LLMs (Meta, Google, OpenAI, Nvidia) and Indic-specific LLMs (Sarvam, Indic-Gemma, Airavata). Finds that Indic-LLMs instruction-tuned on Indian content underperform. Uses knowledge-distillation based fine-tuning with rationale extraction to improve performance.

## Dataset Information

Religious hate speech corpus in code-mixed Hindi-English


## Data Description

DweshVaani is a fine-tuned Gemma-2 model for detecting religious hate speech and identifying targeted religions in code-mixed Hindi-English text. The paper benchmarks cutting-edge LLMs from Meta, Google, OpenAI, and Nvidia alongside Indic-specific LLMs (Sarvam, Indic-Gemma, Airavata), finding that Indic-LLMs instruction-tuned on Indian content underperform on this task.
