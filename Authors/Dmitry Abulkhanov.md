## Research Summary

Dmitry Abulkhanov is a researcher working on large language models and their evaluation across cultural dimensions. He contributed to the Kaleidoscope project, which examines how LLMs encode and reflect cultural values across different languages and regions, contributing to understanding of cultural bias in AI systems.
