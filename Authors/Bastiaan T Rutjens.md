# Bastiaan T Rutjens

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bastiaan T. Rutjens is an Associate Professor at the University of Amsterdam, where he leads the PsySci lab. He holds a PhD in Social Psychology and has conducted extensive research on the psychology of science, belief systems, and how humans interact with algorithmic agents.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research explores science skepticism, trust in scientific findings, and the worldview predictors of attitudes toward Artificial Intelligence. He investigates the social and moral psychology underlying human-AI interactions.

### Significant Achievements
A Fellow of several prestigious psychological societies, Dr. Rutjens has published influential work on the "psychology of science" and the relationship between religious/secular worldviews and trust in technology.

## Major Publications

- [Worldview predictors of attitudes towards Artificial Intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000438) (2022) - *Large-scale study on how different belief systems shape AI acceptance.*
- [Science Skepticism: Review and Theoretical Framework](https://doi.org/10.1177/10892680211013402) (2021) - *Comprehensive framework for understanding science rejection.*
