## Abstract

28,000+ human-curated VQA samples across 11 ASEAN countries covering food, landmarks, notable figures, paintings, places of worship. Includes SEA-LAVE metric for cultural alignment assessment.

## Data Description

RICE-VL is a multimodal benchmark with over 28,000 human-curated VQA samples across 11 ASEAN countries covering True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank, and open-ended formats, plus 1,000 image-bounding box pairs for Visual Grounding, annotated by culturally trained experts across 14 sub-categories.

**Relevance:** Vision-language cultural understanding benchmark across ASEAN countries. Reveals performance gaps in low-resource countries and abstract cultural domains.
