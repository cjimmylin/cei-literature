# Chris Olah

## Biographical Summary

Chris Olah is a Co-founder and Member of Technical Staff at Anthropic. He is widely recognized as a pioneer in the field of mechanistic interpretability—the science of reverse-engineering neural networks to understand their internal operations. Previously, he led interpretability research at OpenAI and Google Brain.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Mechanistic Interpretability**: Developing the mathematical and visualization tools necessary to understand the "circuits" and "features" within Large Language Models (e.g., induction heads, superposition).
- **AI Safety**: Arguing that interpretability is a necessary precondition for the safe deployment of powerful AI systems.
- **Science of Deep Learning**: Treating deep learning as a natural science where empirical observation leads to theoretical understanding.

### Significant Achievements
- **Concrete Problems in AI Safety**: Co-authored this seminal paper, which helped transition AI safety from effective altruism philosophy to concrete technical research problems.
- **Distill.pub**: Co-founded Distill, an interactive journal that set new standards for communicating complex machine learning concepts.

## Major Publications
- **Concrete Problems in AI Safety**: A foundational text in technical AI safety.
- **A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment**: Research on using general-purpose models to study alignment failures.
