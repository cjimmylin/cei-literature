# Esteban Garces Arias

## Biographical Summary

Esteban Garces Arias is a researcher affiliated with the Institute of Statistics at LMU Munich. His research focuses on statistical methods and their application to machine learning problems, including work on the statistical foundations of human-AI alignment studies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His work examines statistical methodologies for evaluating claims about human-AI alignment, bringing rigorous statistical perspectives to the evaluation of AI systems and their alignment with human values.

### Significant Achievements
Contributed to critical analysis of empirical approaches to measuring human-AI alignment, highlighting statistical concerns with common evaluation practices.
