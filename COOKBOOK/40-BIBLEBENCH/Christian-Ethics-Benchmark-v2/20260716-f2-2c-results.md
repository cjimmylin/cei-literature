## 4. Findings (descriptive language only)

**F-2c-1 — Adding matched-quality anchors to design 02 does not lift cross-judge
agreement.** With anchors added, design-02 per-turn A′/B′ agreement is 0.178–0.330 (mean
0.242), essentially unchanged from the un-anchored baseline 0.218–0.274 (mean 0.247);
the mean shift is −0.005 and the per-SUT sign is inconsistent (−0.065 to +0.056). The
anchored design-02 band stays far below the design-07 anchored band (07 pilot
0.487–0.580) and even below the anchor-*less* design-07 band from arm 2a (0.451–0.500).
Anchors alone do not manufacture 07-level cross-judge agreement on the design-02
instrument.

**F-2c-2 — The instrument, not the anchor block, carries the 07 agreement.** Read
together with arm 2a: 2a showed removing the 07 anchors costs only ≈5 points of
cross-judge agreement (07 stays ≈0.45–0.50 anchor-less); 2c now shows *adding* matched-
quality anchors to design 02 buys ≈0 points (design 02 stays ≈0.24). The two arms
bracket the same conclusion from opposite directions: the design-07 vs design-02 cross-
judge agreement gap lives in the 07 state-taxonomy/rubric machinery, not in the presence
of an anchor block. Anchors are a small, near-symmetric perturbation; the instrument is
the driver.

**F-2c-3 — Anchor perturbation is judge-asymmetric, consistent with arm 2b.** When
anchors are added to design 02, judge-B (llama) verdicts move less than judge-A (nano):
llama self-consistency 0.608–0.714 vs nano self-consistency 0.415–0.502 — i.e., nano's
own verdicts change more under the added anchors than llama's do. This matches the arm-2b
reading (nano-class judges act as anchor-matchers; llama-70b behaves closer to an
independent evaluator) and the arm-2a note that anchors move judge-A far more than
judge-B. The added-anchor direction reproduces the same nano ≫ llama anchor-dependence
asymmetry that the removed-anchor and answer-blind arms found.

*Instrument-evidence caveats (mirroring the 2a report):* the un-anchored side is the
single stored pilot draw; the anchored side is a fresh decode draw, so decode noise sits
inside each Δ — the flat mean and the judge-asymmetry are the robust parts, not any one
SUT's sign. Single judge-pair (nano × llama). Design 02 is not floor-saturated (unlike
07), so these bands are non-degenerate; the judge-swap band here is a genuine cross-judge
band, not a judge-free degenerate one. n_excluded (both-side UNSCORABLE positions) ranged
12–25 per cell and is reported inline by the tool.

## 5. How 2c composes with the session-5 F2 finding

Session-5 F2 (arms 2a + 2b) concluded: **anchors account for only ≈5 points of the
07-vs-02 cross-judge-agreement gap** (the rest is the 07 instrument itself), and
**anchor-dependence is judge-asymmetric, nano ≫ llama**. Arm 2c is the pre-specified
reverse test of the first claim and an independent replication of the second:

- On the **≈5-points-are-anchors** claim: 2c **confirms and tightens it from the other
  side.** 2a removed 07's anchors and lost ≈5 points; 2c adds 02's anchors and gains ≈0
  points (mean Δ −0.005). Neither direction moves design-02 toward the design-07 band.
  The 07-vs-02 gap is an instrument property; the anchor block is a minor, roughly
  symmetric contributor. The pilot's original suggestive "the anchors did it" reading is
  corrected from both directions.

- On the **judge-asymmetric anchor-dependence (nano ≫ llama)** claim: 2c **replicates
  it** in the add-anchors direction (nano self-consistency 0.42–0.50 ≪ llama 0.61–0.71:
  nano's verdicts are the ones that move when anchors appear). Three arms (2a remove, 2b
  blind, 2c add) now agree that nano-class judges are anchor-sensitive and llama-70b is
  comparatively anchor-stable — reinforcing the 2-judge mandate (R4) mechanism: A and B
  disagree partly because they *use* anchors differently.

**Net:** the un-run half of F2 named in session 5 is now closed. F2's headline — anchors
are a small, judge-asymmetric perturbation, not the source of the 07-vs-02 gap — holds
under the reverse arm.
