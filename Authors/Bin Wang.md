# Bin Wang

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bin Wang is an AI Research Scientist at MiroMind in Singapore and a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He earned his PhD from the University of Southern California (USC). He has held scientific positions at A*STAR's Institute for Infocomm Research (I2R). He is a specialist in language and representation learning, with a prolific record of publications in top-tier NLP venues.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bin’s research focuses on the development of multimodal large language models and conversational AI systems. He investigates how knowledge can be effectively represented and learned within neural architectures. His recent work spans AGI, agentic AI, and the integration of speech and language processing.

### Significant Achievements
He has received multiple best paper awards and has served in leadership roles for the NLP community, including Publication Chair for EMNLP 2023. His work on knowledge representation learning is widely cited and forms the basis for advanced conversational agents in the Singaporean AI ecosystem.
