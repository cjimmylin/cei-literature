# James Zou

## Research Summary

James Zou is an Associate Professor of Biomedical Data Science (and by courtesy, Computer Science and Electrical Engineering) at Stanford University. He is one of the most influential researchers working at the intersection of machine learning foundations and scientific applications, with many of his innovations widely used in tech and biotech industries.

### Research Areas

Zou's work spans machine learning, computational biology, computational health, statistics, and biotech. His research has been particularly impactful in:

**AI Bias and Fairness:** His seminal 2016 paper "Man is to Computer Programmer as Woman is to Homemaker: Debiasing Word Embeddings" was foundational in demonstrating and quantifying gender bias in word embeddings, spawning an entire field of research on bias in NLP systems.

**Trustworthy AI:** Key contributions include TrustLLM, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating trustworthiness in large language models, and research quantifying AI psychology in LLMs.

**AI for Science:** In 2025, Zou's team created "virtual labs" - AI-driven tools for scientific discovery featuring AI principal investigators and virtual scientists, published in Nature.
