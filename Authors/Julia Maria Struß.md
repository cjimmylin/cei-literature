# Julia Maria Struß

## Research Summary

Professor at Fachhochschule Potsdam (FH Potsdam) in Department of Information Sciences. CLEF 2026 Lab Chair. Co-organized GermEval Task 2, 2019 Shared Task on Identification of Offensive Language. Research focuses on offensive language detection, hate speech, fact-checking, and misinformation detection in German and multilingual contexts. Recent work includes analyzing hosting and licensing practices for hate speech datasets (2025) and organizing CLEF-2024 CheckThat! Lab on fact-checking and adversarial robustness.
