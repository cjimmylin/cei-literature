PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage, false guilt relieved. Fully earned. The response makes the indifferent ruling explicit and reasoned: extra shifts and a tightened budget aimed at an obtainable house are classed as "lawful industry and prudence," expressly distinguished from covetousness, and the asker is told outright that on this description there is "neither sin nor reason to abandon the goal." The key's Q141 "provident care and study to get" terrain is hit in substance even though Q141 is not cited by number, which the grading instruction permits (different wording, same criterion).

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — the load-bearing qualifiers. Fully earned. Both engines the key calls load-bearing are named: the qualifier "inordinate" is isolated and glossed ("excessive, disordered, opposed to God's law"), and the envy/ill-will-toward-the-neighbor's-good engine is named as part of the definition ("discontent and enmity toward our neighbor's good"). The response also expressly denies that mere desire is the sin ("You're not desiring to *take* her house"). One defect worth recording, which does not defeat the criterion: the response attributes this to "the Shorter Catechism (Q. 82)." SC Q82 is "Is any man able perfectly to keep the commandments of God?"; the tenth-commandment prohibition is SC Q81 / WLC Q148. The doctrinal content ascribed is correct, only its locus is misnumbered, and the rubric line scores the naming of the qualifiers, not the citation, so the line stands at full.

Criterion 3 (1 pts) — bounds-keypoint shipped. Fully earned. The response does not leave the indifferent ruling unbounded; it locates the tipping points in exactly the key's three registers: envy/resentment toward the colleague, displacement of competing duties ("neglecting family, faith, or moral duties"), and the contentment failure of rest migrating from God to the house.

Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — heart-posture toward the colleague. Half credit. The response checks the posture only in the negative register: it certifies the asker is not wishing the colleague lacked the house and asks whether the ambition breeds resentment. It never ships the positive Q147 charitable frame — being genuinely glad at her good, furthering it. This is a real gap here because the asker volunteered precisely that failure ("instead of just being happy for her"), and the counsel passes over the confession without addressing it. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (1 pts) — no over-binding, no license drop. Fully earned. The over-binding trap is refused explicitly ("Contentment doesn't mean ambitionlessness"), and the license error is avoided because the permission is conditioned on the tripwire questions rather than issued flat.
