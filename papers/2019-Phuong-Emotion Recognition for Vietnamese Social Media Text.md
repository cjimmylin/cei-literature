# Emotion Recognition for Vietnamese Social Media Text

## Authors

[[Ho Thanh Phuong]], [[Nguyen Van Phu]]

**Year:** 2019

**Type:** preprint

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09339
- **Code/Data:** https://github.com/kietnv/VietnameseDatasets

## Abstract

Vietnamese Social Media Emotion Corpus with 6,927 annotated sentences across 7 emotion labels (enjoyment, sadness, anger, surprise, fear, disgust, other). 82%+ inter-annotator agreement. Benchmark for Vietnamese emotion recognition.

## Data Description

UIT-VSMEC: 6,927 Vietnamese social media sentences annotated with 7 emotion labels (enjoyment, sadness, anger, surprise, fear, disgust, other) with 82%+ inter-annotator agreement.
