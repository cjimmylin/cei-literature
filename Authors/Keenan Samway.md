# Keenan Samway

## Research Summary

Keenan Samway is a Research Assistant at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Tübingen. He recently completed his M.Sc. in Computational Statistics and Machine Learning at UCL (University College London). His research interests lie at the intersection of privacy, safety, interpretability, and NLP, with broad interests in topics related to responsible AI development. His recent research includes presentations at the ICML 2025 World Models Workshop.
