# Israfel Salazar

## Research Summary

ELLIS PhD fellow at University of Copenhagen, advised by Desmond Elliot. Research focuses on visual and language understanding and representation. First author on Kaleidoscope (2025), the most comprehensive exam benchmark for multilingual evaluation of vision-language models, covering 18 languages and 14 subjects with 20,911 multiple-choice questions, built through open science collaboration ensuring linguistic and cultural authenticity.
