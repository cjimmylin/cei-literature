# Zhiyu An

## Research Summary

Zhiyu An (Ryan Zhiyu An) is a postdoctoral scholar at University of California, Merced, in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, School of Engineering. He completed his PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Merced and his BA in Computer Science at UC Berkeley. He developed MoralReason for generalizable moral decision alignment in LLM agents using reasoning-level reinforcement learning (AAAI 2025), addressing fundamental questions of AI alignment through enhanced moral decision frameworks for autonomous agents.
