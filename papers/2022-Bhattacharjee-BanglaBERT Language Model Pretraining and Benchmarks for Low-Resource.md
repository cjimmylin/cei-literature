## Abstract

First Bangla Language Understanding Benchmark (BLUB) with four NLU tasks (text classification, sequence labeling, span prediction). Introduces BanglaBERT pretrained model. Establishes baseline for Bangla NLU evaluation.

## Data Description

Introduces BLUB (Bangla Language Understanding Benchmark) with four NLU tasks and two new datasets (BNLI for NLI and BQA for QA). Also releases BanglaBERT and BanglishBERT pretrained models and the Bangla2B+ pretraining corpus.
