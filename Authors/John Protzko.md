# John Protzko

## Research Summary

John Protzko is a researcher at the University of California, Santa Barbara, specializing in cognitive and moral psychology. His work includes large-scale replication studies examining how psychological phenomena hold up across different contexts and cultures.

He is known for his contributions to meta-science and the study of replication in psychology, helping establish which findings are robust and which are context-dependent.
