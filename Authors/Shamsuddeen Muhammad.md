# Shamsuddeen Muhammad

## Research Summary

Note: This appears to be the same person as "Shamsuddeen Hassan Muhammad". Advanced Research Fellow at Imperial College London and Google DeepMind Academic Fellow. Also affiliated with Bayero University, Kano. Research focuses on Natural Language Processing for low-resource African languages. Published in top venues including ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, ICLR, and NeurIPS. Co-founder of HausaNLP research group. Known for work on AfriSenti and other African language NLP resources.
