# Betty Li Hou

## Biographical Summary

Betty Li Hou is a PhD student in Computer Science at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, NYU. Her research is situated at the intersection of computer science and moral philosophy, focusing on the technical and ethical challenges of AI alignment.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates how to measure and mitigate automated bias in Large Language Models (LLMs) and how to ground AI alignment in foundational moral values. She also studies the risks of models acting as "misleading assistants" in human-AI conversation.

### Significant Achievements
Hou has contributed to significant benchmarking efforts, including the "Foundational Moral Values for AI Alignment" framework and the "GPQA" benchmark for evaluating graduate-level reasoning in language models.

## Major Publications

- [Foundational Moral Values for AI Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00000) (2024) - *Framework for grounding AI in transcultural moral principles.*
- [Large language models as misleading assistants in conversation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00000) (2024) - *Research on the risks of deceptive behavior in LLMs.*
