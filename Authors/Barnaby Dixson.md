# Barnaby Dixson

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Barnaby Dixson is a Senior Lecturer in Psychology at the University of the Sunshine Coast and an anthropologist with the Australian Centre for Pacific Islands Research. His career spans over 15 years, during which he has led extensive field research in the Pacific, integrating evolutionary ecology and social psychology.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates human partner preferences, gender and sexuality, and family life across different cultures. He uses lab-based behavioral experiments, eye-tracking, and EEG to study face perception and perceptions of facial expressions.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Dixson has published widely on sexual selection and evolutionary theory, with over 6,800 citations. He is a Fellow of the Behavioural and Brain Sciences Foundation and has received multiple awards for his doctoral and post-doctoral research.

## Major Publications

- [The role of facial hair in women's perceptions of men's attractiveness, masculinity, and dominance](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jeb.12917) (2016) - *Highly cited study on human mating signals.*
- [Body image and partner preferences in Samoa](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ajhb.21046) (2010) - *Early influential work on cross-cultural psychology.*
