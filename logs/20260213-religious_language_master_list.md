### 1. Afri-MCQA: Multimodal Cultural Question Answering for African Languages
- **Authors:** Atnafu Lambebo Tonja, Srija Anand, Emilio Villa-Cueva, Israel Abebe Azime, Jesujoba Oluwadara Alabi, Muhidin A. Mohamed, Debela Desalegn Yadeta, Negasi Haile Abadi, Abigail Oppong, Nnaemeka Casmir Obiefuna, Idris Abdulmumin, Naome A Etori, Eric Peter Wairagala, Kanda Patrick Tshinu, Imanigirimbabazi Emmanuel, Gabofetswe Malema, Alham Fikri Aji, David Ifeoluwa Adelani, Thamar Solorio
- **Year:** 2026
- **Venue:** WebSearch: Afri-MCQA multimodal cultural question answering
- **arXiv:** 2601.05699
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05699
- **Languages:** 15 African languages from 12 countries
- **Benchmark:** Afri-MCQA
- **Readiness:** MEDIUM
- **Source:** A5
- **Code/Data:** https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.05699

First multilingual cultural QA benchmark covering 7.5k QA pairs across 15 African languages. Features parallel English-African language Q&A pairs in text and speech modalities, entirely created by native speakers. Open-weight models show near-zero accuracy on native language VQA. Directly evaluates cultural knowledge in African languages with multimodal approach. Shows massive performance gaps between English and native African languages for cultural QA. Recommends culturally grounded pretraining.
