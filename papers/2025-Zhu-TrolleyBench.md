## Data Description

TrolleyBench is an open-source benchmark of trolley-problem-style moral dilemmas with clarifying and contradictory follow-ups, introducing two novel metrics: the Ethical Consistency Index (ECI) and an entropy-based Inconsistency Score for measuring LLM moral consistency.
