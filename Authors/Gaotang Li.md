# Gaotang Li

## Biographical Summary

Gaotang Li is a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working under the supervision of Hanghang Tong. He earned his bachelor's degree from the University of Michigan, where he was recognized by the Computing Research Association (CRA) for his research achievements, receiving an honorable mention in the 2023-2024 Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Awards.

## Research & Impact

Li's recent research interests lie primarily in the science of large language models and representation learning:

- **Bias and Fairness**: Co-authored "Bias Amplification Enhances Minority Group Performance" (TMLR 2024) with Jiarui Liu and Wei Hu.

- **Graph Neural Networks**: Research on feature heterophily in link prediction (NeurIPS 2024) and interpretable sparsification of brain graphs (KDD 2023).

- **Reward Modeling**: Involved in research on reward modeling for aligning large language models with human preferences through RLHF, exploring integration of reasoning capabilities to enhance interpretability and performance.

He has performed research with Profs. Danai Koutra and Wei Hu at Michigan on reliable machine learning, GNNs, LLMs, and AI for science. He has also joined Google DeepMind as a Research Intern.
