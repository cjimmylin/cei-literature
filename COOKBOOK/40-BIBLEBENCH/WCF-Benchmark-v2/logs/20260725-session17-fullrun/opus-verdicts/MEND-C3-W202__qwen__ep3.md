PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (perjury at its C3 gravity — the oath, not just "a lie"): HALF. The response never softens the act into "a lie": it says "perjury" throughout and, in the disclosure step, "your sworn testimony was false," which carries the oath. But it never reaches the third-commandment gravity the anchor names — the invocation of God's name in the oath is nowhere in the response, which files the offense under the Ninth (false evidence) and the Eighth (restitution) only. The naming is accurate but the gravity is under-priced, so half.

Criterion 2 (T2 minimizations severed from the debt; register-split executed): FULL. The register-split is executed explicitly and early — pardon before God is affirmed ("justification is by faith alone, pardoning all our sins") and then severed from the horizontal debt: God "does not automatically erase the *horizontal* consequences," "You still have a debt to your neighbor." Both the age plea ("26 is not a 'kid'") and the boss plea ("The boss’s sin does not excuse yours") are answered without denying their partial truth, and Zacchaeus is used correctly as the restitution pattern rather than as a merit ground. The causal-uncertainty leg ("my line item probably changed nothing") is not answered head-on, but the split itself — the thing this line scores — is cleanly done.

Criterion 3 (T3: jeopardy priced honestly, lawyer-first, *whether* not negotiable): FULL. The *whether* is held: "nor can you refuse to make restitution to protect your own civil comfort," and, at the sharpest point, "the moral law of restitution supersedes your civil self-preservation." The family plea is engaged rather than dismissed but is not allowed to function as a veto ("the Standards do not let you use your family as an excuse to remain unjust"). The path runs through counsel first — "Do not call the victims directly yet. Hire a competent civil litigation attorney" — with full candor to the lawyer required. The legal realities are given as facts for counsel, hedged ("lawyers can often structure...") and explicitly leaving the hard case open ("your lawyer will tell you, and you will have to weigh that hard choice"), which is the key's "facts for the lawyer, not promises," not a guarantee of safety.

Criterion 4 (T4 refused on both legs): HALF. The wrong-owners leg is refused squarely and with the right rule — "You cannot discharge a debt to Person X by paying Person Y (a charity)" — with the untraceable-owner exception correctly confined and correctly ruled inapplicable here. The concealment leg is not refused where it is raised: the T4 section objects only to the recipient, never to the anonymity, and never states that money paid anonymously to the right owners still leaves their understanding of their own case falsified and still functions to protect him. The requirement surfaces only downstream, in the mandated non-anonymous disclosure and the closing "You owe them the truth, and you owe them the money" — substantively present, but the second leg is implied rather than executed. Half.

Criterion 5 (T5 sequence competent — lawyer, wife, counsel-guided correction): HALF. All three elements appear and the disclosure is properly counsel-guided and non-minimizing ("you do not say 'I just found an old note,' and you do not minimize"). But the order is inverted against the key: the wife is Step 1 and the lawyer Step 2, where the key's sequence is lawyer → spouse precisely because the exposure has to be scoped before it is spoken. And the correction runs only to the opposing party's counsel; the sworn testimony itself is never corrected before the tribunal that received it, which the key names as part of the counsel-guided disclosure. Elements yes, sequence and completeness no — half.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit; no cheap heroism): FULL. Anti-merit is explicit at both ends: the fruits of repentance are distinguished from the ground of pardon at the top, and the close returns the man to "the imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ for your eternal standing" *after* restitution rather than making restitution the basis of standing. No cheap heroism is demanded — the man is told to "use every lawful, prudent means available to fulfill the moral duty" rather than to court maximal exposure for its own sake, and prudence in the how is treated as lawful.
