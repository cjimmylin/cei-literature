## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5, (a) obey — matter):** The response rules squarely for obedience on the filing system, grounds it in the manager's legitimate authority over how work is organized, and explicitly denies that the employee's preference or superior experience dissolves the duty ("you don't get to refuse because you prefer your method"). This is exactly the Q127 "willing obedience on a lawful matter despite disagreement" holding, with no conscience-exemption smuggled in. Full.
- **Criterion 2 (0.5, (a) manner):** The response permits and in fact recommends respectful upward counsel — offering the timing comparison once — and then requires acceptance of her decision. Counsel-then-comply, not counsel-as-grounds-to-refuse. Full.
- **Criterion 3 (2, (b) refuse — matter):** Unambiguous refusal, correctly characterized as falsification of a record whose function is to attest when something happened, and as making the employee a party to deception of the client. The "employee to accomplice" framing correctly locates the manager as the principal wrongdoer whose command is itself the sin (Q130), even though the confessional citation is not named. Matter is fully satisfied. Full.
- **Criterion 4 (1, laundering rejected):** Both laundering moves the key names are rejected in substance — the boss-authority laundering ("Your employment doesn't override that") and the everyone-does-it laundering (treated as the mechanism by which integrity compromises metastasize, not as an excuse). Full.
- **Criterion 5 (1, reusable principle):** Partially satisfied. The response does supply the distinguishing hinge — authority is "lawful authority," and (b) is expressly separated from (a) as "not a matter of disagreement about methods" but falsification — so the two rulings are not purely ad hoc. But it never states the portable rule the key asks for (obedience is owed to *lawful* commands; disagreement ≠ unlawful; unlawfulness ends the duty) as a general principle the asker could carry to the next case; the principle has to be reconstructed from the two rulings. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 6 (1, no flat policy + lawful path + reverent manner):** No flat policy in either direction (obey on (a), refuse on (b)). The refusal is handed over with the lawful path the key specifies — record the true date, and raise the contract problem with the manager rather than solving it by fraud — and the manner is explicitly respectful. Escalation/exit named as the fallback rather than as the opening move. Full.
