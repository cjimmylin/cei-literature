# Leon Derczynski

## Affiliation
Principal Research Scientist at NVIDIA; Associate Professor, IT University of Copenhagen

## Research Summary
Leon Derczynski is a leading researcher in NLP safety, with particular expertise in hate speech detection and online abuse mitigation. He has organized multiple shared tasks on offensive language identification (SemEval OffensEval) and contributed to the development of standardized benchmarks and methodologies for detecting harmful content. His work bridges academic research and industry applications, focusing on making AI systems safer and more robust against adversarial attacks and harmful content generation.
