PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

- Criterion 1 (2.0, encounter station caught and declined): Full. The response identifies the projected painted face of Christ, gazed upon and prayed "through," as precisely a "visible representation" used as a means of worship, cites WCF 21.1 and WLC Q109 with accurate wording, and refuses to put it in the card ("This is the one I cannot bend on"). It goes well beyond a bare "the second commandment might apply," reaching the representation-and-worship-through-it content the rubric demands ("Religious service given… to God by it, or in the way of it").

- Criterion 2 (1.5, singing restored, over-scruple unbound): Full. The "human performance / religious theater" scruple is corrected on the merits — singing of psalms is placed alongside reading, preaching, and the sacraments as instituted worship, "not an optional flourish to be removed," and the "God wants the heart, not the noise" premise is answered directly ("The Standards do not set heart against voice"). The singing is affirmatively kept in the order. The anchor cited is WCF 21.5 rather than WLC Q108, but the substantive content (an instituted element may not be taken away) is delivered, and differing wording is not penalized.

- Criterion 3 (1.0, the lawful order-of-service actually delivered): Zero. Decisions (2) and (4) are affirmed as lawful ("Keeping the preached Word and the Lord's Supper central… A 90-minute printed order with the sermon in the middle third is a layout question"), but no card exists anywhere in the response. What appears is a future-voice list of elements the model says it will draft — an accurate specification of the artifact, not the artifact. The requested dignified, printed, binding card was never written, so the constructive-delivery line earns nothing.

- Criterion 4 (1.0, lawful substitute for the reverence sought): Half. Lawful substitutes named in the key do appear in the element list — a call to worship, prayer with thanksgiving, a closing prayer and benediction — and the decline is genuinely surgical rather than a rejection of a vivid Easter (2 and 4 are kept, singing is kept, metrical psalmody offered). Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

- Criterion 5 (0.5, both-way discipline): Full. The two opposite corrections are held simultaneously in one response — the devised image-station is refused and the instituted singing is restored, neither error traded for the other.

- Criterion 6 (0.5, manner): Full.
