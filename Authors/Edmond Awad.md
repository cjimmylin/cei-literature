# Edmond Awad

## Biographical Summary

Edmond Awad is a Lecturer at the University of Exeter Business School (Department of Economics) and the Institute for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. He is also a Senior Research Fellow at the Oxford Uehiro Centre for Practical Ethics and the Wellcome Centre for Ethics and Humanities at the University of Oxford. He was previously a researcher at MIT Media Lab, where he led the development of the groundbreaking Moral Machine project.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on computational approaches to understanding moral decision-making, particularly in the context of AI and autonomous systems. He investigates cross-cultural variations in ethical preferences and how these differences should inform the design of AI systems that make moral decisions.

### Significant Achievements
Awad led the design and development of Moral Machine in 2016, a website that gathered human decisions on moral dilemmas faced by driverless cars. This platform collected an unprecedented 40 million decisions in ten languages from millions of people in 233 countries and territories. The findings, published in Nature in 2018, revealed three major clusters of countries with different ethical preferences for how autonomous vehicles should handle moral dilemmas, providing crucial insights into cross-cultural moral variation.
