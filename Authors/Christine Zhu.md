# Christine Zhu

## Biographical Summary
Christine Zhu is a researcher affiliated with the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), working on developing AI systems that can behave morally. Her research focuses on creating environments and methods for evaluating and guiding the ethical behavior of AI agents.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Behavior in AI Agents**: Developing evaluation frameworks for AI systems that need to make ethical decisions
- **Artificial Conscience**: Creating methods to steer AI agents toward moral behavior without sacrificing task performance

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of the Jiminy Cricket environment suite, introducing 25 text-based adventure games with thousands of morally salient scenarios for AI evaluation
- Contributed to research demonstrating that artificial conscience approaches can guide agents toward moral behavior
- Part of the team that created foundational benchmarks for moral AI agents at NeurIPS 2021
