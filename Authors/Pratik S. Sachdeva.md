# Pratik S. Sachdeva

## Research Summary

Pratik S. Sachdeva is a Research Scientist in the D-Lab at UC Berkeley. His research focuses on the societal impacts of machine learning models, with an emphasis on understanding the norms and values elicited from large language models. He previously obtained his PhD in the Physics Department at UC Berkeley, conducting research in the Redwood Center for Theoretical Neuroscience.
