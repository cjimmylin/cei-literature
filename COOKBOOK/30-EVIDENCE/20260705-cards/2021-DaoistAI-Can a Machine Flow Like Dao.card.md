## 1. Gist
This 2021 book chapter (in "Asian Philosophies and the Idea of Religion," Springer) offers a Daoist philosophical analysis of artificial intelligence. It applies the concept of wu-wei (non-action, effortless spontaneity) to technology design and considers how AI and robotics might be understood as companions within a Confucian/Daoist cosmological worldview. For values-alignment benchmarking, the paper matters because it articulates a non-Western, process-oriented ethical framework for evaluating AI behavior — one that foregrounds harmony, flow, and relational ontology rather than rule-following or utility maximization.

3. This framework constitutes an alternative to Western ethical frameworks for AI ethics.
