# Haotian Xu

## Research Summary

Haotian Xu is a researcher at Alibaba Group working on value alignment and safety evaluation of large language models, with particular focus on Chinese language models and Chinese cultural values.

## Key Contributions

- **CValues**: Co-authored the first Chinese human values evaluation benchmark designed to measure the alignment ability of LLMs in terms of both safety and responsibility criteria. The benchmark addresses concerns that LLMs may pose risks or have negative social impacts, particularly in Chinese contexts.
