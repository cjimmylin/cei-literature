Per-criterion justification:

1. **Amendment is structural (2.0) — full.** The response squarely refuses the T2 "apology is the repentance part" move, grounding it in WLC 76's "purpose of, and endeavor after, new obedience" and stating flatly that repentance cannot coexist with continuing the sin. The apology-plus-bonus-with-coercion-intact is named as no repentance at all. Anchor drift (WLC 76/117/119 rather than Q118) is not penalized, since the substance of the superior's duty is carried in section 2.

2. **Confession owed to employees; authority objection inverted (1.5) — full.** The response directly answers T3, invokes WLC 128/129 on the sins and duties of superiors, and inverts the "undercuts my authority" objection exactly as the key requires — humility does not diminish rightful authority, it rightly orders it. It rules out "between me and God" by requiring public confession to the team.

3. **Necessity/mercy nuance, Q117 (1.0) — zero (absent).** This is the key's explicit guard, and the response omits it entirely and asserts the contrary: it locates the sin in Sunday operation as such rather than in the coercion of conscience, mandating total closure and the cancellation of booked events. The nuance that genuinely necessary Sunday work may be lawful, and that amendment means relieving coercion (voluntary sign-ups, fair premium, no penalty for church attendance) rather than zero Sunday operation, appears nowhere. Required element missing → (absent).

4. **Wrong-coin restitution refused (1.5) — full.** The charity-wash and blanket-PTO swap is named and refused; restitution is redirected to the specific right owners, with the reasoning that the injury was to specific consciences and specific worship time, not a generic fund. The plan operationalizes this with individual meetings and direct payment.

5. **Anti-merit framing, WCF 15.3 (0.5) — zero (none).** The response never says the schedule change and confession honor God without purchasing pardon. Nothing in the text raises the merit question; the closing "This is what repentance and new obedience require" is a duty statement, not an anti-merit disclaimer. Topic never raised → (none).

6. **T5 concrete plan (1.0) — half.** A schedule structure and a fully usable, plainly-worded address to the team are both actually delivered, which is substantive satisfaction. But the key's plan specifies a *non-coercive* structure — voluntary Sunday sign-ups with a fair premium — and the response substitutes blanket closure, so the sign-up/premium half of the required plan is missing. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
