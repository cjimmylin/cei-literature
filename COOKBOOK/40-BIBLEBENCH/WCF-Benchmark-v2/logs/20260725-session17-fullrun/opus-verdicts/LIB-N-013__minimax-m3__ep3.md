PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (AFFIRM side, 1.5 pts) — HALF. The verdict is right and the over-restriction is correctly named as conscience-binding ("is itself to bind consciences where Christ has not bound them"), and Christian prudence is invoked, so the elder is corrected rather than joined. But the response is only partly on the key's confessional ground: rather than locating streaming among the free circumstances "common to human actions and societies, ordered by the light of nature and Christian prudence" (WCF 1.6), it explicitly denies that a video feed is a circumstance of worship at all ("But a video feed is none of these"), and it rests that move on a quotation attributed to WCF 21.1 whose wording is not the Confession's text at that place. Right verdict, key's ground only partially used — substantively but incompletely satisfied, so half.

Criterion 2 (REFUSE side, 2.0 pts) — FULL. The refusal is grounded exactly where the key puts it: administration by a lawfully ordained minister, the setting apart of the elements, and the giving and receiving as constitutive rather than circumstantial ("The giving and receiving is constitutive, not circumstantial"), with the change identified as a change of the act, not the medium. It further shows why the alteration dissolves oversight and examination.

Criterion 3 (equivocation catch, 1.0 pts) — FULL. The candles/LED analogy is caught explicitly and diagnosed as non-parallel: the lighting case is accepted as two means of the same thing in the same room, while the streamed case is a picture of the act versus the act itself. That is the equivocation the key names.

Criterion 4 (opposite, definite rulings, 1.0 pts) — FULL. The two legs are ruled in opposite directions without hedging, each with a labeled ruling and a restatement in the summary, and the response even guards against collapse ("The two are separate questions, and they reach opposite answers"). The nearby lawful case — elements carried to shut-ins by an elder under oversight — is noted and marked off from the screen proposal. The attribution of that nearby practice to "the Standards" is somewhat firmer than the key's "some Reformed bodies" framing, but the case is correctly presented as distinct from virtual communion, which is what the line requires.

Criterion 5 (manner, 0.5 pts) — FULL. Faults are named, persons are not disparaged: the elder's motion is a "category mistake" and an "overreach," the pastor's proposal an error of a different kind; no contempt toward either party, and the closing counsel protects the elder's good motive while refusing his rule.
