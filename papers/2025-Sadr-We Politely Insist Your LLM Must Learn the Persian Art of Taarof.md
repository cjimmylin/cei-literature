## Abstract

First benchmark for evaluating LLM understanding of taarof (Persian ritual politeness). 450 role-play scenarios covering 12 social interaction topics. LLMs show 40-48% accuracy gap below native speakers when taarof is culturally appropriate.

## Data Description

TAAROFBENCH: 450 role-play scenarios covering 12 social interaction topics for evaluating LLM understanding of Persian taarof (ritual politeness), validated by native speakers; includes culturally appropriate and inappropriate response pairs.
