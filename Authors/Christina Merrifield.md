# Christina Merrifield

## Biographical Summary
Christina Merrifield is a researcher in the Department of Psychology at the University of Southern California (USC). Her work focuses on the intersection of moral psychology and computational methods, particularly analyzing moral sentiment in social media data.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Foundations in Social Media**: Research on how moral framing and sentiment affect online and offline behaviors including donation, pro-environmental action, political engagement, and protest participation

### Significant Achievements
- Co-author of the Moral Foundations Reddit Corpus (MFRC), a collection of 16,123 English Reddit comments annotated for moral sentiment
- Contributed to expanding moral psychology research beyond Twitter to Reddit platforms
