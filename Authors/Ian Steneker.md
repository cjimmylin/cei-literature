# Ian Steneker

## Research Summary

Ian Steneker is a researcher affiliated with the Center for AI Safety (CAIS), working on AI safety and biosecurity. His research focuses on evaluating and mitigating hazardous capabilities in large language models, particularly in the domains of biosecurity, cybersecurity, and chemical security. He contributed to the development of the Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy (WMDP) benchmark, a dataset of 4,157 multiple-choice questions that serve as a proxy measurement of hazardous knowledge. His work on multi-turn human jailbreaks has demonstrated significant vulnerabilities in LLM defenses, exceeding 70% attack success rate against defenses that report single-digit rates with automated single-turn attacks.

## Key Contributions

- Co-developed the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark for measuring hazardous knowledge in LLMs
- Co-authored research on Multi-Turn Human Jailbreaks (MHJ), compiling 2,912 prompts across 537 multi-turn jailbreaks
- Demonstrated that human jailbreaks can successfully recover dual-use biosecurity knowledge from unlearned models
