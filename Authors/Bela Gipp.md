# Bela Gipp

## Biographical Summary

Professor Dr. Bela Gipp holds the Chair for Scientific Information Analytics at the University of Göttingen. He is also the Scientific Director of the Göttingen State and University Library. He earned his PhD from the University of Magdeburg and conducted extensive research at UC Berkeley.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focus is on developing NLP techniques for information retrieval and visualization. He also explores the societal and industrial implications of blockchain technology.

### Significant Achievements
With over 10,000 citations, Professor Gipp is a leading researcher in information science. He is well-known for his work on citation-based plagiarism detection and scientific paper mining. He co-founded SciPlore and Docear during his time at UC Berkeley.

## Major Publications

- [Citation-based Plagiarism Detection](https://doi.org/10.1145/2467696.2467743) (2013) - *Foundational work on using citation patterns for identity detection.*
- [Blockchain Technology for Industry and Society](https://gipplab.org/blockchain/) (2020) - *Research on the decentralization of trust.*
