# Daniel Kilov

## Affiliation
Researcher, Australian National University

## Research Summary

Daniel Kilov is a researcher working at the intersection of philosophy and artificial intelligence. His work focuses on foundational questions in AI ethics, including how to operationalize moral philosophy for AI alignment. He has contributed to research on discernment in AI systems and resource-rational approaches to AI ethics, drawing on contractualist moral philosophy to guide AI development.

## Research Interests
- AI Ethics and Alignment
- Philosophy of Mind
- Moral Philosophy and Contractualism
- Decision-Making in AI Systems
- Resource-Rational Approaches to Ethics
