## Abstract

Assessment of gender bias in pretrained Bangla language models. Evaluates how pre-existing gender stereotypes are encoded in Bangla PLMs.

## Data Description

Pokkhopat: a curated Bangla dataset of 237 words across Male, Female, STEM, and SHAPE categories for evaluating gender bias in Bangla language model embeddings.
