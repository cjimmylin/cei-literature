# Hwaran Lee

## Biographical Summary

Hwaran Lee is affiliated with the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), Department of Electrical Engineering. Their research focuses on natural language processing and culturally-aware AI safety, with particular emphasis on developing benchmarks and datasets for Korean language models. Their work addresses the critical gap in AI safety research for non-English languages.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Their research focuses on identifying and mitigating social biases in AI systems, with particular attention to Korean language and culture. They develop benchmarks that measure how well large language models align with Korean social values and common knowledge, addressing the cultural specificity of AI safety challenges.

### Significant Achievements
Lee created two groundbreaking datasets for Korean AI safety: KoSBI, a social bias dataset of 34,000 pairs of contexts and sentences covering 72 demographic groups in 15 categories, and KorNAT, the first benchmark measuring national alignment between LLMs and South Korea. KorNAT contains 4,000 and 6,000 multiple-choice questions for social value and common knowledge respectively, with ground truth obtained from a large-scale public survey of 6,174 South Koreans. Through filtering-based moderation using KoSBI, social biases in generated content were reduced by 16.47 percentage points on average for HyperClova models.
