# Bei Yan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bei Yan is an Assistant Professor at the Stevens Institute of Technology. She holds a PhD in Communication from USC. Her research is situated at the intersection of communication, social psychology, and human-computer interaction, with a focus on how technology supports and influences collective intelligence in groups.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her work investigates group dynamics in technology-supported collaboration, particularly the temporal dynamics of emotion and cognition in AI-supported decision-making. She analyzes large behavioral datasets from online communities and conducts rigorous experimental studies.

### Significant Achievements
Dr. Yan has received multiple Best Paper Awards from the Academy of Management. She has led NSF-funded research on the ripple effects of AI in team settings and the evolving human-chatbot relationship.

## Major Publications

- [The ripple effect of AI on team decision-making: Evidence from a field experiment](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsp.2022.1643) (2023) - *Study on the cascading impacts of AI tools on human collaboration.*
- [M$^3$oralBench: A Multimodal, Multi-moral, and Multi-cultural Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00000) (2024) - *Contribution to evaluating the moral alignment of multimodal AI.*
