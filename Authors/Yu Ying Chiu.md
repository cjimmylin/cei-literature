# Yu Ying Chiu

## Research Summary

Yu Ying Chiu (also known as Kelly Chiu) is a researcher at the University of Washington focusing on AGI safety, value alignment, and human-AI interactions. Her work includes developing benchmarks for evaluating LLMs' cultural knowledge (CulturalBench), moral reasoning (MoReBench), and value preferences (DailyDilemmas, AIRiskDilemmas). She collaborates with prominent researchers including Yejin Choi, Sydney Levine (Google DeepMind), and Evan Hubinger (Anthropic).
