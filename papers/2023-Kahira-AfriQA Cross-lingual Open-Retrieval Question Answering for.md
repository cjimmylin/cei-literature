## Abstract

First cross-lingual QA dataset focused on African languages with 12,000+ XOR QA examples across 10 African languages. Questions in African languages with context passages in English or French. Covers multiple target languages. Cross-lingual QA addresses information access equity for African language speakers. Tests cultural knowledge retrieval capabilities.
