# Kedi Chen

## Research Summary

Kedi Chen is affiliated with the School of Computer Science and Technology at East China Normal University in Shanghai, China. As lead author of DiaHalu (EMNLP 2024 Findings), Chen developed the first dialogue-level hallucination evaluation benchmark for large language models, covering four common multi-turn dialogue domains and five hallucination subtypes extended from factuality and faithfulness hallucination. This work addresses a critical gap in evaluating LLM reliability across sustained conversations rather than single-turn interactions. The research provides systematic methods for detecting and analyzing when models generate content that is inconsistent with facts or the dialogue context during multi-turn exchanges.
