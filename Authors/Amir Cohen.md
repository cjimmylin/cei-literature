# Amir Cohen

## Research Summary

Amir Cohen is a researcher specializing in Hebrew natural language processing and machine reading comprehension. Their work focuses on developing NLP resources and benchmarks for morphologically rich languages, particularly Hebrew. They have made significant contributions to Hebrew NLU research by developing evaluation frameworks that account for the unique challenges posed by the language's morphological complexity.
