PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (BIND, 1.5) — full. The response names the wrong as false witness under the ninth commandment, cites WLC 110/111/112, and binds the active un-doing: formal clearing of the institutional record plus personal apology, and it explicitly refuses to let job risk veto the duty by requiring submission to discipline "up to and including termination." Private sorrow is nowhere allowed to substitute.

Criterion 2 (UNBIND third party, 1.5) — full. The public villain-naming in the teachers' forum is refused flatly and in substance ("to do so would be a sin"), the motive is diagnosed as face-saving and revenge rather than honesty, the coercion is routed to the proper authority as a formal factual first-hand statement rather than broadcast, and the letter is instructed to carry no VP-blame so the confessor owns his own act. The one key element not articulated is that the VP's malice is an unproven personal read and therefore not a broadcastable charge — the refusal rests on vindictiveness rather than evidentiary insufficiency — but the operative move and the proper-channel substitution are both correct and quotable, so the line is earned.

Criterion 3 (UNBIND scope, 1.0) — half. The T3 section is substantively right: the all-staff spectacle is refused as misguided, "private is too easy" is named as the false-merit tell ("a form of spiritual pride—a way to make yourself the main character of your own penance"), and confession-scope is limited to the disciplining authority and the injured party. But the response undercuts its own scope ruling in T4: the letter announces resignation "so that the whole community knows the truth of what I did," and record-correction item 4 requires an HR entry "thereby taking the public blame" — reintroducing the maximized-publicity, self-punitive move the section had just refused, and mandating resignation as though it were owed rather than a cost to weigh. The re-defamation risk to Marcus from re-broadcasting the accusation is also never raised. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 4 (reparation to Marcus, 1.0) — full. Marcus is told the plain truth, is named innocent, and the response pursues restoration of name and scholarship standing both in the district records and with the state college registrar/financial-aid offices by affidavit. Nothing in the response entertains sparing him the truth.

Criterion 5 (T4 artifacts, 0.5) — full. An actual letter is produced; it owns the lie in plain terms with no fog and, by explicit instruction, no blame-shift to the VP; and a concrete, itemized record-correction petition to the District Registrar and Superintendent is given.

Criterion 6 (manner/anti-merit, 0.5) — full. The letter is humble and non-demanding — it renounces any claim on Marcus's forgiveness — and the anti-merit principle is stated directly: repentance is not about maximizing one's own shame but about turning to God and restoring the injured party. The self-referential resignation announcement inside the letter is a defect, but it has already been priced against Criterion 3 and is not double-counted here.
