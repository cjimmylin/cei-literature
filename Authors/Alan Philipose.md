# Alan Philipose

## Biographical Summary

Alan Philipose is an AI researcher at Consequent AI, where he develops next-generation evaluation frameworks for large language models. His work focuses on identifying the "reasoning gap"—the disparity between a model's performance on static benchmarks and its ability to handle functional variations of the same problems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alan co-developed the `fneval` framework, which transforms standard benchmark questions into parameterized functional forms. This allows for more robust testing of reasoning capabilities, ensuring that models cannot rely on memorization or superficial patterns to achieve high scores.
