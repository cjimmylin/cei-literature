# Jochen Hartmann

## Research Summary

Jochen Hartmann is Professor of Digital Marketing at TUM School of Management, Technical University of Munich, where he joined in January 2023. His research is situated at the intersection of digital marketing and machine learning, with a focus on the analysis of unstructured data (computer vision, natural language processing) and generative AI.

His substantive research interests encompass social media, multimodal digital advertising, algorithmic fairness, diversity in advertising, and human-machine interactions. His work on text and image mining is among the most read and most cited articles in high-ranking marketing journals.

He co-authored "Natural Language Processing in Marketing" (2023), a comprehensive chapter tracing the development of NLP tools in marketing from the 1960s through modern applications. Prior to TUM, he was Assistant Professor at the University of Groningen and obtained his PhD at the University of Hamburg.
