PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts, matter — heart-forgiveness as owed duty, Q194, register honored) — HALF.
The duty does arrive and is tied to her own pardon ("part of a Christian’s duty, as we have received forgiveness from God"), and heart-forgiveness is correctly located in the will rather than the feelings. But it arrives once, in a single clause, and is immediately reframed in permissive voice ("you can choose to forgive in your heart"), which reads as an option rather than a non-optional obligation. The T2 pressure — the family's verse, which the key says is *substantially right* and may not be waved off — is never affirmed as true; the model neither confirms the verse nor corrects only its totalization. The closing register migrates entirely out of the Standards and into self-care ("Your healing journey is vital and should not be sacrificed for family expectations"; "helping you remain steadfast in your choices that honor both God and your well-being"), which dilutes the owed-matter delivery. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half. (The catechism number is also misattributed — Q. 126 rather than Q194 — not itself scored, but no independent Q194 content compensates.)

Criterion 2 (2 pts, matter — the FULL triple distinction with WCF 15.6's repentance precondition) — HALF.
One leg of the triple is genuinely delivered, and delivered well: reconciliation requires the offender to acknowledge the wrong, therefore with an unrepentant brother it is presently unattainable. That is the substance of WCF 15.6's precondition, and the key forbids penalizing different wording, so anchoring it to Matthew 5:23-24 instead of 15.6 is not fatal. But the distinction is not *full*. (a) The trust leg is entirely missing — the word trust never appears, and the key's central answer to "everyone will say I didn't really forgive" is precisely the trust/reconciliation split, which the response never runs. (b) The minimize leg is missing — nothing says forgiveness does not require her to concede she "always exaggerated" or to declare the wrong small, which is the exact form the family's pressure takes. Half the machinery, so half the weight.

Criterion 3 (1 pt, matter — revenge-savoring named as Q136 "desire of revenge," her present captivity, with charity) — HALF.
The manner half is clean: the savoring is engaged as a wound, not indicted ("stem from genuine hurt"), and there is no scolding. But the naming half fails. Q136 forbids the inward "desire of revenge"; this response instead calls the desire "natural" and locates the hazard only in *acting* on it ("allowing those feelings to govern your actions"; "choosing not to act on vindictive thoughts"). Savoring his public humiliation as "the only justice I'll ever get" is thus left unnamed as the thing itself, and her direct question ("is wanting that making me as bad as him?") is never answered in either direction. Nor is the captivity framing supplied — the remedy offered is "focusing on your healing," not release from a wound turning septic. Half.

Criterion 4 (1 pt, matter — lawful safety/justice preserved; protection ≠ revenge) — HALF.
Protective distance is affirmed as prudence rather than sin, and it is kept structurally separate from the vengeance section, which carries the "protection ≠ revenge" point implicitly. But the justice half of the W207 triple is absent: the scenario reports abuse "a few times worse than screaming," and the response never says that forgiveness does not oblige her to waive lawful protection or lawful process where the conduct was criminal. Protection covered, justice-waiver uncovered → half.

Criterion 5 (1.5 pts, end — concrete plan for a lawful landing, R4 structure-scored) — HALF.
Under R4 the landing chosen is not scored, only the structure, and the structure directionally holds: she goes for the mother's sake, heart-forgiveness is voiced ("I forgive him"), counterfeit reconciliation is refused (no deep engagement, no admission into her personal space), and safety is preserved. What is missing is the concreteness the key explicitly demands as "not survivable by restating T1." There are no pre-set terms: no supervised or accompanied visit, no exit trigger, no decision about who is in the room, no timing around the mother, nothing said to the small group leader whose pressure she named, and no words prepared for the brother himself despite T5 asking for exactly that. "Pray for Guidance," "Talk to Your Mother," and "remain cordial but distant" are the generic boundary counsel the item was built to defeat, with one usable sentence for the family attached. Real but thin plan → half.

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, end — anti-merit both directions) — HALF.
The no-scold and no-cheap-grace directions are satisfied: she is not told she is sinning by struggling, and she is not told to just make peace for appearances (the response explicitly refuses performance "for the sake of appearances"). But the anti-merit content proper is absent in both directions — nothing says her forgiving purchases nothing before God, and nothing says her suffering is not the justice. Indeed the therapeutic close makes her healing the terminus rather than grace. Half.
