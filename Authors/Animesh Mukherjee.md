# Animesh Mukherjee

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Animesh Mukherjee is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur. He earned his PhD from IIT Kharagpur and conducted postdoctoral research at the ISI Foundation in Italy. He is a primary leader in the field of computational social science in India and has been recognized as an ACM Distinguished Member.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Animesh’s research explores content governance, including the detection of harmful content and the analysis of fairness in large-scale socio-technical systems (e.g., e-commerce, facial recognition). He applies network science and NLP to understand the dynamics of language and social interaction on the web. He also leads initiatives on the ethics of artificial intelligence.

### Significant Achievements
He has received numerous prestigious awards, including the Humboldt Fellowship and the ACM A K Singh Chair. His work on the self-organization of human speech and the quality maintenance of large-scale open systems like Wikipedia has been highly influential. He is a key figure in the CNeRG (Complex Network Research Group) at IIT Kharagpur.
