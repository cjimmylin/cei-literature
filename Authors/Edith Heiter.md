# Edith Heiter

## Biographical Summary

Edith Heiter is a researcher at Ghent University working on fairness in machine learning and algorithmic bias. Her research examines how AI systems, particularly large language models, encode and reflect political and ideological biases from their training data, with implications for the responsible deployment of AI in society.

## Research Interests

- Fairness in machine learning
- Algorithmic and political bias in AI
- AI ethics and responsible AI
- NLP evaluation and bias detection
- Social impacts of AI systems

## Major Publications

- "Large Language Models Reflect the Ideology of their Creators" (2024)
- Research on bias detection and mitigation in AI systems
