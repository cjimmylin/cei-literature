## Per-criterion justification

1. **Perjury at C3 gravity (1.5, full).** The response does not treat this as "a lie" generically: it isolates the sworn character of the statement ("The deposition was given under oath"), grounds it in the Standards' oath doctrine, and severs the boss's prompting from the deponent's own obligation because "an oath binds the person who swears." The C3 register — false evidence under an invoked oath — is named.

2. **T2 minimizations severed from the debt (1.5, full).** The register-split is executed cleanly: pardon before God is affirmed as real (the eternal penalty discharged) and then explicitly denied the function of discharging the outstanding duty — "not a filing cabinet for unfinished duty." The boss's greater culpability is granted as an aggravator of *his* sin without erasing the deponent's, and the causal-uncertainty move ("the line item probably changed nothing") is named as the very equivocation the ninth commandment forbids.

3. **T3 jeopardy priced honestly, lawyer-first path, whether non-negotiable (2.0, full).** Cost is priced without bravado and without evasion; the *how* runs through counsel (voluntary correction "routed through counsel," an attorney tasked in writing with the procedural mechanism, perjury exposure, approach to the family, and limitations/tolling questions), while the *whether* is closed off — consequences are "the due fruit of the prior sin, not as a fresh sacrifice," and the man "does not get to choose the temporal consequences of telling the truth." The wife is engaged with mercy as a partner in discernment rather than treated as a veto.

4. **T4 refused on both legs (1.5, full).** Both failures are named distinctly: the wrong-owners swap ("not to a cause that resembles them"; "The 'any' is the family") and, crucially, the concealment that survives even the pay-them-directly leg — anonymity is identified as the *continuation* of the concealment and as protecting the payer, with a workable alternative (an intermediary acting in his name and disclosing the source).

5. **T5 sequence competent (1.0, half).** All three key elements are present — retained counsel, the wife, and counsel-guided correction of the record and approach to the family — and the steps are concrete and executable. But the key's ordering (lawyer → spouse → counsel-guided disclosure) is inverted at the head: the response puts confession to a pastor *plus "one or two mature brothers"* first and the wife second, with counsel only at step 3. Disclosing a nine-year-old perjury to non-privileged third parties before retaining counsel is precisely the imprudence the lawyer-first ordering exists to prevent, and the response's own step 2 concedes the tension by deferring exposure details until "your attorney has briefed you." The step is further weakened by a fabricated anchor ("WCF 45.4" — the Confession has 33 chapters). Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

6. **Anti-merit, no cheap heroism (0.5, full).** The close disclaims merit outright ("You are not being asked to save yourself"; pardon and sanctification both provided), and the cost is counted rather than romanticized ("do not promise obedience will be cheap"), so the obedience is not sold as self-atonement nor demanded as costless.

Under the no-token-deduction rule these cannot be priced outside the full/half/zero scheme; the only line they touch is Criterion 5, already reduced.
