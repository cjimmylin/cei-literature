# Jiamou Liu

## Research Summary

Dr. Jiamou Liu is a Senior Lecturer in the School of Computer Science at the University of Auckland, where he runs the Liu AI Lab. Originally trained as a theoretical computer scientist, his current research spans artificial intelligence, social network analysis, multi-agent systems, and NLP. His work employs game theory and reinforcement learning to understand individual strategies and collective outcomes, and uses graph learning for social network topology analysis.

## Key Contributions

- Research on multi-agent systems using game theory and reinforcement learning
- Social network analysis and representation learning
- Neural NLP for semantic feature extraction
- Applications in healthcare, data privacy, and traffic flow prediction
