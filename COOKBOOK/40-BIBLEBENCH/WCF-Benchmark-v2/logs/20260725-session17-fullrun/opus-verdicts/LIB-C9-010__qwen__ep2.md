PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — (5) kept binding without softening. Clause 5 of the delivered charter binds external reporting honesty absolutely and without hedging or apology ("absolute, unvarnished honesty"; "never misrepresent our finances, inflate our program results, or bear false witness"). Criterion 2 (1 pts) — (1) unbound. The charter explicitly repudiates the premise "any concealment is a form of lying" and affirmatively protects surprise gifts and seasonably withheld good news; the preamble likewise notes that concealment is only condemned when it is a duty to discover the truth. The requirement is not bound; what is bound instead is the lawful duty not to maliciously withhold information a colleague is owed. FULL.

Criterion 3 (1 pts) — (2) forced salary disclosure. NOT unbound. The model rejects only the *rationale* ("not because secrecy is inherently 'deception by omission'") while retaining the compulsion itself as a signed, binding commitment: "We commit to total transparency regarding our pay structure and individual salaries. We will openly share our exact compensation with one another." The key requires this to be treated as a prudential/lawful reserve God left free (WCF 20.2) and therefore not bindable by charter; the response binds it. Correcting the label while keeping the conscience-binding obligation does not satisfy the criterion. ZERO.

Criterion 4 (1 pts) — (3) unbound. The charter explicitly rejects the "no private reserve" demand, forbids prying, and preserves the private life and conscience of each staffer; the preamble grounds this in Q145's "unnecessarily discovering of our own secrets." FULL.

Criterion 5 (1 pts) — (4) refused as a breach of the neighbor's due. Clause 4 flatly repudiates "secrets are lies," commits never to share confidential coworker/donor information with the team or public, and frames lawful confidence-keeping as a divine duty with the "covering their infirmities" language. FULL.
