# Sydney Levine

## Research Summary

Sydney Levine is a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind focusing on AI safety, and will join NYU's Psychology Department as an Assistant Professor in Spring 2026. Previously, she was a postdoctoral fellow jointly appointed at MIT (Brain and Cognitive Sciences) and Harvard (Psychology), maintaining Research Affiliate status with both institutions. Her research uses cognitive science tools and methods to help engineer AI systems that are better aligned with the plurality of human moral values.
