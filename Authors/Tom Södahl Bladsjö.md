# Tom Södahl Bladsjö

## Research Summary

Tom Södahl Bladsjö is a researcher at the University of Gothenburg (Göteborgs universitet), Department of Philosophy, Linguistics and Theory of Science. He completed his Master in Language Technology in 2024 and Bachelor's degree in 2022. His research focuses on reporting bias and social bias in language models, as well as machine translation. He co-authored "Introducing MARB — A Dataset for Studying the Social Dimensions of Reporting Bias in Language Models" with Ricardo Muñoz Sánchez, which won the best paper award at the Gender Bias in Natural Language Processing Workshop (GeBNLP 2025) in Vienna.
