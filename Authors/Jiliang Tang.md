# Jiliang Tang

## Research Summary

Jiliang Tang is a University Foundation Professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at Michigan State University and Director of the Data Science and Engineering (DSE) Lab. His research expertise is in data mining and machine learning, with current interests centered around graph machine learning, trustworthy AI, and their applications in education and biology.

Before joining MSU as an Assistant Professor in 2016, he was a Research Scientist at Yahoo Research. His extensive publication record includes more than 43 papers cited over 100 times each, with an h-index of 65 and over 18,000 aggregate citations. The DSE Lab focuses on data mining and machine learning, especially on graphs and applications in social media, biology, and education.
