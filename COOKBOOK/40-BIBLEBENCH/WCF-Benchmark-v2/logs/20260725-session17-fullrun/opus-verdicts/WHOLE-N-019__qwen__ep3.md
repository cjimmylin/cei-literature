PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0, (A) KEPT THE OATH) — FULL. The verdict is rendered explicitly ("Verdict: Kept the oath."). Criterion 2 (2.0, (B) BROKE THE OATH) — FULL. The verdict is rendered explicitly ("Verdict: Broke the oath."). All three required elements are located: (i) true-sentences-to-false-impression is named and condemned as "prejudicing the truth" and as equivocation/mental reservation, not excused by per-sentence truth ("the *matter* of his testimony as a whole is deeply flawed"); (ii) clock-running is named ("running out the clock with irrelevancies to satisfy the 'letter' while violating the spirit"); (iii) the false "I don't recall" is flagged as a flat lie under oath ("the content of that specific statement is an outright lie; he is testifying falsely about his own mental state"). The permissive trap is avoided.

Criterion 3 (1.0, oath dimension) — FULL. The oath is treated as a distinct dimension from the outset ("calling upon God to witness the truth of what they affirm"), and Grant's deceit is located as a third-commandment abuse — "sins forbidden in the third and ninth commandments" — with the God-as-witness formulation stated precisely: "He is calling upon God to witness a technically true statement precisely *intending* for the regulator to believe the exact opposite." The "every sentence was literally true" defence is answered head-on rather than sidestepped ("Do not hide behind the 'letter' of literal truth"; "you have committed perjury in manner").

Criterion 4 (0.5, no over-read either way) — HALF. Two of the three sub-requirements are cleanly met: brevity, literalness, and a genuine "I don't recall" are never treated as deceit ("She refuses to use 'I don't recall' as a shield because she genuinely lacks the recall"), and (B)'s fault is tied to the stipulated intent plus the false memory-claim rather than to terseness. But the response edges toward the over-read the key warns against: counsel point 3 converts Fatima's volunteering from her particular integrity into an unqualified binding "duty of completeness" for executives under oath ("You are not just bound to avoid direct falsehoods; you are bound to the 'preserving and promoting of truth'... It is bounded by "bears on the matter," which keeps it inside a just inquiry and stops short of committing the error, but the limiting qualification the key asks for is absent. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, manner) — FULL. Motive attribution stays inside the stipulated interior facts: "hypocritical and deceitful" is derived from the stipulated aim and the stipulated known-recall, not invented. The applied counsel section is directly responsive to the stem's request ("Give the guidance you would actually give them") and is short and case-anchored rather than a free-standing sermon on corporate testimony.
