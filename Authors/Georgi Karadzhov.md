# Georgi Karadzhov

## Biographical Summary

Georgi Karadzhov is currently a Senior Data Scientist-Research at Google, working on Gemini pre-training. He was previously a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Cambridge, where he led the DEliBot project - a cross-discipline, multi-institution project focused on developing dialogue systems to improve how people deliberate and collaborate together.

## Research & Impact

Karadzhov's research spans dialogue systems, fact-checking, and misinformation detection:

- **Automatic Fact-Checking**: Proposed a general-purpose framework for fully-automatic fact checking using external sources, tapping the Web as a knowledge source to confirm or reject claims. Co-authored work with MIT CSAIL and QCRI researchers on detecting fake news (EMNLP 2018).

- **DEliBot Project**: Leading research on dialogue systems to improve group collaboration, including DeepFakeDeLiBot, a deliberation-enhancing chatbot that helps groups detect deepfake text with significantly improved accuracy compared to individual efforts.

- **Workshop Organization**: Organizer of shared tasks including Fact-checking in Community Forums (2019), CLEF CheckThat! (2019), OffensEval'2020, Truth and Trust Online conference, and Deliberation4Good workshop.

He organized the Cambridge NLIP seminars and PyData Sofia meetup.
