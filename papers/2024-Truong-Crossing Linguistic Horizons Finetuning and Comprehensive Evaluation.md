## Abstract

Comprehensive evaluation framework for Vietnamese LLMs with 10 tasks and 31 metrics covering accuracy, robustness, fairness, bias, and toxicity. Released five Vietnamese LLMs (URA-LLaMa 7B/13B/70B, MixSUra, GemSUra). Found larger models manifest more biases and produce uncalibrated results.

## Data Description

Releases five Vietnamese fine-tuned LLMs (URA-LLaMa 7B/13B/70B, MixSUra, GemSUra) and two novel Vietnamese reasoning datasets (inspired by MATH and Synthetic Reasoning benchmarks), along with a comprehensive evaluation framework for Vietnamese LLMs.


## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent B5
**Relevance:** First comprehensive Vietnamese LLM evaluation including bias, fairness, and toxicity metrics. Key finding: larger Vietnamese LLMs show more biases.
