# Allison Koenecke

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Allison Koenecke is an Assistant Professor of Information Science at Cornell Tech. Her work is at the intersection of computer science and social science, with a focus on identifying and mitigating inequities in automated systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Allison is a leading researcher in the field of algorithmic fairness. Her work often uncovers racial and social disparities in widely-used technologies, such as speech-to-text systems. She advocates for more inclusive data collection and evaluation practices to ensure that AI benefits all segments of society, particularly in critical domains like healthcare and public services.
