## Abstract

A new offensive language corpus in Hebrew consisting of 15,881 tweets labeled with five classes (abusive, hate, violence, pornographic, none offensive) by Arabic-Hebrew bilingual speakers. Fine-tuned HeBERT and AlephBERT models for offensive content detection.
