# Alireza S. Ziabari

## Biographical Summary

Alireza S. Ziabari is a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southern California (USC). His research leverages NLP to study human values and social behavior at scale, working within the intersection of computer science and psychology.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alireza is a key contributor to the development of the *Moral Foundations Reddit Corpus*, a large-scale dataset for analyzing moral discourse in online environments. His work focuses on building models that can accurately detect and classify moral sentiment, helping researchers understand how ethical values propagate through social networks.
