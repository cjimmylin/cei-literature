# Zhuang Chen

## Research Summary

Zhuang Chen is a researcher focusing on Natural Language Processing, particularly Theory of Mind in Large Language Models. He is the lead author of ToMBench (ACL 2024), a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Theory of Mind in LLMs featuring 8 tasks and 31 abilities in social cognition. His research revealed that even advanced LLMs like GPT-4 lag behind human performance by over 10% points, highlighting critical gaps in AI's understanding of social cognition and mental state attribution.
