# Alexander Pan

## Biographical Summary

Alexander Pan is a researcher at UC Berkeley and a core member of the Center for AI Safety (CAIS). His work is dedicated to identifying and mitigating the foundational risks posed by advanced artificial intelligence systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexander's research tackles the problem of "reward hacking" and inner alignment—ensuring that AI systems do not pursue unintended strategies to maximize rewards at the expense of human safety. He is a lead author of the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark and a critical voice in identifying "safetywashing" practices in the industry.

## Major Publications
- [Do the Rewards Justify the Means? Measuring Action-State Reward Hijacking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00000) (2023) - *Quantifying hidden risks in alignment training.*
- [The WMDP Benchmark: Measuring and Reducing Malicious Use With Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00000) (2024) - *Standardizing bio-security evaluations for LLMs.*
