# Roman Krzanowski

## Research Summary

Assistant Professor at The Pontifical University of John Paul II in Krakow, Poland. Graduate degrees in engineering, philosophy, and information science from universities in Poland, UK, Canada, and USA. Research interests include philosophy of information and informatics, ontology and metaphysics of computation science, epistemology of robotics, philosophical foundations of AI, and ethics. Author of "Ontological Information" published by World Scientific.
