## Abstract

Curated dataset of 226 Burmese hate speech posts from Facebook collected during the 2020 Myanmar national elections. Develops process for coordinating ML work in low-resource language settings with context experts. 873 posts categorized with toxicity scores. PRIMARY Burmese hate speech dataset. Directly relevant to toxicity and values evaluation in Myanmar context. Addresses real-world hate speech challenges during critical political events.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Burmese Hate Speech Facebook Dataset

## Data Description

A dataset of 873 Burmese (Myanmar) social media posts annotated for hate speech toxicity, collected from Facebook during the 2020 Myanmar national elections, curated with assistance from context experts.
