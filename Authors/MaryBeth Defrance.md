# MaryBeth Defrance

## Research Summary

MaryBeth Defrance is a PhD student in the Department of Electronics and Information Systems at Ghent University, specializing in fairness in AI and algorithmic fairness. As lead author of ABCFair (NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Track), Defrance developed an adaptable benchmark approach for comparing fairness methods that addresses the critical problem of inconsistent evaluation across pre-, in-, and post-processing bias mitigation techniques. ABCFair allows adaptation to real-world problem settings, enabling proper comparability between methods for any use case, and was applied to both large-scale traditional datasets and dual-label (biased and unbiased) datasets to sidestep the fairness-accuracy trade-off. Her research also includes work on "Maximal Fairness," which aims to identify maximal sets of commonly used fairness measures that can be simultaneously satisfied, addressing gaps left by the fairness "Impossibility Theorem." This work standardizes the evaluation of fairness algorithms and provides essential tools for practitioners navigating the complex landscape of fairness interventions.
