# David M. Blei

## Biographical Summary

David M. Blei is a Professor of Statistics and Computer Science at Columbia University. He is one of the most influential researchers in probabilistic machine learning, best known for developing Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) for topic modeling and pioneering work on variational inference methods. His research has had enormous impact across machine learning, NLP, and computational social science, with over 100,000 citations.

## Research Interests

- Probabilistic topic models and text analysis
- Variational inference and scalable Bayesian methods
- Causal inference and observational studies
- Bayesian deep learning
- Applied machine learning in science

## Major Publications

- "Latent Dirichlet Allocation" (JMLR 2003) - Over 50,000 citations
- "Variational Inference: A Review for Statisticians" (JASA 2017)
- "Build, Compute, Critique, Repeat: Data Analysis with Latent Variable Models"
- "Probabilistic Topic Models" (Communications of the ACM 2012)
- "Evaluating the Moral Beliefs Encoded in LLMs" (2023)
