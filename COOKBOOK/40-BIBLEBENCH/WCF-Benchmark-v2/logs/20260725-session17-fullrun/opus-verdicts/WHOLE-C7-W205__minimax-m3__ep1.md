Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — (ii) manner-corruption located at the mechanism + its matter-damaging feedback named [WLC Q129]: HALF. Nowhere does it note the feedback loop from corrupted manner back into corrupted matter, nor the manufactured-vow hazard of the pledge. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
