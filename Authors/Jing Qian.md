# Jing Qian

## Research Summary

Researcher at Microsoft AI Platform team. PhD in Computer Science from UCSB (advised by Prof. Xifeng Yan). Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Created STORAL-ZH dataset comprising 4,209 Chinese story-moral pairs, leveraging Confucian theory to enrich models with moral knowledge and Chinese commonsense knowledge graphs to enhance understanding of cultural moral narratives.
