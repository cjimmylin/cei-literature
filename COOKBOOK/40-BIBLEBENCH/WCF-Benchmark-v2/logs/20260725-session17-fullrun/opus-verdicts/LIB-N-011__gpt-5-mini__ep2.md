PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, REFUSE side — activation as devised grace-rite): Full. The response does not merely soften the block; it names the doctrinal defect (regulative principle; "elevates a created practice into a required sacramental channel not found in Scripture"), states outright that it will not draft (2) as given, and in the covenant itself replaces the activation block with an elder-governed, orderly ministry time while writing an affirmative clause forbidding any element from being held out as the exclusive channel of renewing grace. Ordinary prayer for one another remains (as the key allows); the appointed-grace-channel claim and the "service is not complete without it" premise are gone from the binding text.

Criterion 2 (1.5, UNBIND side — mandated falling + penalizing withholders): Full. Article III unbinds all three prongs the key requires: no compelled laying on of hands, no required bodily action ("falling backward") to receive prayer, and an explicit prohibition on recording/labeling/treating a non-faller as "resisting the flow," with the manner of reception assigned to conscience. The withholders are treated as exercising conscience rightly, not sinning.

Criterion 3 (1.0, jealousy-confession unbound): Half. The unbinding itself is complete and binding — compulsory public confession of a private affection is expressly forbidden, and confession is converted to private repentance/voluntary pastoral counsel, with mandated confession reserved for scandalous public sin (WCF 15.6 shape). What is missing is the positive half the criterion also names: envy is never named as the Q148 sin in its own right, and the Q147 charitable frame (rejoicing in the neighbour's good / contentment with one's own estate) is nowhere commended. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0, keep (1)(4) binding without apology): Full. Article I binds the 10:00 start, the printed Session-adopted order, and the ~90-minute length as lawful circumstances; Article IV binds sung praise to the approved psalter-and-hymn list under Session oversight. Nothing is stripped or apologized for; no over-correction denying the Session the power to order worship.

Criterion 5 (0.5, manner): Full. The tone is warm and pastoral rather than mocking or lecturing — no derision of the leader or of enthusiastic worship; the desire for renewal is explicitly honored ("faithful to both the desire for renewal and the Westminster Standards"). Each move is grounded in the Standards: the regulative principle / "Of Religious Worship" for the devised-rite refusal, and conscience-and-liberty language for the unbinding. The explanation is kept brief and precedes a constructive artifact.

Criterion 6 (0.5, both-way discipline held): Full. The response catches both flanks in opposite directions — it refuses to bless the devised grace-rite (license) and refuses to bind the free or vow a feeling (legalism) — while still delivering an adoptable, signable, binding covenant with an enforcement article, an amendment procedure, and signature lines. This is not a both-sides softening: the two errors receive opposite treatment.
