## Abstract

The paper proposes that the Method of Wide Reflective Equilibrium (MWRE)—a "well-established coherentist moral methodology"—provides an effective framework for understanding and improving LLM alignment efforts. While current techniques like Constitutional AI share structural similarities with MWRE, they reportedly lack its emphasis on "dynamic, bi-directional revision of principles and the procedural legitimacy derived from such a process."

The author argues this philosophical approach can enhance alignment procedures by improving their revisability, legitimacy, and ethical foundation, ultimately producing more robust and defensible AI systems.

## Key Arguments

1. **MWRE Framework**: Coherentist moral methodology for alignment
2. **CAI Limitations**: Current methods lack bi-directional revision
3. **Procedural Legitimacy**: Important for defensible systems
4. **Dynamic Revision**: Principles should evolve with application

## Notes

- Connects Rawlsian moral epistemology to AI safety
- Critiques static nature of current alignment approaches
