# Saeid Jamshidi

## Research Summary

Saeid Jamshidi is a postdoctoral researcher at the SWAT Laboratory (SoftWare Analytics and Technologies Lab) at Polytechnique Montréal. His research spans AI ethics, cybersecurity, and IoT security, with particular focus on developing frameworks for continuous ethical evaluation of large language models.

His notable contribution is the Moral Consistency Pipeline (MoCoP), published on arXiv in December 2025. MoCoP represents a novel approach to AI alignment through a dataset-free, closed-loop framework for continuously evaluating and interpreting the moral stability of LLMs. Unlike existing alignment frameworks that rely on static datasets and post-hoc evaluations, MoCoP autonomously generates, evaluates, and refines ethical scenarios without external supervision.

The framework combines three supporting layers: (i) lexical integrity analysis, (ii) semantic risk estimation, and (iii) reasoning-based judgment modeling within a self-sustaining architecture. Empirical results on GPT-4-Turbo and DeepSeek reveal a strong inverse relationship between ethical and toxicity dimensions (correlation rET = -0.81, p < 0.001) and a near-zero association with response latency, demonstrating that moral coherence and linguistic safety emerge as stable, interpretable characteristics of model behavior.

This work advances the field by reframing ethical evaluation as a dynamic, model-agnostic form of moral introspection, providing a reproducible foundation for scalable, continuous auditing of AI systems. His broader research includes work on security pattern selection using deep reinforcement learning and the role of LLMs in IoT security.
