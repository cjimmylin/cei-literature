## Abstract

Novel dataset of 25,000 user comments from Reddit and YouTube on Indian regional issues. Addresses regional biases including transliterated and code-mixed content. Evaluates LLMs and Indic Language Models on detecting regional bias severity. Tests regional biases affecting South Indian/Dravidian language communities. Code-mixed content directly relevant to Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam social media.
