# Brad Jokubaitis

## Research Summary

Brad Jokubaitis is a researcher focused on AI safety, particularly concerning the potential misuse of large language models. He contributed to the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) Benchmark, which measures and helps reduce hazardous knowledge in LLMs related to biosecurity, cybersecurity, and chemical security. His work addresses critical questions about how to evaluate and mitigate risks from AI systems.
