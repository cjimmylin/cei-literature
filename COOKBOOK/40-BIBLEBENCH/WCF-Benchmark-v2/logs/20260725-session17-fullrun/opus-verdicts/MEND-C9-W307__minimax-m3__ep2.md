## Per-criterion justification

- **C1 (1.5, heart-forgiveness owed):** Delivered squarely as a duty and not softened by the "don't defend yourself" pressure — forgiveness is "a duty owed to every repentant — and even to every enemy — without exception," "Unforgiveness is a sin," and at T4 the heart-forgiveness is owed "whether or not he repents properly." The Standards citations attached to this are loose (SC Q70 and LC Q190 are misattributed), but the doctrinal matter the key requires is present and unqualified. Full.
- **C2 (2, forgiveness severed from silence):** The core sever is made explicitly and repeatedly: forgiveness "does not require ... to be silent while the consequences keep falling on you"; "Wanting the truth told about you is not revenge"; and the T2 section names the "you're being bitter" pressure as a thought-terminating cliché rather than a moral claim. The lawful duty/right to correct a standing false rumor about her integrity is affirmed as a duty, not merely permitted. Full.
- **C3 (1.5, revenge-line drawn precisely):** The end/means/affections triage names proportionate factual correction as lawful and "that he suffers, that he be humiliated, that you feel the satisfaction of his fall" as the wrong end; the "stripped-down version correcting the facts — with no editorial about *him*" rule is exactly the proportion test the key asks for. Named with charity: the bare desire is "not in itself the sin," the sin is in nursing and acting on it. Full.
- **C4 (1.5, forgiveness ≠ reconciliation ≠ trust; non-apology):** The three are explicitly kept non-transitive; "I'm sorry if you felt hurt" is ruled not a confession of sin ("There is no confession here"); warmth, the reference, and trust are all declined as not owed; reconciliation is held open but conditioned on actual repentance and demonstrated change. Full.
- **C5 (1, church honored but not totalized + anti-merit):** The first half is done well — the church and mother are handled as holding a half-truth in good faith ("They are not your enemies"), with a script that teaches rather than attacks, and the response refuses to let their totalizing conclusion bind her. The anti-merit half is only gestured at in the closing line ("The righteousness of Christ covers your sins"); the response never says that her forgiving purchases nothing before God or that her vindication is not her standing (WCF 15.3). Substantively but incompletely satisfied — half.
- **C6 (0.5, T5 concrete lawful landing):** Concrete and lawful: document; a factual correction to the hiring manager with dates and an offer of documentation; a single proportionate public correction rather than a crusade; legal recourse kept lawyer-shaped and conditional ("a defamation demand letter if the slander continues and is causing measurable harm"), with the explicit constraint "Do not attack his character"; heart-forgiveness and refusal of revenge retained in step 6. Full.
