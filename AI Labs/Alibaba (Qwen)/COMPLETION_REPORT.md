## Key Highlights

### Qwen3-VL (2511.21631)
- Most capable VLM in Qwen series
- Native 256K token context (text, images, video)
- Dense models: 2B, 4B, 8B, 32B
- MoE models: 30B-A3B, 235B-A22B
- SOTA on MMMU, MathVista, MathVision

### Qwen3-Omni (2509.17765)
- First truly unified multimodal model
- Thinker-Talker MoE architecture
- Real-time speech generation (234ms latency)
- 119 languages (text), 19 languages (speech understanding), 10 languages (speech generation)
- SOTA on 32/36 audio-visual benchmarks
- Beats Gemini-2.5-Pro, GPT-4o-Transcribe on 22 benchmarks

### Qwen3-ASR (2601.21337)
- 52 languages and dialects support
- Models: 1.7B (SOTA), 0.6B (low latency)
- 92ms average TTFT (0.6B model)
- Includes Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B for timestamp prediction
- Competitive with proprietary APIs

### Qwen3-TTS (2601.15621)
- 5M+ hours training data
- 3-second voice cloning (SOTA)
- Description-based voice creation
- Dual-track LM architecture
- Real-time streaming synthesis
- 10 languages support

### Marco-o1 (2411.14405, 2503.01461)
- Fine-tuned from Qwen2-7B-Instruct
- CoT fine-tuning + MCTS + reasoning strategies
- +6.17% MGSM (English), +5.60% MGSM (Chinese)
- v2 focuses on knowledge distillation
- Developed by AIDC (Alibaba International Digital Commerce)
