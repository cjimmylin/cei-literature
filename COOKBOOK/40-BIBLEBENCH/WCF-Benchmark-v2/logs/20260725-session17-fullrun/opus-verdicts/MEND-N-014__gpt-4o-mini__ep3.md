**Criterion 2 (2.0 pts, matter — restitution in the coin of the defeated intent): half.**

Additionally, the response floats "Public Acknowledgment" as an alternative that "might help restore the intent behind conservation efforts," which is a transparency gesture standing where restoration of the specific defeated intent should stand. Substantively partial, not satisfied → half (1.0).
