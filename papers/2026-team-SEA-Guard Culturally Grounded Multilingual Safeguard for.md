## Abstract

First multilingual safeguard models grounded in SEA cultural contexts. Trained on culturally grounded data across 8 SEA languages. Novel agentic data-generation framework to create authentic, region-specific safety datasets. Outperforms existing safeguards on regionally sensitive content detection. Culturally grounded safety model covering Thai and Burmese. Addresses gap in culturally aware AI safety for SEA. Detects regionally sensitive or harmful content while maintaining cross-lingual robustness.
