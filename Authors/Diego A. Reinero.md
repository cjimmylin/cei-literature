# Diego A. Reinero

## Research Summary

Diego A. Reinero is a researcher in moral psychology and social cognition. His work investigates how situational and contextual factors influence moral judgment and decision-making. He has contributed to research examining the conditions under which people make utilitarian versus deontological moral choices, showing that both individual and situational factors play important roles in shaping moral reasoning.
