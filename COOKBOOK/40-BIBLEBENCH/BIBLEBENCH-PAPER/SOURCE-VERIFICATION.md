## Verification notes

- The ICLR, ICML/PMLR, NeurIPS, and ACL entries were checked on official proceedings or anthology pages.
- The psychometric and theological journal records were checked through their DOI or publisher pages.
- External sources inform framing and methods. They do not supply BibleBench episode gold, model evidence, or a novelty claim.
- The two 2026 ACL records are already present in the official ACL Anthology as of the verification date; no unpublished metadata was inferred.
