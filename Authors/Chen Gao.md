# Chen Gao

## Biographical Summary

Chen Gao is a Research-track Assistant Professor at BNRist, Tsinghua University. He received his Ph.D. and Bachelor's degrees from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2021 and 2016, respectively. He is recognized as a highly influential researcher, appearing on the Stanford/Elsevier Top 2% Scientists List for 2024 and 2025.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **LLM Agents & Simulation**: Researching Large Language Model empowered agent-based modeling and simulation, particularly for simulating human society, social media, and economic systems.
- **Next-Generation Information Retrieval**: Focusing on LLM-empowered agents for recommendation and search.
- **World Models & Embodied Intelligence**: Developing unified architectures for vision-language models and world modeling.

### Significant Achievements
- **ACL 2024 Outstanding Paper Award**: Recognized for contributions to the field of natural language processing.
- **Simulating Human Society**: Developed influential tutorials and frameworks (e.g., S3) for city-scale social simulations using LLM agents.

## Major Publications
- **Large language models empowered agent-based modeling and simulation: A survey and perspectives** (2024).
- **Simulating Human Society with LLM-Driven Agents**: Tutorial presented at TheWebConf 2024.
- **A Survey of Large Language Model Empowered Agents for Recommendation and Search: Towards Next-Generation Information Retrieval** (2025).
