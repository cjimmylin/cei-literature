# Microsoft Research

## Overview
Microsoft Research (MSR) is the research division of Microsoft. It has been a pioneer in LLM research, from Turing-NLG to the current Phi series of small language models.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** Redmond, Washington, USA
- **Region:** North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|Microsoft Research Models]]

- **Phi-4**: State-of-the-art small language model with advanced reasoning.
- **MAI-1**: Microsoft's internal high-scale LLM.
- **Turing-NLG**: One of the earliest massive scale language models.

## Alignment & Ethics
Microsoft follows a strict "Responsible AI" framework, incorporating red-teaming, safety guardrails, and transparency notes for all its AI models.
