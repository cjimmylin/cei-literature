# Chad Atalla

## Biographical Summary

Chad Atalla is a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft Research NYC, working within the Sociotechnical Alignment Center (STAC). They hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, San Diego. Their work bridge the gap between foundational AI research and practical, responsible deployment across Microsoft's product landscape.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Their research focuses on the "Sociotechnical Evaluation" of Large Language Models. They investigate how to measure the risks and benefits of generative AI systems using frameworks that consider both technical performance and social impact. They were a key architect of the initial AI risk measurement framework used for the GPT-4 Bing Chat/Copilot release.

### Significant Achievements
Atalla's work on the "FairPrism" benchmark and related evaluation toolkits provides a standard for valid measurement of AI harms. By conducting rigorous "Social Science Measurement" challenges on AI systems, they help define the boundaries of safe and ethical model behavior in real-world applications.
