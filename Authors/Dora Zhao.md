## Research Summary

Dora Zhao is a PhD student at Stanford University working on AI alignment and pluralistic approaches to AI development. Her research focuses on how data curation choices affect AI system behavior and alignment with diverse human values. She has contributed to understanding how to build AI systems that can represent and respect value pluralism rather than defaulting to a single moral framework.

- Pluralistic AI and value diversity
- Data curation and dataset design
