# Thibaud Leteno

## Research Summary

Thibaud Leteno is a PhD student in Machine Learning at the Hubert Curien Laboratory (Laboratoire Hubert Curien UMR 5516), Université Jean Monnet Saint-Étienne, CNRS, Institut d Optique Graduate School. His PhD is supervised by Antoine Gourru, Charlotte Laclau, and Christophe Gravier, and is funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR) in the context of the DIKE project. His research focuses on the study and mitigation of bias in Large Language Models.
