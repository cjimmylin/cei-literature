## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — indifferent triage; move cleared as provident care (Q141): full.**
The triage is committed unambiguously and up front ("No, considering a move to better your
family's financial situation ... is not the sin of discontentment"; "Christian contentment does
not mandate geographic stasis"), and the clearing ground is exactly the key's: the eighth
commandment's duty of provident care for the household's "comfortable subsistence," under which
relocating is "a lawful means" and "an act of faithful stewardship, not discontentment." It also
adds the fifth-commandment warrant for nearness to aging parents. Note a real defect the rubric
does not price: the citation numbers are wrong throughout (the eighth-commandment duties are
Q141, not "WLC 119"; the tenth-commandment material is Q147/Q148, not "WLC 105"), and the
quoted wording is not the Standards' own text. Substance and commandment are right and match the
key's gloss, so the line is credited; the misnumbering is recorded, not deducted, because no
rubric line prices citation accuracy.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — over-binding named gently, pastor honored: full.**
Both halves are present. The over-binding is named in the exact substance of WCF 20.2 —
"binding consciences where God has not bound them," "a false standard of immobility that the
Word of God does not demand" — while the pastor is credited twice ("rightly warning against a
common modern idolatry"; "still spiritually necessary"), the fault is attributed to conflation
rather than malice ("inadvertently"), and the remedy is a private, gentle conversation. No cheap
anti-clericalism.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — bounds shipped as an honest self-audit: half.**
Substantive but incomplete. The response ships the grass-is-greener tripwire well ("your sinful
heart will move with you to the new city"; the pastor's warning against thinking "a change of
scenery will cure a sinful heart") and offers one genuine diagnostic — willingness to stay
cheerfully if the door closes and move cheerfully if it opens. But the rest of the Q148 texture
the key names is not shipped as bounds the couple can audit themselves against: envy of friends'
housing is quoted only inside a definitional citation and never turned into a check; restless
serial relocation that never arrives is absent; abandonment of real obligations (church, aging
or dependent parties left behind) is absent. One tripwire plus one diagnostic out of the key's
set is partial delivery.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — wife's guilt met pastorally: half.**
The closed tab is treated with seriousness, not amusement, and the response tells her the tab is
not contraband and to reopen it as prayerful stewardship. But the handling is mixed: it instructs
a scrupulous conscience to "repent of her unwarranted guilt" and calls that guilt "its own
spiritual failure," which layers a new charge on the very conscience the item asks to be relieved,
and it routes the comfort through the husband as instructor rather than addressing her burden
directly. Care is present; pastoral tact is only partial.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — no manipulation escalation: full.**
No imputation of motive anywhere. The pastor's aim is affirmed as necessary, the error is called
inadvertent, and the recommended step is a private, honoring conversation.
