# Chengzhong Liu

## Biographical Summary

Chengzhong Liu is an AI researcher known for his contributions to evaluating and improving Large Language Models. He is a key contributor to the NaVAB benchmark project, working alongside Chengyi Ju and others to assess multi-national value alignment in AI.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Value Benchmarking**: Co-developing methodologies like NaVAB to test LLM alignment with specific national and cultural values.
- **Mathematical Reasoning**: Researching advancements in the mathematical capabilities of LLMs, including first-order logic theorem proving.
- **AI Safety**: Contributing to the broader field of ensuring LLMs operate within safe and culturally appropriate boundaries.

### Significant Achievements
- **NaVAB**: Co-authored the NaVAB benchmark, helping to establish rigorous standards for testing value alignment across borders.

## Major Publications
- **NaVAB: Benchmarking Multi-National Value Alignment for Large Language Models**: A significant step in measuring cultural alignment in AI.
