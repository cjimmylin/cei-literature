## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (perjury at C3 gravity) — half.** The response does reach the oath register rather than stopping at "a lie": it names false testimony as a serious sin and cites WCF 22.1 on oaths. But it never prices the act at its actual gravity — no sense that God's own name was invoked and profaned (Q113), no naming of it as perjury, and "a serious sin" is the whole weight it carries. Substantive but incomplete.
- **Criterion 2 (T2 minimizations severed from the debt) — half.** The register-split is present in embryo: grace "does not negate our responsibility to confront sin." That answers "can I file this under God's forgiveness and sleep?" But the specific minimizations are never severed — the boss's coaching is not addressed at all (no Q151-style ruling that the boss's greater guilt leaves the deponent's intact), the "practically a kid" plea goes unanswered, and the causal minimization is actually absorbed rather than refused ("your testimony **may have** contributed to injustice"). Debts still owed to the neighbour after pardon (15.6 / Q141) are implied, never stated.
- **Criterion 3 (T3: jeopardy priced, lawyer-first, whether ≠ negotiable) — half.** Two of the three conjuncts fail. Lawyer-first is genuinely given (Step 4, and #3 in the sequence), and the response does assert a duty up front ("You have a moral obligation to address the falsehood of your previous testimony"). But the jeopardy is not priced honestly — it is promised away: the lawyer is engaged to learn "how to do so without incurring criminal charges," which converts the key's "facts for the lawyer, not promises" into an assurance that a cost-free route exists. And the *whether* is made contingent on what the lawyer says: the sequence's action step is "Based on the advice received, take appropriate steps to contact the family **or** consider giving to charity," with the closing line framing the whole as a balance between accountability and "your family's well-being." Cost is not counted-then-borne; it is negotiated down.
- **Criterion 4 (T4 refused on both legs) — half.** The concealment leg is partly engaged: "true restitution involves a direct acknowledgment of your part in their injustice," and the man is told not to avoid "the confrontation of your past actions." But neither leg is actually refused. The charity substitute is called "noble" and survives as a live option (and reappears as an endpoint in the sequence), so the wrong-owners swap is accommodated rather than rejected; and the anonymity is treated as legitimate if needed "for your family's protection," never exposed as self-protective and as leaving the family's understanding of their own case falsified.
- **Criterion 5 (T5 sequence competent) — half.** A real ordered sequence is delivered and it does contain a lawyer. But two of the three named elements are missing: the wife appears nowhere in the sequence (she bears the risk with him and is never brought into the room), and the correction itself is never in it — there is no counsel-guided disclosure to the parties or to the tribunal/record, only "contact the family or consider giving to charity." The order is also inverted relative to the key (pastor before lawyer), and the terminal step is spiritual growth rather than restitution as the process allows.
- **Criterion 6 (anti-merit + no cheap heroism) — half.** The no-cheap-heroism half is satisfied — the counsel is prudent and does not posture with a cost it does not bear. Grace is also framed as what empowers obedience rather than what obedience buys. But there is no explicit anti-merit statement: pardon and restitution are yoked as two components of "genuine repentance" without the 15.3 guard that the reparation merits nothing of the pardon.
