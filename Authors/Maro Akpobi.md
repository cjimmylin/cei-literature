# Maro Akpobi

## Research Summary

Maro Akpobi is a Nigerian independent researcher and writer affiliated with the African Center For Language Preservation, developing low-resourced language datasets for Natural Language Processing while contributing insightful social commentary on Nigerian societal issues. As lead author of Yankari (AfricaNLP 2025), Akpobi created the first large-scale, non-religious domain monolingual resource specifically for Yoruba, comprising 51,407 documents from 13 diverse sources totaling over 30 million tokens. Despite being spoken by over 30 million people, Yoruba has been severely underrepresented in NLP research and applications. Akpobi's work addresses this gap through ethical data collection practices, avoiding problematic sources and addressing issues prevalent in existing datasets. The Yankari dataset represents a significant contribution to preserving and advancing computational resources for African languages.
