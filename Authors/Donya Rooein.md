## Affiliation
Researcher at University of Tehran

## Research Summary

Donya Rooein is a researcher working on natural language processing and cultural aspects of AI systems. Her research examines how AI systems handle culturally-specific social norms and expectations, contributing to understanding the challenges of developing culturally-aware NLP systems. She has worked on projects exploring how language models navigate questions about social appropriateness across different cultural contexts.
