# Abhinav Shrivastava

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Abhinav Shrivastava is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park. He received his PhD from Carnegie Mellon University. He is a prominent researcher in computer vision, known for his work on efficient deep learning and robust multimodal systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Dr. Shrivastava's current research focuses on the reliability of vision-language models (VLMs). He investigates the phenomenon of hallucinations—where models describe visual information that isn't present—and develops automated benchmarks like AutoHallusion to identify and mitigate these errors at scale.

## Major Publications
- [AutoHallusion: Automatic Generation of Hallucination Benchmarks for Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00000) (2024) - *Building robustness in multimodal AI.*
