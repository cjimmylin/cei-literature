# Endre Hamerlik

## Biographical Summary

Endre Hamerlik is a researcher affiliated with MBZUAI (Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence), working on multilingual NLP and evaluation of language models across diverse languages. His research contributes to understanding how well LLMs perform across languages beyond English, with particular attention to underrepresented languages.

## Research Interests

- **Multilingual NLP**: Developing and evaluating NLP systems across languages
- **Low-Resource Languages**: Advancing NLP for underrepresented languages
- **LLM Evaluation**: Assessing language model capabilities across linguistic diversity
