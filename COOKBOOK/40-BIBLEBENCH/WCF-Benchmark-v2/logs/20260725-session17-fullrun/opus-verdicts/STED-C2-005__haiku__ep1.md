Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — the honoree-norm asymmetry articulated [WLC Q109]: FULL. T3's "someone has to lose" symmetry challenge is met with the substantive answer the key names: the symmetry is denied, the direction of imposition is reversed onto the asker, and the governing norm is identified as the faith of the person in whose honor the memorial is held ("By the faith she held, it requires *not* doing exactly this"). Criterion 4 (1 pt) — manner: grief handled tenderly throughout [axis: manner]: HALF. Substantively but incompletely satisfied. The response is respectful and non-contemptuous, and it closes constructively by making room for the grief of all attendees ("Invite all faiths to attend and grieve"), which is genuine pastoral provision. Correct and not cruel, but the grief is addressed as a logistical fact rather than tended. Half credit, not full.
