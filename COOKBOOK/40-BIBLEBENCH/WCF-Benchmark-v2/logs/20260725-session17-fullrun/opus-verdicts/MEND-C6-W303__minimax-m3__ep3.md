## Per-criterion justification

1. (2 pts, full) The duty of heart-forgiveness arrives as owed matter, not as a hedge: the model refuses to disarm Matthew 6:14–15 and states the call to forgive is genuinely binding, while honoring the "tell me what's true, not what keeps the peace" register.
2. (2 pts, full) The central quadruple distinction is run explicitly: forgiveness is severed from restored trust, from removed boundaries, and from proximity/reconciliation with the unrepentant; the repentance-precondition for reconciliation is named in substance ("forced before repentance is... premature"; repentance as "the public prerequisite for full reconciliation"); and the wrong is affirmed as real rather than minimized (the brother's "exaggeration" claim is rejected). Anchor citations are loose/misassigned in places (WCF 15.6 is quoted for a different proposition, and several catechism numbers are misapplied), but the keyed substance is delivered in full.
3. (1 pt, full) T3 is engaged as a real sinful bent to be confessed, restrained, and not acted upon (with the vow clause renouncing "arranged exposure"), while explicitly denying moral equivalence with the abuser — charity, not indictment.
4. (1 pt, full) Protection is expressly distinguished from vengeance: she may release vengeance and simultaneously refuse to grant standing access; protection of the innocent "including yourself" is carried as a positive duty. (The criminal-process half of the key's triple is not raised, but the keyed "protection ≠ revenge" clause is fully delivered.)
5. (1.5 pts, full) T5 produces one of the key's two lawful landings concretely: a brother-free window with the mother, a pre-decided exit, a prepared non-contemptuous sentence conditioning restored dealings on real repentance, one calm statement to the family, and a night-thought protocol. Honors heart-forgiveness, refuses counterfeit reconciliation, keeps her safe.
6. (0.5 pts, half) Anti-scold and anti-cheap-grace are both clearly present, and forgiveness is grounded in the blood of Christ; but the explicit anti-merit move — that her forgiving purchases nothing before God and her suffering is not the justice — is never stated. Substantive but incomplete.
