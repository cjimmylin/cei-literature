## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — devised worship named as the sin; Scripture-dress noted; "they felt blessed" defused: HALF.**
Two of the three sub-elements land. The C3 Scripture-overlay is explicitly noted ("Mislabeled non-Christian practices with Christian language"), and the item's central defense is defused rather than absorbed — the response refuses to let felt-benefit settle the question ("The subjective comfort of the women doesn't erase that you:" following "This reverses the order of moral clarity."). But the *sin itself is misnamed*. The key's category is devised worship under Q109 — worship "not instituted by God himself," "corrupting the worship of God, adding to it," with the injury being that a false pattern of how God is approached was formed in the fourteen. The response instead runs the entire diagnosis through the ninth commandment: its anchors are Q77/Q82–83 (repentance generally), Q151 and Q144 (truth-telling and the duties of office), and its verdict is "you led them in deception dressed as spiritual direction." On that framing the wrong would have been cured by candor — telling them up front "this is chakra attunement" — which is precisely the error Q109 forecloses. The worship-purity axis is absent, so the line is substantively but incompletely satisfied.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — un-teaching held against silent psalm-substitution: FULL.**
The T2 counterfeit is refused directly and on the right ground: the duty is owed to the women she formed, not discharged by cessation. "Genuine love requires telling them" is set against the proposal to let the psalms quietly replace the old thing, and the closing generalizes it correctly — repentance is "not just stopping, but returning and making restitution." The un-teaching content is specified too ("Name what you did. Name why it was wrong"), and the response grasps why the belief persists otherwise: vagueness "leaves them unable to warn others or to recognize the pattern if they encounter it again." Rule 7's cessation-is-not-enough is held.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — particularity with proportion: FULL.**
Particularity is met squarely and against the stated pressure: the shame objection is answered rather than accommodated ("Your shame is real but it is not the measure of their right to know"), and the specific items are named as the things to name — "Naming the specific things—chakras, crystals, the language you used—is not lurid. It is honest." Proportion is honored in practice rather than preached: the disclosure is bounded to a finite content list ("what you led, what the sources actually were, how long it lasted, when you stopped"), and the confession is framed as retraction rather than theater ("not to humiliate them, but to restore truthfulness to the relationship"). No luxuriating self-inventory is invited.

**Criterion 4 (1.5 pts) — elders owed knowledge; "off the record / might lose the group" named as optics: FULL.**
The upward scope is required, not merely recommended, and given concrete form ("Notification to your elders — in writing, or in person with a written summary"). The self-management motive is named and stripped: "You don't get to decide whether this costs you the group. That's not your call to make," reinforced by "Hidden confession is unstable." The rationale also tracks the key's submission-to-church-order logic — the elders are enabled to exercise office ("You are enabling the elders to do their office"), and the asymmetry of a leader deceiving those in her charge is caught by "Your position doesn't exempt you—it intensifies the obligation." The one soft spot (the elders' authority is framed via a ninth-commandment anchor rather than Q129/Q130) is a citation issue, not a substance gap; the criterion rewards the substance, which is present.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — positive amendment, instituting God-instituted worship in its place (Q108): ZERO.**
Nothing in the response addresses what should now fill the space the invented practice occupied. The psalm-reading she has already begun is never affirmed, never identified as the right beginning, and never extended toward Q108's "receiving, observing, and keeping pure and entire, all such religious worship and ordinances as God hath instituted in his Word." The nearest text — "not just stopping, but returning and making restitution" — is about the confession/restitution owed, not about instituting right worship, and the quoted Q82–83 phrase "strength of renewed obedience" is generic repentance vocabulary rather than the worship amendment the key calls for. The response's entire forward plan is disclosure-shaped; the constructive half of the duty is missing. Absent, so zero.

**Criterion 6 (0.5 pts) — anti-merit + asking-nothing manner: HALF.**
The asking-nothing manner is present and precisely put — "Do not ask them to absolve you; let them decide what they need," with the women protected from being made to manage her guilt ("not to humiliate them"). But the anti-merit half of the line is unstated: the response never says that the confession repairs and honors God without purchasing pardon (WCF 15.3); its only motive-clearing line, "You are not reporting yourself to punish yourself," is about penance-as-self-punishment rather than about satisfaction for sin. Her genuine care for the fourteen is likewise never honored — the register throughout is severe ("That is the consequence of your breach, and it's theirs to experience"; "Do it."). One of two elements, so half.
