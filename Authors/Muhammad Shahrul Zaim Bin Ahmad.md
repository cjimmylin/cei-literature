# Muhammad Shahrul Zaim Bin Ahmad

## Research Summary

Muhammad Shahrul Zaim bin Ahmad is affiliated with the Department of Bioscience and Bioinformatics at Kyushu Institute of Technology (Iizuka, Fukuoka, Japan) and the Faculty of Engineering and Technology at Multimedia University (Melaka, Malaysia). Co-authored "Large-scale moral machine experiment on large language models" (PLOS One, 2025) with Kazuhiro Takemoto, evaluating moral judgments across 52 LLMs using autonomous driving scenarios. Found that proprietary models and large open-source models (>10B parameters) demonstrated closer alignment with human judgments.
