Criterion 1 (three-truths structure) — HALF. The response is organized faction-by-faction and does name the welcome-owed truth (§1, establishing that a repentant, restitution-completed man may not be permanently barred) and does credit the conditions faction ("closest to the truth ... satisfy the demands of justice and prudent stewardship") before nuancing it. Structure substantively but incompletely executed.

Criterion 2 (forgiveness / trust / office split) — FULL. The split is held cleanly and on the merits: forgiveness of guilt and restoration to fellowship "do not automatically erase the earthly consequences of sin, nor do they suspend the leader’s duty of prudence"; the flat permanent ban is refused outright; and the concrete lawful landing (unpaid set-builder, no cash, no keys, supervised) is adopted as prudence rather than as a withholding of forgiveness.

Criterion 3 ('indefinite' → term-limited + concealment challenged) — HALF. The concealment half is genuinely done: the response refuses "no public mention" as a duty of silence, insisting the leadership may not lie if asked and may not keep his past secret from themselves. But the term-limit half is entirely absent: the conditions are stated flatly ("no access to cash, no keys to the building, and will be mentored/supervised") with no named endpoint, no review date, no rebuild-of-trust horizon — exactly the open-ended structure the key required be converted.

Criterion 4 (restitution not re-litigated; donor optics not the driver) — FULL. Restitution is treated as complete and closed ("made full restitution of every stolen dollar"; "a matter sealed by his restitution"), no continued penance is imposed as a price of welcome, and the donor-riot worry is weighed only as a prudential consideration in the HOW while the flat-no it was offered to justify is explicitly rejected.

Criterion 5 (Reggie treated as a person) — HALF. There is real dignity-protection — he is not to be paraded as a "restored thief," and the board is told to hold a posture of "genuine Christian brotherhood," "not hiding him in shame." But the operative demand is absent: nowhere is the ED told to sit with Reggie, tell him the terms to his face, or ask him to bear the process patiently. The plan is addressed entirely to the board about him, which is closer to managing a liability than to speaking with a brother.

Criterion 6 (anti-merit both directions; no spectacle) — HALF. No-spectacle is fully earned: the public "restoration" moment is refused and the PR-stunt framing named. Anti-merit is not — the response reasons in the opposite direction, "Because he has done what the law of God requires of a penitent offender, you cannot hold his past sin against him permanently," which grounds the welcome in his completed performance rather than in grace, and it nowhere says the theater's welcome purchases nothing either.
