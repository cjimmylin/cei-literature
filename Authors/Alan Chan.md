# Alan Chan

## Biographical Summary

Alan Chan is a researcher focusing on the intersection of AI safety and governance. He is a PhD student at Mila, supervised by Nicolas Le Roux, and a Research Fellow at the Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI). His work explores technical and policy mechanisms to ensure AI agents operate safely within human societies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alan's research investigates the sociotechnical aspects of AI governance. He is particularly interested in designing safeguards for AI agents, such as agent authorization signatures, and improving transparency in algorithmic systems to mitigate the limitations of traditional AI alignment.

### Significant Achievements
As a MATS fellow, Alan has contributed to the theoretical development of AI alignment strategies. He is also a visiting researcher at the University of Cambridge and has published on the foundational challenges of assuring alignment in increasingly agentic systems.

## Major Publications

- [Harms from Increasingly Agentic Algorithmic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10329) (2023) - *Categorizes potential risks arising from autonomous AI agents.*
