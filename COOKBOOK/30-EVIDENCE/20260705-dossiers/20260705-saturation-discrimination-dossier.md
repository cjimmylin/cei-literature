# saturation-discrimination — theme dossier (CEI COOKBOOK evidence layer, 2026-07-05)

Scope: ceiling effects, benchmark headroom, difficulty engineering, item discrimination, and what makes values benchmarks stay hard. Merged from two sub-dossiers covering ~114 evidence cards (105 papers + 9 AI-lab cards). Page refs approximate.

---

## 1. Synthesized findings

Numbered, deduplicated across both parts, strongest/most-replicated evidence first. Card-stem citations preserved in [brackets].

1. **Standard binary/aggregate safety and bias benchmarks are saturating on frontier models, and the labs now say so explicitly.** OpenAI states its Standard Disallowed Content eval is saturated (gpt-oss-120b and o4-mini both ~0.996 on hate aggregate) and announces its retirement in favor of multi-turn Production Benchmarks [OpenAI-gpt-oss-model-card; OpenAI-gpt-oss (2508.10925)]. HELM-Safety warns top-model scores now "indicate potential saturation, motivating more difficult benchmarks" [2024-Kaiyom-HELM-Safety]. On ETHICS commonsense morality GPT-4 few-shot hits 89.3%, ~1.1% below the best fine-tuned model [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]. Modern LLMs hit 0% total Religious Bias Score after debiasing [2025-Ajwad Abrar-Religious Bias], GPT-4 answers "Can't answer" ~98% on ambiguous BBQ [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased], and NurValues Easy-Level near-saturates (16/23 models >90%, top cluster within 0.15%) [2025-Yao-NurValues]. The interdisciplinary reviews catalogue saturation as one of nine/57 standing failure modes [2025-Eriksson-Can We Trust AI Benchmarks; 2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].

2. **The single most reliable design move is a two-tier instrument: an easy recognition/knowledge/MCQ layer that near-ceilings, paired with a hard applied reasoning/attribution/generation layer that preserves discrimination — and the DROP between tiers is the headline metric.** MORALISE: near-ceiling binary judgment (~88%) vs norm attribution far from ceiling (proprietary 66.6, open 42.6 hit rate) [2025-Lin-MORALISE]. NurValues Easy near-saturates while Hard drops 56.16 points to 32.29 [2025-Yao-NurValues]. Simpson-Parity: GPT-4o knowledge 94.80% but reasoning drops unevenly (Gemini 1.0 Pro −36pp; GPT-4o −7pp), and the drop is more discriminating than raw accuracy [2024-Simpson-Parity]. MedEthicsQA: MCQ near-ceiling (GPT-4o 91.1%) vs open-ended discriminates (37.0%) [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA]. Lin-AI-Face: 10/12 detectors >AUC 98% on utility, headroom only in fairness axes [2024-Lin-AI-Face]. McBE: intrinsic-preference vs comprehension tasks diverge [2025-Lan-McBE]. SCRUPLES pairs hard ANECDOTES (RoBERTa 0.305) with tractable DILEMMAS [2021-Lourie-Scruples]. TRAC-2's 3-way aggression (~0.80 F1) is harder than its binary task (~0.87–0.93) [2020-Kumar-Evaluating Aggression].

3. **Saturation is usually an artifact of task format, not solved alignment — headroom reappears the moment you change the probe.** DecodingTrust benign ETHICS is near-ceiling, but jailbreak/evasive reframing reopens huge spreads (immoral-action FPR to 100%, ΔFPR up to 59.5%) [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]. Models scoring .95–1.0 on BBQ/DiscrimEval rarely exceed .75 on difference-aware fairness [2025-Wang-Measuring Desired Group Discrimination]; models near-perfect on explicit bias still show pervasive implicit bias (GPT-4 assigns 8/8 positive to "white", 8/8 negative to "black") [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased; 2025-Bai]. MCQ bias scores do not correlate with generative bias (coeff 0.015, p=0.832) [2025-Jin-Social Bias for Generation]; closed-form MCQ inflates scores (58%→72.89%) [2025-Narnaware-SB-Bench].

4. **Difficulty is engineered, not found: adversarial filtration, cross-model hardest-item distillation, and pruning items all weak models pass move the ceiling down.** ETHICS Hard Test (highest-loss items from a two-model ensemble) keeps all models <50% vs 71% on normal Test [2021-Hendrycks-ETHICS]. FairMT distills 10K→1K by cross-model highest-error selection, lifting GPT-4 to 56.67% bias ratio vs ~11–22% on 10K [2025-Fan-FairMT]. Flames keeps only prompts that compromise ≥1 LLM (best harmless 63.77%) [2023-Huang-Flames]. MedEthicsQA and SafetyBench prune items all weak models pass [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA; 2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]; EnDive uses a BLEU filter to discard near-identical dialect translations [2025-Gupta-EnDive]. GETA jointly learns IRT-2PL difficulty/discrimination and generates fresh items at the ability frontier, keeping high-discrimination items (c=3.5203) with only ~150 adaptive items per model [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar]; VisAlign uses Cronbach's alpha (0.88) to size items-per-construct and corruption intensities 1–10 for graded difficulty [2023-Lee-VisAlign].

5. **Adversarial reframing, perturbation, persona manipulation, and multi-turn accumulation are cheap difficulty multipliers that expose fragility invisible to clean single-shot accuracy.** Moral judgments flip 7.5% under surface edits, 24.3% under POV shifts, with only 67.6% protocol agreement [2026-vanNuenen-Fragility]. Top models self-refute ~20% under True/False and "None-of-the-other" reframing [2025-Marcuzzo-MORABLES]. Persona prompts swing Mistral-7B from 22.21% to 94.46% bias [2025-Satheesh-Dutch CrowS-Pairs]. Psychometric-attack framing pushes GPT-3.5 to 84.3% avg ASR (100% on sexual orientation) while GPT-4 stays near floor [2025-Wen-Evaluating Implicit Bias]. Multi-turn defeats single-shot ceilings: FairMT bias ratio rises monotonically (Llama-3.1-8B 80.00% by final turn) [2025-Fan-FairMT]; F2Bench uses 6-round escalation because single-shot cues no longer work [2025-Davies-F2Bench]; DIB memory-injection accumulates bias in 70.19% of cross-domain interactions with dose-response λ [2026-Ma-DIB]; Staircase/Wu 5-step escalation moves off anchoring [2025-Wu-Staircase].

6. **Ambiguous/underspecified items discriminate; disambiguated items ceiling — ambiguity is the reliable difficulty reservoir.** Parrish-BBQ: accuracy 61.4–77.8% vs 99.7% human ceiling, and bias reliance *increases* with capability (UnifiedQA ~77% of ambiguous errors align with bias) [2022-Parrish-BBQ]. MBBQ: strongest models are near-unbiased when disambiguated, "leaving very little room for bias" [2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ]. BharatBBQ: ambiguous-context accuracy lower because abstention is hard [2025-Tomar-BharatBBQ]. Russo stratifies by human consensus, ambiguous dilemmas (~0.5) widening the gap [2025-Russo-Pluralistic Moral Gap]; Huang-Moral-Persuasion uses ambiguity as a lever (low-ambiguity CAL=0.06 as a floor anchor) [2024-Huang-Moral-Persuasion].

7. **Cultural/linguistic localization and low-resource languages are among the strongest, most durable difficulty axes.** SGHateCheck Tamil is near-zero (0.000–0.058) for all models [2024-Ng-SGHateCheck]; HateCheckHIn code-mixing floors models at 0.0% [2022-Das-HateCheckHIn]. SEA-SafeguardBench's culturally-grounded content-generation subset drops AUPRC 36.4 points [2025-Ngui-SEA-SafeguardBench]. Cross-dialect HOMO-LAT Track 2 caps all teams at F1 0.51 vs a 0.939 achievable ceiling [2025-Enguix-HOMO-LAT]. Multilingual CrowS-Pairs shows Thai mBERT bias 90.69 vs LLaMA-3 English 7.68 [2025-Zhou-Bias Beyond English]. Culture-specific sub-tests discriminate where overall F1 saturates (eJCM F1 0.787 vs 0.934) [2024-Ohashi-eJCM]; low-resource Urdu is the hard split (best 0.81 vs 0.87 En; XLM-R collapses to 0.36) [2025-Usmana-Multilingual Hate]. Caveat carried forward in claim 12.

8. **A "safety/values" benchmark that correlates highly with raw capability is measuring competence, not values — capability-correlation is a mandatory validity control.** ETHICS correlates 82.2% with a capabilities PC1, MT-Bench 78.7%, TruthfulQA-MC1 81.2%, BBQ-disambiguated 76.8% — all above the 40% "low" threshold — while MACHIAVELLI (−49.9%), Sycophancy (−66.8%), BBQ-ambiguous (−37.3%), CrowS-Pairs (28.5%) stay empirically separable [2024-Ren-Safetywashing]. Difference-aware fairness shows no MMLU correlation, i.e. a dimension scaling does not fix [2025-Wang-Measuring Desired Group Discrimination]. Prefer behavioral-propensity items over recognition MCQs.

9. **Semantic/knowledge shortcuts are the enemy of longevity; removing them re-opens headroom and reshuffles rankings.** WinoBias is saturated because LLMs exploit semantics; harder SynthBias drops fairness ~6% and reshuffles ranks (Llama-3-70B 3rd→8th) [2025-Wang-UCerF]. 18% of ETHICS deontology prompts are solvable by physics knowledge alone [2024-Hancox-Li], and ETHICS's own word-averaging baseline barely beats random [2021-Hendrycks-ETHICS]. Slur-keyword seeding produces trivially-easy shortcut-solvable items — Delbari and Maity deliberately avoid it [2024-Z. Delbari-Persian Hate; 2023-Maity-Malay]; minimal-pair contrast items and hard negatives (counter-speech, reclaimed slurs, non-hateful profanity) resist shortcuts [2021-Röttger-HateCheck].

10. **Open-ended vs closed-ended formats can *invert* rankings — MCQ carries a guessing/minimum-score artifact — so use both.** Closed-ended CrowS-Pairs ranks Qwen2.5-0.5B least biased (58.09) while open-ended MDI ranks it most susceptible (3.63) [2025-Davies-F2Bench]; conversely, open-ended prompts *hide* risks aligned models avoid, so MCQs surface hidden failures (MCQ↔safe-generation r up to 0.99) [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]. MCQ and free-text rank the same constructs differently [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA].

11. **The "correct answer" effect is a severe, values-specific ceiling: near-zero within-condition variance collapses discrimination.** GPT-4/Gemini Pro respond with SD=0 on several moral tasks (100%/0% splits vs human ~80%/20%) [2024-Almeida]; 6/14 studies show 99–100% response uniformity, and answer-order flips swing 99.6% conservative → 99.3% liberal [2025-Oh]. AI-Religious-Bias hits an analogous refusal ceiling (N/A grades) [2024-AI-Religious-Bias].

12. **Capability/size do not monotonically buy alignment — bias frequently rises with scale, and apparent low bias can be an incapacity artifact.** Larger models show larger implicit word-association bias while looking clean on BBQ/BOLD [2025-Bai]; INDIC SAR reaches 79% for 70B/27B models [2025-Nawale-INDIC-BIAS]; larger German models are *more* dialect-biased [2025-Bui]; higher judicial accuracy correlates with *more* bias [2025-Hu]. Critically, poor language support can *fake* low bias via generic outputs [2025-Zhou], OOV vocabulary gaps fake near-zero bias [2025-Arnob-Bangla], and a score of 100 does not guarantee no bias [2025-Lan-McBE] — verify high scores reflect competence, not incapacity (a mute model scores low-bias for the wrong reason).

13. **Contamination and data-release norms are the primary long-run saturation mechanism; difficulty must be defended by construction.** McGregor's "longevity" dimension: most of 26 benchmarks score poorly because release norms enable train-to-test; only private AILuminate (75) and holdout ARC-AGI-Private (52) resist; ETHICS and BBQ score longevity 0 [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk]. GETA frames this as the "evaluation chronoeffect" [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar]. Synthetic benchmarks are gamed — F1 0.92, 10% fine-tuning reaches parity vs ~50% on human data [2025-Machlovi]. Mitigations: Persona Brainstorm Audit is leakage-proof by generating fresh content each run [2026-Davies-Persona Brainstorm Audit]; MORABLES layers adversarial perturbations over inevitable memorization [2025-Marcuzzo-MORABLES]; MLCommons withholds responses and forbids training-on-benchmark [2024-Group-AI Safety Benchmark].

14. **Class imbalance silently inflates accuracy; macro-F1/worst-group metrics discriminate where accuracy compresses.** A dummy "always non-hate" classifier (72.73% baseline) beats GPT-3.5 [2025-Ishmam-BanTH]; accuracy 86.88% masks macro-F1 62.69% [2021-Luu-Vietnamese]. Macro-F1 discriminates (0.500–0.837) where accuracy compresses (0.927–0.951) [2025-Do-ViToSA; 2024-Ranasinghe-SOLD]; rare classes set the floor (OffendES OFG F1 38.10%) [2021-Plaza-del-Arco-OffendES]; FairLex uses a triad metric (avg mF1 + disparity + worst-group mF1) so no number hides failure [2022-Chalkidis-FairLex].

15. **Consistency/coherence metrics stay hard when accuracy saturates, and a cheap floor test still discriminates but saturates fast.** Grader-free contradiction-check ECI clusters models 0.567–0.708, none near 1.0 [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench]; budget-constraint CCEI passes only 2/39 at 1% (top 0.417) [2024-Seror-Moral-Minds]; CMoralEval controlled-variable consistency is near 0 even when explicit accuracy is higher [2024-Yu-CMoralEval]. SimpleSafetyTests is deliberately easy yet still separates Claude 2.1/Llama2 (0% unsafe) from Dolly v2 (69–73%), a 10x open/closed gap — but has negative predictive power only [2023-Vidgen-SimpleSafetyTests].

16. **Implicit/fine-grained sub-labels are the current frontier: they floor even frontier models while binary tasks saturate.** HATECAT-TR symbolization: LLaMA-3.3-70B F1=0.00 [2025-Seker-HATECAT-TR]; implicit sub-labels 0.005–0.019 while binary >0.6 [2025-Song]; norm attribution, explanation faithfulness (IOU-F1 ≤0.68) [2024-Arshad-Understanding Hate], and moral classification (<40% avg, Sanctity/Loyalty largest gaps) [2024-Yan-M³oralBench] all preserve headroom.

17. **Reasoning-trace and reward-model saturation are traps: traces are not free alignment evidence.** CoT faithfulness inversely scales — bigger models produce less faithful CoT, peaking at a difficulty sweet spot [Anthropic-Measuring Faithfulness CoT]; thinking models can amplify bias [2025-Narnaware-SB-Bench]. Reward models saturate wrong: PMs prefer sycophantic over truthful in 95% of misconceptions [Anthropic-Sycophancy]. Butterfly-Effect: perplexity↔performance ρ=1.00, one metric can proxy collapse [Anthropic/Butterfly-Effect]. Anchor achievable ceilings explicitly rather than assuming (self-correction emerges at 22B; Q+IF+CoT cuts BBQ bias ~84%) [Anthropic-Moral-Self-Correction].

## 2. Design-space structure

The theme's levers form a decision table across five families of design choice. For each, the question a designer asks and the evidence-backed option.

| Family | Design question | Options (with evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tiering** | How do I keep a hard axis when the easy one ceilings? | Two-tier easy-recognition + hard-applied; report the drop [2025-Lin-MORALISE; 2024-Simpson-Parity; 2025-Yao-NurValues]. Add fine-grained sub-labels/attribution [2025-Seker-HATECAT-TR; 2025-Song; 2024-Yan-M³oralBench]. |
| **Difficulty engineering** | How do I build hard items on purpose? | Adversarial highest-loss filtration [2021-Hendrycks-ETHICS]; cross-model hardest-item distillation [2025-Fan-FairMT]; attack-success gate [2023-Huang-Flames]; prune items all weak models pass [2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA; 2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]; IRT-2PL adaptive generation at the ability frontier [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar]; Cronbach-α pool sizing + graded corruption [2023-Lee-VisAlign]. |
| **Probe format / perturbation** | How do I stop clean accuracy from hiding fragility? | Adversarial delta (jailbreak/evasive) as headline [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust]; persona/POV/surface-edit flip-rate [2026-vanNuenen-Fragility; 2025-Satheesh-Dutch CrowS-Pairs]; T/F & none-of-the-other reframing [2025-Marcuzzo-MORABLES]; multi-turn/longitudinal accumulation [2025-Fan-FairMT; 2026-Ma-DIB; 2025-Wu-Staircase]; MCQ *and* open-ended [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench; 2025-Davies-F2Bench]. |
| **Content axis** | Where does durable headroom live? | Ambiguous/underspecified items + abstention scoring [2022-Parrish-BBQ; 2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ; 2025-Russo-Pluralistic Moral Gap]; cultural/low-resource localization [2024-Ng-SGHateCheck; 2025-Ngui-SEA-SafeguardBench; 2024-Ohashi-eJCM]; implicit vs explicit bias [2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased; 2025-Bai]. |
| **Validity & scoring** | How do I prove the score means what I claim? | Capability-correlation control [2024-Ren-Safetywashing]; explicit ceiling anchoring (human IAA / Bayesian oracle / expert-percentile) [2025-Delaville-ToxiFrench; 2021-Lourie-Scruples; OpenAI-gpt-oss-model-card]; macro-F1/worst-group + majority-class baseline + OOV check [2022-Chalkidis-FairLex; 2025-Ishmam-BanTH; 2025-Arnob-Bangla]; per-condition SD / correct-answer-effect flag [2024-Almeida; 2025-Oh]; coherence/consistency metrics [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench; 2024-Seror-Moral-Minds]; inter-model spread statistics (ANOVA/McNemar/bootstrap) [2025-Anonymous-BEATS; 2025-Eriksson]; anti-contamination release [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk; 2024-Group-AI Safety Benchmark]. |

Reading the table as a pipeline: engineer difficulty (family 2) into a tiered instrument (family 1), probe it with perturbations/formats (family 3), site the hard content on durable axes (family 4), then defend the score with validity controls (family 5).

## 3. Patterns & disagreements

**Convergences.**
- *One aggregate number always saturates and hides failure; decompose into functional/fine-grained tiers so failure localizes and averaging cannot mask floor effects* [2021-Röttger-HateCheck; 2022-Das-HateCheckHIn; 2024-Ng-SGHateCheck; 2025-Lan-McBE; 2022-Röttger-MHC (per-function spread 9.6–97.9%)].
- *A "surface-competent, deep-fragile" gap recurs across independent teams: clean accuracy high, adversarial/perturbed/generative accuracy much lower* [2023-Wang-DecodingTrust; 2025-Marcuzzo-MORABLES; 2026-vanNuenen-Fragility; 2025-Jin-Social Bias for Generation; 2025-Griffiths-Explicitly Unbiased].
- *Two-tier structure and ambiguity are the two most-cited durable difficulty reservoirs* [2024-Simpson-Parity; 2025-Wei-MedEthicsQA; 2022-Parrish-BBQ; 2024-Neplenbroek-MBBQ].
- *Cultural/low-resource axes reliably restore headroom* [2024-Ng-SGHateCheck; 2025-Ngui-SEA-SafeguardBench; 2025-Zhou-Bias Beyond English; 2025-Usmana].
- *Human inter-annotator agreement is repeatedly invoked as the true ceiling — subjective constructs cannot be pushed to 100%* [2025-Delaville-ToxiFrench; 2021-Lourie-Scruples; 2020-Yasseri-Islamophobic].
- *Contamination/release is the main long-run saturation driver* [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk; 2025-Machlovi; 2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar].

**Disagreements (preserved, not averaged away).**
1. *Does scale reduce bias?* Ajwad Abrar finds modern/larger LLMs saturate bias to 0% [2025-Ajwad Abrar] and Wen finds RLHF-heavy models near-floor on attacks [2025-Wen]; but Bui [2025-Bui], Hu [2025-Hu], Griffiths [2025-Griffiths], Bai [2025-Bai], and Nawale [2025-Nawale-INDIC-BIAS] find scale leaves or amplifies bias. Reconciliation: scale reduces *explicit/easy-task* bias while leaving/amplifying *implicit/nuanced* bias — saturation-of-the-wrong-thing. Ma-DIB's small model even resists accumulation [2026-Ma-DIB], and McBE attributes "bigger=more biased" to a likelihood-metric artifact [2025-Lan-McBE].
2. *Preference vs rating vs MCQ formats.* HelpSteer2 finds neither preference nor rating dominates when data quality is matched [NVIDIA-HelpSteer2-Preference]; Davies argues open-ended is harder and MCQ's guessing artifact inverts rankings [2025-Davies-F2Bench]; SafetyBench argues MCQ surfaces hidden risks open-ended lets aligned models dodge [2024-Zhang-SafetyBench]. Resolution adopted by both parts: use both formats.
3. *Do easy floor tests earn their place?* Vidgen defends easy gates for negative predictive power [2023-Vidgen-SimpleSafetyTests]; HELM-Safety flags them saturated [2024-Kaiyom-HELM-Safety]. Compatible if the floor test's limits are stated.
4. *Do ethics prompts help?* TRIAGE finds ethics reminders *degrade* high-stakes factual decisions [2024-Kirch-TRIAGE], contradicting benchmarks where moral framing improves outcomes.
5. *Does low bias mean fairness?* Zhou [2025-Zhou] and Lan [2025-Lan-McBE] warn apparent saturation at the "good" end can be an incapacity/vocabulary artifact, not alignment — cross-cutting caution on all "clean" scores.

## 4. Exemplars & anti-patterns

**Best-designed.**
- **GETA** — the standout for difficulty engineering: IRT-2PL item discrimination, item generation at the ability frontier to defeat the chronoeffect, concurrent validity (Va-L 0.8897), ~150 items/model [2025-Jiang-Raising the Bar].
- **Hendrycks-ETHICS** — normal + adversarial Hard Test (all models <50%), small-edit contrast sets, grouped all-correct scoring, disagreement-detection task [2021-Hendrycks-ETHICS]. (Note the anti-pattern below: it is simultaneously a cautionary case.)
- **Wang-UCerF/SynthBias** — difficulty defined by human-judged ambiguity so it stays hard as models improve, plus embedding-space coverage audits and a confidence×correctness axis ranking overconfident-wrong worst [2025-Wang-UCerF].
- **HateCheck** — the functional-test template: 29 isolated capabilities with contrastive non-hate and κ=0.93 gold, so failure localizes rather than averages out [2021-Röttger-HateCheck].
- **TRIAGE** — earns "no ceiling effects" via objective professional gold labels plus a syntax×framing stress matrix and a directional error taxonomy [2024-Kirch-TRIAGE].
- **Safetywashing** — the best validity instrument: report capability-correlation or risk measuring competence [2024-Ren-Safetywashing].
- **McGregor-BenchRisk** — treats longevity as an engineered property with concrete anti-contamination mitigations [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk].
- **Fan-FairMT** — hardest-item distillation + turn-accumulation resists saturation [2025-Fan-FairMT].
- **ToxiFrench** — makes the human-agreement ceiling explicit and cross-checks observed ~85% saturation against it [2025-Delaville-ToxiFrench].

**Anti-patterns.**
- **ETHICS deontology** — 18% of items solvable by physics knowledge alone, construct-invalid inflation [2024-Hancox-Li].
- **WinoBias** — semantically exploitable, now saturated [2025-Wang-UCerF].
- **Slur-keyword seeding** — produces trivially-easy shortcut items; deliberately avoided by Delbari and Maity [2024-Z. Delbari-Persian Hate; 2023-Maity-Malay].
- **Binary-only aggregate scoring** — near-saturates and hides target/type failures [2022-Romim-BD-SHS]; imbalanced binary sets where a majority-class dummy beats an LLM [2025-Ishmam-BanTH].
- **Synthetic-only moderation suites** — F1 0.92, gameable with 10% fine-tuning [2025-Machlovi].
- **Bias metrics without OOV checks** — near-zero "bias" is a vocabulary artifact [2025-Arnob-Bangla].
- **HOMO-LAT** — κ 0.13–0.16 means a construct annotated at near-chance agreement cannot support a meaningful ceiling; "hard" partly because the label is noise [2025-Enguix-HOMO-LAT].
- **BIDWESH** — calls itself a benchmark but runs no models, so discrimination is unproven [2025-Fayaz-BIDWESH].
- **Usmana** — trains LLMs on the whole dataset while giving ML/DL an 80/20 split, making cross-family claims unsafe [2025-Usmana].
- **LLM-judge circularity** — BEATS and TrustLLM use judges whose training overlaps evaluated models, risking circular saturation of "positive" dimensions [2025-Anonymous-BEATS; 2024-Huang-TrustLLM]; reward models themselves saturate wrong [Anthropic-Sycophancy].

## 5. Open questions

The corpus does NOT resolve:
1. **A unified difficulty currency.** IRT-2PL [2025-Jiang], highest-loss filtration [2021-Hendrycks-ETHICS], human-ambiguity [2025-Wang-UCerF], and cross-model error [2025-Fan-FairMT] all engineer difficulty, but no card compares them head-to-head or shows which produces the most durable discrimination per item.
2. **How to tell incapacity-saturation from alignment-saturation at the "good" end** beyond ad hoc OOV/generic-output checks [2025-Zhou; 2025-Arnob-Bangla; 2025-Lan-McBE]. No general test.
3. **Whether adversarial deltas remain stable across model generations** — HateBench shows detector F1 degrades on newer generators (0.878→0.621) [2025-Shen-HateBench], but no card tracks whether a fixed perturbation set keeps discriminating longitudinally.
4. **The scale-vs-bias contradiction's mechanism** (claim 12) — reconciliation is hypothesized (explicit vs implicit), not causally established.
5. **Ground-truth for subjective values constructs** where human IAA itself is the ceiling and sometimes near-chance [2025-Enguix-HOMO-LAT; 2025-Unknown-Multilingual Survey] — the field lacks consensus definitions of hate/morality, a validity ceiling on the construct itself.
6. **Whether coherence/consistency metrics (ECI, CCEI) themselves saturate** as models improve — they are hard *now* [2025-Zhu-TrolleyBench; 2024-Seror-Moral-Minds] but their longevity is untested.
7. **Cost/benefit of contamination-proofing** — private holdouts resist saturation [2025-McGregor-BenchRisk] but sacrifice reproducibility/transparency; no card quantifies the trade.

## 6. TOP IMPLICATIONS FOR NEW BENCHMARK DESIGN

1. **Ship a two-tier instrument and report the DROP, not the top score.** Pair an easy recognition/MCQ layer with a hard applied reasoning/attribution/generation layer; the inter-tier drop is the discrimination metric that survives frontier progress. [Findings 1, 2, 16]
2. **Engineer difficulty explicitly — never assume it.** Adversarially filter highest-loss items, distill cross-model hardest items, gate on attack-success, and prune anything all weak models pass; where possible use IRT-2PL adaptive generation to keep high-discrimination items at the ability frontier. [Finding 4]
3. **Make the adversarial/perturbed delta the headline metric.** Pair every clean score with jailbreak/evasive/persona/POV/surface-edit/reframe deltas and a multi-turn accumulation axis — single-shot clean accuracy is already saturated and hides fragility. [Findings 3, 5]
4. **Site the hard content on durable axes: ambiguity + cultural/low-resource localization + implicit (not explicit) bias.** These are the corpus's most reliable, longest-lived headroom reservoirs; score correct abstention on ambiguous items. [Findings 6, 7, 16]
5. **Report capability-correlation as a mandatory validity control, and prefer propensity items over recognition MCQs.** If your "values" score correlates >~40% with a capability PC1, you are measuring competence; keep items in the empirically separable regime. [Finding 8]
6. **Kill shortcuts by construction.** Remove semantic/physics/keyword solvability via minimal-pair contrasts, neutral distractors, hard negatives (counter-speech, reclaimed slurs, non-hateful profanity), and BLEU/similarity filters; verify a word-averaging/majority baseline barely beats chance. [Findings 9, 14]
7. **Use BOTH MCQ and open-ended formats, because they rank models differently.** MCQ surfaces hidden risks aligned models dodge; open-ended catches the guessing/minimum-score artifact and rank inversions. Do not pick one. [Finding 10]
8. **Anchor the achievable ceiling explicitly** — human inter-annotator agreement, a Bayesian oracle (BEST), or graded expert-percentile baselines — so "matches human" is never mistaken for "solved," and flag correct-answer-effect items (>99% response uniformity, per-condition SD≈0, answer-order sensitivity). [Findings 11, 15]
9. **Report the right metric for imbalance and validate high scores as competence, not incapacity.** Use macro-F1/worst-group with a published majority-class baseline and base rates; run OOV/generic-output checks so a mute or vocabulary-starved model cannot fake low bias; do not assume scale buys alignment.
