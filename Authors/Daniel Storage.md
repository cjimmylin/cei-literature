# Daniel Storage

## Affiliation
Assistant Professor, Department of Psychology, University of Denver

## Research Summary

Daniel Storage is a social psychologist at the University of Denver whose research examines stereotypes, gender representation in STEM fields, and moral psychology. His work on moral judgment explores how situational factors and context influence ethical decision-making, contributing to our understanding of the psychology underlying moral reasoning. He has studied the "brilliance" stereotype and its effects on gender disparities in academic disciplines.
