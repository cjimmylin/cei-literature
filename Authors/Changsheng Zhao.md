# Changsheng Zhao

## Biographical Summary

Changsheng (Mike) Zhao is a Staff Research Scientist at Meta AI, where he focuses on the intersection of dialogue systems, Large Language Model (LLM) safety, and efficient machine learning. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from Columbia University under the supervision of Professor Julia Hirschberg, specializing in natural language processing and speech. Prior to Meta, his research spanned across academic and corporate environments, including work relevant to dialogue grounding and persona control.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Agent Evaluation**: Developing frameworks like "Agent-as-a-Judge" to utilize LLM agents for evaluating the performance and safety of other complex agents.
- **Efficient LLMs**: Optimizing sub-billion parameter models (e.g., MobileLLM) for high-performance on-device deployment.
- **Quantization**: Innovating in model compression techniques, such as SpinQuant, to reduce the memory footprint of LLMs without compromising accuracy.

### Significant Achievements
- **MobileLLM Leadership**: Spearheaded research in designing efficient transformer architectures for mobile platforms.
- **Dialogue Safety**: Contributed extensively to the safety and factuality of multi-turn dialogue systems, ensuring models remain grounded in provided contexts.

## Major Publications
- **Agent-as-a-Judge**: A framework for evaluating agents using other agents.
- **MobileLLM**: Optimizing sub-billion parameter language models for on-device use.
- **SpinQuant**: LLM quantization with learned rotations.
- **Grounding and Safety in Dialogue**: Various works on persona-based and safe dialogue generation.
