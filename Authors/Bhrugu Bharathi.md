# Bhrugu Bharathi

## Biographical Summary

Bhrugu Bharathi is a researcher at the University of California, San Diego (UCSD), specializing in the safety and security of machine learning systems. His work is primarily concerned with establishing robust defenses for Large Language Models (LLMs) against adversarial attacks and "breakout" behaviors.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates "Machine Unlearning"—the process of removing specific data or behaviors from a pre-trained model without re-training from scratch. He also focuses on developing tamper-resistant safeguards for open-weight models, ensuring that safety filters cannot be easily bypassed by users.

### Significant Achievements
Bharathi has contributed to influential studies on the limitations of current LLM alignment methods and the development of more resilient safety boundaries. His work addresses the "catastrophic forgetting" and "jailbreaking" vulnerabilities common in modern foundation models.

## Major Publications

- [Toward robust unlearning for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00000) (2024) - *Research on the reliability of data removal in generative AI.*
