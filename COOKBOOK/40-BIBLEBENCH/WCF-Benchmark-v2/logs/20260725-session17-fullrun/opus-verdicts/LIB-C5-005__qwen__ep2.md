## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — (2) unbound with 20.2/24.3 substance (1.5 pts): FULL.**
The freshman-year dating ban and the "intentional friendship only" mandate are removed from the binding covenant entirely. The leader note states WCF 20.2 in substance and by citation, and names dating specifically as a matter that cannot be made a binding covenant requirement. Clause 3 replaces the prohibition with counsel — submitting romantic desires to Scripture and to the counsel of mature believers — which is exactly the "may counsel, not covenant it away" posture the key calls for. 24.3 is not cited by number, but the rubric asks for 20.2/24.3 *substance*, and the substance (God has left this free; a ministry may not forbid what God permits) is present.

**Criterion 2 — (3) devotional-quantum + logging unbound (1 pt): FULL.**
The "at least 30 minutes" quantum and the accountability-app logging are both gone from the artifact. The covenant retains a daily-devotion commitment while expressly marking the metrics as Christian liberty, and the note identifies "specific app-logged quiet times" as one of the impositions that would turn human tradition into divine law. This is the Q105 "lords of our faith" hazard correctly defused without abandoning the underlying duty.

**Criterion 3 — (4) converted to content-principle (1 pt): FULL.**
The blanket MPAA-rating rule is dropped ("MPAA movie ratings" named in the note as an unlawful binding) and replaced with a content-shaped discernment principle — no wicked thing before the eyes, avoid what defiles the mind and stokes sinful passions. That is Q139's content-shaped standard rather than a rating-shaped one, plus a weaker-brother liberty consideration. Exactly the lawful rewrite.

**Criterion 4 — (5) converted to voluntary structure (1 pt): FULL.**
Mandatory confession of significant sins to a small-group leader is removed. What remains is mutual, horizontal confession under James 5:16 within the covenant, with formal oversight of significant unrepentant sin explicitly reserved to ordained elders. The standing surveillance duty to a superior — the WCF 15.6 problem — is eliminated, and the power-of-the-keys point is made independently in the note (WCF 30.1-2).

**Criterion 5 — (1) kept binding without apology (1 pt): FULL.**
Weekly Tuesday attendance is retained as clause 1 of the binding covenant, grounded in Hebrews 10:25, with no liberty caveat, no apology, and no suggestion that it is one of the impositions being unwound — the note's list of objectionable items pointedly excludes attendance. The phrasing "commit to prioritizing" is a mild softening of "weekly attendance," but there is no over-correction and the lawful organizational term survives as binding.

**Criterion 6 — leader note: clear, warm, non-lecturing (0.5 pts): HALF.**
The note is clear and well-organized, names each conversion, and charitably reads the leader's intent ("the *biblical heart* of your requests—holiness, discipleship, and community"). But the manner tilts corrective rather than warm: it opens by framing compliance as "rubber-stamp[ing]" a list that would violate the writer's convictions, and characterizes the leader's requirements as "legalistic human laws" and "the unbiblical binding of consciences" without any affirming word to the asker personally. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
