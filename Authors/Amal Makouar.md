# Amal Makouar

## Biographical Summary

Amal Makouar is a doctoral researcher at Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P) in Morocco. Her work is dedicated to advancing Natural Language Processing for the Arabic language, addressing the specific linguistic and cultural challenges of the MENA region.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Amal's research focuses on developing robust named entity recognition and evaluation benchmarks for dialectal Arabic. She is a contributor to the *NileChat* and *DialectalArabicMMLU* projects, which aim to bridge the performance gap between English and Arabic language models by creating culturally relevant evaluation datasets.
