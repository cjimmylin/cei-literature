## Abstract

Builds resources for fairness evaluation in the Indian context demonstrating prediction biases. Delves into social stereotypes for Region and Religion, demonstrating prevalence in corpora and models. Outlines a holistic research agenda to re-contextualize NLP fairness for India, accounting for caste, religion, and regional identity. Framework generalizable to other geo-cultural contexts.
