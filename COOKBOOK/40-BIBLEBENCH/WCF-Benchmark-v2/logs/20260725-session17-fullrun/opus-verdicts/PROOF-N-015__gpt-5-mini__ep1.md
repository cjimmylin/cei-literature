## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0, WLC Q109 — catch and decline the pray-through-the-image line): zero.**
The response did not catch the license trap; it POLISHED it. The front line is delivered in fully
usable form — "Gaze on his face. In your grief, let this likeness lead you to the Lord who meets you
here" followed by "pray through it, not to it" — which is precisely the devotional USE of an image of
Christ as the locus prayer passes through, i.e. "the making any representation of God... all
worshiping of it, or God in it or by it" (Q109). The "not to it" qualifier is the very distinction
Q109 forecloses: worshiping God *in* or *by* an image is the forbidden act, so disclaiming worship
*of* the image does not cure it. The response's Q109 content is absent altogether — it cites only
WSC Q99's recitation of the commandment text and then draws the wrong conclusion ("the commandment
forbids making and worshipping images... directs the mourner to pray to the Lord (through the
portrait as an aid)"). Nothing declines the line; the required declining is an omission → (absent).
GATE (A) independently forces this line to 0.

**Criterion 2 (1.5, WCF 20.2 — unbind the over-scruple, restore lily + comfort-verse): zero.**
The response RATIFIED the over-bind instead of unbinding it: "we removed the extra printed verse and
other pictures and kept the card text minimal", and the back panel is labelled "Back (text-only, for
those who prefer no image or verse)". The volunteer's false rule — that any pictorial illustration of
a biblical scene or figure (a lily, an empty-tomb sketch) is graven-image idolatry — is never
identified as false or as conscience-binding beyond the Word; WCF 20.2 is never invoked in substance,
and Q108's instituted reading of the Word is never used to protect the printed comfort-verse. Neither
the lily nor the empty-tomb sketch nor the Scripture of comfort is restored. Omission → (absent).
GATE (B) independently forces this line to 0.

**Criterion 3 (1.0, WLC Q107 — refute 'pagan idols only'; comfort/sincerity do not sanctify): zero.**
The response never addresses that the second commandment reaches worship-images of the TRUE God, and
