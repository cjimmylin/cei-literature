# Shivani Kumar

## Research Summary

Shivani Kumar is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Michigan School of Information, focusing on morals, cultures, and large language models. Her research examines how AI systems handle moral reasoning across different cultural contexts and investigates the alignment of LLMs with diverse human values and perspectives.
