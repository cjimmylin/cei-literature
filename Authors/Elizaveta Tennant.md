# Elizaveta Tennant

## Biographical Summary

Elizaveta Tennant is a PhD student at the University of Bath, working on the intersection of AI ethics, moral alignment, and multi-agent systems. Her research focuses on understanding how moral behavior emerges in heterogeneous populations of agents and developing hybrid approaches for aligning AI systems with human moral values. She applies computational methods including evolutionary game theory to study moral dynamics.

## Research Interests

- **Moral Alignment for AI**: Developing methods to align LLM agents with diverse human moral values
- **Moral Dynamics**: Studying how moral behavior evolves in heterogeneous agent populations
- **Hybrid Alignment Approaches**: Combining multiple ethical frameworks for robust AI alignment
- **Computational Ethics**: Using computational models to understand moral decision-making

## Major Publications

- "Moral Alignment for LLM Agents" (2024) - Proposes frameworks for aligning language model agents with human moral values
- "Dynamics of Moral Behavior in Heterogeneous Populations" (2024) - Studies moral evolution using game-theoretic methods
- "Hybrid Approaches for Moral Value Alignment in AI Agents" (2025) - Advances hybrid methods for AI moral alignment
