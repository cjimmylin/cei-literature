## Abstract

Regional MTEB extension for African languages covering 59 languages, 14 tasks, 38 datasets. AfriMTEB-Lite includes 9 diverse languages. Includes hate speech detection, intent detection, emotion classification tasks. Covers 5 target languages (Amharic, Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa, Swahili, Zulu). Includes hate speech detection and emotion classification directly relevant to values/bias assessment.

## Benchmark

**Name:** AfriMTEB

## Data Description

AfriMTEB is a regional MTEB extension covering 59 African languages across 38 datasets and 8 task families, including 6 newly added datasets for hate speech detection, intent detection, and emotion classification. Also releases AfriE5-Large-Instruct, a cross-lingually adapted embedding model for African languages.
