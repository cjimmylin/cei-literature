# Hua Xu

## Research Summary

Hua Xu is a tenured Associate Professor at Tsinghua University's State Key Laboratory of Intelligent Technology and Systems, Department of Computer Science and Technology. He is a leading expert on Intelligent Natural Interaction and Service Robots, serving as Editor-in-Chief of Intelligent Systems with Applications and Associate Editor of Expert Systems. He authored a book on "Multi-Modal Sentiment Analysis" covering feature representation, fusion, and classification for human-computer interaction. He developed M-SENA, an open-source platform for multimodal sentiment analysis, and introduced CH-SIMS, a Chinese multimodal sentiment analysis dataset with 2,281 refined video segments and independent unimodal annotations. His team's self-supervised multi-task learning approach for multimodal sentiment analysis achieved state-of-the-art results on MOSI and MOSEI datasets.
