# Ahmed Sorkatti

## Research Summary

Ahmed Sorkatti is a researcher contributing to Arabic natural language processing and culturally inclusive AI development. He is part of the PALM project, a community-driven initiative spanning all 22 Arab countries that developed culturally sensitive and linguistically diverse datasets for Arabic LLMs. His work helps ensure AI systems properly represent Arab cultural contexts and dialectal Arabic variations across the Arabic-speaking world.
