## Abstract

The paper argues that AI systems solve problems based on mechanically computational, means-end reasoning (called "mechanical instrumental rationality"), while human intelligence, to the extent that it is rational, is not mechanically computable—not reducible to mechanical computations for at least four basic reasons:

1. Human intelligence is **categorically normative**
2. AI lacks **autonomous reasons-responsiveness**
3. Rational human intelligence necessarily involves **indispensable affective experience**
4. **Practical wisdom (phronesis)** requires genuine understanding

## Key Arguments

- Many in AI adopt impoverished conception of intelligence (value-free means-end reasoning)
- This contrasts fundamentally with Aristotelian ethics
- Computational moral reasoning cannot replicate human moral intelligence
- Phronesis is irreducibly non-computational
