# Christina Shane-Simpson

## Biographical Summary
Dr. Christina Shane-Simpson is a Senior Researcher with more than 10 years of experience investigating relationships between human behavior, cultural norms, social capital, and well-being in digital environments. She holds an M.S.W. and Ph.D. in Developmental Psychology from The Graduate Center, CUNY, where she studied college students' choices on social networking sites.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Digital Behavior Research**: Investigating dynamic interactions among humans in computer-mediated environments
- **Moral Judgment Studies**: Research on how situational factors shape moral judgments across cultures
- **Equity in Digital Spaces**: Identifying prejudice targeting individuals with disabilities and gender inequalities on collaborative platforms

### Significant Achievements
- Authored more than 30 peer-reviewed publications
- Co-author of large-scale international study on moral judgments in the trolley dilemma across Eastern, Southern, and Western cultures
- Created technology-based interventions to assist students with autism navigate social networking sites
- Presented at cross-disciplinary regional, national, and international conferences

## Notes
Her research on cross-cultural moral judgments directly informs AI ethics by revealing how moral decisions vary across cultures. This work is essential for developing AI systems that account for cultural diversity in moral reasoning, avoiding the imposition of culturally-specific ethical frameworks as universal standards.
