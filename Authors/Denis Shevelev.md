# Denis Shevelev

## Affiliation
Research Scientist, SberDevices / Sberbank AI Lab, Russia

## Research Summary
Denis Shevelev is a research scientist at SberDevices contributing to the development of language model evaluation frameworks for Russian. He has worked on the MERA (Multilingual Evaluation of Russian Aspects) benchmark, which provides comprehensive evaluation of large language models across diverse Russian language tasks. His research helps establish rigorous standards for assessing LLM capabilities in non-English languages.
