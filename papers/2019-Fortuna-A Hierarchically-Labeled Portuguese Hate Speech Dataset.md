## Abstract

5,668 Portuguese tweets annotated with hierarchical hate speech scheme containing 81 categories. Expert-annotated with fine-grained intersection of hate speech types.

## Data Description

A dataset of 5,668 Portuguese tweets annotated with a hierarchical hate speech scheme of 81 fine-grained categories, with both binary and expert multi-label annotations.
