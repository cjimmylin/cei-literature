# Helen Jiahe Zhao

## Biographical Summary

Helen Jiahe Zhao is a researcher affiliated with the Department of Computer Science at The University of Auckland, New Zealand. She collaborates with Jiamou Liu on developing neural network approaches for domain-specific question answering tasks, particularly in religious text analysis.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Her research focuses on domain-specific question answering using neural networks, with particular emphasis on transfer learning and domain adaptation. She investigates how pre-trained models can be adapted to specialized domains that have limited training data available, addressing a fundamental challenge in NLP.

### Significant Achievements
Zhao co-created BibleQA, a new dataset based on Bible trivia questions designed for answer sentence selection tasks where questions are answered by selecting relevant verses from the Bible. She proposed three neural network models for this task that were pre-trained on the large-scale SQuAD dataset and investigated the effect of transferring weights on model accuracy. Her research demonstrated that transfer learning provides noticeable improvement in model accuracy, with shorter context lengths yielding relatively good results while longer context lengths decreased model accuracy. The work was presented at the 2018 International Joint Conference on Neural Networks (IJCNN) and published on arXiv.
