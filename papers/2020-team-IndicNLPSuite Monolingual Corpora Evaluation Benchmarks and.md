## Abstract

NLP resources for 11 Indian languages including large-scale corpora, pre-trained embeddings, language models, and IndicGLUE evaluation benchmark. Foundational Indic NLP benchmark suite covering all four target languages.

## Benchmark

**Name:** IndicGLUE

## Data Description

IndicNLPSuite: monolingual corpora (8.8B tokens across 11 Indian languages), FastText word embeddings (IndicFT), ALBERT-based language model (IndicBERT), and IndicGLUE benchmark with NLU tasks including genre classification, headline prediction, NER, QA, and NLI.
