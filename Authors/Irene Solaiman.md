# Irene Solaiman

## Research Summary

Irene Solaiman is Chief Policy Officer at Hugging Face (since 2025), previously serving as Head of Global Policy. She focuses on AI policy, ethics, and responsible AI development. Before joining Hugging Face in 2022, she was a researcher at OpenAI where she was the first person to test for social-impact bias on large language models. She also served as an AI policy manager at Zillow for nearly a year. Her work has significantly contributed to understanding and mitigating social biases in AI systems, and she plays a key role in shaping AI governance and policy frameworks. She holds a Master in Public Policy from Harvard Kennedy School and a B.A. in International Relations from the University of Maryland.
