# Bowen Qin

## Biographical Summary

Bowen Qin is a researcher at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences. He is also an intern at Alibaba's DAMO Academy, working on the Conversational AI team. He is known for achieving state-of-the-art results on several NLP leaderboards.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focus is on the robustness of dialogue systems and semantic parsing (Text-to-SQL). He investigates how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be made more stable and grounded, specifically by mitigating hallucinations in complex reasoning tasks.

### Significant Achievements
Qin achieved the #1 spot on the Spider (Text-to-SQL) and CSQA leaderboards. His work on "Proton" (probing schema linking) significantly improved the ability of models to understand and interact with structured data without losing contextual nuance.

## Major Publications

- [Proton: Probing Schema Linking Information from Pre-trained LMs for Text-to-SQL Parsing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00000) (2022) - *Research on grounding language in data structures.*
- [Analyzing Hallucinations in Multi-turn Dialogue Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00000) (2023) - *Research on the stability of long-context AI interactions.*
