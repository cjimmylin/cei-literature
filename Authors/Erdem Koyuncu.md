# Erdem Koyuncu

## Biographical Summary

Erdem Koyuncu is an Assistant Professor at the University of Illinois Chicago, working on machine learning, information theory, and natural language processing. His research includes applications of NLP to understanding religious and philosophical texts, contributing to the development of specialized corpora and analysis tools for Buddhist teachings and other religious traditions.

## Research Interests

- **Machine Learning**: Developing and applying ML techniques
- **Information Theory**: Theoretical foundations of data and communication
- **NLP for Religious Texts**: Analyzing religious and philosophical texts computationally
- **Corpus Development**: Creating specialized text corpora for research
