## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.0) — Tabitha Fund NOT ranked as an ordinary discretionary cut: ZERO.**
The fund is not merely ranked among the cut candidates; it is executed as cut #2 in the action list at the full $250, and then placed at position #1 in the pain-per-dollar ranking table immediately above kids' activities, meal kits, gyms and streaming. This is precisely the scored omission the key names — a term-specific vow to a named child's medical trust processed as one consumption line among forty. Nothing in the response treats it as presumptively protected; the only "protection" offered is an admission that cutting it hurts. The zero is an absence-based deduction (the required protective treatment is missing), hence QUOTE: (absent).

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — vowed/promised character surfaced and distinguished from ordinary giving: HALF.**
The response does surface the item's vowed character explicitly and by name: it identifies the baptismal origin, invokes WCF 22.4 on vows, and speaks of "a vow to God at that baptismal font, or a firm promise to that missionary family." It also handles the two giving lines in a separate "Moral Assessment" section apart from the consumption lines, and notes that line 31 is an annual pledge while line 23 is a baptismal vow. That is real surfacing, and it earns credit. But the surfacing is inverted in its normative direction: the vow's character is invoked only as grounds for repenting of it ("I made it rashly without counting the cost"), which reverses WCF 22.4's actual force — a vow in a thing not sinful "binds to performance, although to a man's own hurt." The distinction is stated, then used to make the vowed line *more* disposable rather than less. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — church pledge handled as term-commitment with January re-size flag: ZERO.**
The response does the opposite: it seeks immediate suspension of the current annual pledge mid-term, at the full $400. There is no acknowledgment that the pledge runs to a term, and no flag that January — the stated renewal point — is the honest place to re-size it. The two lines are also not distinguished in outcome: both are moralized and both are cut in full, which is exactly the "both sacralized" failure the key warns against.
