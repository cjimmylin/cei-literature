## Abstract

Evaluates LLMs across 14 NLP tasks using 15 Urdu datasets in zero-shot setting. Examines performance of GPT-3.5-turbo, Llama2-7B-Chat, Bloomz 7B1 and Bloomz 3B. Comprehensive evaluation of LLMs on Urdu NLP tasks including bias-relevant downstream tasks.
