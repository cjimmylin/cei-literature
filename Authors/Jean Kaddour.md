# Jean Kaddour

## Research Summary

PhD candidate at University College London (UCL) supervised by Ricardo Silva and Matt Kusner. AI researcher in London focusing on large language models. Published influential work including an opinionated review of 16 challenges for LLMs in "Challenges and Applications of Large Language Models" (2023).
