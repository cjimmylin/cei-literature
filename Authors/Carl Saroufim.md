# Carl Saroufim

## Biographical Summary

Carl Saroufim is a researcher and engineer at Google Research, specializing in the evaluation of Large Language Models. His work is part of the broader effort at Google to build robust, trustworthy AI systems that can ground their responses in verifiable evidence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research focuses on "Factuality and Grounding"—specifically developing the "FACTS Grounding Leaderboard." This benchmark evaluates LLMs on their ability to generate long-form responses that are fully grounded in a provided 32k-token context document, which is critical for reducing hallucinations in complex user requests.

### Significant Achievements
Saroufim is a key technical contributor to the "FACTS Grounding" project, which uses automated judge models (comprehensively evaluated against human test sets) to score model factuality. This work addresses the "Evaluation Bias" often found in simpler LLM benchmarks and provides a dynamic, maintained standard for the industry.
