## Abstract

Three-layered annotated corpus of 10,563 Farsi data samples for offensive language detection. Uses similarity-based and keyword-based data selection. Best model BERT+fastText achieves F1-Macro of 89.57.

## Data Description

Pars-OFF is a three-layered annotated corpus of 10,563 Farsi social media samples for offensive language detection, constructed using similarity-based and keyword-based data selection methods.
