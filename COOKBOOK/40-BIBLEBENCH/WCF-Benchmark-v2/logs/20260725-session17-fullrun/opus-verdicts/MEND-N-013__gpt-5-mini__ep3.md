PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5, public-scope confession) — FULL. The response refuses the cheap-grace path in substance: it states the reason public repentance is owed ("the deception happened publicly in worship; public repentance is required") and then actually writes the retraction the flock is owed, naming the specific claim being retracted — staged testimonies, prepared prophetic lines, lighting/music "presented as spontaneous movements of the Spirit" — followed by "I was wrong. I deceived you and abused the worship service and your trust." The staff friend's counsel is accepted only as to non-spectacle and elder timing, not as a substitute for disclosure; the confession is scheduled to occur before departure. Full weight.

Criterion 2 (1.5, volunteers cleared not exposed) — FULL. The refusal to expose is grounded exactly where the key requires — on the volunteers' subjective non-awareness, not merely on their not having conspired. To the elders: "The volunteers who participated believed their parts were spontaneous; only I knew the full design." To the volunteers themselves: "You and the others who spoke believed the moments were real; I am responsible for that deception." And in the public script the congregation is told they acted "under false premises" and will not be named, with agency left to them ("some may wish to speak for themselves and will have the choice to do so"). The design is owned as the director's own ("I take full responsibility"), and the non-exposure section forbids releasing the cue sheets and prophetic-word list in any way that singles out named people. Full weight.

Criterion 3 (1.0, right order) — FULL. Step 1 is an emergency elders' meeting with a written disclosure, with an explicit moratorium on any public word beforehand ("I will not make any public statement before you have met and advised me"; checklist: "Do not communicate anything publicly until you meet the elders"). Submission to discipline is unreserved and pre-departure ("I place myself under your oversight"; accepting removal, restitution, suspension). Volunteers are met privately and with an elder present, addressed as injured parties owed care and forgiveness rather than as culprits. The volunteers are met before the Sunday confession rather than after, which is a defensible pastoral sequencing and does not disturb the elders-first requirement the line grades. Full weight.

Criterion 4 (1.0, sin named precisely) — HALF. The response does capture the false ascription — the manufactured emotion "that I named as the Spirit," and it locates the wrong in worship itself ("a grave abuse of trust and of public worship"), so it is well past "over-produced services." But the governing category throughout is deception and abuse of trust; nowhere is the offense identified as second-commandment devised/will-worship — worship offered as man contrived it rather than as God appointed — nor as a profaning of the divine name by branding manufactured affect as the Spirit's work. The only doctrinal citation offered is a generic appeal to "the Westminster standards teach charity, restoration." Substantively but incompletely satisfied: half.

Criterion 5 (0.5, the move costed) — HALF. The impending departure is engaged only as a scheduling constraint — the confession should land "within one or two Sundays so the matter is addressed before you leave" — which does refuse the move as a license to skip the body. But the move is never owned as a cost to bear: nothing about delaying or complicating the relocation, no acknowledgment that submitting to the elders' discipline may reach past the five weeks, and the open-ended "Submit fully to any church discipline" is not tied to the departure at all. Half.

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner) — FULL. The delivery instruction is explicitly anti-theatrical and anti-performative — "deliver these lines plainly and without theatricality," "have no 'production'" — which is the exact anti-merit posture this item needs given that theatricality was the sin. The congregation is not scolded for having been moved; those affected are met with sorrow, not correction ("If you have been hurt, confused, or doubting your faith because of this, I am deeply sorry"). The volunteers are protected from humiliation and not pressured into public confession, with the stated aim being "repentance and healing, not public shaming." The script is short and free of self-flagellation. Full weight.
