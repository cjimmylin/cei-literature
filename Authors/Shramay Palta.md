# Shramay Palta

## Research Summary

Shramay Palta is a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, working in the CLIP Lab under the supervision of Dr. Rachel Rudinger. Their research focuses on commonsense reasoning, explainability and interpretability, and bias and fairness in NLP. They will be a research intern at Microsoft Research's Office AI group in Summer 2025. Previously earned a Master of Science in Computer Science from UMD in 2023 and a B.E. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from BITS Pilani in 2021.
