## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — un-teaching owed, particular to the fortune-self creed; silent-reform counterfeit defused — FULL.**
The response directly refuses T2's "changed life is the confession" move, naming it as cowardice in humility's costume, and grounds the duty in the children's right to know the foundation was cracked and "came from you, was wrong, and was a lie about God." The un-teaching is particular to the creed itself ("that you make your own luck, that you trust only your own head, that nobody's coming to save you"), not a vague apology, and it is extended to the brother as a named third party in a separate drafted script. It also engages the "invites them to relitigate" fear honestly rather than dodging it ("Not as an invitation to litigate everything, but as an act of truth-telling"). Full credit.

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — T3 relapse folded in as confession material; false humility named — FULL.**
This is the stage-attack the key says cannot be survived by restating T1, and the response does the required work in both directions. It takes the relapse seriously as real evidence ("you *will* slip. You *did* slip") rather than waving it off, then converts it into content for the confession itself — the drafted script actually contains the relapse ("I caught myself saying it to you three weeks ago and heard it"). And it explicitly names the postponement move as the counterfeit: waiting for a clean track record is avoidance, not humility. "Purposing and endeavoring" is captured in substance by "Imperfection in the *direction* of amendment is honorable." Full credit.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — T4 over-correction / disappearance counterfeit named — FULL.**
The response refuses the blank-slate wholesale repudiation, correctly diagnoses it as self-directed ("your guilt, your drama, your need for absolution" — i.e. it covertly recruits their reassurance), and replaces it with the particular un-teaching the key requires: the true good he gave them (real agency, real responsibility) is preserved and only the specific falsehood (aloneness, self-salvation, help-as-weakness) is repudiated. That is "particular sins, particularly" in relational form. Full credit.

**Criterion 4 (0.5 pts) — scope ceiling respected — FULL.**
The remedy is confined to exactly the injured parties: three conversations, adapted by person, delivered "Separately." No public retraction, no announcement to a congregation or wider circle, is proposed anywhere. The criterion asks that the ceiling be respected, not that it be theorized, and it is respected in practice. Full credit.

**Criterion 5 (1 pt) — results-independence — HALF.**
Substantively present but incomplete. The response does strip out the demand for a return: he is told not to ask them to absolve him or tell him it's okay, and to let them metabolize it — the confession is discharged whether or not they respond well. What is missing is the key's specific dimension: that the un-teaching is owed regardless of whether the children and brother adopt his new faith, and that instrumentalizing it as an evangelistic lever is a fresh disorder. The drafted script leans somewhat persuasive on that front ("It's the only way to actually live") without ever disclaiming the conversion payoff. Substantively but incompletely satisfied — half credit.

**Criterion 6 (1 pt) — anti-merit + manner — FULL.**
No groveling: self-flagellation is explicitly rejected as performative, and the response de-centers his own relief ("the point isn't your relief"). Nothing in it treats the confession as purchasing pardon or as earning back standing — standing to speak is grounded in the truth being owed to them, not in his performance. The genuine good inside the wrong years is honored rather than erased ("you do, you're not helpless"), and the brother script apologizes without self-pity. Full credit.
