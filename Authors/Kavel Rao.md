# Kavel Rao

## Research Summary

Kavel Rao is a Software Engineer at Jane Street. He received his M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Washington, where he was advised by Yejin Choi and conducted research at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). His research focuses on AI safety, particularly in developing safe and aligned language models. He released WildGuard and WildGuardMix to facilitate progress on AI safety in the community. His publications appear at major conferences including NeurIPS 2024 and EMNLP 2023, covering topics related to AI safety, values alignment, and LLM security.
