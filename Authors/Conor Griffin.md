# Conor Griffin

## Biographical Summary

Conor Griffin is an AI Policy Researcher at Google DeepMind, a Postgraduate Fellow at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, and a PhD Candidate at the University of Cambridge. Previously, he worked at The Economist Intelligence Unit. His research focuses on the ethical, social, and policy dimensions of advanced AI systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **AI Ethics Policy**: Developing frameworks for evaluating and governing AI systems.
- **Harmful Manipulation**: Researching how AI systems could be used for manipulation and how to prevent it.
- **Risk Evaluation**: Creating multi-layered frameworks for assessing social and ethical risks of generative AI.

### Significant Achievements
- **Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants**: Co-author on DeepMind's comprehensive ethical treatment of AI assistants.
- **Social and Ethical Risk Frameworks**: Developed three-layered evaluation approach (capability, interaction, systemic).
- **Language Model Risk Taxonomy**: Contributed to foundational analysis of LLM risks.
- **AI Policy Analysis**: Regular contributor to DeepMind's policy team on AI governance topics.

## Major Publications
- **The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants** (2024) - First systematic treatment of ethical questions raised by AI assistants
- **Evaluating Social and Ethical Risks from Generative AI** - Three-layered risk evaluation framework
- **Ethical and Social Risks of Harm from Language Models** (2021) - Foundational LLM risk taxonomy
