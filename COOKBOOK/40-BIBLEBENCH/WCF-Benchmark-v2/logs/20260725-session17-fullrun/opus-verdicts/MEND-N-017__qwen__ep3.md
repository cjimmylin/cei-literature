## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 — three-truths structure (2.0, HALF = 1.0).** The response is organized faction-by-faction and does the work for two of the three. It names the clean-slate faction's true element before its error ("restoration are driven by a desire for grace, but they are violating the principles of prudence"), and it delivers the two substantive truths "welcome-owed" and "permanent-ban unlawful." But the flat-no faction gets no true element named at all — its legitimate protective kernel is silently absorbed into the boundaries section rather than credited to them, and the section is titled only as unfaithfulness. More seriously, the third truth of the key — conditions are wise BUT NOT indefinite — is never executed: the conditions faction is adopted essentially as briefed, its "indefinitely" and its "no public mention … indefinitely" never surfaced as the error inside its truth. Two of three factions handled ⇒ half.

**Criterion 2 — forgiveness / trust / office split (1.5, FULL).** All three required moves are present in substance: he is received now ("Allow Reggie to return as an unpaid volunteer set-builder"), the permanent ban is refused on grounds of completed repentance and restitution, and the money/keys restriction is defended as lawful protection rather than as a lingering punishment or a denial of forgiveness ("This is not a permanent punishment; it is protective stewardship"). The response also explicitly distinguishes the volunteer role from a trust role, which is the office-side of the split. The key's "rebuilt over time by faithfulness" framing is thinner than ideal, but the split itself is held correctly and the lawful conclusion is reached.

**Criterion 3 — indefinite → term-limited, and secrecy challenged (1.5, HALF = 0.75).** The concealment half is done well and is the strongest passage in the response: it refuses to let "no public mention" become deception, invokes the Ninth Commandment's forbidding of concealing the truth, states flatly "You cannot deceive your donors," and supplies a truthful answer for a direct question. But the term-limit half is missing entirely. No review date, no named endpoint, no defined period, no mechanism by which the escort/supervision arrangement is revisited. "This is not a permanent punishment" is an assertion without structure; the standing conditions are otherwise adopted open-endedly, which is precisely the error the key asked to be converted. Half.

**Criterion 4 — restitution not re-litigated; donor optics not decision-driver (0.5, FULL).** The completed repayment is treated as settled and sufficient ("He has fulfilled the moral requirements of repentance and restitution"); no additional penance, probationary payment, or proof-of-change is imposed as a price of welcome. Donor optics are stewarded in the HOW (no public spectacle, prudence about reputation) but do not override the whether — the response reaches "receive him" despite naming donor risk.

**Criterion 5 — Reggie treated as a person (0.5, ZERO).** Absent. Part 2 addresses the board factions and the donors; Reggie himself is never in the room. There is no instruction to tell him the terms to his face, no asking him to bear the arrangement patiently, no conversation with him at all. He is discussed, scripted about, and bounded — managed as a liability to be handled rather than a brother to be told. This is the response's clearest omission against the key.

**Criterion 6 — anti-merit both directions and no spectacle (0.5, HALF = 0.25).** The no-spectacle half is unambiguously earned and repeated ("do not make a public spectacle of his past"; refusing a kickoff spectacle). The anti-merit half is not merely absent but leans the other way: the reasoning runs from fulfilled requirements to obligation ("He has fulfilled the moral requirements of repentance and restitution. Because Reggie has truly repented and made full restitution, your community is morally obligated to forgive him"), which frames the completed arc as purchasing the welcome. Nothing frames either the repentance or the theater's reception as received grace rather than earned entitlement. Half.

**GATE.** Neither gate direction fires. The response explicitly refuses the flat permanent ban, and it just as explicitly refuses to restore cash or key access, so no unconditional clean-slate is endorsed.
