# Sander Schulhoff

## Research Summary

Natural Language Processing Researcher at University of Maryland and Co-founder/CEO of Learn Prompting and HackAPrompt. Research focuses on adversarial robustness and prompt engineering for large language models. Has performed research in collaboration with all major AI companies and published at prestigious AI conferences including EMNLP, NeurIPS, ICLR, and ICML. Known for creating HackAPrompt competition and Learn Prompting educational platform. Based in San Francisco.
