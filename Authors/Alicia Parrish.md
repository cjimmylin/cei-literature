# Alicia Parrish

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alicia Parrish is a Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind based in New York. She earned her PhD in Linguistics from New York University, where her research focused on the intersection of theoretical linguistics and computational methods.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alicia's work centers on improving the quality and reliability of NLP evaluations. She has published extensively on how linguistic nuances and data artifacts can bias the assessment of large language models. Her research is pivotal for building safe and robust AI systems that understand human language with precision.
