# MultiLoKo: A Multilingual Local Knowledge Benchmark for LLMs Spanning 31 Languages

## Authors

[[Hupkes, Dieuwke]], [[Bogoychev, Nikolay]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Hupkes, Dieuwke; Bogoychev, Nikolay

**Year:** 2025

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** Meta Research

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10356
- **Code/Data:** https://github.com/facebookresearch/multiloko

## Abstract

Multilingual local knowledge benchmark with 500 questions per language separately sourced to be locally relevant. Khmer identified among the most difficult languages alongside Farsi and Malay. Covers Khmer with locally relevant knowledge questions. Performance difficulties in Khmer highlight knowledge representation gaps. Tests culturally local knowledge.

## Benchmark

**Name:** MultiLoKo

## Data Description

MultiLoKo benchmark dataset with 500 locally-sourced questions per language across 31 languages, plus human- and machine-authored translations, totalling 15,500 parallel samples. Includes language-specific prompts, few-shot examples, and evaluation scripts.
