## Abstract

4,359 comments in European Spanish from online newspapers annotated for toxicity, stance, target, stereotype, sarcasm, mockery, insult, improper language, aggressiveness, and intolerance. Multi-level annotation considering discourse context.

## Data Description

NewsCom-TOX is a corpus of 4,359 Spanish-language comments on news articles annotated for toxicity. Provides fine-grained toxicity annotations for online discourse analysis. An annotation resource with no LLMs directly tested.
