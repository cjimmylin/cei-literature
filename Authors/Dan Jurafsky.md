# Dan Jurafsky

## Affiliation
Professor of Linguistics and Computer Science, Stanford University

## Research Summary

Dan Jurafsky is one of the most influential figures in natural language processing and computational linguistics. He is a Professor at Stanford University with appointments in both Linguistics and Computer Science. He co-authored the widely-used textbook "Speech and Language Processing" (with James H. Martin), which has trained generations of NLP researchers. His research spans speech recognition, computational semantics, and increasingly the study of social bias in language models and NLP systems.
