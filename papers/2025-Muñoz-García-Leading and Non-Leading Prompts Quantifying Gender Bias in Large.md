## Abstract

Validated methodology to quantify gender bias in Spanish LLM-generated text. Creates gendered seed-word lists and a Spanish-specific corpus using curated prompts. Finds male:female gender disparity ranging 4:1 to 6:1 in benchmark datasets.

## Data Description

Spanish-specific corpus constructed using curated prompts and gendered seed-word lists to quantify gender bias in Spanish LLM-generated text; finds 4:1 to 6:1 male:female gender disparity.
