# Ali Dasdan

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ali Dasdan is the Chief Technology Officer at Dropbox and a veteran technical leader with previous roles at ZoomInfo, eBay, and Google. Alongside his executive leadership, he maintains an active interest in the ethical governance of the AI systems he oversees.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ali's research contributions focus on the "Auditing and Governance" side of AI safety. He co-authored work on auditing the ethical logic of generative models, advocating for more rigorous internal and external oversight to prevent harmful outputs and ensure transparency in enterprise AI applications.

## Major Publications
- [Auditing the Ethical Logic of Generative AI Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00000) (2024) - *Proposing frameworks for technical and ethical audits of LLMs.*
