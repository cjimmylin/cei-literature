# Hao Chen

## Research Summary

Hao Chen is a 4th-year PhD candidate at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Bhiksha Raj. His research focuses on building more reliable and adaptable foundation models in realistic scenarios using data-centric methods. This includes exploring pre-training data imperfections and investigating how different types of data imperfections (corruption, bias, diversity) influence the physics of foundation models.

## Key Contributions

- Co-author of "A Survey on Evaluation of Large Language Models," accepted by ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology (TIST)
- Contributor to PromptBench, a unified evaluation framework for large language models
- NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight paper on "Slight Corruption in Pre-training Data Makes Better Diffusion Models"
- Presenting two tutorials at AAAI 2025 on LLM evaluation and semi-supervised learning
