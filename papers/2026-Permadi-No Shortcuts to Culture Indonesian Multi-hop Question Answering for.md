## Abstract

First large-scale multi-hop QA dataset (15,590 questions) for assessing LLM cultural understanding grounded in Indonesian traditions. Transforms single-hop cultural questions into multi-hop reasoning chains spanning six clue types.

## Notes

**Source:** Language-Organized Benchmark Search (20260213), Agent A6
**Relevance:** Directly evaluates LLM understanding of Indonesian cultural values through complex multi-hop reasoning about traditions and customs.
