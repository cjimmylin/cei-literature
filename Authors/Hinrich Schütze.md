# Hinrich Schütze

## Research Summary

Hinrich Schütze is Professor of Computational Linguistics and Co-director of the Center for Information and Language Processing at LMU Munich, Germany. Before moving to Munich in 2013, he taught at the University of Stuttgart. His research spans large language models, knowledge expansion in NLP models, representation learning and interpretability, multilingual NLP, and the intersection of NLP and robotics. His lab explores the behavior, structure, and potential of LLMs, examining their capabilities, biases, and self-assessment mechanisms. The lab receives funding from the Munich Center for Machine Learning, the Elitenetzwerk Bayern, the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft, and holds an ERC Advanced Grant. He is the principal LMU partner for the AI4Dignity Project.
