## 2. The P0 coupling (the load-bearing consequence)

The 2026-07-05 gate made DUPLEX's discrimination premise **conditional on a falsification-first P0
smoke test** (direct evidence today: one instrument, one unreplicated single-model effect). This cuts
both ways for FRUITBENCH:

- **Risk inheritance.** The four DUPLEX-collinear virtue designs inherit P0's risk wholesale: if
  frontier models' process/stability axes converge the way content axes do, equanimity, long-fuse,
  marshmallow, and one-fruit headline metrics will not discriminate either. Nobody should author
  items for these four before P0 reports.
- **Opportunity.** FRUITBENCH's manipulations are themselves *process manipulations* — mood induction
  (02), escalating provocation ladders (04), payoff × detection-probability × time-pressure factorials
  (09). **Recommendation: contribute 1–2 cells from each to DUPLEX's P0 manipulation set.** P0 gets
  more independent shots at finding a discriminating manipulation, and FRUITBENCH gets its
  discrimination question answered for free, on someone else's harness, before spending on items.
- Either P0 outcome is informative here: pass → the four re-badged conditions proceed on DUPLEX
  machinery; fail → they are re-scoped alongside DUPLEX as universal-signature (descriptive)
  measurement — which for a confessional audience may still be worth shipping, but under Law-7
  framing, not as a leaderboard.

## 4. Denominational conditioning: adjudicated ruling

The FRUITBENCH audit graded denominational conditioning MISSING and "fixable, not
appropriate-by-construct." **Partially overruled: apply selective forks, not a blanket D-layer.**
Virtue *conduct* is largely pan-Christian (patience is patience), so per-item Catholic/Orthodox/
Protestant forks would mostly manufacture divergence — the exact anti-pattern KB-12 warns about.
But documented divergences exist and should be forked where real: 03 eirene (pacifist vs just-war
traditions on what peacemaking permits), 08 prautes (authority/obedience traditions on when
gentleness must yield), and arguably 02 chara (ascetic vs affirmational readings of joy).
Everything else conditions at panel level (Prot+Cath+Orth raters), which the pack already has.
INTRA's D-flag machinery is imported only for the forked items.
