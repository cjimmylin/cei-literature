# Rayane El Masri

## Research Summary

PhD candidate at Queensland University of Technology (QUT) specializing in AI ethics and value alignment in generative AI. Thesis focuses on "Social Value, Ethics and Diversity in Generative AI Value Alignment – the case of Care Ethics" under supervision of Dr Aaron Snoswell. Affiliated with the GenAI Lab, QUT Digital Media Research Centre, and the ARC Centre of Excellence for Automated Decision-Making and Society (ADM+S) - QUT Node. U.S. State Department Alumni dedicated to empowering underprivileged individuals through education.
