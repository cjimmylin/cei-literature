# Christopher Carnahan

## Biographical Summary
Christopher Carnahan is a researcher affiliated with the University of Arizona who specializes in AI security and prompt engineering. He was a competition winner in the HackAPrompt challenge and has contributed to understanding vulnerabilities in large language model deployments.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **LLM Security**: Investigating vulnerabilities in large language model deployments
- **Prompt Injection**: Analyzing techniques for manipulating AI systems through adversarial prompts
- **AI Red-Teaming**: Developing methods for testing AI system robustness

### Significant Achievements
- Competition winner in HackAPrompt, a global prompt hacking competition
- Co-author of influential EMNLP 2023 paper documenting 29 prompt hacking techniques
- Contributed to the largest prompt injection dataset to date (600K+ adversarial prompts)
- Helped discover new attack techniques including the Context Overflow attack
- Published blog post "How a $5000 Prompt Injection Contest Helped Me Become a Better Prompt Engineer" (2023)
