# Julian Rodemann

## Research Summary

PhD student at LMU Munich (Statistics) and researcher at CISPA. Research focuses on optimization, online learning, and decision theory within machine learning, emphasizing reliability through uncertainty representation using imprecise probabilities. Published "A Statistical Case Against Empirical Human-AI Alignment" (May 2025), arguing empirical alignment can introduce statistical biases and advocating for prescriptive alignment alternatives. Part of Graduate Center at Bavarian Research Institute for Digital Transformation (bidt), funded by Federal Statistical Office of Germany.
