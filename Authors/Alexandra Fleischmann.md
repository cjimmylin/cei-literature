# Alexandra Fleischmann

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alexandra Fleischmann is a social psychologist specializing in the cognitive processes underlying moral judgment. She earned her PhD from the University of Cologne, where she researched the relativity of morality and the impact of social comparison on ethical decisions. She currently applies her expertise in advanced analytics in the private sector.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alexandra's academic work focuses on why human moral judgments are often inconsistent and highly dependent on situational context. She has contributed to large-scale global studies investigating whether core moral intuitions—such as the preference for the "greater good" in the Trolley Problem—hold steady across different nations and demographic groups.
