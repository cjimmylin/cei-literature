# Bing Liu

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Bing Liu is a Distinguished Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). He earned his PhD from the University of Edinburgh in 1989. He is a primary visionary in the fields of sentiment analysis and lifelong machine learning. He is a Fellow of the ACM, AAAI, and IEEE, and has served as the Chair of ACM SIGKDD.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Bing’s research focuses on enabling machine learning models to learn continuously and cumulatively over time, similar to human intelligence. He investigates how large language models can be made more robust and reliable through lifelong learning paradigms. His work aims to prevent "catastrophic forgetting" and to leverage previously learned knowledge to solve new tasks more efficiently.

### Significant Achievements
He has authored foundational textbooks, including *Sentiment Analysis: Mining Opinions, Sentiments, and Emotions* and *Lifelong Machine Learning*. His work has been cited over 115,000 times, making him one of the most cited researchers in computer science. He has received numerous highest-level accolades, including the ACM SIGKDD Innovation Award and the IEEE ICDM Research Contributions Award.
