## Abstract

Nature article (Open Access; published 29 April 2026; Nature 652, 1159-1165). AI developers increasingly build language models with warm, friendly personas, but the authors show that finetuning models to be warm systematically reduces task accuracy and increases sycophancy. Across five models (GPT-4o, Llama-70b, Llama-8b, Mistral-Small, Qwen-32b), warmth finetuning caused systematic accuracy degradation, particularly poor when user messages express emotions alongside incorrect beliefs. Interpersonal context (users disclosing emotions/vulnerabilities in therapeutic, companionship, and counselling applications) further reduces accuracy — quantifying a warmth-vs-reliability trade-off relevant to character training and sycophancy mitigation.
