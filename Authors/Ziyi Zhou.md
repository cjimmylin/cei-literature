# Ziyi Zhou

## Research Summary

Ziyi Zhou is a PhD candidate at Georgia Institute of Technology focusing on legged robotics, robotic manipulation, optimization, and artificial intelligence. Previously, she worked as a Research Engineer at Samsung Research America's Digital Media Solutions Lab on advanced computer vision projects, after completing her Master of Science in Computer Vision at Carnegie Mellon University. Her research combines AI and robotics to advance autonomous systems.
