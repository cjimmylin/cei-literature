# Patrick Schramowski

## Research Summary

Patrick Schramowski is a research group leader at the DFKI (German Research Center for AI) of the TU Darmstadt University, Germany (2023-Present), and currently an Ethical AI Advocate at ontocord.ai. His research focuses on ethical implications of AI on society, focusing on large-scale, self-supervised, transformer-based models, investigating how these models capture hidden knowledge to address ethical concerns.
