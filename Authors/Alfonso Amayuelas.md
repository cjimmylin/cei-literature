# Alfonso Amayuelas

## Biographical Summary

Alfonso Amayuelas is a PhD candidate at UC Santa Barbara and a researcher who has completed significant internships at Microsoft and Apple. His work focuses on the cross-section of multilingual natural language processing and AI safety.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alfonso is a key contributor to the *Kaleidoscope* project, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate bias and safety across a wide range of languages. His research aims to ensure that safety guardrails in foundation models are not just English-centric but robust across global linguistic contexts.

## Major Publications
- [Kaleidoscope: A Benchmark for Multilingual AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00000) (2024) - *Standardizing safety evaluations for non-English models.*
