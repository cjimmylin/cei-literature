## Abstract

First comprehensive Persian NLU benchmark with 6 tasks: reading comprehension, multiple-choice QA, textual entailment, sentiment analysis, query paraphrasing, and machine translation. Expert-annotated by native Persian speakers.

## Data Description

ParsiNLU: first comprehensive Persian NLU benchmark with 14.5k instances across 6 tasks: reading comprehension, multiple-choice QA, textual entailment, sentiment analysis, query paraphrasing, and machine translation.
