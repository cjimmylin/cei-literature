# Jared Celniker

## Research Summary

Jared B. Celniker is a Research Director at Arizona State University, leading the Humility in Inquiry Project. He has a PhD in social psychology with a major concentration in Social Psychology and a minor in Quantitative Psychology, with research emphases in political psychology and moral psychology.

His research explores how political and moral beliefs shape how people understand the world and each other. He leads the research arm of a $3.4M interdisciplinary initiative investigating epistemic cognition and strategies for fostering humility in public discourse and academic practice, overseeing project strategy across randomized experiments, longitudinal surveys, philosophical analysis, and public engagement.

Celniker's work reviews empirical data demonstrating robust ingroup favoritism in political judgment, showing that partisans display systematic tendencies to seek out, believe, and remember information that supports their political beliefs and affinities. His meta-analytic work examines whether there is empirical evidence that one side of the political spectrum shows more bias than another.

He communicates complex behavioral science concepts to broad audiences through publications in Scientific American, USA Today, Psychology Today, and the Arizona Republic.
