# Azim Shariff

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Azim Shariff is a Professor and Canada 150 Research Chair in the Department of Psychology at the University of British Columbia. He researches how our moral intuitions evolved and how they shape our behavior in the modern world, particularly in the context of emerging technologies.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Azim’s work explores the intersection of morality, psychology, and technology. He is a primary investigator of the **Moral Machine** project, studying global perspectives on the ethical dilemmas faced by autonomous vehicles. He investigates how sociotechnical systems can be designed to align with human moral values.

### Significant Achievements
He has published in top scientific journals like *Nature*, *Science*, and *The Lancet*. His work on the Moral Machine is one of the largest studies of moral preferences ever conducted, influencing policy discussions around the globe. He is widely recognized for his public scholarship on the ethics of automation.
