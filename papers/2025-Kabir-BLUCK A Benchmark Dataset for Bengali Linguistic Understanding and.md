## Abstract

2,366 multiple-choice questions encompassing extensive knowledge of the culture, history, and language of Bangladesh in 23 subcategories. Tests LLM cultural knowledge of Bengali-speaking communities.

## Data Description

BLUCK is a 2,366-question multiple-choice benchmark for Bengali linguistic understanding and cultural knowledge of Bangladesh, spanning 23 subcategories across history, culture, phonetics, and semantics, sourced from official Bangladeshi examinations.
