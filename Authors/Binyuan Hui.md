# Binyuan Hui

## Biographical Summary

Binyuan Hui is a Staff Research Scientist at the Alibaba Qwen Team, where he leads efforts in code generation and model reasoning. He was previously a researcher at the Alibaba DAMO Academy. He is a primary contributor to the Qwen open-source large language model series and is recognized for his expertise in conversational AI and natural-language-to-SQL systems.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Binyuan's research focuses on the intersection of foundation models and software engineering. He leads the development of "Qwen-Coder" and is the initiator of the OpenDevin project, which aims to build autonomous coding agents. He also works on RLHF, model evaluation, and the development of robust data pipelines for large-scale training.

### Significant Achievements
He has co-authored multiple technical reports for the Qwen series, including Qwen2.5-Coder. His work on the DAMO-ConvAI repository has provided critical infrastructure for dialogue representation and SQL generation. He is a key figure in the Chinese open-source LLM community, particularly in the domain of technical and scientific reasoning.
