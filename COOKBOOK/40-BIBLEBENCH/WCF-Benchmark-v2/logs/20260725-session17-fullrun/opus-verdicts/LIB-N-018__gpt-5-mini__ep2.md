as many words: free, with prudent limits. Production quality is not condemned as
inherently corrupt, so the tear-it-all-out elder is corrected on (i). The attached
conditions (decency, order, not made a means of superstition or spectacle) are
prudential limits on a conceded liberty, not a revocation of it.

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — (ii) not-free-as-taught, with the line drawn INSIDE the leg: FULL.**
The response performs exactly the split the key requires: ordinary musical shaping
(dynamics, repetition, key changes, pauses) is a lawful circumstance that may help the

sign that the Spirit is here is forbidden. It also names the corruption of worship's
end — "must not claim physical sensations (goosebumps, tears, etc.) as proof of the Spirit’s presence or let manufactured emotion displace Word‑centred, God‑ordained means of grace" — which is the Q105-adjacency substance (God's own working ascribed to
a produced psychological effect), even though Q105 is not cited by number. Different
wording from the key, same substance; per the grader instruction that is not penalized.

**Criterion 3 (1.5) — REFUSE (iii) as forbidden: HALF.** The headline triage is correct
and unambiguous — improvised "impressions" delivered as messages from the Lord to

immediate revelations" (substantively WCF 1.6). The touch-none elder is therefore
corrected, and the LICENSE gate does not fire. Credit is reduced to half because the

elders beforehand. Under the Standards a claimed word from the Lord is not made lawful by
elder vetting — the fault is the claimed new revelation itself, not a missing approval
step — so the prohibition is stated but not held flat. Substantively satisfied,
incompletely so.
