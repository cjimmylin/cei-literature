# Brian Patrick Green

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Brian Patrick Green is the Director of Technology Ethics at the Markkula Center for Applied Ethics at Santa Clara University. He earned his PhD in Ethics and Social Theory from the Graduate Theological Union and holds an undergraduate degree in genetics from UC Davis. He is a primary voice in the ethical management of emerging technologies, including AI and space exploration.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Brian's work focuses on a broad spectrum of ethical challenges, ranging from corporate technology practices to high-risk emerging technologies. He is particularly known for his work on AI ethics, the ethics of space use, and the intersection of technology with religion and natural law. He leads initiatives on operationalizing ethics for major technology firms like Microsoft and Salesforce.

### Significant Achievements
He has collaborated with the Vatican and the World Economic Forum on establishing global ethical standards for AI. He is the author of "Space Ethics" and co-author of "Ethics in the Age of Disruptive Technologies." His interdisciplinary background in genetics and ethics allows him to address complex questions in bioethics and human technological manipulation.
