# Katharina Kann

## Research Summary

Katharina Kann (also known as Katharina von der Wense) is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder. Her research focuses on deep learning for natural language processing, with particular interests in transfer learning, approaches for low-resource languages, and computational morphology. She works on making NLP technologies more accessible and effective for underrepresented and low-resource languages.
