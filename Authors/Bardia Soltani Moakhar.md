# Bardia Soltani Moakhar

## Biographical Summary

Bardia Soltani Moakhar is an undergraduate student in Industrial Engineering at Sharif University of Technology. Despite being early in his academic career, he has contributed to significant research in deep learning and machine learning robustness.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research interests include the theory of robustness in deep learning and video anomaly detection. He has also been involved in evaluating multilingual language understanding models.

### Significant Achievements
He co-authored a spotlight paper at ICLR 2025 titled "INCLUDE," which evaluates regional knowledge in multilingual models. He also contributed to "FrameShield," a robust video anomaly detection system presented at NeurIPS 2025.
