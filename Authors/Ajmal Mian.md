# Ajmal Mian

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ajmal Mian is a Professor of Computer Science at The University of Western Australia (UWA) and leads the Machine Intelligence Group. He is an ARC Future Fellow and a Fellow of the International Association for Pattern Recognition (IAPR). He is internationally recognized for his contributions to 3D computer vision and biometric recognition.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Ajmal's research program encompasses 3D point cloud analysis, human action recognition, and robust deep learning for computer vision. He investigates adversarial attacks and defenses on deep models and applies his expertise to medical imaging, remote sensing, and digital dentistry.

### Significant Achievements
He has published over 300 scientific papers and has been recognized as a highly cited researcher in computer vision and face recognition. He has secured major grants from the ARC, NHMRC, and DARPA, reflecting the global impact and utility of his work in automated sensing and machine intelligence.

## Major Publications

- [Automatic 3D Face Recognition Using Model-Based Representation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4107149) (2007) - *A foundational paper in biometric recognition.*
- [Deep Affinity Network for Multiple Object Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11760) (2018) - *Advanced research on video analysis and tracking.*
