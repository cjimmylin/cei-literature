# Zhiting Fan

## Research Summary

Zhiting Fan is a researcher at Zhejiang University focusing on fairness in Large Language Models. He developed FairMT-Bench, a comprehensive fairness benchmark for LLMs in multi-turn dialogue scenarios accepted to ICLR 2025 as a Spotlight paper. The benchmark addresses fairness challenges in conversational complexity and bias accumulation across dialogue turns, formulating a task taxonomy targeting LLM fairness capabilities across context understanding, user interaction, and instruction trade-offs. He also contributed to FairSteer for inference-time debiasing in LLMs.
