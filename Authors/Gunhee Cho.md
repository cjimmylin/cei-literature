# Gunhee Cho

## Biographical Summary

Gunhee Cho is a researcher working on moral reasoning in AI systems, particularly focused on analyzing the moral evolution of characters in long-form narratives using large language models.

## Research Focus

Cho's research focuses on understanding how AI systems can classify and analyze moral reasoning in complex narrative contexts. His work involves developing frameworks for automatically classifying actions as moral or immoral based on context.

## Notable Contributions

Cho is a co-author of "CharMoral: A Character Morality Dataset for Morally Dynamic Character Analysis in Long-Form Narratives," published at COLING 2025. The research was authored by Suyoung Bae, Gunhee Cho, Yun-Gyung Cheong, and Boyang Li.

Key features of CharMoral:
- Built from 1,337 movie synopses with annotations for character actions, context, and morality labels
- Proposes a four-stage framework utilizing Large Language Models to automatically classify actions as moral or immoral based on context
- Human evaluations and various experiments confirmed the framework's effectiveness in moral reasoning tasks across multiple genres
- The code and CharMoral dataset are publicly available at https://github.com/BaeSuyoung/CharMoral
