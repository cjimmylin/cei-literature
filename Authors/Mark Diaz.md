# Mark Diaz

## Biographical Summary

Mark Diaz is a Research Scientist at Google Research working on responsible AI and fairness in machine learning systems. His research focuses on understanding how diverse perspectives shape AI development, improving annotation practices for AI training data, and developing more equitable AI systems. He has contributed to major initiatives on AI safety evaluation and rater diversity.

## Research Focus

Diaz's work examines how human annotator diversity affects AI system behavior and fairness. He studies crowdsourcing methodologies, demographic representation in training data, and methods to better capture diverse perspectives in AI evaluation. His research on the DICES dataset addresses safety evaluation in conversational AI.

## Major Publications

- "DICES Dataset: Diversity in Conversational AI Evaluation for Safety" (2023) - Major dataset for AI safety evaluation with diverse rater perspectives
- Research on rater diversity and annotation quality
- Studies on fairness and representation in ML systems
