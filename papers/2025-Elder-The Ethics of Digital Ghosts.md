# The Ethics of Digital Ghosts: Confucian, Mohist, and Zhuangist Perspectives on AI and Death

## Authors

[[Alexis M. Elder]]

## Links


- **ResearchGate:** https://www.researchgate.net/

## Abstract

This book explores the ethics of AI from multiple Chinese philosophical traditions, including **Confucianism**, Mohism, and Zhuangism. Drawing on **virtue ethics** concepts like **ren** (benevolence), **li** (propriety), and **yi** (righteousness), Elder examines how these frameworks can inform the ethical development of AI systems, particularly those related to digital representations of deceased individuals. The book argues that **Confucian virtue ethics** views caregiving as a significant source of meaning and moral self-cultivation, offering insights for designing AI systems that support **human flourishing** and relational responsibilities.
