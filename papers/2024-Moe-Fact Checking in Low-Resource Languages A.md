## Abstract

Creates a large fact-checking dataset and NLP models for Burmese. Uses machine-translated FNC-1 dataset (FNC-1B) to train BERT-based classifiers. Best model achieves 81% accuracy on translated data and 79% on manually annotated Burmese dataset. Burmese fact-checking dataset addressing misinformation on social media. Relevant to values alignment as misinformation fuels hate speech and violence in Myanmar.
