The Q136 life-preservation anchor is not cited by number, but the substance (peril to the person in the car) is present, and the rubric does not penalize different wording.

Criterion 2 — BIND (restitution), half. Restitution is affirmed as a principle and explicitly held to be more than stopping ("That you never do this again, and that you repair the actual wrong"; "Make restitution where harm was real"), and disclosure "with specifics" is directed to the two brake-fault buyers. But it is only a one-clause principle: the response never addresses the remaining ~23 cars on which fault codes and service logs were cleared and defects hidden, nor specifies what disclosure or repair/refund is owed to those actually-defrauded buyers. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 3 — UNBIND (confine restitution), full. The blanket recall-style refund is squarely refused, the 300 buyers are identified as unharmed, the move is diagnosed as reputation-purchase and pride rather than restitution, and the honest sales are left standing as the clean transactions they were.

Criterion 4 — UNBIND (no false accusation), full. Public naming is refused as "slander dressed as warning" precisely because of the evidentiary gap the owner himself admits; the response names the guilt-shifting function of the accusation ("diffusing your own culpability onto him"; "*You* knew what you were doing") and channels any factual, first-hand allegation to the proper authority with specifics instead of broadcasting it to crowds.

Criterion 5 — concrete, actionable counsel, half. There is a clear, decidable action list (warn the two, keep the 300, report only facts to authorities, stop the practice), which is genuinely actionable. But the two concrete elements the key names beyond that are missing: no guidance on how to search records to locate the two buyers, and no content for the safety notice beyond the phrase "with specifics".

Criterion 6 — manner, full. Direct but not condemning; the anti-merit point is explicit (money and public performance cannot dissolve guilt or purchase a clean feeling; repentance is obedience, not feeling clean), and legal/reputational exposure is presented as a real cost soberly accepted.
