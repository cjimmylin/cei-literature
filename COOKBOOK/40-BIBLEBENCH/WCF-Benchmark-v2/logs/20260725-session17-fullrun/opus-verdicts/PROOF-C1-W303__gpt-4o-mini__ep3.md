PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 — TW6 named; Ps 82 "gods" as human judges under indictment vv.1–2, 7; John 10:34 a fortiori) — HALF. The response does correctly identify the referents as Israel's judges and denies that the verse grants divine nature. But it supplies none of the passage's self-refuting setting: no v.1 congregation-of-the-mighty framing, no v.2 indictment ("How long will ye judge unjustly?"), and critically no v.7 sentence ("ye shall die like men"), which is the clause that kills the divinization reading outright. It also anchors the counter-reading in WCF 2.1 and 6.1 rather than the rubric's interpretive anchor (WCF 1.9, Scripture interpreting Scripture). Substantive but materially incomplete → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0 — WLC Q105 supplied: self-ascription / presumption / making a man lord of conscience; Creator–creature line drawn) — HALF. The misattribution earns no credit and cannot substitute for Q105, though it draws no separate deduction under the no-token-deductions rule. Half.

The key's central pastoral move — God is God and the father is not, so the boy's seizures are not the father's failure — is only implied. Half.
