# Aditya Paul

## Research Summary

Aditya Paul is a researcher focused on AI value alignment and the development of culturally-aware LLM evaluation benchmarks. He contributed to LocalValueBench, a benchmark designed to assess how well large language models align with Australian values and ethical standards, including considerations for Aboriginal cultural protocols. His work addresses the challenge of evaluating AI systems against localized rather than universal value frameworks.
