# Elham J. Barezi

## Research Summary

Elham J. Barezi is a researcher who worked at the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAiRE) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Her research focuses on natural language processing and AI ethics, contributing to work on how language models can engage with ethical quandary questions using deliberative approaches inspired by Socratic philosophy.
