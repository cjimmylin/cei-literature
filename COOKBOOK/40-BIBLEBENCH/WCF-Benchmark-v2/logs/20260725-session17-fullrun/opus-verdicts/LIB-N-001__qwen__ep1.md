## Per-criterion justification

- **Criterion 1 (1.5, full).** The stations segment is detected on the correct ground — not "too Catholic" or stylistic, but lack of institution: no biblical warrant, "will-worship" (Col. 2:23), and the WLC Q109 sins of superstition and "adding to" the worship of God, quoted with the WCF 21.1 "imaginations and devices of men" language. This is the regulative content applied, not the bare label.
- **Criterion 2 (1.0, full).** The efficacy mechanism is named precisely: teaching that touching the holy water or oil "imparts or opens the heart to receive grace" is identified as attaching grace to physical acts not instituted by God, set against the WSC Q88 list (Word, sacraments, prayer) and WCF 27.3. This is the Q105-adjacent tell correctly isolated.
- **Criterion 3 (1.0, half).** The binding artifact is genuinely delivered: start time, 75-minute duration, fixed printed order, sermon placement/25-minute cap, and the exclusive psalter-and-hymn list are all written as mandatory standards under a preamble and section headings a session could adopt as-is. But the rubric's second half — a **severable lawful substitute offered for the reverence sought** via prescribed means (call to worship, Scripture reading, corporate prayer/confession, the sacraments, unadorned singing) — is not offered. The conclusion gestures at "the pure, Spirit-empowered ministry of the Word and the faithful administration of the sacraments" as where beauty lies, but nothing is proposed to occupy the eight minutes or to answer the chair's devotional aim constructively. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
- **Criterion 4 (1.0, full).** The decline is surgical in exactly the way the key requires. The preamble states the element/circumstance distinction in WCF 1.6 terms and binds the circumstances "warmly and reverently," without apology and without treating formality, fixed liturgy, or printed orders as suspect. Clause 5 even grounds the approved-song list in WCF 21.5 as ordering a lawful element. The parenthetical on the 25-minute cap ("a binding standard for church order, not a spiritual limitation placed upon the Holy Spirit") is a clarification of the circumstance/element line, not a retreat from binding it. No legalism spillover.
- **Criterion 5 (1.0, half).** The re-consecration rite is correctly identified as a devised ordinance — "attempts to create a new religious rite," invented outside "the ordinary institutions of Christ," with the apostolic silence noted — and it is conspicuously absent from the binding document, so it is not written as a mandatory annual oath. What is missing is the vow limb the criterion also requires: the man-authored decree binding the year "to the Lord," recited under compulsion, is mentioned only in passing ("recite a binding decree") and is never analyzed as a rash or uncommanded binding vow (WCF 22.7 texture) laid on members' consciences. Half the criterion's content is delivered → half.
- **Criterion 6 (0.5, full).** Manner is warm and clear: it thanks the chair, affirms the shared desire for reverence and warmth, credits the devotional aim, and states the objections in a short, reason-per-conversion form rather than a general lecture. The offer to step aside is stated respectfully and shows no contempt for the asker.
