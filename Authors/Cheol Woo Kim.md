# Cheol Woo Kim

## Biographical Summary

Cheol Woo Kim is a PhD student in Computer Science and Operations Research at the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS). His research focuses on the intersection of machine learning and economics, specifically how to optimize AI systems for multiple, often competing, social objectives.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Multi-objective RL**: Developing "portfolio" approaches to reinforcement learning that can balance trade-offs between different stakeholder values (e.g., efficiency vs. equity).
- **Social Welfare**: Applying economic theory to AI alignment, using concepts like the "Social Welfare Frontier" to navigate complex decision spaces.
- **Fair Allocation**: Researching algorithms that ensure equitable distribution of resources in automated systems.

### Significant Achievements
- **Social Welfare Frontier**: Lead author of research proposing a novelty framework for navigating the trade-offs in multi-objective value alignment.

## Major Publications
- **Navigating the Social Welfare Frontier: Portfolios for Multi-objective Reinforcement Learning**: A key paper on balancing competing values in AI agents.
