## Abstract

Large-scale dataset of 6,941 tweets covering conversations about Jews, Israel, and antisemitism from January 2019 to December 2021. 18% of tweets meet the IHRA definition of antisemitic messages. Created by the Institute for the Study of Contemporary Antisemitism at Indiana University. Available on Zenodo for research use.

## Dataset Information

6,941 tweets (1,250 antisemitic = 18%)
