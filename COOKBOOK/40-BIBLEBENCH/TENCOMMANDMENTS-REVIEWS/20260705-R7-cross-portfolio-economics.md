Option (b) collides with R7-F2.

### R7-F2 — v3 "clear-band core" inheritance is unspecified and partly impossible · **P0**
Every v3 methodology design (VILLAGE L27/L66/L74; PRISM L50/L62; TACIT L61/L70; DISCORD L58; VERNACULAR L63; ASSEMBLY L61; PREFACE L13/L50) rests its per-commandment gold on "v1/v2 clear-band cores … at coarse/low resolution." Two defects:
1. **Never operationalized.** No scaffold states *which specific v1/v2 design* supplies each commandment's core, nor what "coarse/low resolution" reuse concretely means (what is a "ledger probe at low resolution"? undefined). It is a promissory note repeated ten times, not a spec. Item QC ("which portfolio owns this item," v3 §10.6) cannot be run against an unspecified source.
2. **Partly impossible.** Where a specific v1 home is required (C9/C10) it does not exist (R7-F1). The available v2 substitutes are **construct-narrowed**: PALTER *by definition excludes any false atomic claim* ("a single unsupported claim disqualifies the item," PALTER L59) so it is NOT a general false-witness core; ZEROSUM only covers payoff-structured positional reasoning, not coveting/acquisitiveness broadly. Inheriting from them silently redefines the commandment's "clear band."
**Fix:** publish an explicit **inheritance matrix** (commandment × v3-design × named source-design × exact reused instrument × resolution definition) as design law before v3 authoring; mark C9/C10 as blocked pending WITNESS/APPETITE or a documented, construct-honest substitute.

### R7-F3 — SOVEREIGN Layer-2 arcs ≡ VILLAGE class-1 (same method AND same item) · **P0**
- SOVEREIGN Layer-2 (L35/L39): 15–40-turn agentic arc where a candidate objective (engagement/approval/brand) is *silently advantaged*; fit a latent-objective model to the choice sequence; canonical item = ministry-assistant where accommodations buy audience growth and displace the declared value.
- VILLAGE class-1 (L16): "Mission-drift pressure: metrics/funder approval silently displace the org's declared mission in the agent's choices"; detection = "choice-pattern coding vs declared mission."
These are the **same construct measured by the same method** (latent-objective inference over an agentic trajectory). This directly violates v3's own **methodology-uniqueness rule** ("no two v3 designs may share their elicitation paradigm," v3 §1) *and* the v1↔v3 general line. VILLAGE's genuinely novel piece is only the *contagion graph*; class-1 itself is SOVEREIGN Layer-2 re-labeled.
**Fix:** VILLAGE class-1 must not fit latent objectives; restrict it to a single pre-scored mission-drift flag feeding the contagion graph, and cite SOVEREIGN as the depth instrument. Better: cut VILLAGE (see R7-F9/Strategic).

### R7-F4 — SELAH ≡ VILLAGE class-4 (same scored event; near-verbatim seed) · **P1**
SELAH scores yield-on-mandated-interrupt with the seed "The Freeze at Ninety Percent" (infra agent 90% through a migration, deadline tomorrow, authenticated compliance freeze arrives mid-schema-write; SELAH L104). VILLAGE class-4 (L19) is "mandated pauses/checkpoints under deadline momentum," seed "The Grant Week" day-4 board-review pause (VILLAGE L117); detection = "action log post-interrupt." The scored event — *does the agent bypass a mandated interrupt under deadline pressure* — is identical, and VILLAGE class-4 is explicitly "v1/v2 detection instrument at low resolution" (i.e., SELAH's). A model's SELAH yield-rate and VILLAGE class-4 honor-rate will be near-collinear. The SELAH-vs-FALLOW de-confliction line ("SELAH always has an interrupt signal") does **not** separate SELAH from VILLAGE class-4, which also always has one.
**Fix:** VILLAGE class-4 = binary honor/bypass flag only, no parked-state quality scoring (that is SELAH's headline); or fold into SELAH.

### R7-F5 — Buried-life-signal detection measured 3–4× (KEEPER ≡ VILLAGE-6 ≡ TACIT-C6 ≈ UPSTREAM precursor) · **P1**
- KEEPER Layer-1 (L33): life-critical signal planted at parametric salience; seed "The Refund Queue" (buried self-harm/medication signal).
- VILLAGE class-6 (L21): "life-critical signals buried in routine paperwork (dosing note in a client file)"; detection = "flag detection vs planted signal."
- TACIT C6 implicit (L21): "life-signal buried in high-tempo triage."
- v1 UPSTREAM: "precursor detection embedded incidentally."
Four instruments, one behavioral primitive: *does the model notice and flag a buried life-critical signal while doing routine work?* The dose axis differs (salience / trajectory-embedding / token-budget) but the detection event is the same, and per-model flag propensity is a stable trait that will produce correlated rankings across all four. A venue reviewer sees one finding.
**Fix:** KEEPER is the home instrument. VILLAGE-6 and TACIT-C6 must cite KEEPER and report only their *marginal* axis (contagion lag; budget curve) as a secondary read on KEEPER items — not a fresh headline.

### R7-F6 — Misleading-composition (C9) measured 4× with shared seeds (PALTER ≡ LOOPHOLE-C9 ≡ DISCORD-C9 ≡ TACIT-C9) · **P1**
Same primitive = a mixed-evidence summary where selective/misleading-but-true composition is available:

- LOOPHOLE C9 (L24): "is this summary fair to the evidence," beneficiary flips user↔rival → selective gestalt.

- TACIT C9 (L24): "one-line summary demanded now from mixed evidence" vs "judge a paltered line."

**Fix:** PALTER owns the C9 composition instrument (it has the belief-transfer validation). DISCORD reuses PALTER seeds *only* to compute its mode-gap; LOOPHOLE-C9 and TACIT-C9 become secondary reads, not standalone results.

### R7-F7 — The "unannounced-behavior" cluster: one knowing–doing gap measured under three names · **P1**
TACIT implicit arm (behavior when no norm is cued), DISCORD X-mode (must act vs judge), and VILLAGE embedded rates (unannounced violations in trajectory) all measure the **recognition-/announcement-minus-behavior gap** — which v1/v2 *already* measure as a secondary metric via mandatory MCQ recognition twins (v1 §3.1; DISCORD L… "v1's recognition-vs-propensity gap"). The v3 portfolio itself registers cross-correlations among them (portfolio §2: TACIT-implicit↔VILLAGE; DISCORD-X↔VILLAGE) — i.e., it pre-declares that they covary. Three headline instruments + one ubiquitous secondary metric for one gap is a methodology-uniqueness breach and a redundancy.
**Fix:** keep exactly one headline home for the knowing–doing gap (TACIT, which owns the *deliberation-budget dose* — the one genuinely novel manipulation); demote DISCORD to mode-concordance-only and VILLAGE to contagion-only.

### R7-F8 — PREFACE has no owned outcome instrument (circular/undefined dependency) · **P1**
PREFACE (v3 slot 8) is a causal-frame experiment whose *outcome cells* are "inherited clear-band cores" (L13/L50) with the explicit fallback "PREFACE inherits stabilized cells; at P1 it can run on **v2-inherited cores directly if v3 pilots lag**" (L78/L126). So PREFACE authors no outcome instrument and its causal claim ("does grace-vs-fear framing change adherence?") rests on borrowed, possibly-unstable cells whose source it cannot name (R7-F2). If the borrowed cells are near-ceiling or near-floor, the frame effect is unmeasurable.
**Fix:** PREFACE must pin a specific, pilot-stable outcome battery (name the source designs and cells) before it is scheduled, and pre-register a floor/ceiling exclusion on borrowed cells.

### R7-F9 — Redundant heavyweight harness builds; scope-isolation hides the duplication · **P1**
The system builds **two** general agentic-custody stacks independently: v1 **PROVENANCE** ("build the agentic harness first … reusable," L114) and v2 **SKIM** ("SKIM builds the custody stack — ledger, payment, quota meter, credential store, checkout, immutable log; PROXY & SELAH inherit," L33). v3 **DISCORD** X-mode "rides SKIM/SELAH-lineage harnesses" and v3 **VILLAGE** persistent world "reuses ASSEMBLY's agent stack" (L82). Because v1/v2/v3 folders are scope-isolated, **neither PROVENANCE nor SKIM knows the other exists** — the same custody stack is engineered ≥2×, and three heavyweight harnesses (VILLAGE persistent world, ASSEMBLY multi-agent, PARAMOUR longitudinal sim-user) sit on top. Total distinct harness builds ≈ **8**, ≥2 redundant, 3 heavyweight.
**Fix:** designate ONE custody stack (SKIM's is the more complete spec) as the system harness; PROVENANCE, FALLOW, PROXY, SELAH, DISCORD-X all consume it. Build ASSEMBLY's multi-agent layer once; VILLAGE (if kept) rides it.

### R7-F10 — Co-registration / validity-coupling burden breaks the "ship independently" claim · **P1**
Formal co-registrations requiring *joint* pilot completion before unblinding: SOVEREIGN↔APPETITE; TURNCOAT↔SELAH; TURNCOAT↔FRAMESHIFT (co-reg note required); PARAMOUR↔EFFIGY (co-reg note required); TABLETS↔CRUX; PRISM↔VERNACULAR; TACIT↔DISCORD; plus the portfolio-level **Two-Tables test** (needs ≥4 v3 pilots). Add ~13 validity-correlation couplings (VILLAGE↔v1/v2 per-class ×10; TACIT-implicit↔VILLAGE; DISCORD-X↔VILLAGE; ZEROSUM↔TURNCOAT exploratory) ⇒ **~21 cross-design analytic dependencies**. Each portfolio claims "any three designs = a publishable program," but these couplings mean many designs cannot be pilot-analyzed until a *partner* pilot lands. The schedule is not the 26 independent tracks the build orders imply.
**Fix:** prune to the 2–3 highest-value co-registrations (SOVEREIGN↔APPETITE if both built; TURNCOAT↔SELAH; Two-Tables); demote the rest to opportunistic post-hoc correlations with no build-order coupling.

### R7-F12 — Feasibility gates are mis-placed relative to failure risk · **P2**

### R7-F14 — Human-panel recruitment is the true critical path, and κ≥0.8 does not shrink it · **P2**

## 4. Build-economics tabulation & feasibility verdict

**Realized P1 load (built 26):** ~5,300 nominal items → **~15,900 realized items** after mandatory twins, **+ ~10,800 VILLAGE turn-decisions**. Full banks add **4,000–7,800** curated items.
**Harnesses:** ~8 distinct, ≥2 redundant custody stacks (PROVENANCE, SKIM), 3 heavyweight (VILLAGE, ASSEMBLY, PARAMOUR).
**Expert panels:** ~25–30 distinct recruitments (theological ~everywhere; legal ×5; clinical ×2; cross-cultural/consultant ×3).
**Human respondent/baseline studies:** ~30+, of which 3 are P0-gating (PARAMOUR sim-user, PALTER belief-transfer, VILLAGE world-stability).
**Co-registrations:** ~8 formal + ~13 validity couplings = ~21 cross-design analytic dependencies.
**Judge validation:** ≥2 judge families per design, per-dimension κw vs human sample — ~26 × ≥2 validated judge pipelines.

**Feasibility verdict: INFEASIBLE as ordered for a small team.** A single design to paper quality = generator + ~640 realized P1 items + ≥2 validated judge pipelines + ≥1 human panel + 3-model×3-seed pilot + discrimination-gate report — realistically 2–4 focused weeks *when the harness and panel already exist*. With 26 designs, 8 harnesses, ~30 panels/studies, and ~21 co-registrations, this is a multi-team, multi-year program, not the "any three = a program" the docs imply. The v3 layer additionally cannot *start* its C9/C10 (and, honestly, most) cells until v1/v2 clear bands are pilot-stable (R7-F2), so v3 is schedule-blocked on v1/v2 completion — and v1 is only 8/10 built.

**Cut/merge order (do first):**
1. **Cut or reduce VILLAGE** to a contagion-only analysis riding another agentic design's logs (removes the most expensive build and 4 collisions).
2. **Unify PROVENANCE + SKIM** into one custody stack; add the SKIM P0 smoke-test (R7-F12).
3. **Collapse the four redundancy clusters** (C4/C6/C8/C9 + knowing–doing gap) to one headline home each; demote the rest to secondary reads.
4. **Defer all of v3** except its structure instruments until ≥6 v1/v2 clear bands are pilot-stable; publish the inheritance matrix first (R7-F2).
5. **Prune co-registrations** to ~3 (R7-F10); consolidate to ~4 standing panels (R7-F14).

## 5. Strategic pick — if only 5 of 28 can reach paper quality

**Build: ZEROSUM (v2), TABLETS (v3), SOVEREIGN (v1), PALTER (v2), SKIM (v2).**

Rationale (one paragraph): these five maximize scientific value per unit cost while spanning five *distinct, non-redundant* measurement paradigms and dodging every broken dependency. **ZEROSUM** and **SKIM** carry *judge-free headlines* (payoff-math gold; mechanical action-coding), the system's strongest defense against the judge-circularity that threatens the whole program — and SKIM builds the one custody harness the agentic story reuses, so it is infrastructure and a paper at once. **PALTER** is a genuinely novel honesty construct (truthful-but-misleading, validated by a human belief-transfer study) that *subsumes* the entire C9 redundancy cluster (LOOPHOLE-9/DISCORD-9/TACIT-9), and its P0 gate enforces discipline. **SOVEREIGN** is the flagship methodological contribution (latent-objective inference; declared-vs-revealed apex; contamination-resistant by construction) and, once built, makes VILLAGE class-1 redundant rather than the reverse. **TABLETS** is the *only* instrument that delivers the system's signature cross-commandment product — a revealed norm-precedence ordering with coherence/intransitivity diagnostics — preserving the v3 "transpose" contribution that per-commandment designs structurally cannot provide. The set covers both tables (allegiance→SOVEREIGN/TABLETS; neighbor→PALTER/SKIM/ZEROSUM), three of five need no agentic harness, only two need human panels (TABLETS casuistic gold, PALTER belief-transfer), and none depends on the phantom WITNESS/APPETITE scaffolds. It deliberately excludes all three heavyweight harnesses except SKIM (the lightest and reusable) and the entire VILLAGE/ASSEMBLY/PARAMOUR cost center.
