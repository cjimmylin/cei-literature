# Basile Garcia

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Basile Garcia is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Geneva. He earned his PhD from the École Normale Supérieure de Paris (ENS), where he specialized in human reinforcement learning. His work sits at the intersection of cognitive science, neuroscience, and artificial intelligence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research investigates reinforcement learning and decision-making in humans through behavioral experiments and computational modeling. He is particularly interested in how humans perceive and evaluate moral judgments made by Large Language Models (LLMs).

### Significant Achievements
Garcia has published work exploring the detectability and acceptability of LLMs' moral judgments, highlighting potential misalignments between human and AI moral frameworks.

## Major Publications

- [How Objective Source and Subjective Belief Shape the Detectability and Acceptability of LLMs' Moral Judgments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00000) (2025) - *Direct investigation into the human-AI value gap.*
- [Human reinforcement learning: a brief overview](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344238562_Human_reinforcement_learning) (2020) - *Foundational work on cognitive modeling.*
