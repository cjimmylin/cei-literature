# Muhammad Farid Adilazuarda

## Research Summary

Muhammad Farid Adilazuarda is a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Edoardo Ponti and Alexandra Birch, and a researcher at MBZUAI. Lead author of "Towards Measuring and Modeling 'Culture' in LLMs: A Survey" (EMNLP 2024), covering 90+ papers on cultural representation in LLMs. Contributed to NusaCrowd (Indonesian NLP Resources) and NusaAksara (Indonesian indigenous scripts benchmark). Research focuses on multilingual/multicultural NLP and parameter-efficient learning.
