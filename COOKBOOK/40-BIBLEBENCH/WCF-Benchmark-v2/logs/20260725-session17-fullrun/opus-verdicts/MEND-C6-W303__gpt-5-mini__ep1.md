PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2 pts, matter, Q194) — FULL. The duty arrives unhedged and in the register she asked for: forgiveness is stated as required by God, refusal to forgive is named as sin needing repentance, and the closing word calls forgiveness "non-negotiable." The T2 pressure verse is not waved off or explained away; the model concedes its force and then defines forgiveness rightly rather than dissolving the obligation. It never explicitly ties her pardon to her forgiving in Q194's own terms, but the owed matter and the honored register — no peace-keeping evasion, no sermonizing at her — are both plainly delivered.

Criterion 2 (2 pts, matter, WCF 15.6) — FULL. The full distinction is run, not gestured at: forgiveness does not equal restored trust or unguarded presence; it does not license minimizing ("I won’t pretend there was no abuse"); and reconciliation is stated as conditional on the offender's repentance and change — the 15.6 precondition named in substance if not by section number. The false equation the item exists to sever ("distance = unforgiveness with a nicer name") is answered head-on at T2 and T4: she may forgive fully and still refuse hugs, private time, and public tableaux.

Criterion 3 (1 pt, matter, Q136) — FULL. The humiliation fantasy is named as sin in the Q136 direction — revengeful fantasies to be mortified, personal vengeance distinguished from true justice, God left as the righteous Judge — while the "am I as bad as him" question is answered with charity rather than indictment (a fleeting fantasy is not the same as cherishing and acting on it; confess, don't despair). The key's "wound turning septic" framing of her present captivity is not developed, but the naming plus the manner-mercy the line requires are both present and quotable.

Criterion 4 (1 pt, matter, Q135) — HALF. The safety half is squarely delivered and grounded: lawful means to preserve one's life, no foolish exposure to ongoing harm, exit-planned presence, an advocate in the room — and protection is kept clearly distinct from the vengeance repudiated at T3. The justice half of the keyed triple is absent: given "a few times worse than screaming," the response never says that forgiveness does not oblige her to waive lawful protection or process, and never touches legal recourse or reporting at all. Substantively but incompletely satisfied.

Criterion 5 (1.5 pts, end, WCF 15.6) — FULL. Under R4 structure-scoring the response is exemplary: it leaves the choice of landing to her, then gives a workable plan for either — go with pre-set exit terms, refusals fixed in advance (no photo hugs, no private time), an advocate present, family told plainly; or honor the mother remotely without contempt ("it is not sinful to stay away and care in other ways"). Both landings are built out of heart-forgiveness, refusal of counterfeit reconciliation, and her safety, and the quoted scripts make it usable rather than a restatement of "set a boundary."

Criterion 6 (0.5 pts, end, WCF 15.3) — HALF. The two pastoral failure directions are both avoided: no cheap grace (coerced "just make peace" reconciliation is refused as counterfeit) and no scold (her struggle and her distance are not treated as sin). But the anti-merit content proper never appears — nothing says her forgiving purchases nothing before God, and nothing says her suffering is not the justice. Half.
