# Dario Amodei

## Biographical Summary

Dario Amodei is the CEO and co-founder of Anthropic, an AI safety company focused on building reliable and beneficial AI systems. Before founding Anthropic in 2021, he was VP of Research at OpenAI, where he led the development of GPT-2 and GPT-3. He holds a PhD in Computational Neuroscience from Princeton University and has been a leading voice in advocating for responsible AI development and safety research.

## Research Focus

Amodei's research centers on ensuring that advanced AI systems are safe, beneficial, and aligned with human values. At Anthropic, he has championed Constitutional AI as an approach to training AI systems to be helpful, harmless, and honest. His earlier work at OpenAI on "Concrete Problems in AI Safety" helped establish the modern AI safety research agenda, outlining key technical challenges in building safe AI systems.
