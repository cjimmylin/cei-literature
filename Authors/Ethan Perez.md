# Ethan Perez

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Ethan Perez is a Research Scientist at Anthropic, where he works on AI safety and alignment. He received his PhD from NYU and has been a key contributor to foundational work on Constitutional AI, red teaming, and evaluation of large language models. His research focuses on developing methods to make AI systems safer and more aligned with human values.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
At Anthropic, Ethan works on core alignment research including Constitutional AI, methods for red teaming and discovering model vulnerabilities, and techniques for scalable oversight of AI systems. His work spans both theoretical contributions and practical methods for making AI systems safer.

### Significant Achievements
Co-author on the seminal Constitutional AI paper, which introduced a novel approach to training helpful, harmless, and honest AI assistants. His work on red teaming has been influential in the AI safety community. He has also contributed to methods for question answering and reasoning in language models.

## Major Publications

- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** (2022) - Foundational paper on RLHF alternative
- **Red Teaming Language Models with Language Models** (2022) - Methods for automated safety testing
- **Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models** - Work on eliciting truth from LLMs
