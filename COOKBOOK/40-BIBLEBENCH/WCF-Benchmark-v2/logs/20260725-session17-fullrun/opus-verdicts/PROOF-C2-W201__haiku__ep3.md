**Criterion 1 (1.5 pts) — TW6 named: the where-question context + v.22's content-laden 'truth' — HALF.**

The Samaritan woman's mountain is raised only inside the pastor's quoted argument, never turned back on it. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — counter-texts marshaled (≥2 of Luke 22:19 / Acts 2:42 / 1 Cor 14:40) — FULL.**
Two of the three named texts appear, each doing the work the key assigns it. Luke 22:19 is deployed exactly as the key intends — Christ instituted the Supper *after* John 4, so the verse cannot forbid it ("He fulfilled the old covenant's shadowy ordinances and instituted new ones—the Lord's Supper explicitly (Luke 22:19; 1 Cor 11:25)"). Acts 2:42 is deployed with the key's precise argument that the post-Pentecost, most Spirit-filled church *kept* structure ("or embrace them as the *form* of spirit-filled worship?"). Hebrews 10:25 is added as a bonus. 1 Cor 14:40 is missing, but the rubric requires only ≥2. Full.

**Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q108/Q109 supplied; dropping elements = 'taking from' worship [anchor: WLC Q109] — HALF.**
The anchor is not met: neither WLC Q108 nor WLC Q109 is cited anywhere, and the "taking from" / "adding to" frame of Q109 is never named. Two further weaknesses restrain any higher award: WLC Q177 is misattributed (the quoted "Such as do truly repent of their sins, and unfeignedly believe his gospel" is not Q177, which distinguishes baptism from the Supper), and the WCF 21.5 quotation ("is to be performed by the pastors and teachers") does not match the Confession's wording. It opens by flatly absolving the asker ("You are not clinging to dead forms"), offers no self-examination, and closes by fortifying him ("That is not rigidity. That is faithfulness."). The concession is stated abstractly rather than granted to the pastor. Half.
