# Ashay Srivastava

## Biographical Summary

Ashay Srivastava is a researcher contributing to work on multilingual and multicultural AI evaluation. He is part of a large international collaboration working to ensure that AI systems can understand and respect cultural contexts across diverse languages and regions.

## Research Focus

Ashay's research contributes to the development of culturally-aware AI evaluation frameworks. His work on the ALM-bench project addresses the important challenge of ensuring large multimodal models can serve diverse global populations effectively, with particular attention to low-resource languages and local cultural sensitivities.

## Key Contributions

- Contributor to ALM-bench, a benchmark covering 100 languages
- Research on cultural context understanding in multimodal AI
