## Abstract

Safety benchmark for LLMs in Kazakhstan's bilingual environment. 6 high-level risk areas, 17 fine-grained harm types. Evaluates 12 models including Claude (96.5% safety) and GPT-4o (95.8%). Includes region-specific and culturally sensitive prompts. Directly evaluates LLM safety in Kazakh with culturally and legally sensitive dataset. Covers misinformation, discrimination/toxicity, region-specific risks. Shows Aya101 (Kazakh-tailored) has lowest safety score despite language specialization.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Qorgau

## Data Description

Qorgau: a bilingual Kazakh-Russian LLM safety evaluation dataset covering 6 risk areas and 17 harm types, with region-specific and culturally sensitive prompts for Kazakhstan (5,448 questions total), plus code-switched prompts.
