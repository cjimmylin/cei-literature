# Yuxi Sun

## Research Summary

Yuxi Sun is a researcher at Hong Kong Baptist University focusing on explainable moral judgment in Large Language Models. He developed ClarityEthic, a novel moral judgment approach that leverages LLMs' reasoning ability and contrastive learning to make explainable moral judgments. His work, conducted in collaboration with researchers from Singapore Management University and Alibaba DAMO Academy, addresses the safety and ethical behavior of LLMs through enhanced reasoning-based moral evaluation frameworks.
