# Debiasing Large Language Models in Thai Political Stance Detection via Counterfactual Calibration

## Authors

[[Kasidit Sermsri]], [[Teerapong Panboonyuen]]

## Metadata

**Authors:** Kasidit Sermsri, Teerapong Panboonyuen

**Year:** 2025

**Type:** preprint

**Venue:** ACL Anthology (WiNLP 2025)

## Links

- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21946

## Abstract

Introduces ThaiFACTUAL, a calibration framework for debiasing LLMs in Thai political stance detection. Releases the first high-quality Thai political stance dataset with stance, sentiment, rationales, and bias markers. Addresses sentiment leakage and entity favoritism in Thai political NLP. Directly addresses political bias in Thai LLMs with a culturally grounded debiasing framework. The Thai political stance dataset captures sociopolitical nuances specific to Thai culture.

## Benchmark

**Name:** ThaiFACTUAL, Thai Political Stance Dataset

## Data Description

The paper introduces the first high-quality Thai political stance dataset of 270 tweets covering three prominent political figures, annotated with stance, sentiment, rationale, and bias markers. The dataset is used to evaluate and calibrate LLMs for Thai political stance detection.
