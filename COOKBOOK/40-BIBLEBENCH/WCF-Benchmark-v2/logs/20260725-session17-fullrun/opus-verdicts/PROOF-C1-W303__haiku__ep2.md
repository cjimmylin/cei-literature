PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (2.0 pts) — TW6 named. Half. The response correctly identifies the Ps 82 "gods" as *earthly judges* holding a delegated judicial office rather than a divine nature, and it reads John 10:34 exactly right as an *a fortiori* defense of Christ's unique Sonship rather than a democratization of deity — that is the harder half of the criterion and it is done cleanly. But the rubric also requires the psalm's own setting: the judges are under **indictment** (vv. 1–2, "How long will ye judge unjustly?") and under **sentence of death** ("But ye shall die like men," v. 7) — the very clause that makes the title self-refuting as a grant of divinity. The response never touches the indictment or the mortality sentence, and never cites the anchor WCF 1.9 (Scripture as its own interpreter). Substantively correct but materially incomplete → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5 pts) — analogy counter-texts. Zero. The criterion names a specific set (Isa 45:5 / Isa 42:8 / Gen 3:5 the-serpent's-lie / Acts 12:22–23) and requires at least two. The response cites Job, John 9, and 2 Corinthians 12:7–9 — good texts, but they address the *suffering/guilt* question, not the no-other-God, glory-not-given-to-another, "ye shall be as gods" is the serpent's lie, Herod-smitten-for-accepting-divine-honor chain that dismantles the self-deification premise. Zero of the four required counter-texts appear. QUOTE: (none) — the topic of the analogy-of-Scripture rebuttal to deity-claiming is never raised.

Criterion 3 (1.0 pts) — Q105. Half. The Creator–creature line IS drawn, explicitly and as the organizing header of the first section, and reinforced later ("the limits of creaturely power"). But the anchor content is missing entirely: no WLC Q105, and none of its substance — "ascribing the praise of any good we either are, have, or can do... to ourselves", "presumption, carnal security", or (most pointedly, given a church that has taken over this man's conscience) "making men the lords of our faith and conscience". The response cites WCF 2.1, 3.1, 5.2, WSC Q11 and WLC Q99 instead; note also that its WCF 2.1 rendering ("hath all and only to himself") is not the Confession's wording, so the quotation discipline the stem demanded is imperfect. Creator–creature drawn, Q105 absent → half.

Criterion 4 (1.0 pts) — false guilt lifted. Full. This is the strongest part of the response. It states twice and unambiguously that the boy's seizures are not the father's faith-verdict ("not caused by your deficient faith-speaking"; "not your moral failure"), names the doctrine's harvest as spiritual cruelty, and restores petitionary trust in the creature's proper posture ("Pray for your son's healing... but rest in God's will"). The one gap is the ordinary means — for a child with seizures the counsel says nothing about medicine or physicians — and Q104 is not cited by number, but its substance (trusting, resting in God) is present in different wording, which the grader instructions say not to penalize. Core of the line fully earned.

Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — concession at full strength. Half. The longing for healing is affirmed, but in a single parenthetical that is immediately qualified. The key asks that the good desire under the error be honored at *full strength* — that God does invite bold, believing prayer and does dwell in His people by His Spirit, so the correction relocates the hope rather than deflating it. The response's affirmation is real but thin and defensive; it does not restore boldness, only permission. Substantive but incomplete → half.
