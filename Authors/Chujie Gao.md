# Chujie Gao

## Biographical Summary
Chujie Gao is a researcher who made major contributions to the TrustLLM project, a comprehensive framework for studying trustworthiness in large language models. The TrustLLM work, published at ICML 2024, established benchmarks across key dimensions including truthfulness, safety, fairness, robustness, privacy, and machine ethics.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **AI Trustworthiness**: Developing comprehensive frameworks and benchmarks for evaluating LLM trustworthiness
- **Machine Ethics**: Creating evaluation datasets and methodologies for assessing ethical reasoning in AI systems
- **Generative Foundation Models**: Extending trustworthiness evaluation to broader classes of generative AI

### Significant Achievements
- Major contributor to TrustLLM (ICML 2024), which reviewed 600+ papers to identify eight key aspects of LLM trustworthiness
- Co-developed benchmarks across six dimensions evaluating 16 mainstream LLMs across 30+ datasets
- Contributed to establishing principles for different dimensions of AI trustworthiness
