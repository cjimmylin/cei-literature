# Sualeha Farid

## Research Summary

Sualeha Farid is a Graduate Student and Researcher at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor, where she focuses on Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Human-Centered Computing. She completed her BS in Computer Science from LUMS (Lahore University of Management Sciences) in 2023. Her research includes work on multilingual NLP, particularly Urdu question answering systems and cross-linguistic moral reasoning in large language models.
