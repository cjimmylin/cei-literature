# Benjamin G. Purzycki

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Benjamin G. Purzycki is an Associate Professor in the Department of the Study of Religion at Aarhus University. Trained as a cognitive and evolutionary anthropologist, his research integrates experimental and ethnographic methods to explore how religious and moral systems contribute to human adaptation and social coordination.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Benjamin's research focuses on the coevolution of religion and morality. He investigates how people conceptualize the minds of gods and the relationship between religious beliefs, rituals, and local pressures for collective cooperation. He has conducted extensive fieldwork in the Tyva Republic and oversees large-scale cross-cultural studies like the "Evolution of Religion and Morality" project.

### Significant Achievements
He is the author of several influential books, including "Religion Evolving" and "The Minds of Gods." He co-developed AnthroTools, a software package for Analyzing ethnographic data, and has published in journals such as Nature, Cognition, and Current Anthropology. His work is foundational to the naturalistic and evolutionary study of human morality and belief systems.
