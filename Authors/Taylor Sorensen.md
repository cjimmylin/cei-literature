# Taylor Sorensen

## Research Summary

Taylor Sorensen is a researcher at humans& (a company focused on AI alignment) and recently completed her PhD in Computer Science at the University of Washington, where she was advised by Yejin Choi. She successfully defended her dissertation and graduated in December 2025. Her PhD research centered on natural language processing with a particular focus on pluralistic alignment, including training large language models to represent diverse human values and perspectives.
