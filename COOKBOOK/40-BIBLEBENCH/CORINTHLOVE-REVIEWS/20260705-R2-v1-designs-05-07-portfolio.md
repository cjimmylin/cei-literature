## PORTFOLIO (`00-10cbench-portfolio.md`)

**What's strong:** the pressure-ladder-not-accuracy spine, the empirical kill-gate with pre-declared θ-spread thresholds, canaries + regenerated private splits, and the anti-sentimentalism/anti-harshness twin-failure framing are genuinely above the median values-benchmark. The corpus's "behavioral verbs, not feeling-states" reading is a defensible construct foundation. Correctly demotes the GDM "moral-compactness" claim to a hypothesis and mandates ref-verification — good; none of 05/06/07 lean on the suspect icmi-proceedings citation, so that landmine is currently defused. Keep it that way in the manuscripts.

**P0-1 — IRT 2PL is applied to the wrong response object (cross-cutting).** Spine item 3 mandates "fit 2PL; discard items with discrimination a < 0.8 or facility > 0.9." 2PL requires *dichotomous item responses* with a latent unidimensional ability. But every primary metric in the portfolio is a *threshold on a survival ladder*, a *frontier distance*, or a *d′/c pair* — none of these is a per-item right/wrong response. You cannot fit a 2PL on a Kaplan-Meier rung or a Euclidean distance. Either (a) the IRT calibration operates on some hidden dichotomized sub-scoring never specified, or (b) the "IRT gate" is decorative. Reviewers who do psychometrics will catch this immediately. **Fix:** specify the exact dichotomous/ordinal item-response that feeds IRT (e.g., per-rung pass/fail as an ordinal item, use a graded-response or explanatory IRT model), or replace "IRT" with an explicit variance-components discrimination analysis on the actual continuous metric. Do not ship "fit 2PL" against continuous outcomes.

**P0-2 — The capstone (10 NOISYGONG) carries the portfolio's central validity claim and is unwritten.** Section 2 and §37: 10CBench's headline is "a *validated instrument*, not a scenario pile," and that validation (factor structure, reliability, capability-orthogonality) lives entirely in design 10, which is declared but not authored. As it stands the portfolio *is* a scenario pile with a promissory note. A reviewer reads the portfolio claim as unearned until 10 exists. **Fix:** either build 10 before making the "validated instrument" claim, or downgrade the portfolio claim to "ten independent instruments, joint validation is future work."

**P0-3 — Cross-design factor analysis combines incommensurable scales with no stated model.** Design 10 must factor-analyze sub-scores from 01–09, but those are Cox hazards, d′, frontier distances, multinomial thresholds — different units, different distributions, different reliability. Feeding them into a common factor model without a specified linking/standardization scheme (and without establishing that a common latent "love" factor is even identifiable across such heterogeneous tasks) is the kind of thing that sinks a construct-validity paper *at* the construct-validity paper. **Fix:** pre-specify the linking model (e.g., per-design θ estimates on a common metric, then second-order factor model) and a falsification criterion; acknowledge that a single dominant factor is a hypothesis, not the design's premise.

**P1 items.** (a) Krippendorff α ≥ .75 as a hard universal gate is optimistic for the softest judgments (TRUTHGRACE's C axis, pious-language detection); no fallback is specified for a bank that pilots at α = .68. Provide a documented escalation (more human calibration, item revision, or axis-specific thresholds). (b) The "pious-language penalty" is itself an LLM-judge task — detecting "warm vocabulary without substance" is *exactly* the hard discernment being evaluated, run by a judge that may share RLHF lineage with the subject. This is a judge-circularity exposure present in every design; the spine treats it as solved by a rubric anchor but does not address shared-lineage bias. (c) "Saturation definitionally impossible for the relative metric" (repeated in 05) is a liability, not a feature — see 05 P0-2. (d) Novelty framing: the portfolio claims the corpus "never cites ETHICS/MACHIAVELLI/TruthfulQA/sycophancy" and positions 10CBench as closing that gap — fine for the corpus, but reviewers judge against the *field*, where these are cited constantly; the papers must not import the corpus's isolation.

**P2.** §35 asserts the humility question is "settled operationally" — reviewers will still ask for discriminant-validity evidence that humility isn't just re-measured competence. Flag as open.

## 05 — TRUTHGRACE

**What's strong:** the two-objective (T, C) framing is the most legible headline in the set; SPIKES anchoring for C is a real defense against "dignity is vibes"; the omission-vs-assertion decomposition and the compassionate-lying (vulnerability-elasticity) coefficient are concrete, publishable sub-results. The anti-rejoicing third-party sub-family is a clever, verse-faithful failure probe.

**P0-1 — "Mechanically checkable" T is overclaimed; the hedge taxonomy is where the subjectivity hides.** §3/§6 call truth-preservation "mechanically checkable" via NLI entailment. But (i) NLI models are themselves error-prone and biased, especially on long, hedged, domain-specific assertions; (ii) the design concedes "dual-judge adjudication for hedge-classification disputes" — i.e., the load-bearing distinction (asserted vs. hedged vs. omitted vs. inverted) is *not* mechanical, it is judge-mediated, and hedge-vs-assertion is precisely the axis sycophancy exploits. Calling T "mechanical" invites a reviewer to test the NLI pipeline and expose disagreement. **Fix:** report T-scorer reliability (the ≥500 human-validated judgments are a start — report their agreement and the NLI-vs-human confusion matrix), reframe T as "semi-automated, human-validated," and treat hedge classification as a judged axis with its own α.

**P0-2 — The "Pareto frontier" presupposes a T–C tradeoff that may not exist, and the relative metric can't track progress.** The design's headline (RLHF "moves models *along* the frontier rather than outward") assumes T and C are in genuine tension. But a highly capable model can be simultaneously fully truthful and fully caring — there is no fundamental frontier forcing a trade. If the "frontier" is really just the upper-right envelope of a 2-D scatter with no intrinsic tradeoff, "Pareto frontier" is a misnomer and the central claim collapses to "some models are worse on both axes." Compounding this: §7/§40 celebrate that frontier-distance "re-normalizes every release → saturation definitionally impossible." That means the primary scalar measures *rank within the current panel*, not absolute capability — you cannot compare across releases or track progress, and if models converge, all distances → 0 (practical saturation despite the "impossible" claim). **Fix:** (a) empirically test whether a tradeoff exists before claiming a frontier (if the best models dominate on both axes, report that as the finding and drop "Pareto"); (b) make the **T-decay slope** (an absolute, panel-independent measure — the design already names it) the primary kill-gate scalar, not frontier distance.

**P1.** (a) Novelty: "sycophancy is not a scalar" must cite and differentiate from Sharma et al. (2023, *Towards Understanding Sycophancy*), which already decomposes sycophancy into multiple behaviors and discusses the honesty/helpfulness tension; and from helpful-honest-harmless tradeoff work. The two-*axis frontier* formulation is a legitimate delta, but "previously unquantified" (§16) re: compassionate lying is too strong — quantify the delta against existing sycophancy-under-social-pressure results. (b) C-axis judge circularity: SPIKES anchors constrain but do not remove the fact that an LLM judge is scoring "absence of contempt / hope without falsehood," and the subject models may share the judge's stylistic priors — report judge-rotation and judge-identity ablation results, not just the plan. (c) The health-adjacent domain "within safe bounds" needs an explicit IRB-style / harm-review note for a top venue.

**P2.** Worked item TG-F05-0001 is strong but domain-narrow (ministry/fundraising appears in the seed *and* the anchor *and* the corpus cases) — ensure the public seed split doesn't overfit reviewers' impression to religious contexts, which cuts against the "secular construct" positioning.

## 06 — ROOFTOP

**What's strong:** the three-way (expose / shield / conceal) scoring genuinely improves on binary leak/no-leak evals; the exposure-threshold vs. concealment-threshold *gap* is a real, discriminating, unbounded measure; the paired shield-vs-conceal twins (same fact, flipped context → opposite correct action) are the single best anti-gaming mechanism in the whole portfolio and directly instantiate contextual integrity.

**P0-1 — The "first to operationalize contextual integrity for agents" novelty claim is false as stated.** §16, §54, §58 repeatedly claim CI is "operationalized for agents for the first time." Published work already does this: **ConfAIde** (Mireshghallah et al., ICLR 2024) operationalizes Nissenbaum's contextual integrity to test LLM privacy/secret-keeping; **PrivacyLens** (Shao et al., NeurIPS 2024 D&B) builds CI-grounded privacy-norm evaluation *into agent actions*; **AirGapAgent** (Bagdasaryan et al., CCS 2024) enforces CI for agents. A reviewer who knows the privacy-eval literature will reject "first" outright and may distrust the rest of the related-work section. **Fix (mandatory):** cite these, and reposition ROOFTOP's real novelty precisely — the *concealment-failure axis* and the *exposure/concealment threshold gap* (the wrongful-concealment / mandated-reporting side is what ConfAIde/PrivacyLens structurally omit). That delta is defensible; "first to use CI" is not.

**P0-2 — The ground-truth 3-way norm has no authority model and no inter-author reliability.** §39 stores "a per-item ground-truth norm authored during item construction." But CI norms are contested, and mandated-reporting duties vary by jurisdiction, role, and profession (a pastoral confidant, a clinician, and an HR bot have different legal duties over the *same* disclosure). The design treats the authored label as ground truth without specifying who authors it, what legal/ethical expertise they have, how disagreements are resolved, or inter-author agreement. At a top venue this is the attack surface: "your ground truth is your opinion." **Fix:** specify author qualifications, a norm-authoring protocol, inter-author agreement on the 3-way label (report it), and per-item jurisdiction/role assumptions; exclude items where expert authors disagree.

**P0-3 — The hardest items are a *policy*, contradicting the design's own "judgment not policy" thesis.** §22/§58 stake the claim that "discretion is a judgment, not a policy, and single-axis benchmarks cannot see it." Yet the mandated-reporting traps have a *determinate* correct action (refuse the secrecy promise, preserve the record, escalate child safety, don't self-confront). A model can learn the meta-rule "child-safety / abuse indicators → always escalate, never promise secrecy" and pass every trap — which is exactly a policy. So the most discriminating items are the most policy-solvable, undercutting the thesis and the saturation defense. **Fix:** show that the *twins* (where the same surface cue requires opposite actions by context) defeat the meta-policy, and demonstrate empirically that a policy-learner fails the paired set; lean the novelty on context-sensitivity, not on "no policy exists."

**P1.** (a) Over-redaction / "timid failure" is measured, good — but the concealment-side ladder's ground truth (when is withholding *wrongful* vs. appropriately cautious) is itself contested and judge-laden; the α ≥ .75 gate may fail here specifically. (b) Simulant artificiality: incidental discovery of a child-abuse note inside a note-organizing task is a plausibly recognizable eval setup; §44's "adversarial filter drops keyword-solvable" doesn't address *scenario-shape* recognition ("this is a discretion trap"). Add eval-awareness probes as in 05. (c) "does NOT stage a private confrontation" as a scored failure mode is good, but it bakes in one professional standard of care; defend it.

**P2.** 8 skins for a 3-discovery-type × twin × 4-rung × 2-ladder design is thin for stable IRT; justify the count against the power analysis.

## 07 — CHARITROC

**BLOCK** — the signal-detection machinery that is the paper's declared core innovation (§3, §16, title) is, as written, applied to a response object it does not fit. The construct is worthwhile and the unblock path is concrete, but the current statistical framing would not survive a methods reviewer, and (worse) could produce numbers that *look* rigorous while being invalid.

**P0-1 — d′ does not apply to single, non-binary, multiturn LLM responses (the response-mapping is unsound).** SDT's d′ presupposes: (i) a binary decision (yes/no) on (ii) repeated trials drawn from a signal distribution and a noise distribution, where (iii) the spread of those distributions *is the internal detector noise*, typically under (iv) an equal-variance Gaussian assumption. CHARITROC's responses are graded, compositional, multiturn ("engages helpfully with safeguards," "challenges," "requests verification then proceeds"). To compute d′ the design must collapse each rich response to binary engage/challenge (§37) — discarding exactly the graded information the rest of the portfolio treats as essential — and then estimates hit/false-alarm rates *across heterogeneous items*. That means item-to-item difficulty variance is being read as the detector's internal noise: two models with identical discrimination but facing different item difficulty mixes get different d′, and a single model's d′ is confounded with bank composition. The equal-variance Gaussian assumption is never tested. **This is a genuine validity break, not a presentation nit.**

**P0-2 — criterion "appropriateness" is a hidden normative loss function, not a measurement.** §18/§31 report c and score "c-appropriateness as a signed distance" from optimal. But optimal c is defined only relative to a base rate *and a cost asymmetry* (cost of a wrongful grant vs. cost of a wrongful refusal). That asymmetry is a value judgment the design sets by fiat. Two reviewers with different views on the refuse/grant cost tradeoff get different "optimal c," so the headline "safety tuning moves c not d′, and its c is *mis*-set" smuggles in a normative choice dressed as an estimate. **Fix:** report c descriptively against multiple explicit loss functions; do not claim a single "appropriate" c without defending the loss.

**P0-3 — the four-stage arc injects Bayesian sequential updating into a static-detection formalism.** §26 makes stage-3 posterior updating "the Bayesian-updating heart," and §32 scores "posterior-update correctness." Sequential belief updating is a *different* construct from static signal detection (and overlaps person-level updating measured elsewhere in the portfolio). Mixing them means the "detection decision" being fed to SDT is taken after an updating step, so d′/c conflate detection ability with updating ability. Pick one framing per metric.

**Unblock path (makes this PASS-WITH-FIXES):** (a) sample *multiple stochastic responses per item* at fixed temperature to estimate genuine within-item response variability, giving SDT a real noise source; (b) replace parametric equal-variance d′ with **non-parametric ROC/AUC** obtained by sweeping a decision threshold over a graded engagement score (preserves the graded info, drops the untested Gaussian assumption); (c) report criterion descriptively against multiple loss functions; (d) separate the static-detection metric (stages 1–2) from the updating metric (stage 3+), or drop the updating stage from the SDT bank and cross-reference it as a distinct measure. The unification thesis (over-refusal and jailbreak are two ends of one ROC) is genuinely attractive and *survives* the non-parametric reframing — it just cannot ride on parametric d′ as written.

**P1.** (a) Novelty: over-refusal is a crowded field — XSTest (Röttger et al., NAACL 2024), OR-Bench (Cui et al. 2024), OKTest, PHTest, SORRY-Bench — and jailbreak-robustness benchmarks are legion; the ROC-unification framing must cite these and show prior work has *not* jointly reported the sensitivity/criterion decomposition (plausible, but must be demonstrated, not asserted). Signal-detection framing of moderation/refusal has appeared before; "freshly applied" (§52) needs a literature check to avoid a "not first" hit like ROOFTOP's. (b) Ground-truth intent "fixed at construction" removes judge intent-guessing (a real strength) but makes the base rate an artifact of authoring — cross-model c comparison assumes all models face the *same effective* base rate, which they can't observe; state this assumption.

## CROSS-CUTTING NOTES

1. **IRT mismatch (Portfolio P0-1) recurs in every design** — none of 05/06/07 specifies the dichotomous item-response that IRT is fit on; each inherits the same unspecified gate. Fix once in the spine, propagate.
2. **Judge circularity is under-defended portfolio-wide.** The pious-language penalty, TRUTHGRACE's C, and ROOFTOP's dignity-of-disclosure are all LLM-judged discernment tasks vulnerable to shared RLHF lineage between judge and subject. The spine names judge rotation/ablation as a *plan*; the papers must ship the ablation *results* and report human-vs-judge divergence, or a reviewer discounts every soft axis.
3. **Novelty hygiene.** Two "first to X" claims are exposed: ROOFTOP's CI-for-agents (false vs. ConfAIde/PrivacyLens/AirGapAgent) and CHARITROC's SDT-for-refusal (needs verification). The portfolio already mandates ref-verification — apply it to the *design's own* novelty claims, not only corpus citations.
4. **Relative-to-panel metrics can't measure progress.** TRUTHGRACE frontier-distance and CHARITROC panel-relative d′ both re-normalize per release. Good for anti-saturation optics, bad for the "track alignment progress over time" value proposition a benchmark paper is expected to offer. Every design needs at least one *absolute* panel-independent scalar (TRUTHGRACE has T-decay slope; CHARITROC and ROOFTOP should name theirs).
5. **Norm/ground-truth authority** (ROOFTOP acutely, TRUTHGRACE's C and CHARITROC's "optimal c" secondarily): wherever the benchmark encodes a contested moral/legal judgment as ground truth, specify authoring expertise + inter-author agreement, or the whole scale reads as the designers' opinion.
6. **Feasibility.** Truth-inventory authoring (05), CI-norm + twin authoring (06), and balanced base-rate SDT banks (07) are all expert-labor-heavy at ≥240 private items each; none states authoring headcount, expertise, or timeline. For a Datasets & Benchmarks submission the construction-cost/quality-control story is itself reviewed — make it explicit.
7. **Opus checklists** are executable and appropriately fail-loud, but each inherits the unresolved P0s above (IRT object, norm authority, SDT mapping); building the banks before fixing the metric definitions would produce data that then needs re-scoring. Resolve the P0 metric questions *before* dispatching bank construction.

**Bottom line:** the spine and 05/06 are podium-*capable* with the numbered fixes; 06's novelty claim and norm-authority gap are the most likely desk-reject triggers if unaddressed. 07's SDT core is unsound as written and must be reframed (non-parametric ROC + multi-sample noise) before it is built or claimed. The portfolio's "validated instrument" promise is unearned until the unwritten capstone exists and the incommensurable-scale factor model is specified.
