# Anaum Khan

## Research Summary

Anaum Khan is a researcher affiliated with the University of Maryland, working on AI security and adversarial robustness of large language models. She contributed to HackAPrompt, a landmark study on prompt hacking that organized a global competition eliciting over 600K adversarial prompts against state-of-the-art LLMs. The work established a comprehensive ontology of adversarial prompt types and empirically demonstrated the vulnerability of current LLMs to prompt injection and jailbreaking attacks.
