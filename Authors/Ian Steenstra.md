# Ian Steenstra

## Research Summary

Ian Steenstra is a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at Northeastern University, advised by Dr. Timothy Bickmore. His dissertation focuses on building "A Simulation Framework for Assessing Quality of Care & Risk in AI Psychotherapy." He has developed and evaluated virtual agents powered by LLMs for health communication, counseling, social skills training, and education, with a focus on substance use, vaccination, emotional grounding, multi-party interactions, patient simulations, and engaging adolescent populations. His research on AI-powered psychotherapy agents addresses the proliferation of LLMs acting as psychotherapists, which presents significant opportunities for expanding mental healthcare access but has also been linked to serious adverse outcomes including user harm.

## Key Contributions

- Developed a novel risk taxonomy specifically designed for the systematic evaluation of conversational AI psychotherapists
- Won Best Paper Award at CHI 2025 for "Scaffolding Empathy: Training Counselors with Simulated Patients"
- Won Best Paper Award at IVA 2024 for "Virtual agents for alcohol use counseling: Exploring LLM-powered motivational interviewing"
