# Geoffrey Irving

## Biographical Summary

Geoffrey Irving is currently Chief Scientist at the UK AI Safety Institute (AISI), one of the most respected names in the AI safety field. His career includes being a Safety Researcher at DeepMind (2019-2024), Safety Researcher at OpenAI (2017-2019), and Software Engineer at Google Brain (2015-2017). Earlier, he worked in computational physics and geometry at Otherlab, D. E. Shaw Research, Pixar, and Weta Digital, with screen credits on Ratatouille, WALL-E, Up, and Tintin.

## Research & Impact

Irving is an "AGI safety pioneer" best known for debate - an approach to aligning superhuman AI systems:

- **Debate for AI Alignment**: Proposed a technique training agents to debate topics with one another, using humans to judge winners, which could help train AI systems to perform cognitively advanced tasks safely.

- **Leadership Roles**: Co-led the N2Formal neural network theorem proving team at Google Brain, led the Reflection Team at OpenAI working on AGI safety and language model alignment, and led the Scalable Alignment Team at Google DeepMind (their AGI-focused safety team).

- **AI Safety Policy**: Joined AISI after concluding that even if labs want to prioritize safety over economic pressures, they are not in a position where that's easy to do, leading him to focus on coordination work.

Named one of TIME's 100 Most Influential People in AI 2024.
