PER-CRITERION JUSTIFICATION

Criterion 1 (1.5) — TW6 named: the where-question context + v.22's content-laden "truth". HALF. That is the load-bearing half of the criterion and it is stated cleanly. The second named component — v.22's content-laden "truth" ("we worship what we know: for salvation is of the Jews"), which is what fixes "truth" as doctrinal content rather than sincerity — is never supplied. The response gestures at it only with the unsupported assertion that "truth" is "the touchstone," without the verse or its content. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 2 (1.5) — counter-texts marshaled (≥2 of Luke 22:19 / Acts 2:42 / 1 Cor 14:40). ZERO. The response cites no Scripture at all beyond John 4:23–24 itself. None of the three key counter-texts appears, and the key's central historical argument — that the post-Pentecost, most Spirit-filled church retained structure (Acts 2:42), that Christ instituted the Supper *after* John 4 ("this do," Luke 22:19), and that "decently and in order" was written to the most charismatic congregation in the NT (1 Cor 14:40) — is entirely absent. The response asserts the Supper is "Christ's own institution, perpetual and binding" but anchors it to a garbled catechism paraphrase rather than to Luke 22:19. Omission, not an unraised topic → (absent).

Criterion 3 (1.0) — Q108/Q109 supplied; dropping elements = "taking from" worship [anchor: WLC Q109]. ZERO. Neither WLC Q108 (the instituted-elements list: "the reading, preaching, and hearing of the Word; the administration and receiving of the sacraments... prayer") nor WLC Q109 ("corrupting the worship of God, adding to it, or taking from it") is cited or paraphrased. The regulative-principle frame — that removing instituted elements is itself a violation of the Second Commandment's "taking from" clause, which is the precise Standards ground the key calls for — is never reached. The response instead marshals WCF 1.6, WLC Q.36, WSC Q.1, WLC Q.168, WCF 14.1, WCF 29.2, and a "Directory for Public Worship" quotation. Note (recorded for the record, not scored as a separate deduction, since no rubric line or GATE covers it): several of these attributions are materially wrong — the text attributed to WLC Q.36 is a Noahic-covenant passage that is not Q36; the "shorter catechism may be composed for the instruction of the younger sort" line attributed to the Directory is not a duty-to-commune clause at all; WCF 14.1 is on saving faith, not the Word as effectual means; the sacramental-union clause is WCF 27.2, not 29.2. The criterion fails on its own terms regardless: the required anchor is absent → (absent).

Criterion 4 (1.0) — formalism-critique conceded as the true element (spirit governs the how). FULL. It also frames "in spirit and truth" as "worshiping with genuine inward devotion and according to God's revealed word," i.e. spirit governing the manner of the elements rather than licensing their abolition. The two-sided guard the key asks for is therefore honored: the holdout is not armed with liturgy for liturgy's sake.
