# Adam Elwood

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Adam Elwood is a physicist turned AI researcher with a deep focus on the intersection of reality models, consciousness, and artificial intelligence. He collaborates closely with Ruben Laukkonen on the "Contemplative AI" initiative, which seeks to integrate insights from wisdom traditions into the technical alignment problem.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Adam's research explores "Contemplative Superalignment"—the theory that AI systems can be aligned by embedding intrinsic moral architectures inspired by meditative states and contemplative philosophy. He investigates how these frameworks can provide more robust safety guarantees than extrinsic value-learning alone.
