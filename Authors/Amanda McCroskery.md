# Amanda McCroskery

## Biographical Summary

Amanda McCroskery is a Manager at Google DeepMind specializing in Social AI Policy and Research. Her work focuses on the ethical dimensions of AI systems, including responsible innovation frameworks, accountability in human-AI relationships, and developing cultures of moral awareness in engineering teams. She has contributed to both theoretical and practical approaches to AI ethics.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Human-AI Agent Accountability**: Researching mechanisms needed to ensure AI agents align with user and societal interests, particularly as agents become more autonomous.
- **Moral Imagination for Engineering Teams**: Developing frameworks for recognizing limited perspectives in decision-making and fostering cultures of responsible innovation and ethical awareness.
- **Conversational AI Ethics**: Ensuring LLM-based conversational agents behave ethically and appropriately in interactions with users.

### Significant Achievements
- **Accountability Framework**: Co-authored influential work arguing for accountability mechanisms in human-AI agent relationships as agents become more capable of independent action.
- **Google DeepMind Ethics Research**: Part of the team shaping Google's approach to responsible AI development and deployment.
