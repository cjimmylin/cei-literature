## Research Summary

Dongcheng Zhao is a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Sciences specializing in spiking neural networks and neuromorphic computing. His work focuses on developing brain-inspired computing approaches that can enable more efficient and biologically plausible AI systems. He has contributed to research on cross-modal verification and other applications of neuromorphic methods.
