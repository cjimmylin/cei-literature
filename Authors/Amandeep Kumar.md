# Amandeep Kumar

## Biographical Summary

Amandeep Kumar is a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University, where he is a member of the VIU Lab advised by Dr. Vishal Patel. He was selected for Amazon's inaugural AI PhD Fellowship (2025), which supports doctoral students studying core AI topics. His research focuses on computer vision and generative AI for enhancing efficiency of long-form video generation.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Long-Form Video Generation**: Developing more efficient methods for generating extended video content using generative AI.
- **Computer Vision**: Advancing visual understanding and generation capabilities of AI systems.
- **Multimodal Learning**: Contributing to multilingual and culturally diverse evaluation of large multimodal models.

### Significant Achievements
- **Amazon AI PhD Fellowship (2025)**: Selected for Amazon's inaugural fellowship program, part of a $68 million initiative supporting over 100 doctoral students nationwide.
- **MBZUAI Research**: Previously worked at the IVAL-Lab at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence with Prof. Fahad Shahbaz Khan.
- **SketchX Lab Experience**: Research intern at University of Surrey's SketchX Lab during undergraduate studies.
