# Alice Gatti

## Biographical Summary

Alice Gatti is a researcher at UC Berkeley's Center for AI Safety (CAIS) and Scale AI. She is part of the "new wave" of safety researchers dedicated to identifying deceptive practices and building more rigorous evaluation standards for frontier models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alice focuses on the problem of "Safetywashing"—where companies claim high safety performance while their models remain vulnerable to subtle or catastrophic failures. She contributed to the WMDP (Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy) benchmark, helping define the boundaries of what models should be explicitly unlearned to prevent malicious misuse.

## Major Publications
- [The WMDP Benchmark: Measuring and Reducing Malicious Use With Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00000) (2024) - *Standardizing bio-security and safety unlearning.*
- [Safetywashing: Do Safety Benchmarks Actually Measure Safety?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00000) (2024) - *Critical audit of existing industry safety claims.*
