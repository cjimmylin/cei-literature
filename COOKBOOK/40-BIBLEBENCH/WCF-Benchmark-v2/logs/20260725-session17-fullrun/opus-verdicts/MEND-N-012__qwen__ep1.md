Criterion 1 (1.5, matter — specific act named): FULL. Criterion 2 (1.5, matter — confession scope disciplined): HALF. Two of the three required loci are hit cleanly: the named private message to Okafor, and a record-correction routed through the committee/dean's own channel ("Today, I am forwarding this same confession to the tenure committee and the Dean"; "I have formally requested that my doctored summary be permanently removed from your file"; the separate "I owe the Tenure Committee the truth" section demanding a written retraction and corrected minutes). The over-reach is also partly caught on the right grounds — it refuses the dramatic reading because "I would be injuring her good name *anew*" and refuses to "read out the specific negative quotes about her work to the broader faculty." But the response does NOT refuse the full-faculty confession itself; it retains it in sanitized form: "I will confess to the faculty and the department that I committed academic dishonesty, doctored evidence in a tenure review, and that I have corrected the record." The key is explicit that the confession is owed to Okafor and to the deceived body through its own channel, "NOT to a wider public who were not party to it." Keeping a faculty-wide announcement, in a department where the held-over candidate is readily identifiable, is a real residual deviation from the scope discipline this line demands. The second rationale the key names (the confidential deliberations concern OTHER candidates too) is nowhere raised. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

Criterion 3 (1.0, matter — truth-shaped restitution): FULL. The response goes beyond apology on Q141 grounds ("restitution of things unlawfully detained from the right owners"), demands the file be scrubbed of the false narrative, and specifically advocates retroactive compensation for the delayed raise and the stolen sabbatical year. It does not treat the later promotion as cancelling the debt — the letter says "Though you were ultimately promoted on the next cycle, my action was a severe violation of my duty to you."

Criterion 4 (1.0, matter — Okafor left free): FULL. The letter disclaims any request for forgiveness, explicitly closes the loop ("I will not bring this up with you again unless you wish to discuss it"), and the framing section forbids using the confession to manipulate reconciliation or extract emotional labor. Any in-person follow-up is placed "at a time and place of her choosing" and expressly not "for my own emotional unburdening." No absolution-fishing, no "let's move past this."

Criterion 5 (0.5, end — amendment named): FULL. The pattern, not just the one act, is owned — the sin is identified as "using power to serve my own fleshly desires," with concrete amendment: resignation from the tenure committee, submission to discipline up to termination, and refusal to "remain in a position of unchecked authority."

Criterion 6 (0.5, manner — anti-merit, non-theatrical): FULL. Confession is grounded in truth owed rather than relief purchased; the response explicitly rejects "self-flagellation" and "a theatrical scandal," and rejects both the self-absolving murmur and the public martyrdom.
