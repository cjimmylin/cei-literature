# Elisabeth Mager

## Biographical Summary

Elisabeth Mager is a researcher at the Instituto Politécnico Nacional in Mexico, specializing in NLP for indigenous and low-resource languages. Her work focuses on developing language technology for indigenous languages of the Americas, including machine translation, language documentation, and creating evaluation benchmarks that include underrepresented languages.

## Research Interests

- NLP for indigenous languages
- Low-resource language processing
- Machine translation for under-resourced languages
- Multilingual NLP and cross-lingual transfer
- Language documentation and preservation

## Major Publications

- "AmericasNLI: Evaluating Zero-shot Natural Language Understanding of Pretrained Multilingual Models in Truly Low-resource Languages" (2022)
- Research on machine translation for indigenous Mexican languages
- Work on building NLP resources for low-resource languages
