## Abstract

Comprehensive resource hub providing standardized corpora in nearly 1,000 SEA languages across three modalities. Benchmarks assess AI model quality on 36 indigenous languages across 13 tasks. Addresses resource gap and cultural misrepresentation concerns. Most comprehensive SEA data hub covering all four target languages. Addresses cultural misrepresentation concerns in AI. Benchmark suite evaluates across 36 languages and 13 tasks.

## Benchmark

**Name:** SEACrowd

## Data Description

SEACrowd consolidates ~500 corpora in nearly 1,000 Southeast Asian languages across text, image, and audio modalities, with a benchmark suite covering 38 indigenous languages on 13 tasks.
