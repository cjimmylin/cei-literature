## Abstract

Dataset compiled from YouTube, Telegram, and VK using keyword-based crawling for destructive speech in Kazakh. Covers cyberbullying, racism, religious extremism, nationalistic extremism. Kazakh-specific hate speech detection dataset addressing culturally relevant categories of harmful content including nationalistic extremism and religious extremism specific to Kazakhstan.

## Data Description

A Kazakh-language hate speech detection dataset compiled from YouTube, Telegram, and VK using keyword-based crawling. The dataset covers culturally relevant categories including cyberbullying, racism, religious extremism, and nationalistic extremism specific to Kazakhstan. No specific LLMs were evaluated; the dataset addresses the gap in Kazakh-language content moderation resources.
