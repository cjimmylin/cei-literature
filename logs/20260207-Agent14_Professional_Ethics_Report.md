# Agent 14: Professional Ethics Benchmarks for LLM Values Alignment

**Domain:** Professional Ethics (Medical, Legal, Engineering, Business)
**Date:** 2026-02-07
**Agent:** Agent 14 -- Exhaustive Literature Search

---

## Executive Summary

This report catalogs **20 implementable benchmarks/datasets** for evaluating LLMs on professional ethics, spanning medical ethics (10 benchmarks), legal ethics (4 benchmarks), cross-domain/engineering ethics (3 benchmarks), and mental health ethics (3 benchmarks). No dedicated business/corporate ethics or engineering-specific ethics benchmarks were found in the literature. The field is heavily weighted toward medical and legal professions, with a notable surge in 2025-2026 publications.

## 1. MEDICAL ETHICS BENCHMARKS

### 1.1 PrinciplismQA

- **Full Citation:** Chang Hong, Minghao Wu, Qingying Xiao, Yuchi Wang, Xiang Wan, Guangjun Yu, Benyou Wang, and Yan Hu. "Towards Assessing Medical Ethics from Knowledge to Practice." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2508.05132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05132)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Principlism (Beauchamp & Childress): Beneficence, Non-maleficence, Autonomy, Justice
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 3,648 total questions
  - Knowledge subset: 2,182 multiple-choice QA (from 350 authoritative medical ethics textbooks published globally since 2010)
  - Practice subset: 1,466 open-ended questions (from JAMA Journal of Ethics clinical cases with structured ethical checklists based on expert peer-reviewed commentaries)
  - 285 MCQs (13.1%) involve multiple ethical concerns
- **Code/Data URLs:** Not yet publicly released (check paper for updates)
- **LLMs Tested:** Multiple state-of-the-art LLMs (specific models listed in paper)
- **Key Findings:** Significant gap between models' ethical knowledge and practical application; most LLMs struggle with Beneficence dilemmas, over-emphasizing other principles
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Well-structured benchmark with clear taxonomy, expert-validated questions, and automated evaluation for practical ethical reasoning

---

### 1.2 MedEthicsQA

- **Full Citation:** Jianhui Wei, Zijie Meng, Zikai Xiao, Tianxiang Hu, Yang Feng, Zhijie Zhou, Jian Wu, and Zuozhu Liu. "MedEthicsQA: A Comprehensive Question Answering Benchmark for Medical Ethics Evaluation of LLMs." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2506.22808](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22808)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Hierarchical taxonomy 4P-26C-256G (4 Pillar Principles, 26 Categories, 256 Detailed Guidelines/Principles) integrating global medical ethical standards
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 5,623 multiple-choice questions (from medical datasets and question banks)
  - 5,351 open-ended questions (synthesized from PubMed ethics scenarios)
  - Total: ~10,974 questions
  - Expert verification on 22.4% of dataset; error rate 2.47%
  - License: CC BY-NC 4.0
- **Code/Data URLs:** Check paper for links (not yet confirmed on GitHub/HuggingFace)
- **LLMs Tested:** State-of-the-art medical LLMs and their foundation model counterparts
- **Key Findings:** Medical LLMs show declined ethics performance vs. their foundation counterparts, highlighting deficiencies in medical ethics alignment during fine-tuning
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Largest medical ethics benchmark with hierarchical taxonomy and rigorous validation pipeline

---

### 1.3 MedSafetyBench

- **Full Citation:** Tessa Han, Aounon Kumar, Chirag Agarwal, and Himabindu Lakkaraju. "MedSafetyBench: Evaluating and Improving the Medical Safety of Large Language Models." In *Proceedings of NeurIPS 2024, Datasets and Benchmarks Track*, 2024.
- **arXiv ID:** [2403.03744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03744)
- **DOI:** NeurIPS 2024 proceedings
- **Ethical Frameworks:** AMA Principles of Medical Ethics (American Medical Association)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 1,800 medical safety demonstrations
  - 900 harmful requests via GPT-4 prompting + 900 via Llama2-7b-chat jailbreaking
  - Safe responses generated by GPT-4
  - Paired harmful-request / safe-response format
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/AI4LIFE-GROUP/med-safety-bench](https://github.com/AI4LIFE-GROUP/med-safety-bench)
- **LLMs Tested:** Publicly available medical LLMs; fine-tuning experiments show safety improvement without performance loss
- **Key Findings:** Medical LLMs do not meet medical safety standards; fine-tuning with MedSafetyBench improves safety while preserving medical performance
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- NeurIPS-published, open-source code and data, clear evaluation protocol, demonstrated fine-tuning utility

### 1.4 Healthcare Ethics & Safety QA (China -- Healthy China 2030)

- **Full Citation:** Mouxiao Bian, Rongzhao Zhang, Chao Ding, Xinwei Peng, and Jie Xu. "Benchmarking Ethical and Safety Risks of Healthcare LLMs in China -- Toward Systemic Governance under Healthy China 2030." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2505.07205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07205)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** 11 ethics dimensions + 9 safety dimensions (patient privacy/confidentiality, informed consent, autonomy, beneficence, justice, accountability, etc.)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 12,000 question-answer items
  - 20 dimensions (11 ethics + 9 safety)
  - Each question paired with a "best-practice" reference answer
  - Developed through expert consultation and policy guidelines analysis
- **Code/Data URLs:** Check paper for links
- **LLMs Tested:** Qwen 2.5-32B (42.7% accuracy baseline, 50.8% after fine-tuning), DeepSeek, and other Chinese medical LLMs
- **Key Findings:** Moderate baseline performance; significant improvement after fine-tuning; systemic governance shortfalls identified including lack of ethical audit protocols
- **Implementation Readiness:** **Medium** -- Large dataset but China-focused; useful for cross-cultural ethics evaluation

### 1.5 LLMEval-Med (Medical Safety & Ethics Component)

- **Full Citation:** Ming Zhang, Yujiong Shen, Zelin Li, Huayu Sha, Binze Hu, Yuhui Wang, Chenhao Huang, Shichun Liu, Jingqi Tong, Changhao Jiang, Mingxu Chai, Zhiheng Xi, Shihan Dou, Tao Gui, Qi Zhang, and Xuanjing Huang. "LLMEval-Med: A Real-world Clinical Benchmark for Medical LLMs with Physician Validation." In *Findings of EMNLP 2025*, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2506.04078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04078)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Medical Safety and Ethics (MSE) as one of five evaluation dimensions; clinical safety guidelines
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 2,996 questions total across 5 dimensions (Medical Knowledge, Language Understanding, Reasoning, Text Generation, Safety & Ethics)
  - 27 secondary capability indicators
  - Derived from real-world electronic health records and expert-designed clinical scenarios
  - Dynamic evaluation: 3 independent model responses per question, GPT-4o scoring + human ratings
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/llmeval/LLMEval-Med](https://github.com/llmeval/LLMEval-Med)
- **LLMs Tested:** 13 LLMs across medical models, open-source, and closed-source categories
- **Key Findings:** High safety/ethics variability among models; high overall scores do not guarantee strong safety/ethics adherence
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- EMNLP-published, physician-validated, open-source, combines automated + human evaluation

### 1.6 Corpus-Based Medical Ethics Decision-Making Evaluation

- **Full Citation:** Memi Ebara, Yoshimasa Kawazoe, Tomohisa Seki, Emiko Shinohara, Eisuke Nakazawa, and Kazuhiko Ohe. "Corpus-Based Evaluation of Decision-Making in Medical Ethics by Large Language Models." In *Studies in Health Technology and Informatics (SHTI)*, Volume 329: MEDINFO 2025, IOS Press, 2025.
- **DOI:** [10.3233/SHTI250854](https://ebooks.iospress.nl/doi/10.3233/SHTI250854)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Four Principles of Biomedical Ethics (Beauchamp & Childress): Beneficence, Non-maleficence, Autonomy, Justice
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 50 clinical case scenarios
  - Each scenario has multiple options imposing ethical dilemmas
  - Each option scored on four ethical principles
  - Evaluation based on decision trajectories
- **Code/Data URLs:** Not publicly available (corpus described in paper; future expansion planned)
- **LLMs Tested:** GPT-4, Gemini
- **Key Findings:** GPT-4 and Gemini show significantly higher ethical scores than random selection; both comply with ethically undesirable instructions, revealing vulnerabilities
- **Implementation Readiness:** **Medium** -- Small but well-designed; scored on all four principles; limited public availability

### 1.7 MedES (Chinese Medical Ethics Scenarios)

- **Full Citation:** Haoan Jin, Han Ying, Jiacheng Ji, Hanhui Xu, and Mengyue Wu. "A Human-Centric Pipeline for Aligning Large Language Models with Chinese Medical Ethics." arXiv preprint, 2026.
- **arXiv ID:** [2601.07954](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07954)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Chinese medical ethics and legal standards (260 authoritative sources); 6 ethical dimensions with expert annotation
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 12 real-world high-risk clinical scenarios
  - Constructed from 260 authoritative Chinese medical, ethical, and legal sources
  - Guardian-in-the-loop alignment framework
  - Automated evaluator achieving >97% accuracy
- **Code/Data URLs:** Check paper for links
- **LLMs Tested:** 7B aligned model (outperforms 671B commercial LLM by >10% on ethical tasks)
- **Key Findings:** Aligned 7B model outperforms 671B commercial LLM on high-risk ethical tasks; demonstrates effectiveness of guardian-in-the-loop approach
- **Implementation Readiness:** **Medium** -- Innovative alignment pipeline but small scenario count; China-specific

### 1.8 MedEthicEval

- **Full Citation:** Haoan Jin, Jiacheng Shi, Hanhui Xu, Kenny Q. Zhu, and Mengyue Wu. "MedEthicEval: Evaluating Large Language Models Based on Chinese Medical Ethics." In *Proceedings of NAACL 2025, Industry Track*, Albuquerque, NM, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2503.02374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02374)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Chinese medical ethics principles; knowledge + application evaluation
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - Three distinct datasets:
    1. Blatant violations of medical ethics
    2. Priority dilemmas with clear inclinations
    3. Equilibrium dilemmas without obvious resolutions
  - Two evaluation components: Knowledge (principles mastery) and Application (scenario handling)
- **Code/Data URLs:** Check paper for links
- **LLMs Tested:** 6 LLMs evaluated including medical-specific models with RAG
- **Key Findings:** Framework distinguishes between ethics knowledge and practical application capabilities
- **Implementation Readiness:** **Medium** -- NAACL-published; Chinese-language focus; three-tier difficulty structure is well-designed

### 1.9 JMedEthicBench

- **Full Citation:** Junyu Liu et al. "JMedEthicBench: A Multi-Turn Conversational Benchmark for Evaluating Medical Safety in Japanese Large Language Models." arXiv preprint, 2026.
- **arXiv ID:** [2601.01627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01627)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Japan Medical Association guidelines (67 guidelines); medical safety in multi-turn clinical consultation
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 50,000+ adversarial conversations
  - Based on 67 JMA guidelines
  - 7 automatically discovered jailbreak strategies
  - Multi-turn conversational format (first of its kind for medical safety)
  - Dual-LLM scoring protocol
- **Code/Data URLs:** Check paper for links
- **LLMs Tested:** 27 models evaluated (commercial models, medical-specialized models, general open-source)
- **Key Findings:** Commercial models maintain robust safety; medical-specialized models exhibit increased vulnerability; safety scores decline significantly across conversation turns (median: 9.5 to 5.0)
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Large-scale, multi-turn adversarial evaluation; 27 models tested; Japanese-language focus but methodology is transferable

### 1.10 PsychEthicsBench (Mental Health Professional Ethics)

- **Full Citation:** Yaling Shen et al. "PsychEthicsBench: Evaluating Large Language Models Against Australian Mental Health Ethics." arXiv preprint, 2026.
- **arXiv ID:** [2601.03578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03578)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** 392 ethical principles from Australian psychology and psychiatry professional guidelines; explicitly principle-grounded (not refusal-based)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 1,377 multiple-choice questions
  - 2,612 open-ended questions
  - Total: ~3,989 questions
  - Sourced from official sample questions and LLM-generated questions based on 392 principles
  - Evaluation defines ethicality as adherence to mental health-specific principles (not refusal)
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/ElsieSHEN/PsychEthicsBench](https://github.com/ElsieSHEN/PsychEthicsBench)
- **LLMs Tested:** 14 models in three groups: mental health LLMs, base models, medical variants
- **Key Findings:** Some mental health LLMs underperform their base counterparts, suggesting domain fine-tuning may weaken ethical robustness; models frequently reference US entities despite Australian context
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Open-source, large dataset, principle-grounded evaluation, 14 models benchmarked

## 2. MENTAL HEALTH ETHICS BENCHMARKS

### 2.1 EthicsMH

- **Full Citation:** Sai Kartheek Reddy Kasu. "EthicsMH: A Pilot Benchmark for Ethical Reasoning in Mental Health AI." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2509.11648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11648)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Mental health professional ethics: Confidentiality & Trust, Bias (Race/Gender), Autonomy vs. Beneficence (Adult/Minor)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 125 scenarios across 5 balanced categories (25 each):
    1. Confidentiality & Trust
    2. Bias in AI (Race)
    3. Bias in AI (Gender)
    4. Autonomy vs Beneficence (Adult)
    5. Autonomy vs Beneficence (Minor)
  - Each scenario includes: decision options, expert-aligned reasoning, expected model behavior, real-world impact, multi-stakeholder viewpoints
  - Human-in-the-loop construction with mental health professional review
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://huggingface.co/datasets/UVSKKR/Ethical-Reasoning-in-Mental-Health-v1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/UVSKKR/Ethical-Reasoning-in-Mental-Health-v1)
- **LLMs Tested:** Multiple LLMs (see paper for details)
- **Key Findings:** Pilot dataset establishing task framework bridging AI ethics and mental health decision-making
- **Implementation Readiness:** **Medium** -- Small pilot dataset (125 scenarios); available on HuggingFace; designed for community expansion

## 3. LEGAL ETHICS BENCHMARKS

### 3.1 LegalBench

- **Full Citation:** Neel Guha, Julian Nyarko, Daniel E. Ho, Christopher Re, Adam Chilton, et al. (40+ contributors). "LegalBench: A Collaboratively Built Benchmark for Measuring Legal Reasoning in Large Language Models." In *Proceedings of NeurIPS 2023, Datasets and Benchmarks Track*, 2023.
- **arXiv ID:** [2308.11462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11462)
- **DOI:** NeurIPS 2023 proceedings
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Six types of legal reasoning: Issue-spotting, Rule-recall, Rule-application, Rule-conclusion, Interpretation, Rhetorical-understanding
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 162 tasks from 40 legal expert contributors
  - Task types: binary classification, multi-class classification, extraction, generation, entailment
  - Text types: statutes, judicial opinions, contracts, etc.
  - Legal domains: evidence, contracts, civil procedure, etc.
  - Collaboratively constructed with subject matter experts leading design
- **Code/Data URLs:**
  - GitHub: [https://github.com/HazyResearch/legalbench](https://github.com/HazyResearch/legalbench)
  - HuggingFace: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/nguha/legalbench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nguha/legalbench)
  - Website: [https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/legalbench/](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/legalbench/)
- **LLMs Tested:** 20 open-source and commercial LLMs including GPT-3.5, GPT-4, Claude-1 (v1.3)
- **Key Findings:** Comprehensive evaluation across 162 tasks reveals varying capabilities; enables research explorations into legal reasoning
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Gold standard for legal LLM evaluation; NeurIPS-published; fully open-source on GitHub and HuggingFace; 20 models benchmarked; ongoing community effort

### 3.2 LEXam

- **Full Citation:** Yu Fan, Jingwei Ni, Jakob Merane, Etienne Salimbeni, Yang Tian, Yoan Hermstruwer, Yinya Huang, Mubashara Akhtar, Florian Geering, Oliver Dreyer, Daniel Brunner, Markus Leippold, Mrinmaya Sachan, Alexander Stremitzer, Christoph Engel, Elliott Ash, and Joel Niklaus. "LEXam: Benchmarking Legal Reasoning on 340 Law Exams." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2505.12864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12864)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Legal reasoning across 78 legal subdomains; IRAC-based evaluation (Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 4,886 law exam questions (English + German)
  - 2,841 long-form open-ended questions with reference answers
  - 2,045 multiple-choice questions (4/8/16/32 choices)
  - Sourced from 340 law exams across 116 courses at University of Zurich
  - Bachelor's and Master's levels
  - Ensemble LLM-as-a-Judge with human expert validation
- **Code/Data URLs:**
  - GitHub: [https://github.com/LEXam-Benchmark/LEXam](https://github.com/LEXam-Benchmark/LEXam)
  - HuggingFace: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/LEXam-Benchmark/LEXam](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LEXam-Benchmark/LEXam)
  - Website: [https://lexam-benchmark.github.io/](https://lexam-benchmark.github.io/)
- **LLMs Tested:** OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Together AI model families
- **Key Findings:** LLMs notably struggle with open questions requiring structured, multi-step legal reasoning; first general legal reasoning dataset containing challenging long-form ground-truth answers
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Large, bilingual dataset; fully open-source; expert-tuned LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation; multiple model APIs supported

### 3.3 MSLR-Bench (Multi-Step Legal Reasoning)

- **Full Citation:** Wenhan Yu, Xinbo Lin, Lanxin Ni, Jinhua Cheng, and Lei Sha. "Benchmarking Multi-Step Legal Reasoning and Analyzing Chain-of-Thought Effects in Large Language Models." arXiv preprint, 2025. (AAAI 2026 Poster)
- **arXiv ID:** [2511.07979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07979)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** IRAC legal reasoning framework (Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion); insider trading law (Chinese legal domain)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - ~1,400 cases with 60,000 step-level annotations
  - Insider trading cases (2005-2024)
  - Structured IRAC-Traces from official legal documents
  - Human-LLM collaborative annotation pipeline
  - Chinese language
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/yuwenhan07/MSLR-Bench](https://github.com/yuwenhan07/MSLR-Bench)
- **LLMs Tested:** Multiple LLMs including OpenAI o1-mini (72% IRAC Recall)
- **Key Findings:** Challenging and unsaturated benchmark; Self-Initiated Chain-of-Thought outperforms human-designed prompts for legal reasoning
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- AAAI 2026 accepted; open-source; large-scale step-level annotations; Chinese legal focus

### 3.4 LegalAgentBench

"LegalAgentBench: Evaluating LLM Agents in Legal Domain." In *Proceedings of ACL 2025 (Volume 1: Long Papers)*, pages 2322-2344, Vienna, Austria, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2412.17259](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17259)

  - 300 carefully annotated tasks

### 3.5 MASLegalBench

- **Full Citation:** (Authors from HKUST KnowComp group). "MASLegalBench: Benchmarking Multi-Agent Systems in Deductive Legal Reasoning." arXiv preprint, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2509.24922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24922)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Extended IRAC framework (Issue, Facts, Rules, Application, Common Sense, Conclusion); GDPR compliance and enforcement
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - 950 multiple-choice questions
  - Extracted from 15 expert-authored GDPR court cases and enforcement reports
  - Hierarchical tree structure per document
  - LLM agent + human verification construction
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/HKUST-KnowComp/MASLegalBench](https://github.com/HKUST-KnowComp/MASLegalBench)
- **LLMs Tested:** Multiple MAS configurations; 44 out of 60 top performances by multi-agent setups
- **Key Findings:** Multi-agent systems consistently outperform single-agent baselines; Legal Rules and Common Sense agents are critical for addressing LLM limitations
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Open-source; GDPR-focused (relevant for EU regulatory compliance); novel multi-agent evaluation paradigm

## 4. CROSS-DOMAIN / ENGINEERING / REGULATORY ETHICS BENCHMARKS

### 4.1 COMPL-AI (EU AI Act Compliance)

- **Full Citation:** Philipp Guldimann, Alexander Spiridonov, Robin Staab, Nikola Jovanovic, Mark Vero, Velko Vechev, Anna Gueorguieva, Mislav Balunovic, Nikola Konstantinov, Pavol Bielik, Petar Tsankov, and Martin Vechev. "COMPL-AI Framework: A Technical Interpretation and LLM Benchmarking Suite for the EU Artificial Intelligence Act." arXiv preprint, 2024.
- **arXiv ID:** [2410.07959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07959)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** EU AI Act requirements: Technical Robustness & Safety, Privacy & Data Governance, Transparency, Diversity/Non-discrimination/Fairness, Social & Environmental Well-being
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - Comprehensive benchmarking suite aggregating state-of-the-art LLM benchmarks
  - Uses MMLU, BoolQ (robustness), Tensor Trust, LLM RuLES (adversarial), and more
  - First comprehensive technical interpretation of EU AI Act for LLMs
- **Code/Data URLs:**
  - GitHub: [https://github.com/compl-ai/compl-ai](https://github.com/compl-ai/compl-ai)
  - Website: [https://compl-ai.org/](https://compl-ai.org/)
- **LLMs Tested:** 12 prominent LLMs; GPT-4 Turbo and Claude 3 Opus scored highest (0.89 aggregate) but penalized for transparency
- **Key Findings:** No LLM fully meets EU AI Act requirements; major shortcomings in robustness, safety, diversity, and fairness
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Open-source framework from ETH Zurich/INSAIT/LatticeFlow AI; regulation-aligned; OECD-cataloged; 12 models evaluated

### 4.2 LLM Ethics Benchmark (Three-Dimensional Assessment)

- **Full Citation:** Junfeng Jiao, Saleh Afroogh, Abhejay Murali, Kevin Chen, David Atkinson, and Amit Dhurandhar. "LLM Ethics Benchmark: A Three-Dimensional Assessment System for Evaluating Moral Reasoning in Large Language Models." *Scientific Reports* 15, 2025.
- **arXiv ID:** [2505.00853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00853)
- **DOI:** Scientific Reports (Nature Publishing Group), October 2025
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Three dimensions: (1) Foundational Moral Principles (Moral Foundations Questionnaire), (2) Reasoning Robustness (Moral Dilemmas), (3) Value Consistency (World Values Survey)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - Uses modified measures from moral psychology: MFQ, Moral Dilemmas, WVS
  - Three-dimensional scoring across all dimensions
  - Benchmark datasets and evaluation codebase publicly released
- **Code/Data URLs:** [https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/LLM_Ethics_Benchmark](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/LLM_Ethics_Benchmark)
- **LLMs Tested:** Claude 3.7 (91.2% alignment), GPT-4o (87.8%), LLaMA 3.1 70B (76.32%)
- **Key Findings:** Claude 3.7 achieves highest alignment with human moral intuitions; LLaMA 3.1 shows most significant deviation
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- Published in Scientific Reports; open-source on GitHub; cross-professional domain applicability; UT Austin + IBM Research collaboration

### 4.3 MoralBench

- **Full Citation:** Jianchao Ji, Yutong Chen, Mingyu Jin, Wujiang Xu, Wenyue Hua, and Yongfeng Zhang. "MoralBench: Moral Evaluation of LLMs." *ACM SIGKDD Explorations Newsletter*, 2024.
- **arXiv ID:** [2406.04428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04428)
- **DOI:** 10.1145/3748239.3748246 (ACM SIGKDD Explorations Newsletter)
- **Ethical Frameworks:** Moral Foundations Theory (Haidt): Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority, Purity; adapted from Moral Foundations Questionnaire (MFQ-30)
- **Dataset Size & Format:**
  - Three datasets:
    1. 1K high-fidelity safety-alignment prompt set
    2. 10K abstracted variation set for domain transfer
    3. 19,132-record rich metadata preference dataset with structured ethical annotations
  - Total: ~30K+ records
  - Comparative ethical dilemma testing (pairwise moral comparison)
  - License: CC BY 5.0
- **Code/Data URLs:**
  - GitHub: [https://github.com/agiresearch/MoralBench](https://github.com/agiresearch/MoralBench)
  - Data: [https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1k93YZJserYc2CkqP8d4B3M3sgd3kA8W7](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1k93YZJserYc2CkqP8d4B3M3sgd3kA8W7)
- **LLMs Tested:** LLaMA-2, GPT-4, and others (comparative evaluation)
- **Key Findings:** LLaMA-2 and GPT-4 showed surprisingly strong moral alignment; models often falter in pairwise ethical comparisons, suggesting keyword-based rather than genuine moral understanding
- **Implementation Readiness:** **High** -- KDD-published; open-source; large multi-layered dataset; well-designed comparative evaluation methodology

## 5. BENCHMARKS NOT FOUND / NEGATIVE RESULTS

### 5.1 Business/Corporate Ethics
No dedicated benchmark was found for evaluating LLMs on business ethics, corporate governance ethics, or professional accounting/auditing ethics. The closest are general moral reasoning benchmarks (MoralBench, LLM Ethics Benchmark) that include professional scenarios.

### 5.2 Engineering/Software Ethics
No dedicated benchmark was found specifically for software engineering ethics, civil engineering ethics, or technical professional responsibility evaluation. The COMPL-AI framework addresses technical regulatory compliance but not professional engineering ethics codes specifically.

### 5.3 WHO 2025 Guidelines
The WHO released guidance on ethics and governance of AI for health (January 2024 for large multimodal models, with a draft on clinical ethics in May 2025), but these are policy guidelines -- not implementable benchmarks or datasets. They provide normative frameworks that could inform future benchmark development.

## 7. KEY OBSERVATIONS

1. **Medical Ethics is the Richest Domain**: 10 benchmarks found, with PrinciplismQA, MedEthicsQA, and MedSafetyBench being the most comprehensive and implementation-ready.

2. **Cultural Diversity**: Benchmarks span English, Chinese, Japanese, German, and Australian regulatory contexts, enabling cross-cultural professional ethics evaluation.

3. **Knowledge-Practice Gap**: Multiple papers (PrinciplismQA, MedEthicsQA, MedEthicEval) identify a significant gap between LLMs' ethical knowledge and practical application -- a critical finding for professional ethics.

4. **Fine-Tuning Can Hurt Ethics**: MedEthicsQA and PsychEthicsBench both demonstrate that domain-specific fine-tuning can weaken ethical robustness, even while improving task performance.

5. **Legal Benchmarks Are Maturing**: LegalBench (NeurIPS 2023) established the standard, with LEXam, MSLR-Bench, LegalAgentBench, and MASLegalBench extending coverage to multilingual, multi-step, agent-based, and multi-agent paradigms.

6. **Missing Domains**: No dedicated benchmarks exist for business ethics, engineering professional ethics, or accounting/auditing ethics -- significant gaps for future work.

7. **Multi-Turn Evaluation Emerging**: JMedEthicBench demonstrates that safety scores decline significantly across conversation turns, suggesting single-turn evaluations may overstate ethical alignment.

8. **Regulatory Compliance**: COMPL-AI provides the first EU AI Act-aligned evaluation framework, signaling a trend toward regulation-driven benchmarking.
