# Mingke Yang

## Research Summary

Mingke Yang is affiliated with ShanghaiTech University. Lead author of "DistillSeq: A Framework for Safety Alignment Testing in Large Language Models using Knowledge Distillation" (ISSTA 2024). The framework generates malicious queries to evaluate LLM resilience, achieving 93% average increase in attack success rate. Research focuses on cost-effective testing of LLM safety alignment using knowledge distillation.
