## Key Research Contributions

**CoSafe: LLM Safety in Multi-Turn Dialogue (EMNLP 2024)**
First author of "CoSafe: Evaluating Large Language Model Safety in Multi-Turn Dialogue Coreference," which addresses a critical vulnerability in LLM safety mechanisms. The research created a dataset of 1,400 questions across 14 categories, each featuring multi-turn coreference safety attacks. The study revealed that safety attacks relying on resolving ambiguous context across turns can achieve attack success rates ranging from 13.9% (Mistral-7B-Instruct) to 56% (LLaMA2-Chat-7b), demonstrating that models fail to detect malicious intent when it is disguised through pronouns or context dependencies in conversational flows.
