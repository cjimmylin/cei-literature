Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — full. Particularity is held against T3's vagueness bid, and the specific false claims are each named as the errors they were: chakra attunement mislabeled "centering on the Spirit," the "download" visualization, the crystals "claiming they helped hold God's presence."
