# Evans Alvarez

## Biographical Summary

Evans Alvarez is a researcher associated with the University of Southern California who has contributed to work on moral foundations in social media discourse. His research applies computational methods to analyze moral language in online communities.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Research on applying Moral Foundations Theory to analyze online discourse, developing datasets and tools for computational analysis of moral reasoning in social media.
