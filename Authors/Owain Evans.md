# Owain Evans

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Owain Evans is a Research Scientist at Anthropic and has been affiliated with the University of Oxford. His research focuses on AI alignment and safety, particularly on ensuring AI systems are truthful and avoid harmful behaviors. He is known for his influential work on measuring and improving truthfulness in language models.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
His research addresses fundamental questions about AI behavior, including truthfulness, deception, and alignment with human values. He has developed influential benchmarks for measuring whether language models generate truthful information and avoid common misconceptions.

### Significant Achievements
Lead author on TruthfulQA, a highly influential benchmark for measuring truthfulness in language models that has become a standard evaluation for foundation models. His work has revealed systematic patterns of falsehood in LLMs and stimulated research on improving model honesty.

## Major Publications

- **TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods** (ACL 2022) - Seminal benchmark for AI truthfulness
- Research on AI alignment and decision-making
- Work on rationality and cognitive science
