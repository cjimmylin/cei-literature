# Aditi Khandelwal

## Research Summary

Aditi Khandelwal is a PhD student at Mila - Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute (2024-2028), advised by Siva Reddy and Golnoosh Farnadi. Previously a Researcher at Microsoft (2022-2024) and undergraduate at Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (2018-2022). Her research focuses on democratizing LLMs through multilingual mixture-of-experts models, ethical reasoning in LLMs, and handling value pluralism. Her work includes studies on moral judgment and reasoning abilities of LLMs across multiple languages including Chinese, Hindi, Russian, Spanish, and Swahili using the Defining Issues Test.
