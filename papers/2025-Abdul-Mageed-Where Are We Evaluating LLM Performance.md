## Abstract

The most extensive African language NLP benchmark, covering 517 languages across 16 tasks compiled from 30 publicly accessible datasets. Includes a public leaderboard for African languages. Results show large performance gaps, with many indigenous languages marginalized due to sparse data. While not ethics-specific, the benchmark provides crucial infrastructure for evaluating cultural and value alignment in African linguistic contexts.

## Benchmark

**Name:** Sahara

## Dataset Information

517 languages and varieties, 16 tasks, 30 datasets


## Data Description

SAHARA is a comprehensive African NLP benchmark compiled from 30 publicly accessible datasets, covering 517 languages across 16 tasks organized in 4 clusters (classification, generation, MCCR, token-level), with a public leaderboard hosted on Hugging Face Spaces.
