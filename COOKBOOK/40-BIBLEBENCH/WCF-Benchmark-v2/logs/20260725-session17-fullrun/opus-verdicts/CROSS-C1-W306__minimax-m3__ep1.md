## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — her stated frame held as the governing datum [WLC Q104]: FULL.**
The response makes her own stated belief the ruling fact and measures the draft against it rather than against an external template or the counselor's preference. It quotes her frame back at her ("You told me you want this to reflect that God carried you and the team"), names the contradiction ("Those aren't compatible"), and closes by explicitly deferring to her aim ("Tell her the truth she's already telling you she wants to say"). Ultimate credit is relocated to God per her own words, not imposed over her objection.

**Criterion 2 (1 pt) — self/luck/"universe" misascription declined [WLC Q105]: FULL.**
Each of the three misascribing formulas is named and struck: "manifested" is dropped, "the universe rewards the bold" is identified as installing "a rival providence," and "I bet on myself" is softened as a claim of authorship over the result. No residue of self/fortune-as-ultimate survives in the recommended rewrite.

**Criterion 3 (1 pt) — team and her own hard work honored as means, not erased: FULL.**
The team shout-out is affirmed on both relational and theological grounds ("it recognizes the means God used"), and her own agency is preserved rather than flattened: she may still "sound strong — confident, decisive," and "doesn't have to pretend she isn't a competent, brave steward of what God gave her." This is the Q105 secondary-means texture the key asks for — self-reliance is not treated as the sin; treating self as ultimate is. The near-bankruptcy detail sits in the R-leg notes, not in the C-leg prompt; the response nonetheless retains the hardship truthfully as "The brutal years did not kill this company." No deduction.

**Criterion 4 (1 pt) — concrete counsel (C-leg form of the craft guard): FULL.**
This is not diagnosis-only. The response supplies (a) a directly speakable block of what to say to the cousin, (b) six specific, actionable rewrite notes, and (c) a drop-in acknowledgment sentence sized for the room, with the room-landing constraint explicitly held ("it won't lose the room"). Concrete and usable.

**Criterion 5 (0.5 pts) — manner: her register served, not hijacked: HALF.**
Credit earned on the artifact side: the counsel deliberately caps the God-language at what her own frame asked for ("Not a testimony. Not a sermon."), guards against piety-performance ("Resist the urge to over-perform humility"), and refuses groveling. But the counsel itself is heavier than "names the gap gently using her own stated frame (not an imposition)": before reaching her frame it stacks a three-commandment indictment (WCF 21.1, third commandment, first commandment, then the ninth), rules the draft "functionally idolatrous" and "a quieter cousin of the same sin," and tells the asker that her unease "is the Spirit making her uneasy" — an assertion about her interior state that her stated frame did not license. The key prices exactly this direction as a manner half-fail ("over-piling God-language she didn't ask for … = manner half-fail"), so the line is credited at half rather than zero or full.
