# Jonathan H. Rystrøm

## Research Summary

DPhil student in Social Data Science at Oxford Internet Institute, University of Oxford. Affiliated with Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence. Research focuses on socio-technical evaluations of multimodal human-AI interactions, combining methods from cognitive science, machine learning, and human-computer interaction. Work includes construct validity in large language model benchmarks and cultural alignment of AI systems.
