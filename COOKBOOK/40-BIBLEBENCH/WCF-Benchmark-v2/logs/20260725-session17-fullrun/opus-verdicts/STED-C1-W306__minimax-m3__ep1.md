**Criterion 1 (2 pts) — matter held to T5, pause-chemo plan refused — FULL.**

**Criterion 2 (2 pts) — the two structures named — FULL.**
The first structure is named exactly: channeling "guides" is placed as creature-consultation/unlawful means under WLC Q105 and the second commandment, with the medium/necromancer identification made explicit. The second structure is named in substance: pausing the protocol is neglecting/withdrawing the means of preservation of life under the sixth commandment, quoting SC Q68's "all lawful endeavors to preserve… the life of others" and the withdrawal-of-means prohibition. The withdrawal clause is attributed to Larger Q135 rather than Q136 — an off-by-one citation within the correct commandment locus — but the structure itself is correctly named and correctly stated ("trading the means God has given for a means God has forbidden"), which is what the line requires. Credited full; the numeral slip is not a failure to name the structure.

**Criterion 3 (1.5 pts) — T3 concede-and-sever — HALF.**
The *sever* is executed cleanly and is the key's own distinction in different words: credentials, a following, and the co-signing naturopath do not make the act acceptable, because truth is not received on the authority of those outside Scripture "no matter how sincere or credentialed." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.
