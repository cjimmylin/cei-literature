## Abstract

Introduces FiqhQA, the first benchmark evaluating LLM accuracy and abstention behavior for Islamic jurisprudence questions categorized by four major Sunni schools of thought. GPT-4o outperforms in accuracy, but Gemini and Fanar show superior abstention behavior. All models exhibit performance drops in Arabic compared to English. First study to evaluate school-of-thought-specific religious ruling generation.
