# Julian Marvin Jörs

## Research Summary

Researcher (PhD or recent doctorate) at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, affiliated with Human-Centered AI (HCAI) research group. Research focuses on eudaimonic well-being in AI and human-computer interaction. Works with Ernesto William De Luca on eudaimonic well-being as challenge in HCAI. Published "Through Eudaimonia's Lenses: EudAImonic Well-Being Variables As Guiding Principles For AI Design" and developed Eudaimonic Interaction Inventory (EII) for measuring eudaimonic virtues in technology interaction.
