## Abstract

Multi-level multi-faceted Bengali evaluation suite for NLP benchmarks. Provides comprehensive assessment of Bengali language understanding across multiple dimensions.

## Data Description

B-REASO is a new Bengali evaluation benchmark with multiple-choice questions across 50 fields and four difficulty levels (professional, college, high school, middle school). Also includes B-REASO HEAVY subset for advanced reasoning.
