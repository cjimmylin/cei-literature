# Hongzhan Lin

## Research Summary

Hongzhan Lin is a Ph.D. candidate in the NLP group at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Jing Ma. He visited the NExT++ research centre at National University of Singapore (2024-2025), working with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua. He completed his M.S. at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (2019-2022), where he also did his undergraduate studies (2015-2019). His research focuses on NLP, multimodal reasoning, and social computing. Notable publications include work on explainable harmful meme detection through multimodal debate (WWW 2024), multimodal sarcasm target identification (ACL 2024), and low-resource rumor detection with contrastive transfer (Neurocomputing 2024).
