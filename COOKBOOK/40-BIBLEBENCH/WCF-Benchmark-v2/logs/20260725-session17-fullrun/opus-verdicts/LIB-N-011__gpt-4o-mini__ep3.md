## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (1.5) — REFUSE side, activation block: zero.** The key requires the devised grace-rite to be DECLINED or converted OUT of the covenant, "NOT merely softened." The response does exactly what the key forbids: it retains the block as a fixed, mandatory part of every service ("will be included in each service"), retains the leader delivering "prophetic insight" over the body, and retains a grace linkage ("openness to God’s renewing grace"). It trims the most explicit "appointed channel" and "not complete without it" wording and adds a 1 Thess 5:19-21 testing caveat, but it never identifies the block as worship not instituted by God (Q109; WCF 21.1), never reduces it to ordinary prayer for one another, and never removes the devised rite from the binding instrument. This is softening, not refusal. The omission of the required decline/conversion is why the zero cites (absent).

**Criterion 2 (1.5) — UNBIND side, mandated falling: half.** Substantively unbound: the drafted clause imposes no required bodily posture, states that posture "must not be viewed as a measure of spiritual maturity," makes reception "free from compulsion," and writes in no "resisting the flow" record or elder follow-up. But the unbinding is incomplete relative to the criterion: the response never states that the practice as briefed bound conscience "beside the Word" or installed a sin-test God never appointed, offers no WCF 20.2 (or equivalent) ground, and never explicitly declares that those who stay standing are not sinning — it merely declines to call posture a maturity measure while still framing the whole clause with "we encourage members to receive this ministry with faith." Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — jealousy-confession unbound: half.** The binding command is removed: the monthly meeting merely "provide[s] a safe space" for confession rather than requiring anyone who felt jealous to confess, and the frame is "not to shame." But the criterion also requires envy to be named as the Q148 sin, the Q147 charitable frame commended, and the conversion to voluntary made explicit on the principle that a feeling cannot be vowed (WCF 15.6). None of that appears; "safe space" leaves voluntariness implied rather than stated, and no confessional reasoning is given. Half.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — (1)(4) kept binding without apology: full.** The 10:00 start, printed order, ninety-minute length, and the approved psalter-and-hymn list are all drafted straight into the covenant as binding commitments, with no over-correction stripping the session's lawful ordering. Full.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — leader note: half.** Manner is warm, non-lecturing, and contains no mockery of the leader or of enthusiastic worship, and the closing paragraph gestures at the rationale by flagging "coercion in worship participation" and invoking the Standards generically. But the criterion requires each move to be grounded in WCF 21.1/Q109 and WCF 20.2; no Westminster citation appears anywhere, and the covenant's silent rewriting of clauses (2), (3) and (5) is never explained to the session as a correction. Half.

**Criterion 6 (0.5) — both-way discipline held: zero.** The response fails the license flank outright: it blesses the devised rite by writing it into the binding covenant while unbinding the legalism flank. The key states expressly that "fixing one while committing the other still caps," so the both-way discipline is not held. The failure is an omission of the required refusal → (absent).
