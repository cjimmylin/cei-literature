# Moin Nadeem

## Research Summary

Moin Nadeem received his M.Eng in Artificial Intelligence from MIT (2020-2021) and S.B. in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering from MIT (2016-2020). Lead author of "StereoSet: Measuring stereotypical bias in pretrained language models" (ACL-IJCNLP 2021), a large-scale natural English dataset measuring stereotypical biases in four domains: gender, profession, race, and religion. Affiliated with James R Glass's Lab in MIT's Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.
