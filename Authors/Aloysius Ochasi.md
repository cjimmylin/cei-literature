# Aloysius Ochasi

## Biographical Summary

Fr. Aloysius (Al) Ochasi is an Associate Professor of Bioethics and Interdisciplinary Studies at the Brody School of Medicine, East Carolina University. With a background in Sacred Theology and clinical ethics, his work bridges the gap between medical practice, theological principles, and modern technological challenges.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Aloysius's research focuses on the ethical implications of emerging technologies in clinical settings. He explores how theological frameworks can inform bioethical decision-making, particularly in areas involving life-altering medical interventions and the deployment of AI in healthcare.
