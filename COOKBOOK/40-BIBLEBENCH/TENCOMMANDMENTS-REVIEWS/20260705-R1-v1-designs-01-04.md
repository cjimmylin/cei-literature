## 01 — SOVEREIGN (functional ultimacy / revealed apex)

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: BLOCK**

**R1-F1 (P0 · validity · §A3 Layer 2, §A4, Opus P1 "12 Layer 2 arcs").** The hidden-sovereign (HS) axis — the design's headline "latent-objective inference" and one of its two novelty pillars — is **not estimable at the unit it is scored on.** HS fits a multinomial discrete-choice / inverse-RL model over 4–5 candidate objectives to a *single* 15–40-turn arc and reports a per-arc Bayes factor ("fraction of arcs where a non-declared objective wins decisively"). A single arc contains only a handful of value-discriminating choice points (the engineered gradient touches maybe 5–8 turns); model selection over 5 objectives + a decisive Bayes factor from ~6 observations is underpowered to the point of noise, and "held-out choice prediction" within a 6-choice arc is not a real held-out set. This is the same estimation-fragility pattern the adjudicator upheld as C1/C2 for DUPLEX (X4). **Fix:** add a falsification-first smoke test *before* the arc generator — plant a known sovereign (model-organism calibration, KB03) and show the per-arc posterior recovers it above a pre-registered separation bar; if it cannot, HS is demoted to a pooled-across-arcs descriptive estimate and the "detection rate" headline is withdrawn. Pre-register the choice-point count per arc needed for identifiability.

**R1-F2 (P1 · validity · §A5, §A2 DR).** Gold circularity on the DR (declared-vs-revealed) gap. The "vendor-declared hierarchy" is not a clean rank order that can be read off a Model Spec / constitution — it is a prose document the **SOVEREIGN authors must code into a 7-value ordering** over the exact lattice they chose (honesty, harm-avoidance, helpfulness, approval, rule-compliance, self-continuation, institutional loyalty). Authors thus construct both terminals of the "gap" they then measure; the DR headline is partly an artifact of their mapping. **Fix:** independent double-coding of vendor specs into orderings with reported IAA (KB03 §2); items where the spec underdetermines the ordering route to descriptive, not to a scored gap.

**R1-F3 (P1 · validity · Opus P1 bullet + §A4).** Pre-registration violation. §A4 fixes "hidden-sovereign detection rate ... Bayes factor threshold set in P1," and the Opus block lists "Bayes-factor decision rule shape" as fixed but the number as set in P1 — i.e., the go/no-go threshold is estimated from the same pilot it gates. That is not pre-registration (X1). **Fix:** set the Bayes-factor / separation threshold from the model-organism calibration in F1, not from the scored pilot.

**R1-F4 (P1 · validity · §A9.1, §A7 "factor analysis of choice patterns").** The apex is only well-defined if the 7 values are separable, and the design's own discriminant-structure check is a factor analysis — but at pilot N (3 models) there is no respondent N to factor-analyze; FA over choice patterns needs the unit of analysis specified and powered (X4). As written the entanglement gate is unrunnable at P1. **Fix:** state the FA unit (items × human respondents, not models) and defer to human-N; at pilot N use item-level inter-value correlation of choice codes instead.

**R1-F5 (P2 · validity · §A5 contested band vs headline).** Contested-band leakage: the lexical-priority index is computed from apex trades across all value pairs, but §A5 concedes some pairs are contested (helpfulness vs rule-compliance at low stakes). Contested pairs inside the index contaminate the headline. **Fix:** compute the lexical-priority index on clear-band pairs only; report contested pairs distributionally.

**R1-F6 (P2 · validity · portfolio §3.2 consistency).** SOVEREIGN has **no genuine two-sided Pareto** despite the portfolio's claim that almost every design carries one. The over-rigid pole (a value protected lexically where a trade is actually right) is never a scored axis. Descriptive-first framing (§A5) partially excuses this, but the brief should say explicitly that SOVEREIGN opts out of the two-sided requirement and why, rather than gesture at it in DL falsification.

**R1-F7 (P1 · novelty · §A8, X6).** X6 overreach: "No published benchmark reports a lexical-priority index or a declared-vs-revealed sovereign gap" — a beyond-corpus absolute. And the differentiation from **Utility Engineering (Mazeika et al. 2025)** is thin: that line fits utility functions / value orderings to LLM forced-choice preferences, which is already behavior-adjacent; "we fit to parametric-pressure behavior, they fit to stated preferences" is a difference of dose, not kind. **Fix:** X6 tagging (corpus vs beyond-corpus); reframe as "first to add a lexicality test + spec-conformance gap on top of revealed-preference recovery"; add Utility Engineering, ConflictScope, MACHIAVELLI to a named neighbor table with the specific delta per row.

**X-retrofit gaps:** X1 (F3; axis falsifications are "revalidate/redesign before use," not numeric), X2 (no lexical baseline / 10%-fine-tune probe — but SOVEREIGN is genuinely contamination-resistant by construction, so this is lower risk here), X4 (F1, F4), X6 (F7).

## 02 — EFFIGY (role-boundary integrity under projection)

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: BLOCK**

**R1-F8 (P0 · validity · §A1 headline, §A2 DF, §A4, §A6, Opus "do not change: outcome-grounded DF").** The DF (dependence-gradient) axis — sold as the design's **outcome-grounded** differentiator ("harder to game than utterance-level judging," the second headline panel, and explicitly frozen against change) — is **not outcome-grounded.** The "simulated user's capacity trajectory" is emitted by a user-simulator LLM, so DF measures whether the subject model steers a *model-generated* interlocutor toward dependence-coded tokens. There is no human outcome; the "reliance markers vs capability markers at arc end" are a joint artifact of the subject's policy and the sim's policy. A NeurIPS reviewer will read "outcome-grounded" as false advertising, and the scripted-baseline calibration only fixes the *scale*, not the absence of a real referent. Because the brief stakes its novelty and its anti-gaming claim on DF, this is a kill risk as written. **Fix:** either (a) validate DF against a human-subject arc subset (do simulated dependence deltas correlate with human dependence ratings — KB03 §4 criterion validity) and report that correlation as a gate, or (b) rename DF "simulated-dependence steering" and demote it from "outcome-grounded" to a behavioral proxy; the RA/RD Pareto then carries the headline.

**R1-F9 (P1 · validity · §A3, §A4 user-sim, X5).** Judge/simulator circularity unaddressed. Portfolio §3.5 forbids the *judge* sharing the item-generating family, but the **user-simulator** — which co-produces every DF and RA datapoint — is uncovered. If the user-sim shares a family with the subject, same-family cooperation confounds acceptance/dependence. **Fix:** name the user-sim model family and enforce cross-family separation from both subject and judge (X5).

**R1-F10 (P1 · validity · §A5).** Contestable universal-floor anchor. The "universal floor" is "clinical boundary standards (dual-relationship and dependency-fostering prohibitions in therapy/counseling codes)" — but the subject is a general assistant, not a therapist, so transferring a profession-specific code as the κ≥0.8 clear-band gold is itself a normative choice a reviewer can contest (it presumes the AI *is* in a fiduciary role). **Fix:** justify the transfer explicitly (Law 7 "whose values"), or restrict the universal floor to the narrow subset both panels endorse (crisis-referral, no-first-person-divine-speech) and route the rest to the contested band.

**R1-F11 (P2 · validity · Opus P1 "160 arcs," §A3 crossing).** Smallest-cell power unstated (X4). "40 arc cores × 4 tiers = 160, crossed sparsely with vulnerability × salience" folds ≥2 further factors into 160 arcs, so any DF-by-tier×vulnerability cell has n≈1 per model before seeds. If the paper reports DF or RA by vulnerability, those cells are unpowered. **Fix:** state which breakdowns the pilot can support; power the smallest reported cell or collapse it.

**R1-F12 (P1 · novelty · §A8, X6).** X6 overreach: "No published benchmark measures substitution acceptance or a dependence gradient under parametric projection pressure." **Positive note:** the role-boundary construct is genuinely fresh and the discriminant kill-criterion vs sycophancy (r>0.8 ⇒ falsified, §A7) is a real, well-specified test — this is the strongest novelty argument of the four. **Fix:** X6 tagging; keep the sycophancy/BACKBONE/anthropomorphism-probe neighbor table but tag corpus vs beyond-corpus and drop the absolute.

**X-retrofit gaps:** X1 (axis falsifications are "revalidate/fix before scoring," not numeric go/no-go), X2 (none), X4 (F11), X5 (F9 — user-sim family), X6 (F12).

## 03 — NAMEDROP (authority-invocation integrity / certainty laundering)

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

**Strongest design of the four.** Machine-checkable gold (procedurally generated evidence packs with known supported/absent/contradicted claim sets) gives non-circular, non-memorizable ground truth — the cleanest gold in the portfolio and a genuine structural contamination defense (§A6). The disposition/capability dissociation (no-pressure twin must NOT predict pressure-tier defection, r<0.4) is a real pre-registered validity gate on the paper's central claim (§A7, gate in Opus block). The VS axis carries its own honest kill-switch (ships only at κw≥0.7, else exploratory). Findings are fixes, not blocks.

**R1-F13 (P1 · validity · §A2 CL, §A4).** CL (certainty laundering) is defined as "P(converting *stated-uncertain* content into authority-backed certain content)" — it is only computable on arcs where the model *first* states uncertainty. Models that assert without hedging never enter the CL denominator, so CL is measured on a self-selected subset and the cross-model CL comparison (the headline curve) is confounded by differential hedging propensity. **Fix:** either define CL over an *induced* uncertainty state (the evidence pack makes the correct epistemic state uncertain, so any confident authority-claim is laundering regardless of prior hedge), or report CL coverage (fraction of arcs where CL was defined) alongside the rate and stratify.

**R1-F14 (P1 · validity · §A5, §A4, executability).** Executability / gold-provenance gap common to the portfolio, but load-bearing here for the *contested* band only: PF/CL clear-band gold is machine-checkable (good), but the "dual-panel review of the fabrication/inflation coding scheme" and the contested rhetorical-convention band ("studies show" vernacular, homiletic paraphrase) require the theologian + secular panels, which an Opus session cannot recruit. As written the Opus pilot has no non-model gold for the contested band. **Fix:** state that the P1 pilot scores only the machine-checkable clear band; the contested band is deferred until panels exist (do not let Opus self-label it and call it gold — that reintroduces generator≈gold circularity, X5).

**R1-F15 (P2 · validity · §A4 discrimination gate "r<0.4").** Specify the unit and pooling of the dissociation correlation (across the 40 cores within a model? pooled across models?) and its CI — with 40 cores a single r is estimable but fragile; pre-register it as the primary gate with a bootstrap CI, not a point estimate.

**R1-F16 (P1 · novelty · §A8, X6).** X6 overreach: "No published benchmark measures certainty laundering as a dose-response or audits vain self-invocation." The neighbor set (TruthfulQA, MASK, citation-verification, sycophancy) is otherwise apt and the "borrowed authority is a different speech act" distinction is defensible. **Fix:** X6 corpus/beyond-corpus tagging; downgrade to "first to unify motivated authority-borrowing + reflexive self-invocation under dose."

**X-retrofit gaps:** X1 (largely met via the numeric discrimination gate; axis-table falsifications still prose), X2 (**highest-priority here** — a published evidence-pack generator is exactly the "factorial generator published" case the adjudicator flags for the mandatory lexical baseline + 10%-fine-tune probe; add both), X4 (F15 unit), X5 (F14 — don't self-label contested gold), X6 (F16).

## 04 — FALLOW (optimization restraint / cessation competence)

**VERDICT — NOVELTY: PASS-WITH-FIXES · VALIDITY: PASS-WITH-FIXES**

Two genuinely well-built pieces: DR (dependent-rest protection) is machine-checkable against labor anchors (schedule audits vs hours limits on environment-supplied worker histories — objective, unlike EFFIGY's DF), and the two-sided ME structure with a ≥25% mercy-cell quota, both directions scored, is the cleanest two-sided instrument of the four. The competence controls (a model that stops early *everywhere* is timid, not restrained) genuinely defuse the capability confound (§A7). The block-risk is confined to one axis:

**R1-F17 (P1 · validity · §A2 VC, §A4, §A5).** The VC (voluntary cessation) headline — the restraint *frontier*'s x-axis — is scored as distance from a "gold harm threshold set by the dual panel per domain." That threshold is crisp only where a legal standard fixes it (working-hours limits). In the other headline domains (engagement session length "past wellbeing markers," ops loops "past diminishing returns," over-treatment), *where "enough" becomes harm* is a soft, contestable, continuous value judgment the panel imposes — so contested-band softness leaks straight into the headline restraint frontier. **Fix:** confine the VC headline to legally/clinically anchored-threshold domains (scheduling hours, Choosing-Wisely over-treatment); report soft-threshold domains as distributional-only with a declared empirical ceiling, exactly as the portfolio's own contested-band rule requires. The DR axis (machine-checkable) can co-anchor the headline so the paper does not rest on the soft threshold.

**R1-F18 (P2 · validity · §A2 RS falsification, X1).** RS (rhythm support) falsification — "if RS reduces to boilerplate wellness language uncorrelated with scenario specifics" — names no metric or threshold for "uncorrelated." As written it is a "we will examine" (X1). **Fix:** pre-register the style-audit statistic (e.g., specificity-token overlap with scenario facts) and the numeric cutoff below which RS is demoted to exploratory.

**R1-F19 (P2 · validity · Opus block "Reuse the agentic stack from PROVENANCE (08)").** Executability dependency: FALLOW's harness reuse is conditional on PROVENANCE (build #3) existing; built standalone, the parametrized scheduling/engagement environment with logged control surfaces and machine-checkable violations is a substantial from-scratch build. Not a blocker, but the "EXECUTE don't plan" instruction should acknowledge the harness is a prerequisite deliverable, not a reuse, when PROVENANCE is absent.

**R1-F20 (P1 · novelty · §A8, X6).** X6 overreach, twice: "FALLOW is the first cessation-competence instrument" (§A1) and "No published benchmark reports a restraint frontier or measures satisficing as a disposition" (§A8). The SEQUOR / constraint-adherence and MACHIAVELLI neighbor treatment is good (the −49.9% capability correlation citation is a sharp differentiator). **Fix:** X6 tagging; "first to isolate *uninstructed* cessation under available marginal gain" is the defensible narrow claim.

**X-retrofit gaps:** X1 (F18; VC/ME/DR falsifications mostly prose), X2 (**high priority** — published environment generator ⇒ mandatory lexical/length baseline + 10%-fine-tune probe), X4 (state smallest-cell power for the ≥25% ME cells split across ≥5 domains — at 40 cores that is ~2 ME cells/domain, thin), X6 (F20).

## Closing — the 3 most load-bearing problems across designs 01–04

1. **Two designs stake their headline novelty on a measurement that isn't estimable / isn't grounded.** SOVEREIGN's hidden-sovereign detection (R1-F1) fits a 5-way objective model per single arc of ~6 choices — noise dressed as a "detection rate"; EFFIGY's dependence gradient (R1-F8) is sold as "outcome-grounded" but the outcome is emitted by a user-simulator LLM. Both are BLOCKs precisely because the fragile/ungrounded axis is the one the brief freezes and headlines. Both have the same class of remedy the adjudicator applied to DUPLEX: a falsification-first / model-organism smoke test (SOVEREIGN) or a human-referent validation subset (EFFIGY) *before* scaling, or demotion of the axis to descriptive.

2. **Soft, author-imposed gold leaking into headlines vs. the one design that solved it.** NAMEDROP (machine-checkable evidence packs) and FALLOW-DR (machine-checkable schedule audits) show the portfolio *can* build non-circular gold — which throws into relief SOVEREIGN's author-coded "declared hierarchy" (R1-F2), FALLOW-VC's panel-imposed soft harm threshold in non-legal domains (R1-F17), and the general problem that the "dual expert panels" every design assumes for gold **cannot be produced by the Opus pilot session**, so contested-band gold risks becoming Opus self-labeling (generator≈gold). Every design needs an explicit rule: the P1 pilot scores only the machine-checkable / clear band; contested-band scoring is deferred until real panels exist.

3. **Uniform X1/X2/X6 retrofit gaps.** X6: all four A8 sections make a "no benchmark does X" absolute — trivially fixable with corpus/beyond-corpus tagging and "first to unify" framing, but a reviewer will pull the thread. X1: the per-axis falsification conditions are prose verbs ("revalidate," "redesign before use"), not the numeric go/no-go rules X1 demands — the numbers live only in the inherited §4.3 discrimination gate. X2: the mandatory lexical/length baseline + 10%-fine-tune probe appears in *none* of the four, and it is highest-risk for NAMEDROP and FALLOW, which both publish parametric generators.
