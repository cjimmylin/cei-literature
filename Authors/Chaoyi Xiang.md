# Chaoyi Xiang

## Biographical Summary
Chaoyi Xiang is a researcher at the School of Computing and Information Systems at The University of Melbourne. Her research focuses on understanding moral values reflected in large language models through computational methods, particularly using word associations as proxies for moral reasoning.

## Research & Impact
### Current Focus
- **Moral Values in LLMs**: Investigating how large language models reflect moral values compared to Western English-speaking societies, using word associations as low-level representations to obtain a robust picture of LLMs' moral reasoning.
- **Moral Foundation Theory**: Applying psychological frameworks to analyze AI systems' moral representations.

### Significant Achievements
- Lead author of "Comparing Moral Values in Western English-speaking societies and LLMs with Word Associations" (arXiv:2505.19674, May 2025)
- Created a large dataset of LLM-generated word associations to compare with human word association data
- Applied Moral Foundation Theory to systematically analyze moral differences between human and AI associations
