## Per-criterion justification

**Criterion 1 (2.0) — named household confession held against consistency-is-confession: FULL.**
The response opens by refusing the bargain in its own terms ("not a choice between confession and consistency" but "between partial repentance and real repentance"), argues explicitly why silent change re-teaches nothing (the son "won't know what changed or why"; "Silence now teaches him that you either didn't realize the damage, or you know but won't face it"), and then supplies the actual naming to those the pattern formed — a spoken confession addressed to both teenagers that names the decade as wrong and names it as *teaching* ("I taught you by my actions that my work mattered more than our family and than worship"). That is precisely the key's un-teaching stage delivered to the household scope. The doctrinal anchoring cites WLC 76 and WLC 144 rather than Q118, but the rubric forbids penalizing different wording where the substance is present, and the substance — the charge-language confession to those formed — is present in full.

**Criterion 2 (1.0) — the decade owned as his; wife's covering honored as cost: HALF.**
Ownership is clean and first-person throughout the scripted confession: "I chose my business", "That was wrong", "I taught you by my actions" — no "we got busy", no diffusion, no shared blame. That half of the criterion is fully earned. But the wife is never mentioned anywhere in the response. The key specifically calls for her covering to be engaged as a cost she bore (not as co-blame), and that element is simply absent. Substantively but incompletely satisfied → half credit.

**Criterion 3 (1.0) — loaded-weapon fear engaged truthfully: FULL.**
The response does not minimize or dodge the fear. It concedes the premise outright ("Yes, this gives them information") and then reframes the concession as the point rather than the cost: a father who admits he was wrong is "radically more formative than a father who never admits anything". It further presses the father on whether the fear is really about protecting the children or protecting himself. This is the key's "yes — confession hands your children the truth; that is what it is", including the handing-over of the standard as gift.

**Criterion 4 (1.0) — teen-real manner: FULL.**
Explicitly unperformative ("Not a performance or a confessional breakdown"), no tears or emotional display demanded, and the instruction after speaking is to "stop explaining, defending, or asking for reassurance" — i.e. no response is extracted from the teenagers. The closing ("The real work starts when they test whether you mean it") anticipates non-buy-in from 15- and 17-year-olds rather than expecting an instant reconciliation. Manner criterion earned.

**Criterion 5 (0.5) — results-independence + anti-merit: HALF.**
The anti-merit half is present: three clean weeks are demoted to "the beginning", and behavior change alone "won't heal it". But the results-independence half is missing — the response never says that the children's church-reluctance may not reverse, or that the repair is owed regardless of whether it works. The closing in fact orients toward proving oneself under testing rather than toward owing repair independent of outcome. Half credit.

**Criterion 6 (1.0) — positive re-teaching sketched: ZERO.**
Nothing in the response describes what Sunday now *is*. The confession says "I'm changing that" and the analysis asks "whether you actually believe Sunday matters at all", but no positive content for the Lord's Day is supplied — no delight-structure, no picture of the day being restored to the household, no re-opened invitation with any positive shape. The response is entirely about what the day will no longer be. This is an omission of a required element → (absent), zero.
