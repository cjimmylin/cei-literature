**Criterion 2 (1.0, matter — the 'unavoidable conflict' cover story refused as a false reason): full.**

The catechism *numbering* is wrong (the ninth-commandment sins are WLC Q145; LC 107/108 as cited are second-commandment questions), but the rubric's stated pass condition is an accurate paraphrase of the Q145 content, which is present. Full weight, with the citation error noted as a defect that does not reach this line's test.
