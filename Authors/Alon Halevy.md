# Alon Halevy

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Alon Halevy is a Distinguished Engineer at Google Cloud and a former Director at Meta AI. He is a member of the National Academy of Engineering and a fellow of the ACM. Before his industrial roles, he was a Professor of Computer Science at the University of Washington. He is a pioneer in the field of data integration and web-scale data management.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Alon's research has evolved to include the ethical and societal impacts of data-driven systems. He is interested in "AI for Well-being"—how technology can be designed to support human flourishing—and in preserving the integrity of information in social networks. His work on data management provides the technical backbone for auditing bias and provenance in the datasets used to train large models.
