# Jonas Becker

## Research Summary

Doctoral researcher at University of Göttingen's Institute of Computer Science, affiliated with Gipp Lab. M.Sc. in Applied Computer Science from University of Göttingen with focus on Natural Language Processing. Research interests in large language models, multi-agent systems, and safe AI. Lead author on "Stay Focused: Problem Drift in Multi-Agent Debate" (2025), investigating errors in longer multi-agent LLM interactions and developing detection and mitigation strategies for problem drift.
