# Anjali Gopal

## Biographical Summary

Dr. Anjali Gopal is a Research Scientist at MIT and a researcher at Anthropic, specializing in the intersection of biosecurity and AI safety. She earned her PhD in Bioengineering from UC Berkeley, where her work focused on engineering principles for pandemic response. She is a recognized expert in biosecurity policy and has held several prestigious fellowships, including with the Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
Anjali’s primary research explores the biological risks posed by large language models and other emerging AI technologies. She investigates how AI can be misused to design or facilitate the spread of novel pathogens and works on developing policy recommendations for the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) and other regulatory bodies. She also works on wastewater monitoring and metagenomic sequencing for early detection of pandemic threats.

### Significant Achievements
She has been a vocal advocate for rigorous biosecurity guardrails in the development of foundation models. Her work has informed NIST's standards for AI and biotechnology, and she is a core member of the AI safety community focusing on catastrophic biological risks. Her doctoral work on PPE decontamination was critical during the COVID-19 pandemic.
