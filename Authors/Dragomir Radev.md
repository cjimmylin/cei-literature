# Dragomir Radev

## Biographical Summary

Dragomir Radev is the A. Bartlett Giamatti Professor of Computer Science at Yale University. He is a pioneering figure in natural language processing, particularly known for his foundational work on multi-document summarization, graph-based NLP methods, and scientific text processing. Previously a professor at the University of Michigan for nearly two decades, he has made significant contributions to computational linguistics and has mentored numerous PhD students who have become leaders in the field.

## Research Focus

Radev's research spans text summarization, question answering, and the application of graph-based algorithms to NLP problems. He developed LexRank, an influential graph-based method for sentence extraction in summarization. His recent work includes scientific NLP, particularly tools for processing academic papers and building knowledge graphs from scientific literature.

## Major Publications

- "LexRank: Graph-based Lexical Centrality as Salience in Text Summarization" (2004) - Foundational work on graph-based summarization
- "Centroid-based summarization of multiple documents" (2004)
- "MEAD: A platform for multidocument multilingual text summarization" (2004)
- "SciSummNet: A Large Annotated Corpus and Content-Impact Models for Scientific Paper Summarization" (2019)
