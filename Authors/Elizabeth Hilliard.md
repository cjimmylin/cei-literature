# Elizabeth Hilliard

## Biographical Summary

Elizabeth Hilliard is a researcher working on AI alignment and the measurement of AI systems' alignment with human flourishing. Her work focuses on developing frameworks and metrics for evaluating whether AI systems contribute positively to human wellbeing and thrive according to ethical principles.

## Research Interests

- AI alignment and safety
- Human flourishing metrics
- AI ethics and value alignment
- Evaluation frameworks for beneficial AI
- Philosophy of wellbeing in AI
