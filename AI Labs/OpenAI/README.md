# OpenAI

## Overview
OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company based in San Francisco. Its mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity.

## Location
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, USA
- **Region:** North America

## Key Models
See the full list of models: [[models|OpenAI Models]]

- **GPT-4o**: Latest flagship omnimodal model.
- **o1**: First in a new series of reasoning models.
- **GPT-3.5/4**: Foundational large language models.

## Alignment & Ethics
OpenAI pioneered RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) and regularly publishes system cards detailing safety evaluations and mitigations for its large-scale models.
