# Chee Wei Tan

## Biographical Summary

Chee Wei Tan is an Associate Professor at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. Previously, he served as an Associate Professor at the City University of Hong Kong. His research career has evolved from fundamental signal processing and optimization to the cutting edge of Generative AI and Large Language Model (LLM) intelligence.

## Research & Impact

### Current Focus
- **Generative AI Intelligence**: Investigating the capabilities, limitations, and evaluation metrics for LLMs.
- **LLM Alignment**: Developing methods to align AI outputs with human feedback, particularly in specialized domains like code generation.
- **Optimization**: Applying distributed optimization techniques to enhance the efficiency and performance of machine learning systems.

### Significant Achievements
- **Award-Winning AI Research**: Recognized for his work on aligning human feedback for code generation.
- **Multidisciplinary Impact**: Bridging signal processing, networking, and modern NLP.

## Major Publications
- **A Systematic Survey and Critical Review on LLM Evaluation**: A comprehensive study on how to measure the performance of modern language models.
- **Optimization in Networks**: Extensive work on power control and distributed systems.
