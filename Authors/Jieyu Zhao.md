# Jieyu Zhao

## Research Summary

Assistant Professor at USC specializing in fairness of ML/NLP models. PhD from UCLA (2017-2021), Postdoc at University of Maryland (2022-2023). Research analyzes potential stereotypes in machine learning models and develops computational approaches to enhance fairness in NLP applications. Evaluates and mitigates biases, hallucinations, privacy leakage, and safety concerns in LLMs. Recipient of 2020 Microsoft PhD Fellowship, EMNLP Best Long Paper Award 2017, and participant in 2021 Rising Stars in EECS. Over 7,700 citations.
