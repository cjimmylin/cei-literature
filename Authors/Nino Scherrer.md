# Nino Scherrer

## Research Summary

Nino Scherrer is a research scientist at Google in Zurich. Their research interests include rigorous approaches for language model evaluations and novel model architectures, drawing inspiration from causality, cognitive and social science, and synthetic data. Previously held positions at Patronus AI, FAR AI, Vector Institute, Mila, and Max Planck Institute. Completed Masters and Bachelors at ETH Zürich.
